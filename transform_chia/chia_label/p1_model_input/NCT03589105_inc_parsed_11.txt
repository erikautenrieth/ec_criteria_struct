Age >/=18 years at screening
Patients with relapsing forms of multiple sclerosis (RMS) with active disease defined by clinical [OR] or imaging features: (i) at least one clinical relapse over a 6-month period prior to screening; (ii) AND/OR at least one T1 gadolinium-enhancing lesion [OR] or new [OR] and/or enlarging T2 lesion [AND] as detected by brain Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) [AND] performed over a 3 months period prior to screening with [NOT] no change of Disease-Modifying Treatment(s) (DMT) compared to a previous MRI performed within 24 months before screening
