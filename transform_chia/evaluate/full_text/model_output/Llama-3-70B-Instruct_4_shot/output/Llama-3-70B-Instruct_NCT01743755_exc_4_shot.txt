Immunocompromised patients:
Patients with a known congenital [OR] acquired immunodeficiency.
Patients who received chemotherapy [AND] less than 6 weeks ago.
Patients who received corticosteroids [AND] in the last 6 weeks.
Patients who received immunosuppressive medication [AND] in the last 6 weeks (e.g. cyclosporin [OR], cyclophosphamide [OR], azathioprine [OR]).
Patients with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [AND] who are on systemic corticosteroids.
Patients who require intensive care unit treatment.
Patients with tropical worm infection.
Patients with dexamethasone intolerance.
Pregnant [AND] breastfeeding women.
