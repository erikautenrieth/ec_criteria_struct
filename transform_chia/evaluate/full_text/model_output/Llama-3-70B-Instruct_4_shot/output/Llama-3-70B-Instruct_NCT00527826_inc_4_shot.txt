Subject must have a diagnosis of COPD [AND] based on the American Thoracic Society (ATS)/ European Respiratory Society (ERS) criteria. 
Male [OR] female subjects, aged >=40 years [AND]. Females must be of Non Child Bearing Potential [AND]. The definition of Non Child Bearing Potential is as following: Females, regardless of their age, with functioning ovaries [AND] who have a current documented tubal ligation [OR] hysterectomy [OR], or females who are post-menopausal. 
Have diagnosed COPD stage III [OR] IV according to GOLD criteria: a baseline post-bronchodilator Forced Expiratory Volume, measured at 1 second (FEV1) <50% of predicted normal [AND] a baseline post- bronchodilator FEV1/Inspiratory Vital Capacity (IVC) ratio <70%. 
Have experienced at least 2 moderate [OR] severe COPD exacerbations leading to medical consultation (requiring oral corticosteroids [OR] increasing dosage of oral corticosteroids [OR] and/or antibiotics [OR] hospitalization) within the 12 months preceding Visit 1. 
Have stable COPD medication within 4 weeks prior to Visit 1 ([NOT] no new medication added [AND] [NOT] no dosage changes in medication). 
Current [OR] ex-smokers [AND] with a smoking history of at least 10 pack years ([NOT] number of pack years = [number of cigarettes per day / 20] x number of years smoked, e.g., 20 cigarettes per day for 10 years, or 10 cigarettes per day for 20 years). 
Are currently managed at home (outpatients) [AND] are ambulatory [AND] able to travel to the clinic [AND]. Subjects can be treated with all relevant COPD medication [AND]. This includes vaccines [OR] inhaled short-acting beta-2-agonists as needed [OR] short-acting or long-acting anticholinergics (tiotropium) [OR] systemic beta-2-agonists [OR] theophylline [OR] mucolytics [OR] antioxidants [OR] beta-1-agonists (for cardiovascular indication) [OR] non-invasive ventilation [OR] long term oxygen therapy [OR] and can have Cor Pulmonale. 
A signed [AND] dated written informed consent is obtained prior to participation. 
Able to comply with the requirements of the protocol [AND] be available for study visits over 52 weeks.
