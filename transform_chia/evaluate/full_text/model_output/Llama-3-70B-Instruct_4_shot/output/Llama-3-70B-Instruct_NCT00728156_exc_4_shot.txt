Contraindication to Clopidogrel 
Smoking ([AND] current smokers [OR] and patients who quit smoking less than six months) 
Malignancy([AND] diagnosed [OR] or under investigation) 
Haematological disorders ([AND] Anaemia [OR] malignancy [OR] bleeding disorders) 
Women of child-bearing potential 
Use of corticosteroids [OR] other antithrombotic agents(warfarin) 
Chronic liver disease ([AND] Cirrhosis [OR] malignancy [OR] and patients with more than twice the upper limit of liver function tests) 
Unable to consent. 
Use of other investigational study drugs within 1 year prior to study entry 
Previous participation in this study
