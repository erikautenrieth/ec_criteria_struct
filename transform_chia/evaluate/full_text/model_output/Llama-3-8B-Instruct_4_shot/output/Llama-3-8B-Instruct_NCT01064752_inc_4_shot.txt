Here is the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], and [NOT] inserted:

1. HIV infection [AND] with plasma [AND] CSF HIV RNA concentrations (using Roche Amplicor assay) > 1,000 copies/ mL [AND] (available after baseline LP).
2. Off antiretroviral therapy (ART) [AND] for > 6 weeks [AND] before the study [AND] and [NOT] no plans to begin treatment [AND] for the study duration. (The decision of whether or not a subject takes antiretroviral therapy will be made by the subject [AND] in consultation with his/her primary care provider [AND] prior to screening [AND] for this study.)
3. Predicted [OR] adherence to the medication.
4. Capable [AND] of providing informed consent.
5. > 18 years old.
6. CD4 cell counts [AND] > 150 cells/μL [AND] (though likely most, if not all, will be > 250 cells/μL).
7. When available, subjects will be screened [AND] for stability [AND] of blood CD4 [AND] and HIV RNA levels.
