Patients must have histologic proof [AND] of a malignancy suitable [AND] for radiation therapy. 
Patients must have received prior external beam radiation therapy [AND] to the region proposed [AND] for HDR brachytherapy treatment; evaluation of doses previously delivered [AND] to spinal cord/cauda equine [OR], pelvis [OR], and other critical structures (bowel [OR], kidneys [OR], rectum [OR]) will be taken into consideration. 
If [NOT] repeat irradiation would exceed any normal tissue constraint set by MSKCC Radiation Oncology Department dose constraint criteria, the patient will potentially be eligible. 
If the total prior radiation dose [AND] to the cord [OR] or pelvis exceeds 100 Gy BED equivalent, the patient will be potentially eligible, where a total of 100 BED Gy equivalent is determined by the biological equivalent dose (BED) calculation; BED = nd(1 + d/α/β), where n = number of fractions and d = dose per fraction; α/β is the constant for spinal cord late effect and equals 2 [Rades 2005, Nieder 2005, Sahgal 2012].
