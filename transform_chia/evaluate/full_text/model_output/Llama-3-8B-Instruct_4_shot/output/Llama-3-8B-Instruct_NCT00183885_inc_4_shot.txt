Unresectable, histologically confirmed hepatocellular carcinoma [AND] with evident disease limited to liver [AND] with [NOT] any other site of measurable disease. 
Tissue from tumor must be available [AND] this may be paraffin embedded tissue from previous biopsy/resection [OR] if it is not available, a repeat biopsy must be performed [AND] the requirement for biopsy may be waived if alpha-fetoprotein is greater than 500 ng/mL [AND] in the investigators opinion not explained by a concurrent hepatic inflammatory process. 
Patients must agree to have a 20 cc blood sample drawn in addition to routine labs with each cycle of chemotherapy [AND]. 
Patients must have measurable disease [AND] if prior radiation therapy was administered, measurable disease must be outside the radiation field [AND]. 
Patients must have a Zubrod performance status of 0 [OR], 1 [OR], or 2 [AND]. 
Patients must have a predicted life expectancy of at least 12 weeks [AND]. 
Patients must have a pre-treatment granulocyte count (i.e., segmented neutrophils + bands) of greater than or equal to 1,500/mm3 [AND] a hemoglobin level of greater than or equal to 9 gm/dl [AND] and platelet count greater than or equal to 50,000/mm3 [AND] the granulocyte requirement may be waived if in the investigator's opinion the lower count reflects hypersplenism with adequate bone marrow reserves [OR]. 
Patients must have adequate renal function as documented by a calculated creatinine clearance ≥ 60 [AND]. 
Patients must have adequate hepatic function as documented by a serum bilirubin less than or equal to 2x the institutional upper limit of normal [AND] regardless of whether patients have liver involvement secondary to tumor [AND] patients may not have ascites [OR] or the ascites must be responsive to diuretics [AND].
