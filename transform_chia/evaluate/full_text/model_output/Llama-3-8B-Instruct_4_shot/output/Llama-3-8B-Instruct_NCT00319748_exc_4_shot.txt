Had/have the following prior/concurrent therapy: 
Systemic corticosteroids (oral or injectable) [AND] within 7 days of first dose of 852A [NOT] (topical or inhaled steroids are allowed). 
Investigational drugs/agents [AND] within 14 days of first dose of 852A. 
Immunosuppressive therapy, including cytotoxic agents [AND] within 14 days of first dose of 852A [NOT] (nitrosoureas within 30 days of first dose). 
Drugs known to induce QT interval prolongation [OR] and/or induce Torsades de pointes [NOT] unless best available drug required to treat life-threatening conditions. 
Radiotherapy [AND] within 3 weeks of the first dose of 852A. 
Hematopoietic cell transplantation [AND] within 4 weeks of first dose of 852A. 
Evidence of active infection [AND] within 3 days of first dose of 852A. 
Active fungal infection [OR] or pulmonary infiltrates [AND] (prior treated disease stable for 2 weeks is allowable). 
Cardiac ischemia [OR] cardiac arrhythmias [OR] congestive heart failure [NOT] uncontrolled by medication. 
History of [OR] clinical evidence of [NOT] a condition which, in the opinion of the investigator, could confound the results of the study [OR] put the subject at undue risk. 
Uncontrolled intercurrent [OR] chronic illness. 
Active autoimmune disease [AND] requiring immunosuppressive therapy within 30 days. 
Active coagulation disorder [NOT] controlled with medication. 
Pregnant [OR] or lactating. 
Concurrent malignancy [NOT] (if in remission, at least 5 years disease free) except for localized (in-situ) disease [OR] basal carcinomas [OR] cutaneous squamous cell carcinomas that have been adequately treated. 
Any history of brain metastases [OR] any other active central nervous system (CNS) disease.
