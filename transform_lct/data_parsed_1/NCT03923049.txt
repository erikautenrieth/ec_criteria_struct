Inclusion Criteria:
1.Subjects older than 18 years of age  [AND] with high suspicion of cardiac sarcoidosis  [OR] and/or
biopsy proven extracardiac sarcoidosis and/or positive HRS  [OR] criteria will be enrolled.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Coronary artery disease
  2. Insulin dependent diabetes
  3. Claustrophobia
  4. Pregnancy/ [OR] nursing
  5. Presence of pacemaker  [OR] or automatic implantable cardioverter-defibrillator
  6. Impaired renal function (estimated glomerular filtration rate <45 ml/min/1.73 m2) will be excluded.
  7. Inability to undergo PET/ [OR] MRI due to any other condition.
