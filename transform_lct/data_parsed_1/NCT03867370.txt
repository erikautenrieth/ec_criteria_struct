Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Confirmed by histopathological  [OR] or cytological examination;
  2. The criteria for resectability is met
  3. Has at least one evaluable lesion  [AND] according to the RECIST 1.1 standard and has not received local treatment
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients who previously received anti-programmed death receptor-1 (PD-1) antibody, anti-programmed death ligand-1 (PD-L1) antibody, anti-programmed death ligand-2 (PD-L2) [OR]  antibody or anti-cytotoxic T lymphocyte-associated antigen-4 (CTLA-4) antibodies, including those who have participated in a JS001 clinical study;
  2. Patients who previously received any TACE, [OR]  radiofrequency ablation  [OR] and other liver cancer treatments;
  3. Patients with a history of gastroesophageal varices  [OR] or active cardia ulcers associated with a high risk of bleeding;
  4. Patients who have upper gastrointestinal hemorrhage within 1 year;
  5. Patients known to have fibrous layer HCC, sarcomatoid HCC  [OR] or mixed cholangiocarcinoma  [AND] and HCC; Other protocol defined inclusion/exclusion criteria could apply
