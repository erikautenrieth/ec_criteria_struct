Inclusion Criteria:
  1. DSM-V diagnosis of an Anxiety disorder (Generalized Anxiety Disorder, [OR]  Social Anxiety Disorder, [OR]  Panic Disorder, [OR]  Agoraphobia)
  2. Anxiety Sensitivity Index (ASI-3) total score > 29
  3. Overall Anxiety Severity and Impairment Scale (OASIS) score > 8
  4. Between 18-55 years of age
  5. Subject must be "treatment- [AND] seeking" and willing to enroll in the 10-week group-based Exposure Therapy
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Comorbid Bipolar Disorder, [OR]  Schizophrenia, [OR]  Anorexia  [OR] or Bulimia Nervosa, [OR]  or Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder
  2. Current Substance Use Disorder > moderate (within the past 6 months)
  3. Currently taking a psychotropic medication
  4. Currently receiving psychotherapy/ [OR] counseling for anxiety
  5. Currently being treated as an inpatient
  6. Active suicidal ideation with intent  [OR] or plan
  7. History of neurological, [OR]  cardiovascular or respiratory  [OR] problems (e.g., asthma, severe brain injury)
