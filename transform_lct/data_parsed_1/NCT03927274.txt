Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Histologically confirmed diagnosis of supratentorial WHO Grade III or IV Glioma (High Grade Glioma) [AND]  that has undergone surgical biopsy  [OR] or resection followed by adjuvant chemoradiotherapy, [AND]  that has evidence of recurrence  [OR] or progression based on imaging  [AND] studies and a stereotactic biopsy is indicated for confirmation of recurrence/ [OR] progression.
  -  18 years of age or older
  -  Karnofsky Performance Status 70-100;
  -  MRI demonstration of a stereotactically accessible enhancing  [OR] or predominantly non- enhancing mass  [AND] that does not require resection to relieve clinically significant mass effect;
  -  Participant understands the procedures and agrees to comply with the study requirements by providing written informed consent
  -  Adequate organ function as indicated in protocol
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Participant is mentally  [OR] or legally incapacitated at the time of the study;
  -  Known HIV( [OR] +) or has been diagnosed with AIDS
  -  Participation in another investigational drug study in the prior 4 weeks
  -  Positive pregnancy test in a female
  -  Patient, in the opinion of the investigator, is likely to be poorly compliant.
  -  Diffuse subependymal  [OR] or Cerebral Spinal Fluid (CSF) disease
  -  Tumors involving the cerebellum
  -  Tumor enhancement involving both hemispheres
  -  Active infection requiring treatment
  -  Unexplained febrile illness
  -  Radiation  [OR] or chemotherapy within 4 weeks of enrollment
  -  Systemic diseases associated with unacceptable anesthesia  [OR] or operative risk
  -  Inability to undergo magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)
