Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients who are > 18 years of age
  -  Patients with clinical signs, symptoms  [AND] and presentation consistent with acute thrombosis; onset of symptoms < 14 days
  -  Patients cleared medically for invasive endovenous procedures
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with IVC filter in area to be treated
  -  Patients who are < 18 years of age.
  -  Known anaphylactic reaction of radiographic contrast agents that cannot be pre- treated
  -  Imaging  [OR] evidence or other evidence that suggests the participant is not appropriate for mechanical thrombectomy intervention
  -  Female  [AND] who is pregnant  [OR] or nursing
  -  Concurrent participation in another investigational drug  [OR] or device treatment study
