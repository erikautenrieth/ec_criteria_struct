Inclusion Criteria:
  -  two parents in the home
  -  an adolescent  [AND] who is between 14 and 18 years of age
  -  marital distress
  -  youth reports of stress  [OR] or anxiety
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  two parents  [AND] in the home, but they are pursuing legal divorce  [OR] or separation
  -  parents are not willing to receive marital education/ [OR] support
  -  adolescents are clinically depressed  [OR] or report suicidal ideation at baseline
