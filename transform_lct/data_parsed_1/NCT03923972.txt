Inclusion Criteria:
  -  all adult patients with non-dialysis dependent chronic kidney disease, [OR]  peritoneal dialysis, [OR]  hemodialysis, [OR]  renal transplantation
  -  all active physicians in the department of nephrology involved in patient care
  -  all active nurses in the department of nephrology involved in patient care
  -  all heads of nephrology departments
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  children
  -  no physical disability making it impossible to conduct exercise training
  -  no mental disability  [OR] or language problem making it impossible to understand and fill in a questionnaire
