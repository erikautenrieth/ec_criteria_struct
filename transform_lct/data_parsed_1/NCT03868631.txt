Inclusion Criteria:
  -  non-smoking
  -  body mass index (BMI) 18.5-24.9
  -  recreationally active
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  participated in structured weight training during the previous 12 month
  -  vegetarian  [OR] or vegan
  -  presence of chronic disease
  -  pregnant, [OR]  postpartum up to six months, lactating, [OR]  or intention to become pregnant
  -  allergy to whey  [OR] or soy
  -  changes in body weight more than 10 pounds in the past three months
