Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients suffering from chronic pain due to tennis elbow for at least 3 months
  -  A clinical diagnosis of chronic lateral epicondylalgia,
  -  Pain associated with functional activities such as gripping  [OR] and pain with resisted contraction  [OR] of the wrist extensors or extensor carpi radialis brevis, [OR]  or with passive stretching of the wrist extensors.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Clinical diagnosis of chronic lateral epicondylalgia on both sides, [OR]  cervicothoracic spinal pathology, other upper limb musculoskeletal disorders  [OR] or neurological disorders
  -  Pregnancy
  -  Drug addiction defined as the use of cannabis, [OR]  opioids  [OR] or other drugs
  -  Previous  [OR] or current neurologic, [OR]  musculoskeletal  [OR] or mental illnesses defined as an on-going systemic condition
  -  Lack of ability to cooperate
  -  Current use of medications that may affect the trial e.g. analgesics, [OR]  anti- inflammatory drugs
  -  Consumption of alcohol, [OR]  caffeine, [OR]  nicotine  [OR] or painkillers throughout the study period
  -  Past history of chronic pain, neurological  [OR] or psychiatric conditions
  -  Participation in other pain trials throughout the study  [OR] period and one month prior to participation
  -  Participation in more than two sessions of muscle training exercises per week involving the upper limb  [OR] and/or lower back
  -  Evidence of other sources of elbow pain such as exacerbation of pain with neck movement  [OR] or manual examination
  -  Sensory disturbances
  -  History of fractures
  -  Elbow surgery
  -  Contraindications to the use of TMS
