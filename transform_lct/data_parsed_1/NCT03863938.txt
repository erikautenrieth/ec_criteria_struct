Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Healthy adult males aged ≥ 19 years  [AND] and ≤ 50 years
  -  Body weight  [AND] of ≥ 55.0 kg  [AND] and ≤ 90.0 kg, with body mass index (BMI) of ≥ 18.0 kg/m2  [AND] and ≤ 27.0 kg/m2 at the time of screening
  -  Helicobacter pylori negative
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Presence  [OR] or history of clinically significant diseases
  -  Presence  [OR] or history of gastrointestinal disorder (gastric ulcer, [OR]  GERD, [OR]  Crohn's disease, etc.)
  -  Hypersensitivity  [OR] to drugs containing study drug  [OR] or proton pump inhibitor  [OR] and other drugs (aspirin, [OR]  antibiotics, etc.) or history of clinically significant hypersensitivity
  -  Serologic test positive
  -  Abnormal obstacle to insertion  [AND] and maintenance of pH meter catheter
  -  History of drug abuse
  -  Excessive caffeine  [OR] intake or persistent alcohol intake
  -  Not use of a medically acceptable method of contraception
