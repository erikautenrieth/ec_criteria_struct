Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Selected will be subjects to meeting the following criteria: male  [OR] and female subjects age ≥ 20 years old, diagnosed with Osteoarthritis stages III and IV according to the American College of Rheumatology, manifesting pain, [OR]  functional impairment for more than 3 months, able to wander with  [OR] or without canes.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Will be excluded from study subjects with history of cancer, unstable cardiovascular disease, uncontrolled hypertension, [OR]  morbid obesity, that is, BMI ≥ 40, terminal illness, [OR]  cerebrovascular disease, [OR]  dementia, [OR]  heart pacemaker, superior  [OR] or inferior limb fractures.
