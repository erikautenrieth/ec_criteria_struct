Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age ≥18 years
  -  No systemic diseases  [OR] or pregnancy.
  -  Self-reported smoking ≤ 10 cigarettes/day.
  -  Full-mouth plaque score (FMPS) [AND]  and full-mouth bleeding score (FMBS) ≤ 10% (measured at four sites per tooth).
  -  Presence of single RT1 buccal gingival recessions ≥ 2 mm of depth located in the anterior area of the upper jaw (central  [OR] and lateral incisors, [OR]  canine, [OR]  first and second pre-molars, [OR]  first molar) and associated with aesthetic complains  [OR] and or dental sensitivity.
  -  Presence of NCCL associated with recession
  -  No history of mucogingival  [OR] or periodontal surgery at experimental sites.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Prosthetic crown at experimental teeth.
  -  Gingival recessions presenting minimal amount (< 1mm) of apico-coronal keratinized tissue (KT) extension apical to recession area.
