Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age 24-55
  -  Former professional football play from 1960 to present.
  -  Prior completed the first Health and Wellness Questionnaire (Q1)
  -  "Healthy" subjects: ability to give informed consent, comply with study procedures, and have a Telephone-Montreal Cognitive Assessment (T-MoCA) score of >20.
  -  "Afflicted" participants: must meet criteria for at least one of the following subjective afflictions based on Q1 responses: 1) Cardiovascular Dysfunction, 2) Neurocognitive Disease, 3) Chronic Pain, and/or 4) Sleep Apnea
  -  "Afflicted" participants must be able to give informed consent or have a legal guardian present to provide informed consent (if participants demonstrate on T-MoCA score <19) and to comply with study procedures.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Prior metallic device implants such as cardiac pacemakers/ [OR] defibrillators  [OR] or perfusion pumps that cannot be removed.
  -  Ferromagnetic implants  [OR] or objects that cannot be removed such as aneurysm clips, [OR]  surgical clips, [OR]  prostheses, [OR]  artificial hearts, [OR]  valves with steel parts, metal fragments, [OR]  shrapnel, [OR]  tattoos  [OR] near the eye, or steel implants  [OR] or jewelry
  -  Body weight >450 lbs
  -  Inability to complete the telephone screener
  -  Low T-MoCA score (<20) and absence of an available legal guardian who can accompany the participant through all phases of the study.
  -  Significant behavioral  [OR] or functional disability due to cognitive impairment
