Inclusion Criteria:
  -  All patients with microcytic hypochromic anemia ( according to WHO , MCV  [AND] less than 80 fl and HB level below the lower limit of normal value specified by age and gender .
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Beta thalassemia major patients .
  -  Chronic diseases  [OR] or infections .
  -  Lead poisoning .
  -  Sideroblastic anaemia .
