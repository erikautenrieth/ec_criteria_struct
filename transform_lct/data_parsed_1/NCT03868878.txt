Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age greater than 60 years;
  -  is not engaged in physical exercise programs for at least one year;
  -  do not  [AND] present severe cardiovascular disease, [OR]  kidney disease, [OR]  liver disease  [OR] or limiting cognitive  [OR] or neuromuscular disease and;
  -  does not present visual  [OR] or auditory inability.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  failing  [AND] to perform any of the evaluations suggested during the intervention protocol and;
  -  absence in five or more Capoeira training sessions.
