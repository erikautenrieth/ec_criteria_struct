Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Positive hepatitis b surface antigen  [OR] or hepatitis b virus DNA > 0.5 year;
  2. Age from 18 to 55 years old;
  3. Serum total bilirubin level > 10 times upper limit of normal;
  4. Prothrombin time activity  [OR] < 40% or prothrombin time international ratio > 1.5；
  5. Do not receive nucleotide/ [OR] nucleoside analogues treatment in the past half year.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Other active liver diseases;
  2. Hepatocellular carcinoma  [OR] or other malignancy;
  3. Pregnancy  [OR] or lactation;
  4. Human immunodeficiency virus infection  [OR] or congenital immune deficiency diseases;
  5. Severe diabetes, [OR]  autoimmune diseases;
  6. Other important organ dysfunctions;
  7. Using glucocorticoid;
  8. Patients can not follow-up;
  9. Investigator considering inappropriate.
