Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Pregnant women  [AND] at least 18 years of age
  -  Planned delivery via C-section
  -  Pfannenstiel skin incision
  -  Lower uterine segment transverse hysterotomy
  -  English speaking
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Major intra-operative  [OR] or post-operative complication such that clinician recommends patient should not receive non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs  [OR] or that patient requires acetaminophen to treat fever (ie suspected endometritis)
  -  Unplanned surgery (hysterectomy, bowel/ [OR] bladder repair)
  -  Allergy  [OR] or contraindication to study medication
  -  Non-English speaking
  -  Inability to provide informed consent
  -  History of opioid, other illicit substance, [OR]  or alcohol use disorder either before or during pregnancy
  -  Severe renal  [OR] or hepatic impairment
