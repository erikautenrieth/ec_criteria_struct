Inclusion Criteria:
  -  preeclampsia
  -  healthy pregnancy
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  pregnant women with any systemic condition (such as chronic hypertension, [OR]  diabetes mellitus, [OR]  thyroid diseases, liver  [OR] and kidney diseases)
  -  women  [AND] with a history of drug use throughout pregnancy
  -  history of medication for PE treatment at the time of first admission
  -  patients who had fetal congenital abnormalities  [OR] or genetic syndromes
  -  multiple gestation
  -  active labor
