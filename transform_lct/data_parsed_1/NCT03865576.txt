Inclusion Criteria:
  -  ICU patients suspected of having an abnormality of ICP undergoing monitoring with commercially-available, FDA-approved instrumentation.
Exclusion Criteria:
History of:
  -  Glaucoma  [OR] or optic hypertension
  -  Severe orbital  [OR] or ophthalmic injury
  -  Orbital  [OR] or intraocular surgery that might interfere with orbital compliance
  -  Congenital malformations of the eye  [OR] and/or orbit that might interfere with orbital compliance  [OR] or orbital vascular anatomy
