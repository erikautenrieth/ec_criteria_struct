Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Subject diagnosed with lipedema according to the criteria of revised Wold.
  -  Subject did not participate in any exercise program within the last three months.
  -  No pregnancy  [OR] / breastfeeding
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History of acute infection, [OR]  cancer, inflammatory rheumatic  [OR] / connective tissue diseases
  -  History of cardiovascular  [OR] or musculoskeletal problems that may prevent them from participating in the exercise program
