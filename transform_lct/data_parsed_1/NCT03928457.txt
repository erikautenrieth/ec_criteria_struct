Inclusion Criteria:
  -  preterm neonates born at 26 to 34 weeks of gestational age
  -  signed written informed consent form
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  infants infected
  -  infants suffering from neurological disorders
  -  serious heart, [OR]  respiratory, [OR]  digestive  [OR] or metabolic diseases
  -  infants born from mothers aged  [OR] less than 18 years old or deprived of their parental rights
  -  non covered by national health insurance
