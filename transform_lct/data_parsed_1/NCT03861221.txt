Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Female, older than 35 years. Already have mammography, [OR]  or other standard exams (ultrasound, [OR]  MRI, etc). Could participate this trial within 2 weeks after mammography. Have the ability of consent inform.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  During pregnancy  [OR] or lactation period. Patients that may not be capable of prone position, such as patients with scapulohumeral periarthritis, [OR]  cardiac pacemaker  [OR] or have recent cardiac surgery.
Lymphoma patients that under therapy. Severe scoliosis.
