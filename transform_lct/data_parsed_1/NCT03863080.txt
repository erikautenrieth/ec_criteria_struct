Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Male  [OR] or female ≥ 18 years of age.
  2. Myasthenia Gravis Foundation of America (MGFA) [AND]  Class II-IVa and likely not in need of a respirator for the duration of the study as judged by the Investigator.
  3. QMG score ≥12 at Screening  [AND] and Baseline.
Other, more specific inclusion criteria are defined in the protocol.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Use of rituximab, [OR]  belimumab, [OR]  eculizumab  [OR] or any monoclonal antibody for immunomodulation within 6 months prior to first dosing.
  2. Immunoglobulins  [OR] given by SC, [OR]  IV (IVIG) [OR] , or intramuscular route, or plasmapheresis/plasma exchange (PE) within 4 weeks before Screening.
  3. Thymectomy performed < 12 months prior to screening.
  4. Total IgG level <6 g/L (at screening).
  5. Absolute neutrophil count <1500 cells/mm3(at screening).
Other, more specific exclusion criteria are defined in the protocol
