Inclusion Criteria:
Diabetic nephropathy, because of type 2 diabetes, undergoing hemodialysis  [AND] and subjects with chronic kidney disease of other causes undergoing hemodialysis.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Active infection that increases the risk of secondary infection  [AND] in the skin where the vesicles containing interstitial fluid have been emptied of interstitial fluid with a fine needle  [AND] and the bladder roof is intact.
  -  Treatment with Warfarin due to increased risk of Calciphylaxis (calcific uremic arteriolopathy, [OR]  CUA)
