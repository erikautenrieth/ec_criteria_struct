Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Primary liver cancer [AND] patients (ICD 10 is C22.0) and Barcelona stage (BCLC stage) 0-A.
 2. At least 20 years of age.
 3. [NOT] No mental illness.
 4. Patients who can communicate in Mandarin [OR] or Taiwanese.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Don't know himself condition.
 2. Unconscious patients.
 3. Patients with liver cancer resection [OR] or partial liver resection were performed within 3 months.
