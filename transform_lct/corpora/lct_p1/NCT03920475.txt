Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with uni- [OR] or bipolar depression, diagnosed according to the criteria of the DSM-IV via MINI 6.0 diagnostic tool.
 - At the time of inclusion in the study, the symptoms must meet at least the requirements of a moderately severe depression, defined by a minimum score of ≥ 22 on the Montgomery-Åsberg Depression Scale.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with severe somatic, [OR] rheumatic, [OR] endocrine [OR] or neurological comorbidities. This includes in particular neurological disorders associated with cognitive disorder, severe liver, [OR] kidney [OR] and cardiac diseases.
 - Patients, who are being treated permanently with anti-inflammatory [OR] or immunosuppressive drugs (e.g. corticosteroids or alpha [OR] / beta-a(nta)gonists, [OR] immuno suppressant drugs).
 - Patients with severe psychiatric disorders (Axis I) such as schizophrenia, [OR] dementia, [OR] ADHD, [OR] obsessive-compulsive disorder, current alcohol, [OR] drugs [OR] or drug addiction.
 - Patients who have already been treated unsuccessfully with sertraline [OR] and all alternate medications allowed in the study.
 - Pregnant [OR] or lactating (breast feeding) women.
