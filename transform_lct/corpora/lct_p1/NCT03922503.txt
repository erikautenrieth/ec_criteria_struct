Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Stage III periodontitis  [AND] patient having at least one tooth  [AND] with one wall  [OR] or combined1- to 2-wall intraosseous defect ≥ 3 mm deep (assessed by trans-gingival probing, [OR]  radiographic examination) with clinical attachment level (CAL) ≥ 5mm and pocket depth (PD) ≥ 6 mm.
  -  Defect [NOT] not extending to a root furcation area
  -  Vital teeth
  -  [NOT] Non-smokers.
  -  [NOT] No history of intake of antibiotics  [OR] or other medications affecting the periodontium in the previous 6 months.
  -  [NOT] No periodontal therapy carried out in the past 6 months.
  -  Able to sign an informed consent form.
  -  Patients age between 25 and 50 years old.
  -  Patients who are cooperative, motivated, and hygiene conscious.
  -  Systemically free according to Cornell Medical Index
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pregnancy  [OR] or breast feeding
  -  The presence of an orthodontic appliance
  -  Teeth mobility greater than grade I
