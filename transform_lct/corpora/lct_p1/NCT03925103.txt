Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Clinical NSCLC early stage (stage I-II).
  -  Both forced expiratory volume in one second (FEV1) [AND]  and diffusion capacity of carbon monoxide (DLCO)>60%
  -  Both predicted postoperative (PPO) FEV1  [AND] and PPO DLCO >35%
  -  American Society of Anesthesia (ASA) score<=2
  -  Body mass index (BMI) >18 <28
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Clinical NSCLC stage> II
  -  History of Neoadjuvant chemotherapy  [OR] or radiotherapy
  -  Radiologic evidence of extensive pleural adhesions.
  -  Age <18  [OR] or >80 years.
  -  Patients with previous pleurodesis  [OR] or thoracotomy in the affected hemithorax.
  -  Patients who will undergo surgical lung resection other than lobectomy.
  -  Patients with severe comorbidity contraindicating lobectomy.
  -  Patients [NOT] refusal  [OR] or [NOT] noncompliance to general surgery  [OR] and one-lung ventilation.
