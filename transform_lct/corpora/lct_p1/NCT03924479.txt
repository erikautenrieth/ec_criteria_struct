Inclusion Criteria:
Healthy subjects: Adults (≥21 years of age) in the [NOT] absence of a history of HFpEF  [OR] or HFrEF, pulmonary, [OR]  neurologic, [OR]  orthopedic, or other diseases affecting the neuromuscular system.
Controls will be matched for age and sex.
All HFpEF patients will be managed by their primary care physician or cardiologist with additional review and oversight by Dr. Borlaug (Co-Investigator) prior to enrollment to ensure adequacy of inclusion and exclusion criteria and that participation in exercise testing and resistance exercise training is safe.
  -  Clinical diagnosis of HFpEF.
  -  Patients with a history stable ([NOT] no medication changes in past 6 weeks (w/duration of diagnosis >6 months).
  -  New York Heart Association class I-III.
  -  Current [NOT] non-smokers with <15 pack year history.
  -  [NOT] Non-pregnant women, [AND]  and individuals who are able to exercise (i.e. [NOT] without orthopedic limitations  [OR] or neuromuscular disorders).
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  history of dangerous arrhythmias
  -  body mass index >35 kg/m2
  -  current smokers  [OR] and/or smoking history >15 pack years
  -  pregnant women
  -  uremia, history of allergy to iodides
  -  impaired renal function
  -  creatinine value greater than or equal to 1.3 mg/dL (via clinical record within the past 6 months)
  -  diagnosis of liver disease
  -  individuals who are [NOT] not able to engage in exercise
