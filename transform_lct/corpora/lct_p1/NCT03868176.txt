Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient with germ cell tumor.
  -  Patient with a mobile phone.
  -  Patient on surveillance.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Testicular cancer [NOT] without germ cell component.
  -  Prior history of other malignancy except for: cutaneous cancers excluding melanoma, superficial bladder tumor, localized prostate cancer  [AND] with undetectable PSA.
  -  Cognitive condition that would [NOT] preclude patient's understanding and completion of study procedures.
