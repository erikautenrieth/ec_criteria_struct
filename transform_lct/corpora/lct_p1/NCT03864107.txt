Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Men  [OR] and women aged 18-70 years
  -  BMI 18.5-50 kg/m2
  -  [NOT] Not taking antibiotics  [OR] and antimicrobial drugs for at least three months

Excusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] No cardiometabolic  [OR] (e.g. heart disease, high blood pressure) or inflammatory illness
  -  Smokers (including the use of vaporisers  [OR] and e-cigarettes)
