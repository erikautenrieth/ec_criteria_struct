Inclusion Criteria:
 - Male
 - Age 25 to 65 years old.
 - Type 2 diabetes.
 - In Metformin [OR] or sulfonylureas
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Treatment with other antidiabetics
 - Osteopososis [OR] or gastrointestinal disease
 - Smoking
 - Long term steroid treatment
 - Weight change more than 3 kg within the last 3 months.
 - Overweight [OR] or intestinal surgery
