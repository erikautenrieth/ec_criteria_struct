Inclusion Criteria:
 - Women called back from a screening mammography [AND] by either FFDM [OR] or tomosynthesis with soft tissue abnormalities including masses, [OR] asymmetries, [OR] focal asymmetries [OR] or architectural distortion with [OR] or [NOT] without calcifications. Patients will be questioned regarding the possibility of pregnancy [AND] and will need a negative pregnancy test prior the study intervention.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Age <30 years old
 - Screening mammography with only calcifications abnormalities
 - Male patients
 - Pregnant [OR] or lactating patients
 - Patients with any allergy to iodinated contrast
 - Patients with eGFR < 45
 - Patients that may be treated with radioactive iodine
