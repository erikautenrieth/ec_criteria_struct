Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adults [AND] with biopsy proven metastatic cervical nodal squamous cell carcinoma
 - CT of the neck [AND] with contrast that does [NOT] not confidently identify a primary oropharyngeal site of disease
 - Planned standard of care 18F-FDG PET CT examination
 - Planned standard of care exam under anesthesia [AND] with oropharyngeal surgical biopsy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnant women
 - Known allergy to FDG, [OR] fluciclovine, [OR] or iodine-based contrast agents
 - Severe renal dysfunction (glomerular filtrate rate [within 30 days] less than 30)
 - Inability to tolerate lying supine, relatively motionless for up to 1 hour
