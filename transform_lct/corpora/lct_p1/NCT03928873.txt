Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Older than 20 years of age
 2. Grade 2~3 trigger finger based on the Quinnell classification
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Previous treatment by physical therapy, [OR] local corticosteroid injection, [OR] or surgical release for trigger finger before the study
 2. The presence of a musculoskeletal disease [OR] or previous nerve injuries at the upper extremities
 3. Multiple trigger finger; local infection; malignancy; inflammatory arthritis; cardiac arrhythmia [OR] or cardiac pacemaker; [OR] and pregnancy.
