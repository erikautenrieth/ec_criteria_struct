Inclusion Criteria:
  -  men  [OR] and women aged over 18 years
  -  signed informed consent form
  -  ametropia below 3 diopters
  -  ability and willingness to follow instructions
for healthy volunteers:
  -  normal ophthalmologic findings
for primary open angle glaucoma patients (POAG):
  -  diagnosed POAG
  -  MD <= 10 dB
for age-related macular degeneration (AMD):
  -  diagnosed dry AMD
  -  diagnosed stage II  [OR] or stage III AMD
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Presence of any abnormalities [NOT] preventing reliable measurements
  -  Ocular inflammation  [OR] and ocular disease interfering with the study aims
  -  Use of photosensitizing medication (phototoxic drugs, [OR]  photoallergic drugs) in the 3 months preceding the study
  -  Presence of any condition with the possibility of causing photosensitivity, including systemic lupus erythematosus SLE, [OR]  Porphyria, [OR]  Vitiligo, [OR]  Xeroderma Pigmentosum  [OR] and Albinism
  -  Presence of any form of epilepsy
  -  Ocular surgery in the 3 months preceding the study
  -  Pregnancy, planned pregnancy  [OR] or lactating
  -  Any medical or surgical history, disorder  [OR] or disease such as acute  [OR] or chronic severe organic disease
