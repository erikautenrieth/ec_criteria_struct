Inclusion Criteria:
 - Moderate [OR] to severe OSA (AHI>15 )
 - Age 18 years or older
 - BMI<40,
 - Baseline polysomnogram [AND] completed within the last 12 months with no significant weight changes (<10 lbs. weight fluctuation).
 - The baseline sleep study must be diagnostic with adequate sample
Exclusion Criteria:
 - BMI ≥ 40
 - pregnancy
 - Central sleep apnea (CSA) - 5 or more central apneas per hour
 - Psychoactive medications that influence sleep
 - Chronic opioid use
 - Other sleep disorders
 - Active temporomandibular joint (TMJ) Disorders
 - Fibromyalgia [AND] with significant facial pain
 - [NOT] Inability to protrude the mandible beyond 5 mm
 - [NOT] Inability to follow-up after surgery
 - Uncontrolled psychiatric disorders
 - Dental disorders including loose teeth [OR] and periodontal disease
