Inclusion Criteria:
• Couples with primary [OR] / secondary infertility [AND] who are planned to undergo IVF [OR] / ICSI
treatment in one of the above mentioned centers and who agree to take part in this study.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Couples planned for oocyte [OR] and sperm donation
 - Couples in whom the female partner has a history of:
 - Chemotherapy [OR] or radiation which impacts the ovarian reserve
 - Surgery at the ovaries [OR] / adnex region
 - Tubal ligation
 - Reversal of tubal ligation
 - Endometriosis
 - Couples in whom the male partner has a history of:
 - Chemotherapy [OR] / Radiation which impacts the semen result
 - Surgery at the testicles
 - Vasectomy
 - Surgery for reversal of vasectomy
