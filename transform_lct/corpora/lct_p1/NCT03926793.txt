Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Adult (males  [OR] and females) aged  [AND] 18 to 75 years (inclusive) with pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH)
  2. A current diagnosis of symptomatic PAH classified by one of the following:
       1. Idiopathic (IPAH) [OR]  or heritable pulmonary arterial hypertension (HPAH)
       2. PAH associated with one of the following connective tissue diseases (CTDs):
          limited scleroderma, [OR]  mixed connective tissue disease  [OR] or overlap syndrome, [OR]  or systemic lupus erythematosus
       3. PAH associated with anorexigen  [OR] or methamphetamine use
  3. World Health Organization (WHO) [OR] /New York Heart Association (NYHA) functional class II-IV symptomatology
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Clinically significant systemic hypertension  [OR] or hypotension
  2. History of left-sided heart disease  [OR] and/or clinically significant cardiac disease
  3. History of decompensated right heart failure within 30 days of screening (e.g., hospitalization for PAH  [OR] or the need to add an additional PAH medication)
