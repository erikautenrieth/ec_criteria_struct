Inclusion Criteria:
 - ≥1 Primary Anterior Teeth with early carious lesion [AND] (Class V) of ICDAS scores 1-2.
 - Subjects whose parents are willing and able to maintain good oral hygiene for their children [AND] and attend study visits.
 - Subjects with Saliva [AND] of normal flow rates (30-60sec) and with healthy saliva pH 6.75-7.25. 
 - Patients aged between 3-5 years.
 - Subjects with a minimum of 1 and a maximum of 12 primary anterior teeth with early carious lesions [AND] Class V of ICDAS score 1-2.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Primary anterior teeth in which carious lesions [AND] are scored with ICDAS ≥3.
 - Subjects on medication that affects the salivary flow rate.
 - Subjects with evidence of reduced salivary flow [OR] (>60 sec) or with acidic saliva pH˂5.8.
