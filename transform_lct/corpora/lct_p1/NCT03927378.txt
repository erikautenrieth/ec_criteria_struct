Inclusion Criteria:
 - Parturients with age ≥18 years;
 - Presence of prenatal depression (EPDS score ≥10);
 - Provide written informed consents.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - History of psychiatric disease [OR] (schizophrenia) or communication barriers that [NOT] prevent normal communication before childbirth;
 - Severe complications during pregnancy (such as severe preeclampsia, [OR] placenta accreta, [OR] or HELLP [Hemolysis, Elevated Liver enzymes and Low Platelets] syndrome);
 - ASA physical status classification ≥III;
 - Presence of contraindications to ketamine, including refractory hypertension, severe cardiovascular disease [OR] (heart function classification ≥III), or hyperthyroidism;
 - Refuse to participate.
