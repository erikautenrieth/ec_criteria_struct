Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with structural heart disease  [OR] and implantable cardioverter defibrillator (ICD)
  -  Age > 18 years
  -  either
       1. Recurring symptomatic monomorphic ventricular tachycardia that requires ICD intervention (e.g. shock or [NOT] anti-tachycardia stimulation). At least 3 episodes  [OR] within the 3 months prior to inclusion or
       2. induction of symptomatic monomorphic ventricular tachycardia that requires ICD intervention (e.g. shock  [OR] or [NOT] anti-tachycardia stimulation). Induction triggered by ICD or during electrophysiology studies (EPS) [OR]  or both, a) and b)
  -  Refractory to antiarrhythmic combination therapy
  -  Beside the cardiac conditions: [NOT] No competing illness that would additionally limit the life expectancy to less than 6 months
  -  [NOT] No prior radiation therapy in the thorax area
  -  [NOT] No pregnancy  [AND] and [NOT] no active breastfeeding
  -  Ability to consent and consent to study participation
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  ICD electrode malfunction of ICD readings outside reference range
  -  [NOT] Lack of evidence of a myocardial scar (Computer tomography  [OR] or magnetic resonance tomography for MRI-capable ICD aggregates  [OR] or electrophysiological measurement)
  -  [NOT] No possible induction of symptomatic monomorphic ventricular tachycardia [NOT] non-persistent  [OR] or persistent with delivery of ICD therapies such as antitachycardic pacing  [OR] or shock
  -  Contraindication to radiosurgery (e.g. precise target volume definition [NOT] not possible due to image artifacts created from a left ventricular assist device [LVAD])
  -  Inability to consent or missing or withdrawn consent
