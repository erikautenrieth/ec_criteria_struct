Inclusion Criteria:
 - Men [OR] and women aged 18 - 65
 - Generalized HSD (G-HSD) defined with Beighton score cut-off ≥ 5 for women [OR] up to the age of 50 years and ≥ 4 for those [OR] above 50 years and all men, [OR] or Historical HSD (H-HSD) if the Beighton score was 1 point below the age and sex-specific cut-off AND the five-part questionnaire (5PQ) was positive (= at least two positive items).
 - Present with one or more secondary symptomatic musculoskeletal manifestations, defined as either
 1. musculoskeletal pain [OR] in minimum one shoulder for at least three months and/or
 2. recurrent joint dislocations [OR] or joint instability [NOT] without a reported history of trauma defined as either a) minimum three atraumatic dislocations in same shoulder, b) minimum two atraumatic dislocations [OR] in two different joints (minimum one in the shoulder) occurring at different times, or c) medical confirmation of joint instability in minimum two joints (minimum one in the shoulder) [NOT] not related to trauma.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Clinically suspected referred pain from the cervical spine
 - Systemic inflammatory rheumatic diseases
 - Connective tissue diseases (e.g. Marfans, [OR] Stickler's [OR] or Loeys Dietz syndromes, [OR] Ehlers-Danlos Syndromes except hypermobile type)
 - Neurological diseases
 - Pregnancy [OR] or childbirth [OR] within the past year or planning to get pregnant during the study period
 - Shoulder surgery within the past year
 - Steroid injection in the affected shoulder within three months
 - [NOT] Inability to speak and understand Danish.
 - Unable to comply with protocol
