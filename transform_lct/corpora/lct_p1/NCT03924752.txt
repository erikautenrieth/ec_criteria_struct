Inclusion Criteria:
 - Between 18-85 years of age
 - Subjects should be capable of [AND] walking, [OR] ascending/ [OR] descending stairs and ramps with full capability in lower extremity passive range of motion (knee flexion contracture of >10 degrees, knee flexion ROM < 90 degrees, hip flexion contracture [AND] < 25 degrees, and ankle plantar flexion contracture of >15 degrees).
 - Subjects must be able to walk [AND] for at least 5 minutes and willing and able to participate over a 1-6 hours experiment with breaks and rest enforced regularly and as needed.
 - Subjects must be able to transfer (sit-to-stand [AND] and stand-to-sit) with no external support (arm rests OK) and to ambulate over small slopes [AND] (3 degrees) and a few steps (6 steps).
Exclusion Criteria:
 - History of neurological injury, [OR] gait pathology, [OR] or cardiovascular condition that would limit your ability to ambulate for multiple hours.
