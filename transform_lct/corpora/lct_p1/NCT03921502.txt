Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Symptomatic choledocholithiasis (choledocholithiasis demonstrated radiologically  [OR] or highly suspected by clinical data (acute cholangitis  [OR] or obstructive jaundice), analytical  [OR] and imaging according to the criteria of high probability of choledocholithiasis established in the clinical guidelines (ASGE Guide).
  -  Discarded for surgical treatment due to age, comorbidity or refusal of the patient.
  -  Age>75 years
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Charlson comorbidity scale adjusted to age <4.
  -  Prior ERCP.
  -  Previous episodes of cholangitis, [OR]  pancreatitis  [OR] or lithiasic cholecystitis.
  -  Hepatobiliary surgery  [OR] or previous superior digestive tract.
  -  Ascitis.
  -  Inability to tolerate  [OR] sedation of endoscopy, perforation of the digestive tract or other contraindication to endoscopy.
  -  Coagulopathy  [AND] with INR (international normalized ratio) > 1.5 not correctable or thrombocytopenia <50000 / mm3 not correctable.
  -  Other diagnoses at admission (acute cholecystitis, [OR]  acute pancreatitis, [OR]  biliopancreatic neoplasia).
  -  Hemodynamic instability.
  -  Urgent procedure performed after hours
  -  No availability of expert material / endoscopist in drainage.
  -  Anatomical impossibility of performing biliary drainage ([NOT] absence of vesicular distension, [OR]  contact between gallbladder  [AND] and stomach  [OR] or duodenum, contact area <10 mm).
  -  Baseline ECOG (Easthern Cooperative Oncology Group) > = 4
  -  Expectancy of survival <6 months.
  -  Refusal to participate.
  -  Distance between the gallbladder  [AND] and upper digestive tract> 1cm, scleroatrophic vesicle, [NOT] lack of stable acoustic window for drainage
  -  ERCP failed ([NOT] inhability to dain common bile duct)
