Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adult [AND] patients with IBS according to Rome IV criteria
Exclusion Criteria:
 - allergy [OR] or documented intolerance to food,
 - severe cardiovascular, [OR] hepatic, [OR] neurological [OR] or psychiatric disease
 - serious gastrointestinal disease: including celiac disease [OR] and inflammatory bowel diseases (IBD),
 - diabetes
 - bowel dysfunction related to previous surgery
 - pregnant [OR] or lactating women
 - usage of antibiotics within 4 weeks before inclusion
 - strict avoidance of foods
