Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Women  [AND] with a twin pregnancy (mono-  [OR] and di-chorionic)
  -  16 0/7 to 22 0/7 weeks of gestation
  -  Maternal age ≥18 yrs
  -  Cervical length ≤28 mm
  -  Informed consent
  -  [NOT] Not participating in another preterm birth study at the same time
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Uterine anomalies
  -  Cervical dilation  [AND] with visible amniotic membranes  [OR] or amniotic membranes prolapsed into the vagina
  -  Twin-to-twin transfusion syndrome
  -  Stillbirth  [OR] or major congenital abnormalities in any of the fetus
  -  Severe vaginal discharge
  -  Acute vaginitis  [OR] or cervicitis
  -  Vaginal bleeding
  -  Placental preavia
  -  Vasa preavia
  -  Premature rupture of membranes
  -  Premature labor  [AND] with/without ruptured membrane
  -  Suspicion of chorioamnionitis
  -  Cerclage in place  [OR] or [NOT] unable to undergo cervical cerclage  [OR] or pessary
