Inclusion Criteria:
• Clinical Diagnosis of Primary Raynaud's Phenomenon
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Less than 18 years of age
  -  Pregnant women
  -  Current smokers
  -  Have Digital ulcers
  -  Diagnosed Pulmonary hypertension
  -  Currently on vasodilators (i.e. Phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitors, [OR]  prostacyclin, [OR]  nitroglycerine, [OR]  or other nitric oxide derivatives)
  -  Currently on Calcium Channel Blockers
