Inclusion Criteria:
  -  30-60 years
  -  Increased fasting blood glucose  [OR] from 6.1 to 7.0 mmol / L or,
  -  Decreased glucose tolerance of 7 , 8 to 11.1 mmol / L or,
  -  Glycated hemoglobin with values between 5.7 and 6.4%
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Use hypoglycemic, [OR]  hypolipidemic drugs,
  -  Use dietary supplements (vitamins, [OR]  minerals, [OR]  bioflavonoids, [OR]  prebiotics, [OR]  probiotics or other bioactive compounds),
  -  Exercise intensely (more than 10 hours per week)
  -  History of cardiovascular diseases , diabetes mellitus, liver  [OR] and kidney disease.
