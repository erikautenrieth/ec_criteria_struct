Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Diagnosis as heroin dependence according to DSM-IV criteria
  -  No definite history of neurological diseases  [OR] and psychological problems
  -  Volunteer to participate the study, cooperate to be followed up
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Acute withdrawal state and CIWA score > 9
  -  With other neurological diseases  [OR] and psychological problems
  -  With ever brain trauma  [OR] and damage
  -  With other psychological medications  [OR] or other substance dependence
  -  With other contraindications to have transcranial magnetic stimulation
