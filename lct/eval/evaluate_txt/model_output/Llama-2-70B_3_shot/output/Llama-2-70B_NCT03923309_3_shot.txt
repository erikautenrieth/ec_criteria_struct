  Inclusion Criteria:

* Biopsy-proven rectal cancer (anal verge ≤ 10cm) [AND]
* Neoadjuvant radiotherapy [OR] chemoradiotherapy
* No radical surgery after neoadjuvant therapy

Exclusion Criteria:

* Other malignant disease [OR] other fatal disease
* Incompletion of half of radiotherapy course
* Metastasis on initial presentation [NOT]
