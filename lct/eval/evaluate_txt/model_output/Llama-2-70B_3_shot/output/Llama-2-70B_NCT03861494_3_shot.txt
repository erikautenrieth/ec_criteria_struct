  Inclusion Criteria:

* Patients hospitalized with primary diagnosis of acute or subacute ischemic cerebral infarction [AND] 18 years of age or older
* English speaking [AND] admitted to hospital Sunday through Thursday
* Patient or responsible primary caregiver able to understand and provide informed consent [AND] prognosis for survival greater than 6 months
* One or more vascular risk factors (HTN, DLP, current smoker, DM, obesity) [AND] live in NWA area (Benton, Boone, Carroll, Madison, and Washington counties)
* Have a working phone

Exclusion Criteria:

* Admitted with transient ischemic attack [OR] admitted with an intracerebral hemorrhage
* Experience an in-hospital ischemic cerebral infarction [OR] hospital to hospital transfer patients
* Nursing home as primary residence before or after discharge [OR] hospice
* Inability to comply with post discharge follow up
