Inclusion Criteria:

* Male or female patients with invasive bladder cancer scheduled for radical cystectomy at UNC within the next 30 days [AND]
* English-speaking [AND]
* Ability to access the Internet (either themselves or with assistance from their caregiver) [AND]
* Ability to read [AND]
* Ability to provide informed consent [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:

* Inability to read [NOT]
* Inability to provide informed consent [NOT]
* Severe psychiatric illness [NOT]
* Cystectomy surgery without cancer diagnosis [NOT]
* Non-English speaking [NOT]
