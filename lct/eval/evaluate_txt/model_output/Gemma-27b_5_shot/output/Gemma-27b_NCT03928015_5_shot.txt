Inclusion Criteria:
 1. [AND] Male [OR] female, 18 years [AND] to 65 years old (inclusive)
 2. [AND] Index admission for traumatic injury
 3. [AND] High initial morphine equivalent use ≥ 90 mg in the first 24 hours from admission
 4. [AND] Baseline pain assessed at screening of ≥ 5 on the pain numeric rating score (NRS)
 5. [AND] Willing to divulge habitual marijuana usage (yes [OR] no. Yes, habitual/chronic usage; no, recreational, former, [OR] never usage)     
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [AND] Patients on a pain management agreement
  2. [AND] Patients who are nil per os (NPO) at the time of randomization [OR] are expected to be NPO within the next 48 hours
  3. [AND] Patients who have received [OR] are expected to receive neuraxial/locoregional blocks for pain within the next 48 hours
  4. [AND] Known allergy [OR] previous hypersensitivity reaction to dronabinol [OR] sesame oil
  5. [AND] Patients prescribed dronabinol between arrival [AND] prior to screening/randomization
  6. [AND] Pregnancy [OR] breast feeding
  7. [AND] Incarceration
