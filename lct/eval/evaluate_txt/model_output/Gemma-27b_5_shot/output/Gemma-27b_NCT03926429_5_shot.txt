Inclusion Criteria:
 - reactive arthritis diagnosed in one of the two rheumatology departments from Centre Hospitalier Lyon Sud [OR] or Besançon
Exclusion Criteria:
 - presence [AND] of other known causes [OR] of arthritis, such as other defined spondyloarthritides, septic arthritis, Lyme disease, microcrystalline arthritis [OR] or rheumatoid arthritis
