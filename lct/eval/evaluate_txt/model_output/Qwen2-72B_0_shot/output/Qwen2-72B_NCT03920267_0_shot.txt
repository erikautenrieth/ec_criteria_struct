Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND]Completion of SLE Study (NCT03252587) through the protocol-required treatment period, [AND]currently receiving blinded study drug
  - [AND]Women of childbearing potential (WOCBP) must have a negative urine pregnancy test, [AND]must agree to use correctly a highly effective method(s) of contraception for the duration of treatment with study drug

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [AND][NOT]Any disease or medical condition that, in the opinion of the investigator, would make the subject unsuitable for this study, [OR]would interfere with the interpretation of subject safety or study results, [OR][NOT]considered unsuitable by the investigator for any other reason
  - [AND][NOT]Evidence of active tuberculosis (TB)
  - [AND]Other protocol defined inclusion[OR]exclusion criteria could apply
