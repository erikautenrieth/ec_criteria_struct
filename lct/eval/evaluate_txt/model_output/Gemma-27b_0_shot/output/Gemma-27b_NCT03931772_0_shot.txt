Inclusion Criteria:
  - 18 years [OR] older
  - [AND] Able to read [AND] understand English
  - [AND] Desire to quit smoking (specifically, report a motivation of ≥ 5 out of 10 to quit smoking on a 10-point Likert-type scale)
  - [AND] Having access to wireless internet connection [OR] mobile data
  - [AND] Reporting being a daily smoker for at least one year, smoking an average ≥ 5 cigarettes per day
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Meeting criteria for drug abuse of depressants, dissociative anesthetics, hallucinogens, opioids, cocaine, [OR] amphetamine within the last 3 months.
  - [NOT] Severe psychiatric [OR] structural brain disease (i.e. psychosis, stroke with functional impairment, dementia) [OR] [NOT] current/recent risk to self. Indicated by denial of question pertaining to inpatient hospitalization
  - [NOT] Hearing impairment that would impede ability to listen the auditory intervention
  - [NOT] Major illnesses impacting the study results
  - Currently taking Wellbutrin, Chantix, [OR] other pharmacological smoking cessation aids that could confound results of the study
