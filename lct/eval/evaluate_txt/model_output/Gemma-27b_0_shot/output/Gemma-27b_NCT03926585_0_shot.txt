Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients admitted to the department of endocrinology [AND] with the diagnose hypothyroidism because of persistent hypothyroid symptoms [AND] despite treatment with L-thyroxine mono-therapy [AND] normal [AND] stable TSH (for at least 6 months).
  - Started in combination therapy with L-thyroxine [OR] Liothyronine in an approximately 17/1 ratio
  - [NOT] Exclusion of an alternative explanation for persistent hypothyroid symptoms
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Initiation of L-thyroxine treatment in patients with s-TSH below upper normal limit (with assay in current use, [THAT] is TSH < 4 mU/L)
  - Ongoing pregnancy
  - Age below 18 years [OR] above 80 years.
  - Patients who [NOT] read [AND] understand information material given
  - Patients who are [NOT] competent to give informed consent
