<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Subject-reported history [AND] of dry eye disease in both eyes [AND] for at least 6 months;
 - History [AND] of use of artificial tear eye drops [AND] for dry eye symptoms;
 - Total score [AND] of ≥40 on SANDE;
 - TFBUT;
 - Corneal fluorescein staining;
 - Lissamine green conjunctival staining;
 - Schirmer's test score.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Have participated in a previous tavilermide (MIM-D3) study;
 - Have clinically significant slit lamp findings [AND] at Visit 1;
 - Have a history [AND] of lacrimal duct obstruction [AND] within 12 months [AND] of Visit 1;
 - Have an uncontrolled systemic disease;
 - Be a woman [AND] who is pregnant, [OR] nursing [OR] or planning a pregnancy;
 - Be a woman [AND] of childbearing potential [AND] who is not using an acceptable means of birth control;
 - Have a condition [OR] or be in a situation [AND] which the investigator feels may put the subject at significant risk, [OR] may confound the study results, [OR] or may interfere significantly with the subject's participation in the study;
 - Be currently enrolled in an investigational drug [OR] or device study [OR] or have used an investigational drug [OR] or device within 45 days [AND] prior to Visit 1.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
