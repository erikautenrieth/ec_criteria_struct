Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Male [OR] aged 18 years or older
  -  Willing to provide written consent
  -  Pathologically confirmed prostate cancer on previous biopsy
  -  Suitable for high-dose-rate brachytherapy for treatment as standard of care [AND] consenting to high-dose-rate brachytherapy.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Previous radiotherapy to the prostate
