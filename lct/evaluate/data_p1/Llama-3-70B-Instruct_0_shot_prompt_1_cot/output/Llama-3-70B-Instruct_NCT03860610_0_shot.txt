Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients with an unilateral THA or TKA, which they received for the treatment of osteoarthritis [AND] (Patient groups 1+2)
  - Patients diagnosed with unilateral OA of the knee or hip [OR] planned for a treatment with THA or TKA [AND] (Patient groups 3+4)
  - Age ≥ 30 years [AND] (Healthy control group)

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Body mass index > 35kg/m2 [OR]
  - Use of walking aids [OR]
  - Neuromuscular disorders affecting gait [OR]
  - Inability to follow procedures due to psychological disorders [OR] dementia [OR]
  - Diagnosed hip or knee OA [AND] (Healthy control group) [NOT]
  - Current pain in the lower extremities [OR] lower back [AND] (Healthy control group) [NOT]
  - Rheumatoid Arthritis [AND] (Healthy control group) [NOT]
  - Previous corrective osteotomy [AND] (Healthy control group) [NOT]
  - Regular intake of pain medication (more than 4 times per week) [AND] (Healthy control group) [NOT]
