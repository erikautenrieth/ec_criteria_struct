Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] incorporated:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Gynecological procedure
  - Age 18-50 years
  - General anesthesia planned
  - (Systolic blood pressure < 150 mmHg) AND (HR < 100 beats/min)

Exclusion Criteria:
  - (Pre-existing hypertension) OR (Diabetes)
  - (Ischemic heart disease) OR (cerebrovascular disease)
  - Heart valve disease
  - Verified cardiac arrhythmia other than extrasystoles
  - Verified anaemia with hemoglobin level below 9.0 gr/dl
  - (Kidney disease) OR (hepatic disease)
  - Hypersensitivity for (propofol) OR (soya) OR (eggs) OR (peanuts)
  - Pregnancy
  - Poor health state
  - Illicit substance use
  - (BMI <20) OR (BMI >35 kg/m2)
  - NOT (meeting all inclusion criteria)
