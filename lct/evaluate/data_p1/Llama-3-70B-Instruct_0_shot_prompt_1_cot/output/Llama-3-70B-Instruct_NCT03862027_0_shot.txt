Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Adult patients (age >/= 18) [AND] admitted to the Neuro ICU of UNC Hospital [AND] requiring mechanical ventilation [AND] with ICP monitoring in place.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Pneumothorax [OR] pneumomediastinum
  2. Life expectancy < 24 hours [OR] expected to require mechanical ventilation for < 24 hours
  3. [NOT] able to place an esophageal balloon due to [esophageal or nasopharyngeal pathology preventing insertion of the esophageal balloon catheter (esophageal ulcerations, tumors, diverticulitis, bleeding varices) [OR] sinusitis [OR] epistaxis [OR] recent nasopharyngeal surgery] [OR] severe thrombocytopenia (platelet count < 30) [OR] coagulopathy (INR > 3 [OR] on oral anticoagulants)
  4. Pre-enrollment ICP > 20 mm Hg
  5. Pre-enrollment CPP < 60 mm Hg
  6. Planned change in the external ventricular drain set point during the [pre-intervention [OR] intervention [OR] post-intervention] periods
  7. Incarceration
  8. Variation in ICP of > 2 cm H2O in the hour prior to intervention
