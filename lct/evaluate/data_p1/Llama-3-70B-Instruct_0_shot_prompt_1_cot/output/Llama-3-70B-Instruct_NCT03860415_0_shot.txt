Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the added logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Chronic (more than three months) ESBL colonization of the same strain as determined by species, phenotype AND antibiogram.
  - Patients must be able to swallow sachets AND must be able to speak AND understand Swedish.
  - Must have a permanent residence in Sweden AND must be able to sign informed consent in Swedish.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Immunosuppression (i.e. chemotherapy, treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors) OR in patient care.
  - Immunodeficiency OR psychiatric disorder OR alcohol OR substance abuse.
  - Dementia OR Invasive catheters.
  - Patients with immunosuppression NOT due to chemotherapy or treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors.
