Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Female [AND] older than 35 years [AND] (already have mammography [OR] other standard exams (ultrasound, MRI, etc)) [AND] could participate this trial within 2 weeks after mammography [AND] have the ability of consent inform.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  (During pregnancy [OR] lactation period) [OR] patients that may not be capable of prone position, such as patients with scapulohumeral periarthritis [OR] cardiac pacemaker [OR] have recent cardiac surgery [OR] lymphoma patients that are under therapy [OR] severe scoliosis.
