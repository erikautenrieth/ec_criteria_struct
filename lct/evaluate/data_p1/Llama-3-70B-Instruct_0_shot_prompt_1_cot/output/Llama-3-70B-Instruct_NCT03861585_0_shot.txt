Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] incorporated:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Children between 7 and 12 years old……………………………. [AND]
  -  Normal or corrected visual acuity ………………………………. [AND]
  -  ADHD diagnostic given by a doctor according to DSM 5 criteria (Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders) [AND]
  -  Free from drug treatment until 24 hours before the experiment session………………………. [AND]
  -  Affiliation to a social security system……………………. [AND]
  -  Signature of the authorization documents from one parent or the holder of parental authority [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History of neurological troubles [OR]
  -  Dysphasia [OR]
  -  Autistic spectrum disorder [OR]
  -  Intellectual disabilities [NOT] eligible for participation if any of the above conditions are met.
