Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Gestational age < 32 weeks
  - Birthweight < 1500 g
  - Age less than 72 hours
  - PDA diameter > 1.5 mm
  - Signed informed consent obtained from both parents [AND] all of the above criteria

Exclusion Criteria:
  - (Birthweight ≥ 1500 g [OR] gestation age ≥ 32 weeks) [OR] 
  - Lack of informed consent of the parents [OR] 
  - (Congenital heart defect [AND] NOT (PDA [OR] patent foramen ovale (PFO))) [OR] 
  - The presence of a clinically apparent hemorrhagic syndrome [OR] 
  - (Any intraventricular hemorrhage (IVH) in the first 48 hours [OR] IVH grade 3-4) [OR] 
  - A platelet count of < 50,000/mm3 [OR] 
  - A serum creatinine concentration of > 110 μmol/L [OR] 
  - Oliguria <1 ml/kg/h [OR] 
  - Suspected/apparent NEC [OR] 
  - Suspected/apparent lung hypoplasia
