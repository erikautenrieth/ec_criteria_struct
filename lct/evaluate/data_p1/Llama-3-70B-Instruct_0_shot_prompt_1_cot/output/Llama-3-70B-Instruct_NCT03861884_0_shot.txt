Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Ability to give informed consent.
  2. [OR] pathological evidence to suggest Fronto-temporal Lobar Degeneration [OR] genetic evidence to suggest Alzheimer's Disease [OR] imaging biomarker evidence to suggest Vascular Cognitive Impairment [OR] defined clinical diagnoses.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] ability to give informed consent, i.e., lack of mental capacity to consent to study involvement.
  2. [NOT] speaking English before age 5 years.
  3. Learning disability.
  4. [AND] MRI contraindications.
