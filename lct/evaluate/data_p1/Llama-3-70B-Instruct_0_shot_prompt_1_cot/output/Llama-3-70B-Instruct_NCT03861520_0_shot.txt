Inclusion Criteria:
  1. pregnant woman between (16-24) weeks of gestation AND

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Multiple pregnancies OR
  2. Mental condition rendering the patients unable to understand the nature and scope of the study AND NOT
  3. (removed, as there was no third criterion)

Note: I added the logical operators to make the criteria more explicit and clear. The [AND] operator is used to combine the two inclusion criteria, indicating that both must be true for a participant to be eligible. The [OR] operator is used to combine the two exclusion criteria, indicating that either one can make a participant ineligible. The [NOT] operator is used to negate the second exclusion criterion, but since there was no third criterion, it was removed.
