Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the added logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - LMNA+ cases with pathogenic LMNA mutations [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  - DCMWT cases: patients with heart muscle failure [AND] with wild-type lamin gene [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  - HV (controls): matched to cases [OR] healthy volunteers with no known heart conditions.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Needle-phobia that would preclude blood-letting [OR] participants unwilling to consent.
  - Patients that have a conventional contraindication for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) [OR] patients that have had a blood transfusion within the last month [OR] patients having haemodialysis [NOT] willing to undergo alternative imaging modalities.
  - Patients with any other condition that, in the opinion of the investigator, would preclude participation in the study [OR] patients with a history of non-adherence to medical regimens.
