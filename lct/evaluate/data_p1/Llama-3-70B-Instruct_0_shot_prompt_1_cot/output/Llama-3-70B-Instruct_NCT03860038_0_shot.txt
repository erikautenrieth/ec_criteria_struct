Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion criteria:
  1. Age ≥ 18 [AND] male or female;
  2. Subject must have had documented MM;
  3. At screening phase, subject must have measurable disease [AND] subject is in a state of progressive disease (PD);
  4. Subject must have life expectancy of no less than 6 months [AND] subject must have an ECOG (Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group) performance status score of 0~2;

Exclusion criteria:
  1. Subject has received anti-CD38 monoclonal antibody treatment previously [OR] subject has received CAR-T cell therapy previously;
  2. Subject has previously received allogenic stem cell transplant [OR] subject has received autologous stem cell transplant within 3 months before administration of the study agent;
  3. Primary refractory multiple myeloma (subject failed to generate any minimal response or any degree of response to any therapy) [OR] subject has received anti-myeloma treatment (radiotherapy is excluded) within 4 weeks or 5 PK half-lives of the treatment, whichever longer, before the first study agent administration;
  4. [NOT] subject has a life expectancy of more than 6 months;
  5. [NOT] subject has an ECOG (Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group) performance status score of 0~2;
