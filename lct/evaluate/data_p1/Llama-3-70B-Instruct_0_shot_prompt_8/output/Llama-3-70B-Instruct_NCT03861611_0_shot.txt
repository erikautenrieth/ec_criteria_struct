<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Men [OR] women age 18-64.
  -  Present to ED primary for management of Low Back Pain (LBP) [AND] require treatment.
  -  Functionally impairing back pain: A baseline score of greater than 5 on the Roland-Morris Disability Questionnaire.
  -  Musculoskeletal etiology of low back [AND] no other underlying condition.
  -  Non-radicular pain [AND] no evidence of nerve root involvement.
  -  Pain duration <2 weeks (336 hours) [AND] acute onset.
  -  Non-traumatic LBP [AND] no history of injury.
  -  Participant is to be discharged home [AND] able to follow-up.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Flank pain, that is pain originating from tissues lateral to the paraspinal muscles [NOT] originating from the spine.
  -  [NOT] available for follow-up.
  -  Pregnant [OR] breastfeeding.
  -  Chronic pain syndrome [NOT] acute pain.
  -  Allergic to [OR] intolerant of investigational medications.
  -  Contra-indications to investigational medications [NOT] suitable for treatment.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
