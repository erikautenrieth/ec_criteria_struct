Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. NS-TAD probands operated on (n=16).
  2. FDR and SDR, aged 16 and above:
       1. At least two relatives [AND] willing to participate in the screening programme.
       2. Relatives [AND] able to understand English.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Probands [WITH] syndromic aortopathies, including Marfan Syndrome, Loeys-Dietz Syndrome, Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome, Shprintzen-Goldberg syndrome, aneurysm-osteoarthritis syndrome, arterial tortuosity syndrome, [OR] cutis laxa syndrome.
  2. Probands [WITH] aortic lesions associated [AND] with trauma [OR] infections.
  3. Probands/relatives [NOT] able to give informed consent
