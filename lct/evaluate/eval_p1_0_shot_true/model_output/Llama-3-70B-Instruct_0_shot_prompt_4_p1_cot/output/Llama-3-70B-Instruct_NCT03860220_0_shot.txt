<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  145 mmHg ≤ msSBP ≤ 190 mmHg
  -  Triglycerides < 500 mg/dL
  -  LDL-C ≤ 250 mg/dL

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  sSBP ≥ 20mmHg [AND] sDBP ≥ 10mmHg
  -  symptomatic orthostatic hypotension [AND] secondary/iatrogenic hypertension [AND] dyslipidemia
  -  history of moderate to severe cerebral ischemia [OR] cerebral hemorrhage [OR] transient ischemic attack [OR] myocardial infarction [OR] unstable angina in the past 6 months; severe heart failure (New York Heart Association functional class III [AND] IV)
  -  hypersensitivity to telmisartan [OR] rosuvastatin
  -  history of angioedema after treatment with angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors [OR] ARBs; creatinine phosphokinase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal
  -  estimated glomerular filtration rate ≤30 mL/min; aspartate aminotransferase [AND] alanine aminotransferase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal
  -  potassium levels >5.5 mmol/L; [OR] any diseases that could affect the results of the study
  -  [NOT] No history of moderate to severe cerebral ischemia [NOT] cerebral hemorrhage [NOT] transient ischemic attack [NOT] myocardial infarction [NOT] unstable angina in the past 6 months
  -  [NOT] No hypersensitivity to telmisartan [NOT] rosuvastatin
  -  [NOT] No history of angioedema after treatment with angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors [NOT] ARBs
  -  [NOT] No estimated glomerular filtration rate ≤30 mL/min
  -  [NOT] No aspartate aminotransferase [AND] alanine aminotransferase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal
  -  [NOT] No potassium levels >5.5 mmol/L
  -  [NOT] No diseases that could affect the results of the study
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
