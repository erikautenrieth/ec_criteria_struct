Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Surgical plan for total hip replacement
  -  Signing of consent form to participate in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Patient refusal
  -  BMI > 40 kg/m2
  -  Surgical plan for revision of hip replacement
  -  [NOT] Patient able to quantify pain level
  -  Chronic kidney disease [AND] with a Glomerular Filtration Rate < 50ml/min
  -  Previously medicated [AND] with opioids
  -  [NOT] Patient able to perform the surgery with spinal block
  -  Allergy [AND] to local anesthetics
  -  Infection [AND] in the site of the Erector Spinae Plane [OR] Fascia Iliaca block
  -  Allergy [OR] contraindication to the use of morphine
