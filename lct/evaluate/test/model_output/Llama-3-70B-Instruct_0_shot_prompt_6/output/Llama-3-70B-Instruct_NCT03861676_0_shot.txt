<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adenocarcinoma of the prostate
  -  Performance Status < 2
  -  Clinical stages (not radiographic stage) T1c [OR] T2a, Nx [OR] N0, Mx [OR] M0
  -  Gleason 6 [OR] 7 cancer
  -  Prostate volume < 60 cc [AND] (if MRI and TRUS have conflicting values, then MRI value will be utilized)
  -  International Prostate symptom score (IPSS) ≤ 20
  -  Ability to undergo DCF-Pyl PSMA PET as part of pretreatment staging
  -  Signed study-specific consent form prior to registration

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Prior history of pelvic radiation therapy
  -  [NOT] Major medical or psychiatric illness which, in the investigator's opinion, would prevent completion of treatment [AND] would interfere with follow up
  -  [NOT] Implanted device or apparatus which obstruct visibility of the implanted sources on fluoroscopy
  -  [NOT] Metallic implants [OR] claustrophobia not amenable to medication [OR] known contraindications to undergoing MR scanning
  -  [NOT] History of other malignancy diagnosed within the past 3 years
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
