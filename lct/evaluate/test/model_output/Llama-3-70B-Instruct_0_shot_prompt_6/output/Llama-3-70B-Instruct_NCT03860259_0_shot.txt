<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Subject is greater than 18 years of age [AND]
  2. Subject is willing [AND] able to provide informed consent
  3. Subject is scheduled to undergo elective rotator cuff surgery [AND]
  4. Subject has consented to an interscalene block

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Opioid dependence [OR]
  2. Any subject diagnosed with a chronic pain condition which daily opioid use is needed
  3. [NOT] anatomically normal, specifically anatomical malformation, which in the investigator's opinion may interfere with the placement of the nerve block
  4. [NOT] absence of Raynaud's disease diagnosis
  5. [NOT] absence of Vasculopathy
  6. Patient refusal
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
