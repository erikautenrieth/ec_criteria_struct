Inclusion Criteria-- Participants must be:
  -  Healthy (determined by self-report) [AND]
  -  Between the ages of 18-55 years old [AND]
  -  Current cigarette smokers [AND]
  -  Willing to provide informed consent [AND]
  -  Able to attend the lab session [AND] abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required [AND]
  -  Agree to use designated products according to the study protocol
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Women if they are breast-feeding [OR] test positive for pregnancy (by urinalysis) at screening.
  -  Individuals who weigh less than 110 pounds [OR] are unable to meet the inclusion criteria.
  -  Participants with [NOT] healthy status (determined by self-report).
  -  Participants [NOT] between the ages of 18-55 years old.
  -  Participants who are [NOT] current cigarette smokers.
  -  Participants [NOT] willing to provide informed consent.
  -  Participants [NOT] able to attend the lab session [OR] unable to abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required.
  -  Participants who do [NOT] agree to use designated products according to the study protocol.
