<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Unilateral ischemic stroke [AND] residual arm weakness (Fugl-Meyer Upper Limb < 60) more than 6 months prior to enrollment.
  -  Ability to give informed consent [AND] understand the tasks involved.
  -  Age over 18 years.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Hemorrhagic Stroke [OR] contraindications to TMS: history of seizure/epilepsy, pacemaker, other neurological disorders, brain surgery, metal implant/fragment in the head, pregnancy.
  -  Taking medications [OR] substances that are known to affect PAS-induced plasticity within the past 2 months: selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors, dopamine, dopamine agonists, haloperidol, lithium, acetylcholinesterase inhibitors, beta-blockers, nimodipine, levetiracetam, ethosuximide, benzodiazepines, baclofen, nicotine.
  -  [NOT] free of peripheral neuropathy [OR] history of nerve injury in the paretic upper limb.
  -  [NOT] able to return for all study visits due to social [OR] personal circumstances that interfere with ability.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
