<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  undergoing unilateral [OR] bilateral breast surgery with at least moderate postoperative pain anticipated [AND] analgesic plan includes a single-injection peripheral nerve block(s) [AND] age 18 years or older
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  morbid obesity as defined by a body mass index > 40 (BMI=weight in kg / [height in meters]2) [OR]
  -  renal insufficiency (preoperative creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL) [OR]
  -  chronic opioid use (daily use within the 2 weeks prior to surgery [AND] duration of use > 4 weeks) [OR]
  -  history of opioid abuse [OR]
  -  any comorbidity which results in moderate [OR] severe functional limitation [AND] inability to communicate with the investigators [OR] hospital staff [OR]
  -  pregnancy [OR]
  -  planned regional analgesic with perineural catheter placement [OR]
  -  incarceration [OR]
  -  [NOT] able to provide informed consent
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
