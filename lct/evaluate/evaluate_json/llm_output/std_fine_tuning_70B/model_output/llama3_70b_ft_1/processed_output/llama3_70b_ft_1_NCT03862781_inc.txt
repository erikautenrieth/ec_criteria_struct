- Eligible patients are those over 18 years of age who are regularly scheduled to undergo a right hemicolectomy via a minimally invasive approach (robotic [OR] or laparoscopic) [AND] with the creation of an anastomosis. Right colectomy will be defined as removal of the ascending colon, ligation of the ileocolic artery [OR] and vein, +/- removal of the terminal ileum, [OR] +/- removal of the proximal transverse colon, [OR] and +/- removal of the right branch of the middle colic artery [OR] and vein.
