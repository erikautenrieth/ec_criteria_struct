Type II diabetic patients, 18 years and older, were included if the participants had: [AND] 1. severe non-PDR in either eyes with/without diabetic macular edema; [AND] 2. ETDRS letters-measured best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) ranging from 20/40 to 20/320 Snellen equivalent; [AND] [NOT] 3. no previous treatment (of any type) in either eye.
