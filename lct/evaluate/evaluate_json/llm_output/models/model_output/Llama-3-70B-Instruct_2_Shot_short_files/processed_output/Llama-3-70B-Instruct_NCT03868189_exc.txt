[OR] Patient whose neurological examination is abnormal and suggestive of neuropathy. [OR] Patient presenting a pathology that could cause neuropathy (e.g. diabetes, renal failure, etc.). [OR] Patient under the protection of justice, under guardianship or under curatorship. [OR] Patient refusing to sign the consent form. [OR] Patient for which it is impossible to provide information about the study. [OR] Pregnant patient, parturient, or breastfeeding.
