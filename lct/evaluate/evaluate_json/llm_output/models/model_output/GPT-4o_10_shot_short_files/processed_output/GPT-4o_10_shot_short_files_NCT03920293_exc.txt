Medical Conditions 1. Any active or untreated thymoma. History of thymic carcinoma or thymic malignancy unless deemed cured by adequate treatment with no evidence of recurrence for ≥ 5 years before screening. [AND] 2. History of thymectomy within the 12 months prior to screening. [AND] 3. History of N meningitidis infection. [AND] 4. Use of the following within the time period specified below: [AND] - IV immunoglobulin within 4 weeks of randomization [OR] - Use of plasma exchange within 4 weeks of randomization [OR] - Use of rituximab within 6 months of screening [AND] 5. Participants who have received previous treatment with complement inhibitors (for example, eculizumab).
