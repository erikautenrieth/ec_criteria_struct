and be seeking treatment and supportive care at the UF Health Cancer Center (Medical Plaza) [AND]
- Patients must plan to receive chemotherapy known to be associated with the need for supportive care within 30 days of enrollment [AND]
- Life expectancy must be greater than 6 months [AND]

- The ability for the patient to comply with all the study-related procedures.
