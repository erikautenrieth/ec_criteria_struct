The chILD syndrome exists when a child with DLD has had the common causes of DLD excluded as the primary diagnosis [AND]
and has at least three of the following four criteria: [AND]
(1) respiratory symptoms (e.g., cough, [OR]
rapid and/or difficult breathing, [OR]
or exercise intolerance) [OR]
(2) respiratory signs (e.g., resting tachypnea, [OR]
adventitious sounds, [OR]
retractions, [OR]
digital clubbing, [OR]
failure to thrive, [OR]
or respiratory failure) [OR]
(3) hypoxemia; [OR]
(4) diffuse abnormalities on CXR or a CT scan.
