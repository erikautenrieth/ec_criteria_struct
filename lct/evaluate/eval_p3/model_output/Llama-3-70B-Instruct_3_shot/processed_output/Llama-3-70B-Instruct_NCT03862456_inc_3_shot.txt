- Diagnosis of periodontitis (stages III or IV) that may require periodontal surgery [AND]
- Have at least 10 teeth in function, excluding third molars. [AND]
- Present locations with probing depth (PS)> 6 mm in at least 30% of the teeth. [AND]
- Present radiographic evidence of moderate-severe bone loss in at least 30% of the dentition. [AND]
- Detection of P. gingivalis in subgingival samples taken at the screening visit as well as in the post-scaling and root planning visit and processed by culture. [AND]
- Systemically healthy patients.
