- Thyroid disease that requires active treatment [AND]
- Skin disease that requires active treatment and causes significant changes in the area of the skin that is to be investigated [AND]
- Treatment with oral glucocorticoids [AND]
- Levels of SR, TSH, T4 or Hemoglobin in the blood pronouncedly outside the reference range [AND]

For healthy controls: [AND]
- Registered disease in the health declaration that requires continuous systemic treatment. [AND]
[NOT] Exception to the above: well controlled hypertension in which case the subject should not have treatment with more than one antihypertensive drug, the drug must not be an alpha-blocker or beta-blocker or thiazide diuretic
