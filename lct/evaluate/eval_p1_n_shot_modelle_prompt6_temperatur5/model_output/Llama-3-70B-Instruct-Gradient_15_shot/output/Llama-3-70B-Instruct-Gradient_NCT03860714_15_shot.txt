Inclusion Criteria:
 - Non-cardiac surgery patients;
 - Age is greater than or equal to 60 years old;
 - Han Nationality, [AND] mother tongue is Chinese;
 - The MMSE score:
   - Illiteracy is greater than or equal to 17 points,
   - primary school is greater than or equal to 20 points,
   - higher secondary school is more than 24 points;
 - The people signed informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - The diagnosis of preoperative delirium;
 - The diagnosis Unstable hypertension (such as：pheochromocytoma or aortic dissection);
 - Severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] or congestive heart failure;
 - Severe liver [OR] and kidney dysfunction
