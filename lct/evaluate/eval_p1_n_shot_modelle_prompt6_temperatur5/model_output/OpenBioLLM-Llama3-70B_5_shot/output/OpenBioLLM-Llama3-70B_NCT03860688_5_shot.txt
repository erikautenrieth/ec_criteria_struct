Inclusion Criteria:
 - Men [AND] women, aged 18 to 60 years.
 - Body mass index 20 to 27 kg/m2
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients [AND] active inflammatory bowel disease
 2. Patients [AND] pre-existing Celiac disease, [OR] exocrine pancreatic insufficiency [OR] or small bowel malabsorption
 3. Patients [AND] active bowel malignancy
 4. Patients [AND] diabetes mellitus [OR] or known/ suspected motility disorders of the gut
 5. Patients [AND] decompensated liver disease
 6. Patients on ezetimibe [OR] or bile acid sequestrants
 7. Patients who are pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding.
 8. Patients [AND] renal disease.
 9. Patients on benzodiazepine.
 10. Unstable cardiac [OR] or respiratory disease
 11. Any changes to medication in the preceding month
 12. Patients [AND] hypersensitive to Revestive [OR] or any ingredient, [OR] active [OR] or a history of the drug within the last 5 years
 13. Patients [AND] GI malignancy (GI tract, hepatobiliary, pancreatic)
