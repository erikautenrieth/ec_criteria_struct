Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Men [AND] women, aged 18 to 60 years.
  -  Body mass index 20 to 27 kg/m2
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients [AND] active inflammatory bowel disease
  2. Patients [AND] pre-existing Celiac disease [OR] exocrine pancreatic insufficiency [OR] small bowel malabsorption
  3. Patients [AND] active bowel malignancy
  4. Patients [AND] diabetes mellitus [OR] known/ suspected motility disorders of the gut
  5. Patients [AND] decompensated liver disease
  6. Patients [AND] on ezetimibe [OR] bile acid sequestrants
  7. Patients [AND] who are pregnant [OR] breastfeeding.
  8. Patients [AND] renal disease.
  9. Patients [AND] on benzodiazepine.
 10. Patients [AND] unstable cardiac [OR] respiratory disease
 11. Any changes to medication in the preceding month
 12. Patients [AND] hypersensitive to Revestive [OR] any ingredient, [OR] active [OR] a history of the drug within the last 5 years
 13. Patients [AND] GI malignancy (GI tract [OR] hepatobiliary [OR] pancreatic)
