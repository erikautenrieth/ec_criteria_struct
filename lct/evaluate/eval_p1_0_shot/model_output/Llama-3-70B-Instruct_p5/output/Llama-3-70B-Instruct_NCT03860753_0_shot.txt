Inclusion Criteria:
  - treatment-seeking individuals diagnosed with AUD diagnostic statistical manual 5 (DSM-5)
  - fluent in English
  - past month excessive alcohol use (>7 drinks/week for woman, [OR] >14 drinks/week for men, [OR] >3 drinks/occasion for women [OR] >4 drinks/occasion for men)
  - baseline Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A) [OR] Perceived Stress Scale (PSS) core indicative of mild to moderate anxiety (score 8 to 23) [OR] moderate stress (score 14 to 26), respectively
  - increase in alcohol craving following the baseline stress reactivity assessment.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Individuals will be excluded for exhibiting severe scores on the HAM-A, [OR] PSS, [OR] post-traumatic (PTSD) checklist (PCL-5) at the discretion of the admitting physician
  - physical dependence on alcohol [AND] Alcohol Use Disorders Inventory (AUDIT) score indicative of severe alcohol dependence (≥13 for women, [OR] ≥15 for men)
  - greater than mild substance use disorder on drugs other than alcohol, [OR] nicotine, [OR] marijuana
  - contraindications for taking pioglitazone; medical conditions contraindicating pioglitazone pharmacotherapy [OR] taking contraindicated medications
  - [NOT] be pregnant, [NOT] nursing, [OR] [NOT] planning on becoming pregnant during the course of the study
  - have any other illness, [OR] condition, [OR] use of medications, which in the opinion of the PI [AND] /or admitting physician would preclude safe [AND] /or successful completion of the study.
