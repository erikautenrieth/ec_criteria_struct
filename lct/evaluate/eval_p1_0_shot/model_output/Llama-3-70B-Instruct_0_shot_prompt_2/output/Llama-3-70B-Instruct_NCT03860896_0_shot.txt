Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients aged 18-74 [AND] receiving therapy for active ulcerative colitis confirmed by Mayo Score assessment [AND] and evidence of colonic inflammation.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Evidence of Crohn's disease [OR] indeterminate colitis [OR] or presence of bacterial [OR] parasitic infection.
  -  Patients [NOT] receiving biologic agents [AND] experimental agents are excluded.
