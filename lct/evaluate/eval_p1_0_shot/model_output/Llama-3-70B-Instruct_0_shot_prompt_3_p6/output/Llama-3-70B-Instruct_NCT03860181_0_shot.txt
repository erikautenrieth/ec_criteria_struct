<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - Subjects are 18 years or older;
  - Subjects are undergoing primary total shoulder arthroplasty by the principal [AND] co-investigator.
  - Subjects are willing [AND] have the capacity to provide informed consent;
  - Subjects expect to continue their post-operative follow up care with their operating surgeons at Medical University of South Carolina (MUSC).

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Subjects have a unique, identifying tattoo [OR] skin marking within 2 inches of intended site of surgical incision
  - Subjects self-report a known hypersensitivity to cyanoacrylate, [OR] formaldehyde, [OR] benzalkonium chloride, [OR] pressure sensitive adhesive;
  - Subjects self-report [OR] have a documented prior ipsilateral shoulder arthroplasty [OR] other open ispilateral shoulder surgery utilizing the delto-pectoral approach;
  - Subjects' medical record shows that they are HIV positive [OR] otherwise immunocompromised;
  - Subjects' medical record shows a skin abnormality [OR] dermatological condition which affects skin healing;
  - Subjects report a personal [OR] family history of significant keloid [OR] significant hypertrophic scar formations, [OR] other problems with wound healing.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
