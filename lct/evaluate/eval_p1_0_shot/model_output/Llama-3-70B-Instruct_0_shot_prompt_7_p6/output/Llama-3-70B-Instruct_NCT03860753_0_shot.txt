<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - treatment-seeking individuals diagnosed with AUD diagnostic statistical manual 5 (DSM-5) [AND] fluent in English [AND] past month excessive alcohol use (>7 drinks/week for woman, >14 drinks/week for men, >3 drinks/occasion for women [OR] >4 drinks/occasion for men) [AND] baseline Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A) [OR] Perceived Stress Scale (PSS) core indicative of mild to moderate anxiety (score 8 to 23) [OR] moderate stress (score 14 to 26), respectively [AND] increase in alcohol craving following the baseline stress reactivity assessment.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Individuals will be excluded for exhibiting severe scores on the HAM-A [OR] PSS [OR] post-traumatic (PTSD) checklist (PCL-5) at the discretion of the admitting physician [OR] physical dependence on alcohol Alcohol Use Disorders Inventory (AUDIT) score indicative of severe alcohol dependence (≥13 for women [OR] ≥15 for men) [OR] greater than mild substance use disorder on drugs other than alcohol, nicotine, [AND] marijuana [OR] contraindications for taking pioglitazone; medical conditions contraindicating pioglitazone pharmacotherapy [OR] taking contraindicated medications [OR] [NOT] be pregnant [OR] nursing [OR] planning on becoming pregnant during the course of the study [OR] have any other illness, condition, [OR] use of medications, which in the opinion of the PI [AND] admitting physician would preclude safe [AND] successful completion of the study.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
