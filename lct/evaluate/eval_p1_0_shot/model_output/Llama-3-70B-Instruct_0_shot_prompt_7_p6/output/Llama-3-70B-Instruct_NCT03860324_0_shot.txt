Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Surgical plan for total hip replacement [AND] signing of consent form to participate in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient refusal [OR] BMI > 40 kg/m2 [OR] surgical plan for revision of hip replacement [OR] patient unable to quantify pain level [OR] chronic kidney disease [AND] Glomerular Filtration Rate < 50ml/min [OR] previously medicated with opioids [OR] patient unable to perform the surgery with spinal block [OR] allergy to local anesthetics [OR] infection in the site of the Erector Spinae Plane [OR] infection in the site of the Fascia Iliaca block [OR] allergy to the use of morphine [OR] contraindication to the use of morphine
