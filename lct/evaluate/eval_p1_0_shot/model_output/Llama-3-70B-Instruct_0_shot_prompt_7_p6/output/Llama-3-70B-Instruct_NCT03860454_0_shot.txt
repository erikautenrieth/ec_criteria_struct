<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  LMNA+ cases [AND] pathogenic LMNA mutations [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients [AND] scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  DCMWT cases: patients [AND] heart muscle failure [AND] wild-type lamin gene [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients [AND] scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  HV (controls): matched [AND] cases.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Needle-phobia [OR] unwillingness that would preclude blood-letting
  -  Participants [NOT] willing to consent
  -  Patients [AND] conventional contraindication for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).
  -  Patients [AND] had a blood transfusion within the last month [OR] patients [AND] having haemodialysis will be excluded.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
