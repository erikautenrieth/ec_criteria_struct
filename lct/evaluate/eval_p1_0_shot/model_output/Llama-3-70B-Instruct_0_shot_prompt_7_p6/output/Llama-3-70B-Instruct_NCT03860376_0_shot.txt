Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients aged 21 years or younger at the time of enrollment [AND] of any gender, race [OR] ethnicity.
       -  Subjects [AND] suspected or confirmed diagnosis of recurrent [OR] refractory cancer
       -  Subjects [AND] scheduled for [OR] have recently had biopsy [OR] tumor excised (solid tumors) [OR] bone marrow aspirate (blood cancers)
       -  Subjects willing [AND] to have a blood draw [OR] buccal swab done for the purposes of genetic testing
       -  Subjects [OR] their parents [OR] legal guardians willing [AND] to sign informed consent
       -  Subjects aged 7 to 17 willing [AND] to sign assent

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Subjects [NOT] having malignant tissue available [AND] accessible
  -  The amount of excised malignant tissue [NOT] sufficient for the ex vivo drug testing [AND]/or genetic profiling.
  -  Patients [NOT] having newly diagnosed tumors [AND] tumors that have high (>90%) cure rate [AND] with safe standard therapy.
