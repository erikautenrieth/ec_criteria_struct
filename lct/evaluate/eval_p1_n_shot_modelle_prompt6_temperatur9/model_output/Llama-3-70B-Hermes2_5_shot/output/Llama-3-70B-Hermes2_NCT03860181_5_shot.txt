<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Subjects will be included if:
 - They are 18 years or older;
 - They are undergoing primary total shoulder arthroplasty by the principal [AND] co-investigator.
 - They are willing [AND] have the capacity to provide informed consent;
 - They expect to continue their post-operative follow up care with their operating surgeons at Medical University of South Carolina (MUSC).

Subjects will be excluded if:
 - They have a unique, identifying tattoo [OR] or skin marking within 2 inches of intended site of surgical incision
 - They self-report a known hypersensitivity to cyanoacrylate, [OR] formaldehyde, [OR] benzalkonium chloride, [OR] or pressure sensitive adhesive;
 - They self-report [OR] or have a documented prior ipsilateral shoulder arthroplasty [OR] or other open ispilateral shoulder surgery utilizing the delto-pectoral approach;
 - Their medical record shows that they are HIV positive [OR] or otherwise immunocompromised;
 - Their medical record shows a skin abnormality [OR] or dermatological condition which affects skin healing;
 - They report a personal [OR] or family history of significant keloid [OR] or significant hypertrophic scar formations, [OR] or other problems with wound healing.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
