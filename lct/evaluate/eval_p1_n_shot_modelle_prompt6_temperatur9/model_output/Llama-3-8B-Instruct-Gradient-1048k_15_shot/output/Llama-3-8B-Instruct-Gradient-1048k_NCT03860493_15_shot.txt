Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients of both genders
 2. Minimum age: 18 years old
 3. Spoken [AND] written command of the language spoken in the country's center
 4. Ability to understand [AND] follow the study procedures and sign the informed consent
 5. [NOT] Open surgical procedures
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Known [OR] allergies to iodides
 2. Known history [AND] coagulopathy
 3. Women who [AND] are pregnant [OR] breastfeeding, or for whom possibility of pregnancy was not ruled out
