Inclusion Criteria:
 - Healthy
 - Male
 - 18-40 years of age
 - No known contraindications for anabolic drugs (e.g. cancer)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Abnormal ECG
 - Steroid abuse
 - Ongoing use of prescription medication
 - heavy resistance training [OR] more than 2 times weekly
 - Disease [AND] deemed by the MD to [NOT] infer a risk [OR] to participate in the trial
