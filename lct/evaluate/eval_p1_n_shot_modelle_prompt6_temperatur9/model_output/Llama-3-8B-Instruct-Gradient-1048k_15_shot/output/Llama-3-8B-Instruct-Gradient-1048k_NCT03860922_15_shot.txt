Inclusion Criteria:
 - patients in different age groups [AND] and both sex referred from orthopedic outpatient clinic with chronic ankle pain [AND] and suspected clinically to have impingement syndrome.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Inflammatory arthritis [OR] of ankle joint, infection [OR] in ankle joint, neoplasm [OR] around ankle joint.
 2. Patients with contraindications for MRI, e.g. [AND] an implanted magnetic device, pacemakers [AND] or claustrophobia.
