<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Surgical plan for total hip replacement
  -  Signing of consent form to participate in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient [NOT] refusal
  -  BMI > 40 kg/m2 [OR] BMI < 20 kg/m2
  -  Surgical plan for revision [AND] of hip replacement
  -  Patient [NOT] able to quantify pain level
  -  Chronic kidney disease [AND] with a Glomerular Filtration Rate < 50ml/min [OR] > 150ml/min
  -  Previously medicated with opioids [OR] other pain medications
  -  Patient [NOT] able to perform the surgery with spinal block
  -  Allergy [OR] sensitivity to local anesthetics
  -  Infection in the site of the Erector Spinae Plane [OR] Fascia Iliaca block
  -  Allergy [OR] contraindication to the use of morphine [OR] other opioids

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
