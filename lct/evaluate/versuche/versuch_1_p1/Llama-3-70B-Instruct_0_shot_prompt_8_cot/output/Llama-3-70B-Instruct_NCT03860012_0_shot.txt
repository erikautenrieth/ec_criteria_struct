<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. inflammatory bowel disease
  2. on methotrexate at appropriate dosing
  3. normal folate levels at onset of study
  4. treatment with folic acid [OR] other equivalent supplements
  5. ages 2-21 years [AND] able to provide informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. abnormal folate levels [OR] inadequate folate supplementation
  2. age > 21 [OR] less than 2 [OR] unable to provide informed consent
  3. [NOT] on methotrexate at appropriate dosing
  4. [NOT] on folic acid treatment [OR] equivalent supplements
  5. [NOT] inflammatory bowel disease diagnosis
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
