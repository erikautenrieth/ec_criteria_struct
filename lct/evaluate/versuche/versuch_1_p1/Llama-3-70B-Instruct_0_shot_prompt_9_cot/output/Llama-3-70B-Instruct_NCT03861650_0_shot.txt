<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients [NOT] having any disease affecting bone formation of both sexes were included in the study.
  2. Patients younger than 25 years old.
  3. Patients included in the study were post traumatic patients [OR] hemifacial microsomia [OR] Treacher Collins [OR] post ankylotic mandibular deformity.
  4. Distraction range was between 15-20 mm.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. The presence of uncontrolled systemic disease.
  2. Patients who have had previous radiotherapy on head and neck region.
  3. Patients who have had chemotherapy within the past 12-month period.
  4. The presence of an active infection at the distraction site.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
