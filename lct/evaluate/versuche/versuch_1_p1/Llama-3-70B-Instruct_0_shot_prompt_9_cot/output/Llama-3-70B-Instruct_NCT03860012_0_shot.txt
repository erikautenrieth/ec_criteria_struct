<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. inflammatory bowel disease [AND]
  2. on methotrexate at appropriate dosing [AND]
  3. normal folate levels at onset of study [AND]
  4. treatment with folic acid [AND]
  5. ages 2-21 years

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. abnormal folate levels [OR]
  2. age > 21 [OR] less than 2 [OR]
  3. [NOT] on methotrexate at appropriate dosing [OR]
  4. [NOT] treatment with folic acid
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
