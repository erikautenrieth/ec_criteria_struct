<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients undergoing first time cardiopulmonary bypass surgery for correction of atrial septal defects [OR] tissue mitral valve repair
  2. Receiving intravenous Heparin for cardiopulmonary bypass [AND] undergoing first time cardiopulmonary bypass surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with Concomitant Aortic Stenosis which is associated with an acquired von Willebrand Syndrome (aVWS) [NOT] without aVWS
  -  Patients [NOT] having a baseline platelet count of ≥100x109/L
  -  Patients [NOT] having a known coagulation factor deficiency [OR] platelet function disorder
  -  Patients [NOT] receiving heparin therapy prior to CPB within last 7 days [OR] receiving heparin therapy prior to CPB more than 7 days ago
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
