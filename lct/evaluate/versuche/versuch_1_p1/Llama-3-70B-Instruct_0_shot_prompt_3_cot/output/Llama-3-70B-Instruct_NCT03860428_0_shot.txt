<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Gestational age < 32 weeks
  -  Birthweight < 1500 g
  -  Age less than 72 hours
  -  PDA diameter > 1.5 mm
  -  Signed informed consent obtained from both parents [AND] no other exclusion criteria present

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Birthweight ≥ 1500 g [OR] gestation age ≥ 32 weeks
  -  [NOT] Signed informed consent obtained from both parents
  -  Congenital heart defect [NOT] PDA [AND] [NOT] patent foramen ovale (PFO)
  -  The presence of a clinically apparent hemorrhagic syndrome [AND] [NOT] adequately treated
  -  Any intraventricular hemorrhage (IVH) in the first 48 hours [OR] IVH grade 3-4
  -  A platelet count of < 50,000/mm3 [AND] [NOT] platelet transfusion given
  -  A serum creatinine concentration of > 110 μmol/L [AND] [NOT] adequate hydration
  -  Oliguria <1 ml/kg/h [AND] [NOT] adequate fluid resuscitation
  -  Suspected/apparent NEC [OR] suspected/apparent lung hypoplasia
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
