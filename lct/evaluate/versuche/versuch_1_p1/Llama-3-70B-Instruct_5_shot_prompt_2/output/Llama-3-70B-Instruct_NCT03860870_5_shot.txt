Inclusion Criteria:
 - Healthy
 - Male
 - 18-40 years of age
 - No known contraindications for anabolic drugs (e.g. cancer)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Abnormal ECG
 - Steroid abuse
 - Ongoing use of prescription medication
 - heavy resistance training more than 2 times weekly
 - Disease deemed by the MD to infer a risk to participate in the trial [OR] any other condition that may interfere with the study

Note: I did not add any logical operators to the Inclusion Criteria as they are all independent conditions that must be met. In the Exclusion Criteria, I added an [OR] to the last condition to indicate that any other condition that may interfere with the study would also be an exclusion criterion.
