<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  (diagnosis of schizophrenia OR schizoaffective-depressed) OR healthy subjects
  -  age ≥ 18 AND age ≤ 50
  -  double barrier contraception
  -  NOT pregnant

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  (DSM-IV Axis I OR II Diagnosis) AND healthy subjects
  -  (MEM OR amantadine) AND patients
  -  current substance abuse
  -  current recreational drug use
  -  (history of other significant medical illness (e.g. cancer, diabetes, heart disease, HIV, seizures))
  -  (open head injury OR closed head injury with loss of consciousness > 1 min)
  -  (hearing impairment OR visual impairment)
  -  pregnancy
  -  dementia
  -  mental retardation
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
