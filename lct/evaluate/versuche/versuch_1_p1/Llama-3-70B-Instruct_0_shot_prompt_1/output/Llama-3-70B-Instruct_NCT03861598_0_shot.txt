<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Grade IV Glioblastoma [AND] receiving chemotherapy [OR] TTfields.
  -  Progressive [OR] recurrent disease [AND] requiring second line treatment
  -  evidence of progression of disease by brain MRI [AND] are planned to receive chemotherapy

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Current use of another beta blocker [AND] cannot be switched to carvedilol at the onset of the trial.
  -  Systolic blood pressure <90 mmHg [OR] heart rate <60 bpm [AND] [NOT] taking hypertensive medications
  -  Allergy to beta blockers
  -  Severe [OR] uncontrolled asthma [OR] COPD (chronic obstructive pulmonary disease)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
