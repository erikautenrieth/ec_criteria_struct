<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Children whose age ≥ 24 months AND ≤ 36 months
  -  Full-term OR prematurely born (<33 SA) at Nice University Hospital
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Children with congenital pathologies (with malformations in the oro-facial sphere) OR
  -  Children with neurological AND developmental pathologies unrelated to prematurity OR
  -  Children with ENT deformities OR
  -  Parental language barrier
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
