Inclusion Criteria:
• Patients ([age > 18 years old] AND [ASA status I-III] AND [undergoing VATS for lobectomy or wedge]).

Non-inclusion Criteria:
  - ([age < 18 years old] OR [BMI > 35] OR [chronic pain history with regular opioid and/or gabapentinoids use during the 2 weeks before surgery] OR [regular marijuana use] OR [history of thoracic surgery on the operated side] OR [epidural analgesia preferred due to an anticipated high risk of conversion to thoracotomy] OR [unable to communicate with the investigators] OR [receiving anticoagulation or experiencing any bleeding disorder] OR [surgery for empyema and sympathectomy] OR [known allergy to local anesthetics, fentanyl or hydromorphone] OR [active infection at injection sites] OR [preexisting neurological deficit or psychiatric illness] OR [severe cardiovascular disease] OR [liver failure] OR [renal failure (estimated glomerular filtration rate <15 mL/min/1.73 m2)] OR [pregnancy]).

Exclusion Criteria:
  - ([perioperative conversion to thoracotomy] OR [severe intra- or postoperative bleeding] OR [patients requiring postoperative mechanical ventilation] OR [technical inability to proceed with the blocks]).
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
