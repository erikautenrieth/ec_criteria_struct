<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient is 18 years old or more
  -  Patient is intubated AND ventilated in intensive care unit for more than 24 hours
  -  Patient is ready for weaning evaluation
  -  Patient has had a failure of a first T-piece SBT

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient has chronic neuromuscular disease OR Guillain-Barré Syndrome OR central nervous system disease with consciousness disorder
  -  Patient has tracheostomy
  -  Patient has chronic disease with life expectancy less than 1 year
  -  Patient is pregnant OR breast feeding
  -  Withholding life support regarding a reintubation
  -  Patient is a prisoner OR patient interned in psychiatric hospital
  -  Patient has guardianship
  -  Patient has language barrier
  -  Patient lacks medical insurance
  -  Patient lacks consent (or of the next of kin where appropriate) OR patient is under an exclusion period after enrollment in another research study
  -  NOT (patient meets all inclusion criteria)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
