<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
- (Age > 18 years)
Neonates:
  - ((Age >= 1h of life) AND (Age <= 28 days of life)) 
  - ((Gestational Age >= 33 Week of Amenorrhea + 1day) AND (Birth Weight >= 2kg)) 
  - (Hospitalized in the medical-surgical centre of Pediatrics of the hospital of Strasbourg) 
  - ((Blood sample prescribed) AND (Part of routine care))
Exclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
  - (Systemic inflammatory disease) OR (Transient inflammatory state) OR ((Drug modifying coagulation cascade) AND (Taken within 48h preceding the sampling))
Neonates:
  - (Risk of anemia < 7g/DL) OR (Current anticoagulant treatment)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
