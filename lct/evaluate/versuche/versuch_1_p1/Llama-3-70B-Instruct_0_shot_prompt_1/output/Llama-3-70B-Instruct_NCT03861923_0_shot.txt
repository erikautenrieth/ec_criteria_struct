<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Primary complaint of shoulder pain and stiffness [AND] gradual onset of symptoms [AND] glenohumeral passive range of motion limited in multiple directions [AND] glenohumeral passive range of motion decreases as the humerus is abducted from 45 degrees to 90 degrees [AND] age 18 to 65 years [AND] reports pain with normal activity greater than or equal to 2/10 on the numeric pain rating scale [AND] read and speak English
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [ Signs or symptoms of cervical radiculopathy, radiculitis, or referral from the cervical spine ] [OR] [ Clinical evidence as determined in the examination of an alternative shoulder diagnosis ] [OR] [ Normal passive shoulder range of motion ] [OR] [ Known radiographic evidence of glenohumeral osteoarthritis ] [OR] [ Known neurologic disease ] [OR] [ Received dry needling or acupuncture within the past 6 months ] [OR] [ Fear of needling ] [OR] [ Currently being treated with anticoagulant medication ] [OR] [ Shoulder pain and disability index (SPADI) score less than 10% ] [OR] [ Shoulder pain and disability index (SPADI) score greater than 90% ]
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
