Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Body mass Index > 30 AND
  -  Age 18-64

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pregnancy OR
  -  Health reasons preventing exercise and exercise test OR
  -  Medication preventing exercise and exercise test OR
  -  Previously diagnosed diabetes 

Note: I've added the logical operators [AND] and [OR] to connect the criteria, but I didn't use [NOT] as it's not necessary in this case. The exclusion criteria are already negations, so using [NOT] would be redundant.
