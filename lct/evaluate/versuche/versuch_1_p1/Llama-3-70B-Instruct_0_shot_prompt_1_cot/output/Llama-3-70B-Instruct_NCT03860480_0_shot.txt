Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
• Patients > 18 years old [AND] with ASA status I-III [AND] undergoing VATS for lobectomy [OR] wedge.

Non-inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients < 18 years old
  - BMI > 35
  - Patients with chronic pain history [AND] regular opioid [OR] gabapentinoids use during the 2 weeks before surgery
  - Patients with regular marijuana use
  - Patients with history of thoracic surgery on the operated side
  - Patients who prefer epidural analgesia due to an anticipated high risk of conversion to thoracotomy
  - Patients unable to communicate with the investigators
  - Patients receiving anticoagulation [OR] experiencing any bleeding disorder
  - Patients undergoing surgery for empyema [AND] sympathectomy
  - Patients with known allergy to local anesthetics [OR] fentanyl [OR] hydromorphone
  - Patients with active infection at injection sites
  - Patients with preexisting neurological deficit [OR] psychiatric illness
  - Patients with severe cardiovascular disease
  - Patients with liver failure
  - Patients with renal failure (estimated glomerular filtration rate <15 mL/min/1.73 m2)
  - Pregnant patients

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients who experience perioperative conversion to thoracotomy
  - Patients with severe intra- [OR] postoperative bleeding
  - Patients who require postoperative mechanical ventilation
  - Patients with technical inability to proceed with the blocks
