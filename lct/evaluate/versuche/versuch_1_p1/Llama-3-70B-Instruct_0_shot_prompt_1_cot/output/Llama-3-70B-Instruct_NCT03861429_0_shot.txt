Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Resident Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Identified as having Alzheimer's or related dementia
  AND  Mild to moderate cognitive impairment (BIMS = 8-15, (MDS C0500) (nursing home residents only)
  AND  Makes Self Understood = <1 (MDS B0700)/present in medical record
  AND  Understood By Others = <1 (MDS B0800)/present in medical record
  AND  Reside at the facility at least 2 weeks identified as a long-term resident
  AND  Age >65 years
  AND  Speaks English

Family Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Speaks English
  AND  Willing and able to watch the videos
  AND  Willing to complete short surveys
  OR  Being unable to attend care conference to view the video (e.g. due to distance) will not exclude family from participating

Staff Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Speaks English
  AND  Willing to watch the videos
  AND  Willing to complete short surveys

Resident Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Limited communication capacity (e.g. aphasia)
  OR  Comatose
  OR  Cognitively intact
  OR  Severe cognitive impairment
  NOT  Meeting any of the inclusion criteria
