Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the added logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Participants must have [AND] histologically or cytologically confirmed invasive or in-situ carcinoma of the breast [AND] will undergo unilateral breast cancer surgery
  -  [AND] Willingness to complete SEI-BCLE Questionnaire
  -  [AND] Age >18 years
  -  [AND] Ability to understand [AND] the willingness to verbally consent to the trial

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [OR] History of primary lymphedema
  -  [OR] Any patient with a current case of cellulitis
  -  [OR] Participants with known brain metastases [BECAUSE] they often develop progressive neurologic dysfunction that would confound the evaluation of neurologic and other adverse events
  -  [OR] Uncontrolled intercurrent illness [INCLUDING, BUT NOT LIMITED TO] ongoing or active infection, symptomatic congestive heart failure, unstable angina pectoris, cardiac arrhythmia, or psychiatric illness/social situations that would limit compliance with study requirements
  -  [OR] Participants unable to [NOT] abduct upper-extremity perpendicular to body [OR] to [NOT] stand comfortably without aid for several minutes, both of which are necessary to perform a LymphaTech Scan
