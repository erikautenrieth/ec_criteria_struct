Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the added logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  BMI 30-40 kg/m2
  OR -  BMI 18-25 kg/m2; those with BMI up to 28kg/m2 will be included if waist circumference is <96cm
  AND -  Males
  AND -  Aged 18-60yrs

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Acute illness in the preceding 6 weeks
  OR -  Taking regular medication
  OR -  History of deep vein thrombosis or clotting disorders
  OR -  Hypertension
  OR -  Diabetes
  OR -  Any clinically significant findings at screening
  OR -  History of substance abuse
  OR -  Demonstrating factors precluding safe MRI
  OR -  History of gastrointestinal motility disorders (e.g. gastroesophageal reflux disease -irritable bowel syndrome, gastroparesis, sphincter of Oddi dysfunction, etc.)
  OR -  Previous thoracic or abdominal surgery
  OR -  Those who report having ≤3 bowel movements/week
  OR -  Those who report having >2/day
  OR -  NOT meeting all inclusion criteria
