Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - admitted to the ICU at the University Hospital Basel AND
  - suffered from one or more epileptic seizures (detected clinically OR by EEG) during their ICU-stay

Exclusion Criteria:
  - patients with epileptic seizures NOT transforming into Status epilepticus (SE)
