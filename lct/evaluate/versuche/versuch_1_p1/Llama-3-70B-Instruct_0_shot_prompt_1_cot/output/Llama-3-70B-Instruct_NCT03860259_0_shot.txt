Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Subject is greater than 18 years of age AND
  2. Subject is willing and able to provide informed consent AND
  3. Subject is scheduled to undergo elective rotator cuff surgery AND
  4. Subject has consented to an interscalene block

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Opioid dependence OR
  2. Any subject diagnosed with a chronic pain condition which daily opioid use is needed OR
  3. Anatomical malformation, which in the investigator's opinion may interfere with the placement of the nerve block OR
  4. Raynaud's disease diagnosis OR
  5. Vasculopathy OR
  6. Patient refusal NOT
  (Note: The NOT operator is not typically used in exclusion criteria, as it would imply that the presence of a certain condition is required for exclusion. Instead, the criteria are usually written as a list of conditions that, if present, would exclude a subject from the study.)
