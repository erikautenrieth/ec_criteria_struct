Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] incorporated:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Men and women, aged 18 to 60 years.
  - Body mass index 20 to 27 kg/m2

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients with active inflammatory bowel disease OR patients with pre-existing Celiac disease, exocrine pancreatic insufficiency OR small bowel malabsorption.
  2. Patients with active bowel malignancy AND patients with GI malignancy (GI tract, hepatobiliary, pancreatic).
  3. Patients with diabetes mellitus OR known/suspected motility disorders of the gut.
  4. Patients with decompensated liver disease NOT stabilized with treatment.
  5. Patients on ezetimibe OR bile acid sequestrants.
  6. Patients who are pregnant OR breastfeeding.
  7. Patients with renal disease AND patients with unstable cardiac or respiratory disease.
  8. Patients on benzodiazepine OR any other medication that may interact with Revestive.
  9. Patients with any changes to medication in the preceding month OR patients who have taken Revestive within the last 5 years.
 10. Patients with hypersensitive to Revestive OR any ingredient, active OR a history of the drug within the last 5 years.

Note: I've added the logical operators to make the criteria more specific and clear, but please review and validate the revised criteria to ensure they accurately reflect the original intent.
