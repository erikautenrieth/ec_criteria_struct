Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients undergoing first time cardiopulmonary bypass surgery for correction of atrial septal defects [OR] or tissue mitral valve repair
 2. Receiving intravenous Heparin [AND] for cardiopulmonary bypass
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Concomitant Aortic Stenosis [AND] which is associated with an acquired von Willebrand Syndrome (aVWS)
  -  Patients with a baseline platelet count of <100x109/L
  -  Patients with a known coagulation factor deficiency [OR] or platelet function disorder
  -  Patients receiving heparin therapy prior to CPB within last 7 days [NOT] within the last 7 days
