Inclusion Criteria:
 - Has a 2-year history of seasonal allergic rhinitis
 - Has a positive skin test response to a local seasonal allergen (current, [OR] or performed in investigator's office within the past year)
 - Is in good health [AND] free of any unstable, clinically-significant disease, other than allergic rhinitis, that would interfere with the study schedule [OR] or evaluation of seasonal allergic rhinitis
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Women who are pregnant [OR] breastfeeding
 - Women of childbearing potential who are [NOT] using an acceptable form of birth control
 - Has asthma that requires therapy with inhaled [OR] or systemic corticosteroids, [OR] cromolyn, [OR] or nedocromil
 - Has significant renal, [OR] hepatic, [OR] neurologic, [OR] cardiovascular, [OR] hematologic, [OR] metabolic, [OR] cerebrovascular, [OR] respiratory, [OR] gastrointestinal, [OR] or other significant medical illness or disorder which, in the judgment of the investigator, could interfere with the study, [OR] or required treatment which might interfere with the study
 - Is on immunotherapy with the exception of maintenance therapy
 - Has a clinically significant upper respiratory [OR] or sinus infection
 - Has used an investigational drug within the previous 30 days
 - Has nasal structural abnormalities, including large nasal polyps [AND] marked septal deviation, that significantly interfere with nasal air flow
 - Has a history of multiple drug allergies [OR] or an allergy to antihistamines [OR] or corticoids
 - Has dependence upon nasal, [OR] oral [OR] or ocular decongestants, [OR] or nasal topical antihistamines, in the opinion of the investigator
 - Has rhinitis medicamentosa
 - Is using [NOT] of any chronic medication which could affect the course of seasonal allergic rhinitis
