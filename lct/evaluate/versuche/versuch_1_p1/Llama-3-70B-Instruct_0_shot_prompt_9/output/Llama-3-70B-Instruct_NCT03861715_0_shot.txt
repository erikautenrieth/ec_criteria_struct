Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age 20-34 years;
  -  body mass index (BMI) 18-29kg/m2;
  -  regular menstrual cycle of 26-35 days [AND] AMH levels age appropriate (≥2.3 ng/ml) [AND] early follicular- phase serum concentration of FSH within normal limits (1-12 IU/l).

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  women with diabetes [AND] other metabolic disease;
  -  women with hereditary genetic diseases;
  -  women with heart disease [OR] QT prolongation [OR] heart failure;
  -  elevated liver enzymes [OR] liver failure [OR] hepatitis;
  -  women with inflammatory [OR] autoimmune disease;
  -  abnormal karyotype;
  -  endometriosis stage III [OR] IV;
  -  history of recurrent miscarriage;
  -  severe OHSS in a previous stimulation cycle [OR] any contraindication for the use of gonadotrophins;
  -  [NOT] women with no evidence of metabolic disease;
  -  [NOT] women without hereditary genetic diseases;
  -  [NOT] women without heart disease;
  -  [NOT] women with normal liver enzymes;
  -  [NOT] women without inflammatory [OR] autoimmune disease;
  -  [NOT] women with normal karyotype;
  -  [NOT] women without endometriosis;
  -  [NOT] women without history of recurrent miscarriage;
