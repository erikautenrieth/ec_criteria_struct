<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Non-cardiac surgery patients;
    - Age [AND] is greater than or equal to 60 years old;
    - Han Nationality, [AND] mother tongue is chinese;
    - The MMSE score:Illiteracy [AND] is greater than or equal to 17 points, [OR] primary school [AND] is greater than or equal to 20 points, [OR] higher secondary school [AND] is more than 24 points;
    - The people signed informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - The diagnosis of preoperative delirium;
    - The diagnosis Unstable hypertension（Such as：pheochromocytoma [OR] or aortic dissection）;
    - Severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] or congestive heart failure;
    - Severe liver [OR] and kidney dysfunction;
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
