Inclusion Criteria:
 - patients in different age groups [AND] and both sex referred from orthopedic outpatient clinic [WITH] with chronic ankle pain [AND] and suspected clinically to have impingement syndrome.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Inflammatory arthritis of ankle joint [OR] or infection in ankle joint [OR] or neoplasm around ankle joint.
 2. Patients [WITH] with contraindications for MRI, e.g. an implanted magnetic device [OR] or pacemakers [OR] or claustrophobia.
