<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - [AND] Surgical plan for total hip replacement
 - [AND] Signing of consent form to participate in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
 - [OR] Patient refusal
 - [OR] BMI > 40 kg/m2
 - [OR] Surgical plan for revision of hip replacement
 - [OR] Patient [NOT] unable to quantify pain level
 - [OR] Chronic kidney disease [AND] with a Glomerular Filtration Rate < 50ml/min
 - [OR] Previously medicated with opioids
 - [OR] Patient [NOT] unable to perform the surgery with spinal block
 - [OR] Allergy to local anesthetics
 - [OR] Infection in the site of the Erector Spinae Plane [OR] or Fascia Iliaca block
 - [OR] Allergy [OR] or contraindication to the use of morphine
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
