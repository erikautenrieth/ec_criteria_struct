<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Men [OR] or women age 18-64.
 - Present to ED primary [AND] for management of Low Back Pain (LBP)
 - Functionally impairing back pain: A baseline score [AND] of greater than 5 on the Roland-Morris Disability Questionnaire
 - Musculoskeletal etiology of low back.
 - Non-radicular pain.
 - Pain duration [AND] less than 2 weeks (336 hours).
 - Non-traumatic LBP
 - Participant is to be discharged home.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Flank pain, [OR] that is pain originating from tissues lateral to the paraspinal muscles.
 - [NOT] Not available for follow-up
 - Pregnant
 - Chronic pain syndrome
 - Allergic [OR] or intolerant of investigational medications
 - Contra-indications to investigational medications
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
