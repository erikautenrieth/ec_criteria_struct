<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - patients in different age groups [AND] both sex referred from orthopedic outpatient clinic [AND] with chronic ankle pain [AND] suspected clinically to have impingement syndrome.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Inflammatory arthritis of ankle joint [OR] infection in ankle joint [OR] neoplasm around ankle joint.
  2. Patients [AND] with contraindications for MRI, e.g. [NOT] an implanted magnetic device, [NOT] pacemakers [OR] [NOT] claustrophobia.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
