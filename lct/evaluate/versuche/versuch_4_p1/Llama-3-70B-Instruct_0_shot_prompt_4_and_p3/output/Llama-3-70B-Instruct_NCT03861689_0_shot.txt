<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Age ≥ 18 years old
 2. Confirmed diagnosis of perianal Crohn's disease
 3. On biologics [OR] will start biologics

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients who have perianal fistula due to causes other than Crohn's disease
 2. Fistulation into other organs, including bladder [AND] intestine
 3. Patients who have allergic reaction [OR] contraindications to anti-TNF
 4. Patients who have active cancer
 5. Patients who have contraindications for MRI
 6. Known pregnancy
 7. Patients who [NOT] refuse to participate in the tight monitoring arm, they will be consented [AND] recruited to the control arm for comparison.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
