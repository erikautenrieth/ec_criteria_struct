<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients aged 18-74 [AND] receiving therapy for active ulcerative colitis confirmed by Mayo Score assessment [AND] evidence of colonic inflammation.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Evidence of Crohn's disease [OR] indeterminate colitis [OR] presence of bacterial or parasitic infection.
  -  Patients receiving biologic agents [OR] experimental agents [NOT] excluded.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
