  -  Non-cardiac surgery patients [AND]
       -  Age is greater than or equal to 60 years old [AND]
       -  ③Han Nationality, mother tongue is chinese [AND]
       -  ④The MMSE score:Illiteracy is greater than or equal to 17 points, primary school is greater than or equal to 20 points, higher secondary school is more than 24 points [AND]
       -  ⑤The people signed informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:

       -  The diagnosis Unstable hypertension（Such as：pheochromocytoma or aortic dissection） [OR]
       -  ③Severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] congestive heart failure [OR]
       -  ④Severe liver and kidney dysfunction [OR]
