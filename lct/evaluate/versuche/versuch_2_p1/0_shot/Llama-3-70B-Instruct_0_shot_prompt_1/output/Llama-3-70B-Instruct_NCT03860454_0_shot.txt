<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  LMNA+ cases with pathogenic LMNA mutations [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  DCMWT cases: patients with heart muscle failure [AND] with wild-type lamin gene [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  HV (controls): matched to cases [AND] not meeting any of the above criteria.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Needle-phobia that would preclude blood-letting [OR] participants unwilling to consent [OR] patients that have a conventional contraindication for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) [OR] patients that have had a blood transfusion within the last month [OR] patients having haemodialysis.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
