<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with an unilateral THA [AND] TKA, which they received for the treatment of osteoarthritis (Patient groups 1+2)
  -  Patients diagnosed with unilateral OA of the knee [OR] hip planned for a treatment with THA [OR] TKA (Patient groups 3+4)
  -  Age ≥ 30 years [AND] [NOT] having any of the following conditions (Healthy control group)
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Body mass index > 35kg/m2 [OR] use of walking aids
  -  Neuromuscular disorders affecting gait [AND] inability to follow procedures due to psychological disorders [OR] dementia
  -  [NOT] able to ambulate independently [OR] having diagnosed hip [OR] knee OA (Healthy control group)
  -  Current pain in the lower extremities [OR] lower back (Healthy control group)
  -  Rheumatoid Arthritis [OR] previous corrective osteotomy (Healthy control group)
  -  Regular intake of pain medication (more than 4 times per week) [AND] [NOT] willing to discontinue medication for the study duration (Healthy control group)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
