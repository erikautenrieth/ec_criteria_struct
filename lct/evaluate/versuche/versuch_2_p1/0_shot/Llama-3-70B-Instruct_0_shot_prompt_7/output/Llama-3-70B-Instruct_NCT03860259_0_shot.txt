<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Subject is greater than 18 years of age [AND] 
  2. Subject is willing [AND] able to provide informed consent
  3. Subject is scheduled to undergo elective rotator cuff surgery [AND] 
  4. Subject has consented to an interscalene block

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Opioid dependence [OR] any subject diagnosed with a chronic pain condition which daily opioid use is needed
  2. Anatomical malformation [OR] any condition, which in the investigator's opinion may interfere with the placement of the nerve block
  3. [NOT] cleared by the investigator to participate in the study due to anatomical reasons
  4. Raynaud's disease diagnosis [OR] vasculopathy
  5. [NOT] willing to participate in the study [OR] patient refusal
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
