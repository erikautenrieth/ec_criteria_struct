<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age 1-12 months
  -  IgE-mediated CMA [AND] no other underlying conditions that may interfere with the study

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  cow's milk protein-induced anaphylaxis [OR] food protein induced enterocolitis syndrome [OR] other food allergies [OR] other allergic diseases
  -  non-CMA-related atopic eczema [OR] eosinophilic disorders of the gastrointestinal tract
  -  chronic systemic diseases [OR] congenital cardiac defects [OR] active tuberculosis
  -  autoimmune diseases [OR] immunodeficiency
  -  chronic inflammatory bowel diseases [OR] celiac disease [OR] cystic fibrosis
  -  metabolic diseases [OR] malignancy
  -  chronic pulmonary diseases [OR] malformations of the gastrointestinal [AND] respiratory tract
  -  [NOT] able to discontinue administration of prebiotics or probiotics during the 4 weeks before enrolment [OR] unwilling to do so
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
