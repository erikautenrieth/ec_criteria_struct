<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Children whose age ≥ 24 months [AND] ≤ 36 months
  -  Children born full-term [OR] prematurely (<33 SA) at Nice University Hospital

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Children with congenital pathologies [AND] (with malformations in the oro-facial sphere)
  -  Children with neurological [AND] developmental pathologies [NOT] related to prematurity
  -  Children with ENT deformities
  -  Children with a parental language barrier

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
