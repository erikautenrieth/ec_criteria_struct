<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Men [OR] women age 18-64.
  -  Present to ED primary for management of Low Back Pain (LBP) [AND] requiring treatment.
  -  Functionally impairing back pain: A baseline score of greater than 5 on the Roland-Morris Disability Questionnaire [AND] impacting daily activities.
  -  Musculoskeletal etiology of low back [AND] no other underlying conditions.
  -  Non-radicular pain [NOT] radiating to legs or arms.
  -  Pain duration <2 weeks (336 hours) [AND] no prior episodes.
  -  Non-traumatic LBP [NOT] resulting from injury or accident.
  -  Participant is to be discharged home [AND] able to follow up with primary care physician.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Flank pain, that is pain originating from tissues lateral to the paraspinal muscles [OR] other non-musculoskeletal sources.
  -  [NOT] available for follow-up [OR] unable to comply with study schedule.
  -  Pregnant [OR] breastfeeding [OR] planning to become pregnant during the study.
  -  Chronic pain syndrome [OR] fibromyalgia [OR] other chronic pain conditions.
  -  Allergic to [OR] intolerant of investigational medications [OR] have a history of adverse reactions.
  -  Contra-indications to investigational medications [OR] currently taking medications that interact with investigational medications.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
