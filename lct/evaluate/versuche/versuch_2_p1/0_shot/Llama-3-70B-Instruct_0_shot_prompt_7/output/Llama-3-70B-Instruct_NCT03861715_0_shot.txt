<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age 20-34 years;
  -  body mass index (BMI) [AND] 18-29kg/m2;
  -  regular menstrual cycle [AND] of 26-35 days,
  -  AMH levels [AND] age appropriate (≥2.3 ng/ml)
  -  early follicular- phase serum concentration of FSH [AND] within normal limits (1-12 IU/l).

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  women [WITH] diabetes [OR] other metabolic disease
  -  women [WITH] hereditary genetic diseases;
  -  women [WITH] heart disease [OR] QT prolongation [OR] heart failure
  -  elevated liver enzymes [OR] liver failure [OR] hepatitis
  -  women [WITH] inflammatory [OR] autoimmune disease
  -  abnormal karyotype;
  -  endometriosis [AND] stage III/IV;
  -  history of recurrent miscarriage;
  -  severe OHSS [IN] a previous stimulation cycle [OR] any contraindication [FOR] the use of gonadotrophins [NOT] suitable for this study.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
