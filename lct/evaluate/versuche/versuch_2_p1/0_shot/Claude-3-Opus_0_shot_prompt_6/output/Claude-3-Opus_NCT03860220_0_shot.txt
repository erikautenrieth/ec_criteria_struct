<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  145 mmHg ≤ msSBP ≤ 190 mmHg [AND]
  -  Triglycerides < 500 mg/dL [AND]
  -  LDL-C ≤ 250 mg/dL
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  sSBP ≥ 20mmHg [AND] sDBP ≥ 10mmHg [OR]
  -  symptomatic orthostatic hypotension [OR] secondary/iatrogenic hypertension [OR] dyslipidemia [OR]
  -  history of moderate to severe cerebral ischemia [OR] cerebral hemorrhage [OR] transient ischemic attack [OR] myocardial infarction [OR] unstable angina in the past 6 months [OR] severe heart failure (New York Heart Association functional class III [AND] IV) [OR]
  -  hypersensitivity to telmisartan [OR] rosuvastatin [OR]
  -  history of angioedema after treatment with angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors [OR] ARBs [OR] creatinine phosphokinase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal [OR]
  -  estimated glomerular filtration rate ≤30 mL/min [OR] aspartate aminotransferase [AND] alanine aminotransferase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal [OR]
  -  potassium levels >5.5 mmol/L [OR] any diseases that could affect the results of the study
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
