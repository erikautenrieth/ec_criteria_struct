<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Surgical plan for total hip replacement [AND]
  -  Signing of consent form to participate in the study [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient refusal [NOT]
  -  BMI > 40 kg/m2 [NOT]
  -  Surgical plan for revision of hip replacement [NOT]
  -  Patient unable [NOT] to quantify pain level
  -  Chronic kidney disease [AND] with a Glomerular Filtration Rate < 50ml/min
  -  Previously medicated [OR] with opioids
  -  Patient unable [NOT] to perform the surgery with spinal block [AND]
  -  Allergy [OR] to local anesthetics
  -  Infection [OR] in the site of the Erector Spinae Plane [OR] Fascia Iliaca block
  -  Allergy [OR] or contraindication [NOT] to the use of morphine
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
