<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patient 18 years old or more
  - Intubated [AND] ventilated in intensive care unit for more than 24 hours
  - Patient ready for weaning evaluation
  - Failure of a first T-piece SBT

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Chronic neuromuscular disease
  - Guillain-Barré Syndrome
  - Central nervous system disease [AND] consciousness disorder
  - Tracheostomy
  - Chronic disease [AND] life expectancy less than 1 year
  - Pregnancy [OR] breast feeding
  - [NOT] Withholding life support regarding a reintubation
  - Prisoner [OR] patient interned in psychiatric hospital
  - Guardianship
  - Language barrier
  - [NOT] Lack of medical insurance
  - [NOT] Lack of the patient's consent (or of the next of kin where appropriate)
  - Patient under an exclusion period after enrollment in another research study
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
