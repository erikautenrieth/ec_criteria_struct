Create an edited version of the following inclusion and exclusion criteria that incorporates the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT].
An AND, OR, NOT must not come before a line segment (\n).
[And]: serves as a conjuctive between two Event or Entity annotations. Exceptions: When And is used in an Equality Comparison (i.e., between two numeric values) it should not be annotated.
[Or]: Similar to And, Or rather serves as a disjunctive between a combination of Event or Entity annotations. The word or, or phrase such as and/or. If the surrounding criteria logically mean 'Or' (even if written as 'and'), 'and' may also be annotated as Or.
If examples or instances of an Event follow in parentheses, Or should not be annotated, and instead individual examples (sepsis, pneumonia, urinary tract infection) should each be annotated as an Example Of the condition (e.g., bacterial infection).
If multiple criteria, implicitly or'd - only use Or between criteria if the span between is "or" or "and/or". In other words, only annotate if "or" is explicit. 
[NOT]: Insert NOT to represent a logical negation, a [NOT] can often be placed before words like: "no","not", "unable", "none".
Do not replace the words like or, and, not with [OR] [AND] [NOT] but write the operators in front of them.
