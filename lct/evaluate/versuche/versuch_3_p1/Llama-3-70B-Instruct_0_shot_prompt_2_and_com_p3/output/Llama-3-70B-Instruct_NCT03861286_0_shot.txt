<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients undergoing first time cardiopulmonary bypass surgery for correction of atrial septal defects [OR] tissue mitral valve repair
  2. Receiving intravenous Heparin for cardiopulmonary bypass [AND] surgery
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Concomitant Aortic Stenosis which is associated [AND] with an acquired von Willebrand Syndrome (aVWS)
  -  Patients [NOT] with a baseline platelet count of ≥100x109/L
  -  Patients [NOT] with a known coagulation factor deficiency [OR] platelet function disorder
  -  Patients [NOT] receiving heparin therapy prior to CPB within last 7 days
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
