Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Gynecological procedure
  -  Age 18-50 years
  -  General anesthesia planned

  -  Hypersensitivity for propofol [OR] soya [OR] eggs [OR] peanuts
  -  Pregnancy
  -  Poor health state
  -  Illicit substance use
  -  BMI <20 [OR] >35 kg/m2
  - [NOT] Having a good health state
