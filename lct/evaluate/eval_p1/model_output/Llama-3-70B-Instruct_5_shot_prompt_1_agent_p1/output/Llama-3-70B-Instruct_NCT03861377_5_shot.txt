Inclusion Criteria:
 - Gynecological procedure
 - Age 18-50 years
 - General anesthesia planned
 - Systolic blood pressure < 150 mmHg, [AND] HR < 100 beats/min
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pre-existing hypertension
 - Diabetes
 - Ischemic heart disease [OR] cerebrovascular disease
 - Heart valve disease
 - Verified cardiac arrhythmia [NOT] other than extrasystoles
 - Verified anaemia [AND] with hemoglobin level below 9.0 gr/dl.
 - Kidney [OR] or hepatic disease
 - Hypersensitivity for propofol, [OR] soya, [OR] eggs [OR] or peanuts
 - Pregnancy
 - Poor health state
 - Illicit substance use
 - BMI <20 [OR] >35 kg/m2
