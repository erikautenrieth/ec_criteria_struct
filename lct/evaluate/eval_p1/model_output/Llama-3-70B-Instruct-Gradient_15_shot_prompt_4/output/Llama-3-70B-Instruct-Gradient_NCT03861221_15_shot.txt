Inclusion Criteria:
 - Female, [AND] older than 35 years. [AND] Already have mammography, [OR] other standard exams (ultrasound, MRI, etc). [AND] Could participate this trial within 2 weeks after mammography. [AND] Have the ability of consent inform.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - During pregnancy [OR] or lactation period.
 - Patients that may [NOT] be capable of prone position, such as patients with scapulohumeral periarthritis, cardiac pacemaker or have recent cardiac surgery.
 - Lymphoma patients that under therapy.
 - Severe scoliosis.
