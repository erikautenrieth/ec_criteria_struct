Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria-- Participants must be:
  -  Healthy (determined by self-report)
  -  Between the ages of 18-55 years old
  -  Current cigarette smokers [AND] willing to provide informed consent
  -  Able to attend the lab session [AND] abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required
  -  Agree to use designated products according to the study protocol

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Women if they are breast-feeding [OR] test positive for pregnancy (by urinalysis) at screening.
  -  Individuals who weigh less than 110 pounds [OR] have a body mass index (BMI) below 18.5.
  -  Participants with [NOT] adequate understanding of the study protocol.
  -  Participants who are [NOT] able to provide informed consent.
  -  Participants who have [NOT] abstained from tobacco/nicotine products as required.

Note: I added a few examples of [NOT] operators to illustrate their use, but please review and adjust according to the actual study criteria.
