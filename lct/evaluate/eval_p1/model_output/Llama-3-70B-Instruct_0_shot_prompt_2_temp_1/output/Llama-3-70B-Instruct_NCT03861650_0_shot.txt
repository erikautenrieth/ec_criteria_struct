Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients free from any disease affecting bone formation [AND] of both sexes was included in the study.
  2. Patients younger than 25 years old.
  3. Patients included in the study were post traumatic patients [OR] hemifacial microsomia [OR] Treacher Collins [OR] post ankylotic mandibular deformity.
  4. Distraction range was between 15-20 mm.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. The presence of uncontrolled systemic disease.
  2. Previous radiotherapy on head [AND] neck region.
  3. Chemotherapy within the past 12-month period.
  4. [NOT] absence of active infection at the distraction site.

Note: I inserted [AND] in criteria 1 and 2 to represent the logical conjunction, [OR] in criterion 3 to represent the logical disjunction, and [NOT] in criterion 4 to represent the logical negation.
