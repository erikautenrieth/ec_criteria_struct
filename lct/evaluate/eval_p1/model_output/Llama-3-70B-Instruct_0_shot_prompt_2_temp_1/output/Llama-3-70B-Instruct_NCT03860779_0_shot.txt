Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age above 18
  -  ASA classification I [AND] II
  -  Indication for colonoscopy [AND] with propofol sedation
  -  Written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding
  -  [NOT] able to provide written informed consent
  -  [NOT] indication for colonoscopy
  -  [NOT] ASA classification I [OR] II
