Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Subject is greater than 18 years of age
  2. Subject is willing [AND] able to provide informed consent
  3. Subject is scheduled to undergo elective rotator cuff surgery
  4. Subject has consented to an interscalene block

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Opioid dependence
  2. Any subject diagnosed with a chronic pain condition [AND] daily opioid use is needed
  3. Anatomical malformation, which in the investigator's opinion may interfere with the placement of the nerve block
  4. Raynaud's disease diagnosis
  5. Vasculopathy
  6. Patient [NOT] willing to participate (refusal)

Note: I inserted [AND] in criteria 2 and 4 to represent the logical conjunction, and [NOT] in criterion 6 to represent the logical negation. I did not insert [OR] as it was not necessary in this case.
