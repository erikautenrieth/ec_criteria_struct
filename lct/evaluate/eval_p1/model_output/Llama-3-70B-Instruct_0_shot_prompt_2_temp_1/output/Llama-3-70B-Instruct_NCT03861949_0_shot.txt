Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  adult patients of ASA physical status I [AND] II undergoing surgery under general anaesthesia

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  anticipated difficult mask ventilation [OR] intubation
  -  individuals with significant cardiorespiratory [OR] cerebrovascular disease
  -  pregnant ladies
  -  patient with history of epilepsy
  -  body mass index >25 kg/m2
  -  hemoglobin <8 gm/dL
  -  patients who were [NOT] ambulant for >24 h, having SpO2 < 97% while breathing in room air
  -  phobia to facemask.

Note: I inserted [AND] in the inclusion criteria to represent the logical conjunction between ASA physical status I and II. In the exclusion criteria, I inserted [OR] to represent the logical disjunction between "difficult mask ventilation" and "intubation", and between "cardiorespiratory" and "cerebrovascular disease". I also inserted [NOT] to represent the logical negation of "ambulant" in the seventh exclusion criterion.
