Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Older adults (≥ 65 years)
  -  Subject has lower appendicular muscle mass index (AMMI) (<7 kg/m^2 in men; <5.7 kg/m^2 in women) using Bioelectrical impedance analysis (BIA, TANITA BC-418®, Tokyo, Japan)
  -  Subject has [OR] lower handgrip strength (<26 kg in men; <18 kg in women) using dynamometer (CAMRY®, Zhongshan, Guangdong) [OR] slowly gait speed (<1 m/s)

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Subject's life expectancy was shorter than 6 months
  -  [AND] Nursing home resident
  -  Subject has [AND] the history of kidney failure
  -  Subject has [AND] the history of liver failure
  -  Subject has [AND] the history of diabetes mellitus
  -  Subject has [NOT] resistance exercise habit, meaning they do not have a resistance exercise habit.

Note: I inserted [OR] in the inclusion criteria to indicate that the subject can have either lower handgrip strength or slowly gait speed. I inserted [AND] in the exclusion criteria to indicate that the subject must meet each of the specified conditions to be excluded. I inserted [NOT] in the last exclusion criterion to indicate that the subject must not have a resistance exercise habit to be eligible.
