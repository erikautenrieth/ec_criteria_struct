Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients undergoing first time cardiopulmonary bypass surgery for correction of atrial septal defects [OR] tissue mitral valve repair
  2. Receiving intravenous Heparin for cardiopulmonary bypass [AND] undergoing first time cardiopulmonary bypass surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Concomitant Aortic Stenosis which is associated with an acquired von Willebrand Syndrome (aVWS) [AND] not receiving treatment for aVWS
  -  Patients with a baseline platelet count of <100x109/L [OR] patients with a history of thrombocytopenia
  -  Patients with a known coagulation factor deficiency [OR] platelet function disorder [NOT] receiving treatment for the deficiency or disorder
  -  Patients receiving heparin therapy prior to CPB within last 7 days [NOT] having a normal coagulation profile
