Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with a endovenous pacemaker [OR] defibrillator indication.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pre-existing [AND] ipsilateral pacing electrode.
  -  Previous [AND] ipsilateral lymphadenectomy.
  -  Indication of cardiac resynchronization therapy [NOT] present.

Note: I inserted [OR] in the inclusion criteria to indicate that patients with either a pacemaker or defibrillator indication are eligible. In the exclusion criteria, I inserted [AND] to indicate that both pre-existing and ipsilateral conditions are required for exclusion. I also inserted [NOT] to indicate that the absence of cardiac resynchronization therapy indication is required for inclusion.
