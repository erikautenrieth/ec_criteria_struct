 Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] A minimum of 18 years old
  - [AND] Experienced user (≥ 6 months) of a MED-EL cochlear implant ([OR]) (C40+ and later model) or Experienced user of a MED-EL SONNET audio processor ([OR]) (≥ 6 months)
  - [AND] Post-lingual onset of bilateral severe to profound sensory-neural hearing loss
  - [AND] Unilateral CI user
  - [AND] A minimum of 10 active electrodes
  - [AND] A minimum of 40% speech recognition in the Freiburg Monosyllables test in quiet at 65 dB sound pressure level (SPL; at the last time tested)
  - [AND] Fluent in German (the language of the test centre)
  - [AND] Signed and dated Informed Consent Form before the start of any study-specific procedure.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Lack of compliance with any inclusion criteria
  - [AND] CI user with [NOT] contralateral hearing equal to or better than 60 dB (PTA measured at 500, 1000, and 2000Hz)
  - [AND] User with electric-acoustic stimulation (EAS; user of an EAS audio processor)
  - [AND] Implanted with C40X and C40C or Implanted with an Auditory Brainstem Implant or Split electrode array
  - [AND] Known allergic reactions to components of the investigational medical device
  - [AND] Unstable psychological status
  - [AND] Anything that, in the opinion of the Investigator, would place the subject at increased risk or preclude the subject's full compliance with or completion of the study.
