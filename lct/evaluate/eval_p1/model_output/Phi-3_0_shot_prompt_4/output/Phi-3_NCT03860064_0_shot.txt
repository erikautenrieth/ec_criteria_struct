 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  18-35-year-old women [AND]
  -  BMI: >18 [AND] and < 30 kg/m2 [AND]
  -  Normal female karyotype [AND]
  -  Normal uterus and ovaries at transvaginal ultrasound [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  > 35 years [OR]
  -  PCO syndrome carriers [OR]
  -  Null follicle aspiration [OR]
  -  History of chemotherapy or radiotherapy [OR]
