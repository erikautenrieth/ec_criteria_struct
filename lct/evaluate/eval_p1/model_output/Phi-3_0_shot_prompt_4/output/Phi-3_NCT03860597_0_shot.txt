 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  diagnosis of schizophrenia [OR] schizoaffective-depressed [OR] healthy subjects
  -  ages 18-50 [AND] not pregnant [AND] not dementia [AND] not mental retardation [AND] not hearing or visual impairment [AND] not open head injury or closed head injury with loss of consciousness > 1 min [AND] not current substance abuse [AND] not current recreational drug use [AND] not history of other significant medical illness (e.g. cancer, diabetes, heart disease, HIV, seizures)
  -  double barrier contraception [AND] not pregnant

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  DSM-IV Axis I or II Diagnosis [AND] not healthy subjects [AND] not dementia [AND] not mental retardation [AND] not hearing or visual impairment [AND] not open head injury or closed head injury with loss of consciousness > 1 min [AND] not current substance abuse [AND] not current recreational drug use [AND] not history of other significant medical illness (e.g. cancer, diabetes, heart disease, HIV, seizures)
