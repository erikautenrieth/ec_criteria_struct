 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  [AND] Primary complaint of shoulder pain and stiffness
  -  Gradual onset of symptoms
  -  [AND] Glenohumeral passive range of motion limited in multiple directions
  -  [AND] Glenohumeral passive range of motion decreases as the humerus is abducted from 45 degrees to 90 degrees
  -  [AND] Age 18 to 65 years
  -  [AND] Reports pain with normal activity [OR] greater than or equal to 2/10 on the numeric pain rating scale
  -  [AND] Read and speak English

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Signs or symptoms of cervical radiculopathy, radiculitis, or referral from the cervical spine
  -  [AND] Clinical evidence as determined in the examination of an alternative shoulder diagnosis [NOT] normal
  -  [AND] Normal passive shoulder range of motion [NOT]
  -  [AND] Known radiographic evidence of glenohumeral osteoarthritis [NOT]
  -  [AND] Known neurologic disease [NOT]
  -  [AND] Received dry needling or acupuncture within the past 6 months [NOT]
  -  [AND] Fear of needling [NOT]
  -  [AND] Currently being treated with anticoagulant medication [NOT]
  -  [AND] Shoulder pain and disability index (SPADI) score [NOT] less than 10% or greater than 90%
