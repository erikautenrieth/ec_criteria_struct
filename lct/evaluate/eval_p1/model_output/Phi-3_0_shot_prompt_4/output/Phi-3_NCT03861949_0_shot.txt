 Inclusion Criteria:
  - adult patients of ASA physical status I and II [AND] undergoing surgery under general anaesthesia [AND] [NOT] anticipated difficult mask ventilation/ intubation [AND] [NOT] individuals with significant cardiorespiratory or cerebrovascular disease [AND] [NOT] pregnant ladies [AND] [NOT] patient with history of epilepsy [AND] body mass index ≤25 kg/m2 [AND] hemoglobin ≥8 gm/dL [AND] ambulant patients [AND] SpO2 ≥97% while breathing in room air [AND] [NOT] phobia to facemask.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - anticipated difficult mask ventilation/ intubation [OR] individuals with significant cardiorespiratory or cerebrovascular disease [OR] pregnant ladies [OR] patient with history of epilepsy [OR] body mass index >25 kg/m2 [OR] hemoglobin <8 gm/dL [OR] patients who were non-ambulant for >24 h [OR] having SpO2 < 97% while breathing in room air [OR] phobia to facemask.
