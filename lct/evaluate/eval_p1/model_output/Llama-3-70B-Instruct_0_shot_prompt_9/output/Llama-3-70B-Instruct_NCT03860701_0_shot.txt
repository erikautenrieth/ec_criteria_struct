<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with NEC (stages I-III Bell's Classification) [AND] weight greater or equal to 750 g [AND] gestational age < 36 weeks [AND] patients whose parents consent to participate in the study.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with major congenital anomalies [OR] patients with [NOT] antecedent of limb ischemia/limb thrombotic events [OR] patients with [NOT] antecedent diagnosis of occlusive arterial or venous thrombosis [OR] hemodynamic instability.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
