Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients met diagnostic criteria for schizophrenia [OR] or schizoaffective disorder using the Structural Clinical Interview for Diagnostic and Statistical Manual Diploma in Social Medicine (DSM)-IV (SCID, Version 2.0).
  -  Patients remain their psychotropic medication at steady dosages for at least 4 weeks prior to study entry [AND] for the duration of the trial.
  -  Verbal intelligence quotient > 85 as measured by using a Chinese version of the National Adult Reading Test.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History of significant head trauma [OR] or neurological disorders
  -  Alcohol [OR] or drug abuse
  -  Focal brain lesions on T1- [OR] or T2-weighted fluid-attenuated inversion-recovery magnetic resonance images
  -  a prior history of a seizure [NOT] induced by drug withdrawal, [OR] first degree relative with epilepsy, [OR] significant neurological illness [OR] or head trauma, [OR] endocrine disease, such as thyroid disease, [OR] significant unstable medical condition, [OR] recent aggression [OR] or other forms of behavioral dyscontrol
  -  left-handedness [OR] pregnancy
  -  estimated intelligence quotient < 80
  -  current alcohol [OR] or drug abuse
  -  inability to provide informed consent.
  -  Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale [OR] or the Hamilton Depression Rating Scale score > 10
