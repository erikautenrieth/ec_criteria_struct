Inclusion Criteria:
 1. ≥ 20 y/o patients with NLCs: (1) 4 times/week, [AND] last 2 weeks; (2) Occurred in nighttime [OR] or resting time of daytime
 2. Patients with MTrPs on gastrocnemius (According to the diagnostic criteria proposed by Simons [AND] Travell)
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. (1) Taking medication for leg cramps (eg. Quinine, [OR] Magnesium oxide); (2) Other drugs that affect research [AND] evaluation (eg. diuretics, [OR] statins, [OR] calcium channel blockers, [OR] anticonvulsants)
 2. Congenital lower limb musculoskeletal diseases, [OR] Lower limb or spine surgery
 3. Uncommunicated [OR] cognitive impaired
 4. Patients [NOT] refused to be recruited
