Inclusion Criteria:
 - White European [OR] or black African [OR] Caribbean heritage
 - BMI >18 kg/m2
 - Ability to give written informed consent
 - English speaking
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Any significant medical condition
 - Uncontrolled hypertension
 - Alcohol consumption >14 units / week
 - Random blood Glucose concentration above 7.8mmol/l
 - Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) >47mmol/mol (>6.4%)
 - Use of regular medication, [NOT] (oral contraceptive pill [OR] or antihypertensive medication are acceptable if use has been for >6 months)
 - History of Irritable Bowel Syndrome, [OR] food intolerances [OR] or any gastrointestinal disorders
 - Previous bariatric surgery, [OR] or gastrointestinal surgery which may affect normal function (e.g. bowel resection)
