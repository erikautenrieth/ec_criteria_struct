Inclusion Criteria:
  - clinical suspicion of urinary tract infection (UTI) defined by the presence of at least one of these signs: fever > 38°C [OR] and/or sus-pubic pain [OR] and/or dysuria [OR] and/or pollakiuria [OR] and/or urinary burns [OR] and/or macroscopic hematuria [OR] and/or acute urinary retention [OR] and/or pain with the rectal examination [OR] and/or confusion
  - And urinary analysis with leukocyturia > 10 / mm3 [AND] bacteriuria > 10^3 CFU/mL [AND] with ESBL producing enterobacteriaceae [AND] resistance associated with fluoroquinolones (FQ) [AND] and cotrimoxazole (CTX) [AND] but sensitive to fosfomycin.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - allergy to fosfomycin [OR] and/or trometamol
  - Presence of material in the urinary tract
  - Severe immunosuppression
  - Chronic prostatitis
  - Prostate abscess
  - Acute pyelonephritis
  - Hemodynamic instability
  - Chronic renal failure (clearance <60 mL/min)
  - Prior antibiotic therapy, with an antibiotic sensitive on the antibiotic susceptibility test [NOT] except: amoxicillin - clavulanic acid, cefixime, nitrofurantoin.
  - Co-treatment with metoclopramide
