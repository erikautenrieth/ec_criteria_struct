Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Clinically diagnosed facial skin aging.
  -  Glogau photoaging score II [OR] and III.
  -  Body mass index ≥20 [AND] with adequate abdominal [OR] or other subcutaneous adipose tissue accessible for lipoaspiration.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History of keloid formation.
  -  Any coincidental chronic illness (e.g. metabolic, [OR] autoimmune [OR] or endocrinal) [OR] malignancy.
  -  Any bleeding [OR] or coagulation disorder [OR] recent use of anticoagulant therapy.
  -  Active infection.
  -  History of any previous aesthetic procedure on the face within the past 6 months.
  -  History of intake of anti-aging systemic [OR] or topical medications within the previous 3 months.
