Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient candidate to pulmonary metastasectomy with curative intent by mean of both major anatomical [OR] or limited resection;
 - Age > 18 years.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with widespread metastatic disease [AND] where surgical resection is performed only for diagnostic purposes;
 - History of previous intrapleural talc injection;
 - Any anatomical [OR] or pathological condition (eg. diffuse adhesions, [OR] severe emphysema) that may interfere with execution of ultrasonographic investigation;
 - Incomplete lung exclusion during surgical procedure [NOT] complete.
