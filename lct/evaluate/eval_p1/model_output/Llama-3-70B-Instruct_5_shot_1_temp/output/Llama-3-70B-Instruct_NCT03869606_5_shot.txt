Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients 11 [AND] to no older than 17 years of age

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  severed developmental delay,
  -  orthopedic patients with leg injuries
  -  injury that would prevent passive leg elevation.
  -  severe aortic insufficiency,
  -  severe anatomic abnormalities of the thoracic aorta
  -  patients with external cardiac pacemakers.

Note: There were no opportunities to insert [OR] or [NOT] operators in this specific eligibility criteria.
