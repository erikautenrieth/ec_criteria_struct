Inclusion Criteria:
 - central venous catheter more than 48 hours in situ
 - consent of a patient

Exclusion Criteria:
 - technical difficulties of ultrasound machine
 - unfavorable local findings - inflammation of skin [OR] excoriation etc.

Note: There are no opportunities to insert [AND] or [NOT] operators in this specific eligibility criteria, as the conditions are simple and do not require logical conjunction or negation.
