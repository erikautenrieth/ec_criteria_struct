Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Prediabetic subjects.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Confirmed diabetes.
  -  Hepatic [OR] renal impairment.

Note: The original text is quite short, and the logical operators are only needed in the Exclusion Criteria section. The [OR] operator is used to indicate that either hepatic or renal impairment is a reason for exclusion.
