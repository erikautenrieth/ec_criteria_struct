Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients of both sexes [AND] with a diagnosis of stable CAD as follows: acute myocardial infarction [OR] percutaneous coronary revascularization [OR] coronary artery bypass graft surgery occured at least 12 months before entering the study [OR] chronic stable angina.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Mental illness [AND] limiting the capacity of self-care [OR] any condition [AND] limiting seriously the life expectancy less than 12 months.
