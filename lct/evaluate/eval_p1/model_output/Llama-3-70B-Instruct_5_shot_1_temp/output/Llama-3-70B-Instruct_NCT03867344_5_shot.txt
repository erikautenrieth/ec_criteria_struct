Inclusion Criteria:
 - Healthy volunteers
 - Males [AND] Females age 18 to 45 years
 - BMI 18-30 kg/m2
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnancy
 - Lactation
 - Menopause
 - Any medical condition
 - Current [OR] prior alcohol [OR] drug abuse
 - Active tobacco use
 - Abnormal ECG
 - In all subjects, any individuals on oral, [OR] injected, [OR] inhaled [OR] topical corticosteroids within the last year [OR] oral contraceptives within the past 3 months will be excluded.
 - Use of medications
 - Serum potassium >5.0 mmol/L
 - Estimated GFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m2
 - Hemoglobin A1c ≥6.5%
 - LDL >130 mg/dL; [OR] Triglycerides >150 mg/dL
 - Patient Health Questionnaire (PHQ9) for depression score ≥15
 - GAD-73 Questionnaire for anxiety score ≥10
 - Primary Care PTSD Screen for DSM-5 for post-traumatic stress disorder score ≥10
 - Blood pressure systolic ≥140 [OR] <100 mmHg; [OR] Blood pressure diastolic >90 mmHg
 - Metal in the body including: cardiac pacemakers, [OR] stents, [OR] artificial heart valves, [OR] artificial limbs [OR] hands, [OR] brain stimulator devices, [OR] implanted drug pumps, [OR] ear implants, [OR] eye implants [OR] known metal fragments in eyes, [OR] exposure to shrapnel [OR] metal filings (wounded in military combat, [OR] sheet metal workers, [OR] welders, [AND] others), [OR] other metallic surgical hardware in vital areas, [OR] certain tattoos with metallic ink, [OR] certain transdermal medication patches, [OR] and metal-containing IUDs
