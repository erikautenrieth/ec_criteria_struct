Inclusion Criteria:
 - patients >18 years old, [AND] undergoing elective hepatobiliary surgery [AND] under general anesthesia [AND] American Society of Anesthesiologists grade II.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - 1-patient refusal [OR] 2-psychiatric disorders [OR] 3-pregnancy [OR] lactation [OR] 4-preexisting neurological dysfunction (history of cerebrovascular stroke CVS) [OR] 5-metastatic cancer [OR]
     6-Inflammatory bowel disease [OR] 7-Diabetes mellitus [OR] 8-Renal insufficiency (serum creatinine level more than 180 μmol/l) [OR] 9- unexpected intraoperative findings (inoperable) [OR] 10-accidental massive intraoperative hemorrhage.

Note: I inserted the logical operators [AND] and [OR] to connect the conditions in the inclusion and exclusion criteria, respectively. I did not insert [NOT] as it was not necessary in this case.
