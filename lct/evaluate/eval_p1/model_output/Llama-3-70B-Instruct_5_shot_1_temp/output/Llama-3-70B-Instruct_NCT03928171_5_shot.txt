Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adults (≥ 18 years old)
  -  Scheduled for robot-assisted colorectal laparoscopic surgery
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Severe liver- [OR] renal disease
  -  Pregnancy [OR] or lactation
  -  Planned diagnostics [OR] or treatment with radioactive iodine < 1 week after surgery
  -  BMI >35 kg/m2
  -  Known [OR] or suspected hypersensitivity to indocyanine green, [OR] sodium iodide [OR] or iodine
  -  Hyperthyroidism [OR] or thyroid adenomas
  -  Use of medication [NOT] interfering with ICG absorption as listed in the summary of product characteristics.
