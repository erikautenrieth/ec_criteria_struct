Inclusion Criteria:
 -  Adults over 50 years old [AND] with degenerative scoliosis (n=20) [AND] with pain (>2/10). Other inclusion criteria are: (1) curves [≥] 15° [AND] [≤] 45°.

Exclusion Criteria:
 -  Curves over 50° are surgical candidates, [AND] will be excluded. Patients who have had cortisone [OR] anesthetic injections within the last 3 months, [OR] surgery [OR] presenting contra-indications to exercises ([NOT] fractures, [NOT] cancer, [NOT] severe cardio-vascular co-morbidities, [OR] osteopenia) will be excluded.
