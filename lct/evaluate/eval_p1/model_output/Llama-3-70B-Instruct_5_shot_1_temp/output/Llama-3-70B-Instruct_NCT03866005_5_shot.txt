Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Diagnosed diabetes mellitus [AND] treatment-naïve CI-DME requiring anti-VEGF therapy per retinal specialist's customary treatment regimen will be recruited to participate in the study. CI-DME will be defined as a central subfield thickness (CST) > 300 microns on spectral domain optical coherence tomography (SD-OCT) [OR] CST > 250 microns with definite intra-retinal cystic fluid accumulation. Additional inclusion criteria are age > 18 years, [AND] and ability to give informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Age < 18 years, [OR] inability to give informed consent, [OR] evidence of proliferative diabetic retinopathy, [OR] evidence of other serious ocular disease (age-related macular degeneration, [OR] glaucoma, [OR] significant media opacity), [OR] history of previous intraocular surgery, including macular [OR] or panretinal photocoagulation (except uncomplicated cataract [OR] or keratorefractive surgery more than 6 months prior to enrollment), [OR] pregnant [AND] nursing women, [OR] known sensitivity to any of the supplement ingredients.
