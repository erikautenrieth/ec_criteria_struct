Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient with germ cell tumor.
  -  Patient with a mobile phone.
  -  Patient on surveillance.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Testicular cancer [NOT] without germ cell component.
  -  Prior history of other malignancy [NOT] except for: cutaneous cancers [NOT] excluding melanoma, [OR] superficial bladder tumor, [OR] localized prostate cancer with undetectable PSA.
  -  Cognitive condition that would preclude patient's understanding [AND] completion of study procedures.
