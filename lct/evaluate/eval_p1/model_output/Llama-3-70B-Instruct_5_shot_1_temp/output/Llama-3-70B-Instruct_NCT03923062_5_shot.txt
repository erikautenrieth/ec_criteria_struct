Inclusion Criteria:
  - Subjects presented with melasma.
  - Age 20-40 years old.
  - Melasma persisting for more than 6 months [AND] that has failed to respond to conventional treatment with hydroquinone [OR] other topical lightening agents.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - History of photosensitivity, [OR] keloids, [OR] hypertrophic scarring [AND] post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation.
  - Pregnancy [OR] lactation.
  - Subjects with local inflammatory skin disorder [OR] active herpes infection at the site of procedure.
  - Subjects with history of medical diseases which contraindicate cryosurgery such as; cold intolerance, [OR] cold urticaria, [OR] Raynaud`s disease [AND] history of allergic reactions to cryosurgery.
