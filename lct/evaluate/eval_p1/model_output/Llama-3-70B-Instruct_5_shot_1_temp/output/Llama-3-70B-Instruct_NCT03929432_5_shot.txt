Inclusion Criteria:
  - Presence of post-stroke aphasia
  - Single left hemisphere stroke etiology
  - At least 6 months post-stroke
  - Age range between 18 [AND] 80 years old
  - Speak English as a native language
  - Adequate hearing [AND] vision to complete the tasks
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Severe auditory comprehension deficits (determined by pretest) (i.e., global aphasia, [OR] Wernicke's aphasia, [OR] transcortical sensory aphasia)
  - Inability to provide informed consent
  - Co-occurring history of neurological disease [OR] disorder [OR] injury (e.g., traumatic brain injury, [OR] right hemisphere stroke, [OR] dementia)
  - Co-occurring history of a major mental illness (e.g., schizophrenia, [OR] drug addiction, [OR] bipolar)
  - Clinical conditions contraindicated for MRI [OR] tDCS (e.g., implanted electrical devices, [OR] claustrophobia, [OR] seizure disorder)
  - Positive pregnancy test [NOT] (for females)
