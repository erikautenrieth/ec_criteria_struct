Inclusion Criteria:
  -  The selection criteria of the subjects will be as follows:
       1. they need to be [NOT] on medication [AND] [NOT] on intake of dietary supplements
       2. they need to have [NOT] blood clotting disorders [OR] dyslipidaemia
Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects with clotting disorders [OR] dyslipidemia [OR] being on medication (i.e. statins) [OR] or other supplements during the study will be excluded.
