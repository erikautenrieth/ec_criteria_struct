Inclusion Criteria
  -  Age > or = 18 years old
  -  Patients willing [AND] to participate [AND] provide an informed consent
  -  Patients undergoing an elective surgical procedure [AND] that requires use of non-depolarizing NMBA agents administered intraoperatively.
Exclusion Criteria
  -  Patients with unilateral disorders, such as stroke, [OR] carpal tunnel syndrome, [OR] broken wrist with nerve damage, [OR] Dupuytren contracture, [OR] or any similar wrist injury.
  -  Patients with systemic neuromuscular diseases such as myasthenia gravis
  -  Patients with significant organ dysfunction [AND] that can significantly affect pharmacokinetics of neuromuscular blocking [AND] reversal agents, i.e., severe renal impairment [OR] or end-stage liver disease.
