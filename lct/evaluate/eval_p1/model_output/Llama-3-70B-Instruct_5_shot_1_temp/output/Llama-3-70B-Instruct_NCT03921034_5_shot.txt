Inclusion Criteria:
 - Unilateral, primary tricompartment total knee arthroplasty
 - Age 18 years or older
 - ASA I-[OR] III
 - Eligible for spinal [OR] or combined spinal epidural anesthetic
 - Able to speak, [AND] read, [AND] understand English
 - Willing [AND] able to participate in the trial
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Contraindication to regional anesthesia [OR] peripheral nerve blocks
 - Allergy to local anesthetics
 - Allergy to NSAIDs
 - Chronic renal insufficiency [AND] with Cr > 1.4 [OR] GFR < 60
 - Have chronic pain [NOT] that is related to their knee joint
 - Have been using opioids on a chronic basis (daily [OR] almost daily opioid use for 3 months or longer)
 - Have a pre-existing peripheral neuropathy [AND] involving the operative site
 - Body mass index greater than [OR] equal to 40
