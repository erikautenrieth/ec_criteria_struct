 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Healthy [AND]
  -  Male [AND]
  -  18-40 years of age [AND]
  -  No known contraindications for anabolic drugs (e.g. cancer) [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Abnormal ECG [OR]
  -  Steroid abuse [OR]
  -  Ongoing use of prescription medication [OR]
  -  heavy resistance training more than 2 times weekly [OR]
  -  Disease deemed by the MD to infer a risk to participate in the trial [AND]
