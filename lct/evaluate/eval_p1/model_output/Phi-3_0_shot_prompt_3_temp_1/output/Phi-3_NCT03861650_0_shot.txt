 Inclusion Criteria:
1. [AND] Patients free from any disease affecting bone formation of both sexes was included in the study.
2. [AND] Patients younger than 25 years old.
3. [AND] Patients included in the study were post traumatic patients, [OR] hemifacial microsomia, [OR] Treacher Collins, [OR] post ankylotic mandibular deformity.
4. [AND] Distraction range was between 15-20 mm.

Exclusion Criteria:
1. [AND] The presence of uncontrolled systemic disease.
2. [AND] Previous radiotherapy on head and neck region.
3. [AND] Chemotherapy within the past 12-month period.
4. [AND] An active infection at the distraction site.
