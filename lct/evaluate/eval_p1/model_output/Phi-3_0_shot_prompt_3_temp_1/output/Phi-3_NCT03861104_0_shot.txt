 Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] patients who will undergo septoplasty/septorhinoplasty procedures
  - [AND] patients who will receive nasal packing at their septoplasty/septorhinoplasty procedures

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] patients who will want to participate in the study
  - [AND] revision surgeries
  - [AND] if the patient is taking antidepressants during the last 6 months
  - [AND] if the patient has a history of a disease that can affect anxiety levels
  - [NOT] if the patient wants to be excluded from the study
