 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age [8yr AND 18yrs]
  -  Female [AND]
  -  Likely to have order placed for transabdominal pelvic ultrasound/ovarian ultrasound [AND]
  -  No history of pelvic or bladder reconstructive surgery [NOT]

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pregnancy [NOT] (known)
  -  Critically ill patients [NOT]
  -  Patients with known renal or genitourinary structural abnormalities or prior pelvic/genitourinary surgery [NOT]
  -  Chronic renal disease [NOT]
  -  Patients presenting outside the defined treatment windows [NOT]
