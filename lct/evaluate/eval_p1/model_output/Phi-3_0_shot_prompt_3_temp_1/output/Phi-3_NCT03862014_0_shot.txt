 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  [AND] Patients and parents of the patients who accept to participate and sign the informed consent
  -  [AND] Patients whose molar teeth are diagnosed as molar incisor hypomineralization by EAPD
  -  [AND] Teeth that are fully erupted
  -  [AND] Patients who have at least two [OR] permanent first molars that need fissure sealant
  -  [AND] Teeth that have white-yellow-brown discoloration that indicate high-caries risk

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [AND] Teeth that are previously restored or have caries
  -  [AND] Patients who have orthodontic treatment
  -  [AND] Patients who are not cooperative for the dental procedure
  -  [AND] Teeth that have dental flourosis or enamel malformation according to the specific syndromes
