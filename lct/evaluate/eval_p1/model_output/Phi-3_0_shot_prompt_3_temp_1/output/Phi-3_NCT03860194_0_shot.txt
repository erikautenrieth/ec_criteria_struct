 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Older adults [AGE ≥ 65 years]
  -  Subject has lower appendicular muscle mass index (AMMI) [<7 kg/m^2 in men OR <5.7 kg/m^2 in women] using Bioelectrical impedance analysis (BIA, TANITA BC-418®, Tokyo, Japan)
  -  Subject has lower handgrip strength [<26 kg in men AND <18 kg in women] using dynamometer (CAMRY®, Zhongshan, Guangdong) OR subject has slower gait speed [<1 m/s]
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Subject's life expectancy was shorter than [6 months NOT]
  -  Nursing home resident [NOT]
  -  Subject has the history of kidney failure [NOT]
  -  Subject has the history of liver failure [NOT]
  -  Subject has the history of diabetes mellitus [NOT]
  -  Subject has resistance exercise habit [NOT]
