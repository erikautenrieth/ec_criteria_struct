 Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Has a 2-year history of seasonal allergic rhinitis
  - [AND] Has a positive skin test response to a local seasonal allergen ([OR] current, [OR] performed in investigator's office within the past year)
  - [AND] Is in good health and free of any unstable, clinically-significant disease, other than allergic rhinitis ([NOT] that would interfere with the study schedule or evaluation of seasonal allergic rhinitis)

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Women who are pregnant or breastfeeding
  - [AND] Women of childbearing potential who are not using an acceptable form of birth control
  - [AND] Has asthma that requires therapy with inhaled or systemic corticosteroids, cromolyn, or nedocromil
  - [AND] Has significant renal, hepatic, neurologic, cardiovascular, hematologic, metabolic, cerebrovascular, respiratory, gastrointestinal, or other significant medical illness or disorder ([NOT] which, in the judgment of the investigator, could interfere with the study, or required treatment which might interfere with the study)
  - [AND] Is on immunotherapy ([NOT] with the exception of maintenance therapy)
  - [AND] Has a clinically significant upper respiratory or sinus infection
  - [AND] Has used an investigational drug within the previous 30 days
  - [AND] Has nasal structural abnormalities, including large nasal polyps and marked septal deviation, that significantly interfere with nasal air flow
  - [AND] Has a history of multiple drug allergies or an allergy to antihistamines or corticoids
  - [AND] Has dependence upon nasal, oral or ocular decongestants, or nasal topical antihistamines, in the opinion of the investigator
  - [AND] Has rhinitis medicamentosa
  - [AND] Is using of any chronic medication which could affect the course of seasonal allergic rhinitis
