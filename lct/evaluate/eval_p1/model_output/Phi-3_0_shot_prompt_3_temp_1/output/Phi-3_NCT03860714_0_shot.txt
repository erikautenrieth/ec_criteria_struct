 Inclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] cardiac surgery patients;
  - [AGE] is greater than or equal to 60 years old;
  - [HAN] Nationality is Han;
  - [NOT] mother tongue is not Chinese;
  - [NOT] MMSE score: Illiteracy is less than 17 points OR primary school is less than 20 points OR higher secondary school is less than 24 points;
  - [NOT] people did not sign informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] diagnosis of preoperative delirium;
  - [NOT] diagnosis of Unstable hypertension [OR] (such as: pheochromocytoma or aortic dissection);
  - [NOT] severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease OR congestive heart failure;
  - [NOT] severe liver and kidney dysfunction.
