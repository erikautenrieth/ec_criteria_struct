Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  diagnosis of schizophrenia [OR] schizoaffective-depressed [OR] healthy subjects
  -  ages 18-50 [AND] for all subjects
  -  double barrier contraception [AND] for all subjects
  -  [NOT] pregnant [AND] for all subjects

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  DSM-IV Axis I [OR] II Diagnosis [NOT] for healthy subjects
  -  MEM [OR] amantadine [NOT] for patients
  -  current substance abuse [NOT] for all subjects
  -  current recreational drug use [NOT] for all subjects
  -  history of other significant medical illness (e.g. cancer, diabetes, heart disease, HIV, seizures) [NOT] for all subjects
  -  open head injury [OR] closed head injury with loss of consciousness > 1 min [NOT] for all subjects
  -  hearing [OR] visual impairment [NOT] for all subjects
  -  pregnancy [NOT] for all subjects
  -  dementia [NOT] for all subjects
  -  mental retardation [NOT] for all subjects
