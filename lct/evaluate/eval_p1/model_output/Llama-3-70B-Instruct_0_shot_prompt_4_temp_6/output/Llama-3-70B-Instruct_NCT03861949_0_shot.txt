Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  adult patients [AND] of ASA physical status I [OR] II undergoing surgery under general anaesthesia

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  anticipated difficult mask ventilation [OR] intubation
  -  individuals [WITH] significant cardiorespiratory [OR] cerebrovascular disease
  -  [NOT] non-pregnant individuals (i.e., pregnant ladies)
  -  patient [WITH] history of epilepsy
  -  body mass index >25 kg/m2
  -  hemoglobin <8 gm/dL
  -  patients who were [NOT] ambulant for ≤24 h, [AND] having SpO2 < 97% while breathing in room air
  -  [NOT] individuals without phobia to facemask (i.e., phobia to facemask)
