Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Handball players;
  -  Healthy [AND] 
  -  Participation of at least one official match in the last year [AND] attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] free of musculoskeletal injury in the last three months;
  -  [NOT] free of cardiovascular diseases;
  -  use of anabolic, anti-inflammatory [OR] analgesic drugs 72 hours before the evaluations;
  -  use of alcohol [OR] illicit drugs during the collection period;

Note: I added the phrase "[NOT] free of" to the exclusion criteria to make it clear that the absence of these conditions is required for eligibility.
