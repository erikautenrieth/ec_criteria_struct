Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Men [OR] women age 18-64.
  -  Present to ED primary for management of Low Back Pain (LBP) [AND] require treatment.
  -  Functionally impairing back pain: A baseline score of greater than 5 on the Roland-Morris Disability Questionnaire.
  -  Musculoskeletal etiology of low back [AND] no other underlying condition.
  -  Non-radicular pain [NOT] radiating to lower extremities.
  -  Pain duration <2 weeks (336 hours) [NOT] chronic pain.
  -  Non-traumatic LBP [NOT] resulting from injury.
  -  Participant is to be discharged home [AND] able to follow post-discharge instructions.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] eligible if flank pain, that is pain originating from tissues lateral to the paraspinal muscles.
  -  [NOT] available for follow-up [OR] unable to comply with follow-up schedule.
  -  [NOT] eligible if pregnant.
  -  [NOT] eligible if chronic pain syndrome [OR] history of chronic pain.
  -  [NOT] eligible if allergic to [OR] intolerant of investigational medications.
  -  [NOT] eligible if contra-indications to investigational medications [OR] any other medication that may interact with investigational medications.
