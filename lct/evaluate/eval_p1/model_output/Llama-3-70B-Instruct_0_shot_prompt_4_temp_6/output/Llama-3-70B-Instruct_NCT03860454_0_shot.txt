Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  LMNA+ cases [AND] with pathogenic LMNA mutations for LMNA+ [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  DCMWT cases: patients [AND] with heart muscle failure [AND] with wild-type lamin gene [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  HV (controls): matched to cases [OR] matched to LMNA+ cases [OR] matched to DCMWT cases.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Participants without needle-phobia that would [NOT] preclude blood-letting
  -  Participants who are [NOT] unwilling to consent
  -  Patients that have [NOT] a conventional contraindication for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) [NOT].
  -  Patients that have had a blood transfusion within the last month [OR] patients having haemodialysis will be excluded [NOT].
