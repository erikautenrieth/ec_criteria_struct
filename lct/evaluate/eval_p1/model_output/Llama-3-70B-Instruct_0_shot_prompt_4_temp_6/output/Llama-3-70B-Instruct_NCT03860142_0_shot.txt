Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Children whose age ≥ 24 months [AND] ≤ 36 months
  -  Children born full-term [OR] prematurely born (<33 SA) at Nice University Hospital

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Children with congenital pathologies [AND] with malformations in the oro-facial sphere
  -  Children with neurological [AND] developmental pathologies [NOT] related to prematurity
  -  Children with ENT deformities
  -  Children whose parents have a language barrier [NOT] able to communicate effectively
