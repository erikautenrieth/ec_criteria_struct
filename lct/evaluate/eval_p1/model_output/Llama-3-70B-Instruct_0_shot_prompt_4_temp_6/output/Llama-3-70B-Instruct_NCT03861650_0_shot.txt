Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients [NOT] having any disease affecting bone formation of both sexes were included in the study.
  2. Patients younger than 25 years old.
  3. Patients included in the study were post traumatic patients [OR] hemifacial microsomia [OR] Treacher Collins [OR] post ankylotic mandibular deformity.
  4. Distraction range was between 15-20 mm.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. The presence of uncontrolled systemic disease [NOT] controlled by treatment.
  2. Previous radiotherapy on head [AND] neck region.
  3. Chemotherapy within the past 12-month period [NOT] completed more than 12 months ago.
  4. An active infection at the distraction site [NOT] fully resolved.
