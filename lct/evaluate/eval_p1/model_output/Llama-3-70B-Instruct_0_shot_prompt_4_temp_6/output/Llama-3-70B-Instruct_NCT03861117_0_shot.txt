Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the inserted logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient 18 years old [OR] more
  -  Intubated [AND] ventilated in intensive care unit for more than 24 hours
  -  Patient ready for weaning evaluation
  -  Failure of a first T-piece SBT

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Absence of chronic neuromuscular disease
  -  [NOT] Absence of Guillain-Barré Syndrome
  -  Central nervous system disease [AND] with consciousness disorder
  -  Tracheostomy
  -  Chronic disease [AND] with life expectancy less than 1 year
  -  Pregnancy [OR] breast feeding
  -  Withholding life support [AND] regarding a reintubation
  -  Prisoner [OR] patient interned in psychiatric hospital
  -  Guardianship
  -  Language barrier
  -  [NOT] Presence of medical insurance
  -  [NOT] Presence of the patient's consent (or of the next of kin where appropriate)
  -  Patient under an exclusion period [AND] after enrollment in another research study
