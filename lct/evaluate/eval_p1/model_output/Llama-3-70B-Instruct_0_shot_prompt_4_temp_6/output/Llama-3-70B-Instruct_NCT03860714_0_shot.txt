Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Non-cardiac surgery patients [AND]
       -  Age is greater than or equal to 60 years old [AND]
       -  Han Nationality [OR] mother tongue is chinese [AND]
       -  The MMSE score: Illiteracy is greater than or equal to 17 points [OR] primary school is greater than or equal to 20 points [OR] higher secondary school is more than 24 points [AND]
       -  The people signed informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] The diagnosis of preoperative delirium [AND]
       -  [NOT] The diagnosis Unstable hypertension（Such as：pheochromocytoma [OR] aortic dissection） [AND]
       -  [NOT] Severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] congestive heart failure [AND]
       -  [NOT] Severe liver dysfunction [OR] kidney dysfunction
