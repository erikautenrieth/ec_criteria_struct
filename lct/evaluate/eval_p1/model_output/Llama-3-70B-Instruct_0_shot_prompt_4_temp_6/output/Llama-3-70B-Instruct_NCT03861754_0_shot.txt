Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Body mass Index > 30
  -  Age 18-64

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pregnancy
  -  Health reasons [NOT] allowing exercise [AND] exercise test
  -  Medication [NOT] allowing exercise [AND] exercise test
  -  Previously diagnosed diabetes

Note: I inserted [NOT] before "preventing" in the exclusion criteria, as it indicates the absence or lack of a required condition (ability to exercise and take the exercise test). I also inserted [AND] before "and" in the exclusion criteria, as it connects related conditions that must both be satisfied for exclusion.
