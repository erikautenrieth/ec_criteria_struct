Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Unilateral ischemic stroke [AND] residual arm weakness (Fugl-Meyer Upper Limb < 60) more than 6 months prior to enrollment.
  -  Ability to give informed consent [AND] understand the tasks involved.
  -  Age over 18 years.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Hemorrhagic Stroke [OR] other types of stroke that are [NOT] ischemic in nature.
  -  Contraindications to TMS: history of seizure/epilepsy [OR] pacemaker [OR] other neurological disorders [OR] brain surgery [OR] metal implant/fragment in the head [OR] pregnancy.
  -  Taking medications or substances that are known to affect PAS-induced plasticity within the past 2 months: selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors [OR] dopamine [OR] dopamine agonists [OR] haloperidol [OR] lithium [OR] acetylcholinesterase inhibitors [OR] beta-blockers [OR] nimodipine [OR] levetiracetam [OR] ethosuximide [OR] benzodiazepines [OR] baclofen [OR] nicotine.
  -  Peripheral neuropathy [OR] history of nerve injury in the paretic upper limb.
  -  Social and/or personal circumstances that [NOT] allow for ability to return for all study visits [OR] interfere with ability to return for all study visits.
