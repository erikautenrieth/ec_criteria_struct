Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
• Patients > 18 years old [AND] with ASA status I-III, undergoing VATS for lobectomy [OR] wedge.

Non-inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients < 18 years old
  -  BMI > 35
  -  Patients with chronic pain history [AND] regular opioid [OR] gabapentinoids use during the 2 weeks before surgery
  -  Patients with regular marijuana use
  -  History of thoracic surgery on the operated side
  -  Epidural analgesia preferred due to an anticipated high risk of conversion to thoracotomy
  -  Patients [NOT] able to communicate with the investigators
  -  Patients receiving anticoagulation [OR] experiencing any bleeding disorder
  -  Surgery for empyema [AND] sympathectomy
  -  Patients with known allergy to local anesthetics, fentanyl [OR] hydromorphone
  -  Active infection at injection sites
  -  Patients with preexisting neurological deficit [OR] psychiatric illness
  -  Patients with severe cardiovascular disease
  -  Patients with liver failure
  -  Patients with renal failure (estimated glomerular filtration rate <15 mL/min/1.73 m2)
  -  Pregnant patients

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with perioperative conversion to thoracotomy
  -  Patients with severe intra- [OR] postoperative bleeding
  -  Patients requiring postoperative mechanical ventilation
  -  Patients with technical [NOT] ability to proceed with the blocks
