Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age 55-80
  -  smoked at least 30 pack-years in lifetime
  -  if former smoker, quit smoking within the last 15 years
  -  English speaker [AND] no other primary language spoken [OR] proficient in English
  -  [NOT] prior history of lung cancer
  -  [NOT] had a lung cancer screening test within the last year
  -  PCP does [NOT] object to patient's participation
  -  have a scheduled visit at University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) internal medicine clinics.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  speaking a language other than English [AND] [NOT] proficient in English
  -  has a history of lung cancer
  -  had a lung cancer screening test within the last year
  -  PCP objects to patient's participation.

Note: I added "[AND] no other primary language spoken [OR] proficient in English" to the fourth inclusion criterion to make it clear that participants can be included if they are English speakers or proficient in English, even if they have another primary language.
