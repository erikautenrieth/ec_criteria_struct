Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria-- Participants must be:
  -  Healthy (determined by self-report) [AND] 
  -  Between the ages of 18-55 years old
  -  Current cigarette smokers
  -  Willing to provide informed consent
  -  Able to attend the lab session [AND] abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required
  -  Agree to use designated products according to the study protocol

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Women if they are breast-feeding [OR] test positive for pregnancy (by urinalysis) at screening.
  -  Individuals who weigh less than 110 pounds.
  -  Participants who are [NOT] healthy (determined by self-report) [OR] are outside the ages of 18-55 years old [OR] are [NOT] current cigarette smokers [OR] are [NOT] willing to provide informed consent [OR] are [NOT] able to attend the lab session [OR] are unable to abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required [OR] do [NOT] agree to use designated products according to the study protocol.

Some study details about the eligibility criteria are purposely omitted at this time to preserve scientific integrity. Full details will be posted at the conclusion of the study.
