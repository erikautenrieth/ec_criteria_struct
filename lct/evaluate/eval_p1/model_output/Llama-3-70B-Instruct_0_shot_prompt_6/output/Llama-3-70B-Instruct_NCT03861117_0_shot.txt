<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient 18 years old [OR] more
  -  Intubated [AND] ventilated in intensive care unit for more than 24 hours
  -  Patient ready for weaning evaluation
  -  Failure of a first T-piece SBT

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Chronic neuromuscular disease
  -  [NOT] Guillain-Barré Syndrome
  -  [NOT] Central nervous system disease [AND] consciousness disorder
  -  [NOT] Tracheostomy
  -  [NOT] Chronic disease [AND] life expectancy less than 1 year
  -  [NOT] Pregnancy [OR] breast feeding
  -  [NOT] Withholding life support regarding a reintubation
  -  [NOT] Prisoner [OR] patient interned in psychiatric hospital
  -  [NOT] Guardianship
  -  [NOT] Language barrier
  -  [NOT] Lack of medical insurance
  -  [NOT] Lack of the patient's consent [OR] of the next of kin where appropriate
  -  [NOT] Patient under an exclusion period after enrollment in another research study
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
