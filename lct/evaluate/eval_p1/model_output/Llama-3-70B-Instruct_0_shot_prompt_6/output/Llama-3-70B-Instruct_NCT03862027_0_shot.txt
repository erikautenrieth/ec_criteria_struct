<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adult patients (age >/= 18) admitted to the Neuro ICU of UNC Hospital requiring mechanical ventilation [AND] with ICP monitoring in place.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Pneumothorax [OR] pneumomediastinum
  2. Life expectancy < 24 hours [OR] expected to require mechanical ventilation for < 24 hours
  3. [NOT] Condition that precludes placement of an esophageal balloon [esophageal [OR] nasopharyngeal pathology preventing insertion of the esophageal balloon catheter (esophageal ulcerations, tumors, diverticulitis, bleeding varices) [OR] in patients with sinusitis, epistaxis [OR] recent nasopharyngeal surgery), [NOT] severe thrombocytopenia (platelet count < 30) [OR] coagulopathy (INR > 3 [OR] on oral anticoagulants).
  4. [NOT] Pre-enrollment ICP > 20 mm Hg
  5. [NOT] Pre-enrollment CPP < 60 mm Hg
  6. [NOT] Planned change in the external ventricular drain set point during the pre-intervention, intervention [OR] post-intervention periods
  7. [NOT] Incarceration
  8. [NOT] Variation in ICP of > 2 cm H2O in the hour prior to intervention.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
