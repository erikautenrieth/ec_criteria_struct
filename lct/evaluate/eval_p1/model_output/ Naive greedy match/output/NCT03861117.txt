Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient 18 years old  [OR] or more
  -  Intubated  [AND] and ventilated in intensive care unit for more than 24 hours
  -  Patient ready for weaning evaluation
  -  Failure of a first T-piece SBT
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Chronic neuromuscular disease
  -  Guillain-Barré Syndrome
  -  Central nervous system disease  [AND] with consciousness disorder
  -  Tracheostomy
  -  Chronic disease  [AND] with life expectancy less than 1 year
  -  Pregnancy, [OR] breast feeding
  -  Withholding life support regarding a reintubation
  -  Prisoner  [OR] or patient interned in psychiatric hospital
  -  Guardianship
  -  Language barrier
  -  Lack of medical insurance
  -  Lack of the patient's consent ( [OR] or of the next of kin where appropriate)
  -  Patient under an exclusion period after enrollment in another research study
