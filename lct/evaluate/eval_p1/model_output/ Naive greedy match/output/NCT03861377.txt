Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Gynecological procedure
  -  Age 18-50 years
  -  General anesthesia planned
  -  Systolic blood pressure < 150 mmHg, [OR] HR < 100 beats/ [OR] min
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pre-existing hypertension
  -  Diabetes
  -  Ischemic heart disease  [OR] or cerebrovascular disease
  -  Heart valve disease
  -  Verified cardiac arrhythmia other than extrasystoles
  -  Verified anaemia  [AND] with hemoglobin level below 9.0 gr/ [OR] dl.
  -  Kidney  [OR] or hepatic disease
  -  Hypersensitivity for propofol, [OR] soya, [OR] eggs  [OR] or peanuts
  -  Pregnancy
  -  Poor health state
  -  Illicit substance use
  -  BMI <20  [OR] or >35 kg/ [OR] m2
