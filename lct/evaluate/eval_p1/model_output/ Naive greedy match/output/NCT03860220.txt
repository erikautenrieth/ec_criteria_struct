Inclusion Criteria:
  -  145 mmHg ≤ msSBP ≤ 190 mmHg
  -  Triglycerides < 500 mg/ [OR] dL
  -  LDL-C ≤ 250 mg/ [OR] dL
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  sSBP ≥ 20mmHg  [AND] and sDBP ≥ 10mmHg
  -  symptomatic orthostatic hypotension  [AND] and secondary/ [OR] iatrogenic hypertension  [AND] and dyslipidemia
  -  history of moderate to severe cerebral ischemia, [OR] cerebral hemorrhage, [OR] transient ischemic attack, [OR] myocardial infarction, [OR]  [OR]  [OR] or unstable angina in the past 6 months; severe heart failure (New York Heart Association functional class III  [AND] and IV)
  -  hypersensitivity to telmisartan  [OR] or rosuvastatin
  -  history of angioedema after treatment  [AND] with angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors  [OR] or ARBs; creatinine phosphokinase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal
  -  estimated glomerular filtration rate ≤30 mL/ [OR] min; aspartate aminotransferase  [AND] and alanine aminotransferase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal
  -  potassium levels >5.5 mmol/ [OR] L;  [OR] or any diseases  [AND] that could affect the results of the study.
