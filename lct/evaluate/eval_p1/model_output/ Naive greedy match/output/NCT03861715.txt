Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age 20-34 years;
  -  body mass index (BMI) 18-29kg/ [OR] m2;
  -  regular menstrual cycle of 26-35 days, [OR]   -  AMH levels age appropriate (≥2.3 ng/ [OR] ml)
  -  early follicular- phase serum concentration of FSH within normal limits (1-12 IU/ [OR] l).
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  women  [AND] with diabetes  [AND] and other metabolic disease
  -  women  [AND] with hereditary genetic diseases;
  -  women  [AND] with heart disease, [OR] QT prolongation,heart failure
  -  elevated liver enzymes, [OR] liver failure, [OR] hepatitis
  -  women  [AND] with inflammatory  [OR] or autoimmune disease
  -  abnormal karyotype;
  -  endometriosis stage III/ [OR] IV;
  -  history of recurrent miscarriage;
  -  severe OHSS in a previous stimulation cycle  [OR] or any contraindication for the use of gonadotrophins.
