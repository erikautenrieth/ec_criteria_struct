Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients  [AND] who are day -10 pre- to day +30 post-allogeneic hematopoietic cell transplant (AHCT) from any donor  [OR] or graft source  [AND] and for any conditioning regimen
  -  Patients  [AND] who have received treatment  [AND] with meropenem  [OR] or piperacillin-tazobactam (pip-tazo) intravenously (IV) (of at least 24 hours duration) in past 7 days
  -  Controlled infection defined as hemodynamically stable  [AND] and  [NOT] not requiring supplemental oxygen of more than 2 liters via nasal cannula
  -  Patients  [AND] who are able to take oral medications in suspension form
  -  Patients  [AND] who are able to provide informed consent (IC)  [AND] and comply  [AND] with all study visits  [AND] and procedures
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients  [AND] who are anticipated to require continued broad spectrum antibiotics  [AND] with meropenem  [OR] or pip-tazo IV for > 96 hours post-engraftment such as for known, [OR] documented infections necessitating prolonged treatment
  -  Patients  [AND] with a prior documented infection  [AND] with mycormycetes
  -  Patients  [AND] who are greater than 2 days from time of neutrophil engraftment post AHCT
  -  Patients  [AND] with active enteric infections
  -  Patients  [AND] with acute GVHD >= grade II
  -  Patients unwilling  [OR] or  [NOT] unable to undergo the FMT via retention enema procedure
  -  Patients  [AND] who have received treatment  [AND] with an investigational agent within 2 weeks of enrollment
  -  Patients  [NOT] unable to tolerate oral decontamination regimen of pip-tazo  [AND] and nystatin due to prior allergy  [OR] or intolerance of these medications
  -  Patients  [AND] with any medical  [OR] or psychological condition  [AND] that, [OR] in the opinion of the investigator, [OR] might interfere  [AND] with the subject's participation in the trial, [OR] pose any additional risk for the subject, [OR]  [OR]  [OR] or confound the assessments of the subject
