Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age ≤ 60 years
 - Good nutrition
 - No systemic infection [AND] and
 - Elective laparoscopic surgery
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Age > 75 years
 - Malnutrition [OR] or an organ system infection
 - Associated with obstruction, bleeding, emergency surgery [OR] or other surgical intervention
 - Tumor with extensive metastasis
 - Prior to surgery, patient was fasting, underwent gastrointestinal decompression [NOT] and received nutritional support
 - Previous history of abdominal surgery
 - Patient have previously undergone gastrostomy
