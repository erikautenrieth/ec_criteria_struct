Inclusion Criteria:
  -  18 - 85 years of age
  -  ASA I  [OR] or IV
  -  Undergoing primary unilateral elective total knee arthroplasty with spinal anesthesia
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  BMI over 40*
  -  Contraindication  [OR] or allergy to opioid pain medication or ketamine*
  -  Daily opioid use for pain control before surgery in excess of systemic morphine equivalent to 10 mg*
  -  Ejection fraction (EF) [NOT] less than 30%
  -  Creatinine clearance [NOT] less than 30 mL/min*
  -  History of chronic liver failure
  -  Desire for nerve block  [OR] or general anesthesia
  -  Any neurologic  [OR] or psychiatric disorder (including bipolar disorder, post traumatic stress disorder, schizophrenia)
  -  Prior surgery on ipsilateral knee within the last 6 months
  -  [NOT] alcohol abuse
