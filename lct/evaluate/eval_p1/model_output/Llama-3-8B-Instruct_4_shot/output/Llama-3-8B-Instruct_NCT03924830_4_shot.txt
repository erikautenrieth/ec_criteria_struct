Inclusion Criteria:
 - All patients must have at only 20 teeth in function, must have at only 3 carious occlusal or proximo-occlusal lesions, or old restorations that need to be changed, in different teeth
 - Those lesions must be more than 2 mm deep [AND] should have exposed dentin [AND] must have at only 50% of margins in enamel
 - Teeth should not present periodontal mobility
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Volunteers with periodontal disease [OR] with gingival bleeding
 - Use of anti-inflammatory drugs in the last 30 days
