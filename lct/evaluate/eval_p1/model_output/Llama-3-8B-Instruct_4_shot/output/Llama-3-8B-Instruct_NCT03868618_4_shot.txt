Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Body mass index (BMI) limitations
  -  Likely suffer from moderate to severe OSA based on history and physical
  -  Has either not tolerated, has failed  [OR] or refused positive airway pressure (PAP) treatments
  -  Willing  [AND] and capable of providing informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Inadequately treated sleep disorders other than OSA  [NOT]
  -  Significant co-morbidities that contraindicates surgery  [OR] or general anesthesia
