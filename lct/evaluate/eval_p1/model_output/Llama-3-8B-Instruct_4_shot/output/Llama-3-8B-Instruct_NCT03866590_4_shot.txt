Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Informed consent is obtained from the participant or its legal representatives
  -  The participant is older than 14 years  [AND] and younger than 25 years old
  -  The patient exhibits chronic anaemia  [OR] or cholelithiasis  [OR] or cholecystitis of undetermined aetiology  [OR] or The patient has a high-grade PKD suspicion due to any clinical item  [OR] or The patient is diagnosed biochemically and/or genetically with PKD
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Inability to provide informed consent
  -  The patient does not suffer from chronic anaemia  [AND] or cholelithiasis  [AND] or cholecystitis
  -  The aetiology of chronic anaemia  [AND] or cholelithiasis  [AND] or cholecystitis is determined  [AND] and is not due to PKD
  -  The participant is younger than 14 years  [OR] or older than 25 years old
  -  Previously enrolled in the study
