Inclusion Criteria:
 - All pregnant women undergoing a C/S in on the hospitals during the study period will be eligible to participate in this study.

Exclusion Criteria:
The following conditions will not be considered as outcome, unless they present with systemic repercussion due to infection. For example:
 - Vaginosis, candidiasis [NOT] with systemic repercussion due to infection
 - Lower tract urinary infection [NOT] with systemic repercussion due to infection
 - Fungal infections of the skin (athlete's foot, jockitch, ringworm, and yeast infections) [NOT] with systemic repercussion due to infection
 - Otitis [NOT] with systemic repercussion due to infection
 - Pharyngitis [NOT] with systemic repercussion due to infection
 - Herpes simplex, Herpes Zoster (Shingles) [NOT] with systemic repercussion due to infection
 - Uncomplicated chronic infection
 - Sexually transmitted infections (Gonorrhoea, Syphilis, Trichomonas, Chlamydia, Hepatitis, HIV) [NOT] with systemic repercussion due to infection
 - Tuberculosis [NOT] with systemic repercussion due to infection
 - Bacterial colonization (presence of microorganisms without clinical signs/symptoms)
 - Known vaginal, urethral and/or rectal GBS colonization
 - Asymptomatic bacteriuria
 - Known oropharyngeal colonization
 - Non-infectious hypothermia/hyperthermia (e.g. related to epidural, thyroid storm, prostaglandin administration) during hospital stay
