Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Female, older than 35 years
  -  Already have mammography, [OR] or other standard exams (ultrasound, MRI, etc)
  -  Could participate this trial within 2 weeks after mammography
  -  Have the ability of consent inform
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  During pregnancy  [OR] or lactation period
  -  Patients that may not be capable of prone position, [including] patients with scapulohumeral periarthritis, [NOT] cardiac pacemaker or have recent cardiac surgery
  -  Lymphoma patients that are under therapy
  -  Severe scoliosis
