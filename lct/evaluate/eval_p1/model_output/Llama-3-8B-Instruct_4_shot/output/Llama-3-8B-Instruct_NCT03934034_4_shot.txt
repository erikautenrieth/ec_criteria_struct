Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Clinical diagnosis of NTM pulmonary disease.
 2. The causative agent of NTM pulmonary disease includes: M. avium complex, [OR] M. abscessus subspecies abscessus, [OR] M. abscessus subspecies massiliense, [OR] M. kansasii.
 3. Patients who start new treatment [AND] those who have previous history of treatment are eligible
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients who have been treated for more than 4 weeks.
 2. The causative agent of NTM pulmonary disease [NOT] is none of M. avium complex, M. abscessus subspecies abscessus, M. abscessus subspecies massiliense, or M. kansasii.
