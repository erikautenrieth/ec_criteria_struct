Inclusion Criteria:
  -  The selection criteria of the subjects will be as follows:
       1. they need to be off medication  [AND] and off intake of dietary supplements
       2. they need to have no blood clotting disorders  [AND] or dyslipidaemia

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Subjects with clotting disorders
  -  Subjects with dyslipidemia
  -  Subjects being on medication  [OR] (i.e. statins)  [OR] or other supplements during the study
