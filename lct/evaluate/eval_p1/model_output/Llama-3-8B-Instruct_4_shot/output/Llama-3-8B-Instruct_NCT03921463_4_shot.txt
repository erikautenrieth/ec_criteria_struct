Inclusion Criteria:
 - 1) Age: 20 ~35 years old;
 - 2) first parturients registered in the obstetrics department of our hospital;
 - 3) no medical complications before pregnancy, no UI [AND] and POP (according to the standard of POP-Q stage);
 - 4) no obstetric complications;
 - 5) the patients agreed to conduct the study [AND] signed the informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - 1) patients with [NOT] uncontrolled epilepsy, central nervous system diseases or mental disorders, whose clinical severity shall be determined by the researcher to [NOT] affect clinical research compliance;
 - 2) patients with follow-up difficulty [OR] patients with difficulty.
