Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of GCA as classified according to protocol-specified criteria;
 - Participants entering Period 1 must be receiving treatment with TCZ 8 mg/kg IV Q4W
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Treatment with any other investigational agent besides TCZ [NOT] within 12 weeks (or 5 half-lives of the investigational drug, whichever is longer) prior to screening;
 - Evidence of serious uncontrolled disease;
 - Known active current [OR] or history of recurrent bacterial, viral, fungal, mycobacterial, or other infections;
 - Active TB requiring treatment [NOT] within the previous 3 years.
