Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Primigravida  [OR] or Primipara
  -  Pregnancy between 36  [AND] and 42 weeks of gestation
  -  A live singleton fetus in cephalic presentation
  -  No history of uterine surgery  [NOT]
  -  Clinically adequate pelvis
  -  Modified Bishop's score <5
  -  Reactive Non stress test (NST)
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Known hypersensitivity or contraindications to oral misoprostol (uterine surgery)  [NOT]
  -  Any antenatal medical complications
  -  A situation requiring LSCS (Maternal or Fetal) e.g. fetal distress
  -  Non-reactive NST  [NOT]
  -  Patient's refusal to give consent
