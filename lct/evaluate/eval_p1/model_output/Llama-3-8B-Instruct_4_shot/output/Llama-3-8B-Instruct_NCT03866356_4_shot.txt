Inclusion Criteria:
 - The inclusion criteria were being female, ≥ 18 years, having an initial diagnosis of SI, being literate, having no sensory disorder affecting communication, and agreeing to participate in the research
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnancy
 - Presence of a urinary tract infection
 - Previous treatment for SI
 - Patient [NOT] non-compliance with the protocol
 - Failure in contacting the patient
 - Patient's wish to withdraw from the study
