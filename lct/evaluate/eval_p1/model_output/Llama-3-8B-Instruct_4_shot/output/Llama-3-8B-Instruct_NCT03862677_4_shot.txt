Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with (suspicion of) primary or recurrent EOC with an indication for surgery, chemotherapy [AND] or immunotherapy.
 - Age ≥ 18 years.
 - WHO performance status 0-2.
 - Accessible for treatment [AND] and follow-up.
 - Written informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Other active malignancy in past 5 years prior to entry into the study, except for treated [NOT] non-melanoma skin cancer.
 - Any known severe infection [NOT] like HIV, hepatitis A, B and C.
 - Receiving immune suppressive treatment.
 - Medical or psychological condition which in the opinion of the investigator would [NOT] permit the patient to complete the study or sign meaningful informed consent.
