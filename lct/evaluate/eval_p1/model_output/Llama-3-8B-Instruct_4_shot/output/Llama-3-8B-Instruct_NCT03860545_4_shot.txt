Inclusion Criteria:
  -  a planned operation of coronary artery bypass grafting with the use of cardiopulmonary bypass

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  emergency operations  [OR] or re-operations;
  -  a known pathology of the urinary tract  [OR] or renal failure;
  -  chronic use of the following medications: iron, non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs), immunosuppression  [OR] or steroids in the preoperative period;
  -  polycythemia  [OR] or porphyria or pathological hemoglobin species in anamnesis;
  -  preoperative signs of hepatic failure;
  -  active autoimmune  [OR] or neoplastic diseases, active infection;
  -  anticipated significant bleeding  [NOT] suggesting the use of blood-derived products during the operation and afterward;
  -  acute myocardial infarction  [NOT] after operation
