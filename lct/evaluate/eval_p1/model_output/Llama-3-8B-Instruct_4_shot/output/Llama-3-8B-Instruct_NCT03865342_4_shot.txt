Inclusion criteria:
  -  English-speaking
  -  18 years of age  [AND] and older
  -  Referral from the patient's physician
  -  Hemoglobin A1C: 5.7  [AND] to 6.4 within the 3 months prior to enrollment
  -  Own a smartphone  [AND] (Apple  [OR] or Android)
Exclusion criteria:
  -  Recent weight loss (by patient report, >5 lbs  [AND] in the 6 months preceding enrollment visit)
  -  previous diagnosis of type 1  [OR] or type 2 diabetes
  -  serious  [OR] persistent mental illnesses
  -  more than 72 hours of hospitalization in the last 30 days
  -  Enrollment in a structured weight-loss program currently  [OR] or within the 1 month preceding study enrollment
  -  Pregnant
  -  Currently nursing
  -  Have given birth within the past 3 months
  -  Has been discouraged by a physician to enroll in a DPP program
