Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Having mild or moderate severity of AD
  -  Lack of communication difficulties or problems
  -  Volunteering to participate in the study
  -  Not having a cerebrovascular event
  -  Not having a neurological disease such as Parkinson, MS, that disturbs balance and coordination
  -  Mini Mental Test Score: 18 to 24 points
  -  Age: 65-80
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients diagnosed with dementia  [NOT] (infection, vascular, hematological diseases)
  -  Patients who could not be contacted during the follow-up period  [OR] or who did not come to control
  -  Cardiac and cerebrovascular event
  -  Endocrine disorder, fluid-electrolyte imbalance, or infection
  -  Patients with malignancy, chemotherapy, or radiotherapy
  -  Patients with delirium  [OR] or depression
  -  Those with protective sensation loss (Semmes-Weinstein Monofilament Thickness < 4.56)
  -  Patients with lower or upper extremity amputations at any level
