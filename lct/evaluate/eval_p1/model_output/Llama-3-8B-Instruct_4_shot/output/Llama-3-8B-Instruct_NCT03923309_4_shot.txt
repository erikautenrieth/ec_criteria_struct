Inclusion Criteria:
  -  biopsy proven rectal cancer (anal verge ≤ 10cm)
  -  neoadjuvant radiotherapy  [OR] or chemoradiotherapy
  -  no radical surgery after neoadjuvant therapy
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  other malignant disease
  -  other fatal disease
  -  [NOT] incompletion of half of radiotherapy course
  -  metastasis on initial presentation
