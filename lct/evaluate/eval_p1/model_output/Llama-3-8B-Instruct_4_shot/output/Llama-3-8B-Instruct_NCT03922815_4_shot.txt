Inclusion Criteria:
  -  written consent to participate in the study
  -  written consent to undergo deep sedation for colonoscopic procedure
  -  general heath condition I-III of American Society of Anaesthesiology
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  necessity of administration of vasoactive drugs [AND] influencing SPI monitoring
  -  pregnancy
  -  anatomical malformation that [NOT] makes monitoring using SE sensor impossible
  -  general atherosclerosis  [OR] heart rhythm disturbances [OR] impairing SPI monitoring
  -  chronic medication using opioid drugs leading to resistancy to opioids
