Inclusion Criteria:
VA Patient
 - Diagnosed with depression
 - Initiating or changing to a new antidepressant medication
 - Cell phone with text messaging capabilities
 - Registered with My HealtheVet
 - Follow-up appointment with their provider in the next 12 weeks
 - Internet access
 - English speaking
 - VA Providers: provide care to a study participant [AND] diagnosed with depression

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Lack of cell phone access/willingness to receive text messages
 - Diagnosis of cognitive disorders [NOT] or psychotic disorders, bipolar disorder, or active substance abuse.
