Inclusion Criteria:
 - Both sexes aged between 20-90 years.
 - Received stable hemodialysis at least 3 months.
 - Written informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - patients with severe infections, severe heart disease, severe liver disease, malignancy, autoimmune disorders, severe malnutrition, or clinical conditions requiring oral nutrition supplements [NOT] (excluding patients with mild or moderate conditions);
 - Inability to follow protocol.
 - Pregnancy [OR] or wishing/trying to get pregnant.
