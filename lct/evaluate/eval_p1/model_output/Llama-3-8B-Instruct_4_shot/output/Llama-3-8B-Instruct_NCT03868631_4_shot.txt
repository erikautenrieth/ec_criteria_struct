Inclusion Criteria:
  -  non-smoking
  -  body mass index (BMI) 18.5  [AND] and 24.9
  -  recreationally active
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  participated in structured weight training during the previous 12 months
  -  vegetarian  [OR] or vegan
  -  presence of chronic disease
  -  pregnant  [OR] postpartum up to six months  [OR] lactating  [OR] intention to become pregnant
  -  allergy to whey  [OR] or soy
  -  changes in body weight more than 10 pounds in the past three months
