Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Being hospitalized in the hospital between January 2015 and July 2018, according to ICD 10 code; (hemiplegia  [OR]  flaccid hemiplegia  [OR]  hemiplegia, unspecified  [OR]  Spastic hemiplegia)
  -  Being ischemic  [OR] or hemorrhagic stroke
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Being hemiplegic due to [NOT] trauma, [NOT] cancer, and [NOT] intracranial operation
