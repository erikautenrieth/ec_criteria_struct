Inclusion Criteria:
 - Common European Framework of Reference for Languages level B2 in German language
 - Subject has the ability to give consent for all relevant aspects of the experiment
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Amisulpride allergy
 - Allergy to other components of amisulpride / placebo capsules [AND] like lactose
 - Daily intake of other medication [OR] including contraceptives
 - Tendency to seizures
 - Diagnosis of cancer [NOT] especially pheochromocytoma, prolactinoma, or breast cancer
 - Kidney dysfunction: creatinine clearance below 10 ml per minute
 - High risk for stroke [OR] or thrombosis
 - Known prolongation of the QT interval
 - Any substantial medical condition [NOT] that is capable of reducing the volunteer's ability to participate at the study
 - Suicidal thoughts [OR] or suicide attempts in the past [OR] or at present
 - Substantial mental disorders [NOT] especially schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, drug abuse, or personality disorders
 - Pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding
