Inclusion Criteria:
 - Meets diagnostic criteria for fibromyalgia
 - Has insomnia
 - Has internet access [AND] and a device that can access the web-based program

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Active sleep disorder which is not treated [NOT] (obstructive sleep apnea, obesity hypoventilation syndrome)
 - Active psychiatric disorder [NOT] (Bipolar affective disorder, anxiety, depression, schizophrenia) which is not optimally managed
 - Chronic fatigue syndrome
 - Morbid obesity
