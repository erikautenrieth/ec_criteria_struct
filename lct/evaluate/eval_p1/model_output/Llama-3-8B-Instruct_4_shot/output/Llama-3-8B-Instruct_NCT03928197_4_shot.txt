Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age between 25 years [AND] and 60 years
 - For the volunteers with venous insufficiency: clearly visual venous insufficiency (CEAP 1 [AND] or 2)
 - BMI between 24 [AND] and 30
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Volunteers using medication [NOT] (exception for contraception)
 - Volunteers having comorbidities
 - Pregnant women
 - Women who menstruate on the test date
 - Volunteers with an implanted elektronical device (IPG, Pacemaker)
