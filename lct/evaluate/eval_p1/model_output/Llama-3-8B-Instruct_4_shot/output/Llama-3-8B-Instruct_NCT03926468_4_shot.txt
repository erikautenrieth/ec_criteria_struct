Inclusion Criteria:
 - histologically confirmed head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC)
 - WHO performance status 0 [AND] or 2
 - clinical stage III patients with bulky T3 primary [AND] +/- neck metastasis
 - all stage IV patients
 - referral to definitive radiotherapy [OR] or chemoradiotherapy [OR] or multimodality treatment
Exclusion Criteria:
 - patients who [NOT] are able to sign written informed consent
