Inclusion Criteria:
• Couples with primary / secondary infertility who are planned to undergo IVF / ICSI treatment in one of the above mentioned centers and who agree to take part in this study.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Couples planned for oocyte and sperm donation
 - Couples in whom the female partner has a history of: [NOT] Chemotherapy or radiation which impacts the ovarian reserve
 - Couples in whom the female partner has a history of: [NOT] Surgery at the ovaries / adnex region
 - Couples in whom the female partner has a history of: [NOT] Tubal ligation
 - Couples in whom the female partner has a history of: [NOT] Reversal of tubal ligation
 - Couples in whom the female partner has a history of: [NOT] Endometriosis
 - Couples in whom the male partner has a history of: [NOT] Chemotherapy / Radiation which impacts the semen result
 - Couples in whom the male partner has a history of: [NOT] Surgery at the testicles
 - Couples in whom the male partner has a history of: [NOT] Vasectomy
 - Couples in whom the male partner has a history of: [NOT] Surgery for reversal of vasectomy
