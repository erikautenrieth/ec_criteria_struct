Inclusion Criteria:
 - -Subjects presented with melasma.
 - Age 20-40 years old.
 - Melasma persisting for more than 6 months that has failed to respond to conventional treatment with hydroquinone [OR] or other topical lightening agents.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - -History of photosensitivity
 - History of keloids, hypertrophic scarring, and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation [NOT] excluded
 - Pregnancy [AND] or lactation.
 - Subjects with local inflammatory skin disorder [OR] or active herpes infection at the site of procedure.
 - Subjects with history of medical diseases which contraindicate cryosurgery such as; cold intolerance, cold urticaria, Raynaud`s disease, and history of allergic reactions to cryosurgery.
