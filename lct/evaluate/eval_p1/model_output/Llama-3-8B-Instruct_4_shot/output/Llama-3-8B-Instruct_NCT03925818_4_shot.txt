Inclusion Criteria:
- Positive for H. pylori infection
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Presence of malignancy
 - Pregnancy [OR] or lactation
 - Clinically significant diseases
 - History of drug [OR] or alcohol abuse
 - Allergy to pantoprazole [OR] or to any component of regimens used in the study [NOT]
 - Use of anti-secretory drugs, antibiotics [OR] or probiotics 4 weeks preceding the enrollment
