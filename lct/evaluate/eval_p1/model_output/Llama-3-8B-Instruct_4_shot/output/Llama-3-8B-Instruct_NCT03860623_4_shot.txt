Inclusion Criteria:
  -  BMI 30-40 kg/m2
  -  BMI 18-25 kg/m2 [AND] (those with BMI up to 28kg/m2 [AND] waist circumference is <96cm)
  -  Males
  -  Aged 18-60yrs

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Acute illness in the preceding 6 weeks
  -  Taking regular medication [OR] not taking regular medication
  -  History of deep vein thrombosis [OR] clotting disorders
  -  Hypertension [OR] NOT hypertension
  -  Diabetes [OR] NOT diabetes
  -  Any clinically significant findings at screening [OR] no clinically significant findings at screening
  -  History of substance abuse
  -  Demonstrating factors precluding safe MRI
  -  History of gastrointestinal motility disorders (e.g. gastroesophageal reflux disease -irritable bowel syndrome, gastroparesis, sphincter of Oddi dysfunction, etc.) [OR] no history of gastrointestinal motility disorders
  -  Previous thoracic or abdominal surgery [OR] no previous thoracic or abdominal surgery
  -  Those who report having ≤3 bowel movements/week [OR] >2/day
