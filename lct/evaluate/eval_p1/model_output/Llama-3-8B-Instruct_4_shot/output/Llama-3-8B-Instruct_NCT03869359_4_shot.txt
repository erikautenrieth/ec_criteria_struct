Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adult patients with IBS according to Rome IV criteria
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  allergy  [OR] or documented intolerance to food,
  -  severe cardiovascular, hepatic, neurological  [OR] or psychiatric disease
  -  serious gastrointestinal disease: including celiac disease  [AND] and inflammatory bowel diseases (IBD),
  -  diabetes
  -  bowel dysfunction related to previous surgery
  -  pregnant  [NOT] or lactating women
  -  usage of antibiotics within 4 weeks before inclusion
  -  strict avoidance of foods
