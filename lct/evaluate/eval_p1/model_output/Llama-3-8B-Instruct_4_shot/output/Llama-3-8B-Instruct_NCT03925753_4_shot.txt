Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Both sexes aged between 20-90 years.
  -  Received stable hemodialysis at least 3 months.
  -  Written informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  patients with severe infections, severe heart disease [OR] or liver disease, malignancy, autoimmune disorders, severe malnutrition, or clinical conditions requiring oral nutrition supplements;
  -  Inability to follow protocol.
  -  [NOT] Pregnancy or wishing/trying to get pregnant
