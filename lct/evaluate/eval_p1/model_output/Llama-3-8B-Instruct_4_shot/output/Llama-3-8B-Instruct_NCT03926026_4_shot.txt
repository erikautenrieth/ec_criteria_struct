Inclusion Criteria:
  -  history of blepharitis  [AND] meet qualifying criteria for an acute exacerbation of blepharitis in both eyes at Screening and Baseline/Day 1 Visits
  -  have a qualifying best-corrected visual acuity
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  abnormality of the eyelids or lashes  [NOT] (other than blepharitis), or previous eyelid surgery
  -  IOP > 21 mmHg  [AND] at Screening or Baseline/Day 1 Visits
  -  use of steroids in the past 30 days  [OR] or retinoids in the past 12 months
  -  uncontrolled systemic disease
