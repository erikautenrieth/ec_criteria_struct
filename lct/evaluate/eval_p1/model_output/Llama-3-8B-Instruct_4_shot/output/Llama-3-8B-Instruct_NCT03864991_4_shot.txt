Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient diagnosed with T1D > 6 months
  -  Patient's age ≥ 15 years
  -  Patient  [OR] any one of the family member (father, mother, or sibling) using a smart phone
  -  Patient  [OR] any one of the family member knows how to use (receive calls or read text message) a smart phone
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient on insulin pump
  -  Patient  [NOT] pregnant females
  -  Patient with neurodevelopmental delay  [OR] or thalassemia
  -  Patient currently using any kind of authentic e-device for step count or physical activity
