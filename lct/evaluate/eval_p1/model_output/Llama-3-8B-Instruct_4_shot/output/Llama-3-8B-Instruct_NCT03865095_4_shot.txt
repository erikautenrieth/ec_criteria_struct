Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age ≥18 years
  -  mechanical ventilation expected for at least 48 hours
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Clinical Frailty Score above 7 prior admission
  -  neuromuscular disease  [NOT] in previous medical history
  -  amputated lower extremities
  -  trauma of lower extremities involving tights
  -  age  [NOT] under 18 years
  -  inability to perform ultrasound examination
  -  death  [OR] or discharge prior to completion of protocol
  -  full verticalization  [NOT] prior to completion of protocol
