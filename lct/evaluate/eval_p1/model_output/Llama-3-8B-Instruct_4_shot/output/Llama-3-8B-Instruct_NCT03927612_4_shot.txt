Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Aged 9-12
  -  English-speaking
  -  Meet DSM-5 criteria for oppositional defiant disorder (ODD), conduct disorder (CD), or Other Specified or Unspecified Disruptive, Impulse-Control, and Conduct Disorder
  -  Right-handed
  -  Estimated full-scale IQ greater than 75
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Bipolar disorder, [OR] any disorder involving psychosis, [OR] pervasive developmental disorders, [OR] current or past substance use disorder, [OR] current major depressive disorder
  -  History of neurological problems (e.g., epilepsy, traumatic brain injury)
  -  Contraindications for MRI
