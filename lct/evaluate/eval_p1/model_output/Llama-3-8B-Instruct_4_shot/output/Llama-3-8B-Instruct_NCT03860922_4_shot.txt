Inclusion Criteria:
  -  patients in different age groups  [AND] and both sex referred from orthopedic outpatient clinic with chronic ankle pain  [AND] and suspected clinically to have impingement syndrome.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Inflammatory arthritis of ankle joint, infection in ankle joint, neoplasm around ankle joint  [NOT] (patients with these conditions will be excluded)
  2. Patients with contraindications for MRI, e.g. an implanted magnetic device, pacemakers  [OR] or claustrophobia.
