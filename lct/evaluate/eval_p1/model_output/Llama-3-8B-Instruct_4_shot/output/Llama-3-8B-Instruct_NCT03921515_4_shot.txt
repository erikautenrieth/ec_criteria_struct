INCLUSION CRITERIA:
 1. Meets one of the following:
 1. has documentation of HIES [OR] or CGD (patient populations); or
 2. does not have clinically apparent evidence of an immune defect [AND] or history of invasive or recurrent S. aureus infections (healthy volunteers).
 2. Between the following age limits (inclusive):
 1. 18 [AND] and 65 years old for healthy volunteers;
 2. 7 [AND] and 65 years old for patients with CGD;
 3. 18 [AND] and 65 years old for patients with HIES.
 3. Willing to allow storage of blood, RNA, bacterial and fungal cultures, and other tissue samples [AND] for future research.
 4. Able to provide informed consent.
EXCLUSION CRITERIA:
The following exclusion criteria apply to all subjects:
 1. For individuals undergoing blister or skin biopsy procedures, history of keloid formation [NOT] allowed.
 2. Current or prior (within 3 months) anticoagulant or anti-platelet therapy (other than aspirin or non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs [NSAIDs]) [NOT] allowed.
 3. Current or prior (within 3 months) use of immunomodulatory drugs (e.g., chemotherapy, steroids), except if approved by the principal investigator [NOT] allowed.
 4. Pregnancy [NOT] allowed.
 5. Any condition that, in the opinion of the investigator, contraindicates participation in the study [NOT] allowed.
