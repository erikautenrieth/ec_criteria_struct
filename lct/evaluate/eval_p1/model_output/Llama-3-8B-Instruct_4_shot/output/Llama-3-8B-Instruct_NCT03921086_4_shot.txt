Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adult  [AND] (older than 18 years)
  -  Elective surgery at preoperative in-hospital visit
  -  All stage 1  [AND] and stage 2 hypertensive patients as defined by the South African Hypertension Practice Guideline 2014
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient refusal
  -  Day case surgery
  -  Obstetric  [AND] and cardiac surgery
  -  Patients with severe hypertension  [NOT] (>180/110 mmHg) as defined by the South African Hypertension Practice Guideline 2014
