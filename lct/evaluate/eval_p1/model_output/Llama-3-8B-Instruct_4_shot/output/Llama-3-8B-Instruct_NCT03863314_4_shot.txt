Inclusion Criteria:
 - Greater than 18 years of age
 - Personnel working [OR] or volunteering at Lucile Packard Children's Hospital (LPCH) or Stanford Health Care (SHC) facilities
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who do not consent
 - Are currently taking beta blockers [OR] or other chronotropic heart medication(s)
 - Have a history of severe motion sickness
 - Currently have nausea
 - Currently experiencing seizures [NOT]
 - Are clinically unstable
