Inclusion Criteria:
  -  •Both sexes will be included.
       -  Age (20-50)
       -  Patient with Fitzpatrick skin types III-IV
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  • Patients with known platelet dysfunction syndrome, critical thrombocytopenia (<50,000/ul), [NOT] any hemodynamic instability.
       -  Patients with chronic medical illness  [OR] (e.g. diabetes, chronic infections, and blood dyscrasias).
       -  Patients with local inflammatory skin disorders  [OR] or active herpes infection at the site of the procedure.
       -  Patients on consistent use of anti-coagulants  [OR] or non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAID) within 48 hours of procedure.
       -  Haemoglobin level < 10 g/dl  [NOT] were also excluded.
       -  Patients with cancer - especially hematopoietic  [OR] of bone.
       -  Pregnant females  [NOT].
       -  Patients with corticosteroids injection at treatment site within 1 month  [OR] or systemic use of corticosteroids within 2 weeks.
