Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Diagnosis of esophageal squamous cell carcinoma by pathological cytology and histology.
  2. Clinically judged patients with p0~pIV initial treatment  [AND]  (1 Variable risk factors are overeating food (habits)  [AND]  and family history)  [AND]  (2 Among all the patients, the ratio of male to female is 3:1, and the age is between 50  [AND]  and 70 years old)  [AND]  (3 In order to rule out the interference of other diseases, each sample needs to be determined to have no history of other diseases (such as neurodegenerative diseases, leukemia))  [AND]  3. The normal population comes from the healthy population of Nanfang Hospital Health Center of Southern Medical University.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Preoperative patients have received relevant treatment, such as radiotherapy  [OR]  or chemotherapy;
  2. The subject has other diseases, such as tumors in other parts;
  3. Patients do not meet the age  [NOT]  (between 50 and 70 years old);
  4. There is no guarantee that other eating habits (such as eating fresh fruits and vegetables, not eating preserved foods)  [NOT]  and other factors (such as BMI, smoking, drinking, etc.) are basically the same.
