Inclusion Criteria:
 - 1. Ages of 18 [AND] and above
 - 2. Unilateral transtibial [OR] or transfemoral amputee

 - 2. Cognitive deficits [OR] or mental health problems that would limit ability to consent and participate fully in the study protocol
 - 3. Women who are [NOT] pregnant or who plan to become pregnant in the near future
