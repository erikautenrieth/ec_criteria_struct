Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age over 18
  -  Total knee arthroplasty for primary osteoarthritis performed by Dr. Rick Wright.
  -  Primary diagnosis of knee osteoarthritis
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Diminished mental capacity
  -  Patient has a Vancomycin  [OR] Tobramycin allergy
  -  Patient has a history requiring IV administration of vancomycin or tobramycin perioperatively
  -  Patient has Chronic kidney disease stage III  [OR] stage IV
