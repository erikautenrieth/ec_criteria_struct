Inclusion Criteria:
Patients who are diagnosed as ulcerative colitis [OR] Cron's disease, however in activity [OR] or in remission, attending the the outpatient clinics [AND] and inpatient of gastroenterology units of Internal medicine department

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Previous history of hypertension [OR] diabetes mellitus [OR] Dyslipidemia
 - Previous history of cardiovascular [OR] or cerebrovascular events
 - Autoimmune [NOT] diseases
 - Neoplastic [NOT] diseases
 - Thromboembolic [NOT] diseases
