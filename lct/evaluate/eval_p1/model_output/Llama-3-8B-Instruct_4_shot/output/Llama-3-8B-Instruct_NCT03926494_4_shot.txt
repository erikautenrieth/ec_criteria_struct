Inclusion Criteria:
 - All comatose patients (Glasgow coma score <8) due to carbon monoxide poisoning, treated by hyperbaric oxygen therapy in a tertiary hospital in Île de France area;
 - Aged ≥ 18 years;
 - Hospitalized in hyperbaric medicine unity of Raymond Poincaré hospital in Garches [AND] (patient must be hospitalized in this specific hospital);
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnant woman;
 - Patient present clinical signs of stroke [AND] without CO detection;
 - Patient with unusable data [NOT] (exclusion criterion in case of unusable data).
