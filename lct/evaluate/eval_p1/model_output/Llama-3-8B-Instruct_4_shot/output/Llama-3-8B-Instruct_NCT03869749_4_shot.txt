Inclusion Criteria:
  -  two parents in the home
  -  an adolescent who is between 14  [AND] and 18 years of age
  -  marital distress
  -  youth reports of stress  [OR] or anxiety
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  two parents in the home  [AND] but they are pursuing legal divorce  [OR] or separation
  -  parents are not willing to receive marital education/support
  -  adolescents are clinically depressed  [NOT] or report suicidal ideation at baseline
