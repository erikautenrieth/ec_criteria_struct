Inclusion Criteria:
  -  American Society of Anesthesiologists GradeⅠ－II, Patients undergo elective surgery for benign Reproductive diseases (including laparoscopic ovarian cyst removal, laparoscopic tubal ligation, laparoscopic tubal drainage, laparoscopic salpingoplasty, etc.)  [OR]  for breast disease.
  -  Aged 18-45 years
  -  Body Mass Index 18  [AND] and < 25kg/m2, body weight 40  [AND] and < 65 kilograms
  -  Agreed to participate the research
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Difficult airway
  -  upper respiratory tract infection within 2 weeks
  -  history of allergy  [OR] or long-term use of propofol and opioids
  -  self-rating anxiety scale before operation indicates anxiety  [NOT]
  -  pregnancy  [OR] or lactation
