Inclusion Criteria:
  -  A diagnosis of painful TMD Research Diagnostic Criteria for TMD (RDC/TMD)
  -  A history of orofacial pain at least three months prior to the study (TREEDE et al, 2015)
  -  age ranging between 18  [AND] and 55 years, considering the greater prevalence of TMD associated with this age period
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with  [NOT] illiteracy, severe depression (medical diagnoses)
  -  clinical history of tumors in the craniofacial region
  -  patients in the post dental surgery period  [OR] or submitted to previous physical therapy in the past year  [OR] or to any health/pain education strategy
  -  pregnant women
  -  infections
  -  whiplash-associated disorders
  -  with chronic degenerative inflammatory  [OR] or neurologic disorders
  -  Patients will be instructed to not use pain relief medications during the intervention period of this trial  [AND] if any medication be used, participants will be encouraged to report
