Inclusion Criteria:
 - ASA physical status 1 [AND] and 2, underwent gynicological laparotomic surgery under general anesthesia, followed up in PACU (postoperative care unit)

Exclusion Criteria:
 - ASA 3 [OR] 4
 - mental retardation
 - psychiatric disease
 - history of malignant hyperthermia in patient [OR] or her family
 - neurological disease
 - morbid obesity
 - history of asthma
 - follow-up in the intensive care unit
