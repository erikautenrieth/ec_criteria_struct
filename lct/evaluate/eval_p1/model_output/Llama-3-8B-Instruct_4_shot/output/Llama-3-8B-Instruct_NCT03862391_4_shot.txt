Inclusion Criteria:
  -  ASA physical status 1 [AND] 2, underwent gynecological laparotomic surgery under general anesthesia, followed up in PACU (postoperative care unit)

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  ASA 3 [OR] 4
  -  mental retardation
  -  psychiatric disease
  -  [NOT] history of malignant hyperthermia in patient or her family
  -  neurological disease
  -  morbid obesity
  -  history of asthma
  -  follow-up in the intensive care unit
