Inclusion Criteria:
  -  ICU patients suspected of having an abnormality of ICP undergoing monitoring with commercially-available, FDA-approved instrumentation.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History of: [NOT] Glaucoma or optic hypertension
  -  History of: Severe orbital or ophthalmic injury
  -  History of: Orbital or intraocular surgery that might interfere with orbital compliance
  -  History of: Congenital malformations of the eye and/or orbit that might interfere with orbital compliance or orbital vascular anatomy
