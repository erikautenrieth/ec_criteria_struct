Inclusion Criteria:
 - English Speaking
 - Age ≥65 years
 - Body Composition Body Fat >25% [FOR] Men
 - Body Composition Body Fat >30% [FOR] Women
 - Participating in the Dartmouth Hitchcock Weight and Wellness Center (WWC) Healthy Lifestyle Program (HLP)
 - Willing [AND] and able to give informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Unable to perform measures
 - Individuals [NOT] willing/unable to provide consent
 - Severe mental [OR] or life-threatening illness
 - History of bariatric surgery
