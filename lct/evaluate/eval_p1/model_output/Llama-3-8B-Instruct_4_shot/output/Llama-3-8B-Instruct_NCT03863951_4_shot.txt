Inclusion Criteria:
  -  diagnosed with the first ever acute ischemic stroke occurring within 2 weeks  [AND] and were confirmed by CT or MRI,
  -  has no history of depression  [NOT] or use of antidepressants  [NOT] or antipsychotics,
  -  were competent to consent to take part in this research.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  significant acute or severe illness such as infection  [OR] or heart failure  [OR] or tumor,
  -  previous diagnosis of depression  [OR] or other mental disorder  [OR] who had recently accepted antidepressants  [OR] or antipsychotics,
  -  antibiotics in the last two weeks,
  -  severe aphasia  [OR] or dysarthria,  [OR] irritable bowel syndrome,  [OR] asthma,  [OR] and pregnant  [OR] or lactating women,
  -  a previous history of neurological illness including AD  [OR] and PD，
