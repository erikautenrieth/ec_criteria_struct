Inclusion Criteria:
 - diagnosed with the first ever acute ischemic stroke occurring within 2 weeks and were confirmed by CT or MRI,
 - has no history of depression [AND] or use of antidepressants [AND] or antipsychotics,
 - were competent to consent to take part in this research
Exclusion Criteria:
 - significant acute or severe illness such as infection, heart failure, and tumor [NOT] (i.e., not having any of these conditions)
 - previous diagnosis of depression [OR] or other mental disorder, or who had recently accepted antidepressants [OR] or antipsychotics
 - antibiotics in the last two weeks
 - severe aphasia [OR] or dysarthria, irritable bowel syndrome, asthma, and pregnant or lactating women
 - a previous history of neurological illness including AD [AND] or PD
 - Inflammatory bowel disease
