Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who have seen the study PI (Remy Coeytaux, MD, PhD) for an integrative medicine consultation between September 1, 2018 and April 30, 2019
 - who report having been diagnosed with PTSD [OR] who believe they may have PTSD [OR] who report PTSD-related symptoms
 - are eligible to participate
Exclusion Criteria:
 - No exclusion criteria
