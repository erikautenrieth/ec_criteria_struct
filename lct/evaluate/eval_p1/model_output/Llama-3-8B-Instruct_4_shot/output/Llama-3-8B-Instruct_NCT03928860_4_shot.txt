Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with at least 3 months of clinical onset of peripheral vascular disease
  -  Patients with ABI between 0.4  [AND] and 1.4
  -  Patients up to Stage 2b according to the Fontaine classification system
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients undergoing vascular or endovascular surgery in the last 3 months
  -  Patients with unstable angina
  -  Patients with myocardial infarction (MI)  [OR] or stroke
  -  Patients with heart failure
  -  Patients with significant liver  [OR] or renal failure (ie, dialysis)
  -  Patients with acute infectious diseases  [NOT] or neoplasia
