Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Psoriatic patients that are treated in our institute.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients suffering from cardiovascular diseases [AND] (peripheral vascular disease, congestive heart failure, status post cerebrovascular accident).
  -  Conditions that are harmful to the inner ear [AND] (chronic renal failure, history of noise exposure, usage of ototoxic drugs, chronic otitis media, ear surgery in the past, significant head trauma in the past, ear drum perforation).
  -  participant that complains of vertigo/ dizziness/ unsteadiness.
  -  [NOT] participant who is/ was suffering from known inner ear disease: meniere disease, acoustic neurinoma, vestibular neuronitis.
