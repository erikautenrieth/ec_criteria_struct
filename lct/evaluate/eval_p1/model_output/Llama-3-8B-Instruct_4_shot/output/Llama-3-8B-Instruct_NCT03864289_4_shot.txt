Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Children with ASD  [OR] high risk for autism;
  -  Han ethnic;
  -  Parents were at least middle school educated  [AND] and agreed to do at least 20 hours per week of planned practice activities with the child in daily life.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Children with Syndromic ASD caused by known genetic defects  [OR] or inherited metabolic diseases, such as Rett Syndrome, Fragile X Syndrome, Angelman Syndrome, Prader-Willi Syndrome, tuberous sclerosis [NOT];
  -  Visual, auditory and physical disabilities;
  -  Complication with other serious chronic diseases, such as liver and kidney failure, connective tissue diseases, tumors.
