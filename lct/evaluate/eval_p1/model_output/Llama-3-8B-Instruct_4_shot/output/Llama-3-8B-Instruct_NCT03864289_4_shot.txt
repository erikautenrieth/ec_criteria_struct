Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Children with ASD  [OR] high risk for autism;
  -  Han ethnicity;
  -  Parents were at least middle school educated  [AND] and agreed to do at least 20 hours per week of planned practice activities with the child in daily life.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Children with Syndromic ASD caused by known genetic defects or inherited metabolic diseases  [NOT] such as Rett Syndrome, Fragile X Syndrome, Angelman Syndrome, Prader-Willi Syndrome, tuberous sclerosis;
  -  Visual, auditory, and physical disabilities;
  -  Complication with other serious chronic diseases  [NOT] such as liver and kidney failure, connective tissue diseases, tumors.
