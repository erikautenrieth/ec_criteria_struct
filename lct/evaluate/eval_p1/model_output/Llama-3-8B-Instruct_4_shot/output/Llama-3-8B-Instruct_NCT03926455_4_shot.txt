Inclusion Criteria:
 - Healthy adult men [OR] or women who are not pregnant [AND] or planning to become pregnant during study duration, aged 18 to 55 years.
 - Clinical laboratory parameters within normal laboratory limits [OR] or not found to be clinically significant by the PI
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Relevant history of physical [OR] or psychiatric illness [OR] or medical disorder that required treatment.
 - Known [OR] or suspected hypersensitivity to investigational product
 - Immunocompromised subjects [NOT] excluded
 - Previous Typhoid vaccination [OR] or elevated anti-Vi antibodies at screening
 - Known history of Typhoid infection in the previous 6 months
 - Positive HIV [OR] or HBsAg [OR] or HCV screen
 - Any other condition [OR] or abnormality that, in the opinion of the Investigator, may compromise the safety of the patients
