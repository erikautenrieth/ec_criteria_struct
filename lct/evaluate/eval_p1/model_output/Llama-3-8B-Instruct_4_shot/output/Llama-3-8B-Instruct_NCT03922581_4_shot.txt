Inclusion Criteria:
  -  ability to provide informed consent
  -  willingness to participate in study
  -  self-identity as African American
  -  history of childhood and/or adult trauma exposure (3 total criterion A traumas) [AND] 
  -  positive PTSD screen (PC-PTSD ≥ 3) [AND] 
  -  positive MDD screen (PHQ-9 ≥ 5)
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  presence of intellectual disability [OR]  bipolar [OR] or psychotic disorder
  -  presence of current substance use disorder (past 1 month; see cut off scores in psychological measures below) [NOT] 
  -  active suicidality
