Inclusion Criteria:
  -  carpal tunnel syndrome diagnosed either by physical exam  [OR] or EMG
  -  age > 18
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  allergy to bupivacaine
  -  pre-operative opioid usage within the last six months
  -  patients  [NOT] allergic to Percocet or acetaminophen
  -  patients taking tricyclic antidepressants (interaction with bupivacaine)
  -  patients who are prisoners  [OR] or pregnant women
