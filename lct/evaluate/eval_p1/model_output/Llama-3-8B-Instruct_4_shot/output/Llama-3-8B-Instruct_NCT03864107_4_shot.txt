Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Men and women aged 18-70 years
  -  BMI 18.5-50 kg/m2
  -  Not taking antibiotics  [AND] and antimicrobial drugs for at least three months
  -  Both physically active  [OR] and sedentary individuals will be eligible to take part in the study
  -  Weight stable (±5kg) for at least 6 months
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  No cardiometabolic (e.g. heart disease, high blood pressure) or inflammatory illness
  -  Smokers  [NOT] (including the use of vaporisers and e-cigarettes)
  -  Taking anti-inflammatory drugs  [NOT] (excluding aspirin)
