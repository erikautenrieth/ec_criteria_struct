Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Participant is willing  [AND] and able to give informed consent for participation in the study
  -  Male  [OR] or Female, aged 40 years to 85 years.
  -  Diagnosed with COPD
  -  Able  [AND] and willing to comply with all study requirements.
  -  Participant is willing to attend visits at baseline  [AND] and 8 weeks (sub-group: baseline, 8 weeks)     
  -  Able to read  [AND] and understand English
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Age <40
  -  Attended a pulmonary rehabilitation programme  [OR] or participating in a physical activity intervention study in current time or in the last 6 months.
  -  Any other significant diseases or disorders that are a contraindication to be enrolled in a pulmonary rehabilitation programme  [NOT]
