Inclusion Criteria:
  -  singleton parturient
  -  planned for elective LSCS under spinal anesthesia
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  gestational age < 36 weeks
  -  emergency cases
  -  placenta previa  [OR] or pre-eclampsia
  -  BMI > 40
  -  Reynauld disease  [NOT] (assuming this is a typo and the correct term is Reynaud's disease)
  -  patient refusal
  -  cardiovascular disease
