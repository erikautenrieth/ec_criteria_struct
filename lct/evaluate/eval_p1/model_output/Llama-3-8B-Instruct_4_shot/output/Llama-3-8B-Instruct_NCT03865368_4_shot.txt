Inclusion Criteria:
 - Individuals between age 18-65 with Body Mass Index (BMI) of >29.9 kg/m2

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Unstable hypertensive patients
 - History of [NOT] cardiovascular, neurologic, or orthopedic diseases
 - Presence of knee prosthesis [OR] or hip replacement
 - Chronic inflammatory disease
 - Anti-inflammatory treatment
 - Cognitive impairments
 - Pregnancy
