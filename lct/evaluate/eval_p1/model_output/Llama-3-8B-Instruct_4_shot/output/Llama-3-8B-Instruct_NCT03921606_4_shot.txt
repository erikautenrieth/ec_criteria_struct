Inclusion Criteria:
  -  aged 18 years  [OR] above,
  -  able to speak Cantonese  [AND] and read Chinese
  -  do not intend to quit smoking  [NOT] (pre-contemplation stage), but are willing to promote their health
  -  use a smartphone  [AND] and can use an instant messaging tool (e.g., WhatsApp/WeChat)
  -  willing to receive health promotion advice  [AND] and interact with our interventionist via WhatsApp/WeChat on a smartphone throughout the study period
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  inability to give informed consent  [OR] or participate in our intervention due to impaired mental status, cognitive impairment  [OR] or communication barriers
  -  participate in another smoking cessation programme  [OR] or service
