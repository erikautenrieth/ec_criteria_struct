Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adults patients ≥ 18 years of age with Crohn's disease (CD) with at least colonic involvement, ulcerative colitis (UC), or non-IBD controls who have been referred for colonoscopy for clinical reasons. The clinical reasons may include colorectal cancer screening  [OR] or surveillance, diagnostic for CD or UC flare, or gastrointestinal symptoms.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pregnant patients.
  -  Patients with known current colorectal cancer, infectious colitis, diverticulitis, or microscopic colitis.
  -  Patients who have undergone surgery involving the cecum  [OR] or rectum.
