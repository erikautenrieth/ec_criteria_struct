Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adults patients ≥ 18 years of age with Crohn's disease (CD) with at least colonic involvement, ulcerative colitis (UC), or non-IBD controls who have been referred for colonoscopy for clinical reasons
  -  The clinical reasons may include colorectal cancer screening, surveillance, diagnostic for CD or UC flare, or gastrointestinal symptoms [AND] no [NOT] exclusion criteria

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pregnant patients
  -  Patients with known current colorectal cancer, infectious colitis, diverticulitis, or microscopic colitis
  -  Patients who have undergone surgery involving the cecum or rectum
