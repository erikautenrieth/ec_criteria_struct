Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Males [AND] and females aged 13-17 years directly impacted by the 2017 wildfires having lost their home [OR] or been temporarily displaced
 2. Screen positive for PTSD (Child Trauma Screening Questionnaire = 5 [OR] or higher)
 3. Have regular access to a smart phone [AND] and a computer
 4. Have a personal email address
 5. Speak [AND] and read English fluently
 6. Have parental consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. A positive self-reported history of psychosis, bipolar, [OR] or schizophrenia
 2. Serious self-reported physical health concerns necessitating surgery [OR] or with prognosis <6 months
 3. A positive screen for a current Substance User Disorder (CRAFFT = 2 [OR] or higher)
 4. Current self-reported suicidal ideation
 5. Self-reported pregnancy
 6. Self-reported less than four weeks of stable prescription medication for anxiety, anti-depressant, [AND] and sleep promoting medications prior to the screening assessment
