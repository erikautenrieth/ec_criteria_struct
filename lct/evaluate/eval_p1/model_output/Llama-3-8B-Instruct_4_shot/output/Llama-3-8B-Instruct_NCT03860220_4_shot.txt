Inclusion Criteria:
  -  145 mmHg  [AND] ≤ msSBP ≤ 190 mmHg
  -  Triglycerides  [AND] < 500 mg/dL
  -  LDL-C  [AND] ≤ 250 mg/dL
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  sSBP  [AND] ≥ 20mmHg  [AND] and sDBP  [AND] ≥ 10mmHg
  -  symptomatic orthostatic hypotension  [AND] and secondary/iatrogenic hypertension  [AND] and dyslipidemia
  -  history of moderate to severe cerebral ischemia, cerebral hemorrhage, transient ischemic attack, myocardial infarction, or unstable angina in the past 6 months; severe heart failure  [NOT] (New York Heart Association functional class III and IV)
  -  hypersensitivity to telmisartan  [OR] or rosuvastatin
  -  history of angioedema after treatment with angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors or ARBs; creatinine phosphokinase levels  [AND] ≥3 times the upper limit of normal
  -  estimated glomerular filtration rate  [AND] ≤30 mL/min; aspartate aminotransferase and alanine aminotransferase levels  [AND] ≥3 times the upper limit of normal
  -  potassium levels  [NOT] >5.5 mmol/L; or any diseases that could affect the results of the study.
