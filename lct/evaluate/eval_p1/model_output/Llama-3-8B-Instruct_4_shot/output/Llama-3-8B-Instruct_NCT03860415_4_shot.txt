Inclusion Criteria:
 - Chronic (more than three months) ESBL colonization of the same strain as determined by [AND] species, phenotype and antibiogram.
 - Patients must be able to swallow sachets.
 - Must be able to speak [AND] understand Swedish.
 - Must have a permanent residence in Sweden.
 - Must be able to sign informed consent in Swedish.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Immunosuppression [NOT] (i.e. chemotheraphy, treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors).
 - In patient care.
 - immunodeficiency
 - psychiatric disorder
 - alcohol or substance abuse
 - dementia
 - Invasive catheters.
