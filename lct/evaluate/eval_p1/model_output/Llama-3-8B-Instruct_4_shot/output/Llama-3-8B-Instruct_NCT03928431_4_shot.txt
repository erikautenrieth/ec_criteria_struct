Inclusion Criteria:
 - Infants of healthy mothers with uncomplicated pregnancies at term, mothers of any ethnic or social background that can speak, read, and understand Swedish to the extent that they can consent in Swedish
 - An additional inclusion criterion for mothers in the CS groups is vaginal pH ≤ 4 at the time of birth
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Infants of mothers with pre-eclampsia [OR] or complicated pregnancies
 - Mothers <18 years [OR] or >40 years of age
 - Genital herpes simplex
 - Genital warts
 - HIV
 - Hepatitis B
 - Complications during delivery
 - Infants delivered prior to 37 weeks of gestation
 - Mothers positive to Group B Streptococcus [OR] or Group A Streptococcus
 - Bacterial vaginosis
 - Vaginal pH > 4 at the time of birth [NOT] (for mothers in the CS group)
 - Mothers with fecal samples positive for Salmonella [OR] or Shigella [OR] or Campylobacter [OR] or Yersinia
