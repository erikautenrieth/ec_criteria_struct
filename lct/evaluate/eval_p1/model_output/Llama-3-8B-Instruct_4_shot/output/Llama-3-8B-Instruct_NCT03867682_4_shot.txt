Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Refractory or relapsed AML which will include:
 1. Refractory disease will be defined as at least 1 prior treatment with no remission [AND].
 2. Relapsed disease will be defined as 5% or more blasts in bone marrow seen after remission [AND].
 3. Patients with AML arising from myelodysplastic syndromes (including CMML) [OR] or myeloproliferative neoplasms (secondary AML, ts-AML) are also eligible.
 2. Circulating blast count ≤ 200/μL within 10 days prior to first cycle of treatment. Hydroxyurea should be used to keep the peripheral blast count ≤ 200/μL until the first day of protocol treatment, to the extent that this is possible [AND].
 3. ECOG ≤ 2 [AND].
 4. Estimated creatinine clearance ≥ 50 mL/min [AND].
 5. AST and ALT ≤ 3.0 x ULN [AND].
 6. Bilirubin ≤ 3.0 x ULN [AND].
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Active CNS Leukemia [NOT].
 2. Known HIV infection [OR] or known hepatitis B [OR] or hepatitis C infection (with a detectable viral load) [NOT].
 3. Participant has received strong and/or moderate CYP3A inducers within 7 days prior to the initiation of study treatment [NOT].
 4. Have received prior radiation to maximally tolerated levels to any critical normal organ [NOT].
 5. Clinically significant cardiac disease [NOT].
 6. Active, uncontrolled serious infection [NOT].
 7. Have other non-myeloid malignancy within 2 years of entry (with exceptions) [NOT].
 8. Psychiatric disorder that would preclude study participation [NOT].
 9. Previous solid organ transplant (prior treatment with SCT is allowed but not if patient has GVHD or is still receiving immunosuppression/GVHD therapy) [NOT].
