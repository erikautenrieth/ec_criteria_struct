Inclusion criteria:
pregnant women 34 weeks or less admitted to Women's Health Hospital with a diagnosis of:
  -  severe preeclampsia, defined as: 1- blood pressure ≥ 160/110 mmHg  [AND] 2- proteinurea ≥ +3 or more by dipstick  [AND] 3- HELLP syndrome
  -  threatened preterm labour that will receive Magnesium Sulphate, either for tocolysis  [OR] or neuroprotection
Exclusion criteria:
- women with more than 34 weeks gestation  [AND] with any contraindication for Magnesium Sulphate intake, including: severe renal impairment  [OR] myasthenia gravis  [NOT]
