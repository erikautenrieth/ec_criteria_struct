Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with moderate to severe sleep apnoea who require CPAP as standard care (AHI >15)
  -  Patients between the age of 18  [AND] and 75
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with a diagnosis of atrial fibrillation (AF), atrial flutter  [OR] or ventricular tachycardia (VT)
  -  Patients with an ILR already in-situ  [OR] or an established indication for ILR
  -  Patients with a palliative diagnosis i.e. life expectancy  [NOT] less than 3 years
  -  Patients who lack capacity
