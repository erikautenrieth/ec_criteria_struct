Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adults (≥ 18 years old)
 - Scheduled for robot-assisted colorectal laparoscopic surgery
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Severe liver- [OR] or renal disease
 - Pregnancy [OR] or lactation
 - Planned diagnostics or treatment with radioactive iodine < 1 week after surgery
 - BMI >35 kg/m2
 - Known or suspected hypersensitivity to indocyanine green, sodium iodide [OR] or iodine
 - Hyperthyroidism [OR] or thyroid adenomas
 - Use of medication interfering with ICG absorption [NOT] as listed in the summary of product characteristics.
