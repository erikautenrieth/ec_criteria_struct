Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Postmenopausal women
  2. Men >50-year-old
  3. After Denosumab treatment ≥ 2 years due to osteoporosis

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. secondary osteoporosis
  2. metabolic bone disease
  3. [NOT] cancer history
  4. continuous steroid therapy
  5. hormone replacement therapy
  6. ever used any other kind of anti-resorptive treatment
  7. estimated Glomerular filtration rate < 35 mL/min
  8. allergy to Zoledronic acid
  9. hypocalcaemia
  10. any contraindications to use Zoledronic acid
