Inclusion Criteria:
  -  complex anal fistula according to Parks classification
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Non cryptoglandular fistulas
  -  acute sepsis
  -  inflammatory bowel diseases
  -  simple fistulas  [NOT] (defined as subcutaneous, intersphincteric and low transsphincteric fistula)
  -  pregnant patients
  -  ASA IV
  -  patients < 18  [AND] years
  -  immunocompromised
  -  patients who refused consent
