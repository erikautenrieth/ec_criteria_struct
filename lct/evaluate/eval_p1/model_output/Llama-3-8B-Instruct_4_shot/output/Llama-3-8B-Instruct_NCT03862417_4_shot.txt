Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adults over 50 years old with degenerative scoliosis (n=20) with pain (>2/10)
  -  Curves 15°-45°

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Curves over 50°  [NOT] are surgical candidates, and will be excluded
  -  Patients who have had cortisone or anesthetic injections within the last 3 months  [OR] 
  -  surgery or presenting contra-indications to exercises (fractures  [OR] or cancer  [OR] or severe cardio-vascular co-morbidities  [OR] or osteopenia)
