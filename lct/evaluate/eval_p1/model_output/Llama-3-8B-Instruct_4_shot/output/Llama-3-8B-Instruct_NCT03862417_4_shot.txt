Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adults over 50 years old with degenerative scoliosis (n=20) with pain (>2/10)
 - Curves 15°-45°
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Curves over 50° [NOT] are surgical candidates, and will be excluded
 - Patients who have had cortisone or anesthetic injections within the last 3 months
 - Patients who have had surgery
 - Patients presenting contra-indications to exercises [OR] including: fractures, cancer, severe cardio-vascular co-morbidities, or osteopenia
