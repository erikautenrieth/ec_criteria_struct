Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Female [AND] (mandatory)
  2. Not currently pregnant [OR] or nursing [AND] (mandatory)
  3. Aged 21-45 [AND] (advanced age can hinder hormone levels)
  4. Not be diagnosed as sub fertile [AND] (infertility causes inconsistent hormone levels)
  5. Not have used any form of hormonal birth control in the past 2 months [AND] (mandatory)
  6. Currently trying to conceive [OR] (ideal but not required)
  7. Currently reside in the US [AND] (mandatory, this study is for US residents only)
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Male [NOT] (mandatory)
  2. Pregnant [NOT]
  3. Nursing [NOT]
