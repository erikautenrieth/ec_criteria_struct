Inclusion Criteria:
  -  over 21 years of age
  -  able to understand  [AND] and speak English
  -  self-report of having difficulty with a healthy diet  [AND] and engaging in regular physical activity
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  On chemotherapy for cancer treatment
  -  Current substance abuse disorder  [OR]  (i.e., drugs, alcohol)
  -  Current diagnoses of Post-traumatic stress disorders  [OR]  severe anxiety  [OR]  severe depression
  -  On hemodialysis
  -  [NOT] Pregnant (Pregnant women may develop Preeclampsia, which is a pregnancy complication characterized by high blood pressure)
