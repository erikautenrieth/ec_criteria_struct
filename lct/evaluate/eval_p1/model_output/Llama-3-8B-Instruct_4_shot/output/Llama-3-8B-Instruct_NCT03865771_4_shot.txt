Inclusion Criteria:
Principal:
  -  normal psychomotor development
  -  informed consent signed by both parents  [AND] and subject if able
  -  affiliated to social security regimen
Specific:
  -  Patients from "severe" and "benign" groups:
    -  focal age related epilepsy: BECTS, ABPE, ECSWS (ILAE criteria)
    -  children hospitalized for their follow-up
    -  normal neuroimaging
  -  Control group:
    -  children hospitalized for a non neurologic disease
Exclusion Criteria:
Principal:
  -  Psychiatric trouble (DSM V)  [NOT] Sensorial trouble without correction  [NOT] Poor command of French language  [NOT] Minor under care
Specific:
  -  Patients from "severe" and "benign" groups:
    -  degenerative disease
    -  abnormal neuroimaging  [NOT] normal neuroimaging
    -  mental deficiency
  -  Control group:
    -  neurologic trouble
    -  abnormal sleep EEG
    -  intellectual deficiency
