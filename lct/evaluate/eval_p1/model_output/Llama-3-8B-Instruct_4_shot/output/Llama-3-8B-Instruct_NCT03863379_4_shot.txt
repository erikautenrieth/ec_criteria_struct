Inclusion Criteria:
Persons with
  -  stroke  [OR] cerebrovascular disease
  -  traumatic brain injury
  -  multiple sclerosis
  -  spinal cord injuries  [AND] (including spinal cord lesions i.e. myelopathies etc.)
Exclusion Criteria:
Persons with
  -  heterotopic ossifications
  -  chronic administration of drugs, which promote fat increase
  -  chronic administration of drugs, which promote bone  [NOT] or muscle loss
  -  co-existing other diseases such as: endocrinopathies, muscular dystrophies, non-alcoholic fatty liver disease
