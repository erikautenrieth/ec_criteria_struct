Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Children aged 6 - 11 years with -1- to -10 diopters (D) myopia

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Any organic eye disease
  -  Strabismus
  -  Astigmatism
  -  Anisometropia >1.5 D
  -  Prematurity
  -  Use of mono- [OR] multifocal contact lenses
  -  Pre-treatment with atropine
