Inclusion Criteria:
  -  ≥18 years of age
  -  Currently treated with peritoneal dialysis (APD  [OR] or CAPD)
  -  Serum ionized calcium between 1.10  [AND] and 1.40 mmol/L for >3 months
  -  Plasma phosphate between 0.7  [AND] and 3.0 mmol/L for >3 months
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Earlier parathyroidectomized
  -  [NOT] other conditions, which hinders the participation in the trial
