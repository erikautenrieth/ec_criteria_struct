Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adults who are critically ill [AND] and with MRSA infection suspicion.

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Pregnancy [OR] or breast feeding.
 2. Known allergy to either vancomycin [OR] or ascorbic acid.
 3. Base line serum creatinine ≥2mg/dl [NOT] <2mg/dl.
 4. Patients receiving other nephrotoxic drug (e.g., aminoglycosides, amphotericin B, cisplatin [OR] or calcinurine inhibitors).
 5. Anticipated administration of contrast medium within 7 days.
 6. Patients suffering from some underlying diseases (e.g., cancer, HIV infection, systemic lupus erythematosus [OR] or urinary tract stones).
 7. Unlikelyhood of receiving the study medications for at least 72 hours.
