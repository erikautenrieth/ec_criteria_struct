Inclusion Criteria:
 - age greater than 60 years;
 - is not engaged in physical exercise programs for at least one year;
 - does not present severe cardiovascular disease, kidney disease, liver disease, or limiting cognitive or neuromuscular disease [AND] and does not present visual or auditory inability;
Exclusion Criteria:
 - failing to perform any of the evaluations suggested during the intervention protocol [AND] and;
 - absence in five or more Capoeira training sessions.
