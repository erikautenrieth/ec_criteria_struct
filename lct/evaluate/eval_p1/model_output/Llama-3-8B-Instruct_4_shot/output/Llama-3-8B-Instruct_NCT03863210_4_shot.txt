Inclusion Criteria:
 - female sex
 - age 45 to 60 years
 - one or more CV risk factors [AND] (overweight or obesity, i. e. body mass index (BMI) ≥25 kg/m2, [AND] and/or central obesity - waist circumference ≥88 cm, [AND] high blood pressure (systolic blood pressure ≥140 mm Hg [AND] or diastolic blood pressure ≥90 mm Hg), [AND] high blood cholesterol (≥5.2 mmol/L), [AND] active smoking)
 - participants on antihypertensive therapy [AND] were also included in the study
Exclusion Criteria:
 - current cardiovascular disease (CVD) (ischemic heart disease, peripheral artery disease, [OR] stroke)
 - malignant diseases
 - serious systemic [NOT] or mental diseases
