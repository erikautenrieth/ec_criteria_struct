Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Participants will be male  [AND] or female subjects who suffered total acute Achilles tendon rupture, and which underwent surgical repair.
  -  In addition, to participate in this study all volunteers will need to present medical  [AND] and/or physiotherapeutic release for physical/sports activities practice.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Volunteers that did not have Achilles tendon surgical reconstruction
  -  Volunteers that did not present medical  [AND] and/or physiotherapeutic release for physical/sports activities
  -  Volunteers who have participated in strength training program for the plantar flexors in the last 6 months
  -  Patients with diabetic diseases
  -  Those with difficulty for understanding  [NOT] and/or executing the test and training protocols in the isokinetic dynamometer will be excluded.
