Inclusion Criteria:
 - Normal hearing [AND] and vision
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Left-handedness
 - History of seizure, head injury (including neurosurgery), brain injury, diagnosis or a neurological or psychiatric disorder, metal in the head, sensitive scalp [NOT]
 - History of anxiolytic and/or antidepressant medications
 - Participation in Experiment #1 [OR] or Experiment #2 of the larger study
