Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Male  [AND] or Female participants providing written informed consent,
  -  ASA grade 1- 4,
  -  aged over 18
  -  undergoing a VATS procedure under General Anaesthesia
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Absence of informed written consent,
  -  pre existing infection at block site,
  -  severe coagulopathy,
  -  allergy to local anaesthesia,
  -  pre existing neurological deficit,
  -  previous history of opiate abuse,
  -  pre existing chronic pain condition,
  -  pre-existing dementia
