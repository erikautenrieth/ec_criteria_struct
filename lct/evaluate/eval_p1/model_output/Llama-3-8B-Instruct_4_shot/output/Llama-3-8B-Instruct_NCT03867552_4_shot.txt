Inclusion Criteria:
 - Woman ≥ 18 years old
 - undergoing laparoscopic surgery for endometriosis, uterine myoma's, hysterectomy [OR] or colpopexy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Women [NOT] < 18 years old
 - Males
 - Pregnancy
 - Conditions associated with chronic pain, such as peripheral neuropathy, pathology of the vertebral column [AND] or osteo-articular disease
 - Conditions causing acute pain e.g. abdominal trauma
