Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Controlled Type-2 diabetes mellitus
  -  Stage-II  [AND] and III periodontitis
  -  At least 2 sites with clinical attachment loss 3 or 4 mm  [AND] and to ≥5 mm, and radiographic bone loss 15%  [AND] extending to mid-third of root and beyond.
  -  Probing depth from ≤5 mm  [AND] to ≥6 mm
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Any periodontal  [OR] or Antibiotic treatment during the last six months.
  -  Receiving trace element supplements in the previous three months
  -  Pregnant  [OR] or lactating females
  -  Gastric  [OR] or diuretic treatment
  -  Acute renal failure
  -  smoking
