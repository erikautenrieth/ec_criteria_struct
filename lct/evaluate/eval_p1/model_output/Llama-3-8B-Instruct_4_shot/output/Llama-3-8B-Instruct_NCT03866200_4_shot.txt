Inclusion Criteria:
  -  The diagnosis of pseudoachondroplasia is based on clinical assessment either in person or by photographic review by skeletal dysplasia specialist (JTH)
  -  Healthy beyond pseudoachondroplasia associated complications
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Current use of resveratrol
  -  [NOT] Current use of blood thinners, lovastatin, ketoconazole, itraconazole, fexofenadine and triazolam
  -  Other non-pseudoachondroplasia related health conditions, e.g. cancers
  -  Pregnancy  [OR] or breastfeeding. Women must use adequate contraception during the study
  -  Participation in another clinical study  [AND] or using investigational agents
  -  Use of Non-steroid anti-inflammatory (NSAIDs)  [OR] or aspirin
  -  [NOT] Current use of Alfentanil, Cyclosporine, Dihydroergotamine, Dofetilide, Ergotamine, Fentanyl, Flibanserin, Oxycodone, Pimavanserin, Pimozide, Quinidine, Saquinavir, Sirolimus, Tacrolimus, Temsirolimus, Theophylline, Tizanidine, Thioridazine, Fosphenytoin, Phenytoin or Warfarin
  -  Baseline level of pain ≥ 2  [OR] on 10 point scale
  -  Platelet count below 50,000 per ul on baseline complete blood count (CBC)
