Inclusion Criteria:
  -  preeclampsia
  -  healthy pregnancy
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  pregnant women with any systemic condition  [AND] such as chronic hypertension, diabetes mellitus, thyroid diseases, liver and kidney diseases
  -  women with a history of drug use  [OR] throughout pregnancy
  -  history of medication for PE treatment  [NOT] at the time of first admission
  -  patients who had fetal congenital abnormalities  [OR] genetic syndromes
  -  multiple gestation
  -  active labor
