Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Healthy women
  -  Age 18-50 years
  -  Gynecological procedures
  -  General anesthesia

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pre-existing hypertension
  -  Diabetes for several years
  -  Ischemic heart disease
  -  Cerebrovascular disease
  -  Heart valve disease
  -  Verified cardiac arrhythmia
  -  Anaemia
  -  Kidney or hepatic disease
  -  Hypersensitivity for soya, eggs  [OR] or peanuts
  -  Pregnancy
  -  Poor health state
  -  Illicit substance use
  -  BMI [NOT] <20  [AND] or >35 kg/m2
  -  SBP >150 mmHg
  -  HR >100 beats/min
