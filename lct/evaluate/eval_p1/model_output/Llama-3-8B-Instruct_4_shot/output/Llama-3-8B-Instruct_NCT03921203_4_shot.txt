Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Over 18 years.
  -  For the T2D study group: Diagnosed with type 2 diabetes.

Exclusion Criteria:
For subjects with type 2 diabetes:
  -  Systemic inflammatory disease that requires active treatment
  -  Thyroid disease that requires active treatment
  -  Skin disease that requires active treatment  [AND] causes significant changes in the area of the skin that is to be investigated
  -  Treatment with oral glucocorticoids
  -  Levels of SR, TSH, T4 or Hemoglobin in the blood pronouncedly  [NOT] within the reference range
  -  Pregnancy

For healthy controls:
  -  Registered disease in the health declaration that requires continuous systemic treatment, except well-controlled hypertension that does not require more than one antihypertensive drug, which is not an alpha-blocker, beta-blocker, or thiazide diuretic
  -  Skin disease that requires active treatment  [AND] causes significant changes in the area of the skin that is to be investigated
  -  Blood pressure pronouncedly above 140/90
  -  Blood test results pronouncedly  [NOT] within the reference range
  -  Pregnancy
