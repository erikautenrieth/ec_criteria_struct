Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients of both sexes with a diagnosis of stable CAD as follows: acute myocardial infarction  [OR] or percutaneous coronary revascularization  [OR] or coronary artery bypass graft surgery occurred at least 12 months before entering the study  [AND] or chronic stable angina.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Mental illness limiting the capacity of self-care  [OR] or any condition limiting seriously the life expectancy less than 12 months.
