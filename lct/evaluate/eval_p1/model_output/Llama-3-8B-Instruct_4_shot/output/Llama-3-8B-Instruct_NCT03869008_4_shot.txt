Inclusion Criteria:
• Clinical Diagnosis of Primary Raynaud's Phenomenon
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Less than 18 years of age
 - Pregnant women
 - Current smokers
 - Have Digital ulcers
 - Diagnosed Pulmonary hypertension
 - Currently on vasodilators [OR] (i.e. Phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitors, prostacyclin, nitroglycerine, or other nitric oxide derivatives) [NOT]
 - Currently on Calcium Channel Blockers
