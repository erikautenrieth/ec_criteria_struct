Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Male  [OR] and Female; 18 years of age  [AND] or older
  -  Confirmed diagnosis of Type 2 diabetes mellitus
  -  Willing to use CGM  [AND] and BGM
  -  A1c result ≥8.0%  [AND] and ≤12.0%
  -  Receive their diabetes care from Premier Medical Associates  [AND] or the Palo Alto Medical Foundation
  -  Own a Smartphone with a data plan  [AND] and be the primary user of that smartphone
  -  Smartphone must use a supported Android OS  [OR] or iOS
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pregnant  [OR] or breastfeeding
  -  Use of an insulin pump
  -  Any condition or situation  [NOT] that the Sponsor or Investigator determines as inappropriate for study inclusion
