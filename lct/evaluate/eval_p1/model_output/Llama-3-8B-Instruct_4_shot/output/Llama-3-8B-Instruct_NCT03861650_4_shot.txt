Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients free from any disease affecting bone formation [AND] of both sexes were included in the study.
  2. Patients younger than 25 years old.
  3. Patients included in the study were post traumatic patients, hemifacial microsomia, Treacher Collins [OR] and post ankylotic mandibular deformity.
  4. Distraction range was between 15-20 mm.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. The presence of uncontrolled systemic disease.
  2. Previous radiotherapy on head and neck region.
  3. Chemotherapy within the past 12-month period [NOT].
  4. An active infection at the distraction site.
