Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Presence of post-stroke aphasia
  -  Single left hemisphere stroke etiology
  -  At least 6 months post-stroke
  -  Age range between 18  [AND] and 80 years old
  -  Speak English as a native language
  -  Adequate hearing  [AND] and vision to complete the tasks
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Severe auditory comprehension deficits  [NOT] (determined by pretest) (i.e., global aphasia, Wernicke's aphasia, transcortical sensory aphasia)
  -  Inability to provide informed consent
  -  Co-occurring history of neurological disease/disorder/injury  [OR]  (e.g., traumatic brain injury, right hemisphere stroke, dementia)
  -  Co-occurring history of a major mental illness  [OR]  (e.g., schizophrenia, drug addiction, bipolar)
  -  Clinical conditions contraindicated for MRI or tDCS  [NOT] (e.g., implanted electrical devices, claustrophobia, seizure disorder)
  -  Positive pregnancy test  [NOT] (for females)
