Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Body mass index (BMI) equal or greater than 35 kg/m2.
  -  American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) physical status I  [OR] II  [OR] III patients.
  -  Scheduled to undergo laparoscopic roux-en-Y gastric bypass  [AND] or gastric sleeve placement surgery for weight loss.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Chronic obstructive pulmonary disorder (COPD)  [NOT] with pulmonary function test (PFT) showing forced expiratory volume in one second (FEV1) ≥ 50% predicted.
  -  Treatment with continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP) in the past three months.
  -  Severe neurological, cardiopulmonary, psychiatric, or untreated thyroid disorder.
  -  Chronic pain condition that was being treated with opioids.
  -  Patients with a hematocrit lower than 35%.
