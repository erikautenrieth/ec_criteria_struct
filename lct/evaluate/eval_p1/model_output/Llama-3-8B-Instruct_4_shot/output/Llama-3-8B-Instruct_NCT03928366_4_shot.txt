Inclusion Criteria:
 - Healthy adult volunteers (ASA 1 physical state) who agree to participate voluntarily in the study, previous information about it by the Principal Investigators (IP) [AND]
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Psychological, psychiatric or neurological disorders [OR] consumption of drugs
 - Alterations cutaneous or anatomical cranial [NOT] idiomatic or communication barrier
 - Allergy to propofol, remifentanil or to some of its excipients
 - Body mass index (BMI) <18 [OR] > 30 kg / m2
 - Pregnancy
 - Airway or ventilation criteria hard
 - Absence of accompanying adult at the end of the study
