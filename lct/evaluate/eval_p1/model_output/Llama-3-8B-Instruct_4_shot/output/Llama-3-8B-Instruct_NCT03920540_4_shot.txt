Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients diagnosed with Hunter syndrome
  -  Male at the age of ≥ 5
  -  Adequate abilities  [AND] including 6-MWT to participate in this study in the opinion of the investigator.
  -  Voluntarily signed written informed consent to participation in this study
  -  Consent to contraception
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Prior treatment with iduronate-2-sulfatase ERT
  -  History of bronchotomy, bone marrow transplantation, or cord blood transplantation  [NOT] known hypersensitivity reactions to any of the components of the investigational product
  -  Known hypersensitivity reactions to any of the components of the investigational product
  -  Prior or planned administration of other investigational products within 30 days before treatment with the investigational product in this study  [OR] during this study.
  -  Unable to perform 6-MWT.
  -  Female [NOT]
