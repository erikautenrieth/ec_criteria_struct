Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Older than 20 years of age
 2. Grade 2~3 trigger finger based on the Quinnell classification
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Previous treatment by physical therapy, local corticosteroid injection, or surgical release for trigger finger before the study [NOT]
 2. The presence of a musculoskeletal disease [OR] or previous nerve injuries at the upper extremities
 3. Multiple trigger finger [OR] local infection [OR] malignancy [OR] inflammatory arthritis [OR] cardiac arrhythmia [OR] cardiac pacemaker [OR] pregnancy
