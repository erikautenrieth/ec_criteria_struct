Inclusion Criteria:
  -  ASA I  [OR] or II physical status
  -  18-65 years
  -  Scheduled for elective colonoscopy
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History of allergy to any of the study drugs
  -  Patient has a history of alcohol  [OR] or drug abuse
  -  Second and third-degree heart block
  -  Morbid obesity
  -  Patient is  [NOT] pregnant and lactating women
  -  Patient has a history of psychiatric disorders
  -  Patient has severe cardiac, respiratory, renal, and liver diseases
