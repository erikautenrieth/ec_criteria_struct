Inclusion Criteria:
 - Volleyball players
 - Federated that at the time of the study compete in clubs of the Community of Madrid
 - Over 18 years old
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] Present a medical diagnosis of injury or illness
 - [NOT] Have suffered a knee injury in the 6 months prior to the study
 - [NOT] Doing some pharmacological or physiotherapy treatment external to the study
 - [NOT] Not signed the informed consent document
