Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Chronic Kidney Disease stage 1-5
  -  Hyperuricemic (UA >= 5.5 mg/dL)
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Contraindication to Allopurinol
  -  Elevated baseline liver function tests
  -  Receiving acute  [OR] or chronic dialysis
  -  Primary metabolic disorder
  -  [NOT] Sickle cell disease
  -  [NOT] Autosomal Dominant Polycystic Kidney Disease
  -  [NOT] Cystinosis
  -  [NOT] Bartter or Gitelman Disease
  -  Pregnant  [OR] or nursing
