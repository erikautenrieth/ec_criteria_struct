Inclusion Criteria:
  -  First time stroke
  -  Patients who have spasticity due to stroke
  -  At least 6 months after stroke
  -  Individuals between the ages of 18  [AND] and 75 will be included in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Any contraindication for dry needling (e.g., anticoagulants, infections, bleeding, etc.)  [NOT]
  -  Having diabetes
  -  Having cardiovascular diseases
  -  Having any other neurological problems
  -  Application of Botox within 6 months prior to the study
  -  Individuals included in another treatment program  [NOT] will not be included in the study
