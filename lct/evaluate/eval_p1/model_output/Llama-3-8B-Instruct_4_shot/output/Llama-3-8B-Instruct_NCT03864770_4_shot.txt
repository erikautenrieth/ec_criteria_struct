Inclusion Criteria:
 1. ≥ 20 y/o patients with NLCs: (1) 4 times/week, last 2 weeks; (2) Occurred in nighttime [OR] or resting time of daytime
 2. Patients with MTrPs on gastrocnemius (According to the diagnostic criteria proposed by Simons & Travell)
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. (1) Taking medication for leg cramps [NOT] (eg. Quinine, Magnesium oxide); (2) Other drugs that affect research and evaluation [NOT] (eg. diuretics, statins, calcium channel blockers, anticonvulsants)
 2. Congenital lower limb musculoskeletal diseases [OR] or Lower limb or spine surgery
 3. Uncommunicated [OR] or cognitive impaired
 4. Patients refused to be recruited
