Inclusion Criteria:
 1. pregnant women on their first visit to the hospital (regardless of gestational age);
 2. aged 18 [OR] above;
 3. agree to participate [AND] and sign an informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. systemic or topical vaginal antibiotics use within 2 weeks prior to the first perinatal visit;
 2. comorbidities that are known to be related to adverse pregnancy outcomes [NOT] (e.g., diabetes and hypertension);
 3. diagnosed adverse pregnancy outcomes at recruitment [NOT] (e.g., stillbirth);
 4. Women with a previous history of CT or NG [NOT] and women with a prior adverse outcome will not be excluded. Instead, the investigators will conduct sub-analysis and check whether the intervention has different effects on different groups of pregnant women.
