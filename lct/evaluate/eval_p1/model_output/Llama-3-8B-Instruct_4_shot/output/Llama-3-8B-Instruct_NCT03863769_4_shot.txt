Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age greater than 48 years old
 - Patient has leg or buttock pain while walking
 - Patient feels relief of symptoms with bending (flexing) forward
 - Patient feels relief when using a shopping cart or bicycle
 - Patient has motor or sensory disturbance while walking
 - Patient has normal and symmetrical foot pulses
 - Patient has lower extremity weakness
 - Patient has low back pain
 - Duration of symptoms and signs for more than 1 month
 - Patient is able to stand and walk without assistive devices for at least 20 minutes
 - Patient is able to give written informed consent and complete interviews and questionnaires in English
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Complicated spinal pain due to fracture, tumor, infection, or severe spondyloarthropathy
 - Patient has other severe co-morbidities, including progressive neurological deficit [NOT]
 - Patient has other serious pathologies of the locomotor system
 - Patient has no correspondence availability through email
 - Patient is unable to read/ comprehend English
 - Patient is seeking compensation from a third-party liability or workers' compensation
 - Patient has received previous spinal manipulation within the past month
