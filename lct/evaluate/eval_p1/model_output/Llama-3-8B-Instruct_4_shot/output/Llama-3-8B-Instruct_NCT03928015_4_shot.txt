Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Male [OR] or female, 18 years to 65 years old (inclusive)
 2. Index admission for traumatic injury
 3. High initial morphine equivalent use ≥ 90 mg [AND] in the first 24 hours from admission
 4. Baseline pain assessed at screening of ≥ 5 [AND] on the pain numeric rating score (NRS)
 5. Willing to divulge habitual marijuana usage (yes [OR] or no)
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients on a pain management agreement
 2. Patients who are nil per os (NPO) [AND] at the time of randomization or are expected to be NPO [AND] within the next 48 hours
 3. Patients who have received [OR] or are expected to receive neuraxial/locoregional blocks for pain within the next 48 hours
 4. Known allergy [OR] or previous hypersensitivity reaction to dronabinol or sesame oil
 5. Patients prescribed dronabinol between arrival [AND] prior to screening/randomization
 6. Pregnancy [OR] or breast feeding
 7. Incarceration
