Inclusion Criteria:
  -  All participants were included if their age  ≥ 18 years
  -  Body mass index < 30 kg/m2
  -  Total cholesterol < 200mg/dl
  -  Serum triglycerides < 200 mg/dl
  -  Random blood glucose level < 126 mg/dl

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Participants had acute  [OR] or chronic metabolic disorders (liver, kidney, thyroid, endocrine or gastrointestinal disorders)
  -  Females with irregular menstrual cycle  [OR] or on estrogen therapy
  -  Participants with organic cerebral lesion  [NOT] detected on cranial computed tomography (CT) or cranial magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)
