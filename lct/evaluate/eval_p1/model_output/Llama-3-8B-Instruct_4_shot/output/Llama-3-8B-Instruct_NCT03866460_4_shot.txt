Inclusion Criteria:
 - Signed informed consent indicating awareness of the investigational nature of the protocol.
 - Children with retinoblastoma (RB) aged 3 months [AND] and older who are scheduled to receive their first dose of IA carboplatin chemotherapy.
 - Retinoblastoma is rarely seen in children over 8 years old, however we will not include an upper age range [NOT] so as not to exclude any possible participants
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Children with known and documented preexisting hearing loss.
