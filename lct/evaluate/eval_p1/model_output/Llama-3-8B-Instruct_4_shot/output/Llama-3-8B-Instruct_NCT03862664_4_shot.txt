Inclusion Criteria:
 - Excellent general physical and mental condition,
 - Free of chronic disease [OR] previous major abdominal surgery,
 - Body mass index (BMI) <28,
 - ABO blood group identical to recipients [AND] and
 - Negative for hepatitis B, C and human immunodeficiency viruses (HIV).
Exclusion Criteria:
 - ASA physical status > II [NOT] II
 - BMI > 28
 - Psychiatric or mental retardation
 - pregnant [OR] and nursing women
 - Patients with significant laboratory abnormalities
