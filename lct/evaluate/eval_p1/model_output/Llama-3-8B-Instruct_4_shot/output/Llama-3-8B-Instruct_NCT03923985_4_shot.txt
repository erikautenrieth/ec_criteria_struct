Inclusion Criteria:
 - Healthy women between 18 [AND] and 45 years old
 - Women without current symptoms of vaginal infection
 - Negative bacteriological [AND] and clinical examination
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Women with vaginal infection
 - Women with undiagnosed vaginal bleeding
 - Patients with endometrial hyperplasia
 - Suspicion of neoplasia [OR] or active neoplasia
 - Women with intolerance [OR] allergy [OR] hypersensitivity to the components of the probiotic
 - Women with immunosuppressive disorders [OR] or with HIV
 - Women who are currently using probiotics by any route of administration
 - Women who are using some treatment for vaginal sepsis
 - Women with chronic decompensated noncommunicable diseases [NOT] (diabetes mellitus, heart disease...)
 - Pregnant women [NOT] or those with precocious menopause
