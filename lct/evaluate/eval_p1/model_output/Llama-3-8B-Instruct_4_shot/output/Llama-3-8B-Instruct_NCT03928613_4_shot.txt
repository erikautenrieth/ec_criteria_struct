Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Aged above 60
  -  Confirmed literacy
  -  Diagnosed with mild cognitive impairment by International Working Group on Mild Cognitive Impairment  [AND] and Clinical Dementia Rating (CDR) of 0  [OR] or 0.5
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Evidence of delirium  [OR] or confusion
  -  Any neurological conditions causing cognitive decline such as Parkinson's disease  [OR] or brain hemorrhage  [OR] or brain tumor  [OR] or normal pressure hydrocephalus
  -  Evidence of severe cerebrovascular pathology
  -  Presence of depressive symptoms that could influence cognitive function  [NOT]
  -  Presence of medical comorbidities that could result in any difficulties in study participation
