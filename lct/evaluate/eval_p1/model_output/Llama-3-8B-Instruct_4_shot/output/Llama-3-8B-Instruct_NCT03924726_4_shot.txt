Inclusion Criteria:
 - Aged 18 years [AND] and above at the start of the treatment.
 - Molar relationship either Class I, < ½ unit Class II [OR] or Class III.
 - Extraction of all four first premolar teeth [AND] as part of orthodontic treatment.
 - Maximum anchorage required using mini-implant.
 - No systemic disease [NOT] requiring long-term antibiotic use, phenytoin, cyclosporin, anti-inflammatory drugs, bisphosphonates, systemic corticosteroids or calcium channel blockers.
 - Good oral hygiene.
 - No periodontal disease.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Significant vertical skeletal discrepancies.
 - Systemic diseases [OR] requiring long-term antibiotic use, phenytoin, cyclosporin, anti-inflammatory drugs, bisphosphonates, systemic corticosteroids or calcium channel blockers.
 - Poor oral hygiene [NOT] good oral hygiene.
 - Active periodontal disease.
