Inclusion Criteria:
  -  patients > 18 years old, undergoing elective hepatobiliary surgery under general anesthesia [AND] American Society of Anesthesiologists grade II.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  1-patient refusal
  -  2-psychiatric disorders
  -  3-pregnancy [AND] lactation
  -  4-preexisting neurological dysfunction (history of cerebrovascular stroke [NOT] CVS)
  -  5-metastatic cancer
  -  6-Inflammatory bowel disease
  -  7-Diabetes mellitus
  -  8-Renal insufficiency (serum creatinine level more than 180 μmol/l)
  -  9- unexpected intraoperative findings (inoperable) [OR] 10-accidental massive intraoperative hemorrhage
