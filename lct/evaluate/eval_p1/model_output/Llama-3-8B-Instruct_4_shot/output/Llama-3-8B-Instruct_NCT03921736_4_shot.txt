Inclusion Criteria:
 - Treatment-naive postmenopausal women
 - Diagnosed hypertension [AND] (grade 1 [OR] or 2 according to ESH/ESC 2013 guidelines)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - breast cancer [NOT] in a first-degree relative
 - hyperplasia diagnosed by endometrial biopsy,
 - history of thromboembolic diseases,
 - current or history of use of estrogen-progestin therapy or contraceptives
 - diabetes,
 - kidney failure,
 - thyroid disease,
 - heart and other chronic diseases [NOT] (secondary hypertension, atrial fibrillation)
