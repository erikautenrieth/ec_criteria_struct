Inclusion Criteria:
 - Unilateral and/or bilateral conductive hearing loss (CHL)
 - Conductive hearing loss >10 decibel on average
 - Subjective benefit from the device
 - Subjects aged 13 years [AND] or older
 - Capable of the German language
 - Willingness [AND] and ability to perform all tests required for the study
 - Signed, and dated informed consent before the start of any study specific procedure
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding
 - Patient uses a hearing aid [OR] or plans to acquire a hearing aid
 - Patient is intolerant of the materials as described by Manufacturer
 - Patient presents with a skin or scalp condition that [NOT] may preclude the attachment of the adhesive adapter
 - Patient cannot perform the audiological tests [OR] or is unable to fill out the questionnaires
 - Patient presents with retrocochlear, or central auditory disorder
 - any physical, psychological, or emotional disorder that would [NOT] interfere with the ability to perform on test and rehabilitation procedures
