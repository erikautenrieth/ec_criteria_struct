Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Male  [OR] Female
  -  Between 18  [AND] and 80 years
  -  ASA 1-3
  -  Scheduled for open thoracotomy

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  ASA >3
  -  Morbid obesity BMI >40
  -  Previous cardiac  [OR] or ipsilateral thoracic surgery
  -  Renal  [OR] or hepatic failure
  -  Anemia
  -  Allergy to local anesthetics
  -  Contraindications to receive regional anesthesia
  -  Patient refusal
  -  Reiteration due to complication
