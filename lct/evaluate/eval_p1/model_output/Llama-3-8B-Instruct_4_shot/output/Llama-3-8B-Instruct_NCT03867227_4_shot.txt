Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient with paroxysmal/persistent/permanent atrial fibrillation
 - CHA2DS2VASc score ≥2 [AND] for male and ≥3 [AND] for female
 - Without [NOT] contraindications to anticoagulation therapy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - LAA thrombosis
 - mechanical prosthetic heart valve
 - moderate [OR] or severe mitral valve stenosis
