Inclusion Criteria:
 - Intact cognition
 - Poor sleep quality
 - Self-reported presence of pain
 - Capacity for mild to moderate physical activity
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Non-ambulatory [OR] or have severely impaired mobility
 - Visual [OR] or hearing impairment that interfered with assessments
 - An acute [NOT] or a terminal illness
 - Neuropathic pain
