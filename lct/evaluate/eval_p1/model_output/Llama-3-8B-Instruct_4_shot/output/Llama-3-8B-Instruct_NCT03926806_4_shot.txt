Inclusion Criteria:
 - BMI 27-36
 - HbA1C < 8.5%
 - Constant body weight [AND] and dietary habits for the last 3 months
 - Stable dose of oral medication [AND] for at least 3 months
 - Diagnosed with T2DM [AND] for more than 1 year
Exclusion Criteria:
 - History of cardiovascular, gastrointestinal, renal [OR] or endocrinological diseases
 - Treatment for weight reduction
 - Lactose intolerance
 - Allergies to milk [OR] or dairy products
 - Alcohol consumption > 2 drinks per day
 - Intake of supplements
 - Receiving insulin
