Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients 18 years of age  [OR] or older
  2. Patients screened at risk of malnutrition by Canadian Nutrition Screening Tool (CNST) and identified as malnourished by subjective global assessment (SGA) B  [AND] or C
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients undergoing minor  [OR] or laparoscopic surgery
  2. Pregnancy
  3. Patients with severe systemic diseases defined by American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) classification [NOT] III to V
  4. Patients with diabetes mellitus
  5. Patients with planned palliative treatment
