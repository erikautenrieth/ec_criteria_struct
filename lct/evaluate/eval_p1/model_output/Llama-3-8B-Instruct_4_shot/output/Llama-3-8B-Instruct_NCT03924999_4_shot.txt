Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Subject diagnosed with lipedema according to the criteria of revised Wold.
  -  Subject did not participate in any exercise program within the last three months [AND].
  -  No pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History of acute infection, cancer, inflammatory rheumatic [OR] connective tissue diseases
  -  History of cardiovascular [OR] musculoskeletal problems that may prevent them from participating in the exercise program [NOT].
