Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients who underwent fibula free flap for mandibular reconstruction
  -  Patient ≥18 years old
  -  patient alive  [AND] 6 months after completion of treatment
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient without post-operative scan evaluation
  -  Patient with a [NOT] chirurgical contraindication
  -  Pregnant  [OR] or nursing woman
  -  Adult requiring protection (guardianship, supervision)
