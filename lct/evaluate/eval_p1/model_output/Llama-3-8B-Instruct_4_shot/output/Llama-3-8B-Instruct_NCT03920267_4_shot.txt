Inclusion Criteria:
 - Completion of SLE Study (NCT03252587) through the protocol-required treatment period, [AND] currently receiving blinded study drug
 - Women of childbearing potential (WOCBP) must have a negative urine pregnancy test [AND] must agree to use correctly a highly effective method(s) of contraception for the duration of treatment with study drug

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Any disease or medical condition that, in the opinion of the investigator, would make the subject [NOT] unsuitable for this study, would interfere with the interpretation of subject safety or study results, or considered unsuitable by the investigator for any other reason
 - Evidence of active tuberculosis (TB)

Note: There are no logical operators needed in the exclusion criteria, as the conditions are stated as "Any disease or medical condition" and "Evidence of active tuberculosis (TB)", which are straightforward exclusions.
