Here is the modified eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], and [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patient [AND] ≥ 7 years old
  - Patient affiliated to a social security system
  - Patient giving their informed consent for the study [AND] (adults, parents of minors)
  - Patients with PAH associated with congenital heart disease requiring cardiac catheterization [AND] 2D MRI for the diagnosis [OR] follow-up of PAH associated with congenital heart disease

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Patient ≥ 7 years old
  - Pregnant woman
  - Contraindications to MRI [AND] (claustrophobia, intraocular metal foreign bodies, pacemaker, neurostimulator, cochlear implants, old heart valves)
  - Contraindication to gadolinium injection [NOT] (allergy, renal failure with DFG < 30 ml/min/1.73m²)
  - [NOT] Hemodynamically stable patients
  - Refusal to participate
