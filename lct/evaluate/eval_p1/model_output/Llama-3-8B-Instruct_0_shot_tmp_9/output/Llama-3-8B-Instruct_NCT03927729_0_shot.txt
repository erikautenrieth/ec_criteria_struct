Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Conscious patient [AND] age ≥ 18 years
  - Acute pain of monotraumatic origin
  - Pain > 4 on a visual numerical scale

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] state of shock with unstable hemodynamics (PA <90/60) [AND] suspected or proven trauma to the chest, abdomen or pelvis
  - Serious head trauma
  - Consciousness disorder with Glasgow score <15
  - Patient who has already received analgesics [NOT] with the exception of paracetamol
  - Patient receiving an intravenous approach for analgesia
  - Renal or hepatic disorders known
  - Hypersensitivity to fluoridated anesthetics [OR] history of malignant hyperthermia in the patient or family
  - Pregnant or nursing woman
  - Patient under judicial protection
  - [NOT] non-communicating patient or with difficulties of understanding

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Intravenous injection for analgesia
