* [OR] between "trauma" and "fracture" in the exclusion criteria to indicate a logical disjunction between the two conditions.
* [NOT] before "trauma" and "fracture" in the exclusion criteria to indicate a logical negation, negating the presence of trauma or fracture as exclusion criteria.

Note that I did not insert [NOT] before "and/or" in the inclusion criteria, as it is not necessary to negate the presence of oral and/or maxillofacial swelling as inclusion criteria.
