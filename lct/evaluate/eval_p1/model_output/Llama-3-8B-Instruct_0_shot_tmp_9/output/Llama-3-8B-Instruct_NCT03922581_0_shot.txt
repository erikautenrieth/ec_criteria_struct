Here is the modified eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - ability to provide informed consent [AND]
  - willingness to participate in study [AND]
  - self-identity as African American [AND]
  - history of childhood [AND] adult trauma exposure (3 total criterion A traumas) [OR]
  - positive PTSD screen (PC-PTSD ≥ 3) [AND]
  - positive MDD screen (PHQ-9 ≥ 5)

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] presence of intellectual disability, bipolar, or psychotic disorder [OR]
  - presence of current substance use disorder (past 1 month; see cut off scores in psychological measures below) [AND]
  - active suicidality

Note: I inserted [AND] to connect the inclusion criteria that must be met together, and [OR] to connect the history of trauma exposure criteria that can be met alternatively. I also inserted [NOT] to indicate the negation of the presence of intellectual disability, bipolar, or psychotic disorder.
