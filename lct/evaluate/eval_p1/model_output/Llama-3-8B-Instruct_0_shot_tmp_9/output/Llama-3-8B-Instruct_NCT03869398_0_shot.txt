Inclusion Criteria:
  - All patients [AND] age >18 [AND] undergoing total thyroidectomy are eligible.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] partial thyroidectomy [OR] lobectomy [OR] concurrent parathyroidectomy.

Note that I inserted the operators in the following way:

* In the inclusion criteria, I used [AND] to connect the two conditions "age >18" and "undergoing total thyroidectomy", as they must both be true for a patient to be eligible.
* In the exclusion criteria, I used [NOT] to indicate that the listed procedures are excluded, rather than included. I then used [OR] to connect the three procedures, as a patient would be excluded if they underwent any one of these procedures.
