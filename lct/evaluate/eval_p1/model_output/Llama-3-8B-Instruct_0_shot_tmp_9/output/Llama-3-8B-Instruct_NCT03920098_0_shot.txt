Inclusion Criteria:
  - Primipara [AND] (no previous pregnancies)
  - 18 to 25 years of age
  - Gestation <15 weeks at first prenatal visit
  - No intention to move outside the city of Santiago [NOT] in the next two years

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Multipara
  - [NOT] Pre-existing cancer [except NOT non-melanoma skin cancer]
  - Family history of breast cancer [AND] (one first-degree female relative diagnosed with breast cancer)
  - High-risk pregnancy according to national guidelines
  - During the follow-up, [NOT] pregnant women under any treatment that may affect the levels of metabolic markers will be excluded from the analysis
  - Pregnant women with [OR] pre-eclampsia [OR] with preterm birth (less than 37 weeks of gestational age) will be also excluded
