Inclusion Criteria:
  - Collaborative ICU patients with length of stay > 48h [AND] patients who meet other inclusion criteria
  - Surgical patients scheduled for colorectal surgery [AND] who are undergoing elective surgery
  - Healthy subjects [NOT] with any comorbidities

Exclusion Criteria:
  - a score [NOT] equal to 1 on the Richmond Agitation and Sedation Scale (RASS) [OR] a score < -1 on the Richmond Agitation and Sedation Scale (RASS)
  - coma [AND] patients who are unconscious or unresponsive
  - total hip or knee arthroplasty in the dominant limb [AND] patients who have undergone previous surgery on the same limb
  - unauthorized support on the dominant leg [AND] patients who are using assistive devices
  - open wound located at the ankle's anterior face of the dominant leg [AND] patients with wounds that are not healing properly
  - pre-existing myopathy or polyneuropathy [AND] patients who have a history of neuromuscular disease
  - para- or tetraparesis, para- or tetraplegia [AND] patients with severe motor impairments
  - refusal [AND] patients who are unable or unwilling to participate in the study
