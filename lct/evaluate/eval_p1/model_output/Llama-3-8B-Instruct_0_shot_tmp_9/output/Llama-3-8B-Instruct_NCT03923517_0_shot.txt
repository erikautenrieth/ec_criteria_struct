Here is the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  informal carer of a person with YOD who has been diagnosed with either [OR] Frontal Temporal Dementia (FTD) or Alzheimer's Disease (AD) 12 months ago or less;
  -  the carer has face-to-face contact with the person with YOD [AND] at least twice a week;
  -  the carer has a working computer [AND] internet access in his home;
  -  the carer is available for the intervention duration [AND] approximately eight weeks;
  -  the carer is fluent in German [AND] signed consent form.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] the carer has face-to-face contact with the person with YOD less than twice a week;
  -  the person with YOD the carer is caring for has a different dementia cause [NOT] other than FTD or AD or the diagnosis is older than 12 months;
  -  the carer does not have a working computer [OR] internet access at home or is not confident to use them;
  -  the carer is not available for the approximately eight week intervention duration;
  -  the carer is not fluent in German;
  -  the carer has a significant health problem [OR] disability, which precludes him/her from participating in the study;
  - [NOT] no signed informed consent.

Note: I inserted the logical operators [OR] where the criteria are alternatives, [AND] where the criteria are conjunctive, and [NOT] where the criteria are negations. I did not insert [NOT] for "no signed informed consent" as it is not a negation, but rather the absence of something.
