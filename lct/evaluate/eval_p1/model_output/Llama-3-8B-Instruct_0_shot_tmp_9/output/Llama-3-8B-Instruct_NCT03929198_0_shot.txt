Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Dysfunctional lipids, blood pressure and glycemic control determined at a screening visit:
       -  Triglycerides > 150 mg/dL [OR] HDL <50 [AND] in females, <40 mg/dL [AND] in males
       -  Systolic blood pressure ≥130 mmHg [OR] diastolic blood pressure ≥80 mmHg [AND] (or on hypertension medication)
       -  HbA1c ≥5.7% [OR] higher
  -  [NOT] diagnosis or treatment for cancer in the past year
  -  [NOT] diagnosis of mild cognitive impairment [OR] dementia in the past year
  -  Ability to perform daily exercise, including aerobic activity [AND] resistance exercise
  -  Non-smoker [AND] in the past year

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Positive exercise stress test
