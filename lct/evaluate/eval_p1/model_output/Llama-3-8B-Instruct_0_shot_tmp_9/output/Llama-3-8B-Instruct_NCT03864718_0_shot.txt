Here is the modified eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], and [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - complex anal fistula according to Parks classification [AND] 
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Non cryptoglandular fistulas
  - acute sepsis
  - inflammatory bowel diseases
  - [NOT] simple fistulas (defined as subcutaneous, intersphincteric and low transsphincteric fistula)
  - [NOT] pregnant patients
  - ASA IV
  - patients <18
  - immunocompromised
  - patients who refused consent

Note: The [AND] operator is inserted between the inclusion criteria, as it is not possible to combine exclusion criteria with the inclusion criteria. The [NOT] operator is inserted before the exclusion criteria that are negations, as per the instructions.
