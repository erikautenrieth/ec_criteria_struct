Here is the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Diagnosis of Alzheimer's Dementia according to NIA-AA Criteria for dementia [55] [AND] NIA-AA Criteria for dementia
  2. MMSE < 15
  3. Cohen-Mansfield Agitation Inventory Short Version (CMAI) score of [NOT] ≥4 on at least one item, [OR] a score of 3 on at least two items, [OR] a score of 2 on at least three items.
  4. [NOT] At least one failed pharmacological intervention to manage behavioral symptoms
  5. Medically stable for safe administration of ECT verified by standard physical examination, urinalysis and serum chemistries [AND] standard physical examination, urinalysis and serum chemistries
  6. Comprehension of English language [AND] English language
  7. Authorized legal representative able [AND] willing to give informed consent
  8. Age [NOT] 55 - 89 years old (inclusive)

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] Current diagnosis of co-morbid delirium, measured by the Confusion Assessment Measure (CAM) [AND] clinical diagnosis
  2. Diagnosis of [NOT] Non-AD Dementia
  3. Lifetime [OR] current diagnosis of Schizophrenia, Bipolar Disorder [OR] Schizoaffective Disorder
  4. [NOT] Current episode of Major Depressive Disorder (MDD)
  5. [NOT] Active substance use disorder within past 6 months
  6. Treatment with ECT [OR] other neurostimulation therapies (e.g., TMS or vagal nerve stimulation) within the past 3 months
