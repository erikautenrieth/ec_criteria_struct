Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patient has provided written informed consent [AND] patient is 18 years or older.
  - Patient is a planned recipient of a living donor kidney transplant [AND] patient is a planned recipient of an ABO blood group-compatible kidney graft.
  - Patient is a planned recipient of a kidney graft from a donor that is not HLA (human leukocyte antigen)-identical [AND] patient is negative for DSA (donor-specific antibodies).
  - Women of child-bearing potential (WOCBP) must have a negative pregnancy test at inclusion [AND] WOCBP must be using an adequate method of contraception to avoid pregnancy throughout the study and for up to 12 weeks after the study in such a manner that the risk of pregnancy is minimized.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] patient is EBV (epstein barr virus)-negative on serology.
  - Patient is HIV-positive [OR] suffering from chronic viral hepatitis.
  - Patient is CMV (cytomegalo virus)-negative [AND] receiving a kidney from a CMV-positive donor.
  - Patient has a positive T-cell lymphocytotoxic cross match.
  - Patient has prior kidney transplant or non-renal solid organ transplant.
  - Patient has a known contraindication to any of the protocol-specified treatments.
  - Patient has been diagnosed with a malignancy within 5 years prior to study entry, excluding non-metastatic basal or squamous cell carcinoma of the skin [NOT].
  - Female patients are breast-feeding [OR] female patients have a positive pregnancy test.
