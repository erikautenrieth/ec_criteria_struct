Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients who fulfilled the following criteria were included in the study:
  -  Adult renal transplant patients [AND] (≥21 years),
  -  Informed consent,
  -  Stable maintenance subcutaneous ESA therapy with constant dose interval during the last 2 months,
  -  Haemoglobin (Hb) ≥10 mg/dL [AND] Haemoglobin (Hct) 32%,
  -  Transferrin saturation (TSAT) ≥20% [AND] during the screening phase,
  -  [AND] serum ferritin ≥100 ng/mL

Exclusion Criteria:
We excluded patients with the following criteria:
  -  [NOT] patients with Acute or chronic bleeding, [OR] erythrocyte transfusion, within the preceding 8 weeks [AND] change in Hb level ≥ 2 g/dL during screening phase,
  -  Hemolytic anemia,
  -  [NOT] patients with Recent infection or rejection,
  -  Diastolic blood pressure >100 mmHg [OR] discontinuation of ESA due to hypertension in the 6 months prior to study,
  -  Vitamin B12 or folic acid deficiency,
  -  [NOT] patients with Uncontrolled or secondary hyperparathyroidism,
  -  Acute or chronic systemic inflammatory disease [AND]/OR C-reactive protein (CRP) >30 mg/L,
  -  Hemodialysis due to failure of a kidney transplant,
  -  [AND] malignancy
