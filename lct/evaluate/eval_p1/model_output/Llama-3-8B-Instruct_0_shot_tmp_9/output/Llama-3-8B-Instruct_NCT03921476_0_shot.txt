Here is the modified eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
Female subjects [AND] between 18 and 65 years of age [AND] currently taking spironolactone for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris [OR] Female subjects [AND] between 18 and 65 years of age [AND] currently taking oral antibiotics for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris [AND] Subjects [AND] able to complete an online survey in English

Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects [NOT] less than 18 years of age [OR] older than 65 years of age Subjects [NOT] that have ever been diagnosed with any mood disorder by a clinician prior to starting spironolactone (history of mood disorder [OR] current mood disorder) Subjects taking hormonal contraception (including but not limited to oral contraceptive pills) [NOT] for a duration of less than 6 months Subjects [NOT] that have ever been diagnosed with any type of sexual dysfunction prior to starting spironolactone Subjects [NOT] able to complete an online survey in English Subjects taking oral isotretinoin [NOT] for a diagnosis of acne Subjects [NOT] that are currently pregnant
