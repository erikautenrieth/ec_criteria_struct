Inclusion Criteria:
  1. TNM stage I-II [AND] T1-4N0M0 colorectal cancer cases
  2. receive radical surgical resection [AND] have completed data of tumor location, histological type, behavioral characteristics [OR] TNM staging
  3. have tumor specimens [AND] either a valid microsatellite instability (MSI) [OR] immuno-histochemistry (IHC) data
  4. have valid V-raf murine sarcoma viral oncogene homolog B1 (BRAF), kirsten rat sarcoma viral oncogene (KRAS), CpG island methylator phenotype (CIMP) results [NOT] having incomplete or invalid results
  5. have at least 4 years of follow-up [AND] have valid time to local recurrence/metastasis in follow-up
  6. have clinical/treatment record data [AND] valid preoperative status of intestinal obstruction or perforation (IOP), counts of lymph node removed in surgical resection

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] having a previous diagnosis of any cancer or presence of any tumor other than the CRC
  2. have had inflammatory bowel disease
  3. have had hereditary colorectal cancer syndromes, including [NOT] Familial adenomatous polyposis, mutyh (MYH)-associated polyposis, Peutz-Jeghers syndrome, Juvenile polyposis coli, phosphate and tension homology deleted on chromosome ten (PTEN) tumor-hamartoma syndromes, Lynch Syndrome, and Familial Colorectal Cancer Type X
