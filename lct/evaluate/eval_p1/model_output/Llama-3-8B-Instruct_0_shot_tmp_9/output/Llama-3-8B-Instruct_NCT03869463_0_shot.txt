Here is the modified eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Age 18 [AND] 29.
  2. Current diagnosis of MDD/persistent depressive disorder [OR] HDRS greater than or equal to 12.
  3. Daily access to smartphone or tablet with internet connection for the study duration.
  4. Willing [AND] able to complete MRI, mood, and neuropsychological testing.
  5. IQ > 85.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] English-speaking ability as determined by self-report and clinical evaluation.
  2. Evidence of schizophrenia [OR] schizoaffective disorder [OR] psychosis [OR] bipolar I and II disorder.
  3. Active suicidal ideation [OR] intent [OR] plan [OR] past attempt within 1 year.
  4. Severe depression (HDRS > 30).
  5. Neurological disorder [AND] (epilepsy [OR] multiple sclerosis [OR] traumatic brain injury [OR] clinical stroke).
  6. Use of medications known to have negative impact on cognition [AND] (benzodiazepines [AND] lorazepam equivalents > 1mg daily [OR] narcotics [OR] anticholinergics).
  7. History of alcohol or drug abuse [OR] dependence within past year.
  8. Acute [AND] severe [AND] unstable medical illness. For cancer, acutely ill patients [NOT] (including those with metastases) are excluded, but past history of successfully treated cancer does not result in exclusion.
  9. Regular online brain training [OR] regular player of non-fluency verbal games, defined as doing these procedures at a frequency of twice weekly or greater during the year prior to screening.

Note that I did not insert the logical operators at the end of sentences, but instead used them to separate the conditions within each sentence. I also avoided inserting them before line segments (\n).
