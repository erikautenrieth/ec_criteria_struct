Inclusion Criteria:
  - Age: > 8 [AND] 40 years (we selected this range due to lack of data in younger population and the difficulty in getting patient reported outcomes in younger patients)
  - Diagnosis of RDEB [AND] (by a dermatologist [OR] EB specialist [OR] genetic confirmation)
  - Evidence of neuropathy defined by: thermal sensory loss (determined by a thermal roller, ROLLTEMP2, Sometic, Sweden) [AND] > 4/10 score using a screening tool for neuropathic pain, the DN4 questionnaire [15]
  - Pain intensity of > 4/10 on a 0-10 VAS scale measured daily [AND] (reduced frequency is also acceptable) at night over 2 weeks
  - Itch intensity of > 4/10 on a 0-10 VAS scale measured daily [AND] (reduced frequency is also acceptable) at night over 2 weeks
  - Consent to follow with study procedures [NOT] NO [NOT] patient reported outcomes in younger patients

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Intolerance [AND/OR] allergy to Pregabalin or gabapentin
  - Lactose intolerance [NOT] none
  - Pregabalin use within 2 weeks before study enrolment [NOT] NO
  - Ongoing treatment with gabapentin, amitriptyline, duloxetine, nortriptyline, other tricyclics or SNRIs
  - Medical conditions that would be considered as contraindications for pregabalin treatment [NOT] no ischemic heart disease, cardiac dysrhythmia, glaucoma, history of urinary retention
  - Pregnancy [NOT] no
  - History of use of restrictive substances or alcohol abuse [NOT] none
  - Allergy to gelatin [NOT] none
