Inclusion
  - Age > 18 years [AND] Unilateral fracture of distal radius without misalignment (dorsal angulation <10°, axial radial shortening <5mm) [AND] <2mm intraarticular stepoff;
  - Independent in activities of daily living [AND] Capable and willing of signing informed consent.
Exclusion
  - [NOT] Children under the age of 18;
  - Fracture of the contralateral arm [OR] Other fractures of the ipsilateral arm;
  - Open fractures [OR] Fractures that require reduction.

Note: I inserted the logical operators to separate the conditions that must be met together (conjunction) and those that can be met alternatively (disjunction). I also inserted the negation operator [NOT] to indicate the exclusion criteria.
