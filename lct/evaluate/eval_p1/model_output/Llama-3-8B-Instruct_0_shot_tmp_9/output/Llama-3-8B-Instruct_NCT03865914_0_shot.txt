Inclusion Criteria:
  - Age ≥ 18 [AND] male [OR] female
  - Clinical diagnosed as type 2 diabetes mellitus
  - The presence of renal impairment including: microalbuminuria [OR] overt proteinuria [OR] renal insufficiency

  - Obtaining the signed informed consent from patients

Exclusion Criteria:
  - The history of the disease was [NOT] complete
  - Clinical diagnosed as [NOT] other secondary renal diseases
  - Patients with hereditary kidney disease
  - Autoimmune diseases
  - Patients with malignant tumor were expected to survive [NOT] less than 6 months
  - Pregnancy [AND] lactation

Note that I inserted the operators in the following way:

* In the Inclusion Criteria, I used [AND] to connect the age and gender criteria, and [OR] to connect the different types of renal impairment.
* In the Exclusion Criteria, I used [NOT] to negate the completeness of the disease history, the presence of other secondary renal diseases, and the expected survival time of patients with malignant tumors. I also used [AND] to connect pregnancy and lactation as exclusion criteria.
