Here is the eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Subject has a diagnosis of MDD [AND] symptoms that have been present for at least a 4-week period, as diagnosed by SCID-5-CT.
  2. Subject is in good physical health [AND] has no clinically significant findings [NOT] determined by the Investigator on physical examination, 12-lead ECG, or clinical laboratory tests.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Subject has attempted suicide [NOT] associated with the current episode of MDD.
  2. Subject has a medical history of [OR] bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, and/or schizoaffective disorder.
  3. Subject has had [NOT] vagus nerve stimulation, electroconvulsive therapy, or has taken ketamine (including esketamine) within the current major depressive episode.

Note: I inserted [AND] to represent a logical conjunction between the diagnosis of MDD and the presence of symptoms, and between being in good physical health and having no clinically significant findings. I inserted [OR] to represent a logical disjunction between the medical history of bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, and schizoaffective disorder. I inserted [NOT] to represent logical negation for the attempted suicide and medical history conditions.
