Inclusion Criteria:
  - patients presenting a multi-bullous pemphigoid [AND] newly diagnosed [OR] relapsed more than 3 months after stopping corticosteroids [AND] treated according to the national protocol for diagnosis and care issued by the reference center for autoimmune bullous diseases of April 2016
  - patients having received [NOT] oral information [AND] signed informed consent [AND] written information
  - patients covered by [NOT] national health insurance

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [NOT] under tutorship [OR] curatorship [OR] unable to give informed consent
  - Patients receiving [NOT] an anti-osteoporotic treatment
  - Patients requiring [NOT] an anti-osteoporotic baseline treatment [AND] (T-score ⩽ -3DS on at least 1 site [OR] FRAX score above the therapeutic intervention threshold)
  - Patients having one or more [NOT] major risk factors for osteoporosis
  - Patients who have received [NOT] topical corticosteroids in less than 3 months
