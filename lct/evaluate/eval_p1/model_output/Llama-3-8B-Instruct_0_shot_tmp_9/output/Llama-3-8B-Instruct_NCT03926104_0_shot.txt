Here is the modified eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Ability to provide an informed consent [AND]
  - Age >18 years old [AND]
  - Elective abdominal aortic aneurysm surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Emergency surgery
  - Age < 18 years old
  - Creatinine > 2mg/dl
  - [OR] Diseases of ascending aorta, aortic arch or thoracic aorta
  - Chronic atrial fibrillation
  - Patient refusal to provide [NOT] informed consent
