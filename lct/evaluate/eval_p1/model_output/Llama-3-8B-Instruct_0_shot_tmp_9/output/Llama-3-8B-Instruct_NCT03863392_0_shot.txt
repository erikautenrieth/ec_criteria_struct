Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], and [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Primigravida [OR] Primipara
  - Pregnancy between 36 [AND] 42 weeks of gestation.
  - A live singleton fetus in cephalic presentation [AND] no history of cephalic malpresentation.
  - No history of [NOT] uterine surgery.
  - Clinically adequate pelvis [AND] no history of pelvic abnormalities.
  - Modified Bishop's score [LESS THAN] 5.
  - Reactive Non-stress test (NST) [AND] no history of NST non-reactivity.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] known hypersensitivity or contraindications to oral misoprostol (uterine surgery) [OR] any other uterine surgery.
  - Any antenatal medical complications [AND] any fetal complications.
  - A situation requiring [NOT] LSCS (Maternal or Fetal) e.g. fetal distress [OR] maternal distress.
  - Non-reactive Non-stress test (NST) [AND] no history of NST reactivity.
  - Patient's [NOT] refusal to give consent.

Note: I did not insert [AND] between "uterine surgery" and "contraindications" in the exclusion criteria because it is not a logical conjunction. Similarly, I did not insert [AND] between "Non-stress test (NST)" and "reactivity" in the exclusion criteria because it is not a logical conjunction.
