Here is the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Diagnosis of periodontitis [AND] (stages III [OR] IV) that may require periodontal surgery
  - Have at least 10 teeth in function, [NOT] excluding third molars.
  - Present locations with probing depth (PS) > 6 mm [AND] in at least 30% of the teeth.
  - Present radiographic evidence of moderate-severe bone loss [AND] in at least 30% of the dentition.
  - Detection of P. gingivalis [AND] in subgingival samples taken at the screening visit [AND] in the post-scaling and root planning visit [AND] processed by culture.
  - Systemically [NOT] healthy patients.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Pregnant [OR] lactating women.
  - Presenting systemic pathology [AND] / [OR] taking medication that may affect the periodontal situation [AND] / [OR] patients requiring antibiotic prophylaxis.
  - Have received systemic antimicrobial treatment [NOT] in the previous 6 months.
  - Have received periodontal treatment [NOT] in the 6 months prior to the beginning of the study.
  - Patients [NOT] allergic to metronidazole [OR] to any of the components of commercial formulations thereof (Flagyl®).
  - Patients [NOT] allergic to azithromycin [OR] to any of the components of commercial formulations thereof (Zithromax®).
  - Patients who [NOT] refuse to sign the informed consent.
