Inclusion Criteria:
  - Diagnosis of ALS [OR] PLS
  - Age greater than 18 years [AND] no upper age limit
  - Clinically significant spasticity [AND] symptoms must be moderate to severe

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Individuals [NOT] currently taking carbidopa-levodopa [OR] with known hypersensitivity of any component of carbidopa-levodopa
  - Individuals with narrow-angle glaucoma [AND] uncontrolled glaucoma
  - Individuals currently taking a non-selective monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI)
  - Individuals with a history of malignant melanoma [OR] suspicious skin lesions
  - Individuals with a history of depression [OR] suicidal ideation [OR] psychosis
  - Individuals with a history of myocardial infarction [OR] ventricular arrhythmia [OR] severe cardiopulmonary disease
  - Individuals with uncontrolled hypertension [AND] hypertension that is not well-controlled with medication
  - Individuals with asthma [AND] chronic asthma
  - Individuals with renal disease [AND] chronic kidney disease
  - Individuals with hepatic disease [AND] liver disease
  - Individuals with endocrine disease [AND] chronic endocrine disorder
  - Individuals with a history of peptic ulcer [AND] ongoing peptic ulcer disease
  - Individuals who are pregnant [AND] breastfeeding
  - Individuals currently participating in another interventional study
