Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
Men [AND] women aged 20 [AND] 80
  - MDD diagnosis objected to MRI (grade II/III) [AND] MDD diagnosis confirmed by a psychiatrist
  - Joint pain for more than 3 months [AND] absence of any other joint pain condition
  - Absence of bone lesion [NOT] or moderate knee osteoarthritis stage <2 on the Kellgren Laurence scale (objectified on radiography in schuss) in the 6 months prior to inclusion
  - Axial deformity of the lower limbs less than or equal to 5 ° [AND] ability to walk without assistance
  - Signed consent signed by the patient [AND] patient has not been previously excluded from the study
  - Ability to perform procedures as part of the follow-up (walk 50 steps on a flat surface and up / down stairs) [AND] patient is able to understand the study protocol
  - Be affiliated to a health insurance scheme [OR] patient has a sponsor who can cover study-related expenses

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Axial deformity > 5 ° [NOT] corrected by surgery
  - Ligament instability [OR] ligament laxity
  - Major injuries or surgery of the target knee or lateral contralateral knee in the 12 months preceding the inclusion visit [AND] patient has not fully recovered
  - Ligament reconstruction in the 12 months prior to the inclusion visit [AND] patient has not been cleared for physical activity
  - Need a cane or assistance to move [AND] patient is unable to perform daily activities without assistance
  - BMI > 35 [AND] patient is unable to achieve a healthy weight
  - Thrombocytopenia <150 G / L [OR] thrombocytosis > 450 G / L
