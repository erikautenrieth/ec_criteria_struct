Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Skeletal maturity [AND] presentation within a normal range
  2. AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures
  3. Wound primarily closeable [NOT] with flap or delay in closure due to contamination needed
  4. Palpable pedal pulses [NOT] with vascular injury sustained
  5. Presentation within 24 hours from injury [AND] no delay in treatment

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] current injury is a pathologic fracture
  2. Sustained bilateral tibia fracture [OR] sustained comminuted femur fracture
  3. Sustained severe Traumatic Brain Injury (GCS<12) [AND] severe spinal cord injury (lower extremity paresis/paralysis)
  4. Sustained severe burns (>10% total body surface area (TBSA) [OR] >5% TBSA with full thickness or circumferential injury)
  5. Prior ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery [OR] prior or current lower limb deformity or abnormality
  6. [NOT] able to complete follow-up visits
