Here is the modified eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Caregiver:
       -  Are currently a primary caregiver to an adult with PD [AND] are between the age of 40-80 years
       -  Have less than 5 years of music training [AND] speak and read English fluently
       -  Are fluent in English
  -  Adult with PD:
       -  Have stage 1-3 Parkinson's disease [AND] are between the age of 40-80 years
       -  Have less than 5 years of music training [AND] speak and read English fluently

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] meets all the inclusion criteria

Note: I inserted [AND] where two conditions are required to be met together, and [NOT] before the word "meets" to indicate that it is an exclusion criterion. I did not insert [OR] as there are no conditions that require alternative options.
