Here is the modified eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - diagnosis of typical AFL confirmed by 12-lead ECG [AND] no documented AF on ECG, ambulatory monitor, pacemaker or ICD at any time
  - scheduled to undergo catheter ablation of the CTI for treatment of AFL
  - history of hypertension (HTN) [OR] heart failure (HF) with reduced or preserved systolic function

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] history of known AF episodes
  - previous CTI or PVI ablation procedure
  - other SVT mechanisms demonstrated [AND] AVNRT, AVRT, or accessory pathways
  - amiodarone usage within the past 3 months [NOT]
  - unwillingness to participate [OR] undergo insertable monitor implantation
  - hyperkalemia (potassium > 5.0 mEq/L)
  - severe renal disease (Cr >2.5 mg/dL [men], >2.0 mg/dL [women], GFR < 30 mL/min/1.73 m2) [AND] life expectancy < 18 months
  - prior intolerance to treatment with an aldosterone antagonist [OR] current treatment with an aldosterone antagonist
  - need for treatment with a class I or III AAD for another indication
  - operative AFL (occurring within 30 days of surgery) that is expected to resolve [NOT]
  - presence of a cardiac rhythm device (pacemaker or ICD) capable of AF monitoring [NOT]
  - currently pregnant [OR] nursing a child
  - unwilling [NOT] to become pregnant and to use birth control while taking spironolactone
