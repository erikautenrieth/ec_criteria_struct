Here is the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - full-term [AND] ≥ 37 weeks of gestation
  - singleton
  - vertex-presenting gestations
  - with no [NOT] contraindication to vaginal delivery
  - intact membranes
  - Bishop score < 7 [AND] cervical dilation ≤ 2 cm

Exclusion Criteria:
  - contraindication for [NOT] misoprostol
  - history of [NOT] previous caesarean
  - rupture of membranes [OR] fetal or maternal morbidities ([AND] fetal major abnormalities, FIGO definition of pathological CTG, HELLP syndrome, preeclampsia or hypertension with severe features, fetal growth restriction)

Note: I did not insert [AND] between "fetal major abnormalities, FIGO definition of pathological CTG, HELLP syndrome, preeclampsia or hypertension with severe features, fetal growth restriction" as it is not a logical conjunction between these conditions.
