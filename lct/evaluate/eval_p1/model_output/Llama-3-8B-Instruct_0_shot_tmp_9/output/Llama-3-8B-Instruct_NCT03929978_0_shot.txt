Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Inclusion criteria and justification hereof:
       1. Have a colostomy [AND] ileostomy (jejunostomy) [OR] loop stoma [OR] two stomas placed closely together
       2. Have given written informed consent [AND] (the legal authorized representative of the subject)

       Are currently suffering from [NOT] peristomal skin problems i.e. bleeding [OR] broken skin (assessed by the investigator)
