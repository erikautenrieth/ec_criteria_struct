Here is the modified eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
1. Subject is greater than 18 years of age [AND] subject is willing [AND] able to provide informed consent
2. Subject is scheduled to undergo elective rotator cuff surgery
3. Subject has consented to an interscalene block

Exclusion Criteria:
1. [NOT] opioid dependence
2. Any subject diagnosed with a chronic pain condition which daily opioid use is needed [OR] any subject with a history of daily opioid use
3. Anatomical malformation [AND] in the investigator's opinion, the malformation may interfere with the placement of the nerve block
4. Raynaud's disease diagnosis [OR] vasculopathy
5. Patient refusal
