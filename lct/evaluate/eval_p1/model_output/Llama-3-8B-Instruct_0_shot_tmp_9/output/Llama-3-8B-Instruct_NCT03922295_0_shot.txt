Here is the modified eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], and [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with chronic suppurative otitis media [AND] with total or subtotal tympanic membrane perforation [AND] with limited anterior remnant.
  -  Patients with dry perforation [AND] for at least 3 months.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with recurrent perforation [NOT] after previous myringoplasty.
  -  Patients with ossicular disruption [OR] ossicular fixation.
  -  Patients who are immune-compromised [INCLUDING] uncontrolled diabetics [OR] chronic liver disease [OR] chronic kidney disease.

Note: I inserted the operators to clarify the logical connections between the conditions. I used [AND] to represent logical conjunction, [OR] to represent logical disjunction, and [NOT] to represent logical negation. I also added [INCLUDING] to indicate that the list of immune-compromised conditions is inclusive.
