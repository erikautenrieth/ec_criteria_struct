Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with moderate to severe sleep apnoea [AND] who require CPAP as standard care (AHI >15)
  -  Patients between the age of 18 [AND] 75

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with a diagnosis of atrial fibrillation [OR] atrial flutter [OR] ventricular tachycardia
  -  Patients with an ILR already in-situ [NOT] an established indication for ILR
  -  Patients with a palliative diagnosis i.e. life expectancy [NOT] less than 3 years
  -  Patients [NOT] who lack capacity

Note that I inserted [AND] to represent the logical conjunction between the two age ranges, [OR] to represent the logical disjunction between the different cardiac diagnoses, [NOT] to represent the negation of the ILR indication, life expectancy, and lack of capacity.
