Inclusion Criteria
  1. Written informed consent must be obtained before any assessment is performed.
  2. Male [AND] female patients [AND] age 18 to 85 years of age [AND] inclusive at screening
  3. Presence of type I [OR] type II diabetes mellitus
  4. The ETDRS letter score in the study eye must be between 24 and 70 letters (approximate Snellen equivalent of 20/40-20/320) [AND] the non-study eye (fellow eye) should be ≥34 letters or better (approximate Snellen equivalent of 20/200) at screening
  5. Presence of DME in the study eye [AND] with decrease in vision due to foveal thickening of central macular thickness ≥ 320 µm in the central subfield, as assessed on SD-OCT [AND] confirmed by the central reading center at screening
Other protocol specified inclusion/exclusion criteria apply.
