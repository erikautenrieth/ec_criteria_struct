Here is the modified eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - patients with lumbar pain present for more than 12 weeks [AND] pain during changing of the position, [AND] radicular pain.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Cauda equina syndrome,
  - ankylosing spondylitis [OR] thoracic deformities (pectus carinatum, excavatum),
  - spina bifida [OR] fractures,
  - post-operative spinal conditions [OR] diabetes,
  - inflammatory processes [OR] tumors,
  - [NOT] pregnancy.

Note: I inserted the logical operators [AND] to connect the inclusion criteria, [NOT] to indicate exclusion criteria, and [OR] to connect the exclusion criteria that are implicitly "or'd". I did not insert [OR] between categories that are separated by commas, as this would make the text messy and unreadable.
