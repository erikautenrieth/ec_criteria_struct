Inclusion Criteria:
  - Healthy women [AND] age 18-50 years [AND] gynecological procedures [AND] general anesthesia
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] pre-existing hypertension
  - Diabetes for several years [OR] ischemic heart disease
  - Cerebrovascular disease [OR] heart valve disease
  - Verified cardiac arrhythmia [OR] anaemia
  - Kidney or hepatic disease [OR] hypersensitivity for soya, eggs or peanuts
  - Pregnancy [OR] poor health state
  - Illicit substance use [OR] BMI <20 [OR] BMI >35 kg/m2
  - SBP >150 mmHg [OR] HR >100 beats/min

Note that I inserted [AND] to separate the inclusion criteria that must be met together, and [OR] to separate the exclusion criteria that can be met separately. I also inserted [NOT] to indicate the negation of pre-existing hypertension.
