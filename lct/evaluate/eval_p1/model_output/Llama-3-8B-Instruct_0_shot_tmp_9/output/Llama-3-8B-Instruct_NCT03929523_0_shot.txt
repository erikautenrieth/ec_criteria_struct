Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Provide written informed consent prior to the performance of any study specific procedure [AND] affiliated to the French social security system
  -  Recipient age ≥ 18 years
  -  Patients undergoing primary liver transplantation [OR] candidate for a first elective liver transplantation, whatever the indication, with a liver graft harvested from a brain-dead ECD defined by the presence of at least one of the following criteria:
       -  Donor age > 65 years [OR] intensive care unit stay > 7 days [OR] BMI > 30 [OR] proven macro-steatosis biopsy ≥ 30% [OR] natremia > 155 mmol/L at any time [OR] AST > 150 IU/mL at any time [OR] ALT > 170 IU/mL at any time.

  -  [NOT] patient participating in other interventional research, excluding routine care research (old regulation) and category 2 research not interfering with primary endpoint analysis [AND] patient under legal protection
  -  Patient deprived of liberty by a judicial or administrative decision [OR] patient refusing to participate in the study
  -  Pregnant or lactating women [AND] inability to understand information concerning the protocol
  -  Fulminant hepatic failure [OR] retransplantation [OR] split liver transplantation [OR] living donor liver transplantation [OR] grafts donated after cardiac arrest (DCD grafts) [OR] domino transplantation [OR] combined liver transplant [OR] unexpected medical contraindication to liver transplantation
