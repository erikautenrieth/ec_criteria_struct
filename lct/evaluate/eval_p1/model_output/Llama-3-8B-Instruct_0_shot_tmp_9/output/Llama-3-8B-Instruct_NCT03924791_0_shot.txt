Here is the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], and [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Diagnosed with sciatica by GP [AND] NRS leg pain of 6 [OR] more on a 10-point NRS scale
  - Minimum duration of symptoms of 3 weeks [AND] maximum duration of 8 weeks

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Age [NOT] under 18 years
  - Condition preventing to receive transforaminal epidural injection
  - Severe scoliosis
  - Transforaminal epidural injection received in 6 months [NOT] before randomization date
  - Surgery for sciatica at the same level
  - Surgery for sciatica at another level within one year [NOT] before inclusion
  - [NOT] Pregnancy
