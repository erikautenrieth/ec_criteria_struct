Here is the modified eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. ≥ 18 years old.
  2. Medical record documentation of either [OR] a) recurrent CDI per the study definition, that includes at least one recurrence after a primary episode and has completed at least one round of standard-of-care oral antibiotic therapy, [AND] b) or has had at least two episodes of severe CDI resulting in hospitalization.
  3. A positive stool test for the presence of toxigenic C. difficile or C. difficile toxin within 30 days prior to [OR] on the date of enrollment.
  4. Is currently taking [OR] was just prescribed antibiotics to control CDI related diarrhea at the time of enrollment.

[Note: Subject's CDI diarrhea must be controlled (<3 unformed/loose stools/day) while taking antibiotics during screening.]

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Has continued CDI diarrhea despite being on a course of antibiotics prescribed for CDI treatment [NOT].
  2. Requires systemic antibiotic therapy for a condition [NOT] other than CDI.
  3. Fecal microbiota transplant (FMT) within the past 6 months [NOT].
  4. FMT with an associated serious adverse event related to the FMT product or procedure [NOT].
  5. Bezlotoxumab (CDI monoclonal antibodies) if received within the last year [NOT].
  6. CD4 count <200/mm3 during Screening [NOT].
  7. An absolute neutrophil count of <1000 cells/µL during Screening [NOT].
  8. Is [NOT] pregnant, breastfeeding, or intends to become pregnant during study participation.
