Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Signed consent obtained before any study-related activities [AND] (study-related activities are any procedure related to recording of data according to the protocol)
  -  The decision to initiate treatment with commercially available NovoMix® 30 has been made by the patient and the treating physician [AND] before and independently from the decision to include the patient in this study
  -  Male [OR] female, greater than or equal to 19 years at the time of signing informed consent
  -  Diagnosed with type 2 diabetes [AND] treated with basal insulin plus 1-3 bolus insulin injections per day for at least 24 weeks prior to informed consent
  -  Available and documented HbA1c value less than or equal to 12 weeks prior to initiation of NovoMix® 30 treatment [NOT] requiring medical treatment for uncontrolled hyperglycemia

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Previous participation in this study [NOT] Participation is defined as having given informed consent in this study
  -  Mental incapacity, unwillingness [OR] language barriers precluding adequate understanding or cooperation
  -  Participation in any clinical trial of an approved or non-approved investigational medicinal product within 24 weeks prior to initiation of NovoMix® 30 treatment [NOT] clinical trials do not include non-interventional studies
  -  Hypersensitivity to NovoMix® 30 [OR] to any of the excipients
  -  Pregnancy [NOT] intention of becoming pregnant
