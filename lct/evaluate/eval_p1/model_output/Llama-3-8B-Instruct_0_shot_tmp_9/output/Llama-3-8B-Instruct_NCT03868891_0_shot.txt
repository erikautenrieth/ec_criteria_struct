Inclusion Criteria:
  - 6-17 years old [AND] otherwise healthy
  - Currently have unilateral [OR] bilateral ventilation tube(s) (VTs) inserted for otitis media with effusion (OME) [OR] tympanic membrane retraction/retraction pocket (TM-R/RP) [OR] a TM perforation after extrusion of a VT
  - History of at least 2 sets of VT insertions in the past [AND] currently have ventilation tube(s) (VTs) inserted
  - Eustachian tube (ET) function (ETF) tests showing an active muscular pattern of Eustachian tube dysfunction [AND] tubomanometry test results showing evidence of velopharyngeal dysfunction during swallowing
  - Cleft palate (CP) cohort: non-syndromic; prior Furlow palatoplasty without complications [NOT] need for revision
  - Non-CP cohort: have had prior adenoidectomy [AND] have unilateral [OR] bilateral ventilation tube(s) (VTs) inserted

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] concurrent or past diagnosis of cancer [OR] history of radiation
  - Have or had vestibular pathology [OR] cranial base surgery [OR] ossicular chain reconstruction
  - Craniofacial dysmorphology (other than non-syndromic CP with or without cleft lip in the CP cohort) [OR] other syndrome
  - A [NOT] patent nasal cavity
  - Patulous Eustachian tube [OR] pathologically low Eustachian tube opening [OR] closing pressures
  - Pregnancy [AND] unable or unwilling to perform the tests and exercises outlined in the study
