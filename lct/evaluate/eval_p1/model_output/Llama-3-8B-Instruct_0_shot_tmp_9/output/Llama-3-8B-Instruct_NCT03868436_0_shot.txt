Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Conscious adult patients: ≥ 18 years of age [AND] moderate to severe pain associated with trauma (NRS 0-10 ≥ 4) which is unlikely to require surgery/hospitalization, such as
       -  Musculoskeletal injuries [AND] physical wounds
       -  Fractures
       -  Lacerations
       -  Burns
       -  Dislocations
       -  Contusions
       -  Injury due to foreign bodies
  2. Patient [AND/OR] patient's authorized legal representative should understand the nature of the study and provide written informed consent
  3. Patient is able to follow all study requirements [AND] procedures and complete required questionnaires

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Multi-level trauma [NOT] women of child bearing potential who are pregnant or peri partum, including labour
  2. [NOT] altered level of consciousness, due to any cause, including head injury, drugs, or alcohol
  3. Clinically significant renal impairment
  4. [NOT] history of liver dysfunction after previous MEOF use or other halogenated anesthetics
  5. Hypersensitivity to MEOF [OR] other halogenated anesthetics, [OR] to butylated hydroxytoluene
  6. Known [OR] genetically susceptible to malignant hyperthermia or a history of severe adverse reactions in either patient or relatives
  7. Exacerbation of an underlying condition [i.e., chronic pain]
  8. Clinically evident [OR] potential hemodynamic instability as per the opinion of the investigator
  9. Clinically evident respiratory impairment as per the opinion of the investigator
  10. [NOT] prior treatment with PENTHROX within 3 months
