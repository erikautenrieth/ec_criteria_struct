Inclusion Criteria:
  - Established diagnosis of UC for at least 3 months prior to screening, with minimum disease extent [AND] 15 cm from the anal verge
  - Mildly to moderately active UC [AND] inadequate response to ongoing treatment with oral mesalamine ≥2.4 g/day for ≥4 weeks from screening visit
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] possible or confirmed diagnosis of Crohn's Disease, but possible or confirmed diagnosis of other forms of inflammatory bowel disorders [OR] inflammatory bowel syndrome
  - Ongoing or failed prior treatment for UC with methotrexate, azathioprine, 6-mercaptopurine, cyclosporine, tofacitinib, mycophenolate mofetil, sirolimus (rapamycin), thalidomide, tacrolimus (FK-506), or biologics (e.g., TNF-alpha-antagonists, anti-integrin therapies, or agents targeting IL-12 or IL-23, etc.) [OR] any other treatments for UC
  - Any immunosuppressive condition [OR] treatment with immunosuppressive medications
  - History of prior surgical intervention in any region of the gastrointestinal tract (excluding minor surgery) [AND] prior diagnosis of any cardiovascular, renal, hepatic, endocrine, infectious, hematological, oncologic, neuro-psychiatric or immune-mediated disorder, which in the opinion of the Principal Investigator might impact the subject's safety or compliance, or the interpretation of results
  - Treatment with any other investigational drugs [NOT] ≤12 weeks prior to baseline visit
  - The participant has a condition [OR] is in a situation which, in the Principal Investigator's opinion, may put the participant at significant risk, may confound the study results, or may interfere significantly with the participant's participation in the study
