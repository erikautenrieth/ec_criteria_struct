Here is the modified eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], and [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - BMI 30-40 kg/m2
  - BMI 18-25 kg/m2 [AND] (those with BMI up to 28kg/m2 [OR] waist circumference is <96cm)
  - Males
  - Aged 18-60yrs

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Acute illness in the preceding 6 weeks
  - Taking regular medication
  - History of deep vein thrombosis [OR] clotting disorders
  - Hypertension
  - Diabetes
  - Any clinically significant findings [NOT] at screening
  - History of substance abuse
  - Demonstrating factors precluding safe MRI
  - History of gastrointestinal motility disorders [NOT] (e.g. gastroesophageal reflux disease -irritable bowel syndrome, gastroparesis, sphincter of Oddi dysfunction, etc.)
  - Previous thoracic [OR] abdominal surgery
  - Those who report having ≤3 bowel movements/week [OR] >2/day
