Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Histologically diagnosed high-grade serous [AND] endometrioid ovarian cancer (including primary peritoneal [AND] fallopian tube cancer)
  2. Completion of ≥2 previous platinum-containing regimens [AND] no prior treatment with a non-platinum-based chemotherapy regimen
  3. Complete response (CR) [OR] partial response (PR) achieved with last platinum-based chemotherapy regimen as determined by investigator
  4. Ability to be randomized ≤8 weeks after last dose of platinum [AND] no evidence of progressive disease

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Prior treatment with a [NOT] poly (ADP-ribose) polymerase (PARP) inhibitor
  2. Patients who have received other study drug treatment within 4 weeks prior to the first administration (< 5 elimination half-lives of the study drug molecular targeted anti-cancer drugs) [AND] patients who have not received any treatment for at least 5 elimination half-lives of the study drug
  3. Patients with clinical symptoms of cancer ascites, pleural effusion, who need to drainage [OR] who have undergone ascites drainage within 2 months prior to the first administration [NOT] patients who have undergone successful treatment for cancer ascites
