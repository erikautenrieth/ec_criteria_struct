Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], and [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Signed written informed consent
  2. Age 18 [AND] 80 years
  3. Capable of understanding the test [AND] cooperating with the questionnaire

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. History of psychosis, including schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, depression, anxiety, obsessive-compulsive disorder, phobia, somatoform disorder, stress-related disorders [OR] history of insomnia, currently with medication
  2. History of obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) [OR] history of other sleep disorders, including narcolepsy/hypersomnias, circadian rhythm sleep disorder, parasomnias, sleep-related dyskinesia
  3. History of heart failure, that is New York heart association (NYHA) Class Ⅲ-[NOT] Ⅳ
  4. History of atrial fibrillation [AND] frequent atrial or ventricular extrasystoles
  5. Ongoing substance [OR] alcohol abuse
  6. Currently receiving sedative hypnotic medication within 1 week [NOT] history of insomnia, currently with medication
  7. Pregnant [NOT] women
  8. Ongoing involvement in night-shift work (22:00-6:00) within 1 week [OR] ongoing need of care of families at home that wake during the night within 1 week
  9. Incomprehensible [NOT] willing to fill in informed consent or questionnaire
