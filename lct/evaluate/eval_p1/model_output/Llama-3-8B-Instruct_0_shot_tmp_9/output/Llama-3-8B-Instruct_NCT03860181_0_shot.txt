Here is the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Subjects will be included if:
  - They are 18 years [AND] older;
  - They are undergoing primary total shoulder arthroplasty by the principal [AND] co-investigator;
  - They are willing [AND] have the capacity to provide informed consent;
  - They expect to continue their post-operative follow up care with their operating surgeons at Medical University of South Carolina (MUSC) [AND] not have any other plans for post-operative care.

Subjects will be excluded if:
  - They have a unique, identifying tattoo [OR] skin marking within 2 inches of intended site of surgical incision;
  - They self-report [NOT] a known hypersensitivity to cyanoacrylate, formaldehyde, benzalkonium chloride, or pressure sensitive adhesive;
  - They self-report [OR] have a documented prior ipsilateral shoulder arthroplasty [OR] other open ipsilateral shoulder surgery utilizing the delto-pectoral approach;
  - Their medical record shows that they are HIV positive [OR] otherwise immunocompromised;
  - Their medical record shows a skin abnormality [OR] dermatological condition which affects skin healing;
  - They report [OR] have a personal or family history of significant keloid [OR] significant hypertrophic scar formations, [OR] other problems with wound healing.

Note: I inserted [AND] to connect the conditions within each sentence, and [OR] to connect the exclusion criteria that are alternatives to each other. I also inserted [NOT] to indicate the negation of hypersensitivity.
