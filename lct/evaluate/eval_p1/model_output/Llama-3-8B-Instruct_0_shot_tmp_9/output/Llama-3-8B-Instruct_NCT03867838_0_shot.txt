Inclusion Criteria:
  -  greater than 6 months post-stroke [AND] 
  -  passive abduction to 90 degrees at shoulder 
  -  reduced active (retro)flexion/extension at shoulder when abducted to 90 degrees [AND] 
  -  reduced active flexion/extension at elbow 

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] able to give informed consent 
  -  [NOT] able to comprehend and follow instructions 
  -  have a condition [NOT] affecting sensorimotor function [OR] have a condition other than stroke affecting sensorimotor function 
  -  show evidence of unilateral spatial neglect 
  -  [NOT] able to sit in a chair without armrests for 2 hours
