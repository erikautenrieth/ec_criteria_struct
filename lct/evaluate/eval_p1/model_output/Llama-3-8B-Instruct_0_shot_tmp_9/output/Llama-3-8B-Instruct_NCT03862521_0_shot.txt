Inclusion criteria
  -  Prepubertal children [AND] 2 to <12 years old (i.e., before 12th birthday)
  -  Type 1 diabetes [AND] for at least 6 months
  -  Patients [AND] must be using insulin pump therapy
  -  [NOT] patients presently on low carbohydrate diet

Exclusion criteria
  -  On low [OR] very low carbohydrate diet
  -  History of endocrine [OR] metabolic disorder that could affect counter-regulation (well-controlled hypothyroidism is acceptable [AND] on stable dose of l-thyroxine for ≥3 months)
  -  History of metabolic disorder [AND] affection lipid metabolism
  -  Severe hypoglycemic event [NOT] in past 3 months
