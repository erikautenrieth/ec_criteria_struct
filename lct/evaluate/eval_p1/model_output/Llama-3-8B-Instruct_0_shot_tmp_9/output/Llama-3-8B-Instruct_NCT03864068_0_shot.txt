Inclusion Criteria:
  - Women with chronic anovulation [OR] oligomenorrhea defined as spontaneous intermenstrual periods of greater than or equal to 45 days [AND] a total of less than or equal to 8 menses per year.
  - Women with Hyperandrogenism defined as a total testosterone greater than 50 ng/dL [AND] a free androgen index greater than 10.
  - Women with Polycystic Ovaries on Ultrasound defined as either 12 or more follicles measuring 2-9 mm in diameter [OR] increased ovarian volume greater than 10 cm.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Women with Hyperprolactinemia defined as 2 prolactin levels at least one week apart [NOT] less than 30 ng/mL.
  - Women with known 21-hydroxylase deficiency [OR] other enzyme deficiency leading to the congenital adrenal hyperplasia.
  - Women with elevated FSH levels [NOT] less than 10 mIU/mL.
  - Women with uncorrected thyroid disease as per ASRM guidelines for nonpregnant subjects [NOT] (TSH [NOT] less than 0.45 mIU/mL [AND] greater than 4.5 MIU/mL).
  - Women with a suspected adrenal or ovarian tumor secreting androgens [NOT].
  - Women with Cushing's syndrome [NOT].
  - Women on confounding medications which affect ovarian function including metformin [OR] hormonal contraceptives [OR] other medications for type 2 diabetes.
  - Women with medical conditions that are contraindications to OTC inositol [OR] previous allergic reactions to the supplement [OR] to the placebo maltodextrin [OR] inulin [NOT].
