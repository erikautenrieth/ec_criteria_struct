Here is the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Scheduled for elective thoracotomy [AND] ASA status 1,2
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Inability to provide adequate informed consent
  - [NOT] Any contraindication to the placement of thoracic paravertebral catheters
  - [NOT] Unstable vertebral and transverse process fractures
  - [NOT] (Any chronic painful conditions [OR] preoperative opioid use)
  - [NOT] Coagulation abnormalities [OR] expectation to be on therapeutic anticoagulants postoperatively
  - [NOT] Allergy to any of the drugs/agents used in study protocol
  - [NOT] Altered mental status [OR] emergency surgery
  - [NOT] (Comorbid conditions such as sepsis [OR] unstable angina [OR] congestive heart failure)

Note that I inserted [AND] between the two conditions in the inclusion criteria, and [NOT] before each exclusion criterion. I also inserted [OR] between the alternatives in the exclusion criteria where it was implied by commas.
