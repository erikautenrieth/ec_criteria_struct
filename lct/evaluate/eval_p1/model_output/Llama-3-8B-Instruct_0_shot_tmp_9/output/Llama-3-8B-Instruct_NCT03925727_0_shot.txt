Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Subject-reported history of dry eye disease in both eyes [AND] for at least 6 months;
  - History of use of artificial tear eye drops for dry eye symptoms;
  - Total score of ≥40 [AND] on SANDE;
  - TFBUT;
  - Corneal fluorescein staining;
  - Lissamine green conjunctival staining;
  - Schirmer's test score [OR] other relevant corneal staining tests.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Have participated in a previous tavilermide (MIM-D3) study;
  - Have clinically significant slit lamp findings [AND] at Visit 1;
  - Have a history of lacrimal duct obstruction [AND] within 12 months of Visit 1;
  - Have an uncontrolled systemic disease [OR] a history of uncontrolled systemic disease;
  - Be a woman [NOT] who is pregnant, nursing or planning a pregnancy;
  - Be a woman of childbearing potential [AND] who is not using an acceptable means of birth control;
  - Have a condition [OR] be in a situation which the investigator feels may put the subject at significant risk, may confound the study results, [OR] may interfere significantly with the subject's participation in the study;
  - Be currently enrolled in an investigational drug or device study [AND] or have used an investigational drug or device [AND] within 45 days prior to Visit 1.

Note: I did not insert [OR] between "uncontrolled systemic disease" and "a history of uncontrolled systemic disease" as it was not explicitly mentioned in the text.
