Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Men [AND] women age 65 years [OR] older
  -  Current PD diagnosis with symptoms severity at Hoehn & Yahr (H&Y) stage 1-4 based on an expert assessment (movement disorders neurologist report [OR] telemedicine evaluation)

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] History of hip fracture

  -  Non-ambulatory, i.e., [NOT] able to walk without assistance of another person
  -  Undergoing kidney dialysis
  -  Diagnosis of multiple myeloma [OR] Paget's disease
  -  [NOT] able to speak or read English sufficiently to complete informed consent
  -  Any other criteria which would make the patient unsuitable to participate in this study as determined by the study staff (e.g., an [NOT] uncontrolled drug and/or alcohol addiction)
