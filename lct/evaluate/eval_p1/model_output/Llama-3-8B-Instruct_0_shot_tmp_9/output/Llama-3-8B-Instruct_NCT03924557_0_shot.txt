Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], and [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Must have a clinical diagnosis of solid tumor cancer [AND] be seeking treatment and supportive care at the UF Health Cancer Center (Medical Plaza)
  -  Patients must plan to receive chemotherapy known to be associated with the need for supportive care within 30 days of enrollment [OR] receive other supportive care
  -  Life expectancy must be greater than 6 months [NOT] less than or equal to 6 months
  -  Must have Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-2
  -  Written informed consent obtained from the patient
  -  The patient must have the ability to comply with all the study-related procedures

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients [NOT] willing or able to provide voluntary informed consent
  -  Patients who are unwilling or unable to comply with protocol requirements [AND] follow-up procedures
  -  Patients planned to undergo cancer therapy other than chemotherapy [i.e., radiation, surgery, or hormonal treatment alone]
  -  Prisoners [OR] patients who are involuntarily incarcerated, including those compulsorily detained for treatment of either a psychiatric or physical illness
