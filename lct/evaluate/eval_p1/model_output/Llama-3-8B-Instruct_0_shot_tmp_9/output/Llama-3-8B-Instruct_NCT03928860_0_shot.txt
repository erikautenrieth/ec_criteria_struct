Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients with at least 3 months of clinical onset of peripheral vascular disease [AND] Patients with ABI between 0.4-1.4
  - Patients up to Stage 2b according to the Fontaine classification system

Exclusion Criteria:

  - Patients with unstable angina
  - Patients with myocardial infarction (MI) [OR] Patients with stroke
  - Patients with heart failure [OR] Patients with significant liver or renal failure (ie, dialysis)
  - Patients with acute infectious diseases [NOT] Acute infectious diseases
  - Patients with neoplasia
