Here is the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients with CPM [AND] expected life expectancy of > 6 months.
  2. ECOG Scale of Performance Status (PS) scores [OR] 0 [OR] 1.
  3. Patients living within a 15-mile catchment area to facilitate overseeing systemic chemotherapy administration.
  4. Patients receiving concomitant systemic chemotherapy regimens [OR] with FOLFIRI, FOLFOX, Mitomycin C [AND] Fluorouracil, Capecitabine [NOT] including Lonsurf [OR] without systemic chemotherapy if no systemic options available to patient.
  5. Neutrophil count on [OR] just before chemotherapy due date of >1.5.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients [NOT] aged <18.
  2. Patients [NOT] suitable for PIPAC, as determined by MDT.
  3. Patients [NOT] fit for general anaesthesia and [OR] laparoscopy, as determined by Preoperative Assessment Department or Consultant Anaesthetist.
  4. Patients with clinically evident gross ascites.
  5. Patients with bowel obstruction.
  6. Patients receiving bevacizumab as part of systemic chemotherapy regime, which would result in a time from chemotherapy to surgery that is too long for PIPAC to be feasible.
  7. Patients with previous bone marrow suppression due to chemotherapy, given the risk of post-operative neutropenia.
