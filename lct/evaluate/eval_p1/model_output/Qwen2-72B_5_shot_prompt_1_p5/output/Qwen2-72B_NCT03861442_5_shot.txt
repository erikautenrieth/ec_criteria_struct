<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - A minimum of 18 years old
 - Experienced user (≥ 6 months) [AND] of a MED-EL cochlear implant (CI; C40+ and later model)
 - Experienced user [AND] of a MED-EL SONNET audio processor (≥ 6 months)
 - Post-lingual onset [AND] of bilateral severe [OR] to profound sensory-neural hearing loss
 - Unilateral CI user
 - A minimum of 10 active electrodes
 - A minimum of 40% speech recognition [AND] in the Freiburg Monosyllables test in quiet at 65 dB sound pressure level (SPL; at the last time tested)
 - Fluent [AND] in German (the language of the test centre)
 - Signed [AND] and dated Informed Consent Form before the start of any study-specific procedure.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] Lack of compliance with any inclusion criteria
 - CI user [AND] with contralateral hearing equal to [OR] or better than 60 dB (PTA measured at 500, [OR] 1000, [OR] and 2000Hz)    
 - User [AND] with electric-acoustic stimulation (EAS; user of an EAS audio processor)
 - Implanted [AND] with C40X [OR] and C40C
 - Implanted [AND] with an Auditory Brainstem Implant [OR] or Split electrode array
 - Known allergic reactions [AND] to components of the investigational medical device
 - Unstable psychological status
 - Anything that, in the opinion of the Investigator, would place the subject at increased risk [OR] or preclude the subject's full compliance [OR] with completion of the study
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
