Inclusion Criteria:
 - Affected children [AND] will be recruited if the treating clinician has made a diagnosis of possible Kawasaki disease (even if they do not fulfil the criteria below for Kawasaki disease).
 - The presence of fever for at least five days [AND] plus four of the following criteria:
   1. Changes in the peripheral extremities Acute: erythema [OR] and oedema of hands [OR] and feet Convalescent: membranous desquamation of fingertips
   2. Polymorphous exanthema
   3. Bilateral painless bulbar conjunctival injection without exudate
   4. Changes in lips [OR] and oral cavity: erythema [OR] and cracking of lips, strawberry tongue, diffuse injection of oral [OR] and pharyngeal mucosae
   5. Cervical lymphadenopathy (>1.5cm diameter), usually unilateral Patients meeting not all of these criteria may meet the criteria for atypical Kawasaki disease, ie. if they have fever [AND] and two [OR] or three of the above criteria [AND] and elevation of CRP [OR] or echocardiographic evidence of coronary artery dilatation.
 - Parents [AND] of affected child must be biological parents.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - children [NOT] who do not have a diagnosis of possible Kawasaki disease
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
