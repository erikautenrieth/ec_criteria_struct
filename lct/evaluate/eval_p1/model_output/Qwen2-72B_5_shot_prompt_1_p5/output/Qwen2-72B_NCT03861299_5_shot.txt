<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Age ≥18 years [AND] and ≤ 90 years
 2. Tumor diagnosed as Glioblastoma Multiforme on MRI [WITH] distinct ring-like pattern of contrast enhancement [WITH] thick irregular walls [AND] and a core area reduced signal suggestive of tumour necrosis as assessed by the surgeon
 3. Tumors situated in [OR] or near eloquent areas; motor cortex, [OR] sensory cortex, [OR] subcortical pyramidal tract [OR] or speech areas as indicated on MRI (Sawaya Grading II [AND] and II)
 4. The tumor is suitable for resection (according to neurosurgeon)
 5. Karnofsky performance scale 80 [OR] or more
 6. Written Informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Tumors of the cerebellum, [OR] brain stem [OR] or midline
 2. Multifocal contrast enhancing lesions
 3. Substantial non-contrast enhancing tumor areas suggesting low grade gliomas [WITH] with malignant transformation
 4. Medical reasons precluding MRI (eg, pacemaker)
 5. [NOT] Inability to give consent because of [OR] or language barrier
 6. Psychiatric history
 7. Previous brain tumour surgery
 8. Previous low-grade glioma.
 9. Second primary malignancy within the past 5 years [WITH] with the exception of adequately treated in situ carcinoma of any organ [OR] or basal cell carcinoma of the skin.
 10. Severe aphasia [OR] or dysphasia
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
