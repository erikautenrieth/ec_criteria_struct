 - 145 mmHg [AND] ≤ msSBP [AND] ≤ 190 mmHg

 - [NOT] hypersensitivity to telmisartan [OR] or rosuvastatin
 - [NOT] history of angioedema after treatment with angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors [OR] or ARBs [OR] creatinine phosphokinase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal
 - [NOT] estimated glomerular filtration rate ≤30 mL/min [OR] aspartate aminotransferase [OR] and alanine aminotransferase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal
