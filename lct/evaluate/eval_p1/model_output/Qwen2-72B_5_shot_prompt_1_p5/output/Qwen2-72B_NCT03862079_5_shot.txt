<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who are day -10 pre- [OR] to day +30 post-allogeneic hematopoietic cell transplant (AHCT) [AND] from any donor [OR] or graft source [AND] and for any conditioning regimen
 - Patients who have received treatment with meropenem [OR] or piperacillin-tazobactam (pip-tazo) intravenously (IV) (of at least 24 hours duration) [AND] in past 7 days
 - Controlled infection defined as hemodynamically stable [AND] and not requiring supplemental oxygen of more than 2 liters via nasal cannula
 - Patients who are able to take oral medications in suspension form
 - Patients who are able to provide informed consent (IC) [AND] and comply with all study visits [AND] and procedures
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who are anticipated to require continued broad spectrum antibiotics with meropenem [OR] or pip-tazo IV for > 96 hours post-engraftment [AND] such as for known, documented infections necessitating prolonged treatment
 - Patients with a prior documented infection with mycormycetes
 - Patients who are greater than 2 days from time of neutrophil engraftment post AHCT
 - Patients with active enteric infections
 - Patients with acute GVHD >= grade II
 - Patients unwilling [OR] or unable to undergo the FMT via retention enema procedure
 - Patients who have received treatment with an investigational agent within 2 weeks of enrollment
 - Patients unable to tolerate oral decontamination regimen of pip-tazo [AND] and nystatin due to prior allergy [OR] or intolerance of these medications
 - Patients with any medical [OR] or psychological condition that, in the opinion of the investigator, might interfere with the subject's participation in the trial, [OR] pose any additional risk for the subject, [OR] or confound the assessments of the subject
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
