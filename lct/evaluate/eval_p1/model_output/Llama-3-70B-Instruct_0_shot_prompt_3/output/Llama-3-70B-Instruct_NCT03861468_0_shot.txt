Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients aged from 18 to 80 years
  -  Obese (BMI ≥ 30kg/m²)
  -  Referred by GPs to the medical analysis laboratory for usual biological assessment
  -  Patients [NOT] having respiratory follow-up already in place
  -  Bicarbonate - Concentration ≥ 27 mmol/L
  -  STOP-BANG score ≥ 3

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with acute disease [OR] recently diagnosed chronic disease (< 2 months)
  -  Patients [NOT] hospitalized for respiratory, metabolic [OR] cardiovascular event (< 2 months)
  -  Patients with renal insufficiency stage 4 [OR] 5, [OR] autoimmune disease, [OR] viral hepatitis, [OR] cirrhosis
  -  Pregnant women
  -  Patients already included in an interventional study [AND] end of the study < 1 month
