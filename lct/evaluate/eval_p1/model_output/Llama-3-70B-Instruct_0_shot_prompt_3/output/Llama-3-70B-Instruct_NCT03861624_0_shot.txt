<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Skeletal maturity [AND]
  2. AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures [AND]
  3. Wound primarily closeable [NOT] no flap [OR] delay in closure due to contamination needed [NOT]
  4. Palpable pedal pulses [NOT] no vascular injury sustained [AND]
  5. Presentation within 24 hours from injury [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Current injury is a pathologic fracture [AND]
  2. Sustained bilateral tibia fracture [AND]
  3. Sustained comminuted femur fracture [AND]
  4. Sustained severe Traumatic Brain Injury (GCS<12) *** [AND]
  5. Sustained severe spinal cord injury (lower extremity paresis/paralysis) [AND]
  6. Sustained severe burns (>10% total body surface area (TBSA) [OR] >5% TBSA with full thickness [OR] circumferential injury) [AND]
  7. Prior ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery [AND]
  8. Prior [OR] current lower limb deformity [OR] abnormality [AND]
  9. Unable [NOT] to complete follow-up visits [AND]
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
