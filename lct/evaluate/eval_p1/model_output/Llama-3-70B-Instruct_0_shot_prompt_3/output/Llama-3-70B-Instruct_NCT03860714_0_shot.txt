Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Non-cardiac surgery patients;
       -  Age is greater than or equal to 60 years old [AND] ③Han Nationality, mother tongue is chinese [OR], other ethnicities with chinese as their mother tongue;
       -  The MMSE score: Illiteracy is greater than or equal to 17 points [OR] primary school is greater than or equal to 20 points [OR] higher secondary school is more than 24 points;
       -  The people [NOT] unable to sign informed consent, [AND] have signed informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  The diagnosis of preoperative delirium [OR] history of delirium;
       -  The diagnosis of Unstable hypertension [NOT] well-controlled hypertension, such as：pheochromocytoma [OR] aortic dissection [OR] other unstable hypertension conditions;
       -  ③Severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] congestive heart failure [OR] severe respiratory diseases;
       -  ④Severe liver dysfunction [OR] severe kidney dysfunction [OR] end-stage liver or kidney disease [NOT] mild liver or kidney dysfunction.
