Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  LMNA+ cases with pathogenic LMNA mutations [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  DCMWT cases: patients with heart muscle failure [OR] wild-type lamin gene. Heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  HV (controls): matched to cases.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Participants with [NOT] ability to overcome needle-phobia that would preclude blood-letting.
  -  Participants who are [NOT] willing to consent.
  -  Patients with [NOT] conventional contraindication for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).
  -  Patients who have had a blood transfusion within the last month [OR] patients having haemodialysis will be excluded.
