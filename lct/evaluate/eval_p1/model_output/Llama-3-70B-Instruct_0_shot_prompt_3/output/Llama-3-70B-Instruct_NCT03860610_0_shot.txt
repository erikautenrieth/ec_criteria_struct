<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with an unilateral THA [OR] TKA, which they received for the treatment of osteoarthritis [AND] (Patient groups 1+2)
  -  Patients diagnosed with unilateral OA of the knee [OR] hip planned for a treatment with THA [OR] TKA (Patient groups 3+4)
  -  Age ≥ 30 years [AND] (Healthy control group)
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Body mass index > 35kg/m2 [AND]
  -  Use of walking aids [AND]
  -  Neuromuscular disorders affecting gait [AND]
  -  [NOT] Ability to follow procedures due to psychological disorders [OR] dementia [AND]
  -  Diagnosed hip [OR] knee OA (Healthy control group) [AND]
  -  Current pain in the lower extremities [OR] lower back (Healthy control group) [AND]
  -  Rheumatoid Arthritis (Healthy control group) [AND]
  -  Previous corrective osteotomy (Healthy control group) [AND]
  -  Regular intake of pain medication (more than 4 times per week) [AND] (Healthy control group)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
