<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Aged at least 18 years [AND] 
  -  Histologically [OR] cytologically proven diagnosis of NSCLC that is locally advanced [OR] metastatic.
  -  Radiological documentation of disease progression while on a previous continuous treatment with an first [OR] second generation of EGFR TKI e.g. gefitinib [OR] erlotinib [AND].
  -  patients must also have confirmation of tumour T790M mutation status (confirmed positive) after disease progression on the prior EGFR TKI [AND].
  -  Eastern cooperative oncology group performance status (ECOG PS) of 0-1 [AND].
  -  a minimum life expectancy of 12 weeks [AND].
  -  At least one lesion, [NOT] previously irradiated during the study screening period, that can be accurately measured at baseline according to RECIST 1.1 [AND].
  -  Agree to use routine adequate [AND] effective contraceptive measures during the entire study period [AND] within 6 weeks after the last dose pre-menopausal fertility possible; Female patients need to exclude pregnancy (ie negative pregnancy test) [AND] in non-lactation period [AND].
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Treatment with a first [OR] second generation of EGFR-TKI within 7 days of study entry [AND]; previous treatment with a third generation of EGFR-TKI [AND].
  -  Unresolved toxicities from prior therapy [AND].
  -  Unstable spinal cord compression/brain metastases [AND].
  -  Severe/uncontrolled systemic diseases, including uncontrolled hypertension, bleeding diatheses [OR] infection [AND].
  -  QTcF>470 msec during the screening period [AND].
  -  Severe respiratory diseases such as interstitial lung disease, severe asthma, pulmonary embolism, etc. [AND].
  -  previous treatment with 2 [OR] more lines of Chemotherapy [OR] immunotherapy [AND].
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
