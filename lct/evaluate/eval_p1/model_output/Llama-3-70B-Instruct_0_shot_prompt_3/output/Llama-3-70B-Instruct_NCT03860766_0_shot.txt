Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Handball players;
  -  Healthy [AND] with no known medical conditions;
  -  Participation of at least one official match in the last year [OR], attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Presence of musculoskeletal injury in the last three months [NOT] fully recovered;
  -  Diagnosis of cardiovascular diseases [NOT] controlled by medication;
  -  Use of anabolic, anti-inflammatory, [OR] analgesic drugs 72 hours before the evaluations;
  -  Use of alcohol, [OR] illicit drugs during the collection period [NOT] abstinent;
