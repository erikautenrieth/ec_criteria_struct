<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Nursing home residents with ≥2 of the following risk factors for atrial fibrillation [AND]:
  -  age ≥75 years
  -  female sex [OR]
  -  obstructive sleep apnea [OR]
  -  peripheral vascular disease [OR]
  -  diabetes mellitus [OR]
  -  obesity (body mass index [BMI] >30 kg/m2) [OR]
  -  hypertension [OR]
  -  congestive heart failure [OR]
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  prior diagnosis of AF [NOT]
  -  continuous rhythm monitoring over the past year [NOT]
  -  pacemaker or implantable cardioverter defibrillator in situ [NOT]
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
