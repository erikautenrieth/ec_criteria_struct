Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Children whose age ≥ 24 months [AND] ≤ 36 months
  -  Full-term [OR] prematurely born (<33 SA) at Nice University Hospital

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Children with congenital pathologies [AND] malformations in the oro-facial sphere
  -  Children with neurological [AND] developmental pathologies [NOT] related to prematurity
  -  Children with ENT deformities
  -  Children with parental language barrier [NOT] able to communicate effectively
