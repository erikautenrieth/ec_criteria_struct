Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria-- Participants must be:
  -  Healthy (determined by self-report) [AND] 
  -  Between the ages of 18-55 years old
  -  Current cigarette smokers
  -  Willing to provide informed consent [AND] 
  -  Able to attend the lab session [AND] abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required
  -  Agree to use designated products according to the study protocol

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Women if they are breast-feeding [OR] test positive for pregnancy (by urinalysis) at screening.
  -  Individuals who weigh less than 110 pounds [OR] 
  -  Participants with [NOT] ability to provide informed consent
  -  Participants who are [NOT] willing to attend the lab session [OR] unable to abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required
  -  Participants who are [NOT] agreeable to use designated products according to the study protocol
