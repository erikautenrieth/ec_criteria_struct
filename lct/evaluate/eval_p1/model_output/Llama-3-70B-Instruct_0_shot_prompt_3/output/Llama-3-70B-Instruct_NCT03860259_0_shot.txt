Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Subject is greater than 18 years of age
  2. Subject is willing [AND] able to provide informed consent
  3. Subject is scheduled to undergo elective rotator cuff surgery
  4. Subject has consented to an interscalene block

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Opioid dependence
  2. Any subject diagnosed with a chronic pain condition which daily opioid use is [NOT] adequate [OR] needed
  3. Anatomical malformation, which in the investigator's opinion may [NOT] interfere with the placement of the nerve block
  4. [NOT] Raynaud's disease diagnosis
  5. [NOT] Vasculopathy
  6. Patient [NOT] refusal
