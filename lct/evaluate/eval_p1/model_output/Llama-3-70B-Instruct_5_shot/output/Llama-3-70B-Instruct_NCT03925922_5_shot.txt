Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Nulliparous [AND] singleton pregnancy
  -  gestational age between 37week [AND] 42 weeks
  -  Cephalic presentation
  -  Bishop score of 5 [OR] less
  -  No clinical evidence of regular contraction
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Rupture of membrane
  -  Vaginal bleeding
  -  Any contraindication to vaginal delivery
