Inclusion Criteria:
 - ≥18 years of age
 - Currently treated with peritoneal dialysis (APD [OR] or CAPD)
 - Serum ionized calcium between 1.10 [AND] 1.40 mmol/L for >3 months
 - Plasma phosphate between 0.7 [AND] 3.0 mmol/L for >3 months
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Earlier parathyroidectomized
 - Other conditions [AND] which hinders the participation in the trial
