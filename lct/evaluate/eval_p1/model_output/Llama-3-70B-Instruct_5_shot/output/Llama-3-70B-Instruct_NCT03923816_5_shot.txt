Inclusion Criteria:
 - patients >18 years old, [AND] undergoing elective hepatobiliary surgery [AND] under general anesthesia [AND] American Society of Anesthesiologists grade II.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - 1-patient refusal [OR] 2-psychiatric disorders [OR] 3-pregnancy [OR] lactation [OR] 4-preexisting neurological dysfunction [AND] (history of cerebrovascular stroke CVS) [OR] 5-metastatic cancer [OR]
     6-Inflammatory bowel disease [OR] 7-Diabetes mellitus [OR] 8-Renal insufficiency ([NOT] serum creatinine level ≤ 180 μmol/l) [OR] 9- unexpected intraoperative findings (inoperable) [OR] 10-accidental massive intraoperative hemorrhage.
