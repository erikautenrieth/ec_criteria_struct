Inclusion Criteria:
 - Dialysis [OR] non-dialysis patients with chronic renal insufficiency [AND] with a GFR < 20ml / min eligible for transplant
 - Patient wishing to start the registration process at the transplant
 - Patient giving consent for the transmission of his data via an electronic platform
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patient already enrolled
 - Patient having a pathology [AND] with a life expectancy of less than two years
 - Patient with serious psychiatric disorders [OR] or dementia
