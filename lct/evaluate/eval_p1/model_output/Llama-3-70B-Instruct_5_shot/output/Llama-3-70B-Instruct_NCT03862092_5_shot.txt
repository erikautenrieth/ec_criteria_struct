Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients aged ≥18 [AND] and <80 years
  -  ER-visit patients with acute abdominal pain
       -  NRS ≥ 6
       -  Unresponsive to gastrointestinal motility drug [AND] antacid
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Unwilling [OR] unable to give informed consent
  -  Patients who take antiviral agents (acyclovir, valacyclovir, famciclovir, ganciclovir, valganciclovir, foscarnet...)
  -  Patients complaining abdominal pain with NRS ≤ 5 [OR] improved symptom by gastrointestinal motility drug [OR] antacid
