Inclusion Criteria:
 - 30-60 years
 - Increased fasting blood glucose from 6.1 to 7.0 mmol / L [OR]
 - Decreased glucose tolerance of 7 [OR] 8 to 11.1 mmol / L [OR]
 - Glycated hemoglobin with values between 5.7 [AND] 6.4%
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Use hypoglycemic [OR] hypolipidemic drugs,
 - Use dietary supplements (vitamins, [OR] minerals, [OR] bioflavonoids, [OR] prebiotics, [OR] probiotics [OR] or other bioactive compounds),
 - Exercise intensely (more than 10 hours per week)
 - History of cardiovascular diseases [OR] diabetes mellitus [OR] liver [OR] and kidney disease.
