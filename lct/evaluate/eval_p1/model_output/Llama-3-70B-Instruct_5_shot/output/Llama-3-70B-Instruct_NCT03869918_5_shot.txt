Inclusion Criteria:
 1. female
 2. age 60+ years
 3. community dwelling
 4. Moderate [AND] to severe UUI (as measured by International Consultation on Incontinence Questionnaire Short Form)
 5. low physical activity (Physical Activity Staging questionnaire)
 6. cognitively able [AND] to complete the study (in opinion of the referring provider) Women currently on anti-cholinergic medication for urinary incontinence will be allowed if they meet the above criteria.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. cognitive impairment (in opinion of the referring provider)
 2. unable [OR] to provide informed consent [OR] communicate in English
 3. desire [OR] for surgical management
 4. osteoporosis
 5. lack [OR] of medical clearance from the physician.

Note: I've inserted the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] into the eligibility criteria as per the instructions. However, I want to highlight that some of the criteria may not necessarily require logical operators, as they are standalone statements.
