Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with a endovenous pacemaker [OR] or defibrillator indication.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pre-existing ipsilateral pacing electrode.
 - Previous ipsilateral lymphadenectomy.
 - Indication of cardiac resynchronization therapy.

Note: There are no opportunities to insert [AND] or [NOT] operators in this specific eligibility criteria, as the conditions are simple and do not require logical conjunctions or negations.
