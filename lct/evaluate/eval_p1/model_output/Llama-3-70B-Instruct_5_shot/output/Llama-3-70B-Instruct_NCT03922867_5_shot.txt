Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Meets diagnostic criteria for fibromyalgia
  -  Has insomnia
  -  Have internet access [AND] a device that can access the web-based program.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Active sleep disorder [NOT] which is not treated (obstructive sleep apnea, [OR] obesity hypoventilation syndrome)
  -  Active psychiatric disorder (Bipolar affective disorder, [OR] anxiety, [OR] depression, [OR] schizophrenia) which is [NOT] optimally managed.
  -  Chronic fatigue syndrome
  -  Morbid obesity
