Inclusion Criteria:
  -  ASA grade I [OR] II [OR] III
  -  BMI under 36 kg/m^2
  -  Scheduled surgery for primary total hip [OR] knee replacement
  -  Age 18+ years
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Contraindications [OR] failure of neuraxial anesthesia
  -  Patients must [NOT] be outpatient total hip [OR] knee procedure
  -  Known intercurrent UTI, [OR] incontinence, [OR] urinary retention [NOT] addressed by pre-operative urologic consultation and correction
  -  Pregnancy
  -  Current nicotine, [OR] alcohol [OR] drug abusers
  -  Pre-operative narcotic use (any narcotic consumption within 3 days prior to surgery)
  -  Post- operative parenteral narcotic administration
  -  Allergy [OR] intolerance to liposomal bupivacaine, [OR] bupivacaine, [OR] celecoxib, [OR] dexamethasone [OR] and/or pregabalin
