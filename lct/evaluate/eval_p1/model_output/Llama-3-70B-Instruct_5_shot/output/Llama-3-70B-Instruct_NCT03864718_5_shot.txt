Inclusion Criteria:
  - complex anal fistula according to Parks classification
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Non cryptoglandular fistulas
  - acute sepsis
  - inflammatory bowel diseases
  - simple fistulas (defined as subcutaneous, [OR] intersphincteric [OR] and low transsphincteric fistula)
  - pregnant patients
  - ASA IV
  - patients <18
  - immunocompromised
  - patients who [NOT] refused consent.

Note: I inserted the [NOT] operator in the last exclusion criterion, as it seems to be a negation of the refusal of consent, meaning that patients who did not refuse consent are excluded. If this is not the case, please let me know and I'll correct it.
