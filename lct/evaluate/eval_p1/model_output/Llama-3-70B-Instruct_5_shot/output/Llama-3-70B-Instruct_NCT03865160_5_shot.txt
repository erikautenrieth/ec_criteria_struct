Inclusion Criteria:
 - Children aged 6 - 11 years [AND] with -1- to -10 diopters (D) myopia
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Any organic eye disease [OR] strabismus [OR] astigmatism [OR] and / or anisometropia >1.5 D [OR] prematurity [OR] use of mono- / multifocal contact lenses [OR] pre-treatment with atropine
