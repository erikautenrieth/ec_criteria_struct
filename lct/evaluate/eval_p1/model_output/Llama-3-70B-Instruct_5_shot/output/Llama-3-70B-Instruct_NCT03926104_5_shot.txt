Inclusion Criteria:
 - Ability to provide an informed consent
 - Age >18 years old
 - Elective abdominal aortic aneurysm surgery
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Emergency surgery
 - Age < 18 years old
 - Creatinine >2mg/dl
 - Diseases of ascending aorta, [OR] aortic arch [OR] or thoracic aorta
 - Chronic atrial fibrillation
 - Patient [NOT] refusal to provide informed consent
