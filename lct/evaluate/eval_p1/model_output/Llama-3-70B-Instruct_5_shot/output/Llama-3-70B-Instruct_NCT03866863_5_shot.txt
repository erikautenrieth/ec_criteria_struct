Inclusion Criteria:
 - All birth more than 24 + 0 weeks of amenorrhea [AND] at the maternity ward of the hospital Femme-Mère-Enfant
 - from 1st of january 2011 [AND] to 31 december 2017.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Infants born out of the hospital [AND] secondarily hospitalized in the hospital Femme-Mère-Enfant.
 - Multiple pregnancies,
 - children with congenital fetal anomalies
 - pregnancies [NOT] without first trimester ultrasound (to date the pregnancy)
