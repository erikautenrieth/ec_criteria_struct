Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age: 19~45
 - Body weight: 55kg or higher(female: 50kg or higher) [AND] BMI 18~27kg/m2
 - SBP 90~150mmHg, [AND] DBP 60-95mmHg
 - Fasting glucose 70~120mg/dL
 - Infertility
       1. Surgically infertile
       2. To prevent pregnancy, participants who agreed using 2 [OR] more contraceptive methods. Such as
  - Barrier methods: Condom, [OR] Diaphragm, [OR] Cervical cap (Pessary), [OR] Spermicide
  - Hormonal methods: Pills, [OR] Injection (Depot), [OR] Skin patch, [OR] Hormonal implant (Implanon), [OR] Vaginal ring
  - Intrauterine Devices (IUDs): Cooper IUD (Loop), [OR] Hormonal IUD (Mirena)
  - Natural methods: Basic body temperature, [OR] Ovulation period, [OR] Coitus interruptus, [OR] Abstinent
  - People who perfectly understood clinical trial [AND] independently decided to participate in clinical trial.
  - People who will be able to collect blood sample during clinical trial period.
  - People who are suitable to participate clinical trial by physical examination, [AND] lab test [AND] medical examination by interview.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Genetic problems such as galactose intolerance, [OR] Lapp lacatase deficiency, [OR] glucose-galactose malabsorption
  - Clinically significant disease such as liver, [OR] kidney, [OR] digestive, [OR] pulmonary, [OR] endocrine system, [OR] cardiovascular disease etc.
  - People who have gastrointestinal disease [OR] history of surgery which would affect absorption of drug.
  - History of clinically significant drug induced hyper-sensitive reaction [OR] drug related muscular disease
