Inclusion Criteria:
Age [≥ 18]
Indications of breast reconstruction: histologically confirmed breast cancer, [OR] precancerous lesions (DCIS, [OR] LCIS), [OR] mutation carrier with increased breast cancer risk, [OR] strong family history (lifetime risk > 15%)
The patient is capable to realise the nature, [AND] aims [AND] possible consequences of the clinical trial (MPG §20.2.1)
Patient information has been provided [AND] all written consents of the patient are available
Exclusion Criteria:
Metastatic breast cancer
Patient with known contraindications against mesh-assisted [OR] plastic-reconstructive breast surgery according to the instruction for use
Patient is [NOT] kept in an institution under judicial [OR] official orders (MPG §20.3)
[NOT] Participate in another operative clinical trial, [AND] if it relates to the area of reconstructive breast surgery [AND] and/or influences the primary endpoint of the clinical trial
