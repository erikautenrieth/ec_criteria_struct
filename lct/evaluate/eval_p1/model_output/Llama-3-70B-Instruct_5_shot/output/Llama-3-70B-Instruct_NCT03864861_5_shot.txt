Inclusion Criteria:
  -  All adult patients (aged ≥18 years) undergoing elective bariatric surgery [AND] by any approach (Includes surgery with laparoscopic, [OR] assisted laparoscopic approach [OR] or open approach) in a participating hospital during the 30-day cohort period [AND] with a planned overnight stay.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients undergoing emergency surgery
  -  Endoscopic procedures
  -  Patients [NOT] under 18 years of age
  -  Patients with previous bariatric surgery who undergo revision surgery
  -  Patients who [NOT] refuse to participate
