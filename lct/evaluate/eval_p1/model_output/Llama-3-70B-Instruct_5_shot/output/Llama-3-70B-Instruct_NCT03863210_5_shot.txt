Inclusion Criteria:
  -  female sex
  -  age 45 [AND] to 60 years
  -  one [OR] more CV risk factors [overweight [OR] obesity, i. e. body mass index (BMI) ≥25 kg/m2, [AND] or central obesity - waist circumference ≥88 cm, [OR] high blood pressure (systolic blood pressure ≥140 mm Hg [AND] or diastolic blood pressure ≥90 mm Hg), [OR] high blood cholesterol (≥5.2 mmol/L) [AND] active smoking]
  -  participants on antihypertensive therapy were also included in the study
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  current cardiovascular disease (CVD) ([AND] ischemic heart disease, [OR] peripheral artery disease, [AND] stroke)
  -  malignant diseases
  -  serious systemic [OR] mental diseases
