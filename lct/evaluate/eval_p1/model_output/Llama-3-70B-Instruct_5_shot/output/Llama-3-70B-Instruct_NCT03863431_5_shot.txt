Inclusion Criteria:
  -  18-40 years
  -  BMI = 18.5-29.9 kg/m2
  -  Male [OR] female
  -  Physically active (> 3 x 30 min moderate exercise per week)
  -  Non-smoker ([NOT] including the use of vaporisers [AND] e-cigarettes)
  -  Healthy ([NOT] determined by the School's standard health questionnaire)
  -  [NOT] No cardiometabolic [OR] inflammatory illness
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Outside of age-range
  -  Outside BMI range
  -  Inactive (< 3 x 30 min moderate exercise per week)
  -  Smoker
  -  Taking medication
  -  History of cardiometabolic [OR] inflammatory illness
  -  Vegetarian/ vegan ([NOT] as the high-fat diet is based on animal fats)
  -  Other dietary restrictions [AND] which prevent consumption of the experimental diet
  -  Consumption of probiotics within last 3 months ([NOT] these influence the gut microbiota)
  -  Participation in another clinical trial
  -  Those with known [OR] suspected food intolerances, [OR] allergies [OR] hypersensitivity
  -  Women who are known to be pregnant [OR] who are intending to become pregnant over the course of the study
