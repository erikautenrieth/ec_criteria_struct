Inclusion Criteria:
  - Clinically diagnosed facial skin aging.
  - Glogau photoaging score II [OR] III.
  - Body mass index ≥20 [AND] with adequate abdominal [OR] or other subcutaneous adipose tissue accessible for lipoaspiration.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - History of keloid formation.
  - Any coincidental chronic illness (e.g. metabolic, [OR] autoimmune [OR] endocrinal) [OR] malignancy.
  - Any bleeding [OR] coagulation disorder [OR] recent use of anticoagulant therapy.
  - Active infection.
  - History of any previous aesthetic procedure on the face within the past 6 months.
  - History of intake of anti-aging systemic [OR] topical medications within the previous 3 months.
