Inclusion Criteria:
Patients meet at least three out of the following six conditions:
  -  Any gender [AND] aged 50 years or above;
  -  Have less than 30 mins of morning stiffness;
  -  Crepitus on active motion;
  -  Bony tenderness;
  -  Bony enlargement;
  -  No palpable warmth.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Subjects who are [NOT] able to walk.
  -  Malignancy [OR] 
  -  Any acute medical condition [OR]
  -  Poorly controlled diabetes [OR] hypertension [OR]
  -  A motor [OR] sensory nerve defect [OR]
  -  Blood clotting disease [OR]
  -  Mental illness [OR]
  -  Dementia [OR]
  -  Mental retardation [OR] other abnormal person on the organic mind [OR]
  -  Subjects with intra-articular solid [OR] hyaluronic acid injection in the past 3 months.
  -  Knee surgery [OR]
  -  Knee trauma [OR]
  -  Congenital knee deformation [OR]
  -  Severe knee varus [OR] valgus deformation [OR]
  -  Endocrine [OR] metabolic [OR] infectious [OR] inflammatory [OR] secondary degenerative knee arthritis caused by problems with rheumatic immune diseases [OR]
  -  Subjects who are hypersensitive to needles.
  -  Subjects who are [NOT] willing to cooperate [OR] sign the subject consent.
