Inclusion Criteria:
 - elective cardiac surgery.
 - age 18-65 years.
 - patients with median sternotomy.
 - body mass index <30kg.m2.
 - left ventricular ejection fraction >50%.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - significant aortic stenosis.
 - left main coronary artery disease.
 - patient on anti-coagulants.
 - pre-existing respiratory [OR] neurological [OR] renal disease.
 - CSF [OR] or blood tap during the procedure.
 - failure to thread the catheter.
 - anomalies of vertebral column.

Note: I did not insert [NOT] operators as they were not necessary in this case. If you want me to insert [NOT] operators, please let me know where you want them to be inserted.
