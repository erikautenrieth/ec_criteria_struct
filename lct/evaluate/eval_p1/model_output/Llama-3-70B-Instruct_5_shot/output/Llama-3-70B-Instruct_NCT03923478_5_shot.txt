Inclusion Criteria:
 - Established diagnosis of UC for at least 3 months prior to screening, with minimum disease extent of 15 cm from the anal verge
 - Mildly [OR] to moderately active UC
 - Inadequate response to ongoing treatment with oral mesalamine ≥2.4 g/day for ≥4 weeks from screening visit
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Possible [OR] confirmed diagnosis of Crohn's Disease [OR] or other forms of inflammatory bowel disorders
 - Ongoing [OR] failed prior treatment for UC with methotrexate, azathioprine, [OR] 6-mercaptopurine, [OR] cyclosporine, [OR] tofacitinib, [OR] mycophenolate mofetil, [OR] sirolimus (rapamycin), [OR] thalidomide, [OR] tacrolimus (FK-506), [OR] or biologics (e.g., TNF-alpha-antagonists, [OR] anti-integrin therapies, [OR] or agents targeting IL-12 [OR] or IL-23, etc.)
 - Any immunosuppressive condition [OR] treatment with immunosuppressive medications
 - History of prior surgical intervention in any region of the gastrointestinal tract (excluding minor surgery)
 - Prior diagnosis of any cardiovascular, [OR] renal, [OR] hepatic, [OR] endocrine, [OR] infectious, [OR] hematological, [OR] oncologic, [OR] neuro-psychiatric [OR] or immune-mediated disorder, which in the opinion of the Principal Investigator might impact the subject's safety [OR] compliance, [OR] or the interpretation of results
 - Treatment with any other investigational drugs ≤12 weeks prior to baseline visit
 - The participant has a condition [OR] is in a situation which, in the Principal Investigator's opinion, may put the participant at significant risk, [OR] may confound the study results, [OR] or may interfere significantly with the participant's participation in the study
