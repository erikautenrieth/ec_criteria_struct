Inclusion Criteria:
 - central venous catheter more than 48 hours in situ
 - consent of a patient

Exclusion Criteria:
 - technical difficulties of ultrasound machine
 - unfavorable local findings - inflammation of skin [OR] excoriation etc.

Note: There are no logical operators needed in the Inclusion Criteria as they are two separate conditions. In the Exclusion Criteria, I added an [OR] operator between "inflammation of skin" and "excoriation etc." to indicate that either of these conditions would exclude a patient from the study.
