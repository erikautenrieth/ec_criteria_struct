Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients scheduled for first-time non-emergent open-heart surgery for coronary artery bypass graft (CABG) revascularization, [OR] valvular repair [OR] or replacement, [OR] combined CABG with valve repair [OR] or replacement, [OR] or valve sparing aortic root repair
  -  Normal sinus rhythm
  -  [NOT] No documented history of atrial fibrillation
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Prior surgical procedures involving heart surgery [AND] cardiopulmonary bypass
  -  Any prior documented history of atrial fibrillation, [OR] atrial flutter [OR] or atrial tachycardia lasting longer than 30 seconds
  -  Prior history of Amiodarone treatment for the management of supraventricular tachy-arrhythmias
  -  Patients requiring surgical treatment of atrial arrhythmias such as pulmonary vein isolation [OR] or Maze procedure.
