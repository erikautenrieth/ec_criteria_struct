Inclusion Criteria:
 - Majors patients
 - Score American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) 1 [OR] 2
 - Managed in the operating theater of the University Hospital of Saint-Etienne [AND] for a neurosurgical intervention.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Single [OR] bilateral eye surgery modifying the possibilities of variation of the pupillary diameter
 - Having been asleep under general anesthesia in the 7 days prior to the current surgery
 - History of Parkinson's disease [OR] insulin-dependent [OR] non-insulin-dependent diabetes at a dysautonomic stage [OR] chronic alcoholism at a dysautonomous stage
