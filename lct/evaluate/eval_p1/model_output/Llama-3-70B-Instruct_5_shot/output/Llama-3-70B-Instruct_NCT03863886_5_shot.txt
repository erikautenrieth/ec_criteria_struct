Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adults patients ≥ 18 years of age [AND] with CD with at least colonic involvement, [OR] UC, [OR] non-IBD controls who have been referred for colonoscopy for clinical reasons. The clinical reasons may include colorectal cancer screening, [OR] surveillance, [OR] diagnostic for CD [OR] UC flare, [OR] or gastrointestinal symptoms.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pregnant patients.
  -  Patients with known current colorectal cancer, [OR] infectious colitis, [OR] diverticulitis, [OR] or microscopic colitis.
  -  Patients who have undergone surgery involving the cecum [OR] or rectum.
