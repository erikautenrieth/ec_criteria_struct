Inclusion Criteria:
  -  American Society of Anesthesiologists GradeⅠ－II, Patients undergo elective surgery for benign Reproductive diseases (including laparoscopic ovarian cyst removal, laparoscopic tubal ligation, laparoscopic tubal drainage, laparoscopic salpingoplasty, etc.) [OR] or breast disease.
  -  Aged 18-45 years
  -  Body Mass Index 18-25kg/m2, [AND] body weight 40-65 kilograms
  -  Agreed to participate [AND] the research
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Difficult airway
  -  upper respiratory tract infection within 2 weeks
  -  history of allergy [OR] long-term use of propofol [AND] opioids
  -  self-rating anxiety scale before operation indicates anxiety
  -  pregnancy [OR] lactation
