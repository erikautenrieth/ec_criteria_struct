Inclusion Criteria:
 - Otherwise healthy men [AND] women
 - Aged 18-60 years
 - Continuous pain (>3days pain/week) episode lasting at least 3 months, sufficient to limit daily activities
 - Pain in the region posteriorly between the inferior border of the 12th rib and the lower gluteal fold (low back pain) at the time of recruitment
 - Able to speak, [AND] read [AND] understand English

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnancy
 - Low back pain associated with menstruation
 - Currently seeking active treatment for low back pain
 - Red flags symptoms (i.e. fever, [OR] malaise, [OR] progressive neurologic deficit, [OR] significant trauma, [OR] prolonged corticosteroid use [OR] or osteoporosis, [OR] pain worst at night, [OR] urinary [OR] or faecal incontinence, [OR] or unintended weight loss)
 - Drug addiction defined as the use of cannabis, [OR] opioids [OR] or other drugs
 - Current [OR] or previous neurologic, [OR] musculoskeletal, [OR] mental, [OR] or other illnesses which may affect the trial
 - Current [OR] or previous chronic [OR] or recurrent pain condition other than low back pain
 - Current regular use of analgesic medication [OR] or other medication which may affect the trial
 - Lack of ability to cooperate
