Inclusion Criteria:
 - First time stroke,
 - Patients who has spasticity due to stroke,
 - At least 6 months after stroke,
 - Individuals between the ages of 18 [AND] 75 will be included in the study.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Any contraindication for dry needling (e.g., anticoagulants, [OR] infections, [OR] bleeding, etc.),
 - Having diabetes,
 - Having cardiovascular diseases,
 - Having any other neurological problems,
 - Application of Botox within 6 months prior to the study,
 - Individuals [NOT] included in another treatment program will not be included in the study.
