Inclusion Criteria:
  - patients with lumbar pain present for more than 12 weeks,
  - pain during changing of the position,
  - radicular pain.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Cauda equina syndrome,
  - ankylosing spondylitis,
  - thoracic deformities (pectus carinatum [OR] excavatum),
  - spina bifida,
  - fractures,
  - post-operative spinal conditions,
  - diabetes,
  - inflammatory processes,
  - tumors,
  - pregnancy.

Note: There are no opportunities to insert [AND] or [NOT] operators in this specific eligibility criteria, as the conditions are listed as separate exclusions.
