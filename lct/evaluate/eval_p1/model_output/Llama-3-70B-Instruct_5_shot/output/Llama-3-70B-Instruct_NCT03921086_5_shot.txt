Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adult (older than 18 years)
  -  Elective surgery [AND] at preoperative in-hospital visit
  -  All stage 1 [OR] and stage 2 hypertensive patients as defined by the South African Hypertension Practice Guideline 2014
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient refusal
  -  Day case surgery
  -  Obstetric [OR] and cardiac surgery
  -  Patients with severe hypertension (>180/110 mmHg) [NOT] as defined by the South African Hypertension Practice Guideline 2014
