Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Meets the diagnostic criteria for sepsis-3 developed by the American Society of Critical Care Medicine (SCCM)/European Critical Care Medicine Association (ESICM) [AND]
  -  Age ≥18 years old [AND] and age ≤80 years old.
  -  Procalcitonin ≥2ng/ml

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Age [NOT] <18 years, or age [NOT] >80 years.
  -  Pregnancy [OR] or lactating
  -  A solid-organ [OR] or bone marrow transplant patients.
  -  Patients with myocardial infarction within the past 3 months [NOT].
  -  Advanced pulmonary fibrosis [NOT].
  -  Patients with cardiopulmonary resuscitation before enrollment [NOT].
  -  HIV-positive patients [NOT].
  -  Granulocyte-deficient patients [NOT].
  -  Blood/lymphatic system tumors are not in remission [NOT].
  -  Patients with limited care (lack of commitment to full, aggressive life support) [NOT].
  -  Patients with long-term use of immunosuppressive drugs or with immunodeficiency [NOT].
  -  Patients with advanced tumors [NOT].
  -  Patients combined with non-infectious factors leading to death (uncontrollable major bleeding, brain hernia, etc.) [NOT].
  -  Surgically unresolved infection sources (such as some intraperitoneal infection, etc.) [NOT].
  -  Patients allergic to granisetron [NOT].
  -  Patients with intestinal obstruction [NOT].
