Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adult [AND] (older than 18 years)
 - Elective surgery [AND] at preoperative in-hospital visit
 - All stage 1 [OR] and stage 2 hypertensive patients as defined by the South African Hypertension Practice Guideline 2014
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patient [NOT] refusal
 - Day case surgery
 - Obstetric [OR] and cardiac surgery
 - Patients with severe hypertension [NOT] (>180/110 mmHg) as defined by the South African Hypertension Practice Guideline 2014
