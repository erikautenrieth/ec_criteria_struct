Inclusion Criteria:
  - Parturients with age ≥18 years;
  - Presence of prenatal depression (EPDS score ≥10) [AND] ;
  - Provide written informed consents.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - History of psychiatric disease [NOT] (schizophrenia) or communication barriers that prevent normal communication before childbirth;
  - Severe complications during pregnancy (such as severe preeclampsia, placenta accreta, [OR] or HELLP [Hemolysis, Elevated Liver enzymes and Low Platelets] syndrome);
  - ASA physical status classification ≥III;
  - Presence of contraindications to ketamine, including refractory hypertension, severe cardiovascular disease [NOT] (heart function classification ≥III), or hyperthyroidism;
  - Refuse to participate.
