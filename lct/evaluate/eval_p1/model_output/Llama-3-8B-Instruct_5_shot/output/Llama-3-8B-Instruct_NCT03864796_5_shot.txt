Inclusion Criteria:
 - newly diagnosed primary immune thrombocytopenic patients
Exclusion Criteria:
 - We will exclude patients with any other possible cause of thrombocytopenia either [OR] immune [OR] non immune as:
 - Patients with [NOT] diabetes
 - HCV
 - other autoimmune disease [AND] (as SLE [OR] RA)
 - Chronic Liver [OR] kidney disease
