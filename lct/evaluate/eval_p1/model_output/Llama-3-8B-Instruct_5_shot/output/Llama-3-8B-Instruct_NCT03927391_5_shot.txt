Inclusion Criteria:
 - Frailᵃ male patients with prostate cancer who will start treatment with enzalutamide within label [AND] - Age at least 18 years
 - Patient who are able [AND] and willing to give written informed consent prior to screening
 - Patients from whom it is possible to collect blood samples
 - Patients who are willing to answer the questionnaires and test
 - Life expectancy of > 6 months
 - Capable of understanding [AND] and answering Dutch tests [AND] questionnaires, as determined by the investigator
ᵃ Frail is defined as:
  - a score on the comprehensive G8 assessment with cut-off ≤14 points [AND] - score ≥grade 1 for Central Nervous Disorders according to the Common Toxicity Criteria Adverse Event (CTCAE) criteria, of one of the following: Fatigue, Concentration impairment, cognitive disturbance, amnesia, depressed level of consciousness, memory impairment, hypersomnia.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - change in dose of opioids/sedatives/benzodiazepines during last 2 weeks before study [NOT]
 - Use of psychostimulants such as methylphenidate within 1 week of start of study
 - Diagnosed with medical conditions that affect cognition: Dementia, Alzheimer disease, Parkinson's disease, psychiatric disorders that affect cognition other than depression [OR] anxiety complaints related to the disease
 - Active infection [OR] or other comorbidities that may contribute to REDOSE, February 2019 Page 7 of 53 fatigue [OR] cognition change within 4 weeks of study entry
 - Clinical relevant anaemia
 - MoCa score <20
 - Hypersensitivity to the active substance [OR] to any of the excipients.
