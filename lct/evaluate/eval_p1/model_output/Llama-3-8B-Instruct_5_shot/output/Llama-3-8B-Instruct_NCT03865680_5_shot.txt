Inclusion Criteria:
 - Healthy children with no systemic diseases.
 - High caries risk children [AND] with the presence of at least one visible active WSL in primary teeth with ICDAS II score of 1, 2, or 3
 - Completion of a parental consent to participate in the study.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Selected tooth with [NOT] cavitated lesion.
 - Selected tooth with [NOT] enamel defects.
 - Participants [NOT] allergic to milk products.
