Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Signed informed consent
 2. Advanced cancers
 3. Immune checkpoint inhibitor therapy initiated [AND] within +/- 2wks
 4. Age >18y
 5. ECOG 0-3
 6. Patient [AND] compliant with the study procedures
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Immune checkpoint inhibitor therapy initiated [NOT] > 2wks ago
 2. General vulnerability [AND] affecting the participation in the trial
 3. No [NOT] internet access
