Inclusion Criteria:
 - ASA physical status 1 [AND] 2, underwent gynecological laparotomic surgery under general anesthesia, followed up in PACU (postoperative care unit)

Exclusion Criteria:
 - ASA 3 [OR] 4, mental retardation, psychiatric disease, history of malignant hyperthermia [AND] in patient [OR] her family, neurological disease, morbid obesity, history of asthma [AND] follow-up in the intensive care unit.
