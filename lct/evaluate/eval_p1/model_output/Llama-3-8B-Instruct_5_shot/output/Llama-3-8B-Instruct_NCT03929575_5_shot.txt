Inclusion Criteria:
 - Body Mass Index [AND] between 18.5 [AND] and 35 kg/m^2
 - Non-smoking [AND] (assessed by Health History Questionnaire)
 - English-speaking
 - Regular participation in physical activity [AND] (>4 hours per week)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Medical conditions [NOT] that prohibit physical activity (assessed by Health History Questionnaire)
 - Pregnant women [OR] or women expecting/trying to become pregnant
 - BMI [NOT] greater than or equal to 35 kg/m^2
 - Current smoker [NOT] (assessed by Health History Questionnaire)
