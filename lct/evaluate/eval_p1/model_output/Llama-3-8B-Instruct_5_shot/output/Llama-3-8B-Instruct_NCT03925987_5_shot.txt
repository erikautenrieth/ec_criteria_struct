Inclusion Criteria:
  1. DSM-V diagnosis of an Anxiety disorder [AND] (Generalized Anxiety Disorder, Social Anxiety Disorder, Panic Disorder, Agoraphobia)
  2. Anxiety Sensitivity Index (ASI-3) total score > 29
  3. Overall Anxiety Severity and Impairment Scale (OASIS) score > 8
  4. Between 18 [AND] 55 years of age
  5. Subject must be "treatment-seeking" [AND] willing to enroll in the 10-week group-based Exposure Therapy
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Comorbid [NOT] Bipolar Disorder, Schizophrenia, Anorexia [OR] or Bulimia Nervosa, [OR] or Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder
  2. Current [NOT] Substance Use Disorder > moderate (within the past 6 months)
  3. Currently [NOT] taking a psychotropic medication
  4. Currently [NOT] receiving psychotherapy/counseling for anxiety
  5. Currently [NOT] being treated as an inpatient
  6. Active [NOT] suicidal ideation with intent [OR] plan
  7. History of [NOT] neurological, cardiovascular [OR] or respiratory problems (e.g., asthma, severe brain injury)
