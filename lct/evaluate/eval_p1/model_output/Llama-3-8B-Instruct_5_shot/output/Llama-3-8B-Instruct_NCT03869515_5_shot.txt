Inclusion Criteria:
  - The chILD syndrome exists when a child with DLD has had the common causes of DLD [NOT] excluded as the primary diagnosis and has at least three of the following four criteria: (1) respiratory symptoms (e.g., cough, rapid [AND] or difficult breathing, or exercise intolerance); (2) respiratory signs (e.g., resting tachypnea, adventitious sounds, retractions, digital clubbing, failure to thrive, or respiratory failure); (3) hypoxemia; [AND] (4) diffuse abnormalities on CXR [OR] or a CT scan.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - These include cystic fibrosis, [OR] congenital [OR] acquired immunodeficiency, congenital heart disease, bronchopulmonary dysplasia, pulmonary infection, primary ciliary dyskinesia [AND] presenting with newborn respiratory distress [AND] recurrent aspiration.
