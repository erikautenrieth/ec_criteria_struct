Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of ALS [OR] PLS
 - Age greater than 18 years
 - Clinically significant spasticity.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Individuals currently taking carbidopa-levodopa [OR] with known hypersensitivity of any component of carbidopa-levodopa [NOT]
 - Narrow-angle glaucoma
 - Current use of a non-selective monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI)
 - History of malignant melanoma [OR] or suspicious skin lesions
 - History of depression [AND] suicidal ideation [OR] psychosis
 - History of myocardial infarction [OR] ventricular arrhythmia [OR] severe cardiopulmonary disease
 - Uncontrolled hypertension
 - Asthma
 - Renal disease
 - Hepatic disease
 - Endocrine disease
 - History of peptic ulcer
 - Pregnant [AND] and/or breastfeeding
 - Current participation in another interventional study
