Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients age: ≥ 22 years
  - Patients presenting for routine colonoscopy for screening [AND] or surveillance purposes.
  - Willingness to undergo two withdrawals [AND] with [OR] without the use of computer-aided software while undergoing conventional colonoscopy with sedation
  - Ability to provide written, informed consent [AND] understand the responsibilities of trial participation
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Minors [NOT] aged < 22 years.
  - People with [NOT] diminished cognitive capacity
  - Patients undergoing diagnostic colonoscopy [NOT] (e.g. as an evaluation for active gastrointestinal bleed, referring collectively to the stomach and the small and large intestine).
  - Patients with [NOT] incomplete colonoscopies (those where endoscopists did not successfully intubate the cecum due to technical difficulties [OR] or poor bowel preparation)
  - Patients that have [NOT] standard contraindications to colonoscopy in general (e.g. documented acute diverticulitis, fulminant colitis [AND] and known or suspected perforation).
  - Patients with [NOT] inflammatory bowel disease
  - Patients with [NOT] any polypoid/ulcerated lesion > 2 cm concerning for invasive cancer on endoscopy
  - Patients referred for endoscopic mucosal resection (EMR), which is a procedure to remove early-stage cancer [AND] and precancerous growths from the lining of the digestive tract.
