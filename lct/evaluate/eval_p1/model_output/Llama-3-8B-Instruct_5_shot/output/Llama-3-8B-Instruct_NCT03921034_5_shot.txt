Inclusion Criteria:
 - Unilateral, primary tricompartment total knee arthroplasty
 - Age 18 years [AND] or older
 - ASA I-III
 - Eligible for spinal [OR] combined spinal epidural anesthetic
 - Able to speak, read, [AND] understand English
 - Willing to participate in the trial
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Contraindication to regional anesthesia [OR] peripheral nerve blocks
 - Allergy to local anesthetics [NOT]
 - Allergy to NSAIDs [NOT]
 - Chronic renal insufficiency with Cr > 1.4 [OR] GFR < 60
 - Have chronic pain that is not related to their knee joint [NOT]
 - Have been using opioids on a chronic basis (daily or almost daily opioid use for 3 months or longer) [NOT]
 - Have a pre-existing peripheral neuropathy involving the operative site [NOT]
 - Body mass index greater than or equal to 40 [NOT]
