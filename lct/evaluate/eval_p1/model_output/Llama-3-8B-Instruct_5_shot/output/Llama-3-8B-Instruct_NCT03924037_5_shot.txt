Inclusion Criteria:
 - American Indian youth aged 13-24 years old [AND] who are San Felipe tribal members living in the Pueblo
 - Endorse suicidal ideation [OR] or behaviors on a universal screen by their primary care provider.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Younger than age of 13 years old [NOT] or
 - Older than the age of 24 [NOT],
 - Not American Indian [NOT],
 - Youth who do not receive health services at either of the IHS clinics at the Pueblo of San Felipe [NOT],
 - Youth who screen negative for suicidal ideation [AND] and/or behavior [NOT],
 - Refuse to participate in the study [NOT] and/or
 - Cognitively unable to provide informed consent as demonstrated by a brief cognitive screen prior to completion of the baseline interview [NOT].
