Inclusion Criteria:
 - patients with lumbar pain present for more than 12 weeks [AND] pain during changing of the position [AND] radicular pain.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Cauda equina syndrome
 - ankylosing spondylitis
 - thoracic deformities (pectus carinatum [OR] excavatum)
 - spina bifida
 - fractures
 - post-operative spinal conditions
 - diabetes [NOT] or inflammatory processes
 - tumors
 - pregnancy
