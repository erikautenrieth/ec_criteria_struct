Inclusion criteria:
  - Women [AND] Men (Women premenopausal)
  - BMI 29-37
  - Weight stable
  - Not pregnant/nursing
Exclusion criteria:
  - Ischemic heart disease
  - Atherosclerotic valvular disease
  - Smokers (>20 cigarettes per week) [NOT]
  - Bilateral oophorectomy
  - Concomitant use of medications that can alter serum lipid profile:
       - High dose fish oil (>3g per day) [OR]
       - STATINS (if yes hold for 6 weeks and receive PCP's approval) [NOT]
       - Niacin [OR]
       - Fibrates [OR]
       - thiazolidinediones [OR]
       - Beta-blockers [OR]
       - Atypical antipsychotics [OR]
  - Lidocaine [OR] Niacin/Niaspan allergy
  - Subjects with 1.5 times upper limit of normal of serum creatinine [NOT] Alkaline phosphatase [NOT] Aspartate aminotransferase (AST) [NOT] Alanine aminotransferase (ALT) [NOT] unless participant has fatty liver disease, Total bilirubin [NOT] unless the patient has documented Gilbert's syndrome [NOT]
