Inclusion Criteria:
• patients undergoing elective, primary knee joint replacement in combined general anaesthesia

Exclusion Criteria:
  - heart insufficiency [NOT] NYHA >2
  - liver insufficiency [NOT] > CHILD B
  - evidence of diabetic polyneuropathy
  - severe adipositas [AND] BMI >40
  - patients [NOT] < 18 years
  - pregnancy
  - in case of [NOT] police custody
  - participation in a paralleled interventional RCT [OR] in a time frame of 30 days
  - chronic opioid therapy [NOT] >3 months before scheduled surgery
  - allergy [OR] against medication required for surgery [OR] or anaesthesia
