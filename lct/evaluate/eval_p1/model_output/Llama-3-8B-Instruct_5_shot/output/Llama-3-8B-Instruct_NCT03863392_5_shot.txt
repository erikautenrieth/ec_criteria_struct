Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Primigravida [OR] Primipara
  -  Pregnancy between 36 [AND] 42 weeks of gestation.
  -  A live singleton fetus [AND] in cephalic presentation.
  -  No history of uterine [NOT] surgery.
  -  Clinically adequate [NOT] pelvis.
  -  Modified Bishop's score <5.
  -  Reactive [NOT] Non-stress test (NST).
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  •Known hypersensitivity [OR] or contraindications to oral misoprostol (uterine [NOT] surgery).
  -  Any antenatal [NOT] medical complications.
  -  A situation requiring LSCS (Maternal [OR] Fetal) e.g:fetal distress
  -  Non-reactive [NOT] NST.
  -  Patient's [NOT] refusal to give consent
