Inclusion Criteria:
  - Histology: Part 1: Metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC) [AND] with histologic confirmation of adenocarcinoma. Adenocarcinoma with small-cell [OR] neuroendocrine features is allowed. mCRPC is defined by prostate Cancer Working Group (PCWG) [3] criteria. Part 2: mCRPC [AND] as defined above [OR] pathologically confirmed metastatic renal cell carcinoma (RCC) [AND] as defined by world health organization (WHO) [2016] Classifications
  - Measurable [OR] evaluable disease: Part 1: Either measurable [OR] evaluable disease for prostate cancer. Part 2: At least one measurable lesion [AND] as per RECIST v1.1.
  - Evidence of disease progression on prior therapy [AND] that requires a new line of treatment
  - Participants with accessible lesions enrolled in selected pharmacokinetic (PK)/pharmacodynamics (PD) cohorts [AND] in Part 2 must agree to undergo the mandatory fresh tumor biopsies, unless collection of the biopsy presents a safety risk [NOT]
Exclusion Criteria:
  - At least 2 weeks between prior anticancer treatment (including radiotherapy) discontinuation [AND] and the first dose of study drug, and toxicities have returned to Grade [NOT] less than or equal to (<=) 1 [OR] baseline
  - Prior treatment with prostate-specific membrane antigen (PSMA)-targeted therapy [EXCEPT] for PSMA-targeted vaccine is permitted [NOT]
  - Solid organ [OR] bone marrow transplantation
  - Seizure [OR] known condition that may predispose to seizure [OR] intracranial masses
  - Other active malignancy requiring systemic treatment [AND] <=12 months prior to enrollment
