Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Infants [AND] is inborn
 2. Infant [AND] is ≤ 750 grams at birth [AND] and/or < 27 weeks gestation at birth by best OB estimate
 3. Infant [AND] is 12-36 hours of age.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Unable to enroll infant by 36 hours of age [NOT]
 2. Previous phototherapy [NOT]
 3. Known hemolytic disease [NOT]
 4. TSB reported as >6.0 mg/dL before 12 hours age [NOT]
 5. Major anomaly [NOT]
 6. Overt nonbacterial infection [NOT]
 7. Infant is likely to expire soon: Limiting or withdrawal of intensive care is being recommended to the parents, the parents are requesting withdrawal of care, or the pH is < 6.80 [OR] or persistent bradycardia with hypoxemia for >2h [NOT]
