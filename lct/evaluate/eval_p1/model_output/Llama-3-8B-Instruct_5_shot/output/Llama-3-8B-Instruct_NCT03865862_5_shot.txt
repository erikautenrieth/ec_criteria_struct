Inclusion Criteria:
 - Volleyball players
 - Federated [AND] that at the time of the study compete in clubs of the Community of Madrid
 - Over 18 years old.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Present a medical diagnosis of injury [OR] illness
 - Have suffered a knee injury in the 6 months prior to the study
 - Doing some pharmacological [OR] physiotherapy treatment external to the study [NOT]
 - Not signed the informed consent document.
