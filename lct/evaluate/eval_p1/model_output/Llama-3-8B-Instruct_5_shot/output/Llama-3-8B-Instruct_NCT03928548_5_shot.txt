Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adult [AND] pediatric acute care patients at participating facility
 - Newly identified as needing in-patient nutrition care through current facility policy
 - Receive initial nutrition care one day before study enrollment day
 - Patient is over one month of age [AND] including infants, children, teenagers, [AND] and adult patients who are intubated [OR] and/or sedated
 - Patient, parent, legal guardian [OR] or legally authorized representative provide informed consent; children 7 and older provide assent
 - Patient [AND] or parent, legal guardian [OR] or legally authorized representative are fluent in English [OR] or Spanish
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Received nutrition care previously during current admission
 - Pregnant [NOT] women
 - Prisoners
 - Infants currently in the neonatal intensive care unit (NICU) with a diagnosis of prematurity
 - Neonates of uncertain viability [OR] or non-viable neonates
 - Patients receiving palliative/hospice care
 - Patient admitted for long-term acute care (LTAC)
