Inclusion Criteria:
  - Men [AND] women
  - Age 18 [AND] to 55
  - Meet the physical standards for military personnel
  - Weigh less than 250 pounds
  - Be able to remain in the study position [AND] for the duration of the study.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Previous injuries to sacrum/buttocks with scarring [NOT]
  - History of pressure injuries [NOT]
  - Cardiovascular disease [OR] neurological disease that would affect vascular response
  - Diabetes [OR] history of malignancy
  - Current skin condition (e.g., eczema [OR] or psoriasis)
  - Current neck/back pain [OR] history of chronic neck/back pain
  - Known vertebral/spinal cord disease/injury (scoliosis [OR] kyphosis [OR] or lordosis)
  - Self-reported pregnancy [NOT]
  - Concern about ability to remain in the vacuum spine board for 120 minutes [NOT]
