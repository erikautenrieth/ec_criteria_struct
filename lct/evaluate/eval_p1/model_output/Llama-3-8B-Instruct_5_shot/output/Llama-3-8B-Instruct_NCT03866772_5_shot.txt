Inclusion Criteria:
 - unfavourable cervix (Bishop score [≤] 4)
 - indication for induction of labor (medical [OR] or obstetrical)
 - 37 completed gestational weeks
 - vertex presentation
 - singleton pregnancy
 - intact membranes
Exclusion Criteria:
 - previous [NOT] cesarean delivery
 - previous uterine surgery (e.g.: myomectomy)
 - noncephalic presentation
 - multiple pregnancy
 - pre-eclampsia with severe features
 - oligohydramnios (Maximal vertical pocket [≤] 2)
 - estimated fetal weight [<] 10% percentile
 - any [NOT] contraindication to Vaginal delivery
