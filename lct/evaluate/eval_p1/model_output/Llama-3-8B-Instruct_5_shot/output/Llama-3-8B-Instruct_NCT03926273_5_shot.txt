Inclusion Criteria:
 - All participants were included if their age [AND] more than or equal to 18 years. Body mass index < 30 kg/m2, total cholesterol < 200mg/dl, serum triglycerides < 200 mg/dl [AND] and random blood glucose level < 126 mg/dl.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Participants [NOT] had acute [OR] chronic metabolic disorders (liver, kidney, thyroid, endocrine [OR] or gastrointestinal disorders).
 - Females with [NOT] irregular menstrual cycle [OR] on estrogen therapy (7).
 - Participants with organic cerebral lesion [NOT] that was detected on cranial computed tomography (CT) [OR] cranial magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).
