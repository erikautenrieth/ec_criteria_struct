Inclusion Criteria:
For all participants:
  - age from 50 to 75 years old [AND] affiliated to the French health care system
For AD patients:
  - meeting International Working Group (IWG)-2 criteria for diagnosis [AND] Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) ≥20 (Greco version)
For healthy volunteers:
  - MMSE > 25 [AND] Dubois 5 words test ≥ 9
Exclusion Criteria:
For all participants:
  - Pregnancy [OR] people not able to give consent
  - contraindication for MRI (metallic body parts, claustrophobia) [OR] aphasia, apraxia, or agnosia
  - neurological (other than AD) [OR] any other serious disease (cancer, addiction, systemic disease)
  - sleep apnea [OR] major depression or anxiety for more than 3 months (Beck > 10) [OR] psychiatric disease
  - documented epilepsy [OR] use of neuroleptics (more than one dose per day) [OR] use of antiepileptics [OR] use of benzodiazepines at a dose superior or equal to two intakes per day [OR] use of antidepressants [OR] restless leg syndrome treated by dopaminergic agonists
For AD patients:
  - other causes of dementia [OR] non-degenerative neurological lesions [OR] white matter hypersignals [OR] acute cognitive deficits
