Inclusion Criteria:
 - ASA I [OR] II physical status
 - 18-65 years
 - Scheduled for elective colonoscopy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - History of allergy [NOT] to any of the study drugs
 - [NOT] alcohol or drug abuse.
 - Second [AND] third-degree heart block.
 - Morbid [NOT] obesity.
 - Pregnant [OR] and lactating women.
 - Psychiatric [NOT] disorders.
 - Severe [NOT] cardiac, respiratory, renal, and liver diseases
