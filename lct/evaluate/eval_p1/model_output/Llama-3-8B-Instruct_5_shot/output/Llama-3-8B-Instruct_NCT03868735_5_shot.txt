Inclusion Criteria:
 - Orally intubated patient with endotracheal tube
 - Mechanically ventilated [AND] less than 24 hours prior to enrollment
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Less than 18 years old
 - Tracheostomy tube [NOT]
 - Extracorporeal membrane oxygenation
 - Transfer from outside hospital [AND] with more than 24 hours of mechanical ventilation
