For community patients :
Inclusion Criteria:
  - the selection criteria for blood donation
  - age ≥ 18 years old [AND] no refusal of the person
Exclusion Criteria:
  - None

For hospitalized patients :
Inclusion Criteria:
  - age ≥ 18 years old [AND] hospitalization in one of the services participating in the study [AND] no refusal of the person
Exclusion Criteria:
  - None
