Inclusion Criteria:
Type II diabetic patients, 18 years and older, were included if the participants had:
  1. severe non-PDR [AND] in either eyes with/without diabetic macular edema;
  2. ETDRS letters-measured best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) ranging from 20/40 [OR] to 20/320 Snellen equivalent;     
  3. no previous treatment [NOT] of any type in either eye.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. history of prior laser treatment [OR] or vitrectomy in the study eye;
  2. history of thromboembolic event - including myocardial infarction [OR] or cerebral vascular accident;
  3. major surgery within the prior 6 months [AND] or planned within the next 28 days;
  4. history of glaucoma [OR] or ocular hypertension;
  5. loss of vision as a result of other causes [NOT] related to diabetes;
  6. history of systemic corticosteroid therapy within the last 3 months;
  7. severe systemic disease [NOT] other than diabetes mellitus;
  8. known coagulation abnormalities [OR] current use of anticoagulative medication [NOT] other than aspirin
  9. any condition that could affect followup [OR] or documentation (including preretinal [OR] or vitreous hemorrhage).
