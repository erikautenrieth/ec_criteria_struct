Inclusion Criteria:
 - age ≥ 45 [AND] ≤ 90 years;
 - patients with severe (70-99%) carotid stenosis, diagnosed with color doppler echography [OR] or angio-MRI [OR] or angiography;
 - written informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - carotid stenosis [NOT] <70% at carotid bifurcation [OR] diagnosed with color doppler echography [OR] or angio-MRI [OR] or angiography;
 - severe neoplasia;
 - participation to other clinical trial, ongoing [OR] or terminated less than one month before enrolment in this study.
