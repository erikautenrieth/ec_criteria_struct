Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients of both sexes [AND] with a diagnosis of stable CAD as follows: acute myocardial infarction [OR] or percutaneous coronary revascularization [OR] or coronary artery bypass graft surgery occurred [AND] at least 12 months before entering the study [OR] chronic stable angina.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Mental illness [AND] limiting the capacity of self-care [OR] any condition [AND] limiting seriously the life expectancy [NOT] less than 12 months.
