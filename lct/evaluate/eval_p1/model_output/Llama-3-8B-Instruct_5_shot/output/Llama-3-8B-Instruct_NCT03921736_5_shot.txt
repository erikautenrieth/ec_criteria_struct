Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Treatment-naive postmenopausal women
  -  Diagnosed hypertension (grade 1 [OR] or 2 according to ESH/ESC 2013 guidelines)
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  breast cancer [AND] in a first-degree relative
  -  hyperplasia [NOT] diagnosed by endometrial biopsy,
  -  history of thromboembolic diseases,
  -  current [OR] history of use of estrogen-progestin therapy [OR] or contraceptives
  -  diabetes,
  -  kidney failure,
  -  thyroid disease,
  -  heart [AND] other chronic diseases (secondary hypertension, atrial fibrillation)
