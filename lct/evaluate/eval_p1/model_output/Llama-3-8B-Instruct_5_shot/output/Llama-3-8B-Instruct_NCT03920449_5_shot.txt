Inclusion Criteria:
 - Chronic anal fissure lasting more than 6 weeks

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnant
 - cardiovascular disease [OR] heart failure
 - on antihypertensive medications
 - recurrent anal fissure [AND] after previous sphincterotomy
 - prior history of anal surgery
 - other benign anorectal diseases [OR] with inflammatory bowel diseases
