Inclusion Criteria:
 - Mechanically ventilated patients
 - ASAI [AND] ASAII [AND] ASAIII
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [OR] guardians refuse
 - Patients with pneumothorax
 - Patients with pleural effusion
 - Patients with surgical emphysema
 - Glasco coma score [NOT] more than 8
 - Patients on high inotropic support
 - patients with diaphragmatic paralysis
