Inclusion Criteria:
  - Over 18 years.
  - For the T2D study group: Diagnosed with type 2 diabetes.

Exclusion Criteria:
For subjects with type 2 diabetes:
  - Systemic inflammatory disease that requires active treatment [AND] not [NOT] controlled with medication.
  - Thyroid disease that requires active treatment [AND] not [NOT] controlled with medication.
  - Skin disease that requires active treatment [AND] causes significant changes in the area of the skin that is to be investigated [AND] not [NOT] controlled with medication.
  - Treatment with oral glucocorticoids.
  - Levels of SR, TSH, T4 or Hemoglobin in the blood pronouncedly outside the reference range.
  - Pregnancy.
For healthy controls:
  - Registered disease in the health declaration that requires continuous systemic treatment [AND] not [NOT] well controlled with medication. Exception to the above: well controlled hypertension in which case the subject should not have treatment with more than one antihypertensive drug, the drug must not be an alpha-blocker [OR] beta-blocker [OR] thiazide diuretic.
  - Skin disease that requires active treatment [AND] causes significant changes in the area of the skin that is to be investigated.
  - Blood pressure pronouncedly above 140/90 [AND] not [NOT] controlled with medication.
  - Blood test results pronouncedly outside the reference range.
  - Pregnancy.
