Inclusion Criteria
  - Adults [AND] age 18 years or older.
  - Diagnosed within 8 weeks with upfront resectable, [OR] borderline resectable, [OR] or locally advanced unresectable PDAC as defined by the National Comprehensive Cancer Network (NCCN).
  - Planning to undergo TNT at MGH.
  - Planning to receive modified FOLFIRINOX for neoadjuvant chemotherapy.
  - Planning to undergo surgical resection of PDAC at MGH.
  - Verbal fluency [NOT] in English.
Exclusion Criteria
  - Metastatic disease.
  - Previous treatment for pancreas adenocarcinoma [AND] and no prior therapy for any cancer within 5 years.
  - Known history of cognitive [OR] psychologic impairment.
  - Known history of physical impairment [such as] immobility, [OR] use of walking aids, [OR] required wheelchair use.
  - Known history of the following orthopedic comorbidities: [NOT] advanced osteoarthritis [OR] rheumatoid arthritis, [OR] mobility-limiting amputations, [OR] orthopedic injuries, [OR] widespread chronic pain (e.g. fibromyalgia).
  - Known history of the following pulmonary comorbidities: [NOT] severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) defined as FEV1 ≤ 50%, [OR] emphysema, [OR] interstitial lung disease, [OR] and/or use of supplemental oxygen.
  - Known history of the following cardiovascular comorbidities: [NOT] uncontrolled hypertension, [OR] severe congestive heart failure (NYHA Class IV), [OR] and/or myocardial infarction within the last 6 months.
  - Inability to tolerate diet through routine oral routes [AND] and/or patients with strict nutritional restrictions, [OR] allergies, [OR] and/or dietary preferences.
