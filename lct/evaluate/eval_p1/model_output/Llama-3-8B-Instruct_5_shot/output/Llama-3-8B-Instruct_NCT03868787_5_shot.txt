Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Women [AND] ≥ 18 years of age
 2. Gestational age between 14 weeks [AND] and 23 weeks 6 days
 3. Willing [AND] and able to sign an informed consent in English
 4. No [NOT] contraindications to TENS
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Incarceration
 2. Preterm Premature Rupture of Membranes [OR] or evidence of intra-amniotic infection
 3. Presence of implanted cardiac device
 4. Lack of sensation to touch on area of electrode placement
 5. Prior TENS use
 6. Opioid dependence
