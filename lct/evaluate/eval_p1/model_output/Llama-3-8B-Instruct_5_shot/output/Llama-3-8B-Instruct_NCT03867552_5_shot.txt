Inclusion Criteria:
 - Woman [AND] ≥ 18 years old
 - undergoing laparoscopic surgery for endometriosis, uterine myoma's, hysterectomy [OR] or colpopexy.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Women [NOT] < 18 years old
 - Males
 - Pregnancy
 - Conditions associated with chronic pain, such as peripheral neuropathy [OR] pathology of the vertebral column [OR] and osteo-articular disease.
 - Conditions causing acute pain [AND] e.g. abdominal trauma
