Inclusion Criteria:
Principal:
  -  normal psychomotor development
  -  informed consent signed by both parents [AND] and subject if able
  -  affiliated to social security regimen
Specific
Patients from "severe" [OR] and "benign" groups:
  -  focal age related epilepsy: BECTS, ABPE, ECSWS (ILAE criteria)
  -  children hospitalized for their follow-up
  -  normal neuroimaging
Control group
-children hospitalized for a non neurologic disease [NOT]
Exclusion Criteria:
Principal:
Psychiatric trouble (DSM V) [NOT]
Sensorial trouble without correction [NOT]
Poor command of French language [NOT]
Minor under care [NOT]
Specific
Patients from "severe" [OR] and "benign" groups:
  -  degenerative disease [NOT]
  -  abnormal neuroimaging [NOT]
  -  mental deficiency [NOT]
Control group
  -  neurologic trouble [NOT]
  -  abnormal sleep EEG [NOT]
  -  intellectual deficiency [NOT]
