Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Women 18 years of age [OR] or older at enrollment
 2. Has an established viable singleton pregnancy
 3. Is recommended for routine GDM screening [AND] between 24-28 weeks gestation
 4. Has confirmed gestational age of 24-28 weeks [NOT] at the Screening Visit
 5. Is willing [AND] and able to provide documentation of informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Has diabetes [NOT] before pregnancy [OR] has already been diagnosed with GDM
 2. Has contraindications [OR] for drinking oral glucose solution up to 100 g of sugar
 3. Is taking metformin [NOT] for any reason
 4. Has any concomitant illness, disease [OR] or condition that, in the clinical judgment of the investigator, is likely to prevent the subject from complying with any aspect of the protocol, or that may put the subject at unacceptable risk
