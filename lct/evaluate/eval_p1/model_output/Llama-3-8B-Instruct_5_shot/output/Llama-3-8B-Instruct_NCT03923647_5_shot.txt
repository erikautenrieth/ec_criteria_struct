Inclusion Criteria:
 - stable patients [AND] with definite occurrence of intra-abdominal injury
 - patients [AND] with equivocal result by clinical [OR] radiological evaluation
Exclusion Criteria:
 - patients [NOT] with penetrating abdominal trauma
 - stable patients [NOT] not in need for any intervention for conservative management
 - patients with marked haemodynamic instability
 - polytraumatized [OR] Patients
