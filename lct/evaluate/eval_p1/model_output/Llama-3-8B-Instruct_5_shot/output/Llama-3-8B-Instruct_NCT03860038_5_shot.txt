Inclusion criteria:
  1. Age ≥ 18, male [OR] female;
  2. Subject must have had documented MM [AND] MM;
  3. At screening phase, subject must have measurable disease [AND] measurable disease;
  4. Subject is in a state of progressive disease [AND] progressive disease;
  5. Subject must have life expectancy of no less than 6 months [AND] no less than 6 months;
  6. Subject must have an ECOG (Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group) performance status score of 0 [OR] 1 [OR] 2;
Exclusion criteria:
  1. Subject has received anti-CD38 monoclonal antibody treatment previously [AND] previously;
  2. Subject has received CAR-T cell therapy previously [AND] previously;
  3. Subject has previously received allogenic stem cell transplant [OR] or subject has received autologous stem cell transplant within 3 months before administration of the study agent [AND] within 3 months;
  4. Primary refractory multiple myeloma [AND] (subject failed to generate any minimal response [OR] any degree of response to any therapy);
  5. Subject has received anti-myeloma treatment (radiotherapy is excluded) [NOT] within 4 weeks [OR] 5 PK half-lives of the treatment, whichever longer, before the first study agent administration.
