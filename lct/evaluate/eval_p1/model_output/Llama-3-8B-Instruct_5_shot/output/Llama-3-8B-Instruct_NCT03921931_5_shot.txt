Inclusion Criteria:
  - men [AND] women aged over 18 years
  - signed informed consent form
  - ametropia below 3 diopters
  - ability [AND] willingness to follow instructions
for healthy volunteers:
  - normal ophthalmologic findings [OR] normal ophthalmologic examination
for primary open angle glaucoma patients (POAG):
  - diagnosed POAG
  - MD [LEQ] 10 dB
for age-related macular degeneration (AMD):
  - diagnosed dry AMD
  - diagnosed stage II [OR] stage III AMD
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Presence of any abnormalities [NOT] preventing reliable measurements
  - Ocular inflammation [OR] ocular disease [NOT] interfering with the study aims
  - Use of photosensitizing medication (phototoxic drugs, photoallergic drugs) in the 3 months preceding the study
  - Presence of any condition [OR] with the possibility of causing photosensitivity, including systemic lupus erythematosus SLE, [OR] Porphyria, [OR] Vitiligo, [OR] Xeroderma Pigmentosum and Albinism
  - Presence of any form of epilepsy
  - Ocular surgery in the 3 months preceding the study
  - Pregnancy, planned pregnancy [OR] lactating
  - Any medical [OR] surgical history, disorder [OR] disease such as acute [OR] chronic severe organic disease
