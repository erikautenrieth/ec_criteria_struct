Inclusion Criteria:
 - Post-9/11 Veterans who report some reintegration, readjustment, or anger difficulty
       -  i.e., Veterans who report "some difficulty" (Likert rating) [AND] on at least one of the primary measures: M2CQ; PDRI; STAXI-2
 - 18-65 years old [AND] (to avoid outcomes being affected by aging)
 - English-speaking [AND] (sessions will be conducted in English)
 - Agreeing to participate
       -  i.e., completion of ICF/HIPAA
Exclusion Criteria:
 - schizophreniform disorder [OR] active psychosis
 - bipolar disorder
 - active suicidality/homicidality [NOT] requiring crisis intervention
 - other severe psychiatric disorders [NOT] prohibiting appropriate group participation
 - neurological diagnosis [NOT] prohibiting appropriate group participation (excluding TBI)
 - current substance dependence
 - current participation in any other form of active behavioral therapy [NOT] at the time of enrollment
       -  e.g., Cognitive Processing Therapy, cognitive rehabilitation for mTBI, or other psychotherapy
