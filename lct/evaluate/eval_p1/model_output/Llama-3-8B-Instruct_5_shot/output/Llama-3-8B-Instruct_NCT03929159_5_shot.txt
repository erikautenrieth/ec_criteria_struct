Inclusion Criteria:
 - Electively scheduled for surgical operation that require general anesthesia [AND] and expected duration of hospitalization of longer than one day (for patients in Perioperative Evaluation & Management [POEM]) [OR] high clinical suspicion of sepsis by the emergency physician (for patients in emergency center)
 - Ability to give informed consent [NOT] or if the patient is incapacitated and unable to give informed consent, the next-of-kin [OR] or a person who has the power of attorney must be present for informed consent.
 - For patients in the emergency center only, two [OR] more of the following SIRS criteria:
       -  Leukocytes > 12,000/mm^3 [OR] or < 4,000/mm^3 [OR] or > 10% immature (band) forms, provided that no filgrastim [OR] or pegfilgrastim was administered within 30 days [AND] no leukemia
       -  Heart rate > 90 beats/minute (min)
       -  Respiratory rate > 20 breaths/min [OR] or partial pressure of carbon dioxide (CO2) < 32 mmHg
       -  Oral temperature > 38 degrees Celsius (C) [OR] or < 36 degrees C [OR] or axillary temperature > 37 degrees C [OR] or < 35 degrees C
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Inability to give informed consent [OR] a person who has power of attorney for medical decision is not available
 - Being moribund (for patients in emergency center) [OR] or cancellation of surgery (for patients in POEM)
 - Active "Do Not Resuscitate" [OR] or "Do Not Intubate" order
