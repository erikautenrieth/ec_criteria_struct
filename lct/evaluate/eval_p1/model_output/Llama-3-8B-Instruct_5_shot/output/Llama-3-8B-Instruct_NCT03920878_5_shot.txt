Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adults over age 18 [AND] with diabetes mellitus, diabetic macular edema [AND] and visually significant cataracts planning to undergo cataract surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who have [NOT] undergone panretinal photocoagulation (PRP) in prior 4 months [OR] an ocular condition (other than cataract [AND] DME) that might affect visual acuity during course of study.
 - Patients with history of vitrectomy.
 - Patients with neovascular glaucoma.
