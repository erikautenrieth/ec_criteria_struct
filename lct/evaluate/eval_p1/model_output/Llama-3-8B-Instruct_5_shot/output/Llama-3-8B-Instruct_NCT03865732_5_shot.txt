Inclusion Criteria:
 - Molecular confirmation of a pathogenic [OR] or likely pathogenic PCDH19 variant
 - Failure to control seizures [AND] despite 2 [OR] more anti-seizure medications

 - On a stable regimen of anti-seizure treatments (Vagus nerve stimulator, ketogenic diet, [AND] and modified Atkins diet should be unchanged for 3 months prior to screening)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Previous exposure to ganaxolone

 - Use of tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) [OR] or non-approved cannabidiol (CBD) is prohibited during the double-blind phase
 - Exposure to any other investigational drug [NOT] within 30 days [OR] fewer than 5 half-lives prior to screening
