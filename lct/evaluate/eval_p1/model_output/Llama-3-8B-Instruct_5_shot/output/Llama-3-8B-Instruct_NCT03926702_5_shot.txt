Inclusion Criteria:
 -  Healthy Volunteers: Subjects must be aged 50 [AND] over. These individuals will all be of good general medical health with no neurological diseases [AND] and capable of providing informed consent.
 -  Patients: Subjects must be aged 50 [AND] over. Patients will meet diagnostic criteria at UCSF for Alzheimer's disease [OR] mild cognitive impairment [OR] or progressive supranuclear palsy (PSP).

Exclusion Criteria:
 -  Any medical contraindications to an MRI scan [NOT]
 -  A pacemaker [NOT]
 -  Metal clips in brain [OR] or tattoos above neck [NOT]
 -  Metallic implants [OR] or shrapnel in body [NOT]
 -  Any body jewelry [OR] or piercings that are not removable [NOT]
 -  A history of claustrophobia [NOT]
 -  A known major systemic disease [NOT]
 -  A history of a psychiatric disorder [NOT]
 -  A history of substance abuse (prescription [OR] non-prescription) within the past 5 years [NOT]
 -  A current weight of less than 45 kilograms (100 pounds) [NOT]
 -  Difficulty in urinating [OR] or emptying the bladder [NOT]
 -  Under age 50 [NOT]
 -  Participating in an experimental radiotracer study [NOT]
 -  Subjects [NOT] must be fluent English speakers
