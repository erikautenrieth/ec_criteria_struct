Inclusion Criteria:
 - adult patients (≥18 years of age) [AND] who are undergoing esophago-gastro duodenoscopy (EGD) for any reason [AND] and are consenting to be enrolled in the study during the study period.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - the patients who [NOT] cannot speak English in Canada [OR] who cannot speak Japanese in Japan [OR] and cannot speak Persian in Iran.
