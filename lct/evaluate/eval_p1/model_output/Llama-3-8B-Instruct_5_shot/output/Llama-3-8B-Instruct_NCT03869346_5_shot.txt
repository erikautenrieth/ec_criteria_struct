Inclusion Criteria:
 - American Society of Anesthesiologists GradeⅠ－II, Patients undergo elective surgery for benign Reproductive diseases [AND] (including laparoscopic ovarian cyst removal, laparoscopic tubal ligation, laparoscopic tubal drainage, laparoscopic salpingoplasty, etc.) [OR] breast disease.
 - Aged 18-45 years
 - Body Mass Index 18-25kg/m2, body weight 40-65 kilograms
 - Agreed to participate the research
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Difficult airway
 - upper respiratory tract infection within 2 weeks [NOT]
 - history of allergy [OR] or long-term use of propofol [AND] and opioids
 - self-rating anxiety scale before operation indicates anxiety
 - pregnancy [OR] or lactation
