Inclusion Criteria:
 - Female, older than 35 years [AND] already have mammography, [OR] or other standard exams (ultrasound, MRI, etc). Could participate this trial within 2 weeks after mammography. Have the ability of consent inform.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - During pregnancy [OR] or lactation period.
 - Patients that [NOT] may not be capable of prone position, such as patients with scapulohumeral periarthritis, [OR] cardiac pacemaker [OR] or have recent cardiac surgery.
 - Lymphoma patients that [AND] under therapy.
 - Severe scoliosis.
