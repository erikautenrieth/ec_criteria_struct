Inclusion Criteria:
 - Mid shaft [OR] or subtrochanteric femur fracture appropriate for antegrade fixation
 - Surgeon agreement [AND] for participation in study
 - Ability to obtain perioperative imaging (CT scans)
 - Provision of informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Inability [NOT] to provide informed consent (e.g. cognitive disability, language barrier)
 - Bilateral Femoral Fractures
 - Inability [OR] or lack of willingness to attend follow up
 - Ipsilateral tibial fracture
 - Ipsilateral femoral neck fracture
 - Ipsilateral acetabular fracture
 - Pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding
 - Contraindications to CT imaging [including] impaired kidney/liver function, [OR] or lack of timely availability
 - Periprosthetic fracture
 - Pathologic fracture
 - Significant delirium [OR] or dementia
