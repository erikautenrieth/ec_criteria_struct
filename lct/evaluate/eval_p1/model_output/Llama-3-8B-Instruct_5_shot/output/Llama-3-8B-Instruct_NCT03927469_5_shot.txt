Inclusion Criteria:
 - Being hospitalized in the hospital between January 2015 and July 2018, according to ICD 10 code; hemiplegia [OR] (G81), flaccid hemiplegia [OR] (G81.0), hemiplegia, unspecified [OR] (G81.9), Spastic hemiplegia [OR] (G81.1)
 - Being ischemic [OR] or hemorrhagic stroke
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Being hemiplegic [NOT] due to trauma, [OR] cancer, [OR] and intracranial operation
