Inclusion Criteria (kidney transplanted patients):
  -  Outpatient at the department of nephrology at either Rigshospitalet [OR] or Herlev Hospital scheduled for a kidney transplantation with a living donor
  -  Impaired glucose tolerance: fasting glucose concentration < 7,0 mmol/ l [AND] and a 2-hour glucose load ≥ 7,8 mmol/ l [AND] < 11,1 mmol/ l [OR] Impaired fasting glycaemia: Fasting blood glucose ≥ 6,1 mmol/ l [AND] < 7,0 mmol/ l [AND] and a 2-hour glucose load < 7,8 mmol/ l [OR] Normal glucose tolerance: fasting glucose concentration < 6,1 mmol/ l [AND] and a 2-hour glucose load < 7,8 mmol/ l.
Inclusion Criteria (control group):
  -  Normal kidney function
  -  Normal glucose tolerance
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  End stage liver disease [NOT] as diagnosed by MELD (model for end stage liver disease) criteria [OR]
  -  At the waiting list for liver transplantation [OR]
  -  Daily alcohol intake above 20 g [AND] 30 g for women [AND] men respectively [OR]
  -  Known hepatitis A [OR] B [OR] C [OR] hepatocellular carcinoma [OR] or other known liver disease [OR]
  -  Pregnancy [OR]
  -  Weight > 130 kg [OR]
  -  Implanted pacemaker
