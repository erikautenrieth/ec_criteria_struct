Inclusion Criteria:
  - Adults who are critically ill [AND] with MRSA infection suspicion.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Pregnancy [OR] breast feeding.
  2. Known allergy to either vancomycin [OR] or ascorbic acid.
  3. Base line serum creatinine [NOT] ≥2mg/dl.
  4. Patients receiving other nephrotoxic drug (e.g., aminoglycosides, amphotericin B, cisplatin [OR] or calcinurine inhibitors).
  5. Anticipated administration of contrast medium within 7 days.
  6. Patients suffering from some underlying diseases (e.g., cancer, HIV infection, systemic lupus erythematoses [OR] or urinary tract stones).
  7. Unlikelyhood of receiving the study medications [NOT] for at least 72 hours.
