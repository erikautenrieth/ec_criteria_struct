Inclusion Criteria:
  - Eligible for the early supported discharge pathway this includes:
       - Confirmed diagnosis of a new stroke [AND].
       - Over 18 Years old [AND].
       - Able to consent to being in the study [AND].
       - The patient [AND] their family/carer agree to rehabilitation process continuing at home [AND].
       - The home environment is conducive to the community-based rehabilitation [AND].
       - Achievable rehabilitation goals can be identified [AND].
       - Document acknowledgement that the patient is sufficiently well enough from a medical perspective to be managed in the community [AND].
       - The patient's language [AND] /or cognitive skills are at a level for them to manage on their own for periods as is necessary [AND].
  - In addition to ESSD criteria participants must;
  - Have a good grasp of the English language [AND].
  - Have been discharged from the ESSD pathway [AND].
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Participants will be excluded if they:
       - Have any communication difficulties [OR] that would prevent them from engaging in the intervention (including hearing impairments, visual impairments, inability to understand English). These will be assessed at researcher's discretion during baseline assessments [NOT].
       - Had a diagnosis of dementia prior to the stroke [NOT].
       - Are deemed to lack capacity to give informed consent [NOT]. This will be judged via a qualified clinician [NOT].
       - Have had previous experience of CFT [NOT].
       - Were receiving medical [OR] or psychological treatment for a mental illness at the time of their stroke [NOT].
