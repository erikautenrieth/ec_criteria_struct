Inclusion Criteria:
  -  older than 18 years old
  -  diagnosed with DME [AND] with central macular thickness (CMT) more than 250 µm measured by OCT
  -  best corrected visual acuity (BCVA) between 20/400 [AND] and 20/40.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  patients [NOT] with intra-ocular surgery as cataract surgery within 6 months
  -  prior intravitreal injection of any drug [OR] within the preceding 6 months
  -  panretinal photocoagulation (PRP) [NOT] within the former 4 months.
  -  Patients [NOT] with previous macular laser
  -  patients [NOT] with vitreo-macular traction (VMT) syndrome
  -  severe glaucoma
  -  other retinal vascular diseases
  -  conditions [OR] that impede the OCT interpretation
