Inclusion Criteria:
  - 18 years [OR] or older
  - Able to read [AND] understand English
  - Desire to quit smoking [AND] (specifically, report a motivation of ≥ 5 out of 10 to quit smoking on a 10-point Likert-type scale)
  - Having access to wireless internet connection [OR] or mobile data
  - Reporting being a daily smoker [AND] for at least one year, smoking an average ≥ 5 cigarettes per day
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Meeting criteria for drug abuse [OR] of depressants, dissociative anesthetics, hallucinogens, opioids, cocaine, or amphetamine within the last 3 months.
  - Severe psychiatric [OR] or structural brain disease (i.e. psychosis, stroke with functional impairment, dementia) [OR] or [NOT] current/recent risk to self. Indicated by denial of question pertaining to inpatient hospitalization
  - Hearing impairment that [NOT] would impede ability to listen the auditory intervention
  - Major illnesses [OR] impacting the study results
  - Currently taking Wellbutrin, Chantix, [OR] or other pharmacological smoking cessation aids that could confound results of the study
