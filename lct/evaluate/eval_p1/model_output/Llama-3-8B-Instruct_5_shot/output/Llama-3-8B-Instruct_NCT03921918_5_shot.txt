Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Subject [AND] ≥ 18 years old
 2. Infant is [AND] ≥1 [AND] ≤6 month old
 3. Subject [AND] and baby are healthy [AND] according to self-declaration in the "Mother and infant health questionnaire"
 4. Subject is [AND] predominantly breastfeeding (80% of all feeds, i.e. ≤150 ml formula/day [OR] equal representative amount of solid food)
 5. The subject agrees to pump [AND] or feed the last time a minimum of 2 hours (hr) before the start of the pumping session at the study site
 6. The subject agrees to photographs/[NOT] 3 D scan of the breast [AND] upper body (no face),
 7. The subject agrees to a video while pumping
 8. The subject signs the informed consent documentation
 9. Subject agrees that a maximum of 2 [OR] male team members can be present in the room to conduct the usability part of the study
 10. The mother accepts that the pumped milk in visit 2 must be donated to the study [AND] i.e. cannot be fed to the baby
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. The subject has a nipple size [NOT] ≥ 21 mm
 2. Mastitis (any breast [NOT] within two weeks prior to enrolment)
 3. Engorgement (any breast [NOT] within two weeks prior to enrolment).
 4. Case of current infection as self-declared in the Mother and infant health questionnaire
