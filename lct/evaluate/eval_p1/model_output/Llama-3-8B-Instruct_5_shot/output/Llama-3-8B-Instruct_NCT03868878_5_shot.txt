Inclusion Criteria:
 - age greater than 60 years;
 - is not engaged in physical exercise programs for at least one year;
 - do not present severe cardiovascular disease [OR] kidney disease [OR] liver disease [OR] or limiting cognitive [OR] or neuromuscular disease [AND] and;
 - does not present visual [NOT] or auditory inability.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - failing to perform any of the evaluations suggested during the intervention protocol [AND];
 - absence in five or more Capoeira training sessions.
