Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients met diagnostic criteria for schizophrenia [OR] or schizoaffective disorder using the Structural Clinical Interview for Diagnostic and Statistical Manual Diploma in Social Medicine (DSM)-IV (SCID, Version 2.0).
 - Patients remain their psychotropic medication at steady dosages for at least 4 weeks prior to study entry [AND] and for the duration of the trial.
 - Verbal intelligence quotient > 85 [AND] as measured by using a Chinese version of the National Adult Reading Test.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - History of significant head trauma [OR] or neurological disorders
 - Alcohol [OR] or drug abuse [AND] Focal brain lesions on T1- [OR] or T2-weighted fluid-attenuated inversion-recovery magnetic resonance images
 - a prior history of a seizure [NOT] induced by drug withdrawal, first degree relative with epilepsy, significant neurological illness [OR] or head trauma, endocrine disease, such as thyroid disease, significant unstable medical condition, recent aggression [OR] or other forms of behavioral dyscontrol
 - left-handedness [OR] pregnancy
 - estimated intelligence quotient < 80
 - current alcohol [OR] or drug abuse
 - inability to provide informed consent [NOT]
 - Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale [OR] or the Hamilton Depression Rating Scale score > 10
