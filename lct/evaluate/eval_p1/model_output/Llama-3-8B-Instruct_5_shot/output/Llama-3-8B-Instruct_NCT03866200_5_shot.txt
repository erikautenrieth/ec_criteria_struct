Inclusion Criteria:
 - The diagnosis of pseudoachondroplasia is based on clinical assessment either in person [OR] by photographic review by skeletal dysplasia specialist (JTH),
 - Healthy [AND] beyond pseudoachondroplasia associated complications,
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Current use of resveratrol
 - Current use of blood thinners [OR] lovastatin [OR] ketoconazole [OR] itraconazole [OR] fexofenadine [OR] triazolam.
 - Other non-pseudoachondroplasia related health conditions [AND] e.g. cancers.
 - Pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding. Women must use adequate contraception during the study [NOT].
 - Participation in another clinical study [AND] /using investigational agents.
 - Use of Non-steroid anti-inflammatory (NSAIDs) [OR] aspirin.
 - Current use of Alfentanil [OR] Cyclosporine [OR] Dihydroergotamine [OR] Dofetilide [OR] Ergotamine [OR] Fentanyl [OR] Flibanserin [OR] Oxycodone [OR] Pimavanserin [OR] Pimozide [OR] Quinidine [OR] Saquinavir [OR] Sirolimus [OR] Tacrolimus [OR] Temsirolimus [OR] Theophylline [OR] Tizanidine [OR] Thioridazine [OR] Fosphenytoin [OR] Phenytoin [OR] Warfarin.
 - Baseline level of pain [NOT] of 2 or higher on 10 point scale.
 - Platelet count [NOT] below 50,000 per ul on baseline complete blood count (CBC).
