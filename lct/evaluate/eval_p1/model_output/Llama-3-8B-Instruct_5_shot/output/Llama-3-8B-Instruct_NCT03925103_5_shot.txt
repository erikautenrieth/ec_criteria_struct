Inclusion Criteria:
  - Clinical NSCLC early stage (stage [AND] I-II).
  - Both forced expiratory volume in one second (FEV1) [AND] and diffusion capacity of carbon monoxide (DLCO)>60%
  - Both predicted postoperative (PPO) FEV1 [AND] and PPO DLCO >35%
  - American Society of Anesthesia (ASA) score [NOT]>2
  - Body mass index (BMI) [AND] >18 [AND] <28
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Clinical NSCLC stage [NOT]> II
  - History of Neoadjuvant chemotherapy [OR] or radiotherapy
  - Radiologic evidence of extensive pleural adhesions.
  - Age [NOT]<18 [OR] >80 years.
  - Patients with previous pleurodesis [OR] or thoracotomy in the affected hemithorax.
  - Patients who will undergo surgical lung resection [NOT] other than lobectomy.
  - Patients with severe comorbidity [NOT] contraindicating lobectomy.
  - Patients refusal [OR] or noncompliance to general surgery [AND] one-lung ventilation.
