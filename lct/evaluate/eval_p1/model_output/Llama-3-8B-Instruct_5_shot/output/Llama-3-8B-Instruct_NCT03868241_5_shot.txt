Inclusion Criteria:
 - All patients admitted to the intensive care unit [AND] which will require simultaneous insertion of all three devices (endotracheal tube, central venous catheter [AND] and urinary catheter) due to illness severity as defined by the attending physician.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients admitted to the intensive care unit for more than 48 hours [OR] admitted in the hospital for more than seven days
 - Presence of any of the invasive devices (endotracheal tube, central venous catheter [OR] or urinary catheters) before randomization [AND] absence of the intention to exchange the devices;
 - Previous use of any type of coated devices;
 - Age [NOT] < 18 years;
 - Known pregnancy
 - Known allergy to gold, silver [OR] and palladium;
 - Suspected [OR] confirmed brain death;
 - Previously enrolled in the study
 - Severe chronic pulmonary obstructive disease [AND] which may limit catheter site selection
 - Previous irradiation [AND] /or thrombosis in site selected for catheter insertion
