Inclusion Criteria:
  - Non-cardiac surgery patients;
       - Age is greater than or equal to 60 years old [AND] Han Nationality, mother tongue is Chinese;
       - The MMSE score: Illiteracy is greater than or equal to 17 points, primary school is greater than or equal to 20 points, higher secondary school is more than 24 points;
       - The people signed informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - The diagnosis of preoperative delirium [OR] The diagnosis Unstable hypertension (Such as: pheochromocytoma [OR] aortic dissection);
       - Severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] or congestive heart failure;
       - Severe liver [AND] kidney dysfunction;
