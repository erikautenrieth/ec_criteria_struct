Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients and/or legal guardians [AND] exhaustively informed by the investigator, and who have voluntarily provided written informed consent [OR], when unable to read and/or write, verbal consent put into writing by a third person
 - Patients who underwent elective laparoscopic [OR] or video assisted right hemicolectomy at U.O. General and Thoracic Surgery for malignant [OR] or benign pathology.
 - Patients who had prior chemotherapy [AND] and radiotherapy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients operated in emergency [NOT]
 - Pregnant women
 - Right laparotomic hemicolectomy [NOT]
 - BMI > 35
