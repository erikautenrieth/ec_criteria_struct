Inclusion Criteria:
 - All comatose patients (Glasgow coma score <8) [AND] due to carbon monoxide poisoning, treated by hyperbaric oxygen therapy in a tertiary hospital in Île de France area;
 - Aged ≥ 18 years;
 - Hospitalized in hyperbaric medicine unity of Raymond Poincaré hospital [AND] in Garches.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnant woman [NOT];
 - Patient [NOT] present clinical signs of stroke [AND] without CO detection.
In case of unusable data, patient [NOT] will be excluded from study.
