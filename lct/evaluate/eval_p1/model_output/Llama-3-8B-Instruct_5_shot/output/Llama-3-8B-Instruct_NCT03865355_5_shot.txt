Inclusion Criteria:
 1. All patients with suspected newly diagnosed glioma (grade I, II, III or glioblastoma) with planned surgical intervention (resection or biopsy).
 2. Patient aged 18 years or older [AND] 
 3. Patients have to be able to give informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Prior Radiotherapy to the central nervous system [NOT]
 2. Prior Chemotherapy within the last 5 years [NOT]
 3. Any prior central nervous system malignancy [NOT]
 4. Any surgery during last 6 months [NOT]
