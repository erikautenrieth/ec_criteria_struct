Inclusion Criteria:
For the two retrospective cohorts:
  1. All patients that are [NOT] younger than 18 treated for localized adenocarcinoma of the prostate between 2000 and 2017 with either radical prostatectomy [OR] or radiotherapy
  2. All treated patients had a rising post-treatment PSA, with at least one post-treatment free PSA blood test [AND].
For the biobank validation cohort:
1. All patients treated with radical prostatectomy for localized prostate cancer between 2000 and 2017 who had biobank samples taken when developing biochemical recurrence.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients that are [NOT] younger than 18,
  2. Patients with prostate cancer [NOT] other than adenocarcinoma, such as small cell [OR] and neuroendocrine cancer
  3. Patients with prostate adenocarcinoma that did not develop biochemical recurrence.
  4. In the retrospective cohorts - patients that did not have at least one post-treatment free PSA blood test [NOT].
