Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Moderate [OR] to severe OSA (AHI > 15)
  -  Age 18 years [AND] or older
  -  BMI < 40
  -  Baseline polysomnogram completed within the last 12 months with no significant weight changes [NOT] (<10 lbs. weight fluctuation)
  -  The baseline sleep study must be diagnostic [AND] with adequate sample
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  BMI ≥ 40 [NOT]
  -  Pregnancy
  -  Central sleep apnea (CSA) - 5 [OR] more central apneas per hour
  -  Psychoactive medications that influence sleep
  -  Chronic opioid use
  -  Other sleep disorders
  -  Active temporomandibular joint (TMJ) Disorders
  -  Fibromyalgia with significant facial pain
  -  Inability to protrude the mandible beyond 5 mm
  -  Inability to follow-up after surgery
  -  Uncontrolled psychiatric disorders
  -  Dental disorders including loose teeth [OR] and periodontal disease
