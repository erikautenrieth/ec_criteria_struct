Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients aged 18 to 80 years old
 - Multiple sclerosis according to McDonald criteria;
 - Expanded Disability Status Scale (EDSS) between 0 [AND] and 6.5;
 - Patient [AND] able to move with [OR] without mechanical assistance.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Deterioration of neurological symptomatology [NOT] within 60 day before enrollment;
 - Changes brought to the multiple sclerosis treatment [NOT] within 6 months before enrollment;
 - Introduction of a treatment acting on spasticity [OR] or fatigue within 30 day before enrollment;
 - Changes brought to reeducation protocol [NOT] throughout the duration of the study;
 - Patient [NOT] able to provide an effort equivalent to 3 times the resting metabolic value = 3 Metabolic Equivalent of Task (METs).
