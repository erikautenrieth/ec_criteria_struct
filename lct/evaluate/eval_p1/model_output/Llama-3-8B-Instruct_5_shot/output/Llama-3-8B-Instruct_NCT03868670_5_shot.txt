Inclusion Criteria:
  - BMI 45-60 kg/m2
  - Failure of at least one weight loss medication [AND] 
  - Previous Gastric Bypass Surgery [AND] lead to less than 50% of excess weight lost by 2 years after surgery
  - Significant Loss of Control over eating
  - Subject is able to attend all scheduled clinic appointments on their own [OR] with a caregiver.
  - Premenopausal women must agree to use acceptable methods of birth control [AND] 
  - Participants provide voluntary, decisionally capable, and appropriately informed consent [AND] 
  - Subject is able to comply w/ all testing and follow-up requirements defined by the study protocol [AND] 
  - Participant has no immediate plan for relocation beyond 6 hours of the study site [AND] 
  - Proficiency with the English language [NOT] 
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Subject has an implanted medical device [OR] that delivers electrical energy to the brain [OR] 
  - Subject has an implantable cardiac pacemaker [OR] defibrillator [OR] or neurostimulator [OR] 
  - Subject is likely to require repeat MR imaging [AND] after implant of the RNS Neurostimulator and Leads [AND] 
  - Subject is unable to fit into CT scanner (500lb upper weight limit for CT scanner) [OR] 
  - Inability to provide informed consent to treatment [NOT]
