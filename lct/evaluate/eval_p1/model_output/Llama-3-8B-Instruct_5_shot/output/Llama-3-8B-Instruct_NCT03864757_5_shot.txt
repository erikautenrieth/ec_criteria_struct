Inclusion Criteria:
 - Male [AND] female patients between 18 [AND] 80 years
 - Diagnosed with permanent UVFP [AND] and insufficient glottal closure [AND] and planned type I thyroplasty with conventional implant
 - Ability to co-operate with the investigator [AND] and to comply with the requirements of the entire study
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Any other significant disease [OR] disorder which, in the opinion of the investigator, may either put the participant at risk [OR] because of participation in the clinical investigation may influence the result of the clinical investigation, [OR] or the participant's ability to participate in the clinical investigation
 - Scheduled elective surgery [OR] or other procedures requiring general anaesthesia during clinical investigation
 - Had medialization thyroplasty [NOT] before
 - Had injection medialization laryngoplasty [NOT] in the past two years.
 - Presence of structural vocal fold lesions [OR] such as polyp [OR] nodules
 - Status post total cordectomy
 - Previous laryngeal framework surgery [OR] (any type of thyroplasty, arytenoid adduction)
 - Significant non-laryngeal speech abnormality [NOT] (severe dysarthria determined by a panel of trained speech therapists)
 - Severe coagulopathy
