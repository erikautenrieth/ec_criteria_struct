Inclusion Criteria:
 - Undergoing an elective surgical procedure for which it is expected they will receive a prescription pain medicine for management of pain during recovery at home.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Undergoing [NOT] non-elective procedure
 - Cognitive impairment [AND] precluding self-reporting of pain
 - Hematologic [OR] oncologic condition
 - Liver [OR] kidney conditions precluding usual analgesic prescription patterns (opioid [AND] plus a non-opioid)
 - Use of opioids for prolonged pain pre-operatively [NOT] (<2 weeks)
