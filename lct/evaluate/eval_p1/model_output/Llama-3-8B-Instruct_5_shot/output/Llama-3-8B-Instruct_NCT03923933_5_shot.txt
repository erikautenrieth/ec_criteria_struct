Inclusion Criteria:
 - glomerular filtration rate [AND] less than 30 ml / min / 1.73m
 - Without replacement therapy (dialysis [OR] or hemodialysis)
 - Volume overload
 - At least 100 ml per day of residual diuresis
 - Use of a loop diuretic for at least one month [NOT] without any contraindications
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Allergies [AND] known to diuretics
 - Patients with severe [AND] infections
 - Patients with hemodynamic [AND] instability
 - Amputees
 - Patients with cognitive [AND] impairment
 - Patients with acute [AND] renal failure
 - Patients with graft [AND] loss
