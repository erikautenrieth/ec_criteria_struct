Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients aged between 19 [AND] 65 years
 - Patients with American Society of Anesthesiologists physical status I [OR] II
 - Patients scheduled for elective laparoscopic cholecystectomy under balanced general anesthesia for benign cholecystic diseases
 - Patients obtaining written informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with asthma [OR] hypertension
 - Patients with [NOT] an inability to express their pain accurately
 - Patients with [NOT] an inability to understand the pain scale
 - Patients with chronic abdominal pain [OR] chronic pain syndrome
 - Patients who [NOT] required to convert to laparotomy from laparoscopic surgery
 - Patients who [NOT] required to receive incidental lower abdominal procedures owing to adhesion, injury, or other incidental findings at the lower abdominal cavity
 - Patients with a history of drug [OR] alcohol abuse
 - Pregnant [NOT] women
