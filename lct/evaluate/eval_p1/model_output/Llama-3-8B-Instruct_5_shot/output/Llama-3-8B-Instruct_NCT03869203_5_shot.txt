Inclusion Criteria:
  - 1. elective total knee arthroplasty for degenerative osteoarthritis
  - 2. elective total hip arthroplasty for osteoarthritis [OR] of osteonecrosis of femoral head
Exclusion Criteria:
  - 1. ASA class >3
  - 2. severe medical comorbidities
  - 3. inflammatory arthritis [NOT] including rheumatoid arthritis
  - 4. severe instability [NOT] of knee [OR] hip
  - 5. revision surgery
