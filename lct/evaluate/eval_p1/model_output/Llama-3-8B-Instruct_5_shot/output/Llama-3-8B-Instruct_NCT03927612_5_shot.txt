Inclusion Criteria:
 - Aged 9-12
 - English-speaking
 - Meet DSM-5 criteria for oppositional defiant disorder [OR] conduct disorder [OR] or Other Specified or Unspecified Disruptive, Impulse-Control, and Conduct Disorder
 - Right-handed
 - Estimated full-scale IQ [NOT] less than or equal to 75
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Bipolar disorder [OR] any disorder involving psychosis, pervasive developmental disorders, current [OR] past substance use disorder, or current major depressive disorder
 - History of neurological problems [AND] (e.g., epilepsy, traumatic brain injury)
 - Contraindications for MRI
