Inclusion Criteria:
 - Burn patients
 - severe neuropatic pain [AND] rated at least 5 on the 10-point numerical rating scale (NRS)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - cardiac arrest history
 - history of neurologi [NOT] disease or brain surgery
 - unstable heart disease [OR] or presence of a cardiac pacemaker
 - pain resulting from other causes [AND] like as neuromuscular diseases
 - psychiatric disorder
 - diabetes mellitus
 - abnormal renal function
 - contraindication for MRI, [OR] pregnancy
