Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with (suspicion of) primary [OR] recurrent EOC with an indication for surgery, chemotherapy and/or immunotherapy.
 - Age ≥18 years [AND] years.
 - WHO performance status 0-2.
 - Accessible for treatment [AND] and follow-up.
 - Written informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Other active malignancy in past 5 years prior to entry into the study [NOT] except for treated non-melanoma skin cancer.
 - Any known severe infection [OR] like HIV, hepatitis A, B [AND] C.
 - Receiving immune suppressive treatment.
 - Medical [OR] or psychological condition which [NOT] in the opinion of the investigator would not permit the patient to complete the study [OR] sign meaningful informed consent.
