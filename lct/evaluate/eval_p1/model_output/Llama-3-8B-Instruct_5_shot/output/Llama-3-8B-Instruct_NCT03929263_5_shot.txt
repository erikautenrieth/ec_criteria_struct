Inclusion Criteria:
  - Eligible for fMRI [AND] 
  - Sufficient English fluency to complete tasks
  - BMI ≤ 40.0
  - Right-handed
  - Biological female [NOT]
  - Co-residing at least 4 days/week
  - Adult participants: Biological mother of adolescent participant [AND] 
  - Adult participants:Primary caregiver > 50% of child's lifespan [AND] 
  - Adult participants:BMI ≥ 18.0
  - Adolescent participants: Age 13-17 years [AND] 
  - Adolescent participants: BMI ≥ 16.0
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Current psychiatric diagnosis
  - Medications influencing fMRI [OR] 
  - Medical conditions influencing fMRI [OR] 
  - Alcohol [OR] or psychoactive drug on scan day
  - Adolescent participants: Neurodevelopmental delay
  - Adolescent participants: History of mood [OR] or psychotic disorder
  - Adolescent participants: History of obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD) [OR] or attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD)
