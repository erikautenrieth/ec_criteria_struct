Inclusion Criteria:
 - outpatients 2 to 6 years of age;
 - males [AND] and females who are physically healthy;
 - diagnosis of autism spectrum disorder [AND] based on clinical evaluation [AND] and DSM-5 criteria, and confirmed using the Autism Diagnostic Interview-Revised, [AND] and the Autism Diagnostic Observation Schedule;
 - care provider who could reliably bring subject to clinic visits, could provide trustworthy ratings, [AND] and interacted with subject on a regular basis;
 - ability of subject to swallow the compound;
 - stable concomitant medications for at least 2 weeks [OR] (4 weeks if patient took fluoxetine);
 - no planned changes in psychosocial interventions during the open-label trial.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - DSM-5 diagnosis of schizophrenia [OR] schizoaffective disorder [OR] or psychotic disorder not otherwise specified;
 - prior adequate trial of Esomeprazole;
 - active medical problems such as [NOT] unstable seizures, or significant physical illness (e.g., serious liver [OR] renal pathology).
