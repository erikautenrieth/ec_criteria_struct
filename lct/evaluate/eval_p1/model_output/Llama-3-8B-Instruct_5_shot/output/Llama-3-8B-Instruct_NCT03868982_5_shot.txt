Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of ARDS by Berlin definition
 - P/F ratio between 100-300
 - Completed the case enrolment within 48 hours

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Less than 18 years old
 - Pregnancy
 - Participants with tracheostomy [OR] unable to insert the NG-tube
 - [NOT] able to insert the NG-tube
