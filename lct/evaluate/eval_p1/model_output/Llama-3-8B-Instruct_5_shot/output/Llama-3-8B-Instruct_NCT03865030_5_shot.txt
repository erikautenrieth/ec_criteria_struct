Inclusion Criteria:
  - Psoriatic patients that are treated in our institute.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [NOT] suffering from cardiovascular diseases (peripheral vascular disease, [OR] congestive heart failure, [OR] status post cerebrovascular accident).
  - Conditions that [AND] are harmful to the inner ear (chronic renal failure, [OR] history of noise exposure, [OR] usage of ototoxic drugs, [OR] chronic otitis media, [OR] ear surgery in the past, [OR] significant head trauma in the past, [OR] ear drum perforation).
  - participant that [NOT] complains of vertigo/ dizziness/ unsteadiness.
  - Participant who [NOT] is/ was suffering from known inner ear disease: meniere disease, [OR] acoustic neurinoma, [OR] vestibular neuronitis.
