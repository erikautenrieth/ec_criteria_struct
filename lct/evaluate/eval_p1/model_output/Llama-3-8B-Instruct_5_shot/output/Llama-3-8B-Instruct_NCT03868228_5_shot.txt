Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients with CPM with expected life expectancy of > 6 months [AND].
 2. ECOG Scale of Performance Status (PS) scores 0 [OR] 1.
 3. 15 mile catchment area to facilitate overseeing systemic chemotherapy administration [AND].
 4. Concomitant systemic chemotherapy regimens with FOLFIRI [OR] FOLFOX [OR] Mitomycin C [AND] Fluorouracil, Capecitabine (excluding Lonsurf) [OR] without systemic chemotherapy if no systemic options available to patient [AND].
 5. Neutrophil count on or just before chemotherapy due date of >1.5 [AND].
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Age <18 [NOT].
 2. MDT decision that patient not suitable for PIPAC [AND].
 3. Decision by Preoperative Assessment Department [OR] Consultant Anaesthetist that patient not fit for general anaesthesia [AND] / or laparoscopy [AND].
 4. Clinically evident gross ascites [AND].
 5. Bowel obstruction [AND].
 6. Bevacizumab as part of systemic chemotherapy regime - time from chemotherapy to surgery would be too long for PIPAC to be feasible [AND].
 7. Previous bone marrow suppression due to chemotherapy given risk of post-operative neutropenia [AND].
