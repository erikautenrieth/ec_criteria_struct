Inclusion Criteria:
 - preterm neonates born at 26 to 34 weeks of gestational age
 - signed written informed consent form
Exclusion Criteria:
 - infants [NOT] infected
 - infants suffering from neurological disorders
 - serious heart, respiratory, digestive [OR] or metabolic diseases
 - infants born from mothers [NOT] aged less than 18 years old [OR] or deprived of their parental rights
 - infants [NOT] non covered by national health insurance
