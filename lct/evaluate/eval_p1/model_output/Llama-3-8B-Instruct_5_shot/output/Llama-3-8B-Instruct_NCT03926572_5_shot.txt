Inclusion Criteria:
  - Adult patients over 18 years
  - Diagnosis of Pulmonary arterial hypertension [OR] or non-operable chronic thromboembolic pulmonary hypertension established by right cardiac catheterization prior to inclusion in the study
  - Patients admitted in intensive care unit for acute decompensation of pulmonary hypertension requiring intravenous diuretic therapy [AND] ± use of inotropes [OR] or vasopressors.
  - Patients must have given their written informed consent to participate in the study [AND] after having received adequate previous information [AND] prior to any study-specific procedures
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients with post-capillary pulmonary hypertension [NOT]
  - Patients with pulmonary hypertension associated with chronic respiratory disease [NOT]
  - Patients with pulmonary hypertension with unclear [OR] or multifactorial mechanisms [NOT]
  - Patients with operable chronic thromboembolic pulmonary hypertension [NOT]
  - Shock due to another cause than acute decompensation of pulmonary hypertension [NOT]
  - Pregnant [OR] women, or breast feeding women [NOT]
  - Adult protected person [NOT]
  - Person deprived of liberty [NOT]
  - Person admitted without consent [NOT]
  - Pregnant [OR] breastfeeding women [NOT]
