Inclusion Criteria:
  - Healthy [AND] (determined by self-report)
  - Between the ages of 18-55 years old
  - Current cigarette smokers
  - Willing to provide informed consent
  - Able to attend the lab session [AND] and abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required
  - Agree to use designated products [AND] according to the study protocol

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Women [IF] they are breast-feeding [OR] test positive for pregnancy (by urinalysis) at screening.
  - Individuals who weigh [NOT] less than 110 pounds.
