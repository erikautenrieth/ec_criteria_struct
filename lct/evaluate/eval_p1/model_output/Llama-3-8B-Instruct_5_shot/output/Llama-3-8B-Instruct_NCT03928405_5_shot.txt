Inclusion Criteria:
 - Having mild [OR] moderate severity of AD
 - Lack of communication difficulties [AND] or problems
 - Volunteering to participate in the study
 - Cerebrovascular event [NOT]
 - Having disturbing the balance [AND] and coordination of neurological disease such as Parkinson [OR] MS,
 - Mini Mental Test Score, 18 [AND] to 24 points from being scored
 - being 65 [AND] to 80 aged.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients diagnosed with dementia [AND] (infection, vascular, hematological diseases), patients who could not be contacted during the follow-up period [OR] who did not come to control, cardiac [AND] and cerebrovascular event,
 - Endocrine disorder, fluid-electrolyte imbalance [AND] and infection
 - Patients with malignancy [AND] and malignancy, chemotherapy [OR] and radiotherapy,
 - Patients with delirium [OR] depression
 - Those with protective sensation loss [NOT] (those with Semmes-Weinstein Monofilament Thickness 4.56 [AND] above)
 - patients with lower [OR] upper extremity amputations at any level
