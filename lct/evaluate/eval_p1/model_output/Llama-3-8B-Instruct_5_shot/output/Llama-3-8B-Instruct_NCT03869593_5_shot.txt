Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patient with S. aureus [OR] or E. coli bacteriostatic bacteremia defined by at least one positive blood culture bottle
  - Patient requiring a blood test [AND] as part of his bacteremia
  - not subject to a judicial protection measure
  - Signature of the non-opposition of consent [AND] (for minor patients signed by one of the parents [OR] or the representative of the parental authority)
  - Affiliation to social security
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Immunocompromised patient defined by: [AND]
       - current immunosuppressive therapies: corticosteroids, chemotherapy, biotherapy [OR]
       - solid organ transplant patient [OR] or hematopoietic stem cell transplant
       - chemotherapy-induced neutropenia
       - Congenital immune deficiency [NOT]
  - bacteremia related to a peripheral [OR] or central catheter
  - Urinary obstruction [NOT] not lifted within the first 24 hours of management
  - Intra-abdominal infection collected undrained [NOT] in the first 24 hours of management
  - primary infectious focus represented by mechanically ventilated pneumonia
  - Pregnant [OR] or lactating woman
