Patient participants:
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients admitted to the ICU who are receiving vasoactive drugs [AND].
  -  Age greater than or equal to 18 years old.
  -  Expected to remain admitted to the ICU for at least 24 hours post-enrolment.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Any patient who is [NOT] expected to die imminently, as per clinical opinion.
  -  Any patient where mobilization is [NOT] contraindicated by the nature of their existing injuries.
  -  Where it is [NOT] clear from the medical records that participants are prisoners [OR] or offenders on probation.
  -  Patients with neuromuscular disease [OR] or acute brain injury [OR] or spinal cord injury.
  -  If the patient [AND] or their consultee is [NOT] able to speak English.

Clinicians participating in a survey on hypothetical randomization of patient participants:
Inclusion criteria:
  -  Clinicians who work in the intensive care unit where a patient participant has been admitted.
Exclusion criteria:
  -  None.

Clinician participants in the survey on the feasibility of the ICU physical rehabilitation adverse event tool:
Inclusion criteria:
  -  An ICU clinician at the research site.
  -  Has used the ICU physical rehabilitation adverse event tool as part of this research study.
Exclusion criteria:
  -  None.
