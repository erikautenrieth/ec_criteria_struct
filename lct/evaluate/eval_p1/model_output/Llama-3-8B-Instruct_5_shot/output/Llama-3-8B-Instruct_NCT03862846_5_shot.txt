Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Subjects must give written, signed and dated informed consent
  -  Confirmed diagnosis of PMM [AND] according to the 2016 Rome Consensus recommendations
  -  Confirmed mitochondrial mutation [AND] with evidence of myopathy
  -  Able to remain on stable medication [AND] throughout the study
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Documented evidence [NOT] of ongoing rhabdomyolysis
  -  Subjects with motor abnormalities [OR] other than related to mitochondrial disease
  -  Treatment with an investigational drug [OR] within 3 months prior to Day 1
  -  Hospitalised [OR] within 3 months prior to screening for any major medical condition
  -  Pregnant [OR] or nursing females
