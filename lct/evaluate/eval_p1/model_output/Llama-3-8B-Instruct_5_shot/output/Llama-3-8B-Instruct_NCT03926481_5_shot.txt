Inclusion Criteria:
 - English Speaking
 - Age ≥65 years
 - Body Composition Body Fat >25% [AND] for Men
 - Body Composition Body Fat >30% [AND] for Women
 - Participating in the Dartmouth Hitchcock Weight and Wellness Center (WWC) Healthy Lifestyle Program (HLP)
 - Willing [AND] and able to give informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Unable [NOT] to perform measures
 - Individuals unwilling [OR] unable to provide consent
 - Severe [NOT] mental [OR] life-threatening illness
 - History of bariatric surgery
