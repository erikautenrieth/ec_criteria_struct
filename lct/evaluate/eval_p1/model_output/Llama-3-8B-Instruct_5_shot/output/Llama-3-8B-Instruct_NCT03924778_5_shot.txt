Inclusion Criteria:
 - female
 - non-Hispanic, black [OR] or white
 - age 19-65 years
 - BMI >= 30 kg/m^2
 - able to visit Bionutrition Unit daily
Exclusion Criteria:
 - gastrointestinal (GI) conditions [AND] i.e., irritable bowel, diverticulitis, peptic ulcers, Crohn's, GI cancers, and adenatomous polyps
 - antibiotic [OR] or probiotic use in the previous 90 days
 - tobacco [NOT] use
 - heavy [NOT] alcohol consumption
 - major medical conditions [AND] (e.g., renal disease, diabetes, cancer)
