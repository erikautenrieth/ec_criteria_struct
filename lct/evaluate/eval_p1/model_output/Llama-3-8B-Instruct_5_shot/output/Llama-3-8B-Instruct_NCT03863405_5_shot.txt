Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients older than 18 years (of both sexes) diagnosed with rheumatoid arthritis according to ACR / EULAR 2010 criteria [AND]
  - Patients with moderate [OR] high disease activity (DAS - 28 score greater than 3.2)
  - Patients received the standard therapy (i.e. one or more conventional DMARDs) for at least three months [AND]
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Known hypersensitivity to metformin [NOT]
  - Patients who have a prior diagnosis with diabetes mellitus [NOT]
  - Patients receive metformin for any other indications [NOT]
  - Patients with congestive heart failure [NOT]
  - Patients with a history of myocardial infarction [NOT]
  - Patients with severe anemia [NOT]
  - Patients with active infections [OR] or other inflammatory diseases [NOT]
  - Patients receiving biological therapy [NOT]
  - Pregnancy [OR] lactation [NOT]
  - Patients with impaired liver functions [NOT]
  - Patients with impaired kidney functions (serum creatinine concentrations ≥1.5 [AND] ≥1.4 mg/dL in males [AND] females respectively) [NOT]
  - Patients with malignancies [NOT]
