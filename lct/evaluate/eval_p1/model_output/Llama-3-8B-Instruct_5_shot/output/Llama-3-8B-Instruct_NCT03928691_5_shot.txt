Inclusion criteria:
pregnant women 34 weeks or less admitted to Women's Health Hospital with a diagnosis of:
  -  severe preeclampsia [AND] (It is defined as 1- blood pressure 160/110 mmHg or more [AND] 2- proteinurea (+3 or more by dipstick) [AND] 3-HELLP syndrome)
  -  threatened preterm labour [AND] that will receive Magnesium Sulphate [AND] either for tocolysis [OR] neuroprotection
Exclusion criteria:
- women with more than 34 weeks gestation [AND] with any contraindication for Magnesium Sulphate intake [NOT] like severe renal impairment [OR] myasthenia gravis
