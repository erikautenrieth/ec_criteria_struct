Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients with suitable indication for ASD closure according to recent guidelines:
       1. Impaired functional capacity.
       2. Right atrial and/or RV enlargement.
       3. Left-to-right shunt with [Qp: Qs] ≥1.5:1 [AND].
       4. Paradoxical embolism.
  - Patients with isolated secundum ASD with sufficient rims [AND] suitable for device closure.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Small ASD with [Qp/Qs] <1.5:1 [OR] no signs of RV volume overload
  - A single defect too large for closure (>38 mm) [NOT]
  - Multiple ASDs [NOT] unsuitable for percutaneous closure.
  - Anterior, posterior, superior, or inferior rim <5 mm [NOT]
  - Abnormal pulmonary venous drainage [NOT]
  - Associated structural heart disease requiring cardiac surgery [NOT]
  - ASD with severe pulmonary arterial hypertension [AND] and bidirectional or right-to-left shunting.
  - Intracardiac thrombi diagnosed by echocardiography [NOT].
