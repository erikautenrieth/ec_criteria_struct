Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Between the ages of 60 [AND] and 85;
 2. Speaks fluent English;
 3. Visual and auditory acuity adequate for neuropsychological [AND] and computerized testing;
 4. Good general health with no disease(s) expected to interfere with the study;
 5. Willing [AND] and able to participate for the duration of the study [AND] in all study procedures including MRI [AND] and PET;
 6. Normal cognition defined as a Clinical Dementia Rating of 0 [AND] and a Mini-Mental State Examination score of 27 [OR] higher.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Significant co-morbid neurologic disease [AND] such as Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, brain cyst, tumor [OR] aneurysm;
 2. Major health conditions [AND] such as uncontrolled diabetes mellitus, uncontrolled hypertension, nutritional deficiency [OR] uncontrolled thyroid disease;
 3. Significant psychiatric disorders [AND] such as schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, anxiety disorder, major depressive disorder [OR] attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder;
 4. ADRC consensus diagnosis of dementia [OR] mild cognitive impairment;
 5. Cognitive impairment when tested at baseline [AND] defined as a score on any neuropsychological test of 1.5 SDs [OR] more outside the age norm;
 6. [NOT] alcohol [OR] substance abuse [OR] dependence within the past 2 years (DSM-IV criteria);
 7. MRI contraindications, e.g. pacemakers, aneurysm clips, artificial heart valves, ear implants, metal fragments [OR] foreign objects in the eyes, skin [OR] body. Females who are [NOT] pregnant [OR] trying to get pregnant are also excluded;
 8. PET contraindications, e.g. significant prior radiation exposure [AND] pregnancy.
