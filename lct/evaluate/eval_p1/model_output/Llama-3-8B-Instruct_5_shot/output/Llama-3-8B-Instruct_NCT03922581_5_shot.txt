Inclusion Criteria:
  - ability to provide informed consent
  - willingness to participate in study
  - self-identity as African American
  - history of childhood [AND] or adult trauma exposure (3 total criterion A traumas)
  - positive PTSD screen (PC-PTSD [GE] 3)
  - positive MDD screen (PHQ-9 [GE] 5)
Exclusion Criteria:
  - presence of [NOT] intellectual disability, [OR] bipolar, [OR] psychotic disorder
  - presence of current [NOT] substance use disorder (past 1 month; see cut off scores in psychological measures below)
  - active [NOT] suicidality
