Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with chronic suppurative otitis media [AND] with total or subtotal tympanic membrane perforation with limited anterior remnant.
 - dry perforation for at least 3 months.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Recurrent perforation [NOT] after previous myringoplasty.
 - Ossicular disruption [OR] or fixation.
 - immune-compromised patients including uncontrolled diabetics [OR] chronic liver [OR] and kidney diseases.
