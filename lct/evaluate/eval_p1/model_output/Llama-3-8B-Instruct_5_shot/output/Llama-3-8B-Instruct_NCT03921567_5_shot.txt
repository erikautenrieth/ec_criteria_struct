Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age of both genders from 18 to 75 years
 - ASA physiological status I-III
 - Participation in abdominal, [OR] orthopedic, [OR] laparoscopical, [OR] gynecological, [OR] urological interventions.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patient [NOT] rejection
 - History of chronic opioid intake
 - History of renal, [OR] hepatic, [OR] psychiatric disorders
 - Heart failure,
 - Organ transplant history,
 - Lidocaine [NOT] allergy
 - Family history of [NOT] malignant hyperthermia
