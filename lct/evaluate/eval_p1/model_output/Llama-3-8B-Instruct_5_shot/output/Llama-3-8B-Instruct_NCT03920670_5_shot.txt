Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age [OR] ≥ 18 years old
 - Patients willing to participate [AND] and provide an informed consent
 - Patients undergoing an elective surgical procedure that requires use of non-depolarizing NMBA agents administered intraoperatively.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with unilateral disorders [NOT] such as stroke, carpal tunnel syndrome, broken wrist with nerve damage, Dupuytren contracture, or any similar wrist injury.
 - Patients with systemic neuromuscular diseases [OR] such as myasthenia gravis
 - Patients with significant organ dysfunction that can significantly affect pharmacokinetics of neuromuscular blocking and reversal agents, i.e., severe renal impairment [OR] or end-stage liver disease.
