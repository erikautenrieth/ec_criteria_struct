Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patient diagnosed with T1D > 6 months
  - Patient's age ≥ 15 years
  - Patient [OR] any one of the family member like father, mother, or sibling using a smart phone.
  - Patient [OR] any one of the family member know how to use (receive calls [OR] or read text message) a smart phone.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patient on insulin pump
  - Pregnant females [NOT]
  - Patient with neurodevelopmental delay [OR] or thalassemia
  - Patient currently using any kind of authentic e-device for step count [OR] or physical activity.
