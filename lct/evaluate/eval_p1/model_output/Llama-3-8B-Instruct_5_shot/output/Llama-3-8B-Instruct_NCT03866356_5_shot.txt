Inclusion Criteria:
 - The inclusion criteria were being female [AND] ≥ 18 years, having an initial diagnosis of SI, being literate, having no sensory disorder affecting communication, and agreeing to participate in the research

Exclusion Criteria:
 - The exclusion criteria were pregnancy [AND] presence of a urinary tract infection [AND] previous treatment for SI.
 - The removal criteria were patient [NOT] non-compliance with the protocol, failure [NOT] in contacting the patient, and the patient's wish to withdraw from the study.
