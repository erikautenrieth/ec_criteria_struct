Inclusion Criteria:
 - all patients with cerebral thrombectomy
 - Adults
 - Patients able to express consent [AND] and
 - Signed written informed consent form
 - Covered by national health insurance
Exclusion Criteria:
 - minors [NOT]
 - pregnancy [OR] or breast feeding
 - patient deprived of liberty by administrative [OR] or judicial decision [OR] or placed under judicial protection (guardianship [OR] or supervision)
 - obstruction to participate
 - non covered by national health insurance
