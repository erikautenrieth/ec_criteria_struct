Inclusion Criteria:
  - Selected will be subjects to meeting the following criteria: male [AND] and female subjects age ≥ 20 years old, diagnosed with Osteoarthritis stages III [AND] and IV according to the American College of Rheumatology, manifesting pain, functional impairment for more than 3 months, able to wander with [OR] without canes.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Will be excluded from study subjects with history of [NOT] cancer, unstable [OR] cardiovascular disease, uncontrolled [OR] hypertension, morbid obesity, that is, BMI ≥ 40, terminal illness, cerebrovascular disease, dementia, heart pacemaker, superior [OR] inferior limb fractures.
