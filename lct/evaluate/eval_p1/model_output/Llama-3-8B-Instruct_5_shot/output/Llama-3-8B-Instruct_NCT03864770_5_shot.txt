Inclusion Criteria:
 1. ≥ 20 y/o patients with NLCs: (1) 4 times/week, last 2 weeks; (2) Occurred in nighttime [OR] or resting time of daytime
 2. Patients with MTrPs on gastrocnemius (According to the diagnostic criteria proposed by Simons [AND] Travell)
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. (1) Taking medication for leg cramps [NOT] (eg. Quinine, Magnesium oxide); (2) Other drugs that affect research [AND] evaluation (eg. diuretics, statins, calcium channel blockers, anticonvulsants)
 2. Congenital lower limb musculoskeletal diseases [OR] Lower limb [OR] spine surgery
 3. Uncommunicated [OR] cognitive impaired
 4. Patients [NOT] refused to be recruited
