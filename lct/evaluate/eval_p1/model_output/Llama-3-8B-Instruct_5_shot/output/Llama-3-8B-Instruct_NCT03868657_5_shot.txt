 - Healthy men [AND] women
 - Age ≥20 [AND] ≤40 years
 - BMI ≥18,5 [AND] ≤27,5

 - Regular medication (contraceptive pills [NOT] allowed)
 - Pregnancy [AND] breastfeeding
