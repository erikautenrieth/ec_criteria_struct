Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Refractory [OR] or relapsed AML which will include:
       1. Refractory disease [AND] will be defined as at least 1 prior treatment with no remission.
       2. Relapsed disease [AND] will be defined as 5% or more blasts in bone marrow seen after remission.
       3. Patients with AML [AND] arising from myelodysplastic syndromes (including CMML) [OR] or myeloproliferative neoplasms (secondary AML, ts-AML) are also eligible.
  2. Circulating blast count [NOT] > 200/μL within 10 days prior to first cycle of treatment. Hydroxyurea should be used to keep the peripheral blast count [NOT] > 200/μL until the first day of protocol treatment, to the extent that this is possible
  3. ECOG [NOT] > 2
  4. Estimated creatinine clearance [NOT] < 50 mL/min
  5. AST and ALT [NOT] > 3.0 x ULN
  6. Bilirubin [NOT] > 3.0 x ULN
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Active [NOT] CNS Leukemia.
  2. Known HIV [OR] or known hepatitis B [OR] or hepatitis C [OR] infection (with a detectable viral load).
  3. Participant has received strong [AND] or moderate CYP3A inducers within 7 days prior to the initiation of study treatment.
  4. Have received prior radiation to maximally tolerated levels to any critical normal organ [NOT].
  5. Clinically significant [NOT] cardiac disease.
  6. Active, uncontrolled [NOT] serious infection.
  7. Have other non-myeloid malignancy within 2 years of entry [NOT] (with exceptions).
  8. Psychiatric disorder that would [NOT] preclude study participation
  9. Previous solid organ transplant [NOT] (prior treatment with SCT is allowed but not if patient has GVHD or is still receiving immunosuppression/GVHD therapy).
