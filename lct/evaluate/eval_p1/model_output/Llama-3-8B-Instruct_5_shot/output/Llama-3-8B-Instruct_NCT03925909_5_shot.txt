Inclusion Criteria:
 - 30-60 years
 - Increased fasting blood glucose from 6.1 to 7.0 mmol / L [OR] or, Decreased glucose tolerance of 7.8 to 11.1 mmol / L [OR] or, Glycated hemoglobin with values between 5.7 [AND] and 6.4%
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Use hypoglycemic [OR] hypolipidemic drugs,
 - Use dietary supplements [NOT] (vitamins, minerals, bioflavonoids, prebiotics, probiotics or other bioactive compounds),
 - Exercise intensely [NOT] (more than 10 hours per week)
 - History of cardiovascular diseases [OR] diabetes mellitus [OR] liver [AND] and kidney disease.
