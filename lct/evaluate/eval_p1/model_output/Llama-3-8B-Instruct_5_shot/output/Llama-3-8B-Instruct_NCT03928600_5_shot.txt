Inclusion Criteria:
 - full-term (≥ 37 weeks of gestation)
 - singleton
 - vertex-presenting gestations
 - with no contraindication to vaginal delivery
 - intact membranes
 - Bishop score < 7 [AND] cervical dilation ≤2 cm
Exclusion Criteria:
 - contraindication [NOT] for misoprostol
 - history of previous caesarean
 - rupture of membranes [OR] 
 - fetal or maternal morbidities (fetal major abnormalities, FIGO definition of pathological CTG, HELLP syndrome, preeclampsia [OR] or hypertension with severe features, fetal growth restriction)
