Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Received liver transplant for treatment of hATTR amyloidosis [AND] ≥12 months before study start
  -  Has increase in polyneuropathy disability (PND) score [AND] after liver transplant
  -  Has received stable immunosuppressive regimen [AND] with ≤10 mg/day of prednisone [AND] for at least 3 months before study start
  -  Has Karnofsky Performance Status (KPS) [NOT] of <70%
  -  Has vitamin A level [NOT] less than the lower limit of normal
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Has previously received [NOT] inotersen or patisiran
  -  Has clinically significant [OR] liver function test abnormalities
  -  Has known [OR] portal hypertension with ascites
  -  Has estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) [NOT] >30 mL/min/1.73 m^2
  -  Has known [OR] leptomeningeal amyloidosis
  -  Has infection [OR] with hepatitis B, hepatitis C [OR] or human immunodeficiency virus (HIV)
  -  Has New York Heart Association heart failure classification [NOT] of >2
  -  Is [NOT] wheelchair bound [OR] bedridden
  -  Has received [NOT] organ transplants other than liver transplant
  -  Will be using [NOT] an other tetramer stabilizer during the study
