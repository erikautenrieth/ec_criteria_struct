Inclusion Criteria:
  -  complex anal fistula according to Parks classification
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Non cryptoglandular fistulas
  -  acute sepsis
  -  inflammatory bowel diseases
  -  simple fistulas [AND] (defined as subcutaneous, intersphincteric and low transsphincteric fistula)
  -  pregnant patients
  -  ASA IV
  -  patients [AND] <18
  -  immunocompromised
  -  patients who [NOT] refused consent.
