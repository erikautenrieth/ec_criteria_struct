Inclusion Criteria:
 - 19 years and older
Exclusion Criteria:
 - aspartate aminotransferase (AST) [OR] or alanine aminotransferase (ALT) more than twice the highest reference value
 - serum creatinine ≥ 1.5 mg/dL [AND] (normal range: <1.5 mg/dL)
 - uncontrolled diabetes mellitus (a fasting glucose of >160 mg/dL [AND] normal range: <160 mg/dL)
 - uncontrolled hypertension (BP > 160/100 mmHg [AND] normal range: ≤160/100 mmHg)
 - severe gastrointestinal symptoms [NOT] controlled with medication
 - alcohol abuser [NOT] willing to abstain during the study
 - pregnant [OR] or lactating women
 - allergic [OR] to the ingredient involved
 - cardiocebrovascular diseases [OR] or any cancer during the six months prior to study [AND] not in remission
 - smoker [NOT] willing to quit during the study
