Inclusion Criteria:
Persons with
  - stroke [OR] cerebrovascular disease
  - traumatic brain injury
  - multiple sclerosis
  - spinal cord injuries [AND] (including spinal cord lesions i.e. myelopathies etc.)
Exclusion Criteria:
Persons with
  - [NOT] heterotopic ossifications,
  - chronic administration of drugs, which promote fat [OR] increase
  - chronic administration of drugs, which promote bone [OR] muscle loss
  - co-existing other diseases [AND] such as: endocrinopathies, muscular dystrophies, non-alcoholic fatty liver disease.
