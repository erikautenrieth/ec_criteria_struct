Inclusion Criteria:
 - patients >18 years old, undergoing elective hepatobiliary surgery under general anesthesia [AND] and American Society of Anesthesiologists grade II.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - 1-patient refusal
 - 2-psychiatric disorders
 - 3-pregnancy [AND] lactation
 - 4-preexisting neurological dysfunction [NOT] (history of cerebrovascular stroke CVS)
 - 5-metastatic cancer
 - 6-Inflammatory bowel disease
 - 7-Diabetes mellitus
 - 8-Renal insufficiency [NOT] (serum creatinine level more than 180 μmol/l)
 - 9-unexpected intraoperative findings [OR] (inoperable)
 - 10-accidental massive intraoperative hemorrhage
