Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patient requiring an electroneuromyogram whose indication [AND] allows the application of a standard protocol of examination.
  - Patient that has given his/her free [AND] informed consent.
  - Patient that signed the consent form.
  - Patient with an health insurance plan.
  - The patient is at least 18 years old [AND] (≥).
  - The patient is under 80 years old [AND] (≤).
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patient with symptoms [NOT] of neuropathy during the screening.
  - Patient whose Neurological severity score (NSS) score is [OR] ≥ 3.
  - Patient whose neurological examination is [OR] abnormal and suggestive of neuropathy.
  - Patient presenting a pathology [OR] that could cause neuropathy (e.g. diabetes, renal failure, etc.).
  - Patient already participating in a category 1 research study [OR] (Jardé law).
  - Patient in an exclusion period [OR] determined by another study.
  - Patient under the protection [NOT] of justice, under guardianship [OR] or under curatorship.
  - Patient refusing [NOT] to sign the consent form.
  - Patient for which it is [NOT] impossible to provide information about the study.
  - Pregnant [OR] patient, parturient, or breastfeeding.
