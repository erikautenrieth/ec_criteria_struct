Inclusion Criteria:
 - healthy volunteers
 - fluent in German language
Exclusion Criteria:
 - mental disorders
 - allergic to capsaicin [OR] sesame oil
 - intake of psychopharmacological drugs [AND] in the last four weeks
 - intake of illegal drugs [AND] in the last two weeks
 - consumption of alcohol [NOT] in the last twelve hours
 - students [AND] in medicine, pharmacy, or psychology
 - completed studies [AND] in medicine, pharmacy or psychology
 - current [NOT] pregnancy or lactation
 - cardio vascular disease
 - kidney disease
 - liver disease
