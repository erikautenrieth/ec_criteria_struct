Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients have been diagnosed with Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia
  -  Childhood patients [AND] who were undergoing chemotherapy [OR] or continuous follow-up after completion of chemotherapy
  -  Patients received the phase of maintenance therapy that included oral 6-MP (>4 weeks) [AND] and completion of ≥ 6 months according to the CCLG (Chinese Children's Leukemia Group) protocol-ALL 2015
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients [NOT] with high-risk ALL (presence of higher-risk features: MRD ≥ 1% at 46 day, [OR] or age < 6 month [AND] and white blood cell (WBC) count ≥ 300×109/L with translocations t(9;22) (q34;q11) [BCR-ABL], t(4;11) (q21;q23) [AF4/MLL], t(1;19) (q23;p13) [E2A-PBX1] [OR] or other MLL-rearrangements) were removed
