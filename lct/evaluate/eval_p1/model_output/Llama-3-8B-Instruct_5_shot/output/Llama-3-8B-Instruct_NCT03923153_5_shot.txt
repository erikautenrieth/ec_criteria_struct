Inclusion Criteria:
 - No contraindication for pulmonary physiotherapy
 - Anjina patients who are clinically stable [AND] and
 - have no other disease that may affect respiratory function [AND]
 - Individuals who have the good cooperation
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [NOT] under the age of 18
 - Pregnancy
 - Active infection
 - Patients with known malignancies
 - patients [NOT] without consent
 - known arrhythmia [OR] dilated [OR] hypertrophic cardiomyopathy [OR] heart failure (EF <40%)
