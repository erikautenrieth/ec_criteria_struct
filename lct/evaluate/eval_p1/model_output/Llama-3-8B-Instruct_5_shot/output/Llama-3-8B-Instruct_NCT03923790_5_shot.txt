Inclusion Criteria:
  - Ischemic [OR] and hemorrhagic stroke patients
  - Presence of at least one of the following high risk criteria: [AND] uninsured, [OR] Medicaid payer status, [OR] small vessel ischemic stroke, [OR] hypertensive ICH
  - Age ≥ 18 [AND] presence of hypertension (by clinical history [OR] hospital BP ≥140/90 on two occasions)
  - Plan to discharge home [AND] after stroke
  - Ability to provide consent (patient [OR] caregiver)
  - Ability to communicate in English
Exclusion Criteria:
  - modified Rankin scale > 4 [NOT] at the time of enrollment (severe disability)
  - life expectancy < 1 year [OR] terminal illness,
  - eGFR < 30 [NOT] at time of discharge
  - pregnancy
  - symptomatic flow limiting carotid stenosis [AND] without plan for intervention
  - urine toxicology positive for cocaine [OR] methamphetamine [OR] recent use
  - long-term BP goal ≥ 130/80 mmHg [NOT] according to clinical team
