Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age > 18 years;
 - Unilateral fracture of distal radius [AND] without misalignment (dorsal angulation <10°, axial radial shortening <5mm), [AND] <2mm intraarticular stepoff;
 - Independent in activities of daily living;
 - Capable [AND] and willing of signing informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Children [NOT] under the age of 18;
 - Fracture of the contralateral arm;
 - Other fractures of the ipsilateral arm;
 - Open fractures;
 - Fractures [NOT] that require reduction.
