Inclusion Criteria:
Healthy and T2D (controlled by diet and lifestyle [OR] metformin) volunteers; Age 18+; males [AND] and females.

Exclusion Criteria:
For the healthy group:
  - taking prescribed medication [NOT]
  - HbA1c above 48mmol/mol (or 6.5%)
  - pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding
  - blood donation in the last three months
  - unable to give written informed consent
  - eating disorders such as anorexia [OR] bulimia [OR] binge eating [OR] night eating syndrome
  - significant health issue [NOT]
  - taking vitamin or mineral supplements [NOT] (unless they agree to discontinue supplementation 2 weeks or more before the start of the study)
  - Food allergies/intolerances

For the T2D groups:
  - T2D controlled by medication [NOT] other than metformin
  - pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding
  - blood donation in the last three months
  - unable to give written informed consent
  - eating disorders such as anorexia [OR] bulimia [OR] binge eating [OR] night eating syndrome
  - any other significant health issue [NOT] other than T2D
  - taking vitamin or mineral supplements [NOT] (unless they agree to discontinue supplementation 2 weeks before the start of the study)
  - Food allergies/intolerances
