Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients scheduled for elective laparoscopic colorectal surgery at UWMC requiring inpatient stay

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients on chronic pain medications [AND] equaling or exceeding > 25 morphine daily equivalents
 - Patients [NOT] allergic to bupivacaine
 - Patients with ASA status [OR] IV, [OR] V, [OR] VI
 - Patients [NOT] able to consent
 - Patients [OR] that are pregnant
 - Patients [OR] that are incarcerated
 - Patients [OR] receiving procedures in addition to laparoscopic colorectal procedure
 - Patients on systemic anticoagulation [NOT] precluding them from regional blocks
