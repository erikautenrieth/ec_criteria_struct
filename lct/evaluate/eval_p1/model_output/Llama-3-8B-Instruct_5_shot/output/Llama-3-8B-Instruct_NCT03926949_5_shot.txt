Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients 18 years of age [OR] older
 2. Patients screened at risk of malnutrition by Canadian Nutrition Screening Tool (CNST) [AND] and identified as malnourished by subjective global assessment (SGA) B [OR] C

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients undergoing minor [OR] laparoscopic surgery
 2. Pregnancy
 3. Patients with severe systemic diseases [NOT] defined by American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) classification I [OR] II
 4. Patients with diabetes mellitus
 5. Patients with planned palliative treatment
