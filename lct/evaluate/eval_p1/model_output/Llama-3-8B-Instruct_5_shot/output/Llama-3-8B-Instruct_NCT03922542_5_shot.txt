Inclusion Criteria:
 - Documented keratoconus [OR] or ectasia after refractive surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Insufficient corneal thickness
 - Ocular condition [OR] that may predispose the eye to complications
 - History of chemical injury [OR] or delayed epithelial healing
 - Condition [NOT] that would interfere with [OR] prolong epithelial healing
 - Known sensitivity [OR] to treatment medications
 - Pregnancy
