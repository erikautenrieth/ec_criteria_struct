Inclusion Criteria:
 - Aged above 60
 - Confirmed literacy [AND] Diagnosed with mild cognitive impairment by International Working Group on Mild Cognitive Impairment [AND] Clinical Dementia Rating (CDR) of 0 [OR] 0.5.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Evidence of delirium [OR] confusion
 - Any neurological conditions causing cognitive decline [AND] such as Parkinson's disease, brain hemorrhage, brain tumor, normal pressure hydrocephalus
 - Evidence of severe cerebrovascular pathology
 - Presence of depressive symptoms that [NOT] could influence cognitive function
 - Presence of medical comorbidities that [NOT] could result in any difficulties in study participation
