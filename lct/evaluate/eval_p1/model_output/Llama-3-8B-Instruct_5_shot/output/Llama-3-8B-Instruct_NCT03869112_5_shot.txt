Inclusion Criteria:
 - Participant is willing [AND] able to give informed consent for participation in the study
 - Male [OR] Female, aged 40 years to 85 years.
 - Diagnosed with COPD
 - Able [AND] (in the Investigators opinion) [AND] willing to comply with all study requirements.
 - Participant is willing to attend visits at baseline [AND] 8 weeks (sub-group: baseline, 8 weeks)     
 - Able to read [AND] understand English
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Age <40
 - Attended a pulmonary rehabilitation programme [OR] participating in a physical activity intervention study in current time [OR] in the last 6 months.
 - Any other significant diseases [OR] disorders that are [NOT] a contraindication to be enrolled in a pulmonary rehabilitation programme.
