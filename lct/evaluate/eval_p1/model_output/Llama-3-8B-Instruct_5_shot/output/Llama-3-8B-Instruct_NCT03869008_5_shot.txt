Inclusion Criteria:
• Clinical Diagnosis of Primary Raynaud's Phenomenon
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Less than 18 years of age
  - Pregnant [NOT] women
  - Current [NOT] smokers
  - Have [NOT] Digital ulcers
  - Diagnosed [NOT] Pulmonary hypertension
  - Currently on vasodilators [OR] (i.e. Phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitors, prostacyclin, nitroglycerine, or other nitric oxide derivatives)
  - Currently on Calcium Channel Blockers
