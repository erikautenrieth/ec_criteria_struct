Inclusion Criteria:
 - male 18-50 years
 - oligozoospermia: < 5 million spermatozoa/mL;
 - asthenozoospermia: < 32% progressive motile spermatozoa;
 - teratozoospermia: < 4% normal forms
Exclusion Criteria:
 - azoospermia [NOT]
 - smoke [OR] use drugs [OR] use drugs with proven fertility toxicity [OR] exposition to any environmental or occupational toxic substances [OR] exposition to radiation [OR] or heat
 - orchitis secondary to mumps [NOT]
 - sexually transmitted diseases [NOT]
 - history of cryptorchidism [NOT]
 - previous testicular torsion [NOT]
 - genitourinary anomalies [NOT]
 - alterations of the epididymis [OR] or deferens [NOT]
 - inguinal surgery [NOT]
