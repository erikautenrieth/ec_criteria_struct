Inclusion Criteria:
 - The reproductive age of the woman
 - Blood reflux in the parametric, uterine, gonadal veins
 - The absence of any concomitant pathology [AND] accompanied by chronic pelvic pain
Exclusion Criteria:
 - The absence of blood reflux [NOT] in the parametric, uterine, gonadal veins
 - The presence of diseases [OR] the clinical course of which suggests the presence of chronic pelvic pain [AND] and other varieties of chronic pain, including migraine
 - Pregnancy
