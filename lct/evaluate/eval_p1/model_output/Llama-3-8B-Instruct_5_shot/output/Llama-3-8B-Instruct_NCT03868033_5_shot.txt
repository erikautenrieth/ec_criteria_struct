Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Postmenopausal women
 2. Men >50-year-old [AND] 
 3. After Denosumab treatment ≥ 2 years due to osteoporosis

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. secondary osteoporosis
 2. metabolic bone disease
 3. cancer history
 4. continuous steroid therapy
 5. hormone replacement therapy
 6. ever used any other kind of anti-resorptive treatment [OR] 
 7. estimated Glomerular filtration rate [NOT] < 35 mL/min 
 8. allergy to Zoledronic acid
 9. hypocalcaemia 
 10. [NOT] any contraindications to use Zoledronic acid
