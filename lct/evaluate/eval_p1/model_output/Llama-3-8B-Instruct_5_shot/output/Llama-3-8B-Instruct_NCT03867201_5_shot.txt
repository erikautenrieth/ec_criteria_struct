Key inclusion Criteria:
 1. History of at least 5 attacks of migraine
 2. ≥ 15 headache days [AND] of which ≥ 8 headache days meet criteria as migraine days during the baseline period
 3. >=80% diary compliance during the baseline period
Key exclusion Criteria:
 1. Older than 50 years of age at migraine onset [NOT]
 2. History of cluster [OR] or hemiplegic headache
 3. Evidence of seizure [OR] or major psychiatric disorder
 4. Cardiac [OR] or active hepatic disease
 5. Pregnant [OR] or nursing
