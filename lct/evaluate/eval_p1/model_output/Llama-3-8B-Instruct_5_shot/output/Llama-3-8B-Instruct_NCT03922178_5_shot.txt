Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient of the CHU Brugmann Hospital receiving elective coronary surgery.
 - Patient in sinusal rhythm [AND] before the operation [AND] during the collection of hemodynamic data.
 - Patients with conserved left ventricular function [AND] (based on left ventricular ejection fraction assessed by preoperative echocardiography) [AND] superior to 50%

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy [NOT] (diastolic septal thickness> 15 mm)
 - Heart failure with left ventricular ejection fraction <50%
 - Presence of cardiac valvulopathy [OR] concerning both right [AND] and left atrio-ventricular [AND] and ventriculo-arterial valves
 - Presence of valvular prosthesis
 - Congenital heart disease
 - Pregnancy
 - Participation to another clinical study
