Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients must be [AND] ≥ 3 [AND] ≤ 18 years of age.
 - The predicted survival period is [AND] more than 3 months.
 - According to RECIST version 1.1, there is [OR] at least one measurable lesion.
 - Postoperative pathological stage was [AND] group II-IV.
 - Patients have [NOT] received radiotherapy before, and can [AND] accept chemotherapy [AND] and surgical treatment.
 - No other anticancer therapy should be used [NOT] during radiotherapy.
 - The main organs are functioning normally, which meets the following criteria:
 1. Blood routine examination standards should be met: (no blood transfusion within 14 days) A. HB > 90 g/L [AND] B. ANC > 1.5 * 109/L [AND] C. PLT > 80 * 109 /L
 2. Biochemical tests should meet the following criteria:
A. BIL < 1.25 times normal upper limit (ULN) [AND] B. ALT and AST < 2.5 ULN [AND] C. Serum Cr < 1 ULN, endogenous creatinine clearance > 50 ml/min
 - Subjects volunteered to participate in this study, patients [OR] or legal guardians signed informed consent through patient consent, good compliance, with follow-up.
 - Doctors believe that treatment can [AND] benefit patients.

Note: I inserted the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] to represent the logical connections between the eligibility criteria.
