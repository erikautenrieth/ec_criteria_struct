Inclusion Criteria:
 1. ≥ 20 years of age
 2. fluent in Mandarin [OR] or Taiwanese
 3. diagnosed with AF
 4. treated with warfarin [AND] or NOACs for anticoagulation.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. diagnosed with [NOT] psychological diseases
 2. diagnosed with [NOT] uncontrolled hypertension
 3. diagnosed with the New York Heart Association (NYHA) grade VI [NOT] heart failure
 4. implanted with a [NOT] cardiac pacemaker
 5. had a [NOT] cardiac surgery in the past three months
 6. hospitalized for AF [NOT] in the past three months.
