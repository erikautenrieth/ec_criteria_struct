Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Newborn [AND] and infant age group
  -  Undergoing elective surgery [AND] requiring intubation under general anesthesia
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History [NOT] of congenital maxillofacial defect,
  -  History [OR] of upper airway pathology (tumor, cleft palate-lips, etc.)
  -  History [OR] of head [AND] and neck trauma (fracture, swelling, scar )
