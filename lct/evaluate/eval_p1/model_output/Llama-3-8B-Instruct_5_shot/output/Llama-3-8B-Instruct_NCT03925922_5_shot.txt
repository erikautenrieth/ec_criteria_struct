Inclusion Criteria:
 - Nulliparous singleton pregnancy
 - gestational age between 37week [AND] and 42 weeks
 - Cephalic presentation
 - Bishop score of 5 [OR] less
 - No clinical evidence of regular contraction
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Rupture of membrane
 - Vaginal bleeding
 - Any contraindication to vaginal delivery [NOT]
