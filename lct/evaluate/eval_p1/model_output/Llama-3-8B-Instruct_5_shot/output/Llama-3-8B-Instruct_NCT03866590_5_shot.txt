Inclusion Criteria:
  - Informed consent is obtained from the participant [OR] its legal representatives
  - The participant is [AND] older than 14 years [AND] younger than 25 years old
  - The patient [AND] exhibits chronic anaemia [OR] or cholelithiasis [OR] or cholecystitis of undetermined aetiology [OR] or The patient has a high-grade PKD suspicion due to any clinical item [OR] or The patient is diagnosed biochemically [AND] and/or genetically with PKD
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Inability to provide informed consent
  - The patient [NOT] does not suffer from chronic anaemia [OR] or cholelithiasis [OR] or cholecystitis
  - The aetiology of chronic anaemia [OR] or cholelithiasis [OR] or cholecystitis is [NOT] determined and is [NOT] due to PKD
  - The participant is [NOT] younger than 14 years [OR] older than 25 years old
  - Previously enrolled in the study
