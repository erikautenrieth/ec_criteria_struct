Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Male [OR] Female aged ≥ 19 years
 2. Episode of heartburn for 3 months [OR] or more during prior to randomization visit.
 3. Episode of heartburn for 2 days [OR] or more during the last 7 days prior to randomization visit.
 4. Grade N [AND] M by EGD test.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Surgery history on stomach [OR] or esophagus
 2. Active medical history of stomach [OR] esophagus area
 3. Other system disorder which [NOT] can disturb this trial
 4. Patients taking the contraindication [OR] of concomitant medications
 5. Clinically significant [NOT] Abnormal Lab test
 6. Pregnant [NOT] woman, Breastfeeding [NOT] woman.
