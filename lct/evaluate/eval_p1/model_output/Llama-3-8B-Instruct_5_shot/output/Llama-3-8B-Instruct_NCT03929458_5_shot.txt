Inclusion Criteria:
 - Both sexes [AND] aged between 20-90 years.
 - Received stable hemodialysis at least 3 months.
 - Written informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - patients with severe infections [OR] severe heart disease [OR] and liver disease, malignancy, autoimmune disorders, severe malnutrition, [OR] or clinical conditions requiring oral nutrition supplements;
 - Inability [NOT] to follow protocol.
 - Pregnancy [OR] or wishing/trying [NOT] to get pregnant.
