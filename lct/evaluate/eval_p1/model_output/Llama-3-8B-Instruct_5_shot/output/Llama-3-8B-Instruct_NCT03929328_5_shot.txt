Inclusion Criteria:
  - Adult patients [AND] aged 18 years or more, [AND] both gender
  - Receiving endotracheal intubation [AND] and mechanical ventilation for more than 12 hours
  - Recovery from the precipitating illness
  - Weaning readiness [AND] according to the following criteria :
       1. Respiratory criteria :
            1. PaO2:FIO2 >150 [AND] with FIO2 ≤0.4, PEEP <8 cm H2O
            2. Arterial pH >7.35
            3. Rapid shallow breathing index (RSBI) < 105
            4. Maximum inspiratory pressure (MIP) < -20 cm H20
       2. Clinical criteria :
            1. Absence of electrocardiographic signs of myocardial ischemia
            2. No vasoactive drugs [OR] or vital signs are stable with using vasoactive drugs
            3. Heart rate <140/min,
            4. Hemoglobin >8 g/dL
            5. Temperature <38°C
            6. No need for sedatives [OR] or mental status are stable with sedatives
            7. Presence of respiratory stimulus, [AND] and appropriate spontaneous cough
            8. Absence of excessive tracheobronchial secretions
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Tracheostomy status [NOT]
  - Decision to stop life-supportive therapies [NOT] before enrollment
