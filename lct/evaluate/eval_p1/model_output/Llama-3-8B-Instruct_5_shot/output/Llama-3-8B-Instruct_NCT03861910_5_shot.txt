Inclusion Criteria:
 - age 1-12 months
 - IgE-mediated CMA
Exclusion Criteria:
 - cow's milk protein-induced anaphylaxis
 - food protein induced enterocolitis syndrome
 - other food allergies [OR] other allergic diseases
 - non-CMA-related atopic eczema
 - eosinophilic disorders of the gastrointestinal tract
 - chronic systemic diseases [AND] chronic inflammatory bowel diseases
 - congenital cardiac defects
 - active tuberculosis
 - autoimmune diseases [OR] immunodeficiency
 - celiac disease
 - cystic fibrosis
 - metabolic diseases
 - malignancy
 - chronic pulmonary diseases
 - malformations of the gastrointestinal and/or respiratory tract
 - [NOT] administration of prebiotics or probiotics during the 4 weeks before enrolment
