Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of periodontitis (stages [OR] III [OR] IV) that may require periodontal surgery
 - Have at least 10 teeth [AND] in function, excluding third molars.
 - Present locations with probing depth (PS)> 6 mm [AND] in at least 30% of the teeth.
 - Present radiographic evidence of moderate- [OR] severe bone loss [AND] in at least 30% of the dentition.
 - Detection of P. gingivalis [AND] in subgingival samples taken at the screening visit [AND] as well as in the post-scaling and root planning visit [AND] processed by culture.
 - Systemically [NOT] healthy patients.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnant [OR] or lactating women.
 - Presenting systemic pathology [AND] / or taking medication [AND] that may affect the periodontal situation [AND] / or patients requiring antibiotic prophylaxis.
 - Have received systemic antimicrobial treatment [NOT] in the previous 6 months.
 - Have received periodontal treatment [NOT] in the 6 months prior to the beginning of the study.
 - Patients [NOT] allergic to metronidazole, or to any of the components of commercial formulations thereof (Flagyl®).
 - Patients [NOT] allergic to azithromycin, or to any of the components of commercial formulations thereof (Zithromax®).
 - Patients who [NOT] refuse to sign the informed consent.
