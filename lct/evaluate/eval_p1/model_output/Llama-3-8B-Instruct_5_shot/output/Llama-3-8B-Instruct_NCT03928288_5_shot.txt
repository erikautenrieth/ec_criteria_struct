Inclusion Criteria:
 - Female with surgically-confirmed endometriosis, determined at laparoscopy performed within 3y [NOT] prior to study baseline
 - Age 15 years [AND] to 40 years
 - Current use ≥ 2 months duration of hormonal therapy [AND] such as combined oral contraceptives, norethindrone acetate, [OR] or levonorgestrel intrauterine device
 - Current pelvic pain (score ≥ 3 on Visual Analog Scale, where 0 represents absence of pain [AND] and 10 indicates unbearable pain) present for ≥ 14 days/month [AND] over the 2 months prior to study enrollment
 - Willingness to comply with visit schedule [AND] and protocol
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pre-menarche [OR] or post-menopause
 - Contraindications to cabergoline (e.g., cardiac valve disorder; pulmonary, pericardial, retroperitoneal fibrotic disorder; hypersensitivity to ergot derivatives; uncontrolled hypertension)
 - Significant mental [OR] or chronic systemic illness that might confound pain assessment [OR] or the ability to complete the study
 - Pregnant [OR] breastfeeding [OR] or planning to become pregnant in the next 6 months
 - Impaired liver function (ALT > 2x normal) [OR] or liver disease
 - Breast cancer, current [OR] previous
 - Thromboembolic disease, current [OR] previous
 - Use of other drugs that affect dopamine (e.g., phenothiazines, metoclopramide, butyrophenones)
