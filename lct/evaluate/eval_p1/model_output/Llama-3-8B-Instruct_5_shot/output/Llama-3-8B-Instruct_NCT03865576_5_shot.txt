Inclusion Criteria:
 - ICU patients suspected of having an abnormality of ICP [AND] undergoing monitoring with commercially-available, FDA-approved instrumentation.

Exclusion Criteria:
History of:
  - [NOT] Glaucoma [OR] optic hypertension
  - [NOT] Severe orbital [OR] ophthalmic injury
  - [NOT] Orbital [OR] intraocular surgery that might interfere with orbital compliance
  - [NOT] Congenital malformations of the eye [AND] /or orbit that might interfere with orbital compliance [OR] orbital vascular anatomy
