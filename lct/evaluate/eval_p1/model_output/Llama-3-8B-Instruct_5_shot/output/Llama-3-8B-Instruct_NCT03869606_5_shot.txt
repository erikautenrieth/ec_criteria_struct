Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients 11 to no older than 17 years of age
Exclusion Criteria:
 - severed [NOT] developmental delay,
 - orthopedic patients with leg injuries [AND] injury that would prevent passive leg elevation.
 - severe aortic insufficiency
 - severe [AND] anatomic abnormalities of the thoracic aorta
 - patients with external [NOT] cardiac pacemakers.
