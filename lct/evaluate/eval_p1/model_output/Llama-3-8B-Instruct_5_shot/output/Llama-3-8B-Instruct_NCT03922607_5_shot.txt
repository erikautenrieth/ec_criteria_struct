Inclusion Criteria:
 - Volunteer should be between 18 [AND] and 55 years of age [AND] in general good health for Substudy 1 [AND] participant with moderate to severe chronic plaque psoriasis between 18 [AND] and 75 years of age for Substudy 2 at the time of enrollment.
 - Participant should meet the laboratory assessments as mentioned in the protocol.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Participant has a history of [NOT] epilepsy, any significant [OR] cardiac, [OR] respiratory, [OR] renal, [OR] hepatic, [OR] gastrointestinal, [OR] opthalmologic, [OR] hematologic, [OR] psychiatric disease or disorder, or any [NOT] uncontrolled medical illness.
