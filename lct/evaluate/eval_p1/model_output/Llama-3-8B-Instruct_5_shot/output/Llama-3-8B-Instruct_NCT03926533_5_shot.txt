Inclusion Criteria:
 - index ICU stay with a diagnosis of sepsis [OR] or acute respiratory distress syndrome projected to be discharged alive as these patients are at the highest risk for developing PICS

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. no access to computer, electronic device (e.g., tablet, smartphone), and/or internet connection for virtual clinic visit, [AND] or
 2. primary care received outside of VUMC network, [OR]
 3. hospice care at discharge, [NOT]
 4. substance abuse [OR] or psychiatric disorder that prevents independent living, [AND]
 5. blind [OR] deaf [OR] unable to speak English, [OR]
 6. severe dementia prior to index hospitalization, [NOT]
