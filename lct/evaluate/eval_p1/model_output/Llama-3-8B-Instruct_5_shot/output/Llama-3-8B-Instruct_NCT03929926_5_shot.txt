Inclusion Criteria:
 - Had a recent office visit with a primary care physician [AND] in one of the study practices.
 - History of smoking [OR] (current [OR] former) in the electronic health record (EHR).
Exclusion Criteria:
 - LDCT performed [NOT] in the 12 months prior to study initiation according to EHR.
 - Diagnosis of lung cancer [NOT] indicated in problem list in the EHR.
