Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient who accepted to participate in the study
 - Woman [AND] over 18 years old
 - Affiliated to a social security scheme
 - Mono-fetal pregnancy
 - Patients with normal pregnancy [AND] between 11 [AND] 42 weeks of amenorrhea
 - Patients hospitalized for preeclampsia [OR] and / or intrauterine growth retardation, beyond 20 weeks of amenorrhea
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Minors [NOT], adults under guardianship [NOT], protected persons
 - Not affiliated to a social security scheme
 - Multiple pregnancy
