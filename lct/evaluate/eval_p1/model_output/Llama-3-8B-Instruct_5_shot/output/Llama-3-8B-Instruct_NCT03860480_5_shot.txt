Inclusion Criteria:
• Patients > 18 years old [AND] with to ASA status I-III, undergoing VATS for lobectomy [OR] or wedge.

Non-inclusion Criteria:
  -  < 18 years old
  -  BMI > 35
  -  Chronic pain history [AND] with regular opioid [OR] and/or gabapentinoids use during the 2 weeks before surgery
  -  Regular marijuana use
  -  History of thoracic surgery [NOT] on the operated side
  -  Epidural analgesia preferred [NOT] due to an anticipated high risk of conversion to thoracotomy
  -  Unable to communicate [NOT] with the investigators
  -  Receiving anticoagulation [OR] experiencing any bleeding disorder
  -  Surgery for empyema [AND] sympathectomy
  -  Known allergy [NOT] to local anesthetics, fentanyl [OR] or hydromorphone
  -  Active infection [NOT] at injection sites
  -  Preexisting neurological deficit [OR] psychiatric illness
  -  Severe cardiovascular disease
  -  Liver failure
  -  Renal failure (estimated glomerular filtration rate <15 mL/ min/1.73 m2)
  -  Pregnancy

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Perioperative conversion [NOT] to thoracotomy
  -  Severe intra- [OR] postoperative bleeding
  -  Patients requiring postoperative mechanical ventilation
  -  Technical inability [NOT] to proceed with the blocks
