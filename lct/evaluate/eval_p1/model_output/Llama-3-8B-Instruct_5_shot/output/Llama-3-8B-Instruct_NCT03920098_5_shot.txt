Inclusion Criteria:
 - Primipara [AND] (no previous pregnancies)
 - 18 to 25 years of age
 - Gestation <15 weeks at first prenatal visit
 - No intention to move outside the city of Santiago in the next 2 years

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Multipara
 - Pre-existing cancer [NOT] (except non-melanoma skin cancer)
 - Family history of breast cancer [OR] (one first-degree female relative diagnosed with breast cancer)
 - High-risk pregnancy [AND] according to national guidelines
 - During the follow-up, pregnant women under [NOT] any treatment that may affect the levels of metabolic markers will be excluded from the analysis
 - Pregnant women with pre-eclampsia [OR] or with preterm birth (less than 37 weeks of gestational age) will be also excluded
