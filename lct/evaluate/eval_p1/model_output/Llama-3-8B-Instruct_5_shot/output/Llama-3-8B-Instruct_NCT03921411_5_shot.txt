Key Inclusion Criteria
  - Male [OR] female subjects ≥ 12 to < 18 years of age
  - Chronic AD [AND] that has been documented for at least 2 years
  - Eczema Area and Severity Index (EASI) score ≥ 16
  - IGA score ≥ 3
  - AD involvement ≥ 10% of Body Surface Area (BSA)
  - Documented recent history of inadequate response to topical medications
  - Women of childbearing potential [NOT] must agree to be strictly abstinent [OR] to use an effective and approved method of contraception throughout the study and for 12 weeks after the last study drug injection.

Key Exclusion Criteria
  - Body weight [NOT] < 30 kg
  - Cutaneous infection [NOT] within 1 week [OR] any infection requiring treatment with oral [OR] parenteral antibiotics, antivirals, antiparasitics, [OR] antifungals within 1 week
  - History of hypersensitivity [NOT] (including anaphylaxis) to an immunoglobulin product (plasma-derived [OR] recombinant, eg, monoclonal antibody)
  - Any [NOT] clinically significant issue, based investigator judgement
