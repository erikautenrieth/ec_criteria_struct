Inclusion Criteria:
 - All newly registered patients with smear-positive recurrent pulmonary TB
 - Adults [AND] as well as children (no age limit)
 - Able [AND] and willing to provide written informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [NOT] transferred to a health facility not supported by Damien Foundation will be excluded. This includes patients diagnosed with HIV [OR] TB-coinfection.
