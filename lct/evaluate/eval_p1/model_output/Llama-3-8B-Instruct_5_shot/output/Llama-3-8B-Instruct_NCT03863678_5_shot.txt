Inclusion Criteria:
 - First time stroke [AND] patients who has spasticity due to stroke
 - At least 6 months after stroke
 - Individuals between the ages of 18 [AND] 75 will be included in the study
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Any contraindication for dry needling [OR] (eg, anticoagulants, infections, bleeding, etc.)
 - Having diabetes [OR] having cardiovascular diseases [OR] having any other neurological problems
 - Application of Botox within 6 months prior to the study [NOT]
 - Individuals included in another treatment program [NOT] will not be included in the study
