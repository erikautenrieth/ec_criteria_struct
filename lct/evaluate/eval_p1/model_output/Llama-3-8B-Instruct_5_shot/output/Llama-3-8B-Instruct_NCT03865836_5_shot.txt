Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Subject must have a diagnosis of LOPD [AND] based on documentation of one of the following:
       1. deficiency of GAA enzyme
       2. GAA genotyping
 2. Subject must provide signed informed consent [AND] prior to any study-related procedures being performed.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Subject is [NOT] eligible for enrollment in an ongoing clinical trial using ATB200/AT2221.
 2. Subject, if female, is [NOT] pregnant [OR] breastfeeding at screening.
 3. Subject, whether male [OR] female, is [NOT] planning to conceive a child during the study.
