Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Age >18 years
 2. on PD treatment for at least one month [AND] 
 3. signed the consent

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Peritonitis [OR] recovery from peritonitis for [NOT] less than one month.
 2. On any intra peritoneal treatment [AND] apart from peritoneal dialysis.
 3. Evidence of decompensated liver cirrhosis [OR] heart failure.
 4. Evidence of extra peritoneal leakage [OR] eg. leakage to thoracic cavity [OR] post peritoneal [OR] hernia.
