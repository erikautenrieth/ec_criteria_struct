Inclusion Criteria:
  - Must have a clinical diagnosis of solid tumor cancer [AND] be seeking treatment and supportive care at the UF Health Cancer Center (Medical Plaza)
  - Patients must plan to receive chemotherapy known to be associated with the need for supportive care [AND] within 30 days of enrollment
  - Life expectancy must be greater than 6 months [NOT] less than 6 months
  - Must have Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status [AND] of 0-2
  - Written informed consent [NOT] obtained from the patient
  - The ability for the patient [AND] to comply with all the study-related procedures.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [NOT] unwilling or unable to provide voluntary informed consent
  - Patients who are [NOT] unwilling or unable to comply with protocol requirement [OR] and/or follow-up procedures
  - Patients planned to undergo cancer therapy [NOT] other than chemotherapy (i.e., radiation, surgery [OR] or hormonal treatment alone)
  - Prisoners [OR] or patients who are involuntarily incarcerated. Patients who are compulsorily detained for treatment of either a psychiatric [OR] or physical illness.
