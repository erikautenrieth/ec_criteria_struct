Inclusion Criteria:
 - AIS.
 - 10-16 years old, with brace treatment [AND].
 - Ability to read and understand English [OR] French.
 - Physical [AND] mental ability to adhere to bracing protocol.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of other musculoskeletal [OR] developmental illness that might be responsible for the spinal curvature [NOT].
 - Patients with symptom of a neurological disorder [NOT].
 - Patients with any other disorder of the musculoskeletal system affecting the lower limbs [NOT].
