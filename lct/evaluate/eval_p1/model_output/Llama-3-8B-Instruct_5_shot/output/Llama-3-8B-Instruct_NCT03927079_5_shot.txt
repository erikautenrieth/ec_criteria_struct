 - patients who undergo a flexible bronchoscopic lavage [AND] with a beta lactamines treatment as cephalosporin of 3rd generation (CEFTRIAXONE / CEFOTAXIME, CEFTAZIDIME) [OR] 4th generation (CEFEPIM) [OR] AMOXICILLIN-CLAVULANIC ACID [OR] PIPERACILLIN-TAZOBACTAM
