Inclusion Criteria:
 - over 21 years of age
 - able to understand [AND] speak English
 - self-report of having difficulty with a healthy diet [AND] engaging in regular physical activity.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - On chemotherapy for cancer treatment
 - Current substance abuse disorder [OR] (i.e., drugs, alcohol).
 - Current diagnoses of Post-traumatic stress disorders, severe anxiety [OR] or severe depression.
 - On hemodialysis
 - Pregnant [NOT] (Pregnant women may develop Preeclampsia, which is a pregnancy complication characterized by high blood pressure)
