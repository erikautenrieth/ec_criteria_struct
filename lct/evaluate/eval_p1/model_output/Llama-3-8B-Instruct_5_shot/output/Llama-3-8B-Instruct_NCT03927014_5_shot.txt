Inclusion Criteria:
 - preeclampsia
 - healthy pregnancy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - pregnant women with any systemic condition [OR] (such as chronic hypertension, diabetes mellitus, thyroid diseases, liver and kidney diseases)
 - women with a history of drug use [AND] throughout pregnancy
 - history of medication for PE treatment [NOT] at the time of first admission
 - patients who had fetal congenital abnormalities [OR] or genetic syndromes
 - multiple gestation
 - active labor
