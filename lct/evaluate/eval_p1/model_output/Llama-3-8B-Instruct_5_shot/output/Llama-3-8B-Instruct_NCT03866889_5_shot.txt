Inclusion Criteria:
 - Military personnel belonging to the Military Emergency Unit
 - male
 - With an age of 25 [AND] to 45 years
 - No history of musculoskeletal injury in the 3 months prior to the study
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Present a diagnosis of injury [AND] in the period prior to [OR] during the study
 - Who are [NOT] receiving a physiotherapy intervention at the time of the study
 - [NOT] signed the informed consent document
