Inclusion Criteria:
- Critically ill cirrhotics with early onset septic shock (<24 hours) [AND] with norepinephrine of more than 10 ug/min [AND] and lactate >4 mmol/L.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients with age [NOT] less than 18 years
  - Severe known cardiopulmonary disease (structural [OR] or valvular heart disease, coronary artery disease, COPD)
  - Severe coagulopathy (platelets [NOT] <20,000 [AND] INR [NOT] >5)
  - Active Bleed (Mucosal [OR] or variceal)
  - Pregnancy
  - Chronic kidney disease
  - Extremely moribund patients with an expected life expectancy [NOT] of less than 24 hours
  - Failure to give informed consent from family members.
  - Hemodynamic instability requiring very high dose of vasopressors, late presentation [OR] or spontaneous reversal of shock
  - Patient enrolled in other clinical trials
