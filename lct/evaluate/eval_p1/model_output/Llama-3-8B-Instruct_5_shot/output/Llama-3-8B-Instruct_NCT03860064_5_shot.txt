Inclusion Criteria:
 - 18-35-year-old women
 - BMI: >18 [AND] < 30 kg/m2
 - Normal female karyotype
 - Normal uterus [AND] and ovaries at transvaginal ultrasound
Exclusion Criteria:
 - > 35 years
 - PCO syndrome [OR] carriers
 - Null follicle aspiration
 - History of [NOT] chemotherapy [OR] radiotherapy
