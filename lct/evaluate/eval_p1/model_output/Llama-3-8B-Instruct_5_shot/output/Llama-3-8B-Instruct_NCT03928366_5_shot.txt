Inclusion Criteria:
  - Healthy adult volunteers (ASA 1 physical state) [AND] who agree to participate voluntarily in the study, previous information about it by the Principal Investigators (IP).
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Psychological, psychiatric [OR] or neurological disorders.
  - Consumption of drugs [OR] (NOT) previous information about it by the Principal Investigators (IP).
  - Alterations cutaneous [OR] or anatomical cranial.
  - Idiomatic [OR] or communication barrier.
  - Allergy to propofol, remifentanil [OR] or to some of its excipients.
  - Body mass index (BMI) [NOT] <18 [OR] > 30 kg / m2.
  - Pregnancy.
  - Airway [OR] or ventilation criteria hard.
  - Absence of accompanying adult [NOT] at the end of the study.
