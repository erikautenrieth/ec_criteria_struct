Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with treatment response:
  - Age ≥18 years
  - Subject is diagnosed with type 2 diabetes [AND] subject showed response to GLP-1RA treatment
  - Ability to sign informed consent
Patients without treatment response:
  - Age ≥18 years
  - Subject is diagnosed with type 2 diabetes [AND] subject showed no response to GLP-1RA treatment
  - Ability to sign informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] liver disease
  - [NOT] renal disease
  - Pregnancy [OR] or the wish to become pregnant within 6 months after the study
  - Breastfeeding
  - Age <18 years
  - Pituitary disorder
  - [NOT] ability to sign informed consent
  - Exclusion criteria for MR:
  - Fragments, clips [OR] or devices in brain, eyes, spinal canal
  - Implantable defibrillator [OR] or pacemaker (wires)
  - Mandibular magnetic implants
  - Neurostimulator, bladder stimulator, non-removable insulin pump
  - Metal tissue-expander in chest
  - Cochlear implant
  - Ossicular replacement prosthesis
