 - Patients who underwent fibula free flap for mandibular reconstruction
 - Patient [AND] ≥18 years old
 - patient [AND] alive 6 months after completion of treatment
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patient [NOT] without post-operative scan evaluation

 - Adult [NOT] requiring protection (guardianship, supervision)
