Inclusion Criteria:
 - Meeting the diagnostic criteria of CTTH [AND] in the international classification of headache disorders, 3rd edition (beta version) (ICHD-III beta);
 - Aged 18-55 years;
 - Having the ability of understanding [AND] completing the headache dairy;
 - Provide written informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - No headache during the previous 3 months;
 - Taking any prophylactic medication during the previous one month;
 - Secondary headache [OR] or any systematic cause
 - Having serious conditions of the heart, liver, kidney [OR] or other organs;
 - In pregnancy [OR] or lactation, or planning to be pregnant in 6 months;
 - [NOT] Drug and Substance abuse
 - [NOT] Diagnosed psychiatric condition
 - Taking any other complementary and alternative medicines;
 - Practicing yoga of any kind in last three months.
