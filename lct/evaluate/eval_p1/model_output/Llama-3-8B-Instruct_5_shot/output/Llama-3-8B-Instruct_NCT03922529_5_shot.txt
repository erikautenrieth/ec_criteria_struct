Inclusion Criteria:
  - Age ≥70 year
  - Eligible cardiovascular disease (CVD) diagnosis [AND] (hospitalization for Acute myocardial infarction [OR] Acute coronary syndrome [OR] stable Ischemic Heart Disease [OR] revascularization [OR] Coronary artery bypass graft surgery [OR] Percutaneous Coronary Intervention [CABG, PCI]) [OR] valvular heart disease [specifically, surgical [OR] or percutaneous intervention for mitral regurgitation [OR] or aortic stenosis] [OR] heart failure (HF)
  - English speaking
  - able to provide written informed consent
  - able to be assessed [AND] undergo study interventions.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Unstable medical condition [NOT] as indicated by history, physical exam, [AND] or laboratory findings
  - Presence of non-CVD conditions [NOT] likely to be fatal within 12 months (e.g., metastatic cancer)
  - Severe cognitive impairment: Short Blessed screening with a score of [NOT] 13 or greater [OR] cannot consent [as indicated by medical record]
  - Long-term care resident at admission [AND] with no plans to return to independent living
  - anticipates inability to return for required reassessments at 3, 6 [AND] 12 months
  - long-term care residents at admission [AND] with no plans to return to independent living.
