Inclusion Criteria:
 - Male [OR] or female ≥ 18 years
 - Patient for whom a vascular surgery is programmed [AND] and whose nature allows obtaining of arterial segment without any harm for the health of the patient
 - Patient for whom a blood sample is planned on the day of the procedure
 - Person who has received complete information on the organization of the research [AND] who has not objected to his participation [AND] and the exploitation of his data
 - Person with compulsory affiliation to social security
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patient who has previously undergone radiotherapy [NOT] at the sampling site
 - Patient with cancer [NOT] at the sampling site
