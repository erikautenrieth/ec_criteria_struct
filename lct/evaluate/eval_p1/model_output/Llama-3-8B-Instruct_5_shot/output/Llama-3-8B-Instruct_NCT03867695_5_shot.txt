Inclusion criteria:
  -  All patients undergoing lobectomy VATS
  -  Older [AND] > 18 years
  -  Patients who meet criteria of ASA 1 [OR] to 3 class
Exclusion criteria:
  -  Patient's [NOT] refusal to participate in the study
  -  Psychiatric disorder [OR] (impossibility to collect the informed consent)
  -  Patient [NOT] under juridical protection
  -  On going [OR] an other study
  -  Non [NOT] balanced epilepsy
  -  3 grade auriculo-ventricular heart block [NOT] without pacing
  -  Severe [NOT] hepatocellular insufficiency
  -  Anti arrhythmic treatment : class III [OR] of the Vaughan William's classification
  -  Pregnant patient [OR] and/or breastfeeding
  -  History of opioid [NOT] abuse
  -  Allergy to local anesthetic drug [OR] opioids
