Inclusion Criteria:
  - All infants or fetuses with microcephaly, (defined as head circumference ≤-3SD according to INTERGROWTH standards for gestational age and sex, detected during the mother's pregnancy, [OR] or at the end of the mother's pregnancy at one of the participating maternities) [AND] with abnormal ultrasound and/or clinical examination findings for newborns.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Infants/fetuses with microcephaly whose mothers are [NOT] under the age of 18 years,
  - or inability [OR] or refusal of mothers/ guardians to give informed consent.
