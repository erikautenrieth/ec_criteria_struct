Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients diagnosed as AS according to the modified New York diagnostic criteria were included
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [NOT] previously diagnosed with OP,
 - Patients [NOT] previously treated with OP,
 - Patients with bone fractures [OR] previously associated with OP,
 - Those using drugs that affect bone metabolism, [OR] metabolic bone diseases (Paget's disease, osteomalacia, hyperparathyroidism),
 - Diabetes mellitus,
 - Hypothyroidism, [OR] hyperthyroidism,
 - Patients with kidney, liver, [OR] intestinal diseases,
 - Those using corticosteroids
