Inclusion Criteria:
 - Hoehn & Yahr Classification of Disability, stage [AND] 1-3
 - can ambulate with [OR] without an assistive device for at least 50 feet
 - able to get up [AND] and down from the floor with minimal assistance
 - has a score of 24 [OR] above on the Folstein Mini-Mental State Exam
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Greater than Stage 3 [NOT] on the Hoehn & Yahr Classification of Disability
 - decline in immune function such as pneumonia [OR] or systemic infection
 - progressive degenerative disease [AND] besides PD
 - spinal fusion [OR] or other orthopedic surgery in the past six months
 - mental disease/psychosis such as dementia
 - greater than minimal assistance [NOT] required for gait and transfers
 - inability to make regular time commitments [NOT] to the scheduled training sessions
 - participation in resistance training on a regular basis [AND] within the past six months
 - inability to provide consent
 - pregnancy
 - a prisoner
