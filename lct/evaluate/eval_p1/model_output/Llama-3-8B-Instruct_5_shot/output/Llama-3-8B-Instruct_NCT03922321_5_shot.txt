Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Male [OR] or female ≥ 18 years of age.
 2. Clinical diagnosis of Graves' disease [AND] with hyperthyroidism associated with active, moderate to severe GO with a Clinical Activity Score (CAS) ≥ 4 for the most severely affected eye at Screening (on the 7-item scale) [AND] and Baseline (on the 10-item scale).
 3. Onset of active GO [NOT] within 9 months of screening.
 4. Moderate-to-severe active GO [NOT] (not sight-threatening but has an appreciable impact on daily life), usually associated with one [OR] or more of the following: lid retraction ≥ 2 mm, moderate [OR] or severe soft tissue involvement, proptosis ≥ 3 mm above normal for race and gender, and/or inconstant [OR] or constant diplopia.
 5. Other, more specific inclusion criteria are defined in the protocol
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Use of any steroid (intravenous [IV] [OR] oral) with a cumulative dose equivalent to ≥ 1 g of methylprednisolone for the treatment of GO [NOT] within 3 weeks prior to Screening.
 2. Use of rituximab, tocilizumab, [OR] or any monoclonal antibody for immunomodulation [NOT] within the past 9 months prior to Baseline.
 3. Total IgG level < 6g/L [NOT] at Screening.
 4. Absolute neutrophil count <1500 cells/mm3 [NOT] at Screening.
 5. Participants with decreased best corrected visual acuity due to optic neuropathy [NOT] as defined by a decrease in vision of 2 lines on the Snellen chart, new visual field defect, or color defect secondary to optic nerve involvement within the last 6 months at Screening.
 6. Previous orbital irradiation [OR] or surgery for GO.
 7. Other, more specific exclusion criteria are defined in the protocol
