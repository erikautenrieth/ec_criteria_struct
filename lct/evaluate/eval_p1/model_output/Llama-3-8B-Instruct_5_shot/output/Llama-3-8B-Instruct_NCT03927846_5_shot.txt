Inclusion Criteria:
 - patients at the primary care [OR] or rheumatology clinic with daily pain for 6 months or longer affecting the low back, neck, hip, knee [OR] or widespread pain;
 - at least moderate [AND] in average pain severity, defined as a weekly average pain severity score of 5 [OR] greater 24;
 - at least 18 years of age;
 - reliable phone (landline [OR] or cell), [AND] and
 - have home computer with reliable internet access.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - planned elective surgery [NOT] during the study period;
 - very severe symptoms of depression (i.e., PHQ8 score of ≥ 20);
 - ongoing unresolved disability claims;
 - inflammatory arthritis (e.g., lupus, [OR] rheumatoid arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, etc.);
 - cancer-related musculoskeletal pain;
 - history of bipolar disorder [OR] or schizophrenia; and
 - takes daily opioid for more than one year.
