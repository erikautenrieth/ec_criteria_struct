Inclusion Criteria:
  - Be 60 [OR] older, and in the case of women, they must be postmenopausal (interruption of menstruation for more than one year) [AND] and not pregnant.
  - Have not participated in any systematic exercise program for at least six months prior to the start of this project [AND] and not currently participating in any exercise program.
  - To have a body mass index (BMI) between 22 [AND] and 30 kg / m2.
  - Are not cardiopathic [OR] diabetic [OR] or have any other metabolic disease [NOT] including hypertension.
  - Do not present joint problems [OR] or any other injury that prevents the proper execution of the exercises of the training routine [NOT] including musculoskeletal injuries.
  - Non-smoker [OR] have quit smoking for at least five years ago [NOT] including those who have relapsed.
  - Release of the doctor (medical certificate) to perform the physical exercises [AND] and not have any medical condition that would prevent participation in the study.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Do not participate in at least 20 training sessions [OR] miss more than three consecutive training sessions.
