Inclusion Criteria:
 - Participant in the SURVIVE registry
 - Signed informed consent form for PROACT
 - Histological diagnosis of early [OR] or metastatic breast cancer, lymphoma, sarcoma, leukemia, myeloma, [OR] or lung cancer (patients with treated [AND] and clinically stable brain metastasis are acceptable)
 - Scheduled to receive anti-cancer therapy (radiation therapy is [NOT] prohibited)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Contraindication to magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) [NOT]
 - Unable to comply with study investigations (in the judgment of the investigator) [NOT]
 - Life expectancy less than 1 year
