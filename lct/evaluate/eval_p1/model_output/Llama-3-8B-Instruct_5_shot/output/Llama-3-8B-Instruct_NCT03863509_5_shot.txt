Inclusion Criteria:
  - able to read and write English
  - no plans to quit smoking [AND] or e-cig use in the next month
  - not using cigars/smokeless/snus tobacco [NOT] > /= 1 time per week
  - having a stable pattern of current product use
  - able to walk at least 2 blocks without assistance [OR] or stopping
  - Specific to Exclusive Smokers:
       - smokes daily [AND] >/= 5 cigs/day for last 6 months [AND] < 3 uses E-cigs in lifetime [AND] >/= 5 ppm carbon monoxide (CO) [AND] Cotinine > 100 ng/ml
  - Specific to Exclusive E-cig users:
       - </= 2 days per month cigarette use for last 6 months [AND] >/= 5 days per week E-cig use for last 3 months [AND] </= 4 ppm CO [AND] Cotinine > 100 ng/ml
  - Specific to Dual users:
       - >/= 5 cigs/day for last 6 months [AND] >/= 5 days per week E-cig use for last 3 months [AND] >/= 5 ppm CO [AND] Cotinine > 100 ng/ml
  - Specific to Never-users:
       - < 100 cigarettes in a lifetime [AND] none for > 5 years [AND] < 3 E-cig uses in a lifetime [AND] </= 4 ppm CO [AND] Cotinine < 100 ng/ml
Exclusion Criteria:
  - current use of a smoking cessation medication
  - women who are [NOT] pregnant or plan to get pregnant in the coming month
  - women who [NOT] might be pregnant
  - incarcerated individuals
  - history of sarcoidosis in past 5 years, or active interstitial lung/pulmonary fibrosis
