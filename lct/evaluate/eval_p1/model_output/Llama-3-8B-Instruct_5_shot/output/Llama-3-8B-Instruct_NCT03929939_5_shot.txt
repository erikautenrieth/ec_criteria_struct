Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Be either a female veteran [AND] with a current diagnosis of PTSD (for the PTSD group) [OR] a female non-veteran [AND] without a current diagnosis of PTSD (for the non-PTSD control group).
 2. Be between the ages of 18-65.
 3. Sedentary (exercises [NOT] less than three times a week for thirty minutes or less).
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Any evidence of cardiopulmonary [AND] or neurological diseases by history [OR] by physical examination.
 2. Chronic kidney disease (serum creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL).
 3. Peripheral vascular disease.
 4. Peripheral neuropathy.
 5. Current substance use disorder [NOT] other than tobacco related.
 6. Endurance-trained athletes [NOT] due to the effects of exercise training on sympathetic neural control [AND] cardiac-vascular function.
 7. Current pregnancy.
