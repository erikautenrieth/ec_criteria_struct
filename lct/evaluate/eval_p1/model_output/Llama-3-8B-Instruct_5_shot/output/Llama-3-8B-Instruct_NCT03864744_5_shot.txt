Inclusion Criteria:
 - Subject scheduled for total pancreatectomy [OR] or pancreaticoduodenectomy
 - Informed consent signed prior to any study-related procedure
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Known liver disease [NOT] before total pancreatectomy or pancreaticoduodenectomy (excluding NAFLD)
 - Severe co-morbid disease [AND] (besides from the indication for the pancreas surgery)
 - Pregnancy
 - Any condition that the investigator [NOT] feels would interfere with the safety of the trial participation [OR] or the safety of the subject
 - Metastatic disease
Percutaneous liver biopsy exclusion criteria (to be evaluated before last visit)
 - MR spectroscopy demonstrating lipid content <2%
 - Haemoglobin <6 mmol/L
 - INR >1.5
 - Trombocytes <40 × 109/L
 - Skin infection in area where biopsy will be sampled
