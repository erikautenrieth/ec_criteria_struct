Inclusion Criteria:
 - Female
 - Handball players federated in the Madrid Handball Federation
 - With an age range between 18 [AND] and 37 years
 - More than 2 years of handball sports
 - Currently participate in amateur competitions of First National Women's category.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Have suffered a shoulder injury [NOT] in the last six months
 - Who [NOT] are currently doing some physiotherapy treatment
 - Who [NOT] fail to participate in the established intervention and evaluation sessions
 - Refusing to sign the informed consent document.
