Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with structural heart disease [AND] and implantable cardioverter defibrillator (ICD)
 - Age > 18 years
 - either [OR]
 1. Recurring symptomatic monomorphic ventricular tachycardia that requires ICD intervention (e.g. shock or anti-tachycardia stimulation). At least 3 episodes within the 3 months prior to inclusion [OR]
 2. induction of symptomatic monomorphic ventricular tachycardia that requires ICD intervention (e.g. shock or anti-tachycardia stimulation). Induction triggered by ICD [OR] or during electrophysiology studies (EPS) [OR] or both
 - Refractory to antiarrhythmic combination therapy
 - Beside the cardiac conditions: No competing illness [NOT] that would additionally limit the life expectancy to less than 6 months
 - No prior radiation therapy [NOT] in the thorax area
 - No pregnancy [OR] and no active breastfeeding
 - Ability to consent [AND] and consent to study participation
Exclusion Criteria:
 - ICD electrode malfunction [OR] of ICD readings outside reference range
 - Lack of evidence [NOT] of a myocardial scar (Computer tomography or magnetic resonance tomography for MRI-capable ICD aggregates or electrophysiological measurement)
 - No possible induction [NOT] of symptomatic monomorphic ventricular tachycardia non-persistent [OR] or persistent with delivery of ICD therapies such as antitachycardic pacing or shock
 - Contraindication [NOT] to radiosurgery (e.g. precise target volume definition not possible due to image artifacts created from a left ventricular assist device [LVAD])
 - Inability [NOT] to consent or missing [OR] or withdrawn consent
