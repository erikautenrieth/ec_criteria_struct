Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients [NOT] free from any disease affecting bone formation [AND] of both sexes was included in the study.
 2. Patients [AND] younger than 25 years old.
 3. Patients included in the study were post traumatic patients [OR] hemifacial microsomia [OR] Treacher Collins [OR] and post ankylotic mandibular deformity.
 4. Distraction range was between 15-20 mm.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. The presence of uncontrolled systemic disease.
 2. Previous radiotherapy [AND] on head and neck region.
 3. Chemotherapy [AND] within the past 12-month period.
 4. An active infection [AND] at the distraction site.
