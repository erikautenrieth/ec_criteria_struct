Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Clinical diagnosis of NTM pulmonary disease.
  2. The causative agent of NTM pulmonary disease [AND] includes: M. avium complex, [OR] M. abscessus subspecies abscessus, [OR] M. abscessus subspecies massiliense, [OR] M. kansasii.
  3. Patients who [AND] start new treatment (those who have previous history of treatment [NOT] are eligible)
Exclusion Criteria
  1. Patients who have been treated for more than 4 weeks.
  2. The causative agent of NTM pulmonary disease [NOT] is none of M. avium complex, [OR] M. abscessus subspecies abscessus, [OR] M. abscessus subspecies massiliense, [OR] M. kansasii.
