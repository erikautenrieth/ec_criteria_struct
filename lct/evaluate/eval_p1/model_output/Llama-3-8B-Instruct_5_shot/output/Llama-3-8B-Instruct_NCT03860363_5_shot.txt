Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adult (age ≥ 18) [AND] 
 - Patient undergoing non-emergent cardiac surgery [AND] 
 - Anticipated requirement for mediastinal chest tube drainage

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Emergency surgery [NOT]
 - Re-do surgery [NOT]
 - Prior cardiac surgery [NOT]
 - Congenital cardiac disease [OR] or deformation [NOT]
 - Transplant surgery [NOT]
 - Ventricular Assist Device Surgery [NOT]
 - Suspected pulmonary air leak [NOT]
 - Atrial fibrillation [NOT]
 - In the opinion of the investigator the patient is unsuitable for the study for any other legitimate reason [including incarceration, pre-existing medical [OR] or psychiatric condition, or interfering medications] [NOT]
 - Known previous or concurrent enrollment in a clinical trial that, in the opinion of the investigator, might interfere with the objectives of this clinical trial [NOT]
 - Pregnancy [NOT]
