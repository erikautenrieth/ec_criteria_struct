Inclusion Criteria:
• Couples with primary [OR] secondary infertility who are planned to undergo IVF [OR] ICSI treatment in one of the above mentioned centers and who agree to take part in this study.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Couples planned for oocyte [AND] sperm donation
  - Couples in whom the female partner has a history of:
  - [NOT] Chemotherapy or radiation which impacts the ovarian reserve
  - [NOT] Surgery at the ovaries [OR] adnex region
  - [NOT] Tubal ligation
  - [NOT] Reversal of tubal ligation
  - [NOT] Endometriosis
  - Couples in whom the male partner has a history of:
  - [NOT] Chemotherapy [OR] Radiation which impacts the semen result
  - [NOT] Surgery at the testicles
  - [NOT] Vasectomy
  - [NOT] Surgery for reversal of vasectomy
