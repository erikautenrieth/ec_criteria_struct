Inclusion Criteria:
  - Subjects presented with melasma.
  - Age 20-40 years old.
  - Melasma persisting for more than 6 months that has failed to respond to conventional treatment with hydroquinone [OR] or other topical lightening agents.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - History of photosensitivity, keloids, hypertrophic scarring [AND] and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation.
  - Pregnancy [AND] lactation.
  - Subjects with local inflammatory skin disorder [OR] or active herpes infection at the site of procedure.
  - Subjects with history of medical diseases which contraindicate cryosurgery [AND] such as; cold intolerance, cold urticaria, Raynaud's disease [AND] and history of allergic reactions to cryosurgery.
