Inclusion Criteria:
  - Subjects over 60 years of age [AND] with a clinical diagnosis of Parkinson's disease
  - Stages of the disease 2-3 [AND] of Hoehn and Yahr
  - Not exposed to tobacco [NOT] during any stage of their life
  - No history of lung diseases
  - No laboratory abnormalities
  - No history of adverse reactions to nicotine
  - Able to use nicotine nasal spray
  - Residents of Mexico City [AND] able to attend for evaluations
  - Under current treatment with levodopa [AND] at a stable dose
  - Not currently receiving a monoamine oxidase inhibitor treatment
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Unable to complete follow-up protocol
  - Drug adverse reaction
  - Death
