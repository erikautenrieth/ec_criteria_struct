Inclusion Criteria:
 - age 55-80
 - smoked at least 30 pack-years in lifetime
 - if former smoker, quit smoking [AND] within the last 15 years
 - English speaker
 - no prior history [NOT] of lung cancer
 - did not have a lung cancer screening test [NOT] within the last year
 - PCP [NOT] objects to patient's participation
 - have a scheduled visit at University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) internal medicine clinics.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - speaking a language [NOT] other than English
 - has a history [NOT] of lung cancer
 - had a lung cancer screening test [NOT] within the last year
 - PCP [NOT] objects to patient's participation.
