Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adult patient (> 18 years old)
 - Obtaining consent to participate in the study
 - Patients whose clinical presentation meets at least one of the following criteria:
       - Undetermined nephropathy [AND] despite renal biopsy,
       - Nephroangiosclerosis [OR] as the predominant lesion
       - Chronic tubulointerstitial nephropathy,
       - Glomerulosclerosis,
       - Segmental [AND] focal hyalinosis.
       - Optically normal kidney [OR] or seat of minimal lesions
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patient who has already been screened for Fabry Disease
 - At least one of the following criteria:
       - Nephrotic syndrome [AND] or/and Glomerular nephropathy
       - Histological diagnosis [NOT] of certain nephropathies (specific kidney lesions)
