 Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] planned operation of coronary artery bypass grafting [AND] with the use of cardiopulmonary bypass

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [OR] emergency operations or re-operations;
  - [OR] a known pathology of the urinary tract or renal failure;
  - [AND] chronic use of the following medications: iron, non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs), immunosuppression, or steroids in the preoperative period;
  - [OR] polycythemia, porphyria or pathological hemoglobin species in anamnesis;
  - [OR] preoperative signs of hepatic failure;
  - [OR] active autoimmune or neoplastic diseases, active infection;
  - [OR] anticipated significant bleeding (anti-platelet agents), suggesting the use of blood-derived products during the operation and afterward;
  - [AND] acute myocardial infarction after operation.
