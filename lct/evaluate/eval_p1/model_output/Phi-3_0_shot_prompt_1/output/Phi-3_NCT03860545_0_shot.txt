 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  [AND] planned operation of coronary artery bypass grafting [AND] with the use of cardiopulmonary bypass

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [OR] emergency operations or re-operations;
  -  [AND] a known pathology of the urinary tract [OR] renal failure;
  -  [AND] chronic use of the following medications: iron [OR] non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) [OR] immunosuppression [OR] steroids in the preoperative period;
  -  [AND] polycythemia [OR] porphyria [OR] pathological hemoglobin species in anamnesis;
  -  [AND] preoperative signs of hepatic failure;
  -  [AND] active autoimmune or neoplastic diseases [OR] active infection;
  -  [AND] anticipated significant bleeding (anti-platelet agents) [OR] suggesting the use of blood-derived products during the operation [AND] afterward.
