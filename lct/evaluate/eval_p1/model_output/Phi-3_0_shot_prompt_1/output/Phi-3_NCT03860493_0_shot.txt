 Inclusion Criteria:
1. Patients of both genders [AND]
2. Minimum age: 18 years old [AND]
3. Spoken and written command of the language spoken in the country's center [AND]
4. Ability to understand and follow the study procedures [AND] [AND] sign the informed consent
5. Not excluded by open surgical procedures [NOT]

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Known allergies to iodides [AND]
2. Known history of coagulopathy [AND]
3. Women who are pregnant or breastfeeding [OR] for whom possibility of pregnancy was not ruled out [OR]
