 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with an unilateral THA or TKA AND they received for the treatment of osteoarthritis (Patient groups 1+2)
  -  Patients diagnosed with unilateral OA of the knee OR hip AND planned for a treatment with THA OR TKA (Patient groups 3+4)
  -  Age ≥ 30 years AND Healthy control group

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Body mass index > 35kg/m2 OR Use of walking aids
  -  Neuromuscular disorders affecting gait OR Inability to follow procedures due to psychological disorders OR dementia
  -  Diagnosed hip OR knee OA AND Healthy control group
  -  Current pain in the lower extremities OR lower back AND Healthy control group
  -  Rheumatoid Arthritis AND Healthy control group
  -  Previous corrective osteotomy AND Healthy control group
  -  Regular intake of pain medication more than 4 times per week AND Healthy control group
