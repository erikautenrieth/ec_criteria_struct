 Inclusion Criteria:
  - Among the pregnant women who were administered cesarean section in this study AND those who agreed to participate in this study.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Fetal distress requiring fast delivery.
  - Patients with hypersensitivity, allergic response, resistance to the drugs used in this study [NOT] (fentanyl, ropivacaine, lidocaine).
  - Patients who is not possible for regional anesthesia.
  - Patients who [NOT] refused to participate in this study.
  - Patients who [NOT] can read or understand the agreement.
  - Patients whose weight is less than 50kg [OR] exceeds 100kg.
