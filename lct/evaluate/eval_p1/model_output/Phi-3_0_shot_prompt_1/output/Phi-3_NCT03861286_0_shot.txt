 Inclusion Criteria:
1. Patients undergoing first time cardiopulmonary bypass surgery for correction of atrial septal defects [AND] tissue mitral valve repair
2. Receiving intravenous Heparin for cardiopulmonary bypass [AND] NOT patients receiving heparin therapy prior to CPB within last 7 days

Exclusion Criteria:
- Concomitant Aortic Stenosis [AND] which is associated with an acquired von Willebrand Syndrome (aVWS)
- Patients with a baseline platelet count of <100x109/L [AND] NOT patients with a known coagulation factor deficiency or platelet function disorder
- Patients receiving heparin therapy prior to CPB within last 7 days [AND] NOT patients receiving intravenous Heparin for cardiopulmonary bypass
