 Inclusion Criteria:

- Ages 18-59 AND able to row on an indoor rowing apparatus
- Not participating in regular physical exercise OR more than 60 minutes of moderate intensity or 30 minutes of vigorous intensity exercise per week
- Not actively trying to lose weight through a dietary approach AND no diagnosis of malignancy (except non-melanoma skin cancer)
- No absolute contra-indications to exercise AND no recent (<6 months) acute cardiac event unstable angina OR uncontrolled dysrhythmias causing symptoms or hemodynamic compromise OR symptomatic aortic stenosis OR uncontrolled symptomatic heart failure OR acute pulmonary embolus OR acute myocarditis or pericarditis OR suspected or known dissecting aneurysm AND no acute systemic infection
- No other form of chronic liver disease (as confirmed by serology and histology) AND no liver cirrhosis (stage 4 fibrosis on liver biopsy) OR a clinical diagnosis of cirrhosis as ascertained by a hepatologist
- No coronary artery disease OR chronic obstructive pulmonary disease
- No use of immunosuppression OR chronic nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory medication
- No more than 14 drinks per week (if male) OR 7 drinks a week (if female) in the last 2 years AND no past or present history of portal hypertension (esophageal varices, ascites, hepatic encephalopathy)
- Cognitively able to provide consent AND able to read, write, and understand English
