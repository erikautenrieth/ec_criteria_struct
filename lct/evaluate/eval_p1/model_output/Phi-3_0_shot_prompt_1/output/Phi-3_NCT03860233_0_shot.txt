 Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Men or women 18 years of age or older.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] subjects not willing to or otherwise unable to use CPAP for treatment of OSA.
  - [AND] Presence of other sleep disorders
  - [AND] Pregnant or breastfeeding women
  - [AND] Women of child-bearing age (WOCBA) not willing or unable to use an accepted method to avoid pregnancy for the entire duration of the study
  - [AND] Prisoners or subjects who are involuntarily incarcerated
  - [AND] Subjects who are compulsorily detain or treatment of either a psychiatric or physical (i.e. infectious disease) illness
  - [AND] Patients unable to give consent because of a language barrier, or other reason.
