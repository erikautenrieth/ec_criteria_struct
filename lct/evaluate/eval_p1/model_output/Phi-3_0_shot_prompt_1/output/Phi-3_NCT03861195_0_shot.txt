 Inclusion Criteria:

- age ≥18 years AND

- BMI ≤40 kg/m^2

- indication for hysterectomy for benign disease OR prophylactic surgery due to hereditary cancer OR indication for radical hysterectomy and/or pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for cervical cancer OR indication for modified radical hysterectomy and/or pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for endometrial cancer

- size of uterus and vagina allows for retrieval by the vaginal route in cancer patients AND

- written informed consent


Exclusion Criteria:

- known extensive intra-abdominal adhesions OR

- anaesthesiological contraindications to laparoscopy OR

- women with pacemaker or other implants where electrosurgery is to be avoided OR

- women with known defects of the hemostasis OR

- pregnancy OR

- other internal or anatomical criteria that preclude a minimal invasive approach
