 Inclusion Criteria:
  - [age ≥18 years] AND [BMI ≤40 kg/m^2] AND [indication for hysterectomy for benign disease OR indication for hysterectomy for prophylactic surgery due to hereditary cancer] AND [indication for radical hysterectomy and/or pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for cervical cancer] AND [indication for modified radical hysterectomy and/or pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for endometrial cancer] AND [size of uterus and vagina allows for retrieval by the vaginal route in cancer patients] AND [written informed consent]

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [known extensive intra-abdominal adhesions] OR [anaesthesiological contraindications to laparoscopy] OR [women with pacemaker or other implants where electrosurgery is to be avoided] OR [women with known defects of the hemostasis] OR [pregnancy] OR [other internal or anatomical criteria that preclude a minimal invasive approach] OR [inability to understand patient information]
