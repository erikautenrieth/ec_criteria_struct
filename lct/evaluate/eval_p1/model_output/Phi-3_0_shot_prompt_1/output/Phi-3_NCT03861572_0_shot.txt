 Inclusion Criteria:
  - Participants will be male [AND] female subjects who suffered total acute Achilles tendon rupture [AND] and which underwent surgical repair [AND] in addition, to participate in this study all volunteers will need to present medical and/or physiotherapeutic release for physical/sports activities practice [AND] NOT [OR] have participated in strength training program for the plantar flexors in the last 6 months [AND] NOT [OR] have diabetic diseases [AND] NOT [OR] have difficulty for understanding and/or executing the test and training protocols in the isokinetic dynamometer.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Volunteers that did not have Achilles tendon surgical reconstruction [AND] that did not present medical and/or physiotherapeutic release for physical/sports activities [AND] who have participated in strength training program for the plantar flexors in the last 6 months [AND] who have diabetic diseases [AND] who have difficulty for understanding and/or executing the test and training protocols in the isokinetic dynamometer.
