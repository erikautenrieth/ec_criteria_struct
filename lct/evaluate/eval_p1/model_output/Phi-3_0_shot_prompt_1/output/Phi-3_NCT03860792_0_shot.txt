 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Diagnosis of AD [AND] by current McKhann et al. criteria
  -  CDR global score of [0.5 OR] 1
  -  Agreed cooperation from an appropriate study partner [AND]
  -  Speaks English [AND] as primary language
  -  Age [BETWEEN] 50 to 90
  -  No medication changes [NOT] within the past 30 days

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Resides in a nursing home [OR] dementia special care unit [OR] cannot control diet
  -  A potentially confounding serious medical risk [AND] including insulin-requiring diabetes [OR] cancer requiring chemotherapy or radiation [OR] within the past 5 years, or a recent cardiac event (i.e. heart attack, angioplasty, etc.)
  -  Participating in another clinical trial [OR] using an investigational drug or therapy [OR] within 30 days of the Screening Visit
  -  A history of renal stones [OR]
