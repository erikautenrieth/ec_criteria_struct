 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  The inclusion criteria were as follows: a) [AND] men and women aged between 18- and 65-years b) [AND] medical diagnosis of Non-Specific Chronic Neck Pain with more than 6 months of evolution of neck pain.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Exclusion criteria included the following: a) [AND] patients with rheumatic diseases, cervical hernia, cervical whiplash syndrome, neck surgeries or history of arthrodesis, b) [AND] systemic diseases, c) [AND] vision, hearing or vestibular problems, d) [AND] Severe trauma or traffic accident which has had an impact on the cervical area.
