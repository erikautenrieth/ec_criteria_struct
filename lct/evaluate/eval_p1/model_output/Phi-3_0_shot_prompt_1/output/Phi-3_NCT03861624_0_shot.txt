 Inclusion Criteria:

1. Skeletal maturity AND AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures

2. Wound primarily closeable AND NOT requiring a flap OR delay in closure due to contamination

3. Palpable pedal pulses AND NOT vascular injury sustained

4. Presentation within 24 hours from injury


Exclusion Criteria:

1. Current injury is a pathologic fracture OR sustained bilateral tibia fracture

2. Sustained comminuted femur fracture

3. Sustained severe Traumatic Brain Injury AND GCS<12 ***

4. Sustained severe spinal cord injury AND lower extremity paresis/paralysis

5. Sustained severe burns AND (>10% total body surface area (TBSA) OR >5% TBSA with full thickness or circumferential injury)

6. Prior ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery

7. Prior or current lower limb deformity OR abnormality

8. Unable to complete follow-up visits
