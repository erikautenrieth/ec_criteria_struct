 Inclusion Criteria:
1. Age [AND] ≥18 years [AND] ≤90 years
2. Tumor [AND] diagnosed as Glioblastoma Multiforme [AND] on MRI [AND] with distinct ring-like pattern of contrast enhancement [AND] with thick irregular walls [AND] and a core area reduced signal suggestive of tumor necrosis [AND] as assessed by the surgeon
3. Tumors [AND] situated in or near eloquent areas [AND] (Sawaya Grading II [OR] II) [AND] (motor cortex [OR] sensory cortex [OR] subcortical pyramidal tract [OR] speech areas) [AND] as indicated on MRI
4. The tumor [AND] is suitable for resection [AND] (according to neurosurgeon)
5. Karnofsky performance scale [AND] 80 [OR] more
6. Written Informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Tumors [AND] of the cerebellum [AND] brain stem [AND] or midline
2. Multifocal contrast enhancing lesions
3. Substantial non-contrast enhancing tumor areas [AND] suggesting low grade gliomas [AND] with malignant transformation
4. Medical reasons [AND] precluding MRI (eg, pacemaker)
5. Inability to give consent [AND] because of or language barrier
6. Psychiatric history
7. Previous brain tumor surgery
8. Previous low-grade glioma
9. Second primary malignancy [AND] within the past 5 years [AND] [NOT] (adequately treated in situ carcinoma of any organ [OR] basal cell carcinoma of the skin)
10. Severe aphasia or dysphasia
