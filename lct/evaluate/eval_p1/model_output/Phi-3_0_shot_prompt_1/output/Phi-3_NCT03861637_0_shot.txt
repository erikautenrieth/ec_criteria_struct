 Inclusion Criteria:
- Patients who fulfilled the following criteria AND had informed consent were included in the study:
- Adult renal transplant patients (≥21 years) AND stable maintenance subcutaneous ESA therapy with constant dose interval during the last 2 months were included in the study:
- Haemoglobin (Hb) ≥10mg/dL (Hct 32%) AND serum ferritin ≥100 ng/mL were included in the study during the screening phase AND Transferrin saturation (TSAT) ≥20% were included in the study during the screening phase.

Exclusion Criteria:
- Patients with the following criteria OR acute or chronic systemic inflammatory disease AND/OR C-reactive protein (CRP) >30 mg/L were excluded from the study:
- Patients with acute or chronic bleeding, or erythrocyte transfusion, within the preceding 8 weeks OR change in Hb level ≥ 2 g/dL during screening phase were excluded from the study:
- Patients with hemolytic anemia OR recent infection or rejection OR diastolic blood pressure >100mmHg or discontinuation of ESA due to hypertension in the 6 months prior to study were excluded from the study:
- Patients with vitamin B12 or folic acid deficiency OR uncontrolled or secondary hyperparathyroidism were excluded from the study:
- Patients undergoing hemodialysis due to failure of a kidney transplant OR malignancy were excluded from the study.
