 Inclusion Criteria:
1. Patients free from any disease affecting bone formation of both sexes AND younger than 25 years old.
2. Patients included in the study were post traumatic AND hemifacial microsomia AND Treacher Collins AND post ankylotic mandibular deformity.
3. Distraction range was between 15-20 mm.

Exclusion Criteria:
1. The presence of uncontrolled systemic disease OR previous radiotherapy on head and neck region OR chemotherapy within the past 12-month period OR an active infection at the distraction site.
