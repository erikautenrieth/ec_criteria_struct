 Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Aged at least 18 years.
  - [AND] Histologically or cytologically proven diagnosis of NSCLC that is locally advanced or metastatic.
  - [AND] Radiological documentation of disease progression while on a previous continuous treatment with an [OR] first or second generation of EGFR TKI e.g. gefitinib or erlotinib.
  - [AND] patients must also have confirmation of tumour T790M mutation status [NOT] negative after disease progression on the prior EGFR TKI.
  - [AND] Eastern cooperative oncology group performance status (ECOG PS) of 0-1.
  - [AND] a minimum life expectancy of 12 weeks.
  - [AND] At least one lesion, not previously irradiated during the study screening period, that can be accurately measured at baseline according to RECIST 1.1.
  - [AND] Agree to use routine adequate and effective contraceptive measures during the entire study period and within 6 weeks after the last dose pre-menopausal fertility possible; [OR] Female patients need to exclude pregnancy (ie negative pregnancy test) and in non-lactation period.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Treatment with a first or second generation of EGFR-TKI within 7 days of study entry; [AND] previous treatment with a third generation of EGFR-TKI.
  - [AND] Unresolved toxicities from prior therapy.
  - [AND] Unstable spinal cord compression/brain metastases.
  - [AND] Severe/uncontrolled systemic diseases, including uncontrolled hypertension, bleeding diatheses or infection.
  - [AND] QTcF>470 msec during the screening period.
  - [AND] Severe respiratory diseases such as interstitial lung disease, severe asthma, pulmonary embolism, etc.
  - [AND] previous treatment with 2 or more lines of Chemotherapy or immunotherapy.
