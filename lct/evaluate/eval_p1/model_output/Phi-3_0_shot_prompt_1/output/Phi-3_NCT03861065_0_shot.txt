 Inclusion Criteria:
- Patients with high risk class II AND class III AND class IV abdominal wounds
- Patients undergoing laparotomy for gynecologic related disorders AND NOT pregnant
- Patients undergoing laparotomy for both benign AND malignant diagnoses

Exclusion Criteria:
- Patients with pregnancy
- Patients with allergy to triclosan
- Patients undergoing HIPEC AND NOT having high risk class II, class III, class IV abdominal wounds OR undergoing laparotomy for gynecologic related disorders OR undergoing laparotomy for both benign AND malignant diagnoses
