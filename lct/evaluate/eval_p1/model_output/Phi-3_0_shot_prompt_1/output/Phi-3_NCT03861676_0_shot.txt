 Inclusion Criteria:
- Adenocarcinoma of the prostate AND Performance Status < 2
- Clinical stages (not radiographic stage) T1c - T2a AND (Nx OR N0) AND (Mx OR M0)
- Gleason 6-7 cancer AND Prostate volume < 60 cc (if MRI and TRUS have conflicting values, then MRI value will be utilized)
- International Prostate symptom score (IPSS) 20 or less AND Ability to undergo DCF-Pyl PSMA PET as part of pretreatment staging
- Signed study-specific consent form prior to registration AND NOT Prior history of pelvic radiation therapy
- NOT Major medical or psychiatric illness which, in the investigator's opinion, would prevent completion of treatment and would interfere with follow up
- NOT Implanted device or apparatus which obstruct visibility of the implanted sources on fluoroscopy
- NOT Metallic implants, claustrophobia not amenable to medication, or known contraindications to undergoing MR scanning
- NOT History of other malignancy diagnosed within the past 3 years

Exclusion Criteria:
- Prior history of pelvic radiation therapy OR Major medical or psychiatric illness which, in the investigator's opinion, would prevent completion of treatment and would interfere with follow up
- Implanted device or apparatus which obstruct visibility of the implanted sources on fluoroscopy OR Metallic implants OR claustrophobia not amenable to medication OR known contraindications to undergoing MR scanning
- History of other malignancy diagnosed within the past 3 years
