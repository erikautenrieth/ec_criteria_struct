 Inclusion Criteria:
  - CAC score >10 AND Framingham risk score (10 % or above)
  - Subjects are required to be on stable concomitant medications for at least 12 weeks prior to randomization AND Subjects with diabetic must have HbA1C < 8.0 AND stable HbA1C level variation range within 0.5% for three months.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Hypersensitivity to AGE therapy OR Unstable medical, psychiatric, or substance abuse disorder that may interfere with continuation in the study OR Weight ≥325 pounds OR Bleeding disorder OR History of myocardial infarction OR Stroke OR Life-threatening arrhythmia within prior 6 months OR Resting hypotension (systolic < 90 mmHg) OR hypertension (resting blood pressure >170/110) OR Heart failure NYHA class III or IV OR History of malignancy within the last 5 years (other than skin cancer) OR evidence of active cancer which would require concomitant cancer chemotherapy OR Serum creatinine >1.4 mg/dl OR Triglycerides > 400 at baseline visit OR Diabetic subjects with HbA1C > 8 %, OR Drug or alcohol abuse OR Conditions interfering with accurate assessment of coronary calcification (metal clips, bypass patients, intracoronary stents) AND drug absorption (partial ileal bypass or malabsorption syndrome) OR Current use of anticoagulants (except for antiplatelet agents) OR Chronic renal failure OR Liver failure OR Hematological or biochemical values at baseline visit outside the reference ranges considered as clinically significant.
