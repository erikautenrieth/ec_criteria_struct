 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  undergoing unilateral or bilateral breast surgery AND anticipating at least moderate postoperative pain
  -  analgesic plan includes a single-injection peripheral nerve block(s) AND
  -  age 18 years OR older

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  morbid obesity as defined by a body mass index > 40 AND (preoperative creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL)
  -  renal insufficiency (preoperative creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL) AND chronic opioid use (daily use within the 2 weeks prior to surgery AND duration of use > 4 weeks)
  -  chronic opioid use (daily use within the 2 weeks prior to surgery AND duration of use > 4 weeks) AND history of opioid abuse
  -  any comorbidity which results in moderate or severe functional limitation AND inability to communicate with the investigators or hospital staff
  -  pregnancy AND planned regional analgesic with perineural catheter placement
  -  incarceration AND planned regional analgesic with perineural catheter placement
