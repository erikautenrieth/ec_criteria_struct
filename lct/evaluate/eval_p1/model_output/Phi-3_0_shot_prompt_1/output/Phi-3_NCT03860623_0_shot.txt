 Inclusion Criteria:

- BMI 30-40 kg/m^2 AND Males

- BMI 18-25 kg/m^2 OR (BMI up to 28kg/m^2 AND waist circumference <96cm) AND Aged 18-60yrs

- NOT Acute illness in the preceding 6 weeks

- NOT Taking regular medication

- NOT History of deep vein thrombosis or clotting disorders

- NOT Hypertension

- NOT Diabetes

- NOT Any clinically significant findings at screening

- NOT History of substance abuse

- NOT Demonstrating factors precluding safe MRI

- NOT History of gastrointestinal motility disorders (e.g., gastroesophageal reflux disease, irritable bowel syndrome, gastroparesis, sphincter of Oddi dysfunction, etc.)

- NOT Previous thoracic or abdominal surgery

- NOT Reporting having ≤3 bowel movements/week or >2/day.


Exclusion Criteria:

- Acute illness in the preceding 6 weeks OR Taking regular medication OR History of deep vein thrombosis or clotting disorders OR Hypertension OR Diabetes OR Any clinically significant findings at screening OR History of substance abuse OR Demonstrating factors precluding safe MRI OR History of gastrointestinal motility disorders (e.g., gastroesophageal reflux disease, irritable bowel syndrome, gastroparesis, sphincter of Oddi dysfunction, etc.) OR Previous thoracic or abdominal surgery OR Reporting having ≤3 bowel movements/week or >2/day.
