 Inclusion Criteria:
- patients hospitalized with primary diagnosis of acute or subacute ischemic cerebral infarction AND 18 years of age or older
- English speaking OR admitted to hospital Sunday through Thursday
- patient or responsible primary caregiver able to understand AND provide informed consent AND prognosis for survival greater than 6 months
- one or more vascular risk factors (HTN AND DLP OR current smoker OR DM OR obesity)
- live in NWA area (Benton AND Boone AND Carroll AND Madison AND Washington counties) AND have a working phone

Exclusion Criteria:
- admitted with transient ischemic attack OR admitted with an intracerebral hemorrhage
- experience an in-hospital ischemic cerebral infarction
- hospital to hospital transfer patients OR nursing home as primary residence before or after discharge
- hospice OR inability to comply with post discharge follow up
