 Inclusion Criteria:
1. Subject is greater than 18 years of age AND willing and able to provide informed consent AND scheduled to undergo elective rotator cuff surgery AND has consented to an interscalene block.

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Opioid dependence OR any subject diagnosed with a chronic pain condition which daily opioid use is needed OR Anatomical malformation, which in the investigator's opinion may interfere with the placement of the nerve block OR Raynaud's disease diagnosis OR Vasculopathy OR Patient refusal.
