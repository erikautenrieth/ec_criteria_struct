 Inclusion Criteria:
1. [AND] Subject is greater than 18 years of age
2. [AND] Subject is willing and able to provide informed consent
3. [AND] Subject is scheduled to undergo elective rotator cuff surgery
4. [AND] Subject has consented to an interscalene block

Exclusion Criteria:
1. [AND] Opioid dependence
2. [AND] Any subject diagnosed with a chronic pain condition which daily opioid use is needed
3. [AND] Anatomical malformation, which in the investigator's opinion may interfere with the placement of the nerve block
4. [AND] Raynaud's disease diagnosis
5. [AND] Vasculopathy
6. [AND] Patient refusal
