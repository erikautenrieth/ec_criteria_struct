 Inclusion Criteria:
  - [Female AND] older than 35 years.
  - [Already have mammography OR other standard exams (ultrasound, MRI, etc)] [AND] Could participate this trial within 2 weeks after mammography.
  - [Have the ability of consent inform]

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [During pregnancy OR lactation period]
  - [Patients that may not be capable of prone position] [AND] [Patients with scapulohumeral periarthritis OR cardiac pacemaker OR have recent cardiac surgery]
  - [Lymphoma patients that under therapy]
  - [Severe scoliosis]
