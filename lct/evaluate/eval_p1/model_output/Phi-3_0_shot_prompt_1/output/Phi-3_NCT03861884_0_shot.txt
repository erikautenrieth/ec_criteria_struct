 Inclusion Criteria:
1. Ability to give informed consent AND
2. Either pathological, genetic OR imaging biomarker evidence to suggest Fronto-temporal Lobar Degeneration OR Alzheimer's Disease OR Vascular Cognitive Impairment, OR defined clinical diagnoses.

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Lack of mental capacity to consent to study involvement AND
2. NOT speaking English before age 5 years AND
3. Learning disability AND
4. MRI contraindications.
