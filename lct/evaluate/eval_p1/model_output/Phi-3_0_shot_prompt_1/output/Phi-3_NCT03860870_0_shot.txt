 Inclusion Criteria:
  - Healthy [AND] Male
  - 18-40 years of age [AND] No known contraindications for anabolic drugs (e.g. cancer) [AND] NOT Abnormal ECG [AND] NOT Steroid abuse [AND] NOT Ongoing use of prescription medication [AND] NOT heavy resistance training more than 2 times weekly [AND] NOT Disease deemed by the MD to infer a risk to participate in the trial

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Abnormal ECG [OR] Steroid abuse [OR] Ongoing use of prescription medication [OR] heavy resistance training more than 2 times weekly [OR] Disease deemed by the MD to infer a risk to participate in the trial
