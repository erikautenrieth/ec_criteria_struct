 Inclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers:
- Over 18 years of age AND not having a systemic inflammatory disease
- Not in a transient inflammatory state AND not taking any drug that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h preceding the sampling

Neonates:
- Aged between 1h of life AND 28 days of life
- Born beyond 33 Week of Amenorrhea + 1day AND 2kg of birth weight
- Hospitalized in the medical-surgical center of Pediatrics of the hospital of Strasbourg AND for whom a blood sample was prescribed as part of their routine care

Exclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers:
- Having a systemic inflammatory disease OR in a transient inflammatory state OR taking any drug that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h preceding the sampling

Neonates:
- Risk of anemia > 7g/DL OR currently on anticoagulant treatment
