 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Handball players AND Healthy AND [NOT] musculoskeletal injury in the last three months AND [NOT] cardiovascular diseases AND [NOT] use of anabolic, anti-inflammatory and analgesic drugs 72 hours before the evaluations AND [NOT] use of alcohol and illicit drugs during the collection period AND Participation of at least one official match in the last year AND attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  musculoskeletal injury in the last three months OR cardiovascular diseases OR use of anabolic, anti-inflammatory and analgesic drugs 72 hours before the evaluations OR use of alcohol and illicit drugs during the collection period OR NOT Handball players OR NOT Healthy OR NOT Participation of at least one official match in the last year OR NOT attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season.
