 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient 18 years old [AND] not less than 18 years old
  -  Intubated [AND] ventilated in intensive care unit [AND] for more than 24 hours [AND] not less than 24 hours
  -  Patient ready for weaning evaluation [AND] not excluded by other criteria
  -  Failure of a first T-piece SBT [AND] not excluded by other criteria

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Chronic neuromuscular disease [OR] Guillain-Barré Syndrome [OR] Central nervous system disease with consciousness disorder [OR] Tracheostomy [OR] Chronic disease with life expectancy less than 1 year [OR] Pregnancy [OR] Breast feeding [OR] Withholding life support regarding a reintubation [OR] Prisoner [OR] Patient interned in psychiatric hospital [OR] Guardianship [OR] Language barrier [OR] Lack of medical insurance [OR] Lack of the patient's consent (or of the next of kin where appropriate) [OR] Patient under an exclusion period after enrollment in another research study [OR] not excluded by other criteria
