 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Newly diagnosed [AND] histologically proven locoregional oral squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC) [AND] without evidence of distant metastases [AND] OSCC includes the subsites of oral tongue, floor of mouth, gingiva, retromolar trigone and buccal mucosa [OR] Recurrent or persistent histologically proven locoregional OSCC that was initially treated with surgery alone.
  -  must be eligible for surgical resection [AND] greater than 18 years of age [AND] not in the Exclusion Criteria.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  prior immunotherapy or treatment with another anti PD-1 agent besides nivolumab [OR] prior chemotherapy including cetuximab or radiation therapy [OR] concomitant malignancies except cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma or basal cell carcinoma [OR] unresectable primary tumor or regional disease or distant metastases [AND] not in the Inclusion Criteria.
