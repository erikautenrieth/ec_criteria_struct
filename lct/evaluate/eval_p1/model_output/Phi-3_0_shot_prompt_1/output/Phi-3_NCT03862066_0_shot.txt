 Inclusion Criteria:

- Newly diagnosed histologically proven locoregional oral squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC) [AND] without evidence of distant metastases. OSCC includes the subsites of oral tongue, floor of mouth, gingiva, retromolar trigone and buccal mucosa [OR] Recurrent or persistent histologically proven locoregional OSCC that was initially treated with surgery alone.

- Must be eligible for surgical resection [AND]

- Greater than 18 years of age [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:

- Prior immunotherapy or treatment with another anti PD-1 agent besides nivolumab [OR]

- Prior chemotherapy including cetuximab or radiation therapy [OR]

- Concomitant malignancies except cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma or basal cell carcinoma [AND]

- Unresectable primary tumor or regional disease or distant metastases [AND]
