 Inclusion Criteria:

1. [AGE] Between the ages of 60 and 85;

2. [LANGUAGE] Speaks fluent English;

3. [VISUAL AND AUDITORY ACCUITY] Visual and auditory acuity adequate for neuropsychological and computerized testing;

4. [GENERAL HEALTH] Good general health with no [DISEASE] expected to interfere with the study;

5. [PARTICIPATION] Willing and able to participate for the duration of the study and in all study procedures including MRI and PET;

6. [COGNITION] Normal cognition defined as a Clinical Dementia Rating of 0 and a Mini-Mental State Examination score of [27] or higher.

Exclusion Criteria:

1. [COMMORBIABLE NEUROLOGICAL DISEASE] Significant co-morbid neurologic disease such as Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, brain cyst, tumor or aneurysm;

2. [MAJOR HEALTH CONDITIONS] Major health conditions such as uncontrolled diabetes mellitus, uncontrolled hypertension, nutritional deficiency or uncontrolled thyroid disease;

3. [PSYCHIATRIC DISORDERS] Significant psychiatric disorders such as schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, anxiety disorder, major depressive disorder, or attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder;

4. [DEMENTIA OR MILD COGNITIVE IMPAIRMENT] ADRC consensus diagnosis of dementia or mild cognitive impairment;

5. [COGNITIVE IMPAIRMENT] Cognitive impairment when tested at baseline (defined as a score on any neuropsychological test of 1.5 SDs or more outside the age norm);

6. [ALCOHOL OR SUBSTANCE ABUSE OR DEPENDENCE] Alcohol or substance abuse or dependence within the past [2] years (DSM-IV criteria);

7. [MRI CONTRAINDICATIONS] MRI contraindications, e.g. pacemakers, aneurysm clips, artificial heart valves, ear implants, metal fragments or foreign objects in the eyes, skin or body. Females who are pregnant or trying to get pregnant are also excluded;

8. [PET CONTRAINDICATIONS] PET contraindications, e.g. significant prior radiation exposure and pregnancy.
