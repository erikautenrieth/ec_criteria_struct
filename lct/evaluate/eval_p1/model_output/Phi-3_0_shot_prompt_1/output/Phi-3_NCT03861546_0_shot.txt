 Inclusion Criteria:

1. age > 18 years AND

2. confirmed diagnosis of prediabetes or T2D (documented A1c of ≥ 5.7%, 2-hour post-load glucose of >140, or FBG of >100) AND

3. a family member or peer willing to participate AND attend all sessions as a social influencer AND

4. proficiency in English of at least one member of each dyad AND

5. willingness to provide written consent.


Exclusion Criteria:

1. type 1 diabetes OR diabetes secondary to other conditions (e.g. steroid-induced, pancreatic insufficiency, or chemotherapy-induced) AND

2. malignancy OR life-threatening illness with life expectancy of <5 years AND

3. end-stage disease OR serious illness that prohibits participation (e.g. end-stage renal disease or class IV congestive heart failure) AND

4. inability to perform unsupervised physical activity AND

5. pregnancy OR

6. diagnosed cognitive deficits OR limited decision-making capacity AND

7. alcohol OR substance abuse OR

8. homelessness OR no fixed address.
