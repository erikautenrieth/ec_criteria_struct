 Inclusion Criteria:

- Chronic (more than three months) ESBL colonization of the same strain as determined by species, phenotype, and antibiogram AND patients must be able to swallow sachets.
- Patients must be able to speak and understand Swedish AND must have a permanent residence in Sweden.
- Patients must be able to sign informed consent in Swedish AND NOT immunosuppression (i.e., chemotherapy, treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors).
- NOT in patient care AND NOT immunodeficiency AND NOT psychiatric disorder AND NOT alcohol or substance abuse AND NOT dementia AND NOT invasive catheters.

Exclusion Criteria:

- Immunosuppression (i.e., chemotherapy, treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors) OR in patient care OR immunodeficiency OR psychiatric disorder OR alcohol or substance abuse OR dementia OR invasive catheters.
