 Inclusion Criteria:

- age 55-80 AND smoked at least 30 pack-years in lifetime

- if former smoker, NOT quit smoking within the last 15 years OR smoked at least 30 pack-years in lifetime

- English speaker AND no prior history of lung cancer

- NOT had a lung cancer screening test within the last year AND PCP does not object to patient's participation

- have a scheduled visit at University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) internal medicine clinics AND PCP does not object to patient's participation


Exclusion Criteria:

- speaking a language other than English OR no prior history of lung cancer

- has a history of lung cancer OR had a lung cancer screening test within the last year

- PCP objects to patient's participation OR had a lung cancer screening test within the last year
