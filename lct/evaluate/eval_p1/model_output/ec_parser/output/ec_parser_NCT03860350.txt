Inclusion Criteria:
  -  CAC score >10
  -  Framingham risk score (10 %  [OR] or above)
  -  Subjects are required to be on stable concomitant medications for at least 12 weeks prior to randomization
  -  Subjects  [AND] with diabetic must have HbA1C < 8.0 [OR] ,  [AND] and stable HbA1C level variation range within 0.5% for three months.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Hypersensitivity to AGE therapy [OR] ,
  -  Unstable medical [OR] , psychiatric [OR] ,  [OR] or substance abuse disorder  [AND] that may interfere  [AND] with continuation in the study [OR] ,
  -  Weight ≥325 pounds [OR] ,
  -  Bleeding disorder [OR] ,
  -  History of myocardial infarction [OR] ,
  -  Stroke
  -  Life-threatening arrhythmia within prior 6 months [OR] ,
  -  Resting hypotension (systolic < 90 mmHg)  [OR] or hypertension (resting blood pressure >170 [OR] /110)
  -  Heart failure NYHA class III  [OR] or IV [OR] ,
  -  History of malignancy within the last 5 years (other than skin cancer)  [OR] or evidence of active cancer which would require concomitant cancer chemotherapy
  -  Serum creatinine >1.4 mg [OR] /dl
  -  Triglycerides > 400 at baseline visit
  -  Diabetic subjects  [AND] with HbA1C > 8 % [OR] ,
  -  Drug  [OR] or alcohol abuse
  -  Conditions interfering  [AND] with accurate assessment of coronary calcification (metal clips [OR] , bypass patients [OR] , intracoronary stents)  [AND] and drug absorption (partial ileal bypass  [OR] or malabsorption syndrome).
  -  Current use of anticoagulants (except for antiplatelet agents)
  -  Chronic renal failure
  -  Liver failure
  -  Hematological  [OR] or biochemical values at baseline visit outside the reference ranges considered as clinically significant.
