Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Affected children will be recruited if the treating clinician has made a diagnosis of possible Kawasaki disease (even if they do  [NOT] not fulfil the criteria below for Kawasaki disease).
The current standard diagnostic criteria for KD (Circulation 2001 103 335-336 doi: 10.1161 [OR] /01.CIR 103.2.335) are:
The presence of fever for at least five days  [AND] plus four of the following criteria:
  1. Changes in the peripheral extremities Acute: erythema  [AND] and oedema of hands  [AND] and feet Convalescent: membranous desquamation of fingertips
  2. Polymorphous exanthema
  3. Bilateral painless bulbar conjunctival injection  [NOT] without exudate
  4. Changes in lips  [AND] and oral cavity: erythema  [AND] and cracking of lips [OR] , strawberry tongue [OR] , diffuse injection of oral  [AND] and pharyngeal mucosae
  5. Cervical lymphadenopathy (>1.5cm diameter) [OR] , usually unilateral Patients meeting  [NOT] not all of these criteria may meet the criteria for atypical Kawasaki disease [OR] , ie. if they have fever  [AND] and two  [OR] or three of the above criteria  [AND] and elevation of CRP  [OR] or echocardiographic evidence of coronary artery dilatation.
Parents of affected child must be biological parents.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  children  [AND] who do  [NOT] not have a diagnosis of possible Kawasaki disease
