Inclusion Criteria:
  -  diagnosis of schizophrenia OR schizoaffective-depressed OR healthy subjects
  -  ages 18-50 for all subjects
  -  double barrier contraception for all subjects
  -   [NOT] not pregnant for all subjects
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  DSM-IV Axis I  [OR] or II Diagnosis for for healthy subjects
  -  MEM  [OR] or amantadine for patients
  -  current substance abuse for all subjects
  -  current recreational drug use for all subjects
  -  history of other significant medical illness (e.g. cancer [OR] , diabetes [OR] , heart disease [OR] , HIV [OR] , seizures) for all subjects
  -  open head injury  [OR] or closed head injury  [AND] with loss of consciousness > 1 min for all subjects
  -  hearing  [OR] or visual impairment for all subjects
  -  pregnancy for all subjects
  -  dementia for all subjects
  -  mental retardation for all subjects
