Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients  [AND] who fulfilled the following criteria were included in the study:
  -  Adult renal transplant patients (≥21years) [OR] ,
  -  Informed consent [OR] ,
  -  Stable maintenance subcutaneous ESA therapy  [AND] with constant dose interval during the last 2 months [OR] ,
  -  Haemoglobin (Hb) ≥10mg [OR] /dL (Hct 32%) [OR] ,
  -  Transferrin saturation (TSAT) ≥20% (during the screening phase) [OR] ,
  -   [AND] and serum ferritin ≥100 ng [OR] /mL
Exclusion Criteria:
We  [NOT] excluded patients  [AND] with the following criteria:
  -  Acute  [OR] or chronic bleeding [OR] ,  [OR] or erythrocyte transfusion [OR] , within the preceding 8 weeks. change in Hb level ≥ 2 g [OR] /dL during screening phase [OR] ,
  -  Hemolytic anemia [OR] ,
  -  Recent infection  [OR] or rejection [OR] ,
  -  Diastolic blood pressure >100mmHg  [OR] or discontinuation of ESA due to hypertension in the 6 months prior to study [OR] ,
  -  Vitamin B12  [OR] or folic acid deficiency [OR] ,
  -  Uncontrolled  [OR] or secondary hyperparathyroidism [OR] ,
  -  Acute  [OR] or chronic systemic inflammatory disease  [OR]  [AND] and/or C-reactive protein (CRP) >30 mg [OR] /L [OR] ,
  -  Hemodialysis due to failure of a kidney transplant [OR] ,
  -   [AND] and malignancy
