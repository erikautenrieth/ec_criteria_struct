Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Aged at least 18 years.
  -  Histologically  [OR] or cytologically proven diagnosis of NSCLC  [AND] that is locally advanced  [OR] or metastatic.
  -  Radiological documentation of disease progression while on a previous continuous treatment  [AND] with an first  [OR] or second generation of EGFR TKI e.g. gefitinib  [OR] or erlotinib.
  -  patients must also have confirmation of tumour T790M mutation status (confirmed positive) after disease progression on the prior EGFR TKI.
  -  Eastern cooperative oncology group performance status (ECOG PS) of 0-1.
  -  a minimum life expectancy of 12 weeks.
  -  At least one lesion [OR] ,  [NOT] not previously irradiated during the study screening period [OR] ,  [AND] that can be accurately measured at baseline according to RECIST 1.1.
  -  Agree to use routine adequate  [AND] and effective contraceptive measures during the entire study period  [AND] and within 6 weeks after the last dose pre-menopausal fertility possible; Female patients need to exclude pregnancy (ie negative pregnancy test)  [AND] and in non-lactation period.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Treatment  [AND] with a first  [OR] or second generation of EGFR-TKI within 7 days of study entry; previous treatment  [AND] with a third generation of EGFR-TKI.
  -  Unresolved toxicities from prior therapy.
  -  Unstable spinal cord compression [OR] /brain metastases.
  -  Severe [OR] /uncontrolled systemic diseases [OR] , including uncontrolled hypertension [OR] , bleeding diatheses  [OR] or infection.
  -  QTcF>470 msec during the screening period.
  -  Severe respiratory diseases such as interstitial lung disease [OR] , severe asthma [OR] , pulmonary embolism [OR] , etc.
  -  previous treatment  [AND] with 2  [OR] or more lines of Chemotherapy  [OR] or immunotherapy.
