Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Ages 18-59
  -  Histologically confirmed nonalcoholic steatohepatitis  [AND] and fibrosis (stage 1-3) from liver biopsy
  -  Able to row on an indoor rowing apparatus
  -  Not participating in regular physical exercise (more than 60 minutes of moderate intensity  [OR] or 30 minutes of vigorous intensity exercise per week)
  -  Not actively trying to lose weight through a dietary approach
  -  No absolute contra-indications to exercise: recent (<6 months) acute cardiac event unstable angina [OR] , uncontrolled dysrhythmias causing symptoms  [OR] or hemodynamic compromise [OR] , symptomatic aortic stenosis [OR] , uncontrolled symptomatic heart failure [OR] , acute pulmonary embolus [OR] , acute myocarditis  [OR] or pericarditis [OR] , suspected  [OR] or known dissecting aneurism  [AND] and acute systemic infection
  -  No other form of chronic liver disease (as confirmed by serology  [AND] and histology)
  -  No liver cirrhosis (stage 4 fibrosis on liver biopsy)  [OR] or a clinical diagnosis of cirrhosis as ascertained by a hepatologist
  -  No coronary artery disease  [OR] or chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.
  -  No diagnosis of malignancy (except non-melanoma skin cancer)
  -  No past  [OR] or present history of portal hypertension (esophageal varices [OR] , ascites [OR] , hepatic encephalopathy)
  -  No use of immunosuppression  [OR] or chronic nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory medication
  -  No more than 14 drinks per week (if male)  [OR] or 7 drinks a week (if female) in the last 2 years
  -  Cognitively able to provide consent
  -  Able to read [OR] , write [OR] ,  [AND] and understand English
  -  Transportation to the exercise  [AND] and testing facility
