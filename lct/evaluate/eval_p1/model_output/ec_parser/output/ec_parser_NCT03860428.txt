Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Gestational age < 32 weeks
  -  Birthweight <1500 g
  -  Age less than 72 hours
  -  PDA diameter > 1.5 mm
  -  Signed informed consent obtained from both parents
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Birthweight ≥ 1500 g  [OR]  [AND] and/or gestation age ≥ 32 weeks
  -  Lack of informed consent of the parents
  -  Congenital heart defect [OR] , other than PDA  [OR]  [AND] and/or patent foramen ovale (PFO)
  -  The presence of a clinically apparent hemorrhagic syndrome
  -  Any intraventricular hemorrhage (IVH) in the first 48 hours  [OR] or IVH grade 3-4
  -  A platelet count of < 50,000 [OR] /mm3
  -  A serum creatinine concentration of > 110 μmol [OR] /L
  -  Oliguria <1 ml [OR] /kg [OR] /h
  -  Suspected [OR] /apparent NEC
  -  Suspected [OR] /apparent lung hypoplasia
