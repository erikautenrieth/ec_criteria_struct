Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age ≥ 18 years old
  -  Confirmed diagnosis of perianal Crohn's disease
  -  On biologics  [OR] or will start biologics
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients  [AND] who have perianal fistula due to causes other than Crohn's disease
  -  Fistulation into other organs [OR] , including bladder  [AND] and intestine
  -  Patients  [AND] who have allergic reaction  [OR] / contraindications to anti-TNF
  -  Patients  [AND] who have active cancer
  -  Patients  [AND] who have contraindications for MRI
  -  Known pregnancy
For patients  [AND] who refuse to participate in the tight monitoring arm [OR] , they will be consented  [AND] and recruited to the control arm for comparison.
