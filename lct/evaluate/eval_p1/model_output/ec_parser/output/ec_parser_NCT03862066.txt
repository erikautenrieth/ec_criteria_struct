Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Newly diagnosed histologically proven locoregional oral squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC)  [NOT] without evidence of distant metastases. OSCC includes the subsites of oral tongue [OR] , floor of mouth [OR] , gingiva [OR] , retromolar trigone  [AND] and buccal mucosa OR Recurrent  [OR] or persistent histologically proven locoregional OSCC  [AND] that was initially treated  [AND] with surgery alone.
  -  must be eligible for surgical resection
  -  greater than 18 years of age
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  prior immunotherapy  [OR] or treatment  [AND] with another anti PD-1 agent besides nivolumab
  -  prior chemotherapy including cetuximab  [OR] or radiation therapy
  -  concomitant malignancies except cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma  [OR] or basal cell carcinoma
  -  unresectable primary tumor  [OR] or regional disease  [OR] or distant metastases
