Inclusion Criteria:
  -  adult patients of ASA physical status I  [AND] and II undergoing surgery under general anaesthesia
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  anticipated difficult mask ventilation [OR] / intubation
  -  individuals  [AND] with significant cardiorespiratory  [OR] or cerebrovascular disease
  -  pregnant ladies
  -  patient  [AND] with history of epilepsy
  -  body mass index >25 kg [OR] /m2
  -  hemoglobin <8 gm [OR] /dL
  -  patients  [AND] who were non-ambulant for >24 h [OR] ,  [AND] having SpO2 < 97% while breathing in room air
  -  phobia to facemask.
