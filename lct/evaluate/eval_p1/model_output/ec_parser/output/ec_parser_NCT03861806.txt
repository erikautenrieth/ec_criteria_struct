Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Unilateral ischemic stroke  [AND] with residual arm weakness (Fugl-Meyer Upper Limb < 60) more than 6 months prior to enrollment.
  -  Ability to give informed consent  [AND] and understand the tasks involved.
  -  Age over 18 years.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Hemorrhagic Stroke
  -  Contraindications to TMS: history of seizure/ [OR] epilepsy, [OR] pacemaker, [OR] other neurological disorders, [OR] brain surgery, [OR] metal implant/ [OR] fragment in the head, [OR] pregnancy
  -  Taking medications  [OR] or substances  [AND] that are known to affect PAS-induced plasticity within the past 2 months: selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors, [OR] dopamine, [OR] dopamine agonists, [OR] haloperidol, [OR] lithium, [OR] acetylcholinesterase inhibitors, [OR] beta-blockers, [OR] nimodipine, [OR] levetiracetam, [OR] ethosuximide, [OR] benzodiazepines, [OR] baclofen, [OR] nicotine
  -  Peripheral neuropathy  [OR] or history of nerve injury in the paretic upper limb.
  -  Social  [OR]  [AND] and/ [OR]  [OR] or personal circumstances  [AND] that interfere  [AND] with ability to return for all study visits.
