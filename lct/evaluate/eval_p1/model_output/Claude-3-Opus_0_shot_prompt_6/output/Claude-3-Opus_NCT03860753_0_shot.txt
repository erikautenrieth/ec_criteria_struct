Inclusion Criteria:
  -  treatment-seeking individuals diagnosed with AUD diagnostic statistical manual 5 (DSM-5) [AND]
  -  fluent in English [AND]
  -  past month excessive alcohol use (>7 drinks/week for woman, [OR] >14 drinks/week for men, [OR] >3 drinks/occasion for women [OR] >4 drinks/occasion for men)14 [AND]
  -  baseline Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A) [OR] Perceived Stress Scale (PSS) core indicative of mild to moderate anxiety (score 8 to 23) [OR] moderate stress (score 14 to 26), respectively [AND]
  -  increase in alcohol craving following the baseline stress reactivity assessment.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Individuals will be excluded for exhibiting severe scores on the HAM-A, [OR] PSS, [OR] post-traumatic (PTSD) checklist (PCL-5) at the discretion of the admitting physician [OR]
  -  physical dependence on alcohol Alcohol Use Disorders Inventory (AUDIT) score indicative of severe alcohol dependence (≥13 for women, [OR] ≥15 for men) [OR] 
  -  greater than mild substance use disorder on drugs other than alcohol, nicotine, [AND] marijuana [OR]
  -  contraindications for taking pioglitazone; [OR] medical conditions contraindicating pioglitazone pharmacotherapy [OR] taking contraindicated medications [OR]
  -  be pregnant, [OR] nursing, [OR] planning on becoming pregnant during the course of the study [OR]
  -  have any other illness, [OR] condition, [OR] use of medications, which in the opinion of the PI [AND]/[OR] admitting physician would preclude safe [AND]/[OR] successful completion of the study.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
