Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age ≥18 years [AND]
  -  BMI ≤40 kg/m2 [AND]
  -  indication for hysterectomy for benign disease [OR] prophylactic surgery due to hereditary cancer [OR]
  -  indication for radical hysterectomy [AND]/[OR] pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for cervical cancer [OR]  
  -  indication for modified radical hysterectomy [AND]/[OR] pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for endometrial cancer [AND]
  -  size of uterus [AND] vagina allows for retrieval by the vaginal route in cancer patients [AND]
  -  written informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:  
  -  known extensive intra-abdominal adhesions [OR]
  -  anaesthesiological contraindications to laparoscopy [OR]
  -  women with pacemaker [OR] other implants where electrosurgery is to be avoided [OR]
  -  women with known defects of the hemostasis [OR]
  -  pregnancy [OR]
  -  other internal [OR] anatomical criteria that preclude a minimal invasive approach [OR]
  -  inability to understand patient information
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
