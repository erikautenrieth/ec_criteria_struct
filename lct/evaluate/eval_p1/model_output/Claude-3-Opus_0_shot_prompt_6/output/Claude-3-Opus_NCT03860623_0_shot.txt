Inclusion Criteria:
  -  BMI 30-40 kg/m2 [OR]
  -  BMI 18-25 kg/m2; [AND] those with BMI up to 28kg/m2 will be included if waist circumference is <96cm.
  -  Males [AND]
  -  Aged 18-60yrs
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Acute illness in the preceding 6 weeks [OR]
  -  Taking regular medication [OR]
  -  History of deep vein thrombosis [OR] clotting disorders [OR]
  -  Hypertension [OR]
  -  Diabetes [OR]
  -  Any clinically significant findings at screening [OR]
  -  History of substance abuse [OR]
  -  Demonstrating factors precluding safe MRI [OR]
  -  History of gastrointestinal motility disorders (e.g. gastroesophageal reflux disease, irritable bowel syndrome, gastroparesis, sphincter of Oddi dysfunction, etc.) [OR]
  -  Previous thoracic [OR] abdominal surgery. [OR]
  -  Those who report having ≤3 bowel movements/week [OR] >2/day.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
