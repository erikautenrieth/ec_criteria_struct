Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria-- Participants must be:
  -  Healthy (determined by self-report)
  -  Between the ages of 18-55 years old
  -  Current cigarette smokers
  -  Willing to provide informed consent
  -  Able to attend the lab session [AND] abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required
  -  Agree to use designated products according to the study protocol

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Women if they are breast-feeding [OR] test positive for pregnancy (by urinalysis) at screening.
  -  Individuals who weigh less than 110 pounds
  -  [NOT] able to provide informed consent
  -  [NOT] able to attend the lab session
  -  [NOT] able to abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required
  -  [NOT] agree to use designated products according to the study protocol

Note: I added the additional exclusion criteria based on the negation of the inclusion criteria, as per the rules provided.
