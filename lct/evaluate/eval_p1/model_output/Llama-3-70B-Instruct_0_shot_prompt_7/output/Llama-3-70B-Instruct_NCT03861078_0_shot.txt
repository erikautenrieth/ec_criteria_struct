<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria-- Participants must be:
  -  Healthy (determined by self-report) [AND] 
  -  Between the ages of 18-55 years old [AND]
  -  Current cigarette smokers [AND]
  -  Willing to provide informed consent [AND]
  -  Able to attend the lab session [AND] abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required [AND]
  -  Agree to use designated products according to the study protocol [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Women if they are breast-feeding [OR] test positive for pregnancy (by urinalysis) at screening [OR] both.
  -  Individuals who weigh less than 110 pounds [OR] have a body mass index (BMI) below a certain threshold [NOT] meeting the specified weight requirement.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
