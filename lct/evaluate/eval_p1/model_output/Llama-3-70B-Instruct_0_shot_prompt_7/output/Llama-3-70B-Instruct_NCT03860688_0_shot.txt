<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Men [AND] women, aged 18 to 60 years.
  -  Body mass index [OR] BMI of 20 to 27 kg/m2.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients with active inflammatory bowel disease [OR] those with a history of inflammatory bowel disease.
  2. Patients with pre-existing Celiac disease [OR] exocrine pancreatic insufficiency [OR] small bowel malabsorption [NOT] adequately managed.
  3. Patients with active bowel malignancy [OR] those with a history of bowel malignancy.
  4. Patients with diabetes mellitus [OR] known [OR] suspected motility disorders of the gut [NOT] well-controlled.
  5. Patients with decompensated liver disease [OR] those with liver disease that is [NOT] stable.
  6. Patients on ezetimibe [OR] bile acid sequestrants [NOT] discontinued for at least 4 weeks.
  7. Patients who are pregnant [OR] breastfeeding [NOT] willing to use acceptable contraception.
  8. Patients with renal disease [OR] those with impaired renal function [NOT] on dialysis.
  9. Patients on benzodiazepine [NOT] discontinued for at least 2 weeks.
  10. Unstable cardiac [OR] respiratory disease [NOT] adequately managed.
  11. Any changes to medication in the preceding month [NOT] approved by the investigator.
  12. Patients with hypersensitive to Revestive [OR] any ingredient [OR] active [OR] a history of the drug within the last 5 years [NOT] resolved.
  13. Patients with GI malignancy (GI tract, hepatobiliary, pancreatic) [OR] those with a history of GI malignancy.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
