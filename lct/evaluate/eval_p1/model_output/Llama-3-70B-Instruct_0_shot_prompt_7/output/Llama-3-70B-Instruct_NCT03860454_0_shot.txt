<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  LMNA+ cases [AND] with pathogenic LMNA mutations for LMNA+ [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  DCMWT cases: patients [AND] with heart muscle failure [AND] but with wild-type lamin gene. Heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  HV (controls): matched to cases [OR] matched to other controls.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Participants [WITH] needle-phobia that would preclude blood-letting [OR] participants with other phobias that would interfere with the study.
  -  Participants unwilling to consent [OR] unable to provide informed consent.
  -  Patients that have [ANY] conventional contraindication for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) [OR] other imaging modalities.
  -  Patients that have had a blood transfusion within the last month [AND] patients having haemodialysis [OR] patients with other conditions that may interfere with the study will be excluded [NOT] eligible for the study.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
