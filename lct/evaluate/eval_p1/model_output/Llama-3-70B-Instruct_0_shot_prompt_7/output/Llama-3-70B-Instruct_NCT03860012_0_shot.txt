<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. inflammatory bowel disease
  2. on methotrexate at appropriate dosing [AND] treatment with folic acid
  3. normal folate levels at onset of study
  4. ages 2-21 years [OR] equivalent developmental stage

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. abnormal folate levels [OR] folate deficiency
  2. age > 21 [OR] age < 2 [OR] [NOT] between 2-21 years
