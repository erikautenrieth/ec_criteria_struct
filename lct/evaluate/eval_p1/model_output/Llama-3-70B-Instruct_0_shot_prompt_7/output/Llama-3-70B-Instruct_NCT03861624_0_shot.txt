<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Skeletal maturity [AND] 
  2. AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures [OR] other fractures that meet the study criteria
  3. Wound primarily closeable [AND] no flap or delay in closure due to contamination needed
  4. Palpable pedal pulses [AND] no vascular injury sustained
  5. Presentation within 24 hours from injury [OR] presentation beyond 24 hours with a compelling reason for delayed treatment

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Current injury is a pathologic fracture [OR] has a history of pathologic fractures
  2. Sustained bilateral tibia fracture [AND] no possibility of weight-bearing on the affected limb
  3. Sustained comminuted femur fracture [OR] any other fractures that may compromise the study outcome
  4. Sustained severe Traumatic Brain Injury (GCS<12) [AND] no likelihood of full recovery ***
  5. Sustained severe spinal cord injury (lower extremity paresis/paralysis) [OR] any other spinal cord injury that may impact the study
  6. Sustained severe burns (>10% total body surface area (TBSA) [OR] >5% TBSA with full thickness or circumferential injury) [AND] no possibility of timely wound healing
  7. Prior ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery [OR] any other prior leg injuries that may affect the study outcome
  8. Prior or current lower limb deformity or abnormality [AND] no possibility of correction through surgical or non-surgical means
  9. Unable to complete follow-up visits [NOT] willing to comply with the study protocol

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
