Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age ≥ 18 years old
 - Confirmed diagnosis of perianal Crohn's disease
 - On biologics [OR] or will start biologics

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who have perianal fistula due to causes [NOT] other than Crohn's disease
 - Fistulation into other organs, including bladder [OR] and intestine
 - Patients who have allergic reaction [OR] / contraindications to anti-TNF
 - Patients who have active cancer
 - Patients who have contraindications for MRI
 - Known pregnancy
For patients who refuse to participate in the tight monitoring arm, they will be consented [AND] and recruited to the control arm for comparison.
