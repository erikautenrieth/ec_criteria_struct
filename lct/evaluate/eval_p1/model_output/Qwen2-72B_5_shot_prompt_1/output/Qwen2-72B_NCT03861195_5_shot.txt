Inclusion Criteria:
 - age ≥18 years
 - BMI ≤40 kg/m2
 - indication for hysterectomy [AND] for benign disease [OR] or prophylactic surgery due to hereditary cancer
 - indication for radical hysterectomy [AND] and/or pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy [AND] for cervical cancer
 - indication for modified radical hysterectomy [AND] and/or pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy [AND] for endometrial cancer
 - size of uterus [AND] and vagina allows for retrieval by the vaginal route [AND] in cancer patients
 - written informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - known extensive intra-abdominal adhesions
 - anaesthesiological contraindications to laparoscopy
 - women [WITH] with pacemaker [OR] or other implants where electrosurgery is to be avoided
 - women [WITH] with known defects of the hemostasis
 - pregnancy
 - other internal [OR] or anatomical criteria that preclude a minimal invasive approach
 - inability to understand patient information
