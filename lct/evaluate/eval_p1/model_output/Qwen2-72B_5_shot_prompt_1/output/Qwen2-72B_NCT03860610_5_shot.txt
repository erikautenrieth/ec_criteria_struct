<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] with an unilateral THA [OR] or TKA, [AND] which they received for the treatment of osteoarthritis (Patient groups 1+2)
 - Patients [AND] diagnosed with unilateral OA of the knee [OR] or hip [AND] planned for a treatment with THA [OR] or TKA (Patient groups 3+4)
 - Age ≥ 30 years (Healthy control group)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Body mass index > 35kg/m2
 - Use of walking aids
 - Neuromuscular disorders [AND] affecting gait
 - Inability [AND] to follow procedures due to psychological disorders [OR] or dementia
 - Diagnosed hip [OR] or knee OA (Healthy control group)
 - Current pain in the lower extremities [OR] or lower back (Healthy control group)
 - Rheumatoid Arthritis (Healthy control group)
 - Previous corrective osteotomy (Healthy control group)
 - Regular intake of pain medication (more than 4 times per week) (Healthy control group)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
