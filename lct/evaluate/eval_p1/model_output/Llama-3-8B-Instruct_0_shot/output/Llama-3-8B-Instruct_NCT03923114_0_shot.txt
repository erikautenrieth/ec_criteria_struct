Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with treatment response:
  - Age ≥18 years [AND] 
  - Subject is diagnosed with type 2 diabetes [AND] 
  - Subject showed response to GLP-1RA treatment [AND] 
  - Ability to sign informed consent

Patients without treatment response:
  - Age ≥18 years [AND] 
  - Subject is diagnosed with type 2 diabetes [AND] 
  - Subject showed [NOT] response to GLP-1RA treatment [AND] 
  - Ability to sign informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Liver disease [OR] Renal disease
  - Pregnancy [OR] the wish to become pregnant within 6 months after the study
  - Breastfeeding
  - Age <18 years
  - Pituitary disorder
  - Inability to sign informed consent
  - Exclusion criteria for MR:
       - Fragments, clips or devices in brain, eyes, spinal canal [OR] Implantable defibrillator or pacemaker (wires) [OR] Mandibular magnetic implants [OR] Neurostimulator, bladder stimulator, non-removable insulin pump [OR] Metal tissue-expander in chest [OR] Cochlear implant [OR] Ossicular replacement prosthesis
