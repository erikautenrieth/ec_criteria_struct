Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Over 18 years [AND] 
  - For the T2D study group: Diagnosed with type 2 diabetes.

Exclusion Criteria:
For subjects with type 2 diabetes:
  - Systemic inflammatory disease that requires active treatment [OR] Thyroid disease that requires active treatment [OR] Skin disease that requires active treatment [AND] causes significant changes in the area of the skin that is to be investigated
  - Treatment with oral glucocorticoids [NOT] for other medical conditions
  - Levels of SR, TSH, T4 or Hemoglobin in the blood pronouncedly outside the reference range [NOT] due to a known medical condition
  - Pregnancy [NOT] planned or intended

For healthy controls:
  - Registered disease in the health declaration that requires continuous systemic treatment [EXCEPT] well controlled hypertension in which case the subject should not have treatment with more than one antihypertensive drug, the drug must not be an alpha-blocker or beta-blocker or thiazide diuretic
  - Skin disease that requires active treatment [AND] causes significant changes in the area of the skin that is to be investigated
  - Blood pressure pronouncedly above 140/90 [NOT] due to a known medical condition
  - Blood test results pronouncedly outside the reference range [NOT] due to a known medical condition
  - Pregnancy [NOT] planned or intended
