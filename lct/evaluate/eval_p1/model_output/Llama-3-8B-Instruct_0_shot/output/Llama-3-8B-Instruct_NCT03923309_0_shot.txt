Note that I inserted the logical operators to represent the logical connections between the conditions. I used [AND] to represent the conjunction between the biopsy proven rectal cancer and the distance from the anal verge, and between the two treatment options. I used [OR] to represent the disjunction between the two treatment options. I used [NOT] to represent the negation of the radical surgery condition, as well as the negation of the malignant disease, fatal disease, and incompletion of radiotherapy course conditions.
