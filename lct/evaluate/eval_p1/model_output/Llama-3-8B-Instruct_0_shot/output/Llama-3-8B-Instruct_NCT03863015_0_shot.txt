Inclusion Criteria:
Each of the following criteria must be fulfilled for a subject to be eligible:
  1. Age [AND] ≥ 18 years
  2. OHCA [AND] of a presumed cardiac cause
  3. Unconsciousness upon admission [AND] i.e. a GCS [AND] < 9
  4. Sustained ROSC [AND] for more than 20 minutes

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] None of the following criteria must be fulfilled for a subject to be eligible:
  1. Consciousness upon admission [AND] i.e. a GCS [NOT] ≥ 9
  2. Presumed [NOT] non-cardiac cause of arrest
  3. Unwitnessed [AND] asystole
  4. Suspected [OR] confirmed intracranial bleeding [OR] stroke
  5. Pregnancy [OR] females in fertile age [NOT] unless a negative serum HCG can rule out pregnancy within the inclusion window
  6. Temperature on admission [NOT] < 30 °C
  7. Persistent [AND] cardiogenic shock [NOT] that is not reversed within the inclusion window
  8. Known [NOT] disease making 180 day survival unlikely
  9. Known [NOT] limitations in therapy
  10. Known [NOT] pre-arrest Cerebral Performance Category of 3 [OR] 4
  11. > 240 minutes [NOT] from ROSC to randomization
