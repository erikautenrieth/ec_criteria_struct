Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - 6-17 years old [AND] otherwise healthy
  - Currently have unilateral [OR] bilateral ventilation tube(s) (VTs) inserted for otitis media with effusion (OME) [OR] tympanic membrane retraction/retraction pocket (TM-R/RP) [OR] a TM perforation after extrusion of a VT
  - History of at least 2 sets of VT insertions in the past [AND] eustachian tube (ET) function (ETF) tests showing an active muscular pattern of eustachian tube dysfunction
  - Tubomanometry test results showing evidence of velopharyngeal dysfunction during swallowing [AND] cleft palate (CP) cohort: non-syndromic; prior Furlow palatoplasty without complications or need for revision
  - Non-CP cohort: have had prior adenoidectomy [AND] not have a concurrent or past diagnosis of cancer [NOT] history of radiation

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Concurrent or past diagnosis of cancer [OR] history of radiation [NOT] diagnosis of vestibular pathology, cranial base surgery, or ossicular chain reconstruction
  - Craniofacial dysmorphology (other than non-syndromic CP with or without cleft lip in the CP cohort) [OR] other syndrome [NOT] non-patent nasal cavity
  - Patulous eustachian tube [OR] pathologically low eustachian tube opening or closing pressures [NOT] able or willing to perform the tests and exercises outlined in the study
  - Pregnancy [NOT] have a concurrent or past diagnosis of cancer [OR] history of radiation
