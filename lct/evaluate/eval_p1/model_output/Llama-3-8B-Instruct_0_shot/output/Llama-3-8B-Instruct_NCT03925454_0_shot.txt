Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Receiving HHD or ICHDF for more than 3 months [AND] less than 36 months.
  - Haemoglobin [AND] equal to or greater than 100 g/L [AND] at enrolment.
  - Willing [AND] physically able to undertake the study assessments/tests.
  - Willing to provide blood for storage [AND] future analysis.
  - Able to give informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Living donor transplant or change to peritoneal dialysis planned.
  - Physical assessments contraindicated for the following clinical reasons:
       - Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS) within the last 3 months [OR] chest pain, ECG changes or typical biomarker elevation.
       - Any current uncontrolled cardiac dysrhythmias causing symptoms [OR] chest pain, palpitations, syncope or dizziness.
       - Symptomatic aortic stenosis [OR] New York Heart Association grade IV Heart failure.
       - Severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] Acute pulmonary embolus or pulmonary infarction in the last 3 months.
       - Current acute myocarditis or pericarditis [OR] Suspected or known dissecting aneurysm.
       - Acute systemic infection, accompanied by fever, body aches or swollen lymph glands [OR] Pregnancy.
  - Life expectancy of less than twelve months.
