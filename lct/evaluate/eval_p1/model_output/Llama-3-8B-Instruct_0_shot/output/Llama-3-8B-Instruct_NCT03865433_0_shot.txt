Inclusion Criteria:
  1. are aged 13-17 [AND] a high school student-athlete who attends school where an athletic trainer can supervise the exercise protocol;
  2. have sustained a concussion within 2-7 days of clinic presentation [AND] diagnosed by a study physician;
  3. demonstrate symptom exacerbation during a graded treadmill exercise test [AND] cannot complete the test;
  4. are currently participating in a school or club sport [AND] are English speaking [AND] capable of giving assent;

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. have a reported history of neurological condition or disorder [NOT] including but not limited to brain surgery, special education, seizure disorder, speech pathology, previous diagnosis of Post-Concussion Syndrome (PCS);
  2. are unwilling to exercise [OR] have a reported history of neurological condition or disorder including but not limited to brain surgery, special education, seizure disorder, speech pathology, previous diagnosis of Post-Concussion Syndrome (PCS);
  3. have focal neurologic deficit that would represent risk for walking/running on treadmill [AND] cannot exercise;
  4. exhibit an inability to exercise due to injury [OR] known heart disease [OR] increased cardiac risk;
  5. have experienced more than 3 previous concussions;
  6. have suffered a substantial comorbidity (e.g., cervical injury);
  7. are currently taking β-blockers [OR] calcium channel blockers [OR] prophylactic headache medications (e.g., amitriptyline, topiramate);
  8. have a resting blood pressure of > 140/90;
  9. are unable to fully understand the study protocol or study risks due to injury [OR] developmental delay.
