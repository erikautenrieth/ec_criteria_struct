Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Skeletal maturity [AND] 
  2. AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures 
  3. Wound primarily closeable [NOT] no flap [OR] delay in closure due to contamination needed 
  4. Palpable pedal pulses [AND] no vascular injury sustained 
  5. Presentation within 24 hours from injury 

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] current injury is a pathologic fracture 
  2. Sustained bilateral tibia fracture 
  3. Sustained comminuted femur fracture 
  4. Sustained severe Traumatic Brain Injury [NOT] GCS<12 
  5. Sustained severe spinal cord injury [NOT] lower extremity paresis/paralysis 
  6. Sustained severe burns [NOT] >10% total body surface area (TBSA) [OR] >5% TBSA with full thickness or circumferential injury 
  7. Prior ipsilateral leg injury [NOT] requiring surgery 
  8. Prior or current lower limb deformity [OR] abnormality 
  9. [NOT] unable to complete follow-up visits
