Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], and [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients with structural heart disease [AND] implantable cardioverter defibrillator (ICD)
  - Age > 18 years
  - either [OR]
       1. Recurring symptomatic monomorphic ventricular tachycardia that requires ICD intervention (e.g. shock or anti-tachycardia stimulation). At least 3 episodes within the 3 months prior to inclusion [OR]
       2. induction of symptomatic monomorphic ventricular tachycardia that requires ICD intervention (e.g. shock or anti-tachycardia stimulation). Induction triggered by ICD [AND] during electrophysiology studies (EPS) [AND] both
  - Refractory to antiarrhythmic combination therapy
  - Beside the cardiac conditions: [NOT] No competing illness that would additionally limit the life expectancy to less than 6 months
  - [NOT] No prior radiation therapy in the thorax area
  - [NOT] No pregnancy [AND] no active breastfeeding
  - Ability to consent [AND] consent to study participation

Exclusion Criteria:
  - ICD electrode malfunction [OR] ICD readings outside reference range
  - [NOT] Lack of evidence of a myocardial scar (Computer tomography or magnetic resonance tomography for MRI-capable ICD aggregates or electrophysiological measurement)
  - [NOT] No possible induction of symptomatic monomorphic ventricular tachycardia non-persistent [OR] persistent with delivery of ICD therapies such as antitachycardic pacing or shock
  - Contraindication to radiosurgery (e.g. precise target volume definition not possible due to image artifacts created from a left ventricular assist device [LVAD])
  - [NOT] Inability to consent [OR] missing or withdrawn consent
