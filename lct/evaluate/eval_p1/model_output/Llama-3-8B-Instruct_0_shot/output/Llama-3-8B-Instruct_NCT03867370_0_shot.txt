Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Confirmed by histopathological [OR] cytological examination;
  2. The criteria for resectability is met [AND] has at least one evaluable lesion according to the RECIST 1.1 standard [AND] has not received local treatment;
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients who [NOT] previously received anti-programmed death receptor-1 (PD-1) antibody, anti-programmed death ligand-1 (PD-L1) antibody, anti-programmed death ligand-2 (PD-L2) antibody or anti-cytotoxic T lymphocyte-associated antigen-4 (CTLA-4) antibodies, including those who have participated in a JS001 clinical study;
  2. Patients who previously received [OR] any TACE, radiofrequency ablation and other liver cancer treatments;
  3. Patients with a history of gastroesophageal varices [OR] active cardia ulcers associated with a high risk of bleeding;
  4. Patients who have upper gastrointestinal hemorrhage within 1 year [AND] not otherwise excluded;
  5. Patients known to have fibrous layer HCC, sarcomatoid HCC [OR] mixed cholangiocarcinoma and HCC;
  6. Other protocol defined inclusion/exclusion criteria could apply
