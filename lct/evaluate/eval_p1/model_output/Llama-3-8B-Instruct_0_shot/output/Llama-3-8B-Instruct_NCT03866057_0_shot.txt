Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], and [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion criteria:
  - age 18-90 [AND] acute ischemic or hemorrhagic stroke (within 30 days) [OR] consent to participate and to anonymous processing of data.

Additional inclusion criteria for Sub-project Neurophysiological Markers:
  - signing of informed consent for the participation in the sub-project [AND] age 18-90.

Additional inclusion criteria for Epigenetic subproject:
  - signing of informed consent for participation in the subproject [AND] age 18-90.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] stroke occurred more than 30 days after the transfer to intensive rehabilitation.

Additional exclusion criteria for Epigenetic subproject (for retrospective analysis):
  - history of major psychiatric disorder or recent exacerbation [AND] global aphasia [AND] refusal of the patient to participate in the study.

Note: I did not insert [OR] between the criteria in the exclusion criteria for Epigenetic subproject, as it is not explicitly mentioned in the original text. If the criteria are intended to be implicitly OR'd, it would be necessary to rephrase the text to make it clear.
