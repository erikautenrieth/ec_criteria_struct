Inclusion Criteria:
  - 18-40 years [AND] BMI = 18.5-29.9 kg/m2
  - Male [OR] female
  - Physically active (> 3 x 30 min moderate exercise per week) [AND] Non-smoker (including the use of vaporisers and e-cigarettes)

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Outside of age-range
  - [NOT] Outside BMI range
  - Inactive (< 3 x 30 min moderate exercise per week)
  - Smoker
  - Taking medication
  - History of cardiometabolic or inflammatory illness
  - Vegetarian/ vegan (as the high-fat diet is based on animal fats) [AND] Other dietary restrictions which prevent consumption of the experimental diet
  - Consumption of probiotics within last 3 months (these influence the gut microbiota)
  - Participation in another clinical trial
