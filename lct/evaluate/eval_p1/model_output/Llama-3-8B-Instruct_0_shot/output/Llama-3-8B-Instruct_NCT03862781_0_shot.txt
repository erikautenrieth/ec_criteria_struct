Inclusion Criteria:
  - Eligible patients are those over 18 years of age [AND] who are regularly scheduled to undergo a right hemicolectomy via a minimally invasive approach [OR] robotic [AND] laparoscopic [AND] with the creation of an anastomosis. Right colectomy will be defined as removal of the ascending colon [AND] ligation of the ileocolic artery and vein [OR] +/- removal of the terminal ileum [OR] +/- removal of the proximal transverse colon [OR] +/- removal of the right branch of the middle colic artery and vein.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Pregnant women
  - Additional colon resection is planned [AND] i.e. left colectomy [OR] proctectomy
  - Vulnerable populations [AND] such as prisoners [OR] adults [NOT] able to give consent
  - If the scheduled surgery is planned at [NOT] Butterworth hospital [AS] to standardize the nursing care received postoperatively
  - Emergent cases

Patients will be excluded from the study intra-operatively if:
  - The procedure is converted to an open resection
  - If a loop ileostomy is performed [AND] in addition to the right colectomy
