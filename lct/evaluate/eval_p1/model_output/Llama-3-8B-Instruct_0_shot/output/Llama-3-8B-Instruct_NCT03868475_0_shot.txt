Inclusion Criteria:
  - Women over age 18 [AND] with a core biopsy proven borderline or high-risk breast lesion that requires further excision for management based on the surgeon's assessment
  - Informed consent must be obtained [AND] is signed by the patient

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Women who are considered high-risk [NOT] based on a lifetime risk of breast cancer as per the IBIS (International Breast Cancer Intervention Study) Breast Cancer Risk Evaluation Tool
  - Pathology that is felt to be [NOT] discordant with imaging
  - Extensive calcifications extending more than 20 mm [OR] masses greater than 30 mm

  - Lesions that are suspicious for borderline or malignant Phyllodes, DCIS (ductal carcinoma in situ), invasive mammary carcinoma, or encapsulated papillary carcinoma
  - Any lesion that either the radiologist, pathologist or surgeon feels is [NOT] amenable to VAPE or surgical excision
