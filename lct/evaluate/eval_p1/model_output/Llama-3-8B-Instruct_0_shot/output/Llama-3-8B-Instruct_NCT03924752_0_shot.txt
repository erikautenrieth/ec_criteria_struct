Inclusion Criteria:
  - Between 18-85 years of age [AND]
  - Subjects should be capable of walking, ascending/descending stairs and ramps with full capability in lower extremity passive range of motion [AND] (knee flexion contracture of >10 degrees [OR] knee flexion ROM < 90 degrees [OR] hip flexion contracture < 25 degrees [OR] ankle plantar flexion contracture of >15 degrees).
  - Subjects must be able to walk for at least 5 minutes [AND] willing and able to participate over a 1-6 hours experiment with breaks and rest enforced regularly [AND] as needed [AND].
  - Subjects must be able to transfer (sit-to-stand and stand-to-sit) with no external support (arm rests OK) [AND] to ambulate over small slopes (3 degrees) [AND] a few steps (6 steps).

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] History of neurological injury [OR] gait pathology [OR] cardiovascular condition that would limit your ability to ambulate for multiple hours.
