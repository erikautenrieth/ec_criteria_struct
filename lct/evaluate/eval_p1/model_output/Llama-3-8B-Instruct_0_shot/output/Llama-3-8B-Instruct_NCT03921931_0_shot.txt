Inclusion Criteria:
  -  men [AND] women aged over 18 years
  -  signed informed consent form
  -  ametropia below 3 diopters
  -  ability [AND] willingness to follow instructions
for healthy volunteers:
  -  normal ophthalmologic findings
for primary open angle glaucoma patients (POAG):
  -  diagnosed POAG
  -  MD [NOT] > 10 dB
for age-related macular degeneration (AMD):
  -  diagnosed dry AMD
  -  diagnosed stage II [OR] stage III AMD

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Presence of any abnormalities [NOT] preventing reliable measurements
  -  Ocular inflammation [OR] ocular disease interfering with the study aims
  -  Use of photosensitizing medication [NOT] in the 3 months preceding the study
  -  Presence of any condition [OR] with the possibility of causing photosensitivity, including systemic lupus erythematosus SLE, Porphyria, Vitiligo, Xeroderma Pigmentosum and Albinism
  -  Presence of any form [NOT] of epilepsy
  -  Ocular surgery [NOT] in the 3 months preceding the study
  -  Pregnancy [OR] planned pregnancy [OR] lactating
  -  Any medical [OR] surgical history, disorder [OR] disease such as acute [OR] chronic severe organic disease
