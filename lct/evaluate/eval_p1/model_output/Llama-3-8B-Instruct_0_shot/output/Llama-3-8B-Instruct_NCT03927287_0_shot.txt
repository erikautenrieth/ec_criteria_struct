Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
For the two retrospective cohorts:
  1. All patients that are older than 18 [AND] treated for localized adenocarcinoma of the prostate between 2000 and 2017 with either radical prostatectomy [OR] radiotherapy
  2. All treated patients had a rising post-treatment PSA [AND] with at least one post-treatment free PSA blood test [NOT NOT].
For the biobank validation cohort:
1. All patients treated with radical prostatectomy for localized prostate cancer between 2000 and 2017 who had biobank samples taken when developing biochemical recurrence [NOT NOT].

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients that are younger than 18 [NOT]
  2. Patients with prostate cancer other than adenocarcinoma [NOT], such as small cell [AND] neuroendocrine cancer
  3. Patients with prostate adenocarcinoma that did not develop biochemical recurrence [NOT]
  4. In the retrospective cohorts - patients that did not have at least one post-treatment free PSA blood test [NOT]

Note: I inserted [AND] where two conditions are required to be met together, [OR] where two conditions are alternatives, and [NOT] before words like "not", "no", and "unable" to indicate exclusion criteria. I also inserted [NOT NOT] to indicate that a condition is not excluded.
