Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Refractory [OR] relapsed AML which will include:
       1. Refractory disease will be defined as at least 1 prior treatment with no remission [AND] will be defined as such.
       2. Relapsed disease will be defined as 5% or more blasts in bone marrow seen after remission [AND] will be defined as such.
       3. Patients with AML arising from myelodysplastic syndromes [OR] myeloproliferative neoplasms (secondary AML, ts-AML) are also eligible [AND] have a diagnosis of AML.
  2. Circulating blast count ≤ 200/μL within 10 days prior to first cycle of treatment [AND] hydroxyurea should be used to keep the peripheral blast count ≤ 200/μL until the first day of protocol treatment, to the extent that this is possible.
  3. ECOG ≤ 2 [AND] the participant is able to participate in the study.
  4. Estimated creatinine clearance ≥ 50 mL/min [AND] the participant is not undergoing dialysis.
  5. AST and ALT ≤ 3.0 x ULN [AND] the participant has no liver disease.
  6. Bilirubin ≤ 3.0 x ULN [AND] the participant has no liver disease.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Active [NOT] CNS Leukemia.
  2. Known [NOT] HIV infection or known [NOT] hepatitis B or hepatitis C infection (with a detectable viral load).
  3. Participant has received strong [AND/OR] moderate CYP3A inducers within 7 days prior to the initiation of study treatment [AND] is not able to stop taking these medications.
  4. Have received prior radiation to maximally tolerated levels to any critical normal organ [AND] have a history of radiation therapy.
  5. Clinically significant [NOT] cardiac disease.
  6. Active, [NOT] uncontrolled serious infection.
  7. Have other [NOT] non-myeloid malignancy within 2 years of entry (with exceptions).
  8. Psychiatric disorder that would [NOT] preclude study participation.
  9. Previous solid organ transplant [NOT] prior treatment with SCT is allowed but not if patient has GVHD or is still receiving immunosuppression/GVHD therapy.
