Inclusion Criteria:
  - Adult patients [AND] aged 18 years or more, [AND] both gender
  - Receiving endotracheal intubation [AND] mechanical ventilation for more than 12 hours
  - Recovery from the precipitating illness [AND] weaning readiness according to the following criteria :
       1. Respiratory criteria :
            1. PaO2:FIO2 >150 [AND] FIO2 ≤0.4 [AND] PEEP <8 cm H2O
            2. Arterial pH >7.35 [AND] [NOT] electrocardiographic signs of myocardial ischemia
            3. Rapid shallow breathing index (RSBI) < 105 [AND] maximum inspiratory pressure (MIP) < -20 cm H20
       2. Clinical criteria :
            1. [NOT] electrocardiographic signs of myocardial ischemia [AND] absence of vasoactive drugs, or vital signs are stable with using vasoactive drugs
            2. No vasoactive drugs, or vital signs are stable with using vasoactive drugs [AND] heart rate <140/min
            3. Hemoglobin >8 g/dL [AND] temperature <38°C
            4. [NOT] need for sedatives, or mental status are stable with sedatives [AND] presence of respiratory stimulus, and appropriate spontaneous cough
            5. [NOT] excessive tracheobronchial secretions

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] tracheostomy status [OR] decision to stop life-supportive therapies before enrollment
