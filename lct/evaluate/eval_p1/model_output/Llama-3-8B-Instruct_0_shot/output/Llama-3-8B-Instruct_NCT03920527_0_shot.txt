Inclusion Criteria: includes presence of all the following:
  -  one or more clinical symptoms [AND] persistent cough, recurrent hemoptysis, weight loss, malaise, fever, and dyspnea for ≥3 months
  -  slowly progressive or persistent radiological findings [AND] one or more cavities and surrounding fibrosis, infiltrates, consolidation, with or without fungal ball or progressive pleural thickening on computed tomography (CT) of the thorax
  -  immunological [OR] microbiological evidence of Aspergillus infection [AND] A.fumigatus-specific IgG >27 mgA/L or positive Aspergillus precipitins, and
  -  exclusion of other pulmonary disorders with similar presentation [NOT] patients with other forms of pulmonary aspergillosis (allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis, chronic necrotizing aspergillosis, and angio-invasive aspergillosis).

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  failure to provide informed consent [NOT] patients who have provided informed consent
  -  patients on immunosuppressive drugs, intake of prednisolone (or equivalent) >10 mg for at least 3 weeks or a diagnosis of human immunodeficiency virus syndrome [NOT] patients who are not on immunosuppressive drugs or have not taken prednisolone (or equivalent) >10 mg for at least 3 weeks and do not have a diagnosis of human immunodeficiency virus syndrome
  -  intake antifungal azoles for >3 weeks in the preceding six months [NOT] patients who have not taken antifungal azoles for >3 weeks in the preceding six months
  -  subjects with active pulmonary infection due to mycobacterium tuberculosis or mycobacteria other than tuberculosis (MOTT) [NOT] subjects who do not have active pulmonary infection due to mycobacterium tuberculosis or mycobacteria other than tuberculosis (MOTT)
  -  subjects with others forms of pulmonary aspergillosis (allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis, chronic necrotizing aspergillosis and angio-invasive aspergillosis) [NOT] subjects who do not have other forms of pulmonary aspergillosis (allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis, chronic necrotizing aspergillosis and angio-invasive aspergillosis)
  -  pregnancy [NOT] patients who are not pregnant
