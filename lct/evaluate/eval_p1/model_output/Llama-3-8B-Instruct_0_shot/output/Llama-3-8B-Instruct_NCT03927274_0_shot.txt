Inclusion Criteria:
  - Histologically confirmed diagnosis of supratentorial WHO Grade III [OR] IV Glioma [AND] that has undergone surgical biopsy or resection followed by adjuvant chemoradiotherapy, [AND] that has evidence of recurrence or progression based on imaging studies [AND] a stereotactic biopsy is indicated for confirmation of recurrence/progression.
  - 18 years of age [OR] older
  - Karnofsky Performance Status 70-100 [AND] adequate organ function as indicated in protocol
  - MRI demonstration of a stereotactically accessible enhancing [OR] predominantly non-enhancing mass [AND] that does not require resection to relieve clinically significant mass effect
  - Participant understands the procedures [AND] agrees to comply with the study requirements by providing written informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Participant is [NOT] mentally or legally incapacitated at the time of the study
  - Known HIV(+) [OR] has been diagnosed with AIDS
  - Participation in another investigational drug study in the prior 4 weeks [AND] radiation or chemotherapy within 4 weeks of enrollment
  - Positive pregnancy test in a female [AND] patient, in the opinion of the investigator, is likely to be poorly compliant
  - Diffuse subependymal [OR] Cerebral Spinal Fluid (CSF) disease
  - Tumors involving the cerebellum [AND] tumor enhancement involving both hemispheres
  - Active infection requiring treatment [OR] unexplained febrile illness
  - Systemic diseases associated with unacceptable anesthesia or operative risk [AND] inability to undergo magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)
