Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - singleton pregnant women [AND] in vertex presentation
  - GDM that is controlled with only diet [AND] exercise
  - at 37 weeks 0 days [AND] 37 weeks 6 days of gestation
  - more than 18 years old [AND] have no other contraindications to vaginal delivery [NOT] prior caesarean section or myomectomy.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - prior caesarean section [OR] myomectomy
  - any known contraindications to vaginal delivery [NOT] singleton pregnant women
  - uncertain gestational age [NOT] known to the investigator
  - non reassuring foetal wellbeing necessitating delivery [NOT] maternal pregnancy-related disease necessitating delivery (any hypertensive disorder, cardiac disease, renal insufficiency, immune diseases, et al.)
  - placenta previa, accreta, vasa previa [NOT] known to the investigator
  - known foetal anomaly [NOT] unknown to the investigator
  - negative reproductive history [NOT] known to the investigator
  - ruptured membranes [OR] known oligohydramnios (defined as AFI < 5 or MVP < 2 ) before 37 weeks 6 days of gestation
  - fetal growth restriction, defined as EFW < 10th percentile [NOT] known to the investigator
  - known HIV positivity because of modified delivery plan [NOT] unknown to the investigator
  - signs of labor (regular painful contractions with cervical change) before 37 weeks 6 days of gestation [NOT] known to the investigator
