Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Any subject newly implanted [AND] according to the most recent guidelines from the European Society of Cardiology (ESC) for less than 10 days with: 
       -  a Single Chamber (SR) [OR] a Dual Chamber (DR) pacemaker from Microport CRM S.r.l. 
       -  any right atrial [AND] /or right ventricular XFINE lead 
  2. Have reviewed, signed, and dated the informed consent [NOT] under any circumstances

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Included in another clinical study [NOT] that could confound the results of this study such as studies involving intra-cardiac device
  2. Contraindication to a maximum single dose of 310 µg Dexamethasone Sodium Phosphate (DSP) [NOT] under any circumstances
  3. Tricuspid valvular disease [OR] tricuspid replacement heart valve (mechanical or tissue) only for subjects with DR pacemaker [NOT] under any circumstances
  4. Active myocarditis [OR] pocket [AND] /or lead infection
  5. Age less than 18 years old [OR] under guardianship [OR] kept in detention
  6. Life expectancy less than 1 year [NOT] under any circumstances
  7. Known pregnancy [OR] women breastfeeding [OR] in childbearing age without an adequate contraceptive method
  8. Be unavailable for the scheduled follow-up associated with this clinical study [OR] refusal to cooperate
