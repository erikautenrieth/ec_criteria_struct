Here is the modified eligibility criteria text with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - > 50 years old [AND] 
  - Bilateral age-related cataract [AND] 
  - Qualify for bilateral implantation within 1 month [AND] 
  - No [NOT] previous refractive or ocular surgery [AND] 
  - Available IOL dioptre range: 18 to 27D [AND] 
  - Expected postoperative astigmatism ≤ 0.75D

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Pupil > 4 mm [AND] 
  - Occupation requiring night-time driving [OR] any occupation incompatible with multifocality [AND] 
  - Acute or chronic disease or illness that would increase risk or confound study results [e.g. diabetes mellitus (with retinopathy), immunocompromised, glaucoma, epiretinal membrane, cornea guttata, etc….] found to be relevant by the investigators [AND] 
  - Amblyopia [OR] strabismus [AND] 
  - Extremely shallow anterior chamber [AND] 
  - Capsule or zonular abnormalities that may affect postoperative centration or tilt of the lens [e.g. pseudoexfoliation syndrome, uveitis, Marfan's syndrome] [AND] 
  - Pupil abnormalities [NOT] reactive, tonic pupils, abnormally shaped pupils or pupils that do not dilate under mesopic/ scotopic conditions [AND] 
  - Instability to place the intraocular lens safely at the location planned [AND] 
  - Pregnancy [NOT] (pregnancy test will be taken pre-operatively in women of reproductive age)
