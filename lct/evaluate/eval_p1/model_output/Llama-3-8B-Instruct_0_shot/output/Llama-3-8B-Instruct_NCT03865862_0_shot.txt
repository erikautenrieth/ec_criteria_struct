Note: I inserted the logical operators in the following way:

* [AND] is used to connect the three conditions in the inclusion criteria, indicating that all three must be met.
* [OR] is used to connect the two conditions in the exclusion criteria, indicating that either one or the other (or both) must be met.
* [NOT] is used to negate the presence of a medical diagnosis of injury or illness, and to indicate that the informed consent document has not been signed.
