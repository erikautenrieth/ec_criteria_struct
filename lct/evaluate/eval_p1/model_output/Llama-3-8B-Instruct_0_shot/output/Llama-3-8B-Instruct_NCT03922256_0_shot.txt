Inclusion
  - Age > 18 years [AND] 
  - Unilateral fracture of distal radius without misalignment [AND] (dorsal angulation <10° [AND] axial radial shortening <5mm) [AND] <2mm intraarticular stepoff;
  - Independent in activities of daily living [AND] 
  - Capable [AND] willing of signing informed consent [NOT] patients with cognitive impairment.

Exclusion
  - Children [NOT] under the age of 18 [OR] 
  - Fracture of the contralateral arm [OR] 
  - Other fractures of the ipsilateral arm [OR] 
  - Open fractures [OR] 
  - Fractures that require reduction.

Note: I inserted [NOT] before "under the age of 18" to indicate that children under 18 are excluded, and I inserted [OR] between the exclusion criteria to indicate that any of these conditions are sufficient to exclude a patient.
