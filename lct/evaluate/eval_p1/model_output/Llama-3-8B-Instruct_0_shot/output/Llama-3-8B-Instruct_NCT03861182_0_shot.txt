Inclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
- Over 18 years of age [AND] without any systemic inflammatory disease
Neonates:
  -  Aged between 1h of life and 28 days of life [AND] Born beyond 33 Week of Amenorrhea + 1day and 2kg of birth weight [AND] Hospitalized in the medical-surgical centre of Pediatrics of the hospital of Strasbourg [AND] For whom a blood sample was prescribed as part of their routine care
Exclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
  -  [NOT] Systemic inflammatory disease [OR] Transient inflammatory state [OR] Any drug that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h preceding the sampling
Neonates:
  -  [NOT] Risk of anemia < 7g/DL [OR] Current anticoagulant treatment
