Inclusion Criteria:
  -  clinical suspicion of urinary tract infection (UTI) defined by the presence of at least one of these signs: fever > 38°C [AND] sus-pubic pain [OR] dysuria [OR] pollakiuria [OR] urinary burns [OR] macroscopic hematuria [OR] acute urinary retention [OR] pain with the rectal examination [OR] confusion
  -  And urinary analysis with leukocyturia > 10 / mm3 [AND] bacteriuria > 10^3 CFU/mL with ESBL producing enterobacteriaceae [AND] resistance associated with fluoroquinolones (FQ) [AND] cotrimoxazole (CTX) [NOT] but sensitive to fosfomycin.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] allergy to fosfomycin [AND] trometamol
  -  Presence of material in the urinary tract
  -  Severe immunosuppression
  -  Chronic prostatitis
  -  Prostate abscess
  -  Acute pyelonephritis
  -  Hemodynamic instability
  -  Chronic renal failure (clearance <60 mL/min)
  -  Prior antibiotic therapy, with an antibiotic [NOT] sensitive on the antibiotic susceptibility test [EXCEPT] amoxicillin - clavulanic acid, cefixime, nitrofurantoin.
  -  Co-treatment with metoclopramide
