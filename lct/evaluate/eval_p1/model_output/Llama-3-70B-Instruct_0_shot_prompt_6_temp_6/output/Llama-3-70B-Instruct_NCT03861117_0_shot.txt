Here is the rewritten eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], and [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient is 18 years old OR more
  -  Patient is intubated AND ventilated in intensive care unit for more than 24 hours
  -  Patient is ready for weaning evaluation
  -  Patient has experienced failure of a first T-piece SBT

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient has chronic neuromuscular disease
  -  Patient has Guillain-Barré Syndrome
  -  Patient has central nervous system disease WITH consciousness disorder
  -  Patient has tracheostomy
  -  Patient has chronic disease WITH life expectancy less than 1 year
  -  Patient is pregnant OR breast feeding
  -  Patient is under withholding life support regarding a reintubation
  -  Patient is a prisoner OR patient interned in psychiatric hospital
  -  Patient is under guardianship
  -  Patient has a language barrier
  -  Patient lacks medical insurance
  -  Patient lacks consent (or the next of kin lacks consent where appropriate)
  -  Patient is under an exclusion period after enrollment in another research study
  -  NOT meeting any of the above inclusion criteria
