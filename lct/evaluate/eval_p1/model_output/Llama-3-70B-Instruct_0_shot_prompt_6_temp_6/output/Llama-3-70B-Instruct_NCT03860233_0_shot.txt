Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Men OR women 18 years AND older.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  subjects NOT willing to OR otherwise unable to use CPAP for treatment of OSA.
  -  Presence of other sleep disorders.
  -  Pregnant OR breastfeeding women.
  -  Women of child-bearing age (WOCBA) NOT willing OR unable to use an accepted method to avoid pregnancy for the entire duration of the study.
  -  Prisoners OR subjects who are involuntarily incarcerated.
  -  Subjects who are compulsorily detained OR treatment of either a psychiatric OR physical (i.e. infectious disease) illness.
  -  Patients unable to give consent because of a language barrier, OR other reason.

Note: I've inserted the logical operators as follows:

* AND: to indicate that both conditions must be true
* OR: to indicate that at least one of the conditions must be true
* NOT: to indicate that the condition must be false
