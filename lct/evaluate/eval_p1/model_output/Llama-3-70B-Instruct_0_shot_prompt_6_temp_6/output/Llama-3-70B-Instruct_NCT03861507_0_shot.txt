Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Male AND aged 18 years or older
  -  Willing to provide written consent
  -  Pathologically confirmed prostate cancer on previous biopsy
  -  Suitable for high-dose-rate brachytherapy for treatment as standard of care AND consenting to high-dose-rate brachytherapy.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  NOT (Previous radiotherapy to the prostate)

Note: I inserted the logical operators as follows:

* AND was used to connect two conditions that must both be true for the participant to be eligible.
* NOT was used to indicate that the presence of a certain condition (previous radiotherapy to the prostate) makes the participant ineligible.
