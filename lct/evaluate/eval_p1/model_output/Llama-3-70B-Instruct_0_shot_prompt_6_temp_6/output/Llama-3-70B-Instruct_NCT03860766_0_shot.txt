Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Handball players AND
  -  Healthy AND
  -  (Participation of at least one official match in the last year) AND (attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season).

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  (musculoskeletal injury in the last three months) OR
  -  cardiovascular diseases OR
  -  (use of anabolic, anti-inflammatory and analgesic drugs 72 hours before the evaluations) OR
  -  (use of alcohol and illicit drugs during the collection period) OR
  -  NOT (all of the above exclusion criteria are false).

Note: The last exclusion criterion is a bit tricky, as it's implied that if any of the previous exclusion criteria are true, the participant is excluded. The NOT operator is used to negate the entire exclusion criteria list, meaning that if all the exclusion criteria are false, the participant is not excluded.
