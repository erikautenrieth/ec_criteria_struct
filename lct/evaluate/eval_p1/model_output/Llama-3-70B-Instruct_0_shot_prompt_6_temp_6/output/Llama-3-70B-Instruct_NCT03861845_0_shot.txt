Here is the revised text with the logical operators inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  18 years of age OR older
  -  speak English AND
  -  be prescribed to take two OR more medications per day, AND manage their own medications.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  have a significant cognitive impairment OR
  -  unwilling to use a novel pillbox OR
  -  unable to meet with the research team AND NOT willing to make alternative arrangements.

Note: I added "AND NOT" to the last exclusion criterion to make it clear that the participant must be both unable to meet with the research team and unwilling to make alternative arrangements to be excluded.
