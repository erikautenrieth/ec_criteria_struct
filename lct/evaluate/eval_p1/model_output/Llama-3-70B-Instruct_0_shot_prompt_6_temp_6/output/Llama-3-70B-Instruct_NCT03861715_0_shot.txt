Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age 20-34 years;
  -  body mass index (BMI) 18-29kg/m2;
  -  regular menstrual cycle of 26-35 days AND
  -  AMH levels age appropriate (≥2.3 ng/ml) AND
  -  early follicular- phase serum concentration of FSH within normal limits (1-12 IU/l).

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  women with diabetes AND other metabolic disease OR
  -  women with hereditary genetic diseases OR
  -  women with heart disease OR QT prolongation OR heart failure OR
  -  elevated liver enzymes OR liver failure OR hepatitis OR
  -  women with inflammatory OR autoimmune disease OR
  -  abnormal karyotype OR
  -  endometriosis stage III/IV OR
  -  history of recurrent miscarriage OR
  -  severe OHSS in a previous stimulation cycle OR any contraindication for the use of gonadotrophins.

Note: I inserted AND between the inclusion criteria to indicate that all conditions must be met, and OR between the exclusion criteria to indicate that any one of the conditions is sufficient for exclusion.
