Here are the eligibility criteria with the logical operators inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients aged from 18 to 80 years AND
  -  Obese (BMI ≥ 30kg/m²) AND
  -  Referred by GPs to the medical analysis laboratory for usual biological assessment AND
  -  Patients with no respiratory follow-up already in place AND
  -  Bicarbonate - Concentration ≥ 27 mmol/L AND
  -  STOP-BANG score ≥ 3

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Acute disease or recently diagnosed chronic disease (< 2 months) OR
  -  Hospitalization for respiratory, metabolic or cardiovascular event (< 2 months) OR
  -  Renal insufficiency stage 4 or 5, or autoimmune disease, or viral hepatitis, or cirrhosis OR
  -  Pregnant women OR
  -  Patients already included in an interventional study (end of the study < 1 month) OR
  -  NOT (all of the above inclusion criteria)
