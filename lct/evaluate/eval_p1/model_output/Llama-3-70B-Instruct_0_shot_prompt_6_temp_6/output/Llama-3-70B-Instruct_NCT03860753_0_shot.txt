Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], and [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Treatment-seeking individuals diagnosed with AUD according to the diagnostic statistical manual 5 (DSM-5)
  - Fluent in English
  - Past month excessive alcohol use (>7 drinks/week for women, >14 drinks/week for men, >3 drinks/occasion for women >4 drinks/occasion for men) [AND]
  - Baseline Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A) or Perceived Stress Scale (PSS) core indicative of mild to moderate anxiety (score 8 to 23) [OR] moderate stress (score 14 to 26), respectively [AND]
  - Increase in alcohol craving following the baseline stress reactivity assessment.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Individuals will be excluded if they exhibit severe scores on the HAM-A, PSS, or post-traumatic (PTSD) checklist (PCL-5) at the discretion of the admitting physician [OR]
  - Physical dependence on alcohol [AND] Alcohol Use Disorders Inventory (AUDIT) score indicative of severe alcohol dependence (≥13 for women, ≥15 for men) [OR]
  - Greater than mild substance use disorder on drugs other than alcohol, nicotine, and marijuana [OR]
  - Contraindications for taking pioglitazone; medical conditions contraindicating pioglitazone pharmacotherapy [OR] taking contraindicated medications [OR]
  - Pregnant, nursing, or planning on becoming pregnant during the course of the study [OR]
  - Having any other illness, condition, or use of medications, which in the opinion of the PI and/or admitting physician would preclude safe and/or successful completion of the study [NOT] eligible for participation.
