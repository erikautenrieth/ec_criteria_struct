Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Subject is greater than 18 years of age
  2. Subject is willing AND able to provide informed consent
  3. Subject is scheduled to undergo elective rotator cuff surgery
  4. Subject has consented to an interscalene block

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Opioid dependence
  2. Any subject diagnosed with a chronic pain condition WHERE daily opioid use is needed
  3. Anatomical malformation, which in the investigator's opinion may interfere with the placement of the nerve block
  4. Raynaud's disease diagnosis
  5. Vasculopathy
  6. Patient refusal OR (1 OR 2 OR 3 OR 4 OR 5) NOT eligible for the study

Note: I inserted the logical operators as follows:

* AND: to indicate that both conditions must be true (e.g., willing AND able to provide informed consent)
* WHERE: to indicate a conditional statement (e.g., daily opioid use is needed)
* OR: to indicate that at least one of the conditions must be true (e.g., patient refusal OR any of the exclusion criteria 1-5)
* NOT: to indicate the negation of a condition (e.g., NOT eligible for the study)
