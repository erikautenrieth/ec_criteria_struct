Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Serum Glucose > 100 mg/dl AND Serum Glucose < 126 mg/dl
  -  (Waist C > 89 cm AND Sex = Female) OR (Waist C > 102 cm AND Sex = Male)
  -  (Serum HDL-C < 50 mg/dl AND Sex = Female) OR (Serum HDL-C < 40 mg/dl AND Sex = Male)
  -  Serum TG > 150 mg/dl
  -  (BP > 130 mmHg AND BP < 140 mmHg) OR (BP > 85 mmHg AND BP < 90 mmHg)
  -  Serum Endotoxin > 25 EU/ml

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  NOT (Unstable body mass, defined as ±2 kg over prior 3-mo)
  -  Vegetarian OR Food allergies OR lactose intolerance
  -  User of dietary supplements OR probiotics (within past 1-mo)
  -  (Pregnancy OR lactation OR changes in birth control) AND (within 6-mo)
  -  Any gastrointestinal disorders OR Chronic diarrhea
  -  Smoker OR Excess alcohol (>2 drinks/d)
  -  Excess aerobic exercise (>5 h/wk)
  -  Recent antibiotic use OR Recent anti-inflammatory agent use
  -  BP > 140 mmHg OR BP > 90 mmHg
