Inclusion Criteria:
  -  1. diagnosed as HIV positive;
  -  2. initiate antiretroviral therapy on the day of recruitment;
  -  3. was infected through male same-sex behavior;
  -  4. has access to internet on a personal mobile phone AND has a Wechat account;
  -  5. uses Wechat daily;
  -  6. willing to provide written informed consent;

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  1. under severe physical condition OR mental condition which precludes study participation;
  -  2. being an inpatient of the hospital AND NOT willing to participate in the study;

Note: I inserted the logical operators as follows:

* AND: to indicate that both conditions must be met (e.g., has access to internet AND has a Wechat account)
* OR: to indicate that at least one of the conditions must be met (e.g., under severe physical OR mental condition)
* NOT: to indicate the opposite of a condition (e.g., NOT willing to participate in the study)
