Here is the revised text with the logical operators inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age ≥ 18 years old
  -  Confirmed diagnosis of perianal Crohn's disease
  -  On biologics OR will start biologics

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients who have perianal fistula due to causes other than Crohn's disease
  -  Fistulation into other organs, including bladder AND intestine
  -  Patients who have allergic reaction OR contraindications to anti-TNF
  -  Patients who have active cancer
  -  Patients who have contraindications for MRI
  -  Known pregnancy
  -  NOT willing to participate in the tight monitoring arm (for patients who will be recruited to the control arm for comparison)

Note that I inserted the logical operators as follows:

* OR: to indicate that either condition is sufficient (e.g., on biologics or will start biologics)
* AND: to indicate that both conditions must be met (e.g., fistulation into other organs, including bladder and intestine)
* NOT: to indicate negation (e.g., not willing to participate in the tight monitoring arm)
