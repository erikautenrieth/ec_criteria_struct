Inclusion Criteria:
  -  A minimum of 18 years old
  -  Experienced user (≥ 6 months) of a MED-EL cochlear implant (CI; C40+ and later model) AND
  -  Experienced user of a MED-EL SONNET audio processor (≥ 6 months) AND
  -  Post-lingual onset of bilateral severe to profound sensory-neural hearing loss AND
  -  Unilateral CI user AND
  -  A minimum of 10 active electrodes AND
  -  A minimum of 40% speech recognition in the Freiburg Monosyllables test in quiet at 65 dB sound pressure level (SPL; at the last time tested) AND
  -  Fluent in German (the language of the test centre) AND
  -  Signed and dated Informed Consent Form before the start of any study-specific procedure.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Lack of compliance with any inclusion criteria OR
  -  CI user with contralateral hearing equal to or better than 60 dB (PTA measured at 500, 1000, and 2000Hz) OR
  -  User with electric-acoustic stimulation (EAS; user of an EAS audio processor) OR
  -  Implanted with C40X and C40C OR
  -  Implanted with an Auditory Brainstem Implant or Split electrode array OR
  -  Known allergic reactions to components of the investigational medical device OR
  -  Unstable psychological status OR
  -  Anything that, in the opinion of the Investigator, would place the subject at increased risk or preclude the subject's full compliance with or completion of the study.

Note: I've assumed that the "AND" operator is used for the inclusion criteria, as all the conditions must be met to be eligible. I've used the "OR" operator for the exclusion criteria, as any one of the conditions is enough to exclude the subject.
