Here is the revised text with the logical operators inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. inflammatory bowel disease
  2. on methotrexate at appropriate dosing AND
  3. normal folate levels at onset of study AND
  4. treatment with folic acid AND
  5. ages 2-21 years

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. abnormal folate levels OR
  2. age > 21 OR age < 2

Note: I assumed that the age range in the exclusion criteria is meant to be "not within the range of 2-21", which is equivalent to "either less than 2 or greater than 21". If this is not the case, please let me know and I'll revise accordingly.

Alternatively, you could use NOT to express the exclusion criteria as:

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. NOT normal folate levels
  2. NOT (ages 2-21 years)

This would read as "not having normal folate levels" and "not being within the age range of 2-21 years".
