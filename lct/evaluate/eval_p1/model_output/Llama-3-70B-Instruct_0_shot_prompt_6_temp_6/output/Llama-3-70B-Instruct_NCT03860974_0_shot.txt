Inclusion Criteria:
  -  undergoing unilateral or bilateral breast surgery WITH at least moderate postoperative pain anticipated
  -  analgesic plan includes a single-injection peripheral nerve block(s) AND 
  -  age 18 years or older

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  morbid obesity as defined by a body mass index > 40 (BMI=weight in kg / [height in meters]2) OR 
  -  renal insufficiency (preoperative creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL) OR 
  -  chronic opioid use (daily use within the 2 weeks prior to surgery AND duration of use > 4 weeks) OR 
  -  history of opioid abuse OR 
  -  any comorbidity which results in moderate or severe functional limitation OR inability to communicate with the investigators or hospital staff OR 
  -  pregnancy OR 
  -  planned regional analgesic with perineural catheter placement OR 
  -  incarceration

Note: I inserted the logical operators as follows:

* AND: to indicate that both conditions must be true
* OR: to indicate that at least one of the conditions must be true
* NOT: not used in this case, as there were no negations in the original text.
