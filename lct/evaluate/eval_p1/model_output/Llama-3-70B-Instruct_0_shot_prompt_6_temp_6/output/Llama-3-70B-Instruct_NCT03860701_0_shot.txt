Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with NEC (stages I-III Bell's Classification) AND
  -  Weight greater or equal to 750 g AND
  -  Gestational age < 36 weeks AND
  -  Patients whose parents consent to participate in the study.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with major congenital anomalies OR
  -  No antecedent of limb ischemia/limb thrombotic events OR
  -  No antecedent diagnosis of occlusive arterial or venous thrombosis OR
  -  Hemodynamic instability.

Note: I've inserted AND operators for the inclusion criteria, as all conditions must be met for a patient to be included. For the exclusion criteria, I've inserted OR operators, as a patient is excluded if any one of these conditions is true.
