Inclusion Criteria
  -  Age ≥ 18 years old
  -  Patients willing to participate  [AND] provide an informed consent
  -  Patients undergoing an elective surgical procedure that requires use of non-depolarizing NMBA agents administered intraoperatively.
Exclusion Criteria
  -  Patients with unilateral disorders, such as stroke, carpal tunnel syndrome, broken wrist with nerve damage, Dupuytren contracture, [OR] or any similar wrist injury.
  -  Patients with systemic neuromuscular diseases such as myasthenia gravis
  -  Patients with significant organ dysfunction that can significantly affect pharmacokinetics of neuromuscular blocking  [AND] reversal agents, i.e., severe renal impairment [OR] end-stage liver disease.
