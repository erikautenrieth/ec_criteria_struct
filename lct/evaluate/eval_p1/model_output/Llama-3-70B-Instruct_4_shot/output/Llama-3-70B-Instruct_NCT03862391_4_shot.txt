Inclusion Criteria:
 - ASA physical status 1 [OR] 2, underwent gynicological laparotomic surgery under general anesthesia, [AND] followed up in PACU(postoperative care unit)

Exclusion Criteria:
 - ASA 3 [OR] 4, [OR] mental retardation, [OR] psychiatric disease, [OR] history of malignant hyperthermia in patient [OR] her family, [OR] neurological disease, [OR] morbid obesity, [OR] history of asthma, [AND] follow-up in the intensive care unit.
