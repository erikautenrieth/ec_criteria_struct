Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with Spondyloarthritis (SpA) meeting the criteria of Amor [OR] and / or Assessement of spondyloarthritis International Society (ASAS)
 - Indication of standard radiography for the follow-up of the patients
Exclusion Criteria:
 - the presence of a contraindication to the performance of conventional radiography (CR) [OR] or EOS imaging
 - any disease affecting cognition
 - Patients who have had surgery on the spine [AND] or hip
