Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age 18-70
  -  BMI: 25  [AND] 45 kg/m2
  -  Regular internet access
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Currently pregnant,  [OR] <6 months post-partum,  [OR] nursing,  [OR] planning to become pregnant within the next 12 months.
