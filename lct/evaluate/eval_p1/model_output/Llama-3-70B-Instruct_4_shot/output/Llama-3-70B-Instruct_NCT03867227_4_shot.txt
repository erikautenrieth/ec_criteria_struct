Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient with paroxysmal  [OR] persistent  [OR] permanent atrial fibrillation
  -  CHA2DS2VASc score ≥2 for male  [AND] and ≥3 for female
  -  Without contraindications to anticoagulation therapy
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  LAA thrombosis
  -  mechanical prosthetic heart valve
  -  moderate  [OR] severe mitral valve stenosis
