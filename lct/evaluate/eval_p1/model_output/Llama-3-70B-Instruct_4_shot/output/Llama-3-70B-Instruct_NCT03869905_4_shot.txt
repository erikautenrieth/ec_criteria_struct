Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Ulcerative colitis with confirmed diagnosis by histology  [AND] and endoscopy  [AND] in stable remission for 3 months or more without therapy  [OR] or with a maintenance therapy (except steroids  [NOT] and antibiotics  [NOT] for 3 months)
  -  A negative pregnancy test
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History or diagnosis of any of the following conditions: Chron's disease, bleeding disorders, gastrointestinal or colonic malignancy, Kidney disease, including kidney "stones"  [OR] or hypercalcemia, Coagulopathy  [OR] hereditary hemorrhagic disorders  [OR] or receiving therapeutic doses of Coumadin  [OR] or heparin.
  -  Ingested certain medications (Calcium, Vitamin D, fiber supplements  [OR] and Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs - NSAIDs) within 30 days of study start.
