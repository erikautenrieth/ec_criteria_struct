Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adults patients ≥ 18 years of age with CD with at least colonic involvement, [OR] UC, [OR] non-IBD controls who have been referred for colonoscopy for clinical reasons. The clinical reasons may include colorectal cancer screening, [OR] surveillance, [OR] diagnostic for CD or UC flare, [OR] gastrointestinal symptoms.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnant patients.
 - Patients with known current colorectal cancer, [OR] infectious colitis, [OR] diverticulitis, [OR] microscopic colitis.
 - Patients who have undergone surgery involving the cecum [OR] rectum.
