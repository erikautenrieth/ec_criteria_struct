Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients scheduled for elective laparoscopic colorectal surgery at UWMC [AND] requiring inpatient stay
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients on chronic pain mediations equaling [OR] exceeding > 25 morphine daily equivalents
 - Patients allergic to bupivacaine
 - Patients with ASA status IV [OR] V [OR] VI
 - Patients unable to consent
 - Patients that are pregnant
 - Patients that are incarcerated
 - Patients receiving procedures in addition to laparoscopic colorectal procedure
 - Patients on systemic anticoagulation [NOT] precluding them from regional blocks
