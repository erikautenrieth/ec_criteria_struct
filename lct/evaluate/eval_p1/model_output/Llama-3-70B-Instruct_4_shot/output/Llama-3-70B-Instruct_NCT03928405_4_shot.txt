Inclusion Criteria:
 - Having mild [OR] moderate severity of AD
 - Lack of communication difficulties [AND] or problems
 - Volunteering to participate in the study
 - Cerebrovascular event,
 - Having disturbing the balance [AND] coordination of neurological disease such as Parkinson, MS,
 - Mini Mental Test Score, 18 to 24 points from being scored
 - being 65-80 aged.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients diagnosed with dementia (infection, vascular, hematological diseases) [OR] patients who could not be contacted during the follow-up period [OR] who did not come to control, cardiac [AND] cerebrovascular event,
 - Endocrine disorder [OR] fluid-electrolyte imbalance [OR] infection
 - Patients with malignancy [AND] malignancy, chemotherapy [OR] radiotherapy,
 - Patients with delirium [OR] depression
 - Those with protective sensation loss (those with Semmes-Weinstein Monofilament Thickness 4.56 and above)
 - patients with lower [OR] upper extremity amputations at any level
