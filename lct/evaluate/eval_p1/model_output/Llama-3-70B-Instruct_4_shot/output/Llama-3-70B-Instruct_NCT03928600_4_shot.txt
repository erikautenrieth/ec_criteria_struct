Inclusion Criteria:
 - full-term (≥ 37 weeks of gestation)
 - singleton
 - vertex-presenting gestations
 - with no contraindication to vaginal delivery
 - intact membranes
 - Bishop score < 7 [AND] and cervical dilation ≤2 cm
Exclusion Criteria:
 - contraindication for misoprostol
 - history of previous caesarean
 - rupture of membranes
 - fetal or maternal morbidities ([OR] fetal major abnormalities, [OR] FIGO definition of pathological CTG, [OR] HELLP syndrome, [OR] preeclampsia [OR] hypertension with severe features, [OR] fetal growth restriction)
