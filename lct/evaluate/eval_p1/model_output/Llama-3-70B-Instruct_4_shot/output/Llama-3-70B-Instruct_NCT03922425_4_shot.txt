Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age 18-65
 - Current service users, with severe [AND] enduring mental disorder,
 - Severe Mental Illnesses - bipolar disorder [OR] severe depression [OR] schizophrenia
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who [NOT] consent to their data being collected
 - Patients with acute somatic disorders
 - Patients who are under the age of 18 at the start of the study.
