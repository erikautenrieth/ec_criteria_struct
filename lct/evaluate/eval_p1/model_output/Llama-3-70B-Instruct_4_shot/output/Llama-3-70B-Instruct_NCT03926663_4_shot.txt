Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients aged from 18- 60 years
  -  ASA physical status I
  -  Undergoing septoplasty
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with cardiovascular disease (congestive heart failure  [OR]  coronary artery disease  [OR]  hypertension)
  -  History of renal  [OR]  hepatic insufficiency
  -  Endocrinal Diseases
