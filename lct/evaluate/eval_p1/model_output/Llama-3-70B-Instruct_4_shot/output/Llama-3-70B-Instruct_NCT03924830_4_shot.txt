Inclusion Criteria:
 - All patients must have at only 20 teeth in function, [AND] must have at only 3 carious occlusal or proximo-occlusal lesions, [OR] old restorations that need to be changed, in different teeth. Those lesions must be more than 2 mm deep, [AND] should have exposed dentin, [AND] must have at only 50% of margins in enamel. Teeth should [NOT] present periodontal mobility.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Volunteers with periodontal disease, [OR] with gingival bleeding, [OR] use of anti-inflammatory drugs in the last 30 days.
