Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Pregnant women who were diagnosed with GDM from 13  [AND] to 32.6 gestational weeks
  -  18 years old  [AND] older
  -  Singleton pregnancy
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Women with pre-gestational diabetes mellitus
  -  GDM diagnosed ≥ 33 gestational weeks
  -  Women using prophylactic antimicrobial treatment
  -  Immunocompromised women
  -  Multiple pregnancy
  -  Women taking oral probiotic products  [AND] who refuse to stop the consumption (food-containing probiotics is [NOT] considered an exclusion criterion).
