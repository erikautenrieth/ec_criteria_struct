Inclusion Criteria:
 - Healthy men [OR] and women
 - Age ≥20 [AND] and ≤40 years
 - BMI ≥18,5 [AND] and ≤27,5
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Chronic diseases (cardiac, metabolic, liver) [OR] including a family history of congenital Long QT syndrome.
 - QTc values >440 ms (men) [OR] and >450 ms (women)
 - Clinical important [AND] and/or symptomatic bradycardia
 - Regular medication [NOT] (contraceptive pills allowed)
 - Pregnancy [OR] and breastfeeding
