Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Woman ≥ 18 years old
  -  undergoing laparoscopic surgery for endometriosis  [OR] uterine myoma's  [OR] hysterectomy  [OR] colpopexy.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Women < 18 years old
  -  Males
  -  Pregnancy
  -  Conditions associated with chronic pain, such as peripheral neuropathy  [OR] pathology of the vertebral column  [OR] osteo-articular disease.
  -  Conditions causing acute pain  [AND] e.g. abdominal trauma
