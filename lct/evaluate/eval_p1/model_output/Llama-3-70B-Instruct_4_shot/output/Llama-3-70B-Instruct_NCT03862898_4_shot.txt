Inclusion Criteria:
  -  patients with lumbar pain present for more than 12 weeks, [AND] 
  -  pain during changing of the position, [AND]
  -  radicular pain.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Cauda equina syndrome, [OR]
  -  ankylosing spondylitis, [OR]
  -  thoracic deformities (pectus carinatum, excavatum), [OR]
  -  spina bifida, [OR]
  -  fractures, [OR]
  -  post-operative spinal conditions, [OR]
  -  diabetes, [OR]
  -  inflammatory processes, [OR]
  -  tumors, [OR]
  -  pregnancy.

Note: The [AND] operator is used in the inclusion criteria to indicate that all three conditions must be met for a patient to be eligible. The [OR] operator is used in the exclusion criteria to indicate that if a patient meets any one of the listed conditions, they are excluded from the study.
