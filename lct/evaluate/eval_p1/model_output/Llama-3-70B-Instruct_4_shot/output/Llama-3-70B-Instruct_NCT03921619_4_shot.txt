Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Greater than or equal to 65 years of age
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Younger than 65 years of age
  -  Pre-existing cognitive impairment  [OR]  pre-existing mobility impairment
  -  Pre-existing symptomatic upper  [OR]  lower extremity disease
  -  [NOT] meeting the inclusion criteria (i.e., not greater than or equal to 65 years of age)
