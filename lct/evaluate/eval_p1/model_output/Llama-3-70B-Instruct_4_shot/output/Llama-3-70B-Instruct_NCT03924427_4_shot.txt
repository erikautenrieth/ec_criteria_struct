Inclusion Criteria:
a. For participants with plaque psoriasis: 
 i. Stable plaque psoriasis for at least 6 months [AND] 
 ii. Moderate to severe disease [AND] 
 iii. Candidate for phototherapy or systemic therapy 
b. Additional protocol-specified inclusion criteria apply for subjects with psoriatic arthritis [OR] erythrodermic psoriasis [OR] generalized pustular psoriasis
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Guttate psoriasis at Screening or Baseline [OR] inverse psoriasis at Screening or Baseline [OR] drug-induced psoriasis at Screening or Baseline
 2. History of recent infection
 3. Prior exposure to BMS-986165 [NOT] naive to BMS-986165
Other protocol defined inclusion/exclusion criteria could apply
