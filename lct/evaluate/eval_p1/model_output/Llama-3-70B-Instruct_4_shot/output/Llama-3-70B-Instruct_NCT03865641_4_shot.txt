Inclusion Criteria:
  -  The patients' burn scars had re-epithelialized after aseptic care  [OR]  skin graft. We included patients aged ≥18 years  [AND]  with a deep partial-thickness (second-degree) burn  [OR]  a full thickness (third-degree) burn to their hands,  [AND]  with joint contracture(hand and wrist),  [AND]  having been transferred to the rehabilitation department after acute burn treatment,  [AND]  less than 6 months since the onset of the burn injury.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  patients who had fourth-degree burns(involving muscles, tendons, and bone injuries)  [OR]  musculoskeletal diseases(fracture, amputation, rheumatoid arthritis, and degenerative joint diseases) in the burned hand,  [OR]  neurological diseases(such as peripheral nerve disorders),  [OR]  preexisting physical  [AND]  psychologic disability (severe aphasia and cognitive impairment that could influence the intervention),  [OR]  severe pain impeding hand rehabilitation.
