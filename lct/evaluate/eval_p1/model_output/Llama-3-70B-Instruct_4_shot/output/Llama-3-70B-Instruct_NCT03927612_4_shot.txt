Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Aged 9-12
  -  English-speaking
  -  Meet DSM-5 criteria for oppositional defiant disorder (ODD)  [OR] conduct disorder (CD)  [OR] Other Specified or Unspecified Disruptive, Impulse-Control, and Conduct Disorder
  -  Right-handed
  -  Estimated full-scale IQ greater than 75
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Bipolar disorder  [OR] any disorder involving psychosis  [OR] pervasive developmental disorders  [OR] current or past substance use disorder  [OR] or current major depressive disorder
  -  History of neurological problems (e.g., epilepsy  [OR] traumatic brain injury)
  -  Contraindications for MRI
