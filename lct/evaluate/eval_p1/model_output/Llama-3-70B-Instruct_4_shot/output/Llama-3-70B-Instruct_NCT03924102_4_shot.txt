Inclusion Criteria:
 - At least 18 years of age.
 - Enrollment in the UAB CMC-ADHF study under the separate UAB approved research protocol (IRB-300000114, PI Rajapreyar)
 - Negative urine [OR] or serum β-hCG test within 48 hours of [11C] acetate administration in women of child bearing potential. Women who are post-menopausal [AND] with at least 1 year since last menses [OR] documented surgical sterilization will [NOT] require pregnancy testing.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Inability to provide informed consent
 - Pregnancy
 - Inability to lie still for the imaging study
 - Weight exceeding the weight limit of the PET imaging table (500 pounds).
