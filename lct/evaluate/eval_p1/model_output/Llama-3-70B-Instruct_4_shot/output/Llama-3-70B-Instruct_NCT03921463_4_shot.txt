Inclusion Criteria:
 - 1) Age: 20 ~35 years old; 2) first parturients registered in the obstetrics department of our hospital; 3) no medical complications before pregnancy, [AND] no UI [AND] POP (according to the standard of POP-Q stage); 4) no obstetric complications; 5) the patients agreed to conduct the study [AND] signed the informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - 1) patients with uncontrolled epilepsy, [OR] central nervous system diseases [OR] mental disorders, whose clinical severity shall be determined by the researcher to affect clinical research compliance; 2) follow up the patients with difficulty.
