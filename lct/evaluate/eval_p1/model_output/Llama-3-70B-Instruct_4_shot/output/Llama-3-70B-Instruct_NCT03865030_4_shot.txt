Inclusion Criteria:
 - Psoriatic patients that are treated in our institute.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients suffering from cardiovascular diseases (peripheral vascular disease [OR] congestive heart failure [OR] status post cerebrovascular accident).
 - Conditions that are harmful to the inner ear (chronic renal failure [OR] history of noise exposure [OR] usage of ototoxic drugs [OR] chronic otitis media [OR] ear surgery in the past [OR] significant head trauma in the past [OR] ear drum perforation).
 - participant that complains of vertigo [OR] dizziness [OR] unsteadiness.
 - Participant who is [OR] was suffering from known inner ear disease: meniere disease [OR] acoustic neurinoma [OR] vestibular neuronitis.
