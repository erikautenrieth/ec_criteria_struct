Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Male  [OR] Female participants providing written informed consent,
  -  ASA grade 1  [TO] 4,
  -  aged over 18
  -  undergoing a VATS procedure  [AND] under General Anaesthesia
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Absence of informed written consent,
  -  pre existing infection at block site,
  -  severe coagulopathy,
  -  allergy to local anaesthesia,
  -  pre existing neurological deficit,
  -  previous history of opiate abuse,
  -  pre existing chronic pain condition,
  -  pre-existing dementia  [BECAUSE] of need to co-operate in completing QoR-15 score day after surgery.

Note: I replaced "AND" with "TO" in the ASA grade range, as it's a range and not a logical conjunction. Also, I replaced "because" with "[BECAUSE]" as it's not a logical operator, but rather an explanation.
