Inclusion Criteria:
 - Female, older than 35 years [AND] Already have mammography, [OR] other standard exams (ultrasound, MRI, etc) [AND] Could participate this trial within 2 weeks after mammography [AND] Have the ability of consent inform.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - During pregnancy [OR] lactation period.
 - Patients that may [NOT] be capable of prone position, such as patients with scapulohumeral periarthritis, [OR] cardiac pacemaker [OR] have recent cardiac surgery.
 - Lymphoma patients that are under therapy.
 - Severe scoliosis.
