Inclusion Criteria:
 - Meets diagnostic criteria for fibromyalgia
 - Has insomnia
 - Have internet access [AND] and a device that can access the web-based program.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Active sleep disorder which is not treated (obstructive sleep apnea, [OR] obesity hypoventilation syndrome)
 - Active psychiatric disorder (Bipolar affective disorder, [OR] anxiety, [OR] depression, [OR] schizophrenia) which is not optimally managed.
 - Chronic fatigue syndrome
 - Morbid obesity
