Inclusion Criteria:
 - American Indian youth aged 13-24 years old who are San Felipe tribal members [AND] living in the Pueblo
 - Endorse suicidal ideation [OR] behaviors on a universal screen by their primary care provider.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Younger than age of 13 years old [OR] older than the age of 24,
 - Not American Indian,
 - Youth who do not receive health services at either of the IHS clinics at the Pueblo of San Felipe,
 - Youth who screen negative for suicidal ideation [AND] and/or behavior,
 - Refuse to participate in the study, [OR] and/or
 - Cognitively unable to provide informed consent as demonstrated by a brief cognitive screen prior to completion of the baseline interview.
