Inclusion Criteria:
 - The selection criteria of the subjects will be as follows:
 1. they need to be off medication [AND] off intake of dietary supplements
 2. they need to have no blood clotting disorders [AND] no dyslipidaemia
Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects with clotting disorders [OR] dyslipidemia [OR] being on medication (i.e. statins) [OR] other supplements during the study will be excluded.
