Inclusion Criteria:
 - All patients with microcytic hypochromic anemia (according to WHO, MCV less than 80 fl [AND] and HB level below the lower limit of normal value specified by age [AND] and gender.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Beta thalassemia major patients.
 - Chronic diseases [OR] or infections.
 - Lead poisoning.
 - Sideroblastic anaemia.
