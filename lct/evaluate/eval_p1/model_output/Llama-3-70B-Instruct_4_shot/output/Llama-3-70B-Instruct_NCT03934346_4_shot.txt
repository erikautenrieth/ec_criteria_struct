Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Apparently healthy men
  -  Body mass index between 18  [AND] and 30 kg/m2
  -  Willing to eat only the foods provided by the study for a 2 week period
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Those reporting any chronic  [OR] acute diseases, food allergies, smoking  [OR] or alcohol abuse
  -  Use of illicit drugs; regular consumption of medications, micronutrient supplements,  [OR] or both
  -  Vegetarians,  [OR] or those unable to eat meat, are also excluded since the foods for the study contain meat
  -  Unable to refrain from taking medications during the study period
