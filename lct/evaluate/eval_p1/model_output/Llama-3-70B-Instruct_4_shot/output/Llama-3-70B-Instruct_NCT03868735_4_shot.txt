Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Orally intubated patient with endotracheal tube
  -  Mechanically ventilated for less than 24 hours  [AND] prior to enrollment
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Age  [NOT] greater than or equal to 18 years old
  -  Tracheostomy tube
  -  Extracorporeal membrane oxygenation
  -  Transfer from outside hospital with more than 24 hours of mechanical ventilation
