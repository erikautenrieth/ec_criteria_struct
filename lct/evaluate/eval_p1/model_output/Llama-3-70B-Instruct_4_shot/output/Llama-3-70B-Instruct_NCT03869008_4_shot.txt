Inclusion Criteria:
• Clinical Diagnosis of Primary Raynaud's Phenomenon
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Age < 18 years
 - Pregnant women
 - Current smokers
 - Have Digital ulcers
 - Diagnosed Pulmonary hypertension
 - Currently on vasodilators (i.e. Phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitors [OR] prostacyclin [OR] nitroglycerine [OR] other nitric oxide derivatives)
 - Currently on Calcium Channel Blockers
