<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria-- Participants must be:
  -  Healthy (determined by self-report)
  -  Between the ages of 18-55 years old
  -  Current cigarette smokers
  -  Willing [AND] able to provide informed consent
  -  Able [AND] willing to attend the lab session [AND] abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required
  -  Agree [AND] willing to use designated products according to the study protocol
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Women if they are breast-feeding [OR] test positive for pregnancy (by urinalysis) at screening.
  -  Individuals who weigh less than 110 pounds.
  -  [NOT] able to provide informed consent
  -  [NOT] willing [OR] able to attend the lab session [OR] abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required
  -  [NOT] agree [OR] willing to use designated products according to the study protocol
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
