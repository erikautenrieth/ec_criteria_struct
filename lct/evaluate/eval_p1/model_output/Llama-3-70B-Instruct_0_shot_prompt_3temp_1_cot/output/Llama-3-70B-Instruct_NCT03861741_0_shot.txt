<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. NS-TAD probands operated on (n=16).
  2. FDR [AND] SDR, aged 16 and above:
       1. At least two relatives willing to participate in the screening programme.
       2. Relatives able to understand English.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Probands with syndromic aortopathies, including Marfan Syndrome [OR] Loeys-Dietz Syndrome [OR] Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome [OR] Shprintzen-Goldberg syndrome [OR] aneurysm-osteoarthritis syndrome [OR] arterial tortuosity syndrome [OR] cutis laxa syndrome.
  2. Probands with aortic lesions associated with trauma [OR] infections.
  3. Probands/[NOT] relatives able to give informed consent.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
