 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age [13-17 years] AND [18-20 if still living at home with parent]
  -  Agreement of at least one responsible adult parent/caregiver [NOT excluding themselves] to participate in treatment [AND]
  -  Agreement to take part in assessments [AND] videotaping/audiotaping and coding of their sessions by research personnel [AND]
  -  Agreement to pay for mental health services at the DBT- RU on a sliding scale [AND] to participate in research assessments as volunteers [AND]
  -  Residence within commuting distance of clinic [< 45 minutes]
  -  Agreement to discontinue other forms of talk therapy for duration of DBT program [NOT referring to AA/NA programs or psychotropic medication management]
  -  Exhibits dysregulation within the past six months as evidenced by [1] intentional self-injury and/or suicide attempt [AND] [2] substance use disorder [AND] Meets two additional criteria for borderline personality disorder (BPD)

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Clients who need mental health services not available at the DBT- RU, such as treatment for schizophrenia or life-threatening anorexia [AND] who are currently obtaining optimum professional treatment that should not be ended [NOT excluding themselves]
  -  Non-English speaking [AND] Unable to understand research consent forms
  -  Court-ordered to participate in treatment
