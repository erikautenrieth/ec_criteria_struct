 Inclusion Criteria:

- Grade IV Glioblastoma AND receiving chemotherapy with OR without TTfields.
- Progressive or recurrent disease requiring second line treatment AND evidence of progression of disease by brain MRI AND planned to receive chemotherapy.

Exclusion Criteria:

- Current use of another beta blocker AND cannot be switched to carvedilol at the onset of the trial.
- Systolic blood pressure <90 mmHg AND/OR heart rate <60 bpm WITHOUT hypertensive medications.
- Allergy to beta blockers.
- Severe or uncontrolled asthma OR COPD (chronic obstructive pulmonary disease).
