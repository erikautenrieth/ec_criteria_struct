 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  145 mmHg ≤ msSBP AND Triglycerides < 500 mg/dL AND LDL-C ≤ 250 mg/dL

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  sSBP ≥ 20mmHg AND sDBP ≥ 10mmHg
  -  NOT symptomatic orthostatic hypotension AND NOT secondary/iatrogenic hypertension AND NOT dyslipidemia
  -  NOT history of moderate to severe cerebral ischemia AND NOT cerebral hemorrhage AND NOT transient ischemic attack AND NOT myocardial infarction AND NOT unstable angina in the past 6 months; NOT severe heart failure (New York Heart Association functional class III and IV)
  -  NOT hypersensitivity to telmisartan OR NOT hypersensitivity to rosuvastatin
  -  NOT history of angioedema after treatment with angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors OR NOT history of angioedema after treatment with ARBs AND NOT creatinine phosphokinase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal
  -  NOT estimated glomerular filtration rate ≤30 mL/min AND NOT aspartate aminotransferase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal AND NOT alanine aminotransferase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal
  -  NOT potassium levels >5.5 mmol/L AND NOT any diseases that could affect the results of the study.
