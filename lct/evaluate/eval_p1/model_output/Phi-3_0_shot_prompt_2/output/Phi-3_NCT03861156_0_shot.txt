 Inclusion Criteria:
  - Aged at least 18 years AND [NOT] aged less than 18 years.
  - Histologically or cytologically proven diagnosis of NSCLC AND [OR] locally advanced or metastatic.
  - Radiological documentation of disease progression WHILE [AND] on a previous continuous treatment with an first or second generation of EGFR TKI e.g. gefitinib or erlotinib AND [AND] within 7 days of study entry.
  - Patients must also have confirmation of tumour T790M mutation status [AND] [NOT] negative after disease progression on the prior EGFR TKI.
  - Eastern cooperative oncology group performance status (ECOG PS) of 0-1 [AND] [NOT] ECOG PS greater than 1.
  - A minimum life expectancy of 12 weeks [AND] [NOT] life expectancy less than 12 weeks.
  - At least one lesion, not previously irradiated during the study screening period, that can be accurately measured at baseline according to RECIST 1.1 [AND] [NOT] more than one lesion previously irradiated during the study screening period.
  - Agree to use routine adequate and effective contraceptive measures during the entire study period AND [AND] within 6 weeks after the last dose pre-menopausal fertility possible; Female patients need to exclude pregnancy [AND] [NOT] negative pregnancy test AND [AND] in non-lactation period [AND] [NOT] lactation period.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Treatment with a first or second generation of EGFR-TKI within 7 days of study entry [AND] [NOT] previous treatment with a third generation of EGFR-TKI.
  - Unresolved toxicities from prior therapy [AND] [NOT] unresolved toxicities from prior therapy.
  - Unstable spinal cord compression/brain metastases [AND] [NOT] stable spinal cord compression/brain metastases.
  - Severe/uncontrolled systemic diseases, including uncontrolled hypertension [AND] [NOT] controlled hypertension, bleeding diatheses or infection [AND] [NOT] severe/uncontrolled systemic diseases.
  - QTcF>470 msec during the screening period [AND] [NOT] QTcF less than or equal to 470 msec during the screening period.
  - Severe respiratory diseases such as interstitial lung disease [AND] [NOT] severe asthma, pulmonary embolism, etc. [AND] [NOT] severe respiratory diseases.
  - Previous treatment with 2 or more lines of Chemotherapy or immunotherapy [AND] [NOT] previous treatment with 2 or more lines of Chemotherapy or immunotherapy.
