 Inclusion Criteria:

- [AND] Handball players;

- [AND] Healthy;

- [AND] [OR] Participation of at least one official match in the last year and attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season.


Exclusion Criteria:

- [AND] musculoskeletal injury in the last three months;

- [AND] cardiovascular diseases;

- [AND] [NOT] use of anabolic, anti-inflammatory and analgesic drugs 72 hours before the evaluations;

- [AND] [NOT] use of alcohol and illicit drugs during the collection period.
