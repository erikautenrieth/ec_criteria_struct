 Inclusion Criteria:
  - age 20-34 years AND
  - body mass index (BMI) 18-29kg/m2 AND
  - regular menstrual cycle of 26-35 days AND
  - AMH levels age appropriate (≥2.3 ng/ml) AND
  - NOT (early follicular-phase serum concentration of FSH outside normal limits) (1-12 IU/l).

Exclusion Criteria:
  - women with diabetes OR other metabolic diseases
  - women with hereditary genetic diseases
  - women with heart disease OR QT prolongation OR heart failure
  - elevated liver enzymes OR liver failure OR hepatitis
  - women with inflammatory OR autoimmune disease
  - abnormal karyotype
  - endometriosis stage III/IV
  - history of recurrent miscarriage
  - severe OHSS in a previous stimulation cycle OR any contraindication for the use of gonadotrophins.
