 Inclusion Criteria:
- Patient who accepted to participate in the study AND is a woman over 18 years old AND affiliated to a social security scheme AND has a mono-fetal pregnancy AND patients with normal pregnancy between 11 and 42 weeks of amenorrhea AND patients hospitalized for preeclampsia and / or intrauterine growth retardation, beyond 20 weeks of amenorrhea.

Exclusion Criteria:
- Minors OR adults under guardianship OR protected persons AND NOT affiliated to a social security scheme OR multiple pregnancy.
