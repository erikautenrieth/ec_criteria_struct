 Inclusion Criteria:
  - Female AND older than 35 years. Already have mammography OR other standard exams (ultrasound, MRI, etc). Could participate this trial within 2 weeks AND have the ability of consent inform.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - During pregnancy OR lactation period. Patients that may NOT be capable of prone position, such as patients WITH scapulohumeral periarthritis OR cardiac pacemaker OR have recent cardiac surgery. Lymphoma patients that are under therapy. Severe scoliosis.
