 Inclusion Criteria:
  - Non-cardiac surgery patients AND
  - Age is greater than or equal to 60 years old;
  - Han Nationality AND
  - Mother tongue is Chinese;
  - The MMSE score: Illiteracy is greater than or equal to 17 points OR
  - Primary school is greater than or equal to 20 points OR
  - Higher secondary school is more than 24 points;
  - The people signed informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - The diagnosis of preoperative delirium OR
  - The diagnosis of Unstable hypertension (such as: pheochromocytoma or aortic dissection) OR
  - Severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease OR
  - Congestive heart failure OR
  - Severe liver and kidney dysfunction.
