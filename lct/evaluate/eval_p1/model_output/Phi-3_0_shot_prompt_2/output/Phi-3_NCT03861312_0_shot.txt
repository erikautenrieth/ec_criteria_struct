 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  The inclusion criteria were as follows: a) [NOT] men and women aged between 18- and 65-years [AND] [NOT] medical diagnosis of Non-Specific Chronic Neck Pain with more than 6 months of evolution of neck pain.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Exclusion criteria included the following: a) [AND] patients with rheumatic diseases [AND] cervical hernia [AND] cervical whiplash syndrome [AND] neck surgeries or history of arthrodesis [AND] systemic diseases [AND] vision, hearing or vestibular problems [AND] Severe trauma or traffic accident which has had an impact on the cervical area.
