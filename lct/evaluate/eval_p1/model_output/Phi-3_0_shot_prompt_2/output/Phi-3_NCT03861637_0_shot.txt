 Inclusion Criteria:
- Patients who fulfilled the following criteria AND had informed consent were included in the study:
- Adult renal transplant patients (≥21 years) AND stable maintenance subcutaneous ESA therapy with constant dose interval during the last 2 months were included in the study:
- Haemoglobin (Hb) ≥10mg/dL (Hct 32%) AND Transferrin saturation (TSAT) ≥20% (during the screening phase) were included in the study:
- Serum ferritin ≥100 ng/mL were included in the study.

Exclusion Criteria:
- Patients with the following criteria OR acute or chronic bleeding, or erythrocyte transfusion, within the preceding 8 weeks were excluded from the study:
- Patients with a change in Hb level ≥ 2 g/dL during the screening phase were excluded from the study:
- Patients with hemolytic anemia were excluded from the study:
- Patients with recent infection or rejection were excluded from the study:
- Patients with diastolic blood pressure >100mmHg OR discontinuation of ESA due to hypertension in the 6 months prior to the study were excluded from the study:
- Patients with vitamin B12 or folic acid deficiency were excluded from the study:
- Patients with uncontrolled or secondary hyperparathyroidism were excluded from the study:
- Patients with acute or chronic systemic inflammatory disease AND/OR C-reactive protein (CRP) >30 mg/L were excluded from the study:
- Patients on hemodialysis due to failure of a kidney transplant were excluded from the study:
- Patients with malignancy were excluded from the study.
