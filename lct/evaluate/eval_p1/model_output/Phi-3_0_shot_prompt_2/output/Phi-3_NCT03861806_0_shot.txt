 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Unilateral ischemic stroke with residual arm weakness (Fugl-Meyer Upper Limb < 60) AND more than 6 months prior to enrollment.
  -  Ability to give informed consent AND understand the tasks involved.
  -  Age over 18 years AND NOT a hemorrhagic stroke.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  NOT unilateral ischemic stroke with residual arm weakness AND NOT more than 6 months prior to enrollment AND hemorrhagic stroke.
  -  Contraindications to TMS: history of seizure/epilepsy AND NOT history of neurological disorders AND NOT brain surgery AND NOT metal implant/fragment in the head AND NOT pregnancy.
  -  Taking medications or substances that are known to affect PAS-induced plasticity within the past 2 months: NOT selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors AND NOT dopamine AND NOT dopamine agonists AND NOT haloperidol AND NOT lithium AND NOT acetylcholinesterase inhibitors AND NOT beta-blockers AND NOT nimodipine AND NOT levetiracetam AND NOT ethosuximide AND NOT benzodiazepines AND NOT baclofen AND NOT nicotine.
  -  Peripheral neuropathy OR history of nerve injury in the paretic upper limb.
  -  Social and/or personal circumstances that interfere with ability to return for all study visits AND NOT able to attend all study visits.
