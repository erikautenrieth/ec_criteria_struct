 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Older adults [AGE ≥ 65 years] AND
  -  Subject has lower appendicular muscle mass index (AMMI) [<7 kg/m^2 in men OR <5.7 kg/m^2 in women] using Bioelectrical impedance analysis (BIA, TANITA BC-418®, Tokyo, Japan) AND
  -  Subject has lower handgrip strength [<26 kg in men OR <18 kg in women] using dynamometer (CAMRY®, Zhongshan, Guangdong) OR subject has slower gait speed [<1 m/s]
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Subject's life expectancy was shorter than [NOT] 6 months AND
  -  Nursing home resident AND
  -  Subject has the history of kidney failure AND
  -  Subject has the history of liver failure AND
  -  Subject has the history of diabetes mellitus AND
  -  Subject has resistance exercise habit.
