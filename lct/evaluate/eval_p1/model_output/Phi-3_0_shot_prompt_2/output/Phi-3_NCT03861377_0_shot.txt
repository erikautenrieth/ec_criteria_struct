 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Gynecological procedure [AND]
  -  Age 18-50 years [AND]
  -  General anesthesia planned [AND]
  -  Systolic blood pressure < 150 mmHg [AND] [NOT] HR < 100 beats/min

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pre-existing hypertension [OR] Diabetes [OR] Ischemic heart disease or cerebrovascular disease [OR] Heart valve disease [OR] Verified cardiac arrhythmia other than extrasystoles [OR] Verified anaemia with hemoglobin level below 9.0 gr/dl [OR] Kidney or hepatic disease [OR] Hypersensitivity for propofol, soya, eggs or peanuts [OR] Pregnancy [OR] Poor health state [OR] Illicit substance use [OR] BMI <20 or >35 kg/m2
