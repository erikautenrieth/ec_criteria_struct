 Inclusion Criteria:
1. [AND] Patients undergoing first time cardiopulmonary bypass surgery for correction of atrial septal defects [OR] tissue mitral valve repair
2. [AND] Receiving intravenous Heparin for cardiopulmonary bypass [AND] [NOT] patients receiving heparin therapy prior to CPB within last 7 days

Exclusion Criteria:
- [AND] Concomitant Aortic Stenosis [AND] associated with an acquired von Willebrand Syndrome (aVWS)
- [AND] Patients with a baseline platelet count of <100x109/L
- [AND] Patients with a known coagulation factor deficiency [OR] platelet function disorder
- [AND] Patients receiving heparin therapy prior to CPB within last 7 days
