 Inclusion Criteria:
  - Age ≥ 18 years old AND confirmed diagnosis of perianal Crohn's disease AND on biologics or will start biologics

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients who have perianal fistula due to causes other than Crohn's disease OR fistulation into other organs, including bladder and intestine OR patients who have allergic reaction / contraindications to anti-TNF OR patients who have active cancer OR patients who have contraindications for MRI OR known pregnancy

For patients who refuse to participate in the tight monitoring arm, they will be consented AND recruited to the control arm for comparison.
