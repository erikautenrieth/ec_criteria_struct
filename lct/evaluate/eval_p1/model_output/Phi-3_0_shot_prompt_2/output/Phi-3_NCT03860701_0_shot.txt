 Inclusion Criteria:
- Patients with NEC (stages I-III Bell's Classification) AND weight greater or equal to 750 g.
- Patients with gestational age < 36 weeks.
- Patients whose parents consent to participate in the study.

Exclusion Criteria:
- Patients with major congenital anomalies OR no antecedent of limb ischemia/limb thrombotic events.
- Patients with no antecedent diagnosis of occlusive arterial OR venous thrombosis.
- Patients with hemodynamic instability.
