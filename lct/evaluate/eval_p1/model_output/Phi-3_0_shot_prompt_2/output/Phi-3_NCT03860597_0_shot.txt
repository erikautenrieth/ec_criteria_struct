 Inclusion Criteria:
- diagnosis of schizophrenia [OR] schizoaffective-depressed [OR] healthy subjects [AND NOT] pregnant
- ages 18-50 for all subjects [AND] double barrier contraception for all subjects

Exclusion Criteria:
- DSM-IV Axis I or II Diagnosis [NOT] for healthy subjects [AND] current substance abuse [AND] current recreational drug use [AND] history of other significant medical illness (e.g. cancer, diabetes, heart disease, HIV, seizures) [AND] open head injury or closed head injury with loss of consciousness > 1 min [AND] hearing or visual impairment [AND] pregnancy [AND] dementia [AND] mental retardation [AND] NOT] healthy subjects [OR] patients [AND NOT] contraception for all subjects [AND NOT] not pregnant for all subjects
