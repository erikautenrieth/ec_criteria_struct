 Inclusion Criteria:

- Patients with high risk class II AND class III AND class IV abdominal wounds

- Patients undergoing laparotomy for gynecologic related disorders OR undergoing laparotomy for both benign AND malignant diagnoses will be included in this study

Exclusion Criteria:

- Pregnancy

- Allergy to triclosan

- Patients undergoing HIPEC AND NOT having high risk class II, class III, class IV abdominal wounds
