 Inclusion Criteria:
- Gestational age < 32 weeks AND birthweight <1500 g
- Age less than 72 hours AND NOT PDA diameter ≥ 1.5 mm
- Signed informed consent obtained from both parents AND NOT lack of informed consent of the parents

Exclusion Criteria:
- Birthweight ≥ 1500 g OR gestation age ≥ 32 weeks
- Lack of informed consent of the parents OR NOT signed informed consent obtained from both parents
- Congenital heart defect, other than PDA OR NOT patent foramen ovale (PFO)
- The presence of a clinically apparent hemorrhagic syndrome OR NOT absence of a clinically apparent hemorrhagic syndrome
- Any intraventricular hemorrhage (IVH) in the first 48 hours OR IVH grade 3-4 OR NOT presence of any intraventricular hemorrhage (IVH) in the first 48 hours or IVH grade 3-4
- A platelet count of < 50,000/mm3 OR platelet count ≥ 50,000/mm3
- A serum creatinine concentration of > 110 μmol/L OR serum creatinine concentration ≤ 110 μmol/L
- Oliguria <1 ml/kg/h OR oliguria ≥ 1 ml/kg/h
- Suspected/apparent NEC OR NOT suspected/apparent NEC
- Suspected/apparent lung hypoplasia OR NOT suspected/apparent lung hypoplasia
