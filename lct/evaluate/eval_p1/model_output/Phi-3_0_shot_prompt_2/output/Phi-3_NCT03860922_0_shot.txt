 Inclusion Criteria:
  - patients in different age groups [AND] both sex referred [AND] from orthopedic outpatient clinic [AND] with chronic ankle pain [AND] suspected clinically to have impingement syndrome.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] Inflammatory arthritis of ankle joint [AND] [NOT] infection in ankle joint [AND] [NOT] neoplasm around ankle joint.
  2. [NOT] patients with contraindications for MRI [AND] e.g. [AND] an implanted magnetic device [AND] pacemakers [AND] claustrophobia.
