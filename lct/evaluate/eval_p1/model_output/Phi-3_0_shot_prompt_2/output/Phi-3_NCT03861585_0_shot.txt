 Inclusion Criteria:
- Children between 7 and 12 years old AND
- Normal or corrected visual acuity AND
- ADHD diagnostic given by a doctor according to DSM 5 criteria AND
- Free from drug treatment until 24 hours before the experiment session AND
- Affiliation to a social security system AND
- Signature of the authorization documents from one parent or the holder of parental authority

Exclusion Criteria:
- History of neurological troubles AND
- Dysphasia AND
- Autistic spectrum disorder AND
- Intellectual disabilities
