 Inclusion Criteria:

- treatment-seeking individuals diagnosed with AUD [AND] DSM-5

- fluent in English [AND]

- past month excessive alcohol use [OR] (greater than 7 drinks/week for women, greater than 14 drinks/week for men, greater than 3 drinks/occasion for women, greater than 4 drinks/occasion for men)

- baseline Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A) [AND] core indicative of mild to moderate anxiety (score 8 to 23) [AND]

- Perceived Stress Scale (PSS) [AND] core indicative of moderate stress (score 14 to 26)

- increase in alcohol craving following the baseline stress reactivity assessment [AND]


Exclusion Criteria:

- individuals exhibiting severe scores on the HAM-A, PSS, or post-traumatic (PTSD) checklist (PCL-5) [AND] at the discretion of the admitting physician

- physical dependence on alcohol [AND] Alcohol Use Disorders Inventory (AUDIT) score indicative of severe alcohol dependence (≥13 for women, ≥15 for men)

- greater than mild substance use disorder on drugs other than alcohol, nicotine, and marijuana

- contraindications for taking pioglitazone [AND] medical conditions contraindicating pioglitazone pharmacotherapy or taking contraindicated medications

- pregnant, nursing, or planning on becoming pregnant during the course of the study

- have any other illness, condition, or use of medications [AND] which in the opinion of the PI and/or admitting physician would preclude safe and/or successful completion of the study.
