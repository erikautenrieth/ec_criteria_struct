 Inclusion Criteria:
Nursing home residents with [NOT] prior diagnosis of AF [AND] [OR] ≥2 of the following risk factors for atrial fibrillation:
  -  age ≥75 years [AND]
  -  female sex [AND]
  -  obstructive sleep apnea [AND]
  -  peripheral vascular disease [AND]
  -  diabetes mellitus [AND]
  -  obesity (body mass index [BMI] >30 kg/m2) [AND]
  -  hypertension [AND]
  -  congestive heart failure [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] [AND] [OR] ≥2 of the following risk factors for atrial fibrillation [AND] [OR] 
  -  prior diagnosis of AF [AND]
  -  continuous rhythm monitoring over the past year [AND]
  -  pacemaker or implantable cardioverter defibrillator in situ [AND]
