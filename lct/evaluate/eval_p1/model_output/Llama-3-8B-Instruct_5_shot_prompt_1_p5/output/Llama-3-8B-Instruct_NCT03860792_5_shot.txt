Inclusion Criteria:
  - Diagnosis of AD by current McKhann et al. criteria [AND] diagnosis confirmed by a qualified healthcare professional
  - CDR global score of 0.5 [OR] 1
  - Agreed cooperation from an appropriate study partner [AND] the partner is willing and able to provide information and support throughout the study
  - Speaks English as primary language [AND] able to communicate effectively in English
  - Age 50 [AND] to 90
  - No medication changes within the past 30 days [AND] no plans to change medications during the study

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Resides in a nursing home [OR] or dementia special care unit, [OR] cannot control diet [AND] is unable to follow a controlled diet
  - A potentially confounding serious medical risk [AND] including insulin-requiring diabetes [OR] cancer requiring chemotherapy [OR] radiation within the past 5 years [OR] a recent cardiac event (i.e. heart attack, angioplasty, etc.) [AND] the risk is deemed significant by the investigator
  - Participating in another clinical trial [OR] using an investigational drug [OR] therapy within 30 days of the Screening Visit [AND] the investigator deems it incompatible with the current study
  - A history of renal stones [AND] the stones are currently causing symptoms or are deemed significant by the investigator
