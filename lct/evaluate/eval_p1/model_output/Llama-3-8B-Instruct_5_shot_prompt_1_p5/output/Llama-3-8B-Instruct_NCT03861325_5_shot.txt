Inclusion Criteria:
  - The patient [OR] his / her representative has been informed about the implementation of the study, its objectives, its constraints, the rights of the patient and must have received the newsletter [AND] no opposition of the study.
  - The patient must be an affiliate [OR] beneficiary of a health insurance plan.
  - The patient is at least 18 years old.
  - The patient has septic shock [AND] as defined by the 3rd International Conference for the definition of sepsis and septic shock.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - The patient is in an exclusion period [NOT] determined by a previous study.
  - The patient is under [NOT] guardianship, under [NOT] guardianship, or under the [NOT] protection of justice.
  - The patient has already participated [NOT] in this study.
  - The patient [OR] his / her representative refuses to participate in the study.
  - The patient is pregnant [OR] parturient [OR] or is breastfeeding.
  - The patient is a minor [NOT].
  - The patient is in therapeutic [NOT] limitation or moribund.
  - The patient has undergone [NOT] or is going to have scheduled [OR] emergency digestive surgery.
  - The patient is HIV-positive [NOT].
  - The patient has Child C [NOT] cirrhosis.
  - The patient has progressive digestive neoplasia [OR] digestive lymphoma [OR] chronic inflammatory bowel disease (Crohn's disease...).
