Inclusion Criteria:
 - age 20-34 years;
 - body mass index (BMI) 18-29kg/m2;
 - regular menstrual cycle of 26-35 days,
 - AMH levels age appropriate [AND] (≥2.3 ng/ml)
 - early follicular- phase serum concentration of FSH within normal limits [OR] (1-12 IU/l).
Exclusion Criteria:
 - women [NOT] with diabetes [OR] and other metabolic disease
 - women [NOT] with hereditary genetic diseases;
 - women [NOT] with heart disease [OR] QT prolongation [OR] heart failure
 - women [NOT] with elevated liver enzymes [OR] liver failure [OR] hepatitis
 - women [NOT] with inflammatory [OR] autoimmune disease
 - abnormal karyotype;
 - endometriosis stage III/IV;
 - history of recurrent miscarriage;
 - severe OHSS in a previous stimulation cycle [OR] any contraindication for the use of gonadotrophins.
