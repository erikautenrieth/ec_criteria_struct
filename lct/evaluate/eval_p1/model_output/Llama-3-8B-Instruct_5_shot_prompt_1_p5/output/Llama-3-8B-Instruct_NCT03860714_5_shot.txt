Inclusion Criteria:
  - Non-cardiac surgery patients [AND] 
       - Age is greater than or equal to 60 years old [OR] Han Nationality [AND] mother tongue is Chinese;
       - The MMSE score: Illiteracy is greater than or equal to 17 points [OR] primary school is greater than or equal to 20 points [OR] higher secondary school is more than 24 points;
       - The people signed informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - The diagnosis of preoperative delirium [NOT] 
       - The diagnosis Unstable hypertension [OR] severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] congestive heart failure;
  - Severe liver and kidney dysfunction.
