Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients who fulfilled the following criteria were included in the study:
  - Adult renal transplant patients [AND] ≥21years,
  - Informed consent,
  - Stable maintenance subcutaneous ESA therapy [AND] with constant dose interval during the last 2 months,
  - Haemoglobin (Hb) [OR] ≥10mg/dL (Hct 32%),
  - Transferrin saturation (TSAT) [OR] ≥20% (during the screening phase),
  - and serum ferritin [OR] ≥100 ng/mL

Exclusion Criteria:
We excluded patients with the following criteria:
  - Acute [OR] or chronic bleeding, or erythrocyte transfusion, within the preceding 8 weeks [NOT] and change in Hb level [NOT] ≥ 2 g/dL during screening phase,
  - Hemolytic anemia,
  - Recent [OR] infection or rejection,
  - Diastolic blood pressure [OR] >100mmHg [OR] or discontinuation of ESA due to hypertension in the 6 months prior to study,
  - Vitamin B12 [OR] or folic acid deficiency,
  - Uncontrolled [OR] or secondary hyperparathyroidism,
  - Acute [OR] or chronic systemic inflammatory disease [AND] and/or C-reactive protein (CRP) [OR] >30 mg/L,
  - Hemodialysis due to failure of a kidney transplant,
  - and malignancy
