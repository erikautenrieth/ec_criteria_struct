Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
  -  Newly diagnosed histologically proven locoregional oral squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC) [OR] without evidence of distant metastases. OSCC includes the subsites of oral tongue, floor of mouth, gingiva, retromolar trigone [AND] and buccal mucosa [OR] Recurrent or persistent histologically proven locoregional OSCC that was initially treated with surgery alone.
  -  must be eligible for surgical resection [AND]
  -  greater than 18 years of age
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
  -  prior immunotherapy [OR] or treatment with another anti PD-1 agent besides nivolumab
  -  prior chemotherapy including cetuximab [OR] or radiation therapy
  -  concomitant malignancies [NOT] except cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma [OR] or basal cell carcinoma
  -  unresectable primary tumor [OR] or regional disease [OR] or distant metastases
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
