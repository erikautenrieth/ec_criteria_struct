Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Adults ≥ 18 years old
 2. High risk for cardiovascular morbidity and mortality [AND] such as prior history of clinical cardiovascular disease [OR] and/or history of familial hypercholesterolemia
 3. LDL-C within the past 6 months above recommended level [AND] despite maximal tolerated statin therapy [OR] ± ezetimibe for the past 3 months.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. [NOT] current treatment with PCSK9 inhibitor
 2. [NOT] current participation in investigational study
 3. [NOT] prior participation in the GOAL program
