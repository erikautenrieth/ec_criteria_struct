<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Men [OR] women age 18-64.
  - Present to ED primary for management of Low Back Pain (LBP) [AND] with [OR] without symptoms of radiculopathy.
  - Functionally impairing back pain: A baseline score of greater than 5 on the Roland-Morris Disability Questionnaire.
  - Musculoskeletal etiology of low back [AND] with non-radicular pain.
  - Non-traumatic LBP [AND] with pain duration <2 weeks (336 hours).
  - Participant is to be discharged home [AND] without any planned hospitalization.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Flank pain, that is pain originating from tissues lateral to the paraspinal muscles [NOT] with low back pain.
  - Not available for follow-up [OR] without a reliable contact method.
  - Pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding.
  - Chronic pain syndrome [OR] with a history of chronic pain.
  - Allergic to [OR] intolerant of investigational medications [AND] with a history of adverse reactions.
  - Contra-indications to investigational medications [OR] with a history of adverse reactions.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
