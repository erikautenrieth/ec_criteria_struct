<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Chronic (more than three months) ESBL colonization of the same strain [AND] as determined by species, phenotype [AND] antibiogram.
  - Patients [AND] must be able to swallow sachets.
  - Must [AND] be able to speak [OR] understand Swedish.
  - Must [AND] have a permanent residence [OR] in Sweden.
  - Must [AND] be able to sign informed consent [OR] in Swedish.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Immunosuppression [OR] (i.e. chemotheraphy, treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors).
  - In patient care [NOT].
  - immunodeficiency [OR].
  - psychiatric disorder [OR].
  - alcohol [OR] or substance abuse [OR].
  - dementia [OR].
  - Invasive catheters [OR].
