Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient 18 years old [OR] more
 - Intubated [AND] and ventilated in intensive care unit for more than 24 hours
 - Patient ready [AND] for weaning evaluation
 - Failure [OR] of a first T-piece SBT

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Chronic [NOT] neuromuscular disease
 - Guillain-Barré Syndrome
 - Central nervous system disease [AND] with consciousness disorder
 - Tracheostomy
 - Chronic disease [AND] with life expectancy less than 1 year
 - Pregnancy [OR] breast feeding
 - Withholding [NOT] life support regarding a reintubation
 - Prisoner [OR] or patient interned in psychiatric hospital
 - Guardianship
 - Language [NOT] barrier
 - Lack [NOT] of medical insurance
 - Lack [NOT] of the patient's consent (or of the next of kin where appropriate)
 - Patient under an exclusion period [NOT] after enrollment in another research study
