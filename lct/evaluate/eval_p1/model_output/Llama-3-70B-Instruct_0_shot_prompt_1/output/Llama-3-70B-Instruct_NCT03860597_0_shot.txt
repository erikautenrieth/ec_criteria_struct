Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the added logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  diagnosis of schizophrenia OR schizoaffective-depressed OR healthy subjects
  -  ages 18-50 for all subjects AND double barrier contraception for all subjects AND not pregnant for all subjects

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  DSM-IV Axis I or II Diagnosis for healthy subjects OR
  -  MEM or amantadine for patients OR
  -  current substance abuse for all subjects OR
  -  current recreational drug use for all subjects OR
  -  history of other significant medical illness (e.g. cancer, diabetes, heart disease, HIV, seizures) for all subjects OR
  -  open head injury or closed head injury with loss of consciousness > 1 min for all subjects OR
  -  hearing or visual impairment for all subjects OR
  -  pregnancy for all subjects OR
  -  dementia for all subjects OR
  -  mental retardation for all subjects AND NOT meeting any of the inclusion criteria
