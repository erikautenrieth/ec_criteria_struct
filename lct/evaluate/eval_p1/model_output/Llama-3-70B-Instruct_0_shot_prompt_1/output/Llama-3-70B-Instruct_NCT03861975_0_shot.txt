Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Participants must have [histologically OR cytologically] confirmed [invasive OR in-situ] carcinoma of the breast AND will undergo unilateral breast cancer surgery
  -  Willingness to complete SEI-BCLE Questionnaire
  -  Age >18 years
  -  Ability to understand AND the willingness to verbally consent to the trial

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History of primary lymphedema
  -  Any patient with a current case of cellulitis
  -  Participants with known brain metastases
  -  Uncontrolled intercurrent illness [INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO] [(ongoing OR active) infection OR symptomatic congestive heart failure OR unstable angina pectoris OR cardiac arrhythmia OR psychiatric illness] OR social situations that would limit compliance with study requirements
  -  Participants [NOT able] to abduct upper-extremity perpendicular to body OR [NOT able] to stand comfortably without aid for several minutes, both of which are necessary to perform a LymphaTech Scan
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
