Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Gestational age < 32 weeks
  -  Birthweight < 1500 g
  -  Age less than 72 hours
  -  PDA diameter > 1.5 mm
  -  Signed informed consent obtained from both parents [AND] no contraindications to study participation

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Birthweight ≥ 1500 g [OR] gestation age ≥ 32 weeks
  -  Lack of informed consent of the parents [OR] inability to obtain consent
  -  Congenital heart defect [NOT] limited to PDA and/or patent foramen ovale (PFO)
  -  The presence of a clinically apparent hemorrhagic syndrome [OR] bleeding disorder
  -  Any intraventricular hemorrhage (IVH) in the first 48 hours [OR] IVH grade 3-4
  -  A platelet count of < 50,000/mm3 [OR] thrombocytopenia
  -  A serum creatinine concentration of > 110 μmol/L [OR] renal insufficiency
  -  Oliguria <1 ml/kg/h [OR] anuria
  -  Suspected/apparent NEC [OR] intestinal perforation
  -  Suspected/apparent lung hypoplasia [OR] respiratory distress syndrome
