Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Older adults (≥ 65 years) AND
  -  Subject has lower appendicular muscle mass index (AMMI) (<7 kg/m^2 in men; <5.7 kg/m^2 in women) using Bioelectrical impedance analysis (BIA, TANITA BC-418®, Tokyo, Japan) AND
  -  (Subject has lower handgrip strength (<26 kg in men; <18 kg in women) using dynamometer (CAMRY®, Zhongshan, Guangdong) OR slowly gait speed (<1 m/s))

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Subject's life expectancy was shorter than 6 months OR
  -  Nursing home resident OR
  -  Subject has the history of kidney failure OR
  -  Subject has the history of liver failure OR
  -  Subject has the history of diabetes mellitus OR
  -  Subject has resistance exercise habit.

Note: I added AND operators between the inclusion criteria to ensure that all conditions must be met. I added OR operators between the exclusion criteria to indicate that if any of the conditions are met, the subject is excluded.
