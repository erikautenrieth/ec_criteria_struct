Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - patients (adults and children)
  - patients recipients in France from 2008 to 2015 of a first kidney transplant [OR] heart [OR] lung [OR] liver donor living [OR] deceased, non-immunized anti-HLA before the transplant
  - patients having preserved their graft > 2 years [AND] having had no graft rejection episodes during this period
  - having agreed to the use for research purposes in transplantation of the remains of the DNA and serum samples taken as part of the care of which the remains are available [AND] having signed an informed consent form

Exclusion Criteria:
  - NOT meeting one [OR] more of the inclusion criteria
  - presence of any condition that may interfere with the study outcomes [OR] pose a risk to the patient
