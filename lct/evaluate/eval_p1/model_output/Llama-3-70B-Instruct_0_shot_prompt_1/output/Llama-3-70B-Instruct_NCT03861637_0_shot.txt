Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients who fulfilled the following criteria were included in the study:
  - Adult renal transplant patients (≥21years) AND
  - Informed consent AND
  - Stable maintenance subcutaneous ESA therapy with constant dose interval during the last 2 months AND
  - Haemoglobin (Hb) ≥10mg/dL (Hct 32%) AND
  - Transferrin saturation (TSAT) ≥20% (during the screening phase) AND
  - serum ferritin ≥100 ng/mL

Exclusion Criteria:
We excluded patients with the following criteria:
  - Acute or chronic bleeding OR erythrocyte transfusion OR change in Hb level ≥ 2 g/dL during screening phase OR
  - Hemolytic anemia OR
  - Recent infection OR rejection OR
  - Diastolic blood pressure >100mmHg OR discontinuation of ESA due to hypertension in the 6 months prior to study OR
  - Vitamin B12 deficiency OR folic acid deficiency OR
  - Uncontrolled hyperparathyroidism OR secondary hyperparathyroidism OR
  - Acute or chronic systemic inflammatory disease OR C-reactive protein (CRP) >30 mg/L OR
  - Hemodialysis due to failure of a kidney transplant OR
  - malignancy AND NOT
  - any other condition that may interfere with the study results
