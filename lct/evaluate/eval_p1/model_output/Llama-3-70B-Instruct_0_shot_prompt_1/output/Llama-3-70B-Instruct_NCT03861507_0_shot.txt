Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Male, aged 18 years or older AND
  - Willing to provide written consent AND
  - Pathologically confirmed prostate cancer on previous biopsy AND
  - Suitable for high-dose-rate brachytherapy for treatment as standard of care AND
  - Consenting to high-dose-rate brachytherapy for treatment as standard of care.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Previous radiotherapy to the prostate OR
  - (No additional exclusion criteria)

Note: Since there was only one exclusion criterion, I did not add any logical operators. If there were multiple exclusion criteria, I would add the OR operator between them.
