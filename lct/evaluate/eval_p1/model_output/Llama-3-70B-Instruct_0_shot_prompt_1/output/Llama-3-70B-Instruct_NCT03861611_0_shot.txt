<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Men [OR] women age 18-64.
  -  Present to ED primary for management of Low Back Pain (LBP) [AND] who require treatment.
  -  Functionally impairing back pain: A baseline score of greater than 5 on the Roland-Morris Disability Questionnaire.
  -  Musculoskeletal etiology of low back [AND] non-radicular pain.
  -  Pain duration <2 weeks (336 hours).
  -  Non-traumatic LBP [AND] participant is to be discharged home.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Flank pain, that is pain originating from tissues lateral to the paraspinal muscles.
  -  [NOT] Available for follow-up.
  -  [NOT] Pregnant.
  -  [NOT] Chronic pain syndrome.
  -  Allergic to [OR] intolerant of investigational medications.
  -  [NOT] Contra-indications to investigational medications.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
