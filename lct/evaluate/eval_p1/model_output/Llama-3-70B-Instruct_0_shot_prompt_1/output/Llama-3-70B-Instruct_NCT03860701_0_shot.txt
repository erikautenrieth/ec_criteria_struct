Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with NEC (stages I-III Bell's Classification) AND
  -  Weight greater or equal to 750 g AND
  -  Gestational age < 36 weeks AND
  -  Patients whose parents consent to participate in the study.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with major congenital anomalies OR
  -  Patients with an antecedent of limb ischemia/limb thrombotic events OR
  -  Patients with an antecedent diagnosis of occlusive arterial or venous thrombosis OR
  -  Patients with hemodynamic instability NOT
  -  meeting any of the inclusion criteria.

Note: I added the last line in the Exclusion Criteria to make it clear that patients who meet any of the inclusion criteria should not be excluded.
