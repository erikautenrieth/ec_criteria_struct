<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with NEC (stages I-III Bell's Classification) AND
  -  Weight greater or equal to 750 g AND
  -  Gestational age < 36 weeks AND
  -  Patients whose parents consent to participate in the study.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with major congenital anomalies OR
  -  Patients with antecedent of limb ischemia/limb thrombotic events OR
  -  Patients with antecedent diagnosis of occlusive arterial or venous thrombosis OR
  -  Hemodynamic instability AND NOT
  -  Patients who meet any of the above exclusion criteria.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
