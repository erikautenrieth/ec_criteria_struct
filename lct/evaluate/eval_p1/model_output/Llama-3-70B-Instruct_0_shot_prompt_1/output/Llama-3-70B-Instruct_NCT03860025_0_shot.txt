Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] incorporated:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - patients with one or more events of hip dislocation after a primary THA [AND] age ≥ 18 years
  - controls with a primary THA [NOT] having events of hip dislocation [OR] with a history of hip revision surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
- patients with a history of hip fracture [OR] previous hip surgery [NOT] related to the primary THA
