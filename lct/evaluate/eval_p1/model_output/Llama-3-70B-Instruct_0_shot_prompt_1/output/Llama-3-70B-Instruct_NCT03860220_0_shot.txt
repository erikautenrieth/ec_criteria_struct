Inclusion Criteria:
  -  (145 mmHg ≤ msSBP ≤ 190 mmHg) AND (Triglycerides < 500 mg/dL) AND (LDL-C ≤ 250 mg/dL)
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  (sSBP ≥ 20mmHg AND sDBP ≥ 10mmHg) OR 
  -  (symptomatic orthostatic hypotension AND secondary/iatrogenic hypertension AND dyslipidemia) OR 
  -  (history of moderate to severe cerebral ischemia OR cerebral hemorrhage OR transient ischemic attack OR myocardial infarction OR unstable angina in the past 6 months) OR 
  -  (severe heart failure (New York Heart Association functional class III and IV)) OR 
  -  (hypersensitivity to telmisartan OR rosuvastatin) OR 
  -  (history of angioedema after treatment with angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors OR ARBs) OR 
  -  (creatinine phosphokinase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal) OR 
  -  (estimated glomerular filtration rate ≤30 mL/min) OR 
  -  (aspartate aminotransferase and alanine aminotransferase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal) OR 
  -  (potassium levels >5.5 mmol/L) OR 
  -  (any diseases that could affect the results of the study)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
