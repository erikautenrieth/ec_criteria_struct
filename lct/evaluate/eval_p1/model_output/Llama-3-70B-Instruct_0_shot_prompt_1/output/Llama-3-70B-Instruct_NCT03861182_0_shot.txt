Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
- Over 18 years of age
Neonates:
  -  Aged between 1h of life AND 28 days of life
  -  Born beyond 33 Week of Amenorrhea + 1day AND 2kg of birth weight
  -  Hospitalized in the medical-surgical centre of Pediatrics of the hospital of Strasbourg
  -  For whom a blood sample was prescribed as part of their routine care AND NOT for any other reason

Exclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
  -  Systemic inflammatory disease OR transient inflammatory state
  -  Any drug that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h preceding the sampling
Neonates:
  -  Risk of anemia < 7g/DL OR current anticoagulant treatment
  -  NOT meeting the inclusion criteria for neonates
