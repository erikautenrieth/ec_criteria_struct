Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] incorporated:

Inclusion Criteria:
Nursing home residents with ≥2 of the following risk factors for atrial fibrillation:
  - age ≥75 years OR
  - female sex OR
  - obstructive sleep apnea OR
  - peripheral vascular disease OR
  - diabetes mellitus OR
  - obesity (body mass index [BMI] >30 kg/m2) OR
  - hypertension OR
  - congestive heart failure
  AND having at least two of these risk factors.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - prior diagnosis of AF AND
  - NOT having continuous rhythm monitoring over the past year AND
  - NOT having a pacemaker or implantable cardioverter defibrillator in situ
