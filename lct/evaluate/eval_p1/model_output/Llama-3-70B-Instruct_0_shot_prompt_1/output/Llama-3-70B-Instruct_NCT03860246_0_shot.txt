<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Nursing home residents with ≥2 of the following risk factors for atrial fibrillation:
  -  age ≥75 years [OR]
  -  female sex [OR]
  -  obstructive sleep apnea [OR]
  -  peripheral vascular disease [OR]
  -  diabetes mellitus [OR]
  -  obesity (body mass index [BMI] >30 kg/m2) [OR]
  -  hypertension [OR]
  -  congestive heart failure
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  prior diagnosis of AF [AND]
  -  continuous rhythm monitoring over the past year [OR]
  -  pacemaker or implantable cardioverter defibrillator in situ [OR]
  -  NOT (nursing home residents with ≥2 of the above risk factors for atrial fibrillation)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
