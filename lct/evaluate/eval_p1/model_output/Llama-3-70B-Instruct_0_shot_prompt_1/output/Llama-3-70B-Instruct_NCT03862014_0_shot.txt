Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients and parents of the patients who accept to participate AND sign the informed consent
  - Patients whose molar teeth are diagnosed as molar incisor hypomineralization by EAPD
  - Teeth that are fully erupted AND have NOT undergone any restorative treatment
  - Patients who have at least two permanent first molars that need fissure sealant OR have high-caries risk
  - Teeth that have white-yellow-brown discoloration that indicate high-caries risk

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Teeth that are previously restored OR have caries
  - Patients who have orthodontic treatment OR are currently undergoing orthodontic treatment
  - Patients who are NOT cooperative for the dental procedure
  - Teeth that have dental fluorosis OR enamel malformation according to specific syndromes
