Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Chronic (more than three months) ESBL colonization of the same strain as determined by species, phenotype AND antibiogram.
  -  Patients must be able to swallow sachets AND must be able to speak AND understand Swedish.
  -  Must have a permanent residence in Sweden AND must be able to sign informed consent in Swedish.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Immunosuppression (i.e. chemotherapy, treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors) OR immunodeficiency.
  -  In patient care OR psychiatric disorder OR alcohol OR substance abuse.
  -  Dementia OR Invasive catheters.
  -  NOT able to swallow sachets.
  -  NOT able to speak OR understand Swedish.
  -  NOT having a permanent residence in Sweden.
  -  NOT able to sign informed consent in Swedish.
