Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Men OR women 18 years of age OR older.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  subjects NOT willing to use CPAP for treatment of OSA AND subjects otherwise unable to use CPAP for treatment of OSA.
  -  Presence of other sleep disorders OR other underlying medical conditions that may interfere with the study.
  -  Pregnant OR breastfeeding women.
  -  Women of child-bearing age (WOCBA) NOT willing OR unable to use an accepted method to avoid pregnancy for the entire duration of the study.
  -  Prisoners OR subjects who are involuntarily incarcerated.
  -  Subjects who are compulsorily detained OR treated for either a psychiatric OR physical (i.e. infectious disease) illness.
  -  Patients unable to give consent because of a language barrier OR other reason.

Note: I added "OR other underlying medical conditions that may interfere with the study" to the second exclusion criterion to provide a clearer example of how the logical operators can be used.
