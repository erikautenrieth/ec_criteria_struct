Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] incorporated:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age ≥ 18 years old
  -  Confirmed diagnosis of perianal Crohn's disease AND
  -  On biologics OR will start biologics

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients who have perianal fistula due to causes other than Crohn's disease
  -  Fistulation into other organs, including bladder AND intestine
  -  Patients who have allergic reaction OR contraindications to anti-TNF
  -  Patients who have active cancer
  -  Patients who have contraindications for MRI
  -  Known pregnancy
  -  NOT willing to participate in the tight monitoring arm (will be consented and recruited to the control arm for comparison)
