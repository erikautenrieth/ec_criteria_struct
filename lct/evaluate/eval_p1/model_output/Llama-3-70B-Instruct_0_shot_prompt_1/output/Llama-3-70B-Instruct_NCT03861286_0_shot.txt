<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients undergoing first time cardiopulmonary bypass surgery for correction of atrial septal defects [OR] tissue mitral valve repair
  2. Receiving intravenous Heparin for cardiopulmonary bypass [AND] undergoing first time cardiopulmonary bypass surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Concomitant Aortic Stenosis [AND] which is associated with an acquired von Willebrand Syndrome (aVWS)
  -  Patients with a baseline platelet count of <100x109/L [NOT] receiving platelet transfusion
  -  Patients with a known coagulation factor deficiency [OR] platelet function disorder
  -  Patients receiving heparin therapy prior to CPB within last 7 days [NOT] having a normal coagulation profile
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
