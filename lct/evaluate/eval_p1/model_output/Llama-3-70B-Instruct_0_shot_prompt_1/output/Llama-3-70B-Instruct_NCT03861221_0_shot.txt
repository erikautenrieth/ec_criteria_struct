<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Female, older than 35 years. Already have mammography, [OR] other standard exams (ultrasound, MRI, etc). Could participate this trial within 2 weeks after mammography [AND] have the ability of consent inform.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  During pregnancy [OR] lactation period. Patients [AND] who may not be capable of prone position, such as patients with scapulohumeral periarthritis, cardiac pacemaker [OR] have recent cardiac surgery.
  -  [NOT] Lymphoma patients that are under therapy. [NOT] Severe scoliosis.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
