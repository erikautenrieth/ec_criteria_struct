Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Skeletal maturity
  2. AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures
  3. Wound primarily closeable (no flap or delay in closure due to contamination needed) AND palpable pedal pulses (no vascular injury sustained)
  4. Presentation within 24 hours from injury
  5. (Skeletal maturity OR AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures) AND (Wound primarily closeable (no flap or delay in closure due to contamination needed) AND palpable pedal pulses (no vascular injury sustained) AND Presentation within 24 hours from injury)

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Current injury is a pathologic fracture OR sustained bilateral tibia fracture
  2. Sustained comminuted femur fracture
  3. (Sustained severe Traumatic Brain Injury (GCS<12) *** OR sustained severe spinal cord injury (lower extremity paresis/paralysis))
  4. Sustained severe burns (>10% total body surface area (TBSA) or >5% TBSA with full thickness or circumferential injury)
  5. Prior ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery
  6. Prior or current lower limb deformity or abnormality
  7. Unable to complete follow-up visits
  8. (Current injury is a pathologic fracture OR sustained bilateral tibia fracture OR sustained comminuted femur fracture OR sustained severe Traumatic Brain Injury (GCS<12) *** OR sustained severe spinal cord injury (lower extremity paresis/paralysis) OR sustained severe burns (>10% total body surface area (TBSA) or >5% TBSA with full thickness or circumferential injury) OR prior ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery OR prior or current lower limb deformity or abnormality OR unable to complete follow-up visits)
  9. NOT (Skeletal maturity AND AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures AND Wound primarily closeable (no flap or delay in closure due to contamination needed) AND palpable pedal pulses (no vascular injury sustained) AND Presentation within 24 hours from injury)

Note: I've added the logical operators to make the criteria more explicit, but please review and adjust according to your specific needs.
