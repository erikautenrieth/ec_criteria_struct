Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients aged from 18 to 80 years AND
  - Obese (BMI ≥ 30kg/m²) AND
  - Referred by GPs to the medical analysis laboratory for usual biological assessment AND
  - Patients with no respiratory follow-up already in place AND
  - Bicarbonate - Concentration ≥ 27 mmol/L AND
  - STOP-BANG score ≥ 3

Exclusion Criteria:
  - (Acute disease OR recently diagnosed chronic disease) AND (< 2 months) OR
  - Hospitalization for (respiratory OR metabolic OR cardiovascular event) AND (< 2 months) OR
  - (Renal insufficiency stage 4 OR 5) OR autoimmune disease OR viral hepatitis OR cirrhosis OR
  - Pregnant women OR
  - Patients already included in an interventional study AND (end of the study < 1 month) OR
  - NOT meeting any of the inclusion criteria
