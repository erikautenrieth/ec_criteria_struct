<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age ≥18 years
  -  BMI ≤40 kg/m2
  -  indication for hysterectomy for benign disease [OR] prophylactic surgery due to hereditary cancer
  -  indication for radical hysterectomy [AND] pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy [OR] for cervical cancer
  -  indication for modified radical hysterectomy [AND] pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy [OR] for endometrial cancer
  -  size of uterus [AND] vagina allows for retrieval by the vaginal route in cancer patients
  -  written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  known extensive intra-abdominal adhesions
  -  anaesthesiological contraindications to laparoscopy
  -  women with pacemaker [OR] other implants where electrosurgery is to be avoided
  -  women [AND] who have known defects of the hemostasis
  - [NOT] pregnancy
  -  other internal [OR] anatomical criteria that preclude a minimal invasive approach
  -  inability [NOT] to understand patient information
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
