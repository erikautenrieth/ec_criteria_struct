Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. NS-TAD probands operated on (n=16).
  2. FDR and SDR, aged 16 and above [AND] 
       1. At least two relatives willing to participate in the screening programme [AND]
       2. Relatives able to understand English.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Probands with syndromic aortopathies, including Marfan Syndrome, Loeys-Dietz Syndrome, Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome, Shprintzen-Goldberg syndrome, aneurysm-osteoarthritis syndrome, arterial tortuosity syndrome, and cutis laxa syndrome [OR]
  2. Probands with aortic lesions associated with trauma [OR] infections.
  3. Probands/relatives unable to give informed consent [NOT] able to provide consent.
