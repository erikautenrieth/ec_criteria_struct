Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - The patient or his / her representative has been informed about the implementation of the study, its objectives, its constraints, the rights of the patient AND must have received the newsletter AND has expressed no opposition to the study.
  - The patient must be an affiliate OR beneficiary of a health insurance plan.
  - The patient is at least 18 years old.
  - The patient has septic shock as defined by the 3rd International Conference for the definition of sepsis AND septic shock.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - The patient is in an exclusion period determined by a previous study OR has participated in a previous study that has not yet reached its exclusion period.
  - The patient is under guardianship, under curatorship, OR under the protection of justice.
  - The patient has already participated in this study.
  - The patient OR his / her representative refuses to participate in the study.
  - The patient is pregnant, parturient, OR is breastfeeding.
  - The patient is a minor NOT having reached the age of 18.
  - The patient is in therapeutic limitation OR moribund.
  - The patient has undergone OR is going to have scheduled OR emergency digestive surgery.
  - The patient is HIV-positive.
  - The patient has Child C cirrhosis.
  - The patient has progressive digestive neoplasia, digestive lymphoma, OR chronic inflammatory bowel disease (Crohn's disease...).

Note: I've added the logical operators to make the criteria more precise and clear, but please review them carefully to ensure they accurately reflect the original intent and meaning.
