Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - treatment-seeking individuals diagnosed with AUD according to the diagnostic statistical manual 5 (DSM-5)
  - fluent in English
  - past month excessive alcohol use (>7 drinks/week for woman, >14 drinks/week for men, >3 drinks/occasion for women >4 drinks/occasion for men) AND
  - (baseline Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A) score indicative of mild to moderate anxiety (score 8 to 23) OR baseline Perceived Stress Scale (PSS) score indicative of moderate stress (score 14 to 26)) AND
  - increase in alcohol craving following the baseline stress reactivity assessment.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Individuals will be excluded if they exhibit severe scores on the HAM-A, PSS, or post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) checklist (PCL-5) at the discretion of the admitting physician OR
  - physical dependence on alcohol as indicated by an Alcohol Use Disorders Inventory (AUDIT) score indicative of severe alcohol dependence (≥13 for women, ≥15 for men) OR
  - greater than mild substance use disorder on drugs other than alcohol, nicotine, and marijuana OR
  - contraindications for taking pioglitazone (e.g. medical conditions contraindicating pioglitazone pharmacotherapy or taking contraindicated medications) OR
  - pregnant, nursing, or planning on becoming pregnant during the course of the study OR
  - have any other illness, condition, or use of medications, which in the opinion of the PI and/or admitting physician would preclude safe and/or successful completion of the study AND NOT
  - deemed suitable for participation by the PI and/or admitting physician.
