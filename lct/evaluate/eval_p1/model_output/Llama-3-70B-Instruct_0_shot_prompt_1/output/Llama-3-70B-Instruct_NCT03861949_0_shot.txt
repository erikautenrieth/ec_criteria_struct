<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  adult patients of ASA physical status I [AND] II undergoing surgery under general anaesthesia

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  anticipated difficult mask ventilation/ [OR] intubation
  -  individuals [AND] who have significant cardiorespiratory [OR] cerebrovascular disease
  -  pregnant ladies
  -  patient [AND] who have a history of epilepsy
  -  body mass index >25 kg/m2
  -  hemoglobin <8 gm/dL
  -  patients [AND] who were non-ambulant for >24 h, [AND] having SpO2 < 97% while breathing in room air
  -  [NOT] no phobia to facemask.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
