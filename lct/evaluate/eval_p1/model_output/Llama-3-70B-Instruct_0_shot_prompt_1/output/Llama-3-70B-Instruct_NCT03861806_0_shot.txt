Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Unilateral ischemic stroke with residual arm weakness (Fugl-Meyer Upper Limb < 60) more than 6 months prior to enrollment AND ability to participate in the study for the entire duration.
  -  Ability to give informed consent AND understand the tasks involved.
  -  Age over 18 years AND NOT younger than 18 years.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Hemorrhagic Stroke OR other types of stroke that may affect the study outcome.
  -  Contraindications to TMS: history of seizure/epilepsy OR pacemaker OR other neurological disorders OR brain surgery OR metal implant/fragment in the head OR pregnancy.
  -  Taking medications or substances that are known to affect PAS-induced plasticity within the past 2 months: selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors OR dopamine OR dopamine agonists OR haloperidol OR lithium OR acetylcholinesterase inhibitors OR beta-blockers OR nimodipine OR levetiracetam OR ethosuximide OR benzodiazepines OR baclofen OR nicotine, NOT including medications that do not affect PAS-induced plasticity.
  -  Peripheral neuropathy OR history of nerve injury in the paretic upper limb, NOT including minor injuries that do not affect the study outcome.
  -  Social and/or personal circumstances that interfere with ability to return for all study visits, NOT including circumstances that can be accommodated by the study team.
