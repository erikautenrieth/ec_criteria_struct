Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  LMNA+ cases with pathogenic LMNA mutations [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation [AND] at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  DCMWT cases: patients with heart muscle failure [AND] with wild-type lamin gene [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation [AND] at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  HV (controls): matched to cases [OR] matched to DCMWT cases.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Participants with needle-phobia [AND] that would preclude blood-letting
  -  Participants unwilling to consent [OR] unable to provide informed consent
  -  Patients that have a conventional contraindication for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) [OR] other contraindications for MRI.
  -  Patients that have had a blood transfusion within the last month [OR] patients having haemodialysis [NOT] suitable for participation in the study.
