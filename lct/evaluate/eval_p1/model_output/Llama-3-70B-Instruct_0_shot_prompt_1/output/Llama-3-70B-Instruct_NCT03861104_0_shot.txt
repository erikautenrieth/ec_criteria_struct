Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - patients who will undergo septoplasty/septorhinoplasty procedures AND 
  - patients who will receive nasal packing at their septoplasty septorhinoplasty procedures

Exclusion Criteria:
  - patients who will NOT want to participate to the study OR
  - revision surgeries OR
  - if the patient is taking antidepressants during the last 6 months OR
  - if the patient has a history of a disease that can affect anxiety levels OR
  - if patient wants to be excluded from the study
