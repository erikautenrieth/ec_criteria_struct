<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Men and women, aged 18 to 60 years [AND] Body mass index 20 to 27 kg/m2
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  (Patients with active inflammatory bowel disease [OR] Patients with pre-existing Celiac disease [OR] exocrine pancreatic insufficiency [OR] small bowel malabsorption) [AND]
  (Patients with active bowel malignancy [OR] Patients with diabetes mellitus [OR] known/suspected motility disorders of the gut) [AND]
  (Patients with decompensated liver disease [OR] Patients on ezetimibe [OR] bile acid sequestrants) [AND]
  (Patients who are pregnant [OR] breastfeeding) [AND]
  (Patients with renal disease [OR] Patients on benzodiazepine) [AND]
  (Unstable cardiac disease [OR] respiratory disease) [AND]
  (Any changes to medication in the preceding month) [AND]
  (Patients with hypersensitive to Revestive [OR] any ingredient [OR] active [OR] a history of the drug within the last 5 years) [AND]
  [NOT] Patients with GI malignancy (GI tract, hepatobiliary, pancreatic)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
