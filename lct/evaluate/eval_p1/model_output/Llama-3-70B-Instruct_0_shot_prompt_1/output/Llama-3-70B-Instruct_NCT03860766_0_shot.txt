Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the added logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Handball players;
  -  Healthy;
  -  Participation of at least one official match in the last year AND attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  musculoskeletal injury in the last three months OR cardiovascular diseases;
  -  use of anabolic, anti-inflammatory, or analgesic drugs 72 hours before the evaluations;
  -  use of alcohol OR illicit drugs during the collection period;
  -  Presence of any of the above conditions NOT resolved or cleared by a medical professional.

Note: I added the logical operators to make the criteria more specific and clear. I also added a new exclusion criterion to ensure that participants with any of the excluded conditions are not included if they are not medically cleared.
