<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  BMI 30-40 kg/m2
  -  BMI 18-25 kg/m2; those with BMI up to 28kg/m2 will be included if waist circumference is <96cm [AND] males
  -  Males
  -  Aged 18-60yrs

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Acute illness in the preceding 6 weeks [OR] taking regular medication
  -  History of deep vein thrombosis [OR] clotting disorders
  -  Hypertension [OR] diabetes
  -  Any clinically significant findings at screening
  -  History of substance abuse [OR] demonstrating factors precluding safe MRI
  -  History of gastrointestinal motility disorders (e.g. gastroesophageal reflux disease [OR] irritable bowel syndrome, gastroparesis [OR] sphincter of Oddi dysfunction, etc.)
  -  Previous thoracic [OR] abdominal surgery
  -  Those who report having ≤3 bowel movements/week [OR] >2/day
  -  No [NOT] history of cancer
  -  Not [NOT] on hormone replacement therapy
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
