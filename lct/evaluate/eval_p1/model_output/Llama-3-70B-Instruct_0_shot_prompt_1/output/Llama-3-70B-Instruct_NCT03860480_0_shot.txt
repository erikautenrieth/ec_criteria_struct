Here is the edited version of the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
• Patients > 18 years old [AND] with ASA status I-III [AND] undergoing VATS for lobectomy [OR] wedge.

Non-inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients < 18 years old [OR]
  -  BMI > 35 [OR]
  -  Chronic pain history [AND] regular opioid [OR] gabapentinoids use during the 2 weeks before surgery [OR]
  -  Regular marijuana use [OR]
  -  History of thoracic surgery on the operated side [OR]
  -  Epidural analgesia preferred due to an anticipated high risk of conversion to thoracotomy [OR]
  -  Unable to communicate with the investigators [OR]
  -  Receiving anticoagulation [OR] experiencing any bleeding disorder [OR]
  -  Surgery for empyema [AND] sympathectomy [OR]
  -  Known allergy to local anesthetics [OR] fentanyl [OR] hydromorphone [OR]
  -  Active infection at injection sites [OR]
  -  Preexisting neurological deficit [OR] psychiatric illness [OR]
  -  Severe cardiovascular disease [OR]
  -  Liver failure [OR]
  -  Renal failure (estimated glomerular filtration rate <15 mL/min/1.73 m2) [OR]
  -  Pregnancy [OR]
  - [NOT] meeting all of the above criteria

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Perioperative conversion to thoracotomy [OR]
  -  Severe intra- [OR] postoperative bleeding [OR]
  -  Patients requiring postoperative mechanical ventilation [OR]
  -  Technical inability to proceed with the blocks [OR]
