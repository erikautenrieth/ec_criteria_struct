<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Men [OR] women age 18-64.
  -  Present to ED primary for management of Low Back Pain (LBP) [AND] functionally impairing back pain: A baseline score of greater than 5 on the Roland-Morris Disability Questionnaire.
  -  Musculoskeletal etiology of low back [AND] non-radicular pain.
  -  Pain duration <2 weeks (336 hours) [AND] non-traumatic LBP.
  -  Participant is to be discharged home [AND] [NOT] requiring further hospitalization.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Flank pain, that is pain originating from tissues lateral to the paraspinal muscles [OR] other conditions that may interfere with the study.
  -  [NOT] available for follow-up.
  -  Pregnant [OR] breastfeeding.
  -  Chronic pain syndrome [OR] other chronic pain conditions.
  -  Allergic to [OR] intolerant of investigational medications [OR] has a history of allergic reactions.
  -  Contra-indications to investigational medications [OR] has a history of adverse reactions.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
