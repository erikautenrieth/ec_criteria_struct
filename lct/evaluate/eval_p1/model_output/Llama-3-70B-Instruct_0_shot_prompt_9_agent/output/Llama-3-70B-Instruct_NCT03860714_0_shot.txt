<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Non-cardiac surgery patients [AND] 
       - Age is greater than or equal to 60 years old [AND] 
       - Han Nationality [AND] mother tongue is chinese [AND] 
       - The MMSE score: Illiteracy is greater than or equal to 17 points [OR] primary school is greater than or equal to 20 points [OR] higher secondary school is more than 24 points [AND] 
       - The people signed informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - The diagnosis of preoperative delirium [OR] 
       - The diagnosis Unstable hypertension（Such as：pheochromocytoma [OR] aortic dissection）[OR] 
       - Severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] congestive heart failure [OR] 
       - Severe liver dysfunction [OR] kidney dysfunction [OR] 
       - [NOT] Able to sign informed consent.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
