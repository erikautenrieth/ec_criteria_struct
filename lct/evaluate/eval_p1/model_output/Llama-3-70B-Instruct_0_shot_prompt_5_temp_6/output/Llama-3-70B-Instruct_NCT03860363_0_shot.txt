Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adult (age ≥ 18)
  -  Patient undergoing non-emergent [AND] cardiac surgery
  -  Anticipated requirement for mediastinal chest tube drainage

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Emergency surgery
  -  Re-do surgery
  -  Prior cardiac surgery
  -  Congenital cardiac disease [OR] deformation
  -  Transplant surgery
  -  Ventricular Assist Device Surgery
  -  Suspected pulmonary air leak
  -  Atrial fibrillation
  -  [NOT] in the opinion of the investigator the patient is suitable for the study [OR] has a legitimate reason including incarceration, pre-existing medical [OR] psychiatric condition, [OR] interfering medications
  -  [NOT] known previous [OR] concurrent enrollment in a clinical trial that, in the opinion of the investigator, might interfere with the objectives of this clinical trial
  -  [NOT] Pregnancy
