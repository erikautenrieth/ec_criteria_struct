Here is the edited version of the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age 55-80
  -  smoked at least 30 pack-years in lifetime
  -  if former smoker, quit smoking within the last 15 years
  -  English speaker [AND] able to understand and provide informed consent
  -  [NOT] prior history of lung cancer
  -  [NOT] had a lung cancer screening test within the last year
  -  PCP does [NOT] object to patient's participation
  -  have a scheduled visit at University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) internal medicine clinics.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  speaking a language other than English [OR] unable to understand and provide informed consent
  -  has a history of lung cancer [OR] history of cancer that may affect the lungs
  -  had a lung cancer screening test within the last year
  -  PCP objects to patient's participation [OR] has concerns about patient's ability to participate.

Note: I've added some phrases to make the criteria more specific and clear, but feel free to modify them according to your needs.
