Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] incorporated:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Men [OR] women age 18-64.
  -  Present to ED primary for management of Low Back Pain (LBP) [AND] requiring acute care.
  -  Functionally impairing back pain: A baseline score of greater than 5 on the Roland-Morris Disability Questionnaire [AND] impacting daily activities.
  -  Musculoskeletal etiology of low back [AND] no evidence of underlying systemic disease.
  -  Non-radicular pain [AND] no radiation to lower extremities.
  -  Pain duration <2 weeks (336 hours) [AND] no prior episodes of LBP.
  -  Non-traumatic LBP [AND] no history of recent trauma.
  -  Participant is to be discharged home [AND] has a caregiver available for support.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Flank pain, that is pain originating from tissues lateral to the paraspinal muscles [NOT] limited to back pain.
  -  [NOT] available for follow-up [OR] unable to commit to study schedule.
  -  Pregnant [OR] breastfeeding [NOT] using adequate contraception.
  -  Chronic pain syndrome [OR] fibromyalgia [NOT] controlled with current treatment.
  -  Allergic to [OR] intolerant of investigational medications [NOT] willing to undergo desensitization.
  -  Contra-indications to investigational medications [OR] currently taking medications that interact with investigational medications [NOT] able to stop medications for duration of study.
