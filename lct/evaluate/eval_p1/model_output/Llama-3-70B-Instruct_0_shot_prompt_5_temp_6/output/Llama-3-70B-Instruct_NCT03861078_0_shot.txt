Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria-- Participants must be:
  -  Healthy (determined by self-report) [AND]
  -  Between the ages of 18-55 years old
  -  Current cigarette smokers [AND]
  -  Willing to provide informed consent [AND]
  -  Able to attend the lab session [AND] abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required
  -  Agree to use designated products according to the study protocol [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Women if they are breast-feeding [OR] test positive for pregnancy (by urinalysis) at screening [AND] are female.
  -  Individuals who weigh less than 110 pounds [OR] have a body mass index (BMI) below 18.5.
  -  Participants with [NOT] a healthy self-report.
  -  Participants who are [NOT] current cigarette smokers.
  -  Participants who are [NOT] willing to provide informed consent.
  -  Participants who are [NOT] able to attend the lab session [OR] unable to abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required.
  -  Participants who do [NOT] agree to use designated products according to the study protocol.

Note: Some study details about the eligibility criteria are purposely omitted at this time to preserve scientific integrity. Full details will be posted at the conclusion of the study.
