Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - 18-35-year-old women
  - BMI: >18 [AND] < 30 kg/m2
  - Normal female karyotype
  - Normal uterus [AND] ovaries at transvaginal ultrasound

Exclusion Criteria:
  - > 35 years
  - PCO syndrome carriers
  - Null follicle aspiration
  - History of chemotherapy [OR] radiotherapy

Note: I inserted [AND] in the BMI criterion to indicate that both conditions (>18 and <30) must be met. I also inserted [AND] in the ultrasound criterion to indicate that both normal uterus and ovaries are required. In the exclusion criteria, I inserted [OR] in the chemotherapy/radiotherapy criterion to indicate that either condition is sufficient for exclusion.
