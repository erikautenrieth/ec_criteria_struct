Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. inflammatory bowel disease
  2. on methotrexate at appropriate dosing [AND] normal folate levels at onset of study
  3. treatment with folic acid [AND] ages 2-21 years

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. abnormal folate levels [OR] age > 21 [OR] age < 2
  Alternatively, the exclusion criteria can be written as:
  1. [NOT] normal folate levels
  2. age [NOT] between 2-21 years
