Here is the edited version of the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Resident Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Identified as having Alzheimer's [OR] related dementia
  -  Mild to moderate cognitive impairment (BIMS = 8-15, [AND] (MDS C0500) [AND] (nursing home residents only)
  -  Makes Self Understood = <1 (MDS B0700) [AND] present in medical record
  -  Understood By Others = <1 (MDS B0800) [AND] present in medical record
  -  Reside at the facility at least 2 weeks [AND] identified as a long-term resident
  -  Age >65 years
  -  Speaks English

Family Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Speaks English
  -  Willing [AND] able to watch the videos
  -  Willing [AND] able to complete short surveys [NOT] being unable to attend care conference to view the video (e.g. due to distance)

Staff Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Speaks English
  -  Willing [AND] able to watch the videos
  -  Willing [AND] able to complete short surveys

Resident Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] having limited communication capacity (e.g. aphasia)
  -  [NOT] comatose
  -  [NOT] cognitively intact
  -  [NOT] having severe cognitive impairment
