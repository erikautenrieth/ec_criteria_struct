Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
Nursing home residents with ≥2 of the following risk factors for atrial fibrillation:
  -  age ≥75 years
  -  female sex
  -  obstructive sleep apnea [OR] sleep disordered breathing
  -  peripheral vascular disease
  -  diabetes mellitus
  -  obesity (body mass index [BMI] >30 kg/m2) [AND] waist circumference >102 cm
  -  hypertension
  -  congestive heart failure [OR] left ventricular systolic dysfunction

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] prior diagnosis of AF
  -  [NOT] continuous rhythm monitoring over the past year
  -  [NOT] pacemaker or implantable cardioverter defibrillator in situ
