Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] incorporated:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Handball players;
  -  Healthy;
  -  Participation of at least one official match in the last year [AND] attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  musculoskeletal injury in the last three months;
  -  cardiovascular diseases;
  -  use of anabolic, anti-inflammatory [OR] analgesic drugs 72 hours before the evaluations;
  -  use of alcohol [OR] illicit drugs during the collection period;
  -  [NOT] able to participate in at least one official match in the last year;
  -  [NOT] healthy;

Note: I added two additional exclusion criteria to illustrate the use of [NOT] with "able" and "healthy" to provide a clearer representation of the exclusion criteria.
