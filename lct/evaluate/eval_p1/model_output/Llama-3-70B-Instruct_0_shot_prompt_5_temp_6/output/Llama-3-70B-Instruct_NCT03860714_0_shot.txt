Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Non-cardiac surgery patients;
       -  Age is greater than or equal to 60 years old [AND] ③Han Nationality [AND] mother tongue is chinese;
       -  The MMSE score: Illiteracy is greater than or equal to 17 points [OR] primary school is greater than or equal to 20 points [OR] higher secondary school is more than 24 points;
       -  The people signed informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  The diagnosis of preoperative delirium；
       -  [NOT] Stable hypertension [AND] (Such as：pheochromocytoma [OR] aortic dissection）；
       -  ③Severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] congestive heart failure；
       -  ④[NOT] Mild liver and kidney dysfunction [AND] Severe liver and kidney dysfunction;
