Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with a endovenous pacemaker [OR] defibrillator indication.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with [NOT] pre-existing ipsilateral pacing electrode.
  -  Patients who have undergone previous ipsilateral lymphadenectomy.
  -  Patients with an indication of cardiac resynchronization therapy [AND] no other indication for a pacemaker or defibrillator.
