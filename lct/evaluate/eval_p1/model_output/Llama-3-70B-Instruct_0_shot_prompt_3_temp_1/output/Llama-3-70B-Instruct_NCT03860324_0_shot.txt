Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Surgical plan for total hip replacement
  -  Signing of consent form to participate in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient [NOT] providing refusal
  -  BMI > 40 kg/m2
  -  Surgical plan for revision of hip replacement
  -  Patient [NOT] able to quantify pain level
  -  Chronic kidney disease [AND] with a Glomerular Filtration Rate < 50ml/min
  -  Previously medicated [OR] taking opioids
  -  Patient [NOT] able to perform the surgery [AND] with spinal block
  -  Allergy [OR] hypersensitivity to local anesthetics
  -  Infection in the site of the Erector Spinae Plane [OR] Fascia Iliaca block
  -  Allergy [OR] contraindication [OR] intolerance to the use of morphine
