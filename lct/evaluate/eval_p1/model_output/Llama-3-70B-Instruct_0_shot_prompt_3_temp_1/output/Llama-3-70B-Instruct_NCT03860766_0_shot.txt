Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Handball players;
  -  Healthy [AND] with no known medical conditions;
  -  Participation of at least one official match in the last year [AND] attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Presence of musculoskeletal injury in the last three months [OR], history of musculoskeletal injury that [NOT] fully recovered;
  -  Diagnosis of cardiovascular diseases [OR] any cardiovascular condition;
  -  Use of anabolic, anti-inflammatory [OR] analgesic drugs 72 hours before the evaluations;
  -  Use of alcohol [OR] illicit drugs during the collection period [OR] any substance abuse [NOT] disclosed;
  -  Inability to participate in the study due to [NOT] meeting the inclusion criteria [OR] any other reason that may interfere with the study protocol.
