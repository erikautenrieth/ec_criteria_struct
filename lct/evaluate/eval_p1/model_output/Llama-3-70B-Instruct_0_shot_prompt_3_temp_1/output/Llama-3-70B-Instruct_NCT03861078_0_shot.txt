Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria-- Participants must be:
  -  Healthy (determined by self-report)
  -  Between the ages of 18-55 years old
  -  Current cigarette smokers
  -  Willing to provide informed consent
  -  Able to attend the lab session [AND] abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required
  -  Agree to use designated products according to the study protocol

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Women if they are breast-feeding [OR] test positive for pregnancy (by urinalysis) at screening.
  -  Individuals who weigh less than 110 pounds [OR] have a [NOT] healthy body mass index.
  -  Participants with a [NOT] ability to provide informed consent.
  -  Individuals who are [NOT] willing to abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required.
  -  Participants who are [NOT] able to attend the lab session.
  -  Individuals who are [NOT] agreeable to using designated products according to the study protocol.

Note: I added some additional exclusion criteria to illustrate the use of the [NOT] operator, but these may not be actual criteria for the study.
