Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  BMI 30-40 kg/m2
  -  BMI 18-25 kg/m2; those with BMI up to 28kg/m2 will be included if waist circumference is <96cm [AND] they have a waist circumference <96cm
  -  Males
  -  Aged 18-60yrs

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Acute illness in the preceding 6 weeks [OR] any acute illness
  -  Taking regular medication [NOT] taking occasional medication
  -  History of deep vein thrombosis [OR] clotting disorders
  -  Hypertension [NOT] controlled hypertension
  -  Diabetes [NOT] well-controlled diabetes
  -  Any clinically significant findings at screening [NOT] minor, insignificant findings
  -  History of substance abuse [NOT] past history of substance abuse with no current abuse
  -  Demonstrating factors precluding safe MRI [NOT] demonstrating factors allowing safe MRI
  -  History of gastrointestinal motility disorders (e.g. gastroesophageal reflux disease [OR] irritable bowel syndrome [OR] gastroparesis [OR] sphincter of Oddi dysfunction, etc.)
  -  Previous thoracic [OR] abdominal surgery
  -  Those who report having ≤3 bowel movements/week [OR] >2/day [NOT] those with normal bowel movements
