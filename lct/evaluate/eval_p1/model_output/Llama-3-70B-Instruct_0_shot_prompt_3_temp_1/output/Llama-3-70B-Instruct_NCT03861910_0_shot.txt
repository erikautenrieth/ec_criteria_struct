Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age 1-12 months
  -  IgE-mediated CMA

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  cow's milk protein-induced anaphylaxis [OR] 
  -  food protein induced enterocolitis syndrome [OR] 
  -  other food allergies [OR] 
  -  other allergic diseases [OR] 
  -  non-CMA-related atopic eczema [OR] 
  -  eosinophilic disorders of the gastrointestinal tract [OR] 
  -  chronic systemic diseases [OR] 
  -  congenital cardiac defects [OR] 
  -  active tuberculosis [OR] 
  -  autoimmune diseases [OR] 
  -  immunodeficiency [OR] 
  -  chronic inflammatory bowel diseases [OR] 
  -  celiac disease [OR] 
  -  cystic fibrosis [OR] 
  -  metabolic diseases [OR] 
  -  malignancy [OR] 
  -  chronic pulmonary diseases [OR] 
  -  malformations of the gastrointestinal [AND] respiratory tract [OR] 
  -  [NOT] able to administer prebiotics [OR] [NOT] able to administer probiotics during the 4 weeks before enrolment.
