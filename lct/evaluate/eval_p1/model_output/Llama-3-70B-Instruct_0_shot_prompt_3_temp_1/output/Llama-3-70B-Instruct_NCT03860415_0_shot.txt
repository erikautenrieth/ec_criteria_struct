Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Chronic (more than three months) ESBL colonization of the same strain as determined by species, phenotype [AND] antibiogram.
  -  Patients must be able to swallow sachets.
  -  Must be able to speak [AND] understand Swedish.
  -  Must have a permanent residence in Sweden.
  -  Must be able to sign informed consent in Swedish.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Immunosuppression (i.e. chemotherapy, treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors) [OR] immunodeficiency.
  -  [NOT] able to care for themselves, i.e. in patient care.
  -  Psychiatric disorder [OR] alcohol [OR] substance abuse.
  -  [NOT] able to think clearly, i.e. dementia.
  -  Presence of Invasive catheters.
