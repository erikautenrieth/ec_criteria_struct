Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Grade IV Glioblastoma, receiving chemotherapy [OR] TTfields.
  -  Progressive [OR] recurrent disease requiring second line treatment.
  -  Evidence of progression of disease by brain MRI [AND] are planned to receive chemotherapy.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Current use of another beta blocker that cannot be switched to carvedilol at the onset of the trial [NOT] able to be switched.
  -  Systolic blood pressure <90 mmHg [OR] heart rate <60 bpm [NOT] using hypertensive medications.
  -  [NOT] Tolerant of beta blockers (allergy to beta blockers).
  -  Severe [OR] uncontrolled asthma [OR] COPD (chronic obstructive pulmonary disease) [NOT] controlled.
