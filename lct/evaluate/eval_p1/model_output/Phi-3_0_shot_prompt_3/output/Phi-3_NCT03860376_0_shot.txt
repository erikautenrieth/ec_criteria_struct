 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  [AND] Patients aged 21 years or younger at the time of enrollment on this study of any gender, race or ethnicity.
  -  [AND] Subjects with suspected or confirmed diagnosis of recurrent or refractory cancer
  -  [AND] Subjects who are scheduled for or have recently had biopsy or tumor excised ([NOT] blood cancers) or bone marrow aspirate ([NOT] solid tumors)
  -  [AND] Subjects willing to have a blood draw or buccal swab done for the purposes of genetic testing
  -  [AND] Subjects or their parents or legal guardians willing to sign [NOT] informed consent
  -  [AND] Subjects aged 7 to 17 willing to sign [NOT] assent

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [AND] Subjects who do not have malignant tissue available and accessible
  -  [AND] The amount of excised malignant tissue is not sufficient for the ex vivo drug testing and/or genetic profiling.
  -  [AND] Patients with newly diagnosed tumors and tumors that have a high (>90%) cure rate with safe standard therapy.
