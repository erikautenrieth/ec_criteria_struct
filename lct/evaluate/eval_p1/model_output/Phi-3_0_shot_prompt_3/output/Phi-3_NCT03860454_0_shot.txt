 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  [AND] LMNA+ cases with [AND] pathogenic LMNA mutations [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  [AND] DCMWT cases: patients with heart muscle failure [AND] wild-type lamin gene [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  [AND] HV (controls): matched to cases.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [AND] Needle-phobia that would preclude blood-letting [AND] participants unwilling to consent [AND] patients that have a conventional contraindication for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).
  -  [AND] Patients that have had a blood transfusion within the last month [AND] patients having haemodialysis [AND] will be excluded.
