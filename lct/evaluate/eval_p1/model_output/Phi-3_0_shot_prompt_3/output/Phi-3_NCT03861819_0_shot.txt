 Inclusion Criteria:

- Ages [18 AND 59]

- Histologically confirmed nonalcoholic steatohepatitis and fibrosis [NOT stage 4 AND (stage 1 OR stage 2 OR stage 3)] from liver biopsy

- Able to row on an indoor rowing apparatus

- Not participating in regular physical exercise [NOT (more than 60 minutes of moderate intensity OR 30 minutes of vigorous intensity exercise per week)]

- Not actively trying to lose weight through a dietary approach

- No absolute contra-indications to exercise [NOT (recent <6 months acute cardiac event unstable angina OR uncontrolled dysrhythmias causing symptoms OR hemodynamic compromise OR symptomatic aortic stenosis OR uncontrolled symptomatic heart failure OR acute pulmonary embolus OR acute myocarditis OR pericarditis OR suspected or known dissecting aneurysm OR acute systemic infection)]

- No other form of chronic liver disease [NOT confirmed by serology AND histology]

- No liver cirrhosis [NOT stage 4 fibrosis on liver biopsy OR NOT a clinical diagnosis of cirrhosis as ascertained by a hepatologist]

- No coronary artery disease [NOT present]

- No chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [NOT present]

- No diagnosis of malignancy [NOT except non-melanoma skin cancer]

- No past or present history of portal hypertension [NOT (esophageal varices OR ascites OR hepatic encephalopathy)]

- No use of immunosuppression [NOT present]

- No chronic nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory medication [NOT present]

- No more than [14 drinks per week] if male OR [7 drinks per week] if female in the last 2 years

- Cognitively able to provide consent [AND able to read, write, and understand English]

- Transportation to the exercise and testing facility [NOT present]
