 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adult [AND] (age ≥ 18)
  -  Patient [AND] undergoing non-emergent cardiac surgery [AND] [NOT] Emergency surgery
  -  Anticipated requirement for mediastinal chest tube drainage [AND] [NOT] Prior cardiac surgery [AND] [NOT] Transplant surgery [AND] [NOT] Ventricular Assist Device Surgery [AND] [NOT] Suspected pulmonary air leak [AND] [NOT] Atrial fibrillation [AND] [NOT] In the opinion of the investigator the patient is unsuitable for the study for any other legitimate reason including incarceration, pre-existing medical or psychiatric condition, or interfering medications [AND] [NOT] Known previous or concurrent enrollment in a clinical trial that, in the opinion of the investigator, might interfere with the objectives of this clinical trial

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Emergency surgery [OR] Re-do surgery [OR] Prior cardiac surgery [OR] Congenital cardiac disease or deformation [OR] Transplant surgery [OR] Ventricular Assist Device Surgery [OR] Suspected pulmonary air leak [OR] Atrial fibrillation [OR] In the opinion of the investigator the patient is unsuitable for the study for any other legitimate reason including incarceration, pre-existing medical or psychiatric condition, or interfering medications [OR] Known previous or concurrent enrollment in a clinical trial that, in the opinion of the investigator, might interfere with the objectives of this clinical trial
