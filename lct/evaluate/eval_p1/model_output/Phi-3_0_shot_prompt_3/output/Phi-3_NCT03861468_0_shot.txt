 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients aged from 18 to 80 years [AND]
  -  Obese [AND] [NOT] BMI < 30kg/m² [AND] BMI ≥ 30kg/m²
  -  Referred by GPs to the medical analysis laboratory for usual biological assessment [AND]
  -  Patients with no respiratory follow-up already in place [AND] [NOT] respiratory follow-up already in place
  -  Bicarbonate - Concentration ≥ 27 mmol/L [AND]
  -  STOP-BANG score ≥ 3 [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Acute disease [OR] recently diagnosed chronic disease (< 2 months) [AND] [NOT] (< 2 months)
  -  Hospitalization for respiratory [OR] metabolic [OR] cardiovascular event (< 2 months) [AND] [NOT] (< 2 months)
  -  Renal insufficiency stage 4 or 5 [OR] autoimmune disease [OR] viral hepatitis [OR] cirrhosis [AND] [NOT] (stage 4 or 5, autoimmune disease, viral hepatitis, cirrhosis)
  -  Pregnant women [AND] [NOT] not pregnant
  -  Patients already included in an interventional study [AND] (end of the study < 1 month) [AND] [NOT] (end of the study ≥ 1 month)
