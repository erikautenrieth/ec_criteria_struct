 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  [AND] Surgical plan for total hip replacement
  -  [AND] Signing of consent form to participate in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [OR] Patient refusal
  -  [OR] BMI > 40 kg/m2
  -  [AND] Surgical plan for revision of hip replacement
  -  [AND] Patient unable to quantify pain level
  -  [AND] Chronic kidney disease with a Glomerular Filtration Rate < 50ml/min
  -  [AND] Previously medicated with opioids
  -  [AND] Patient unable to perform the surgery with spinal block
  -  [AND] Allergy to local anesthetics
  -  [AND] Infection in the site of the Erector Spinae Plane or Fascia Iliaca block
  -  [AND] Allergy or contraindication to the use of morphine
