 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  [AND] Patients with an unilateral THA or TKA, which they received for the treatment of osteoarthritis ([Patient groups] 1+2)
  -  [AND] Patients diagnosed with unilateral OA of the knee or hip planned for a treatment with THA or TKA ([Patient groups] 3+4)
  -  [AND] Age ≥ 30 years ([Healthy control group])

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [AND] Body mass index > 35kg/m2
  -  [AND] Use of walking aids
  -  [AND] Neuromuscular disorders affecting gait
  -  [AND] Inability to follow procedures due to psychological disorders or dementia
  -  [AND] Diagnosed hip or knee OA ([Healthy control group])
  -  [AND] Current pain in the lower extremities or lower back ([Healthy control group])
  -  [AND] Rheumatoid Arthritis ([Healthy control group])
  -  [AND] Previous corrective osteotomy ([Healthy control group])
  -  [AND] Regular intake of pain medication (more than 4 times per week) ([Healthy control group])
