 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  patients hospitalized with [AND] primary diagnosis of acute or subacute ischemic cerebral infarction [AND] 18 years of age or older
  -  English speaking [AND] admitted to hospital Sunday through Thursday [AND] patient or responsible primary caregiver able to understand and provide informed consent [AND] prognosis for survival greater than 6 months [AND] one or more vascular risk factors ([NOT] HTN [OR] DLP [OR] current smoker [OR] DM [OR] obesity) [AND] live in NWA area (Benton, Boone, Carroll, Madison, and Washington counties) [AND] have a working phone.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  admitted with transient ischemic attack [OR] admitted with an intracerebral hemorrhage [OR] experience an in-hospital ischemic cerebral infarction [OR] hospital to hospital transfer patients [OR] nursing home as primary residence before or after discharge [OR] hospice [OR] inability to comply with post discharge follow up.
