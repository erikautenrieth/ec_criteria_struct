 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients who fulfilled the following criteria were included in the study:
  -  [Adult renal transplant patients (≥21years)] AND
  -  [Informed consent] AND
  -  [Stable maintenance subcutaneous ESA therapy with constant dose interval during the last 2 months] AND
  -  [Haemoglobin (Hb) ≥10mg/dL (Hct 32%)] AND
  -  [Transferrin saturation (TSAT) ≥20% (during the screening phase)] AND
  -  [serum ferritin ≥100 ng/mL]

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [Acute or chronic bleeding, or erythrocyte transfusion, within the preceding 8 weeks] OR [change in Hb level ≥ 2 g/dL during screening phase] AND
  -  [Hemolytic anemia] OR
  -  [Recent infection or rejection] OR
  -  [Diastolic blood pressure >100mmHg] OR [discontinuation of ESA due to hypertension in the 6 months prior to study] AND
  -  [Vitamin B12 or folic acid deficiency] OR
  -  [Uncontrolled or secondary hyperparathyroidism] OR
  -  [Acute or chronic systemic inflammatory disease and/or C-reactive protein (CRP) >30 mg/L] OR
  -  [Hemodialysis due to failure of a kidney transplant] OR
  -  [malignancy]
