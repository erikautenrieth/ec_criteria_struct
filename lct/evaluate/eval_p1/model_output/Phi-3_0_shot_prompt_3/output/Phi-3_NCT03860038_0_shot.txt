 Inclusion criteria:
1. Age [≥] 18, male [OR] female;
2. Subject must have had documented MM;
3. At screening phase, subject must have measurable disease [AND];
4. Subject is in a state of progressive disease [AND];
5. Subject must have life expectancy of no less than 6 months [AND];
6. Subject must have an ECOG performance status score of 0 [AND] [OR] 2;

Exclusion criteria:
1. Subject has received anti-CD38 monoclonal antibody treatment previously [AND];
2. Subject has received CAR-T cell therapy previously [AND];
3. Subject has previously received allogenic stem cell transplant [OR] subject has received autologous stem cell transplant within 3 months before administration of the study agent [AND];
4. Primary refractory multiple myeloma [AND] subject failed to generate any minimal response [OR] any degree of response to any therapy;
5. Subject has received anti-myeloma treatment within 4 weeks [OR] 5 PK half-lives of the treatment, whichever longer, before the first study agent administration [AND].
