 Inclusion Criteria:
1. Adults [AGE AND] ≥ 18 years old
2. High risk for cardiovascular morbidity and mortality [AND] such as prior history of clinical cardiovascular disease [AND] history of familial hypercholesterolemia
3. LDL-C within the past 6 months [AND] above recommended level despite maximal tolerated statin therapy [AND] ± ezetimibe for the past 3 months.

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Current treatment [NOT] with PCSK9 inhibitor
2. Current participation [NOT] in investigational study
3. Prior participation [NOT] in the GOAL program
