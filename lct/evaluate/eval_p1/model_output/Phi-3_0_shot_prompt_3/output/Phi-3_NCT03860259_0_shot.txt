 Inclusion Criteria:
1. [AND] Subject is greater than 18 years of age [AND] Subject is willing and able to provide informed consent [AND] Subject is scheduled to undergo elective rotator cuff surgery [AND] Subject has consented to an interscalene block

Exclusion Criteria:
1. [AND] Opioid dependence [AND] Any subject diagnosed with a chronic pain condition which daily opioid use is needed
2. [AND] Anatomical malformation [NOT] (which in the investigator's opinion may interfere with the placement of the nerve block)
3. [AND] Raynaud's disease diagnosis
4. [AND] Vasculopathy
5. [AND] Patient refusal
