 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  [AND] undergoing unilateral or bilateral breast surgery with at least moderate postoperative pain anticipated
  -  [AND] analgesic plan includes a single-injection peripheral nerve block(s)
  -  [AND] age 18 years or older

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [OR] morbid obesity as defined by a body mass index > 40 (BMI=weight in kg / [height in meters]2)
  -  [OR] renal insufficiency (preoperative creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL)
  -  [AND] chronic opioid use (daily use within the 2 weeks prior to surgery and duration of use > 4 weeks)
  -  [OR] history of opioid abuse
  -  [AND] any comorbidity which results in moderate or severe functional limitation inability to communicate with the investigators or hospital staff
  -  [OR] pregnancy
  -  [AND] planned regional analgesic with perineural catheter placement
  -  [OR] incarceration
