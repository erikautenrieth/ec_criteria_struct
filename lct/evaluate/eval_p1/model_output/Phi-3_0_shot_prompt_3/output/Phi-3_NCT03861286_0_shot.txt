 Inclusion Criteria:
  1. [AND] Patients undergoing first time cardiopulmonary bypass surgery for correction of atrial septal defects [OR] tissue mitral valve repair
  2. [AND] Receiving intravenous Heparin for cardiopulmonary bypass [AND] [NOT] within last 7 days

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [AND] Concomitant Aortic Stenosis [AND] which is associated with an acquired von Willebrand Syndrome (aVWS)
  -  [AND] Patients with a baseline platelet count of <100x109/L [OR] [AND] [NOT] patients receiving heparin therapy prior to CPB within last 7 days
  -  [AND] Patients with a known coagulation factor deficiency [OR] platelet function disorder [AND] [NOT] patients receiving heparin therapy prior to CPB within last 7 days
