 Inclusion Criteria:
  - [age 20-34 years] AND [body mass index (BMI) 18-29kg/m^2];
  - [regular menstrual cycle of 26-35 days] AND [AMH levels age appropriate (≥2.3 ng/ml)];
  - [early follicular-phase serum concentration of FSH within normal limits (1-12 IU/l)]

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [women with diabetes and other metabolic diseases] OR [women with hereditary genetic diseases];
  - [women with heart disease, QT prolongation, heart failure] OR [elevated liver enzymes, liver failure, hepatitis];
  - [women with inflammatory or autoimmune disease] OR [abnormal karyotype];
  - [endometriosis stage III/IV] OR [history of recurrent miscarriage];
  - [severe OHSS in a previous stimulation cycle] OR [any contraindication for the use of gonadotrophins].
