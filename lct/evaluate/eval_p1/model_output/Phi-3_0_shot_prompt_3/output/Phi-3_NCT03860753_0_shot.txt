 Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] treatment-seeking individuals diagnosed with AUD diagnostic statistical manual 5 (DSM-5)
  - [AND] fluent in English
  - [AND] past month excessive alcohol use ([OR] >7 drinks/week for woman [AND] [OR] >14 drinks/week for men [AND] [OR] >3 drinks/occasion for women [AND] [OR] >4 drinks/occasion for men)
  - [AND] baseline Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A) or Perceived Stress Scale (PSS) core indicative of mild to moderate anxiety ([NOT] score <8 [AND] [NOT] score >23 for HAM-A [AND] [NOT] score <14 [AND] [NOT] score >26 for PSS)
  - [AND] increase in alcohol craving following the baseline stress reactivity assessment.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] individuals will be excluded for exhibiting severe scores on the HAM-A, PSS, or post-traumatic (PTSD) checklist (PCL-5) ([NOT] at the discretion of the admitting physician)
  - [AND] physical dependence on alcohol Alcohol Use Disorders Inventory (AUDIT) score indicative of severe alcohol dependence ([OR] ≥13 for women [AND] [OR] ≥15 for men)
  - [AND] greater than mild substance use disorder on drugs other than alcohol, nicotine, and marijuana
  - [AND] contraindications for taking pioglitazone; medical conditions contraindicating pioglitazone pharmacotherapy or taking contraindicated medications
  - [AND] be pregnant, nursing, or planning on becoming pregnant during the course of the study
  - [AND] have any other illness, condition, or use of medications, which in the opinion of the PI and/or admitting physician would preclude safe and/or successful completion of the study.
