 Inclusion Criteria:
  - [Age ≥ 18 years old] AND [Confirmed diagnosis of perianal Crohn's disease] AND [On biologics or will start biologics]

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [Patients who have perianal fistula due to causes other than Crohn's disease] AND [Fistulation into other organs, including bladder and intestine] AND [Patients who have allergic reaction / contraindications to anti-TNF] AND [Patients who have active cancer] AND [Patients who have contraindications for MRI] AND [Known pregnancy]

For patients who refuse to participate in the tight monitoring arm, they will be consented and recruited to the control arm for comparison, provided they meet the inclusion criteria and do not meet any of the exclusion criteria.
