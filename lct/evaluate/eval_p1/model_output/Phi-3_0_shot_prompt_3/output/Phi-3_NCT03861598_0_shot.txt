 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Grade IV Glioblastoma [AND] receiving chemotherapy [AND] with or without TTfields.
  -  Progressive or recurrent disease [AND] requiring second line treatment [AND] [OR] evidence of progression of disease by brain MRI [AND] planned to receive chemotherapy.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Current use of another beta blocker [AND] that cannot be switched to carvedilol [NOT] at the onset of the trial.
  -  Systolic blood pressure <90 mmHg [AND] heart rate <60 bpm [AND] without hypertensive medications.
  -  Allergy to beta blockers [NOT].
  -  Severe or uncontrolled asthma [OR] COPD (chronic obstructive pulmonary disease) [NOT].
