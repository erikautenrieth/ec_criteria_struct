 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  BMI [18-25] kg/m^2 [AND] [BMI 28-40] kg/m^2; those with BMI up to 28kg/m^2 will be included if waist circumference is <[96]cm.
  -  Males [AND] Aged [18-60]yrs.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Acute illness in the preceding [6] weeks [AND] Taking regular medication [OR] History of deep vein thrombosis or clotting disorders [OR] Hypertension [OR] Diabetes [OR] Any clinically significant findings at screening [OR] History of substance abuse [OR] Demonstrating factors precluding safe MRI [OR] History of gastrointestinal motility disorders (e.g. gastroesophageal reflux disease -irritable bowel syndrome, gastroparesis, sphincter of Oddi dysfunction, etc.) [OR] Previous thoracic or abdominal surgery.
  -  Those who report having ≤[3] bowel movements/week [OR] >[2] bowel movements/day.
