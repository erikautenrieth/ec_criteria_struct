 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  [AND] Patients with NEC (stages I-III Bell's Classification)
  -  [AND] Weight greater or equal to 750 g.
  -  [AND] Gestational age < 36 weeks.
  -  [AND] Patients whose parents [AND] consent to participate in the study.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [AND] Patients with major congenital anomalies
  -  [AND] No antecedent of limb ischemia/limb thrombotic events.
  -  [AND] No antecedent diagnosis of occlusive arterial or venous thrombosis
  -  [AND] Hemodynamic instability
