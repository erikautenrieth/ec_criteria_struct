Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - patients with one or more events of hip dislocation after a primary THA
  - controls with a primary THA [AND] without events of hip dislocation

Exclusion Criteria:
- patients with a history of hip dislocation prior to primary THA [OR] with a diagnosis of osteoporosis [OR] with a history of cancer [OR] with a history of neurological disorders [OR] with a history of inflammatory bowel disease [NOT] taking medications that may affect bone metabolism
