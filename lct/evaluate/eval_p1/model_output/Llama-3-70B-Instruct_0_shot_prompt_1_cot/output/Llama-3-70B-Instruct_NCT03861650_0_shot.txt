Inclusion Criteria:
1. Patients free from any disease affecting bone formation [AND] of both sexes was included in the study.
2. Patients younger than 25 years old [AND] 
3. Patients included in the study were post traumatic patients [OR] hemifacial microsomia [OR] Treacher Collins [OR] post ankylotic mandibular deformity.
4. Distraction range was between 15-20 mm.

Exclusion Criteria:
1. The presence of uncontrolled systemic disease [OR] 
2. Previous radiotherapy on head and neck region [OR] 
3. Chemotherapy within the past 12-month period [OR] 
4. An active infection at the distraction site [NOT] controlled by antibiotics.
