Here is the edited version of the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Handball players;
  - Healthy [AND]
  - Participation of at least one official match in the last year [AND] attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - musculoskeletal injury in the last three months [OR]
  - cardiovascular diseases [OR]
  - use of anabolic, anti-inflammatory and analgesic drugs 72 hours before the evaluations [OR]
  - use of alcohol and illicit drugs during the collection period [OR]
  - NOT meeting the inclusion criteria above.
