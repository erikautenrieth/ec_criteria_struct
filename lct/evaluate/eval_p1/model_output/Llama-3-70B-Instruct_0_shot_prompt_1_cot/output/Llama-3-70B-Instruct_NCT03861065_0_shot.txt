Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the added logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients with high risk class II abdominal wounds OR high risk class III abdominal wounds OR high risk class IV abdominal wounds
  - Undergoing laparotomy for gynecologic related disorders AND patients undergoing laparotomy for both benign AND malignant diagnoses
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Pregnancy OR allergy to triclosan OR patients undergoing HIPEC
