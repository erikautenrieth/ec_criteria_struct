Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Age above 18 AND
  - ASA classification I to II AND
  - Indication for colonoscopy with propofol sedation AND
  - Written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Pregnancy 

Note: Since there is only one exclusion criterion, no logical operators are needed. If there were multiple exclusion criteria, we could use [OR] to connect them, e.g.:

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Pregnancy OR
  - Breastfeeding OR
  - Severe cardiovascular disease
