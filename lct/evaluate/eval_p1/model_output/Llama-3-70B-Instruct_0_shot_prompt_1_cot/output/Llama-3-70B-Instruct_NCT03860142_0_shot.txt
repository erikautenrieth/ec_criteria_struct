Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Children whose age ≥ 24 months AND ≤ 36 months
  -  Children born full-term OR prematurely (<33 SA) at Nice University Hospital

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Children with congenital pathologies (with malformations in the oro-facial sphere) OR
  -  Children with neurological AND developmental pathologies unrelated to prematurity OR
  -  Children with ENT deformities OR
  -  Children whose parents have a language barrier (i.e., Parental language barrier NOT met)
