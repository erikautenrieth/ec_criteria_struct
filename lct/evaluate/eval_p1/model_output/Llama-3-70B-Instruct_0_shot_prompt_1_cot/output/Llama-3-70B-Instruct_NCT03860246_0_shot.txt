Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
Nursing home residents with ≥2 of the following risk factors for atrial fibrillation:
  - age ≥75 years [OR]
  - female sex [OR]
  - obstructive sleep apnea [OR]
  - peripheral vascular disease [OR]
  - diabetes mellitus [OR]
  - obesity (body mass index [BMI] >30 kg/m2) [OR]
  - hypertension [OR]
  - congestive heart failure [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  - prior diagnosis of AF [OR]
  - continuous rhythm monitoring over the past year [OR]
  - pacemaker or implantable cardioverter defibrillator in situ [NOT]

Note: I added the [AND] operator at the end of the inclusion criteria to indicate that the participant must have at least two of the listed risk factors. I used [OR] operators to separate each risk factor and exclusion criterion, indicating that the presence of any one of them is sufficient. I used the [NOT] operator to indicate that the presence of a pacemaker or implantable cardioverter defibrillator is an exclusion criterion.
