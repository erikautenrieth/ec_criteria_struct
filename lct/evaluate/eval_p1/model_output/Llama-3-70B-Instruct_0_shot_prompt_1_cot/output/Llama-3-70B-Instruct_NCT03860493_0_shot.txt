Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] incorporated:

Inclusion Criteria:
1. Patients of both genders
2. Minimum age: 18 years old AND
3. Spoken AND written command of the language spoken in the country's center
4. Ability to understand AND follow the study procedures AND sign the informed consent
5. Having undergone open surgical procedures

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Known allergies to iodides OR
2. Known history of coagulopathy OR
3. Women who are pregnant OR breastfeeding, OR for whom possibility of pregnancy was NOT ruled out
