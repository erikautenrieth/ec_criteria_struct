Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Age 8yr-18yrs [AND]
  - Female [AND]
  - Likely to have order placed for transabdominal pelvic ultrasound/ovarian ultrasound [AND]
  - No history of pelvic or bladder reconstructive surgery [NOT]

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Pregnancy (known) [OR]
  - Critically ill patients [OR]
  - Patients with known renal or genitourinary structural abnormalities or prior pelvic/genitourinary surgery [OR]
  - Chronic renal disease [OR]
  - Patients presenting outside the defined treatment windows
