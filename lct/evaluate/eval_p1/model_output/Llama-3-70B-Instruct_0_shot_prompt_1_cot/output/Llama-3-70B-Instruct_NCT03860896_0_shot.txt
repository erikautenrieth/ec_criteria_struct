Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the added logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients aged 18-74 AND receiving therapy for active ulcerative colitis confirmed by Mayo Score assessment AND evidence of colonic inflammation.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Evidence of Crohn's disease OR indeterminate colitis OR presence of bacterial or parasitic infection.
  -  Patients receiving biologic agents AND experimental agents are excluded.
  -  Patients with evidence of Crohn's disease NOT receiving therapy for active ulcerative colitis are excluded.
