Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  18 years of age or older
  -  speak English
  -  be prescribed to take two or more medications per day AND manage their own medications.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  have a significant cognitive impairment OR unwilling to use a novel pillbox OR unable to meet with the research team
  -  NOT able to provide informed consent
