Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Ability to give informed consent.
  2. Either [OR] pathological [OR] genetic [OR] imaging biomarker evidence to suggest Fronto-temporal Lobar Degeneration, Alzheimer's Disease [OR] Vascular Cognitive Impairment, [OR] defined clinical diagnoses.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] Having mental capacity to consent to study involvement.
  2. [NOT] Speaking English before age 5 years.
  3. [NOT] Having a learning disability.
  4. [NOT] Having MRI contraindications
