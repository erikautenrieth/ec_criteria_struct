Here is the edited version of the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Subject is greater than 18 years of age
  2. Subject is willing [AND] able to provide informed consent
  3. Subject is scheduled to undergo elective rotator cuff surgery
  4. Subject has consented to an interscalene block

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Opioid dependence
  2. Any subject diagnosed with a chronic pain condition which daily opioid use is needed
  3. Anatomical malformation [OR] any other condition, which in the investigator's opinion may interfere with the placement of the nerve block
  4. [NOT] absence of Raynaud's disease diagnosis (i.e., the subject has a Raynaud's disease diagnosis)
  5. Vasculopathy
  6. Patient [NOT] willing to participate (i.e., patient refusal)
