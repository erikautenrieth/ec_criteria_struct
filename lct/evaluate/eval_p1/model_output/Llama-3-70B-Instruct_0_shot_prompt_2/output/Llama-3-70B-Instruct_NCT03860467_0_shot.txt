Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  admitted to the ICU at the University Hospital Basel
  -  suffered from one [OR] more epileptic seizures (detected clinically [OR] and/or by EEG) during their ICU-stay

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  patients with epileptic seizures [AND] transforming into Status epilepticus (SE)

Note: I inserted the [OR] operator in the inclusion criteria to represent the logical "or" and "and/or" relationships. I inserted the [AND] operator in the exclusion criteria to represent the logical conjunction between "epileptic seizures" and "transforming into Status epilepticus (SE)". No [NOT] operator was necessary in this case.
