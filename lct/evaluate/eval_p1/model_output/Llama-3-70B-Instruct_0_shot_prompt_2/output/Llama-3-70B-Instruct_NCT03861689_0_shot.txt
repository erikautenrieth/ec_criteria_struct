Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] incorporated:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age ≥ 18 years old
  -  Confirmed diagnosis of perianal Crohn's disease
  -  On biologics [OR] will start biologics

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients who have perianal fistula due to causes other than Crohn's disease
  -  Fistulation into other organs, including bladder [AND] intestine
  -  Patients who have allergic reaction [OR] contraindications to anti-TNF
  -  Patients who have active cancer [AND] are [NOT] in remission
  -  Patients who have contraindications for MRI [OR] are unable to undergo MRI
  -  Known pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding
For patients who refuse to participate in the tight monitoring arm, they will be consented [AND] recruited to the control arm for comparison.
