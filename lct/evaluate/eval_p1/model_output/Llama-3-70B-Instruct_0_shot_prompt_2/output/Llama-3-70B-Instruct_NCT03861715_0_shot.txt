<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age 20-34 years;
  -  body mass index (BMI) 18-29kg/m2;
  -  regular menstrual cycle of 26-35 days,
  -  AMH levels age appropriate (≥2.3 ng/ml) [AND] early follicular- phase serum concentration of FSH within normal limits (1-12 IU/l).

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  women [AND] with diabetes [OR] other metabolic disease
  -  women [AND] with hereditary genetic diseases;
  -  women [AND] with heart disease, [OR] QT prolongation, [OR] heart failure
  -  elevated liver enzymes, [OR] liver failure, [OR] hepatitis
  -  women [AND] with inflammatory [OR] autoimmune disease
  -  abnormal karyotype;
  -  endometriosis stage III/[OR] IV;
  -  history of recurrent miscarriage;
  -  severe OHSS in a previous stimulation cycle [OR] any contraindication for the use of gonadotrophins.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
