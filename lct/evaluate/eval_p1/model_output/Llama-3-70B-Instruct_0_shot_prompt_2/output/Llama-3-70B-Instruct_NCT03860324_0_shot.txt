Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Surgical plan for total hip replacement
  -  Signing of consent form to participate in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient refusal [OR] patient unable to provide informed consent
  -  BMI > 40 kg/m2
  -  Surgical plan for revision of hip replacement [OR] other surgical procedures
  -  Patient [NOT] able to quantify pain level
  -  Chronic kidney disease [AND] a Glomerular Filtration Rate < 50ml/min
  -  Previously medicated with opioids [OR] other pain medications
  -  Patient [NOT] able to perform the surgery with spinal block [OR] other anesthesia methods
  -  Allergy to local anesthetics [OR] other medications
  -  Infection in the site of the Erector Spinae Plane [OR] Fascia Iliaca block [OR] other surgical sites
  -  Allergy [OR] contraindication to the use of morphine [OR] other opioids
