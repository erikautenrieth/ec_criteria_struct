Here is the edited version of the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age 1-12 months
  -  IgE-mediated CMA

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  cow's milk protein-induced anaphylaxis [OR] food protein induced enterocolitis syndrome,
  -  other food allergies [OR] other allergic diseases,
  -  non-CMA-related atopic eczema [AND] eosinophilic disorders of the gastrointestinal tract,
  -  chronic systemic diseases [OR] congenital cardiac defects,
  -  active tuberculosis [OR] autoimmune diseases,
  -  immunodeficiency [OR] chronic inflammatory bowel diseases,
  -  celiac disease [OR] cystic fibrosis,
  -  metabolic diseases [OR] malignancy,
  -  chronic pulmonary diseases [OR] malformations of the gastrointestinal [AND] respiratory tract,
  -  [NOT] administration of prebiotics [OR] probiotics during the 4 weeks before enrolment.

Note that I've inserted the logical operators to make the criteria more readable and logical, but I've avoided replacing the original words to maintain the original meaning and intent of the criteria.
