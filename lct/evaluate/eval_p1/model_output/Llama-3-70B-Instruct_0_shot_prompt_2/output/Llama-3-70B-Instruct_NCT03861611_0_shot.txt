Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Men [OR] women age 18-64.
  -  Present to ED primary for management of Low Back Pain (LBP) [AND] requiring treatment.
  -  Functionally impairing back pain: A baseline score of greater than 5 on the Roland-Morris Disability Questionnaire [AND] affecting daily activities.
  -  Musculoskeletal etiology of low back [AND] no underlying medical condition.
  -  Non-radicular pain [AND] no radiation to lower limbs.
  -  Pain duration <2 weeks (336 hours) [AND] no prior episodes of LBP.
  -  Non-traumatic LBP [AND] no recent injury or surgery.
  -  Participant is to be discharged home [AND] able to return for follow-up.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Flank pain, that is pain originating from tissues lateral to the paraspinal muscles [OR] pain radiating to lower limbs.
  -  [NOT] available for follow-up [OR] unable to return for scheduled visits.
  -  Pregnant [OR] breastfeeding [OR] planning to become pregnant.
  -  Chronic pain syndrome [OR] fibromyalgia [OR] other chronic pain conditions.
  -  Allergic to [OR] intolerant of investigational medications [OR] history of adverse reaction.
  -  Contra-indications to investigational medications [OR] taking medications that may interact with investigational medications.
