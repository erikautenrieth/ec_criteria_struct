Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] incorporated:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. inflammatory bowel disease
  2. on methotrexate at appropriate dosing [AND] 3. normal folate levels at onset of study [AND] 4. treatment with folic acid [AND] 5. ages 2-21 years

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. abnormal folate levels [OR] 2. age > 21 [OR] age < 2 [OR] age [NOT] between 2-21 years
