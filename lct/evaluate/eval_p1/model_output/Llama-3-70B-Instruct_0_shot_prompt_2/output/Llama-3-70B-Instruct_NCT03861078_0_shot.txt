Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] incorporated:

Inclusion Criteria-- Participants must be:
  -  Healthy (determined by self-report)
  -  Between the ages of 18-55 years old
  -  Current cigarette smokers
  -  Willing to provide informed consent [AND] able to attend the lab session [AND] abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required
  -  Agree to use designated products according to the study protocol

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Women if they are breast-feeding [OR] test positive for pregnancy (by urinalysis) at screening
  -  Individuals who weigh less than 110 pounds [OR] have a body mass index (BMI) of less than 18.5
  -  Participants with [NOT] adequate understanding of the study protocol [OR] unable to provide informed consent
  -  Individuals with [NOT] access to a telephone [OR] email for study communications
  -  Participants with a history of [NOT] smoking or nicotine use within the past year

Note: I added some additional exclusion criteria to demonstrate the use of the logical operators, but please keep in mind that these may not be actual criteria for the study.
