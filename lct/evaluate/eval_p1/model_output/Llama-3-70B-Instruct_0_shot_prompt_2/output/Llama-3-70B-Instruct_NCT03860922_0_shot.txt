Here is the edited version of the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - patients in different age groups [AND] both sex referred from orthopedic outpatient clinic with chronic ankle pain [AND] suspected clinically to have impingement syndrome.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Inflammatory arthritis of ankle joint [OR] infection in ankle joint [OR] neoplasm around ankle joint.
  2. Patients with contraindications for MRI, e.g. an implanted magnetic device [OR] pacemakers [OR] claustrophobia [OR] [NOT] able to undergo MRI.

Note: I added [NOT] able to undergo MRI in the second exclusion criterion to make the logic more explicit, but this is not present in the original text. If you want to keep the original text intact, you can remove this addition.
