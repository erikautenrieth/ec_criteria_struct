Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
Nursing home residents with ≥2 of the following risk factors for atrial fibrillation:
  -  age ≥75 years
  -  female sex
  -  obstructive sleep apnea [OR] sleep disordered breathing
  -  peripheral vascular disease
  -  diabetes mellitus
  -  obesity (body mass index [BMI] >30 kg/m2) [AND] waist circumference >102 cm
  -  hypertension [OR] high blood pressure
  -  congestive heart failure [AND] left ventricular dysfunction

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  prior diagnosis of AF [NOT] paroxysmal atrial fibrillation
  -  continuous rhythm monitoring over the past year [NOT] occasional rhythm monitoring
  -  pacemaker or implantable cardioverter defibrillator in situ [NOT] cardiac resynchronization therapy
