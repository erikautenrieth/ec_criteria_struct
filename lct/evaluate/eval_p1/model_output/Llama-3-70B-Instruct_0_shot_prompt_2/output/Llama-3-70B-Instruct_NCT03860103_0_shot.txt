Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient who accepted to participate in the study
  -  Woman over 18 years old [AND] affiliated to a social security scheme
  -  Mono-fetal pregnancy
  -  Patients with normal pregnancy between 11 [AND] 42 weeks of amenorrhea
  -  Patients hospitalized for preeclampsia [OR] intrauterine growth retardation, beyond 20 weeks of amenorrhea

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] adults, minors [OR] under guardianship, protected persons
  -  [NOT] affiliated to a social security scheme
  -  [NOT] Mono-fetal pregnancy, multiple pregnancy
