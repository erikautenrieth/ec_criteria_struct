Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  1. diagnosed as HIV positive;
  -  2. initiate antiretroviral therapy on the day of recruitment;
  -  3. was infected through male same-sex behavior [AND] has no history of injection drug use;
  -  4. has access to internet on a personal mobile phone [OR] has access to internet on a personal computer;
  -  5. has a Wechat account [AND] use it daily;
  -  6. willing to provide written informed consent [AND] able to understand the study procedures;

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  1. [NOT] able to provide written informed consent;
  -  2. under severe physical [OR] mental condition which precludes study participation;
  -  3. being an inpatient of the hospital [AND] unable to participate in the study;
  -  4. has no access to internet on a personal mobile phone [AND] no access to internet on a personal computer [NOT] able to access internet through other means;
