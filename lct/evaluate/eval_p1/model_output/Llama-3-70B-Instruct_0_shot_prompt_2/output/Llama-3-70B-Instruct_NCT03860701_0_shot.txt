Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with NEC (stages I-III Bell's Classification)
  -  Weight greater [OR] equal to 750 g.
  -  Gestational age < 36 weeks.
  -  Patients whose parents consent [AND] agree to participate in the study.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with [NOT] minor congenital anomalies, i.e., patients with major congenital anomalies
  -  [NOT] any antecedent of limb ischemia/limb thrombotic events.
  -  [NOT] any antecedent diagnosis of occlusive arterial [OR] venous thrombosis
  -  Hemodynamic instability, i.e., patients with hemodynamic instability [NOT] stable.
