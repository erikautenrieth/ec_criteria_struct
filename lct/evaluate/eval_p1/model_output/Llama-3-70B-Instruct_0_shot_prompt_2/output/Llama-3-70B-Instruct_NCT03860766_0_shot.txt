Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Handball players;
  -  Healthy [AND] no musculoskeletal injuries in the last three months [AND] no cardiovascular diseases;
  -  Participation of at least one official match in the last year [AND] attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  musculoskeletal injury in the last three months [OR] cardiovascular diseases;
  -  use of anabolic [OR] anti-inflammatory [OR] analgesic drugs 72 hours before the evaluations;
  -  use of alcohol [OR] illicit drugs during the collection period;
  -  [NOT] healthy;
  -  [NOT] participation of at least one official match in the last year [OR] [NOT] attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season.
