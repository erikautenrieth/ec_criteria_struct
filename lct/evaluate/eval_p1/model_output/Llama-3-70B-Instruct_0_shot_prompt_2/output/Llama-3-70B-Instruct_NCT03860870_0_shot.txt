Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] incorporated:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Healthy [AND] Male
  -  18-40 years of age
  -  [NOT] known contraindications for anabolic drugs (e.g. cancer)

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Abnormal ECG [OR] steroid abuse
  -  Ongoing use of prescription medication
  -  heavy resistance training more than 2 times weekly
  -  Disease deemed by the MD to infer a risk to participate in the trial
