Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Body mass Index > 30
  -  Age 18-64

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pregnancy
  -  Health reasons [AND] preventing exercise [AND] exercise test
  -  Medication [AND] preventing exercise [AND] exercise test
  -  Previously diagnosed diabetes [OR] unable to control blood sugar levels

Note: I inserted [AND] to represent the conjunction between "health reasons" and "preventing exercise and exercise test", and between "medication" and "preventing exercise and exercise test". I also inserted [OR] to represent the alternative condition of "unable to control blood sugar levels" in addition to "previously diagnosed diabetes".
