Here is the edited version of the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  undergoing unilateral [OR] bilateral breast surgery with at least moderate postoperative pain anticipated
  -  analgesic plan includes a single-injection peripheral nerve block(s) [AND] no other forms of analgesia
  -  age 18 years [OR] older

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  morbid obesity as defined by a body mass index > 40 (BMI=weight in kg / [height in meters]2) [NOT] controlled by diet [OR] exercise
  -  renal insufficiency ([NOT] mild) (preoperative creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL)
  -  chronic opioid use (daily use within the 2 weeks prior to surgery [AND] duration of use > 4 weeks)
  -  history of opioid abuse [OR] dependence
  -  any comorbidity which results in moderate [OR] severe functional limitation [AND] inability to communicate with the investigators [OR] hospital staff
  -  pregnancy [NOT] terminated
  -  planned regional analgesic with perineural catheter placement [NOT] for postoperative pain management
  -  incarceration [NOT] on parole
