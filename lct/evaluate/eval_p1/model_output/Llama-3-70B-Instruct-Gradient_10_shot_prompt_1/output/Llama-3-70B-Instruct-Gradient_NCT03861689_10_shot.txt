Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age ≥ 18 years old
 - Confirmed diagnosis of perianal Crohn's disease
 - On biologics [OR] or will start biologics
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who have perianal fistula due to causes other than Crohn's disease
 - Fistulation into other organs, including bladder [AND] or intestine
 - Patients who have allergic reaction [OR] or contraindications to anti-TNF
 - Patients who have active cancer
 - Patients who have contraindications for MRI
 - Known pregnancy
