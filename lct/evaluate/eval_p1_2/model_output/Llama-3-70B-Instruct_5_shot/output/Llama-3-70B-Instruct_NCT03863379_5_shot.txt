Inclusion Criteria:
Persons with
  - stroke [OR] cerebrovascular disease
  - traumatic brain injury
  - multiple sclerosis
  - spinal cord injuries (including spinal cord lesions i.e. myelopathies etc.)
Exclusion Criteria:
Persons with
  - heterotopic ossifications
  - chronic administration of drugs, which promote fat increase
  - chronic administration of drugs, which promote bone [OR] or muscle loss
  - co-existing other diseases such as: endocrinopathies, [OR] muscular dystrophies, [OR] non-alcoholic fatty liver disease
