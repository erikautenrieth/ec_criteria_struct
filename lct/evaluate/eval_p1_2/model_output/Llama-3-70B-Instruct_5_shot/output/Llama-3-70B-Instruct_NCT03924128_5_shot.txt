Inclusion Criteria:
 - Selected will be subjects to meeting the following criteria: male [OR] female subjects age ≥ 20 years old, diagnosed with Osteoarthritis stages III [OR] IV according to the American College of Rheumatology, manifesting pain, functional impairment for more than 3 months, able to wander with [OR] or without canes.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Will be excluded from study subjects with history of cancer, [OR] unstable cardiovascular disease, [OR] uncontrolled hypertension, [OR] morbid obesity, that is, BMI ≥ 40, [OR] terminal illness, [OR] cerebrovascular disease, [OR] dementia, [OR] heart pacemaker, [OR] superior [OR] or inferior limb fractures.
