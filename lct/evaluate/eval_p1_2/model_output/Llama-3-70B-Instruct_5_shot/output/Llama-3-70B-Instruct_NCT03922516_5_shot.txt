Inclusion Criteria:
  - all patients who are assigned to a clinically indicated chest X-ray by the emergency department of Vienna General Hospital
  - ability to provide informed consent
  - informed consent [AND] after detailed patient briefing
Exclusion Criteria:
  - the emergency department's assessment of a critical clinical condition [NOT] oppose an examination with both modalities (ULDCT of the chest, [AND] chest X-ray)
  - assigned to chest X-ray as follow-up
  - women [AND] with positive ß-HCG-test
