Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with chronic suppurative otitis media [AND] with total [OR] subtotal tympanic membrane perforation [AND] with limited anterior remnant.
  -  dry perforation for at least 3 months.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Recurrent perforation after previous myringoplasty.
  -  Ossicular disruption [OR] fixation.
  -  immune-compromised patients [INCLUDING] uncontrolled diabetics, [OR] chronic liver [AND] kidney diseases.

Note: I replaced "including" with "[INCLUDING]" to maintain the original text, but in a logical sense, it would be better to use "[OR]" instead of "[INCLUDING]" to indicate that the patient is excluded if they have any of the mentioned conditions.
