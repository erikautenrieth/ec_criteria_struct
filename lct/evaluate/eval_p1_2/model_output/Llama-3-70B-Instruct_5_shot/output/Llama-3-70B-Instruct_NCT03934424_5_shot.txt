Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Generally healthy individuals aged from 18 [AND] to 55 years
  -  BMI 25-35 kg/m2 will be recruited
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Being on a weight-loss [OR] or special diet
  -  Taking any medications [OR] or drugs known to affect appetite
  -  Have serious claustrophobia.
  -  Individuals with mental disorders (self-report) will be excluded.
  -  Due to MRI, any individuals with metallic objects in their body, including surgical staples left in the body following surgery, [OR] middle ear prosthesis, [OR] permanent eye liner, [OR] metal foreign objects lodged inside the eye, [OR] heart pacemakers, [AND] or pins inside the knee [OR] or other joints [OR] who are pregnant [OR] or could be pregnant (assessed through a urine pregnancy test for women) will be excluded from this study because of the risk associated with the MRI scanner.
