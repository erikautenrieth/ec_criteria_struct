Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients under 40 [OR] over 60 years of age who require spinal anesthesia for orthopedic surgery,
  -  with ASA physical status classification system I [OR] II [OR] III
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with contraindication to spinal anesthesia (coagulopathy, [OR] local infection, [OR] allergy to local anesthetic)
  -  Patients with communication difficulties
  -  Patient who [NOT] can take a sitting [OR] lateral decubitus position (fracture)
  -  Patients with a history of spinal surgery
  -  Patient with anatomical abnormality of the spine
