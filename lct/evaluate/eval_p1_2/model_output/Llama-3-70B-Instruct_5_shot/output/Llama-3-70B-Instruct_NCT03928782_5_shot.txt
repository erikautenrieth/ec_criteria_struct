Inclusion Criteria:
 - subjects who suffer from acute leukemic [AND] subjects who accept chemotherapy within one month [AND] will be pretreated by whole-body irradiation [AND] peripheral blood cells of subjects are approximate to those of healthy person [AND] aged between 18 [AND] 50 years old [AND] subjects who are competent to give written informed consent [AND] subjects without other systemic diseases
Exclusion Criteria:
 - subjects who suffer from serious other systemic diseases
 - subjects who are [NOT] willing [OR] unable to cooperate with this clinical study
 - other serious cases that probably hinder this clinical study
