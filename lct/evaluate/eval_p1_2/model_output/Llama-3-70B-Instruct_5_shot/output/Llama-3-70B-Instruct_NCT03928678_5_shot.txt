Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age ≤ 60 years,
 - Good nutrition,
 - No systemic infection [AND] 
 - Elective laparoscopic surgery
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Age >75 years,
 - Malnutrition [OR] an organ system infection
 - Associated with obstruction [OR] bleeding [OR] emergency surgery [OR] other surgical intervention
 - Tumor with extensive metastasis
 - Prior to surgery, patient was fasting [AND] underwent gastrointestinal decompression [AND] received nutritional support
 - Previous history of abdominal surgery
 - Patient have previously undergone gastrostomy
