Inclusion Criteria:
  - preeclampsia
  - healthy pregnancy
Exclusion Criteria:
  - pregnant women with any systemic condition (such as chronic hypertension, [OR] diabetes mellitus, [OR] thyroid diseases, [OR] liver [AND] kidney diseases)
  - women with a history of drug use throughout pregnancy
  - history of medication for PE treatment at the time of first admission
  - patients who had fetal congenital abnormalities [OR] genetic syndromes
  - multiple gestation
  - active labor
