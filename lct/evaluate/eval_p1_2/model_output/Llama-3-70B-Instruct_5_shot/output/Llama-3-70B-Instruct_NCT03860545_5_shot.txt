Inclusion Criteria:
 -  a planned operation of coronary artery bypass grafting [AND] with the use of cardiopulmonary bypass
Exclusion Criteria:
 -  emergency operations [OR] or re-operations;
 -  a known pathology of the urinary tract [OR] or renal failure;
 -  chronic use of the following medications: iron, [OR] non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs), [OR] immunosuppression, [OR] or steroids in the preoperative period;
 -  polycythemia, [OR] porphyria [OR] or pathological hemoglobin species in anamnesis;
 -  preoperative signs of hepatic failure;
 -  active autoimmune [OR] or neoplastic diseases, [OR] active infection;
 -  anticipated significant bleeding (anti-platelet agents), suggesting the use of blood-derived products during the operation [AND] afterward
 -  acute myocardial infarction [NOT] after operation
