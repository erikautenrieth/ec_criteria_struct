Inclusion Criteria:
VA Patient
  - Diagnosed with depression
  - Initiating [OR] changing to a new antidepressant medication
  - Cell phone with text messaging capabilities
  - Registered with My HealtheVet
  - Follow-up appointment with their provider in the next 12 weeks
  - Internet access
  - English speaking
  - VA Providers: provide care to a study participant
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Lack of cell phone access [AND] willingness to receive text messages
  - Diagnosis of cognitive disorders [OR] psychotic disorders [OR] bipolar disorder [OR] active substance abuse
