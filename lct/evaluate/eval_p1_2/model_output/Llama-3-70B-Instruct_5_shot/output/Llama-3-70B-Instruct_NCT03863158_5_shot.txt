Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients presenting acute myocardial infarction [AND] with hemodynamic stability at the diagnosis
 - Patients undergoing coronary artery bypass [AND] in Dijon University hospital
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with Acute pulmonary oedema
 - Patients with cardiorespiratory arrest [OR] before coronary angiography
 - hemodynamically [NOT] unstable patient at diagnosis
 - patients requiring combined surgery
 - patients with unstable angina
