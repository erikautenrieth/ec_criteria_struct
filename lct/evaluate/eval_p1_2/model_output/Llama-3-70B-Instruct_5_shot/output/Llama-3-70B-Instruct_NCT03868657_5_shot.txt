Inclusion Criteria:
 -  Healthy men [AND] women
 -  Age ≥20 [AND] ≤40 years
 -  BMI ≥18,5 [AND] ≤27,5
Exclusion Criteria:
 -  Chronic diseases (cardiac, [OR] metabolic, [OR] liver) including a family history of congenital Long QT syndrome.
 -  QTc values >440 ms (men) [OR] and >450 ms (women)
 -  Clinical important [AND] and/or symptomatic bradycardia
 -  Regular medication [NOT] (contraceptive pills allowed)
 -  Pregnancy [OR] and breastfeeding
