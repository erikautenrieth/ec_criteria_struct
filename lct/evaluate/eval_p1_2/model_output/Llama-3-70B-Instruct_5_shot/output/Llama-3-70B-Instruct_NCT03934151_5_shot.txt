Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients [AND] and/or legal guardians exhaustively informed by the investigator, and who have voluntarily provided written informed consent [OR] or, when unable to read [AND] and/or write, verbal consent put into writing by a third person

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients operated in emergency
  -  Pregnant women
  -  Right laparotomic hemicolectomy
  -  BMI > 35
