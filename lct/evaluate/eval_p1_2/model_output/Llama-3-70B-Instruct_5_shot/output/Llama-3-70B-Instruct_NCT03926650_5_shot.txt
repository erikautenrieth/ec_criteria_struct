Inclusion Criteria:
  - surgery continues more than 1 hour
  - between ages 18 [AND] 65
  - ASA I [OR] II [OR] III patients
Exclusion Criteria:
  - The patients who has allergical reactions of the drugs used
  - neurological [OR] neuromuscular diseases
  - the patients who use drugs effecting the neuromuscular junction
  - pregnant patients
  - electrolyte disregulations
  - major organ failures, liver failure [OR] kidney failure
  - obese [OR] cachectic patients
