Inclusion Criteria:
 - Females aged 18-50 years
 - The study will be offered to women [AND] at > 24 weeks gestation [AND] who are undergoing nonemergent cesarean delivery [AND] with ruptured amniotic membranes.
 - All patients undergoing cesarean delivery [AND] with ruptured amniotic membranes.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Minors
 - Emergent cesarean delivery.
 - [NOT] ruptured membranes.
 - Allergy to chlorhexidine [OR] or povidone-iodine.
