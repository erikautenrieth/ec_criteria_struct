Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Infants [AND] is inborn
 2. Infant is ≤ 750 grams at birth [AND] or < 27 weeks gestation at birth by best OB estimate
 3. Infant is 12-36 hours of age.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Unable to enroll infant by 36 hours of age
 2. Previous phototherapy
 3. Known hemolytic disease
 4. TSB reported as >6.0 mg/dL before 12 hours age
 5. Major anomaly
 6. Overt nonbacterial infection
 7. Infant is likely to expire soon: Limiting [OR] or withdrawal of intensive care is being recommended to the parents, [OR] the parents are requesting withdrawal of care, [OR] the pH is < 6.80 [OR] persistent bradycardia [AND] with hypoxemia for >2h.
