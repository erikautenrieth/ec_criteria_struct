Inclusion Criteria:
 - All patients with microcytic [AND] hypochromic anemia (according to WHO, MCV less than 80 fl [AND] HB level below the lower limit of normal value specified by age [AND] gender).

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Beta thalassemia major patients.
 - Chronic diseases [OR] infections.
 - Lead poisoning.
 - Sideroblastic anaemia.
Note: I did not insert [NOT] in the exclusion criteria as it is already implied by the word "Exclusion". The exclusion criteria are conditions that should NOT be present in the patients.
