Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with a second degree [OR] third degree [OR] or fourth degree perineal tear identified in the immediate postpartum period.
  -  Pregnant patients who received neuraxial anesthesia during their labor course
  -  Women >18 yo
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Unwillingness to participate in the study
  -  Allergy to morphine
  -  Women with history of polysubstance abuse [OR] narcotic abuse
  -  Women <18 yo
