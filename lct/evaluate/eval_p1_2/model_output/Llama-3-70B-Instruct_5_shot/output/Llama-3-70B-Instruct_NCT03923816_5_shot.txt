Inclusion Criteria:
 - patients >18 years old, [AND] undergoing elective hepatobiliary surgery [AND] under general anesthesia [AND] American Society of Anesthesiologists grade II.

Exclusion Criteria:
 -  1-patient refusal.
 2-psychiatric disorders.
 3-pregnancy [OR] lactation.
 4-preexisting neurological dysfunction [AND] (history of cerebrovascular stroke CVS)
 5-metastatic cancer.
 6-Inflammatory bowel disease.
 7-Diabetes mellitus.
 8-Renal insufficiency ([NOT] serum creatinine level ≤ 180 μmol/l).
 9- unexpected intraoperative findings ([NOT] operable).
 10-accidental massive intraoperative hemorrhage.
