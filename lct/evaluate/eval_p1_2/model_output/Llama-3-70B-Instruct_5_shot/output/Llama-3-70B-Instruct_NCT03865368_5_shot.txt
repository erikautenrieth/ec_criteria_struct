Inclusion Criteria:
 - Individuals between age 18-65 [AND] with Body Mass Index (BMI) of >29.9 kg/m2
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Unstable hypertensive patients
 - History of cardiovascular, [OR] neurologic, [OR] or orthopedic diseases,
 - Presence of knee prosthesis, [OR] hip replacement
 - Chronic inflammatory disease,
 - Anti-inflammatory treatment
 - Cognitive impairments
 - Pregnancy
