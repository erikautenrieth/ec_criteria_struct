Inclusion Criteria:
 - men [AND] women aged over 18 years
 - signed informed consent form
 - ametropia below 3 diopters
 - ability [AND] willingness to follow instructions
for healthy volunteers:
 - normal ophthalmologic findings
for primary open angle glaucoma patients (POAG):
 - diagnosed POAG
 - MD <= 10 dB
for age-related macular degeneration (AMD):
 - diagnosed dry AMD
 - diagnosed stage II [OR] or stage III AMD
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Presence of any abnormalities preventing reliable measurements
 - Ocular inflammation [OR] ocular disease interfering with the study aims
 - Use of photosensitizing medication (phototoxic drugs, [OR] photoallergic drugs) in the 3 months preceding the study
 - Presence of any condition [OR] with the possibility of causing photosensitivity, including systemic lupus erythematosus SLE, [OR] Porphyria, [OR] Vitiligo, [OR] Xeroderma Pigmentosum [OR] and Albinism
 - Presence of any form of epilepsy
 - Ocular surgery in the 3 months preceding the study
 - Pregnancy, [OR] planned pregnancy [OR] lactating
 - Any medical [OR] surgical history, disorder [OR] disease such as acute [OR] chronic severe organic disease
