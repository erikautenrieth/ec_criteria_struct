Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Age of 40~75 men [AND] women
 2. Those who visited the menopausal clinic with complaints of climacteric symptoms due to functional deterioration of various organs such as fatigue, [OR] helplessness
 3. Those who have agreed to voluntarily decide to participate [AND] observe the instructions after hearing the detailed explanation of this human body application test

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Those who have suspected symptoms of acute disease (severe infectious disease, [OR] severe trauma, [OR] severe diarrhea, [OR] vomiting, etc.)
 2. Patients with a history of antibiotic treatment for more than 1 month within the last 6 months     
 3. Those who have gastrointestinal diseases (inflammatory growth disease, [OR] active peptic ulcer, etc.) [OR] gastrointestinal surgery (except for simple appendectomy [OR] hernia surgery)
 4. Those taking health functional foods, [OR] lactic acid bacteria, [OR] and red ginseng extracts for the past year
 5. Those who are currently participating in other clinical studies [OR] human trials, [OR] participating in clinical trials [OR] human application tests within the past month
