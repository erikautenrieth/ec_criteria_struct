Inclusion Criteria:
 - stable patients [AND] with definite occurrence of intra-abdominal injury
 - patients [AND] with equivocal result by clinical [AND] radiological evaluation
Exclusion Criteria:
 - patients [AND] with penetrating abdominal trauma
 - stable patients [NOT] in need for any intervention for conservative management
 - patients [AND] with marked haemodynamic instability
 - polyt-traumatized Patients
