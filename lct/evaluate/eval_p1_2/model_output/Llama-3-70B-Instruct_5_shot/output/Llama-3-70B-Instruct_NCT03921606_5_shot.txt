Inclusion Criteria:
  -  aged 18 years or above,
  -  able to speak Cantonese [AND] read Chinese
  -  do [NOT] intend to quit smoking (pre-contemplation stage), but are willing to promote their health
  -  use a smartphone [AND] can use an instant messaging tool (e.g., WhatsApp/[OR] WeChat)
  -  willing to receive health promotion advice [AND] interact with our interventionist via WhatsApp/[OR] WeChat on a smartphone throughout the study period

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  inability to give informed consent [OR] participate in our intervention due to impaired mental status, [OR] cognitive impairment [OR] communication barriers
  -  participate in another smoking cessation programme [OR] service
