Inclusion Criteria:
 - Intact cognition
 - Poor sleep quality
 - Self-reported presence of pain
 - Capacity for mild [AND] to moderate physical activity.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Non-ambulatory [OR] have severely impaired mobility,
 - Visual [OR] or hearing impairment that interfered with assessments, [AND]
 - An acute [OR] or a terminal illness
 - Neuropathic pain
