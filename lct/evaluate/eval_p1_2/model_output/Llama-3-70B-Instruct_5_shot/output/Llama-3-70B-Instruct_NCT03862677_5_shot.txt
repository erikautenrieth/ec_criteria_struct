Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with (suspicion of) primary [OR] recurrent EOC with an indication for surgery, chemotherapy [AND] and/or immunotherapy.
  -  Age ≥18 years.
  -  WHO performance status 0-2.
  -  Accessible for treatment [AND] follow-up.
  -  Written informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Other active malignancy in past 5 years prior to entry into the study, except for treated non-melanoma skin cancer.
  -  Any known severe infection like HIV, [OR] hepatitis A, [OR] B [OR] and C.
  -  Receiving immune suppressive treatment.
  -  Medical [OR] psychological condition which in the opinion of the investigator would [NOT] permit the patient to complete the study [OR] sign meaningful informed consent.
