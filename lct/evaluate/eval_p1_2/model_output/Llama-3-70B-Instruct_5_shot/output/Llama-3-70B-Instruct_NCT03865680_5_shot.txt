Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Healthy children [AND] with no systemic diseases.
  -  High caries risk children [AND] with the presence of at least one visible active WSL in primary teeth [AND] with ICDAS II score of 1,2 [OR] 3
  -  Completion of a parental consent [AND] to participate in the study.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Selected tooth [AND] with cavitated lesion.
  -  Selected tooth [AND] with enamel defects.
  -  Participants [AND] allergic to milk products.
