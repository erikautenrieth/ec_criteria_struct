Inclusion Criteria: -
 1. patients at the primary care [OR] or rheumatology clinic with daily pain for 6 months [OR] or longer affecting the low back, [OR] neck, [OR] hip, [OR] knee [OR] or widespread pain;
 2. at least moderate in average pain severity, defined as a weekly average pain severity score of 5 [OR] greater 24;
 3. at least 18 years of age;
 4. reliable phone (landline [OR] or cell), [AND]
 5. have home computer [AND] with reliable internet access.

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. planned elective surgery during the study period;
 2. very severe symptoms of depression (i.e., PHQ8 score of ≥ 20);
 3. ongoing unresolved disability claims;
 4. inflammatory arthritis (e.g., lupus, [OR] rheumatoid arthritis, [OR] ankylosing spondylitis, etc.);
 5. cancer-related musculoskeletal pain;
 6. history of bipolar disorder [OR] or schizophrenia; and
 7. takes daily opioid for more than one year.
