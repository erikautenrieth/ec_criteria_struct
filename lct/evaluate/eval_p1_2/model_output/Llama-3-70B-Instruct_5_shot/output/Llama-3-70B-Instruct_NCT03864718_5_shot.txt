Inclusion Criteria:
  - complex anal fistula according to Parks classification
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Non cryptoglandular fistulas
  - acute sepsis
  - inflammatory bowel diseases
  - simple fistulas (defined as subcutaneous, [OR] intersphincteric [OR] and low transsphincteric fistula)
  - pregnant patients
  - ASA IV
  - patients <18
  - immunocompromised
  - patients who [NOT] refused consent
