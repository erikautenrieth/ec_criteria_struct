Inclusion Criteria:
Nursing home residents with ≥2 of the following risk factors for atrial fibrillation:
  -  age ≥75 years
  -  female sex
  -  obstructive sleep apnea
  -  peripheral vascular disease
  -  diabetes mellitus
  -  obesity (body mass index [BMI] >30 kg/m2)
  -  hypertension
  -  congestive heart failure
  (Note: The logical operator [OR] is implied between each of the risk factors, as the participant must have ≥2 of these risk factors to be included)
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  prior diagnosis of AF
  -  continuous rhythm monitoring over the past year
  -  pacemaker [OR] or implantable cardioverter defibrillator in situ
  (Note: The logical operator [NOT] is implied before each of the exclusion criteria, as the participant must NOT have any of these conditions to be included)
