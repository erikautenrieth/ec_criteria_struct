Participant-Related Inclusion Criteria:
I. All Diabetics (Groups A, B, C)
  -  Type 1 diabetes of at least 5 years duration [OR] 
  -  Type 2 diabetes of any duration 
II. Group B
  -  Able to return for follow-up over 3 years

Participant-Related Exclusion Criteria:
I. Group B
  -  Significant medical condition [AND] that would make long-term follow-up difficult 
II. Controls (Group D)
  -  Any medical problems [AND] associated with retinal vascular abnormalities (i.e., hypertension, [OR] systemic vasculitis, [OR] carotid insufficiency, etc.)

Eye-Related Inclusion Criteria:
I. Group A:
  -  Presence of active neovascularization, [AND] with or without prior treatment
  -  Presence of involuted fibrovascular proliferans
II. Group B:
  -  NPDR of any severity [AND] as defined by the International Clinical Diabetic Retinopathy Severity Scale
III. Groups C & D:
  -  No evidence of diabetic retinopathy
IV. Group ME:
  -  Presence of center-involving macular edema [AND] requiring treatment

Eye-Related Exclusion Criteria: (Applies to study eye only. May be present in non-study eye.)
  -  Visual acuity worse than 20/200
  -  Inability to maintain stable fixation [NOT] for OCT imaging
  -  History of major eye surgery (vitrectomy, [OR] cataract surgery, [OR] scleral buckle, [OR] other intraocular surgery, etc.) within 90 days of enrollment
  -  History of another eye disease [OR] or condition that may alter retinal perfusion, permeability, [OR] or retinal anatomy
  -  Substantial media opacity (cataract, [OR] corneal scar, [OR] vitreous hemorrhage) [AND] that may interfere with study imaging
