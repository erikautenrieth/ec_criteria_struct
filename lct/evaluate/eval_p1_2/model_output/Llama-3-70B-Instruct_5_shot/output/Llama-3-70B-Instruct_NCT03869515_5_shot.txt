Inclusion Criteria:
  -  The chILD syndrome exists when a child with DLD has had the common causes of DLD excluded as the primary diagnosis and has at least three of the following four criteria: (1) respiratory symptoms (e.g., cough, [OR] rapid and/or difficult breathing, [OR] exercise intolerance);(2) respiratory signs (e.g., resting tachypnea, [OR] adventitious sounds, [OR] retractions, [OR] digital clubbing, [OR] failure to thrive, [OR] or respiratory fail- ure); (3) hypoxemia; [AND] and (4) diffuse abnormalities on CXR [OR] or a CT scan.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  These include cystic fibrosis, [OR] congenital [OR] or acquired immunodeficiency, [OR] congenital heart disease, [OR] bronchopulmonary dysplasia, [OR] pulmonary infection, [OR] primary ciliary dyskinesia presenting with newborn respiratory distress [AND] recurrent aspiration.
