Inclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
 - Over 18 years of age
Neonates:
 1. Aged between 1h of life [AND] and 28 days of life
 2. Born beyond 33 Week of Amenorrhea + 1day [AND] and 2kg of birth weight
 3. Hospitalized in the medical-surgical centre of Pediatrics of the hospital of Strasbourg
 4. For whom a blood sample was prescribed as part of their routine care
Exclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
 1. Systemic inflammatory disease
 2. Transient inflammatory state
 3. Any drug that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h preceding the sampling
Neonates:
 1. Risk of anemia < 7g/DL
 2. Current anticoagulant treatment<|im_end|>
