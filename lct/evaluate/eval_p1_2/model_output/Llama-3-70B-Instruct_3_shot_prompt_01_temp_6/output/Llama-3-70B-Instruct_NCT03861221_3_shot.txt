Inclusion Criteria:
 -  Female, older than 35 years [AND] Already have mammography, [OR] or other standard exams (ultrasound, MRI, etc) [AND] Could participate this trial within 2 weeks after mammography [AND] Have the ability of consent inform.
Exclusion Criteria:
 -  During pregnancy [OR] or lactation period.
 -  Patients that may [NOT] be capable of prone position, such as patients with scapulohumeral periarthritis, [OR] cardiac pacemaker [OR] or have recent cardiac surgery.
 -  Lymphoma patients that [NOT] under therapy.
 -  Severe scoliosis.
