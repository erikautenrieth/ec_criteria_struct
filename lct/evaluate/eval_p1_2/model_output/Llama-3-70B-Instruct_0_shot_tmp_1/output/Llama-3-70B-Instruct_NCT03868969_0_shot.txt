Inclusion Criteria:
  -  clinical suspicion of urinary tract infection (UTI) defined by the presence of at least one of these signs: fever > 38°C [OR] sus-pubic pain [OR] dysuria [OR] pollakiuria [OR] urinary burns [OR] macroscopic hematuria [OR] acute urinary retention [OR] pain with the rectal examination [OR] confusion
  -  [AND] urinary analysis with leukocyturia > 10 / mm3 [AND] bacteriuria > 10^3 CFU/mL with ESBL producing enterobacteriaceae [AND] resistance associated with fluoroquinolones (FQ) [AND] cotrimoxazole (CTX) [AND] but sensitive to fosfomycin.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  allergy to fosfomycin [OR] trometamol
  -  [NOT] Presence of material in the urinary tract
  -  [NOT] Severe immunosuppression
  -  [NOT] Chronic prostatitis
  -  [NOT] Prostate abscess
  -  [NOT] Acute pyelonephritis
  -  [NOT] Hemodynamic instability
  -  [NOT] Chronic renal failure (clearance <60 mL/min)
  -  [NOT] Prior antibiotic therapy, with an antibiotic sensitive on the antibiotic susceptibility test [NOT] except: amoxicillin - clavulanic acid, cefixime, nitrofurantoin.
  -  [NOT] Co-treatment with metoclopramide
