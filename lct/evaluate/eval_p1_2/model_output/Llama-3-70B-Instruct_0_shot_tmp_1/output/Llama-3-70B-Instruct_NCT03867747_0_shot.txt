  -  Patients with structural heart disease [AND] implantable cardioverter defibrillator (ICD)
  -  Age > 18 years
  -  either

  -  Beside the cardiac conditions: [NOT] competing illness that would additionally limit the life expectancy to less than 6 months
  -  [NOT] prior radiation therapy in the thorax area
  -  [NOT] pregnancy [AND] [NOT] active breastfeeding
  -  Ability to consent [AND] consent to study participation

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  ICD electrode malfunction [OR] ICD readings outside reference range
  -  [NOT] evidence of a myocardial scar (Computer tomography or magnetic resonance tomography for MRI-capable ICD aggregates or electrophysiological measurement)
