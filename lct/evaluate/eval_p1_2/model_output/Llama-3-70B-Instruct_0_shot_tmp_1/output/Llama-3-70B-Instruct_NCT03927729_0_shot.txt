Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Conscious patient
  -  Age ≥ 18 years [AND] Acute pain of monotraumatic origin [AND] Pain > 4 on a visual numerical scale

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  State of shock with unstable hemodynamics (PA <90/60) [OR] Suspected or proven trauma to the chest, abdomen [OR] pelvis
  -  Serious head trauma
  -  Consciousness disorder with Glasgow score <15
  -  Patient who has already received analgesics [NOT] with the exception of paracetamol
  -  Patient receiving an intravenous approach for analgesia
  -  Renal [OR] hepatic disorders known
  -  Hypersensitivity to fluoridated anesthetics [OR] history of malignant hyperthermia in the patient [OR] family
  -  Pregnant [OR] nursing woman
  -  Patient under judicial protection
  -  Non communicating patient [OR] with difficulties of understanding
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Intravenous injection for analgesia
