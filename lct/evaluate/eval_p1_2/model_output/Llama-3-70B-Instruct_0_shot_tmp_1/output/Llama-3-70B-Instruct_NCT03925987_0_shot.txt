Inclusion Criteria:
  1. DSM-V diagnosis of an Anxiety disorder (Generalized Anxiety Disorder [OR] Social Anxiety Disorder [OR] Panic Disorder [OR] Agoraphobia)

  1. Comorbid [NOT] Bipolar Disorder [OR] Schizophrenia [OR] Anorexia [OR] Bulimia Nervosa [OR] Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder
  2. Current [NOT] Substance Use Disorder ≤ moderate (within the past 6 months)
  3. [NOT] Currently taking a psychotropic medication
  4. [NOT] Currently receiving psychotherapy/counseling for anxiety
  5. [NOT] Currently being treated as an inpatient
  6. [NOT] Active suicidal ideation with intent or plan
  7. [NOT] History of neurological, cardiovascular [OR] respiratory problems (e.g., asthma, severe brain injury)
