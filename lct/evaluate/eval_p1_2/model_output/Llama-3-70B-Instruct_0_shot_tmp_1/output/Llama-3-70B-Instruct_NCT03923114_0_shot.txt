Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with treatment response:
  -  Age ≥18 years [AND] subject is diagnosed with type 2 diabetes [AND] subject showed response to GLP-1RA treatment [AND] ability to sign informed consent
Patients without treatment response:
  -  Age ≥18 years [AND] subject is diagnosed with type 2 diabetes [AND] subject showed no response to GLP-1RA treatment [AND] ability to sign informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Liver disease [OR] renal disease [OR] pregnancy or the wish to become pregnant within 6 months after the study [OR] breastfeeding [OR] age <18 years [OR] pituitary disorder [OR] inability to sign informed consent
