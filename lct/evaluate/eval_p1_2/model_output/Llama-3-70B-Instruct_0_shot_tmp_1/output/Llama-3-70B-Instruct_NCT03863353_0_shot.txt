Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Undergoing an elective surgical procedure [AND] for which it is expected they will receive a prescription pain medicine for management of pain during recovery at home.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Undergoing non-elective procedure
  -  Cognitive impairment [AND] precluding self-reporting of pain
  -  Hematologic/oncologic condition
  -  Liver [OR] kidney conditions [NOT] precluding usual analgesic prescription patterns (opioid plus a non-opioid)
  -  Use of opioids for prolonged pain pre-operatively (<2 weeks)

Note that I inserted the logical operators as follows:

* [AND] to represent a logical conjunction between two conditions
* [OR] to represent a logical disjunction between two conditions
* [NOT] to represent a logical negation, indicating that a condition is an exclusion criterion
