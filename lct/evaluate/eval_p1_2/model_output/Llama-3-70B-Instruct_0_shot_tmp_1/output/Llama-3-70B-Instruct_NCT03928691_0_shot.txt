Inclusion criteria:
pregnant women 34 weeks or less admitted to Women's Health Hospital with a diagnosis of :
  -  severe preeclampsia [AND] it is defined as 1- blood pressure 160/110 mmHg or more [OR] 2- proteinurea (+3 or more by dipstick) [OR] 3-HELLP syndrome.
  -  threatened preterm labour that will receive Magnesium Sulphate either for tocolysis [OR] neuroprotection.

Exclusion criteria:
- women with more than 34 weeks gestation [AND] with any contraindication for Magnesium Sulphate intake like severe renal impairment [OR] myasthenia gravis [NOT] eligible for the study.
