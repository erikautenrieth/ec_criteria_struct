Here is the modified text with the inserted logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  adult patients of ASA physical status I [AND] II undergoing surgery under general anaesthesia

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  anticipated difficult mask ventilation/ intubation
  -  individuals with significant cardiorespiratory [OR] cerebrovascular disease
  -  pregnant ladies
  -  patient with history of epilepsy
  -  body mass index >25 kg/m2
  -  hemoglobin <8 gm/dL
  -  patients who were [NOT] ambulant for >24 h, having SpO2 < 97% while breathing in room air
  -  phobia to facemask.

Note that I inserted the logical operators as follows:

* [AND] in the inclusion criteria to indicate that both ASA physical status I and II are required.
* [OR] in the exclusion criteria to indicate that either cardiorespiratory or cerebrovascular disease is a reason for exclusion.
* [NOT] in the exclusion criteria to indicate that patients who were not ambulant for >24 h are excluded.
