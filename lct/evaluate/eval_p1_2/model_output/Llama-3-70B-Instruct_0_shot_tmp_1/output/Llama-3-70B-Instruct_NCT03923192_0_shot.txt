Here is the modified text with the inserted logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients aged from 15 to 50 years.
  -  No gender restriction.
  -  Patients should have an acceptable oral hygiene level [AND] have at least one posterior proximal caries.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with a compromised medical history [OR] severe or active periodontal disease [OR] heavy bruxism or a traumatic occlusion [OR] acute or chronic dental infection [OR] pregnant or breastfeeding women [OR] patients with posterior restorations on molars or premolars.

Note: I inserted the [AND] operator in the Inclusion Criteria to indicate that both conditions must be met. In the Exclusion Criteria, I inserted the [OR] operator to indicate that any one of the conditions is sufficient for exclusion. I did not insert the [NOT] operator as it is already implied by the phrase "No gender restriction" and the use of "Exclusion Criteria" section.
