Inclusion Criteria:
  -  All patients with microcytic hypochromic anemia (according to WHO) [AND] MCV less than 80 fl [AND] HB level below the lower limit of normal value specified by age [AND] gender.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Beta thalassemia major patients.
  -  Chronic diseases [OR] infections.
  -  Lead poisoning.
  -  Sideroblastic anaemia.

Note that I inserted [AND] operators in the Inclusion Criteria to indicate that all three conditions must be met simultaneously. In the Exclusion Criteria, I inserted an [OR] operator to indicate that patients with either chronic diseases or infections (or both) are excluded.
