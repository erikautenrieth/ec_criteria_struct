Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Diagnosis of primary knee osteoarthritis according to American College of Radiology [AND] being radiographically K-L grade 2-3
  -  Presence of symptoms for at least 3 months [AND] patients who have been planned a conventional physiotherapy programme because of knee osteoarthritis
  -  Presence of functional capacity for outpatient physical therapy

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with seconder knee osteoarthritis [OR] history of lower extremity or spine surgery
  -  History of intraarticular injection in last 1 month [OR] patients with neurological disease that will affect muscle strength, balance and coordination
  -  [NOT] patients with primary knee osteoarthritis
