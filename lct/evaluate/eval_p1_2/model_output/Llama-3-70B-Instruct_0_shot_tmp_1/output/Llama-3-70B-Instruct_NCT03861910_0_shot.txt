Here is the modified text with the inserted logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age 1-12 months [AND] IgE-mediated CMA

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  cow's milk protein-induced anaphylaxis [OR] food protein induced enterocolitis syndrome [OR] other food allergies [OR] other allergic diseases [OR] non-CMA-related atopic eczema [OR] eosinophilic disorders of the gastrointestinal tract [OR] chronic systemic diseases [OR] congenital cardiac defects [OR] active tuberculosis [OR] autoimmune diseases [OR] immunodeficiency [OR] chronic inflammatory bowel diseases [OR] celiac disease [OR] cystic fibrosis [OR] metabolic diseases [OR] malignancy [OR] chronic pulmonary diseases [OR] malformations of the gastrointestinal [AND] respiratory tract [OR] administration of prebiotics [OR] probiotics during the 4 weeks before enrolment.

Note that I inserted [AND] only once in the Inclusion Criteria, as there are only two conditions that must be met together. In the Exclusion Criteria, I inserted [OR] between each condition, as any one of them can exclude a participant. I did not insert [NOT] as it is not necessary in this case, as the Exclusion Criteria are already phrased in a negative way.
