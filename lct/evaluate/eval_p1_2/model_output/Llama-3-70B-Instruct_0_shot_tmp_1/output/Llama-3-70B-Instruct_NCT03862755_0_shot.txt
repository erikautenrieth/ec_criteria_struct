Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Lung surgical patients who were sent to thoracic postoperative intensive care unit (ICU) ward right after surgery [AND] stayed in ICU for at least 24 hours.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Cases [NOT] received inferior vena filter [OR] anti-coagulation treatment history.

Note that I inserted the [AND] operator to indicate that both conditions (being sent to ICU and staying for at least 24 hours) must be met for inclusion. I also inserted the [NOT] operator to indicate that the exclusion criterion is a negation, and the [OR] operator to indicate that either inferior vena filter or anti-coagulation treatment history (or both) would lead to exclusion.
