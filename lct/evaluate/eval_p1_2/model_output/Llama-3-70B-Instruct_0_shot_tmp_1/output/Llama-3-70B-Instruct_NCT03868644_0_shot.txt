Inclusion Criteria:
-Youth previously participated in an earlier study by Duflo, Dupas and Kremer in 328 primary schools in the same study site in Western Province, Kenya [AND] who consent to participate.

Exclusion Criteria:
-Youth sampled for the study [NOT] consent to participate.

Note that I inserted the [AND] operator in the Inclusion Criteria to indicate that both conditions (participating in the earlier study and consenting to participate) must be met. In the Exclusion Criteria, I inserted the [NOT] operator to indicate that the absence of consent to participate is a reason for exclusion.
