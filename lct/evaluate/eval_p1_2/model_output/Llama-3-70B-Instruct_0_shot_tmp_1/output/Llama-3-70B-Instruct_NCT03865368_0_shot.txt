Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Individuals between age 18-65 [AND] with Body Mass Index (BMI) of >29.9 kg/m2

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Unstable hypertensive patients
  -  History of cardiovascular disease [OR] neurologic disease [OR] orthopedic diseases,
  -  Presence of knee prosthesis [OR] hip replacement
  -  Chronic inflammatory disease,
  -  Anti-inflammatory treatment
  -  Cognitive impairments
  -  [NOT] Pregnancy.

Note that I inserted the logical operators as follows:

* [AND] in the inclusion criterion to indicate that both conditions (age and BMI) must be met.
* [OR] in the exclusion criteria to indicate that any of the listed conditions (cardiovascular, neurologic, or orthopedic diseases) or (knee prosthesis or hip replacement) are excluded.
* [NOT] before "Pregnancy" to indicate that pregnant individuals are excluded.
