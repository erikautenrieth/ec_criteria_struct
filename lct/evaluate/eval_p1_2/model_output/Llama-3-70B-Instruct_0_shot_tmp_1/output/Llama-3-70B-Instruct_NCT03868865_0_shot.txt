Inclusion Criteria:
  -  curative malignancy (breast cancer of TNM (T: primary tumor, N: regional nodes, M: metastasis) staging I-III) [AND] receiving chemotherapy [AND] at least 18 years old [AND] written informed consent [AND] willing and physically/mentally able to participate in a six hours day care clinic program eleven times during chemotherapy.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  insufficient knowledge of the German language [OR] physical or mental disabilities resulting in ineligibility for participation in the program [OR] filling out the questionnaires [NOT] able to participate.

Note: I inserted the logical operators as follows:

* [AND] to represent the conjunction between the inclusion criteria, indicating that all conditions must be met.
* [OR] to represent the disjunction between the exclusion criteria, indicating that any one of the conditions is sufficient for exclusion.
* [NOT] to represent the negation of the ability to participate, indicating that the patient is excluded if they are not able to participate.
