Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age ≥ 18 years old
  -  Confirmed diagnosis of perianal Crohn's disease [AND] on biologics or will start biologics

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients who have perianal fistula due to causes other than Crohn's disease
  -  Fistulation into other organs, including bladder [OR] intestine
  -  Patients who have allergic reaction [OR] contraindications to anti-TNF
  -  Patients who have active cancer
  -  Patients who have contraindications for MRI
  -  Known pregnancy [NOT] willing to participate in the study
For patients who refuse to participate in the tight monitoring arm, they will be consented and recruited to the control arm for comparison.

Note: I inserted the logical operators as follows:

* [AND] in the Inclusion Criteria to indicate that both conditions must be met.
* [OR] in the Exclusion Criteria to indicate that either condition is sufficient for exclusion.
* [NOT] in the Exclusion Criteria to indicate that known pregnancy is an exclusion criterion, but only if the patient is not willing to participate in the study.
