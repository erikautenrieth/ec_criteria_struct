  -  Patients who have had surgery on the spine [AND] / or hip.

Note that I inserted the [OR] operator in the first inclusion criterion to indicate that the patient can meet either the Amor criteria or the ASAS criteria. In the first exclusion criterion, I inserted the [OR] operator to indicate that the presence of a contraindication to either conventional radiography or EOS imaging is an exclusion criterion. In the third exclusion criterion, I inserted the [AND] operator to indicate that the patient must have had surgery on both the spine and/or hip to be excluded.
