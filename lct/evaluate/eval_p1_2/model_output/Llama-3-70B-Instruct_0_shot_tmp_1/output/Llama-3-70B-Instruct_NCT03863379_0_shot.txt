Inclusion Criteria:
Persons with 
  -  stroke / cerebrovascular disease [OR] 
  -  traumatic brain injury [OR] 
  -  multiple sclerosis [OR] 
  -  spinal cord injuries (including spinal cord lesions i.e. myelopathies etc.)

Exclusion Criteria:
Persons with 
  -  heterotopic ossifications [OR] 
  -  chronic administration of drugs, which promote fat increase [OR] 
  -  chronic administration of drugs, which promote bone or muscle loss [OR] 
  -  co-existing other diseases such as: endocrinopathies [OR] muscular dystrophies [OR] non-alcoholic fatty liver disease [NOT] persons without these conditions.
