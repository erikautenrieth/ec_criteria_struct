Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Pathologically confirmed intermediate-risk prostate cancer [OR] high-risk prostate cancer
  -  Biochemical recurrence after radical prostatectomy (Definition: Serial elevation of PSA over 0.2 ng/mL [AND] <=1.0 ng/mL)
  -  ECOG performance status 0 [OR] 1
  -  Appropriate values of blood tests within 6 months after enrollment [AND] Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1500 cells/mm3 [AND] Platelets ≥ 50,000 cells/mm3 [AND] Hemoglobin ≥ 8.0 g/dl
  -  Appropriate values of kidney function within 6 months after enrollment [AND] Creatinine < 2.0 ng/dL
  -  Appropriate values of liver function within 6 months after enrollment [AND] total bilirubin < 1.5 X maximum normal value [AND] alanine aminotransferase [OR] aspartate aminotransferase < 2.5 X maximum normal value

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Clinically gross recurrent tumor [NOT] absent
  -  Presence of distant metastasis [NOT] absent
  -  Presence of pelvic LN metastasis [NOT] absent
  -  History of pelvic irradiation [NOT] absent
  -  History of cryotherapy [OR] brachytherapy for prostate cancer [NOT] absent
  -  Double primary cancer other than skin/thyroid cancer [NOT] absent
  -  Combined serious morbidity [NOT] absent
