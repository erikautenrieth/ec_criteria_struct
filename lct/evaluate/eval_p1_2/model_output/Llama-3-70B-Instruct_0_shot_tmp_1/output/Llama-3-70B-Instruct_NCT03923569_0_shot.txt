Inclusion Criteria:
For all participants:
  -  age from 50 to 75 years old [AND] affiliated to the French health care system
For AD patients:
  -  meeting International Working Group (IWG)-2 criteria for diagnosis [AND] Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) ≥20 (Greco version)
For healthy volunteers:
  -  MMSE>25 [AND] Dubois 5 words test ≥ 9

Exclusion Criteria:
For all participants:
  -  [NOT] Pregnancy
  -  [NOT] people able to give consent
  -  [NOT] contraindication for MRI (metallic body parts, claustrophobia) [OR] aphasia [OR] apraxia [OR] agnosia
  -  [NOT] neurological (other than AD) or any other serious disease (cancer, addiction, systemic disease)
  -  [NOT] sleep apnea
  -  [NOT] major depression or anxiety for more than 3 months (Beck>10) [OR] psychiatric disease
  -  [NOT] documented epilepsy
  -  [NOT] use of neuroleptics (more than one dose per day)
  -  [NOT] use of antiepileptics
  -  [NOT] use of benzodiazepines at a dose superior or equal to two intakes per day
  -  [NOT] use of antidepressants
  -  [NOT] restless leg syndrome treated by dopaminergic agonists
For AD patients:
  -  [NOT] other causes of dementia
  -  [NOT] non-degenerative neurological lesions
  -  [NOT] white matter hypersignals
  -  [NOT] acute cognitive deficits
