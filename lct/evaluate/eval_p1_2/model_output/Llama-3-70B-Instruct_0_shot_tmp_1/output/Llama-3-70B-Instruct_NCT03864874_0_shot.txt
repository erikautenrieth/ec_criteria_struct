Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient candidate to pulmonary metastasectomy with curative intent by mean of both major anatomical [OR] limited resection;
  -  Age > 18 years.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with widespread metastatic disease [AND] where surgical resection is performed only for diagnostic purposes;
  -  History of previous intrapleural talc injection;
  -  Any anatomical [OR] pathological condition (eg. diffuse adhesions, severe emphysema) that may interfere with execution of ultrasonographic investigation;
  -  Incomplete lung exclusion during surgical procedure [NOT] performed with curative intent.

Note that I inserted the logical operators as follows:

* [OR] in the first inclusion criterion to indicate that either major anatomical or limited resection is acceptable.
* [AND] in the first exclusion criterion to indicate that both widespread metastatic disease and surgical resection for diagnostic purposes are required for exclusion.
* [OR] in the third exclusion criterion to indicate that either anatomical or pathological conditions are grounds for exclusion.
* [NOT] in the fourth exclusion criterion to indicate that incomplete lung exclusion is only a reason for exclusion if it is not performed with curative intent.
