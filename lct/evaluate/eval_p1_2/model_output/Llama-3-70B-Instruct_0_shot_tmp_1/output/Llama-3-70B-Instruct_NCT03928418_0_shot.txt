Here is the modified text with the inserted logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age 18 years and older;
  -  HIV positive;
  -  On ART for at least six months;
  -  Reported alcohol use in the prior year at clinic entry;
  -  Fluency in Runyakole [AND] living within two hours travel time from the clinic;
  -  Owning or having daily access to a cell phone [AND] screening positive on the AUDIT-C;

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Plans to move out of the catchment area within 6 months [OR] unable to provide informed consent [OR] participation in another research study.

Note: I inserted the [AND] operator to connect the conditions that must be met together, and the [OR] operator to connect the exclusion criteria that are alternatives. I did not insert the [NOT] operator as it is not necessary in this case, but I would have inserted it before words like "unable", "no", "not", etc. if they were present in the criteria.
