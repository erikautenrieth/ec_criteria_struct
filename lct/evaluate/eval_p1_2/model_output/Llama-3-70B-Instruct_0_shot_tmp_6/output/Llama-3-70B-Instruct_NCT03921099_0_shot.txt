Here is the modified eligibility criteria with the inserted logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adults who are critically ill [AND] with MRSA infection suspicion.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Pregnancy [OR] breast feeding.
  2. Known allergy to either vancomycin [OR] ascorbic acid.
  3. Base line serum creatinine ≥2mg/dl.
  4. Patients receiving other nephrotoxic drug (e.g., aminoglycosides [OR] amphotericin B [OR] cisplatin [OR] calcinurine inhibitors).
  5. Anticipated administration of contrast medium within 7 days.
  6. Patients suffering from some underlying diseases (e.g., cancer [OR] HIV infection [OR] systemic lupus erythematoses [OR] urinary tract stones).
  7. Unlikelihood of receiving the study medications for at least 72 hours [NOT] due to any reason.
