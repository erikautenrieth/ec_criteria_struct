Inclusion Criteria:
  1. female
  2. age 60+ years [AND] 
  3. community dwelling [AND]
  4. Moderate to severe UUI (as measured by International Consultation on Incontinence Questionnaire Short Form) [AND]
  5. low physical activity (Physical Activity Staging questionnaire) [AND]
  6. cognitively able to complete the study (in opinion of the referring provider) [AND] 
Women currently on anti-cholinergic medication for urinary incontinence will be allowed if they meet the above criteria.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. cognitive impairment (in opinion of the referring provider) [OR]
  2. unable to provide informed consent [OR] communicate in English [NOT]
  3. desire for surgical management [OR]
  4. osteoporosis [OR]
  5. lack of medical clearance from the physician [OR]

Note that I inserted the operators according to the logical connections within each criterion. In the Inclusion Criteria, all conditions must be met, so I used [AND] to connect them. In the Exclusion Criteria, any one of the conditions is sufficient for exclusion, so I used [OR] to connect them. I also used [NOT] to indicate negation in criterion 2 of the Exclusion Criteria.
