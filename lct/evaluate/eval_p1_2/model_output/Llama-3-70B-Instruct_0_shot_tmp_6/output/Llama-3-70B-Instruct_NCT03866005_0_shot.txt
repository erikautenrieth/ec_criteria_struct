Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Diagnosed diabetes mellitus [AND] treatment-naïve CI-DME requiring anti-VEGF therapy per retinal specialist's customary treatment regimen will be recruited to participate in the study. CI-DME will be defined as a central subfield thickness (CST) > 300 microns on spectral domain optical coherence tomography (SD-OCT) [OR] CST > 250 microns with definite intra-retinal cystic fluid accumulation. Additional inclusion criteria are age > 18 years [AND] ability to give informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Age < 18 years [OR] inability to give informed consent [OR] evidence of proliferative diabetic retinopathy [OR] evidence of other serious ocular disease (age-related macular degeneration, glaucoma, significant media opacity) [OR] history of previous intraocular surgery, including macular or panretinal photocoagulation ([NOT] except uncomplicated cataract or keratorefractive surgery more than 6 months prior to enrollment) [OR] pregnant and nursing women [OR] known sensitivity to any of the supplement ingredients.
