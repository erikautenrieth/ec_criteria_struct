Inclusion Criteria:
  -  curative malignancy (breast cancer of TNM (T: primary tumor, N: regional nodes, M: metastasis) staging I-III)
  -  receiving chemotherapy [AND] at least 18 years old [AND] written informed consent [AND] willing and physically/mentally able to participate in a six hours day care clinic program eleven times during chemotherapy.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  insufficient knowledge of the German language [OR] physical or mental disabilities resulting in ineligibility for participation in the program [OR] filling out the questionnaires.
  - [NOT] willing to participate in the program.

Note that I inserted the logical operators as follows:

* In the Inclusion Criteria, I used [AND] to connect the conditions that must be met simultaneously.
* In the Exclusion Criteria, I used [OR] to connect the conditions that are alternative reasons for exclusion.
* I also used [NOT] to indicate the negation of willingness to participate in the program, which is an exclusion criterion.
