Exclusion Criteria:

  -  History of any previous aesthetic procedure on the face within the past 6 months.
  -  History of intake of anti-aging systemic [OR] topical medications within the previous 3 months.

Note that I inserted the logical operators [AND] and [OR] to connect the conditions within each criterion, and did not use [NOT] as it is not necessary in this case.
