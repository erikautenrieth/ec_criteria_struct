Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with chronic suppurative otitis media [AND] total or subtotal tympanic membrane perforation [AND] limited anterior remnant.
  -  dry perforation for at least 3 months.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Recurrent perforation after previous myringoplasty.
  -  Ossicular disruption [OR] fixation.
  -  immune-compromised patients [INCLUDING] uncontrolled diabetics [OR] chronic liver diseases [OR] kidney diseases.

Note: I inserted [INCLUDING] instead of [AND] in the last exclusion criterion to indicate that immune-compromised patients include those with uncontrolled diabetics, chronic liver diseases, or kidney diseases.
