Exclusion Criteria:

  -  Clinically significant diseases
  -  History of drug [OR] alcohol abuse

Note: I inserted [OR] between conditions that are alternatives, [NOT] to indicate negation, and did not insert [AND] as there were no conjunctive conditions in the exclusion criteria.
