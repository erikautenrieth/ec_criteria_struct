Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adult patient (> 18 years old)
  -  Obtaining consent to participate in the study
  -  Patients whose clinical presentation meets at least one of the following criteria:
       -  Undetermined nephropathy despite renal biopsy
       [OR] Nephroangiosclerosis as the predominant lesion
       [OR] Chronic tubulointerstitial nephropathy
       [OR] Glomerulosclerosis
       [OR] Segmental and focal hyalinosis
       [OR] Optically normal kidney or seat of minimal lesions
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient who has already been screened for Fabry Disease
  -  At least one of the following criteria:
       -  Nephrotic syndrome [AND] Glomerular nephropathy
       [OR] Histological diagnosis of certain nephropathies (specific kidney lesions)

Note: I inserted [OR] operators to separate the multiple criteria under "Inclusion Criteria" and "Exclusion Criteria", and [AND] operator to indicate the conjunction of "Nephrotic syndrome" and "Glomerular nephropathy".
