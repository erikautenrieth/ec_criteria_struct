Inclusion criteria (non ESRD group):

Exclusion criteria (non ESRD group) :
  -  Immunosuppressive treatment [OR] Relapse of lupus nephritis under treatment [OR] Pregnant or breastfeeding women [OR] Protected adults (individuals under guardianship by court order)
