Here is the modified eligibility criteria with the inserted logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  children at age of 7-9 years
  -  have normal body mass index (BMI) i.e. -2 ≤ z-score < +1 [AND] slightly overweight z-score < +1.5 [AND] slightly underweight z-score > -3.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  allergic to milk
  -  consuming other probiotic product
  -  he/she do [NOT] want to stop consumption [AND] doing washout for 2 weeks.

Note that I inserted [AND] to connect the three conditions for normal body mass index (BMI) in the Inclusion Criteria, and [NOT] to indicate the negation of wanting to stop consumption in the Exclusion Criteria.
