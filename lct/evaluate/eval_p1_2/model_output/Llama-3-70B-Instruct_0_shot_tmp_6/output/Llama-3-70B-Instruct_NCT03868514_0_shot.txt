Inclusion Criteria:
Age [≥ 18]
Indications of breast reconstruction: histologically confirmed breast cancer [OR] precancerous lesions (DCIS, LCIS) [OR] mutation carrier with increased breast cancer risk [OR] strong family history (lifetime risk > 15%)
The patient is capable to realise the nature, aims and possible consequences of the clinical trial (MPG §20.2.1) [AND] patient information has been provided [AND] all written consents of the patient are available

Exclusion Criteria:
Metastatic breast cancer [OR] patient with known contraindications against mesh-assisted or plastic-reconstructive breast surgery according to the instruction for use [OR] patient is kept in an institution under judicial or official orders (MPG §20.3) [OR] participate in another operative clinical trial, if it relates to the area of reconstructive breast surgery [AND] influences the primary endpoint of the clinical trial [NOT]
