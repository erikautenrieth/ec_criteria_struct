Here is the modified eligibility criteria with the inserted logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adult (>18 years old) outpatients on 3-4 times per week HD sessions for at least 6 weeks [AND] with a life expectancy >6 months.
  -  Fluid overload ≥1.1L.
  -  Medically stable patient.
  -  Minimum of 3 hours of dialysis per session.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pregnancy [OR] lactation.
  -  Declined informed consent.
       -  Patients with cognitive dysfunction [OR] severe life-limiting Comorbidities (malignant tumour, tuberculosis,....) [OR] surgery within six weeks of the study [OR] nocturnal dialysis patients [OR] patients expected to receive a transplant [OR] move to another center within the duration of the study [OR] patients with arteriovenous fistula issues.
  -  [NOT] Patients who have given informed consent.
