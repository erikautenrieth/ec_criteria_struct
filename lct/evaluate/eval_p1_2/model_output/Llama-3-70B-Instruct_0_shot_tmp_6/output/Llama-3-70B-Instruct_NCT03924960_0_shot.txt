Here is the modified text with the inserted logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Subjects diagnosed according to the American College of Rheumatology 2016 diagnostic criteria for fibromyalgia.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History of comorbid inflammatory rheumatic [OR] connective tissue diseases
  -  History of cardiovascular [OR] musculoskeletal problems that could prevent them to participate in an exercise program [NOT] able to participate.

Note: I inserted the [OR] operator to indicate that the subject can have either inflammatory rheumatic or connective tissue diseases, and similarly, either cardiovascular or musculoskeletal problems. I also inserted the [NOT] operator to indicate that the subject must not be able to participate in an exercise program.
