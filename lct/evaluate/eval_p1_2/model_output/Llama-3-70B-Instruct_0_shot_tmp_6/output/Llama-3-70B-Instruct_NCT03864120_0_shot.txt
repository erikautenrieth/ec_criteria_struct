Here is the modified text with the inserted logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Age >18 years
  2. on PD treatment for at least one month [AND] signed the consent
  3. (No change, as there are only two conditions)

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Peritonitis [OR] recovery from peritonitis for less than one month.
  2. On any intra peritoneal treatment apart from peritoneal dialysis.
  3. Evidence of decompensated liver cirrhosis [OR] heart failure.
  4. Evidence of extra peritoneal leakage, eg. leakage to thoracic cavity, post peritoneal, hernia [NOT] on PD treatment.

Note: I inserted the operators based on the logical connections between the conditions. In the inclusion criteria, I added an [AND] between the two conditions to indicate that both must be met. In the exclusion criteria, I added [OR] operators to indicate that either condition is sufficient for exclusion. I also added a [NOT] operator in the last exclusion criterion to indicate that the presence of extra peritoneal leakage excludes patients who are on PD treatment.
