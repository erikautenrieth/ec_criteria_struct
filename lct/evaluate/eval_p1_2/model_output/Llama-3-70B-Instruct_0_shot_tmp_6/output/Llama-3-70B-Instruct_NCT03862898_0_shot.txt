Inclusion Criteria:
  -  patients with lumbar pain present for more than 12 weeks [AND] pain during changing of the position [AND] radicular pain.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Cauda equina syndrome,
  - [NOT] ankylosing spondylitis,
  - [NOT] thoracic deformities (pectus carinatum [OR] excavatum),
  - [NOT] spina bifida,
  - [NOT] fractures,
  - [NOT] post-operative spinal conditions,
  - [NOT] diabetes,
  - [NOT] inflammatory processes,
  - [NOT] tumors,
  - [NOT] pregnancy.

Note that I inserted [AND] to connect the three inclusion criteria, and [NOT] to indicate the exclusion of each condition. I also inserted [OR] to indicate that thoracic deformities can be either pectus carinatum or excavatum.
