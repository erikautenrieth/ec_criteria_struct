Exclusion Criteria:

  -  other severe neurological diseases (e.g., epilepsy, brain tumor, [AND] subdural hematoma);
  -  history of severe alcohol [OR] drug abuse;
  -  current severe depression [OR] current psychosis or other relevant psychiatric condition;
  -  very severe apraxia of speech, as revealed by Hierarchical Word Lists [OR] severe non-verbal cognitive deficits, as indicated by the Corsi Block-Tapping Task;
