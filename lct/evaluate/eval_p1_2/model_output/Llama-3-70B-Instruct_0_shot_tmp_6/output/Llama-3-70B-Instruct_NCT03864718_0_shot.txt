Inclusion Criteria:
  -  complex anal fistula according to Parks classification

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Non cryptoglandular fistulas [OR] acute sepsis [OR] inflammatory bowel diseases [OR] simple fistulas (defined as subcutaneous [AND] intersphincteric [AND] low transsphincteric fistula) [OR] pregnant patients [OR] ASA IV [OR] patients <18 [OR] immunocompromised [OR] patients who refused consent.

Note that I inserted the [OR] operator to indicate that any of the exclusion criteria can be met to exclude a patient. I also inserted the [AND] operator within the simple fistulas criterion to indicate that all three types of fistulas (subcutaneous, intersphincteric, and low transsphincteric) must be met to exclude a patient.
