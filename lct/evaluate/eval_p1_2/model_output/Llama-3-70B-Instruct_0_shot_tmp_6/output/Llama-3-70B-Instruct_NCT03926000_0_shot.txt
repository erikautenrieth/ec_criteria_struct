Here is the modified text with the inserted logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients scheduled for elective knee arthroscopy.
  -  Patients aged between 21 and 50 years old.
  -  Patients with American Society of Anaesthesiologists physical status I [OR] II.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with known allergy to any drug used in the study.
  -  Patients with chronic use of analgesics [AND]/or sedatives.
  -  Patients with sleep apnea syndrome.
  -  Patients with renal [OR] hepatic dysfunction.
  -  Patients [NOT] free of psychiatric disorders.

Note: I inserted the operators as follows:

* [OR] in "physical status I or II" to indicate that either status I or II is acceptable.
* [AND]/or in "chronic use of analgesics and/or sedatives" to indicate that both analgesics and sedatives are included.
* [OR] in "renal or hepatic dysfunction" to indicate that either renal or hepatic dysfunction is an exclusion criterion.
* [NOT] in "Patients [NOT] free of psychiatric disorders" to indicate that the presence of psychiatric disorders is an exclusion criterion.
