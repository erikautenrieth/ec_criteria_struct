Here is the modified text with the inserted logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adult patients (≥ 18 years) with no age limit;
  -  Patients receiving peritoneal dialysis on the investigation center between 2012 AND 2014 (Group1) OR between 2016 AND 2018 (Group 2);
  -  Patient information available in this retrospective study;

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients who have started a peritoneal dialysis program before the periods of investigation;
  -  Patients who have been equipped with apTeleCare during the dialysis program;
  -  Subject under guardianship OR curators OR safeguard of justice;
  -  Patient NOT affiliated to a French social protection;

Note that I inserted the operators as follows:

* AND: to connect the two date ranges in the second inclusion criterion
* OR: to connect the two date ranges in the second inclusion criterion, and to connect the three guardianship/care options in the third exclusion criterion
* NOT: to indicate the negation in the fourth exclusion criterion
