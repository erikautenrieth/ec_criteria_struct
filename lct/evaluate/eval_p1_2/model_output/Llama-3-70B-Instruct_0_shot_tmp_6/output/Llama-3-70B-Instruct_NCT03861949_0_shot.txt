Inclusion Criteria:
  -  adult patients of ASA physical status I [AND] II undergoing surgery under general anaesthesia

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  anticipated difficult mask ventilation [OR] intubation
  -  individuals with significant cardiorespiratory [OR] cerebrovascular disease
  -  pregnant ladies
  -  patient with history of epilepsy
  -  body mass index >25 kg/m2
  -  hemoglobin <8 gm/dL
  -  patients who were [NOT] ambulant for >24 h, having SpO2 < 97% while breathing in room air
  -  phobia to facemask.

Note: I inserted the [AND] operator to indicate that both ASA physical status I and II must be met. I inserted the [OR] operator to indicate that either difficult mask ventilation or intubation is an exclusion criterion. I inserted the [NOT] operator to indicate that patients who are not ambulant for >24 h are excluded.
