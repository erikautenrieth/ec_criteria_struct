Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Diagnosis of AD by current McKhann et al. criteria [AND] CDR global score of 0.5 or 1

  -  Age 50 to 90 [AND] No medication changes within the past 30 days

Exclusion Criteria:

  -  Participating in another clinical trial [OR] using an investigational drug or therapy within 30 days of the Screening Visit
  -  [NOT] A history of renal stones
