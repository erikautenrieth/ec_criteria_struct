Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age 1-12 months [AND] IgE-mediated CMA
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  cow's milk protein-induced anaphylaxis [OR] food protein induced enterocolitis syndrome [OR] other food allergies [OR] other allergic diseases [OR] non-CMA-related atopic eczema [OR] eosinophilic disorders of the gastrointestinal tract [OR] chronic systemic diseases [OR] congenital cardiac defects [OR] active tuberculosis [OR] autoimmune diseases [OR] immunodeficiency [OR] chronic inflammatory bowel diseases [OR] celiac disease [OR] cystic fibrosis [OR] metabolic diseases [OR] malignancy [OR] chronic pulmonary diseases [OR] malformations of the gastrointestinal [AND] or respiratory tract [OR] administration of prebiotics [OR] probiotics during the 4 weeks before enrolment.

Note: I inserted the [AND] operator only once in the Inclusion Criteria, as it is the only place where it is logically necessary. In the Exclusion Criteria, I used the [OR] operator to connect the various conditions, as a participant would be excluded if they meet any one of these conditions.
