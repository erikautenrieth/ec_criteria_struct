Inclusion Criteria:
 - Participants will be male and female subjects who suffered total acute Achilles tendon rupture, and which underwent surgical repair.
 - To participate in this study all volunteers will need to present medical and/or physiotherapeutic release for physical/sports activities practice.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Volunteers that did not have Achilles tendon surgical reconstruction.
 - Did not present medical and/or physiotherapeutic release for physical/sports activities practice.
 - Have participated in strength training program for the plantar flexors in the last 6 months.
 - Patients with diabetic diseases.
 - Those with difficulty for understanding and/or executing the test and training protocols in the isokinetic dynamometer will be excluded.
