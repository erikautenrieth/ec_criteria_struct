Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients aged from 18 to 80 years [AND] Obese (BMI ≥ 30kg/m²) [AND] Referred by GPs to the medical analysis laboratory for usual biological assessment [AND] Patients with no respiratory follow-up already in place [AND] Bicarbonate - Concentration ≥ 27 mmol/L [AND] STOP-BANG score ≥ 3
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Acute disease [OR] recently diagnosed chronic disease (< 2 months)
 - Hospitalization for respiratory, metabolic [OR] cardiovascular event (< 2 months)
 - Renal insufficiency stage 4 [OR] 5, or autoimmune disease, or viral hepatitis, or cirrhosis
 - Pregnant women [AND] Patients already included in an interventional study (end of the study < 1 month)
