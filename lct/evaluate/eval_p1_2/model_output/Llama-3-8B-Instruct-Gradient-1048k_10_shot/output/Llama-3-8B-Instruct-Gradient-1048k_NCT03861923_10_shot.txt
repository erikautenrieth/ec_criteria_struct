Inclusion Criteria:
 - Primary complaint of shoulder pain and stiffness [AND] 
 - Gradual onset of symptoms [AND] 
 - Glenohumeral passive range of motion limited in multiple directions [AND] 
 - Glenohumeral passive range of motion decreases as the humerus is abducted from 45 degrees to 90 degrees [AND] 
 - Age 18 to 65 years [AND] 
 - Reports pain with normal activity greater than or equal to 2/10 on the numeric pain rating scale [AND] 
 - Read and speak English [AND]
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Signs or symptoms of cervical radiculopathy, radiculitis, or referral from the cervical spine [NOT]
 - Clinical evidence as determined in the examination of an alternative shoulder diagnosis [NOT]
 - Normal passive shoulder range of motion [NOT]
 - Known radiographic evidence of glenohumeral osteoarthritis [NOT]
 - Known neurologic disease [NOT]
 - Received dry needling or acupuncture within the past 6 months [NOT]
 - Fear of needling [NOT]
 - Currently being treated with anticoagulant medication [NOT]
 - Shoulder pain and disability index (SPADI) score less than 10% [OR] or greater than 90%
