Inclusion Criteria:
 - Men [AND] and women, aged 18 to 60 years.
 - Body mass index 20 to 27 kg/m2
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients [AND] with active inflammatory bowel disease
 2. Patients [AND] with pre-existing Celiac disease, exocrine pancreatic insufficiency [OR] or small bowel malabsorption
 3. Patients [AND] with active bowel malignancy
 4. Patients [AND] with diabetes mellitus [OR] known/ suspected motility disorders of the gut
 5. Patients [AND] with decompensated liver disease
 6. Patients on ezetimibe [OR] bile acid sequestrants
 7. Patients [AND] who are pregnant [OR] breastfeeding.
 8. Patients [AND] with renal disease.
 9. Patients [AND] on benzodiazepine.
10. Unstable cardiac [OR] respiratory disease
11. Any changes to medication [NOT] in the preceding month
12. Patients [AND] with hypersensitive to Revestive [OR] any ingredient, active [OR] a history of the drug within the last 5 years
13. Patients [AND] with GI malignancy (GI tract, hepatobiliary, pancreatic)
