Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] and parents of the patients who accept to participate [AND] sign the informed consent
 - Patients whose molar teeth are diagnosed as molar incisor hypomineralization [OR] by EAPD
 - Teeth that are fully erupted
 - Patients who have at least two permanent first molars [AND] that need fissure sealant
 - Teeth that have white-yellow-brown discoloration that indicate high-caries risk
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Teeth [AND] that are previously restored [OR] have caries
 - Patients [AND] who have orthodontic treatment
 - Patients [AND] who are not cooperative for the dental procedure
 - Teeth [AND] that have dental flourosis [OR] enamel malformation according to the specific syndromes
