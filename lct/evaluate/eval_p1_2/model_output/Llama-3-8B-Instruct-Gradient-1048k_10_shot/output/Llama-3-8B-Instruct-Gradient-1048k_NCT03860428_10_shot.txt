Inclusion Criteria:
 - Gestational age < 32 weeks
 - Birthweight <1500 g
 - Age less than 72 hours
 - PDA diameter > 1.5 mm
 - Signed informed consent obtained from both parents
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Birthweight ≥ 1500 g [AND] and/or gestation age ≥ 32 weeks
 - Lack of informed consent [OR] of the parents
 - Congenital heart defect, other than PDA [OR] and/or patent foramen ovale (PFO)
 - The presence [OR] of a clinically apparent hemorrhagic syndrome
 - Any intraventricular hemorrhage (IVH) [OR] in the first 48 hours [OR] IVH grade 3-4
 - A platelet count [AND] of < 50,000/mm3
 - A serum creatinine concentration [AND] of > 110 μmol/L
 - Oliguria [AND] <1 ml/kg/h
 - Suspected/apparent NEC
 - Suspected/apparent lung hypoplasia
