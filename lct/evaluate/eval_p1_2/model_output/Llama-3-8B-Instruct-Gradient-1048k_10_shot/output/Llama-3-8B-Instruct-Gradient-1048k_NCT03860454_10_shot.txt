Inclusion Criteria:
 - LMNA+ cases [AND] with pathogenic LMNA mutations [AND] for LMNA+ and heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients [AND] who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation [AND] at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
 - DCMWT cases: patients [AND] with heart muscle failure [AND] but with wild-type lamin gene. Heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients [AND] who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation [AND] at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
 - HV (controls): matched [AND] to cases.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Needle-phobia [AND] that would preclude blood-letting
 - Participants [AND] unwilling to consent
 - Patients [AND] that have a conventional contraindication [AND] for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).
 - Patients [AND] that have had a blood transfusion [AND] within the last month [AND] and patients having haemodialysis will be excluded.
