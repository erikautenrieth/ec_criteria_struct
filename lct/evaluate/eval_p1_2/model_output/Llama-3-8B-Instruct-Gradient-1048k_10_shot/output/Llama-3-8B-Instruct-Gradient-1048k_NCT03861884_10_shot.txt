Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Ability to give informed consent. [AND]
 2. Either pathological, genetic or imaging biomarker evidence to suggest Fronto-temporal Lobar Degeneration, Alzheimer's Disease or Vascular Cognitive Impairment, [OR] defined clinical diagnoses. [AND]
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Lack of mental capacity to consent to study involvement. [NOT]
 2. Not speaking English before age 5 years. [NOT]
 3. Learning disability. [NOT]
 4. MRI contraindications [NOT]
