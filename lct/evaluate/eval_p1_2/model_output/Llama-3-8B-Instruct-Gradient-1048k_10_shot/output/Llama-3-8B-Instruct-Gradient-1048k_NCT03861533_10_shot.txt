Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Adults [AND] ≥ 18 years old
 2. High risk [AND] for cardiovascular morbidity [AND] and mortality such as prior history of clinical cardiovascular disease [AND] and/or history of familial hypercholesterolemia
 3. LDL-C [AND] within the past 6 months above recommended level [AND] despite maximal tolerated statin therapy [AND] ± ezetimibe for the past 3 months.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Current treatment [NOT] with PCSK9 inhibitor
 2. Current participation [NOT] in investigational study
 3. Prior participation [NOT] in the GOAL program
