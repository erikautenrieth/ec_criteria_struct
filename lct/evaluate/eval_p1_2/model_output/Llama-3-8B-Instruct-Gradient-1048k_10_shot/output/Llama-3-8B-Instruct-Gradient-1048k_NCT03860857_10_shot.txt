Inclusion Criteria:
 - 1. Between [AND] the ages of 60 [AND] and 85;
 - 2. Speaks [AND] fluent English;
 - 3. Visual [AND] auditory acuity adequate for neuropsychological [AND] computerized testing;
 - 4. Good general health [AND] with no disease(s) expected to interfere with the study;
 - 5. Willing [AND] able to participate for the duration of the study [AND] in all study procedures including MRI [AND] PET;
 - 6. Normal cognition [AND] defined as a Clinical Dementia Rating of 0 [AND] a Mini-Mental State Examination score of 27 [OR] higher.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - 1. Significant co-morbid neurologic disease [OR] such as Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, brain cyst, tumor or aneurysm;
 - 2. Major health conditions [OR] such as uncontrolled diabetes mellitus, uncontrolled hypertension, nutritional deficiency or uncontrolled thyroid disease;
 - 3. Significant psychiatric disorders [OR] such as schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, anxiety disorder, major depressive disorder, or attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder;
 - 4. ADRC consensus diagnosis [NOT] of dementia or mild cognitive impairment;
 - 5. Cognitive impairment [NOT] when tested at baseline (defined as a score on any neuropsychological test of 1.5 SDs [OR] more outside the age norm);
 - 6. Alcohol [OR] substance abuse [OR] dependence within the past 2 years (DSM-IV criteria);
 - 7. MRI contraindications [OR] e.g. pacemakers, aneurysm clips, artificial heart valves, ear implants, metal fragments [OR] foreign objects in the eyes, skin [OR] body. Females who are pregnant [OR] trying to get pregnant are also excluded;
 - 8. PET contraindications [OR] e.g. significant prior radiation exposure [OR] pregnancy.
