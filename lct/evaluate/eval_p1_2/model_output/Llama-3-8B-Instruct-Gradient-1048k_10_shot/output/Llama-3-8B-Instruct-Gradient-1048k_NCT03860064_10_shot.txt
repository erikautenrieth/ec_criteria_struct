Inclusion Criteria:
 - 18-35-year-old women [AND] BMI: >18 [AND] and < 30 kg/m2 [AND] Normal female karyotype [AND] Normal uterus [AND] and ovaries at transvaginal ultrasound
Exclusion Criteria:
 - > 35 years
 - PCO syndrome carriers
 - Null follicle aspiration
 - History of chemotherapy [OR] radiotherapy
