Inclusion Criteria:
 - Chronic (more than three months) ESBL colonization of the same strain as determined by
 - species, phenotype and antibiogram. [AND]
 - Patients must be able to swallow sachets. [AND]
 - Must be able to speak and understand Swedish. [AND]
 - Must have a permanent residence in Sweden. [AND]
 - Must be able to sign informed consent in Swedish. [AND]
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Immunosuppression (i.e. chemotheraphy, treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors). [NOT]
 - In patient care. [NOT]
 - Immunodeficiency. [NOT]
 - Psychiatric disorder. [NOT]
 - Alcohol or substance abuse. [NOT]
 - Dementia. [NOT]
 - Invasive catheters. [NOT]
