Inclusion Criteria:
• Patients > 18 years old [AND] with ASA status I-III, undergoing VATS for lobectomy [OR] or wedge.
Non-inclusion Criteria:
 - < 18 years old
 - BMI > 35
 - Chronic pain history [AND] with regular opioid [OR] and/or gabapentinoids use during the 2 weeks before surgery
 - Regular marijuana use
 - History of thoracic surgery [AND] on the operated side
 - Epidural analgesia preferred [AND] due to an anticipated high risk of conversion to thoracotomy
 - Unable to communicate [NOT] with the investigators
 - Receiving anticoagulation [OR] or experiencing any bleeding disorder
 - Surgery for empyema [AND] sympathectomy
 - Known allergy to local anesthetics, [OR] fentanyl [OR] or hydromorphone
 - Active infection [AND] at injection sites
 - Preexisting neurological deficit [OR] psychiatric illness
 - Severe cardiovascular disease
 - Liver failure
 - Renal failure (estimated glomerular filtration rate <15 mL/ min/1.73 m2)
 - Pregnancy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Perioperative conversion to thoracotomy
 - Severe intra- [OR] postoperative bleeding
 - Patients requiring postoperative mechanical ventilation
 - Technical inability to proceed with the blocks
