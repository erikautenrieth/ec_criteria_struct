Inclusion Criteria:
 - a planned operation of coronary artery bypass grafting [AND] with the use of cardiopulmonary bypass
Exclusion Criteria:
 - emergency operations [OR] or re-operations;
 - a known pathology of the urinary tract [OR] or renal failure;
 - chronic use of the following medications: iron, non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs), immunosuppression, [OR] or steroids in the preoperative period;
 - polycythemia, porphyria [OR] or pathological hemoglobin species in anamnesis;
 - preoperative signs of hepatic failure;
 - active autoimmune [OR] or neoplastic diseases, active infection;
 - anticipated significant bleeding (anti-platelet agents), suggesting the use of blood-derived products during the operation [AND] afterward
 - [NOT] acute myocardial infarction after operation
