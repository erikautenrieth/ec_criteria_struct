Inclusion Criteria-- Participants must be:
 -  Healthy (determined by self-report)
 -  Between the ages of 18-55 years old
 -  Current cigarette smokers
 -  Willing [AND] to provide informed consent
 -  Able [AND] to attend the lab session [AND] abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required
 -  Agree [AND] to use designated products according to the study protocol
Exclusion Criteria:
 -  Women if they are breast-feeding [OR] test positive for pregnancy (by urinalysis) at screening.
 -  Individuals who weigh less than 110 pounds.
 -  [NOT] eligible due to other criteria not disclosed at this time.
