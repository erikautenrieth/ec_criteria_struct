Inclusion Criteria:
 - Ulcerative colitis [AND] with confirmed diagnosis by histology [AND] and endoscopy [AND] in stable remission for 3 months or more without therapy [OR] or with a maintenance therapy (except steroids [OR] and antibiotics for 3 months)
 - A negative pregnancy test
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] No history or diagnosis of any of the following conditions: Chron's disease, bleeding disorders, gastrointestinal [OR] or colonic malignancy, Kidney disease, including kidney "stones" [OR] or hypercalcemia, Coagulopathy/ [OR] hereditary hemorrhagic disorders/ [OR] or receiving therapeutic doses of Coumadin [OR] or heparin.
 - Ingested certain medications (Calcium, [OR] Vitamin D, fiber supplements [OR] and Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs - NSAIDs) within 30 days of study start.
