Inclusion Criteria:
 - Men [OR] and women over 18 years old.
 - Written informed consent according to ICH / GCP and Spanish legislation, obtained before any study procedure.
 - Patient with a BMI equal to or greater than 30 Kg / m2
 - Patients with clinical [OR] and radiological criteria of knee osteoarthritis.
 - Patients with indication for primary TKA according to medical criteria.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnancy [OR] or lactation.
 - Inability to give informed consent in the [NOT] absence of a legal representative.
 - Subjects that are participating in a study [OR] with medicines [OR] or other medical devices.
 - Those who show inability to follow the instructions or collaborate during the development of the study.
 - If in the opinion of the researcher there are findings in the physical examination, abnormalities in the results of the clinical analyzes or other medical, [OR] social [OR] or psychosocial factors that could have a negative influence
 - Having had a previous surgical procedure on [OR] or around the knee in the last 12 months (such as proximal tibial osteotomy, [OR] femoral osteotomy, [OR] open reduction and internal fixation for a fracture, [OR] patellectomy [OR] or patellar realignment).

 - Have a varus angle [OR] or valgus ≥ 15 °.
