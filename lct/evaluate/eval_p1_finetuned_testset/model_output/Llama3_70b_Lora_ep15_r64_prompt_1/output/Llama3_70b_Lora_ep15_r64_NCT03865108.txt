Inclusion Criteria:
 - Singleton gestation.
 - Women [AND] with any of the following:
 - Minor cervical lesions (less than 1.5 cm)
 - Limiting or impaired social support system defined as inability to provide housing, [OR] financial support, [OR] nutritional support, [OR] history of substance abuse, [OR] prior imprisonment, [OR] history of psychiatric treatment
 - History of prior adverse outcome (stillbirth, [OR] preterm delivery, [OR] low birth weight infant, [OR] neonatal death, [OR] infant with congenital anomaly)
 - Multiple failures of tocolysis (defined as failure to respond to oral or intravenous (IV) tocolysis with contraction reduction or resumption of tocolysis after 24 hours)
 - Uterine anomalies (e.g., bi- or septate uterus, [OR] fibroids)
 - Chronic noncontrollable pain (pain that cannot be controlled with oral pain medication)
 - Any contraindication to vaginal or cesarean delivery (e.g., prior "classic" hysterotomy or myomectomy, [OR] placenta accreta, [OR] vasa previa)
 - Women [AND] with any of the following:
 - History of prior cesarean delivery, [OR] any surgery on the uterus
 - Presence of an intrauterine device (IUD)
 - Non-English speaking
 - Not available for follow-up
 - Current use of incontinence briefs or pads for urinary incontinence
 - Current use of any incontinence products (e.g., pads, briefs, adult diapers, sanitary pads, etc.)
 - Women [AND] with major or anatomically distorting cervical lesions (defined as greater than or equal to 1.5 cm)
 - Women [AND] with any contraindication to placement of any of the study devices (e.g., allergic response, [OR] prior reaction to placement of any device, [OR] current presence of any device)
 - Women [AND] with current or previous (before 15 weeks gestation) use of tocolytics (pharmacologic or [OR] non-pharmacologic)
 - Women [AND] with current use of non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs)
 - Women [AND] with current or prior use of any cervical internal or external cerclage, [OR] cervical pessary, [OR] osmotic dilator, [OR] any other device used for cervical support or shortening
 - Previously implanted with the PESSARY System
 - Women [AND] in labor (defined as ruptured membranes [AND] with contractions, [OR] 2 cm or more dilated)
 - Known uteroplacental insufficiency, [OR] known fetal structural abnormalities, [OR] known fetal growth restriction, [OR] known fetal major genetic abnormality, [OR] known immunologic alloimmune thrombocytopenia, [OR] known fetal anemia requiring intrauterine transfusion, [OR] known fetal major nonimmune hydrops, [OR] known oligohydramnios, [OR] known nonimmune fetal anemia
 - Known HIV, [OR] tuberculosis, [OR] hepatitis
 - Immunocompromised
 - Life-threatening maternal illness (e.g., cancer requiring chemotherapy, [OR] uncontrolled diabetes, [OR] uncontrolled hypertension, [OR] cardiac disease, [OR] liver disease, [OR] renal disease, [OR] pulmonary disease)
 - Severe preeclampsia
 - Acute maternal illness (e.g., urinary tract infection, [OR] pneumonia, [OR] pyelonephritis, [OR] sepsis)
 - Acute fetal distress requiring delivery
 - Maternal contraindication to surgery
 - Maternal plan to terminate pregnancy
 - Unable to provide informed consent
 - Unable to be contacted for follow-up
 - Impaired social support system defined as inability to provide housing, [OR] financial support, [OR] nutritional support, [OR] history of substance abuse, [OR] prior imprisonment, [OR] history of psychiatric treatment
 - Previously enrolled in the study and received the PESSARY System
 - Hypersensitivity to any of the device materials
 - Clinical history of uterine surgery, [OR] fibroids, [OR] uterine malformations
 - Any other condition or situation that the physician investigator feels would put the patient at increased risk or preclude the patient's participation in the study
 - Concurrent participation in another interventional study
 - Positive HIV, [OR] Hepatitis B, [OR] Hepatitis C, [OR] Syphilis (Treponema Pallidum) result
 - Vaginal bleeding
 - Vaginal or urinary tract infection
 - Vaginal or pelvic ultrasound evidence of any of the following:
 - Amniotic sac membrane, [OR] placenta previa, [OR] vasa previa, [OR] intrauterine adnexal masses, [OR] empty amniotic sac, [OR] empty intrauterine cavity, [OR] fetal demise, [OR] twin or other multiple gestation, [OR] fetal structural abnormalities, [OR] fetal growth restriction, [OR] fetal major genetic abnormality, [OR] immunologic alloimmune thrombocytopenia, [OR] fetal anemia requiring intrauterine transfusion, [OR] fetal major nonimmune hydrops, [OR] oligohydramnios, [OR] nonimmune fetal anemia
