Inclusion Criteria:
 - LMNA+ cases with pathogenic LMNA mutations [AND] for LMNA+ and heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
 - DCMWT cases: patients with heart muscle failure [OR] with wild-type lamin gene. Heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
 - HV (controls): matched [AND] to cases.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Needle-phobia [NOT] that would preclude blood-letting
 - Participants [NOT] unwilling to consent
 - Patients that have a conventional [NOT] contraindication for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).
 - Patients that have had a blood transfusion [NOT] within the last month [OR] patients having haemodialysis will be excluded.
