Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Subject is greater than 18 years of age [AND] willing and able to provide informed consent
 2. Subject is scheduled to undergo elective rotator cuff surgery
 3. Subject has consented to an interscalene block

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Opioid dependence [OR] any subject diagnosed with a chronic pain condition which daily opioid use is needed
 2. Anatomical malformation [OR] which in the investigator's opinion may interfere with the placement of the nerve block
 3. Raynaud's disease diagnosis [OR] vasculopathy
 4. Patient refusal [NOT] willing to provide informed consent
