Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adult [AND] age ≥ 18
 - Patient undergoing non-emergent cardiac surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Emergency surgery [OR] re-do surgery
 - Prior cardiac surgery
 - Congenital cardiac disease [OR] deformation
 - Transplant surgery
 - Ventricular Assist Device Surgery

 - Atrial fibrillation

 - Known previous [OR] concurrent enrollment in a clinical trial that, in the opinion of the investigator, might [NOT] interfere with the objectives of this clinical trial
 - Pregnancy
