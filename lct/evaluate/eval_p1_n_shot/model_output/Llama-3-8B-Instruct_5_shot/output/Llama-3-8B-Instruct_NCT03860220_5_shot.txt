Inclusion Criteria:
  - 145 mmHg [AND] ≤ msSBP [AND] ≤ 190 mmHg
  - Triglycerides [AND] < 500 mg/dL
  - LDL-C [AND] ≤ 250 mg/dL

Exclusion Criteria:
  - sSBP [NOT] ≥ 20mmHg [AND] sDBP [NOT] ≥ 10mmHg
  - symptomatic orthostatic hypotension [OR] secondary/iatrogenic hypertension [OR] dyslipidemia
  - history of moderate to severe cerebral ischemia, cerebral hemorrhage, transient ischemic attack, myocardial infarction, or unstable angina in the past 6 months; severe heart failure (New York Heart Association functional class III [AND] IV)
  - hypersensitivity to telmisartan [OR] rosuvastatin
  - history of angioedema after treatment with angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors [OR] ARBs; creatinine phosphokinase levels [NOT] ≥3 times the upper limit of normal
  - estimated glomerular filtration rate [NOT] ≤30 mL/min; aspartate aminotransferase [AND] alanine aminotransferase levels [NOT] ≥3 times the upper limit of normal
  - potassium levels [NOT] >5.5 mmol/L; or any diseases that could affect the results of the study.
