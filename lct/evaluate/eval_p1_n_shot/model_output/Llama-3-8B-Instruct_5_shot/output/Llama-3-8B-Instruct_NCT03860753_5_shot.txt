Inclusion Criteria:
  - treatment-seeking individuals diagnosed with AUD [AND] according to Diagnostic Statistical Manual 5 (DSM-5)
  - fluent in English
  - past month excessive alcohol use (>7 drinks/week for women [OR] >14 drinks/week for men [OR] >3 drinks/occasion for women [OR] >4 drinks/occasion for men)
  - baseline Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A) [OR] Perceived Stress Scale (PSS) score indicative of mild to moderate anxiety (score 8-23) [OR] moderate stress (score 14-26), respectively
  - increase in alcohol craving following the baseline stress reactivity assessment.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Individuals will be excluded for exhibiting severe scores on the HAM-A, PSS, [OR] post-traumatic (PTSD) checklist (PCL-5) at the discretion of the admitting physician.
  - physical dependence on alcohol [AND] according to the Alcohol Use Disorders Inventory (AUDIT) score indicative of severe alcohol dependence (≥13 for women [OR] ≥15 for men).
  - greater than mild substance use disorder on drugs other than alcohol, nicotine, [OR] marijuana.
  - contraindications for taking pioglitazone [OR] medical conditions contraindicating pioglitazone pharmacotherapy [OR] taking contraindicated medications.
  - be [NOT] pregnant, nursing, [OR] planning on becoming pregnant during the course of the study.
  - have any other illness, condition, [OR] use of medications, which in the opinion of the PI and/or admitting physician would preclude safe [AND] successful completion of the study.
