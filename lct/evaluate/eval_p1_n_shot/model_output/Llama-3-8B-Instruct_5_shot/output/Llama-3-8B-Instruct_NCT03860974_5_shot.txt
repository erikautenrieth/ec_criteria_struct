Inclusion Criteria:
 - undergoing unilateral [OR] or bilateral breast surgery with at least moderate postoperative pain anticipated
 - analgesic plan includes a single-injection peripheral nerve block[s]
 - age 18 years [AND] or older
Exclusion Criteria:
 - morbid obesity [NOT] as defined by a body mass index > 40 (BMI=weight in kg / [height in meters]2)
 - renal insufficiency [NOT] (preoperative creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL)
 - chronic opioid use [NOT] (daily use within the 2 weeks prior to surgery and duration of use > 4 weeks)
 - history of opioid abuse
 - any comorbidity which results in moderate [OR] or severe functional limitation [AND] inability to communicate with the investigators [OR] hospital staff
 - pregnancy
 - planned regional analgesic with perineural catheter placement
 - incarceration
