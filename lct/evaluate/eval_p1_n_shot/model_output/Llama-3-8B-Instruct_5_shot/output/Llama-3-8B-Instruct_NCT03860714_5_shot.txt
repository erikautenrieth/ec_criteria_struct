Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
  - Non-cardiac surgery patients;
    - Age is greater than or equal to 60 years old;
    - Han Nationality, mother tongue is Chinese;
    - The MMSE score: Illiteracy is greater than or equal to 17 points, primary school is greater than or equal to 20 points, higher secondary school is more than 24 points;
    - The people signed informed consent.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
  - The diagnosis of preoperative delirium;
  - The diagnosis [NOT] unstable hypertension (such as: pheochromocytoma [OR] or aortic dissection);
  - Severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] or congestive heart failure;
  - Severe liver [OR] and kidney dysfunction;
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
