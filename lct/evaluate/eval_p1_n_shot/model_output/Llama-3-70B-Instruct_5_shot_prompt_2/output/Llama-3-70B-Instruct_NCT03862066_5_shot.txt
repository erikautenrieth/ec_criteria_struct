Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Newly diagnosed histologically proven locoregional oral squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC) [AND] without evidence of distant metastases. OSCC includes the subsites of oral tongue, [OR] floor of mouth, [OR] gingiva, [OR] retromolar trigone [OR] and buccal mucosa [OR] Recurrent [OR] or persistent histologically proven locoregional OSCC that was initially treated with surgery alone.
  -  must be eligible for surgical resection
  -  greater than 18 years of age
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  prior immunotherapy [OR] treatment with another anti PD-1 agent besides nivolumab
  -  prior chemotherapy [AND] including cetuximab [OR] radiation therapy
  -  concomitant malignancies [NOT] except cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma [OR] or basal cell carcinoma
  -  unresectable primary tumor [OR] regional disease [OR] or distant metastases
