Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Gynecological procedure [AND]
  -  Age 18-50 years [AND]
  -  General anesthesia planned [AND]
  -  Systolic blood pressure < 150 mmHg [AND] HR < 100 beats/min
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pre-existing hypertension [OR]
  -  Diabetes [OR]
  -  Ischemic heart disease [OR] cerebrovascular disease
  -  Heart valve disease [OR]
  -  Verified cardiac arrhythmia other than extrasystoles [OR]
  -  Verified anaemia with hemoglobin level below 9.0 gr/dl [OR]
  -  Kidney [OR] hepatic disease
  -  Hypersensitivity for propofol [OR] soya [OR] eggs [OR] peanuts
  -  Pregnancy [OR]
  -  Poor health state [OR]
  -  Illicit substance use [OR]
  -  BMI <20 [OR] >35 kg/m2
  -  [NOT] meeting any of the above criteria
