Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with high risk class II, class III, class IV abdominal wounds [AND] undergoing laparotomy for gynecologic related disorders [AND] patients undergoing laparotomy for both benign [OR] and malignant diagnoses will be included in this study
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnancy
 - Allergy to triclosan
 - Patients undergoing HIPEC
