Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Subject is greater than 18 years of age
  2. Subject is willing [AND] and able to provide informed consent
  3. Subject is scheduled [AND] to undergo elective rotator cuff surgery
  4. Subject has consented [AND] to an interscalene block
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Opioid dependence
  2. Any subject diagnosed [OR] with a chronic pain condition which daily opioid use is needed
  3. Anatomical malformation, which in the investigator's [OR] opinion may interfere with the placement of the nerve block
  4. Raynaud's disease diagnosis
  5. Vasculopathy
  6. Patient refusal
