Inclusion Criteria:
 - patients in different age groups [AND] both sex referred from orthopedic outpatient clinic with chronic ankle pain [AND] suspected clinically to have impingement syndrome.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Inflammatory arthritis [OR] of ankle joint, infection [OR] in ankle joint, neoplasm [OR] around ankle joint.
  2. Patients with contraindications for MRI, e.g. [OR] an implanted magnetic device, pacemakers [OR] or claustrophobia.
