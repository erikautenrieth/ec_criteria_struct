<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adenocarcinoma of the prostate [AND]
  -  Performance Status < 2 [AND]
  -  Clinical stages (not radiographic stage) T1c - T2a, Nx [OR] N0, Mx [OR] M0 [AND]
  -  Gleason 6-7 cancer [AND]
  -  Prostate volume < 60 cc (if MRI [AND] TRUS have conflicting values, then MRI value will be utilized) [AND]
  -  International Prostate symptom score (IPSS) 20 [OR] less [AND]
  -  Ability to undergo DCF-Pyl PSMA PET as part of pretreatment staging [AND]
  -  Signed study-specific consent form prior to registration
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Prior history of pelvic radiation therapy [OR]
  -  Major medical [OR] psychiatric illness which, in the investigator's opinion, would prevent completion of treatment [AND] would interfere with follow up. [OR]
  -  Implanted device [OR] apparatus which obstruct visibility of the implanted sources on fluoroscopy [OR]
  -  Metallic implants, claustrophobia [NOT] amenable to medication, [OR] known contraindications to undergoing MR scanning [OR]
  -  History of other malignancy diagnosed within the past 3 years
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
