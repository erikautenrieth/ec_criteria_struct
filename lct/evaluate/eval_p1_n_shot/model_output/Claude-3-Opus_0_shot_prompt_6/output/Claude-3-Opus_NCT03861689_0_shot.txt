Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age ≥ 18 years old [AND]
  -  Confirmed diagnosis of perianal Crohn's disease [AND]
  -  On biologics [OR] will start biologics
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients who have perianal fistula due to causes other than Crohn's disease [OR]
  -  Fistulation into other organs, including bladder [OR] intestine [OR]
  -  Patients who have allergic reaction [OR] contraindications to anti-TNF [OR]
  -  Patients who have active cancer [OR]
  -  Patients who have contraindications for MRI [OR]
  -  Known pregnancy
For patients who refuse to participate in the tight monitoring arm, they will be consented [AND] recruited to the control arm for comparison.
