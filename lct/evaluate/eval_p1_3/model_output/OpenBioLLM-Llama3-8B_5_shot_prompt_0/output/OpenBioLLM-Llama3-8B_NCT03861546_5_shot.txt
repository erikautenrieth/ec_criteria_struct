Inclusion Criteria:
 - age > 18 years;
 - confirmed diagnosis of prediabetes [OR] or T2D (documented A1c of ≥ 5.7%, 2-hour post-load glucose of >140, [OR] or FBG of >100);
 - a family member [OR] or peer willing to participate [AND] and attend all sessions as a social influencer;
 - proficiency in English of at least one member of each dyad,
 - willingness to provide written consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - type 1 diabetes [OR] or diabetes secondary to other conditions (e.g. steroid-induced, pancreatic insufficiency, [OR] or chemotherapy-induced);
 - malignancy [OR] or life-threatening illness with life expectancy of <5 years;
 - end-stage disease [OR] or serious illness that prohibits participation (e.g. end-stage renal disease [OR] or class IV congestive heart failure);
 - inability to perform unsupervised physical activity;
 - pregnancy;
 - diagnosed cognitive deficits [OR] or limited decision-making capacity;
 - alcohol [OR] or substance abuse;
