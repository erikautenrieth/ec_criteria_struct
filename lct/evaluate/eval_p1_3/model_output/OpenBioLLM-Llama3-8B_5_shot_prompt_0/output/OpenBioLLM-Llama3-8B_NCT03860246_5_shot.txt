Inclusion Criteria:
 - Nursing home residents [AND] with ≥2 of the following risk factors for atrial fibrillation:
 1. age ≥75 years
 2. female sex
 3. obstructive sleep apnea
 4. peripheral vascular disease
 5. diabetes mellitus
 6. obesity (body mass index [BMI] >30 kg/m2)
 7. hypertension
 8. congestive heart failure
Exclusion Criteria:
 - prior diagnosis of AF
 - continuous rhythm monitoring over the past year
 - pacemaker [OR] or implantable cardioverter defibrillator in situ<|im_end|>
