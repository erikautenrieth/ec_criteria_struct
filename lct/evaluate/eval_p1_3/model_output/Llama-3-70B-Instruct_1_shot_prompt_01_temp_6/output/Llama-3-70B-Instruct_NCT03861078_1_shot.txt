Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria-- Participants must be:
  -  Healthy (determined by self-report)
  -  Between the ages of 18-55 years old
  -  Current cigarette smokers
  -  [AND] Willing to provide informed consent
  -  [AND] Able to attend the lab session [AND] abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required
  -  [AND] Agree to use designated products according to the study protocol

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Women [IF] they are breast-feeding [OR] test positive for pregnancy (by urinalysis) at screening.
  -  Individuals who weigh less than 110 pounds.
  -  [NOT] meeting the above inclusion criteria

Note: I added [IF] to the first exclusion criterion to clarify the conditional statement.
