Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who fulfilled the following criteria were included in the study:
 1. Adult renal transplant patients (≥21years),
 2. Informed consent,
 3. Stable maintenance subcutaneous ESA therapy with constant dose interval during the last 2 months,
 4. Haemoglobin (Hb) ≥10mg/dL (Hct 32%),
 5. Transferrin saturation (TSAT) ≥20% (during the screening phase),
 6. and serum ferritin ≥100 ng/mL
Exclusion Criteria:
We excluded patients with the following criteria:
 1. Acute or chronic bleeding, or erythrocyte transfusion, within the preceding 8 weeks. change in Hb level ≥ 2 g/dL during screening phase,
 2. Hemolytic anemia,
 3. Recent infection or rejection,
 4. Diastolic blood pressure >100mmHg or discontinuation of ESA due to hypertension in the 6 months prior to study,
 5. Vitamin B12 or folic acid deficiency,
 6. Uncontrolled or secondary hyperparathyroidism,
 7. Acute or chronic systemic inflammatory disease and/or C-reactive protein (CRP) >30 mg/L,
 8. Hemodialysis due to failure of a kidney transplant,
 9. and malignancy
