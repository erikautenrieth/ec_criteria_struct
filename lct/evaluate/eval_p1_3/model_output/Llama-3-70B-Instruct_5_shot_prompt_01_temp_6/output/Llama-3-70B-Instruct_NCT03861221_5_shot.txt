Inclusion Criteria:
 -  Female, older than 35 years [AND] already have mammography, [OR] other standard exams (ultrasound, MRI, etc) [AND] could participate this trial within 2 weeks after mammography [AND] have the ability of consent inform.

Exclusion Criteria:
 -  During pregnancy [OR] lactation period [NOT] capable of prone position, such as patients with scapulohumeral periarthritis, [OR] cardiac pacemaker [OR] have recent cardiac surgery.
 - Lymphoma patients that [NOT] under therapy.
 - Severe scoliosis.
