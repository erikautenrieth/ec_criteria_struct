Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with NEC (stages I-III Bell's Classification) [AND] 
 - Weight greater or equal to 750 g. [AND] 
 - Gestational age < 36 weeks. [AND] 
 - Patients whose parents consent to participate in the study. [AND]
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with major congenital anomalies [AND] 
 - No antecedent of limb ischemia/limb thrombotic events. [AND] 
 - No antecedent diagnosis of occlusive arterial [OR] venous thrombosis. [AND] 
 - Hemodynamic instability
