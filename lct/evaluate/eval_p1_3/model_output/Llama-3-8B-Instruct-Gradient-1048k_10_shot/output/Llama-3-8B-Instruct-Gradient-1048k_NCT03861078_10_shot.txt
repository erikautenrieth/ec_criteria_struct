Inclusion Criteria:
 - Participants [AND] must be healthy (determined by self-report)
 - Participants [AND] must be between the ages of 18-55 years old
 - Participants [AND] must be current cigarette smokers
 - Participants [AND] must be willing to provide informed consent
 - Participants [AND] must be able to attend the lab session [AND] abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required
 - Participants [AND] must agree to use designated products according to the study protocol
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Women [AND] if they are breast-feeding [OR] test positive for pregnancy (by urinalysis) at screening.
 - Individuals [AND] who weigh less than 110 pounds.
