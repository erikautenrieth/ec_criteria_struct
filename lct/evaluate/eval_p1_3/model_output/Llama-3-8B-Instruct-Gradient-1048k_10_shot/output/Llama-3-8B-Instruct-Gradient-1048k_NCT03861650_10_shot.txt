Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients free from any disease affecting bone formation [AND] of both sexes was included in the study.
  2. Patients younger than 25 years old [AND] were included in the study.
  3. Patients included in the study were post traumatic patients [OR] hemifacial microsomia [OR] Treacher Collins [OR] post ankylotic mandibular deformity.
  4. Distraction range was between 15-20 mm [AND]
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. The presence of uncontrolled systemic disease [NOT]
  2. Previous radiotherapy on head and neck region [NOT]
  3. Chemotherapy within the past 12-month period [NOT]
  4. An active infection at the distraction site [NOT]
