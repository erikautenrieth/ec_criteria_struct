Inclusion Criteria:
 - CAC score >10 [AND] Framingham risk score (10 % or above)
 - Subjects are required to be on stable concomitant medications for at least 12 weeks prior to randomization
 - Diabetic subjects must have HbA1C < 8.0, and stable HbA1C level variation range within 0.5% for three months.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Hypersensitivity to AGE therapy,[OR] Unstable medical, psychiatric, or substance abuse disorder that may interfere with continuation in the study,[AND] Weight ≥325 pounds,[OR] Bleeding disorder,[AND] History of myocardial infarction,[OR] Stroke,[OR] Life-threatening arrhythmia within prior 6 months,[OR] Resting hypotension (systolic < 90 mmHg) or hypertension (resting blood pressure >170/110)[OR] Heart failure NYHA class III or IV,[OR] History of malignancy within the last 5 years (other than skin cancer) or evidence of active cancer which would require concomitant cancer chemotherapy[OR] Serum creatinine >1.4 mg/dl[OR] Triglycerides > 400 at baseline visit[OR] Diabetic subjects with HbA1C > 8 %[OR] Drug or alcohol abuse[AND] Conditions interfering with accurate assessment of coronary calcification (metal clips, bypass patients, intracoronary stents) and drug absorption (partial ileal bypass or malabsorption syndrome).[AND] Current use of anticoagulants (except for antiplatelet agents)[OR] Chronic renal failure[OR] Liver failure[OR] Hematological or biochemical values at baseline visit outside the reference ranges considered as clinically significant.
