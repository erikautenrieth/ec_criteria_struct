Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with a endovenous pacemaker [OR] defibrillator indication.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pre-existing ipsilateral pacing electrode.
  -  Previous ipsilateral lymphadenectomy.
  -  Indication of cardiac resynchronization therapy.

Note: I added the [OR] operator in the Inclusion Criteria, as it seems to be the logical connection between "endovenous pacemaker" and "defibrillator indication". If this is not the case, please let me know and I'll correct it.
