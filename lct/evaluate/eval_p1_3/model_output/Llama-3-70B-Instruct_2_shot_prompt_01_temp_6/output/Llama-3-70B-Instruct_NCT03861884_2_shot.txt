Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Ability to give informed consent.
  2. Either [OR] pathological, genetic [OR] or imaging biomarker evidence to suggest Fronto-temporal Lobar Degeneration, Alzheimer's Disease [OR] or Vascular Cognitive Impairment, or defined clinical diagnoses.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] Ability to give informed consent, i.e., Lack of mental capacity to consent to study involvement.
  2. [NOT] speaking English before age 5 years.
  3. Learning disability.
  4. MRI contraindications
