Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with a endovenous pacemaker [OR] or defibrillator indication.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pre-existing [AND] ipsilateral pacing electrode.
  -  Previous [AND] ipsilateral lymphadenectomy.
  -  Indication of cardiac resynchronization therapy.

Note: I added the logical operators [OR] and [AND] to the inclusion and exclusion criteria, respectively, to clarify the relationships between the conditions.
