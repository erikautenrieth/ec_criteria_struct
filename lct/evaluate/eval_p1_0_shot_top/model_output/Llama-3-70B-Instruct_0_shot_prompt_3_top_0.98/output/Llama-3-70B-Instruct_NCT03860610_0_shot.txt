<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with an unilateral THA [OR] TKA, which they received for the treatment of osteoarthritis (Patient groups 1+2)
  -  Patients diagnosed with unilateral OA of the knee [OR] hip planned for a treatment with THA [OR] TKA (Patient groups 3+4)
  -  Age ≥ 30 years [AND] who are part of the Healthy control group
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Body mass index > 35kg/m2
  -  Use of walking aids
  -  Neuromuscular disorders affecting gait
  -  Inability [NOT] to follow procedures due to psychological disorders [OR] dementia
  -  Diagnosed hip [OR] knee OA [AND] who are part of the Healthy control group
  -  Current pain in the lower extremities [OR] lower back [AND] who are part of the Healthy control group
  -  Rheumatoid Arthritis [AND] who are part of the Healthy control group
  -  Previous corrective osteotomy [AND] who are part of the Healthy control group
  -  Regular intake of pain medication (more than 4 times per week) [AND] who are part of the Healthy control group
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
