<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with NEC (stages I-III Bell's Classification)
  -  Weight greater or equal to 750 g.
  -  Gestational age < 36 weeks.
  -  Patients whose parents consent [AND] to participate in the study.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with major congenital anomalies
  -  [NOT] No antecedent of limb ischemia [OR] limb thrombotic events.
  -  [NOT] No antecedent diagnosis of occlusive arterial [OR] venous thrombosis
  -  Hemodynamic instability
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
