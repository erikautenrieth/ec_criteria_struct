Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with a endovenous pacemaker [OR] or defibrillator indication.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pre-existing ipsilateral pacing electrode [AND] with [NOT] a pre-existing ipsilateral lymphadenectomy.
 - Previous ipsilateral lymphadenectomy [OR] with [NOT] a pre-existing ipsilateral pacing electrode.
 - Indication of cardiac resynchronization therapy [NOT] with a endovenous pacemaker [OR] or defibrillator indication.
