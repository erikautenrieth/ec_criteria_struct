Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Age ≥ 18 [AND] male [OR] female;
  2. Subject [AND] must have had documented MM;
  3. At screening phase, subject [AND] must have measurable disease;
  4. Subject [AND] is in a state of progressive disease [AND] (PD);
  5. Subject [AND] must have life expectancy [NOT] of no less than 6 months;
  6. Subject [AND] must have an ECOG (Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group) performance status score [AND] of 0~2;

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Subject [NOT] has received anti-CD38 monoclonal antibody treatment [OR] previously;
  2. Subject [NOT] has received CAR-T cell therapy [OR] previously;
  3. Subject [NOT] has previously received allogenic stem cell transplant [OR] or subject [NOT] has received autologous stem cell transplant [AND] within 3 months before administration of the study agent;
  4. Primary refractory multiple myeloma [AND] (subject failed to generate any minimal response [OR] or any degree of response to any therapy);
  5. Subject [NOT] has received anti-myeloma treatment [OR] (radiotherapy is excluded) [AND] within 4 weeks [OR] or 5 PK half-lives of the treatment, whichever longer, before the first study agent administration.
