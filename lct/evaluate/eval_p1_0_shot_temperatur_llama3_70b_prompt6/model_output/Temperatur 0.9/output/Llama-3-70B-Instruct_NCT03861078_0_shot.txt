Inclusion Criteria-- Participants must be:
  -  [AND] Healthy (determined by self-report)
  -  [AND] Between the ages of 18-55 years old
  -  [AND] Current cigarette smokers
  -  [AND] Wiling to provide informed consent
  -  [AND] Able to attend the lab session [AND] abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required
  -  [AND] Agree to use designated products according to the study protocol

  -  Women [AND] if they are breast-feeding [OR] test positive for pregnancy (by urinalysis) at screening.
