  1. [AND]Clinical diagnosis of post herpetic neuralgia[AND]with pain persisting for at least 3 months after the onset of herpes zoster rash [OR]
  2. [AND]Clinical diagnosis of distal painful polyneuropathy due to Type I [OR]Type II diabetes mellitus[AND]with:

       3. [AND]no change in diabetic medication planned for the duration of the study
  3. [AND]Positive sensory symptoms (mechanical[OR]thermal) associated with neuropathic pain[AND]confirmed by:
       1. [AND]painDETECT questionnaire (PD-Q)[AND]
       2. [AND]Clinical assessment[AND]showing signs of neuropathic pain in either a dermatomal (PHN)[OR]distal symmetrical distribution (DPN)
8. [AND]An average daily pain score on the NPRS of at least 4[AND]no more than 8 in the last five diary entries before randomisation

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [AND]Presence of moderate[OR]severe pain from other causes that may confound assessment[OR]self-evaluation of NP.
  2. [AND]Subjects with both DPN[AND]PHN
