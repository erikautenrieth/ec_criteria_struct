<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age [AND] 45-79 years
  -  Estimated glomerular filtration rate [AND] 20-60mL/min/1.73m2 [AND] based on CKD-EPI(Cr) equation
  -  Non-dialysis dependent [AND] (NDD)
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease
  -  Unable [OR] unwilling to give consent
  -  Previously received chronic dialysis
  -  Previous [OR] any organ transplant
  -  Patients who diagnosed heart failure [AND] with NYHA class 3 [OR] 4
  -  Known liver cirrhosis [AND] (Child-pugh class 2 [OR] 3)
  -  Pregnant women
  -  Single kidney [AND] due to trauma [OR] donation
  -  Patients who received immunosuppressive agent [AND] within the past 1 year
  -  Patients who diagnosed with glomerulonephritis [AND] through kidney biopsy
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
