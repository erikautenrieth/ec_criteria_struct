<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria
  - [AND] Patients will be recruited from the Division of Gastroenterology and Hepatology [OR] the Mayo Clinic in Rochester, MN.
  - [AND] Adult patients with immune mediated enteropathies [WHO] have had improvement in symptoms [AND] histology on oral budesonide therapy.

Exclusion Criteria
  - [AND] Age <18 years
  - [AND] Positive stool gluten testing in patients with refractory celiac disease
  - [AND] Small bowel malignancy [OR] history of small bowel malignancy
  - [AND] Refractory celiac disease type 2
  - [AND] Post-transplant lymphoproliferative disorder associated enteropathy
  - [AND] [NOT] prior improvement in symptoms [AND] histology with budesonide therapy
  - [AND] Discontinuation of budesonide therapy prior to the trial
  - [AND] Other concurrent systemic corticosteroids
  - [AND] Other immune mediating medications, for example [BUT] not limited to azathioprine, 6-mercaptopurine, cyclosporine, methotrexate, anti-TNF monoclonal antibodies, alpha-4 beta-7 integrin inhibiting monoclonal antibody, interleukin 12/23 inhibiting monoclonal antibody, JAK inhibitors.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
