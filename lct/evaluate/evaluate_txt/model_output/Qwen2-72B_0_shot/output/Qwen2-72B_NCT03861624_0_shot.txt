<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. [AND] Skeletal maturity
  2. [AND] AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures
  3. [AND] Wound primarily closeable [OR] (no flap [OR] delay in closure due to contamination needed)
  4. [AND] Palpable pedal pulses [OR] (no vascular injury sustained)
  5. [AND] Presentation within 24 hours from injury

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [AND] [NOT] Current injury is a pathologic fracture
  2. [AND] [NOT] Sustained bilateral tibia fracture
  3. [AND] [NOT] Sustained comminuted femur fracture
  4. [AND] [NOT] Sustained severe Traumatic Brain Injury (GCS<12) ***
  5. [AND] [NOT] Sustained severe spinal cord injury (lower extremity paresis/paralysis)
  6. [AND] [NOT] Sustained severe burns (>10% total body surface area (TBSA) [OR] >5% TBSA with full thickness [OR] circumferential injury)
  7. [AND] [NOT] Prior ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery
  8. [AND] [NOT] Prior [OR] current lower limb deformity [OR] abnormality
  9. [AND] [NOT] Unable to complete follow-up visits
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
