<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
[AND] Female subjects between 18 [AND] 65 years of age [AND] currently taking spironolactone for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris [OR]
[AND] Female subjects between 18 [AND] 65 years of age [AND] currently taking oral antibiotics for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris [AND]
Subjects able to complete an online survey in English

Exclusion Criteria:
[OR] Subjects less than 18 years of age [OR] older than 65 years of age [OR]
Subjects that have ever been diagnosed with any mood disorder by a clinician prior to starting spironolactone ([NOT] history of mood disorder [AND] [NOT] current mood disorder) [OR]
Subjects taking hormonal contraception (including [BUT] not limited to oral contraceptive pills) for a duration of less than 6 months [OR]
Subjects that have ever been diagnosed with any type of sexual dysfunction prior to starting spironolactone [OR]
Subjects [NOT] able to complete an online survey in English [OR]
Subjects taking oral isotretinoin for a diagnosis of acne [OR]
Subjects that are currently pregnant
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
