Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] all patients who are assigned to a clinically indicated chest X-ray by the emergency department of Vienna General Hospital
  - [AND] ability to provide informed consent
  - [AND] informed consent after detailed patient briefing

Exclusion Criteria:

  - [AND] [NOT] assigned to chest X-ray as follow-up
