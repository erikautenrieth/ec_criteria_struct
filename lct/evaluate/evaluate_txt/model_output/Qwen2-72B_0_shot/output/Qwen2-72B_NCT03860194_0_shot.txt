<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Older adults (≥ 65 years)
  - [AND] Subject has lower appendicular muscle mass index (AMMI) (<7 kg/m^2 in men; <5.7 kg/m^2 in women) [AND] using Bioelectrical impedance analysis (BIA, TANITA BC-418®, Tokyo, Japan)
  - [AND] Subject has lower handgrip strength (<26 kg in men; <18 kg in women) [AND] using dynamometer (CAMRY®, Zhongshan, Guangdong) [OR] slowly gait speed (<1 m/s)
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] [NOT] Subject's life expectancy was shorter than 6 months
  - [AND] [NOT] Nursing home resident
  - [AND] [NOT] Subject has the history of kidney failure
  - [AND] [NOT] Subject has the history of liver failure
  - [AND] [NOT] Subject has the history of diabetes mellitus
  - [AND] [NOT] Subject has resistance exercise habit.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
