Inclusion Criteria:
 -  Type 2 diabetes mellitus [AND] on metformin therapy [AND] for at least 3 months
Exclusion Criteria:
 -  [NOT] Pregnant [OR] breastfeeding women
 -  Age < 18 years [OR] > 75 years
 -  Estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) < 30 mL/min/1.73 m2
 -  History of pancreatitis
 -  Active liver disease
 -  Current use of insulin [OR] other glucose-lowering medications [OR] except metformin
 -  [NOT] Willingness to discontinue other glucose-lowering medications [AND] for the duration of the study
 -  Known allergy [OR] or hypersensitivity to the study drug [OR] its excipients
 -  Participation in another clinical trial within the past 30 days
