Inclusion Criteria:
 - [AND] Gastroscopy can be performed
 - [AND] Serologic markers including serum pepsinogen can be measured
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [OR] Age < 20 [OR] Age > 70 years
 - [AND] Anemia (serum hemoglobin level < 10 g/dL)
 - [OR] Severe systemic disease
 - [OR] Advanced chronic liver disease
 - [OR] Use of certain medications, including proton pump inhibitors, H2- receptor antagonists, [OR] or antibiotics
 - [OR] History of H. pylori eradication
 - [OR] History of gastric surgery
 - [OR] Recent history of upper gastrointestinal bleeding
