Inclusion Criteria:
 -  Burn patient treated as outpatient [AND] burn surface area > or = 5% of the total body surface area [AND] mainly 2nd degree burn
Exclusion Criteria:
 -  Psychiatric antecedents [OR] Claustrophobia [OR] Heavy hearing [OR] Visual impairment [OR] Face burn [OR] Conjunctivitis [OR] [NOT] Consent not obtained
