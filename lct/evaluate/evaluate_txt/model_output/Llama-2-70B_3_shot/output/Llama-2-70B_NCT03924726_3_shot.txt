  Inclusion Criteria:

* Aged 18 years and above at the start of the treatment [AND]
* Molar relationship either Class I, < ½ unit Class II or Class III [AND]
* Extraction of all four first premolar teeth as part of orthodontic treatment [AND]
* Maximum anchorage required using mini-implant [AND]
* No systemic disease [AND]
* Good oral hygiene [AND]
* No periodontal disease

Exclusion Criteria:

* Significant vertical skeletal discrepancies [NOT]
* Systemic diseases requiring long-term antibiotic use, phenytoin, cyclosporin, anti-inflammatory drugs, bisphosphonates, systemic corticosteroids or calcium channel blockers [NOT]
* Poor oral hygiene [NOT]
* Active periodontal disease [NOT]
