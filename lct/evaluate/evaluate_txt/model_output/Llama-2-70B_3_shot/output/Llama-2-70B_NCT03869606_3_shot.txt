  Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients 11 to no older than 17 years of age
Exclusion Criteria:
 - severed developmental delay [NOT]
 - orthopedic patients with leg injuries [OR]
 - injury that would prevent passive leg elevation [NOT]
 - severe aortic insufficiency [OR]
 - severe anatomic abnormalities of the thoracic aorta [OR]
 - patients with external cardiac pacemakers [NOT]
