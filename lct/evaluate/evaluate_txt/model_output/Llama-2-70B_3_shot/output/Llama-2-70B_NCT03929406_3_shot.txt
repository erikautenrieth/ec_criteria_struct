  Inclusion Criteria:

* Eligible patients for deep brain stimulation surgery [AND]
* Patients affiliated to social security or benefiting of a similar insurance scheme [AND]
* Patients having signed a consent to participate to the study [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:

* Patient not eligible for deep brain stimulation surgery [NOT]
* Pregnant women [OR] nursing mothers [NOT]
* Persons deprived of liberty by judicial or administrative decision [NOT]
* Persons unable to express their consent [OR] legally protected [NOT]
* Persons in period of disqualification for another interventional research [NOT]
