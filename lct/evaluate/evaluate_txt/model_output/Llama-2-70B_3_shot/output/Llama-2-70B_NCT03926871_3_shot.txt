  Inclusion Criteria:
To Novato workers:

* 1 to 2 days in the company [AND]
* Not have previous experience in a frigorifico, or butcher's shop [OR]
* Not have prior knowledge of the activity [AND]

To experienced workers:

* Work on the task for more than six months [AND]
* Be present in company during the study period [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:

* Had already received ergonomic training (general guidelines and postural adjustments) [NOT]
* Pregnant women [NOT]
* People with disabilities [NOT]
