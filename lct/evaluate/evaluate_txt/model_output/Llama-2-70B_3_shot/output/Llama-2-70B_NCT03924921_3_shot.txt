  Inclusion Criteria:

* Being older than 18;
* Have a diagnosis of HIV;
* Present at least one of the following symptoms during the preceding two weeks: sleep problems, fatigue, pain, anxiety, or symptoms of depression;
* Understand and speak French;
* Be able to follow the instructions to learn the relaxation technique.

Exclusion Criteria:

* [NOT] Being older than 18;
* [NOT] Have a diagnosis of HIV;
* [NOT] Present at least one of the following symptoms during the preceding two weeks: sleep problems, fatigue, pain, anxiety, or symptoms of depression;
* [NOT] Understand and speak French;
* [NOT] Be able to follow the instructions to learn the relaxation technique.

Note: The exclusion criteria are the opposite of the inclusion criteria, so the logical operators [NOT] are used to negate the conditions.
