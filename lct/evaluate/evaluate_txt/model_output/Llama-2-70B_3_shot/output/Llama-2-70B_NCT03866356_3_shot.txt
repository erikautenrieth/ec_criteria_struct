  Inclusion Criteria:
 - Female [AND] ≥ 18 years [AND] having an initial diagnosis of SI [AND] literate [AND] having no sensory disorder affecting communication [AND] agreeing to participate in the research
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnancy [OR] presence of a urinary tract infection [OR] previous treatment for SI
Removal Criteria:
 - Patient non-compliance with the protocol [OR] failure in contacting the patient [OR] the patient's wish to withdraw from the study
