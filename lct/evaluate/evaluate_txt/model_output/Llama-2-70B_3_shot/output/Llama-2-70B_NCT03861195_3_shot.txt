  Inclusion Criteria:

* Age ≥18 years [AND]
* BMI ≤40 kg/m2 [AND]
* Indication for hysterectomy for benign disease or prophylactic surgery due to hereditary cancer [OR]
* Indication for radical hysterectomy and/or pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for cervical cancer [OR]
* Indication for modified radical hysterectomy and/or pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for endometrial cancer [OR]
* Size of uterus and vagina allows for retrieval by the vaginal route in cancer patients [AND]
* Written informed consent [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:

* Known extensive intra-abdominal adhesions [NOT]
* Anaesthesiological contraindications to laparoscopy [NOT]
* Women with pacemaker or other implants where electrosurgery is to be avoided [NOT]
* Women with known defects of the hemostasis [NOT]
* Pregnancy [NOT]
* Other internal or anatomical criteria that preclude a minimal invasive approach [NOT]
* Inability to understand patient information [NOT]
