  Inclusion Criteria:

* Having mild or moderate severity of AD [OR] Lack of communication difficulties or problems [AND] Volunteering to participate in the study [AND] Cerebrovascular event [AND] Having disturbing the balance and coordination of neurological disease such as Parkinson, MS [AND] Mini Mental Test Score, 18 to 24 points from being scored [AND] being 65-80 aged.

Exclusion Criteria:

* Patients diagnosed with dementia (infection, vascular, hematological diseases) [OR] patients who could not be contacted during the follow-up period or who did not come to control [OR] cardiac and cerebrovascular event [OR] Endocrine disorder [OR] fluid-electrolyte imbalance [OR] infection [OR] Patients with malignancy and malignancy [OR] chemotherapy [OR] radiotherapy [OR] Patients with delirium or depression [OR] Those with protective sensation loss (those with Semmes-Weinstein Monofilament Thickness 4.56 and above) [OR] patients with lower or upper extremity amputations at any level.
