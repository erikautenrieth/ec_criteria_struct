  Inclusion Criteria:

* COPD diagnostic [AND] both sex [AND] 18 <Age <80 years old [AND] LTOT- group: PaO2 ≥ 65 mmHg [OR] SaO2≥ 92% [OR] LTOT+ group: long term oxygenotherapy prescribed for more than 3 months, with daily use between 12 and 15 hours.

Patients in both groups will be matched for age (± 5 years), sex, and physical activity estimated by GPAQ questionnaire (± 15% Mets.min/week).

Exclusion Criteria:

* Recent cardiorespiratory exacerbation (<6 weeks) [NOT]
* Pulmonary rehabilitation program during the last 2 months [NOT]
* Continuous LTOT (24 hours) [OR] deambulation O2 therapy alone [NOT]
* Anticoagulant drugs [NOT]
* Hematocrit outside the normal range (35-50%) [NOT]
