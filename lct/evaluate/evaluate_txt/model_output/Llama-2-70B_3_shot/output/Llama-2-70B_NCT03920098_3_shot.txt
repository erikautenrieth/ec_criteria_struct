  Inclusion Criteria:
  - Primipara (no previous pregnancies) [AND] 18 to 25 years of age [AND] Gestation <15 weeks at first prenatal visit [AND] No intention to move outside the city of Santiago in the next two years.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Multipara [OR] Pre-existing cancer (except non-melanoma skin cancer) [OR] Family history of breast cancer (one first-degree female relative diagnosed with breast cancer) [OR] High-risk pregnancy according to national guidelines [OR] During the follow-up, pregnant women under any treatment that may affect the levels of metabolic markers will be excluded from the analysis [OR] Pregnant women with pre-eclampsia [OR] with preterm birth (less than 37 weeks of gestational age) will be also excluded.
