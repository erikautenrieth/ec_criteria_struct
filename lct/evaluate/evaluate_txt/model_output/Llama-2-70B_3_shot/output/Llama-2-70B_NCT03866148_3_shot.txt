  Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients with moderate to severe sleep apnoea who require CPAP as standard care (AHI >15) [AND] patients between the age of 18 and 75.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients with a diagnosis of atrial fibrillation (AF), atrial flutter [OR] and/or ventricular tachycardia (VT) [NOT].
  - Patients with an ILR already in-situ [OR] an established indication for ILR.
  - Patients with a palliative diagnosis i.e. life expectancy less than 3 years.
  - Patients who lack capacity [NOT].
