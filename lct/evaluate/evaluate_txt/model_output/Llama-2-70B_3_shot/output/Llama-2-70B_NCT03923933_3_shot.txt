  Inclusion Criteria:

* Glomerular filtration rate less than 30 ml / min / 1.73m [AND]
* Without replacement therapy (dialysis or hemodialysis) [OR]
* Volume overload [OR]
* At least 100 ml per day of residual diuresis [AND]
* Use of a loop diuretic for at least one month [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:

* Allergies known to diuretics [NOT]
* Patients with severe infections [NOT]
* Patients with hemodynamic instability [NOT]
* Amputees [NOT]
* Patients with cognitive impairment [NOT]
* Patients with acute renal failure [NOT]
* Patients with graft loss [NOT]
