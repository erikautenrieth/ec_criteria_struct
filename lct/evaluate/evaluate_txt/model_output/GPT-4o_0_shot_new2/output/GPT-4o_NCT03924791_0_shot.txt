 Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Diagnosed [AND] with sciatica by GP
  -  NRS leg pain of 6 [OR] more on a 10-point NRS scale
  -  Minimum duration of symptoms of 3 weeks [AND] maximum duration of 8 weeks
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Age under 18 years
  -  Condition [NOT] preventing to receive transforaminal epidural injection
  -  Severe scoliosis
  -  Transforaminal epidural injection received in 6 months [OR] before randomization date
  -  Surgery for sciatica [AND] at the same level
  -  Surgery for sciatica [AND] at another level [OR] within one year [AND] before inclusion
  -  Pregnancy
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
