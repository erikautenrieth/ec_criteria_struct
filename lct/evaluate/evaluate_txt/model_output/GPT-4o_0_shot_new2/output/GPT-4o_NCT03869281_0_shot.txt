<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria (PTA group)
  -  T1D patients [AND] who received their first PTA, [OR] second PTA only if their first graft was explanted within a week, between 2 January 2005 [AND] 31 December 2017
  -  age ≥18 [AND] < 65 years
  -  willingness [AND] to participate in this observational study

Inclusion Criteria (control group)
  -  Type 1 diabetes
  -  age ≥18 years [AND] < 65 years
  -  willingness [AND] to participate in this observational study
  -  minimum disease duration [OR] of 5 years
  -  brittle diabetes, [OR] as defined by the occurrence [OR] of frequent acute complications (ketoacidosis [OR] severe hypoglycaemic episodes requiring third-part assistance [OR] unawareness [AND] despite well-conducted intensive insulin therapy)
  -  presence [AND] of at least one diabetes-related complication (either neuropathy, retinopathy, subclinical nephropathy).

Exclusion Criteria (both groups)
  -  conditions other than T1D known [AND] to affect bone health ([OR] i.e., [NOT] non-compensated hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, hyperparathyroidism, inflammatory bowel disease, malignancy, rheumatoid arthritis, hypogonadism, severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, hepatic insufficiency, alcohol intake ≥ 3 units/day [OR]),
  -  use [OR] of drugs [OR] that can affect bone metabolism ([OR] e.g., bisphosphonates, anticonvulsants, hormone replacement therapy) [NOT] other than glucocorticoids [AND] immunosuppressants
  -  recipients [OR] of simultaneous pancreas kidney transplantation, pancreatic islet transplantation, multiple PTAs, other transplants
  -  BMI <18.5 [OR] ≥ 30 kg/m2
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
