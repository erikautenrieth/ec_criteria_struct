<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Female subjects between 18 [AND] 65 years of age currently taking spironolactone for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris
[OR]
Female subjects between 18 [AND] 65 years of age currently taking oral antibiotics for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris
[AND] Subjects able to complete an online survey in English
Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects less than 18 years of age [OR] older than 65 years of age [OR] Subjects [AND] that have ever been diagnosed [OR] with any mood disorder by a clinician prior to starting spironolactone ([OR] history of mood disorder [OR] current mood disorder) [OR] Subjects taking hormonal contraception ([OR] including [AND] but [NOT] limited to oral contraceptive pills) for a duration of less than 6 months [OR] Subjects [AND] that have ever been diagnosed [OR] with any type of sexual dysfunction prior to starting spironolactone [OR] Subjects [NOT] able to complete an online survey in English [OR] Subjects taking oral isotretinoin for a diagnosis of acne [OR] Subjects [AND] that are currently pregnant
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
