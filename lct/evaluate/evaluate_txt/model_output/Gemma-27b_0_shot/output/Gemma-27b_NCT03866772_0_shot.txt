Inclusion Criteria:
  - unfavourable cervix [AND] Bishop score ≤ 4,
  - indication for induction of labor [OR] medical [OR] obstetrical,
  - 37 completed gestational weeks,
  - vertex presentation,
  - singleton pregnancy
  - intact membranes.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - previous cesarean delivery
  - previous uterine surgery [OR] eg: myomectomy
  - noncephalic presentation
  - multiple pregnancy
  - pre-eclampsia with severe features
  - oligohydramnios [AND] Maximal vertical pocket ≤2
  - estimated fetal weight <10% percentile
  - [NOT] any contraindication [AND] Vaginal delivery
