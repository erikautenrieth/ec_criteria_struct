Inclusion Criteria:
  - Age≥18 [AND] male [OR] female
  - Clinical diagnosed [AND] type 2 diabetes mellitus
  - The presence of renal impairment including: microalbuminuria [OR] overt proteinuria [OR] renal insufficiency
  - The renal biopsy was performed [AND] complete renal pathological diagnosis
  - Obtaining the signed informed consent from patients
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] complete history of the disease
  - Clinical diagnosed [AND] [NOT] other secondary renal diseases
  - Patients [WITH] hereditary kidney disease
  - Autoimmune diseases
  - Patients [WITH] malignant tumor [AND] expected to survive less than 6 months
  - Pregnancy [AND] lactation
