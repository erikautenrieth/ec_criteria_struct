Inclusion Criteria:
  - Pregnant women [AND] diagnosed with GDM from 13 to 32.6 gestational weeks
  - 18 years old [OR] older
  - Singleton pregnancy
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Women [WITH] pre-gestational diabetes mellitus
  - GDM diagnosed ≥ 33 gestational weeks
  - Women using prophylactic antimicrobial treatment
  - Immunocompromised women
  - Multiple pregnancy
  - Women taking oral probiotic products [WHO] refuse to stop the consumption (food-containing probiotics is [NOT] an exclusion criterion).
