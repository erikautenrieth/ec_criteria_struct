Inclusion Criteria:
  - Dialysis [OR] non-dialysis patients [AND] with chronic renal insufficiency [AND] with a GFR < 20ml / min [AND] eligible for transplant
  - Patient wishing to start the registration process at the transplant
  - Patient giving consent for the transmission of his data via an electronic platform
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patient already enrolled
  - Patient [AND] having a pathology [AND] with a life expectancy of less than two years
  - Patient [AND] with serious psychiatric disorders [OR] dementia.
