Inclusion Criteria:
  - Controlled intermittent asthma [AND] step 1 treatment, according to GINA Guideline 2016 [OR] [NOT] association with cat allergy related rhino-conjunctivitis;
  - Sensitization to cat allergen extract as defined by skin prick test for cat extract with a wheal diameter of ≥3 mm greater than negative control [AND] specific IgE anti Fel d 1 ≥ 0.7 kU/L [AND] ImmunoCAP, Thermofisher, Uppsala, Sweden;
  - FEV1 ≥ 70% of the predicted value;
  - [NOT] Cat exposure at home [OR] outside of the home during the study;
  - Positive methacholine challenge test performed using an AeroDoseur DTF-Atomisor ATO-AD 12 [AND] Saint-Etienne, France;     
  - Early asthmatic response during baseline cat allergen exposure.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Uncontrolled asthma, asthma control test inferior to 20 within the 4 weeks preceding the study.
  - Uncontrolled asthma 2 weeks after stopping LABA treatment.
  - LABA treatment within the 2 weeks preceding the study.
  - Severe asthma [AND] > GINA 2
  - Subject lives with a cat
  - [NOT] Cat desensibilisation within the 6 previous months.
  - Active smoker
