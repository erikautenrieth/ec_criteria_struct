Inclusion Criteria:
  - Clinical diagnosis of AECOPD
  - Extubation [AND] must be performed after 48 hours of invasive ventilation
  - [AND] Must be treated sequentially with HFNC [OR] NIV after extubation
  - PaCO2≥50mmHg when extubation
  - Positive End Expiratory Pressure (PEEP) ≤8cmH2O (1cmH2O=0.098 kPa) when extubation
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Do-intubate
  - Tracheostomies
  - Accidental extubation
  - Self-extubation
