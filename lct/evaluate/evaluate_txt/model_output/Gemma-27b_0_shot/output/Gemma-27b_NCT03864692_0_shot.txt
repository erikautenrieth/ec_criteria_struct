Inclusion Criteria:
  - singleton parturient
  - planned for elective LSCS [AND] under spinal anesthesia
Exclusion Criteria:
  - gestational age [NOT] >= 36 weeks
  - emergency cases
  - placenta previa [OR] pre-eclampsia
  - BMI [NOT] <= 40
  - Reynauld disease
  - patient refusal
  - cardiovascular disease
