Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients [AND] with at least 3 months of clinical onset of peripheral vascular disease
  -  Patients [AND] with ABI between 0.4-1.4
  -  Patients [AND] up to Stage 2b according to the Fontaine classification system
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients undergoing vascular [OR] endovascular surgery in the last 3 months
  -  Patients with unstable angina
  -  Patients with myocardial infarction (MI)
  -  Patients with stroke
  -  Patients with heart failure
  -  Patients with significant liver [OR] renal failure (ie, dialysis)
  -  Acute infectious diseases
  -  Patients with neoplasia
