Inclusion criteria:
  -  [AND] All patients undergoing lobectomy VATS [AND] older > 18 years [AND] patients who meet criteria of ASA 1 to 3 class
Exclusion criteria:
  -  [NOT] Patient's refusal to participate in the study
  -  [NOT] Psychiatric disorder (impossibility to collect the informed consent)
  -  [NOT] Patient under juridical protection
  -  [OR] On going an other study [OR] [NOT] balanced epilepsy
  -  3 grade auriculo-ventricular heart block [NOT] with pacing
  -  [NOT] Severe hepatocellular insufficiency
  -  [NOT] Anti arrhythmic treatment : class III of the Vaughan William's classification
  -  [NOT] Pregnant patient [OR] [NOT] breastfeeding
  -  [NOT] History of opioid abuse
  -  [NOT] Allergy to local anesthetic drug [OR] [NOT] opioids
