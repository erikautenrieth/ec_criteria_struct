Inclusion Criteria:
  - Age 18-65
  - Current service users [AND] with severe [AND] enduring mental disorder
  - Severe Mental Illnesses - bipolar disorder [OR] severe depression [OR] schizophrenia
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [NOT] who consent to their data being collected
  - Patients with acute somatic disorders
  - Patients [NOT] who are under the age of 18 at the start of the study.
