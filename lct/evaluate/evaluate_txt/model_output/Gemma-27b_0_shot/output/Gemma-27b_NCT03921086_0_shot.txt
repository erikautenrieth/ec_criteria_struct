Inclusion Criteria:
  - Adult [AND] older than 18 years
  - Elective surgery [AND] at preoperative in-hospital visit
  - [OR] All stage 1 [OR] stage 2 hypertensive patients as defined by the South African Hypertension Practice Guideline 2014
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patient refusal
  - Day case surgery
  - Obstetric [OR] cardiac surgery
  - [NOT] Patients with severe hypertension (>180/110 mmHg) as defined by the South African Hypertension Practice Guideline 2014
