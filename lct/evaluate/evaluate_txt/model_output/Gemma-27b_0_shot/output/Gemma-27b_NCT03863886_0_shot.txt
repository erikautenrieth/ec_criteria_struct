Inclusion Criteria:
  - Adults patients ≥ 18 years of age [AND] with CD [AND] with at least colonic involvement, UC, [OR] non-IBD controls [WHO] have been referred for colonoscopy for clinical reasons. The clinical reasons may include colorectal cancer screening, surveillance, diagnostic for CD [OR] UC flare, [OR] gastrointestinal symptoms.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Pregnant patients.
  - Patients with known current colorectal cancer, infectious colitis, diverticulitis, [OR] microscopic colitis.
  - Patients [WHO] have undergone surgery involving the cecum [OR] rectum.
