Inclusion Criteria:
  - [OR] The patient [OR] his / her representative has been informed about the implementation of the study, its objectives, its constraints, the rights of the patient [AND] must have received the newsletter [AND] [NOT] no opposition of the study.
  - [OR] The patient must be an affiliate [OR] beneficiary of a health insurance plan.
  - [OR] The patient is at least 18 years old.
  - [OR] The patient has septic shock as defined by the 3rd International Conference for the definition of sepsis [AND] septic shock
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [OR] The patient is in an exclusion period determined by a previous study
  - [OR] The patient is under guardianship, under guardianship, [OR] under the protection of justice
  - [OR] The patient has already participated in this study
  - [OR] The patient [OR] his / her representative refuses to participate in the study
  - [OR] The patient is pregnant, parturient [OR] is breastfeeding
  - [OR] The patient is a minor
  - [OR] The patient is in therapeutic limitation [OR] moribund
  - [OR] The patient has undergone [OR] is going to have scheduled [OR] emergency digestive surgery
  - [OR] The patient is HIV-positive
  - [OR] The patient has Child C cirrhosis
  - [OR] The patient has progressive digestive neoplasia, digestive lymphoma, chronic inflammatory bowel disease (Crohn's disease ...).
