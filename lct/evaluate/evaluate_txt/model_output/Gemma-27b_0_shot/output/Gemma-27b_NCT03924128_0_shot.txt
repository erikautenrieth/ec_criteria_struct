Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Selected will be subjects [AND] meeting the following criteria: male [OR] female subjects age ≥ 20 years old, diagnosed with Osteoarthritis stages III [AND] IV according to the American College of Rheumatology, manifesting pain, functional impairment for more than 3 months, able to wander with [OR] without canes.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Will be excluded from study subjects with history of cancer, unstable cardiovascular disease, uncontrolled hypertension, morbid obesity, [THAT] is, BMI ≥ 40, terminal illness, cerebrovascular disease, dementia, heart pacemaker, superior [OR] inferior limb fractures.
