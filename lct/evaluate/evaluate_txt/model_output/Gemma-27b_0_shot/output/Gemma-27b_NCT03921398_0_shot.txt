Inclusion Criteria (ESRD group):
  - Age ≥ 18 years [AND] history of biopsy-proven lupus nephritis [AND] all classes of lupus nephritis [AND] written informed consent [AND] affiliation to a social security regime
Exclusion Criteria (ESRD group):
  - Past-history of kidney transplantation [OR] active infection [OR] active allergy (such as hay fever) [OR] pregnant [OR] breastfeeding women [OR] protected adults (individuals under guardianship by court order)
Inclusion criteria (non ESRD group):
  - Age ≥ 18 years [AND] biopsy proven ACTIVE lupus nephritis [AND] first event of lupus nephritis [AND] [NOT] immunosuppressive therapy (including corticosteroids) [AND] written informed consent [AND] affiliation to a social security regime
Exclusion criteria (non ESRD group) :
  - Immunosuppressive treatment [OR] relapse of lupus nephritis under treatment [OR] pregnant [OR] breastfeeding women [OR] protected adults (individuals under guardianship by court order)
