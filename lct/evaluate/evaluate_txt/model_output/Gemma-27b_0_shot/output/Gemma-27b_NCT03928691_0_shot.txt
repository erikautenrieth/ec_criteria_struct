Inclusion criteria:
pregnant women [OR] 34 weeks [OR] less admitted to Women's Health Hospital [AND] with a diagnosis of :
  -  sever preeclampsia. It is defined as 1- blood pressure 160/110 mmHg [OR] more 2- proteinurea (+3 [OR] more by dipstick) 3-HELLP syndrome
  -  threatened preterm labour [AND] that will receive Magnesium Sulphate either for tocolysis [OR] neuroprotection.
Exclusion criteria:
- women with more than 34 weeks gestation [AND] with [NOT] any contraindication for Magnesium Sulphate intake like severe renal impairment [OR] myasthenia gravis.
