Inclusion criteria:
  -  < 38 years
  -  PCOS patients [AND] with polycystic ovarian morphology: at least 25 follicles (2-9 mm) throughout the whole ovary [OR] increased ovarian volume (>10ml) (it is sufficient that 1 ovary fits these criteria)
  -  [NOT] No major uterine [OR] ovarian abnormalities
Exclusion criteria:
  -  High (>grade 2) grade endometriosis
  -  Cases [WITH] extremely poor sperm (OAT) such as azoospermia [OR] cryptozoospermia
