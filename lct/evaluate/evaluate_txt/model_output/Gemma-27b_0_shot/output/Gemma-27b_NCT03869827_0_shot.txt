Inclusion Criteria:
  -  All birth between 24 + 0 weeks of amenorrhea [AND] 36 + 6 weeks of amenorrhea
  -  [AND] with isolated intrauterine growth restriction at the maternity ward of the hospital Femme-Mère-Enfant
  -  [AND] from 1st of january 2011 to 31 december 2016.
Inclusion Criteria for control group:
  -  All birth between 24 + 0 weeks of amenorrhea [AND] 36 + 6 weeks of amenorrhea
  -  [AND] [NOT] with isolated intrauterine growth restriction at the maternity ward of the hospital Femme-Mère-Enfant
  -  [AND] from 1st of january 2011 to 31 december 2016.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Infants born [NOT] out of the hospital [AND] secondarily hospitalized in our department
  -  [NOT] unaccompanied pregnancies
  -  [NOT] multiple pregnancies
  -  children with malformations [OR] genetic abnormalities,
  -  medical termination of pregnancy
  -  fetal deaths in utero
