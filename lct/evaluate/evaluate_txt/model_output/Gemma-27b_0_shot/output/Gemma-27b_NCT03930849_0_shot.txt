Inclusion Criteria:
VA Patient
  [AND] Diagnosed with depression
  [AND] Initiating [OR] changing to a new antidepressant medication
  [AND] Cell phone with text messaging capabilities
  [AND] Registered with My HealtheVet
  [AND] Follow-up appointment with their provider in the next 12 weeks
  [AND] Internet access
  [AND] English speaking VA Providers: provide care to a study participant
Exclusion Criteria:
  [NOT] Lack of cell phone access/[OR] willingness to receive text messages
  [NOT] Diagnosis of cognitive disorders, psychotic disorders, bipolar disorder, [OR] active substance abuse.
