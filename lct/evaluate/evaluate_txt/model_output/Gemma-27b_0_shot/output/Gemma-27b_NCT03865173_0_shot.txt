Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient [AND] being over 18 years of age [AND] living in metropolitan France;
  -  Patient [AND] with recent stroke (NOT before 2019) [AND] whose diagnosis [AND] confirmed by a neuro-vascular physician;
  -  Patient managed for stroke in one of the 13 participating hospitals of the Aquitaine region;
  -  Patient giving consent to participate to PAPASéPA;
  -  Patient to be alive at the end of the acute management hospital stay
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient [AND] refuse to participate to PAPASéPA
  -  Patient died during the initial stay
