  -  All patients [AND] older than 18 years [AND] undergoing elective surgery [AND] that receive a preoperative visit by anesthesiologists.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients undergoing emergency surgery

       -  Patients [WHO] refuse to participate
       -  Patients [NOT] able to perform the 6MWT
       -  Claudication of the lower extremities [AND] [NOT] ability to maintain a constant pace of walking on level ground
