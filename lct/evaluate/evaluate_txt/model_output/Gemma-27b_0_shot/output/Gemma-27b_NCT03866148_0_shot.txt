  - Patients [AND] with moderate to severe sleep apnoea [AND] who require CPAP as standard care (AHI >15)
  - Patients between the age of 18 [AND] 75
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [WITH] a diagnosis of atrial fibrillation (AF), atrial flutter [OR] ventricular tachycardia (VT)
  - Patients [WITH] an ILR already in-situ [OR] an established indication for ILR
  - Patients [WITH] a palliative diagnosis i.e. life expectancy less than 3 years
  - Patients [WHO] [NOT] lack capacity
