Inclusion Criteria:
- [AND] Positive for H. pylori infection
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Presence of malignancy
  - [OR] Pregnancy [OR] lactation
  - [AND] Clinically significant diseases
  - [AND] History of drug [OR] alcohol abuse
  - [AND] Allergy to pantoprazole [OR] to any component of regimens used in the study
  - [AND] Use of anti-secretory drugs, antibiotics [OR] probiotics 4 weeks preceding the enrollment.
