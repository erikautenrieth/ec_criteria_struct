Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Chinese ethnicity
  -  Age ≥21 [AND] ≤ 60 years
  -  Body mass index between 18.5 [OR] 25 kg/m2
  -  Normal blood pressure <140/90 mmHg
  -  Fasting blood glucose <6.0 mmol/L
  -  [AND] in general good health
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Current Smoker
  -  [AND] have any metabolic diseases (such as diabetes, hypertension etc)
  -  [AND] have known glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase deficiency (G6PD deficiency)
  -  [AND] having medical conditions [OR] taking medications known to affect glycaemia (glucocorticoids, thyroid hormones, thiazide diuretics)
  -  [AND] have intolerances [OR] allergies to any foods
  -  [AND] partake in sports at the competitive [OR] endurance levels
  -  [AND] intentionally restrict food intake
