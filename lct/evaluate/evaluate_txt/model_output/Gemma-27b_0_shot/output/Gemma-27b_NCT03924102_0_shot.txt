Inclusion Criteria:
  - At least 18 years of age.
  - Enrollment in the UAB CMC-ADHF study under the separate UAB approved research protocol (IRB-300000114, PI Rajapreyar)
  - [AND] Negative urine [OR] serum β-hCG test within 48 hours of [11C] acetate administration in women of child bearing potential. [AND] Women who are post-menopausal with at least 1 year since last menses [OR] documented surgical sterilization will [NOT] require pregnancy testing.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Ability to provide informed consent
  - Pregnancy
  - [NOT] Ability to lie still for the imaging study
  - Weight exceeding the weight limit of the PET imaging table (500 pounds).
