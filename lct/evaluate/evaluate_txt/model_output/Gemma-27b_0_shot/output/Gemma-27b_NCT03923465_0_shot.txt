Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Systemically healthy
  -  Clinical diagnosis of advanced periodontitis
  -  [AND] having one isolated intrabony defect with probing depth (PD) ≥7 mm, clinical attachment level (CAL) ≥8 mm [AND] at least 4 mm intrabony component involving predominantly the interproximal area of the affected tooth.
  -  Full-mouth plaque score (FMPS) ≤20%.
  -  Full-mouth bleeding score (FMBS) ≤20%.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Smokers
  -  [AND] patients with known systemic diseases such as diabetes [OR] cardiovascular diseases [OR] using medications that affect periodontal tissues, pregnant [OR] lactating women
  -  One-wall intrabony defects
  -  Defects that involve buccal [AND] lingual sites
  -  [AND] presence of inadequate endodontic treatment [AND]/[OR] restoration in the relevant teeth.
