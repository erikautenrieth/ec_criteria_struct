Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Clinical diagnosis of NTM pulmonary disease.
  2. [OR]The causative agent of NTM pulmonary disease includes: M. avium complex, [OR] M. abscessus subspecies abscessus, [OR] M. abscessus subspecies massiliense, [OR] M. kansasii.
  3. Patients [AND] starts new treatment [OR] those who have previous history of treatment are eligible.
Exclusion Criteria
  1. Patients who have been treated for more than 4 weeks.
  2. [NOT]The causative agent of NTM pulmonary disease is [NOT]one of M. avium complex, M. abscessus subspecies abscessus, M. abscessus subspecies massiliense, [OR] M. kansasii.
