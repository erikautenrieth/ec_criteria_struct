Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Controlled Type-2 diabetes mellitus
  -  Stage-II [OR] Stage-III periodontitis
  -  At least 2 sites [AND] with clinical attachment loss 3 [OR] 4 mm to ≥5 mm [AND] radiographic bone loss 15% Extending to mid-third of root [AND] beyond.
  -  Probing depth from ≤5 mm to ≥6 mm
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT]Any periodontal [OR] Antibiotic treatment during the last six months.
  -  Receiving trace element supplements in the previous three months
  -  [NOT]Pregnant [OR] lactating females
  -  Gastric [OR] diuretic treatment
  -  Acute renal failure
  -  smoking
