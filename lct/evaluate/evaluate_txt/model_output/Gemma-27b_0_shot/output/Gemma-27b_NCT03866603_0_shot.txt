Inclusion Criteria:
  - Informed consent is obtained from the participant.
  - [AND] the participant is clinically diagnosed with Parkinson's disease [OR] the individual is a family member of a participant with LRRK2 parkinsonism [OR] is a member of a high risk population with an early PD onset.
  - [AND] the participant is equal to or older than 18 years old.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT]Ability to provide informed consent.
  - [NOT](the participant is suffering from Parkinson's disease [OR] the individual is a family member of a participant with LRRK2 parkinsonism [OR] is a member of a high risk population).
  - [AND] the participant is younger than 18 years old.
  - [AND] previously enrolled in the study.
  - [AND] participant in custody.
