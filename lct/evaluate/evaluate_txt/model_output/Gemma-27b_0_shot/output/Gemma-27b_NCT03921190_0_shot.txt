Inclusion Criteria:
  - Adult patients [AND] 16 years old [OR] older
  - Healthy [AND] American Society of Anaesthesiologists (ASA) category I [OR] II
  - A maxillary posterior tooth [NOT] third molars [AND] referred for root canal treatment [AND] diagnosed with irreversible pulpitis [AND] normal apical tissues
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Psychological disorders
  - Intraoral soft tissue abnormalities
  - necrotic pulp [OR] [NOT] apical pathology
  - tenderness to palpation [AND]/[OR] percussion
  - The use of analgesics in the preceding 12 hours before the appointment.
