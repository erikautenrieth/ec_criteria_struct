Inclusion Criteria:
  - Treatment-seeking cigarette smoker [AND] meet DSM-IV criteria for nicotine dependence [AND] negative result on urine test for drug use during screening
Exclusion Criteria:
  - History of any Axis I psychiatric diagnosis other than nicotine dependence [OR] medical conditions that might affect brain function [OR] current use of medications that could alter brain function [OR] pregnancy [OR] current illicit drug use other than occasional use of marijuana
