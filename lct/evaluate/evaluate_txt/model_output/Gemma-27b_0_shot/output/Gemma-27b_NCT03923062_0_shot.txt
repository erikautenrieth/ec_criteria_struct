Inclusion Criteria:
  - Subjects [AND] presented [AND] with melasma.
  - Age 20-40 years old.
  - Melasma persisting for more than 6 months [AND] that has failed to respond to conventional treatment [OR] with hydroquinone [OR] other topical lightening agents.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - History of photosensitivity, keloids, hypertrophic scarring [OR] post- inflammatory hyperpigmentation.
  - Pregnancy [AND] lactation.
  - Subjects [AND] with local inflammatory skin disorder [OR] active herpes infection at the site of procedure.
  - Subjects [AND] with history of medical diseases [AND] which contraindicate cryosurgery such as; cold intolerance, cold urticaria, Raynaud`s disease [AND] history of allergic reactions to cryosurgery.
