Inclusion Criteria:
  - Aged 9-12 [AND] English-speaking [AND] meet DSM-5 criteria for oppositional defiant disorder (ODD), conduct disorder (CD), [OR] Other Specified [OR] Unspecified Disruptive, Impulse-Control, [AND] Conduct Disorder [AND] right-handed [AND] estimated full-scale IQ greater than 75
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Bipolar disorder, [OR] any disorder involving psychosis, [OR] pervasive developmental disorders, [OR] current [OR] past substance use disorder, [OR] current major depressive disorder
  - History of neurological problems (e.g., epilepsy, traumatic brain injury)
  - Contraindications for MRI
