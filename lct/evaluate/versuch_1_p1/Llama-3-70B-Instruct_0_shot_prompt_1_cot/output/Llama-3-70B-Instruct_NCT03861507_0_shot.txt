Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Male, aged 18 years or older
  AND willing to provide written consent
  AND pathologically confirmed prostate cancer on previous biopsy
  AND suitable for high-dose-rate brachytherapy for treatment as standard of care
  AND consenting to high-dose-rate brachytherapy for treatment as standard of care.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - NOT previous radiotherapy to the prostate
