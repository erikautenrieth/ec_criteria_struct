Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the added logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Surgical plan for total hip replacement AND
  - Signing of consent form to participate in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patient refusal OR
  - BMI > 40 kg/m2 OR
  - Surgical plan for revision of hip replacement OR
  - Patient unable to quantify pain level OR
  - Chronic kidney disease with a Glomerular Filtration Rate < 50ml/min OR
  - Previously medicated with opioids OR
  - Patient unable to perform the surgery with spinal block OR
  - Allergy to local anesthetics OR
  - Infection in the site of the Erector Spinae Plane or Fascia Iliaca block OR
  - Allergy or contraindication to the use of morphine

Note that I've added the logical operators "AND" and "OR" to connect the criteria, but not "NOT" as it is not necessary in this case. The "NOT" operator would be used to indicate the absence of a particular condition, but in this case, the exclusion criteria are already phrased in a way that implies the absence of a particular condition (e.g. "Patient refusal" implies the absence of consent).
