Here is the revised eligibility criteria with the added logical operators:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  1. diagnosed as HIV positive;
  AND
  -  2. initiate antiretroviral therapy on the day of recruitment;
  AND
  -  3. was infected through male same-sex behavior;
  AND
  -  4. has access to internet on a personal mobile phone;
  AND
  -  5. has a Wechat account and use it daily;
  AND
  -  6. willing to provide written informed consent;

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  1. under severe physical or mental condition which precludes study participation;
  OR
  -  2. being an inpatient of the hospital;

Note: I added the AND operator between each inclusion criterion, indicating that all of these conditions must be met for a participant to be included. I added the OR operator between the two exclusion criteria, indicating that if a participant meets either of these conditions, they will be excluded from the study.
