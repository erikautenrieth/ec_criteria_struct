<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age 20-34 years;
  -  body mass index (BMI) 18-29kg/m2;
  -  regular menstrual cycle [AND] 26-35 days;
  -  AMH levels [AND] age appropriate (≥2.3 ng/ml) [OR] within normal limits;
  -  early follicular- phase serum concentration of FSH [AND] within normal limits (1-12 IU/l).

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  women [WITH] diabetes [OR] other metabolic disease;
  -  women [WITH] hereditary genetic diseases;
  -  women [WITH] heart disease [OR] QT prolongation [OR] heart failure;
  -  elevated liver enzymes [OR] liver failure [OR] hepatitis;
  -  women [WITH] inflammatory [OR] autoimmune disease;
  -  abnormal karyotype [NOT] normal karyotype;
  -  endometriosis stage III/IV [NOT] stage I/II;
  -  history of recurrent miscarriage [NOT] no history of miscarriage;
  -  severe OHSS in a previous stimulation cycle [OR] any contraindication for the use of gonadotrophins [NOT] qualified for gonadotrophin use.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
