Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with a endovenous pacemaker [OR] defibrillator indication.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pre-existing ipsilateral pacing electrode.
  -  Previous ipsilateral lymphadenectomy.
  -  Indication of cardiac resynchronization therapy [NOT] excluded from pacemaker or defibrillator indication.

Note: I added the [NOT] operator to the last exclusion criterion to make it clear that patients with an indication of cardiac resynchronization therapy are excluded from the study if they have a pacemaker or defibrillator indication.
