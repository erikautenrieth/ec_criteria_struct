Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] inserted:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  patients in different age groups [AND] both sex referred from orthopedic outpatient clinic with chronic ankle pain [AND] suspected clinically to have impingement syndrome.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Inflammatory arthritis of ankle joint [OR] infection in ankle joint [OR] neoplasm around ankle joint.
  2. Patients with contraindications for MRI, e.g. [NOT] having an implanted magnetic device [OR] pacemakers [OR] claustrophobia.
