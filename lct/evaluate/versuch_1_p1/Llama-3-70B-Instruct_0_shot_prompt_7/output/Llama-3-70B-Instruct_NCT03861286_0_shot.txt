Here is the edited version of the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT]:

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients undergoing first time cardiopulmonary bypass surgery for correction of atrial septal defects [OR] tissue mitral valve repair
  2. Receiving intravenous Heparin for cardiopulmonary bypass [AND] undergoing first time cardiopulmonary bypass surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with Concomitant Aortic Stenosis which is associated with an acquired von Willebrand Syndrome (aVWS)
  -  Patients [NOT] having a baseline platelet count of ≥100x109/L
  -  Patients with a known coagulation factor deficiency [OR] platelet function disorder
  -  Patients [NOT] receiving heparin therapy prior to CPB within the last 7 days
