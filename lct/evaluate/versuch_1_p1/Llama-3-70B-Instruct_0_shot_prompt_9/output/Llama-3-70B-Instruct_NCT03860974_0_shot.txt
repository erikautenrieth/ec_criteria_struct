Here is the edited version of the eligibility criteria with the logical operators incorporated:

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  undergoing unilateral [OR] bilateral breast surgery with at least moderate postoperative pain anticipated
  -  analgesic plan includes a single-injection peripheral nerve block(s) [AND] age 18 years or older
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  morbid obesity as defined by a body mass index > 40 (BMI=weight in kg / [height in meters]2) [OR] renal insufficiency (preoperative creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL)
  -  chronic opioid use (daily use within the 2 weeks prior to surgery [AND] duration of use > 4 weeks)
  -  history of opioid abuse [OR] any comorbidity which results in moderate [OR] severe functional limitation [OR] inability to communicate with the investigators [OR] hospital staff
  -  [NOT] pregnant
  -  [NOT] planned regional analgesic with perineural catheter placement
  -  [NOT] incarcerated
