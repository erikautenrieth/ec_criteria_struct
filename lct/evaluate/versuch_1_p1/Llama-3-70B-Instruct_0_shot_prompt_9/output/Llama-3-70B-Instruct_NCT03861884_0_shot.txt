Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Ability to give informed consent.
  2. [OR] Pathological [OR] genetic [OR] imaging biomarker evidence to suggest Fronto-temporal Lobar Degeneration [OR] Alzheimer's Disease [OR] Vascular Cognitive Impairment, [OR] defined clinical diagnoses.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] Ability to give informed consent [NOT] due to lack of mental capacity to consent to study involvement.
  2. [NOT] Speaking English before age 5 years.
  3. [NOT] Free of learning disability.
  4. Presence of MRI contraindications.
