The following conditions will not be considered as outcome, unless they present with
systemic repercussion due to infection. For example:
- Vaginosis, [OR] candidiasis
- Lower tract urinary infection
- Fungal infections of the skin (athlete's foot, [OR] jockitch, [OR] ringworm, and yeast infections)
- Otitis
- Pharyngitis
- Herpes simplex, [OR] Herpes Zoster (Shingles)
- Uncomplicated chronic infection
- Sexually transmitted infections (Gonorrhoea, [OR] Syphilis, [OR] Trichomonas, [OR] Chlamydia, [OR] Hepatitis, [OR] HIV)
- Tuberculosis
- Bacterial colonization (presence of microorganisms [NOT] without clinical signs/symptoms)
- Known vaginal, [OR] urethral [OR] and/or rectal GBS colonization
- Asymptomatic bacteriuria
- Known oropharyngeal colonization
- Non-infectious hypothermia/ [OR] hyperthermia (e.g. related to epidural, [OR] thyroid storm, [OR] prostaglandin administration) during hospital stay
