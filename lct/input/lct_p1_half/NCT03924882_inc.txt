1. Female, age ≥18 years [AND] and ≤70 years, signed informed consent.
2. Histologically [OR] or pathologically confirmed diagnosis of epithelial carcinoma of the ovary.
3. Platinum resistant ovarian cancer (defined as relapsing within 6 months after the last administration of platinum-based chemotherapy) [OR] OR platinum refractory ovarian cancer (defined as progressing while on a platinum-based chemotherapy)
4. At least treated with one line of platinum-based chemotherapy
5. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status 0-2
6. Measurable disease according to Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) 1.1 version or CA125 
7. Patients must have a life expectancy of at least 3 months.
8. Patients must have adequate organ function.
