Patients must not meet any of the following exclusion criteria to be eligible: [AND1]
1. The patient is enrolled in any clinical trial of an investigational product. Patients who are enrolled in non-interventional studies (NISs) (e.g. registries) are eligible for inclusion. [AND1]
2. The patient has received ceftazidime-avibactam in a compassionate care program setting. [AND1]
3. The patient was exposed to ceftazidime-avibactam before use for the index infection.
