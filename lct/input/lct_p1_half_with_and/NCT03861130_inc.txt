- Affected children will be recruited if the treating clinician has made a diagnosis of possible Kawasaki disease (even if they do not fulfil the criteria below for Kawasaki disease). [AND1]
The current standard diagnostic criteria for KD (Circulation 2001 103 335-336 doi: 10.1161/01.CIR 103.2.335) are: [AND1]
The presence of fever [AND] for at least five days plus four of the following criteria: [AND1]
1. Changes in the peripheral extremities Acute: erythema [OR] and oedema of hands [OR] and feet Convalescent: membranous desquamation of fingertips [AND1]
2. Polymorphous exanthema [AND1]
3. Bilateral painless bulbar conjunctival injection  [AND1] [NOT] without exudate [AND1]
4. Changes in lips [OR] and oral cavity: erythema [OR] and cracking of lips, [OR] strawberry tongue, [OR] diffuse injection of oral [OR] and pharyngeal mucosae [AND1]
5. Cervical lymphadenopathy (>1.5cm diameter), usually unilateral Patients meeting not all of these criteria may meet the criteria for atypical Kawasaki disease, ie. if they have fever [AND] and two or three of the above criteria and elevation of CRP [OR] or echocardiographic evidence of coronary artery dilatation. [AND1]
Parents of affected child must be biological parents.
