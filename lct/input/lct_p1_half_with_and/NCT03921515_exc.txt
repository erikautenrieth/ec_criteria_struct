The following exclusion criteria apply to all subjects: [AND1]
1. For individuals undergoing blister [OR] or skin biopsy procedures, history of keloid formation. [AND1]
2. Current [OR] or prior (within 3 months) anticoagulant [OR] or anti-platelet therapy (other than aspirin [OR] or non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs [NSAIDs]). [AND1]
3. Current [OR] or prior (within 3 months) use of immunomodulatory drugs (e.g., chemotherapy, [OR] steroids), except if approved by the principal investigator. [AND1]
4. Pregnancy. [AND1]
5. Any condition that, in the opinion of the investigator, contraindicates participation in the study.
