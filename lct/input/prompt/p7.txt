Game Rules:

Placement Guidelines:

Insert an [AND] before words like "with" to link related conditions or requirements.
Place an [OR] before "or", "and/or", after a comma.
Use a [NOT] before negations like "no", "not", "unable", etc., to explicitly exclude certain conditions or criteria.
