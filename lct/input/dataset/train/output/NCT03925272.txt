Inclusion Criteria:
 - unprotected adults with social security who have attested their consent after receiving any relevant information about the study
 - subjects whose ethno-geographical origin of both parents is known
 - subjects for whom data on principal vaccinations (diphtheria, [OR] tetanus, [OR] poliomyelitis, [OR] hepatitis B, possibly tuberculosis) are documented
 - subjects who consented to carry out serological tests HIV, [OR] HCV, [OR] HBV
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Any conditions that would [NOT] not allow participation in the present study, on the opinion of the investigator (documenting), ie any acute [OR] or chronic pathology that may interfere with the immune system, such as progressive [OR] or chronic pathology severe [OR] or uncontrolled by current treatments [OR] or a pathology requiring the administration of immune impact drugs: long-term anti-inflammatory, [OR] immunosuppressive, etc
 - Pregnant [OR] or lactating women
 - For the realization of skin biopsies: allergy to local anesthetics, [OR] cardiac valvulopathy
 - For the realization of Tubertest: Subject presenting a contraindication to tuberculin
