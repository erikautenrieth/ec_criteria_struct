Inclusion Criteria:
 - Common European Framework of Reference for Languages level B2 in German language.
 - Consent ability for all relevant aspects of the experiment.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Amisulpride allergy.
 - Allergy to other components of amisulpride /[OR] placebo capsules like lactose.
 - Daily intake of other medication including contraceptives.
 - Tendency to seizures.
 - Diagnosis of cancer especially pheochromocytoma, [OR] prolactinoma, [OR] or breast cancer.
 - Kidney dysfunction: creatinine clearance below 10 ml per minute.
 - High risk for stroke [OR] or thrombosis.
 - Known prolongation of the QT interval,
 - Any substantial medical condition that is capable of reducing the volunteers ability to participate at the study.
 - Suicidal thoughts [OR] or suicide attempts in the past [OR] or at present.
 - Substantial mental disorders especially schizophrenia, [OR] bipolar disorder, [OR] drug abuse, [OR] or personality disorders.
 - Pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding.
