 6. Normal cognition defined as a Clinical Dementia Rating [AND] of 0 and a Mini-Mental State Examination score of 27 or higher.

 1. Significant co-morbid neurologic disease such as Parkinson's disease, [OR] multiple sclerosis, [OR] brain cyst, [OR] tumor [OR] or aneurysm;
