Inclusion Criteria:
 - All patients must meet the following diagnostic criteria of CAPS [AND] and have pathogenic mutation(s) in NLRP3 gene.
 1. Raised inflammatory markers (CRP/ [OR] SAA) (mandatory criteria)
 2. ≥2 of 6 CAPS typical signs/ [OR] symptoms:
 1. Urticaria-like rash;
 2. Cold/ [OR] stress triggered episodes;
 3. Sensorineural hearing loss;
 4. Musculoskeletal symptoms (arthralgia/ [OR] arthritis/ [OR] myalgia);
 5. Chronic aseptic meningitis;
 6. Skeletal abnormalities (epiphyseal overgrowth/ [OR] frontal bossing).
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients will not be included if meets any of the following criteria:
 1. Being treated with IL-1 inhibitor, other biological agents [OR] and immunosuppressants
 2. Pregnant [OR] and lactating women
 3. Serious organ function failure, [OR] expected life time less than 6 months
