Inclusion Criteria:
 - Able to provide written informed consent in English.
 - Willing to have height [OR] and weight measured and provide demographic information (e.g. age, race, sex)
 - Experience heartburn symptoms at least 2 days a week during the past 3 months.
 - Use OTC product(s), supplements, [OR] or dietary manipulation to relieve heartburn symptoms in the last 3 months.
 - Willing to take the study soy supplement before resorting to OTC product(s) to control heartburn symptoms.
 - Willing to complete questionnaires, [OR] records, [OR] and diaries associated with the study and to complete all clinical visits.
 - Willing to provide information about dietary intake; 3-day online 24-hr recalls during baseline, intervention, [AND] and washout.
 - Willing and able to provide a valid social security for study payment purposes.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Do not meet the above criteria.
 - Soy allergy
 - Severe heartburn problem (cannot be ignored and often limits your concentration on daily activities) during the last week
 - Diagnosed [OR] or currently being treated for any gastrointestinal diseases including GERD, [OR] gastric ulcers, [OR] Crohn's, [OR] celiac, [OR] ulcerative colitis, etc.
 - Self-disclosed as pregnant [OR] or breast-feeding [OR] or planning on becoming pregnant during the study duration.
 - Currently participating in another clinical study.
