Inclusion Criteria:
 - 1)Age: 20 ~35 years old; 2) first parturients registered in the obstetrics department of our hospital; 3) [NOT] no medical complications before pregnancy, [NOT] no UI [OR] and POP (according to the standard of POP-Q stage); 4) [NOT] no obstetric complications; 5) the patients agreed to conduct the study and signed the informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - 1) patients with uncontrolled epilepsy, [OR] central nervous system diseases [OR] or mental disorders, whose clinical severity shall be determined by the researcher to affect clinical research compliance; 2) follow up the patients with difficulty.
