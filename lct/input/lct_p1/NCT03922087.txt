Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Pregnant women  [AND] who have plan to delivery and participate in child care in Huizhou hospital;
  -  Pregnant women  [AND] who have resided in Huizhou for at least 3 years;
  -  Pregnant women  [AND] who are consent for specimens and data collection and follow-ups for both mother  [AND] and infant.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pregnant women  [AND] who are with gestational age more than 24 weeks ;
  -  Pregnant women who refuse to participate or be unwilling to sign informed consent;
  -  Pregnant women  [AND] who are mentally incapacitated and need guardians.
