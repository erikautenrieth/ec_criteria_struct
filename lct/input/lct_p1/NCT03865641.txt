Inclusion Criteria:
 - The patients' burn scars had re-epithelialized after aseptic care [OR] or skin graft. We included patients aged [AND] ≥18 years with a deep partial-thickness (second-degree) burn [OR] or a full thickness (third-degree) burn [AND] to their hands, with joint contracture( [AND] hand [OR] and wrist), having been transferred to the rehabilitation department after acute burn treatment, and less than 6 months since the onset of the burn injury.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - patients who had fourth-degree burns(involving muscles, [OR] tendons, [OR] and bone injuries), [OR] musculoskeletal diseases(fracture, [OR] amputation, [OR] rheumatoid arthritis, [OR] and degenerative joint diseases) in the burned hand, or neurological diseases(such as peripheral nerve disorders), preexisting physical [OR] and psychologic disability [OR] (severe aphasia [OR] and cognitive impairment that could influence the intervention), and severe pain impeding hand rehabilitation.
