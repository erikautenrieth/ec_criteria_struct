Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with moderate to severe sleep apnoea [AND] who require CPAP as standard care (AHI >15)
 - Patients between the age of 18 and 75
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with a diagnosis of atrial fibrillation (AF), atrial flutter [OR] and/or ventricular tachycardia (VT)
 - Patients with an ILR  already in-situ [OR] or an established indication for ILR
 - Patients with a palliative diagnosis i.e. life expectancy less than 3 years
 - Patients who lack capacity
