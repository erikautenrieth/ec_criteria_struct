Inclusion Criteria:
 - Ischemic [OR] and hemorrhagic stroke patients
 - Presence of at least one of the following high risk criteria: [NOT] uninsured, Medicaid payer status, small vessel ischemic stroke, hypertensive ICH)
 - Age ≥ 18; presence of hypertension (by clinical history [OR] or hospital BP ≥140/90 on two occasions)
 - Plan to discharge home after stroke
 - Ability to provide consent (patient or caregiver)
 - Ability to communicate in English
Exclusion Criteria:
 - modified Rankin scale > 4 at the time of enrollment (severe disability)
 - life expectancy [OR] < 1 year or terminal illness,
 - eGFR < 30 at time of discharge
 - pregnancy
 - symptomatic flow limiting carotid stenosis [NOT] without plan for intervention
 - urine toxicology [OR] positive for cocaine [OR] or methamphetamine or recent use
 - long-term BP goal ≥ 130/80 mmHg according to clinical team
