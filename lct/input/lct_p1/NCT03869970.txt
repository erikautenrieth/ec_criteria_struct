Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of mTBI in the past 48 hours
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients having conditions otherwise which [NOT] limit physical activity [OR] or neurocognitive assessment
 - Do not have a smart phone
 - History of past moderate/ [OR] severe TBI,
 - Substance abuse,
 - Major psychiatric condition,
 - Vestibular disorder,
 - Cardiac condition,
 - Unable to provide consent/assent
