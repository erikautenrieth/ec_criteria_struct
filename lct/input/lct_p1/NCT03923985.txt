Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Healthy women between 18 and 45 years old
  -  Women [NOT] without current symptoms of vaginal infection
  -  Negative bacteriological  [AND] and clinical examination
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Women  [AND] with vaginal infection
  -  Women  [AND] with undiagnosed vaginal bleeding
  -  Patients with endometrial hyperplasia
  -  Suspicion of neoplasia  [OR] or active neoplasia
  -  Women  [AND] with intolerance, allergy  [OR] or hypersensitivity to the components of the probiotic
  -  Women  [AND] with immunosuppressive disorders  [OR] or with HIV
  -  Women  [AND] who are currently using probiotics by any route of administration
  -  Women  [AND] who are using some treatment for vaginal sepsis
  -  Chronic decompensated noncommunicable diseases (diabetes mellitus, [OR]  heart disease ...)
  -  Pregnant women  [OR] or those with precocious menopause
