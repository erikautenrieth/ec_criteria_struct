Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients aged 21 years or younger at the time of enrollment on this study of any gender, race or ethnicity.
       -  Subjects with suspected  [OR] or confirmed diagnosis of recurrent  [OR] or refractory cancer
       -  Subjects who are scheduled  [OR] for or have recently had biopsy  [OR] or tumor excised  [OR] (solid tumors) or bone marrow aspirate (blood cancers)

       -  Subjects aged 7 to 17 willing to sign assent
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Subjects who do [NOT] not have malignant tissue available  [OR] and accessible
