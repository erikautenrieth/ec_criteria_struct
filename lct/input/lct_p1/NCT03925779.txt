Inclusion Criteria:
  -  ASA I or II physical status
  -  18-65 years
  -  Scheduled for elective colonoscopy
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History of allergy to any of the study drugs
  -  Alcohol  [OR] or drug abuse.
  -  Second and third-degree heart block.
  -  Morbid obesity.
  -  Pregnant  [OR] and lactating women.
  -  Psychiatric disorders.
  -  Severe cardiac, [OR]  respiratory, [OR]  renal, [OR]  and liver diseases
