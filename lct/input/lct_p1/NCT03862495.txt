Inclusion Criteria:
  1. pregnant women on their first visit to the hospital (regardless of gestational age);
  2. aged 18 or above;
  3. agree to participate and sign an informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. systemic  [OR] or topical vaginal antibiotics use within 2 weeks prior to the first perinatal visit;
  2. comorbidities that are known to be related to adverse pregnancy outcomes (e.g., diabetes  [OR] and hypertension);
  3. diagnosed adverse pregnancy outcomes at recruitment (e.g., stillbirth); Women  [AND] with a previous history of CT  [OR] or NG  [OR] and women  [AND] with a prior adverse outcome will not be excluded. Instead, the investigators will conduct sub-analysis and check whether the intervention has different effects on different groups of pregnant women.
