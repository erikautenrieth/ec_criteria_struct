Inclusion Criteria:
 - clinical suspicion of urinary tract infection [AND] (UTI) defined by the presence of at least one of these signs: fever [OR] > 38°C and/or sus-pubic pain [OR] and/or dysuria [OR] and/or pollakiuria [OR] and/or urinary burns [OR] and/or macroscopic hematuria [OR] and/or acute urinary retention [OR] and/or pain [OR] with the rectal examination and/or confusion
 - And urinary analysis with leukocyturia > 10 / mm3, bacteriuria [AND] > 10^3 CFU/mL with ESBL producing enterobacteriaceae [AND] and resistance [AND] associated with fluoroquinolones (FQ) [OR] and cotrimoxazole (CTX) but sensitive to fosfomycin.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - allergy to fosfomycin [OR] and/or trometamol
 - Presence of material in the urinary tract
 - Severe immunosuppression
 - Chronic prostatitis
 - Prostate abscess
 - Acute pyelonephritis
 - Hemodynamic instability
 - Chronic renal failure (clearance <60 mL/min)
 - Prior antibiotic therapy, [AND] with an antibiotic sensitive on the antibiotic susceptibility test except: amoxicillin [OR] - clavulanic acid, [OR] cefixime, [OR] nitrofurantoin.
 - Co-treatment with metoclopramide
