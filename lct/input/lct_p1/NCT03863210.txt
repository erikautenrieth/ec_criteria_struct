Inclusion Criteria:
  -  female sex
  -  age 45 to 60 years
  -  one or more CV risk factors [overweight  [OR] or obesity, [OR]  i. e. body mass index (BMI) ≥25 kg/m2, and/or central obesity - waist circumference ≥88 cm, high blood pressure (systolic blood pressure  [OR] ≥140 mm Hg and/or diastolic blood pressure ≥90 mm Hg), high blood cholesterol  [AND] (≥5.2 mmol/L) and active smoking]
  -  participants on antihypertensive therapy were also included in the study
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  current cardiovascular disease (CVD) (ischemic heart disease, [OR]  peripheral artery disease, [OR]  and stroke)
  -  malignant diseases
  -  serious systemic  [OR] or mental diseases
