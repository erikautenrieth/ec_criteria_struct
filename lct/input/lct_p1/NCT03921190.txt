Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adult patients (16 years old and older)
 - Healthy (American Society of Anaesthesiologists (ASA) category I or II)
 - A maxillary posterior tooth (excluding third molars) referred for root canal treatment [AND] and diagnosed with irreversible pulpitis [AND] and normal apical tissues
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Psychological disorders
 - Intraoral soft tissue abnormalities
 - necrotic pulp [AND] with/ without apical pathology
 - tenderness to palpation [OR] and/or percussion
 - The use of analgesics in the preceding 12 hours before the appointment.
