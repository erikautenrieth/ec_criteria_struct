Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age 18-55
  -  Diagnosis of major depressive disorder  [OR] or bipolar disorder
  -  Current moderate- [OR] to-severe depressive episode
  -  Episode duration more than 2 months  [AND] and less than 5 years
  -  Failure of at least 2 adequate antidepressant medication trials within the past 2 years
  -  Body mass index < 40
  -  16-item Quick Inventory of Depressive Symptomatology, self-rated > 10
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Contraindication to propofol  [OR] or midazolam
  -  Daily use of benzodiazepine, [OR]  opioid, [OR]  ACE inhibitor, [OR]  or ARB medication
  -  Symptomatic coronary artery disease  [OR] or heart failure
  -  Poorly controlled hypertension  [OR] or diabetes
  -  Abnormal kidney  [OR] or liver function
  -  Pregnant  [OR] or breast feeding
  -  Traumatic brain injury  [OR] or significant neurologic signs (past year)
  -  Substance use disorder (past year)
  -  Obsessive compulsive disorder (current)
  -  Post-traumatic stress disorder (current)
  -  Schizophrenia-spectrum disorder (lifetime)
  -  Neurocognitive disorder (current)
  -  Personality disorder as a current focus of treatment
  -  ECT within the past 3 months
  -  Inappropriate for ECT, [OR]  or poor response to ECT within the past 5 years
  -  Incompetent to provide consent
