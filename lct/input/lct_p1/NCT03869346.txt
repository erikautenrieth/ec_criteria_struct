Inclusion Criteria:
  -  American Society of Anesthesiologists GradeⅠ－II,Patients undergo elective surgery for benign Reproductive diseases  [OR] (including laparoscopic ovarian cyst removal, [OR]  laparoscopic tubal ligation, [OR]  laparoscopic tubal drainage, [OR]  laparoscopic salpingoplasty, etc.) or breast disease.
  -  Aged 18-45 years
  -  Body Mass Index 18-25kg/m2,body weight 40-65 kilograms
  -  Agreed to participate the research
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Difficult airway
  -  upper respiratory tract infection within 2 weeks
  -  history of allergy  [OR] or long-term use of propofol  [OR] and opioids
  -  self-rating anxiety scale before operation indicates anxiety
  -  pregnancy  [OR] or lactation.
