Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Conscious patient
  -  Age ≥ 18 years
  -  Acute pain of monotraumatic origin
  -  Pain > 4 on a visual numerical scale
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  State of shock  [AND] with unstable hemodynamics (PA <90/60)
  -  Suspected  [OR] or proven trauma to the chest, [OR]  abdomen  [OR] or pelvis
  -  Serious head trauma
  -  Consciousness disorder  [AND] with Glasgow score <15
  -  Patient who has already received analgesics (with the exception of paracetamol)
  -  Patient receiving an intravenous approach for analgesia
  -  Renal  [OR] or hepatic disorders known
  -  Hypersensitivity  [OR] to fluoridated anesthetics or history of malignant hyperthermia in the patient  [OR] or family
  -  Pregnant  [OR] or nursing woman
  -  Patient under judicial protection
  -  Non communicating  [OR] patient or with difficulties of understanding
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Intravenous injection for analgesia
