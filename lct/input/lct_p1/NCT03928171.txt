Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adults (≥ 18 years old)
  -  Scheduled for robot-assisted colorectal laparoscopic surgery
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Severe liver- [OR]  or renal disease
  -  Pregnancy  [OR] or lactation
  -  Planned diagnostics  [OR] or treatment with radioactive iodine < 1 week after surgery
  -  BMI >35 kg/m2
  -  Known or suspected hypersensitivity to indocyanine green, [OR]  sodium iodide or iodine
  -  Hyperthyroidism  [OR] or thyroid adenomas
  -  Use of medication interfering with ICG absorption as listed in the summary of product characteristics.
