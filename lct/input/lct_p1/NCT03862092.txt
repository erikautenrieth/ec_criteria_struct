Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients aged ≥18, [OR] and <80 years
 - ER-visit patients [AND] with acute abdominal pain
 - NRS ≥ 6
 - Unresponsive to gastrointestinal motility drug, [OR] antacid
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Unwilling or unable to give informed consent
 - Patients who take antiviral agents (acyclovir, [OR] valacyclovir, [OR] famciclovir, [OR] ganciclovir, [OR] valganciclovir, [OR] foscarnet...)
 - Patients complaining abdominal pain [AND] with NRS  ≤ 5 [OR] or improved symptom by gastrointestinal motility drug, [OR] antacid
