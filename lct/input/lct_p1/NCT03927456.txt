Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Has the pathologically-confirmed diagnosis of locally recurrent [OR] or metastatic, [OR] hormone-receptor positive, [OR] HER2 negative Breast Cancer.
 2. Age: 18 - 75 years old, postmenopausal women [OR] or prepostmenopausal women
 3. Received prior endocrine therapy
 4. One previous line of chemotherapy [AND] for advanced/ [OR] metastatic disease is allowed in addition to endocrine therapy.
 5. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group [ECOG] 0-1
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients who received prior treatment with any CDK4/6 inhibitor, [OR] everolimus [OR] or fulvestant.
 2. Clinically significant cardiovascular [OR] and cerebrovascular diseases,including but not limited to severe acute myocardial infarction within 6 months before enrollment, unstable [OR] or severe angina, [OR] Congestive heart failure (New York heart association (NYHA) [OR] class > 2), or ventricular arrhythmia which need medical intervention.
