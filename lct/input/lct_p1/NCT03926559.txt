Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with a second degree, [OR] third degree, [OR] or fourth degree perineal tear identified in the immediate postpartum period.
 - Pregnant [AND] patients who received neuraxial anesthesia during their labor course
 - Women >18 yo
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Unwillingness to participate in the study
 - Allergy to morphine
 - Women [AND] with history of polysubstance abuse/ [OR] narcotic abuse
 - Women <18 yo
