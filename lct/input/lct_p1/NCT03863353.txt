Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Undergoing an elective surgical procedure for which it is expected they will receive a prescription pain medicine for management of pain during recovery at home.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Undergoing [NOT] non-elective procedure
  -  Cognitive impairment [NOT] precluding self-reporting of pain
  -  Hematologic/ [OR] oncologic condition
  -  Liver of kidney conditions [NOT] precluding usual analgesic prescription patters (opioid  [AND] plus a [NOT] non-opioid)
  -  Use of opioids for prolonged pain pre-operatively (<2 weeks)
