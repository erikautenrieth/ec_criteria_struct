Inclusion Criteria:
  -  6-17 years old
  -  Otherwise healthy
  -  Currently have unilateral  [OR] or bilateral ventilation tube(s) (VTs) inserted for otitis media with effusion (OME) [OR]  or tympanic membrane retraction/ [OR] retraction pocket (TM-R/RP) [OR]  or a TM perforation after extrusion of a VT
  -  History of at least 2 sets of VT insertions in the past
  -  Eustachian tube (ET) function (ETF) tests showing an active muscular pattern of Eustachian tube dysfunction
  -  Tubomanometry test results showing evidence of velopharyngeal dysfunction during swallowing
  -  Cleft palate (CP) cohort: [NOT] non-syndromic; prior Furlow palatoplasty [NOT] without complications  [OR] or need for revision
  -  [NOT] Non-CP cohort: have had prior adenoidectomy
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Concurrent  [OR] or past diagnosis of cancer  [OR] or history of radiation
  -  Have  [OR] or had vestibular pathology, cranial base surgery  [OR] or ossicular chain reconstruction
  -  Craniofacial dysmorphology  [OR] (other than [NOT] non-syndromic CP with or without cleft lip in the CP cohort) or other syndrome
  -  A [NOT] non-patent nasal cavity
  -  Patulous ET  [OR] or pathologically low ET opening  [OR] or closing pressures
  -  Pregnancy
  -  [NOT] Unable  [OR] or [NOT] unwilling to perform the tests  [OR] and exercises outlined in the study
