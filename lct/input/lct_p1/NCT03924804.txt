Inclusion Criteria:
  -  1. ethnic Chinese;
  -  2. age, 18 to 65 years old;
  -  3. American Society of Anaesthesiologists (ASA) physical status I or II;
  -  4. Daytime patients scheduled for general anesthesia
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients unwilling to cooperate with the experiment
  -  Body mass index exceeding 30 kg/m2;
  -  Patients with a history of heart  [OR] ,liver  [OR] and Renal failure  [OR] ,hypertension,diabetes mellitus  [OR] or arteriosclerosis; [OR]  and any allergy to drugs used in the study.
  -  Patients with the urinary diseases, such as kidney, [OR]  ureteral calculi  [OR] and tumors, [OR]  prostatic hypertrophy.
