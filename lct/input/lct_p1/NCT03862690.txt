Inclusion Criteria:
 - Signed consent obtained before any study-related activities (study-related activities are any procedure related to recording of data according to the protocol)
 - The decision to initiate treatment with commercially available NovoMix® 30 has been made by the patient and the treating physician before and independently from the decision to include the patient in this study
 - Male [OR] or female, greater than or equal to 19 years at the time of signing informed consent
 - Diagnosed with type 2 diabetes [AND] and treated with basal insulin plus 1-3 bolus insulin injections per day for at least 24 weeks prior to informed consent
 - Available and documented HbA1c value less than or equal to 12 weeks prior to initiation of NovoMix® 30 treatment
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Previous participation in this study. Participation is defined as having given informed consent in this study
 - Mental incapacity, unwillingness [OR] or language barriers precluding adequate understanding or cooperation
 - Participation in any clinical trial [OR] of an approved or non- approved investigational medicinal product within 24 weeks prior to initiation of NovoMix® 30 treatment. Clinical trials do not include non-interventional studies
 - Hypersensitivity to NovoMix® 30 [OR] or to any of the excipients
 - Pregnancy [OR] or intention of becoming pregnant
