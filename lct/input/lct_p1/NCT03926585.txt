Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients admitted to the department of endocrinology [AND] with the diagnose hypothyroidism because of persistent hypothyroid symptoms despite treatment [AND] with L-thyroxine mono-therapy [AND] and normal [AND] and stable TSH (for at least 6 months).
 - Started in combination therapy with L-thyroxine [AND] and Liothyronine in an approximately 17/1 ratio
 - [NOT] Exclusion of an alternative explanation for persistent hypothyroid symptoms
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Initiation of L-thyroxine treatment in patients [AND] with s-TSH below upper normal limit (with assay in current use, that is TSH < 4 mU/L)
 - Ongoing pregnancy
 - Age below 18 years [OR] or above 80 years.
 - Patients who do not read and understand information material given
 - Patients who are not competent to give informed consent
