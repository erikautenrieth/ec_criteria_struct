Inclusion Criteria:
  1. must be at least 18 years old.
  2. fulfil the DSM-5 PTSD diagnosis criteria.
  3. be referred to PTSD care package treatment (Danske regioner, pakkeforløb for PTSD, 2017)     
  4. have access to a smartphone with iOS (version 10 or higher) or Android (version 5.0.1. or higher)
  5. provide informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. suicidal risk
  2. ongoing episode of bipolar disease  [OR] or psychotic disorder
  3. current abuse of alcohol  [OR] or drugs
  4. [NOT] inability to understand and/or read Danish
  5. concurrent psychiatric  [OR] or psychological treatment of PTSD outside of MHS-CRD.
