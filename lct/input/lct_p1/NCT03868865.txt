Inclusion Criteria:
  -  curative malignancy (breast cancer of TNM (T: primary tumor, N: regional nodes, M: metastasis) staging I-III)
  -  receiving chemotherapy
  -  at least 18 years old
  -  written informed consent
  -  willing  [AND] and physically/mentally able to participate in a six hours day care clinic program eleven times during chemotherapy.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] insufficient knowledge of the German language
  -  physical  [OR] or mental disabilities resulting in ineligibility for participation in the program or filling out the questionnaires.
