Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age ≤ 60 years,
  -  Good nutrition,
  -  [NOT] No systemic infection  [AND] and
  -  Elective laparoscopic surgery
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Age >75 years,
  -  Malnutrition  [OR] or an organ system infection
  -  Associated with obstruction, [OR]  bleeding, [OR]  emergency surgery  [OR] or other surgical intervention
  -  Tumor with extensive metastasis
  -  Prior to surgery, patient was fasting, underwent gastrointestinal decompression  [AND] and received nutritional support
  -  Previous history of abdominal surgery
  -  Patient have previously undergone gastrostomy
