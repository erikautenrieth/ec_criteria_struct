Inclusion Criteria:
For the two retrospective cohorts:
  1. All patients that older than 18 treated for localized adenocarcinoma of the prostate between 2000 and 2017 with either radical prostatectomy  [OR] or radiotherapy
  2. All treated patients had a rising post-treatment PSA, [AND]  with at least one post-treatment free PSA blood test.
For the biobank validation cohort:
1. All patients treated with radical prostatectomy  [AND] for localized prostate cancer between 2000 and 2017 who had biobank samples taken when developing biochemical recurrence.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients that were younger than 18,
  2. Patients with prostate cancer other than adenocarcinoma, such as small cell  [OR] and neuroendocrine cancer
  3. Patients with prostate adenocarcinoma  [AND] that did [NOT] not develop biochemical recurrence.
  4. In the retrospective cohorts - patients that did [NOT] not have at least one post-treatment free PSA blood test.
