Inclusion Criteria:
 - Men/ [OR] Women age >40 years.
 - Diagnoses of COPD according to criteria of the American Thoracic Society (ATS), a disease state characterized by the presence of chronic airway obstruction due to chronic bronchitis  (cough/ [OR] sputum on most days a week for 3 months in a year for at least two successive years) [OR] and/or emphysema
 - Diagnosis of moderate or severe COPD exacerbation (see "Definitions")
 - FEV1 > 0.8 L [AND] and ability to produce sputum after hypertonic saline production
 - Post bronchodilator FEV1/ [OR] Forced Vital Capacity (FVC) ratio <70 % [AND] and post bronchodilator FEV1< 80% pred.
 - A smoking history of >10 pack years
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pneumonia as determined by X-ray
 - > 48 h intake of prednisolon/ [OR] antibiotics
 - Need for mechanical ventilation (either invasive [OR] or non-invasive)
 - Treatment with immune-modulating agents for any disease
 - Experimental interventions for COPD last half year
 - Former/ [OR] concomitant diagnosis of asthma
 - Any significant other pulmonary disease [OR] or disorder
 - Other significant disease [OR] or disorder (like alpha-1-antitrypsine deficiency, significant bronchiectasis, cardiovascular, [OR] gastrointestinal, [OR] liver, [OR] renal, [OR] neurological, [OR] musculoskeletal, [OR] endocrine, [OR] metabolic (including diagnosed diabetes), malignant, [OR] psychiatric, [OR] major physical impairment), which, in the opinion of the investigators may either put the patient at risk because of participation in the study, [OR] or may influence the results of the study, or the patient's ability to participate in the study.
 - Existing pregnancy/ [OR] current willingness for becoming pregnant
