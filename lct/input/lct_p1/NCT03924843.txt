Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Men/ [OR] Women age >40 years.
  -  Diagnoses of COPD according to criteria of the American Thoracic Society (ATS), a disease state characterized by the presence of chronic airway obstruction due to chronic bronchitis  [OR] (cough/ [OR] sputum on most days a week for 3 months in a year for at least two successive years) and/or emphysema
  -  Diagnosis of moderate or severe COPD exacerbation (see "Definitions")
  -  FEV1  [AND] > 0.8 L and ability to produce sputum after hypertonic saline production
  -  Post bronchodilator FEV1/ [OR] Forced Vital Capacity (FVC) [AND]  ratio <70 % and post bronchodilator FEV1< 80% pred.
  -  A smoking history of >10 pack years
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pneumonia as determined by X-ray
  -  > 48 h intake of prednisolon/ [OR] antibiotics
  -  Need for mechanical ventilation (either invasive  [OR] or non-invasive)
  -  Treatment with immune-modulating agents for any disease
  -  Experimental interventions for COPD last half year
  -  Former/ [OR] concomitant diagnosis of asthma
  -  Any significant other pulmonary disease  [OR] or disorder
  -  Other significant disease  [OR] or disorder (like alpha-1-antitrypsine deficiency, significant bronchiectasis, cardiovascular, [OR]  gastrointestinal, [OR]  liver, [OR]  renal, [OR]  neurological, [OR]  musculoskeletal, [OR]  endocrine, [OR]  metabolic (including diagnosed diabetes), malignant, [OR]  psychiatric, [OR]  major physical impairment), which, in the opinion of the investigators may  [OR] either put the patient at risk because of participation in the study, or may influence the results of the study, or the patient's ability to participate in the study.
  -  Existing pregnancy/ [OR]  current willingness for becoming pregnant
