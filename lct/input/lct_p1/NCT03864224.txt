Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients must have persistent low back pain of at least 1 month's duration.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Participating currently in a supervised exercise  [OR] or rehabilitation program  [OR] or enrolled in another interventional clinical trial.
  -  Patients with nerve root involvement, suspected serious pathology, unstable trauma to the spine, aspects of pregnancy  [OR] or other conditions that would [NOT] prevent participation or disallow trial completion within the prescribed time period.
