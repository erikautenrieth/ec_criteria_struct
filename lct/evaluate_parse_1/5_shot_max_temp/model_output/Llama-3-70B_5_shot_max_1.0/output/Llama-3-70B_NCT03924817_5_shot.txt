Inclusion Criteria:
 - Prescription of a Narval CC mandibular advancement device
 - Age ≥ 18 years
 - Ability to understand the study information [AND] information on usage of personal data
 - Signed [AND] dated informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] with central sleep apnea (central AI > 5/hour)
 - Patients [AND] with loose teeth [AND] severe parodontitis
 - Patients [AND] with completely missing teeth [OR] removable tooth replacement, [OR] with a teeth health insufficient to retain an MAD [OR] Patients [AND] with missing molars [OR] Patients [AND] with maximum protrusion of less than 5mm
