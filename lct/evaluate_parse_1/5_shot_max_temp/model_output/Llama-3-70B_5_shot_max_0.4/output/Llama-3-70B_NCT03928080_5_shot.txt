Inclusion Criteria:
 - Aged 18 [AND] above.
 - Having the ability [AND] to learn the standard toothbrushing method with artificial intelligence powered toothbrush.
 - Ability [AND] willingness to give written informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Edentulous mouth.
 - Pregnant females.
 - Having received professional periodontal treatment (other than supragingival cleaning) within the previous 12 months.
 - Having received antibiotic medication within the previous 3 months.
