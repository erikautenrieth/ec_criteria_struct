 - Age range between 2-6 years of age
 - Gestational age range 26-34 weeks
 - To be diagnosed [AND] with retinopathy of prematurity
 - Neurological, [OR] hereditary [AND] [NOT] having metabolic disease
 - Compliance [AND] with visual examinations [AND] physiotherapy evaluations
 - To be children [AND] parents [AND] who agree to participate in the study
Exclusion Criteria:
 - To be a child [AND] who does [NOT] comply with Optical Kohlerans Tomography (OCT)
 - [NOT] participating in one [OR] more of the eye examination [OR] physiotherapy assessments
 - Being children [AND] parents [AND] who do [NOT] agree to participate in the study
