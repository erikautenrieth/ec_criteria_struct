Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Non syndromic patients
  -  Medically fit for Surgery
  -  Patient  [AND] with Primary, [OR] complete cleft lip
  -  Patient's age younger than six months
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient  [AND] with syndromic cleft lip
  -  Previous operated cases
  -  Incomplete cleft lip
  -  Patient older than six months
  -  Patients  [AND] with any systemic condition
