Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Severe COPD (FEV1 [forced expiratory volume in 1 second] / [OR]  FVC [forced vital capacity] < 70% et FEV1 < 50%)
  -  Informed consent
  -  Affiliation to a social security scheme
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Effort oxygen therapy
  -  Cardiovascular, [OR] neuromuscular  [OR] or musculoskeletal disorders  [AND] that can provide significant dyspnea  [OR] or limit exercise
  -  Legal incapacity
  -  Low  [OR] or  [NOT] no cooperation anticipated
