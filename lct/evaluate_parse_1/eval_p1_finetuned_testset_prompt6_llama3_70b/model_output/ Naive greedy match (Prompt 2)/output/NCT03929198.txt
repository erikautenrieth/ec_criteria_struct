Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Dysfunctional lipids, [OR] blood pressure  [AND] and glycemic control determined at a screening visit:
       -  Triglycerides > 150 mg/ [OR] dL OR HDL <50 in females, [OR] <40 mg/ [OR] dL in males
       -  Systolic blood pressure ≥130 mmHg OR diastolic blood pressure ≥80 mmHg ( [OR] or on hypertension medication)
       -  HbA1c ≥5.7%  [OR] or higher
  -  No diagnosis  [OR] or treatment for cancer in the past year
  -  No diagnosis of mild cognitive impairment  [OR] or dementia in the past year
  -  Ability to perform daily exercise, [OR] including aerobic activity  [AND] and resistance exercise
  -  Non-smoker in the past year
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Positive exercise stress test
