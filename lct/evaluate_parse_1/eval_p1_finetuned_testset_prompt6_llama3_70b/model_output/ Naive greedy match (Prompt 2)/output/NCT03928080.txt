Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Aged 18  [AND] and above.
  -  Having the ability to learn the standard toothbrushing method  [AND] with artificial intelligence powered toothbrush.
  -  Ability  [AND] and willingness to give written informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Edentulous mouth.
  -  Pregnant females.
  -  Having received professional periodontal treatment (other than supragingival cleaning) within the previous 12 months.
  -  Having received antibiotic medication within the previous 3 months.
