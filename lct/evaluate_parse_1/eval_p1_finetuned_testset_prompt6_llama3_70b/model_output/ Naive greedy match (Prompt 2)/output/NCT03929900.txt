Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age 45-79 years
  -  Estimated glomerular filtration rate 20-60mL/ [OR] min/ [OR] 1.73m2 based on CKD-EPI(Cr) equation
  -  Non-dialysis dependent (NDD)
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease
  -  Unable  [OR] or unwilling to give consent
  -  Previously received chronic dialysis
  -  Previous any organ transplant
  -  Patients  [AND] who diagnosed heart failure  [AND] with NYHA class 3  [OR] or 4
  -  Known liver cirrhosis (Child-pugh class 2  [OR] or 3)
  -  Pregnant women
  -  Single kidney due to trauma  [OR] or donation
  -  Patients  [AND] who received immunosuppressive agent within the past 1 year
  -  Patients  [AND] who diagnosed  [AND] with glomerulonephritis through kidney biopsy
