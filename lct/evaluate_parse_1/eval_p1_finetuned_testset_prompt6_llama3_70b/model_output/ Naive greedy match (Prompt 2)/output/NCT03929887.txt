Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Diagnosis of glomerulonephritis (MCD, [OR] FSGS, [OR] MN, [OR] IgAN, [OR] RPGN, [OR] lupus nephritis  [AND] and etc.) on first diagnostic kidney biopsy, [OR] as per specified pathology definitions
  -  First diagnostic kidney biopsy since 2010
  -  Willingness to comply  [AND] with study requirements, [OR] including intention to fully participate in protocol-specified follow-up at a clinical study site
  -  Informed consent  [AND] and, [OR] where age appropriate, [OR] informed assent (clinical data, [OR] human derived materials including genomic data)
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Unwillingness to give informed consent
  -  Not enough kidney tissue to confirm a diagnosis (adequate tissue: more than 2 cores of kidney tissues (10 x 1.2 mm) including over 10-15 glomerulus each)
