Inclusion Criteria:
 - patients presenting a multi-bullous pemphigoid [OR] newly diagnosed [OR] or relapsed more than 3 months after stopping corticosteroids [AND] and treated according to the national protocol for diagnosis and care issued by the reference center for autoimmune bullous diseases of April 2016
 - patients having received written and oral information and signed informed consent
 - patients covered by national health insurance
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients under tutorship [OR] or curatorship [OR] or [NOT] inability to give informed consent
 - Patients receiving an anti-osteoporotic treatment
 - Patients requiring an anti-osteoporotic baseline treatment (T-score ⩽ -3DS on at least 1 site [OR] or FRAX score above the therapeutic intervention threshold) 
 - Patients with one or more major risk factors for osteoporosis
 - Patients who have received topical corticosteroids in less than 3 months
