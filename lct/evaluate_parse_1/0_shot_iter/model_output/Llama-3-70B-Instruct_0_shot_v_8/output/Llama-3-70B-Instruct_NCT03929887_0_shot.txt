<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Diagnosis of glomerulonephritis (MCD [OR] FSGS [OR] MN [OR] IgAN [OR] RPGN [OR] lupus nephritis [AND] etc.) on first diagnostic kidney biopsy, as per specified pathology definitions
  -  First diagnostic kidney biopsy since 2010
  -  Willingness to comply [AND] intention to fully participate in protocol-specified follow-up at a clinical study site
  -  Informed consent [AND] where age appropriate, informed assent (clinical data, human derived materials including genomic data)
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Willingness to give informed consent
  -  [NOT] Enough kidney tissue to confirm a diagnosis (adequate tissue: more than 2 cores of kidney tissues (10 x 1.2 mm) [AND] including over 10-15 glomerulus each)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
