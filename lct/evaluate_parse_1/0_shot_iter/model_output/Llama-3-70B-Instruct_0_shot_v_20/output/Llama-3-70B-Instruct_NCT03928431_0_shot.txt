Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Infants of healthy mothers [AND] with uncomplicated pregnancies at term, mothers of any ethnic [OR] social background that can speak, read, [AND] understand Swedish to the extent that they can consent in Swedish. An additional inclusion criterion for mothers in the CS groups is vaginal pH ≤ 4 at the time of birth.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Infants of mothers with pre-eclampsia [OR] complicated pregnancies, mothers <18 years [OR] >40 years of age, genital herpes simplex, genital warts, HIV, Hepatitis B [OR] complications during delivery, infants delivered prior to 37 weeks of gestation. Additional exclusion criteria for mothers in the CS group: Mothers [NOT] negative to Group B Streptococcus, [NOT] negative to Group A Streptococcus, [NOT] free of bacterial vaginosis, vaginal pH > 4 at the time of birth. Mothers with fecal samples [NOT] negative for Salmonella, [NOT] negative for Shigella, [NOT] negative for Campylobacter [OR] Yersinia.
