Inclusion Criteria:
 - Handball players [AND] healthy
 - Participation of at least one official match in the last year [AND] attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] musculoskeletal injury in the last three months
 - [NOT] cardiovascular diseases
 - [OR] use of anabolic, anti-inflammatory and analgesic drugs 72 hours before the evaluations
 - [OR] use of alcohol [OR] illicit drugs during the collection period
