Inclusion Criteria:
 - carpal tunnel syndrome diagnosed either by physical exam [OR] EMG
 - age >18
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] allergy to bupivacaine
 - pre-operative opioid usage within the last six months
 - patients [OR] allergic to Percocet or acetaminophen
 - patients taking tricyclic antidepressants [AND] (interaction with bupivacaine)
 - vulnerable populations [NOT] (prisoners or pregnant women)
