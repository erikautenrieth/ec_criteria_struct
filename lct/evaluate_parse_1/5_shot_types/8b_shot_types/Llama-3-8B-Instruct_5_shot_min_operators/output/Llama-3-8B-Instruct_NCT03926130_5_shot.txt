Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of CD for at least 3 months prior to baseline
 - Confirmed diagnosis of moderate to severe CD [AND] as assessed by SF, AP score, and SES-CD
 - Demonstrated intolerance, loss of response or inadequate response to conventional or to biologic therapy for CD
 - If female, subject must meet the contraception recommendations [OR] if not, provide a valid reason for not meeting them
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] Have a current diagnosis of ulcerative colitis, inflammatory bowel disease-unclassified (IBD-U) (formerly known as indeterminate colitis), abdominal or perianal abscess or short bowel syndrome
 - Have a stoma or ostomy
 - Have had a bowel resection within 6 months, or any kind of intra-abdominal or extra abdominal surgery within 3 months of baseline
 - Have ever received any monoclonal antibodies binding IL-23
