Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age [AND] ≥ 18 ans
 - Diabetes mellitus (type 1 [OR] 2)
 - Patients addressed to a one-day hospitalization to assess cardiovascular comorbidities, between January 2014 [OR] and May 2017, in the diabetes department, in the Pitié-Salpêtrière hospital
 - Coronary artery calcium score available
 - Toe-brachial index available
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] History of coronary artery disease
 - [NOT] History of Raynaud's syndrome
 - Opposition of the patient
