Inclusion Criteria:
 - All newly registered patients with smear-positive recurrent pulmonary TB
 - [AND] Adults as well as children [OR] no age limit
 - Able [AND] and willing to provide written informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [NOT] transferred to a health facility [OR] not supported by Damien Foundation
 - Patients diagnosed with HIV/TB-coinfection
