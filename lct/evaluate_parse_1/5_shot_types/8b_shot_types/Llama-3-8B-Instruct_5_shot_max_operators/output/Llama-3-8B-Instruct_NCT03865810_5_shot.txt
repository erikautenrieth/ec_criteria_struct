Inclusion Criteria:
 - All patients over 18 years of age [AND] undergoing gastric resection surgery due to cancer [AND] within [OR] not of an intensified recovery program (ERAS) with any level of compliance with the protocol (from 0-100%).
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] undergoing emergency surgery [OR]
 - Endoscopic procedures [OR]
 - Non-oncological gastric surgery [OR]
 - Patients [AND] who refuse to participate [NOT].
