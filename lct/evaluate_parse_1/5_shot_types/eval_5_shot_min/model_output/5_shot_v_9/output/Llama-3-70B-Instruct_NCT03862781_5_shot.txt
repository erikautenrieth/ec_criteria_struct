Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Eligible patients are those over 18 years of age [AND] who are regularly scheduled to undergo a right hemicolectomy via a minimally invasive approach (robotic [OR] laparoscopic) [AND] with the creation of an anastomosis. Right colectomy will be defined as removal of the ascending colon, ligation of the ileocolic artery [AND] vein, [OR] +/- removal of the terminal ileum, [OR] +/- removal of the proximal transverse colon, [AND] [OR] +/- removal of the right branch of the middle colic artery [AND] vein.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Pregnant women
  -  Additional colon resection is planned (i.e. left colectomy [OR] proctectomy)
  -  Vulnerable populations such as prisoners [OR] adults [NOT] able to give consent
  -  If the scheduled surgery is planned at Butterworth hospital (as to standardize the nursing care received postoperatively)
  -  [NOT] Emergent cases

Patients will be excluded from the study intra-operatively if:
  -  The procedure is converted to an open resection
  -  If a loop ileostomy is performed [AND] in addition to the right colectomy
