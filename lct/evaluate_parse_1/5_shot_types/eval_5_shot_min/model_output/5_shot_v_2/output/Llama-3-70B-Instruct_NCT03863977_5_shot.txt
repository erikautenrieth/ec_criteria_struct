<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients' age from 18 to 60 years old,
  -  physical status [AND] ASA I-II (by the American Society of Anesthesiologists),
  -  Elective unilateral open inguinal hernia repair,
  -  Under [AND] spinal anesthesia
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  age < 18 [OR] > 60,
  -  American society of anesthesiologist (ASA)class III [OR] IV,
  -  [NOT] known allergy to amino amide local anesthetics,
  -  skin infection [OR] diseases at site of injection.
  -  coagulation disorders,
  -  patients with body mass index (BMI) > 35 kg/m2,
  -  scrotal hernias,
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
