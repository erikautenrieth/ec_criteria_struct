Inclusion Criteria:
 - Burn patients
 - Severe neuropatic pain rated at least 5 on the 10-point numerical rating scale (NRS)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] with a cardiac arrest history
 - Patients [AND] with a history of neurologi disease [OR] brain surgery
 - Patients [AND] with unstable heart disease [OR] presence of a cardiac pacemaker
 - Pain resulting from other causes like as neuromuscular diseases
 - Patients [AND] with a psychiatric disorder
 - Patients [AND] with diabetes mellitus
 - Patients [AND] with abnormal renal function
 - Patients [AND] with a contraindication for MRI, [OR] pregnancy.
