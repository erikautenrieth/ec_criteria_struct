Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Handball players;
  -  Healthy [AND] having no known medical conditions;
  -  Participation of at least one official match in the last year [AND] attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Having a musculoskeletal injury in the last three months;
  -  Having cardiovascular diseases;
  -  Use of anabolic, [OR] anti-inflammatory [OR] and analgesic drugs 72 hours before the evaluations;
  -  Use of alcohol [OR] and illicit drugs during the collection period;
