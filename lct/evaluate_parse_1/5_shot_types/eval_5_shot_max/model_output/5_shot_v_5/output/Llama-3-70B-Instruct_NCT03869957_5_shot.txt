Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients admitted in the non-critical areas of the Instituto Nacional de Ciencias Médicas y Nutrición Salvador Zubirán (INCMNSZ) [AND] with a nutritional risk between January 2019 and July 2020 will be considered eligible.
 - Patients [AND] with recent diagnosis of IF type II (an evolution >28 days) originate from various gastrointestinal [OR] systemic diseases (short bowel, [OR] intestinal fistula, [OR] intestinal dysmotility, [OR] mechanical obstruction, [OR] and extensive small bowel mucosal disease).     
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] with contraindications for PN
 - Patients [AND] with known allergies to the components of the PN formula
 - Severe liver [OR] renal insufficiency
 - Uncontrolled diabetes mellitus
 - Certain acute [OR] life-threatening conditions
 - Immunological diseases (such as autoimmune diseases, [OR] human immunodeficiency virus infection, [OR] cancer, etc.)
 - Those [AND] that take immunosuppressant medications
 - Severe hemorrhagic disorders
 - Pregnant [OR] lactating
