<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - LMNA+ cases [AND] with pathogenic LMNA mutations for LMNA+ [AND] and heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients [AND] who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
 - DCMWT cases: patients with heart muscle failure [OR] but with wild-type lamin gene. Heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients [AND] who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
 - HV (controls): matched to cases.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Needle-phobia [AND] that would preclude blood-letting
 - Participants [NOT] unwilling to consent
 - Patients [AND] that have a conventional contraindication for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).
 - Patients [AND] that have had a blood transfusion within the last month [AND] and patients [AND] having haemodialysis will be [NOT] excluded.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
