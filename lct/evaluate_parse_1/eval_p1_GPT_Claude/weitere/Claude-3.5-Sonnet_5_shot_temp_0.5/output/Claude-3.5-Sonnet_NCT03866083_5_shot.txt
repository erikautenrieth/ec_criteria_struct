<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
- Critically ill cirrhotics [AND] with early onset septic shock (<24 hours) [AND] with norepinephrine
of more than 10 ug/min [AND] and lactate >4 mmol/L.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with age less than 18 years
  -  Severe known cardiopulmonary disease (structural [OR] or valvular heart disease, coronary artery disease, COPD)
  -  Severe coagulopathy platelets <20,000 [AND] and INR >5
  -  Active Bleed (Mucosal [OR] or variceal)
  -  Pregnancy
  -  Chronic kidney disease
  -  Extremely moribund patients [AND] with an expected life expectancy of less than 24 hours
  -  [NOT] Failure to give informed consent from family members.
  -  Hemodynamic instability requiring very high dose of vasopressors, late presentation [OR] or spontaneous reversal of shock
  -  Patient enrolled in other clinical trials
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
