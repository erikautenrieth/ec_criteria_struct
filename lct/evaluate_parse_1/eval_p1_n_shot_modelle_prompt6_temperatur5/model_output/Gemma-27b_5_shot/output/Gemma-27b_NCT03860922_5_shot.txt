Inclusion Criteria:
 -  patients in different age groups [AND] both sex [AND] referred from orthopedic outpatient clinic [AND] with chronic ankle pain [AND] suspected clinically to have impingement syndrome.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Inflammatory arthritis [OR] infection [OR] neoplasm around ankle joint.
  2. Patients with contraindications for MRI, e.g. [NOT] an implanted magnetic device, [OR] pacemakers [OR] [NOT] claustrophobia.
