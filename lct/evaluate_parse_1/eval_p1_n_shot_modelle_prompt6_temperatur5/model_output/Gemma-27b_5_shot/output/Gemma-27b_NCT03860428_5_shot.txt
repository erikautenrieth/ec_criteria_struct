<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Gestational age < 32 weeks [AND] birthweight <1500 g [AND] age less than 72 hours [AND] PDA diameter > 1.5 mm [AND] signed informed consent obtained from both parents
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Birthweight ≥ 1500 g [OR] gestation age ≥ 32 weeks [OR] [NOT] lack of informed consent of the parents [OR] congenital heart defect, other than PDA [OR] and/or patent foramen ovale (PFO) [OR] the presence of a clinically apparent hemorrhagic syndrome [OR] any intraventricular hemorrhage (IVH) in the first 48 hours [OR] IVH grade 3-4 [OR] a platelet count of < 50,000/mm3 [OR] a serum creatinine concentration of > 110 μmol/L [OR] oliguria <1 ml/kg/h [OR] suspected/apparent NEC [OR] suspected/apparent lung hypoplasia
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
