Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients aged 18-74, receiving therapy for active ulcerative colitis confirmed by Mayo Score assessment, [AND] and evidence of colonic inflammation.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Evidence of Crohn's disease, [OR] indeterminate colitis, [OR] or presence of bacterial [OR] or parasitic infection.
 - Patients receiving biologic agents [AND] experimental agents are excluded.
