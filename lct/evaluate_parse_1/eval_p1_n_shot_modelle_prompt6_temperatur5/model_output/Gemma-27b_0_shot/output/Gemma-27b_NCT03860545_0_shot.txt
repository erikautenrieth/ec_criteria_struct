Inclusion Criteria:
  -  a planned operation [AND] with the use of cardiopulmonary bypass [AND] coronary artery bypass grafting
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  emergency operations [OR] re-operations;
  -  a known pathology [AND] renal failure [OR] a known pathology of the urinary tract;
  -  chronic use [AND] iron [OR] non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) [OR] immunosuppression [OR] steroids in the preoperative period;
  -  polycythemia [OR] porphyria [OR] pathological hemoglobin species in anamnesis;
  -  preoperative signs [AND] hepatic failure;
  -  active autoimmune diseases [OR] active neoplastic diseases [OR] active infection;
  -  anticipated significant bleeding [AND] anti-platelet agents [AND] suggesting the use of blood-derived products during the operation [AND] afterward
  -  acute myocardial infarction after operation
