  Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Subject is > 18 years of age [AND] willing and able to provide informed consent
  2. Subject is scheduled to undergo elective rotator cuff surgery [AND] has consented to an interscalene block

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Opioid dependence [OR] any subject diagnosed with a chronic pain condition [AND] daily opioid use is needed
  2. Anatomical malformation [AND] in the investigator's opinion [AND] may interfere with the placement of the nerve block
  3. Raynaud's disease diagnosis [OR] Vasculopathy
  4. Patient refusal
