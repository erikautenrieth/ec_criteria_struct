<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Burn patient treated as outpatient
  -  Burn surface area > or = 5% of the total body surface area
  -  mainly 2nd degree burn

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Psychiatric antecedents
  -  Claustrophobia
  -  Heavy hearing [OR] visual impairment
  -  Face burn
  -  Conjunctivitis
  -  [NOT] Consent obtained
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
