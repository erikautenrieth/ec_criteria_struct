<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Type II diabetic patients, 18 years and older, were included if the participants had:
  1. severe non-PDR in either eyes [AND] with/without diabetic macular edema;
  2. ETDRS letters-measured best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) ranging from 20/40 to 20/320 Snellen equivalent;     
  3. [NOT] any previous treatment (of any type) in either eye.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] no history of prior laser treatment [OR] vitrectomy in the study eye;
  2. [NOT] no history of thromboembolic event - including myocardial infarction [OR] cerebral vascular accident;
  3. [NOT] no major surgery within the prior 6 months [OR] planned within the next 28 days;
  4. [NOT] no history of glaucoma [OR] ocular hypertension;
  5. [NOT] no loss of vision as a result of other causes;
  6. [NOT] no history of systemic corticosteroid therapy within the last 3 months;
  7. [NOT] no severe systemic disease other than diabetes mellitus;
  8. [NOT] no known coagulation abnormalities [OR] current use of anticoagulative medication other than aspirin;
  9. [NOT] any condition that could affect followup [OR] documentation (including preretinal [OR] vitreous hemorrhage).
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
