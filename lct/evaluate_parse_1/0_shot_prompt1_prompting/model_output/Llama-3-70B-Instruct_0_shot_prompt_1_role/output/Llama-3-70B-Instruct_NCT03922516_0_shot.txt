<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  all patients who are assigned to a clinically indicated chest X-ray by the emergency department of Vienna General Hospital [AND] ability to provide informed consent [AND] informed consent after detailed patient briefing

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  the emergency department's assessment of a critical clinical condition [NOT] oppose an examination with both modalities (ULDCT of the chest, chest X-ray)
  -  assigned to chest X-ray as follow-up
  -  women [NOT] with negative ß-HCG-test

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
