<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients with recent stroke/TIA [AND] severe stenosis of intracranial ICA [OR] MCA [AND] impaired CVR within previous three months [AND] not before 3 weeks after acute stroke.
  2. Age >21 years

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients with atrial fibrillation [OR] arrhythmias.
  2. Within 2 weeks of cardiac catheterization [OR] arterial puncture at femoral puncture site.
  3. Decompensated heart failure [NOT] class 1 [OR] 2, usually class 3 [OR] 4
  4. LV EF <30%
  5. Moderate [OR] severe AR
  6. Persistent [AND] uncontrolled hypertension (BP persistently >160/100 mmHg)
  7. Bleeding diathesis
  8. Active thrombophlebitis [OR] venous disease of lower limbs
  9. Severe lower extremity vaso-occlusive disease
  10. Presence of a documented aortic aneurysm [OR] dissection requiring surgical repair
  11. Pregnancy

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
