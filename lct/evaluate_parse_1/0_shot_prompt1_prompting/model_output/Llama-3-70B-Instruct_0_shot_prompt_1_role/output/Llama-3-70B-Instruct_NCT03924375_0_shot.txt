<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Gastroscopy can be performed [AND] Serologic markers including serum pepsinogen can be measured

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Age < 20 [OR] > 70 years
  -  Anemia ([NOT] serum hemoglobin level ≥ 10 g/dL)
  -  Severe systemic disease [OR] Advanced chronic liver disease
  -  Use of certain medications, including proton pump inhibitors [OR] H2- receptor antagonists [OR] antibiotics
  -  [NOT] No History of H. pylori eradication
  -  [NOT] No History of gastric surgery
  -  [NOT] No Recent history of upper gastrointestinal bleeding

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
