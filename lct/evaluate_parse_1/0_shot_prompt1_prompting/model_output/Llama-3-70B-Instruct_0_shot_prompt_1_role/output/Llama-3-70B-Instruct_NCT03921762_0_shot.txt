Inclusion Criteria:
  - Age > 50 years old
  - Bilateral age-related cataract [AND] qualify for bilateral implantation within 1 month
  - No previous refractive [OR] ocular surgery
  - Available IOL dioptre range: 18 to 27D
  - Expected postoperative astigmatism ≤ 0.75D

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Pupil > 4 mm
  - Occupation requiring night-time driving [OR] any occupation incompatible with multifocality
  - Acute [OR] chronic disease [OR] illness that would increase risk [OR] confound study results [e.g. diabetes mellitus (with retinopathy) [OR] immunocompromised [OR] glaucoma [OR] epiretinal membrane [OR] cornea guttata, etc….] found to be relevant by the investigators
  - Amblyopia [OR] strabismus
  - Extremely shallow anterior chamber
  - Capsule [OR] zonular abnormalities that may affect postoperative centration [OR] tilt of the lens (e.g. pseudoexfoliation syndrome [OR] uveitis [OR] Marfan's syndrome)
  - Pupil abnormalities ([NOT] reactive, tonic pupils, abnormally shaped pupils [OR] pupils that do [NOT] dilate under mesopic/ scotopic conditions)
  - Instability to place the intraocular lens safely at the location planned
  - [NOT] Pregnancy (pregnancy test will be taken pre-operatively in women of reproductive age)

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
