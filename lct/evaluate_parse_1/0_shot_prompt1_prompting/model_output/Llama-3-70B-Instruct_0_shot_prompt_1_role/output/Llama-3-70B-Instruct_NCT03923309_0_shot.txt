<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  biopsy proven rectal cancer (anal verge ≤ 10cm) [AND] neoadjuvant radiotherapy [OR] chemoradiotherapy [AND] no radical surgery after neoadjuvant therapy

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  other malignant disease [OR] other fatal disease
  -  incompletion of half of radiotherapy course [OR] metastasis on initial presentation
  -  [NOT] biopsy proven rectal cancer (anal verge > 10cm)
  -  [NOT] radical surgery after neoadjuvant therapy

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
