<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Treatment-seeking cigarette smoker [AND] Meet DSM-IV criteria for nicotine dependence [AND] Negative result on urine test for drug use during screening

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History of any Axis I psychiatric diagnosis other than nicotine dependence
  -  Medical conditions that might affect brain function
  -  Current use of medications that could alter brain function
  -  Pregnancy
  -  Current illicit drug use [NOT] limited to occasional use of marijuana

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
