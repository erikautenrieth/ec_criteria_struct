<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Aged 18 and above.
  -  Having the ability to learn the standard toothbrushing method with artificial intelligence powered toothbrush [AND] ability and willingness to give written informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Edentulous mouth [OR] pregnant females.
  -  Having received professional periodontal treatment (other than supragingival cleaning) within the previous 12 months [OR] having received antibiotic medication within the previous 3 months.
  -  [NOT] Having the ability to learn the standard toothbrushing method with artificial intelligence powered toothbrush.
  -  [NOT] Ability and willingness to give written informed consent.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
