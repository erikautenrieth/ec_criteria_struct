Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Major Depressive Disorder according to Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (DSM-V) [AND] > 25 years [AND] Sufficient understanding of the German language

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  < 25 years

  -  [NOT] Able [OR] willing to discontinue antidepressant medication
  -  Pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding
  -  [NOT] No known hypersensitivity to LSD
  -  Somatic disorders including central nervous system (CNS) involvement
  -  [NOT] No known [OR] suspected non-compliance, drug [OR] alcohol abuse
  -  Metal implants
  -  Weight < 42 kg
  -  Suicide risk [OR] very likely to require psychiatric hospitalisation
