<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Monofetal pregnancy [AND] Age over 18 years old [AND] Term Pregnancy ≥ 37SA [AND] Indication for Elective caesarean section

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Fetal pathology known prenatally like intrauterine severe growth retardation [OR] Doppler abnormalities [OR] fetal malformation [OR] a known fetal genetic defect [NOT]
  -  Anomaly of placental adhesion [NOT]
  -  Caesarean in a context of medical [OR] obstetric emergency [NOT]
  -  Caesarean section performed under general anesthesia following failure of locoregional anesthesia [NOT]
  -  Refusal of the woman to participate in the study [NOT]
  -  Impossibility [OR] failure to study the neonatal acid-base balance on the umbilical cord [NOT]

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
