<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age ≥18 years
  -  BMI ≤40 kg/m2
  -  indication for hysterectomy for benign disease [OR] prophylactic surgery due to hereditary cancer
  -  indication for radical hysterectomy [AND] pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for cervical cancer [OR] indication for modified radical hysterectomy [AND] pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for endometrial cancer
  -  size of uterus and vagina allows for retrieval by the vaginal route in cancer patients
  -  written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] known extensive intra-abdominal adhesions
  -  [NOT] anaesthesiological contraindications to laparoscopy
  -  women with pacemaker [OR] other implants where electrosurgery is to be avoided
  -  [NOT] women with known defects of the hemostasis
  -  [NOT] pregnancy
  -  [NOT] other internal or anatomical criteria that preclude a minimal invasive approach
  -  [NOT] inability to understand patient information

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
