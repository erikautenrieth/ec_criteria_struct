<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Moderate-severe uni-[OR] bipolar depression according to the International Classification of Diseases (ICD-10)
  2. A Hamilton scale score (HDS-17) of ≥ 18 [OR] a sub-scale (HDSS) score of ≥ 9 within 24-48 hours before the first treatment

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] Absence of organic brain disease.
  2. Epileptic seizure < 2 years
  3. Patients [AND] having implanted metal parts in the brain [OR] chest
  4. Patients [AND] with pacemaker
  5. Medical diseases [AND] having cerebral impact
  6. Serious suicidal danger
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
