  -  Gastroscopy can be performed
  -  Serologic markers [AND] including serum pepsinogen can be measured

  -  [NOT] having a History of H. pylori eradication
  -  [NOT] having a History of gastric surgery
  -  [NOT] having a Recent history of upper gastrointestinal bleeding
