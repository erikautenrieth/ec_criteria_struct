Inclusion Criteria:
  1. the patient should be completely edentulous for at least 6 months [AND] first class jaw relation [AND] adequate bone for an implant length of at least 10 mm [OR] diameter of at least 2.75 mm [AND] the patients should be able to speak [AND] write in arabic.
Exclusion Criteria:
1) patients who received radiotherapy to the head [OR] neck region for tumors. (2) a history of chemotherapy. (3) patients on long-term steroids [OR] bisphosphonates. (4) heavy smokers [OR] patients with physical [AND] mental dis -abilities that interfere with the maintenance of implants.
(5) [NOT] controlled systematic disease that could compromise implant surgery. (6) extreme bruxism. (7) infectious disease. (8) temporomandibular joint dysfunction.
