<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Age ≥18 years [AND] ≤ 90 years
  2. Tumor diagnosed as Glioblastoma Multiforme on MRI [AND] with distinct ring-like pattern of contrast enhancement [AND] with thick irregular walls [AND] a core area reduced signal suggestive of tumour necrosis as assessed by the surgeon
  3. Tumors situated [OR] near eloquent areas; motor cortex, sensory cortex, subcortical pyramidal tract [OR] speech areas as indicated on MRI (Sawaya Grading II [OR] II)
  4. The tumor is suitable for resection (according to neurosurgeon)
  5. Karnofsky performance scale 80 [OR] more
  6. Written Informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Tumors of the cerebellum, brain stem [OR] midline
  2. Multifocal contrast enhancing lesions
  3. Substantial non-contrast enhancing tumor areas suggesting low grade gliomas [AND] malignant transformation
  4. Medical reasons [NOT] precluding MRI (eg, pacemaker)
  5. [NOT] ability to give consent because of [OR] language barrier
  6. Psychiatric history
  7. Previous brain tumour surgery
  8. Previous low-grade glioma
  9. Second primary malignancy within the past 5 years [NOT] with the exception of adequately treated in situ carcinoma of any organ [OR] basal cell carcinoma of the skin
  10. Severe aphasia [OR] dysphasia
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
