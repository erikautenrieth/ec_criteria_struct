Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Diagnosis of primary knee osteoarthritis according to American College [AND] Radiology College
  -  Being radiographically K-L grade 2[OR]3
  -  Presence of symptoms for at least 3 months
  -  Patients who have been planned a conventional physiotherapy programme because of knee osteoarthritis [AND] have functional capacity for outpatient physical therapy
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients [NOT] having primary knee osteoarthritis, [OR] with seconder knee osteoarthritis
  -  History of lower extremity surgery [OR] spine surgery
  -  History of intraarticular injection in last 1 month
  -  Patients [NOT] having normal neurological disease [OR] with neurological disease that will affect muscle strength, balance [AND] coordination
