<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Treatment-seeking cigarette smoker
  -  Meet DSM-IV criteria for nicotine dependence
  -  Negative result on urine test for drug use [AND] during screening
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History of any Axis I psychiatric diagnosis other than nicotine dependence
  -  Medical conditions [AND] that might affect brain function
  -  Current use of medications [AND] that could alter brain function
  -  Pregnancy
  -  Current illicit drug use [OR] other than occasional use of marijuana
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
