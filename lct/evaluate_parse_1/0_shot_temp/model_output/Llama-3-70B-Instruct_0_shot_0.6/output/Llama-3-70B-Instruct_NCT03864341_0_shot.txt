<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Be a patient [AND] in the Bellevue Hospital Center ED.
  -  Be ≥18 years old.
  -  Screen positive for homelessness risk using a homelessness risk screening tool (HRST) [OR] screen positive for other risk factors.
  -  Screen positive for unhealthy alcohol [OR] drug use.
  -  Able to provide consent [AND] understand the study.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Medically (e.g., critically ill) [OR] psychiatrically unstable.
  -  [NOT] Able to provide informed consent for other reason (e.g., cognitive deficit, profound intoxication).
  -  Incarcerated [OR] in police custody.
  -  [NOT] Able to understand [AND] speak English.
  -  Lives outside NYC [AND] cannot give a NYC ZIP code [OR] lives outside the United States.
  -  Already homeless (residing in a shelter [OR] on the streets).
  -  [NOT] Have a telephone where can be reached for follow-up.
  -  Has already received specialized peer navigator [OR] addiction social worker services during current ED visit.
  -  Has already participated in the study [AND] cannot participate again.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
