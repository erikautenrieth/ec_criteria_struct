<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Female subjects [AND] between 18 [OR] 65 years of age currently taking spironolactone for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris
[OR]
Female subjects [AND] between 18 [OR] 65 years of age currently taking oral antibiotics for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris
[AND] Subjects able to complete an online survey in English

Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects [NOT] between 18 [OR] 65 years of age Subjects that have ever been diagnosed [AND] any mood disorder by a clinician prior to starting spironolactone ([AND] history of mood disorder [OR] current mood disorder) Subjects taking hormonal contraception ([AND] including but [NOT] limited to oral contraceptive pills) for a duration of less than 6 months Subjects that have ever been diagnosed [AND] any type of sexual dysfunction prior to starting spironolactone Subjects [NOT] able to complete an online survey in English Subjects taking oral isotretinoin for a diagnosis of acne Subjects that are currently pregnant
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
