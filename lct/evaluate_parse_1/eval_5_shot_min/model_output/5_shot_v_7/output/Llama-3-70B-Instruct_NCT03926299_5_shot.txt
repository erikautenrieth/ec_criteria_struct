<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion criteria:
  -  Diagnosis of vulvar lichen sclerosus
  -  Clinical LS score ≥ 4
Exclusion criteria:
  -  Concomitant steroid, [OR] calcineurin inhibitor [OR] any other topical [OR] systemic treatment for LS
  -  < 3 months since start of vaginal estrogen treatment
  -  Malignant disease [AND] the cause of the vulval symptoms
  -  BMI > 35 kg/m²
  -  Acute infection (fungal, [OR] bacterial, [OR] viral) of the vulva, vagina [OR] bladder
  -  [NOT] Presence of contraindications for the laser treatment [OR] topical steroid treatment
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
