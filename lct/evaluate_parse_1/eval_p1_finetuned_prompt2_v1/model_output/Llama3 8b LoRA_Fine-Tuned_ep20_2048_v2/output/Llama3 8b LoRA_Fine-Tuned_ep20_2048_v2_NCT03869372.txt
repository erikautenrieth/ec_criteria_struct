Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adults between age 18 and 75
 - Participants [NOT] without a history of gastrointestinal disorders (control group)
 - Participants with functional dyspepsia (FD group)
 - Participants with moderate- [OR] to-severe irritable bowel syndrome (IBD) defined by ROME III criteria
Exclusion Criteria:
 - history of myocardial infarction, [OR] supplemental oxygen requirement, [OR] or diabetes
 - history of chronic gastrointestinal symptoms (for healthy control population only)
 - history of gastric surgery
 - psychosis [OR] or altered cognitive status
 - history of head injury, [OR] metal in the skull, [OR] stroke, [OR] or a history of seizures
 - implantable devices, such as a pacemaker [OR] or nerve stimulator
 - current use of the following medications [OR] or use of substances which are known to lower the seizure threshold: amitriptyline (Elavil), [OR] nortriptyline (Pamelonil), [OR] imipramine (Tofranil), [OR] doxepin (Sinequan), [OR] clozapine (Clozaril), [OR] chlorpromazine (Thorazine), [OR] amphetamines [OR] or methamphetamine, [OR] Ecstasy, [OR] Ketamine, [OR] Angel Dust/ [OR] phencyclidine (PCP), cocaine, [OR] or 3 or more alcoholic drinks per day
 - pregnancy
