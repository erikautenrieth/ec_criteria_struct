Resident Inclusion Criteria:
 - Identified as having Alzheimer's [OR] or related dementia
 - Mild to moderate cognitive impairment (BIMS = 8-15, [OR] nursing home residents only)
 - Makes Self Understood = <1 (MDS B0700) (nursing home residents only)
 - Understood By Others = <1 (MDS B0800) (nursing home residents only)
 - Reside at the facility at least 2 weeks identified as a long-term resident
 - Age >65 years
 - Speaks English
Family Inclusion Criteria:
 - Speaks English
 - Willing and able to watch the videos
 - Willing to complete short surveys. Being unable to attend care conference to view the video (e.g. due to distance) will [NOT] not exclude family from participating.
Staff Inclusion Criteria:
 - Speaks English
 - Willing to watch the videos
 - Willing to complete short surveys
Resident Exclusion Criteria:
 - Limited communication capacity (e.g. aphasia)
 - Comatose
 - Cognitively intact
 - Severe cognitive impairment
