<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion criteria:
  - age 18-90 [AND] acute ischemic or hemorrhagic stroke (within 30 days) [OR] stroke occurred within 30 days after the transfer to intensive rehabilitation [NOT].
  - consent to participate [AND] to anonymous processing of data.
Additional inclusion criteria for Sub-project Neurophysiological Markers:
  - signing of informed consent for the participation in the sub-project [AND] age 18-90 [AND] acute ischemic or hemorrhagic stroke (within 30 days).
Additional inclusion criteria for Epigenetic subproject:
  - signing of informed consent for participation in the subproject [AND] age 18-90 [AND] acute ischemic or hemorrhagic stroke (within 30 days).

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] history of major psychiatric disorder or recent exacerbation [OR] global aphasia [OR] refusal of the patient to participate in the study.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
