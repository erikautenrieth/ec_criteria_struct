Inclusion Criteria:
  - Cognitively intact, capable of giving informed consent [AND] 
  - Clinical diagnosis of a non-penetrating traumatic SCI [OR] 
  - Asia Impairment Scale grade of A [OR] B [OR] C

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] Prior history of brain injury [OR] 
       recent or ongoing infection [OR] 
       clinically significant cardiovascular, lung, renal, hepatic or endocrine disease [OR] 
       neurodegenerative disorders [OR] 
       cancer [OR] 
       immunosuppression as defined by WBC<3,000 cells/ml at baseline screening [OR] 
       HIV+ [OR] 
       chemical or ETOH dependency
  2. Having a contraindication to MRI scans [OR] 
  3. Other acute or chronic medical conditions that, in the opinion of the investigator, may increase the risks associated with study participation or HB-adMSC administration [OR] 
  4. Participation in other interventional research studies [OR] 
  5. [NOT] Unwillingness to return for follow-up visits
