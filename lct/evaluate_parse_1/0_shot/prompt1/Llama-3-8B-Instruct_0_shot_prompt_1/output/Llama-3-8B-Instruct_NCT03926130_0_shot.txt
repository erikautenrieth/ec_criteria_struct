<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - Diagnosis of CD for at least 3 months prior to baseline [AND] confirmed diagnosis of moderate to severe CD as assessed by SF, AP score, [OR] SES-CD
  - Demonstrated intolerance, loss of response [OR] inadequate response to conventional [OR] biologic therapy for CD
  - If female, subject must meet the contraception recommendations [NOT] with a history of pregnancy or lactation
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Have a current diagnosis of ulcerative colitis, inflammatory bowel disease-unclassified (IBD-U) [OR] abdominal or perianal abscess [OR] short bowel syndrome [NOT] with a history of Crohn's disease
  - Have a stoma [OR] ostomy [NOT] with a history of gastrointestinal surgery
  - Have had a bowel resection within 6 months [OR] any kind of intra-abdominal [OR] extra abdominal surgery within 3 months of baseline [NOT] with a history of surgical complications
  - Have ever received any monoclonal antibodies binding IL-23 [NOT] with a history of immunosuppressive therapy
