<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Type II diabetic patients, 18 years and older, were included if the participants had:
  1. severe non-PDR [OR] with/without diabetic macular edema;
  2. ETDRS letters-measured best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) ranging from 20/40 to 20/320 Snellen equivalent;
  3. [NOT] no previous treatment (of any type) in either eye.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] history of prior laser treatment or vitrectomy in the study eye;
  2. history of thromboembolic event [AND] including myocardial infarction or cerebral vascular accident;
  3. major surgery [AND] within the prior 6 months or planned within the next 28 days;
  4. history of glaucoma [OR] ocular hypertension;
  5. loss of vision [AND] as a result of other causes;
  6. [NOT] history of systemic corticosteroid therapy within the last 3 months;
  7. severe systemic disease [AND] other than diabetes mellitus;
  8. known coagulation abnormalities [OR] current use of anticoagulative medication other than aspirin;
  9. any condition [AND] that could affect followup or documentation [AND] including preretinal or vitreous hemorrhage.
