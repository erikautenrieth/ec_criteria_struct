<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. demonstrate readiness to physical activity by completing the online Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire (PAR-Q);
  2. have a SCI resulting in incomplete [OR] complete (C5 and below) paraplegia [OR] tetraplegia;
  3. have broadband internet access;
  4. be between the ages of 18 [AND] 65 years;
  5. have the ability to converse [AND] read English.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] have significant visual impairment that prevents seeing a computer screen to follow a home exercise program;
  2. have active pressure ulcer;
  3. have any contraindications to exercise based on the American College of Sports Medicine guidelines;
  4. [NOT] have significant hearing impairment impeding ability to hear music to engage in exercise.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
