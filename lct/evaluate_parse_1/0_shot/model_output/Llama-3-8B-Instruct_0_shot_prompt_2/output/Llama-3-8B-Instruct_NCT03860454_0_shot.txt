Inclusion Criteria:
  - LMNA+ cases [AND] with pathogenic LMNA mutations [AND] for LMNA+ [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients [AND] who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation [AND] at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  - DCMWT cases: patients [OR] with heart muscle failure [NOT] with wild-type lamin gene. Heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients [AND] who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation [AND] at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  - HV (controls): matched [OR] to cases.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] needle-phobia that would preclude blood-letting [OR] participants unwilling to consent.
  - Patients [NOT] that have a conventional contraindication for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).
  - Patients [NOT] that have had a blood transfusion within the last month [OR] patients having haemodialysis will be excluded.
