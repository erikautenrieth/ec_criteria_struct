<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - Non syndromic patients [AND] patients who are medically fit for surgery [AND] patients with primary, complete cleft lip [AND] patients whose age is younger than six months [OR] patients whose age is equal to six months.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients who have syndromic cleft lip [NOT] patients who are free from any systemic condition [AND] patients who have previously been operated [OR] patients who have incomplete cleft lip [OR] patients whose age is older than six months [NOT] patients who are unable to undergo surgery.
