Inclusion Criteria:
Patients who are diagnosed as ulcerative colitis [OR] Crohn's disease [AND] however in activity [OR] in remission attending the the outpatient clinics [AND] inpatient of gastroenterology units of Internal medicine department

Exclusion Criteria:
- Previous history of hypertension [NOT] no diabetes mellitus, [NOT] no Dyslipidemia [OR] previous history of cardiovascular [OR] cerebrovascular events
- [NOT] no Autoimmune [OR] Neoplastic diseases
- [NOT] no Thromboembolic diseases
