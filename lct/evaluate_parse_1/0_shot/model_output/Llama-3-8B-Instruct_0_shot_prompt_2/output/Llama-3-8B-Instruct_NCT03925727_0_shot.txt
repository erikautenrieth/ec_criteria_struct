Inclusion Criteria:
  - Subject-reported history of dry eye disease [AND] in both eyes for at least 6 months [OR] history of use of artificial tear eye drops for dry eye symptoms;
  - Total score of ≥40 [AND] on SANDE;
  - TFBUT [AND] corneal fluorescein staining [AND] lissamine green conjunctival staining [AND] Schirmer's test score.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] have participated in a previous tavilermide (MIM-D3) study [OR] have clinically significant slit lamp findings [AND] at Visit 1;
  - [NOT] have a history of lacrimal duct obstruction [AND] within 12 months of Visit 1;
  - [NOT] have an uncontrolled systemic disease [OR] be a woman [AND] who is pregnant [OR] nursing [OR] planning a pregnancy;
  - [NOT] be a woman of childbearing potential [AND] who is not using an acceptable means of birth control [OR] have a condition [AND] or be in a situation which the investigator feels may put the subject at significant risk [OR] may confound the study results [OR] may interfere significantly with the subject's participation in the study;
  - [NOT] be currently enrolled [AND] in an investigational drug or device study [OR] have used an investigational drug or device [AND] within 45 days prior to Visit 1.
