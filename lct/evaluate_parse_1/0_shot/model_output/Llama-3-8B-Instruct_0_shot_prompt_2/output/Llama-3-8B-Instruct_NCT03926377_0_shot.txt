Inclusion Criteria:
  - patients presenting a multi-bullous pemphigoid [AND] newly diagnosed [OR] relapsed more than 3 months after stopping corticosteroids [AND] treated according to the national protocol for diagnosis and care issued by the reference center for autoimmune bullous diseases of April 2016
  - patients [AND] having received written [AND] oral information [AND] signed informed consent
  - patients [OR] covered by national health insurance

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] patients under tutorship [OR] curatorship [OR] inability to give informed consent
  - patients receiving an anti-osteoporotic treatment
  - patients requiring an anti-osteoporotic baseline treatment [OR] (T-score ⩽ -3DS on at least 1 site [OR] FRAX score above the therapeutic intervention threshold)
  - patients [OR] with one [OR] more major risk factors for osteoporosis
  - patients [AND] who have received topical corticosteroids [NOT] in less than 3 months
