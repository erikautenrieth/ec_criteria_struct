  - [AND] Burn patients
  - [AND] Severe neuropatic pain rated at least 5 on the 10-point numerical rating scale (NRS)

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Cardiac arrest history
  - [AND] History of neurological disease [OR] brain surgery
  - [AND] Unstable heart disease [OR] presence of a cardiac pacemaker
  - [AND] Pain resulting from other causes, like neuromuscular diseases
  - [AND] Psychiatric disorder
  - [AND] Diabetes mellitus
  - [AND] Abnormal renal function
  - [AND] Contraindication for MRI [OR] pregnancy
