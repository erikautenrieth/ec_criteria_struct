Inclusion Criteria:
 - BMI 45-60 kg/m2
 - Failure of at least one weight loss medication [OR] or other treatment
 - Previous Gastric Bypass Surgery [AND] lead to less than 50% of excess weight lost by 2 years after surgery
 - Significant [AND] Loss of Control over eating
 - Subject is able to attend all scheduled clinic appointments on their own [OR] with a caregiver.
 - Premenopausal women must agree to use [OR] acceptable methods of birth control.
 - Participants provide voluntary, decisionally capable, and appropriately informed consent [AND] to participate in the study.
 - Subject is able to comply w/ [OR] all testing and follow-up requirements defined by the study protocol.
 - Participant has no immediate plan for relocation beyond 6 hours of the study site [OR] or has a reliable means of transportation.
 - Proficiency with the English language [OR] or ability to communicate effectively with study personnel.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Subject has an implanted medical device that delivers electrical energy to the brain [NOT] or has a history of seizures.
 - Subject has an implantable cardiac pacemaker, defibrillator, [OR] or neurostimulator.
 - Subject is likely to require repeat MR imaging after implant of the RNS Neurostimulator and Leads [OR] or has a contraindication to MRI.
 - Subject is unable to fit into CT scanner (500lb upper weight limit for CT scanner) [OR] or has a body mass index (BMI) greater than 500.
 - Inability to provide informed consent to treatment [NOT] or has a history of cognitive impairment.
