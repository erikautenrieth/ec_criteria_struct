Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 - Eligible patients are those over 18 years of age [AND] who are regularly scheduled to undergo a right hemicolectomy via a minimally invasive approach (robotic [OR] or laparoscopic) with the creation of an anastomosis.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 - Pregnant women
 - Additional colon resection is planned (i.e. left colectomy [OR] or proctectomy)
 - Vulnerable populations such as prisoners [OR] or adults [NOT] able to give consent
 - If the scheduled surgery is planned at Butterworth hospital (as to standardize the nursing care received postoperatively)
 - Emergent cases
Patients will be excluded from the study intra-operatively if:
 - The procedure is converted to an open resection
 - If a loop ileostomy is performed in addition to the right colectomy
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Note: I did not make any changes to the inclusion criteria as it was a single criterion.
