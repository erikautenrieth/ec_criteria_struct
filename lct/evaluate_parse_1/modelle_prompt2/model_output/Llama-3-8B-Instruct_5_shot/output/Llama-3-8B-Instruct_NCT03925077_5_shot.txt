Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. demonstrate readiness to physical activity by completing the online Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire (PAR-Q) [AND] 
 2. have a SCI resulting in incomplete [OR] complete (C5 and below) paraplegia [OR] tetraplegia;
 3. have broadband internet access [AND] 
 4. between the ages of 18 [AND] 65 years;
 5. have the ability to converse [AND] read English.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. significant visual impairment [NOT] that prevents seeing a computer screen to follow a home exercise program;
 2. active pressure ulcer;
 3. any contraindications to exercise based on the American College of Sports Medicine guidelines;
 4. significant hearing impairment [NOT] impeding ability to hear music to engage in exercise.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
