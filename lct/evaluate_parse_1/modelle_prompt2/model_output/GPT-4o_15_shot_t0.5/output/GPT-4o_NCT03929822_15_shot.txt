<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Women called back from a screening mammography by either FFDM [OR] or tomosynthesis [AND] with soft tissue abnormalities including masses, asymmetries, focal asymmetries [OR] or architectural distortion [AND] with [OR] or [NOT] without calcifications. Patients will be questioned regarding the possibility of pregnancy [AND] and will need a negative pregnancy test prior the study intervention.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Age <30 years old
 - Screening mammography [OR] with only calcifications abnormalities
 - Male patients
 - Pregnant [OR] or lactating patients
 - Patients [AND] with any allergy to iodinated contrast
 - Patients [AND] with eGFR < 45
 - Patients [AND] that may be treated with radioactive iodine
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
