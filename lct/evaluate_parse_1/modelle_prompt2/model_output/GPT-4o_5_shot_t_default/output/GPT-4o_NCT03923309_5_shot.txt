<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - biopsy proven rectal cancer (anal verge ≤ 10cm)
 - neoadjuvant radiotherapy [OR] or chemoradiotherapy
 - [NOT] no radical surgery after neoadjuvant therapy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] other malignant disease
 - [NOT] other fatal disease
 - incompletion of half of radiotherapy course
 - metastasis on initial presentation
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
