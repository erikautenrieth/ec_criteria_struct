<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Subject-reported history of dry eye disease in both eyes for at least 6 months;
 - History of use of artificial tear eye drops for dry eye symptoms;
 - Total score of ≥40 on SANDE;
 - TFBUT;
 - Corneal fluorescein staining;
 - Lissamine green conjunctival staining;
 - Schirmer's test score.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] Have participated in a previous tavilermide (MIM-D3) study;
 - [NOT] Have clinically significant slit lamp findings at Visit 1;
 - [NOT] Have a history of lacrimal duct obstruction within 12 months of Visit 1;
 - [NOT] Have an uncontrolled systemic disease;
 - Be a woman [AND] who is pregnant, [OR] nursing [OR] or planning a pregnancy;
 - Be a woman [AND] of childbearing potential [AND] who is [NOT] not using an acceptable means of birth control;
 - [NOT] Have a condition [OR] or be in a situation [AND] which the investigator feels may put the subject at significant risk, [OR] may confound the study results, [OR] or may interfere significantly [AND] with the subject's participation in the study;
 - [NOT] Be currently enrolled in an investigational drug [OR] or device study [OR] or [NOT] have used an investigational drug [OR] or device within 45 days prior to Visit 1.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
