<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - Aged 18 years [AND] above at the start of the treatment.
  - Molar relationship either Class I, < ½ unit Class II [OR] or Class III.
  - Extraction of all four first premolar teeth as part of orthodontic treatment.
  - Maximum anchorage required using mini-implant.
  - [NOT] No systemic disease.
  - Good oral hygiene.
  - [NOT] No periodontal disease.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Significant vertical skeletal discrepancies.
  - Systemic diseases requiring long-term antibiotic use, phenytoin, [OR] cyclosporin, [OR] anti-inflammatory drugs, [OR] bisphosphonates, [OR] systemic corticosteroids [OR] or calcium channel blockers.
  - Poor oral hygiene.
  - Active periodontal disease.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
