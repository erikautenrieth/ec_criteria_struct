Inclusion Criteria
  - Screened to be generally healthy as determined by standard medical assessment on physical [OR] and mental health
  - Tanner stage 2 based on breast development for girls, [AND] and pubic hair in boys. Girls will have to be premenarcheal
  - Having obtained his/her informed consent [AND] and parents [OR] or legal guardians consent
Exclusion Criteria:
  - History of serious medical conditions such as intestinal [OR] or severe metabolic diseases, disorders such as diabetes, renal, [OR] hepatic [OR] or pancreatic diseases, disorders, ulcer, hyperthyroidism, malignance, chronic malnutrition, anaemia
