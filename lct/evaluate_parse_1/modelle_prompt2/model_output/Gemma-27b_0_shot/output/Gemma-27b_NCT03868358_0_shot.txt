Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients [AND] met diagnostic criteria for schizophrenia [OR] schizoaffective disorder using the Structural Clinical Interview for Diagnostic [AND] Statistical Manual Diploma in Social Medicine (DSM)-IV (SCID, Version 2.0).

  -  Verbal intelligence quotient > 85 as measured by using a Chinese version of the National Adult Reading Test.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History of significant head trauma [OR] neurological disorders

  -  left-handedness, pregnancy
  -  estimated intelligence quotient<80
  -  current alcohol [OR] drug abuse
  -  [NOT]ability to provide informed consent.
