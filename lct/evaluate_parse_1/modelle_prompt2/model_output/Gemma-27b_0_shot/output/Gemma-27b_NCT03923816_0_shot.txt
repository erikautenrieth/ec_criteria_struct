Inclusion Criteria:
  - patients >18 years old [AND] undergoing elective hepatobiliary surgery under general anesthesia [AND] American Society of Anesthesiologists grade II.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - 1-[NOT]patient refusal. 2-[NOT]psychiatric disorders. 3-[NOT]pregnancy [OR] lactation. 4-[NOT]preexisting neurological dysfunction (history of cerebrovascular stroke CVS) 5-[NOT]metastatic cancer.
     6-[NOT]Inflammatory bowel disease. 7-[NOT]Diabetes mellitus. 8-[NOT]Renal insufficiency (serum creatinine level more than 180 μmol/l). 9-[NOT]unexpected intraoperative findings (inoperable). 10-[NOT]accidental massive intraoperative hemorrhage.
