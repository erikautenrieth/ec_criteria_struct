Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Hospitalized [AND] in an eligible medical ICU
  2. Age ≥ 18 years old [AND] at the time of hospitalization
  3. [AND] With sepsis as defined by the Sepsis-3 (2016) consensus as a known [OR] suspected infection with a SOFA score of ≥2 points above baseline
  4. Received broad-spectrum antibiotics within the last 24 hours [OR] ordered [AND] pending administration
  5. Able to complete enrollment within 4 hours of ICU admission [AND] for administration of the intervention within 6 hours of ICU admission
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT]Inability to receive oral [OR] enteric fluids
  2. [NOT]Inulin allergy
  3. Hyponatremia (serum sodium ≤128 mEq/L)
  4. Immunosuppression, defined as history of solid organ transplant [OR] as receipt of ablative chemotherapy, steroids at the equivalent of ≥5 mg/day prednisone, antimetabolites, anti-TNFα agents, calcineurin inhibitors, [OR] mycophenolate
  5. Surgery involving the intestinal lumen within 30 days [OR] known intestinal strictures
  6. [NOT]Do [NOT] Resuscitate (DNR) [OR] [NOT]Do [NOT] Intubate (DNI) status, [OR] "no escalation of care" orders
  7. [NOT]Lack capacity for consent [AND] [NOT]no appropriate Legally Authorized Representative (LAR)
