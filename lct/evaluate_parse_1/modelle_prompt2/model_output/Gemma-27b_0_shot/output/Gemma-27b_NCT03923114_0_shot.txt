Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with treatment response:
  -  Age ≥18 years [AND] Subject is diagnosed with type 2 diabetes [AND] Subject showed response to GLP-1RA treatment [AND] Ability to sign informed consent
Patients without treatment response:
  -  Age ≥18 years [AND] Subject is diagnosed with type 2 diabetes [AND] Subject showed [NOT]response to GLP-1RA treatment [AND] Ability to sign informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Liver disease [OR] Renal disease [OR] Pregnancy [OR] the wish to become pregnant within 6 months after the study [OR] Breastfeeding [OR] Age <18 years [OR] Pituitary disorder [OR] [NOT]Ability to sign informed consent
  -  Exclusion criteria for MR:
       -  Fragments, clips [OR] devices in brain, eyes, spinal canal
       -  Implantable defibrillator [OR] pacemaker (wires)
       -  Mandibular magnetic implants
       -  Neurostimulator, bladder stimulator, [NOT]removable insulin pump
       -  Metal tissue-expander in chest
       -  Cochlear implant
       -  Ossicular replacement prosthesis
