Inclusion Criteria:
Patients will only be included in the study if they meet all of the following criteria:
  -  Patient [AND] has given informed consent
  -  Patient [AND] with age 18 years [OR] older
  -  Patient [AND] with locally [OR] distantly advanced breast carcinoma, with at least 1 lesion of at least 12 mm maximal diameter. For lymph node metastases, the largest diameter should be at least 15 mm [AND] the short axis at least 12 mm.
Exclusion Criteria:
Patients will [NOT] be included in the study if one [OR] more of the following criteria
applies:
  -  Patient [AND] is pregnant
  -  Patient [AND] is breast feeding
  -  Patient [AND] with recent (< 1 week) gastrointestinal disorders with diarrhea as major symptom
  -  Patient [AND] with any serious active infection
  -  Patient [AND] who has any other life-threatening illness [OR] organ system dysfunction, which in the opinion of the investigator would either compromise patient safety [OR] interfere with the evaluation of the test radiopharmaceutical
  -  Patient [AND] who cannot communicate reliably with the investigator
  -  Patient [AND] who is unlikely to cooperate with the requirements of the study
  -  Patient [AND] who is unwilling [AND]/[OR] unable to give informed consent
  -  Patient [AND] at increased risk of death from a pre-existing concurrent illness
  -  Patient [AND] who participated already in this study.
