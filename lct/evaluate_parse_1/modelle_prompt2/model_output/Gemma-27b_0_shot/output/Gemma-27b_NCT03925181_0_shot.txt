Inclusion Criteria for Patients:
  - Spent more than 4 days in the ICU [AND]
  - Were discharged into the community within 4 months of transfer out of the ICU [OR] [NOT] (patients who were in hospital long-term)
  - Speaks English [AND]
  - Has a fixed address [AND]
  - [NOT] Has pre-existing psychiatric diagnoses documented in the ICU transfer summary [AND]
  - Lives within or surrounding Calgary.
Inclusion Criteria for Caregivers:
  - Caregiver of an ICU survivor participant meeting criteria above [AND]
  - Speaks English [AND]
  - Has a fixed address [AND]
  - Lives within or surrounding Calgary.
Exclusion Criteria for Patients:
- [NOT] Long-term hospital stay
Excluding Criteria for Caregivers:
- [NOT] None
