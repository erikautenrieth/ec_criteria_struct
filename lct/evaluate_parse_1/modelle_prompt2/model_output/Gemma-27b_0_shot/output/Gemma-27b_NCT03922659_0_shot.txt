Inclusion Criteria:
  - Diagnosis as heroin dependence according to DSM-IV criteria
  - [NOT]Definite history of neurological diseases [AND] psychological problems
  - Volunteer to participate the study, cooperate to be followed up

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Acute withdrawal state [AND] CIWA score > 9
  - [OR] With other neurological diseases [AND] psychological problems
  - [OR] With ever brain trauma [AND] damage
  - [OR] With other psychological medications [OR] other substance dependence
  - [OR] With other contraindications to have transcranial magnetic stimulation
