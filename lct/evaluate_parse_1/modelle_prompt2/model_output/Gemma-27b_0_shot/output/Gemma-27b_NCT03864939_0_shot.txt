  -  indication [AND] for radical prostatectomy
  -  [NOT]no other treatment [OR] of prostate cancer
  -  availability [AND] to informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:

  -  previous prostate cancer therapy
  -  psychiatric disease
  -  participation [AND] at another study
