Inclusion Criteria:
For the two retrospective cohorts:
  1. All patients [AND] older than 18 [AND] treated for localized adenocarcinoma of the prostate between 2000 [AND] 2017 [OR] with radical prostatectomy [OR] radiotherapy
  2. All treated patients had a rising post-treatment PSA, [AND] with at least one post-treatment [NOT]free PSA blood test.
For the biobank validation cohort:
1. All patients treated with radical prostatectomy for localized prostate cancer between 2000 [AND] 2017 [AND] who had biobank samples taken when developing biochemical recurrence.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients that were younger than 18,
  2. Patients with prostate cancer other than adenocarcinoma, such as small cell [OR] neuroendocrine cancer
  3. Patients with prostate adenocarcinoma that did [NOT] develop biochemical recurrence.
  4. In the retrospective cohorts - patients that did [NOT] have at least one post-treatment [NOT]free PSA blood test.
