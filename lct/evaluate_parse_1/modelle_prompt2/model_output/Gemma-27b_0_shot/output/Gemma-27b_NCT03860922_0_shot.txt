Inclusion Criteria:
  -  patients [AND] in different age groups [AND] both sex referred from orthopedic outpatient clinic [AND] with chronic ankle pain [AND] suspected clinically to have impingement syndrome.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Inflammatory arthritis [OR] infection in ankle joint [OR] neoplasm around ankle joint.
  2. Patients with contraindications for MRI, e.g. an implanted magnetic device, pacemakers [OR] claustrophobia.
