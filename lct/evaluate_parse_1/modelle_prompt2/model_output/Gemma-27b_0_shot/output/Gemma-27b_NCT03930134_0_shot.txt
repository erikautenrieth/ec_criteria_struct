Inclusion Criteria:
  - singleton pregnancy
  - Cicatricial uterus [AND] with contraindication to vaginal delivery (uterus bi [OR] multi-cicatricial, corporal uterine scar, intra-mural myomectomy, dystocic presentation)
  - Fetal macrosomia [AND] with a weight greater than 4Kg
  - Placenta previa
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [WHO] refuse to participate in the study
  - prenatally diagnosed fetal pathology (intrauterine growth restriction, malformation, genetics disorders)
  - adherent placenta, [OR] an adnexal mass [OR] a myoma at the lower uterine segment
  - Cesarean section in a context of medical [OR] obstetrical emergency
  - Caesarean section [AND] with a high risk of bleeding
