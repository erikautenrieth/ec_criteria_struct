Inclusion Criteria:
  - patients [AND] who underwent benign laparoscopic hysterectomy [AND] with systematic salpingectomy
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Hysterectomy [AND] in a context of gynecologic cancer
  - Hysterectomy [NOT] carried out using laparoscopy
  - Fimbriated end [NOT] missing [OR] damaged [OR] impossible for pathologist to analyze
