Inclusion Criteria:
  - Stage III periodontitis patient [AND] having at least one tooth [OR] with 2-wall, 3-wall, [OR] combined 2- to 3-wall intraosseous defect ≥ 3 mm in depth (assessed by bone sounding, radiographic examination) [AND] with clinical attachment level (CAL) ≥ 5mm [AND] pocket depth (PD) ≥ 6 mm.
  - Defect [NOT] extending to a root furcation area
  - Vital teeth
  - [NOT]Smokers.
  - [NOT]History of intake of antibiotics [OR] other medications affecting the periodontium in the previous 6 months.
  - [NOT]Periodontal therapy carried out in the past 6 months.
  - Able to sign an informed consent form.
  - Patients age between 25 [AND] 50 years old.
  - Patients [WHO] are cooperative, motivated, [AND] hygiene conscious.
  - Systemically [NOT]free according to Cornell Medical Index (Broadbent, 1951).
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Pregnancy [OR] breast feeding
  - The presence of an orthodontic appliance
  - Teeth mobility greater than grade I
