Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [AND] with both sexes from 40 to 80 years old
  - [AND] who underwent imaging examination evidencing grade II [OR] III knee osteoarthritis according to the Kellgren [AND] Lawrence classification
  - [AND] who were referred to the physiotherapy service by a physician.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [NOT] unable to walk
  - [AND] with a history of cancer
  - [AND] associated neurological diseases
  - [AND] morbid obesity
