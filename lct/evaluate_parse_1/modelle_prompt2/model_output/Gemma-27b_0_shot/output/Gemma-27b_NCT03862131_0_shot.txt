Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Participant [AND] in the SURVIVE registry
  -  Signed informed consent form [AND] for PROACT
  -  Histological diagnosis [OR] of early [OR] metastatic breast cancer, lymphoma, sarcoma, leukemia, myeloma, [OR] lung cancer (patients with treated [AND] clinically stable brain metastasis are acceptable)
  -  Scheduled to receive anti-cancer therapy (radiation therapy is permitted)
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Contraindication to magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)
  -  [NOT]Able to comply with study investigations (in the judgment of the investigator)
  -  Life expectancy less than 1 year
