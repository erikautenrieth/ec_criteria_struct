Inclusion Criteria:
  - Adults [AND] Colles' type fractures [AND] distal radius [AND] undergoing fracture manipulation [AND] ED
  - UDiReCT Trial trained staff available
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Age [NOT] 18
  - Volar displaced (Smith's) type fractures [OR] well established [AND] being unstable [AND] requiring surgical treatment
  - [NOT] able [OR] [NOT] willing [AND] give informed consent
  - [NOT] able [OR] [NOT] willing [AND] be followed up (e.g. orthopaedic follow up in another region)
  - Major trauma [AND] other injuries [AND] ISS (Injury Severity Score) >16
  - Urgent manipulation required due to neurovascular [OR] skin compromise
  - Open fractures [OR] those [AND] associated nerve [OR] tendon involvement (affect functional outcome)
  - Prisoners
