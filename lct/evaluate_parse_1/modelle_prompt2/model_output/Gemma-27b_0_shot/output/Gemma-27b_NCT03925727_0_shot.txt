Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Subject-reported history of dry eye disease in both eyes for at least 6 months [AND] history of use of artificial tear eye drops for dry eye symptoms [AND] total score of ≥40 on SANDE [AND] TFBUT [AND] corneal fluorescein staining [AND] lissamine green conjunctival staining [AND] Schirmer's test score.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Have participated in a previous tavilermide (MIM-D3) study [OR] have clinically significant slit lamp findings at Visit 1 [OR] have a history of lacrimal duct obstruction within 12 months of Visit 1 [OR] have an uncontrolled systemic disease [OR] be a woman who is pregnant [OR] nursing [OR] planning a pregnancy [OR] be a woman of childbearing potential who is [NOT] using an acceptable means of birth control [OR] have a condition [OR] be in a situation which the investigator feels may put the subject at significant risk [OR] may confound the study results [OR] may interfere significantly with the subject's participation in the study [OR] be currently enrolled in an investigational drug or device study [OR] have used an investigational drug or device within 45 days prior to Visit 1.
