Inclusion Criteria:
  - Chronic Kidney Disease stage 1-5
  - [AND] Hyperuricemic (UA >= 5.5 mg/dL)
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Contraindication to Allopurinol
  - [OR] Elevated baseline liver function tests
  - [OR] Receiving acute [OR] chronic dialysis
  - [OR] Primary metabolic disorder
  - [OR] Sickle cell disease
  - [OR] Autosomal Dominant Polycystic Kidney Disease
  - [OR] Cystinosis
  - [OR] Bartter [OR] Gitelman Disease
  - [OR] Pregnant [OR] nursing
