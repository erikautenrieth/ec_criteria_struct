Inclusion Criteria:
  -  1. elective total knee arthroplasty [AND] for degenerative osteoarthritis
  -  2. elective total hip arthroplasty [AND] for osteoarthritis [OR] osteonecrosis of femoral head
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  1. ASA class >3
  -  2. severe medical comorbidities
  -  3. inflammatory arthritis [INCLUDING] rheumatoid arthritis
  -  4. severe instability of knee [OR] hip
  -  5. revision surgery
