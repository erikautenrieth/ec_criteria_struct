Inclusion Criteria:
  -  (1) gestational age 35 [AND] 39 weeks
  -  (2) adults
  -  (3) primipara [OR] their spouses
  -  (4) [AND] having routine prenatal visits [AND] plan to have the birth in the medical center
  -  (5) able to communicate with Mandarin [OR] Taiwanese
  -  (6) willing to participate in this study [AND] give written inform-consent forms
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  (1) [AND] having fetus with abnormality
  -  (2) high-risk pregnancy
  -  (3) [NOT] able to room-in due to newborn's medical conditions
