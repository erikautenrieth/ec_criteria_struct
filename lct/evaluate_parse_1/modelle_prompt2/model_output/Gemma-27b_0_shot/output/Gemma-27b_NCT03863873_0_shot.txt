Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [AND] with mild-to-moderate idiopathic CTS [AND] with clinical manifestations [AND] who failed to respond to conservative treatment ( such as splint, medications, Physical therapy) for at least 3 months [AND] they were diagnosed by electrophysiological study [AND] musculoskeletal ultrasound.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Diabetes
  - Hypothyroidism
  - Rheumatoid arthritis
  - Previous carpal tunnel decompressive surgery
  - Cervical radiculopathy, [OR] polyneuropathy, [OR] brachial plexopathy, [OR] traumatic nerve injury, [OR] thoracic outlet syndrome
  - Previous corticosteroid injection into the carpal tunnel in the preceding 4 weeks
  - Anemia (hemoglobin <10gm%)
  - Coagulopathy
  - Pregnancy
