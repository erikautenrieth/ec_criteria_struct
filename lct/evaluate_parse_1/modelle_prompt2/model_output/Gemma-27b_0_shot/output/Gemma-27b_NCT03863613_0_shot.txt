Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Women [AND] with a twin pregnancy (mono-[OR]di-chorionic)
  -  16 0/7 [OR] 22 0/7 weeks of gestation
  -  Maternal age ≥18 yrs
  -  Cervical length ≤28 mm
  -  Informed consent
  -  [NOT]Participating in another preterm birth study at the same time
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Uterine anomalies
  -  Cervical dilation [AND] with visible amniotic membranes [OR] amniotic membranes prolapsed into the vagina
  -  Twin-to-twin transfusion syndrome
  -  Stillbirth [OR] major congenital abnormalities in any of the fetus
  -  Severe vaginal discharge
  -  Acute vaginitis [OR] cervicitis
  -  Vaginal bleeding
  -  Placental preavia
  -  Vasa preavia
  -  Premature rupture of membranes
  -  Premature labor [AND] with/[OR]without ruptured membrane
  -  Suspicion of chorioamnionitis
  -  Cerclage in place [OR] [NOT]able to undergo cervical cerclage [OR] pessary
