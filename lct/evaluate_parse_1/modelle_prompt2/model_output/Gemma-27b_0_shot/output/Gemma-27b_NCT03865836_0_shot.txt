Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Subject [AND] must have a diagnosis of LOPD based on documentation of one of the following:
       1. deficiency of GAA enzyme
       2. GAA genotyping
  2. Subject [AND] must provide signed informed consent prior to any study-related procedures being performed.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Subject [AND] is eligible for enrollment in an ongoing clinical trial using ATB200/[OR]AT2221.
  2. Subject, [IF] female, is pregnant [OR] breastfeeding at screening.
  3. Subject, [WHETHER] male [OR] female, is planning to conceive a child during the study.
