Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Parturients [AND] age ≥18 years;
  -  Presence [AND] prenatal depression (EPDS score ≥10);
  -  Provide written informed consents.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History [AND] psychiatric disease (schizophrenia) [OR] communication barriers [THAT] prevent normal communication before childbirth;
  -  Severe complications during pregnancy (such as severe preeclampsia, placenta accreta, [OR] HELLP [Hemolysis, Elevated Liver enzymes [AND] Low Platelets] syndrome);
  -  ASA physical status classification ≥III;
  -  Presence [AND] contraindications to ketamine, including refractory hypertension, severe cardiovascular disease (heart function classification ≥III), [OR] hyperthyroidism;
  -  Refuse to participate.
