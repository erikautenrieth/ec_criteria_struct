Inclusion Criteria:
  - complex anal fistula according to Parks classification
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT]cryptoglandular fistulas
  - acute sepsis
  - inflammatory bowel diseases
  - simple fistulas [OR] defined as subcutaneous, intersphincteric [AND] low transsphincteric fistula
  - pregnant patients
  - ASA IV
  - patients <18
  - [NOT]immunocompromised
  - patients who refused consent.
