Inclusion Criteria:
  -  First time stroke [AND]
  -  Patients [WHO] has spasticity due to stroke [AND]
  -  At least 6 months after stroke [AND]
  -  Individuals between the ages of 18-75 will be included in the study.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Any contraindication for dry needling (eg, anticoagulants, infections, bleeding, etc.), [OR]
  -  [NOT] Having diabetes [OR]
  -  [NOT] Having cardiovascular diseases [OR]
  -  [NOT] Having any other neurological problems [OR]
  -  Application of Botox within 6 months prior to the study [OR]
  -  Individuals included in another treatment program will [NOT] be included in the study.
