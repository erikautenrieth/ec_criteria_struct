Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Participants were eligible for inclusion in the study [IF] they had evidence suggesting acute ischemic stroke [AND] that lasted for ≤ 4.5 hours .
  -  Those Participants were treated with intravenous thrombolytic therapy ((recombinant tissue plasminogen activator (rt-PA)).
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Those [WHO] had hemorrhagic stroke
  -  participants presented with acute myocardial infarction [OR] cerebrovascular stroke in the previous three months.
  -  serious head trauma in the previous three months.
  -  urinary tract [OR] lung [OR] gastrointestinal hemorrhage within the three weeks.
  -  serious trauma [OR] major surgery within the previous two weeks.
  -  lumbar [OR] arterial puncture at a non-compressible site within one week.
  -  those [WHO] received heparin within 48 hours [AND] resulting in an activated partial thromboplastin time greater than the upper limit of normal.
  -  systolic pressure > 185 mmHg [OR] diastolic pressure > 110 mmHg.
  -  blood glucose <50 [OR] > 400 mg/dL.
  -  current use of anticoagulants with an International Normalized Ratio > 1.7 [OR] prothrombin time>15 sec [OR] platelet count < 100,000/mm3.
  -  pregnancy [OR] serious heart [OR] lung [OR] kidney [OR] other organ dysfunction.
  -  allergy to active ingredients of rt-PA [OR] patients with > 4.5 hours from the last time known to be asymptomatic.
  -  those [IN WHICH] troponin was [NOT] measured at admission were excluded from the study
