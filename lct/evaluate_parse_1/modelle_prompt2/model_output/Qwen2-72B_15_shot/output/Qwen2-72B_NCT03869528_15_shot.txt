 - All adults [AND] ≥ 18 yrs of age [AND] undergoing flexible bronchoscopy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Uncontrolled hypertension Systolic Blood Pressure [AND] >180 mm Hg
 - Symptomatic coronary artery disease
 - Pregnancy
 - [NOT] Not giving informed consent for the procedure
 - Hypoxemia (oxygen saturation [by pulse oximetry] [AND] < 92% [WITH] with Fio2 of ≥ 0.3
 - Bronchoscopy performed through an artificial airway
