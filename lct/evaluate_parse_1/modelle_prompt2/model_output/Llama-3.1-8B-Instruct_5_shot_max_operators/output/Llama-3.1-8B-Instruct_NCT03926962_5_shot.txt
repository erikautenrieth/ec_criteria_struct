Inclusion Criteria:
 - BMI [OR] : 18-30 kg/m2 (Body Mass Index [BMI] [kg/m2] = Body weight [kg] Height2 [m2])
 - Ability [AND] and willingness to abstain from alcohol, methylxanthine-containing beverages [OR] or food (coffee, tea, cola, chocolate, "powerdrinks"), grapefruit (juice) from 48 h prior to entry in the clinical research center [OR] until discharge
 - Medical history [AND] without major pathology
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Evidence [AND] of clinically relevant pathology
 - Mental [NOT] handicap
 - History [AND] of relevant drug [OR] and/or food allergies
 - Regular/routine treatment [OR] with non-topical medications within 30 days prior to entry into the clinical research center
 - Smoking [OR] within 60 days prior to drug administration [OR] and through the follow-up visit
 - History [AND] of alcohol abuse [OR] or drug addiction (including soft drugs like cannabis products)
