Inclusion Criteria:
 - vaginal delivery
 - an infant age of 6 months ([OR] ±3 weeks)
 - mother ≥15 years of age
 - infant still breastfeeding
 - anticipated residence in the area [AND] for the study duration.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] inability to provide informed consent
 - hemoglobin < 70 g/L
 - Z scores for weight-for-age (WAZ) [OR] or weight-for-height (WHZ) <3,
 - [NOT] any maternal [OR] infant chronic illness
 - administration of [NOT] any infant vitamin [OR] or mineral supplements [OR] for the past 2 months
 - [NOT] any history of infant antibiotic treatment.
