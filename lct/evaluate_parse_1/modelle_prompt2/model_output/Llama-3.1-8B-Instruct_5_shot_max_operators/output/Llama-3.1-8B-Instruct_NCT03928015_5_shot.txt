Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Male [OR] female, 18 years to 65 years old [AND] (inclusive)
 2. Index admission for traumatic injury

 5. Willing [AND] to divulge habitual marijuana usage (yes [OR] or no. Yes, habitual/chronic usage; no, recreational, former, [OR] or never usage)

 1. Patients [AND] on a pain management agreement

 5. Patients prescribed dronabinol between arrival [AND] and prior [OR] to screening/randomization
 6. Pregnancy [OR] or breast feeding
 7. Incarceration
