<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. age > 18 years[OR]
  2. confirmed diagnosis of prediabetes [OR] T2D (documented A1c of ≥ 5.7%[OR] 2-hour post-load glucose of >140[OR] FBG of >100)[OR]
  3. a family member[OR] peer willing to participate [AND] attend all sessions as a social influencer[OR]
  4. proficiency in English of at least one member of each dyad[OR]
  5. willingness to provide written consent
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. type 1 diabetes [OR] diabetes secondary to other conditions (e.g. steroid-induced[OR] pancreatic insufficiency[OR] chemotherapy-induced)[OR]
  2. malignancy [OR] life-threatening illness [AND] life expectancy of <5 years[OR]
  3. end-stage disease [OR] serious illness [AND] prohibits participation (e.g. end-stage renal disease [OR] class IV congestive heart failure)[OR]
  4. [NOT] inability to perform unsupervised physical activity[OR]
  5. pregnancy[OR]
  6. diagnosed cognitive deficits [OR] limited decision-making capacity[OR]
  7. alcohol [OR] substance abuse[OR]
  8. homelessness [OR] [NOT] no fixed address
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
