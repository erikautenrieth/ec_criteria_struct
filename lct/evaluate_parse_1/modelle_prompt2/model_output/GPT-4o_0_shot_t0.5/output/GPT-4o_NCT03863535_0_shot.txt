Inclusion Criteria:
Type II diabetic patients, 18 years [AND] older, were included if the participants had:
  1. severe non-PDR in either eyes [OR] [AND] [NOT] diabetic macular edema;
  2. ETDRS letters-measured best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) ranging from 20/40 to 20/320 Snellen equivalent;
  3. [NOT] previous treatment (of any type) in either eye.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. history [OR] [AND] prior laser treatment [OR] vitrectomy in the study eye;
  2. history [OR] thromboembolic event - including myocardial infarction [OR] cerebral vascular accident;
  3. major surgery within the prior 6 months [OR] planned within the next 28 days;
  4. history [OR] glaucoma [OR] ocular hypertension;
  5. loss [OR] vision as a result [AND] other causes;
  6. history [OR] systemic corticosteroid therapy within the last 3 months;
  7. severe systemic disease [AND] [NOT] diabetes mellitus;
  8. known coagulation abnormalities [OR] current use [OR] anticoagulative medication [OR] [NOT] aspirin;
  9. any condition [AND] could affect followup [OR] documentation (including preretinal [OR] vitreous hemorrhage).
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
