 - Patient requiring an electroneuromyogram whose indication allows the application of a standard protocol of examination.

 - Patient in an exclusion period determined by another study.
 - Patient [AND] under the protection of justice, under guardianship [OR] or under curatorship.
 - Patient [AND] refusing to sign the consent form.
 - Patient for which it is [NOT] impossible to provide information about the study.
