Inclusion Criteria:
 -  Infants of healthy mothers [AND] with uncomplicated pregnancies at term, mothers of any ethnic [OR] or social background that can speak, [OR] read, [AND] and understand Swedish to the extent that they can consent in Swedish. An additional inclusion criterion for mothers in the CS groups is vaginal pH ≤ 4 at the time of birth.
Exclusion Criteria:
 -  Infants of mothers [AND] with pre-eclampsia [OR] or complicated pregnancies, mothers <18 years [OR] or >40 years of age, genital herpes simplex, [OR] genital warts, [OR] HIV, [OR] Hepatitis B [OR] or complications during delivery, infants delivered prior to 37 weeks of gestation. Additional exclusion criteria for mothers in the CS group: Mothers positive to Group B Streptococcus, [OR] Group A Streptococcus, [OR] bacterial vaginosis, [OR] vaginal pH > 4 at the time of birth. Mothers [AND] with fecal samples positive for Salmonella, [OR] Shigella, [OR] Campylobacter [OR] or Yersinia.
