Inclusion Criteria:
 - The inclusion criteria were as follows: a) men [AND] and women aged between 18-[OR]- and 65-years b) medical diagnosis of Non-Specific Chronic Neck Pain [AND] with more than 6 months of evolution of neck pain.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Exclusion criteria included the following: a) patients with rheumatic diseases, [OR] cervical hernia, [OR] cervical whiplash syndrome, [OR] neck surgeries [OR] or history of arthrodesis, b) systemic diseases, c) vision, [OR] hearing [OR] or vestibular problems, d) Severe trauma [OR] or traffic accident which has had an impact on the cervical area.
