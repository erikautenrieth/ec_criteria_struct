Inclusion Criteria:
 - LMNA+ cases [OR] with pathogenic LMNA mutations [AND] for LMNA+ [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients [AND] who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation [AND] at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
 - DCMWT cases: patients with heart muscle failure [AND] but [AND] with wild-type lamin gene. Heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients [AND] who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation [AND] at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
 - HV (controls): matched to cases.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Needle-phobia that would [NOT] preclude blood-letting
 - Participants [NOT] unwilling to consent
 - Patients that have a conventional contraindication [AND] for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).
 - Patients that have had a blood transfusion within the last month [OR] patients having haemodialysis will be excluded.
