Inclusion Criteria:
- Critically ill cirrhotics with early onset septic shock (<24 hours) [AND] with norepinephrine of more than 10 ug/min [OR] and lactate >4 mmol/L.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [NOT] with age less than 18 years
 - Severe known cardiopulmonary disease (structural [OR] or valvular heart disease, coronary artery disease, [OR] COPD)
 - Severe coagulopathy platelets <20,000 [AND] and INR >5
 - Active Bleed (Mucosal [OR] or variceal)
 - Pregnancy
 - Chronic kidney disease
 - Extremely moribund patients with an expected life expectancy of less than 24 hours
 - Failure [NOT] to give informed consent from family members.
 - Hemodynamic instability requiring very high dose of vasopressors, late presentation [OR] or spontaneous reversal of shock
 - Patient [NOT] enrolled in other clinical trials
