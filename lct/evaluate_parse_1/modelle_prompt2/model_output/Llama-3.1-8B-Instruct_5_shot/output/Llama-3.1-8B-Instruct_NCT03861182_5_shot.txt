Inclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
- Over 18 years of age
Neonates:
  - Aged between 1h of life [OR] and 28 days of life
  - Born beyond 33 Week of Amenorrhea [OR] and 1day [OR] and 2kg of birth weight
  - Hospitalized in the medical-surgical centre of Pediatrics [AND] of the hospital of Strasbourg
  - For whom a blood sample was prescribed [OR] as part of their routine care
Exclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
  - Systemic inflammatory disease
  - Transient inflammatory state
  - Any drug [OR] that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h preceding the sampling
Neonates:
  - Risk of anemia < 7g/DL
  - Current anticoagulant treatment
