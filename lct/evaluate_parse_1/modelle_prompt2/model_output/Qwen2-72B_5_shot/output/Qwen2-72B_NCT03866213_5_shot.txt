Inclusion Criteria:
 - The patient [AND] is currently being treated at Queen Elizabeth Central Hospital [OR] or Kamuzu Central Hospital in the neonatal ward.
 - The patient [AND] is less than 28 days old (neonate).
 - The patient [AND] is deemed to be at risk for jaundice [OR] or the patient [AND] is undergoing blue light phototherapy for treatment of jaundice.
 - The patient's parent [OR] or guardian [AND] has provided informed consent for their child to participate.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Parent [OR] or guardian [IS] unable [OR] or unwilling to provide informed consent.
 - The patient [IS] unable to receive a blood draw/heel stick as determined by their clinician.
