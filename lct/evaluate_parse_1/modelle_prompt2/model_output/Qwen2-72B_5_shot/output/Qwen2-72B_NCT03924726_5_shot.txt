<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Aged 18 years [AND] and above at the start of the treatment.
 - Molar relationship either Class I, [OR] < ½ unit Class II [OR] or Class III.
 - Extraction of all four first premolar teeth [AND] as part of orthodontic treatment.
 - Maximum anchorage required [AND] using mini-implant.
 - [NOT] No systemic disease.
 - Good oral hygiene.
 - [NOT] No periodontal disease.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Significant vertical skeletal discrepancies.
 - Systemic diseases requiring long-term antibiotic use, [OR] phenytoin, [OR] cyclosporin, [OR] anti-inflammatory drugs, [OR] bisphosphonates, [OR] systemic corticosteroids [OR] or calcium channel blockers.
 - [NOT] Poor oral hygiene.
 - Active periodontal disease.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
