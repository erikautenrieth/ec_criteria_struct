<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Diagnosis of COPD confirmed by clinical examination [AND] a pulmonary function test
  - [AND] Stable clinical condition (without recent respiratory system infections [OR] any exacerbation of the disease)
  - [AND] Patients must be willing to re evaluate at the end of the study
  - [AND] Patients must be willing to give written consent for their participation in the study
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Age < 18 years old
  - [AND] Unstable cardiac disease
  - [AND] Musculoskeletal [OR] neurological diseases that could affect exercise performance [OR] preclude exercise
  - [AND] Patients of COPD final stage [OR] other serious disease final stage
  - [AND] Significant cognitive [OR] psychiatric impairment that would lead to an inability to follow simple commands in a group setting
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
