<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] > 50 years old
  - [AND] Bilateral age-related cataract
  - [AND] Qualify for bilateral implantation within 1 month
  - [AND] [NOT] previous refractive or ocular surgery
  - [AND] Available IOL dioptre range: 18 [OR] 27D
  - [AND] Expected postoperative astigmatism ≤ 0.75D

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] [NOT] Pupil > 4 mm
  - [AND] [NOT] Occupation requiring night-time driving [OR] any occupation incompatible with multifocality
  - [AND] [NOT] Acute [OR] chronic disease [OR] illness that would increase risk [OR] confound study results [e.g.] diabetes mellitus (with retinopathy) [OR] immunocompromised [OR] glaucoma [OR] epiretinal membrane [OR] cornea guttata [OR] etc.... found to be relevant by the investigators
  - [AND] [NOT] Amblyopia [OR] strabismus
  - [AND] [NOT] Extremely shallow anterior chamber
  - [AND] [NOT] Capsule [OR] zonular abnormalities that may affect postoperative centration [OR] tilt of the lens (e.g. pseudoexfoliation syndrome [OR] uveitis [OR] Marfan's syndrome)
  - [AND] [NOT] Pupil abnormalities (non-reactive [OR] tonic pupils [OR] abnormally shaped pupils [OR] pupils that do not dilate under mesopic[OR] scotopic conditions)
  - [AND] [NOT] Instability to place the intraocular lens safely at the location planned
  - [AND] [NOT] Pregnancy (pregnancy test will be taken pre-operatively in women of reproductive age)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
