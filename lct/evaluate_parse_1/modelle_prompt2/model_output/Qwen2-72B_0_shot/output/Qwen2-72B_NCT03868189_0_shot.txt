<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Patient requiring an electroneuromyogram [AND] whose indication allows the application of a standard protocol of examination.
  - [AND] Patient that has given his/her free [AND] informed consent.
  - [AND] Patient that signed the consent form.
  - [AND] Patient with an health insurance plan.
  - [AND] The patient is at least 18 years old (≥).
  - [AND] The patient is under 80 years old (≤).

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Patient with symptoms of neuropathy during the screening.
  - [AND] Patient whose Neurological severity score (NSS) score is ≥ 3.
  - [AND] Patient whose neurological examination is abnormal [AND] suggestive of neuropathy.
  - [AND] Patient presenting a pathology that could cause neuropathy (e.g. diabetes, renal failure, etc.).
  - [AND] Patient already participating in a category 1 research study (Jardé law).
  - [AND] Patient in an exclusion period determined by another study.
  - [AND] Patient under the protection of justice, under guardianship [OR] under curatorship.
  - [AND] Patient refusing to sign the consent form.
  - [AND] Patient for which it is impossible to provide information about the study.
  - [AND] Pregnant patient, parturient, [OR] breastfeeding.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
