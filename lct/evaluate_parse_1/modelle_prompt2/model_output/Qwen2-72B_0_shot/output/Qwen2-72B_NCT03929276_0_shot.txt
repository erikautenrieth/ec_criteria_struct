<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Subjects with adhesive capsulitis diagnosis by physical examination (Compared with intact side, there is more than 25% limitation in at least two directions of shoulder range of motion) [AND]
  - [AND] Subjects have complaints for at least 1 month [AND]
  - [AND] [NOT] No pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] [NOT] History of bilateral adhesive capsulitis [AND]
  - [AND] [NOT] History of shoulder trauma [OR] fracture [OR] shoulder shoulder surgery [OR] calcific tendinopathy [OR] glenohumeral osteoarthritis [OR] inflammatory rheumatic diseases [OR] tumor [OR] infection [AND]
  - [AND] [NOT] History of corticosteroid injection in the shoulder during the last 3 months [AND]
  - [AND] [NOT] History of recent lung [OR] breast [OR] bypass surgery [AND]
  - [AND] [NOT] History of cervical radiculopathy [OR] brachial plexus lesion [AND]
  - [AND] [NOT] History of neuromuscular disease [AND]
  - [AND] [NOT] History of physical therapy program for the same shoulder in the last 6 months
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
