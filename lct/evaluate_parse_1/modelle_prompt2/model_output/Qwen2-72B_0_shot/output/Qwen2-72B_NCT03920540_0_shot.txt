<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Patients diagnosed with Hunter syndrome
  - [AND] Male at the age of ≥ 5
  - [AND] Adequate abilities (including 6-MWT) to participate in this study in the opinion of the investigator
  - [AND] Voluntarily signed written informed consent to participation in this study
  - [AND] Consent to contraception

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Prior treatment with iduronate-2-sulfatase ERT
  - [AND] History of bronchotomy [OR] bone marrow trasplanation [OR] cord blood transplanation
  - [AND] Known hypersensitivity reactions to any of the components of the investigational product
  - [AND] Prior [OR] planned administration of other investigational products within 30 days before treatment with the investigational product in this study [OR] during this study
  - [AND] Unable to perform 6-MWT
  - [AND] Female
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
