Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Gastroscopy can be performed
  - [AND] Serologic markers including serum pepsinogen can be measured

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [OR] Age < 20 [OR] Age > 70 years
  - [AND] Anemia (serum hemoglobin level < 10 g/dL)
  - [AND] Severe systemic disease
  - [AND] Advanced chronic liver disease
  - [AND] Use of certain medications, [OR] proton pump inhibitors [OR] H2- receptor antagonists [OR] antibiotics
  - [AND] History of H. pylori eradication
  - [AND] History of gastric surgery
  - [AND] Recent history of upper gastrointestinal bleeding
