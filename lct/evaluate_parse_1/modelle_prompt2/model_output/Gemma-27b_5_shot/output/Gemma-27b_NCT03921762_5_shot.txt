Inclusion Criteria:
 -  > 50 years old [AND]
 -  Bilateral age-related cataract [AND]
 -  Qualify for bilateral implantation within 1 month [AND]
 -  [NOT] No previous refractive [OR] ocular surgery [AND]
 -  Available IOL dioptre range: 18 to 27D [AND]
 -  Expected postoperative astigmatism ≤ 0.75D
Exclusion Criteria:
 -  Pupil > 4 mm [OR]
 -  Occupation requiring night-time driving [OR] any occupation incompatible with multifocality [OR]
 -  Acute [OR] chronic disease [OR] illness that would increase risk [OR] confound study results [e.g. diabetes mellitus (with retinopathy), immunocompromised, glaucoma, epiretinal membrane, cornea guttata, etc….] found to be relevant by the investigators [OR]
 -  Amblyopia, strabismus [OR]
 -  Extremely shallow anterior chamber [OR]
 -  Capsule [OR] zonular abnormalities that may affect postoperative centration [OR] tilt of the lens (e.g. pseudoexfoliation syndrome, uveitis, Marfan's syndrome) [OR]
 -  Pupil abnormalities (non-reactive, tonic pupils, abnormally shaped pupils [OR] pupils that [NOT] do [NOT] dilate under mesopic/ scotopic conditions) [OR]
 -  Instability to place the intraocular lens safely at the location planned [OR]
 -  Pregnancy (pregnancy test will be taken pre-operatively in women of reproductive age)
