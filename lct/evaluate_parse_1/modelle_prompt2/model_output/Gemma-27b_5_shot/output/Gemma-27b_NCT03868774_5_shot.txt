Inclusion Criteria:
 1. [AND] Moderate-severe uni- [OR] bipolar depression according to the International Classification of Diseases (ICD-10)
 2. [AND] A Hamilton scale score (HDS-17) of ≥ 18 [AND] [OR] a sub-scale (HDSS) score of ≥ 9 within 24-48 hours before the first treatment
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. [NOT] Organic brain disease.
 2. [NOT] Epileptic seizure < 2 years
 3. [NOT] Patients with implanted metal parts in the brain [OR] chest
 4. [NOT] Patients with pacemaker
 5. [NOT] Medical diseases with cerebral impact
 6. [NOT] Serious suicidal danger
