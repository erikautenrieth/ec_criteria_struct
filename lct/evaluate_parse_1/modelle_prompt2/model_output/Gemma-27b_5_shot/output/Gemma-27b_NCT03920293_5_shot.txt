Inclusion Criteria:
 1. [AND] Diagnosed with Myasthenia Gravis at least 6 months (180 days) prior to the date of the Screening Visit [AND] as confirmed by specific criteria.
 2. [AND] Myasthenia Gravis Foundation of America Clinical Classification Class II to IV at screening.
 3. [AND] MG-ADL profile must be ≥ 6 at screening [AND] and randomization (Day 1).
 4. [AND] Vaccinated against meningococcal infections within 3 years prior to, [OR] at the time of, initiating study drug to reduce the risk of meningococcal infection (N meningitidis).
Exclusion Criteria:
Medical Conditions
  1. [AND] Any active [OR] untreated thymoma. [AND] History of thymic carcinoma [OR] thymic malignancy unless deemed cured by adequate treatment with [NOT] no evidence of recurrence for ≥ 5 years before screening.
  2. [AND] History of thymectomy within the 12 months prior to screening.
  3. [AND] History of N meningitidis infection.
  4. [AND] Use of the following within the time period specified below:
       -  IV immunoglobulin within 4 weeks of randomization
       -  Use of plasma exchange within 4 weeks of randomization
       -  Use of rituximab within 6 months of screening
  5. [AND] Participants who have received previous treatment with complement inhibitors (for example, eculizumab).
