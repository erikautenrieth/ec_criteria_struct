Inclusion Criteria:
 - [AND] The patient is currently being treated at Queen Elizabeth Central Hospital [OR] Kamuzu Central Hospital in the neonatal ward.
 - [AND] The patient is less than 28 days old (neonate).
 - [AND] The patient is deemed to be at risk for jaundice [OR] the patient is undergoing blue light phototherapy for treatment of jaundice.
 - [AND] The patient's parent [OR] guardian has provided informed consent for their child to participate.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] Parent [OR] guardian is unable [OR] unwilling to provide informed consent.
 - [NOT] The patient is unable to receive a blood draw/[OR] heel stick as determined by their clinician.
