Inclusion Criteria:
 - Boys [AND] girls from 7 to 12 years old [AND] with class II skeletal classification attending a consultation in the dental clinic of the UNICIEO foundation, [AND] to be fitted with myofunctional apparatus type Sn1
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who have had previous orthopedic treatment
 - Patients with signs of condyle injuries
 - Patients of Pierre Robin [OR] or any form of cleft
 - Patients with syndromes that associate craniofacial anomalies [AND] muscle alterations.
