Inclusion Criteria:
 1. age > 18 years [AND] confirmed diagnosis of prediabetes [OR] T2D (documented A1c of ≥ 5.7%, 2-hour post-load glucose of >140, [OR] FBG of >100) [AND] a family member [OR] peer willing to participate [AND] attend all sessions as a social influencer [AND] proficiency in English of at least one member of each dyad [AND] willingness to provide written consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. type 1 diabetes [OR] diabetes secondary to other conditions (e.g. steroid-induced, pancreatic insufficiency, [OR] chemotherapy-induced) [OR] malignancy [OR] life-threatening illness with life expectancy of <5 years [OR] end-stage disease [OR] serious illness that prohibits participation (e.g. end-stage renal disease [OR] class IV congestive heart failure) [OR] inability to perform unsupervised physical activity [OR] pregnancy [OR] diagnosed cognitive deficits [OR] limited decision-making capacity [OR] alcohol [OR] substance abuse [OR] homelessness [OR] no fixed address.
