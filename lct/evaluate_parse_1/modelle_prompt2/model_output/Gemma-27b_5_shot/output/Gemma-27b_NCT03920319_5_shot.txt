Inclusion Criteria:
 -  [AND] Treatment-seeking cigarette smoker
 -  [AND] Meet DSM-IV criteria for nicotine dependence
 -  [AND] Negative result on urine test for drug use during screening
Exclusion Criteria:
 -  [AND] History of any Axis I psychiatric diagnosis [OR] other than nicotine dependence
 -  [AND] Medical conditions that might affect brain function
 -  [AND] Current use of medications that could alter brain function
 -  [AND] Pregnancy
 -  [AND] Current illicit drug use [OR] other than occasional use of marijuana
