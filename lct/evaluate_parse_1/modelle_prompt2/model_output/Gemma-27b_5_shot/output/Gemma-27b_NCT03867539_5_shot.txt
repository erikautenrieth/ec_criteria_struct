Inclusion Criteria:
 -  carpal tunnel syndrome diagnosed [OR] either by physical exam [OR] EMG
 -  age >18.
Exclusion Criteria:
 -  allergy [AND] to bupivacaine
 -  pre-operative opioid usage within the last six months
 -  patients allergic [AND] to Percocet [OR] acetaminophen
 -  patients taking tricyclic antidepressants [AND] (interaction [AND] with bupivacaine)
 -  vulnerable populations [AND] (prisoners [OR] pregnant women)
