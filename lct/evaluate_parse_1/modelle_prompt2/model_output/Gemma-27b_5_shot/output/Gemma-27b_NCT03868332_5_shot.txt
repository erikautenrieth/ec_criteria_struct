Inclusion Criteria:
Patients who are diagnosed [AND] as ulcerative colitis [OR] Crohn's disease however in activity [OR] in remission attending the the outpatient clinics [AND] inpatient of gastroenterology units [AND] of Internal medicine department
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Previous history of hypertension [OR] diabetes mellitus [OR] Dyslipidemia
 - Previous history of cardiovascular [OR] cerebrovascular events
 - Autoimmune [OR] Neoplastic diseases
 - Thromboembolic diseases
