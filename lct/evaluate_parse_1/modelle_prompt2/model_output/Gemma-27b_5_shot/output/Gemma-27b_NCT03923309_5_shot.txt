Inclusion Criteria:
 -  biopsy proven rectal cancer (anal verge ≤ 10cm)
 -  neoadjuvant radiotherapy [OR] chemoradiotherapy
 -  [NOT] radical surgery after neoadjuvant therapy
Exclusion Criteria:
 -  other malignant disease
 -  other [NOT] fatal disease
 -  incompletion of half [OR] radiotherapy course
 -  metastasis on initial presentation
