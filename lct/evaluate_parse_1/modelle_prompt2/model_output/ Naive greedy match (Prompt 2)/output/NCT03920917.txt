Inclusion Criteria:
  -  1. Patient [AND] with paroxysmal atrial fibrillation [AND] who is scheduled for ablation procedure [AND] and ≥20 [AND] and ≤80 years of age
  -  2. Left atrium size < 45mm
  -  3. paroxysmal atrial fibrillation [AND] that is recurrence during antiarrhythmic drug treatment or is [NOT] not able to use an antiarrhythmic drug.
  -  4. Patient [AND] who is indicated for anticoagulation therapy (for prevention of cerebral infarction)
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  1. Patients [AND] with persistent or permanent atrial fibrillation
  -  2. Atrial fibrillation associated [AND] with severe cardiac malformation or a structural heart disease [AND] that is hemodynamically affected
  -  3. Patients [AND] with severe renal impairment or CT imaging difficulty using contrast media
  -  4. Patients [AND] with a past history of radiofrequency ablation for atrial fibrillation or other cardiac surgery
  -  5. Patients [AND] with active internal bleeding
  -  6. Patients [AND] with contraindications for anticoagulation therapy(for prevention of cerebral infarction) [AND] and antiarrhythmic drugs
  -  7. Patients [AND] with valvular atrial fibrillation (mitral stenosis >grade 2, [OR] mechanical valve, [OR] mitral valvuloplasty)
  -  8. Patients [AND] with a severe comorbid disease
  -  9. Expected survival < 1 year
  -  10. Drug addicts or alcoholics
  -  11. Patients [AND] who cannot read the consent form (illiterates, [OR] foreigners, [OR] etc.)
  -  12. Other patients [AND] who are judged by the principal or sub-investigator to be ineligible for participation in this clinical study
