Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Admitted from the emergency department to inpatient or observation status at one of:
     Carolinas Medical Center, [OR] Carolinas Medical Center - Mercy, [OR]  or Atrium Health Northeast;
  2. ≥18 years of age upon admission;
  3. oral/ [OR] parenteral antibiotic or bacterial culture order within 24 hours of emergency department presentation [AND] and
       1. culture drawn first, [OR] antibiotics ordered within 48 hours or
       2. antibiotics ordered first, [OR] culture ordered within 48 hours (adapted from criteria applied in development of the Third International Consensus Definitions for Sepsis [AND] and Septic Shock)
  4. deemed as high-risk for 30-day readmission (i.e., [OR] ≥ 20%) or 30-day mortality (i.e., [OR] ≥ 10%) using risk-scoring models
  5. [NOT] not discharged at the time of patient list generation
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. prior randomization to either STAR or usual care study arms;
  2. [NOT] not a North Carolina resident or residence >2.5-hour drive time from treating hospital;
  3. the only antibiotic associated [AND] with patient is administered in the operating room as this likely represents pre-operative infection prophylaxis [AND] and [NOT] not presumed infection;
  4. patients transferred from other acute care hospitals;
  5. patients [AND] with a change in code status (i.e., [OR] do [NOT] not resuscitate, [OR] do [NOT] not intubate) within 24 hours after admission due to the general assumption of increased risk of exposure to less aggressive treatment;
  6. patients [AND] with infection ruled out during the index hospitalization.
