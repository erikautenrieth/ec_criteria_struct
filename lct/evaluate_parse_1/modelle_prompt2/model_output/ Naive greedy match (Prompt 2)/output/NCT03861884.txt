Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Ability to give informed consent.
  2. Either pathological, [OR] genetic or imaging biomarker evidence to suggest Fronto-temporal Lobar Degeneration, [OR] Alzheimer's Disease or Vascular Cognitive Impairment, [OR]  or defined clinical diagnoses.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Lack of mental capacity to consent to study involvement.
  2. Not speaking English before age 5 years.
  3. Learning disability.
  4. MRI contraindications
