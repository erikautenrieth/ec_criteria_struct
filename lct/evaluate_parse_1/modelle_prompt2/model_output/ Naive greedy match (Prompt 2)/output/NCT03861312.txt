Inclusion Criteria:
  -  The inclusion criteria were as follows: a) men [AND] and women aged between 18- [AND] and 65-years b) medical diagnosis of Non-Specific Chronic Neck Pain [AND] with more than 6 months of evolution of neck pain.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Exclusion criteria included the following: a) patients [AND] with rheumatic diseases, [OR] cervical hernia, [OR] cervical whiplash syndrome, [OR] neck surgeries or history of arthrodesis, [OR] b) systemic diseases, [OR] c) vision, [OR] hearing or vestibular problems, [OR] d) Severe trauma or traffic accident which has had an impact on the cervical area.
