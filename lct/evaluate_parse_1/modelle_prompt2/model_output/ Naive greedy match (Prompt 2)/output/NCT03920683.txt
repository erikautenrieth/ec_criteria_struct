Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age ≥ 18 ans
  -  Diabetes mellitus (type 1 [OR] or 2)
  -  Patients addressed to a one-day hospitalization to assess cardiovascular comorbidities, [OR] between January 2014 [AND] and May 2017, [OR] in the diabetes department, [OR] in the Pitié-Salpêtrière hospital
  -  Coronary artery calcium score available
  -  Toe-brachial index available
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History of coronary artery disease
  -  History of Raynaud's syndrome
  -  Opposition of the patient
