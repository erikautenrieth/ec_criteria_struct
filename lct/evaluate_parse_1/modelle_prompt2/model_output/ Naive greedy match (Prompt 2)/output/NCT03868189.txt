Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient requiring an electroneuromyogram whose indication allows the application of a standard protocol of examination.
  -  Patient [AND] that has given his/ [OR] her [NOT] free [AND] and informed consent.
  -  Patient [AND] that signed the consent form.
  -  Patient [AND] with an health insurance plan.
  -  The patient is at least 18 years old (≥).
  -  he patient is under 80 years old (≤).
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient [AND] with symptoms of neuropathy during the screening.
  -  Patient whose Neurological severity score (NSS) score is ≥ 3.
  -  Patient whose neurological examination is abnormal [AND] and suggestive of neuropathy.
  -  Patient presenting a pathology [AND] that could cause neuropathy (e.g. diabetes, [OR] renal failure, [OR] etc.).
  -  Patient already participating in a category 1 research study (Jardé law).
  -  Patient in an exclusion period determined by another study.
  -  Patient under the protection of justice, [OR] under guardianship or under curatorship.
  -  Patient refusing to sign the consent form.
  -  Patient for which it is [NOT] impossible to provide information about the study.
  -  Pregnant patient, [OR] parturient, [OR]  or breastfeeding.
