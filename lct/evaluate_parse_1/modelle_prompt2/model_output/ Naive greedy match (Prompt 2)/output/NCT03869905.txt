Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Ulcerative colitis [AND] with confirmed diagnosis by histology [AND] and endoscopy AND in stable remission for 3 months or more [NOT] without therapy or [AND] with a maintenance therapy (except steroids [AND] and antibiotics for 3 months)
  -  A negative pregnancy test
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  No history or diagnosis of any of the following conditions: Chron's disease, [OR] bleeding disorders, [OR] gastrointestinal or colonic malignancy, [OR] Kidney disease, [OR] including kidney "stones" or hypercalcemia, [OR] Coagulopathy/ [OR] hereditary hemorrhagic disorders/ [OR]  or receiving therapeutic doses of Coumadin or heparin.
  -  Ingested certain medications (Calcium, [OR] Vitamin D, [OR] fiber supplements [AND] and Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs - NSAIDs) within 30 days of study start.
