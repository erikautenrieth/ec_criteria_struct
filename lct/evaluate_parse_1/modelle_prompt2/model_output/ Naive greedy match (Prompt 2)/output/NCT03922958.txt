Inclusion Criteria:
  -  All adults undergoing cardiac surgery [AND] with CPB will be considered for enrollment.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Factors [AND] that affect immune function or AKI assessment are the basis for exclusion criteria:
       -  Concurrent disease associated [AND] with immunosuppression including malignancy, [OR] chronic infection (e.g., [OR] HIV, [OR] Hepatitis C), [OR] organ transplant [AND] and immunosuppressant medications
       -  Documented acute infection [AND] with the past 1 month (e.g., [OR] pneumonia, [OR] urinary tract infection)
       -  Prednisone or other steroid use currently or within the past one month
       -  AKI at the time of surgery
       -  ESRD requiring renal replacement therapy
       -  Estimated GFR <45 mL/ [OR] min (as judged by the Chronic Kidney Disease Epidemiology Collaboration (CKD-EPI) equation
