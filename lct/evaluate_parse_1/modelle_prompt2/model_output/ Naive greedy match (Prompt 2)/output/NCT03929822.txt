Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Women called back from a screening mammography by either FFDM or tomosynthesis [AND] with soft tissue abnormalities including masses, [OR] asymmetries, [OR] focal asymmetries or architectural distortion [AND] with or [NOT] without calcifications. Patients will be questioned regarding the possibility of pregnancy [AND] and will need a negative pregnancy test prior the study intervention.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Age <30 years old
  -  Screening mammography [AND] with only calcifications abnormalities
  -  Male patients
  -  Pregnant or lactating patients
  -  Patients [AND] with any allergy to iodinated contrast
  -  Patients [AND] with eGFR < 45
  -  Patients [AND] that may be treated [AND] with radioactive iodine
