Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Infants of healthy mothers [AND] with uncomplicated pregnancies at term, [OR] mothers of any ethnic or social background [AND] that can speak, [OR] read, [OR] [AND] and understand Swedish to the extent [AND] that they can consent in Swedish. An additional inclusion criterion for mothers in the CS groups is vaginal pH ≤ 4 at the time of birth.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Infants of mothers [AND] with pre-eclampsia or complicated pregnancies, [OR] mothers <18 years or >40 years of age, [OR] genital herpes simplex, [OR] genital warts, [OR] HIV, [OR] Hepatitis B or complications during delivery, [OR] infants delivered prior to 37 weeks of gestation. Additional exclusion criteria for mothers in the CS group: Mothers positive to Group B Streptococcus, [OR] Group A Streptococcus, [OR] bacterial vaginosis, [OR] vaginal pH > 4 at the time of birth. Mothers [AND] with fecal samples positive for Salmonella, [OR] Shigella, [OR] Campylobacter or Yersinia.
