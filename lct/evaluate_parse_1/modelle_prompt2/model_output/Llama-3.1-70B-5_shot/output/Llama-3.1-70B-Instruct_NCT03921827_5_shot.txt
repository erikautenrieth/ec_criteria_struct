Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients [AND] with recent stroke/TIA [AND] with severe stenosis of intracranial ICA [OR] or MCA [AND] with impaired CVR within previous three months [AND] but [NOT] before 3 weeks after acute stroke.
 2. Age >21 years
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients [AND] with atrial fibrillation [OR] arrhythmias.
 2. Within 2 weeks of cardiac catheterization [OR] or arterial puncture at femoral puncture site.
 3. Decompensated heart failure, usually class 3 [OR] or 4
 4. LV EF <30%
 5. Moderate [OR] or severe AR
 6. Persistent [AND] uncontrolled hypertension (BP persistently >160/100 mmHg)
 7. Bleeding diathesis
 8. Active thrombophlebitis [OR] venous disease of lower limbs
 9. Severe lower extremity vaso-occlusive disease
 10. Presence of a documented aortic aneurysm [OR] dissection requiring surgical repair
 11. Pregnancy
