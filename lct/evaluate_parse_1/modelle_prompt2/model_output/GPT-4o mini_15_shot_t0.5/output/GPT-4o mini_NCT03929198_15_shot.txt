<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Dysfunctional lipids, blood pressure [AND] and glycemic control determined at a screening visit:
       - Triglycerides > 150 mg/dL [OR] HDL <50 in females, <40 mg/dL in males
       - Systolic blood pressure ≥130 mmHg [OR] diastolic blood pressure ≥80 mmHg (or on hypertension medication)
       - HbA1c ≥5.7% [OR] or higher
 - [NOT] no diagnosis or treatment for cancer in the past year
 - [NOT] no diagnosis of mild cognitive impairment [OR] or dementia in the past year
 - Ability to perform daily exercise, including aerobic activity [AND] and resistance exercise
 - [NOT] non-smoker in the past year
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Positive exercise stress test
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
