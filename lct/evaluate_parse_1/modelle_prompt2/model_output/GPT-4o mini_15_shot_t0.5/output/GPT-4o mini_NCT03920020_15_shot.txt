Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of primary knee osteoarthritis according to American College of Radiology
 - Being radiographically K-L grade 2-3
 - Presence of symptoms for at least 3 months
 - Patients [AND] who have been planned a conventional physiotherapy programme because of knee osteoarthritis
 - Presence of functional capacity for outpatient physical therapy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [NOT] with seconder knee osteoarthritis
 - History of lower extremity [OR] or spine surgery
 - History of intraarticular injection in the last 1 month
 - Patients [NOT] with neurological disease [THAT] will affect muscle strength, balance [AND] and coordination
