<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Female subjects between 18 [AND] and 65 years of age currently taking spironolactone for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris 
[OR] 
Female subjects between 18 [AND] and 65 years of age currently taking oral antibiotics for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris 
[AND] Subjects able to complete an online survey in English

Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects less than 18 years of age [OR] or older than 65 years of age 
[OR] Subjects that have ever been diagnosed with any mood disorder by a clinician prior to starting spironolactone (history of mood disorder [OR] or current mood disorder) 
[OR] Subjects taking hormonal contraception (including but not limited to oral contraceptive pills) for a duration of less than 6 months 
[OR] Subjects that have ever been diagnosed with any type of sexual dysfunction prior to starting spironolactone 
[OR] Subjects [NOT] able to complete an online survey in English 
[OR] Subjects taking oral isotretinoin for a diagnosis of acne 
[OR] Subjects that are currently pregnant
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
