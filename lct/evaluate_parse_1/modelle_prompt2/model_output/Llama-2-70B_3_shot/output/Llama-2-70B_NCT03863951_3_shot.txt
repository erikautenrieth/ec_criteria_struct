  Inclusion Criteria:

* Diagnosed with first ever acute ischemic stroke occurring within 2 weeks and confirmed by CT or MRI [AND]
* Has no history of depression or use of antidepressants or antipsychotics [AND]
* Were competent to consent to take part in this research [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:

* Significant acute or severe illness such as infection, heart failure, or tumor [OR]
* Previous diagnosis of depression or other mental disorder or who had recently accepted antidepressants or antipsychotics [OR]
* Antibiotics in the last two weeks [OR]
* Severe aphasia or dysarthria, irritable bowel syndrome, asthma, or pregnant or lactating women [OR]
* A previous history of neurological illness including AD and PD [OR]
* Inflammatory bowel disease [OR]
