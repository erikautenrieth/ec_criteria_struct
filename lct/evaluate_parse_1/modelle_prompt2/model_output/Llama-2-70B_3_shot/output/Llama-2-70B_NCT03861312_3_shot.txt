  Inclusion Criteria:
 - Men [OR] women aged between 18- [AND] 65-years
 - Medical diagnosis of Non-Specific Chronic Neck Pain with more than 6 months of evolution of neck pain
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [NOT] with rheumatic diseases, cervical hernia, cervical whiplash syndrome, neck surgeries [OR] history of arthrodesis
 - Systemic diseases
 - Vision, hearing [OR] vestibular problems
 - Severe trauma [OR] traffic accident which has had an impact on the cervical area
