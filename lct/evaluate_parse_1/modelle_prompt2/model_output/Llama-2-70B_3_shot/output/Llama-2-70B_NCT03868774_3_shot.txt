  Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Moderate-severe uni- [OR] bipolar depression according to the International Classification of Diseases (ICD-10)
  2. A Hamilton scale score (HDS-17) of ≥ 18 [OR] a sub-scale (HDSS) score of ≥ 9 within 24-48 hours before the first treatment
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Organic brain disease [NOT].
  2. Epileptic seizure < 2 years [NOT].
  3. Patients with implanted metal parts in the brain [OR] chest [NOT].
  4. Patients with pacemaker [NOT].
  5. Medical diseases with cerebral impact [NOT].
  6. Serious suicidal danger [NOT].
