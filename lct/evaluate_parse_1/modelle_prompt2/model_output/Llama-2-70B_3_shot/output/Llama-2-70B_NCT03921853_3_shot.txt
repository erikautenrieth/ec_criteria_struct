  Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Older than 18 [AND] younger than 65 years of age.
  -  With risk factors to Metabolic syndrome [OR] With obesity [OR] morbid obesity condition.
  -  Medical authorization by a physician.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Physical limitations (e.g., restricting injuries of the musculoskeletal system, or dependent of a third person) [NOT].
  -  Exercise-related dyspnoea [OR] respiratory alterations.
  -  Chronic heart disease [WITH] any worsening in the last month.
  -  Adherence rate of less than 80% [NOT] of the total interventions.
