  Inclusion Criteria:

* Participants must be adults 18 years or older [AND] have the capacity and ability to give informed consent.
* Participants have been given an assessment [AND] received a diagnosis of OCD.
* Participants must speak English [AND] be able to physically attend treatment.

Exclusion Criteria:

* Prisoners [OR] any other high-risk population group [NOT].
* Experiencing active psychosis [OR] active substance abuse disorder [OR] active suicidality [NOT].
