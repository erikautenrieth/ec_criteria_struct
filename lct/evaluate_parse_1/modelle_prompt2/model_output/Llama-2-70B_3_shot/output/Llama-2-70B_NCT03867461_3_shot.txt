  Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adults determined to be candidates for venous sinus stenting [AND] who meet the following criteria:
 - Patients with a clinical diagnosis of idiopathic intracranial hypertension (IIH) [OR] pseudotumor cerebri [OR] ocular motor dysfunction [OR] unexplained headaches [OR] other conditions affecting the venous sinuses.
 - Patients with radiological evidence of venous sinus stenosis or occlusion.
 - Patients with symptoms refractory to medical management, including headache, nausea, vomiting, dizziness, and visual disturbances.
 - Patients willing and able to provide informed consent and comply with study protocol.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with a history of bleeding diathesis or current use of anticoagulant therapy.
 - Patients with a history of previous cranial surgery or significant intracranial trauma.
 - Patients with a history of malignancy or immunological disorders.
 - Patients with a history of significant cardiovascular or pulmonary disease.
 - Patients with a history of significant renal or hepatic dysfunction.
 - Patients who are pregnant or breastfeeding.
 - Patients with a history of drug or alcohol abuse.
 - Patients who are unable to undergo MRI or other imaging studies.
 - Patients who are unable to provide informed consent or comply with study protocol.
