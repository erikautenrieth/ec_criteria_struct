  Inclusion Criteria:

* Diagnosed with sciatica by GP [AND] NRS leg pain of 6 or more on a 10-point NRS scale [AND] Minimum duration of symptoms of 3 weeks [AND] maximum duration of 8 weeks

Exclusion Criteria:

* Age under 18 years [OR] Condition preventing to receive transforaminal epidural injection [OR] Severe scoliosis [OR] Transforaminal epidural injection received in 6 months before randomization date [OR] Surgery for sciatica at the same level [OR] Surgery for sciatica at another level within one year before inclusion [OR] Pregnancy
