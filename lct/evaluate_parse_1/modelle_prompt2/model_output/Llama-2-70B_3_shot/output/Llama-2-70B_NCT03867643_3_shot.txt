  Inclusion Criteria:

* Children who are 6-12 years old, in Chongqing [AND] have received three injections of Hep B vaccine in time according to the procedure of 0, 1, and 6 months (with a vaccination certificate record)
* No HBV booster vaccine has been given since primary HBV immunization
* Children or guardian are aware and agree to accept the study [AND] are willing to participate in the follow-up study and blood collection after vaccination

Exclusion Criteria:

* Unwilling or unable to participate in the research project
* Have a history of allergy or a serious vaccine reaction
* Have immune impairment diseases or exhibit immune impairment
* Are undergoing immunosuppressive treatment, receiving any injection or oral administration of cortisone or cancer chemotherapy
* Have received any vaccine or observation drug in the past four weeks
* Have had any acute disease or other infection requiring antibiotic or antiviral treatment in the past four weeks
* Have had fever symptoms in the past week (axillary temperature ≥ 38 ℃)
* Have a history of blood transfusion
* Have a history of serious infectious diseases (such as hepatitis, AIDS, syphilis, gonorrhea, etc.)
* Are hepatitis B infected or carriers (with a lineal relationship)
* Have abnormal physical examination results
