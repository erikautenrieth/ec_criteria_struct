  Inclusion Criteria:
 - Participants will be male and female subjects who suffered total acute Achilles tendon rupture [AND] underwent surgical repair. In addition, to participate in this study all volunteers [AND] must present medical [OR] physiotherapeutic release for physical/sports activities practice.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Volunteers who did not have Achilles tendon surgical reconstruction [OR] did not present medical [OR] physiotherapeutic release for physical/sports activities [OR] have participated in strength training program for the plantar flexors in the last 6 months [OR] patients with diabetic diseases [OR] those with difficulty for understanding [OR] executing the test [OR] training protocols in the isokinetic dynamometer will be excluded.
