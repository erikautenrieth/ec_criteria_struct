  Inclusion Criteria:
  - All patients [OR] who are assigned to a clinically indicated chest X-ray by the emergency department of Vienna General Hospital [AND] ability to provide informed consent [AND] informed consent after detailed patient briefing
Exclusion Criteria:
  - The emergency department's assessment of a critical clinical condition that opposes an examination with both modalities (ULDCT of the chest, chest X-ray)
  - Assigned to chest X-ray as follow-up
  - Women [AND] with positive ß-HCG-test
