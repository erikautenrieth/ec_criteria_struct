  Inclusion Criteria:

* Burn patients [AND] severe neuropathic pain rated at least 5 on the 10-point numerical rating scale (NRS)

Exclusion Criteria:

* Cardiac arrest history [OR] history of neurological disease or brain surgery
* Unstable heart disease [OR] presence of a cardiac pacemaker
* Pain resulting from other causes like neuromuscular diseases
* Psychiatric disorder
* Diabetes mellitus
* Abnormal renal function
* Contraindication for MRI [OR] pregnancy
