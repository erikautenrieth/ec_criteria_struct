Resident Inclusion Criteria:

* Identified as having Alzheimer's or related dementia [AND]
* Mild to moderate cognitive impairment (BIMS = 8-15, (MDS C0500) (nursing home residents only) [OR]
* Makes Self Understood = <1 (MDS B0700)/present in medical record [OR]
* Understood By Others = <1 (MDS B0800)/present in medical record [OR]
* Reside at the facility at least 2 weeks identified as a long-term resident [AND]
* Age >65 years [AND]
* Speaks English

Family Inclusion Criteria:

* Speaks English [AND]
* Willing and able to watch the videos [OR]
* Willing to complete short surveys [OR]

Staff Inclusion Criteria:

* Speaks English [AND]
* Willing to watch the videos [OR]
* Willing to complete short surveys

Resident Exclusion Criteria:

* Limited communication capacity (e.g. aphasia) [OR]
* Comatose [OR]
* Cognitively intact [NOT]
* Severe cognitive impairment [NOT]
