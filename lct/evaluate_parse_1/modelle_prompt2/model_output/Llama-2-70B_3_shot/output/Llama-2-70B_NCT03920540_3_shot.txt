  Inclusion Criteria:

* Patients diagnosed with Hunter syndrome
* Male [AND] at the age of ≥ 5
* Adequate abilities (including 6-MWT) [AND] to participate in this study in the opinion of the investigator
* Voluntarily signed written informed consent [AND] to participation in this study
* Consent [AND] to contraception

Exclusion Criteria:

* Prior treatment [OR] iduronate-2-sulfatase ERT
* History [OR] bronchotomy, bone marrow transplantation, [OR] cord blood transplantation
* Known hypersensitivity reactions [OR] to any of the components of the investigational product
* Prior [OR] planned administration [OR] other investigational products within 30 days before treatment [OR] the investigational product in this study [OR] during this study
* Unable [NOT] to perform 6-MWT
* Female [NOT]
