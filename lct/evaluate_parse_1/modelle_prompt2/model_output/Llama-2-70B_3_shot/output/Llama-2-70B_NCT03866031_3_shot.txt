  Inclusion Criteria:

1. The patient should be completely edentulous for at least 6 months [AND] have a first-class jaw relation.
2. Adequate bone for an implant length of at least 10 mm [OR] diameter of at least 2.75 mm.
3. The patient should be able to speak [AND] write in Arabic.

Exclusion Criteria:

1. Patients who received radiotherapy to the head or neck region for tumors [OR] have a history of chemotherapy.
2. Patients on long-term steroids [OR] bisphosphonates.
3. Heavy smokers [OR] patients with physical and mental disabilities that interfere with the maintenance of implants.
4. Uncontrolled systematic disease that could compromise implant surgery [OR] extreme bruxism.
5. Infectious disease [OR] temporomandibular joint dysfunction.
