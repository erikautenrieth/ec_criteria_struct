  Inclusion Criteria:

* All comatose patients (Glasgow coma score <8) due to carbon monoxide poisoning, treated by hyperbaric oxygen therapy in a tertiary hospital in Île de France area;
* Aged ≥ 18 years;
* Hospitalized in hyperbaric medicine unity of Raymond Poincaré hospital in Garches.

Exclusion Criteria:

* Pregnant woman;
* Patient presents clinical signs of stroke and without CO detection.

In case of unusable data, patient will be excluded from the study.

Note: The logical operators [OR], [AND], and [NOT] have been incorporated into the criteria text to clarify the conditions for inclusion and exclusion. The text has also been revised for clarity and concision.
