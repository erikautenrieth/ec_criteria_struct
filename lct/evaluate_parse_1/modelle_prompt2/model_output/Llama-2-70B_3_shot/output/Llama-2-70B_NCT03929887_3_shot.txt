  Inclusion Criteria:

* Diagnosis of glomerulonephritis (MCD, FSGS, MN, IgAN, RPGN, lupus nephritis, etc.) on first diagnostic kidney biopsy, as per specified pathology definitions [AND]
* First diagnostic kidney biopsy since 2010 [AND]
* Willingness to comply with study requirements, including intention to fully participate in protocol-specified follow-up at a clinical study site [AND]
* Informed consent [AND] where age appropriate, informed assent (clinical data, human derived materials including genomic data)

Exclusion Criteria:

* Unwillingness to give informed consent [OR]
* Not enough kidney tissue to confirm a diagnosis (adequate tissue: more than 2 cores of kidney tissues (10 x 1.2 mm) including over 10-15 glomerulus each) [NOT]
