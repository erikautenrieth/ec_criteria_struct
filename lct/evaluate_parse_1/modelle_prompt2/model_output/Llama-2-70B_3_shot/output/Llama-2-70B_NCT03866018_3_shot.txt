  Inclusion Criteria:

* Male or Female ≥ 60 years old [AND]
* MMSE score between 4 and 26 / 30 [AND]
* Diagnosis of minor to major NeuroCognitive Disorder of the Alzheimer type or multiple etiology [AND]
* Subject without a major hearing or visual impairment [AND]
* Subject not presenting any contraindications to the practice of adapted physical activity [AND]
* Subject affiliated to Social Security System [AND]
* Signing informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:

* < 60 years [NOT]
* Medical indications: Recent or unstable cardiovascular events: ECG changes, unstable angina, uncontrolled arrhythmia, 3rd degree BAV, acute heart failure [NOT]
* MMSE score < 4 [NOT]
* Subject already engaged in appropriate physical activity [NOT]
* Subject with a perceptual disorder (AMD, deafness...) [NOT]
* Subject with a motor disability [NOT]
* Subject under guardianship, curatorship or safeguard of justice [NOT]
* Persons deprived of their liberty (administrative or judicial) [NOT]
