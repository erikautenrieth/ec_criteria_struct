  Inclusion Criteria:

* Aged 18 and above [AND]
* Having the ability to learn the standard toothbrushing method with artificial intelligence powered toothbrush [AND]
* Ability and willingness to give written informed consent [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:

* Edentulous mouth [NOT]
* Pregnant females [NOT]
* Having received professional periodontal treatment (other than supragingival cleaning) within the previous 12 months [NOT]
* Having received antibiotic medication within the previous 3 months [NOT]
