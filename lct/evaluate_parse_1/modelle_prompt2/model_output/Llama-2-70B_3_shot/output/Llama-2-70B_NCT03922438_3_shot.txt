  Inclusion Criteria:

* Non-syndromic patients [AND]
* Medically fit for surgery [AND]
* Patient with primary, complete cleft lip [AND]
* Patient's age younger than six months [NOT]

Exclusion Criteria:

* Patient with syndromic cleft lip [NOT]
* Previous operated cases [NOT]
* Incomplete cleft lip [NOT]
* Patient older than six months [NOT]
* Patients with any systemic condition [NOT]
