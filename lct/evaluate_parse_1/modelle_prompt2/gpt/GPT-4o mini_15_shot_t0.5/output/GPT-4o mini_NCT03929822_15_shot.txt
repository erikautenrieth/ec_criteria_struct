<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Women called back from a screening mammography by either FFDM [OR] or tomosynthesis [AND] with soft tissue abnormalities including masses, asymmetries, focal asymmetries [OR] or architectural distortion [AND] with [OR] without calcifications. Patients will be questioned regarding the possibility of pregnancy [AND] will need a negative pregnancy test prior to the study intervention.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Age <30 years old
 - Screening mammography with only calcifications abnormalities
 - Male patients
 - Pregnant [OR] or lactating patients
 - Patients with any allergy to iodinated contrast
 - Patients with eGFR < 45
 - Patients [NOT] that may be treated with radioactive iodine
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
