<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Gastroscopy can be performed
  -  Serologic markers including serum pepsinogen can be measured

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Age < 20 [OR] > 70 years
  -  Anemia (serum hemoglobin level < 10 g/dL)
  -  Severe systemic disease
  -  Advanced chronic liver disease
  -  Use of certain medications, [OR] including proton pump inhibitors, [OR] H2- receptor antagonists, [OR] antibiotics
  -  History [OR] of H. pylori eradication
  -  History [OR] of gastric surgery
  -  Recent history [OR] of upper gastrointestinal bleeding
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
