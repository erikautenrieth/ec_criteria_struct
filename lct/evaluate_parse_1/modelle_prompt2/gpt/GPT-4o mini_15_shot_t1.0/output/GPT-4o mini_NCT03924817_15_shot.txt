<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Prescription of a Narval CC mandibular advancement device
 - Age ≥ 18 years
 - Ability to understand the study information [AND] and information on usage of personal data
 - Signed [AND] and dated informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with central sleep apnea (central AI > 5/hour)
 - Patients with loose teeth [AND] and severe parodontitis
 - Patients with completely missing teeth [OR] or removable tooth replacement, [OR] or with a teeth health insufficient to retain an MAD [AND] Patients with missing molars [OR] Patients with maximum protrusion of less than 5mm
