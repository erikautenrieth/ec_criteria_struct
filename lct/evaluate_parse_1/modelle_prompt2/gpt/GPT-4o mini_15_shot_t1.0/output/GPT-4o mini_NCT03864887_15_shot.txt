<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who have had brain death [OR] or serious brain trauma.
 - Healthy people [AND] who are interested in optical assessment of hemodynamic parameters.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who are in pregnancy [OR] or have plan to conception.
 - Patients who have vertebra surgery [OR] or have plan of surgery.
 - Patients who are inappropriate to join this trial judged by the radiologists [OR] or specialists.
   - AIDS, Active Hepatitis, Tuberculosis, Syphilis
   - Patients who regularly take anticoagulants, antiplatelet drugs.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
