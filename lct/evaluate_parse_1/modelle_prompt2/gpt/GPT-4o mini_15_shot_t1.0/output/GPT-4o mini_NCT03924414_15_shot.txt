 - Men [AND] and women age 65 years or older
 - Current PD diagnosis [AND] with symptoms severity at Hoehn & Yahr (H&Y) stage 1-4 based on an expert assessment (movement disorders neurologist report [OR] or telemedicine evaluation)
 - Willing [AND] and able to continue in follow-up for at least 2 years
 - Willing [AND] and able to provide informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - History of hip fracture
 - [NOT] Any use of a bisphosphonate drug within the last 12 months
 - [NOT] Use of any other osteoporosis treatment (such as SERMs [OR] and denosumab) within the last 6 months
 - Tooth extraction [OR] or invasive dental procedures within the past 30 days [OR] or planned/scheduled extraction/procedure in the next 12 months
 - Non-ambulatory, i.e., [NOT] unable to walk [NOT] without assistance of another person.
 - Undergoing kidney dialysis
 - A diagnosis of multiple myeloma [OR] or Paget's disease
 - [NOT] Unable to speak [OR] or read English sufficiently to complete informed consent
 - Any other criteria, which would make the patient unsuitable to participate in this study as determined by the study staff (e.g., an uncontrolled drug [AND] and/or alcohol addiction)
