Inclusion Criteria:
 - Infants of healthy mothers [AND] with uncomplicated pregnancies at term, mothers of any ethnic [OR] or social background [AND] that can speak, read, [AND] and understand Swedish to the extent that they can consent in Swedish. An additional inclusion criterion for mothers in the CS groups is vaginal pH ≤ 4 at the time of birth.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Infants of mothers with pre-eclampsia [OR] or complicated pregnancies, mothers <18 years [OR] or >40 years of age, genital herpes simplex, genital warts, HIV, Hepatitis B [OR] or complications during delivery, infants delivered prior to 37 weeks of gestation. Additional exclusion criteria for mothers in the CS group: Mothers positive to Group B Streptococcus, Group A Streptococcus, bacterial vaginosis, vaginal pH > 4 at the time of birth. Mothers [AND] with fecal samples positive for Salmonella, Shigella, Campylobacter [OR] or Yersinia.
