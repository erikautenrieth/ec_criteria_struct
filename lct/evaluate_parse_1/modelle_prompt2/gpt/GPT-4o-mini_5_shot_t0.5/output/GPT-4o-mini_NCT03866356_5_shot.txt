Inclusion Criteria:
 - The inclusion criteria were being female, ≥ 18 years, having an initial diagnosis of SI, being literate, having [NOT] no sensory disorder affecting communication, [AND] agreeing to participate in the research
Exclusion Criteria:
 - The exclusion criteria were pregnancy, presence of a urinary tract infection [OR] and previous treatment for SI. The removal criteria were patient non-compliance [AND] with the protocol, failure in contacting the patient [AND] the patient's wish to withdraw from the study.
