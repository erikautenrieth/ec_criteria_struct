Inclusion Criteria:
 - Older adults (≥ 65 years)
 - Subject has lower appendicular muscle mass index (AMMI) (<7 kg/m^2 in men; <5.7 kg/m^2 in women) using Bioelectrical impedance analysis (BIA, TANITA BC-418®, Tokyo, Japan)
 - Subject has lower handgrip strength (<26 kg in men; <18 kg in women) using dynamometer (CAMRY®, Zhongshan, Guangdong) [OR] or slowly gait speed (<1 m/s)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Subject's life expectancy was shorter than 6 months
 - Nursing home resident
 - Subjects has the history of kidney failure
 - Subjects has the history of liver failure
 - Subjects has the history of diabetes mellitus
 - Subjects has resistance exercise habit.
