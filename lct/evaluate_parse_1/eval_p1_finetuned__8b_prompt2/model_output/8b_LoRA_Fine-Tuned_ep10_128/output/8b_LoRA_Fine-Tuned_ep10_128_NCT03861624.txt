Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Skeletal maturity
 2. AO/ [NOT] OTA 42 open tibia fractures
 3. Wound primarily closeable (no flap [NOT] and delay in closure due to contamination needed)
 4. Palpable pedal pulses (NOT] no vascular injury sustained)
 5. Presentation within 24 hours from injury
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Current injury is a pathologic fracture
 2. Sustained bilateral tibia fracture
 3. Sustained comm [NOT] comm fem fracture
 4. Sustained severe TraumaticBrain Injury (GCS<12) *
 5. Sustained severe spinal cord injury (lower extremity pares paraly/ paralysis)
 6. Sustained severe burns (>10% total body surface area [NOT] or >5 % TBSA [NOT] and full thickness [NOT] or circumferential injury)
 7. Prior ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery
 8. Prior [NOT] or current lower limb deformity [NOT] or abnormality
 9. Unable to complete follow-up visits
