Inclusion Criteria:
 - age ≥18 years
 - BMI ≤40 kg/m2
 - indication for hysterectomy for benign disease [OR] or prophylactic surgery due to heretic cancer
 - indication for radical hysterectomy [AND] and/or pelvic - para a not.
 - indication for modified radical hysterectomy [AND] and/or pelvic - para a not.
 - indication for modified radical hysterectomy [AND] and/or pelvic - para a not.
 - size of uterus [AND] and vagina allows for retrieval by the vaginal route in cancer patients
 - written informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - known extensive intra ab dominal adhesions
 - anaestheticological contraindicications to laparosscopy
 - women [AND] with pacemaker [OR] or other implants where elect to not be avoided
 - women [AND] with known defects of the hemostasis
 - pregnancy
 - other internal [OR] or anatomical criteria that preclude a minimal invasive approach
 - [NOT] inability to understand patient information
