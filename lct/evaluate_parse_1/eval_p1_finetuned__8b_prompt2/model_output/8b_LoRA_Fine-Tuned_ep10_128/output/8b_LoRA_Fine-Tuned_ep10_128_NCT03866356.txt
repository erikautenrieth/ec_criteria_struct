Inclusion Criteria:
 - The inclusion Criteria were being female, ≥ 18 years, having an initial diagnosis of SI, being literate, having [NOT] no sensory disorder affecting communication, and agreeing to participate in the research
Exclusion Criteria:
 - The exclusion Criteria were pregnancy, presence of a urinary tract infection [AND] and previous treatment for SI. The removal Criteria were patient [NOT] non-compliance with the protocol, [NOT] failure in contacting the patient and the patient's wish to withdraw from the study.
