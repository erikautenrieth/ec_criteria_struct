Patients who are diagnosed as ulcerative colitis / Crhon s disease however in activity or

 of Internal medicine department
Exclusion Criteria:

 - Previous history of cardiovascular [OR] or cerebro vascular events
 - Autoimmune, [OR] Neoplastic diseases
 - Thromboembolic diseases
