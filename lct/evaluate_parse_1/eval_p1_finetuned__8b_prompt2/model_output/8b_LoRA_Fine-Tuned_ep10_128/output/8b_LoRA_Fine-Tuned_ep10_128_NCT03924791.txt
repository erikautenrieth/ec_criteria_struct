Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosed with sciatica by GP
 - NRS leg pain of 6 or more on a 10-point NRS scale
 - Minimum duration of symptoms of 3 weeks and maximum duration of 8 weeks
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Age under 18 years
 - Condition [NOT] preventing to receive transfor aminal epidural injection
 - Severe scolot injection received in 6 months before randomization date
 - Surgery for sciatic at the same level
 - Surgery for sciatic at another level within one year before inclusion
 - Pregnancy
