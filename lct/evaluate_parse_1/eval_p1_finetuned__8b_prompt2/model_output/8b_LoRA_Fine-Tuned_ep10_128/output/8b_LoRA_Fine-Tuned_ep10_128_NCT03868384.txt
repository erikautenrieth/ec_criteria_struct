Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with hypertension [AND] and taking antihypertensive drugs more than 1 year
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Secondary hypertension
 - Hypertensive emergency [AND] and urgency
 - Severe heart failure (NYHA III [AND] and IV)
 - Angina [AND] with 6 months
 - Myocardial inf [NOT] infactionfection within 6 months
 - Peripheral artery disease within 6 months
 - Significant arrhythmia (i.e. ventricular tachycardia [AND] and fibrillation, [OR] atrial fibrillation, [OR] atrial flutter)
 - Pregnancy
 - Night labor, [OR] shift worker
 - Those who are currently participating in other clinical studies
 - Those who have taken other clinical trial drugs within the past month
 - According to the opinion of the researcher, those who have or may have a disease that may interfere with the completion of the study
 - History of drug [OR] or alcohol dependence within 6 months
