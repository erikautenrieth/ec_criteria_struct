Exclusion Criteria:
 - < 60 years
 - Medical indications: Recent [OR] or unstable cardiovascular events: Ecgchanges, [OR] unstable angina, [OR] un controlled arrhythmia, 3rd degree BAV, [OR] acute heart failure.
 - MMSE score < 4

 - Subject under guardianship, [OR] curator [OR] or safeguard of justice
