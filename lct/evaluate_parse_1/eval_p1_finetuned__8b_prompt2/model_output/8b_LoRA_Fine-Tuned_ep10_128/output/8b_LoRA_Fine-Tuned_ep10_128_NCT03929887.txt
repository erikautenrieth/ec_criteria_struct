Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of glomerulone ph [NOT] on first diagnostic kidney biopsy, as per specified pathology definitions
 - First diagnostic kidney biopsy since 2010
 - Will be able to comply with study requirements, including intention to fully participate in protocol-specified follow up at a clinical study site
 - Informed consent and, where age appropriate, informed assent (clinical data, human derived materials including genomic data)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Unwilledness to give informed consent
 - Not enough kidney tissue to confirm a diagnosis (adequate tissue: more than 2 cores of kidney tissues [NOT] 10 x 1.2 mm) including over 10-15 glomer. each)
