Inclusion Criteria:
 - biopsy proven rectal cancer (anal verge ≤ 10 cm)
 - neoadjuvant radiotherapy [OR] or chemoradiotherapy
 - [NOT] not radical surgery after neoadjuvant therapy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - other malignant disease
 - other fatel disease
 - in complete of half of radiotherapy course
 - metastasis on initial presentation
