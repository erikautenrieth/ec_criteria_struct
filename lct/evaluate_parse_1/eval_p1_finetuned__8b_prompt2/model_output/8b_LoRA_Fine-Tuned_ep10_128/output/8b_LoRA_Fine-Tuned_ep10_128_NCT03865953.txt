Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Clinical diagnosis of post herpetic neuralgia, [AND] with pain persisting for at least 3 months after the onset of herpes zoster rash [OR] OR
 2. Clinical diagnosis of distal painful poly ne [NOT] poly ne [NOT] due to Type 1 [OR] or Type II diabetes mellitus [AND] with:
 1. Symmetrical, bilateral pain in the lower extremities for at least 3 months and 2. Diabetes under control for at least 3 months prior to randomisation, as indicated by a glycated haemoglobin level (Hb A1 c) of ≤ 11% (97 mmol mol) and on a stable dose of insulin [OR] or oral diabetic medication for 3 months prior to randomization, and
 3. Diabetes under control for at least 3 months prior to randomization, as indicated by a glycated haemoglobin level (Hb A1 c) of ≤ 11% (97 mmol mol) and on a stable dose of insulin [OR] or oral diabetic medication for 3 months prior to randomization, and
 3. Positive sensory symptoms (mechanical [OR] or thermal) associated with neuropathic pain, confirmed by:
 1. pain DETECT questionnaire (PD-Q) and 2. Clinical assessment, showing signs of neuropathic pain in either a dermatomal (PHN) or distal symmetrical distribution (DPN)
 8. An average daily pain score on the NPRS of at least 4 and no more than 8 in the last five diary entries before randomization
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Presence of moderate to severe pain from other causes that may confound assessment or self-evaluation of NP.
 2. Subjects with both DPN [NOT] and PHN
