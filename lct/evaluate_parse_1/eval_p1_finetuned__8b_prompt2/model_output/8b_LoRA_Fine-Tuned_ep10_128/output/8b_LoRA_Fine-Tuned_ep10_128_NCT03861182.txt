Inclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers:
 - Over 18 years of age
Neon not:
 - Aged between 1 h of life and 28 days of life
 - Born beyond 33 week of Amenorrhea + 1 day and 2 kg of birth weight
 - Hospitalized in the medical-surgical centre of Pediatrics of the hospital of Strough of
 - For whom a blood sample was prescribed as part of their routine care
Exclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers:
 - Systemic inflammatory disease
 - Transient inflammatory state
 - Any drug that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48 h preceding the sampling
Neon:
 - Risk of an [AND] < 7 g/Dl
 - Current anticoagulant treatment
