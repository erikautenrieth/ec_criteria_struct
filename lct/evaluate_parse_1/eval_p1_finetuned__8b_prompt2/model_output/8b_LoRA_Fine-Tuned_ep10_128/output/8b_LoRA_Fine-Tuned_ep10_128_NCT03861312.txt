Inclusion Criteria:
 - The inclusion Criteria were as follows: a) men [OR] and women aged between 18- and 65 years b) Medical diagnosis of Non-Specific Chronic Neck Pain [AND] with more than 6 months of evolution of neck pain.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Exclusion Criteria included the following: a) patients with rheumatic diseases, [OR] cervical hernia, [OR] cervical whiplash syndrome, [OR] neck surgeries [OR] or history of arthrodesis, b) systemic diseases, c) vision, [OR] hearing [OR] or vestibal problems, d) Severe trauma [OR] or traffic accident which had an impact on the cervical area.
