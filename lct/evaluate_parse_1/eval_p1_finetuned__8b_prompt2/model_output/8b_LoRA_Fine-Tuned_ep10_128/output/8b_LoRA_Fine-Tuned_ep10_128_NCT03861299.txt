Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Age ≥18 years [AND] and ≤ 90 years
 2. Tumor diagnosed as Glioblastoma Mult mot [OR] on MRI [AND] with distinct ring like pattern of contrast enhancement [AND] with thick irregular walls [AND] and a core area reduced signal suggestive of tumour necrosis as assessed by the surgeon
 3. Tumors situated in [OR] or near eloquent areas; motor cortex, [OR] sensory cortex, [OR] subcortical pyramidal tract [OR] or speech areas as indicated on MRI (Saw  Gr II II and II)
 4. The tumor is suitable for resection (according to neurosurgeon)
 5. Karn [NOT] Karn performance scale 80 or more
 6. Written Informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Tum of the cerebellum, [OR] brain stem [OR] or midline
 2. Mult [NOT] Mult [NOT] enhancing lesions
 3. Substantial [NOT] non- contrast enhancing tumor areas suggesting low grade gliomas [AND] with malignant transformation
 4. Medical reasons precluding MRI (eg, pacemaker)
 5. Inability to give consent because of or language barrier
 6. Psychiatric history
 7. Previous brain tumour surgery
 8. Previous low grade glioma.
 9. Second primary malignancy within the past 5 years [AND] with the exception of adequately treated in situ carcinoma of any organ [OR] or basal cell carcinoma of the skin.
 10. Severe aphasia [OR] or dys phasia
