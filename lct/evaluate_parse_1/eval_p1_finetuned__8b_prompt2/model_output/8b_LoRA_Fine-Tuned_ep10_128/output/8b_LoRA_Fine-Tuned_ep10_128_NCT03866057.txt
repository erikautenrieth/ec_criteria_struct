Inclusion Criteria:
 - age 18-90
 - acute ischemic [OR] or hemorrhagic stroke (within 30 days)
 - consent to participate and to anonymous processing of data.
Additional inclusion Criteria for Sub project Neurophysiological Markers:
-signing of informed consent for the participation in the sub project.
Additional inclusion Criteria for Epigenetic sub project:
-signing of informed consent for participation in the sub project.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Stroke occurred more than 30 days after the transfer to intensive rehabilitation
Additional exclusion Criteria for Epigenetic sub project (for retrospective analysis):
 - history ofmajor psychiatric disorder [OR] or recent exacerbation
 - global aphasia
 - refusal of the patient to participate in the study.
