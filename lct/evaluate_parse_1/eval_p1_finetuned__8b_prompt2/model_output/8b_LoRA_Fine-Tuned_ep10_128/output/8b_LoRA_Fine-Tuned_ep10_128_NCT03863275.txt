Inclusion Criteria:
 - Boys [OR] and girls from 7 to 12 years old with class II skeletal classification attending a consultation in the dental clinic of the UNIC  foundation, to be fitted with myofunctional apparatus type Sn1
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who have had previous orthopedic treatment
 - Patients with signs of condyle injuries
 - Patients of Pierre Robin [OR] or any form of cleft
 - Patients with syndrom that associate cran cran facial anomalies [AND] and muscle alterations.
