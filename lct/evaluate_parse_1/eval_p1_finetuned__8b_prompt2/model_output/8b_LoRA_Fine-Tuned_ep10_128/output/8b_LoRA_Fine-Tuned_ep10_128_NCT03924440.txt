Inclusion Criteria:
 1.Female (mandatory)
 2. Not currently pregnant [OR] or nursing (pregregn affects P DG levels)
 3. Aged 18-34 (advanced age can hinder P DG levels)
 4. Cycle length of 26-39 days (long [OR] or short cycles have inconsistent P DG levels)
 5. Currently use other fertility monitoring tools (this allows us to compare Pro ov - Ov Double Check test results with other fertility tracking methods)
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Male
 2. Pregnant
 3. Nursing
