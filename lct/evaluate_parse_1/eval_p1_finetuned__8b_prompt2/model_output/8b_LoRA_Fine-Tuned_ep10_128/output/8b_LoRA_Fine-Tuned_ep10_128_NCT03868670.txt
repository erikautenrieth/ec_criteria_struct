Inclusion Criteria:
 - BMI 45-60 kg/m2
 - Failure of at least one weight loss medication
 - Previous Ga stic B Boy Surgery lead to less than 50 % of excess weight lost by 2 years after surgery
 - Significant loss of control over eating
 - Subject is able to attend all scheduled clinic appointments on their own or with a caregiver.
 - Premenotional women must agree to use acceptable methods of birth control.
 - Participants provide voluntary, decisionally capable, and appropriately informed consent.
 - Subject is able to comply w/ all testing and follow-up requirements defined by the study protocol.
 - Participant has [NOT] no immediate plan for relocation beyond 6 hours of the study site.
 - Proficiency with the English language.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Subject has an implanted medical device that delivers electrical energy to the brain.
 - Subject has an implantable cardiac pacemaker, [OR] defibrillator, [OR] or neuro stimulator.
 - Subject is likely to require repeat MR imaging after implant of the RNS Neuro Stimulator [AND] and Leads.
 - Subject is [NOT] unable to fit into CT scanner (500 lb upper weight limit for CT scanner).
 - Inability to provide informed consent to treatment.
