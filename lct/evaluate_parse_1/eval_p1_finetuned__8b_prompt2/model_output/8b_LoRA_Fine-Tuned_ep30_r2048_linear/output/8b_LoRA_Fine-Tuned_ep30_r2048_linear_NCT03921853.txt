Inclusion Criteria:
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Older than 18 [AND] and younger than 65 years of age.
 - With risk factors to Metabolic syndrome
 - With obesity [OR] and morbid obesity condition
 - Medical authorization by a physician.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Physical limitations (e.g., restricting injuries of the musculoskeletal system, [OR] or dependent of a third person).
 - Exercise-related dyspnoea [OR] or respiratory alterations.
 - Chronic heart disease [AND] with any worsening in the last month.
 - Adherence rate of less than 80% of the total interventions.
