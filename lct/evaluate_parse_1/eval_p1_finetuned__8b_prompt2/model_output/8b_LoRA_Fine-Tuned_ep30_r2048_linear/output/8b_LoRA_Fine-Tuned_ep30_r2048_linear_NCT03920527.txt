Inclusion Criteria: includes presence of all the following:
 - one or more clinical symptoms (persistent cough, rapid [OR] and/or difficult breathing, [OR] or exercise intolerance)
 - slowly progressive [OR] or persistent radiological findings (one or more cavities [OR] and surrounding fibrosis, [OR] infiltrates, [OR] consolidation, [AND] with or [NOT] without fungal ball [OR] or progressive pleural thickening) on chest radiography [OR] of the thorax
 - immunological (A.fumigatus-specific IgG >27 mgA/L [OR] or positive Aspergillus precipitins) [OR] or microbiological evidence of Aspergillus infection (growth of Aspergillus in respiratory secretions [OR] or serum galactomannan index >0.5 [OR] or BALF galactomannan index >1) [AND] and,
 - [NOT] exclusion of other pulmonary disorders with similar presentation.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - failure to provide informed consent
 - patients on immunosuppressive drugs, [OR] intake of prednisolone (or equivalent) >10 mg for at least 3 weeks [OR] or a diagnosis of human immunodeficiency virus syndrome
 - intake of Aspergillus-specific IgG >27 mgA/L [OR] or positive Aspergillus precipitins) [OR] or other biological treatment
 - subjects with active pulmonary infection due to mycobacterium tuberculosis [OR] or other mycobacterioses
 - subjects with other pulmonary aspergillosis (allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis, [OR] chronic necrotic aspergillosis [OR] and angio-invasive aspergillosis)
 - history of invasive or recurrent S. aureus infections
 - patients with limited care (lack of commitment to full，aggressive life support)
 - patients with advanced cancers
 - patients combined with non-infectious factors leading to the death(uncontrollable major bleeding, brain hernia, etc.)
 - surgically unresolved infection sources(such as some intraperitoneal infection etc.)
 - patients allergic to granisetron.
 - patients with intestinal obstruction.
