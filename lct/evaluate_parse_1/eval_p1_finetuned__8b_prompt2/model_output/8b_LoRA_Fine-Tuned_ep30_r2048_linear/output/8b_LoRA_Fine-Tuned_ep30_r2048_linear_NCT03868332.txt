Inclusion Criteria:
Patients who are diagnosed as ulcerative colitis [OR] / Crohn's disease however in activity [OR] or
in remission attending the outpatient clinics [OR] and inpatient of gastroenterology units
of Internal medicine department
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Previous history of hypertension, diabetes mellitus, [OR] Dyslipidemia
 - Previous history of cardiovascular [OR] or cerebrovascular events
 - Autoimmune diseases
 - Neoplastic diseases
 - Thromboembolic diseases
