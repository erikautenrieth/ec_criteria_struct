Inclusion criteria:
 - Diagnosis of vulvar lichen sclerosus
 - Clinical LS score ≥ 4
Exclusion criteria:
 - Concomitant steroid, [OR] calcineurin inhibitors [OR] or any other topical [OR] or systemic treatment for LS
 - < 3 months since start of vaginal estrogen treatment
 - Malignant disease as the cause of the vulval symptoms
 - BMI > 35 kg/m²
 - Acute infection (fungal, [OR] bacterial, [OR] viral) of the vulva, [OR] vagina [OR] or bladder
 - Presence of contraindications for the laser treatment [OR] or topical steroid treatment
