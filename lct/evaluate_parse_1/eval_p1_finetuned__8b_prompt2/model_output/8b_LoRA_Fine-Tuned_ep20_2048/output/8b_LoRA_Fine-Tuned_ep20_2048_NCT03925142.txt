Inclusion Criteria:
 - Male [OR] or female
 - 30-69 years old
 - BMI 25-37 kg/m2
 - Total cholesterol <260 mg/dL
 - Low-density lipoprotein cholesterol <189 mg/dL
 - Glucose <110 mg/dL
 - Systolic/diastolic blood pressure < 140/90
 - Body weight stable for 3 months prior (±3 kg)
 - Stable physical activity regimen 3 months prior
 - Medications use stable for 6 months prior (±3 kg)
 - [NOT] Non-smoking
 - [NOT] Not pregnant [AND] and [NOT] not lactating.
 - Participants must be willing and able to consume the prescribed diets (lacto-ovo vegetarian [OR] and omnivorous).
Exclusion Criteria:
 - <30 [OR] or >69 years old
 - BMI <25.0 [OR] or >37 kg/m2
 - Fasting serum total cholesterol > 260 mg/dL
 - Low-density lipoprotein cholesterol >189 mg/dL
 - Glucose >110 mg/dL
 - Systolic/diastolic blood pressure > 140/90
 - Body weight stable for <3 months prior (±3 kg)
 - Stable physical activity regimen < 3 months prior
 - Medications use unstable for 6 months prior (±3 kg)
