<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - glomerular filtration rate less than 30 ml / min / 1.73m
  - [NOT] Without replacement therapy (dialysis [OR] hemodialysis)
  - Volume overload
  - At least 100 ml per day of residual diuresis
  - Use of a loop diuretic for at least one month
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Allergies known to diuretics
  - Patients [AND] with severe infections
  - Patients [AND] with hemodynamic instability
  - Amputees
  - Patients [AND] with cognitive impairment
  - Patients [AND] with acute renal failure
  - Patients [AND] with graft loss
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
