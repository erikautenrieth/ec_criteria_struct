Inclusion Criteria:
 - carpal tunnel syndrome diagnosed either by physical exam [OR] or EMG
 - age >18
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] allergy to bupivacaine
 - pre-operative opioid usage within the last six months
 - patients allergic to Percocet [OR] and acetaminophen
 - patients taking tricyclic antidepressants ([AND] interaction with bupivacaine)
 - [NOT] vulnerable populations ([NOT] prisoners [OR] and [NOT] pregnant women)
