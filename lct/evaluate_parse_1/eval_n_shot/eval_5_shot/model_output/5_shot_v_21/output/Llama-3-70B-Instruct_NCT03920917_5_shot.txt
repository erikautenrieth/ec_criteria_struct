 - 1. Patient [AND] with paroxysmal atrial fibrillation who is scheduled for ablation procedure [AND] ≥20 [AND] ≤80 years of age

 - 6. Patients [AND] with contraindications for anticoagulation therapy(for prevention of cerebral infarction) [AND] antiarrhythmic drugs
 - 7. Patients [AND] with valvular atrial fibrillation (mitral stenosis >grade 2, mechanical valve, mitral valvuloplasty)
 - 8. Patients [AND] with a severe comorbid disease
 - 9. Expected survival < 1 year

 - 11. Patients who [NOT] can read the consent form (illiterates, foreigners, etc.)
