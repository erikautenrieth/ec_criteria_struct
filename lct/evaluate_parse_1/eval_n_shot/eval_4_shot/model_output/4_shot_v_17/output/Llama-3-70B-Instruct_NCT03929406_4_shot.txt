Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Eligible patients for deep brain stimulation surgery
  -  Patients [AND] affiliated to social security [OR] or benefiting of a similar insurance scheme
  -  Patients [AND] having signed a consent to participate to the study
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient [NOT] eligible for deep brain stimulation surgery
  -  Pregnant women [OR] or nursing mothers
  -  Persons [NOT] deprived of liberty by judicial [OR] or administrative decision
  -  Persons [NOT] able to express their consent [OR] legally protected
  -  Persons in period of disqualification for another interventional research
