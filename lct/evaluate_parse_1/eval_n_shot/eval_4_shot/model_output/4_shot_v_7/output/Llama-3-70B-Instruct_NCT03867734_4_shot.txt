Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Persons diagnosed with pharyngeal gonorrhea [OR] gonococcal urethritis who are undergoing pharyngeal gonorrhea testing, [AND] who are [NOT] not yet treated.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Age less than 16 years
  -  Receipt of antibiotics in ≤30 days
  -  Known allergy to aztreonam
  -  History of renal disease (including diagnosis of solitary kidney, chronic renal insufficiency, renal cell carcinoma etc.)
  -  Concurrent infection with syphilis [OR] chlamydia
  -  Pregnancy [OR] and/or nursing
  -  [NOT] Unable to return for a follow-up visit 4-7 days (+/- 1 day).
  -  Study team's discretion
