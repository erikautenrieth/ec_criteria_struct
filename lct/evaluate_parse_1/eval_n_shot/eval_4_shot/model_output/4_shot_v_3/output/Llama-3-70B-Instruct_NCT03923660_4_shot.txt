Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Severe COPD (FEV1 [forced expiratory volume in 1 second] / FVC [forced vital capacity] < 70% [AND] et FEV1 < 50%)
  -  Informed consent
  -  Affiliation to a social security scheme

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Effort oxygen therapy
  -  Cardiovascular, [OR] neuromuscular [OR] or musculoskeletal disorders that can provide significant dyspnea [OR] or limit exercise
  -  Legal incapacity
  -  Low [OR] or no cooperation anticipated
