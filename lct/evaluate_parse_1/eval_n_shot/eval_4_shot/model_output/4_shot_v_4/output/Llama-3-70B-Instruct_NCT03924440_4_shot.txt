Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Female ([NOT] not male)
  2. [NOT] not currently pregnant [OR] nursing (pregnancy affects PdG levels)
  3. Aged 18-34 ([NOT] not advanced age can hinder PdG levels)
  4. Cycle length of 26-39 days ([NOT] not long [OR] short cycles have inconsistent PdG levels)
  5. Currently use other fertility monitoring tools ([AND] this allows us to compare Proov - Ovulation Double Check test results [AND] with other fertility tracking methods)

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Male
  2. Pregnant
  3. Nursing
