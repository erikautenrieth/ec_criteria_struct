<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - carpal tunnel syndrome diagnosed either by physical exam [OR] EMG
 - age >18
Exclusion Criteria:
 - allergy to bupivacaine
 - pre-operative opioid usage within the last six months
 - patients [AND] allergic to Percocet [OR] acetaminophen
 - patients taking tricyclic antidepressants ([AND] interaction with bupivacaine)
 - [NOT] vulnerable populations ([NOT] prisoners [OR] [NOT] pregnant women)
