<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - all patients who are assigned to a clinically indicated chest X-ray by the emergency department of Vienna General Hospital
 - [AND] ability to provide informed consent
 - [AND] informed consent after detailed patient briefing
Exclusion Criteria:
 - the emergency departments assessment of a critical clinical condition [AND] oppose an examination with both modalities (ULDCT of the chest, [OR] chest X-ray)
 - assigned to chest X-ray as follow-up
 - women with positive ß-HCG-test
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
