Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Documented keratoconus [OR] or ectasia after refractive surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Insufficient corneal thickness
  -  Ocular condition that may predispose the eye to complications
  -  History of chemical injury [OR] delayed epithelial healing
  -  Condition that would interfere [AND] or prolong epithelial healing
  -  Known sensitivity to treatment medications
  -  Pregnancy
