Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Severe COPD (FEV1 [forced expiratory volume in 1 second] [OR] / FVC [forced vital capacity] < 70% [AND] FEV1 < 50%)
  -  Patients [AND] with informed consent
  -  Patients [AND] with affiliation to a social security scheme
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients [AND] with effort oxygen therapy
  -  Cardiovascular, [OR] neuromuscular [OR] musculoskeletal disorders that can provide significant dyspnea [OR] limit exercise
  -  Patients [AND] with legal incapacity
  -  Patients [AND] with low [OR] no cooperation anticipated
