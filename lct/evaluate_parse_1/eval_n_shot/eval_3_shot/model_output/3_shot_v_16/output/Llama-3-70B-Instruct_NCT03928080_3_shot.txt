Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Aged 18 [AND] above.
  -  [AND] Having the ability to learn the standard toothbrushing method with artificial intelligence powered toothbrush.
  -  Ability [AND] willingness to give written informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Having natural teeth (edentulous mouth).
  -  Pregnant females.
  -  [NOT] Having received professional periodontal treatment (other than supragingival cleaning) within the previous 12 months, [OR] having received antibiotic medication within the previous 3 months.
