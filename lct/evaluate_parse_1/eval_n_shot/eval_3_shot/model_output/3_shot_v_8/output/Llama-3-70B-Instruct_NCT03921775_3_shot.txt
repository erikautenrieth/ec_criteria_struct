Inclution Criteria:
  1. 18~60 years, female [OR] male
  2. Patients scheduled for an elective surgical procedure([AND] mechanical ventilation via endotracheal tube)
  3. BMI: 18~30 kg/m2
  4. Patients [AND] understand clearly [AND] participate in the study voluntarily, [AND] sign the informed consent
Exclusion criteria:
  1. Patients scheduled for emergency surgery
  2. Patients with a history of drug abuse [OR] and / or alcohol abuse 2 years prior to the screening period
  3. One [OR] more of the laboratory findings fall out of the limitations for this study(platelet,[OR] hemoglobin,[OR] aspartate aminotransferase,[OR] etc.)
  4. Pregnant women [OR] those in lactation period
  5. Allergic to drugs used in the study
  6. Patients with respiratory management difficulties ([AND] Modified Mallampati grade IV)
  7. Patients have participated in other clinical trial within the 3 months prior to randomization
  8. Any patient judged by the Principal Investigator (PI) [OR] Sub-Investigator to be inappropriate for the subject for any other reason
