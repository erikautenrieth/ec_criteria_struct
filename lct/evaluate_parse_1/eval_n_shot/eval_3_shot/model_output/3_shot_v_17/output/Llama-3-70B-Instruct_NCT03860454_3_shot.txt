<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - LMNA+ cases [AND] with pathogenic LMNA mutations for LMNA+ [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients [AND] who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  - DCMWT cases: patients [AND] with heart muscle failure [AND] but with wild-type lamin gene. Heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients [AND] who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  - HV (controls): matched to cases.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Needle-phobia that would preclude blood-letting
  - Participants [NOT] willing to consent
  - Patients [AND] that have a conventional contraindication for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).
  - Patients [AND] that have had a blood transfusion within the last month [OR] and patients having haemodialysis will be excluded.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
