Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Subjects [AND] with adhesive capsulitis diagnosis by physical examination (Compared with intact side, there is more than 25% limitation in at least two directions of shoulder range of motion)
  -  Subjects [AND] have complaints for at least 1 month
  -  [NOT] No pregnancy [OR] / breastfeeding
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History of bilateral adhesive capsulitis
  -  History of shoulder trauma, [OR] fracture, [OR] shoulder shoulder surgery, [OR] calcific tendinopathy, [OR] glenohumeral osteoarthritis, [OR] inflammatory rheumatic diseases, [OR] tumor, [OR] infection
  -  History of corticosteroid injection in the shoulder during the last 3 months
  -  History of recent lung, [OR] breast [OR] bypass surgery
  -  History of cervical radiculopathy [OR] / brachial plexus lesion
  -  History of neuromuscular disease
  -  History of physical therapy program for the same shoulder in the last 6 months
