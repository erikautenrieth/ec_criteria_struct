Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Gastroscopy [AND] can be performed
  -  Serologic markers including serum pepsinogen [AND] can be measured
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Age < 20 [OR] > 70 years
  -  Anemia ([NOT] serum hemoglobin level ≥ 10 g/dL)
  -  Severe systemic disease
  -  Advanced chronic liver disease
  -  Use of certain medications, including proton pump inhibitors [OR] H2- receptor antagonists [OR] or antibiotics
  -  [NOT] History of H. pylori eradication
  -  [NOT] History of gastric surgery
  -  Recent history of upper gastrointestinal bleeding
