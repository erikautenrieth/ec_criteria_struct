Inclusion Criteria:
  -  glomerular filtration rate less than 30 ml / [OR] min / [OR] 1.73m
  -  [NOT] Without replacement therapy ([AND] dialysis [OR] hemodialysis)
  -  Volume overload
  -  At least 100 ml per day of residual diuresis
  -  Use of a loop diuretic for at least one month
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Allergies known to diuretics
  -  Patients [AND] who have severe infections
  -  Patients [AND] who have hemodynamic instability
  -  Amputees
  -  Patients [AND] who have cognitive impairment
  -  Patients [AND] who have acute renal failure
  -  Patients [AND] who have graft loss
