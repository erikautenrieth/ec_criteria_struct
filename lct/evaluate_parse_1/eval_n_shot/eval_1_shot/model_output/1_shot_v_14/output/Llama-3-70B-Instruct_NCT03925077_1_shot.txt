Inclusion Criteria:
  1. demonstrate readiness to physical activity by completing the online Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire (PAR-Q)[AND];
  2. have a SCI resulting [AND] incomplete [OR] complete (C5 [AND] below) paraplegia [OR] tetraplegia;
  3. have broadband internet access[AND];
  4. between the ages of 18 [AND] 65 years[AND];
  5. have the ability to converse [AND] read English[AND].
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. significant visual impairment that [NOT] prevents seeing a computer screen to follow a home exercise program;
  2. active pressure ulcer[AND];
  3. any contraindications to exercise based on the American College of Sports Medicine guidelines[AND];
  4. significant hearing impairment impeding ability to hear music to engage in exercise[AND].
