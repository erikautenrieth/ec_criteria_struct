Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Gastroscopy can be performed
  -  Serologic markers, including serum pepsinogen, [OR] can be measured
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Age < 20 [OR] > 70 years
  -  Anemia ([AND] serum hemoglobin level < 10 g/dL)
  -  Severe systemic disease
  -  Advanced chronic liver disease
  -  Use of certain medications, [AND] including proton pump inhibitors, [OR] H2- receptor antagonists, [OR] antibiotics
  -  [NOT] No History of H. pylori eradication
  -  [NOT] No History of gastric surgery
  -  [NOT] No Recent history of upper gastrointestinal bleeding
