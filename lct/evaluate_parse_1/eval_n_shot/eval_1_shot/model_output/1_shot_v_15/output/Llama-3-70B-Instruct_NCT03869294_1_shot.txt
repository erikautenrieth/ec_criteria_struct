Inclusion Criteria:
  -  1.Histologically confirmed pancreatic adenocarcinoma 
  2.Locally advanced [OR] metastatic PC 
  3.Chemotherapy-naïve 
  4.ECOG performance status of 0 [OR] 1 
  5. [AND] An adequate bone marrow, liver function [AND] kidney function

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  1.Age ≥80 years 
  2.Brain metastasis 
  3.[AND] With other malignancies 
  4.Chronic diarrhea [OR] cardiac disease [OR] pregnancy [OR] breast feeding
