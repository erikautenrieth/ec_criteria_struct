Inclusion Criteria:
  -  The inclusion criteria were being female, ≥ 18 years, [AND] having an initial diagnosis of SI, [AND] being literate, [AND] having [NOT] no sensory disorder affecting communication, [AND] agreeing to participate in the research
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  The exclusion criteria were pregnancy, [AND] presence of a urinary tract infection [AND] previous treatment for SI. The removal criteria were patient [NOT] non-compliance with the protocol, [OR] failure in contacting the patient [AND] the patient's wish to withdraw from the study.
