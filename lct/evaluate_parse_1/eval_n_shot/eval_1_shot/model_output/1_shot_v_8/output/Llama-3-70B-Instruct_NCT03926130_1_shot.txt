Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Diagnosis of CD for at least 3 months prior to baseline
  -  Confirmed diagnosis of moderate to severe CD as assessed by SF [OR] AP score [OR] and SES-CD
  -  Demonstrated intolerance [OR] loss of response [OR] inadequate response to conventional [OR] to biologic therapy for CD
  -  If female, subject must meet [AND] the contraception recommendations
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Have a current diagnosis of ulcerative colitis [OR] inflammatory bowel disease-unclassified (IBD-U) (formerly known as indeterminate colitis) [OR] abdominal [OR] perianal abscess [OR] short bowel syndrome
  -  Have a stoma [OR] ostomy
  -  Have had a bowel resection within 6 months [OR] any kind of intra-abdominal [OR] extra abdominal surgery within 3 months of baseline
  -  [NOT] Have ever received any monoclonal antibodies binding IL-23
