  -  Majors aged between 18 [AND] 55 years, [AND] with an appropriate hearing [AND] able to use Music-Care© ([AND] selection of the playlist via internet access),
  -  Needing treatment in odontology - periodontal surgery ([OR] coronary elongation [OR] sanitation surgery [OR] muco-gingival surgery) [OR] implantary surgery ([AND] placement of an unit implant [OR] two implant in the same localization),

  -  Agreeing to be contacted via phone at J+1 [AND] to fill follow-up study documents,

  -  Protected person, deprived of freedom [OR] under justice safeguard,
