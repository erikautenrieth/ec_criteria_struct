Inclusion Criteria:
Female subjects [AND] between 18 [OR], 65 years of age currently taking spironolactone for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris
[OR]
Female subjects [AND] between 18 [OR], 65 years of age currently taking oral antibiotics for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris
[AND]
Subjects able to complete an online survey in English
Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Subjects less than 18 years of age [OR] older than 65 years of age
Subjects that have ever been diagnosed [AND] any mood disorder by a clinician prior to starting spironolactone ([AND] history of mood disorder [OR] current mood disorder)
[NOT] Subjects taking hormonal contraception ([AND] including [OR] but [OR] not limited to oral contraceptive pills) for a duration of less than 6 months
[NOT] Subjects that have ever been diagnosed [AND] any type of sexual dysfunction prior to starting spironolactone
[NOT] Subjects not able to complete an online survey in English
[NOT] Subjects taking oral isotretinoin for a diagnosis of acne
[NOT] Subjects that are currently pregnant
