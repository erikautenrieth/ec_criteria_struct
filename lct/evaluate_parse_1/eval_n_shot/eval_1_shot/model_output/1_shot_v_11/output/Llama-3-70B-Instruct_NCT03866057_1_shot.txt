Inclusion criteria:
  -  age 18-90
  -  acute ischemic [OR] hemorrhagic stroke (within 30 days)
  -  [AND] consent to participate [AND] to anonymous processing of data.
Additional inclusion criteria for Sub-project Neurophysiological Markers:
-[AND] signing of informed consent for the participation in the sub-project.
Additional inclusion criteria for Epigenetic subproject:
-[AND] signing of informed consent for participation in the sub-project.
Exclusion Criteria:
-[NOT] Stroke occurred more than 30 days after the transfer to intensive rehabilitation
Additional exclusion criteria for Epigenetic subproject (for retrospective analysis):
  -  [AND] history of major psychiatric disorder [OR] recent exacerbation
  -  global aphasia
  -  [AND] refusal of the patient to participate in the study.
