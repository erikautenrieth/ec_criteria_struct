Inclusion Criteria:
  -  carpal tunnel syndrome diagnosed by physical exam [OR] EMG
  -  age >18
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  allergy [AND] to bupivacaine
  -  pre-operative opioid usage within the last six months
  -  patients [AND] allergic [AND] to Percocet [OR] acetaminophen
  -  patients [AND] taking tricyclic antidepressants ([AND] interaction [AND] with bupivacaine)
  -  vulnerable populations ([AND] prisoners [OR] pregnant women)
