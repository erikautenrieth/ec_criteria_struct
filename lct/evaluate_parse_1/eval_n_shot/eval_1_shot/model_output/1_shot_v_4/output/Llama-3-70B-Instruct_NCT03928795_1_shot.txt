Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Monofetal pregnancy
  -  Age [AND] over 18 years old
  -  Term Pregnancy ≥ 37SA
  -  Indication [AND] for Elective caesarean section
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Fetal pathology known prenatally like intrauterine severe growth retardation [AND] with Doppler abnormalities, fetal malformation [OR] a known fetal genetic defect.
  -  Anomaly [AND] of placental adhesion.
  -  Caesarean [AND] in a context of medical [OR] obstetric emergency.
  -  Caesarean section performed [AND] under general anesthesia following failure of locoregional anesthesia.
  -  Refusal [AND] of the woman to participate in the study.
  -  [NOT] Impossibility [OR] failure to study the neonatal acid-base balance on the umbilical cord.
