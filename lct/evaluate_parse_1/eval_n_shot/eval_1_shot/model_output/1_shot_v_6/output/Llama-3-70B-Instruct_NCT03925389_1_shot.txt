<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  at least 18 years old
  -  Patients [AND] eligible for external septorhinoplasty: patients seen in consultation because of nasal obstruction were evaluated. Patients who had symptoms of nasal obstruction for at least 1 year that were the result of an identifiable anatomical cause such as septal deviation, [OR] turbinate hypertrophy, [OR] internal valve collapse, [OR] external valve collapse were included in the study.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] at least 18 years old, < 18 years
  -  mental [OR] physical incapacity to answer the questionnaires
  -  nasal fracture [OR] surgery in the past year
  -  nasal cocaine use in the past year
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
