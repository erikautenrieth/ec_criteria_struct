Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Prescription of a Narval CC mandibular advancement device
  -  Age ≥ 18 years
  -  Ability to understand the study information [AND] information on usage of personal data
  -  Signed [AND] dated informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients [AND] who have central sleep apnea (central AI > 5/hour)
  -  Patients [AND] who have loose teeth [AND] severe parodontitis
  -  Patients [AND] who have completely missing teeth [OR] removable tooth replacement, [OR] with a teeth health [NOT] sufficient to retain an MAD Patients [AND] who have missing molars Patients [AND] who have maximum protrusion of less than 5mm
