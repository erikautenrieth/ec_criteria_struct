Inclusion Criteria
  -  Patients will be recruited from the Division [AND] of Gastroenterology [AND] Hepatology [AND] the Mayo Clinic in Rochester, MN.
  -  Adult patients with immune mediated enteropathies [AND] who have had improvement in symptoms [AND] histology on oral budesonide therapy.
Exclusion Criteria
  -  Age <18 years
  -  Positive stool gluten testing in patients with refractory celiac disease
  -  Small bowel malignancy [OR] history of small bowel malignancy
  -  Refractory celiac disease type 2
  -  Post-transplant lymphoproliferative disorder associated enteropathy
  -  [NOT] prior improvement in symptoms [AND] histology with budesonide therapy
  -  Discontinuation of budesonide therapy prior to the trial
  -  Other concurrent systemic corticosteroids
  -  Other immune mediating medications, for example [OR] but [NOT] limited to azathioprine, [OR] 6-mercaptopurine, [OR] cyclosporine, [OR] methotrexate, [OR] anti-TNF monoclonal antibodies, [OR] alpha-4 beta-7 integrin inhibiting monoclonal antibody, [OR] interleukin 12/23 inhibiting monoclonal antibody, [OR] JAK inhibitors.
