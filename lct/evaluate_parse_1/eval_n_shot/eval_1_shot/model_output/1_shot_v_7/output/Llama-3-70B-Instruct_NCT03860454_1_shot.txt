<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  LMNA+ cases [AND] with pathogenic LMNA mutations for LMNA+ [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  DCMWT cases: patients [AND] with heart muscle failure [AND] but [AND] with wild-type lamin gene. Heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  HV (controls): matched to cases.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] able to undergo blood-letting due to needle-phobia
  -  Participants [NOT] willing to consent
  -  Patients [AND] that have a conventional contraindication for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).
  -  Patients [AND] that have had a blood transfusion within the last month [OR] patients having haemodialysis will be excluded.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
