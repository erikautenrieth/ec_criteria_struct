Inclusion Criteria:
  -  All patients over 18 years of age undergoing gastric resection surgery due to cancer [OR] not of an intensified recovery program (ERAS) [AND] with any level of compliance with the protocol (from 0-[OR]100%).

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients undergoing emergency surgery
       -  Endoscopic procedures
       -  Non-oncological gastric surgery
       -  Patients [WHO] refuse to participate
