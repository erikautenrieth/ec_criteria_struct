  -  Burn patients
  -  severe neuropatic pain rated at least 5 on the 10-point numerical rating scale (NRS)

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] cardiac arrest history
  -  [NOT] history of neurologi disease [OR] brain surgery
  -  [NOT] unstable heart disease [OR] presence of a cardiac pacemaker
  -  pain resulting from other causes like as neuromuscular diseases
  -  psychiatric disorder
  -  diabetes mellitus
  -  abnormal renal function
  -  [NOT] contraindication for MRI [OR] pregnancy.
