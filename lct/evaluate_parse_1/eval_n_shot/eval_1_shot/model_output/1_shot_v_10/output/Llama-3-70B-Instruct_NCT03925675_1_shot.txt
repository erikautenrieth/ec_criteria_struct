Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients evaluated for residual [OR] recurrent gliomas after initial tumor surgery, external beam radiation [OR] / chemotherapy [AND] with temozolamide.
  2. Male [AND] female
  3. Ages 18 to 70
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Patient diagnosis with glioma [AND] before surgery, external beam radiation [OR] /or chemotherapy [AND] with temozolamide.
  2. Women who were pregnant, breast feeding, [OR] possibly pregnant.
  3. Patients [NOT] having hepatic [OR] renal dysfunction.
  4. Patients [NOT] having MRI contraindications (i.e. pacemakers, non-MR compatible devices).
  5. Patients [NOT] having a history of drug hypersensitivity to 18F-Fluciclovine.
