Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Prescription of a Narval CC mandibular advancement device
  -  Age ≥ 18 years
  -  Ability to understand [AND] the study information [AND] information on usage of personal data
  -  Signed [AND] dated informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with central sleep apnea (central AI > 5/hour)
  -  Patients with loose teeth [AND] severe parodontitis
  -  Patients with completely missing teeth [OR] removable tooth replacement, [OR] with a teeth health insufficient to retain an MAD Patients with missing molars [OR] Patients with maximum protrusion of less than 5mm
