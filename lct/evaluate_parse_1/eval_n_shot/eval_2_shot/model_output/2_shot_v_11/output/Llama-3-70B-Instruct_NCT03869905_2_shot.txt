Inclusion Criteria:
  - Ulcerative colitis [AND] with confirmed diagnosis by histology [AND] endoscopy [AND] in stable remission for 3 months [OR] more without therapy [OR] with a maintenance therapy (except steroids [AND] antibiotics for 3 months)
  - A [NOT] negative pregnancy test
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] No history [OR] diagnosis of any of the following conditions: Chron's disease, [OR] bleeding disorders, [OR] gastrointestinal [OR] colonic malignancy, [OR] Kidney disease, including kidney "stones" [OR] hypercalcemia, [OR] Coagulopathy/hereditary hemorrhagic disorders/ [OR] or receiving therapeutic doses of Coumadin [OR] heparin.
  - Ingested certain medications (Calcium, [OR] Vitamin D, [OR] fiber supplements [AND] Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs - NSAIDs) within 30 days of study start.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
