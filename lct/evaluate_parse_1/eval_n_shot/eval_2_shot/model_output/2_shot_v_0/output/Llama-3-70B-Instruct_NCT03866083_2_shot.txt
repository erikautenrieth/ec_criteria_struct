<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
- Critically ill cirrhotics [AND] with early onset septic shock (<24 hours) [AND] with norepinephrine of more than 10 ug/min [AND] lactate >4 mmol/L.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients [NOT] aged 18 years [OR] more
  -  Severe known cardiopulmonary disease ([AND] structural [OR] valvular heart disease, [OR] coronary artery disease, [OR] COPD)
  -  Severe coagulopathy ([AND] platelets <20,000 [AND] INR >5)
  -  Active Bleed ([OR] Mucosal [OR] variceal)
  -  Pregnancy
  -  Chronic kidney disease
  -  Extremely moribund patients [NOT] having an expected life expectancy of more than 24 hours
  -  Failure to give informed consent from family members.
  -  Hemodynamic instability requiring very high dose of vasopressors, [OR] late presentation [OR] spontaneous reversal of shock
  -  Patient enrolled in other clinical trials
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
