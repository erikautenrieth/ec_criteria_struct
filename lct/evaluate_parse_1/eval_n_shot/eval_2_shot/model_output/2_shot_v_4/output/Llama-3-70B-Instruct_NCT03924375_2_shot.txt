<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - Gastroscopy can be performed
  - Serologic markers including serum pepsinogen can be measured
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Age < 20 [OR] > 70 years
  - Anemia ([AND] serum hemoglobin level < 10 g/dL)
  - Severe systemic disease
  - Advanced chronic liver disease
  - Use of certain medications, including proton pump inhibitors, [OR] H2- receptor antagonists, [OR] antibiotics
  - [NOT] No history of H. pylori eradication
  - [NOT] No history of gastric surgery
  - Recent history of upper gastrointestinal bleeding
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
