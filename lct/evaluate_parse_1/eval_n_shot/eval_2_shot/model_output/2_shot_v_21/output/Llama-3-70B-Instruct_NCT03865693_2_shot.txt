<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Burn patients
  -  severe neuropatic pain rated at least 5 on the 10-point numerical rating scale (NRS)
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  They have a history of cardiac arrest
  -  They have a history of neurologi disease [OR] brain surgery
  -  They have unstable heart disease [OR] presence of a cardiac pacemaker
  -  They have pain resulting from other causes like [AND] neuromuscular diseases
  -  They have a psychiatric disorder
  -  They have diabetes mellitus
  -  They have abnormal renal function
  -  They have a contraindication for MRI, [OR] are pregnant.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
