<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adult patients [AND] who plan to undergo simultaneous primary total knee arthroplasty on bilateral knee joint [AND] with a diagnosis of osteoarthritis [OR] aseptic bone necrosis, [NOT] but [NOT] of rheumatoid arthritis;
  -  All patients [AND] who have normal preoperative platelet count, [AND] normal prothrombin time, [AND] normal partial thromboplastin time, [AND] and normal international normalized ratio;
  -  The use of only balanced electrolyte solutions [OR] and/or albumin for plasma volume restitution.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Allergy to tranexamic acid;
  -  Receiving warfarin [OR] heparin; had a history of hemophilia, [OR] deep venous thrombosis, [OR] pulmonary embolism, [OR] or were pregnant;
  -  Patients [AND] with any cardiovascular problems (such as myocardiac infarction history, [OR] atrial fibrillation, [OR] angina);
  -  Patients [AND] with thromboembolic disorders, [OR] or those exhibiting a deteriorating general condition;
  -  Preoperative anemia (a hemoglobin value of <11 g/dL in females [AND] <12 g/dL in males), [AND] refusal of blood products;
  -  Preoperative use of anticoagulant therapy within five days before surgery, [OR] fibrinolytic disorders requiring intraoperative antifibrinolytic treatment, [OR] coagulopathy (as identified by a preoperative platelet count of <150,000/mm3, [OR] an international normalized ratio of >1.4, [OR] or a prolonged partial thromboplastin time [>1.4 times normal]).
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
