<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Skeletal maturity
  2. AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures
  3. Wound primarily closeable ([NOT] no flap [OR] delay in closure due to contamination needed)
  4. Palpable pedal pulses ([NOT] no vascular injury sustained)
  5. Presentation within 24 hours from injury

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Current injury is a pathologic fracture
  2. Sustained bilateral tibia fracture
  3. Sustained comminuted femur fracture
  4. Sustained severe Traumatic Brain Injury (GCS<12) ***
  5. Sustained severe spinal cord injury ([AND] lower extremity paresis/paralysis)
  6. Sustained severe burns ([OR] >10% total body surface area (TBSA) [OR] >5% TBSA with full thickness [OR] circumferential injury)
  7. Prior ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery
  8. Prior [OR] current lower limb deformity [OR] abnormality
  9. [NOT] able to complete follow-up visits
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
