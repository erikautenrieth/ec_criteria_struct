Inclusion Criteria:
  -  The inclusion criteria were as follows: a) men [AND] women aged between 18- [AND] 65-years b) medical diagnosis of Non-Specific Chronic Neck Pain [AND] with more than 6 months of evolution of neck pain.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Exclusion criteria included the following: a) patients [AND] rheumatic diseases, cervical hernia, cervical whiplash syndrome, neck surgeries [OR] history of arthrodesis, b) systemic diseases, c) vision [OR] hearing [OR] vestibular problems, d) Severe trauma [OR] traffic accident which has had an impact on the cervical area.
