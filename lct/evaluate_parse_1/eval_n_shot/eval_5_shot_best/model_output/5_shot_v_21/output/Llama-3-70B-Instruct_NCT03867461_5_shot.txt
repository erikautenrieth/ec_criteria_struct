<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adults determined to be candidates for venous sinus stenting
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Having a history of stroke, transient ischemic attack, or cerebral vasculitis
  -  [NOT] Having a bleeding disorder or taking anticoagulation therapy
  -  [NOT] Having a contraindication to MRI or angiography
  -  [NOT] Having a life expectancy of less than 1 year
  -  [NOT] Being pregnant or breastfeeding
  -  [NOT] Having a history of venous sinus stenting or other intracranial stenting procedures
  -  [NOT] Having a history of intracranial hemorrhage or subarachnoid hemorrhage
  -  [NOT] Having a history of cranial surgery or radiation therapy to the head or neck
  -  [NOT] Having a history of meningitis or encephalitis
  -  [NOT] Having a history of cerebral aneurysm or arteriovenous malformation
  -  [NOT] Having a history of seizure disorder or taking anticonvulsant medications
  -  [NOT] Having a history of chronic steroid use or immunosuppression
  -  [NOT] Having a history of liver or kidney disease
  -  [NOT] Having a history of bleeding or thrombotic disorders
  -  [NOT] Having a history of heart disease or cardiac arrhythmias
  -  [NOT] Having a history of peripheral vascular disease
  -  [NOT] Having a history of cancer or chemotherapy
  -  [NOT] Having a history of HIV or AIDS
  -  [NOT] Having a history of connective tissue disease or collagen vascular disease
  -  [NOT] Having a history of neurodegenerative disease or dementia
  -  [NOT] Having a history of psychiatric disease or taking psychotropic medications
