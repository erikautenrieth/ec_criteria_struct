Inclusion Criteria:
  -  carpal tunnel syndrome diagnosed [OR] either by physical exam [OR] or EMG
  -  age >18.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  allergy to bupivacaine
  -  pre-operative opioid usage within the last six months
  -  patients allergic to Percocet [OR] or acetaminophen
  -  patients taking tricyclic antidepressants [AND] having an interaction with bupivacaine
  -  [NOT] vulnerable populations ([NOT] prisoners [OR] [NOT] pregnant women)
