<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Gestational age < 32 weeks
  -  Birthweight <1500 g
  [AND] -  Age less than 72 hours
  [AND] -  PDA diameter > 1.5 mm
  [AND] -  Signed informed consent obtained from both parents
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Birthweight ≥ 1500 g [OR] gestation age ≥ 32 weeks
  [OR] [NOT] -  Lack of informed consent of the parents
  [OR] -  Congenital heart defect [NOT], other than PDA [OR] patent foramen ovale (PFO)
  [OR] -  The presence of a clinically apparent hemorrhagic syndrome
  [OR] -  Any intraventricular hemorrhage (IVH) in the first 48 hours [OR] IVH grade 3-4
  [OR] -  A platelet count of < 50,000/mm3
  [OR] -  A serum creatinine concentration of > 110 μmol/L
  [OR] -  Oliguria <1 ml/kg/h
  [OR] -  Suspected [OR] apparent NEC
  [OR] -  Suspected [OR] apparent lung hypoplasia
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
