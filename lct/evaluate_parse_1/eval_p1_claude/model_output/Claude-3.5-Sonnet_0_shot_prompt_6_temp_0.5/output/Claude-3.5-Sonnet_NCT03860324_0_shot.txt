<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Surgical plan for total hip replacement
  -  [AND] Signing of consent form to participate in the study
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient refusal
  -  [OR] BMI > 40 kg/m2
  -  [OR] Surgical plan for revision of hip replacement
  -  [OR] Patient [NOT] unable to quantify pain level
  -  [OR] Chronic kidney disease [AND] with a Glomerular Filtration Rate < 50ml/min
  -  [OR] Previously medicated [AND] with opioids
  -  [OR] Patient [NOT] unable to perform the surgery [AND] with spinal block
  -  [OR] Allergy to local anesthetics
  -  [OR] Infection in the site of the Erector Spinae Plane [OR] Fascia Iliaca block
  -  [OR] Allergy [OR] contraindication to the use of morphine
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
