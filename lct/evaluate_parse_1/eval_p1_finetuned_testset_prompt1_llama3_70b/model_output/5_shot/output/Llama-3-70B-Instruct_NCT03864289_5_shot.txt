Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Children with ASD [OR] high risk for autism;
  -  Han ethic;
  -  Parents were at least middle school educated, [AND] agreed to do at least 20 hours per week of planned practice activities with the child in daily life.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Children with Syndromic ASD caused by known genetic defects [OR] inherited metabolic diseases, such as Rett Syndrome, [OR] Fragile X Syndrome, [OR] Angelman Syndrome, [OR] Prader-Willi Syndrome, [OR] tuberous sclerosis;
  -  Visual [OR] auditory [OR] physical disabilities;
  -  Complication with other serious chronic diseases, such as liver [AND] kidney failure, [OR] connective tissue diseases, [OR] tumors.
