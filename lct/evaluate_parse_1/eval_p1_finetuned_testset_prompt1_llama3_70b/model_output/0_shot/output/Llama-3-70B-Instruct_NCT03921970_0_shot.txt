Inclusion Criteria:
  -  American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) classification I [OR] II
  -  Scheduled for laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy [AND] under general anesthesia

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Bleeding diathesis
  -  Receiving anticoagulant treatment

  -  Pregnancy [OR] lactation
