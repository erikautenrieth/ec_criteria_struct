<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion conditions:
  1. The fifth stage of chronic renal failure diagnosed by a physician [AND] with physician's advice and patient's consent.
  2. Patients [OR] who have to undergo renal replacement therapy but have not yet decided what treatment to take.
  3. Ability to communicate in Mandarin [OR] Taiwan.
  4. Ages [OR] over 20 years old.

Exclusion conditions:
  1. [NOT] Cognitive impairment: MMSE score < 21 points [OR] < 16 points for those who are not educated.
  2. Patients who have received [NOT] non-emergency dialysis treatment.
  3. Patients with severe cardiopulmonary dysfunction [OR] severe shock [OR] low blood pressure [OR] large bleeding that is not controlled.
  4. Patients with chronic active hepatitis [OR] cirrhosis [OR] liver failure.
  5. [NOT] A history of cancer that may be transferred.
  6. [NOT] Severe bleeding tendency.
  7. [NOT] Loss of peritoneal function [OR] extensive peritoneal adhesion, unable to use peritoneal dialysis.
  8. [NOT] Unable to correct physiological defects, may interfere with peritoneal dialysis, or increase the chance of infection, such as umbilical hernia.
  9. [NOT] The patient is unable to operate the fluid change, hand shake, and no family members can assist.
  10. [NOT] Patients who are blind [OR] have poor eyesight and have difficulty operating.
