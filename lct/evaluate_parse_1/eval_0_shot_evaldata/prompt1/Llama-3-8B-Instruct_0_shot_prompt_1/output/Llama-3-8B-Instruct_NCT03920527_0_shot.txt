<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria: includes presence of all the following:
  - [AND] one or more clinical symptoms [OR] persistent cough, recurrent hemoptysis, weight loss, malaise, fever, and dyspnea for ≥3 months
  - [AND] slowly progressive or persistent radiological findings [OR] one or more cavities and surrounding fibrosis, infiltrates, consolidation, with [OR] without fungal ball or progressive pleural thickening on computed tomography (CT) of the thorax
  - [AND] immunological [OR] A.fumigatus-specific IgG >27 mgA/L [OR] positive Aspergillus precipitins [OR] microbiological evidence of Aspergillus infection [OR] growth of Aspergillus in respiratory secretions [OR] serum galactomannan index >0.5 [OR] BALF galactomannan index >1
  - [AND] exclusion of other pulmonary disorders with similar presentation.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] failure to provide informed consent
  - [NOT] patients on immunosuppressive drugs [OR] intake of prednisolone (or equivalent) >10 mg for at least 3 weeks [OR] diagnosis of human immunodeficiency virus syndrome
  - [NOT] intake antifungal azoles for >3 weeks in the preceding six months
  - [NOT] subjects with active pulmonary infection due to mycobacterium tuberculosis [OR] mycobacteria other than tuberculosis (MOTT)
  - [NOT] subjects with other forms of pulmonary aspergillosis [OR] allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis [OR] chronic necrotizing aspergillosis [OR] angio-invasive aspergillosis
  - [NOT] pregnancy
