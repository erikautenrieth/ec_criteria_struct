Inclusion Criteria:
  - Monofetal pregnancy
  - Age over 18 years old [AND] not naive
  - Term Pregnancy ≥ 37SA [AND] not excluded
  - Indication for Elective caesarean section [OR] medical necessity

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Fetal pathology known prenatally like intrauterine severe growth retardation with Doppler abnormalities, fetal malformation or a known fetal genetic defect
  - Anomaly of placental adhesion [OR] placental abruption
  - Caesarean in a context of medical or obstetric emergency [NOT] elective
  - Caesarean section performed under general anesthesia following failure of locoregional anesthesia [OR] regional anesthesia
  - Refusal of the woman to participate in the study [NOT] informed consent
  - Impossibility or failure to study the neonatal acid-base balance on the umbilical cord [OR] alternative method of assessment
