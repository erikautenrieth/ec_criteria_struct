<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients diagnosed with Hunter syndrome [AND] male at the age of ≥ 5 [AND] adequate abilities (including 6-MWT) to participate in this study in the opinion of the investigator [AND] voluntarily signed written informed consent to participation in this study [AND] consent to contraception
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Prior treatment with iduronate-2-sulfatase ERT [NOT] prior treatment with other investigational products [NOT] prior treatment with other investigational products within 30 days before treatment with the investigational product in this study or during this study
  - History of bronchotomy [OR] bone marrow transplantation [OR] cord blood transplantation [NOT] known hypersensitivity reactions to any of the components of the investigational product
  - Prior or planned administration of other investigational products within 30 days before treatment with the investigational product in this study or during this study [NOT] unable to perform 6-MWT [NOT] female
