Inclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
  - Over 18 years of age [AND] without systemic inflammatory disease [OR] transient inflammatory state
Neonates:
  - Aged between 1h of life and 28 days of life [AND] born beyond 33 Week of Amenorrhea + 1day [AND] with a birth weight of 2kg [OR] more
  - Hospitalized in the medical-surgical centre of Pediatrics of the hospital of Strasbourg [AND] for whom a blood sample was prescribed as part of their routine care [NOT] free from a blood sample prescription

Exclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
  - Systemic inflammatory disease [NOT] with a transient inflammatory state [OR] any drug that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h preceding the sampling [NOT] without systemic inflammatory disease
Neonates:
  - Risk of anemia < 7g/DL [NOT] without anemia risk assessment [OR] current anticoagulant treatment [NOT] without anticoagulant treatment
