Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Subjects with adhesive capsulitis diagnosis by physical examination [AND] compared with intact side, there is more than 25% limitation in at least two directions of shoulder range of motion
  -  Subjects have complaints [OR] have had complaints for at least 1 month
  -  [NOT] pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History of [NOT] bilateral adhesive capsulitis
  -  History of [OR] shoulder trauma, fracture, shoulder surgery, calcific tendinopathy, glenohumeral osteoarthritis, inflammatory rheumatic diseases, tumor, infection
  -  History of corticosteroid injection in the shoulder [NOT] during the last 3 months
  -  History of [OR] recent lung, breast or bypass surgery
  -  History of [OR] cervical radiculopathy [OR] brachial plexus lesion
  -  History of [NOT] neuromuscular disease
  -  History of physical therapy program for the same shoulder [NOT] in the last 6 months
