Inclusion Criteria:
  - > 50 years old [AND] available for follow-up
  - Bilateral age-related cataract [OR] other cataract types
  - Qualify for bilateral implantation within 1 month [AND] have the necessary IOL dioptre range (18 to 27D)
  - [NOT] have previous refractive or ocular surgery
  - Expected postoperative astigmatism ≤ 0.75D [OR] acceptable astigmatism

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Pupil > 4 mm [NOT] dilate under mesopic/scotopic conditions
  - Occupation requiring night-time driving [OR] any occupation incompatible with multifocality
  - Acute or chronic disease [OR] illness that would increase risk or confound study results, such as [NOT] diabetes mellitus (with retinopathy), immunocompromised, glaucoma, epiretinal membrane, cornea guttata, etc…, found to be relevant by the investigators
  - Amblyopia [OR] strabismus
  - Extremely shallow anterior chamber [OR] capsule or zonular abnormalities that may affect postoperative centration or tilt of the lens, such as pseudoexfoliation syndrome, uveitis, Marfan's syndrome
  - Pupil abnormalities, including non-reactive, tonic pupils, abnormally shaped pupils [OR] pupils that do not dilate under mesopic/scotopic conditions
  - [NOT] stable to place the intraocular lens safely at the location planned
  - Pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding in women of reproductive age [NOT] willing to take a pregnancy test pre-operatively
