<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. demonstrate readiness to physical activity by completing the online Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire ([AND] [NOT] free from any medical conditions that would prevent physical activity);
  2. have a SCI resulting in incomplete or complete ([AND] C5 and below) paraplegia or tetraplegia;
  3. have broadband internet access [OR] alternative internet access methods;
  4. between the ages of 18 [AND] 65 years;
  5. have the ability to converse [AND] read English.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. significant visual impairment [NOT] correctable with glasses or contact lenses that prevents seeing a computer screen to follow a home exercise program;
  2. active pressure ulcer [OR] open wound;
  3. any contraindications to exercise based on the American College of Sports Medicine guidelines [OR] other medical guidelines;
  4. significant hearing impairment [NOT] correctable with hearing aids that impede ability to hear music to engage in exercise.
