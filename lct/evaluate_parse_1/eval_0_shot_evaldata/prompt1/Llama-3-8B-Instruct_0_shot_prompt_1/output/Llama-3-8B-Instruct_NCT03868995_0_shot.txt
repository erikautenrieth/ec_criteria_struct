Inclusion Criteria:
  1. 20-70 age
  2. Chronic ankle sprain [OR] more than 3 months with chronic ankle pain [OR] instability sensation
  3. Ultrasound diagnosed anterior talofibular ligament sprain [AND] with chronic ankle pain or instability sensation

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] Acute ankle sprain [OR] less than 3 months
  2. Lower limb fracture history [AND] with history of previous ankle sprain
  3. [NOT] Cognitive impairment [OR] other neurological disorders [OR] muscular disorders
  4. [NOT] Other neurological or muscular disorders [AND] with chronic ankle pain or instability sensation
  5. [NOT] Severe pain [OR] unable to tolerate examination
