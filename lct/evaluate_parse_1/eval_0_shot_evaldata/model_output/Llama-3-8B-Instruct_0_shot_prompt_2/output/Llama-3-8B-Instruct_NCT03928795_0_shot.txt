Inclusion Criteria:
  - Monofetal pregnancy [AND] age over 18 years old [AND] term pregnancy ≥ 37SA [AND] indication for elective caesarean section
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] fetal pathology known prenatally like intrauterine severe growth retardation with Doppler abnormalities, fetal malformation or a known fetal genetic defect [OR] anomaly of placental adhesion
  - caesarean in a context of medical or obstetric emergency [OR] caesarean section performed under general anesthesia following failure of locoregional anesthesia
  - refusal of the woman to participate in the study [OR] impossibility or failure to study the neonatal acid-base balance on the umbilical cord
