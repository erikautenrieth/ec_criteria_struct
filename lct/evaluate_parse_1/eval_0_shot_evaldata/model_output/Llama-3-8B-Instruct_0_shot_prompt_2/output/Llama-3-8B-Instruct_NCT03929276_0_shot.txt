  - Subjects [AND] with adhesive capsulitis diagnosis by physical examination [OR] Compared with intact side, there is more than 25% limitation in at least two directions of shoulder range of motion
  - Subjects [AND] have complaints [OR] for at least 1 month
  - Subjects [NOT] pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding

Exclusion Criteria:
  - History [NOT] of bilateral adhesive capsulitis
  - History [OR] of shoulder trauma [OR] fracture [OR] shoulder surgery [OR] calcific tendinopathy [OR] glenohumeral osteoarthritis [OR] inflammatory rheumatic diseases [OR] tumor [OR] infection
  - History [OR] of corticosteroid injection in the shoulder [AND] during the last 3 months
  - History [OR] of recent lung [OR] breast [OR] bypass surgery
  - History [OR] of cervical radiculopathy [OR] brachial plexus lesion
  - History [OR] of neuromuscular disease
  - History [OR] of physical therapy program [AND] for the same shoulder [AND] in the last 6 months
