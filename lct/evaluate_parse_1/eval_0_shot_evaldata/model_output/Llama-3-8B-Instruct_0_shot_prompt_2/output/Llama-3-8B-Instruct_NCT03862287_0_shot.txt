<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - ASA [AND] I-III male [OR] female patients aged between 18 [OR] to 80 years old, who will receive spinal anesthesia for surgeries lasting less than one hour.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [NOT] younger than 18 years old.
  - ASA physical status >3.
  - Immunosuppression [OR] high risk of infection.
  - Contraindications [AND] to receiving spinal anesthesia (e.g. coagulation impairment).
  - Patients with psychosis.
  - Patients with preexisting back pain.
  - Patients with cognitive impairment.
  - Allergies [OR] to local anesthetics, analgesics [OR] any medication used in the study.
  - Patients with chronic regular opioid usage.
  - Presence of preexisting neurological symptoms.
