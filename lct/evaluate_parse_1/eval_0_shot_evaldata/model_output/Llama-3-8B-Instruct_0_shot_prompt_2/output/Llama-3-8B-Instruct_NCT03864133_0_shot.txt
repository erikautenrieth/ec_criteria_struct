Inclusion Criteria:
  - Adults [AND] age 55-90 years [OR] with visual significant cataracts in one [OR] both eyes.
  - Healthy individuals [AND] able to tolerate outpatient cataract surgery under local anesthesia [AND/OR] via either phacoemulsification [OR] femtosecond assisted cataract surgery. Well-controlled [NOT] diabetes, hypertension [OR] will be included.
  - Females of childbearing potential [AND] must agree to use a reliable method of birth control [AND] while participating in this study. Reliable methods of birth control are: abstinence, oral contraceptives, intrauterine device (IUD), DepoProvera, tubal ligation [OR] vasectomy of the partner (with confirmed negative sperm counts) [AND] in monogamous relationship (same partner). An acceptable, although less reliable, method involves the careful use of condoms [AND] spermicidal foam or gel [OR] cervical cap or sponge. A pregnancy test is required at least 10 days from the last normal menstrual period, if the patient is a sexually active female of childbearing potential.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Allergy [NOT] to Phenylephrine [OR] NSAIDs.
  - Inability [NOT] to sit steady and upright for the Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT).
  - Complications [NOT] during surgery, including posterior capsular rupture, vitreous loss, zonular dialysis, or iris trauma.
  - Macular thickness above 300 microns [NOT] at baseline.
  - Currently taking [NOT] a prostaglandin analogue.
  - Presence [NOT] of an epiretinal membrane on the preoperative OCT.
  - Retained lens fragment [NOT] post-operatively.
  - Inability [NOT] to return for follow appointments.
  - Female patients who are [NOT] pregnant, lactating or planning to become pregnant during the course of treatment.
