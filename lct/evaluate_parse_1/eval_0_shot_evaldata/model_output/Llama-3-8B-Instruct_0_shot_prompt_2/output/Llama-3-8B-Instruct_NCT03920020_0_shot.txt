<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - Diagnosis of primary knee osteoarthritis according to American College of Radiology [AND] diagnosis is not excluded
  - Being radiographically K-L grade 2-3 [OR] K-L grade 4
  - Presence of symptoms for at least 3 months [AND] symptoms are not absent
  - Patients [AND] who have been planned a conventional physiotherapy programme because of knee osteoarthritis [OR] who have been planned a rehabilitation programme
  - Presence of functional capacity for outpatient physical therapy [AND] functional capacity is not limited

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [NOT] with seconder knee osteoarthritis [OR] with knee osteoarthritis not confirmed
  - History of lower extremity [OR] spine surgery [AND] surgery is not a minor procedure
  - History of intraarticular injection in last 1 month [OR] last injection was more than 1 month ago
  - Patients with neurological disease [AND] disease will affect muscle strength, balance and coordination [OR] disease will not affect functional capacity
