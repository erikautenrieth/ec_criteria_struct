<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - Be a patient in the Bellevue Hospital Center ED [AND] be ≥18 years old.
  - Screen positive for homelessness risk using a homelessness risk screening tool (HRST) [OR] screen positive for unhealthy alcohol or drug use.
  - Able to provide consent [NOT] unable to take medicines.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Medically [NOT] critically ill [OR] psychiatrically unstable.
  - Unable to provide informed consent [NOT] due to cognitive deficit [OR] profound intoxication.
  - Incarcerated [OR] in police custody.
  - Unable to understand [AND] speak English.
  - Lives outside NYC [AND] cannot give a NYC ZIP code.
  - Already homeless [OR] residing in a shelter or on the streets.
  - Does not have a telephone [OR] cannot be reached for follow-up.
  - Has already received specialized peer navigator/addiction social worker services during current ED visit [OR] has already participated in the study.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
