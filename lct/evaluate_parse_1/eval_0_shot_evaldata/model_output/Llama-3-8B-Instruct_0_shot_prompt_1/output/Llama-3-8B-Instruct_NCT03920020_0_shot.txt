<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - Diagnosis of primary knee osteoarthritis according to American College of Radiology [AND] being radiographically K-L grade 2-3 [OR] 3.
  - Presence of symptoms for at least 3 months [AND] patients who have been planned a conventional physiotherapy programme because of knee osteoarthritis.
  - Presence of functional capacity for outpatient physical therapy [AND] patients who have been planned a conventional physiotherapy programme because of knee osteoarthritis.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Patients with secondary knee osteoarthritis.
  - History of lower extremity [OR] spine surgery.
  - History of intraarticular injection in the last 1 month [OR] absence of such history.
  - Patients with neurological disease that [NOT] will affect muscle strength, balance, and coordination.
