Inclusion Criteria:
  - Diagnosis of COPD confirmed by clinical examination [AND] a pulmonary function test
  - Stable clinical condition [AND] without recent respiratory system infections [OR] any exacerbation of the disease
  - Patients [NOT] unwilling to re-evaluate at the end of the study
  - Patients [NOT] unwilling to give written consent for their participation in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Age [NOT] less than 18 years old
  - Unstable [NOT] cardiac disease
  - Musculoskeletal [AND] neurological diseases that could affect exercise performance [OR] preclude exercise
  - Patients with COPD [OR] other serious disease in final stage
  - Significant [NOT] cognitive or psychiatric impairment that would lead to an inability to follow simple commands in a group setting
