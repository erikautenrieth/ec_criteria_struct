Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Age [AND] ≥18 years [AND] ≤ 90 years
  2. Tumor diagnosed as Glioblastoma Multiforme [OR] on MRI with distinct ring-like pattern of contrast enhancement with thick irregular walls and a core area reduced signal suggestive of tumour necrosis as assessed by the surgeon
  3. Tumors situated [OR] in or near eloquent areas; motor cortex, sensory cortex, subcortical pyramidal tract or speech areas as indicated on MRI (Sawaya Grading II and II)
  4. The tumor is [NOT] unsuitable for resection (according to neurosurgeon)
  5. Karnofsky performance scale [AND] 80 or more
  6. Written [NOT] absent Informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Tumors of the cerebellum, brain stem or midline [NOT] allowed
  2. Multifocal contrast enhancing lesions [NOT] allowed
  3. Substantial non-contrast enhancing tumor areas suggesting low grade gliomas with malignant transformation [NOT] allowed
  4. Medical reasons [NOT] absent precluding MRI (eg, pacemaker)
  5. [NOT] able to give consent because of or language barrier
  6. Psychiatric history [NOT] excluded
  7. Previous brain tumour surgery [NOT] allowed
  8. Previous low-grade glioma [NOT] allowed
  9. Second primary malignancy within the past 5 years [NOT] allowed with the exception of adequately treated in situ carcinoma of any organ or basal cell carcinoma of the skin
  10. Severe aphasia or dysphasia [NOT] allowed
