<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - ASA I-III [AND] male and female patients aged between 18 to 80 years old, who will receive spinal anesthesia for surgeries lasting less than one hour.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [NOT] younger than 18 years old.
  - ASA physical status >3 [OR] high risk of infection.
  - Immunosuppression [OR] contraindications to receiving spinal anesthesia (e.g. coagulation impairment).
  - Patients with [OR] psychosis, preexisting back pain, cognitive impairment, chronic regular opioid usage, or presence of preexisting neurological symptoms.
  - Allergies to local anesthetics, analgesics, or any medication used in the study [NOT] excluded.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
