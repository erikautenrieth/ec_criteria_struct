<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - Vaginal delivery [AND] an infant age of 6 months (±3 weeks)
  - Mother ≥15 years of age [AND] infant still breastfeeding
  - Anticipated residence in the area for the study duration [OR] for a shorter period

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] inability to provide informed consent
  - Hemoglobin < 70 g/L [AND] Z scores for weight-for-age (WAZ) or weight-for-height (WHZ) <3
  - [NOT] any maternal or infant chronic illness [OR] active substance abuse
  - Administration of any infant vitamin or mineral supplements for the past 2 months [OR] any history of infant antibiotic treatment [OR] infant with brain disease (i.e. psychosis, stroke with functional impairment, dementia)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
