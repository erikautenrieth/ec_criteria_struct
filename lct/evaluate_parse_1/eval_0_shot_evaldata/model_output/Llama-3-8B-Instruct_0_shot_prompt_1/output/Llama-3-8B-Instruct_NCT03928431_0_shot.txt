Inclusion Criteria:
  - Infants of healthy mothers with uncomplicated pregnancies at term, mothers of any ethnic or social background [AND] that can speak, read, [AND] understand Swedish to the extent that they can consent in Swedish.
  - An additional inclusion criterion for mothers in the CS groups is vaginal pH [OR] ≤ 4 at the time of birth.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Infants of mothers with pre-eclampsia [NOT] or complicated pregnancies, mothers [NOT] <18 years [OR] >40 years of age, genital herpes simplex, genital warts, HIV, Hepatitis B [OR] complications during delivery, infants delivered prior to 37 weeks of gestation.
  - Additional exclusion criteria for mothers in the CS group: Mothers positive to Group B Streptococcus [OR] Group A Streptococcus, bacterial vaginosis, vaginal pH [NOT] > 4 at the time of birth. Mothers with fecal samples positive for Salmonella, Shigella, Campylobacter [OR] Yersinia.
