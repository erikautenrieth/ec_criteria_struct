<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
- Critically ill cirrhotics [AND] early onset septic shock (<24 hours) [AND] norepinephrine of more than 10 ug/min [AND] lactate >4 mmol/L.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients [WITH] age <18 years
  -  Severe known cardiopulmonary disease ([AND] structural [OR] valvular heart disease, [OR] coronary artery disease, [OR] COPD)
  -  Severe coagulopathy ([AND] platelets <20,000 [AND] INR >5)
  -  Active Bleed ([OR] Mucosal [OR] variceal)
  -  Pregnancy
  -  Chronic kidney disease
  -  Extremely moribund patients [WITH] an expected life expectancy of less than 24 hours
  -  [NOT] able to give informed consent from family members
  -  Hemodynamic instability [WITH] very high dose of vasopressors, [OR] late presentation [OR] spontaneous reversal of shock
  -  Patient enrolled in other clinical trials
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
