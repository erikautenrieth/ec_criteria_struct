<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age 45-79 years
  -  Estimated glomerular filtration rate 20-60mL/min/1.73m2 based on CKD-EPI(Cr) equation
  -  Non-dialysis dependent (NDD) [AND] having no history of dialysis

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Autosomal recessive polycystic kidney disease [OR] Autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease
  -  [NOT] Able [OR] willing to give consent
  -  Previously received chronic dialysis
  -  Previous [OR] current any organ transplant
  -  Patients who diagnosed heart failure [AND] NYHA class 3 [OR] 4
  -  Known liver cirrhosis [AND] Child-pugh class 2 [OR] 3
  -  Pregnant women
  -  Single kidney [AND] due to trauma [OR] donation
  -  Patients who received immunosuppressive agent within the past 1 year
  -  Patients who diagnosed [AND] confirmed with glomerulonephritis through kidney biopsy

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
