Inclusion Criteria:
  - Major Depressive Disorder according to Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (DSM-V) [AND]
  - > 25 years [OR]
  - Sufficient understanding of the German language

Exclusion Criteria:
  - < 25 years [NOT]
  - Concomitant diagnosis of past or present psychotic disorder [OR]
  - Concomitant diagnosis of past or present bipolar disorder [OR]
  - First degree relative with a psychotic disorder [OR]
  - Unable [OR] or unwilling to discontinue antidepressant medication
  - Pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding
  - Known hypersensitivity to LSD [NOT]
  - Somatic disorders including central nervous system (CNS) involvement [NOT]
  - Known or suspected non-compliance, drug or alcohol abuse [NOT]
  - Metal implants [NOT]
  - Weight < 42 kg [NOT]
  - Suicide risk [OR] or very likely to require psychiatric hospitalisation
