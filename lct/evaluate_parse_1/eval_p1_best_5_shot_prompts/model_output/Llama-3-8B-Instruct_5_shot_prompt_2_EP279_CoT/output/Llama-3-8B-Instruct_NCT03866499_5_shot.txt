Inclusion Criteria:
  - Histologically [OR] or cytologically confirmed non-small cell lung cancer.
  - The ECOG score of performance status is 0 [AND] 1.
  - Locally advanced [OR] or recurrent metastatic NSCLC that has never received systemic treatment.
  - According to RECIST1.1 criteria, there is at least 1 measurable lesion that has not been previously irradiated [OR] without irradiation.
  - Prior to enrollment, a central laboratory testing report has confirmed that the tumor has one of two common EGFR mutations positive [AND] that are sensitive to EGFR-TKI therapy, accompanied [OR] or not accompanied with other EGFR mutation, with the exception of exon 20 insertion.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Previously received systemic treatment for locally advanced [OR] or recurrent metastatic cancer [NOT].
  - Primary T790M mutation-positive patient [NOT].
  - Previous interstitial lung disease [OR] drug-induced interstitial lung disease [OR] radiation pneumonia requiring hormonal therapy [OR] or any clinically proven active interstitial lung disease [NOT].
  - Known active infections such as hepatitis B [OR] hepatitis C [OR] and human immunodeficiency virus [NOT].
  - Local radiation therapy is carried out within 1 week [OR] more than 30% bone marrow radiation therapy or extensive radiation therapy is performed within 4 weeks [NOT].
  - ≤ 4 weeks from major surgery [OR] ≤ 2 weeks from minor surgery [NOT].
