Inclusion Criteria:
  - age 18-90 [AND] acute ischemic [OR] hemorrhagic stroke (within 30 days)
  - consent to participate [AND] to anonymous processing of data
  - signing of informed consent for the participation in the sub-project (Additional inclusion criteria for Sub-project Neurophysiological Markers)
  - signing of informed consent for participation in the subproject (Additional inclusion criteria for Epigenetic subproject)

Exclusion Criteria:
 - stroke occurred [NOT] more than 30 days after the transfer to intensive rehabilitation
 - history of major psychiatric disorder [OR] recent exacerbation (Additional exclusion criteria for Epigenetic subproject for retrospective analysis)
 - global aphasia (Additional exclusion criteria for Epigenetic subproject for retrospective analysis)
 - refusal of the patient to participate in the study
