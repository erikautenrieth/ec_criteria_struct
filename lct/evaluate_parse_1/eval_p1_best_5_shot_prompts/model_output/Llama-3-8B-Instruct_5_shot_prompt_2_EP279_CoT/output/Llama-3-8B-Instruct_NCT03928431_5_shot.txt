Inclusion Criteria:
 - Infants of healthy mothers with uncomplicated pregnancies at term, mothers of any ethnic or social background [AND] that can speak, read, and understand Swedish to the extent that they can consent in Swedish. An additional inclusion criterion for mothers in the CS groups is vaginal pH [OR] ≤ 4 at the time of birth.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Infants of mothers with pre-eclampsia [OR] or complicated pregnancies, mothers [NOT] <18 years [OR] >40 years of age, genital herpes simplex, genital warts, HIV, Hepatitis B [OR] complications during delivery, infants delivered prior to 37 weeks of gestation. Additional exclusion criteria for mothers in the CS group: Mothers [NOT] positive to Group B Streptococcus, Group A Streptococcus, bacterial vaginosis, vaginal pH [NOT] > 4 at the time of birth. Mothers with fecal samples [NOT] positive for Salmonella, Shigella, Campylobacter [OR] Yersinia.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
