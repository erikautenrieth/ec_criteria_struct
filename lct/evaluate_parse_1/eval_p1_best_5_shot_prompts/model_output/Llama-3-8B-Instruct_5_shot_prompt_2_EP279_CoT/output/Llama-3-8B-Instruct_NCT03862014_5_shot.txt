Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [AND] parents of the patients who accept to participate [AND] sign the informed consent
  - Patients whose molar teeth are diagnosed as molar incisor hypomineralization by EAPD [OR] other dental specialists
  - Teeth that are fully erupted [AND] without any signs of tooth decay
  - Patients who have at least two permanent first molars that need fissure sealant [AND] are willing to receive the treatment
  - Teeth that have white-yellow-brown discoloration [OR] other signs of high-caries risk

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Teeth that are previously restored [OR] have caries [OR] other signs of tooth decay
  - Patients who have orthodontic treatment [OR] are currently undergoing orthodontic treatment
  - Patients who are not cooperative for the dental procedure [OR] are unable to follow the treatment instructions
  - Teeth that have dental fluorosis [OR] enamel malformation [OR] other dental defects according to the specific syndromes
