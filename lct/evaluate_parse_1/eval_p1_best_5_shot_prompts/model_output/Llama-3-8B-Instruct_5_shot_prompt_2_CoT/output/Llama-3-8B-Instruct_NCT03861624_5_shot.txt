Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. Skeletal maturity
 2. AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures
 3. Wound primarily [OR] closeable (no flap or delay in closure due to contamination needed)
 4. Palpable pedal pulses [AND] (no vascular injury sustained)
 5. Presentation within 24 hours from injury
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. Current injury is a [NOT] pathologic fracture
 2. Sustained bilateral tibia fracture
 3. Sustained comminuted femur fracture
 4. Sustained severe Traumatic Brain Injury (GCS<12) [OR] ***
 5. Sustained severe spinal cord injury (lower extremity paresis/paralysis)
 6. Sustained severe burns (>10% total body surface area (TBSA) [OR] or >5% TBSA with full thickness [OR] or circumferential injury)
 7. Prior ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery
 8. Prior or current lower limb deformity [OR] or abnormality
 9. [NOT] able to complete follow-up visits
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
