Inclusion Criteria:
  - Diagnosis of primary knee osteoarthritis according to American College of Radiology [AND] being radiographically K-L grade 2-3 [OR] 3;
  - Presence of symptoms for at least 3 months;
  - Patients who have been planned a conventional physiotherapy programme because of knee osteoarthritis;
  - Presence of functional capacity for outpatient physical therapy.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients with [NOT] secondary knee osteoarthritis;
  - History of lower extremity [OR] spine surgery;
  - History of intraarticular injection in last 1 month;
  - Patients with neurological disease that [NOT] will affect muscle strength, balance, and coordination.
