Inclusion Criteria:
 - Receiving HHD [OR] or ICHDF for more than 3 months [AND] and less than 36 months.
 - Haemoglobin equal to [OR] or greater than 100 g/L at enrolment.
 - Willing [AND] physically able to undertake the study assessments/tests
 - Willing to provide blood for storage [AND] future analysis
 - Able to give informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Living donor transplant [OR] or change to peritoneal dialysis planned
 - Physical assessments contraindicated for the following clinical reasons:
       - Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS) within the last 3 months ([OR] chest pain, ECG changes or typical biomarker elevation).
       - Any current uncontrolled cardiac dysrhythmias causing symptoms ([OR] chest pain, palpitations, syncope or dizziness)
       - Symptomatic aortic stenosis
       - New York Heart Association grade IV Heart failure
       - Severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease
       - Acute pulmonary embolus [OR] or pulmonary infarction in the last 3 months
       - Current acute myocarditis [OR] or pericarditis
       - Suspected [OR] or known dissecting aneurysm
       - Acute systemic infection, accompanied by fever, body aches [OR] or swollen lymph glands
 - Pregnancy
 - Life expectancy of [NOT] less than twelve months
