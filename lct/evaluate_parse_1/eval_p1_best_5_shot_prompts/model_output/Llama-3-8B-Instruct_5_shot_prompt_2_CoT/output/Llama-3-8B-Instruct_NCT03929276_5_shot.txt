<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Subjects with adhesive capsulitis diagnosis by physical examination [AND] (Compared with intact side, there is more than 25% limitation in at least two directions of shoulder range of motion)
  - Subjects have complaints for at least 1 month
  - No [NOT] pregnancy / breastfeeding

Exclusion Criteria:
  - History of bilateral adhesive capsulitis
  - History of shoulder trauma, fracture, shoulder surgery, calcific tendinopathy, glenohumeral osteoarthritis, inflammatory rheumatic diseases, tumor, infection [OR] infection
  - History of corticosteroid injection in the shoulder during the last 3 months
  - History of recent lung, breast or bypass surgery
  - History of cervical radiculopathy [OR] brachial plexus lesion
  - History of neuromuscular disease
  - History of physical therapy program for the same shoulder in the last 6 months
