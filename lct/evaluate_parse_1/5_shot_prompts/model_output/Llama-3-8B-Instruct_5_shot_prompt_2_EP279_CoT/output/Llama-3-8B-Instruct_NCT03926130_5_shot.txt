<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of CD for at least 3 months prior to baseline [AND] and a confirmed diagnosis of moderate to severe CD as assessed by SF, AP score, and SES-CD [OR] and demonstrated intolerance, loss of response or inadequate response to conventional or to biologic therapy for CD [AND] and if female, subject must meet the contraception recommendations.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Have a current diagnosis of ulcerative colitis [OR] inflammatory bowel disease-unclassified (IBD-U) (formerly known as indeterminate colitis) [OR] abdominal or perianal abscess [OR] short bowel syndrome [NOT] and have a stoma or ostomy [AND] and have had a bowel resection within 6 months [OR] any kind of intra-abdominal or extra abdominal surgery within 3 months of baseline [AND] and have ever received any monoclonal antibodies binding IL-23.
