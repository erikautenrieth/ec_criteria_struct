Inclusion Criteria:
 - Cochlear Nucleus cochlear implant users [OR] or Advanced Bionics cochlear implant users
 - Native speakers of English
 - Early onset of deafness [AND] (< 3 years of age); no requirement for age at implantation
 - Late onset of deafness [AND] (> 3 years of age); matched in duration of deafness to the early onset group
 - Has had device experience [AND] for at least one year
 - Can be child [OR] or adult at the time of enrollment

Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] None
