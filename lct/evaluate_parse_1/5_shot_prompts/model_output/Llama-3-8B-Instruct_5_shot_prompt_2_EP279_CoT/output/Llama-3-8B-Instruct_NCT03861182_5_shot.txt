Inclusion Criteria:

Adult Healthy Volunteers:
- Over 18 years of age
Neonates:
  - Aged between 1h of life [AND] and 28 days of life
  - Born beyond 33 Week of Amenorrhea [OR] + 1day [AND] and 2kg of birth weight
  - Hospitalized in the medical-surgical centre of Pediatrics of the hospital of Strasbourg
  - For whom a blood sample was prescribed as part of their routine care [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:

Adult Healthy Volunteers:
  - Systemic [NOT] inflammatory disease
  - Transient [NOT] inflammatory state
  - Any drug that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h [OR] preceding the sampling
Neonates:
  - Risk of anemia [NOT] < 7g/DL
  - Current [NOT] anticoagulant treatment
