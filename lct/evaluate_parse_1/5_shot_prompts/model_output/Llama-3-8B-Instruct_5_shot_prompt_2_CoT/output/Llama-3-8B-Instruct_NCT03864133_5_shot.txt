Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
  - Adults [AND] age 55-90 years [AND] with visual significant cataracts [OR] in one [OR] both eyes.
  - Healthy individuals [AND] able to tolerate outpatient cataract surgery [AND] under local anesthesia [AND] via either phacoemulsification [OR] and/or femtosecond assisted cataract surgery. Well-controlled diabetes [OR] hypertension will be included.
  - Females of childbearing potential [AND] must agree to use a reliable method of birth control [AND] while participating in this study. Reliable methods of birth control are: abstinence [OR] oral contraceptives [OR] intrauterine device (IUD) [OR] DepoProvera [OR] tubal ligation [OR] vasectomy of the partner (with confirmed negative sperm counts) [AND] in monogamous relationship (same partner). An acceptable, although less reliable, method involves the careful use of condoms [AND] spermicidal foam or gel [OR] cervical cap [OR] sponge. A pregnancy test is required at least 10 days from the last normal menstrual period, if the patient is a sexually active female of childbearing potential.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
  - Allergy [NOT] to Phenylephrine [OR] NSAIDs.

  - Female patients [NOT] who are pregnant [OR] lactating [OR] planning to become pregnant during the course of treatment.
