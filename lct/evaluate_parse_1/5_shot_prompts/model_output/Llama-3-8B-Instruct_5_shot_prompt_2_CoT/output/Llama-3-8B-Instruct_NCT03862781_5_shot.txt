Inclusion Criteria:
  - Eligible patients are those [AND] over 18 years of age [AND] who are regularly scheduled to undergo a right hemicolectomy via a minimally invasive approach (robotic [OR] or laparoscopic) with the creation of an anastomosis.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Pregnant [NOT] women
  - Additional colon resection is planned [AND] (i.e. left colectomy [OR] or proctectomy)
  - Vulnerable populations such as prisoners [OR] or adults [NOT] able to give consent
  - If the scheduled surgery is planned at Butterworth hospital [AND] (as to standardize the nursing care received postoperatively)
  - Emergent cases
Patients will be excluded from the study intra-operatively if:
  - The procedure is converted to an open resection [NOT]
  - If a loop ileostomy is performed [AND] in addition to the right colectomy
