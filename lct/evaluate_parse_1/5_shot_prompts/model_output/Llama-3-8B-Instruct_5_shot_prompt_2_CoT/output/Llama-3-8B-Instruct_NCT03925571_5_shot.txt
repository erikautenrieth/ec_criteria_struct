Inclusion Criteria:
 - Majors [AND] aged between 18 [AND] 55 years, with an appropriate [AND] hearing [AND] able to use Music-Care© (selection of the playlist via internet access),
 - Needing treatment in odontology - periodontal surgery [OR] coronal elongation [OR] or sanitation surgery [OR] muco-gingival surgery [OR] implantary surgery (placement of an unit implant [OR] or two implant in the same localization),
 - Oral [AND] dated consent,
 - Agreeing to be contacted via phone at J+1 [AND] to fill follow-up study documents,
 - Profiting of social security [OR] CMU.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [NOT] with a long-term taking of anxiolytic [OR] analgesic,
 - Regular consumer of soft drug (cannabis) [OR] hard drug (opioid),
 - Patient [NOT] with medical history of psychiatry (mental harm) [OR] hearing problem [OR] cognitive and behavioural impairment (claustrophobia) [OR] sensory disabilities,
 - Patient [NOT] with problems of hemostasis,
 - Majors [NOT] under trusteeship [OR] guardianship,
 - Pregnant women [OR] lactating,
 - Minors,
 - Protected person, deprived of freedom [OR] under justice safeguard,
 - Profiting of a medical help from government (AME),
 - Not contactable after care,
 - Patient [NOT] with hepatic impairment,
 - Patient [NOT] with dry mouth,
 - Patient [NOT] relevant a suboptimal hearing incompatible with musicotherapy [OR] inability to use Music-care®.
