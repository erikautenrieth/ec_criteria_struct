Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Admitted from the emergency department to inpatient [OR] or observation status at one of:
 Carolinas Medical Center, Carolinas Medical Center - Mercy, [OR] or Atrium Health Northeast;
 2. ≥18 years of age upon admission;
 3. oral/ [OR] parenteral antibiotic [OR] or bacterial culture order within 24 hours of emergency department presentation and
 1. culture drawn first, antibiotics ordered within 48 hours [OR] or
 2. antibiotics ordered first, culture ordered within 48 hours (adapted from criteria applied in development of the Third International Consensus Definitions for Sepsis [OR] and Septic Shock)
 4. deemed as high-risk for 30-day readmission (i.e., ≥ 20%) [OR] or 30-day mortality (i.e., ≥ 10%) using risk-scoring models
 5. [NOT] not discharged at the time of patient list generation
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. prior randomization to either STAR [OR] or usual care study arms;
 2. [NOT] not a North Carolina resident [OR] or residence >2.5-hour drive time from treating hospital;
 3. the only antibiotic associated with patient is administered in the operating room as this likely represents pre-operative infection prophylaxis [AND] and [NOT] not presumed infection;
 4. patients transferred from other acute care hospitals;
 5. patients with infection ruled out during the index hospitalization.
