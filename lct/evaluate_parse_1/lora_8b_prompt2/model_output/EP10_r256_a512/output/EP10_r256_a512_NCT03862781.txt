Inclusion Criteria:
 - Eligible patients are those over 18 years of age [AND] who are regularly scheduled to undergo a right hemicolectomy via a minimally invasive approach (robotic [OR] or laparoscopic) with the creation of an anastomosis. Right colectomy will be defined as removal of the ascending colon, [OR] ligation of the ileocolic artery [OR] and vein, +/- removal of the terminal ileum, [OR] +/- removal of the proximal transverse colon, [OR] and +/- removal of the right branch of the middle colic artery [OR] and vein.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnant women
 - Additional colon resection is planned (i.e. left colectomy [OR] or proctectomy)
 - Vulnerable populations such as prisoners [OR] or adults unable to give consent
 - If the scheduled surgery is planned at Butterworth hospital (as to standardize the nursing care received postoperatively)
 - Emergent cases
Patients will be [NOT] excluded from the study intra-operatively if:
 - The procedure is converted to an open resection
 - If a loop ileostomy is performed in addition to the right colectomy
