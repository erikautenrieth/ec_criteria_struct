Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adults between age 18 and 75
 - Participants [NOT] without a history of gastrointestinal disorders (control group)
 - Participants with functional dyspepsia (FD group)
 - Participants with moderate- [OR] to-severe irritable bowel syndrome (defined by IBS symptom severity scores (IBS-SSS) greater than 250) (IBS group)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - history of myocardial infarction, [OR] supplemental oxygen requirement, [OR] or diabetes
 - history of chronic gastrointestinal symptoms (for healthy control population only)
 - history of gastric surgery
 - psychosis [OR] or altered cognitive status
 - history of head injury, [OR] metal in the skull, [OR] stroke, [OR] or a history of seizures
 - implantable devices, such as a pacemaker [OR] or nerve stimulator
 - current use of the following medications [OR] or substances which are known to lower the seizure threshold: amantadine, [OR] apathy, [OR] benzodiazepines, [OR] baclofen, [OR] nicotine
 - history of alcohol abuse [OR] or binge drinking
 - pregnancy
