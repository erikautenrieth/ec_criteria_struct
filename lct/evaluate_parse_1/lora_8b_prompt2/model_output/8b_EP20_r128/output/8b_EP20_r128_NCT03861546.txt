Inclusion Criteria:
 1. age > 18 years;
 2. confirmed diagnosis of prediabetes [OR] or T2D (documented A1c of ≥ 5.7%, 2-hour post-load glucose of >140, [OR] or FBG of > 100);
 3. a family member [OR] or peer willing to participate and attend all sessions as a social influencer;
 4. proficiency in English of at least one member of each dyad,
 5. willingness to provide written consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. type 1 diabetes [OR] or diabetes secondary to other conditions (e.g. steroid-induced, [OR] pancreatic insufficiency, [OR] or chemotherapy-induced);
 2. malignancy [OR] or life-threatening illness with life expectancy of <5 years;
 3. end-stage disease [OR] or serious illness that [NOT] prohibits participation (e.g. end-stage renal disease [OR] or class IV congestive heart failure);
 4. [NOT] inability to perform unsupervised physical activity;
 5. pregnancy;
 6. diagnosed cognitive deficits [OR] or limited decision-making capacity;
 7. alcohol [OR] or substance abuse;
 8. homelessness [OR] or no fixed address.
