Inclusion Criteria:
 - Persons diagnosed with pharyngeal gonorrhea [OR] or gonococcal urethritis who are undergoing pharyngeal gonorrhea testing, who are [NOT] not yet treated.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Age less than 16 years
 - Receipt of antibiotics in ≤30 days
 - Known allergy to aztreonam
 - History of renal disease (including diagnosis of solitary kidney, [OR] chronic renal insufficiency, renal cell carcinoma etc.)
 - Concurrent infection with syphilis [OR] or chlamydia
 - Pregnancy [OR] and/or nursing
 - Unable to return for a follow-up visit 4-7 days (+/- 1 day).
 - Study team's discretion.
