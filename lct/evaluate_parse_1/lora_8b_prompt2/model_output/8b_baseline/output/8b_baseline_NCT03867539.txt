Inclusion Criteria:
 - carpal tunnel syndrome diagnosed either by physical exam or EMG
 - age >18.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - allergy to bupivacaine
 - pre-operative opioid usage within the last six months
 - patients [AND] allergic to Percocet [OR] or acetaminophen
 - patients taking tricyclic antidepressants (interaction with bupivacaine)
 - patients [NOT] taking prisoners [OR] or pregnant women
 - vulnerable populations (prisoners [OR] or pregnant women)
