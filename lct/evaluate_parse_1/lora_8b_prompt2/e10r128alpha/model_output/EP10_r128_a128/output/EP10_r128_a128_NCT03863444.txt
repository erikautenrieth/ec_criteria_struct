Inclusion Criteria:
 - (1) gestational age 35 to 39 weeks
 - (2) adults
 - (3) primipara [OR] and their spouses
 - (4) having routine prenatal visits [AND] and plan to have the birth in the medical center
 - (5) able to communicate with Mandarin [OR] or Taiwanese
 - (6) willing to participate in this study and give written inform-consent forms
Exclusion Criteria:
 - (1) having fetus with abnormality
 - (2) high-risk pregnancy
 - (3) [NOT] cannot room-in due to newborn's medical conditions
