Inclusion Criteria:
 - Ulcerative colitis [AND] with confirmed diagnosis by histology [OR] and endoscopy [AND] AND in stable remission for 3 months or more [NOT] without therapy [OR] or with a maintenance therapy (except steroids [OR] and antibiotics for 3 months)
 - A negative pregnancy test
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] No history [OR] or diagnosis of any of the following conditions: Chron's disease, [OR] bleeding disorders, [OR] gastrointestinal [OR] or colonic malignancy, [OR] Kidney disease, including kidney "stones" [OR] or hypercalcemia, [OR] Coagulopathy/ [OR] hereditary hemorrhagic disorders/ [OR] or receiving therapeutic doses of Coumadin [OR] or heparin.
 - Ingested certain medications (Calcium, Vitamin D, [OR] fiber supplements [OR] and Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs - NSAIDs) within 30 days of study start.
