Inclusion Criteria:
 - > 50 years old
 - Bilateral age-related cataract
 - Qualify for bilateral implantation within 1 month
 - [NOT] No previous refractive [OR] or ocular surgery
 - Available IOL dioptre range: 18 to 27D
 - Expected postoperative astigmatism ≤ 0.75D
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pupil > 4 mm
 - Occupation requiring night-time driving [OR] or any occupation [NOT] incompatible with multifocality
 - Acute [OR] or chronic disease [OR] or illness that would increase risk [OR] or confound study results [e.g. diabetes mellitus (with retinopathy), immunocompromised, [OR] glaucoma, [OR] epiretinal membrane, [OR] cornea guttata, etc…] found to be relevant by the investigators
 - Amblyopia, [OR] strabismus
 - Extremely shallow anterior chamber
 - Capsule [OR] or zonular abnormalities that may affect postoperative centration [OR] or tilt of the lens (e.g. pseudoexfoliation syndrome, [OR] uveitis, [OR] Marfan's syndrome)
 - Pupil abnormalities ([NOT] non-reactive, [OR] tonic pupils, [OR] abnormally shaped pupils [OR] or pupils that do not dilate under mesopic/ [OR] scotopic conditions)
 - Instability to place the intraocular lens safely at the location planned
 - Pregnancy (pregnancy test will be taken pre-operatively in women [AND] of reproductive age
