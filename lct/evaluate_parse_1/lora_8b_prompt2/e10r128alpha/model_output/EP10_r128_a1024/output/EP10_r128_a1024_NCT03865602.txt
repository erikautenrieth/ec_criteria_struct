Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Admitted from the emergency department to inpatient [OR] or observation status at one of:
 Carolinas Medical Center, Carolinas Medical Center - Mercy, [OR] or Atrium Health Northeast;
 2. ≥18 years of age upon admission;
 3. oral/ [OR] parenteral antibiotic [OR] or bacterial culture order within 24 hours of emergency department presentation and
 1. culture drawn first, antibiotics ordered within 48 hours [OR] or
 2. antibiotics ordered first, culture ordered within 48 hours (adapted from criteria applied in development of the Third International Consensus Definitions for Sepsis [OR] and Septic Shock)
 4. deemed as high-risk for 30-day readmission  (i.e., do not resuscitate, [OR] do not intubate) within 24 hours after admission due to the general assumption of increased risk of exposure to less aggressive treatment;
 6. patients transferred from other acute care hospitals;
 7. patients with a change in code status (i.e., do not resuscitate, [OR] do not intubate) within 24 hours after admission due to the general assumption of increased risk of exposure to less aggressive treatment;
 8. patients with infections ruled out during the index hospitalization.
