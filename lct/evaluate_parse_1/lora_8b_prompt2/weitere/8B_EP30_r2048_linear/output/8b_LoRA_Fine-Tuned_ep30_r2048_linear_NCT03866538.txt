Inclusion Criteria
 - Patients will be recruited from the Division of Gastroenterology [OR] and Hepatology [AND] and the Mayo Clinic in Rochester, MN.
 - Adult patients [AND] with immune mediated enteropathies [AND] who have had improvement in symptoms [AND] and histology on oral budesonide therapy.
Exclusion Criteria
 - Age <18 years
 - Positive stool gluten testing in patients [AND] with refractory celiac disease
 - Small bowel malignancy [OR] or history of small bowel malignancy
 - Refractory celiac disease type 2
 - Post-transplant lymphoproliferative disorder associated enteropathy
 - [NOT] No prior improvement in symptoms [OR] and histology with budesonide therapy
 - Discontinuation of budesonide therapy prior to the trial
 - Other concurrent systemic corticosteroids
 - Other immune mediating medications, for example but not limited to azathioprine, [OR] 6-mercaptopurine, [OR] cyclosporine, [OR] tofacitinib, [OR] mycophenolate mofetil, in addition to fexofenadine and triazolam.
 - Patients who are pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding.
 - Patients with renal disease.
 - Patients with prior treatment with any investigational drug within 4 weeks before the trial.
 - Patients who have received treatment with any approved or non- approved medication within 4 weeks before the trial.
 - Patients who have received any systemic (including oral) corticosteroids within 4 weeks before the trial.
 - Patients who have received any systemic (including oral) medication that can influence the results of the trial.
 - Patients who have received any concurrent or prior treatment that might confound the safety of the patients.
