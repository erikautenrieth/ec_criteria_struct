Inclusion Criteria:
Female subjects between 18 and 65 years of age currently taking spironolactone for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris
OR
Female subjects between 18 and 65 years of age currently taking oral antibiotics for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris
AND subjects able to complete an online survey in English
Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects less than 18 years of age [OR] or older than 65 years of age Subjects that have ever been diagnosed with any mood disorder by a clinician prior to starting spironolactone (history of mood disorder [OR] or current mood disorder) Subjects taking hormonal contraception (including but not limited to oral contraceptive pills) for a duration of less than 6 months Subjects that have ever been diagnosed with any cardiovascular, [OR] gastrointestinal, [OR] renal [OR] or endocrine disorder. Subjects with a history of hypersensitivity to any ingredient in spironolactone [OR] or probiotics. Subjects that are currently pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding. Subjects that are planning to start a new anti-depressant medication in the next 6 months. Subjects that are already taking anti-depressant medications. Subjects with a history of bipolar disorder [OR] or schizophrenia. Subjects that have a history of taking ketamine (including esketamine) within the last 6 months. Subjects that have participated in any clinical study of an investigational product within the last 6 months. Subjects who are not able to complete an online survey in English.
