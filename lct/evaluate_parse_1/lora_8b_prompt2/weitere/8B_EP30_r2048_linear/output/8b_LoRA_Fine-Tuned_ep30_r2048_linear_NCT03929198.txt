Inclusion Criteria:
 - Dysfunctional lipids, [OR] blood pressure [OR] and glycemic control determined at a screening visit:
 - Triglycerides > 150 mg/dL [OR] OR HDL <50 in females, <40 mg/dL in males
 - Systolic blood pressure  ≥130 mmHg [OR] OR diastolic blood pressure ≥80 mmHg (or on hypertension medication)
 - HbA1c ≥5.7% [OR] or higher
 - [NOT] No diagnosis [OR] or treatment for cancer in the past year
 - [NOT] No diagnosis [OR] of mild cognitive impairment [OR] or dementia in the past year
 - Ability to perform daily exercise, including aerobic activity [OR] and resistance exercise
 - [NOT] Non-smoker in the past year
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Positive exercise stress test
