Inclusion Criteria:
 - > 50 years old
 - Bilateral age-related cataract
 - Qualify for bilateral implantation within 1 month
 - [NOT] No previous refractive [OR] or ocular surgery
 - Available IOL dioptre range: 18 to 27D
 - Expected postoperative astigmatism ≤ 0.75D
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pupil > 4 mm
 - Occupation requiring night-time driving [OR] or any occupation [NOT] incompatible with multifocality
 - Acute [OR] or chronic disease [OR] or illness that would increase risk [OR] or confound study results
 - Pregnant [OR] or nursing women
 - Glaucoma, [OR] epiretinal membrane, [OR] corneal leukoplakia, [OR] cataract with expected survival < 6 months
 - Retinal disorders not associated with cataract
 - Visual acuity worse than 20/200
 - Inability to give informed consent
 - Proliferative diabetic retinopathy
 - Glaucoma [OR] or glaucoma suspicion
 - Optic disc fatigue
 - Optic disc edema
 - Uveitis [OR] and media opacity
 - Blurred of image clarity
