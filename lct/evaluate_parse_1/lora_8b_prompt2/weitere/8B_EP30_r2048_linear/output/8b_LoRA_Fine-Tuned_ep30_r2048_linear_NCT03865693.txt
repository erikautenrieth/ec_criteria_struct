Inclusion Criteria:
 - Burn patients
 - severe neuropatic pain rated at least 5 on the 10-point numerical rating scale (NRS)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - cardiac arrest history
 - history of neurologic disease [OR] or brain surgery
 - unstable heart disease [OR] or presence of a cardiac pacemaker
 - pain resulting from other causes like as neuromuscular diseases
 - psychiatric disorders
 - diabetes mellitus
 - abnormal renal function
 - contraindication for MRI, [OR] or pregnancy.
