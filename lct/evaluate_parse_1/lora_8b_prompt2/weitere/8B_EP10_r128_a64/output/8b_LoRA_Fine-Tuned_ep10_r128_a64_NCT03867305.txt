Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient requiring a first total knee prosthesis (scheduled between Tuesday and Friday).
 - Patient that has given his/her free and informed consent and signed the consent form.
 - The patient must be a member or beneficiary of a health insurance plan
 - The patient is at least 18 years old (≥) [AND] and under 90 years old (<).
Exclusion Criteria:
 - The subject is in a period of exclusion determined by a previous study (therapeutic study)
 - The subject refuses to sign the consent
 - It is impossible to give the subject informed information
 - The patient is under safeguard of justice or state guardianship
 - The patient is pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding
 - The patient is allergic to latex bandages.
 - The subject has contralateral amputation, [OR] pressure ulcers, severe Obstructive Arteriopathy of Lower Limbs, [OR] lymphoedema [OR] or thromboembolic event of the lower limb (ipsilateral [OR] and / or contralateral / superficial [OR] or deep [OR] or detected by postoperative Doppler).
 - The subject has chronic [OR] or active wounds [OR] or any cause that hinders normal rehabilitation [OR] or the application of a bandage.
 - patient with a stroke/ [OR] Disabling Neurological Pathology, Chronic Immunological Pathology/ [OR] Systemic Disease.
 - patient with Hepatic insufficiency.
 - patient with Venous insufficiency (stages 3 and 4).
 - The patient is on dialysis.
 - The subject has cognitive [OR] or behavioral disorders (Parkinson's disease, [OR] cerebellar syndrome, [OR] opposition, [OR] agitation, [OR] dementia).
 - The subject has a history of knee surgery.
