Inclusion Criteria:
 -  Female patients [AND] who have symptoms consistent with endometriosis such as pelvic pain, dysmenorrhea, deep dyspareunia, [AND] and infertility
Exclusion Criteria:
 -  The common contraindication to MRI, peacemaker [OR] metallic foreign bodies, [AND] claustrophobia.  Note: Since 'and' appears before 'pelvic pain', 'dysmenorrhea', 'deep dyspareunia' in the inclusion criteria, it is replaced with [AND]. Similarly, 'and' before 'claustrophobia' in the exclusion criteria is also replaced with [AND]. 'Or' appears after 'peacemaker' and'metallic foreign bodies' in the exclusion criteria, so it is replaced with [OR].
