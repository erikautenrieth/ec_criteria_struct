Inclusion Criteria:
Nursing home residents [AND] with ≥2 of the following risk factors for atrial fibrillation:
1. age ≥75 years
2. female sex
3. obstructive sleep apnea
4. peripheral vascular disease
5. diabetes mellitus
6. obesity (body mass index [BMI] >30 kg/m2)
7. hypertension
8. congestive heart failure
Exclusion Criteria:
1. prior diagnosis of AF
2. continuous rhythm monitoring over the past year
3. pacemaker [OR] or implantable cardioverter defibrillator in situ
