Inclusion Criteria:
Nursing home residents with ≥2 [OR] of the following risk factors for atrial fibrillation:
 - age [AND] ≥75 years
 - female [OR] sex
 - obstructive sleep apnea
 - peripheral vascular disease
 - diabetes mellitus
 - obesity (body mass index [BMI] >30 kg/m2) [OR] hypertension
 - congestive heart failure

Exclusion Criteria:
 - prior [NOT] diagnosis of AF
 - continuous rhythm monitoring over the past [NOT] year
 - pacemaker [OR] or implantable cardioverter defibrillator in situ
