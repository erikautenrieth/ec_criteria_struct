Inclusion Criteria:
  - CAC score >10
  - Framingham risk score (10 % [OR] above)
  - Subjects are required to be on stable concomitant medications for at least 12 weeks prior to randomization [AND] with no significant changes in the past 6 months
  - Subjects with diabetic must have HbA1C < 8.0, and stable HbA1C level variation range within 0.5% for three months

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Hypersensitivity to AGE therapy [NOT]
  - Unstable medical, psychiatric, or substance abuse disorder that may interfere with continuation in the study [OR] without a stable treatment plan
  - Weight ≥325 pounds [OR] or body mass index (BMI) ≥40
  - Bleeding disorder [OR] or history of bleeding events
  - History of myocardial infarction [OR] or unstable angina
  - Stroke [OR] or transient ischemic attack
  - Life-threatening arrhythmia within prior 6 months [OR] or active cardiac condition that may interfere with participation in the study
  - Resting hypotension (systolic < 90 mmHg) [OR] or hypertension (resting blood pressure >170/110) [OR] or recent change in blood pressure medication
  - Heart failure NYHA class III or IV [OR] or active cardiac condition that may interfere with participation in the study
  - History of malignancy within the last 5 years (other than skin cancer) [OR] or evidence of active cancer that would require concomitant cancer chemotherapy
  - Serum creatinine >1.4 mg/dl [OR] or creatinine clearance <60 mL/min
  - Triglycerides > 400 at baseline visit [OR] or recent change in lipid-lowering medication
  - Diabetic subjects with HbA1C > 8 % [OR] or unstable glycemic control
  - Drug or alcohol abuse [OR] or recent substance abuse treatment
  - Conditions interfering with accurate assessment of coronary calcification (metal clips, bypass patients, intracoronary stents) [OR] or drug absorption (partial ileal bypass or malabsorption syndrome)
  - Current use of anticoagulants (except for antiplatelet agents) [OR] or recent change in anticoagulant therapy
  - Chronic renal failure [OR] or liver failure
  - Hematological or biochemical values at baseline visit outside the reference ranges considered as clinically significant [OR] or recent change in medication that may affect these values.
