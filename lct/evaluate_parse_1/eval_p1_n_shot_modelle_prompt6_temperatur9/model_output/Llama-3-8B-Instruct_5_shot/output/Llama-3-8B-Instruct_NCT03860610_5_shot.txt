Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with an unilateral THA [OR] or TKA, which they received for the treatment of osteoarthritis (Patient groups 1 [AND] 2)
  -  Patients diagnosed with unilateral OA of the knee [OR] hip planned for a treatment with THA [OR] TKA (Patient groups 3 [AND] 4)
  -  Age ≥ 30 years [AND] (Healthy control group)

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Body mass index > 35kg/m2 [NOT]
  -  Use of walking aids [NOT]
  -  Neuromuscular disorders affecting gait [NOT]
  -  Inability to follow procedures due to psychological disorders [OR] dementia [NOT]
  -  Diagnosed hip [OR] knee OA (Healthy control group) [NOT]
  -  Current pain in the lower extremities [OR] lower back (Healthy control group) [NOT]
  -  Rheumatoid Arthritis (Healthy control group) [NOT]
  -  Previous corrective osteotomy (Healthy control group) [NOT]
  -  Regular intake of pain medication (more than 4 times per week) (Healthy control group) [NOT]
