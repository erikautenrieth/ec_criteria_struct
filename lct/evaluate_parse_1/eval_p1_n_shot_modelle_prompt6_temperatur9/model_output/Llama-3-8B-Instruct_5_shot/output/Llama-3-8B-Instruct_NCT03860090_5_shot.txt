<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with a endovenous pacemaker [OR] or defibrillator indication.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Pre-existing ipsilateral pacing electrode.
  -  Previous ipsilateral lymphadenectomy [AND] that would represent a risk for complications.
  -  Indication of cardiac resynchronization therapy [NOT].
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
