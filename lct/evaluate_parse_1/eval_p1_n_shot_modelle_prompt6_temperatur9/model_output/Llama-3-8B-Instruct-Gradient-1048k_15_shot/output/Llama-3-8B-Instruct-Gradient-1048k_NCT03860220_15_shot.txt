Inclusion Criteria:
- 145 mmHg ≤ msSBP ≤ 190 mmHg [AND] 
- Triglycerides < 500 mg/dL [OR] 
- LDL-C ≤ 250 mg/dL [AND]
Exclusion Criteria:
- sSBP ≥ 20mmHg [AND] sDBP ≥ 10mmHg [AND] 
- symptomatic orthostatic hypotension [OR] and secondary/iatrogenic hypertension [AND] dyslipidemia [AND] 
- history of moderate to severe cerebral ischemia [OR] cerebral hemorrhage [OR] transient ischemic attack [OR] myocardial infarction [OR] unstable angina in the past 6 months; [AND] severe heart failure (New York Heart Association functional class III [AND] IV [AND]) 
- hypersensitivity to telmisartan [OR] rosuvastatin [AND] 
- history of angioedema after treatment with angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors [OR] ARBs; [AND] creatinine phosphokinase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal [AND] 
- estimated glomerular filtration rate ≤30 mL/min; [AND] aspartate aminotransferase [AND] alanine aminotransferase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal [AND] 
- potassium levels >5.5 mmol/L; [AND] or any diseases that could affect the results of the study [AND].
