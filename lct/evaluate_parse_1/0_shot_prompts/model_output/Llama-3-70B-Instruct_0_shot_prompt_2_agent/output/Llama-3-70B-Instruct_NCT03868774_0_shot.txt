<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Moderate-severe uni-[OR]-bipolar depression according to the International Classification of Diseases (ICD-10)
  2. A Hamilton scale score (HDS-17) of ≥ 18 [OR] a sub-scale (HDSS) score of ≥ 9 within 24-48 hours before the first treatment [AND] having a score within the specified time frame

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] Absence of organic brain disease
  2. Epileptic seizure < 2 years [AND] having a history of seizure within the specified time frame
  3. Patients [WITH] implanted metal parts in the brain [OR] chest
  4. Patients [WITH] pacemaker
  5. Medical diseases [WITH] cerebral impact
  6. [NOT] Absence of serious suicidal danger
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
