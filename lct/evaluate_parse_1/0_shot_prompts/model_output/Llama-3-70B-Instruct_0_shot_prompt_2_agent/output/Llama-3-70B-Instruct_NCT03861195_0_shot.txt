Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age ≥18 years
  -  BMI ≤40 kg/m2
  -  indication for hysterectomy for benign disease [OR] prophylactic surgery due to hereditary cancer
  -  indication for radical hysterectomy [AND] pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy [OR] pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for cervical cancer
  -  indication for modified radical hysterectomy [AND] pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy [OR] pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for endometrial cancer
  -  size of uterus and vagina allows for retrieval by the vaginal route [AND] in cancer patients
  -  written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] absence of known extensive intra-abdominal adhesions
  -  anaesthesiological contraindications to laparoscopy
  -  women [NOT] without pacemaker or other implants where electrosurgery is to be avoided
  -  women [NOT] without known defects of the hemostasis
  -  [NOT] absence of pregnancy
  -  other internal or anatomical criteria that [NOT] preclude a minimal invasive approach
  -  [NOT] ability to understand patient information

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
