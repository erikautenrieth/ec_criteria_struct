<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients diagnosed with Hunter syndrome
  -  Male [AND] at the age of ≥ 5
  -  Adequate abilities (including 6-MWT) [AND] to participate in this study in the opinion of the investigator
  -  Voluntarily signed written informed consent [AND] to participation in this study
  -  Consent [AND] to contraception

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] naive to prior treatment with iduronate-2-sulfatase ERT
  -  History of bronchotomy [OR] bone marrow transplantation [OR] cord blood transplantation
  -  Known hypersensitivity reactions [AND] to any of the components of the investigational product
  -  Prior [OR] planned administration of other investigational products within 30 days before treatment with the investigational product in this study [OR] during this study
  -  [NOT] able to perform 6-MWT
  -  [NOT] male, i.e., Female

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
