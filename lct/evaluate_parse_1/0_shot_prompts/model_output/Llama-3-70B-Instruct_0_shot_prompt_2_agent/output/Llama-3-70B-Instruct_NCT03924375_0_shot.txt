<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Gastroscopy can be performed [AND] Serologic markers including serum pepsinogen can be measured

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Age < 20 [OR] Age > 70 years
  -  Anemia (serum hemoglobin level < 10 g/dL)
  -  Severe systemic disease
  -  Advanced chronic liver disease
  -  Use of certain medications, including proton pump inhibitors [OR] H2- receptor antagonists [OR] antibiotics
  -  [NOT] Naive to H. pylori eradication
  -  [NOT] Free of gastric surgery
  -  [NOT] Without recent history of upper gastrointestinal bleeding
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
