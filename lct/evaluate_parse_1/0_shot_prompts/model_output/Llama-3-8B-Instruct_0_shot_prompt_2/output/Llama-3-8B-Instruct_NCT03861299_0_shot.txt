Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Age [AND] ≥18 years [AND] ≤ 90 years
  2. Tumor diagnosed as Glioblastoma Multiforme on MRI [AND] with distinct ring-like pattern of contrast enhancement [OR] thick irregular walls [AND] a core area reduced signal [OR] suggestive of tumour necrosis as assessed by the surgeon
  3. Tumors situated [AND] in or near eloquent areas; motor cortex [OR] sensory cortex [OR] subcortical pyramidal tract [OR] speech areas as indicated on MRI (Sawaya Grading II [AND] II)
  4. The tumor is [NOT] unsuitable for resection (according to neurosurgeon)
  5. Karnofsky performance scale 80 [OR] more
  6. Written [NOT] absent Informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Tumors of the cerebellum [OR] brain stem [OR] midline
  2. Multifocal contrast enhancing lesions
  3. Substantial non-contrast enhancing tumor areas [NOT] suggestive of low grade gliomas with malignant transformation
  4. Medical reasons [NOT] allowing MRI (eg, pacemaker)
  5. [NOT] ability to give consent because of language barrier
  6. Psychiatric history
  7. Previous brain tumour surgery
  8. Previous low-grade glioma
  9. Second primary malignancy within the past 5 years [NOT] with the exception of adequately treated in situ carcinoma of any organ [OR] basal cell carcinoma of the skin
  10. Severe [NOT] ability to speak or dysphasia
