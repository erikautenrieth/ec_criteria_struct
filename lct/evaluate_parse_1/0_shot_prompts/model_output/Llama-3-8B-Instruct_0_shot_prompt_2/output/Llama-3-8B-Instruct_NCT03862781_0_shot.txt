<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - Eligible patients are those over 18 years of age [AND] who are regularly scheduled to undergo a right hemicolectomy via a minimally invasive approach [OR] robotic [OR] laparoscopic [AND] with the creation of an anastomosis.
  - Right colectomy will be defined as removal of the ascending colon [AND] ligation of the ileocolic artery [AND] vein [OR] +/- removal of the terminal ileum [OR] +/- removal of the proximal transverse colon [OR] +/- removal of the right branch of the middle colic artery [AND] vein.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Pregnant women [NOT] eligible.
  - Additional colon resection is planned [AND] i.e. left colectomy [OR] proctectomy [NOT] allowed.
  - Vulnerable populations [AND] such as prisoners [OR] adults [NOT] able to give consent [NOT] eligible.
  - If the scheduled surgery is planned at Butterworth hospital [AND] as to standardize the nursing care received postoperatively [NOT] allowed.
  - Emergent cases [NOT] eligible.

Patients will be excluded from the study intra-operatively if:
  - The procedure is converted to an open resection [NOT] allowed.
  - If a loop ileostomy is performed [AND] in addition to the right colectomy [NOT] allowed.
