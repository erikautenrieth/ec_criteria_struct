Inclusion Criteria:
  - vaginal delivery [AND] an infant age of 6 months [OR] ±3 weeks
  - mother ≥15 years of age [AND] infant still breastfeeding [AND] anticipated residence in the area for the study duration

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] inability to provide informed consent
  - hemoglobin < 70 g/L [OR] Z scores for weight-for-age (WAZ) or weight-for-height (WHZ) <3
  - any maternal or infant chronic illness [OR] administration of any infant vitamin or mineral supplements for the past 2 months [OR] any history of infant antibiotic treatment
