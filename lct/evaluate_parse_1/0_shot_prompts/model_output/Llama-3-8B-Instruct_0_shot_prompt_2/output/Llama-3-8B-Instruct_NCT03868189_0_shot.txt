<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patient requiring an electroneuromyogram [AND] whose indication allows the application of a standard protocol of examination [OR] patient that has given his/her [NOT] free and informed consent [AND] patient that signed the consent form.
  - Patient [AND] with an health insurance plan.
  - Patient [AND] is at least 18 years old [≥] [AND] patient is under 80 years old [≤].

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patient [NOT] with symptoms of neuropathy during the screening [OR] patient whose Neurological severity score (NSS) score is [NOT] ≥ 3.
  - Patient whose neurological examination is abnormal [AND] suggestive of neuropathy.
  - Patient [OR] presenting a pathology that could cause neuropathy (e.g. diabetes, renal failure, etc.).
  - Patient [AND] already participating in a category 1 research study (Jardé law) [OR] patient in an exclusion period determined by another study.
  - Patient [NOT] under the protection of justice, under guardianship or under curatorship [OR] patient refusing to sign the consent form.
  - Patient [NOT] for which it is impossible to provide information about the study [OR] patient that is pregnant, parturient, or breastfeeding.
