<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - Be a patient in the Bellevue Hospital Center ED [AND] be ≥18 years old [AND] screen positive for homelessness risk using a homelessness risk screening tool [OR] screen positive for unhealthy alcohol or drug use [AND] able to provide consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Medically [NOT] unstable [OR] psychiatrically unstable [OR] unable to provide informed consent for other reason [AND] unable to understand and speak English [OR] lives outside NYC [AND] unable to give a NYC ZIP code [OR] already homeless [AND] does not have a telephone where can be reached for follow-up [OR] has already received specialized peer navigator/addiction social worker services during current ED visit [OR] has already participated in the study [OR] incarcerated [OR] in police custody.
