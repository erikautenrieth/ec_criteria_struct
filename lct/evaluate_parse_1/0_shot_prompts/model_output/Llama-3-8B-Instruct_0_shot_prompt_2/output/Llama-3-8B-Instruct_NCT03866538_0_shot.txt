Inclusion Criteria
  -  Patients will be recruited from the Division of Gastroenterology and Hepatology [AND] the Mayo Clinic in Rochester, MN [OR] other institutions.
  -  Adult patients [AND] who have immune mediated enteropathies [AND] who have had [NOT] no improvement in symptoms [AND] histology on oral budesonide therapy.

Exclusion Criteria
  -  Age [NOT] <18 years.
  -  Positive stool gluten testing in patients with refractory celiac disease [OR] other gastrointestinal disorders.
  -  Small bowel malignancy [OR] history of small bowel malignancy.
  -  Refractory celiac disease type 2 [OR] other types of celiac disease.
  -  Post-transplant lymphoproliferative disorder associated enteropathy [OR] other types of enteropathy.
  -  [NOT] prior improvement in symptoms and histology with budesonide therapy.
  -  Discontinuation of budesonide therapy prior to the trial [OR] other reasons for discontinuation.
  -  Other concurrent systemic corticosteroids [OR] other immunosuppressive medications, for example [AND] but not limited to azathioprine, 6-mercaptopurine, cyclosporine, methotrexate, anti-TNF monoclonal antibodies, alpha-4 beta-7 integrin inhibiting monoclonal antibody, interleukin 12/23 inhibiting monoclonal antibody, JAK inhibitors.
