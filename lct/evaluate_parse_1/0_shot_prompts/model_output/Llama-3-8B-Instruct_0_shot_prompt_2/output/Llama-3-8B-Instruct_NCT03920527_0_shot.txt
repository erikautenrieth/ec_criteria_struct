Inclusion Criteria: includes presence of all the following:
  - [AND] one [OR] more clinical symptoms [AND] persistent cough, recurrent hemoptysis, weight loss, malaise, fever [AND] dyspnea for ≥3 months
  - [AND] slowly progressive [OR] persistent radiological findings [AND] one [OR] more cavities [AND] surrounding fibrosis, infiltrates, consolidation, [OR] with [OR] without fungal ball [OR] progressive pleural thickening on computed tomography (CT) of the thorax
  - [AND] immunological [OR] microbiological evidence of Aspergillus infection [AND] A.fumigatus-specific IgG >27 mgA/L [OR] positive Aspergillus precipitins [AND] and [AND] growth of Aspergillus in respiratory secretions [OR] serum galactomannan index >0.5 [OR] BALF galactomannan index >1
  - [AND] exclusion [NOT] of other pulmonary disorders with similar presentation.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] failure to provide informed consent
  - [AND] patients [NOT] on immunosuppressive drugs, [NOT] intake of prednisolone [OR] equivalent >10 mg for at least 3 weeks [OR] a diagnosis of human immunodeficiency virus syndrome
  - [AND] intake [NOT] antifungal azoles for >3 weeks in the preceding six months
  - [AND] subjects [NOT] with active pulmonary infection due to mycobacterium tuberculosis [OR] mycobacteria other than tuberculosis (MOTT)
  - [AND] subjects [NOT] with other forms of pulmonary aspergillosis [AND] allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis, chronic necrotizing aspergillosis [AND] angio-invasive aspergillosis
  - [AND] pregnancy
