Inclusion Criteria:
  - Diagnosis of COPD confirmed by clinical examination [AND] a pulmonary function test [OR] other diagnostic methods
  - Stable clinical condition [NOT] with recent respiratory system infections [OR] any exacerbation of the disease
  - Patients [AND] their legal representatives must be willing to re-evaluate at the end of the study [OR] at any other time during the study
  - Patients [AND] their legal representatives must be willing to give written consent for their participation in the study [OR] verbal consent in cases where written consent is not possible

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Age [NOT] less than 18 years old
  - Unstable cardiac disease [OR] any other unstable medical condition
  - Musculoskeletal [AND] neurological diseases that could affect exercise performance [OR] preclude exercise
  - Patients with COPD [OR] other serious disease in its final stage
  - Significant cognitive [AND] psychiatric impairment that would lead to an inability to follow simple commands [OR] any other impairment that would affect the patient's ability to participate in the study
