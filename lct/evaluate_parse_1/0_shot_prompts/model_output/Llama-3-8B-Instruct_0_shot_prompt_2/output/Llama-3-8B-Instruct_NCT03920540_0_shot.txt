<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients diagnosed with Hunter syndrome [AND] male at the age of ≥ 5 [OR] female at the age of ≥ 5
  - Adequate abilities [AND] including 6-MWT [OR] other abilities in the opinion of the investigator
  - Voluntarily signed written informed consent to participation in this study [AND] consent to contraception

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Prior treatment with iduronate-2-sulfatase ERT [NOT] free of treatment
  - History of bronchotomy [OR] bone marrow transplantation [OR] cord blood transplantation
  - Known hypersensitivity reactions to any of the components of the investigational product [NOT] absence of reaction
  - Prior or planned administration of other investigational products within 30 days before treatment with the investigational product in this study [OR] during this study
  - Unable to perform 6-MWT [NOT] having ability to perform 6-MWT
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
