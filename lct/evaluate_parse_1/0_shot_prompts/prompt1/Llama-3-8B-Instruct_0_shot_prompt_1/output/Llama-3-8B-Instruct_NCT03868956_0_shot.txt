Inclusion Criteria:
  - Age [AND] >= 18 years
  - Known history of objectively documented deep vein thrombosis of the lower limb [OR] with or without pulmonary embolism
  - Out-patients referred for clinically suspected acute recurrent ipsilateral DVT of the lower limb [OR] i.e. the occurrence of new symptoms and signs of DVT or the increase of symptoms and signs in patients with post-thrombotic syndrome
  - Patients covered by social security [OR] equivalent regimen
  - Signed and dated informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Known current pregnancy [NOT] allowed
  - Any condition [NOT] which, in the opinion of the investigator, may prevent him from performing the colour doppler ultrasound test [OR] plaster cast, inaccessible vein segment after abdominal or pelvic surgery, or other causes that may lead to a technically inadequate CDUS
  - Delay from onset of symptoms to inclusion of more than 10 days [NOT] allowed
  - Therapeutic anticoagulation for more than 48 hours in the two days prior to consent [NOT] allowed
  - Prophylactic anticoagulation for more than 48 hours in the two days prior to consent [NOT] allowed
  - Clinical symptoms of pulmonary embolism [NOT] allowed
  - Life expectancy less than 3 months [NOT] allowed
  - Patient [NOT] able to adhere to protocol or follow-up visits and contacts
  - Participants under legal guardianship or incapacitation [NOT] allowed
  - Patient already enrolled in a deep vein thrombosis (DVT) diagnostic research [NOT] allowed
