<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - The patient is currently being treated at Queen Elizabeth Central Hospital [OR] Kamuzu Central Hospital in the neonatal ward.
  - The patient is less than 28 days old [AND] a neonate.
  - The patient is deemed to be at risk for jaundice [OR] is undergoing blue light phototherapy for treatment of jaundice.
  - The patient's parent or guardian has provided [NOT] no informed consent for their child to participate.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Parent or guardian [NOT] able [OR] willing to provide informed consent.
  - The patient [NOT] able to receive a blood draw/heel stick as determined by their clinician.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
