Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients with a soft tissue sarcoma of the thigh [AND] must have sufficient health to withstand the physical demands of surgery [AND] ≥ 18 years old [AND] ECOG performance status of ≤ 2 [AND] ability to understand [AND] willingness to sign an IRB-approved informed consent document (either directly or via a legally authorized representative)

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] history of clinically significant medical conditions including: Cardiovascular: [OR] Atrial Fibrillation [OR] Ventricular Fibrillation [OR] Significant Coronary Artery Disease. Hepatic: [OR] Viral or Autoimmune Hepatitis [OR] Cirrhosis of the Liver [OR] Liver Metabolism Disorders. Renal: [NOT] any form of renal impairment [NOT] (EXPArel is cleared by the kidneys, thus any form of renal impairment could lead to an adverse reaction)
  - [NOT] medical condition(s) or concurrent surgery that may have required analgesic treatment in the postoperative period for pain that was not strictly related to the study surgery
  - [NOT] any clinically significant event or condition discovered during surgery that may have complicated the patient's post surgical course such as vascular or nerve involvement that was unknown before surgery
  - [NOT] history of allergic reactions attributed to compounds of similar chemical or biologic composition to liposomal bupivacaine
  - Pregnant women [NOT] are excluded from this study because EXPAREL has the potential for teratogenic or abortifacient effects [AND] breastfeeding should be discontinued if the mother is treated with EXPAREL
