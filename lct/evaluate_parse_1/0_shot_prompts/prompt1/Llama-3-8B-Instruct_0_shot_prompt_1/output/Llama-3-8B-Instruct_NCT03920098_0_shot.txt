Inclusion Criteria:
  - Primipara [AND] no previous pregnancies
  - 18 to 25 years of age
  - Gestation [OR] <15 weeks at first prenatal visit
  - No intention [AND] to move outside the city of Santiago in the next two years

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Multipara [NOT] previous pregnancies
  - Pre-existing cancer [NOT] except non-melanoma skin cancer
  - Family history [AND] of breast cancer [OR] one first-degree female relative diagnosed with breast cancer
  - High-risk pregnancy [NOT] according to national guidelines
  - During the follow-up [NOT] pregnant women under any treatment [OR] that may affect the levels of metabolic markers will be excluded from the analysis
  - Pregnant women [NOT] with pre-eclampsia [OR] with preterm birth [NOT] less than 37 weeks of gestational age will be also excluded
