<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patient with a diagnosis of advanced cancer [AND] metastatic or recurrent incurable solid tumors [OR] brain disease (i.e. psychosis, stroke with functional impairment, dementia)
  - Presence of fatigue [AND] >= 4/10 on Edmonton Symptom Assessment System (ESAS) Fatigue item (0-10 severity scale)
  - Patient [NOT] describes fatigue as being present for less than 2 weeks prior to screening
  - Uncontrolled pain [AND] patient is on opioids for the treatment of cancer pain, he/she [NOT] has had a major dose change (> 25%) for at least 48 hours prior to study entry
  - Patient must be willing to engage in telephone follow-up with research staff
  - Patient must have telephone access to be contacted by the research staff
  - Hemoglobin level [AND] >= 9 g/dL [OR] patient may receive packed red blood cell (PRBC) transfusion so as to have hemoglobin level of >= 9 g/dL to participate in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Surgery [OR] pain-relieving procedures within 2 weeks of entry into the study or during the study period
  - Patients with history of substance abuse [OR] cognitively impaired (MD Anderson Symptom [MDAS] > 7) [NOT] diagnosis of cognitive disorders, psychotic disorder, bipolar disorder [OR] active substance abuse
