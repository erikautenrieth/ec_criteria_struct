Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Chronic (more than three months) ESBL colonization of the same strain [OR] as determined by species, phenotype and antibiogram.
  -  Patients must be able to swallow sachets [AND] be able to speak and understand Swedish.
  -  Must have a permanent residence in Sweden [AND] be able to sign informed consent in Swedish.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] Immunosuppression (i.e. chemotheraphy, treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors) [OR] immunodeficiency.
  -  [NOT] In patient care [OR] psychiatric disorder [OR] alcohol [OR] substance abuse.
  -  [NOT] Dementia [OR] invasive catheters.
