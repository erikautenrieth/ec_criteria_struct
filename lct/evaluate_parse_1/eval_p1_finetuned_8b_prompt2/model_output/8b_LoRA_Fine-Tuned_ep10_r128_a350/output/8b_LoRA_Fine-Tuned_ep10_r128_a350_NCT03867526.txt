Inclusion Criteria:
 - Informed consent will be obtained from the patient or the parents before any study related procedures.
 - Patients of both genders older than 6 months [AND] and younger than 80 years
 - The patient has a diagnosis of Wilson dis-ease
Exclusion Criteria:
 - No Informed consent from the patient or the parents before any study related procedures
 - Patients of both genders younger than 6 months [AND] and older than 80 years
 - No diagnosis of Wilson disease
