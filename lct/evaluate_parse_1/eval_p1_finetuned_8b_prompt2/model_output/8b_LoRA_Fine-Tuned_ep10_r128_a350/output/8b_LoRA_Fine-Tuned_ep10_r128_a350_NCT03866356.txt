Inclusion Criteria:
 - The inclusion criteria were being female, ≥ 18 years, having an initial diagnosis of SI, [AND] being literate, [AND] having [NOT] no sensory disorder affecting communication, and agreeing to participate in the research
Exclusion Criteria:
 - The exclusion criteria were pregnancy, [OR] presence of a urinary tract infection and previous treatment for SI. The removal criteria were patient non-compliance with the protocol, [OR] failure in contacting the patient and the patient's wish to withdraw from the study.
