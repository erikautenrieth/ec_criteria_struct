Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Signed informed consent with the study
 2. Men [OR] and women >18 years of age
 3. Patients ≥1 [AND] and ≤6 months after type I myocardial infarction
 4. Physical inactivity before MI defined as the [NOT] absence of moderate to vigorous exercise for at least 30 minutes 5 or more days a week
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Heart failure NYHA IIIB-IV
 2. Planned coronary revascularization
 3. Planned major surgery within the next 12 months
 4. [NOT] Inability to walk for any reason
 5. Comorbidities that would [NOT] preclude adherence to the rehabilitation program (e.g. arthrosis, [OR] active malignancy, [OR] major depression [OR] or other significant psychiatric disorder, [OR] cognitive impairment) - at the discretion of the physician
 6. Life expectancy less than 12 months
 7. Pregnancy
 8. [NOT] Inability to operate the smart-watch
