Inclusion Criteria:
 - Male [OR] or Female ≥ 60 years old
 - MMSE score between 4 and 26 / 30
 - Diagnosis of minor [OR] to major NeuroCognitive Disorder of the Alzheimer type [OR] or multiple etiology;
 - Subject [NOT] without a major hearing [OR] or visual impairment;
 - Subjects [NOT] not presenting any contraindications to the practice of adapted physical activity;
 - Subjects affiliated to Social Security System;
 - Signing informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - < 60 years
 - Medical indications: Recent [OR] or unstable cardiovascular events: ECG changes, unstable angina, uncontrolled arrhythmia, [OR] 3rd degree BAV, acute heart failure.
 - MMSE score < 4
 - Subjects already engaged in appropriate physical activity
 - Subjects with a perceptual disorder (AMD, [OR] deafness...)
 - Subjects with a motor disability
 - Subjects under guardianship, [OR] curatorship [OR] or safeguard of justice
 - Persons deprived of their liberty (administrative [OR] or judicial)
