Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Admitted from the emergency department to inpatient [OR] or observation status at one of:
 - Carolinas Medical Center, [OR] Carolinas Medical Center - Mercy, [OR] or Atrium Health Northeast;
 2. ≥18 years of age upon admission;
 3. oral/ [OR] parenteral antibiotic [OR] or bacterial culture order within 24 hours of emergency department presentation and
 1. culture drawn first, [OR] antibiotics ordered within 48 hours [OR] or
 2. antibiotic ordered first, [OR] culture ordered within 48 hours (adapted from criteria applied in development of the third international consensus definitions for sepsis [OR] and septic shock)
 4. deemed as high-risk for 30-day readmission (i.e., ≥ 20%) [OR] or 30-day mortality (i.e., ≥ 10%) using risk-scoring models
 5. [NOT] not discharged at the time of patient list generation
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. prior randomization to either STAR [OR] or usual care study arms;
 2. [NOT] not a North Carolina resident [OR] or residence >2.5 hours drive time from treating hospital;
 3. the only antibiotic  associated [AND] with patient is administered in the operating room as this likely represents pre-operative infection prophylaxis [AND] and [NOT] not presumed infection;
 4. patients transferred from other acute care hospitals;
 5. patients with a change in code status (i.e., do not resuscitate, do not intubate) within 24 hours after admission due to the general assumption of increased risk of exposure to less aggressive treatment;
 6. patients with infection ruled out during the index hospitalization.
