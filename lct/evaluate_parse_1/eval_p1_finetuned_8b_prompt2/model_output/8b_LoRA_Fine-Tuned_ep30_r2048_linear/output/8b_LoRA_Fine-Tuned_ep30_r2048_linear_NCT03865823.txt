Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient over the age of 18, patient with an anus fistula [AND] with indication for surgical treatment
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Cutaneous Suppuration, [NOT] without fistula (e.g., perianal fistula, [OR] fistula in the context of perianal Crohn's disease)
 - Previous incision and drainage allowed
 - Allergy to any of the components of the peritoneal system
 - Inability to understand the procedure, due to language barriers, [OR] psyschological disorders, [OR] dementia, etc.
 - Severe immunosuppression
 - Patients who are not allowed to participate in the study, at the discretion of the investigator.
