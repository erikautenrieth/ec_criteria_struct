Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Clinical diagnosis of post herpetic neuralgia, [AND] with pain persisting for at least 3 months after the onset of herpes zoster rash OR
 2. Clinical diagnosis of distal painful polyneuropathy due to Type I [OR] or Type II diabetes mellitus [AND] with:
 1. symmetrical, [OR] bilateral pain  in the lower extremities for at least 3 months [AND] and
 2. diabetes under control [AND] for at least 3 months prior to randomisation, as indicated by a glycated haemoglobin level (HbA1c) of ≤ 11% (97 mmol/mol) [AND] and on a stable dose of insulin [OR] or oral diabetic medication for 3 months prior to screening, and
 3. [NOT] No change in diabetic medication planned for the duration of the study
 3. Positive sensory symptoms (mechanical [OR] or thermal) associated with neuropathic pain, confirmed by:
 1. painDETECT questionnaire (PD-Q) [AND] and
 2. Clinical assessment, showing signs of neuropathic pain in either a dermatological (PHN) [OR] or distal symmetrical distribution (DPN)
 8. An average daily pain score on the NPRS of at least 4 [AND] and [NOT] no more than 8 in the last five diary entries prior to randomisation
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Presence of moderate to severe pain from other causes that may confound assessment [OR] or self-evaluation of NP.
 2. Subjects with both DPN [AND] and PHN
 3. An average daily pain score on the NPRS of more than 4/10 in the last month
 4. Pain in the day of recruitment
 5. No correspondence availability through email
 6. Inability to read/ comprehend English
 7. Compensation or seeking compensation from a third-party liability or workers' compensation
 8. Previous treatment with Pregabalin or gabapentin
 9. Gastrointestinal disorder (gastrointestinal ulcer, Crohn's disease, etc.)
 10. Pregabalin use within 2 weeks before study enrolment
 11. Ongoing treatment with gabapentin, amitriptyline, duloxetine, nortriptyline, other tricyclics or SNRIs
 12. Medical conditions that would be considered as contraindications for pregabalin treatment (ischemic heart disease, cardiac dysrhythmia, glaucoma, history of urinary retention)
 13. Pregnancy
 14. History of use of restrictive substances or alcohol abuse
 15. Allergy to gabapentin, amitriptyline, duloxetine, nortriptyline, other tricyclics or SNRIs
 16. Currently taking other medications that affect the data obtained
 17. Serum creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL
 18. Triglycerides > 1.5 mg/dL
 19. Diagnosed with any cardiovascular, gastrointestinal, renal, endocrine, infectious, hematological, oncologic, neuro-psychiatric or immune-mediated disorder, which in the opinion of the investigator might impact the subject's safety or compliance, or the interpretation of results
 20. Treatment with any other investigational drug within 1 week prior to baseline visit
 21. Participants with a history of:
 22. Drug abuse or alcohol abuse
 23. Allergy to anticonvulsants, anti-seizure medications, benzodiazepines, antidepressants, restless leg syndrome treatments, or any other medications that affect seizure frequency within the 2 weeks prior to study entry
 24. Presence of any condition that would be considered as a seizure trigger within the 3 months prior to study entry (febrile seizure, head trauma, infection, etc.)
 25. Presence of any form of epilepsy other than ABSence
