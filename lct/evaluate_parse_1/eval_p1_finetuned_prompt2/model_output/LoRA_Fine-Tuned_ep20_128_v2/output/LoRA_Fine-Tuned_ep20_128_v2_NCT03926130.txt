Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of CD [AND] for at least 3 months prior to baseline
 - Confirmed diagnosis of moderate to severe CD as assessed by SF, [OR] AP score, [AND] and SES-CD
 - Demonstrated [NOT] intolerance, [OR] loss of response [OR] or [NOT] inadequate response to conventional [OR] or to biologic therapy for CD
 - If female, subject must meet the contraception recommendations
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Have a current diagnosis of ulcerative colitis, [OR] inflammatory bowel disease- [NOT] unclassified (IBD-U) (formerly known as indeterminate colitis), [OR] abdominal [OR] or perianal abscess [OR] or short bowel syndrome
 - Have a stoma [OR] or ostomy
 - Have had a bowel resection within 6 months, [OR] or any kind of intra-abdominal [OR] or extra abdominal surgery within 3 months of baseline
 - Have ever received any monoclonal antibodies binding IL-23
