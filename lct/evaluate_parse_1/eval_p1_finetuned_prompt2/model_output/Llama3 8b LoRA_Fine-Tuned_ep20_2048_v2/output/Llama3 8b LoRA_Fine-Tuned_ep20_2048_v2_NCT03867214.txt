Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adult patients [AND] who plan to undergo simultaneous primary total knee arthroplasty on bilateral knee joint [AND] with a diagnosis of osteoarthritis [OR] or aseptic bone necrosis, [AND] but [NOT] not of rheumatoid arthritis;
 - All patients who have normal preoperative platelet count, [AND] normal prothrombin time, [AND] normal partial thromboplastin time, [AND] and normal international normalized ratio;
 - The use of only balanced electrolyte solutions [OR] and/or albumin for plasma volume restitution.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Allergy to tranexamic acid;
 - Receiving warfarin [OR] or heparin; had a history of hemophilia, [OR] deep venous thrombosis, [OR] pulmonary embolism, [OR] or renal impairment; [OR] or were pregnant;
 - Patients with any cardiovascular problems (such as myocardial infarction history, [OR] atrial fibrillation, [OR] angina);
 - Patients with thromboembolic disorders, [OR] or those exhibiting a deteriorating general condition;
 - Preoperative anemia (a hemoglobin value of <11 g/dL in females [AND] and <12 g/dL in males), refusal of blood products;
 - Preoperative use of anticoagulant therapy within five days before surgery, [OR] fibrinolytic disorders requiring intraoperative antifibrinolytic treatment, [OR] coagulopathy (as identified by a preoperative platelet count of <150,000/mm3, an international normalized ratio of >1.4, [OR] or a prolonged partial thromboplastin time [AND] >15 sec; [OR] and abnormal international normalized ratio);
 - Preoperative hemodynamic instability;
 - Preoperative respiratory dysfunction (e.g., need for oxygen or mechanical ventilation);
 - Preoperative infection;
 - Patients who are pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding;
 - Patients with altered mental status/inability to provide informed consent;
 - Patients who are incarcerated, [OR] or otherwise unable to participate in the study;
 - Patients who are currently being treated at a rehabilitation center [OR] or nursing home;
 - Patients who have conditions that require a specialist consult are suspected of needing a specialist consult, [OR] or have conditions that would be aggravated [OR] or worsened if treated by the interventions in this study.
