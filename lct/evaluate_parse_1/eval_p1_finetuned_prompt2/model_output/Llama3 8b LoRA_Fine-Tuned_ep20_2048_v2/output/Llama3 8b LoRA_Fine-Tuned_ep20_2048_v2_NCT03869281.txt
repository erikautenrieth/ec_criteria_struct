Inclusion Criteria (PTA group)
 - T1D patients who received their first PTA, [OR] or second PTA only if their first graft was explanted within a week, between 2 January 2005 and 31 December 2017
 - age ≥18 [AND] and < 65 years
 - willingness to participate in this observational study
Inclusion Criteria (control group)
 - Type 1 diabetes
 - age ≥18 years [AND] and < 65 years
 - willingness to participate in this observational study
 - minimum disease duration of 5 years
 - brittle diabetes, as defined by the occurrence of frequent acute complications (ketoacidosis [OR] or severe hypoglycaemic episodes requiring third-part assistance [OR] or [NOT] unawareness [AND] despite well-conducted intensive insulin therapy)
 - presence of at least one diabetes-related complication (either neuropathy, [OR] retinopathy, [OR] subclinical nephropathy).
Exclusion Criteria (both groups)
 - conditions other than T1D [OR] known to affect bone health (i.e., non-compensated hypothyroidism, [OR] hyperthyroidism, [OR] hyperparathyroidism, inflammatory bowel disease, [OR] malignancy, [OR] rheumatoid arthritis, [OR] hypogonadism, severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, [OR] hepatitis [OR] insufficiency, alcohol intake ≥ 3 units/day), other drugs that can affect bone metabolism (e.g., bisphosphonates, [OR] anticonvulsants, [OR] hormone replacement therapy), other metabolic diseases (e.g., cancer, [OR] diabetes, [OR] heart disease, [OR] HIV, [OR] seizures)
 - pregnancy
 - conditions that require a specialist consult are suspected of needing a specialist consult, [OR] or have conditions that would be aggravated [OR] or worsened if treated by the interventions in this study
