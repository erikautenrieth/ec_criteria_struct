Inclusion Criteria: includes presence of all the following:
 - one or more clinical symptoms (persistent cough, [OR] recurrent hemoptysis, [OR] weight loss, [OR] malaise, [OR] fever [OR] and dyspnea) for ≥3 months
 - slowly progressive [OR] or persistent radiological findings (one or more cavities [OR] and surrounding fibrosis, [OR] infiltrates, [OR] consolidation, [OR] with or [NOT] without fungal ball [OR] or progressive aspergillosis) on computed tomography (CT) of the thorax
 - immunological (A.fumigatus-specific IgG >27 mg/L [OR] or positive Aspergillus precipitans) [OR] or microbiological evidence of Aspergillus infection (growth of Aspergillus in respiratory secretions [OR] or serum galactomannan index >0.5 or BALF galactomannan index >1) [OR] and,
 - [NOT] exclusion of other pulmonary disorders with similar presentation.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - failure to provide informed consent
 - patients on immunosuppressive drugs, [OR] intake of prednisolone (or equivalent) >10 mg for at least 3 weeks [OR] or a diagnosis of human immunodeficiency virus syndrome
 - intake antifungal azoles for >3 weeks in the preceding 6 months
 - subjects with active pulmonary infection due to mycobacterium tuberculosis [OR] or mycobacterium other than tuberculosis (MOTT)
 - subjects with other forms of pulmonary aspergillosis (allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis, [OR] chronic necrotic pulmonary disease, [OR] pulmonary fibrosis, [OR] interstitial lung disease, [OR] asthma, [OR] pulmonary infection, [OR] primary ciliary dyskinesia presenting with newborn respiratory distress [OR] and recurrent aspiration)
 - pregnancy
