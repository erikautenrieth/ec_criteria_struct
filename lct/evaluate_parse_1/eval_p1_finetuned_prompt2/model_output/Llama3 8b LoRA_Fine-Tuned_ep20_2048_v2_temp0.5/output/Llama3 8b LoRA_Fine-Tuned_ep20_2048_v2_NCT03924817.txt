Inclusion Criteria:
 - Prescription of a Narval CC mandibular advancement device
 - Age ≥ 18 years
 - Ability to understand the study information and comply with the study procedures
 - Signed and dated informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with central sleep apnea (central AI > 5/hour)
 - Patients with loose teeth [OR] and severe parodontitis
 - Patients with completely missing teeth [OR] or removable tooth replacement, [OR] or with a teeth health [NOT] insufficient to retain an MAD Patients with [NOT] maximum protrusion of less than 5mm
