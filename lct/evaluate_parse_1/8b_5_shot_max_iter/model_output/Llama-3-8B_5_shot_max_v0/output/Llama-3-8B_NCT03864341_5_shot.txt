Inclusion Criteria:
 - Be a patient in the Bellevue Hospital Center ED [AND] 
 - Be ≥18 years old [OR] 
 - Screen positive for homelessness risk using a homelessness risk screening tool (HRST) [AND] 
 - Screen positive for unhealthy alcohol or drug use [AND] 
 - Able to provide consent [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Medically [NOT] unstable (e.g., critically ill) [OR] psychiatrically unstable [AND] 
 - Unable to provide informed consent for other reason (e.g., cognitive deficit, profound intoxication) [AND] 
 - Incarcerated [OR] in police custody [AND] 
 - Unable to understand and speak English [AND] 
 - Lives outside NYC [OR] cannot give a NYC ZIP code [AND] 
 - Already homeless (residing in a shelter [OR] on the streets) [AND] 
 - Does not have a telephone where can be reached for follow-up [AND] 
 - Has already received specialized peer navigator/addiction social worker services during current ED visit [AND] 
 - Has already participated in the study [AND]
