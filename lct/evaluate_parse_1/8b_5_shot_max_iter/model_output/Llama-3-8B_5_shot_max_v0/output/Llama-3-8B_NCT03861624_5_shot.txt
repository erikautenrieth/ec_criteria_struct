Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. Skeletal maturity
 2. AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures [OR]
 3. Wound primarily closeable (no flap or delay in closure due to contamination needed) [AND]
 4. Palpable pedal pulses (no vascular injury sustained) [AND]
 5. Presentation within 24 hours from injury
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. Current injury is a [NOT] pathologic fracture
 2. Sustained bilateral tibia fracture
 3. Sustained comminuted femur fracture
 4. Sustained severe [NOT] Traumatic Brain Injury (GCS<12) ***
 5. Sustained severe [NOT] spinal cord injury (lower extremity paresis/paralysis)
 6. Sustained severe [NOT] burns (>10% total body surface area (TBSA) or >5% TBSA with full thickness or circumferential injury)
 7. Prior [NOT] ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery
 8. Prior or current [NOT] lower limb deformity or abnormality
 9. Unable to complete [NOT] follow-up visits
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
