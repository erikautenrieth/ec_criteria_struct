Inclusion Criteria:
Type II diabetic patients, 18 years and older, were included if the participants had:
  1. severe non-PDR [OR] in either eyes with/without diabetic macular edema;
  2. ETDRS letters-measured best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) ranging from 20/40 [OR] to 20/320 Snellen equivalent;     
  3. no previous treatment [AND] of any type [NOT] in either eye.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. history of prior [AND] laser treatment [OR] vitrectomy [AND] in the study eye;
  2. history of thromboembolic event [AND] including myocardial infarction [OR] cerebral vascular accident;
  3. major surgery [AND] within the prior 6 months [OR] planned within the next 28 days;
  4. history of glaucoma [OR] ocular hypertension;
  5. loss of vision [AND] as a result of other causes;
  6. history of systemic corticosteroid therapy [AND] within the last 3 months;
  7. severe systemic disease [AND] other than diabetes mellitus;
  8. known coagulation abnormalities [OR] current use of anticoagulative medication [AND] other than aspirin
  9. any condition [AND] that could affect followup [OR] documentation [including preretinal [OR] vitreous hemorrhage].
