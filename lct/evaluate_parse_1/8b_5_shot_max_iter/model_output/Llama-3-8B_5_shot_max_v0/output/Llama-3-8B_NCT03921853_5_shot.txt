Inclusion Criteria:
 - Older than 18 [AND] and younger [AND] ≤65 years of age.
 - With risk factors to Metabolic syndrome [OR] with obesity [OR] and morbid obesity condition.
 - Medical authorization by a physician [AND].
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Physical limitations [AND] (e.g., restricting injuries of the musculoskeletal system [OR] or dependent of a third person [NOT]).
 - Exercise-related dyspnoea [OR] or respiratory alterations [NOT].
 - Chronic heart disease [AND] with any worsening in the last month [NOT].
 - Adherence rate of less than 80% [NOT] of the total interventions [AND].
