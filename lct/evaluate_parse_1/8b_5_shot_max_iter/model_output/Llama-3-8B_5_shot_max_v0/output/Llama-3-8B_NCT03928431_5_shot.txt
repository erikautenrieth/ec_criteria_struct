Inclusion Criteria:
 - Infants of healthy mothers with uncomplicated pregnancies at term, mothers of any ethnic or social background [AND] that can speak, read, [OR] and understand Swedish to the extent that they can consent in Swedish. An additional inclusion criterion for mothers in the CS groups is vaginal pH ≤ 4 at the time of birth.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Infants of mothers with [NOT] pre-eclampsia [OR] complicated pregnancies, mothers <18 years [OR] >40 years of age, genital herpes simplex, genital warts, HIV, Hepatitis B [OR] complications during delivery, infants delivered prior to 37 weeks of gestation.
 - Additional exclusion criteria for mothers in the CS group: Mothers positive to [OR] Group B Streptococcus, Group A Streptococcus, bacterial vaginosis, vaginal pH > 4 at the time of birth. Mothers with fecal samples positive for [OR] Salmonella, Shigella, Campylobacter [OR] Yersinia.
