Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adults age 55-90 years [AND] with visual significant cataracts in one [OR] both eyes.
 - Healthy individuals [AND] able to tolerate outpatient cataract surgery under local anesthesia via either phacoemulsification [OR] and/or femtosecond assisted cataract surgery. Well-controlled diabetes [AND] hypertension will be included.
 - Females of childbearing potential [AND] must agree to use a reliable method of birth control while participating in this study. Reliable methods of birth control are: [OR] abstinence, [OR] oral contraceptives, [OR] intrauterine device (IUD), [OR] DepoProvera, [OR] tubal ligation or vasectomy of the partner (with confirmed negative sperm counts) in monogamous relationship (same partner). An acceptable, although less reliable, method involves the careful use of condoms [OR] and spermicidal foam or gel [OR] and/or cervical cap or sponge. A pregnancy test is required at least 10 days from the last normal menstrual period, if the patient is a sexually active female of childbearing potential.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Allergy to Phenylephrine [OR] NSAIDs.
 - Inability to sit steady and upright for the Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT) [NOT].
 - Complications during surgery, including posterior capsular rupture, vitreous loss, zonular dialysis, or iris trauma [NOT].
 - Macular thickness above 300 microns at baseline [NOT].
 - Currently taking a prostaglandin analogue [NOT].
 - Presence of an epiretinal membrane on the preoperative OCT [NOT].
 - Retained lens fragment post-operatively [NOT].
 - Inability to return for follow appointments [NOT].
 - Female patients who are [NOT] pregnant, lactating or planning to become pregnant during the course of treatment.
