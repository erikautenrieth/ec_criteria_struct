Inclusion Criteria:
 - Major Depressive Disorder according to Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (DSM-V) [AND] > 25 years [AND] Sufficient understanding of the German language
Exclusion Criteria:
 - < 25 years [OR] Concomitant diagnosis of past or present psychotic disorder [OR] Concomitant diagnosis of past or present bipolar disorder [OR] First degree relative with a psychotic disorder
 - Unable or unwilling to discontinue antidepressant medication [OR] Pregnancy or breastfeeding [OR] Known hypersensitivity to LSD [OR] Somatic disorders including central nervous system (CNS) involvement
 - Known or suspected non-compliance, drug or alcohol abuse [OR] Metal implants [OR] Weight < 42 kg [OR] Suicide risk or very likely to require psychiatric hospitalisation
