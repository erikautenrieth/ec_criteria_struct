Inclusion Criteria:
 - Treatment-seeking cigarette smoker
 - Meet DSM-IV criteria for nicotine dependence [AND] with [OR] without
 - Negative result on urine test for drug use during screening

Exclusion Criteria:
 - History [AND] of any Axis I psychiatric diagnosis [NOT] other than nicotine dependence
 - Medical conditions [OR] that might affect brain function
 - Current use [AND] of medications [OR] that could alter brain function
 - Pregnancy [OR] or
 - Current illicit drug use [NOT] other than occasional use of marijuana
