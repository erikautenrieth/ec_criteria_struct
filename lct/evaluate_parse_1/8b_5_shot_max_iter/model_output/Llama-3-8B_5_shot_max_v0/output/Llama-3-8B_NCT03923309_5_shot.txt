Inclusion Criteria:
 - biopsy proven rectal cancer [AND] (anal verge ≤ 10cm)
 - neoadjuvant radiotherapy [OR] chemoradiotherapy
 - no radical surgery after neoadjuvant therapy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - other [NOT] malignant disease
 - other [NOT] fatal disease
 - incompletion of [NOT] half of radiotherapy course
 - metastasis on initial presentation
