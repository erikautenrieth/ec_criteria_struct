 - Completion of SLE Study (NCT03252587) through the protocol-required treatment period [AND] currently receiving blinded study drug

 - Any disease [OR] medical condition that, in the opinion of the investigator, would make the subject [NOT] unsuitable for this study, would interfere with the interpretation of subject safety [OR] study results, or considered unsuitable by the investigator for any other reason
