Inclusion Criteria:
 - Gastroscopy can be performed
 - Serologic markers [AND] including serum pepsinogen can be measured
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Age [NOT] < 20 [OR] > 70 years
 - Anemia (serum hemoglobin level [NOT] < 10 g/dL)
 - Severe systemic disease
 - Advanced chronic liver disease
 - Use of certain medications, including [OR] proton pump inhibitors, [OR] H2- receptor antagonists, [OR] or antibiotics
 - History of H. pylori eradication
 - History of gastric surgery
 - Recent history of upper gastrointestinal bleeding
