Inclusion Criteria:
 - Men [AND] and women over 18 years old [AND] with a BMI equal to or greater than 30 Kg / m2 [AND] with clinical and radiological criteria of knee osteoarthritis [AND] with indication for primary TKA according to medical criteria.
 - Written informed consent according to ICH / GCP and Spanish legislation, obtained before any study procedure.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnancy [OR] or lactation
 - Inability [NOT] to give informed consent in the absence of a legal representative
 - Subjects that are participating in a study with medicines [OR] or other medical devices
 - Those who show inability [NOT] to follow the instructions [OR] collaborate during the development of the study
 - If in the opinion of the researcher there are findings in the physical examination, abnormalities in the results of the clinical analyzes [OR] or other medical, social [OR] or psychosocial factors that could have a negative influence
 - Having had a previous surgical procedure on or around the knee in the last 12 months (such as proximal tibial osteotomy, femoral osteotomy, open reduction and internal fixation for a fracture, patellectomy [OR] or patellar realignment)
 - Need a simultaneous bilateral TKA
 - Have a varus angle [OR] or valgus ≥ 15 °
 - Total revision knee arthroplasty
