Inclusion Criteria:
 - 20-70 age
 - Chronic ankle sprain more than 3 months [AND] with chronic ankle pain [OR] or instability sensation
 - Ultrasound diagnosed anterior talofibular ligament sprain
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Acute ankle sprain less than 3 months [NOT]
 - Lower limb fracture history [OR]
 - Cognitive impairment [OR]
 - Other neurological [OR] or muscular disorders [OR]
 - Sever [NOT] pain that could not tolerate examination
