Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of primary knee osteoarthritis [AND] according to American College of Radiology
 - Being radiographically K-L grade 2-3
 - Presence of symptoms for at least 3 months
 - Patients who have been planned a conventional physiotherapy programme [AND] because of knee osteoarthritis
 - Presence of functional capacity for outpatient physical therapy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with [NOT] secondary knee osteoarthritis
 - History of lower extremity [OR] spine surgery
 - History of intraarticular injection in last [OR] 1 month
 - Patients with neurological disease that will [NOT] affect muscle strength, balance [OR] and coordination
