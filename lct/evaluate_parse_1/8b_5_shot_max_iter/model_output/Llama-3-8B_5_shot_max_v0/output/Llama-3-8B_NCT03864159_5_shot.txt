Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age between 18 [AND] to 25 years
 - Amateur basketball players [OR] who belong to the Celeste club
 - Not having any injury to the hamstring musculature at the time of the study
Exclusion Criteria:
 - At the time of the study, they are included in a program of individualized strength training in lower limbs [NOT]
 - Subjects with alterations in venous return [OR] or congenital coagulopathies
 - Subjects who present hypermobility
 - Not signed the informed consent document
