Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age between 18 [OR] to 25 years
 - Amateur basketball players [AND] who
 - Belong to the Celeste club [AND] with
 - Not having any injury to the hamstring musculature at the time of the study [NOT]

Exclusion Criteria:
 - At the time of the study they are included in a program of individualized strength training in lower limbs [NOT]
 - Subjects with alterations in venous return [OR] or congenital coagulopathies [OR]
 - Subjects who present hypermobility [OR]
 - Not signed the informed consent document [NOT]

Note: I added the [NOT] operator before "not" in the inclusion criteria to make it clear that the condition is not excluded. Similarly, I added the [NOT] operator before "not" in the exclusion criteria to make it clear that the condition is excluded.
