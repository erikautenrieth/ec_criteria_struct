Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
1. Patients [AND] free from any disease affecting bone formation [OR] of both sexes was included in the study.
2. Patients [AND] younger than 25 years old.
3. Patients [AND] included in the study were post traumatic patients [OR] hemifacial microsomia [OR] Treacher Collins [OR] and post ankylotic mandibular deformity.
4. Distraction range was between 15-20 mm.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
1. The presence of uncontrolled systemic disease.
2. Previous radiotherapy on head and neck region.
3. Chemotherapy within the past 12-month period [NOT] free from any disease affecting bone formation.
4. An active infection at the distraction site.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Note: I added the [NOT] operator before "free from any disease affecting bone formation" in exclusion criterion 3, as it seems to be a contradictory statement.
