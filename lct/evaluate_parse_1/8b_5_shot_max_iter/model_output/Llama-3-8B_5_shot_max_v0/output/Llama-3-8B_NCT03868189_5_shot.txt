Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient requiring an electroneuromyogram whose indication allows the application of a standard protocol of examination [AND] with patient that has given his/her [OR] free and informed consent.
 - Patient that signed the consent form [AND] with patient with an health insurance plan.
 - Patient that is at least 18 years old (≥) [AND] with patient that is under 80 years old (≤).

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patient with symptoms of neuropathy during the screening [NOT] and patient whose Neurological severity score (NSS) score is [NOT] ≥ 3.
 - Patient whose neurological examination is abnormal [OR] and suggestive of neuropathy.
 - Patient presenting a pathology that could cause neuropathy (e.g. diabetes, renal failure, etc.) [OR] patient already participating in a category 1 research study (Jardé law).
 - Patient in an exclusion period determined by another study [OR] patient under the protection of justice, under guardianship [OR] or under curatorship.
 - Patient refusing to sign the consent form [OR] patient for which it is [NOT] possible to provide information about the study.
 - Pregnant patient, parturient, or breastfeeding [NOT].
