Inclusion Criteria:
 - patients presenting a multi-bullous pemphigoid newly diagnosed [OR] or relapsed more than 3 months after stopping corticosteroids and treated according to the national protocol for diagnosis and care issued by the reference center for autoimmune bullous diseases of April 2016
 - patients [AND] having received written [AND] and oral information [AND] and signed informed consent
 - patients [AND] covered by national health insurance
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] under tutorship [OR] curatorship [OR] or inability to give informed consent
 - Patients [AND] receiving an anti-osteoporotic treatment
 - Patients [AND] requiring an anti-osteoporotic baseline treatment (T-score ⩽ -3DS [OR] on at least 1 site [OR] or FRAX score above the therapeutic intervention threshold)
 - Patients [AND] with one [OR] more major risk factors for osteoporosis
 - Patients [AND] who have received topical corticosteroids in less than 3 months
