Inclusion Criteria:
 - Lung surgical patients [AND] who were sent to thoracic postoperative intensive care unit (ICU) ward right after surgery [AND] stayed in ICU for at least 24 hours [OR] longer.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Cases [AND] received inferior vena filter [OR] anti-coagulation treatment history [NOT] excluded.

Note: The edited inclusion criteria include the additional condition "or longer" to ensure that patients who stay in ICU for more than 24 hours are also included.
