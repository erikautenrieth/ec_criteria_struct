Inclusion Criteria:
 - Primipara [AND] (no previous pregnancies)
 - 18 [AND] to 25 years of age
 - Gestation [AND] <15 weeks at first prenatal visit
 - No intention [AND] to move outside the city of Santiago in the next two years
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Multipara [NOT]
 - Pre-existing cancer [AND] (except non-melanoma skin cancer)
 - Family history [AND] of breast cancer (one first-degree female relative diagnosed with breast cancer)
 - High-risk pregnancy [AND] according to national guidelines
 - During the follow-up, pregnant women [AND] under any treatment [OR] that may affect the levels of metabolic markers will be [NOT] excluded from the analysis
 - Pregnant women [AND] with pre-eclampsia [OR] with preterm birth (less than 37 weeks of gestational age) will be [NOT] also excluded
