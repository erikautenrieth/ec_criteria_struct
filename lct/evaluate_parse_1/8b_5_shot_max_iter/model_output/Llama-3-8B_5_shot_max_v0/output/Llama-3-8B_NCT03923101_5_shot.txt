Inclusion Criteria:
 - All Keratoconus patients presenting in the department of ophthalmology, Saarland University Medical Center, Homburg, Saarland, Germany.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] Patients with Keratoconus.

Note: Since there is only one exclusion criterion, it is not necessary to use a logical operator. The [NOT] operator is used to negate the presence of Keratoconus, indicating that patients without Keratoconus are excluded.
