Inclusion Criteria:
 - All comatose patients (Glasgow coma score [AND] <8) due to carbon monoxide poisoning, treated by hyperbaric oxygen therapy in a tertiary hospital in Île de France area [OR] 
 - Aged ≥ 18 years [AND] 
 - Hospitalized in hyperbaric medicine unity of Raymond Poincaré hospital in Garches.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnant [NOT] woman;
 - Patient present clinical signes [AND] of stroke [AND] without CO detection.
In case of unusable data, patient will be [NOT] excluded from study.
