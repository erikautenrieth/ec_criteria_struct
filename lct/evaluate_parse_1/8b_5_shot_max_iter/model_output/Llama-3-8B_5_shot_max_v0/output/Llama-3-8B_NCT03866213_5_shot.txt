Inclusion Criteria:
 - The patient is currently being treated at Queen Elizabeth Central Hospital [OR] or Kamuzu Central Hospital in the neonatal ward.
 - The patient is less than 28 days old [AND] (neonate).
 - The patient is deemed to be at risk for jaundice [OR] the patient is undergoing blue light phototherapy for treatment of jaundice.
 - The patient's parent or guardian has provided informed consent for their child to participate.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Parent or guardian [NOT] is able [OR] or unwilling to provide informed consent.
 - The patient [NOT] is able to receive a blood draw/heel stick as determined by their clinician.
