Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] and parents of the patients who accept to participate [OR] and sign the informed consent
 - Patients whose molar teeth are diagnosed as molar incisor hypomineralization by EAPD
 - Teeth that are fully erupted [AND]
 - Patients who have at least two permanent first molars that need fissure sealant [OR]
 - Teeth that have white-yellow-brown discoloration that indicate high-caries risk

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Teeth that are previously restored [OR] or have caries
 - Patients who have orthodontic treatment [OR]
 - Patients who are not cooperative for the dental procedure [NOT]
 - Teeth that have dental flourosis [OR] or enamel malformation according to the specific syndromes
