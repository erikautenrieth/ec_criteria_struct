Inclusion Criteria:
 - Burn patients
 - Severe neuropatic pain rated at least 5 on the 10-point numerical rating scale (NRS) [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Cardiac arrest history [OR]
 - History of neurologi [NOT] disease or brain surgery
 - Unstable heart disease [OR] or presence of a cardiac pacemaker
 - Pain resulting from other causes like as [NOT] neuromuscular diseases
 - Psychiatric disorder [OR]
 - Diabetes mellitus [OR]
 - Abnormal renal function [OR]
 - Contraindication for MRI [OR] or pregnancy
