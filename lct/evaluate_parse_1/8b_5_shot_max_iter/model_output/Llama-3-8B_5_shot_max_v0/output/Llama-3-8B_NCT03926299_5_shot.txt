Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of vulvar lichen sclerosus [AND] clinical LS score ≥ 4
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Concomitant [NOT] steroid, [NOT] calcineurin inhibitor [OR] any other topical [OR] systemic treatment for LS
 - < 3 months since start of vaginal estrogen treatment [OR] any other treatment
 - Malignant disease [AND] as the cause of the vulval symptoms
 - BMI > 35 kg/m² [AND] or
 - Acute infection [AND] (fungal, bacterial, viral) of the vulva, vagina [OR] or bladder
 - Presence of [NOT] contraindications for the laser treatment [OR] or topical steroid treatment
