<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients with recent stroke/TIA [AND] and severe stenosis of intracranial ICA or MCA [AND] and impaired CVR within previous three months [OR] but not before 3 weeks after acute stroke. This is to differentiate between patients with a long-standing fixed-stenosis from patients with partially recanalized intracranial artery (masquerading as severe stenosis).
 2. Age >21 years [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients with atrial fibrillation/arrhythmias [OR]
 2. Within 2 weeks of cardiac catheterization or arterial puncture at femoral puncture site [AND]
 3. Decompensated heart failure [NOT] usually class 3 or 4 [AND]
 4. LV EF <30% [AND]
 5. Moderate or severe AR [AND]
 6. Persistent and uncontrolled hypertension (BP persistently >160/100 mmHg) [OR]
 7. Bleeding diathesis [AND]
 8. Active thrombophlebitis/venous disease of lower limbs [OR]
 9. Severe lower extremity vaso-occlusive disease [AND]
 10. Presence of a documented aortic aneurysm/dissection requiring surgical repair [OR]
 11. Pregnancy [AND]
