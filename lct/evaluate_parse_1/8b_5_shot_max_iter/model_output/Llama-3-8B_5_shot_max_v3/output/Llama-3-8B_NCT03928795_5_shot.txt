Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Monofetal pregnancy [AND] age over 18 years old [AND] term pregnancy ≥ 37SA [AND] indication for elective caesarean section

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] fetal pathology known prenatally like intrauterine severe growth retardation with Doppler abnormalities, fetal malformation [OR] or a known fetal genetic defect
  -  anomaly of placental adhesion
  -  caesarean in a context of medical [OR] obstetric emergency
  -  caesarean section performed under general anesthesia [OR] following failure of locoregional anesthesia
  -  refusal of the woman to participate in the study
  -  impossibility [OR] failure to study the neonatal acid-base balance on the umbilical cord
