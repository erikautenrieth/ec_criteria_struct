<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:

1. Diagnosis of CD for at least 3 months prior to baseline [AND] confirmed diagnosis of moderate to severe CD as assessed by SF, AP score, and SES-CD [OR] demonstrated intolerance, loss of response or inadequate response to conventional or to biologic therapy for CD [AND] if female, subject must meet the contraception recommendations.
2. Diagnosis of CD for at least 3 months prior to baseline [AND] confirmed diagnosis of moderate to severe CD as assessed by SF, AP score, and SES-CD [OR] demonstrated intolerance, loss of response or inadequate response to conventional or to biologic therapy for CD [AND] if female, subject must meet the contraception recommendations.

Exclusion Criteria:

1. Have a current diagnosis of ulcerative colitis, inflammatory bowel disease-unclassified (IBD-U) (formerly known as indeterminate colitis), abdominal or perianal abscess or short bowel syndrome [NOT] have a stoma or ostomy [AND] have had a bowel resection within 6 months, or any kind of intra-abdominal or extra abdominal surgery within 3 months of baseline [OR] have ever received any monoclonal antibodies binding IL-23.
2. Have a current diagnosis of ulcerative colitis, inflammatory bowel disease-unclassified (IBD-U) (formerly known as indeterminate colitis), abdominal or perianal abscess or short bowel syndrome [NOT] have a stoma or ostomy [AND] have had a bowel resection within 6 months, or any kind of intra-abdominal or extra abdominal surgery within 3 months of baseline [OR] have ever received any monoclonal antibodies binding IL-23.

Note: The edited criteria include the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] as per the rules provided.
