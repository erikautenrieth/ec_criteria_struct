Inclusion Criteria (PTA group)
  - T1D patients who received their first PTA [OR] second PTA only if their first graft was explanted within a week, between 2 January 2005 and 31 December 2017
  - Age [AND] ≥18 [AND] < 65 years
  - Willingness to participate in this observational study

Inclusion Criteria (control group)
  - Type 1 diabetes
  - Age [AND] ≥18 years [AND] < 65 years
  - Willingness to participate in this observational study
  - Minimum disease duration of 5 years
  - Brittle diabetes, as defined by the occurrence of frequent acute complications [OR] severe hypoglycaemic episodes requiring third-part assistance [OR] unawareness despite well-conducted intensive insulin therapy
  - Presence of at least one diabetes-related complication [OR] neuropathy [OR] retinopathy [OR] subclinical nephropathy

Exclusion Criteria (both groups)
  - Conditions other than T1D known to affect bone health [OR] non-compensated hypothyroidism [OR] hyperthyroidism [OR] hyperparathyroidism [OR] inflammatory bowel disease [OR] malignancy [OR] rheumatoid arthritis [OR] hypogonadism [OR] severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] hepatic insufficiency [OR] alcohol intake ≥ 3 units/day
  - Use of drugs that can affect bone metabolism [OR] bisphosphonates [OR] anticonvulsants [OR] hormone replacement therapy [OR] other than glucocorticoids [OR] immunosuppressants
  - Recipients of simultaneous pancreas kidney transplantation [OR] pancreatic islet transplantation [OR] multiple PTAs [OR] other transplants
  - BMI <18.5 [OR] ≥ 30 kg/m2
