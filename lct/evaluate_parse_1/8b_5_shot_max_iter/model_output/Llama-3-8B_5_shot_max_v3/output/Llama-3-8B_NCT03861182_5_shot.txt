Inclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
  - Over 18 years of age [AND] with no underlying medical conditions
Neonates:
  - Aged between 1h of life [OR] and 28 days of life
  - Born beyond 33 Week of Amenorrhea [OR] + 1day [AND] and 2kg of birth weight
  - Hospitalized in the medical-surgical centre of Pediatrics of the hospital of Strasbourg [AND] with a blood sample prescribed as part of their routine care
Exclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
  - Systemic inflammatory disease [OR] transient inflammatory state
  - Any drug that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h preceding the sampling [OR] use of illicit drugs
Neonates:
  - Risk of anemia [NOT] < 7g/DL
  - Current anticoagulant treatment [OR] use of medications that modify the coagulation cascade
