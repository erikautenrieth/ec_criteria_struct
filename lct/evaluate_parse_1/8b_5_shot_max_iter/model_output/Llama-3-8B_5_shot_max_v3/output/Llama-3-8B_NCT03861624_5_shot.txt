Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria:

<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:

1. Skeletal maturity [AND] AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures
2. Wound primarily closeable [AND] palpable pedal pulses [AND] presentation within 24 hours from injury

Exclusion Criteria:

1. [NOT] current injury is a pathologic fracture
2. Sustained bilateral tibia fracture [OR] sustained comminuted femur fracture
3. Sustained severe Traumatic Brain Injury (GCS<12) [OR] sustained severe spinal cord injury (lower extremity paresis/paralysis)
4. Sustained severe burns (>10% total body surface area (TBSA) or >5% TBSA with full thickness or circumferential injury)
5. Prior ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery [OR] prior or current lower limb deformity or abnormality
6. Unable to complete follow-up visits

</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Note: I added the [AND] operator to the inclusion criteria to ensure that all conditions are met, and I added the [OR] operator to the exclusion criteria to allow for multiple conditions to be excluded. I also added the [NOT] operator to the first exclusion criterion to indicate that the condition should not be met.
