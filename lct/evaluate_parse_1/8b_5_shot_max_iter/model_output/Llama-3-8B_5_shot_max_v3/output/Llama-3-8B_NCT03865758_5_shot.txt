Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Apparently healthy men [AND] Body mass index between 18 [OR] and 30 kg/m2 [AND] Willing to eat only the foods provided by the study for a 2 week period
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Those reporting any chronic [OR] acute diseases, food allergies, smoking [OR] alcohol abuse [AND] Use of illicit drugs; regular consumption of medications, micronutrient supplements, or both [AND] Vegetarians, or those unable to eat meat, are also excluded since the foods for the study contain meat [AND] Unable to refrain from taking medications during the study period [AND] No evidence of dementia [NOT] probable dementia indicated by prior diagnosis and/or a score of 23 or less on the Brief Alzheimer Screen.
