Inclusion Criteria:
 1. The fifth stage of chronic renal failure diagnosed by a physician [AND] with a physician's advice and patient's understanding of the need for renal replacement therapy, but without a decision on treatment.
 2. Ability to communicate in Mandarin [AND] Taiwan.
 3. Age over 20 years old.

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Cognitive impairment [NOT] with a MMSE score of 21 points or higher, or [NOT] with a national minimum of 16 points or higher.
 2. Patients who have received non-emergency dialysis treatment [OR] have not undergone dialysis treatment.
 3. Patients with severe cardiopulmonary dysfunction, severe shock, low blood pressure, or large bleeding that is [NOT] controlled.
 4. Patients with chronic active hepatitis, cirrhosis, or liver failure.
 5. A history of cancer that may be transferred [OR] has not been transferred.
 6. Severe bleeding tendency [OR] no bleeding tendency.
 7. Loss of peritoneal function or extensive peritoneal adhesion that makes it [NOT] possible to use peritoneal dialysis.
 8. Unable to correct physiological defects that may interfere with peritoneal dialysis [OR] increase the chance of infection, such as umbilical hernia.
 9. The patient is unable to operate the fluid change, hand shake, or has no family members to assist.
 10. Patients who are blind, have poor eyesight, and have difficulty operating [OR] have normal vision and can operate.
