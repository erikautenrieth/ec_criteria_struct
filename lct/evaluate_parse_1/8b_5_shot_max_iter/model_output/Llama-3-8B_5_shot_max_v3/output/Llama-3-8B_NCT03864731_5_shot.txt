Inclusion Criteria:
  - Unilateral [OR] bilateral conductive hearing loss (CHL)
  - Conductive hearing loss >10 decibel on average [AND] with subjective benefit from the device
  - Subject aged 13 years [OR] older
  - Capable of the German language [AND] willing and able to perform all tests required for the study
  - Signed, and dated informed consent before the start of any study specific procedure

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding
  - Patient uses a hearing aid [OR] plans to acquire a hearing aid
  - Patient is intolerant of the materials as described by Manufacturer [AND] patient presents with a skin or scalp condition that may preclude the attachment of the adhesive adapter.
  - Patient cannot perform the audiological tests [OR] is unable to fill out the questionnaires.
  - Patient presents with retrocochlear [OR] central auditory disorder [AND] any physical, psychological, or emotional disorder that would interfere with the ability to perform on test and rehabilitation procedures.
