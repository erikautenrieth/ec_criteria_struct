Inclusion Criteria:
  - Vaginal delivery [AND] an infant age of 6 months (±3 weeks)
  - Mother ≥15 years of age [AND] infant still breastfeeding
  - Anticipated residence in the area for the study duration [AND] mother willing to participate in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] ability to provide informed consent
  - Hemoglobin < 70 g/L [OR] Z scores for weight-for-age (WAZ) or weight-for-height (WHZ) <3
