Inclusion Criteria:
  - Chronic [OR] more than three months ESBL colonization of the same strain as determined by [AND] species, phenotype and antibiogram.
  - Patients must be able to swallow sachets [AND] must be able to speak and understand Swedish.
  - Must have a permanent residence in Sweden [AND] must be able to sign informed consent in Swedish.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Immunosuppression [NOT] i.e. chemotheraphy, treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors.
  - In patient care [OR] immunodeficiency.
  - Psychiatric disorder [OR] alcohol or substance abuse.
  - Dementia [OR] invasive catheters.
