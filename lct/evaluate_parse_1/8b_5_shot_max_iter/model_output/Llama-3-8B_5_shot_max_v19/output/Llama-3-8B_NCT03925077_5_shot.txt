<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - 1. demonstrate readiness to physical activity by completing the online Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire (PAR-Q) [AND] 
  - 2. have a SCI resulting in incomplete or complete (C5 and below) paraplegia or tetraplegia [OR] 
  - 3. have broadband internet access [AND] 
  - 4. between the ages of 18 to 65 years [AND] 
  - 5. have the ability to converse and read English.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - 1. significant visual impairment [NOT] preventing seeing a computer screen to follow a home exercise program [OR] 
  - 2. active pressure ulcer [AND] 
  - 3. any contraindications to exercise based on the American College of Sports Medicine guidelines [OR] 
  - 4. significant hearing impairment [NOT] impeding ability to hear music to engage in exercise.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
