<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Major Depressive Disorder according to Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (DSM-V) [AND] 
  -  > 25 years [OR] 
  -  Sufficient understanding of the German language [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  < 25 years [NOT] 
  -  Concomitant diagnosis of past or present psychotic disorder [OR] 
  -  Concomitant diagnosis of past or present bipolar disorder [OR] 
  -  First degree relative with a psychotic disorder [OR] 
  -  Unable or unwilling to discontinue antidepressant medication [NOT] 
  -  Pregnancy or breastfeeding [NOT] 
  -  Known hypersensitivity to LSD [NOT] 
  -  Somatic disorders including central nervous system (CNS) involvement [OR] 
  -  Known or suspected non-compliance, drug or alcohol abuse [NOT] 
  -  Metal implants [NOT] 
  -  Weight < 42 kg [NOT] 
  -  Suicide risk or very likely to require psychiatric hospitalisation [NOT]

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
