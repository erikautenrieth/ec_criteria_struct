Inclusion Criteria:
 - Documented keratoconus [OR] ectasia after refractive surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Insufficient corneal thickness
 - Ocular condition [AND] that may predispose the eye to complications
 - History [AND] of chemical injury [OR] or delayed epithelial healing
 - Condition [AND] that would [NOT] interfere with or prolong epithelial healing
 - Known sensitivity [AND] to treatment medications
 - Pregnancy
