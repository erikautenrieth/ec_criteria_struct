Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] > 50 years old
  - Bilateral age-related cataract
  - [AND] qualify for bilateral implantation within 1 month
  - [AND] no previous refractive or ocular surgery
  - [AND] available IOL dioptre range: 18 to 27D
  - [AND] expected postoperative astigmatism ≤ 0.75D

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [OR] pupil > 4 mm
  - [OR] occupation requiring night-time driving [OR] any occupation incompatible with multifocality
  - [AND] acute or chronic disease or illness that would increase risk [OR] confound study results [e.g. diabetes mellitus (with retinopathy), [OR] immunocompromised, [OR] glaucoma, [OR] epiretinal membrane, [OR] cornea guttata, etc….] found to be relevant by the investigators
  - [OR] amblyopia, [OR] strabismus
  - [OR] extremely shallow anterior chamber
  - [OR] capsule or zonular abnormalities that may affect postoperative centration [OR] tilt of the lens (e.g. pseudoexfoliation syndrome, [OR] uveitis, [OR] Marfan's syndrome)
  - [OR] pupil abnormalities (non-reactive, [OR] tonic pupils, [OR] abnormally shaped pupils [OR] pupils that do not dilate under mesopic/ scotopic conditions)
  - [OR] instability to place the intraocular lens safely at the location planned
  - [OR] [NOT] pregnancy (pregnancy test will be taken pre-operatively in women of reproductive age)
