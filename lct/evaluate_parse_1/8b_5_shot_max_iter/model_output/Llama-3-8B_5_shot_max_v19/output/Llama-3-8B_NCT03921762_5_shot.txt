Inclusion Criteria:
 - > 50 years old [AND] with bilateral age-related cataract
 - Qualify for bilateral implantation within 1 month [AND] with no previous refractive [OR] ocular surgery
 - Available IOL dioptre range: 18 [OR] to 27D [AND] with expected postoperative astigmatism ≤ 0.75D
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pupil > 4 mm [NOT]
 - Occupation requiring night-time driving [OR] or any occupation incompatible with multifocality [NOT]
 - Acute [OR] chronic disease [OR] illness that would increase risk [OR] confound study results [e.g. diabetes mellitus (with retinopathy), [OR] immunocompromised, [OR] glaucoma, [OR] epiretinal membrane, [OR] cornea guttata, etc….] found to be relevant by the investigators
 - Amblyopia [OR] strabismus [NOT]
 - Extremely shallow anterior chamber [NOT]
 - Capsule [OR] zonular abnormalities that may affect postoperative centration [OR] tilt of the lens (e.g. pseudoexfoliation syndrome, [OR] uveitis, [OR] Marfan's syndrome) [NOT]
 - Pupil abnormalities (non-reactive, tonic pupils, abnormally shaped pupils [OR] or pupils that do not dilate under mesopic/ scotopic conditions) [NOT]
 - Instability to place the intraocular lens safely at the location planned [NOT]
 - Pregnancy (pregnancy test will be taken pre-operatively in women of reproductive age)
