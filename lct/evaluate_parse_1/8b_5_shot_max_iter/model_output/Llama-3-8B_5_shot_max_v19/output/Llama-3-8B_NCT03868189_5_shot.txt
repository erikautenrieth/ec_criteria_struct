Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient requiring an electroneuromyogram whose indication allows the application of a standard protocol of examination [AND] with patient that has given his/her [OR] free and informed consent [AND] with patient that signed the consent form [AND] with patient with an health insurance plan [AND] with patient that is at least 18 years old (≥) [AND] with patient that is under 80 years old (≤)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patient with symptoms of neuropathy during the screening [NOT]
 - Patient whose Neurological severity score (NSS) score is ≥ 3 [NOT]
 - Patient whose neurological examination is abnormal and suggestive of neuropathy [NOT]
 - Patient presenting a pathology that could cause neuropathy (e.g. diabetes, renal failure, etc.) [NOT]
 - Patient already participating in a category 1 research study (Jardé law) [NOT]
 - Patient in an exclusion period determined by another study [NOT]
 - Patient under the protection of justice, under guardianship or under curatorship [NOT]
 - Patient refusing to sign the consent form [NOT]
 - Patient for which it is impossible to provide information about the study [NOT]
 - Pregnant patient, parturient, or breastfeeding [NOT]
