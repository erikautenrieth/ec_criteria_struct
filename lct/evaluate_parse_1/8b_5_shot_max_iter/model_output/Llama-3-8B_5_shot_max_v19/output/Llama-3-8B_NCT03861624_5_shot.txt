<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:

1. Skeletal maturity [AND] AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures
2. Wound primarily closeable [AND] (no flap or delay in closure due to contamination needed) [OR] palpable pedal pulses (no vascular injury sustained)
3. Presentation within 24 hours from injury [OR] skeletal maturity

Exclusion Criteria:

1. Current injury is a pathologic fracture [NOT] or sustained bilateral tibia fracture
2. Sustained comminuted femur fracture [OR] severe Traumatic Brain Injury (GCS<12) [OR] severe spinal cord injury (lower extremity paresis/paralysis)
3. Sustained severe burns (>10% total body surface area (TBSA) or >5% TBSA with full thickness or circumferential injury) [OR] prior ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery
4. Prior or current lower limb deformity or abnormality [OR] unable to complete follow-up visits
