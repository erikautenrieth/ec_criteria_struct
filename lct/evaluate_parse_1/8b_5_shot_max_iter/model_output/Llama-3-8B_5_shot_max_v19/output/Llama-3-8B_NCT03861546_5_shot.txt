Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age > 18 years [AND] confirmed diagnosis of prediabetes [OR] T2D (documented A1c of ≥ 5.7% [OR] 2-hour post-load glucose of >140 [OR] FBG of >100) [AND] a family member [OR] peer willing to participate [AND] attend all sessions as a social influencer [AND] proficiency in English of at least one member of each dyad [AND] willingness to provide written consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Type 1 diabetes [OR] diabetes secondary to other conditions (e.g. [OR] steroid-induced, [OR] pancreatic insufficiency, [OR] chemotherapy-induced) [NOT] type 2 diabetes;
 - Malignancy [OR] life-threatening illness with life expectancy of <5 years;
 - End-stage disease [OR] serious illness that prohibits participation (e.g. [OR] end-stage renal disease [OR] class IV congestive heart failure);
 - Inability to perform unsupervised physical activity [OR] inability to participate in the study;
 - Pregnancy;
 - Diagnosed cognitive deficits [OR] limited decision-making capacity;
 - Alcohol [OR] substance abuse;
 - Homelessness [OR] no fixed address.
