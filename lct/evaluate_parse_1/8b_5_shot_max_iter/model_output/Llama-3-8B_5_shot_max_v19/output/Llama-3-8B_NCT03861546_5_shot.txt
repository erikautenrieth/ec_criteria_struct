Inclusion Criteria:
  1. age [AND] > 18 years;
  2. confirmed diagnosis of prediabetes [OR] or T2D (documented A1c of ≥ 5.7% [OR] 2-hour post-load glucose of >140 [OR] FBG of >100);
  3. a family member [AND] or peer willing to participate [AND] and attend all sessions as a social influencer;
  4. proficiency in English [AND] of at least one member of each dyad [OR],;
  5. willingness to provide written consent [AND].

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. type 1 diabetes [OR] diabetes secondary to other conditions (e.g. steroid-induced [OR] pancreatic insufficiency [OR] or chemotherapy-induced);
  2. malignancy [OR] life-threatening illness with life expectancy of <5 years [OR] ;
  3. end-stage disease [OR] serious illness that prohibits participation (e.g. end-stage renal disease [OR] class IV congestive heart failure);
  4. [NOT] inability to perform unsupervised physical activity;
  5. pregnancy [OR] ;
  6. diagnosed cognitive deficits [OR] limited decision-making capacity [OR] ;
  7. alcohol [OR] substance abuse [OR] ;
  8. homelessness [OR] no fixed address [OR].
