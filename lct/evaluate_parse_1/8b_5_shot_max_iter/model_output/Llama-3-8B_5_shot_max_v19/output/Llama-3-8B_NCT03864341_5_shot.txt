<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Be a patient in the Bellevue Hospital Center ED [AND] Be ≥18 years old [AND] Screen positive for homelessness risk using a homelessness risk screening tool (HRST) [AND] Screen positive for unhealthy alcohol or drug use [AND] Able to provide consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Medically [OR] psychiatrically unstable [OR] Unable to provide informed consent for other reason [OR] (e.g., cognitive deficit, profound intoxication) [OR] Incarcerated [OR] in police custody [OR] Unable to understand and speak English [OR] Lives outside NYC [OR] and/or cannot give a NYC ZIP code [OR] Already homeless (residing in a shelter or on the streets) [OR] Does not have a telephone where can be reached for follow-up [OR] Has already received specialized peer navigator/addiction social worker services during current ED visit [OR] Has already participated in the study.
