  - All adults undergoing cardiac surgery with CPB will be considered for enrollment.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Factors that affect immune function or AKI assessment are the basis for exclusion criteria: [OR]
       - Concurrent disease associated with immunosuppression [AND] including malignancy, [OR] chronic infection (e.g. HIV, [OR] Hepatitis C), [OR] organ transplant [AND] immunosuppressant medications
       - Documented acute infection [AND] with the past 1 month (e.g. pneumonia, [OR] urinary tract infection)
       - Prednisone [OR] or other steroid use currently [OR] within the past one month
       - AKI at the time of surgery
       - ESRD [AND] requiring renal replacement therapy
       - Estimated GFR [AND] <45 mL/min (as judged by the Chronic Kidney Disease Epidemiology Collaboration (CKD-EPI) equation
