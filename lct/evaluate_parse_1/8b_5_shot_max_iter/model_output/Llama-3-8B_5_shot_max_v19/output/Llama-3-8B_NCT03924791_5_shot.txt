Inclusion Criteria:
  - Diagnosed with sciatica by GP [AND] NRS leg pain of 6 [OR] more on a 10-point NRS scale [AND] minimum duration of symptoms of 3 weeks [AND] maximum duration of 8 weeks

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Age under 18 years [NOT] or over 18 years
  - Condition preventing to receive transforaminal epidural injection [OR] severe scoliosis
  - Transforaminal epidural injection received in 6 months before randomization date [OR] surgery for sciatica at the same level [OR] surgery for sciatica at another level within one year before inclusion [OR] pregnancy
