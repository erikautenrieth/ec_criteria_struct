<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients age: ≥ 22 years [AND] 
  - Patients presenting for routine colonoscopy for screening and/or surveillance purposes [OR] 
  - Willingness to undergo two withdrawals with [OR] without the use of computer-aided software while undergoing conventional colonoscopy with sedation [AND] 
  - Ability to provide written, informed consent [AND] understand the responsibilities of trial participation [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Minors aged < 22 years [NOT] 
  - People with diminished cognitive capacity [NOT] 
  - Patients undergoing diagnostic colonoscopy [OR] (e.g. as an evaluation for active gastrointestinal bleed, referring collectively to the stomach and the small and large intestine) [NOT] 
  - Patients with incomplete colonoscopies [OR] (those where endoscopists did not successfully intubate the cecum due to technical difficulties or poor bowel preparation) [NOT] 
  - Patients that have standard contraindications to colonoscopy in general [OR] (e.g. documented acute diverticulitis, fulminant colitis and known or suspected perforation) [NOT] 
  - Patients with inflammatory bowel disease [NOT] 
  - Patients with any polypoid/ulcerated lesion > 2 cm concerning for invasive cancer on endoscopy [NOT] 
  - Patients referred for endoscopic mucosal resection (EMR) [OR] (which is a procedure to remove early-stage cancer and precancerous growths from the lining of the digestive tract) [NOT]
