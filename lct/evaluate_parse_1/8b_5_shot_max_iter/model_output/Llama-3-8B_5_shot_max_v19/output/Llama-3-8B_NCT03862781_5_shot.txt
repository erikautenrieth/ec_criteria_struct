Inclusion Criteria:
 - Eligible patients are those [AND] over 18 years of age [AND] who are regularly scheduled to undergo a right hemicolectomy via a minimally invasive approach [OR] robotic or laparoscopic [AND] with the creation of an anastomosis. Right colectomy will be defined as removal of the ascending colon, [AND] ligation of the ileocolic artery and vein, [OR] +/- removal of the terminal ileum, [OR] +/- removal of the proximal transverse colon, [OR] +/- removal of the right branch of the middle colic artery and vein.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnant women
 - Additional colon resection is planned [AND] i.e. left colectomy or proctectomy
 - Vulnerable populations [AND] such as prisoners [OR] adults unable to give consent
 - If the scheduled surgery is planned at Butterworth hospital [AND] as to standardize the nursing care received postoperatively
 - Emergent cases
Patients will be excluded from the study intra-operatively if:
 - The procedure is converted to an open resection
 - If a loop ileostomy is performed in addition to the right colectomy
