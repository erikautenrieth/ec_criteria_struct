Inclusion Criteria:
 - Singleton gestation [OR] 
 - Twin gestation reduced to singleton either spontaneously [OR] or therapeutically, is eligible unless the reduction occurred after 13 weeks 6 days project gestational age [AND] and higher order multifetal gestations reduced to singletons are eligible.
 - Gestational age at randomization between 16 weeks 0 days [AND] and 23 weeks 6 days based on clinical information [AND] evaluation of the earliest ultrasound as described in Gestational Age.
 - Cervical length on transvaginal examination of less than or equal to 20 mm within 10 days prior to randomization by a study certified sonographer [AND] there is no lower cervical length threshold.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Women [AND] who are ineligible for the TOPS trial [NOT].
