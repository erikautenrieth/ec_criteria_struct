<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  LMNA+ cases [AND] with pathogenic LMNA mutations [OR] for LMNA+ and heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  DCMWT cases: patients [AND] with heart muscle failure but with wild-type lamin gene [OR] Heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
  -  HV (controls): matched [AND] to cases.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Needle-phobia [NOT] that would preclude blood-letting
  -  Participants [NOT] unwilling to consent
  -  Patients [AND] that have a conventional contraindication for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).
  -  Patients [AND] that have had a blood transfusion within the last month [OR] patients having haemodialysis [AND] will be excluded.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
