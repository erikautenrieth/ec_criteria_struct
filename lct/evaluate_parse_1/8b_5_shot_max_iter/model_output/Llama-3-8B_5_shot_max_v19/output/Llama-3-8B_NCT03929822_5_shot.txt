Inclusion Criteria:
  - Women called back from a screening mammography by either FFDM [OR] tomosynthesis with soft tissue abnormalities including masses, asymmetries, focal asymmetries [OR] or architectural distortion with [OR] without calcifications.
  - Patients will be questioned regarding the possibility of pregnancy and will need a negative pregnancy test prior the study intervention.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Age [NOT] <30 years old
  - Screening mammography with only calcifications abnormalities [OR] no soft tissue abnormalities
  - Male patients [OR] patients who are not women
  - Pregnant [OR] or lactating patients
  - Patients with any allergy to iodinated contrast [OR] unable to tolerate contrast
  - Patients with eGFR [NOT] < 45
  - Patients that may be treated with radioactive iodine [OR] have a history of radiation therapy
