<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:

1. The patient has been diagnosed with the fifth stage of chronic renal failure by a physician.
2. The patient has been advised and informed by a physician to undergo renal replacement therapy, but has not yet decided on a treatment plan.
3. The patient is able to communicate in Mandarin and Taiwanese.
4. The patient is over 20 years old.

Exclusion Criteria:

1. Cognitive impairment: The patient's MMSE score is less than 21 points, and the national minimum is less than 16 points, which is not eligible.
2. Patients who have received non-emergency dialysis treatment.
3. Patients with severe cardiopulmonary dysfunction, severe shock, low blood pressure, and large bleeding that is not controlled.
4. Patients with chronic active hepatitis, cirrhosis, or liver failure.
5. A history of cancer that may be transferred.
6. Severe bleeding tendency.
7. Loss of peritoneal function or extensive peritoneal adhesion, which makes it impossible to use peritoneal dialysis.
8. Unable to correct physiological defects, which may interfere with peritoneal dialysis or increase the risk of infection, such as umbilical hernia.
9. The patient is unable to operate the fluid change, hand shake, and has no family members to assist.
10. Patients who are blind, have poor eyesight, and have difficulty operating.
