Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients with recent stroke/TIA [AND] and severe stenosis of intracranial ICA [OR] MCA [AND] impaired CVR within previous three months [AND] but not before 3 weeks after acute stroke. This is to differentiate between patients with a long-standing fixed-stenosis from patients with partially recanalized intracranial artery (masquerading as severe stenosis).
  2. Age [AND] >21 years

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients with atrial fibrillation/ [OR] arrhythmias.
  2. Within [OR] 2 weeks of cardiac catheterization [OR] arterial puncture at femoral puncture site.
  3. Decompensated heart failure [AND], usually class 3 [OR] 4
  4. LV EF [AND] <30%
  5. Moderate [OR] severe AR
  6. Persistent [AND] uncontrolled hypertension (BP persistently >160/100 mmHg)
  7. Bleeding [NOT] diathesis
  8. Active [OR] thrombophlebitis/ venous disease of lower limbs
  9. Severe [AND] lower extremity vaso-occlusive disease
  10. Presence of a documented aortic aneurysm/ dissection [NOT] requiring surgical repair
  11. Pregnancy
