<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:

Adult Healthy Volunteers:
 - Over 18 years of age [AND] healthy
Neonates:
  - Aged between 1h of life [OR] and 28 days of life
  - Born beyond 33 Week of Amenorrhea + 1day [AND] and 2kg of birth weight
  - Hospitalized in the medical-surgical centre of Pediatrics of the hospital of Strasbourg [AND] for whom a blood sample was prescribed as part of their routine care

Exclusion Criteria:

Adult Healthy Volunteers:
  - Systemic inflammatory disease [OR] transient inflammatory state
  - Any drug that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h preceding the sampling [OR] use of illicit drugs
Neonates:
  - Risk of anemia < 7g/DL [AND] current anticoagulant treatment [OR] unable to refrain from taking medications during the study period
