  - Handball players [AND] who are healthy [AND] who have participated in at least one official match in the last year [AND] who attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Those with musculoskeletal injury in the last three months [OR] cardiovascular diseases [OR] use of anabolic, anti-inflammatory and analgesic drugs 72 hours before the evaluations [OR] use of alcohol and illicit drugs during the collection period.
