Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [AND] parents of the patients who accept to participate [AND] sign the informed consent
  - Patients whose molar teeth are diagnosed as molar incisor hypomineralization by EAPD [OR] other qualified professionals
  - Teeth that are fully erupted [AND] have not been previously restored or have caries
  - Patients who have at least two permanent first molars that need fissure sealant [AND] have not had orthodontic treatment
  - Teeth that have white-yellow-brown discoloration that indicate high-caries risk [AND] have not been previously restored or have caries

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Teeth that are previously restored [OR] have caries [OR] dental flourosis [OR] enamel malformation according to the specific syndromes
  - Patients who have orthodontic treatment [OR] are not cooperative for the dental procedure
  - Patients who have any other conditions that would interfere with the study or the dental procedure
