Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients admitted in the non-critical areas of the Instituto Nacional de Ciencias Médicas y Nutrición Salvador Zubirán (INCMNSZ) [AND] with a nutritional risk between January 2019 [OR] and July 2020 will be considered eligible.
  - Patients [AND] with recent diagnosis of IF type II (an evolution >28 days) [AND] originate from various gastrointestinal [OR] or systemic diseases (short bowel, intestinal fistula, intestinal dysmotility, mechanical obstruction, [AND] and extensive small bowel mucosal disease).

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [AND] with contraindications for PN [NOT] free to receive PN.
  - Patients [AND] with known allergies to the components of the PN formula [NOT] able to tolerate PN.
  - Patients [AND] with severe liver [OR] or renal insufficiency [NOT] able to receive PN.
  - Patients [AND] with uncontrolled diabetes mellitus [NOT] able to receive PN.
  - Patients [AND] with certain acute [OR] and life-threatening conditions [NOT] able to receive PN.
  - Patients [AND] with immunological diseases (such as autoimmune diseases, human immunodeficiency virus infection, cancer, etc.) [NOT] able to receive PN.
  - Patients [AND] taking immunosuppressant medications [NOT] able to receive PN.
  - Patients [AND] with severe hemorrhagic disorders [NOT] able to receive PN.
  - Patients [AND] who are pregnant [OR] or lactating [NOT] able to receive PN.
