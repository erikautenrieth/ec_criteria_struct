Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients with recent stroke/TIA [AND] and severe stenosis of intracranial ICA [OR] MCA [AND] impaired CVR within previous three months [AND] but not before 3 weeks after acute stroke. This is to differentiate between patients with a long-standing fixed-stenosis [OR] from patients with partially recanalized intracranial artery (masquerading as severe stenosis).
  2. Age >21 years [OR] 21 years [OR] and older

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients with atrial fibrillation [OR] arrhythmias.
  2. Within 2 weeks of cardiac catheterization [OR] arterial puncture at femoral puncture site [OR].
  3. Decompensated heart failure [AND] usually class 3 [OR] 4.
  4. LV EF <30% [OR] 30% or less.
  5. Moderate [OR] severe AR.
  6. Persistent [AND] uncontrolled hypertension (BP persistently >160/100 mmHg) [OR] 160/100 mmHg or higher.
  7. Bleeding diathesis [OR].
  8. Active thrombophlebitis [OR] venous disease of lower limbs [OR].
  9. Severe lower extremity vaso-occlusive disease [OR].
  10. Presence of a documented aortic aneurysm [OR] dissection requiring surgical repair [OR].
  11. Pregnancy [OR] not pregnant.
