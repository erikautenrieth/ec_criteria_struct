<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Type 2 diabetes mellitus [AND] on metformin therapy for at least 3 months

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Those reporting any chronic or acute diseases [OR] food allergies, smoking [OR] or alcohol abuse
  - Use of illicit drugs [OR] regular consumption of medications, micronutrient supplements, or both
  - Vegetarians [OR] those unable to eat meat, are also excluded since the foods for the study contain meat
  - Unable to refrain from taking medications during the study period
