Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients will be recruited from the Division of Gastroenterology and Hepatology [AND] the Mayo Clinic in Rochester, MN.
 - Adult patients with immune mediated enteropathies [AND] who have had improvement in symptoms [OR] histology on oral budesonide therapy.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Age [NOT] <18 years
 - Positive stool gluten testing in patients with refractory celiac disease [OR] small bowel malignancy [OR] history of small bowel malignancy
 - Refractory celiac disease type 2 [OR] post-transplant lymphoproliferative disorder associated enteropathy
 - No prior improvement in symptoms [AND] histology with budesonide therapy [OR] discontinuation of budesonide therapy prior to the trial
 - Other concurrent systemic corticosteroids [OR] other immune mediating medications, for example [AND] but not limited to azathioprine, 6-mercaptopurine, cyclosporine, methotrexate, anti-TNF monoclonal antibodies, alpha-4 beta-7 integrin inhibiting monoclonal antibody, interleukin 12/23 inhibiting monoclonal antibody, JAK inhibitors.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
