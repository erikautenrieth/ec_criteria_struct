Inclusion Criteria:
  - Completion of SLE Study (NCT03252587) through the protocol-required treatment period [AND] currently receiving blinded study drug
  - Women of childbearing potential (WOCBP) [AND] must have a negative urine pregnancy test [AND] must agree to use correctly a highly effective method(s) of contraception for the duration of treatment with study drug [OR] other acceptable forms of contraception

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] any disease or medical condition that, in the opinion of the investigator, would make the subject unsuitable for this study [OR] would interfere with the interpretation of subject safety or study results [OR] considered unsuitable by the investigator for any other reason
  - Evidence of active tuberculosis (TB) [OR] any other active infection that would interfere with the study

Other protocol defined inclusion/exclusion criteria could apply
