Inclusion Criteria:
  -  ASA I-III male and female patients [AND] aged between 18 to 80 years old, who will receive spinal anesthesia for surgeries lasting less than one hour.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients [NOT] younger than 18 years old.
  -  ASA physical status [OR] >3.
  -  Immunosuppression [OR] or high risk of infection.
  -  Contraindications to receiving spinal anesthesia [AND] (e.g. coagulation impairment).
  -  Patients [OR] with psychosis.
  -  Patients [OR] with preexisting back pain.
  -  Patients [OR] with cognitive impairment.
  -  Allergies [OR] to local anesthetics, analgesics [OR] or any medication used in the study.
  -  Patients [OR] with chronic regular opioid usage.
  -  Presence [OR] of preexisting neurological symptoms.
