Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Prescription of a Narval CC mandibular advancement device [AND] 
  -  Age ≥ 18 years [OR] 
  -  Ability to understand the study information [AND] and information on usage of personal data [AND] 
  -  Signed and dated informed consent [OR] 

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients [AND] who have central sleep apnea (central AI > 5/hour) [OR] 
  -  Patients [AND] who have loose teeth [AND] and severe parodontitis [OR] 
  -  Patients [AND] who have completely missing teeth [OR] or removable tooth replacement [OR] or have a teeth health insufficient to retain an MAD [OR] 
  -  Patients [AND] who have missing molars [OR] 
  -  Patients [AND] who have maximum protrusion of less than 5mm [OR]
