<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Infants of healthy mothers [AND] with uncomplicated pregnancies at term, [AND] mothers of any ethnic or social background [AND] that can speak, read, [AND] understand Swedish to the extent that they can consent in Swedish. [OR] An additional inclusion criterion for mothers in the CS groups is vaginal pH ≤ 4 [AND] at the time of birth.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Infants of mothers with [NOT] pre-eclampsia [OR] complicated pregnancies, mothers [AND] <18 years [OR] >40 years of age, genital herpes simplex, genital warts, HIV, Hepatitis B [OR] complications during delivery, infants delivered prior to 37 weeks of gestation. [OR] Additional exclusion criteria for mothers in the CS group: Mothers positive to Group B Streptococcus, Group A Streptococcus, bacterial vaginosis, vaginal pH > 4 [AND] at the time of birth. [OR] Mothers with fecal samples positive for Salmonella, Shigella, Campylobacter [OR] Yersinia.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
