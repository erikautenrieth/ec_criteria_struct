Inclusion Criteria:
 - Chronic (more than three months) ESBL colonization of the same strain [OR] as determined by species, phenotype [AND] and antibiogram.
 - Patients must be able to swallow sachets [AND] must be able to speak [AND] understand Swedish.
 - Must have a permanent residence in Sweden [AND] must be able to sign informed consent in Swedish.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Immunosuppression [NOT] (i.e. chemotheraphy, treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors) [OR] immunodeficiency
 - In patient care [OR] psychiatric disorder [OR] alcohol [OR] substance abuse [OR] dementia [OR] invasive catheters
