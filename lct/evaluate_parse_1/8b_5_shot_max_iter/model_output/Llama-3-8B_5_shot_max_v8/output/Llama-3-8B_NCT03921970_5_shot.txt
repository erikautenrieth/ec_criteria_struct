Inclusion Criteria:
 - American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) classification [AND] I-II
 - Scheduled for laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy under general anesthesia

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Bleeding diathesis [OR] Receiving anticoagulant treatment
 - Known local anesthetics [OR] opioid allergy
 - Infection of the skin at the site of the needle puncture [OR] Infection of any kind
 - Pregnancy [OR] Lactation
 - Patients who [NOT] do not accept the procedure
