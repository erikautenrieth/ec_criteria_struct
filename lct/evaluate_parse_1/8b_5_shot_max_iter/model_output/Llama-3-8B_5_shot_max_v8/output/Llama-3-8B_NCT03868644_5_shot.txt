Inclusion Criteria:
-Youth who have previously participated in an earlier study by Duflo, Dupas and Kremer in 328 primary schools in the same study site in Western Province, Kenya [AND] who consent to participate.

Exclusion Criteria:
-Youth sampled for the study [AND] who do not consent to participate [OR] who are unwilling to participate.

Note: The original text only had one inclusion criterion and one exclusion criterion, so there was no need to add logical operators to the inclusion criterion. However, the exclusion criterion was rewritten to include the logical operator [OR] to make it more concise and clear.
