<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. age [AND] > 18 years;
  2. confirmed diagnosis of prediabetes [OR] or T2D (documented A1c of ≥ 5.7% [OR] 2-hour post-load glucose of >140 [OR] FBG of >100);
  3. a family member [AND] or peer willing to participate [AND] attend all sessions as a social influencer;
  4. proficiency in English [AND] of at least one member of each dyad, [OR] at least one member of each dyad has proficiency in English;
  5. willingness to provide written consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. type 1 diabetes [NOT] or diabetes secondary to other conditions (e.g. steroid-induced, pancreatic insufficiency, or chemotherapy-induced);
  2. malignancy [OR] life-threatening illness with life expectancy of <5 years;
  3. end-stage disease [OR] serious illness that prohibits participation (e.g. end-stage renal disease [OR] class IV congestive heart failure);
  4. inability [NOT] to perform unsupervised physical activity;
  5. pregnancy [OR] diagnosed cognitive deficits [OR] limited decision-making capacity;
  6. alcohol [OR] substance abuse;
  7. homelessness [OR] no fixed address.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
