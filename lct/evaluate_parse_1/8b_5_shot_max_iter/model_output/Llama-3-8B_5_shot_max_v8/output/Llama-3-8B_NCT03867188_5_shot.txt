Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with a soft tissue sarcoma of the thigh [AND] must have sufficient health to withstand the physical demands of surgery [AND] ≥ 18 years old [AND] ECOG performance status of ≤ 2 [AND] ability to understand [AND] and the willingness to sign an IRB-approved informed consent document (either directly [OR] or via a legally authorized representative)

Exclusion Criteria:
 - History of clinically significant medical conditions including: [OR] Cardiovascular: Atrial Fibrillation [OR] Ventricular Fibrillation [OR] Significant Coronary Artery Disease [OR] Hepatic: Viral [OR] or Autoimmune Hepatitis [OR] Cirrhosis of the Liver [OR] Liver Metabolism Disorders [OR] Renal: Any form of renal impairment [NOT] cleared by the kidneys, thus any form of renal impairment could lead to an adverse reaction
 - Medical condition(s) or concurrent surgery that may have required analgesic treatment in the postoperative period for pain that was [NOT] strictly related to the study surgery
 - Any clinically significant event or condition discovered during surgery that may have complicated the patient's post surgical course such as vascular [OR] nerve involvement that was unknown before surgery
 - History of allergic reactions attributed to compounds of similar chemical [OR] biologic composition to liposomal bupivacaine
 - Pregnant women are [NOT] excluded from this study because EXPAREL has the potential for teratogenic [OR] abortifacient effects [AND] breastfeeding should be discontinued if the mother is treated with EXPAREL
