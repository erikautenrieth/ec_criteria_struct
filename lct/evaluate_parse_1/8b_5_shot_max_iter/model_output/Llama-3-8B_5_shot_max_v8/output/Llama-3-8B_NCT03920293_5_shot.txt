Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Diagnosed with Myasthenia Gravis at least 6 months [OR] (180 days) prior to the date of the Screening Visit as confirmed by specific criteria.
  2. Myasthenia Gravis Foundation of America Clinical Classification Class II [AND] to IV at screening.
  3. MG-ADL profile must be ≥ 6 [AND] at screening [AND] randomization (Day 1).
  4. Vaccinated against meningococcal infections within 3 years [OR] prior to, [OR] or at the time of, initiating study drug to reduce the risk of meningococcal infection (N meningitidis).

Exclusion Criteria:
Medical Conditions
  1. Any active [OR] untreated thymoma. History of thymic carcinoma [OR] thymic malignancy unless deemed cured by adequate treatment with no evidence of recurrence for ≥ 5 years [AND] before screening.
  2. History of thymectomy within the 12 months [OR] prior to screening.
  3. History of N meningitidis infection.
  4. Use of the following within the time period specified below:
       -  IV immunoglobulin within 4 weeks [OR] of randomization
       -  Use of plasma exchange within 4 weeks [OR] of randomization
       -  Use of rituximab within 6 months [OR] of screening
  5. Participants [AND] who have received previous treatment with complement inhibitors (for example, eculizumab).
