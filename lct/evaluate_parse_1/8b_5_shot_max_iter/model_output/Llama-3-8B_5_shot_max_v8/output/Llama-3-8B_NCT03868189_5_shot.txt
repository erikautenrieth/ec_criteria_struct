Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient requiring an electroneuromyogram whose indication [AND] allows the application of a standard protocol of examination [OR] examination.
 - Patient that has given his/her [NOT] free and informed consent.
 - Patient that signed the consent form [AND] with a valid signature.
 - Patient with an [AND] health insurance plan.
 - The patient is at least 18 years old [≥] [AND] under 80 years old [≤].

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patient with symptoms of neuropathy during the screening [OR] with a positive neurological examination.
 - Patient whose Neurological severity score (NSS) score is [≥] 3 [OR] with a high risk of neuropathy.
 - Patient whose neurological examination is abnormal [AND] suggestive of neuropathy.
 - Patient presenting a pathology that could cause neuropathy [OR] with a history of neuropathy.
 - Patient already participating in a category 1 research study (Jardé law) [OR] with a conflicting study.
 - Patient in an exclusion period determined by another study [OR] with a prior study exclusion.
 - Patient under the protection of justice, under guardianship [OR] under curatorship.
 - Patient refusing to sign the consent form [OR] with an invalid signature.
 - Patient for which it is [NOT] possible to provide information about the study.
 - Pregnant patient, parturient, or breastfeeding [OR] with a reproductive history.
