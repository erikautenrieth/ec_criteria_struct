 - Screened to be generally healthy [AND] as determined by standard medical assessment on physical [AND] and mental health
 - Tanner stage 2 [AND] based on breast development for girls, [AND] and pubic hair in boys. Girls will have to be premenarcheal

Exclusion Criteria:
