Inclusion Criteria:
 - All adults [AND] ≥ 18 yrs of age undergoing flexible bronchoscopy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Uncontrolled hypertension [AND] Systolic Blood Pressure >180 mm Hg [OR] Symptomatic coronary artery disease [OR] Pregnancy [OR] Not giving informed consent for the procedure
 - Hypoxemia (oxygen saturation [by pulse oximetry] < 92% [OR] with Fio2 of ≥ 0.3
 - Bronchoscopy performed through an artificial airway
