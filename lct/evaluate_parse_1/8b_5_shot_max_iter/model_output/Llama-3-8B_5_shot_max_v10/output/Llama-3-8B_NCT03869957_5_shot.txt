Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients admitted in the non-critical areas of the Instituto Nacional de Ciencias Médicas y Nutrición Salvador Zubirán (INCMNSZ) [AND] with a nutritional risk between January 2019 [AND] and July 2020 [AND] will be considered eligible.
 - Patients with recent diagnosis of IF type II (an evolution >28 days) [AND] originate from various gastrointestinal [OR] systemic diseases (short bowel, intestinal fistula, intestinal dysmotility, mechanical obstruction, [AND] and extensive small bowel mucosal disease).

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with [NOT] contraindications for PN
 - Patients with known [NOT] allergies to the components of the PN formula
 - Severe [NOT] liver or renal insufficiency
 - Uncontrolled [NOT] diabetes mellitus
 - Certain [NOT] acute and life-threatening conditions
 - Immunological diseases (such as autoimmune diseases, human immunodeficiency virus infection, cancer, etc.) [AND] or those that take immunosuppressant medications
 - Severe [NOT] hemorrhagic disorders
 - Pregnant [OR] lactating
