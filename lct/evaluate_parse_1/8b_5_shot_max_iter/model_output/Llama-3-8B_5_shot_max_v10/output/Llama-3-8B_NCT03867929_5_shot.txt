Inclusion Criteria:
 - Presence of erectile dysfunction
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Presence of uncontrolled diabetes mellitus [OR]
 - Presence of uncontrolled hypertension [OR]
 - Presence of uncontrolled lipid metabolism disorders [OR]
 - Presence of neurological diseases [AND] with [OR] heamatological diseases
 - Presence of urinary tract infection [OR] diagnosis of any malignancies [OR] chronic kidney failure
 - Presence of metabolic syndrome [OR] psychiatric diseases [AND] or medical treatment [OR] smoking
 - History of pelvic surgery [OR] cardiac surgery [OR] pelvic radiotherapy
