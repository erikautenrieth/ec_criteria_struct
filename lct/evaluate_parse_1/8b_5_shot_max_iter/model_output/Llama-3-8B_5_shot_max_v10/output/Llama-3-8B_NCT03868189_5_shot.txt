Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient requiring an electroneuromyogram whose indication allows the application of a standard protocol of examination [AND] with patient that has given his/her [OR] free and informed consent [AND] patient that signed the consent form [AND] patient with an health insurance plan [AND] patient that is at least 18 years old (≥) [AND] patient that is under 80 years old (≤)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patient with symptoms of neuropathy during the screening [OR] patient whose Neurological severity score (NSS) score is ≥ 3 [OR] patient whose neurological examination is abnormal and suggestive of neuropathy [OR] patient presenting a pathology that could cause neuropathy (e.g. diabetes, renal failure, etc.) [OR] patient already participating in a category 1 research study (Jardé law) [OR] patient in an exclusion period determined by another study [OR] patient under the protection of justice, under guardianship [OR] or under curatorship [OR] patient refusing to sign the consent form [OR] patient for which it is [NOT] impossible to provide information about the study [OR] patient who is pregnant, parturient, or breastfeeding
