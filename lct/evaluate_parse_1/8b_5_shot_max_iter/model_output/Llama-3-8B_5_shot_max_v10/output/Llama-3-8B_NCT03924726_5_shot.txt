Inclusion Criteria:
 - Aged 18 years and above at the start of the treatment [AND] with a molar relationship either Class I [OR] < ½ unit Class II [OR] or Class III [AND] with extraction of all four first premolar teeth as part of orthodontic treatment [AND] with maximum anchorage required using mini-implant [AND] with no systemic disease [AND] with good oral hygiene [AND] with no periodontal disease.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Significant vertical skeletal discrepancies [OR] systemic diseases requiring long-term antibiotic use [OR] phenytoin [OR] cyclosporin [OR] anti-inflammatory drugs [OR] bisphosphonates [OR] systemic corticosteroids [OR] calcium channel blockers [OR] poor oral hygiene [OR] active periodontal disease.
