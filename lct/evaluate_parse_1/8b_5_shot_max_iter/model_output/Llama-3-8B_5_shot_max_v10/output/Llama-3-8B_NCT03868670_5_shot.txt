Inclusion Criteria:
 - BMI [AND] 45-60 kg/m2
 - Failure of at least one weight loss medication [OR] previous Gastric Bypass Surgery lead to less than 50% of excess weight lost by 2 years after surgery
 - Significant Loss of Control over eating
 - Subject is [AND] able to attend all scheduled clinic appointments on their own [OR] with a caregiver.
 - Premenopausal women must agree to use [AND] acceptable methods of birth control.
 - Participants provide [AND] voluntary, decisionally capable, and appropriately informed consent.
 - Subject is [AND] able to comply w/ all testing and follow-up requirements defined by the study protocol.
 - Participant has [NOT] no immediate plan for relocation beyond 6 hours of the study site.
 - Proficiency with the English language.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Subject has [NOT] an implanted medical device that delivers electrical energy to the brain.
 - Subject has [NOT] an implantable cardiac pacemaker, defibrillator, [OR] neurostimulator.
 - Subject is [NOT] likely to require repeat MR imaging after implant of the RNS Neurostimulator and Leads.
 - Subject is [NOT] unable to fit into CT scanner (500lb upper weight limit for CT scanner).
 - Inability to provide [NOT] informed consent to treatment.
