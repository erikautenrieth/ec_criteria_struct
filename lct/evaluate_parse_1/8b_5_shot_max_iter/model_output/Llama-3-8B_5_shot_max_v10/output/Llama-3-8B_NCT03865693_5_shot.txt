Inclusion Criteria:
 - Burn patients [AND] severe neuropatic pain rated at least 5 [OR] on the 10-point numerical rating scale (NRS)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Cardiac arrest history
 - History of neurologi [NOT] disease or brain surgery
 - Unstable heart disease [OR] or presence of a cardiac pacemaker
 - Pain resulting from other causes like as neuromuscular diseases
 - Psychiatric disorder
 - Diabetes mellitus
 - Abnormal renal function
 - Contraindication for MRI [OR] or pregnancy
