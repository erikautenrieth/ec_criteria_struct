<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
 - vaginal delivery [AND] an infant age of 6 months ([OR] ±3 weeks) [AND] mother ≥15 years of age [AND] infant still breastfeeding [AND] anticipated residence in the area for the study duration.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] ability to provide informed consent
 - hemoglobin < 70 g/L [OR] Z scores for weight-for-age (WAZ) or weight-for-height (WHZ) <3, [OR] any maternal or infant chronic illness [OR] administration of any infant vitamin or mineral supplements for the past 2 months [OR] any history of infant antibiotic treatment.
