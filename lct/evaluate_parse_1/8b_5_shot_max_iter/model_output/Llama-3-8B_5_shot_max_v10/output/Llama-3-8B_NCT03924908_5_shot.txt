Inclusion Criteria:
 - Burn patient [AND] treated as outpatient
 - Burn surface area [OR] >= 5% of the total body surface area
 - mainly 2nd degree burn
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Psychiatric [NOT] antecedents
 - Claustrophobia
 - Heavy [NOT] hearing
 - Visual [NOT] impairment
 - Face burn
 - Conjunctivitis
 - Consent [NOT] obtained
