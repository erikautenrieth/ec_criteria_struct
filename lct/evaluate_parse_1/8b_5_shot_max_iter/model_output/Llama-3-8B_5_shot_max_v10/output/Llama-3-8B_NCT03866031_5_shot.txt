Inclusion Criteria:
 - The patient should be completely edentulous for at least 6 months [AND] 1. first class jaw relation [OR] 2. adequate bone for an implant length of at least 10 mm [AND] diameter of at least 2.75 mm [AND] 3. the patients should be able to speak [AND] write in Arabic.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who received radiotherapy to the head [OR] neck region for tumors [AND] 1. a history of chemotherapy [OR] 2. patients on long-term steroids [OR] bisphosphonates [OR] 3. heavy smokers [OR] 4. patients with physical [AND] mental disabilities that interfere with the maintenance of implants [OR] 5. uncontrolled systematic disease that could compromise implant surgery [OR] 6. extreme bruxism [OR] 7. infectious disease [OR] 8. temporomandibular joint dysfunction
