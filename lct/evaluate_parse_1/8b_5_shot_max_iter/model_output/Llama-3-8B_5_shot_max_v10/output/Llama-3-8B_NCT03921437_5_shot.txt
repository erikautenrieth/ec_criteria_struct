Inclusion conditions:
  1. The fifth stage of chronic renal failure diagnosed by a physician [AND] with [OR] without a physician's advice on renal replacement therapy.
  2. The patient has not yet decided on treatment [AND] has been informed by a physician of the need for renal replacement therapy.
  3. Ability to communicate in Mandarin [AND] Taiwanese.
  4. Age over 20 years old.

Exclusion conditions:
  1. Cognitive impairment: [NOT] MMSE score of 21 points or less, [OR] MMSE score of 16 points or less.
  2. Patients who have received non-emergency dialysis treatment [OR] have not undergone dialysis.
  3. Patients with severe cardiopulmonary dysfunction [OR] severe shock, low blood pressure, and uncontrolled bleeding.
  4. Patients with chronic active hepatitis, cirrhosis, or liver failure.
  5. A history of cancer that may be transferred [OR] has not been transferred.
  6. Severe bleeding tendency [OR] has no bleeding tendency.
  7. Loss of peritoneal function [OR] extensive peritoneal adhesion, unable to use peritoneal dialysis.
  8. Unable to correct physiological defects, may interfere with peritoneal dialysis, or increase the chance of infection, such as umbilical hernia [OR] has no umbilical hernia.
  9. The patient is unable to operate the fluid change, hand shake, and has no family members to assist [OR] has family members to assist.
  10. Patients who are blind, have poor eyesight, and have difficulty operating [OR] have good eyesight and can operate.
