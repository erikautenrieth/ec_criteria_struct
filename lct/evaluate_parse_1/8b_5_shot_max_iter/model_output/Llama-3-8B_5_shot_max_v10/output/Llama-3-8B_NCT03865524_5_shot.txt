Inclusion Criteria:
 - Men [AND] and women over 18 years old.
 - Written informed consent [AND] according to ICH / GCP [AND] Spanish legislation, obtained before any study procedure.
 - Patient with a BMI [AND] equal to or greater than 30 Kg / m2 [OR] patients with clinical [AND] radiological criteria of knee osteoarthritis.
 - Patients with indication for primary TKA [AND] according to medical criteria.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnancy [OR] or lactation.
 - Inability [NOT] to give informed consent in the absence of a legal representative.
 - Subjects [AND] that are participating in a study with medicines [OR] or other medical devices.
 - Those [AND] who show inability [NOT] to follow the instructions [OR] collaborate during the development of the study.
 - If in the opinion of the researcher there are findings [AND] in the physical examination, abnormalities [OR] in the results of the clinical analyzes [OR] or other medical, social [OR] or psychosocial factors that could have a negative influence.
 - Having had a previous surgical procedure [NOT] on or around the knee in the last 12 months (such as proximal tibial osteotomy, femoral osteotomy, open reduction and internal fixation for a fracture, patellectomy [OR] or patellar realignment).
 - Need a simultaneous bilateral TKA.
 - Have a varus angle [OR] or valgus ≥ 15 °.
 - Total revision knee arthroplasty.
