Inclusion Criteria:
 - 18 [AND] ~ 60 years, female [OR] male
 - Patients [AND] scheduled for an elective surgical procedure (mechanical ventilation via endotracheal tube)
 - BMI: 18 [OR] ~ 30 kg/m2
 - Patients [AND] understand clearly [AND] participate in the study voluntarily, [AND] sign the informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] scheduled for emergency surgery
 - Patients [AND] with a history of drug abuse [OR] and / or alcohol abuse 2 years prior to the screening period
 - One [OR] more of the laboratory findings fall out of the limitations for this study (platelet, hemoglobin, aspartate aminotransferase, etc.)
 - Pregnant women [OR] those in lactation period
 - Allergic to drugs used in the study
 - Patients [AND] with respiratory management difficulties (Modified Mallampati grade IV)
 - Patients [AND] have participated in other clinical trial within the 3 months prior to randomization
 - Any patient [AND] judged by the Principal Investigator (PI) [OR] Sub-Investigator to be inappropriate for the subject for any other reason
