Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with recent stroke/TIA [AND] and severe stenosis of intracranial ICA [OR] MCA [AND] impaired CVR within previous three months [AND] but not before 3 weeks after acute stroke. This is to differentiate between patients with a long-standing fixed-stenosis [AND] from patients with partially recanalized intracranial artery (masquerading as severe stenosis) [OR] 
 - Age >21 years [AND] 

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] who have atrial fibrillation/arrhythmias [OR] 
 - Within 2 weeks of cardiac catheterization [OR] or arterial puncture at femoral puncture site [OR] 
 - Decompensated heart failure [AND] usually class 3 [OR] 4 [OR] 
 - LV EF <30% [OR] 
 - Moderate [OR] or severe AR [OR] 
 - Persistent [AND] and uncontrolled hypertension (BP persistently >160/100 mmHg) [OR] 
 - Bleeding diathesis [OR] 
 - Active thrombophlebitis/venous disease of lower limbs [OR] 
 - Severe lower extremity vaso-occlusive disease [OR] 
 - Presence of a documented aortic aneurysm/dissection requiring surgical repair [OR] 
 - Pregnancy [OR]
