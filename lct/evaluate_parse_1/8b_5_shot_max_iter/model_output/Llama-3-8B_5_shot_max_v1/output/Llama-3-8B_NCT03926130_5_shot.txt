Inclusion Criteria:
  - Diagnosis of CD for at least 3 months prior to baseline [AND] confirmed diagnosis of moderate to severe CD as assessed by SF [OR] AP score [OR] SES-CD [AND] demonstrated intolerance, loss of response [OR] inadequate response to conventional [OR] biologic therapy for CD [AND] if female, subject must meet the contraception recommendations

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Have a current diagnosis of ulcerative colitis [OR] inflammatory bowel disease-unclassified (IBD-U) [OR] formerly known as indeterminate colitis [OR] abdominal [OR] perianal abscess [OR] short bowel syndrome [NOT] have a stoma or ostomy [AND] have had a bowel resection within 6 months [OR] any kind of intra-abdominal [OR] extra abdominal surgery within 3 months of baseline [AND] have ever received any monoclonal antibodies binding IL-23
