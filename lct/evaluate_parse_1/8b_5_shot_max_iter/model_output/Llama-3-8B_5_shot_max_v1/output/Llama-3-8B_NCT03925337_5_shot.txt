Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] age: ≥ 22 years
 - Patients [AND] presenting for routine colonoscopy for screening [OR] and/or surveillance purposes.
 - Willingness [AND] to undergo two withdrawals with [OR] without the use of computer-aided software while undergoing conventional colonoscopy with sedation
 - Ability [AND] to provide written, informed consent [AND] and understand the responsibilities of trial participation

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Minors [NOT] aged < 22 years.
 - People [NOT] with diminished cognitive capacity
 - Patients [NOT] undergoing diagnostic colonoscopy (e.g. as an evaluation for active gastrointestinal bleed, referring collectively to the stomach and the small and large intestine).
 - Patients [NOT] with incomplete colonoscopies (those where endoscopists did not successfully intubate the cecum due to technical difficulties [OR] or poor bowel preparation)
 - Patients [NOT] that have standard contraindications to colonoscopy in general (e.g. documented acute diverticulitis, fulminant colitis [OR] and known or suspected perforation).
 - Patients [NOT] with inflammatory bowel disease
 - Patients [NOT] with any polypoid/ulcerated lesion > 2 cm concerning for invasive cancer on endoscopy
 - Patients [NOT] referred for endoscopic mucosal resection (EMR), which is a procedure to remove early-stage cancer and precancerous growths from the lining of the digestive tract.
