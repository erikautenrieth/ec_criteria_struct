<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Diagnosed with Myasthenia Gravis [AND] at least 6 months (180 days) prior to the date of the Screening Visit [AND] as confirmed by specific criteria.
 2. Myasthenia Gravis Foundation of America Clinical Classification Class II [OR] Class III [OR] Class IV at screening.
 3. MG-ADL profile must be ≥ 6 [AND] at screening [AND] and randomization (Day 1).
 4. Vaccinated against meningococcal infections [OR] within 3 years prior to, [OR] at the time of, initiating study drug to reduce the risk of meningococcal infection (N meningitidis).

Exclusion Criteria:
Medical Conditions
 1. Any active [OR] untreated thymoma. History of thymic carcinoma [OR] thymic malignancy unless deemed cured by adequate treatment with no evidence of recurrence for ≥ 5 years before screening [AND] NOT deemed cured.
 2. History of thymectomy within the 12 months prior to screening [AND] NOT deemed cured.
 3. History of N meningitidis infection [AND] NOT treated.
 4. Use of the following within the time period specified below:
    - IV immunoglobulin within 4 weeks of randomization [AND] NOT used for treatment of MG.
    - Use of plasma exchange within 4 weeks of randomization [AND] NOT used for treatment of MG.
    - Use of rituximab within 6 months of screening [AND] NOT used for treatment of MG.
 5. Participants who have received previous treatment with complement inhibitors (for example, eculizumab) [AND] NOT used for treatment of MG.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
