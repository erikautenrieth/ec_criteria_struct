Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] and parents of the patients who accept to participate [AND] sign the informed consent
 - Patients whose molar teeth are diagnosed as molar incisor hypomineralization by EAPD [OR] teeth that are fully erupted
 - Teeth that have white-yellow-brown discoloration that indicate high-caries risk [AND] have at least two permanent first molars that need fissure sealant

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Teeth that are previously restored [OR] have caries [OR] dental flourosis [OR] enamel malformation according to the specific syndromes
 - Patients who have orthodontic treatment [OR] are not cooperative for the dental procedure
