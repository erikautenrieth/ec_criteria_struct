Inclusion Criteria:
Type II diabetic patients, 18 years and older, were included if the participants had:
[AND]
  1. severe non-PDR in either eyes [OR] with/without diabetic macular edema;
  2. ETDRS letters-measured best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) ranging from 20/40 to 20/320 Snellen equivalent;
  3. no previous treatment [NOT] of any type in either eye.

Exclusion Criteria:
[AND]
  1. history of prior laser treatment [OR] vitrectomy in the study eye;
  2. history of thromboembolic event [OR] including myocardial infarction [OR] cerebral vascular accident;
  3. major surgery [NOT] within the prior 6 months [OR] planned within the next 28 days;
  4. history of glaucoma [OR] ocular hypertension;
  5. loss of vision [NOT] as a result of other causes;
  6. history of systemic corticosteroid therapy [NOT] within the last 3 months;
  7. severe systemic disease [NOT] other than diabetes mellitus;
  8. known coagulation abnormalities [OR] current use of anticoagulative medication [NOT] other than aspirin;
  9. any condition [NOT] that could affect followup [OR] documentation [including preretinal [OR] vitreous hemorrhage].
