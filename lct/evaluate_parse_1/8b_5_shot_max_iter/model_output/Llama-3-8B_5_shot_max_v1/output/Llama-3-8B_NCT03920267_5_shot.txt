Inclusion Criteria:
 - Completion of SLE Study (NCT03252587) [AND] through the protocol-required treatment period, [AND] currently receiving blinded study drug
 - Women of childbearing potential (WOCBP) [AND] must have a negative urine pregnancy test [OR] and must agree to use correctly a highly effective method(s) of contraception for the duration of treatment with study drug

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Any disease [OR] medical condition that, in the opinion of the investigator, would make the subject unsuitable for this study, would interfere with the interpretation of subject safety [OR] study results, [OR] considered unsuitable by the investigator for any other reason
 - Evidence of active tuberculosis (TB) [NOT] excluded

Other protocol defined inclusion/exclusion criteria could apply
