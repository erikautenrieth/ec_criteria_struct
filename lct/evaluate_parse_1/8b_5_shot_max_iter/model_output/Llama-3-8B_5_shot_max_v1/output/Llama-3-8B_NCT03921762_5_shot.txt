Inclusion Criteria:
 - > 50 years old
 - Bilateral age-related cataract
 - [AND] qualify for bilateral implantation within 1 month
 - [AND] no previous refractive or ocular surgery
 - [AND] available IOL dioptre range: 18 to 27D
 - [AND] expected postoperative astigmatism ≤ 0.75D

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pupil > 4 mm
 - Occupation [OR] requiring night-time driving [OR] any occupation incompatible with multifocality
 - Acute [OR] chronic disease [OR] illness that would increase risk [OR] confound study results [e.g. diabetes mellitus (with retinopathy) [OR] immunocompromised [OR] glaucoma [OR] epiretinal membrane [OR] cornea guttata [OR] etc…] found to be relevant by the investigators
 - Amblyopia [OR] strabismus
 - Extremely shallow anterior chamber
 - Capsule [OR] zonular abnormalities that may affect postoperative centration [OR] tilt of the lens [e.g. pseudoexfoliation syndrome [OR] uveitis [OR] Marfan's syndrome]
 - Pupil abnormalities [OR] non-reactive [OR] tonic pupils [OR] abnormally shaped pupils [OR] pupils that do not dilate under mesopic/ scotopic conditions
 - Instability [NOT] to place the intraocular lens safely at the location planned
 - Pregnancy [OR] (pregnancy test will be taken pre-operatively in women of reproductive age)
