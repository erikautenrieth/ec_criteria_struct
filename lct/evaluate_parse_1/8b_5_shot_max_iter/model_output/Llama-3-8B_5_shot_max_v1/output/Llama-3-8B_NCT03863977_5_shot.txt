<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients' age from 18 to 60 years old [AND] physical status ASA I-II (by the American Society of Anesthesiologists) [AND] elective unilateral open inguinal hernia repair [AND] under spinal anesthesia

Exclusion Criteria:
  - age [NOT] < 18 [OR] > 60
  - American society of anesthesiologist (ASA) class III-IV
  - known allergy to amino amide local anesthetics
  - skin infection [OR] diseases at site of injection
  - coagulation disorders
  - patients with body mass index (BMI) > 35 kg/m2
  - scrotal hernias
