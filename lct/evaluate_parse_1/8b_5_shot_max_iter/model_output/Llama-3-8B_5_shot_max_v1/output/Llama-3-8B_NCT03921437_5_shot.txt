Inclusion Criteria:

1. The fifth stage of chronic renal failure diagnosed by a physician [AND] with the physician advising and informing the patient who has to undergo renal replacement therapy but has not yet decided what treatment to take.
2. Ability to communicate in Mandarin [OR] Taiwan.
3. Age over 20 years old.

Exclusion Criteria:

1. Cognitive impairment: MMSE score of < 21 points [OR] national minimum < 16 points, and those who are not educated are < 16 points, and the case is excluded [NOT].
2. Patients who have received non-emergency dialysis treatment [OR] have a history of cancer that may be transferred.
3. Patients with severe cardiopulmonary dysfunction [OR] severe shock, low blood pressure, and large bleeding that are not controlled.
4. Patients with chronic active hepatitis [OR] cirrhosis or liver failure.
5. Severe bleeding tendency [OR] loss of peritoneal function or extensive peritoneal adhesion, unable to use peritoneal dialysis.
6. Unable to correct physiological defects, may interfere with peritoneal dialysis, or increase the chance of infection, such as umbilical hernia [OR] unable to operate the fluid change, hand shake, and no family members can assist.
7. Patients who are blind [OR] have poor eyesight and have difficulty operating.
