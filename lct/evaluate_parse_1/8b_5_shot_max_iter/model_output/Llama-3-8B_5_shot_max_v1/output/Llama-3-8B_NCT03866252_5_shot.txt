Inclusion Criteria:
 - Major Depressive Disorder according to Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (DSM-V) [AND] > 25 years [AND] sufficient understanding of the German language

Exclusion Criteria:
 - < 25 years [OR] concomitant diagnosis of past or present psychotic disorder [OR] concomitant diagnosis of past or present bipolar disorder [OR] first degree relative with a psychotic disorder
 - Unable or unwilling to discontinue antidepressant medication [OR] pregnancy or breastfeeding [OR] known hypersensitivity to LSD
 - Somatic disorders including central nervous system (CNS) involvement [OR] known or suspected non-compliance, drug or alcohol abuse
 - Metal implants [OR] weight < 42 kg [OR] suicide risk or very likely to require psychiatric hospitalisation
