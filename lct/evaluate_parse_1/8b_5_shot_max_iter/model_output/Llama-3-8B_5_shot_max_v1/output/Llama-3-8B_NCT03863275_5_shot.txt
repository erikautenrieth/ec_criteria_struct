Inclusion Criteria:
 - Boys [AND] and girls from 7 to 12 years old [AND] with class II skeletal classification attending a consultation in the dental clinic of the UNICIEO foundation, to be fitted with myofunctional apparatus type Sn1
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] who have had previous orthopedic treatment [OR] signs of condyle injuries
 - Patients with Pierre Robin [OR] any form of cleft
 - Patients with syndromes that associate craniofacial anomalies [AND] muscle alterations [NOT] excluded
