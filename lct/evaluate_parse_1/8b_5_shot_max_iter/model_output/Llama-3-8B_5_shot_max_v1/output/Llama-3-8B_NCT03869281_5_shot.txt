Inclusion Criteria (PTA group):
 - T1D patients [AND] who received their first PTA [OR] second PTA only if their first graft was explanted within a week [AND] between 2 January 2005 and 31 December 2017
 - Age ≥18 [AND] and < 65 years
 - Willingness to participate in this observational study

Inclusion Criteria (control group):
 - Type 1 diabetes [AND] age ≥18 years [AND] and < 65 years
 - Willingness to participate in this observational study
 - Minimum disease duration of 5 years [AND] brittle diabetes, as defined by the occurrence of frequent acute complications (ketoacidosis or severe hypoglycaemic episodes requiring third-part assistance or unawareness despite well-conducted intensive insulin therapy)
 - Presence of at least one diabetes-related complication (either neuropathy, retinopathy, subclinical nephropathy)

Exclusion Criteria (both groups):
 - Conditions other than T1D known to affect bone health [AND] (i.e., non-compensated hypothyroidism [OR] hyperthyroidism [OR] hyperparathyroidism [OR] inflammatory bowel disease [OR] malignancy [OR] rheumatoid arthritis [OR] hypogonadism [OR] severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] hepatic insufficiency [OR] alcohol intake ≥ 3 units/day [OR] )
 - Use of drugs that can affect bone metabolism [AND] (e.g., bisphosphonates [OR] anticonvulsants [OR] hormone replacement therapy [OR] ) other than glucocorticoids [AND] immunosuppressants
 - Recipients of simultaneous pancreas kidney transplantation [OR] pancreatic islet transplantation [OR] multiple PTAs [OR] other transplants
 - BMI <18.5 [OR] ≥ 30 kg/m2
