Inclusion Criteria:
  - Age [AND] 18-90
  - Acute ischemic [OR] hemorrhagic stroke (within 30 days)
  - Consent to participate [AND] to anonymous processing of data
  - [AND] signing of informed consent for the participation in the sub-project (for Sub-project Neurophysiological Markers)
  - [AND] signing of informed consent for participation in the subproject (for Epigenetic subproject)

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Stroke occurred [NOT] more than 30 days after the transfer to intensive rehabilitation
  - [AND] history of major psychiatric disorder [OR] recent exacerbation (for Epigenetic subproject, for retrospective analysis)
  - [AND] global aphasia (for Epigenetic subproject, for retrospective analysis)
  - [AND] refusal of the patient to participate in the study
