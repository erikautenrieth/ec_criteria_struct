Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with moderate to severe sleep apnoea [AND] who require CPAP as standard care (AHI >15)
 - Patients between the age of 18 [AND] and 75

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with a diagnosis of atrial fibrillation [OR] atrial flutter [OR] and/or ventricular tachycardia (VT)
 - Patients with an ILR [NOT] already in-situ [OR] an established indication for ILR
 - Patients with a palliative diagnosis [AND] i.e. life expectancy less than 3 years
 - Patients [AND] who lack capacity
