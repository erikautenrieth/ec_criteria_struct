<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
 - Type 2 diabetes mellitus [AND] on metformin therapy for at least 3 months

Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] Type 1 diabetes mellitus
 - [NOT] Uncontrolled hypertension (systolic blood pressure > 180 mmHg or diastolic blood pressure > 110 mmHg)
 - [NOT] Uncontrolled hyperlipidemia (total cholesterol > 300 mg/dL or LDL cholesterol > 190 mg/dL)
 - [NOT] Active liver disease or liver failure
 - [NOT] Renal impairment (estimated glomerular filtration rate < 30 mL/min)
 - [NOT] Active cardiovascular disease (unstable angina, myocardial infarction, or stroke within the past 3 months)
 - [NOT] Severe peripheral artery disease
 - [NOT] Presence of a pacemaker or implantable cardioverter-defibrillator (ICD)
 - [NOT] Pregnancy or breastfeeding
 - [NOT] Use of other medications that may interact with metformin (e.g. cimetidine, ranitidine, trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole, isoniazid, voriconazole, and certain antacids)
 - [NOT] Any other condition that, in the opinion of the investigator, would make the patient unsuitable for the study.
