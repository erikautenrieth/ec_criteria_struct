Inclusion Criteria:
 - Aged 18 years [AND] and above at the start of the treatment.
 - Molar relationship either Class I [OR] or < ½ unit Class II [OR] or Class III.
 - Extraction of all four first premolar teeth [AND] as part of orthodontic treatment.
 - Maximum anchorage required using mini-implant.
 - [NOT] no systemic disease.
 - Good oral hygiene.
 - [NOT] no periodontal disease.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Significant vertical skeletal discrepancies.
 - Systemic diseases [AND] requiring long-term antibiotic use, [OR] phenytoin, [OR] cyclosporin, [OR] anti-inflammatory drugs, [OR] bisphosphonates, [OR] systemic corticosteroids [OR] or calcium channel blockers.
 - Poor oral hygiene.
 - Active periodontal disease.
