Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of primary knee osteoarthritis [AND] according to American College of Radiology
 - Being radiographically K-L grade 2-3 [OR] 3
 - Presence of symptoms for at least 3 months [AND] months
 - Patients who have been planned a conventional physiotherapy programme [AND] because of knee osteoarthritis
 - Presence of functional capacity for outpatient physical therapy [AND] therapy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with [NOT] secondary knee osteoarthritis
 - History of lower extremity [OR] spine surgery
 - History of intraarticular injection in last 1 [OR] month
 - Patients with [NOT] neurological disease that will affect muscle strength, [OR] balance [OR] coordination
