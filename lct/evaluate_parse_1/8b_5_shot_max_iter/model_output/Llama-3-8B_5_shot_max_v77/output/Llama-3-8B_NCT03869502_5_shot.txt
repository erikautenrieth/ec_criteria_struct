Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age >18 years [AND] 
 - Diagnosis of BCR-Abl positive CML blast crisis according to the WHO criteria: [OR]
 - Blasts 20% or more of peripheral blood white cells [OR] or bone marrow cells [OR] or
 - Extramedullary blast proliferation [OR] or
 - Large foci or clusters of blasts in bone marrow biopsy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] Lack of informed consent
