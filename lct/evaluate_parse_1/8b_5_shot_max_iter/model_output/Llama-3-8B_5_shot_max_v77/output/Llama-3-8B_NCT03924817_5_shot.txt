Inclusion Criteria:
 - Prescription of a Narval CC mandibular advancement device
 - Age [AND] ≥ 18 years
 - Ability [AND] to understand the study information [AND] and information on usage of personal data
 - Signed [AND] and dated informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] with central sleep apnea ([NOT] central AI ≤ 5/hour)
 - Patients [AND] with loose teeth [AND] and severe parodontitis
 - Patients [AND] with completely missing teeth [OR] removable tooth replacement, [OR] or with a teeth health [NOT] insufficient to retain an MAD
 - Patients [AND] with missing molars
 - Patients [AND] with maximum protrusion [NOT] of less than 5mm
