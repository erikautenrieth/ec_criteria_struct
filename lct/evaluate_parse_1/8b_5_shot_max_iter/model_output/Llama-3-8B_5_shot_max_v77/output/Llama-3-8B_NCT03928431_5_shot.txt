Inclusion Criteria:
 - Infants of healthy mothers [AND] with uncomplicated pregnancies at term, mothers of any ethnic [OR] social background that can speak [AND] read [AND] understand Swedish to the extent that they can consent in Swedish. An additional inclusion criterion for mothers in the CS groups is vaginal pH ≤ 4 [AND] at the time of birth.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Infants of mothers with pre-eclampsia [OR] complicated pregnancies, mothers <18 years [OR] >40 years of age, genital herpes simplex [OR] genital warts, HIV, Hepatitis B [OR] complications during delivery, infants delivered prior to 37 weeks of gestation.
 - Additional exclusion criteria for mothers in the CS group: Mothers [NOT] positive to Group B Streptococcus, Group A Streptococcus, bacterial vaginosis, vaginal pH > 4 [AND] at the time of birth. Mothers with fecal samples [NOT] positive for Salmonella, Shigella, Campylobacter [OR] Yersinia.
