<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:

* Age [AND] ≥18 years
* BMI [AND] ≤40 kg/m2
* Indication for hysterectomy for benign disease [OR] prophylactic surgery due to hereditary cancer
* Indication for radical hysterectomy [AND] and/or pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for cervical cancer
* Indication for modified radical hysterectomy [AND] and/or pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for endometrial cancer
* Size of uterus and vagina [AND] allows for retrieval by the vaginal route in cancer patients
* Written informed consent [AND] obtained

Exclusion Criteria:

* Known extensive intra-abdominal adhesions [AND] present
* Anaesthesiological contraindications to laparoscopy [AND] present
* Women with pacemaker [OR] other implants where electrosurgery is to be avoided [AND] present
* Women with known defects of the hemostasis [AND] present
* Pregnancy [AND] present
* Other internal [OR] anatomical criteria that preclude a minimal invasive approach [AND] present
* Inability to understand patient information [AND] present
