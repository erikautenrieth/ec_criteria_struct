Inclusion Criteria:
  - BMI 45-60 kg/m2 [AND] failure of at least one weight loss medication
  - Previous Gastric Bypass Surgery [AND] lead to less than 50% of excess weight lost by 2 years after surgery
  - Significant Loss of Control over eating [OR] subject is able to attend all scheduled clinic appointments on their own [OR] with a caregiver
  - Premenopausal women [AND] agree to use acceptable methods of birth control
  - Participants [AND] provide voluntary, decisionally capable, and appropriately informed consent
  - Subject is able to comply w/ all testing and follow-up requirements defined by the study protocol [AND] participant has no immediate plan for relocation beyond 6 hours of the study site
  - Proficiency with the English language

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Subject has an implanted medical device that delivers electrical energy to the brain [OR] subject has an implantable cardiac pacemaker, defibrillator, or neurostimulator
  - Subject is likely to require repeat MR imaging after implant of the RNS Neurostimulator and Leads [OR] subject is unable to fit into CT scanner (500lb upper weight limit for CT scanner)
  - Inability to provide informed consent to treatment [NOT] subject is able to provide informed consent
