Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patient requiring an electroneuromyogram whose indication allows the application of a standard protocol of examination [AND] patient that has given his/her [OR] free and informed consent.
  - Patient that signed the consent form [AND] patient with an health insurance plan.
  - Patient that is at least 18 years old [AND] patient that is under 80 years old.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patient with symptoms of neuropathy during the screening [AND] patient whose Neurological severity score (NSS) score is [OR] ≥ 3.
  - Patient whose neurological examination is abnormal and suggestive of neuropathy [AND] patient presenting a pathology that could cause neuropathy [OR] e.g. diabetes, renal failure, etc..
  - Patient already participating in a category 1 research study [NOT] (Jardé law) [AND] patient in an exclusion period determined by another study.
  - Patient under the protection of justice, under guardianship or under curatorship [AND] patient refusing to sign the consent form.
  - Patient for which it is [NOT] impossible to provide information about the study [AND] patient that is pregnant, parturient, or breastfeeding.
