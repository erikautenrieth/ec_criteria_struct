<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [AND] parents of the patients who accept to participate [OR] and sign the informed consent
  - Patients whose molar teeth are diagnosed as molar incisor hypomineralization by EAPD
  - Teeth that are fully erupted [AND] 
  - Patients who have at least two permanent first molars that need fissure sealant
  - Teeth that have white-yellow-brown discoloration [OR] that indicate high-caries risk

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Teeth that are previously restored [OR] have caries
  - Patients who have orthodontic treatment [OR] 
  - Patients who are not cooperative for the dental procedure [OR] 
  - Teeth that have dental flourosis [OR] enamel malformation according to the specific syndromes [NOT]
