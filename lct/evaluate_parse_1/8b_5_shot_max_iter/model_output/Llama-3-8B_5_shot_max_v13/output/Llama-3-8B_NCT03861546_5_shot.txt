<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:

1. Age > 18 years [AND] confirmed diagnosis of prediabetes or T2D (documented A1c of ≥ 5.7% [OR] 2-hour post-load glucose of >140 [OR] FBG of >100)
2. A family member or peer willing to participate and attend all sessions as a social influencer [AND] proficiency in English of at least one member of each dyad
3. Willingness to provide written consent

Exclusion Criteria:

1. Type 1 diabetes [OR] diabetes secondary to other conditions (e.g. steroid-induced, pancreatic insufficiency, or chemotherapy-induced)
2. Malignancy [OR] life-threatening illness with life expectancy of <5 years
3. End-stage disease [OR] serious illness that prohibits participation (e.g. end-stage renal disease [OR] class IV congestive heart failure)
4. Inability to perform unsupervised physical activity
5. Pregnancy
6. Diagnosed cognitive deficits [OR] limited decision-making capacity
7. [NOT] alcohol or substance abuse
8. [NOT] homelessness [OR] no fixed address

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
