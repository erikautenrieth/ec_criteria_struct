<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Aged 18 years [AND] and above at the start of the treatment.
  - Molar relationship either Class I [OR] < ½ unit Class II [OR] or Class III.
  - Extraction of all four first premolar teeth [AND] as part of orthodontic treatment.
  - Maximum anchorage required using mini-implant.
  - No systemic disease [NOT] requiring long-term antibiotic use, phenytoin, cyclosporin, anti-inflammatory drugs, bisphosphonates, systemic corticosteroids [OR] calcium channel blockers.
  - Good oral hygiene [AND] with no periodontal disease.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Significant vertical skeletal discrepancies [NOT] requiring orthodontic treatment.
  - Systemic diseases requiring long-term antibiotic use, phenytoin, cyclosporin, anti-inflammatory drugs, bisphosphonates, systemic corticosteroids [OR] calcium channel blockers.
  - Poor oral hygiene [NOT] maintained with regular brushing and flossing.
  - Active periodontal disease [NOT] under treatment and in remission.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
