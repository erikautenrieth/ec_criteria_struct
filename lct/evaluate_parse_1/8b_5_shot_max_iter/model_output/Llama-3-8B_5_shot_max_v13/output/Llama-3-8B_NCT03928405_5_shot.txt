<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] having mild or moderate severity of AD
  - [AND] lack of communication difficulties or problems
  - [AND] volunteering to participate in the study
  - [OR] having a cerebrovascular event
  - [OR] having a neurological disease such as Parkinson's or MS that disturbs balance and coordination
  - [AND] Mini Mental Test Score of 18 to 24 points
  - [AND] being 65-80 years old

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients diagnosed with dementia [OR] infection, vascular, hematological diseases
  - Patients who [NOT] could not be contacted during the follow-up period [OR] who did not come to control
  - Cardiac and cerebrovascular event [OR] endocrine disorder, fluid-electrolyte imbalance, and infection
  - Patients with malignancy [OR] malignancy, chemotherapy, and radiotherapy
  - Patients with delirium [OR] depression
  - Those with protective sensation loss [OR] those with Semmes-Weinstein Monofilament Thickness 4.56 and above
  - Patients with lower or upper extremity amputations at any level
