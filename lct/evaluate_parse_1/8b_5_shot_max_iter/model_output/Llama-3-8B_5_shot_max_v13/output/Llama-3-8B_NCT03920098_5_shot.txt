Inclusion Criteria:
  - Primipara [AND] (no previous pregnancies)
  - 18 to 25 years of age
  - Gestation [OR] <15 weeks at first prenatal visit
  - No intention to move outside the city of Santiago [AND] in the next 2 years

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Multipara [NOT] allowed
  - Pre-existing cancer [AND] (except non-melanoma skin cancer)
  - Family history [AND] of breast cancer (one first-degree female relative diagnosed with breast cancer)
  - High-risk pregnancy [AND] according to national guidelines
  - During the follow-up, pregnant women [AND] under any treatment that may affect the levels of metabolic markers [AND] will be excluded from the analysis
  - Pregnant women [AND] with pre-eclampsia [OR] with preterm birth (less than 37 weeks of gestational age) [AND] will be also excluded
