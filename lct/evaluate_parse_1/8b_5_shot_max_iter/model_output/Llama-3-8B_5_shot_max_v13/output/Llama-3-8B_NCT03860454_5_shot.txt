<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:

* LMNA+ cases [AND] with pathogenic LMNA mutations [OR] for LMNA+ and heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
* DCMWT cases: patients [AND] with heart muscle failure [OR] but with wild-type lamin gene. Heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust.
* HV (controls): matched [AND] to cases.

Exclusion Criteria:

* Needle-phobia [AND] that would preclude blood-letting [OR] Participants unwilling to consent.
* Patients [AND] that have a conventional contraindication [OR] for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).
* Patients [AND] that have had a blood transfusion [OR] within the last month and patients having haemodialysis will be excluded.
