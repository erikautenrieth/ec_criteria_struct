Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Apparently healthy men [AND] with a body mass index between 18 [OR] 30 kg/m2 [AND] willing to eat only the foods provided by the study for a 2 week period

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Those reporting any chronic [OR] acute diseases, food allergies, smoking [OR] alcohol abuse [AND] use of illicit drugs; regular consumption of medications, micronutrient supplements, or both [AND] vegetarians, or those unable to eat meat, are also excluded since the foods for the study contain meat [AND] unable to refrain from taking medications during the study period [NOT] presence of other known causes of arthritis, such as other defined spondyloarthritides, septic arthritis, Lyme disease, microcrystalline arthritis or rheumatoid arthritis
