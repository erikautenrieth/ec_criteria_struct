<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Type 2 diabetes mellitus on metformin therapy for at least 3 months [AND] with [OR] without other anti-diabetic medications
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with type 1 diabetes, gestational diabetes, or diabetes secondary to another medical condition [OR] with a history of pancreatitis or pancreatic surgery
  -  Patients with a history of severe hypoglycemia or hyperglycemia [OR] with a hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) level > 12% at screening
  -  Patients with a history of liver disease, kidney disease, or heart disease [OR] with a creatinine clearance < 60 mL/min
  -  Patients who are pregnant or breastfeeding [OR] with a history of infertility or are unable to conceive
  -  Patients with a history of substance abuse or dependence [OR] with a current substance use disorder
  -  Patients who are unable to provide informed consent [OR] with a history of cognitive impairment or dementia
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
