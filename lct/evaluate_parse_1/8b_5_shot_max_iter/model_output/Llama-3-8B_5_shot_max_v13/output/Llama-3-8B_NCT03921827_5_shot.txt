<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients with recent stroke/TIA and severe stenosis of intracranial ICA or MCA [AND] impaired CVR within previous three months [AND] but not before 3 weeks after acute stroke. This is to differentiate between patients with a long-standing fixed-stenosis [OR] patients with partially recanalized intracranial artery (masquerading as severe stenosis).
  2. Age >21 years [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients with atrial fibrillation/arrhythmias [OR]
  2. Within 2 weeks of cardiac catheterization [OR] arterial puncture at femoral puncture site [OR]
  3. Decompensated heart failure [NOT] usually class 3 or 4 [OR]
  4. LV EF <30% [AND]
  5. Moderate or severe AR [OR]
  6. Persistent and uncontrolled hypertension (BP persistently >160/100 mmHg) [OR]
  7. Bleeding diathesis [AND]
  8. Active thrombophlebitis/venous disease of lower limbs [OR]
  9. Severe lower extremity vaso-occlusive disease [OR]
  10. Presence of a documented aortic aneurysm/dissection requiring surgical repair [OR]
  11. Pregnancy [NOT]

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
