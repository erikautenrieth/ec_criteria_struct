<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:

1. [AND] The fifth stage of chronic renal failure diagnosed by a physician.
2. [AND] The physician advises and informs the patient who has to undergo renal replacement therapy but has not yet decided what treatment to take.
3. [AND] Being able to communicate in Mandarin [OR] Taiwan.
4. [AND] Ages over 20 years old.

Exclusion Criteria:

1. [NOT] Cognitive impairment: The MMSE of the above-mentioned educators was 26 points, and the national minimum was < 21 points, those who are not educated are <16 points, and the case is excluded.
2. [OR] Patients who have received non-emergency dialysis treatment.
3. [OR] Patients with severe cardiopulmonary dysfunction, severe shock, low blood pressure, and large bleeding are not controlled.
4. [OR] Patients with chronic active hepatitis, cirrhosis or liver failure.
5. [OR] A history of cancer that may be transferred.
6. [OR] Severe bleeding tendency.
7. [OR] Loss of peritoneal function or extensive peritoneal adhesion, unable to use peritoneal dialysis.
8. [OR] Unable to correct physiological defects, may interfere with peritoneal dialysis, or increase the chance of infection, such as umbilical hernia.
9. [OR] The patient is unable to operate the fluid change, hand shake, and no family members can assist.
10. [OR] Patients who are blind, have poor eyesight, and have difficulty operating.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
