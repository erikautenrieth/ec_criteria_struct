Inclusion Criteria:
 - Singleton gestation [AND] 
   - Twin gestation reduced to singleton either spontaneously [OR] therapeutically, is eligible unless the reduction occurred before 13 weeks 6 days project gestational age [AND] 
   - Higher order multifetal gestations reduced to singletons [NOT] eligible.
 - Gestational age at randomization between 16 weeks 0 days [AND] 23 weeks 6 days based on clinical information [AND] evaluation of the earliest ultrasound as described in Gestational Age [AND] 
 - Cervical length on transvaginal examination of less than or equal to 20 mm within 10 days prior to randomization by a study certified sonographer [AND] There is no lower cervical length threshold.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Women [NOT] eligible for the TOPS trial.
