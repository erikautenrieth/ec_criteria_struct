Inclusion Criteria:
 1. The fifth stage of chronic renal failure diagnosed by a physician [AND] with a physician's advice and informed consent.
 2. Being able to communicate in Mandarin [OR] Taiwan.
 3. Ages over 20 years old [AND] with a physician's advice and informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Cognitive impairment [NOT] with a MMSE score of 21 points or higher [AND] with a national minimum of 21 points.
 2. Patients who have received non-emergency dialysis treatment [OR] with a history of chronic active hepatitis, cirrhosis, or liver failure.
 3. Patients with severe cardiopulmonary dysfunction, severe shock, low blood pressure, and large bleeding [OR] with a history of cancer that may be transferred.
 4. Severe bleeding tendency [OR] with a loss of peritoneal function or extensive peritoneal adhesion, unable to use peritoneal dialysis.
 5. Unable to correct physiological defects, may interfere with peritoneal dialysis, or increase the chance of infection [OR] with an umbilical hernia.
 6. The patient is unable to operate the fluid change, hand shake, and no family members can assist [OR] with poor eyesight and difficulty operating.
 7. Patients who are blind [OR] with a history of cancer that may be transferred.
