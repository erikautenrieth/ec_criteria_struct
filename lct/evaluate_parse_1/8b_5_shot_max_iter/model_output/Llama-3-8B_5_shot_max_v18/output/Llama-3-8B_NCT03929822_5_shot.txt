Inclusion Criteria:
  - Women [AND] called back from a screening mammography by either FFDM [OR] tomosynthesis with soft tissue abnormalities including masses, asymmetries, focal asymmetries [OR] or architectural distortion with [OR] without calcifications. Patients will be questioned regarding the possibility of pregnancy and will need a negative pregnancy test prior to the study intervention.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Age [NOT] <30 years old
  - Screening mammography with only calcifications abnormalities [OR] no soft tissue abnormalities
  - Male patients [OR] patients who are not women
  - Pregnant [OR] lactating patients [OR] patients who are not of childbearing potential
  - Patients with any allergy to iodinated contrast [OR] patients who are not allergic to iodinated contrast
  - Patients with eGFR [NOT] < 45
  - Patients that may be treated with radioactive iodine [OR] patients who will not be treated with radioactive iodine
