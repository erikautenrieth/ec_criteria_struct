<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient requiring an electroneuromyogram whose indication allows the application of a standard protocol of examination [AND] patient that has given his/her [OR] free and informed consent.
 - Patient that signed the consent form [AND] patient with an health insurance plan.
 - Patient is at least 18 years old [≥] [AND] patient is under 80 years old [≤].

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patient with symptoms of neuropathy during the screening [OR] patient whose Neurological severity score (NSS) score is ≥ 3.
 - Patient whose neurological examination is abnormal and suggestive of neuropathy [OR] patient presenting a pathology that could cause neuropathy (e.g. diabetes, renal failure, etc.).
 - Patient already participating in a category 1 research study (Jardé law) [OR] patient in an exclusion period determined by another study.
 - Patient under the protection of justice, under guardianship or under curatorship [OR] patient refusing to sign the consent form.
 - Patient for which it is [NOT] impossible to provide information about the study.
 - Pregnant patient, parturient, or breastfeeding.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
