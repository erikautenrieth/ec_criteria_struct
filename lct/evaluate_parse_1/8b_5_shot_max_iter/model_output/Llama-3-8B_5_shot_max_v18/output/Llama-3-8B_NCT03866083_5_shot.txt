Inclusion Criteria:
- Critically ill cirrhotics [AND] with early onset septic shock (<24 hours) [OR] with norepinephrine of more than 10 ug/min [AND] and lactate >4 mmol/L.

Exclusion Criteria:
- Patients [AND] with age less than 18 years [NOT]
- Severe known cardiopulmonary disease [OR] (structural or valvular heart disease, coronary artery disease, COPD)
- Severe coagulopathy [AND] platelets <20,000 [AND] INR >5
- Active Bleed [OR] (Mucosal or variceal)
- Pregnancy [NOT]
- Chronic kidney disease [NOT]
- Extremely moribund patients [AND] with an expected life expectancy of less than 24 hours [NOT]
- Failure to give informed consent from family members [NOT]
- Hemodynamic instability [AND] requiring very high dose of vasopressors [OR] late presentation [OR] spontaneous reversal of shock [NOT]
- Patient enrolled in other clinical trials [NOT]
