<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Being older than 18 [AND] having a diagnosis of HIV;
  - Presenting at least one of the following symptoms during the preceding two weeks: [OR] sleep problems, [OR] fatigue, [OR] pain, [OR] anxiety [OR] or symptoms of depression;
  - Understanding and speaking French [AND];
  - Being able to follow the instructions to learn the relaxation technique.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Having a chronic or acute disease [OR] food allergy [OR] smoking [OR] alcohol abuse;
  - Using illicit drugs [OR] regularly consuming medications [OR] micronutrient supplements [OR] both;
  - Being a vegetarian [OR] unable to eat meat, as the study foods contain meat;
  - Being unable to refrain from taking medications during the study period [NOT].
