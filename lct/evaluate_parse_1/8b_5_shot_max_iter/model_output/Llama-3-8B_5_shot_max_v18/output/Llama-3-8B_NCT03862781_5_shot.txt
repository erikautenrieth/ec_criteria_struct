Inclusion Criteria:
 - Eligible patients are those [AND] over 18 years of age [AND] who are regularly scheduled to undergo a right hemicolectomy via a minimally invasive approach [OR] (robotic [OR] laparoscopic) with the creation of an anastomosis. Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnant women
 - Additional colon resection is planned [OR] (i.e. left colectomy [OR] proctectomy)
 - Vulnerable populations such as prisoners [OR] adults unable to give consent
 - If the scheduled surgery is planned at Butterworth hospital [AND] (as to standardize the nursing care received postoperatively)

 - The procedure is converted to an open resection [OR]
