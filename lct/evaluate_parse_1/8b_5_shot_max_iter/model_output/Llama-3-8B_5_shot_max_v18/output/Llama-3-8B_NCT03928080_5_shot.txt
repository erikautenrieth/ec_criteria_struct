<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
 - Aged 18 [AND] above.
 - Having the ability [AND] to learn the standard toothbrushing method with artificial intelligence powered toothbrush.
 - Ability [AND] and willingness to give written informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Edentulous mouth [OR] having no teeth.
 - Pregnant females [OR] women who are expecting a child.
 - Having received professional periodontal treatment (other than supragingival cleaning) within the previous 12 months [OR] having had periodontal treatment in the past year.
 - Having received antibiotic medication within the previous 3 months [OR] having taken antibiotics in the last 3 months.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
