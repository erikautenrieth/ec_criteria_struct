Inclusion Criteria (PTA group):
  -  T1D patients [AND] who received their first PTA [OR] second PTA only if their first graft was explanted within a week [AND] between 2 January 2005 [OR] and 31 December 2017
  -  age ≥18 [AND] < 65 years
  -  willingness to participate in this observational study

Inclusion Criteria (control group):
  -  Type 1 diabetes
  -  age ≥18 years [AND] < 65 years
  -  willingness to participate in this observational study
  -  minimum disease duration of 5 years
  -  brittle diabetes [AND] as defined by the occurrence of frequent acute complications [OR] (ketoacidosis [OR] or severe hypoglycaemic episodes requiring third-part assistance [OR] or unawareness despite well-conducted intensive insulin therapy)
  -  presence of at least one diabetes-related complication [OR] (neuropathy [OR] retinopathy [OR] subclinical nephropathy)

Exclusion Criteria (both groups):
  -  conditions [AND] other than T1D known to affect bone health [OR] (i.e. non-compensated hypothyroidism [OR] hyperthyroidism [OR] hyperparathyroidism [OR] inflammatory bowel disease [OR] malignancy [OR] rheumatoid arthritis [OR] hypogonadism [OR] severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] hepatic insufficiency [OR] alcohol intake ≥ 3 units/day)
  -  use of drugs [AND] that can affect bone metabolism [OR] (e.g. bisphosphonates [OR] anticonvulsants [OR] hormone replacement therapy) [NOT] other than glucocorticoids [AND] immunosuppressants
  -  recipients of simultaneous pancreas kidney transplantation [OR] pancreatic islet transplantation [OR] multiple PTAs [OR] other transplants
  -  BMI <18.5 [OR] ≥ 30 kg/m2
