Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age ≥18 years [AND]
  -  BMI ≤40 kg/m2 [OR]
  -  indication for hysterectomy for benign disease [OR] or prophylactic surgery due to hereditary cancer
  -  indication for radical hysterectomy [AND] or pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for cervical cancer
  -  indication for modified radical hysterectomy [AND] or pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for endometrial cancer

  -  written informed consent [AND]

  -  known extensive intra-abdominal adhesions [AND]
  -  anaesthesiological contraindications to laparoscopy [OR]

  -  women with known defects of the hemostasis [OR]
  -  pregnancy [OR]
  -  other internal [OR] or anatomical criteria that preclude a minimal invasive approach [AND]
  -  [NOT] ability to understand patient information
