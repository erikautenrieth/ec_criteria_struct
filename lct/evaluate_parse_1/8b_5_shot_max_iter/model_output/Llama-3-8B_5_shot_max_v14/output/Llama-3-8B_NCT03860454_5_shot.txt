<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:

* LMNA+ cases with pathogenic LMNA mutations [AND] for LMNA+ and heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust [OR] DCMWT cases: patients with heart muscle failure but with wild-type lamin gene [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust [OR] HV (controls): matched to cases
* Patients with LMNA+ or DCMWT who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust

Exclusion Criteria:

* Patients with needle-phobia that would preclude blood-letting [NOT] willing to consent
* Patients with conventional contraindications for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) [OR] patients who have had a blood transfusion within the last month [OR] patients having haemodialysis
