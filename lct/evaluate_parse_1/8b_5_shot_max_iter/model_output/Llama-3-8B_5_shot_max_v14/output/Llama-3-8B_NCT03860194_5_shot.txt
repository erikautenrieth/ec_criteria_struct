<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Older adults [OR] ≥ 65 years
  -  Subject has lower appendicular muscle mass index (AMMI) [AND] <7 kg/m^2 in men [OR] <5.7 kg/m^2 in women using Bioelectrical impedance analysis (BIA, TANITA BC-418®, Tokyo, Japan)
  -  Subject has lower handgrip strength [AND] <26 kg in men [OR] <18 kg in women using dynamometer (CAMRY®, Zhongshan, Guangdong) [OR] slowly gait speed [AND] <1 m/s

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Subject's life expectancy [NOT] was shorter than 6 months
  -  Nursing home resident [OR] subject has the history of kidney failure [OR] subject has the history of liver failure [OR] subject has the history of diabetes mellitus [OR] subject has resistance exercise habit [OR] unable to refrain from taking medications during the study period
