<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
 - Being older than 18 [AND] 
 - Have a diagnosis of HIV [AND] 
 - Present at least one of the following symptoms during the preceding two weeks: [OR] sleep problems, [OR] fatigue, [OR] pain, [OR] anxiety [OR] or symptoms of depression; 
 - Understand and speak French [AND] 
 - Be able to follow the instructions to learn the relaxation technique.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Those [NOT] reporting any chronic or acute diseases, [OR] food allergies, [OR] smoking, [OR] alcohol abuse;
 - Use of illicit drugs; regular consumption of medications, micronutrient supplements, [OR] both;
 - Vegetarians, [OR] those unable to eat meat, are also excluded since the foods for the study contain meat;
 - Unable to refrain from taking medications during the study period.
