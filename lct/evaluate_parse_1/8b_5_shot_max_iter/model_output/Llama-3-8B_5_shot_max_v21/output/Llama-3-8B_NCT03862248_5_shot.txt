Inclusion Criteria:
 - All newly registered patients [AND] with smear-positive recurrent pulmonary TB
 - Adults [OR] as well as children (no age limit)
 - Able [AND] and willing to provide written informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] transferred to a health facility [NOT] supported by Damien Foundation [OR] will be excluded. This includes patients diagnosed with HIV/TB-coinfection.
