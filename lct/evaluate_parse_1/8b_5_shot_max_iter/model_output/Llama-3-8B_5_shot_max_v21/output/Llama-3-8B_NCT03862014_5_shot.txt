Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] parents of the patients who accept to participate [AND] sign the informed consent
 - Patients whose molar teeth are diagnosed as molar incisor hypomineralization by EAPD [OR] teeth that are fully erupted
 - Patients who have at least two permanent first molars that need fissure sealant [AND] teeth that have white-yellow-brown discoloration that indicate high-caries risk

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Teeth that are previously restored [OR] have caries
 - Patients who have orthodontic treatment [OR] patients who are not cooperative for the dental procedure
 - Teeth that have dental flourosis [OR] enamel malformation according to the specific syndromes
