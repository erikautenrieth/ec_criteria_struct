Inclusion Criteria:
  - Handball players [AND] who are healthy [AND] who have participated in at least one official match in the last year [AND] and attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Those who have experienced a musculoskeletal injury [OR] in the last three months;
  - Those with cardiovascular diseases [OR] ;
  - Those who have used anabolic [OR] anti-inflammatory [OR] analgesic drugs 72 hours before the evaluations;
  - Those who have used alcohol [OR] illicit drugs during the collection period;

</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Note: I added the [AND] operator before the words "who are healthy" and "who have participated in at least one official match in the last year" to ensure that all three conditions are met for inclusion. I also added the [OR] operator before the words "in the last three months" and "with cardiovascular diseases" to indicate that either of these conditions is sufficient for exclusion.
