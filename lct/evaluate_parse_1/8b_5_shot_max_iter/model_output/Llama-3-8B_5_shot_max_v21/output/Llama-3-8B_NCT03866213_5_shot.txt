Inclusion Criteria:
  - The patient [AND] is currently being treated at Queen Elizabeth Central Hospital [OR] Kamuzu Central Hospital in the neonatal ward.
  - The patient [AND] is less than 28 days old [AND] (neonate).
  - The patient [AND] is deemed to be at risk for jaundice [OR] the patient [AND] is undergoing blue light phototherapy for treatment of jaundice.
  - The patient's [AND] parent or guardian [AND] has provided informed consent for their child to participate.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Parent or guardian [NOT] is unable or unwilling to provide informed consent.
  - The patient [NOT] is unable to receive a blood draw/heel stick as determined by their clinician.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
