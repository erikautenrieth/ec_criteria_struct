Inclusion Criteria:
 - All comatose patients (Glasgow coma score <8) [AND] due to carbon monoxide poisoning, treated by hyperbaric oxygen therapy in a tertiary hospital in Île de France area;
 - Aged [OR] ≥ 18 years;
 - Hospitalized in hyperbaric medicine unity [AND] of Raymond Poincaré hospital in Garches.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] pregnant woman;
 - Patient [AND] present clinical signes of stroke [AND] without CO detection.
In case of unusable data, patient [AND] will be excluded from study.
