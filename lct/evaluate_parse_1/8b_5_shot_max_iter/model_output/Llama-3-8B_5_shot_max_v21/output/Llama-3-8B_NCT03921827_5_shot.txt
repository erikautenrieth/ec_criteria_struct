Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. Patients [AND] with recent stroke/TIA [AND] severe stenosis of intracranial ICA [OR] MCA [AND] impaired CVR within previous three months [AND] but not before 3 weeks after acute stroke. This is to differentiate between patients with a long-standing fixed-stenosis from patients with partially recanalized intracranial artery (masquerading as severe stenosis).
 2. Age >21 years [AND]
Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. Patients [AND] with atrial fibrillation [OR] arrhythmias.
 2. Within 2 weeks of cardiac catheterization [OR] arterial puncture at femoral puncture site [AND].
 3. Decompensated heart failure [AND] usually class 3 [OR] 4 [AND].
 4. LV EF <30% [AND].
 5. Moderate [OR] severe AR [AND].
 6. Persistent [AND] uncontrolled hypertension (BP persistently >160/100 mmHg) [AND].
 7. Bleeding diathesis [AND].
 8. Active thrombophlebitis [OR] venous disease of lower limbs [AND].
 9. Severe lower extremity vaso-occlusive disease [AND].
 10. Presence of a documented aortic aneurysm [OR] dissection requiring surgical repair [AND].
 11. Pregnancy [AND].
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
