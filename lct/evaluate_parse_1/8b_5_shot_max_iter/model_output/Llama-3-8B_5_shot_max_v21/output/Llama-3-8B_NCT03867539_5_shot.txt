<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Carpal tunnel syndrome diagnosed either by physical exam [OR] EMG
 - Age > 18 [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Allergy to bupivacaine [NOT]
 - Pre-operative opioid usage within the last six months [NOT]
 - Patients allergic to Percocet [OR] acetaminophen [NOT]
 - Patients taking tricyclic antidepressants (interaction with bupivacaine) [NOT]
 - Vulnerable populations (prisoners [OR] or pregnant women) [NOT]
