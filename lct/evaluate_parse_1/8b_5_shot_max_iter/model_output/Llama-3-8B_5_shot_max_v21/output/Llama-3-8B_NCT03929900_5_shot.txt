<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age 45-79 years [AND] Estimated glomerular filtration rate 20-60mL/min/1.73m2 [OR] based on CKD-EPI(Cr) equation [AND] Non-dialysis dependent (NDD)

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease [OR] Unable [OR] unwilling to give consent
 - Previously received chronic dialysis [OR] Previous any organ transplant
 - Patients who diagnosed heart failure with NYHA class 3 [OR] 4
 - Known liver cirrhosis (Child-pugh class 2 [OR] 3)
 - Pregnant women [OR] Single kidney due to trauma [OR] donation
 - Patients who received immunosuppressive agent within the past 1 year [OR] Patients who diagnosed with glomerulonephritis through kidney biopsy
