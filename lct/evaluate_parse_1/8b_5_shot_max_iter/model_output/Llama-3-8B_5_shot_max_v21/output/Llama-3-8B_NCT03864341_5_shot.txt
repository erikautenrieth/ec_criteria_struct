<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Be a patient in the Bellevue Hospital Center ED [AND] 
  - Be ≥18 years old [AND] 
  - Screen positive for homelessness risk using a homelessness risk screening tool (HRST) [AND] 
  - Screen positive for unhealthy alcohol or drug use [AND] 
  - Able to provide consent [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Medically [NOT] unstable (e.g., critically ill) [OR] psychiatrically unstable [OR] 
  - Unable to provide informed consent for other reason (e.g., cognitive deficit, profound intoxication) [OR] 
  - Incarcerated [OR] in police custody [OR] 
  - Unable to understand and speak English [OR] 
  - Lives outside NYC [AND] /or cannot give a NYC ZIP code [OR] 
  - Already homeless (residing in a shelter [OR] or on the streets) [OR] 
  - Does not have a telephone where can be reached for follow-up [OR] 
  - Has already received specialized peer navigator/addiction social worker services during current ED visit [OR] 
  - Has already participated in the study [OR]

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
