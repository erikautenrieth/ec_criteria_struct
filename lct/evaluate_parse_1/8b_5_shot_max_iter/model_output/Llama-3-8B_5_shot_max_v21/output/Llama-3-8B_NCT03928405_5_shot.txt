Inclusion Criteria:
 - Having mild [OR] moderate severity of AD
 - Lack of communication [AND] difficulties or problems
 - Volunteering to participate in the study
 - Cerebrovascular event, [NOT] having disturbing the balance and coordination of neurological disease such as Parkinson [OR] MS
 - Mini Mental Test Score, 18 [OR] to 24 points from being scored
 - Being 65-80 aged.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients diagnosed with dementia [OR] (infection, vascular, hematological diseases), patients who could not be contacted during the follow-up period [OR] who did not come to control, cardiac and cerebrovascular event
 - Endocrine disorder, fluid-electrolyte imbalance [OR] and infection
 - Patients with malignancy [OR] and malignancy, chemotherapy [OR] and radiotherapy
 - Patients with delirium [OR] depression
 - Those with protective sensation loss (those with Semmes-Weinstein Monofilament Thickness 4.56 [OR] and above)
 - Patients with lower [OR] upper extremity amputations at any level
