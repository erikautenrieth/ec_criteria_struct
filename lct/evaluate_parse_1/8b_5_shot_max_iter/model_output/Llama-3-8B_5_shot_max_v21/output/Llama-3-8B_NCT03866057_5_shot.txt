<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Age 18-90 [AND] acute ischemic [OR] hemorrhagic stroke (within 30 days) [AND] consent to participate [AND] to anonymous processing of data.
  - Additional inclusion criteria for Sub-project Neurophysiological Markers: signing of informed consent for the participation in the sub-project.
  - Additional inclusion criteria for Epigenetic subproject: signing of informed consent for participation in the subproject.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Stroke occurred [NOT] more than 30 days after the transfer to intensive rehabilitation.
  - Additional exclusion criteria for Epigenetic subproject (for retrospective analysis): [NOT] history of major psychiatric disorder [OR] recent exacerbation [OR] global aphasia [OR] refusal of the patient to participate in the study.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
