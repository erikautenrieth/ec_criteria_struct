<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:

1. The fifth stage of chronic renal failure diagnosed by a physician [AND] with a physician's advice to undergo renal replacement therapy but has not yet decided what treatment to take.
2. Ability to communicate in Mandarin [OR] Taiwan.
3. Age over 20 years old.

Exclusion Criteria:

1. Cognitive impairment: MMSE score < 21 points [OR] MMSE score < 16 points for those who are not educated.
2. Patients who have received non-emergency dialysis treatment.
3. Patients with severe cardiopulmonary dysfunction [OR] severe shock [OR] low blood pressure [OR] large bleeding that is not controlled.
4. Patients with chronic active hepatitis [OR] cirrhosis [OR] liver failure.
5. A history of cancer that may be transferred.
6. Severe bleeding tendency.
7. Loss of peritoneal function [OR] extensive peritoneal adhesion, unable to use peritoneal dialysis.
8. Unable to correct physiological defects [OR] may interfere with peritoneal dialysis [OR] increase the chance of infection, such as umbilical hernia.
9. The patient is unable to operate the fluid change [OR] hand shake [OR] no family members can assist.
10. Patients who are blind [OR] have poor eyesight [OR] have difficulty operating.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
