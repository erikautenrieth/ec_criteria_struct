<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Subjects with adhesive capsulitis diagnosis by physical examination [AND] (Compared with intact side, there is more than 25% limitation in at least two directions of shoulder range of motion)
  -  Subjects have complaints for at least 1 month [OR] longer
  -  No [NOT] pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  History of bilateral adhesive capsulitis [OR] adhesive capsulitis in the opposite shoulder
  -  History of shoulder trauma [OR] fracture [OR] shoulder surgery [OR] calcific tendinopathy [OR] glenohumeral osteoarthritis [OR] inflammatory rheumatic diseases [OR] tumor [OR] infection
  -  History of corticosteroid injection in the shoulder during the last 3 months [OR] more
  -  History of recent lung [OR] breast [OR] bypass surgery
  -  History of cervical radiculopathy [OR] brachial plexus lesion [OR] neuromuscular disease
  -  History of physical therapy program for the same shoulder in the last 6 months [OR] longer
