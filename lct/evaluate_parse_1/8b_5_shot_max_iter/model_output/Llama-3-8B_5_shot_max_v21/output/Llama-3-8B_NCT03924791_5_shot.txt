Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosed with sciatica by GP
 - NRS leg pain of 6 [OR] more on a 10-point NRS scale
 - Minimum duration of symptoms of 3 weeks [AND] and maximum duration of 8 weeks

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Age under 18 [NOT] years
 - Condition [AND] preventing to receive transforaminal epidural injection
 - Severe scoliosis
 - Transforaminal epidural injection received in 6 [OR] months before randomization date
 - Surgery for sciatica at the same level
 - Surgery for sciatica at another level within one year [AND] before inclusion
 - Pregnancy
