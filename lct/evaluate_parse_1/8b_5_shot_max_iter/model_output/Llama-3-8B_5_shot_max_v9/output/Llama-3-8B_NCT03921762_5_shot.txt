Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] > 50 years old
  - [AND] Bilateral age-related cataract
  - [AND] Qualify for bilateral implantation within 1 month
  - [AND] [NOT] No previous refractive or ocular surgery
  - [AND] Available IOL dioptre range: 18 [OR] to 27D
  - [AND] Expected postoperative astigmatism ≤ 0.75D

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [OR] Pupil > 4 mm
  - [OR] Occupation requiring night-time driving [OR] or any occupation incompatible with multifocality
  - [OR] Acute or chronic disease or illness that would increase risk or confound study results [e.g. diabetes mellitus [AND] with retinopathy, immunocompromised, glaucoma, epiretinal membrane, cornea guttata, etc….] found to be relevant by the investigators
  - [OR] Amblyopia [OR] strabismus
  - [OR] Extremely shallow anterior chamber
  - [OR] Capsule or zonular abnormalities that may affect postoperative centration or tilt of the lens [e.g. pseudoexfoliation syndrome, uveitis, Marfan's syndrome]
  - [OR] Pupil abnormalities [OR] (non-reactive, tonic pupils, abnormally shaped pupils or pupils that do not dilate under mesopic/ scotopic conditions)
  - [OR] Instability to place the intraocular lens safely at the location planned
  - [OR] Pregnancy [AND] (pregnancy test will be taken pre-operatively in women of reproductive age)
