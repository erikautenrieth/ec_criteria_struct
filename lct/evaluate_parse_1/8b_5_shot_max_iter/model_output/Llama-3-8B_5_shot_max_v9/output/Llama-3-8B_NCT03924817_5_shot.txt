Inclusion Criteria:
  - Prescription of a Narval CC mandibular advancement device [AND] 
  - Age ≥ 18 years [OR] 
  - Ability to understand the study information [AND] information on usage of personal data [OR] 
  - Signed and dated informed consent [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [AND] with central sleep apnea (central AI > 5/hour) [OR] 
  - Patients [AND] with loose teeth [AND] severe parodontitis [OR] 
  - Patients [AND] with completely missing teeth [OR] removable tooth replacement [OR] with a teeth health insufficient to retain an MAD [OR] 
  - Patients [AND] with missing molars [OR] 
  - Patients [AND] with maximum protrusion of less than 5mm [OR]
