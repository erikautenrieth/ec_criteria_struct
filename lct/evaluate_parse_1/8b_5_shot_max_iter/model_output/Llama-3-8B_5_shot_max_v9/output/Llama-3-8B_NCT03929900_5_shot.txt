<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Age 45-79 years [AND] 
  - Estimated glomerular filtration rate 20-60mL/min/1.73m2 [OR] based on CKD-EPI(Cr) equation
  - Non-dialysis dependent (NDD) [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease [OR]
  - Unable [OR] unwilling to give consent [AND]
  - Previously received chronic dialysis [OR]
  - Previous any organ transplant [OR]
  - Patients who diagnosed heart failure with NYHA class 3 [OR] or 4 [AND]
  - Known liver cirrhosis (Child-pugh class 2 [OR] or 3 [AND])
  - Pregnant women [OR]
  - Single kidney due to trauma [OR] or donation [AND]
  - Patients who received immunosuppressive agent within the past 1 year [OR]
  - Patients who diagnosed with glomerulonephritis through kidney biopsy [OR]

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
