<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Being older than 18 [OR] 
  -  Have a diagnosis of HIV [AND] 
  -  Present at least one of the following symptoms during the preceding two weeks: [OR] sleep problems, [OR] fatigue, [OR] pain, [OR] anxiety, [OR] symptoms of depression; 
  -  Understand and speak French [AND] 
  -  Be able to follow the instructions to learn the relaxation technique.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Those reporting any chronic or acute diseases [OR] food allergies, [OR] smoking, [OR] alcohol abuse [OR] 
  - Use of illicit drugs; regular consumption of medications, micronutrient supplements, [OR] both [AND] 
  - Vegetarians, [OR] those unable to eat meat, are also excluded since the foods for the study contain meat [AND] 
  - Unable to refrain from taking medications during the study period [OR] 
  - Those who have a history of any serious medical condition, including but not limited to: heart disease, stroke, cancer, or kidney disease [AND] 
  - Those who are currently taking any medications that may interact with the study treatments [OR] 
  - Those who have a history of substance abuse or addiction [AND] 
  - Those who are pregnant or breastfeeding [OR] 
  - Those who are unable to provide informed consent [AND] 
  - Those who are not willing to follow the study protocol [OR] 
  - Those who have a history of any other condition that may interfere with the study [AND]
