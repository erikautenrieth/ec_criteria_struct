Inclusion Criteria:
  -  ASA [AND] I-III male [AND] female patients [AND] aged between 18 [OR] to 80 years old, who will receive spinal anesthesia for surgeries lasting less than one hour.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients [NOT] younger than 18 years old.

  -  Presence of preexisting neurological symptoms [OR].
