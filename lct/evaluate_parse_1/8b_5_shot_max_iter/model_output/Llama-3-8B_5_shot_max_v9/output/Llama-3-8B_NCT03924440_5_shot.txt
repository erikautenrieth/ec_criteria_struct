<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Female [AND] (mandatory)
  2. [NOT] currently pregnant [OR] nursing (pregnancy affects PdG levels)
  3. Aged 18-34 [AND] (advanced age can hinder PdG levels)
  4. Cycle length of 26-39 days [AND] (long or short cycles have inconsistent PdG levels)
  5. Currently use other fertility monitoring tools [AND] (this allows us to compare Proov - Ovulation Double Check test results with other fertility tracking methods)

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Male [OR] (excluded due to gender)
  2. Pregnant [OR] (excluded due to pregnancy)
  3. Nursing [OR] (excluded due to nursing)

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
