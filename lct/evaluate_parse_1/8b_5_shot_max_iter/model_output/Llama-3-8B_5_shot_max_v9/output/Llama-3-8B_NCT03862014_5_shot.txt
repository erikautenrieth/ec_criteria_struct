Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [AND] parents of the patients who accept to participate [OR] sign the informed consent
  - Patients whose molar teeth are diagnosed as molar incisor hypomineralization by EAPD
  - Teeth that are fully erupted [OR] have white-yellow-brown discoloration that indicate high-caries risk
  - Patients who have at least two permanent first molars that need fissure sealant

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Teeth that are previously restored [OR] have caries
  - Patients who have orthodontic treatment [OR] are not cooperative for the dental procedure
  - Teeth that have dental flourosis [OR] enamel malformation according to the specific syndromes [NOT] diagnosed as molar incisor hypomineralization by EAPD
