Inclusion Criteria:
  - Diagnosis of CD for at least 3 months prior to baseline [AND] 
  - Confirmed diagnosis of moderate to severe CD as assessed by SF [OR] AP score [OR] SES-CD [AND] 
  - Demonstrated intolerance, loss of response [OR] or inadequate response to conventional [OR] or biologic therapy for CD [AND] 
  - If female, subject must meet the contraception recommendations

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Have a current diagnosis of ulcerative colitis [OR] inflammatory bowel disease-unclassified (IBD-U) (formerly known as indeterminate colitis) [OR] abdominal [OR] or perianal abscess [OR] short bowel syndrome [NOT]
  - Have a stoma [OR] ostomy [NOT]
  - Have had a bowel resection within 6 months [OR] any kind of intra-abdominal [OR] or extra abdominal surgery within 3 months of baseline [NOT]
  - Have ever received any monoclonal antibodies binding IL-23 [NOT]

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
