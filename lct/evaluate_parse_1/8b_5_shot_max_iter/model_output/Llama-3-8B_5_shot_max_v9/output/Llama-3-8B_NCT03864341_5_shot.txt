<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Be a patient in the Bellevue Hospital Center ED [AND] be ≥18 years old [AND] screen positive for homelessness risk using a homelessness risk screening tool (HRST) [AND] screen positive for unhealthy alcohol or drug use [AND] able to provide consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Medically [OR] psychiatrically unstable [OR] unable to provide informed consent for other reason [OR] (e.g., cognitive deficit, profound intoxication).
  - Incarcerated [OR] in police custody [OR] unable to understand and speak English.
  - Lives outside NYC [OR] cannot give a NYC ZIP code [OR] already homeless (residing in a shelter or on the streets).
  - Does not have a telephone where can be reached for follow-up [OR] has already received specialized peer navigator/addiction social worker services during current ED visit [OR] has already participated in the study.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
