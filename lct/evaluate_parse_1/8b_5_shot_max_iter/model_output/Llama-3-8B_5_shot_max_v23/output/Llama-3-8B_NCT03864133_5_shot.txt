Inclusion Criteria:
  - Adults age 55-90 years [AND] with visual significant cataracts in one [OR] both eyes.
  - Healthy individuals [AND] able to tolerate outpatient cataract surgery under local anesthesia via either phacoemulsification [OR] and/or femtosecond assisted cataract surgery. Well-controlled diabetes [AND] hypertension will be included.
  - Females of childbearing potential [AND] must agree to use a reliable method of birth control while participating in this study. Reliable methods of birth control are: abstinence [OR] oral contraceptives [OR] intrauterine device (IUD) [OR] DepoProvera [OR] tubal ligation [OR] vasectomy of the partner (with confirmed negative sperm counts) in monogamous relationship (same partner). An acceptable, although less reliable, method involves the careful use of condoms [AND] spermicidal foam or gel [OR] cervical cap [OR] sponge. A pregnancy test is required at least 10 days from the last normal menstrual period, if the patient is a sexually active female of childbearing potential.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Allergy to Phenylephrine [OR] NSAIDs.
  - Inability to sit steady and upright for the Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT) [AND] without assistance.
  - Complications during surgery, including posterior capsular rupture [OR] vitreous loss [OR] zonular dialysis [OR] or iris trauma.
  - Macular thickness above 300 microns at baseline [AND] without any treatment.
  - Currently taking a prostaglandin analogue [AND] without any medical reason.
  - Presence of an epiretinal membrane on the preoperative OCT [AND] without any treatment.
  - Retained lens fragment post-operatively [AND] without any treatment.
  - Inability to return for follow appointments [AND] without any valid reason.
  - Female patients who are pregnant [OR] lactating [OR] planning to become pregnant during the course of treatment [AND] without any exception.
