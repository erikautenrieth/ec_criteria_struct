<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - Handball players [AND] who are healthy;
  - Body mass index between 18 and 30 kg/m2;
  - Willing to eat only the foods provided by the study for a 2 week period;
  - Participation of at least one official match in the last year [AND] attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Those reporting any chronic or acute diseases, food allergies, smoking or alcohol abuse [OR] musculoskeletal injury in the last three months;
  - Use of illicit drugs; regular consumption of medications, micronutrient supplements, or both [OR] use of anabolic, anti-inflammatory and analgesic drugs 72 hours before the evaluations;
  - Vegetarians, or those unable to eat meat, are also excluded since the foods for the study contain meat [OR] use of alcohol and illicit drugs during the collection period;
  - Unable to refrain from taking medications during the study period [OR] unable to attend 70% of the team's activities during the current season.
