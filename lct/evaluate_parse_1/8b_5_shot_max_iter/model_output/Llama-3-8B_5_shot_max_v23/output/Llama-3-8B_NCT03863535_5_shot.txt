Inclusion Criteria:
  Type II diabetic patients, 18 years and older, were included if the participants had:
    [AND] severe non-PDR in either eyes with [OR] without diabetic macular edema;
    [AND] ETDRS letters-measured best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) ranging from 20/40 to 20/320 Snellen equivalent;
    [AND] no previous treatment (of any type) in either eye.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] history of prior laser treatment or vitrectomy in the study eye;
  2. [NOT] history of thromboembolic event - including myocardial infarction [OR] cerebral vascular accident;
  3. [NOT] major surgery within the prior 6 months [OR] planned within the next 28 days;
  4. [NOT] history of glaucoma [OR] ocular hypertension;
  5. [NOT] loss of vision as a result of other causes;
  6. [NOT] history of systemic corticosteroid therapy within the last 3 months;
  7. [NOT] severe systemic disease other than diabetes mellitus;
  8. [NOT] known coagulation abnormalities [OR] current use of anticoagulative medication other than aspirin;
  9. [NOT] any condition that could affect followup or documentation (including preretinal or vitreous hemorrhage).
