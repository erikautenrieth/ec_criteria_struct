Inclusion Criteria:
  1. age > 18 years [AND] 
  2. confirmed diagnosis of prediabetes [OR] T2D (documented A1c of ≥ 5.7% [OR] 2-hour post-load glucose of >140 [OR] FBG of >100) [AND] 
  3. a family member [OR] peer willing to participate [AND] attend all sessions as a social influencer [AND] 
  4. proficiency in English of at least one member of each dyad [AND] 
  5. willingness to provide written consent [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. type 1 diabetes [OR] diabetes secondary to other conditions (e.g. steroid-induced [OR] pancreatic insufficiency [OR] or chemotherapy-induced) [AND] 
  2. malignancy [OR] life-threatening illness with life expectancy of <5 years [AND] 
  3. end-stage disease [OR] serious illness that prohibits participation (e.g. end-stage renal disease [OR] class IV congestive heart failure) [AND] 
  4. [NOT] ability to perform unsupervised physical activity [AND] 
  5. pregnancy [OR] 
  6. diagnosed cognitive deficits [OR] limited decision-making capacity [AND] 
  7. alcohol [OR] substance abuse [AND] 
  8. homelessness [OR] no fixed address [AND]

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
