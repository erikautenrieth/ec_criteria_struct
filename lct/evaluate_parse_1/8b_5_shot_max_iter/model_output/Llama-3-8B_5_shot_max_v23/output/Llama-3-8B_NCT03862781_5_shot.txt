Here is the edited version of the inclusion and exclusion criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] incorporated:

<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Eligible patients are those [AND] over 18 years of age [AND] who are regularly scheduled to undergo a right hemicolectomy via a minimally invasive approach (robotic [OR] laparoscopic) with the creation of an anastomosis. Right colectomy will be defined as removal of the ascending colon, ligation of the ileocolic artery [AND] vein, [AND] +/- removal of the terminal ileum, [AND] +/- removal of the proximal transverse colon, [AND] +/- removal of the right branch of the middle colic artery [AND] vein.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Pregnant women [AND] women who are [NOT] over 18 years of age
  - Additional colon resection is planned (i.e. left colectomy [OR] proctectomy)
  - Vulnerable populations such as prisoners [OR] adults unable to give consent
  - If the scheduled surgery is planned at Butterworth hospital (as to standardize the nursing care received postoperatively) [AND] if the patient is not willing to undergo a minimally invasive approach
  - Emergent cases [AND] if the patient is not regularly scheduled to undergo a right hemicolectomy

Patients will be excluded from the study intra-operatively if:
  - The procedure is converted to an open resection [AND] if a loop ileostomy is performed in addition to the right colectomy
