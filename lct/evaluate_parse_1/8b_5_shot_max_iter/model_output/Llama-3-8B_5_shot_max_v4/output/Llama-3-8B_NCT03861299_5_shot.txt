Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age [AND] ≥18 years [OR] and ≤ 90 years
 - Tumor diagnosed as Glioblastoma Multiforme [AND] on MRI with distinct ring-like pattern of contrast enhancement [AND] with thick irregular walls [AND] and a core area reduced signal suggestive of tumour necrosis as assessed by the surgeon
 - Tumors situated [AND] in or near eloquent areas; motor cortex, sensory cortex, subcortical pyramidal tract [OR] or speech areas as indicated on MRI (Sawaya Grading II [OR] and II)
 - The tumor is [AND] suitable for resection (according to neurosurgeon)
 - Karnofsky performance scale [AND] 80 or more
 - Written Informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Tumors [AND] of the cerebellum, brain stem [OR] or midline
 - Multifocal contrast enhancing lesions
 - Substantial non-contrast enhancing tumor areas [AND] suggesting low grade gliomas with malignant transformation
 - Medical reasons [AND] precluding MRI (e.g. pacemaker)
 - Inability [AND] to give consent because of [OR] language barrier
 - Psychiatric history
 - Previous brain tumour surgery
 - Previous low-grade glioma
 - Second primary malignancy within the past 5 years [AND] with the exception of adequately treated in situ carcinoma of any organ [OR] or basal cell carcinoma of the skin
 - Severe aphasia [OR] or dysphasia
