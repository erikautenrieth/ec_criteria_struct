Inclusion Criteria:
 - Participants must be adults [AND] 18 years or older [OR] older.
 - Participants must have the capacity [AND] ability to give informed consent.
 - Participants have been given an assessment [AND] received a diagnosis of OCD.
 - Participants must speak English.
 - Participants must be able to physically attend treatment.

 - Prisoners [OR] any other high-risk population group [NOT] excluded.
 - Participants experiencing active [NOT] psychosis.
 - Participants experiencing active [NOT] substance abuse disorder.
 - Participants experiencing active [NOT] suicidality.
