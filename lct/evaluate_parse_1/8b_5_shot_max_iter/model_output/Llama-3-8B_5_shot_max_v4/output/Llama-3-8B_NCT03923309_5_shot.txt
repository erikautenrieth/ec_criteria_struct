Inclusion Criteria:
 - Biopsy proven rectal cancer (anal verge ≤ 10cm) [AND] neoadjuvant radiotherapy [OR] chemoradiotherapy [AND] no radical surgery after neoadjuvant therapy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Other [NOT] malignant disease
 - Other [NOT] fatal disease
 - Incompletion of [NOT] half of radiotherapy course
 - Metastasis on initial presentation
