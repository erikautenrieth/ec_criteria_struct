Inclusion Criteria:
 - age [AND] ≥18 years
 - BMI [AND] ≤40 kg/m2
 - indication for hysterectomy for benign disease [OR] or prophylactic surgery due to hereditary cancer
 - indication for radical hysterectomy [AND] and/or pelvic-/[OR] para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for cervical cancer
 - indication for modified radical hysterectomy [AND] and/or pelvic-/[OR] para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for endometrial cancer
 - size of uterus [AND] and vagina allows for retrieval by the vaginal route in cancer patients
 - written informed consent [AND]
Exclusion Criteria:
 - known extensive intra-abdominal adhesions [NOT]
 - anaesthesiological contraindications to laparoscopy [NOT]
 - women with pacemaker [OR] or other implants where electrosurgery is to be avoided [NOT]
 - women with known defects of the hemostasis [NOT]
 - pregnancy [NOT]
 - other internal [OR] or anatomical criteria that preclude a minimal invasive approach [NOT]
 - inability to understand patient information [NOT]
