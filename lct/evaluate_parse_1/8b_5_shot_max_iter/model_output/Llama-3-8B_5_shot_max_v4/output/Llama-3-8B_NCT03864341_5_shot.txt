Inclusion Criteria:
 - <EDITED_CRITERIA>Be a patient in the Bellevue Hospital Center ED [AND] Be ≥18 years old [AND] Screen positive for homelessness risk using a homelessness risk screening tool (HRST) [AND] Screen positive for unhealthy alcohol or drug use [AND] Able to provide consent</EDITED_CRITERIA>
Exclusion Criteria:
 - <EDITED_CRITERIA>Medically [NOT] unstable (e.g., critically ill) [OR] psychiatrically [NOT] unstable [OR] Unable to provide informed consent for other reason (e.g., cognitive deficit, profound intoxication) [OR] Incarcerated [OR] in police custody [OR] Unable to understand and speak English [OR] Lives outside NYC [OR] cannot give a NYC ZIP code [OR] Already homeless (residing in a shelter [OR] or on the streets) [OR] Does not have a telephone where can be reached for follow-up [OR] Has already received specialized peer navigator/addiction social worker services during current ED visit [OR] Has already participated in the study</EDITED_CRITERIA>
