<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
 - Be a patient in the Bellevue Hospital Center ED [AND] be ≥18 years old [AND] screen positive for homelessness risk using a homelessness risk screening tool (HRST) [AND] screen positive for unhealthy alcohol or drug use [AND] able to provide consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Medically [OR] psychiatrically unstable [OR] unable to provide informed consent for other reason (e.g., cognitive deficit, profound intoxication) [OR] incarcerated or in police custody [OR] unable to understand and speak English [OR] lives outside NYC and/or cannot give a NYC ZIP code [OR] already homeless (residing in a shelter or on the streets) [OR] does not have a telephone where can be reached for follow-up [OR] has already received specialized peer navigator/addiction social worker services during current ED visit [OR] has already participated in the study.
