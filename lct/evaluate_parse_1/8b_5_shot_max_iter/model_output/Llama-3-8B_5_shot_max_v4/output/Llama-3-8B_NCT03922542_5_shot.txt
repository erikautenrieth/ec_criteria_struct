Inclusion Criteria:
 - Documented keratoconus [OR] ectasia after refractive surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Insufficient corneal thickness [OR]
 - Ocular condition that [NOT] may predispose the eye to complications
 - History of chemical injury [OR] or delayed epithelial healing
 - Condition that [NOT] would interfere with [OR] prolong epithelial healing
 - Known sensitivity to treatment medications [OR]
 - Pregnancy [OR]
