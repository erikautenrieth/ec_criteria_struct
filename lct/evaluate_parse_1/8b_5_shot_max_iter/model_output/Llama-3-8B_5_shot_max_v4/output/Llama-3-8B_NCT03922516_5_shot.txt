Inclusion Criteria:
 - All patients who are assigned to a clinically indicated chest X-ray by the emergency department of Vienna General Hospital [AND] 
 - Ability to provide informed consent [AND] 
 - Informed consent after detailed patient briefing
Exclusion Criteria:
 - The emergency department's assessment of a critical clinical condition [NOT] opposes an examination with both modalities (ULDCT of the chest, chest X-ray) [OR] 
 - Assigned to chest X-ray as follow-up
 - Women with positive [NOT] ß-HCG-test
