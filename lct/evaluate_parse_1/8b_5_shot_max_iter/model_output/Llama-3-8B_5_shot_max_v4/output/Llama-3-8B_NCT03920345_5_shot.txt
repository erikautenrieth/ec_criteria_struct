Inclusion Criteria:
 - Subject age 18 [AND] - 85 years;
 - SIJ arthropathy [AND] and chronic lower back pain (CLBP);
 - Patient [AND] to undergo endoscopic electrothermic ablation of spinal levels S1-S3 [OR] L4-L5 [AND] and/or L5-S1 facet joint [AND] can be included in the ablation per doctor discretion.
 - Correct spinal levels (ranging L4-S3) [AND] to be treated have been confirmed by 1) diagnostic SIJ injection [AND] followed by 2) diagnostic Medial/Lateral Branch Block at the applicable levels.
 - The subject [AND] is likely to follow standard of care post-operative follow-up for at least 24 months.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] receiving additional invasive back surgery after the study treatment.
 - Inability [AND] to complete follow-up visits [OR] required questionnaires.
 - Non-compliant [AND] patients
 - Difficult [OR] impossible communication with the patient
 - Breastfeeding [OR] pregnant [OR] patients who plan a pregnancy while participating in the study
 - Systemic neurological disorders [AND] with mobility limitations (e.g. advanced Parkinson's disease [OR] multiple sclerosis)
 - Patients [AND] with incompatibilities [OR] known limitations that make participation [NOT] impossible
 - Patients [AND] after stabilization with implants on thoracic [OR] lumbar spine.
 - Inability [AND] to provide informed consent without a legally authorized representative.
