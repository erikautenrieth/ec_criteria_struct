Inclusion Criteria:
 - ASA I-III [AND] male and female patients aged between 18 [OR] to 80 years old, who will receive spinal anesthesia for surgeries lasting less than one hour.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] younger than 18 years old.
 - ASA physical status >3 [AND] not [NOT] eligible.
 - Immunosuppression [OR] high risk of infection.
 - Contraindications to receiving spinal anesthesia (e.g. coagulation impairment) [AND] not [NOT] eligible.
 - Patients [AND] with psychosis.
 - Patients [AND] with preexisting back pain.
 - Patients [AND] with cognitive impairment.
 - Allergies to local anesthetics [OR] analgesics [OR] any medication used in the study.
 - Patients [AND] with chronic regular opioid usage.
 - Presence of preexisting neurological symptoms.
