Inclusion Criteria:
 - Major Depressive Disorder according to Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (DSM-V) [AND] > 25 years [AND] Sufficient understanding of the German language
Exclusion Criteria:
 - < 25 years
 - Concomitant diagnosis of past or present [NOT] psychotic disorder
 - Concomitant diagnosis of past or present [NOT] bipolar disorder
 - First degree relative with a [NOT] psychotic disorder
 - Unable or unwilling to discontinue antidepressant medication
 - Pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding
 - Known hypersensitivity to LSD
 - Somatic disorders including central nervous system (CNS) involvement
 - Known or suspected non-compliance, drug or alcohol abuse
 - Metal implants
 - Weight < 42 kg
 - Suicide risk [OR] or very likely to require psychiatric hospitalisation
