Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with moderate to severe sleep apnoea [AND] who require CPAP as standard care (AHI >15)
 - Patients between the age of 18 [AND] and 75

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] who have a diagnosis of atrial fibrillation (AF) [OR] atrial flutter [OR] and/or ventricular tachycardia (VT)
 - Patients [AND] who have an ILR already in-situ [OR] an established indication for ILR
 - Patients [AND] who have a palliative diagnosis [NOT] i.e. life expectancy less than 3 years
 - Patients [AND] who lack capacity
