Inclusion Criteria:
 - Signed informed consent with the study [AND]
 - Men [OR] women >18 years of age [AND]
 - Patients ≥1 [AND] ≤6 months after type I myocardial infarction [AND]
 - Physical inactivity before MI defined as the absence of moderate to vigorous exercise for at least 30 minutes [OR] 5 or more days a week

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Heart failure NYHA IIIB-IV [AND]
 - Planned coronary revascularization [OR]
 - Planned major surgery within the next 12 months [OR]
 - Inability to walk for any reason [AND]
 - Comorbidities that would preclude adherence to the rehabilitation program (e.g. arthrosis, [OR] active malignancy, [OR] major depression [OR] or other significant psychiatric disorder, [OR] cognitive impairment) - at the discretion of the physician [AND]
 - Life expectancy less than 12 months [AND]
 - Pregnancy [OR]
 - Inability to operate the smart-watch [AND]
