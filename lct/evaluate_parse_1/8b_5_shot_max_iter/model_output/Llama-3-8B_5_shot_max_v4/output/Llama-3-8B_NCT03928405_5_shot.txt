Inclusion Criteria:
 - Having mild [OR] or moderate severity of AD
 - Lack of communication difficulties [OR] or problems
 - Volunteering to participate in the study
 - Cerebrovascular event,
 - Having disturbing the balance and coordination of neurological disease [AND] such as Parkinson, [OR] MS,
 - Mini Mental Test Score, 18 [OR] to 24 points from being scored
 - being 65-80 aged.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients diagnosed with dementia [NOT] (infection, vascular, hematological diseases), patients who could not be contacted during the follow-up period [OR] or who did not come to control, cardiac and cerebrovascular event,
 - Endocrine disorder, [OR] fluid-electrolyte imbalance and infection
 - Patients with malignancy [AND] and malignancy, chemotherapy [OR] and radiotherapy,
 - Patients with delirium [OR] or depression
 - Those with protective sensation loss [NOT] (those with Semmes-Weinstein Monofilament Thickness 4.56 [OR] and above)
 - patients with lower [OR] or upper extremity amputations at any level
