Inclusion Criteria:
 - Treatment-seeking cigarette smoker
 - Meet DSM-IV criteria for nicotine dependence [AND] with
 - Negative result on urine test for drug use during screening [OR] at the time of enrollment

Exclusion Criteria:
 - History of any Axis I psychiatric diagnosis [NOT] other than nicotine dependence
 - Medical conditions that [NOT] might affect brain function
 - Current use of medications that [NOT] could alter brain function
 - Pregnancy
 - Current illicit drug use [NOT] other than occasional use of marijuana
