Inclusion Criteria:
 - Treatment-seeking cigarette smoker
 - Meet DSM-IV criteria for nicotine dependence [AND] with [OR] without
 - Negative result on urine test for drug use during screening

Exclusion Criteria:
 - History of any Axis I psychiatric diagnosis [NOT] other than nicotine dependence
 - Medical conditions that might affect brain function [OR] or
 - Current use of medications that could alter brain function [OR] or
 - Pregnancy [OR] or
 - Current illicit drug use [NOT] other than occasional use of marijuana
