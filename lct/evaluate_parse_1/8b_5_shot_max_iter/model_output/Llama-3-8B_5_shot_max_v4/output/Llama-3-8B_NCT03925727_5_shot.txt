Inclusion Criteria:
 - Subject-reported history of dry eye disease in both eyes for at least 6 months [AND] 
 - History of use of artificial tear eye drops for dry eye symptoms [OR] 
 - Total score of ≥40 on SANDE [AND] 
 - TFBUT [OR] 
 - Corneal fluorescein staining [OR] 
 - Lissamine green conjunctival staining [OR] 
 - Schirmer's test score [AND] 

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Have participated in a previous tavilermide (MIM-D3) study [NOT] 
 - Have clinically significant slit lamp findings at Visit 1 [OR] 
 - Have a history of lacrimal duct obstruction within 12 months of Visit 1 [AND] 
 - Have an uncontrolled systemic disease [OR] 
 - Be a woman who is pregnant [OR] nursing [OR] planning a pregnancy [AND] 
 - Be a woman of childbearing potential who is [NOT] using an acceptable means of birth control [OR] 
 - Have a condition [OR] be in a situation which the investigator feels may put the subject at significant risk [OR] may confound the study results [OR] may interfere significantly with the subject's participation in the study [AND] 
 - Be currently enrolled in an investigational drug or device study [OR] have used an investigational drug or device within 45 days prior to Visit 1 [AND]
