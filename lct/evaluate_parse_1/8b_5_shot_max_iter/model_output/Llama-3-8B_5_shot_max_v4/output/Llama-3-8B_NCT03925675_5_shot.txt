Inclusion Criteria:
 - <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. Patients evaluated for residual or recurrent gliomas after initial tumor surgery, external beam radiation [OR] and / or chemotherapy with temozolamide.
 2. Male [AND] and female
 3. Ages 18 to 70
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 - <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. Patient diagnosis with glioma [NOT] before surgery, external beam radiation [OR] and /or chemotherapy with temozolamide.
 2. Women [AND] who were pregnant [OR] breast feeding [OR] or possibly pregnant.
 3. Patients [AND] with hepatic [OR] renal dysfunction.
 4. Patients [AND] with MRI contraindications (i.e. pacemakers [OR] non-MR compatible devices).
 5. Patients [AND] with a history of drug hypersensitivity to 18F-Fluciclovine.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
