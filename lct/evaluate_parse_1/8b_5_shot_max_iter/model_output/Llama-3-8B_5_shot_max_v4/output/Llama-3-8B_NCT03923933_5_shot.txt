Inclusion Criteria:
 - Glomerular filtration rate [AND] less than 30 ml / min / 1.73m [OR] without replacement therapy (dialysis [OR] or hemodialysis)
 - Without volume overload
 - At least 100 ml per day [AND] of residual diuresis
 - Use of a loop diuretic [AND] for at least one month

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Allergies [NOT] known to diuretics
 - Patients [AND] with severe infections
 - Patients [AND] with hemodynamic instability
 - Amputees
 - Patients [AND] with cognitive impairment
 - Patients [AND] with acute renal failure
 - Patients [AND] with graft loss
