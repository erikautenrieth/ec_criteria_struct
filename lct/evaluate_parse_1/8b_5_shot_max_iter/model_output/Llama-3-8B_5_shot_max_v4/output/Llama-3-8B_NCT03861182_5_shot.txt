Adult Healthy Volunteers :
- Over 18 years of age [AND] 
Neonates:
  -  Aged between 1h of life [OR] and 28 days of life
  -  Born beyond 33 Week of Amenorrhea + 1day [AND] and 2kg of birth weight

  -  For whom a blood sample was prescribed as part of their routine care
Exclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
  -  Systemic inflammatory disease [OR] 
  -  Transient inflammatory state [OR] 
  -  Any drug that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h preceding the sampling [NOT]
Neonates:
  -  Risk of anemia < 7g/DL [OR] 
  -  Current anticoagulant treatment [OR]
