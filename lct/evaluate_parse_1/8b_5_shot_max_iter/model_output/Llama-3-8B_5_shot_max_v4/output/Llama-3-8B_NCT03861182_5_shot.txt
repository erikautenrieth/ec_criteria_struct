Inclusion Criteria:
<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
- Over 18 years of age [AND] 
Neonates:
  - [OR] Aged between 1h of life [AND] and 28 days of life
  - [OR] Born beyond 33 Week of Amenorrhea [AND] + 1day [AND] and 2kg of birth weight
  - [OR] Hospitalized in the medical-surgical centre of Pediatrics [AND] of the hospital of Strasbourg
  - [OR] For whom a blood sample was prescribed as part of their routine care
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
  - [NOT] Systemic inflammatory disease
  - [NOT] Transient inflammatory state
  - [NOT] Any drug that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h preceding the sampling
Neonates:
  - [NOT] Risk of anemia < 7g/DL
  - [NOT] Current anticoagulant treatment
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
