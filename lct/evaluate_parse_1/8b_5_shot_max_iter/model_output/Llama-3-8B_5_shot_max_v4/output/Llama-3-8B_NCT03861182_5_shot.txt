Inclusion Criteria:

Adult Healthy Volunteers:
  - Over 18 years of age [AND] [OR]
Neonates:
  - Aged between 1h of life [AND] and 28 days of life
  - Born beyond 33 Week of Amenorrhea [AND] + 1day [AND] and 2kg of birth weight
  - Hospitalized in the medical-surgical centre of Pediatrics of the hospital of Strasbourg
  - For whom a blood sample was prescribed as part of their routine care

Exclusion Criteria:

Adult Healthy Volunteers:
  - Systemic inflammatory disease [NOT] free of any chronic or acute diseases
  - Transient inflammatory state [NOT] free of any chronic or acute diseases
  - Any drug that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h preceding the sampling [NOT] free of any medications
Neonates:
  - Risk of anemia < 7g/DL [NOT] free of any anemia
  - Current anticoagulant treatment [NOT] free of any anticoagulant therapy

Note: The edited version incorporates the logical operators [AND], [OR], [NOT] as per the given rules.
