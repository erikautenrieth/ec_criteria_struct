Inclusion Criteria:

Adult Healthy Volunteers:
- Over 18 years of age [AND] with no exclusion criteria
Neonates:
  - Aged between 1h of life [OR] and 28 days of life
  - Born beyond 33 Week of Amenorrhea [OR] + 1day [AND] and 2kg of birth weight
  - Hospitalized in the medical-surgical centre of Pediatrics [AND] of the hospital of Strasbourg
  - For whom a blood sample was prescribed [AND] as part of their routine care

Exclusion Criteria:

Adult Healthy Volunteers:
  - Systemic inflammatory disease [AND] or Transient inflammatory state
  - Any drug that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h [OR] preceding the sampling
Neonates:
  - Risk of anemia [NOT] < 7g/DL
  - Current anticoagulant treatment [AND] or any other exclusion criteria
