Inclusion Criteria:
 - Vaginal delivery [AND] an infant age of 6 months (±3 weeks) [AND] mother ≥15 years of age [AND] infant still breastfeeding [AND] anticipated residence in the area for the study duration.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] inability to provide informed consent
 - Hemoglobin < 70 g/L
 - Z scores for weight-for-age (WAZ) [OR] weight-for-height (WHZ) <3,
 - [NOT] any maternal [OR] infant chronic illness
 - Administration of any infant vitamin [OR] mineral supplements for the past 2 months
 - [NOT] any history of infant antibiotic treatment.
