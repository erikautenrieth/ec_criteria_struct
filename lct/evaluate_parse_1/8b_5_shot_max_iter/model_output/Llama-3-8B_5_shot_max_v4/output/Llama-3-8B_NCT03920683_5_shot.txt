 - Age [AND] ≥ 18 ans
 - Diabetes mellitus (type 1 [OR] or 2)
 - Patients [AND] addressed to a one-day hospitalization to assess cardiovascular comorbidities, [OR] between January 2014 [OR] and May 2017, [OR] in the diabetes department, [AND] in the Pitié-Salpêtrière hospital

Exclusion Criteria:
