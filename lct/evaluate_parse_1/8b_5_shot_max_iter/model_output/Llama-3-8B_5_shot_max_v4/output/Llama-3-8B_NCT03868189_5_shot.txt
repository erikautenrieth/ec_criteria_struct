Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient requiring an electroneuromyogram [AND] whose indication allows the application of a standard protocol of examination [OR]
 - Patient that has given his/her [NOT] free and informed consent
 - Patient that signed the consent form
 - Patient with an health insurance plan
 - The patient is at least 18 years old (≥) [AND] under 80 years old (≤)

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patient with symptoms of neuropathy during the screening [OR]
 - Patient whose Neurological severity score (NSS) score is ≥ 3 [OR]
 - Patient whose neurological examination is abnormal [AND] suggestive of neuropathy [OR]
 - Patient presenting a pathology that could cause neuropathy (e.g. diabetes, renal failure, etc.) [OR]
 - Patient already participating in a category 1 research study (Jardé law) [OR]
 - Patient in an exclusion period determined by another study [OR]
 - Patient under the protection of justice, under guardianship or under curatorship [OR]
 - Patient refusing to sign the consent form [OR]
 - Patient for which it is [NOT] impossible to provide information about the study [OR]
 - Pregnant patient, parturient, or breastfeeding
