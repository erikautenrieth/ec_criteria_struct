Inclusion Criteria:
 - Aged 18 [AND] above.
 - Having the ability [AND] to learn the standard toothbrushing method [AND] with artificial intelligence powered toothbrush.
 - Ability [AND] and willingness to give written informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Edentulous mouth
 - Pregnant females [OR] females who are trying to become pregnant
 - Having received professional periodontal treatment (other than supragingival cleaning) within the previous 12 months [OR] having received antibiotic medication within the previous 3 months
