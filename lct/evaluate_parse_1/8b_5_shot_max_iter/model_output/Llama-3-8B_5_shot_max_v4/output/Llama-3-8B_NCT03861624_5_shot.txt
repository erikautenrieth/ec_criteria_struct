Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
1. Skeletal maturity [AND] 
2. AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures [OR]
3. Wound primarily closeable (no flap or delay in closure due to contamination needed) [AND]
4. Palpable pedal pulses (no vascular injury sustained) [OR]
5. Presentation within 24 hours from injury [AND]
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
1. Current injury is a pathologic fracture [NOT]
2. Sustained bilateral tibia fracture [OR]
3. Sustained comminuted femur fracture [AND]
4. Sustained severe Traumatic Brain Injury (GCS<12) *** [OR]
5. Sustained severe spinal cord injury (lower extremity paresis/paralysis) [AND]
6. Sustained severe burns (>10% total body surface area (TBSA) [OR] or >5% TBSA with full thickness or circumferential injury) [AND]
7. Prior ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery [OR]
8. Prior or current lower limb deformity or abnormality [AND]
9. Unable to complete follow-up visits [NOT]
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Note: I've added the [NOT] operator before the words "no", "not", and "unable" as per the rules.
