Inclusion Criteria:
 - <EDITED_CRITERIA>Ulcerative colitis with confirmed diagnosis by histology [AND] and endoscopy [AND] in stable remission for 3 months [OR] more without therapy [OR] with a maintenance therapy (except steroids [AND] and antibiotics for 3 months)</EDITED_CRITERIA>
 - A negative pregnancy test

Exclusion Criteria:
 - <EDITED_CRITERIA>No history [OR] diagnosis of any of the following conditions: Chron's disease, bleeding disorders, gastrointestinal [OR] or colonic malignancy, Kidney disease, including kidney "stones" [OR] or hypercalcemia, Coagulopathy/hereditary hemorrhagic disorders/ [OR] receiving therapeutic doses of Coumadin [OR] or heparin.</EDITED_CRITERIA>
 - Ingested certain medications (Calcium, Vitamin D, fiber supplements [OR] and Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs - NSAIDs) within 30 days of study start.
