Inclusion Criteria:
 - Aged 18 years and above at the start of the treatment [AND] with a molar relationship either Class I [OR] or < ½ unit Class II [OR] or Class III [AND] extraction of all four first premolar teeth as part of orthodontic treatment [AND] maximum anchorage required using mini-implant [AND] no systemic disease [AND] good oral hygiene [AND] no periodontal disease
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Significant vertical skeletal discrepancies [NOT]
 - Systemic diseases requiring long-term antibiotic use [OR] phenytoin [OR] cyclosporin [OR] anti-inflammatory drugs [OR] bisphosphonates [OR] systemic corticosteroids [OR] calcium channel blockers [NOT]
 - Poor oral hygiene [NOT]
 - Active periodontal disease [NOT]
