Inclusion Criteria:
 - Chronic anal fissure lasting more than 6 weeks
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnant [OR] not
 - Cardiovascular disease [OR] or heart failure
 - On antihypertensive medications
 - Recurrent anal fissure after previous sphincterotomy [AND] that has not healed
 - Prior history of anal surgery [AND] that has not healed
 - Other benign anorectal diseases [AND] that are not related to the chronic anal fissure
 - With inflammatory bowel diseases [AND] that are not controlled with medication
