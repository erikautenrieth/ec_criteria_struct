Inclusion Criteria:
 - Chronic anal fissure lasting more than 6 weeks

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnant [OR] not pregnant
 - Cardiovascular disease [OR] or heart failure
 - On antihypertensive medications [OR] not on antihypertensive medications
 - Recurrent anal fissure after previous sphincterotomy [OR] not recurrent anal fissure after previous sphincterotomy
 - Prior history of anal surgery [OR] no prior history of anal surgery
 - Other benign anorectal diseases [OR] not other benign anorectal diseases
 - With inflammatory bowel diseases [OR] without inflammatory bowel diseases
