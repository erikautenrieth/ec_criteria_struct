 - Age >18 years
 - Diagnosis of BCR-Abl positive CML blast crisis according to the WHO criteria: [AND] 
   - Blasts 20% [OR] or more of peripheral blood white cells [OR] or bone marrow cells [OR] or
   - Extramedullary blast proliferation [OR] or
   - Large foci [OR] or clusters of blasts in bone marrow biopsy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] Lack of informed consent
