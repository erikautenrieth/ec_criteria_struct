Inclusion Criteria:
 - Male [OR] female, 18 years [AND] to 65 years old (inclusive)
 - Index admission for traumatic injury
 - High initial morphine equivalent use ≥ 90 mg [OR] in the first 24 hours from admission
 - Baseline pain assessed at screening of ≥ 5 [OR] on the pain numeric rating score (NRS)
 - Willing to divulge habitual marijuana usage (yes [OR] no. Yes, habitual/chronic usage; no, recreational, former, or never usage)

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] on a pain management agreement
 - Patients who are nil per os (NPO) [OR] at the time of randomization or are expected to be NPO within the next 48 hours
 - Patients who have received [OR] or are expected to receive neuraxial/locoregional blocks for pain within the next 48 hours
 - Known allergy [OR] or previous hypersensitivity reaction to dronabinol [OR] or sesame oil
 - Patients prescribed dronabinol between arrival [AND] and prior to screening/randomization
 - Pregnancy [OR] or breast feeding
 - Incarceration
