Inclusion Criteria:
 - Apparently healthy men [AND] with a body mass index between 18 [OR] and 30 kg/m2 [AND] willing to eat only the foods provided by the study for a 2 week period
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Those [AND] reporting any chronic [OR] acute diseases, [OR] food allergies, [OR] smoking [OR] or alcohol abuse
 - Use of illicit drugs [OR] regular consumption of medications, [OR] micronutrient supplements, [OR] or both
 - Vegetarians [OR] or those [AND] unable to eat meat [AND] are also excluded since the foods for the study contain meat
 - Unable [NOT] to refrain from taking medications during the study period
