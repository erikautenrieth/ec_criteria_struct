Inclusion Criteria:
 - All comatose patients (Glasgow coma score <8) [AND] due to carbon monoxide poisoning, treated by hyperbaric oxygen therapy in a tertiary hospital in Île de France area;
 - Aged ≥ 18 years [AND] with;
 - Hospitalized in hyperbaric medicine unity of Raymond Poincaré hospital in Garches.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnant [NOT] woman;
 - Patient [AND] present clinical signes of stroke [AND] without CO detection [OR] in case of unusable data, patient will be [NOT] excluded from study.
