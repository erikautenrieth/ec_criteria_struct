<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
 - age 18-90 [AND] acute ischemic [OR] or hemorrhagic stroke (within 30 days) [AND] consent to participate [AND] and to anonymous processing of data.
 - Additional inclusion criteria for Sub-project Neurophysiological Markers: [AND] signing of informed consent for the participation in the sub-project.
 - Additional inclusion criteria for Epigenetic subproject: [AND] signing of informed consent for participation in the subproject.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Stroke occurred [NOT] more than 30 days after the transfer to intensive rehabilitation.
 - Additional exclusion criteria for Epigenetic subproject (for retrospective analysis): [AND] history of major psychiatric disorder [OR] or recent exacerbation [AND] global aphasia [OR] refusal of the patient to participate in the study.
