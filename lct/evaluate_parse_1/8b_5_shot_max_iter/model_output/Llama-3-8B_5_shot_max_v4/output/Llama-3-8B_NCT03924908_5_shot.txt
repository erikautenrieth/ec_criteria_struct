Inclusion Criteria:
 - Burn patient treated as outpatient [AND] with a burn surface area > or = 5% of the total body surface area [AND] mainly 2nd degree burn
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Psychiatric [NOT] antecedents
 - Claustrophobia [OR] heavy hearing [OR] visual impairment [OR] face burn [OR] conjunctivitis
 - Consent [NOT] obtained
