Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of vulvar lichen sclerosus
 - Clinical LS score ≥ 4

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Concomitant [NOT] steroid, calcineurin inhibitor [OR] any other topical [OR] or systemic treatment for LS
 - < 3 months since start of vaginal estrogen treatment
 - Malignant disease [AND] as the cause of the vulval symptoms
 - BMI > 35 kg/m² [OR]
 - Acute infection (fungal, bacterial, viral) [AND] of the vulva, vagina [OR] or bladder
 - Presence of contraindications [AND] for the laser treatment [OR] or topical steroid treatment
