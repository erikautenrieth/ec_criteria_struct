Inclusion Criteria:
Type II diabetic patients, 18 years and older, were included if the participants had:
  1. severe non-PDR [AND] in either eyes [OR] with/without diabetic macular edema;
  2. ETDRS letters-measured best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) [OR] ranging from 20/40 to 20/320 Snellen equivalent;
  3. [NOT] any previous treatment (of any type) [OR] in either eye.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. history [OR] of prior laser treatment [OR] or vitrectomy [OR] in the study eye;
  2. history [OR] of thromboembolic event - including myocardial infarction [OR] or cerebral vascular accident;
  3. major surgery [OR] within the prior 6 months [OR] or planned within the next 28 days;
  4. history [OR] of glaucoma [OR] or ocular hypertension;
  5. loss of vision [OR] as a result of other causes;
  6. history [OR] of systemic corticosteroid therapy [OR] within the last 3 months;
  7. severe systemic disease [OR] other than diabetes mellitus;
  8. known coagulation abnormalities [OR] or current use [OR] of anticoagulative medication [OR] other than aspirin;
  9. any condition [OR] that could affect followup [OR] or documentation (including preretinal [OR] or vitreous hemorrhage).
