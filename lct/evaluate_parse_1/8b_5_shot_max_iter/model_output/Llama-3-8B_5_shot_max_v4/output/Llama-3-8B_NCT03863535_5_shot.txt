Inclusion Criteria:
<Type II diabetic patients, 18 years and older, were included if the participants had: [AND]
  1. severe non-PDR in either eyes [OR] with/without diabetic macular edema;
  2. ETDRS letters-measured best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) ranging from 20/40 [OR] to 20/320 Snellen equivalent;
  3. no previous treatment [NOT] of any type [NOT] in either eye.

  3. major surgery [OR] within the prior 6 months [OR] planned within the next 28 days;
  4. history of glaucoma [OR] ocular hypertension;
  5. loss of vision [OR] as a result of other causes;
  6. history of systemic corticosteroid therapy [OR] within the last 3 months;
  7. severe systemic disease [NOT] other than diabetes mellitus;

  9. any condition [NOT] that could affect followup [OR] documentation [OR] including preretinal [OR] or vitreous hemorrhage.
