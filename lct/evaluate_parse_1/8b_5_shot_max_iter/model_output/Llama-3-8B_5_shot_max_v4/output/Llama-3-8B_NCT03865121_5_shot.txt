Inclusion Criteria:
 - Subjects over 60 years of age [AND] with a clinical diagnosis of Parkinson's disease [OR] 
 - Stages of the disease 2-3 of Hoehn and Yahr [AND] 
 - Not exposed to tobacco during any stage of their life [OR] 
 - No history of lung diseases [AND] 
 - No laboratory abnormalities [OR] 
 - No history of adverse reactions to nicotine [AND] 
 - Able to use nicotine nasal spray [OR] 
 - Residents of Mexico City [AND] able to attend for evaluations [OR] 
 - Under current treatment with levodopa at a stable dose [AND] 
 - Not currently receiving a monoamine oxidase inhibitor treatment [OR] 

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Unable to complete follow-up protocol [OR] 
 - Drug adverse reaction [OR] 
 - Death [NOT]
