Inclusion Criteria:
 - Subjects over 60 years of age [AND] with a clinical diagnosis of Parkinson's disease
 - Stages of the disease 2-3 [OR] of Hoehn and Yahr
 - Not exposed to tobacco during any stage of their life [AND] with [NOT] any history of lung diseases
 - [NOT] any laboratory abnormalities
 - [NOT] any history of adverse reactions to nicotine
 - Able to use nicotine nasal spray
 - Residents of Mexico City [AND] able to attend for evaluations
 - Under current treatment with levodopa at a stable dose
 - [NOT] currently receiving a monoamine oxidase inhibitor treatment
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Unable to complete follow-up protocol
 - Drug adverse reaction
 - Death
