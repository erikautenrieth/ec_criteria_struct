Inclusion Criteria:
 - Burn patients [AND] with severe neuropatic pain rated at least 5 [OR] on the 10-point numerical rating scale (NRS)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Cardiac arrest history [OR] history of neurologi disease [OR] brain surgery
 - Unstable heart disease [OR] presence of a cardiac pacemaker
 - Pain resulting from other causes [AND] like as neuromuscular diseases [NOT]
 - Psychiatric disorder [OR] diabetes mellitus [OR] abnormal renal function [OR] contraindication for MRI [OR] pregnancy [NOT]
