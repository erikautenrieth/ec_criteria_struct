Inclusion Criteria:
 - Burn patients
 - Severe neuropatic pain [AND] rated at least 5 on the 10-point numerical rating scale (NRS)

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Cardiac arrest [OR] history
 - History [AND] of neurologi [OR] disease [OR] brain surgery
 - Unstable heart disease [OR] presence [AND] of a cardiac pacemaker
 - Pain resulting from other causes [AND] like as neuromuscular diseases
 - Psychiatric disorder
 - Diabetes mellitus
 - Abnormal renal function
 - Contraindication [AND] for MRI [OR] pregnancy
