Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients aged 18-74 [AND] receiving therapy for active ulcerative colitis confirmed by Mayo Score assessment [AND] and evidence of colonic inflammation.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Evidence of [NOT] Crohn's disease, [OR] indeterminate colitis, [OR] presence of bacterial [OR] or parasitic infection.
 - Patients receiving biologic agents [OR] experimental agents are [NOT] excluded.
