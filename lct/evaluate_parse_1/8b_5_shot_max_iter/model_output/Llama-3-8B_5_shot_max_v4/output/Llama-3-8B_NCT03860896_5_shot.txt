Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] aged 18-74 [AND] receiving therapy for active ulcerative colitis confirmed by Mayo Score assessment [AND] and evidence of colonic inflammation.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Evidence [OR] of Crohn's disease [OR] indeterminate colitis [OR] or presence of bacterial [OR] or parasitic infection.
 - Patients [AND] receiving biologic agents [OR] experimental agents are excluded.
