Inclusion Criteria:
 - Primipara [AND] (no previous pregnancies)
 - 18 to 25 years of age
 - Gestation [AND] <15 weeks at first prenatal visit
 - No intention to move outside the city of Santiago [AND] in the next 2 years
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Multipara [NOT]
 - Pre-existing cancer [AND] (except non-melanoma skin cancer)
 - Family history of breast cancer [OR] (one first-degree female relative diagnosed with breast cancer)
 - High-risk pregnancy [AND] according to national guidelines
 - During the follow-up, pregnant women [AND] under any treatment that may affect the levels of metabolic markers [OR] will be excluded from the analysis
 - Pregnant women [AND] with pre-eclampsia [OR] or with preterm birth (less than 37 weeks of gestational age) [OR] will be also excluded
