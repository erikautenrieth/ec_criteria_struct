Inclusion Criteria:
 - LMNA+ cases with pathogenic LMNA mutations for LMNA+ [AND] heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust [OR] 
 - DCMWT cases: patients with heart muscle failure [AND] with wild-type lamin gene. Heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust [OR] 
 - HV (controls): matched to cases.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Needle-phobia [AND] that would preclude blood-letting [NOT]
 - Participants [AND] unwilling to consent [NOT]
 - Patients [AND] that have a conventional contraindication for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) [NOT]
 - Patients [AND] that have had a blood transfusion within the last month [NOT] and patients having haemodialysis [NOT]
