Inclusion Criteria:
 - Admitted from the emergency department to inpatient [OR] or observation status at one of: [AND] Carolinas Medical Center, [OR] Carolinas Medical Center - Mercy, [OR] Atrium Health Northeast;
 - ≥18 years of age [AND] upon admission;
 - oral/parenteral antibiotic [OR] or bacterial culture order within 24 hours of emergency department presentation [AND] and
   1. culture drawn first [AND] antibiotics ordered within 48 hours [OR]
   2. antibiotics ordered first [AND] culture ordered within 48 hours (adapted from criteria applied in development of the Third International Consensus Definitions for Sepsis and Septic Shock);
 - deemed as high-risk for 30-day readmission [OR] (i.e., ≥ 20%) [OR] 30-day mortality (i.e., ≥ 10%) using risk-scoring models;
 - not discharged at the time of patient list generation.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - prior randomization to either STAR [OR] or usual care study arms;
 - not a North Carolina resident [OR] residence >2.5-hour drive time from treating hospital;
 - the only antibiotic associated with patient is administered in the operating room [AND] as this likely represents pre-operative infection prophylaxis [AND] not presumed infection;
 - patients transferred from other acute care hospitals;
 - patients with a change in code status (i.e., do not resuscitate, do not intubate) within 24 hours after admission [AND] due to the general assumption of increased risk of exposure to less aggressive treatment;
 - patients with infection ruled out during the index hospitalization.
