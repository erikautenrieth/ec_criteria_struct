Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. 18~60 years, [AND] female [OR] male
 2. Patients scheduled for an elective surgical procedure ([AND] mechanical ventilation via endotracheal tube)
 3. BMI: 18~30 kg/m2
 4. Patients [AND] understand clearly [AND] participate in the study voluntarily, [AND] sign the informed consent
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. Patients scheduled for emergency surgery
 2. Patients with a history of [NOT] drug abuse [OR] and / or [NOT] alcohol abuse 2 years prior to the screening period
 3. One or more of the laboratory findings [NOT] fall out of the limitations for this study (platelet, [OR] hemoglobin, [OR] aspartate aminotransferase, etc.)
 4. Pregnant women [OR] those in lactation period
 5. Allergic to drugs used in the study
 6. Patients with respiratory management difficulties (Modified Mallampati grade IV)
 7. Patients have participated in other clinical trial within the 3 months prior to randomization
 8. Any patient [NOT] judged by the Principal Investigator (PI) [OR] Sub-Investigator to be inappropriate for the subject for any other reason
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
