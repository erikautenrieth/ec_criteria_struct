Resident Inclusion Criteria:
 - Identified as having Alzheimer's [OR] related dementia
 - Mild [AND] to moderate cognitive impairment (BIMS = 8-15, [OR] (MDS C0500) (nursing home residents only)
 - Makes Self Understood = <1 (MDS B0700)/present in medical record
 - Understood By Others = <1 (MDS B0800)/present in medical record
 - Reside at the facility at least 2 weeks identified as a long-term resident
 - Age >65 years
 - Speaks English

Family Inclusion Criteria:
 - Speaks English
 - Willing [AND] able to watch the videos
 - Willing [AND] to complete short surveys. Being unable to attend care conference to view the video (e.g. due to distance) will not exclude family from participating.

Staff Inclusion Criteria:
 - Speaks English
 - Willing [AND] to watch the videos
 - Willing [AND] to complete short surveys

Resident Exclusion Criteria:
 - Limited communication capacity (e.g. aphasia) [NOT]
 - Comatose
 - Cognitively intact [NOT]
 - Severe cognitive impairment
