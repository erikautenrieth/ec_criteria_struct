Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] and parents of the patients who accept to participate [AND] sign the informed consent
 - Patients whose molar teeth are diagnosed as molar incisor hypomineralization by EAPD [OR] 
 - Teeth that are fully erupted [AND] 
 - Patients who have at least two permanent first molars that need fissure sealant [OR] 
 - Teeth that have white-yellow-brown discoloration that indicate high-caries risk [AND] 

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Teeth that are previously restored [OR] or have caries [NOT] 
 - Patients who have orthodontic treatment [OR] 
 - Patients who are not cooperative for the dental procedure [NOT] 
 - Teeth that have dental flourosis [OR] or enamel malformation according to the specific syndromes [NOT]
