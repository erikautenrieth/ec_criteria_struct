Inclusion Criteria:
 - Monofetal pregnancy [AND] age over 18 years old [AND] term pregnancy ≥ 37SA [AND] indication for elective caesarean section
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Fetal pathology known prenatally [OR] like intrauterine severe growth retardation with Doppler abnormalities, fetal malformation [OR] or a known fetal genetic defect.
 - Anomaly of placental adhesion [OR] caesarean in a context of medical [OR] obstetric emergency [OR] caesarean section performed under general anesthesia following failure of locoregional anesthesia [OR] refusal of the woman to participate in the study [OR] impossibility [OR] failure to study the neonatal acid-base balance on the umbilical cord.
