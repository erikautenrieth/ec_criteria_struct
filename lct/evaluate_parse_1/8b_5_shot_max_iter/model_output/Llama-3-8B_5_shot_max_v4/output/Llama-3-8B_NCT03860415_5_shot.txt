Inclusion Criteria:
 - Chronic (more than three months) ESBL colonization of the same strain [AND] as determined by [OR] species, phenotype, and antibiogram.
 - Patients must be able to swallow sachets.
 - Must be able to speak [AND] understand Swedish.
 - Must have a permanent residence in Sweden.
 - Must be able to sign informed consent in Swedish.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Immunosuppression [NOT] (i.e. chemotheraphy, treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors).
 - In patient care.
 - Immunodeficiency [OR] psychiatric disorder [OR] alcohol or substance abuse [OR] dementia.
 - Invasive catheters [OR] any other condition that would make it impossible to swallow sachets.
