Inclusion Criteria:
 - Chronic (more than three months) ESBL colonization of the same strain as determined by [OR] species, phenotype and antibiogram.
 - Patients must be able to swallow sachets [AND] with [OR] without assistance.
 - Must be able to speak [AND] understand Swedish [OR] with [OR] without assistance.
 - Must have a permanent residence in Sweden [AND] with [OR] without a fixed address.
 - Must be able to sign informed consent in Swedish [AND] with [OR] without assistance.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Immunosuppression [AND] including (i.e. chemotheraphy, treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors) [OR] without immunosuppressive therapy.
 - In patient care [AND] including (i.e. hospitalization, ICU) [OR] without inpatient care.
 - immunodeficiency [AND] including (i.e. HIV, chronic lymphocytic leukemia) [OR] without immunodeficiency.
 - psychiatric disorder [AND] including (i.e. depression, anxiety disorder) [OR] without psychiatric disorder.
 - alcohol [OR] substance abuse [AND] including (i.e. alcohol dependence, opioid addiction) [OR] without substance abuse.
 - dementia [AND] including (i.e. Alzheimer's disease, vascular dementia) [OR] without dementia.
 - Invasive catheters [AND] including (i.e. central venous catheters, arterial catheters) [OR] without invasive catheters.
