Inclusion Criteria:
 - Chronic (more than three months) ESBL colonization of the same strain [OR] as determined by species, phenotype and antibiogram.
 - Patients [AND] must be able to swallow sachets.
 - Must [AND] be able to speak and understand Swedish.
 - Must [AND] have a permanent residence in Sweden.
 - Must [AND] be able to sign informed consent in Swedish.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Immunosuppression [NOT] (i.e. chemotheraphy, treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors).
 - In patient care [OR] immunodeficiency.
 - Psychiatric disorder [OR] alcohol [OR] substance abuse.
 - Dementia [OR] invasive catheters.
