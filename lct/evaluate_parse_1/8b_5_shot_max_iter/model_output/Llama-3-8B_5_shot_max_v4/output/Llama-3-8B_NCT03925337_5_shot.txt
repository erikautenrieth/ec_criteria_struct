 - Patients [AND] age: ≥ 22 years [OR]

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Minors [AND] aged < 22 years [NOT]
 - People [AND] with diminished cognitive capacity [NOT]
 - Patients undergoing diagnostic colonoscopy (e.g. as an evaluation for active gastrointestinal bleed, [OR] referring collectively to the stomach [AND] and the small [AND] and large intestine).
