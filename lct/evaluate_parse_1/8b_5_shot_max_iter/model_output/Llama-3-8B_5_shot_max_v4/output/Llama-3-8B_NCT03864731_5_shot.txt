Inclusion Criteria:
 - Unilateral [OR] and/or bilateral conductive hearing loss (CHL)
 - Conductive hearing loss >10 decibel [OR] on average
 - Subjective benefit from the device
 - Subjects aged 13 years [AND] or older
 - Capable of the German language
 - Willingness [AND] and ability to perform all tests required for the study
 - Signed, [AND] dated informed consent before the start of any study specific procedure
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding
 - Patient uses a hearing aid [OR] plans to acquire a hearing aid
 - Patient is intolerant of the materials as described by Manufacturer [NOT]
 - Patient presents with a skin [OR] scalp condition that may preclude the attachment of the adhesive adapter [NOT]
 - Patient cannot perform the audiological tests [OR] is unable to fill out the questionnaires [NOT]
 - Patient presents with retrocochlear [OR] or central auditory disorder [NOT]
 - any physical [OR] psychological [OR] or emotional disorder that would interfere with the ability to perform on test and rehabilitation procedures [NOT]
