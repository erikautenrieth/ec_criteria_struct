<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Adults [AND] determined to be candidates for venous sinus stenting

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients who are [NOT] candidates for venous sinus stenting
  - Patients with a history of bleeding disorders or taking anticoagulant medication
  - Patients with a history of stroke or transient ischemic attack (TIA) within the past 3 months
  - Patients with a history of bleeding in the past 6 months
  - Patients with a history of head or neck surgery or trauma within the past 6 months
  - Patients with a history of intracranial tumor or abscess
  - Patients with a history of arteriovenous malformation (AVM) or fistula
  - Patients with a history of moyamoya disease or syndrome
  - Patients with a history of vasculitis or inflammatory conditions affecting the cerebral vasculature
  - Patients with a history of significant cardiac disease or cardiac arrhythmias
  - Patients with a history of significant pulmonary disease or respiratory failure
  - Patients with a history of significant liver or kidney disease
  - Patients with a history of significant neurological disease or disability
  - Patients who are [NOT] able to provide informed consent
  - Patients who are [NOT] able to tolerate the procedure or post-procedure care

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
