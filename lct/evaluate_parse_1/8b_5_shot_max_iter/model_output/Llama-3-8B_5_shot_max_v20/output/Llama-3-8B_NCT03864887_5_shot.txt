Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who have had brain death [OR] or serious brain trauma
 - Healthy people [AND] who are interested in optical assessment of hemodynamic parameters
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who are in pregnancy [OR] have plan to conception
 - Patients who have vertebra surgery [OR] have plan of surgery
 - Patients who are [NOT] inappropriate to join this trial judged by the radiologists [OR] specialists
       - AIDS [OR] Active Hepatitis [OR] Tuberculosis [OR] Syphilis
       - Patients who regularly take anticoagulants [OR] antiplatelet drugs
