Inclusion Criteria:
 - Male [OR] female ≥ 18 years
 - Patient [AND] for whom a vascular surgery is programmed, [AND] whose nature allows obtaining of arterial segment without any harm for the health of the patient
 - Patient [AND] for whom a blood sample is planned on the day of the procedure
 - Person [AND] who has received complete information on the organization of the research [AND] who has not objected to his participation [AND] and the exploitation of his data
 - Person [AND] who has compulsory affiliation to social security

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patient [AND] who has previously undergone radiotherapy at the sampling site [NOT]
 - Patient [AND] with cancer at the sampling site [NOT]
