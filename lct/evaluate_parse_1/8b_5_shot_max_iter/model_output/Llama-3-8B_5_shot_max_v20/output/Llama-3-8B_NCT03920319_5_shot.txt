Inclusion Criteria:
 - Treatment-seeking cigarette smoker
 - Meet DSM-IV criteria for nicotine dependence
 - Negative result on urine test for drug use during screening
Exclusion Criteria:
 - History of any Axis I psychiatric diagnosis [NOT] other than nicotine dependence
 - Medical conditions that might [NOT] affect brain function
 - Current use of medications that could [NOT] alter brain function
 - Pregnancy
 - Current illicit drug use [OR] other than occasional use of marijuana
