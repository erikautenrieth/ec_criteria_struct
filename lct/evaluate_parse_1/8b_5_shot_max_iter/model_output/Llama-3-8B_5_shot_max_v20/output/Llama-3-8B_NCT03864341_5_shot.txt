Inclusion Criteria:
 - Be a patient in the Bellevue Hospital Center ED [AND] 
 - Be ≥18 years old [AND] 
 - Screen positive for homelessness risk using a homelessness risk screening tool (HRST) [AND] 
 - Screen positive for unhealthy alcohol or drug use [AND] 
 - Able to provide consent

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Medically [NOT] unstable (e.g., critically ill) [OR] psychiatrically unstable
 - Unable to provide informed consent for other reason (e.g., cognitive deficit, profound intoxication)
 - Incarcerated [OR] in police custody
 - Unable to understand [AND] speak English
 - Lives outside NYC [AND] cannot give a NYC ZIP code
 - Already homeless (residing in a shelter [OR] on the streets)
 - Does not have a telephone where [NOT] can be reached for follow-up
 - Has already received specialized peer navigator/addiction social worker services during current ED visit
 - Has already participated in the study
