Inclusion Criteria:
 - Aged 18 [AND] and above
 - Having the ability [AND] to learn the standard toothbrushing method with artificial intelligence powered toothbrush
 - Ability [AND] and willingness to give written informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Edentulous mouth
 - Pregnant [NOT] females
 - Having received professional periodontal treatment (other than supragingival cleaning) within the previous 12 months [OR] having received antibiotic medication within the previous 3 months
