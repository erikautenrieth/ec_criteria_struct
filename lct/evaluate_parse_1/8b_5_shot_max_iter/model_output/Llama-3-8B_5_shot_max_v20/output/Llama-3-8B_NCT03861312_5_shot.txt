Inclusion Criteria:
 - The inclusion criteria were as follows: a) men [AND] and women aged between 18 [AND] and 65-years [OR] b) medical diagnosis of Non-Specific Chronic Neck Pain [AND] with more than 6 months of evolution of neck pain.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Exclusion criteria included the following: a) patients [AND] with rheumatic diseases [OR] cervical hernia [OR] cervical whiplash syndrome [OR] neck surgeries [OR] history of arthrodesis [OR] b) systemic diseases [OR] c) vision [OR] hearing [OR] or vestibular problems [OR] d) Severe trauma [OR] or traffic accident which has had an impact on the cervical area [NOT].
