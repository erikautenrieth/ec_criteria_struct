Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients diagnosed with Hunter syndrome
 - Male at the age of ≥ 5 [AND] with adequate abilities (including 6-MWT) to participate in this study in the opinion of the investigator.
 - Voluntarily signed written informed consent to participation in this study [AND] consent to contraception.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Prior treatment with iduronate-2-sulfatase ERT [OR] history of bronchotomy, bone marrow transplantation, or cord blood transplantation.
 - Known hypersensitivity reactions to any of the components of the investigational product [OR] prior or planned administration of other investigational products within 30 days before treatment with the investigational product in this study or during this study.
 - Unable to perform 6-MWT [OR] female.
