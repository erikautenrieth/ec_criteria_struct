Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with recent stroke/TIA [AND] and severe stenosis of intracranial ICA [OR] or MCA [AND] and impaired CVR within previous three months [AND] but not before 3 weeks after acute stroke [NOT] to differentiate between patients with a long-standing fixed-stenosis from patients with partially recanalized intracranial artery (masquerading as severe stenosis)
 - Age >21 years
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with atrial fibrillation/ [OR] arrhythmias
 - Within 2 weeks of cardiac catheterization [OR] or arterial puncture at femoral puncture site
 - Decompensated heart failure [NOT] usually class 3 [OR] or 4
 - LV EF <30%
 - Moderate [OR] or severe AR
 - Persistent [AND] and uncontrolled hypertension (BP persistently >160/100 mmHg)
 - Bleeding diathesis
 - Active thrombophlebitis/ [OR] venous disease of lower limbs
 - Severe lower extremity vaso-occlusive disease
 - Presence of a documented aortic aneurysm/ [OR] dissection requiring surgical repair
 - Pregnancy
