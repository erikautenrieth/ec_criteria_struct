Inclusion Criteria:
 - Glomerular filtration rate [AND] less than 30 ml / min / 1.73m
 - Without replacement therapy [OR] (dialysis [OR] or hemodialysis)
 - Volume overload
 - At least 100 ml per day of residual diuresis
 - Use of a loop diuretic for at least one month [AND]
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Allergies [NOT] known to diuretics
 - Patients with severe infections
 - Patients with hemodynamic instability
 - Amputees
 - Patients with cognitive impairment
 - Patients with acute renal failure
 - Patients with graft loss
