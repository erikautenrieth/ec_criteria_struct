Inclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers:
 - Over 18 years of age [AND] with no exclusion criteria
Neonates:
  - Aged between 1h of life [OR] and 28 days of life [AND] with no exclusion criteria
  - Born beyond 33 Week of Amenorrhea + 1day [AND] and 2kg of birth weight [AND] with no exclusion criteria
  - Hospitalized in the medical-surgical centre of Pediatrics of the hospital of Strasbourg [AND] with no exclusion criteria
  - For whom a blood sample was prescribed as part of their routine care [AND] with no exclusion criteria

Exclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers:
  - Systemic inflammatory disease [OR] transient inflammatory state [OR] any drug that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h preceding the sampling [OR] any other exclusion criteria
Neonates:
  - Risk of anemia [NOT] less than 7g/DL [AND] current anticoagulant treatment [OR] any other exclusion criteria
