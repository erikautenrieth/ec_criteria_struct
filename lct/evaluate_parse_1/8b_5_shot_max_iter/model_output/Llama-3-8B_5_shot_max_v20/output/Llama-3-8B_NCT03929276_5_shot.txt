Inclusion Criteria:
 - Subjects with adhesive capsulitis diagnosis by physical examination [AND] (Compared with intact side, there is more than 25% limitation in at least two directions of shoulder range of motion)
 - Subjects have complaints for at least 1 month [AND] no pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding
Exclusion Criteria:
 - History of bilateral adhesive capsulitis
 - History of shoulder trauma [OR] fracture [OR] shoulder surgery [OR] calcific tendinopathy [OR] glenohumeral osteoarthritis [OR] inflammatory rheumatic diseases [OR] tumor [OR] infection
 - History of corticosteroid injection in the shoulder during the last 3 months [AND] no [NOT] recent lung, breast or bypass surgery
 - History of recent lung, breast or bypass surgery [OR] history of cervical radiculopathy [OR] brachial plexus lesion
 - History of neuromuscular disease [AND] no history of physical therapy program for the same shoulder in the last 6 months
