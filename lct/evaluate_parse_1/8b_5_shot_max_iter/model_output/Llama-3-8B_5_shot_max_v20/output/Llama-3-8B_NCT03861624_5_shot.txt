Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Skeletal maturity [AND] 
  2. AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures [OR] 
  3. Wound primarily closeable [AND] (no flap [OR] or delay in closure due to contamination needed)

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Current injury is a [NOT] pathologic fracture

  4. Sustained severe [NOT] Traumatic Brain Injury (GCS<12) ***
  5. Sustained severe [NOT] spinal cord injury (lower extremity paresis/paralysis)
  6. Sustained severe [NOT] burns (>10% total body surface area (TBSA) [OR] or >5% TBSA with full thickness [OR] or circumferential injury)
  7. Prior [NOT] ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery
  8. Prior [NOT] or current lower limb deformity [OR] abnormality
