Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age between 25 years [AND] and 60 years
 - For the volunteers with venous insufficiency: clearly visual venous insufficiency [OR] (CEAP 1 [AND] and 2)
 - BMI between 24 [AND] and 30
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Volunteers [AND] using medication [NOT] (exception for contraception)
 - Volunteers [AND] having comorbidities
 - Pregnant women
 - Women [AND] who menstruate on the test date
 - Volunteers [AND] with an implanted electrical device (IPG, Pacemaker)
