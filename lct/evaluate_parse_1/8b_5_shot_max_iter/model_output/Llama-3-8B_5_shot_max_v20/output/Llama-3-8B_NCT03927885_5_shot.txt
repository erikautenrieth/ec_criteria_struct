Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient with a diagnosis of advanced cancer (metastatic [OR] or recurrent incurable solid tumors)
 - Presence of fatigue of >= 4/10 on Edmonton Symptom Assessment System (ESAS) Fatigue item (0-10 severity scale)
 - Patient should describe fatigue as being present for a minimum of 2 weeks prior to screening [AND] with a diagnosis of advanced cancer (metastatic [OR] or recurrent incurable solid tumors)
 - Uncontrolled pain; patient is on opioids for the treatment of cancer pain, he/she must have had no major dose change (> 25%) for at least 48 hours prior to study entry [AND] with a diagnosis of advanced cancer (metastatic [OR] or recurrent incurable solid tumors)
 - Patient must be willing to engage in telephone follow up with research staff [AND] with a diagnosis of advanced cancer (metastatic [OR] or recurrent incurable solid tumors)
 - Patient must have telephone access to be contacted by the research staff [AND] with a diagnosis of advanced cancer (metastatic [OR] or recurrent incurable solid tumors)
 - Hemoglobin level of >= 9 g/dL [AND] with a diagnosis of advanced cancer (metastatic [OR] or recurrent incurable solid tumors). Patient may receive packed red blood cell (PRBC) transfusion so as to have hemoglobin level of >= 9 g/dL so at participate in the study
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Surgery, [OR] pain relieving procedures within 2 weeks of entry into the study [OR] during the study period
 - Patients with history of substance abuse (Cut down, Annoyed, Guilty, Eye opener [CAGE] >= 2+) [AND] cognitively impaired (MD Anderson Symptom [MDAS] > 7)
