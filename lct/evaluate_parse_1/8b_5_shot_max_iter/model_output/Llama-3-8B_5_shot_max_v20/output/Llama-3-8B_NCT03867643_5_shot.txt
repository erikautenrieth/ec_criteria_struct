Inclusion Criteria:
 - Children who are 6-12 years old [AND] in Chongqing
 - After birth, three injections of Hep B were inoculated in time according to the procedure of 0, 1 and 6 months [AND] (The vaccination certificate record)
 - No HBV booster vaccine given since primary HBV immunization
 - Children [AND] or guardian knew and agreed to accept the study
 - Follow - up study [AND] and blood collection after vaccination can be accepted
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Unwilling [OR] impossible to participate in this research project;
 - Have a history of allergy [OR] have had a serious vaccine reaction
 - Have immune impairment diseases [AND] appear immune impairment;
 - Immunosuppressive treatment [OR] receive any injection [OR] or oral administration of cortisone [OR] or cancer chemotherapy;
 - Any kind of vaccine [OR] or any kind of observation drug has been vaccinated in the past four weeks;
 - Any acute disease [OR] or other infection requiring antibiotic [OR] or antiviral treatment in the past four weeks;
 - Fever symptoms occurred in the past week [AND] (axillary temperature ≥ 38 ℃)
 - Blood transfusion experience;
 - Has a history of more serious infectious diseases [AND] (five types of hepatitis, AIDS, syphilis, gonorrhea, etc.);
 - Hepatitis B infection [OR] carriers (lineal relationship);
 - Abnormal physical examination
