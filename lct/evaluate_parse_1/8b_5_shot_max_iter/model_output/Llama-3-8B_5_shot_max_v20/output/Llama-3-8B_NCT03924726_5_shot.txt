Inclusion Criteria:
 - Aged 18 years [AND] and above at the start of the treatment.
 - Molar relationship either Class I [OR] < ½ unit Class II [OR] or Class III.
 - Extraction of all four first premolar teeth [AND] as part of orthodontic treatment.
 - Maximum anchorage required [AND] using mini-implant.
 - No [NOT] systemic disease.
 - Good [AND] oral hygiene.
 - No [NOT] periodontal disease.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Significant [NOT] vertical skeletal discrepancies.
 - Systemic diseases requiring long-term antibiotic use [OR] phenytoin [OR] cyclosporin [OR] anti-inflammatory drugs [OR] bisphosphonates [OR] systemic corticosteroids [OR] calcium channel blockers.
 - Poor [NOT] oral hygiene.
 - Active [NOT] periodontal disease.
