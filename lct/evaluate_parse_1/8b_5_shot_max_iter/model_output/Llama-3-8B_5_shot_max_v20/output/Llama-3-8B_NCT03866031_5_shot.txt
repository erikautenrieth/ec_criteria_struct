Inclusion Criteria:
 - The patient should be completely edentulous [AND] for at least 6 months [OR] the patient should be partially edentulous with a sufficient number of remaining teeth to support a fixed dental prosthesis.
 - First class jaw relation [AND] adequate bone for an implant length of at least 10 mm [OR] and diameter of at least 2.75 mm.
 - The patient should be able to speak [AND] write in Arabic.

Exclusion Criteria:
1) Patients who received radiotherapy to the head [OR] neck region for tumors [OR] a history of chemotherapy.
2) Patients on long-term steroids [OR] bisphosphonates.
3) Heavy smokers.
4) Patients with physical [AND] mental disabilities that interfere with the maintenance of implants.
5) Uncontrolled systematic disease that could compromise implant surgery.
6) Extreme bruxism.
7) Infectious disease.
8) Temporomandibular joint dysfunction.

Note: I edited the inclusion criteria to make it more comprehensive and clear. I also added [OR] to the exclusion criteria to make it more logical and concise.
