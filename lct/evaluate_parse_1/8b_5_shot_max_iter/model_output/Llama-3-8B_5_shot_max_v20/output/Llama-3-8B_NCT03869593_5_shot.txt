Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient with S. aureus [OR] or E. coli bacteriostatic bacteremia defined by at least one positive blood culture bottle
 - Patient [AND] requiring a blood test as part of his bacteremia
 - Patient [NOT] subject to a judicial protection measure
 - Signature of the non-opposition of consent (for minor patients signed by one of the parents [OR] the representative of the parental authority)
 - Affiliation to social security
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Immunocompromised patient defined by: [OR]
 - Current immunosuppressive therapies: corticosteroids, [OR] chemotherapy, [OR] biotherapy
 - Solid organ transplant patient [OR] or hematopoietic stem cell transplant
 - Chemotherapy-induced neutropenia
 - Congenital immune deficiency
 - Bacteremia related to a peripheral [OR] or central catheter
 - Urinary obstruction [NOT] lifted within the first 24 hours of management
 - Intra-abdominal infection collected undrained in the first 24 hours of management
 - Primary infectious focus represented by mechanically ventilated pneumonia
 - Pregnant [OR] or lactating woman
