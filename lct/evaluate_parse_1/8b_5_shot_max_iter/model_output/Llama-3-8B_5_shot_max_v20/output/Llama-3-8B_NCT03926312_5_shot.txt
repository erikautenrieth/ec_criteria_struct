Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. Signed informed consent with the study [AND] 
 2. Men [OR] women >18 years of age [AND] 
 3. Patients ≥1 [AND] ≤6 months after type I myocardial infarction [AND] 
 4. Physical inactivity before MI defined as the absence of moderate to vigorous exercise for at least 30 minutes [OR] 5 or more days a week
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. Heart failure NYHA IIIB-IV [NOT] 
 2. Planned coronary revascularization [OR] 
 3. Planned major surgery within the next 12 months [OR] 
 4. Inability to walk for any reason [OR] 
 5. Comorbidities that would preclude adherence to the rehabilitation program (e.g. arthrosis, [OR] active malignancy, [OR] major depression [OR] or other significant psychiatric disorder, [OR] cognitive impairment) - at the discretion of the physician [OR] 
 6. Life expectancy less than 12 months [OR] 
 7. Pregnancy [OR] 
 8. Inability to operate the smart-watch [OR] 
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
