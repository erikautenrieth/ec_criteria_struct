<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Type 2 diabetes mellitus [AND] on metformin therapy for at least 3 months

Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] Type 1 diabetes mellitus
 - [NOT] Uncontrolled hypertension
 - [NOT] Uncontrolled hyperlipidemia
 - [NOT] Kidney disease (estimated glomerular filtration rate < 30 mL/min)
 - [NOT] Active liver disease
 - [NOT] History of pancreatitis
 - [NOT] History of gastrointestinal surgery
 - [NOT] Current use of medications that may affect pancreatic function (e.g. sulfonamides, thiazides, beta-blockers)
 - [NOT] Presence of pancreatic cysts or tumors
 - [NOT] Pancreatitis or other pancreatic diseases
 - [NOT] History of pancreatitis or other pancreatic diseases
 - [NOT] Family history of pancreatitis or other pancreatic diseases
 - [NOT] Genetic syndromes associated with pancreatic disease (e.g. cystic fibrosis, familial adenomatous polyposis)
 - [NOT] Other medical conditions that may affect pancreatic function (e.g. chronic pancreatitis, pancreatic pseudocyst)
 - [NOT] Pregnancy or lactation
 - [NOT] Unable to provide informed consent
