Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients evaluated for residual or recurrent gliomas after initial tumor surgery, external beam radiation [OR] and/or chemotherapy with temozolamide.
 - Male [AND] and female
 - Ages 18 [TO] 70
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patient diagnosis with glioma [NOT] before surgery, external beam radiation [OR] and/or chemotherapy with temozolamide.
 - Women [AND] who were pregnant [OR] breast feeding [OR] or possibly pregnant.
 - Patients [AND] with hepatic [OR] renal dysfunction.
 - Patients [AND] with MRI contraindications (i.e. pacemakers [OR] non-MR compatible devices).
 - Patients [AND] with a history of drug hypersensitivity to 18F-Fluciclovine.
