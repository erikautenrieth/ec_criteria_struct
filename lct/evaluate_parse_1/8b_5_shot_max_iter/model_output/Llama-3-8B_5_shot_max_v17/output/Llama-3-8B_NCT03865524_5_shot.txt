Inclusion Criteria:
 - Men [AND] and women over 18 years old [AND] with written informed consent according to ICH / GCP [OR] and Spanish legislation, obtained before any study procedure [AND] with a BMI equal to or greater than 30 Kg / m2 [AND] patients with clinical [AND] radiological criteria of knee osteoarthritis [AND] patients with indication for primary TKA according to medical criteria.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnancy [OR] lactation [OR] inability to give informed consent in the absence of a legal representative [OR] subjects that are participating in a study with medicines [OR] other medical devices [OR] those who show inability to follow the instructions [OR] collaborate during the development of the study [OR] if in the opinion of the researcher there are findings in the physical examination [OR] abnormalities in the results of the clinical analyzes [OR] other medical, social [OR] or psychosocial factors that could have a negative influence [OR] having had a previous surgical procedure on [OR] around the knee in the last 12 months [OR] need a simultaneous bilateral TKA [OR] have a varus angle [OR] valgus ≥ 15 ° [OR] total revision knee arthroplasty.
