Inclusion Criteria:
 - > 50 years old [AND] 
 - Bilateral age-related cataract [AND] 
 - Qualify for bilateral implantation within 1 month [AND] 
 - No previous refractive or ocular surgery [AND] 
 - Available IOL dioptre range: 18 to 27D [AND] 
 - Expected postoperative astigmatism ≤ 0.75D [AND] 

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pupil > 4 mm [OR] 
 - Occupation requiring night-time driving [OR] or any occupation incompatible with multifocality [OR] 
 - Acute or chronic disease or illness that would increase risk or confound study results [e.g. [NOT] diabetes mellitus (with retinopathy), [NOT] immunocompromised, [NOT] glaucoma, [NOT] epiretinal membrane, [NOT] cornea guttata, etc….] found to be relevant by the investigators [OR] 
 - Amblyopia [OR] strabismus [OR] 
 - Extremely shallow anterior chamber [OR] 
 - Capsule or zonular abnormalities that may affect postoperative centration or tilt of the lens [e.g. pseudoexfoliation syndrome, [NOT] uveitis, [NOT] Marfan's syndrome [OR] 
 - Pupil abnormalities [NOT] non-reactive, [NOT] tonic pupils, [NOT] abnormally shaped pupils or pupils that do not dilate under mesopic/ scotopic conditions [OR] 
 - Instability to place the intraocular lens safely at the location planned [OR] 
 - Pregnancy [NOT] (pregnancy test will be taken pre-operatively in women of reproductive age)
