Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patient with a diagnosis of advanced cancer [AND] (metastatic [OR] or recurrent incurable solid tumors)
  - Presence of fatigue [AND] of >= 4/10 on Edmonton Symptom Assessment System (ESAS) Fatigue item [AND] (0-10 severity scale)
  - Patient [AND] should describe fatigue [AND] as being present for a minimum of 2 weeks prior to screening
  - Uncontrolled pain [AND] patient is on opioids for the treatment of cancer pain [AND] he/she must have had no major dose change (> 25%) for at least 48 hours prior to study entry [AND] Change in opioid dose after study entry is allowed
  - Patient [AND] must be willing to engage in telephone follow up with research staff
  - Patient [AND] must have telephone access [AND] to be contacted by the research staff
  - Hemoglobin level [AND] of >= 9 g/dL [AND] patient may receive packed red blood cell (PRBC) transfusion [AND] so as to have hemoglobin level of >= 9 g/dL [AND] so at participate in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Surgery [AND] or pain relieving procedures [AND] within 2 weeks of entry into the study [OR] during the study period
  - Patients [AND] with history of substance abuse [AND] (Cut down [OR] Annoyed [OR] Guilty [OR] Eye opener [CAGE] >= 2+) [AND] cognitively impaired [AND] (MD Anderson Symptom [MDAS] > 7)
