Inclusion Criteria:
  - Type 2 diabetes mellitus [AND] on metformin therapy for at least 3 months

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Those [OR] reporting any chronic or acute diseases, food allergies, smoking or alcohol abuse
  - Use of illicit drugs [OR] regular consumption of medications, micronutrient supplements, or both
  - Vegetarians [OR] or those [NOT] able to eat meat, are also excluded since the foods for the study contain meat
  - Unable [NOT] to refrain from taking medications during the study period
