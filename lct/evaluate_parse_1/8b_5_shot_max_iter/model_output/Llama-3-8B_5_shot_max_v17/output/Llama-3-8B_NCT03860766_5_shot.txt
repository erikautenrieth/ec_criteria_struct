  -  Handball players [AND] apparently healthy;

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] musculoskeletal injury in the last three months;

  -  [NOT] use of anabolic, anti-inflammatory and analgesic drugs 72 hours before the evaluations;
  -  [NOT] use of alcohol and illicit drugs during the collection period;
