<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
 1. The fifth stage of chronic renal failure diagnosed by a physician [AND] with the physician advising and informing the patient who has to undergo renal replacement therapy but has not yet decided what treatment to take.
 2. Ability to communicate in Mandarin [OR] Taiwan.
 3. Age over 20 years old.

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Cognitive impairment: MMSE score < 21 points [OR] less than 16 points for those who are not educated.
 2. Patients who have received non-emergency dialysis treatment [OR] have undergone dialysis treatment in the past.
 3. Patients with severe cardiopulmonary dysfunction [OR] severe shock, low blood pressure, and large bleeding that is not controlled.
 4. Patients with chronic active hepatitis [OR] cirrhosis [OR] liver failure.
 5. A history of cancer that may be transferred [OR] has a history of cancer.
 6. Severe bleeding tendency [OR] has a bleeding disorder.
 7. Loss of peritoneal function [OR] extensive peritoneal adhesion, unable to use peritoneal dialysis.
 8. Unable to correct physiological defects [OR] may interfere with peritoneal dialysis, or increase the chance of infection, such as umbilical hernia.
 9. The patient is unable to operate the fluid change [OR] hand shake, and no family members can assist.
 10. Patients who are blind [OR] have poor eyesight and have difficulty operating.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
