Inclusion Criteria:
1. Diagnosed with Myasthenia Gravis [AND] at least 6 months (180 days) prior to the date of the Screening Visit as confirmed by specific criteria.
2. Myasthenia Gravis Foundation of America Clinical Classification Class [OR] II [OR] III [OR] IV at screening.
3. MG-ADL profile must be [AND] ≥ 6 at screening [AND] randomization (Day 1).
4. Vaccinated against meningococcal infections [AND] within 3 years prior to, [OR] or at the time of, initiating study drug to reduce the risk of meningococcal infection (N meningitidis).

Exclusion Criteria:
Medical Conditions
1. Any active [OR] untreated thymoma. History of thymic carcinoma [OR] thymic malignancy unless deemed cured by adequate treatment with no evidence of recurrence for ≥ 5 years before screening.
2. History of thymectomy within the 12 months prior to screening [AND] not deemed cured by adequate treatment with no evidence of recurrence for ≥ 5 years before screening.
3. History of N meningitidis infection [OR] meningococcal disease.
4. Use of the following within the time period specified below:
    - IV immunoglobulin within 4 weeks of randomization [AND] not deemed necessary for treatment of Myasthenia Gravis.
    - Use of plasma exchange within 4 weeks of randomization [AND] not deemed necessary for treatment of Myasthenia Gravis.
    - Use of rituximab within 6 months of screening [AND] not deemed necessary for treatment of Myasthenia Gravis.
5. Participants who have received previous treatment with complement inhibitors (for example, eculizumab) [AND] not deemed necessary for treatment of Myasthenia Gravis.
