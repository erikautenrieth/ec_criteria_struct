Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with hypertension [AND] taking antihypertensive drugs more than 1 year

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Secondary hypertension [OR] hypotension
 - Hypertensive emergency [OR] urgency [OR] crisis
 - Severe heart failure (NYHA III [OR] IV)
 - Angina with 6 months [OR] less
 - Myocardial infarction within 6 months [OR] less
 - Peripheral artery disease within 6 months [OR] less
 - Significant arrhythmia (i.e. ventricular tachycardia [OR] fibrillation, atrial fibrillation, atrial flutter)
 - Pregnancy [OR] lactation
 - Night labor [OR] shift worker
 - Those who are currently participating in other clinical studies [OR] trials
 - Those who have taken other clinical trial drugs within the past month [OR] year
 - According to the opinion of the researcher, those who have [NOT] or may have a disease that may interfere with the completion of the study
 - History of drug [OR] alcohol dependence within 6 months [OR] year
