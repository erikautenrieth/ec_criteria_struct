Inclusion Criteria:
 - ASA I-III [AND] male and female patients aged between 18 [OR] to 80 years old, who will receive spinal anesthesia for surgeries lasting less than one hour.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] younger than 18 years old.
 - ASA physical status >3.
 - Immunosuppression [OR] or high risk of infection.
 - Contraindications to receiving spinal anesthesia (e.g. coagulation impairment) [OR] patients with psychosis [OR] patients with preexisting back pain [OR] patients with cognitive impairment [OR] allergies to local anesthetics, analgesics [OR] or any medication used in the study.
 - Patients with chronic regular opioid usage [OR] presence of preexisting neurological symptoms.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
