Inclusion Criteria (PTA group)
 - T1D patients [AND] who received their first PTA [OR] second PTA only if their first graft was explanted within a week [AND] between 2 January 2005 and 31 December 2017
 - age ≥18 [AND] and < 65 years
 - willingness to participate in this observational study

Inclusion Criteria (control group)
 - Type 1 diabetes
 - age ≥18 years [AND] and < 65 years
 - willingness to participate in this observational study
 - minimum disease duration of 5 years [AND] 
 - brittle diabetes, as defined by the occurrence of frequent acute complications [OR] (ketoacidosis or severe hypoglycaemic episodes requiring third-part assistance or unawareness despite well-conducted intensive insulin therapy)
 - presence of at least one diabetes-related complication [OR] (neuropathy, retinopathy, subclinical nephropathy)

Exclusion Criteria (both groups)
 - conditions other than T1D known to affect bone health [AND] (i.e., non-compensated hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, hyperparathyroidism, inflammatory bowel disease, malignancy, rheumatoid arthritis, hypogonadism, severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, hepatic insufficiency, alcohol intake ≥ 3 units/day [OR] 
 - use of drugs that can affect bone metabolism [AND] (e.g., bisphosphonates, anticonvulsants, hormone replacement therapy) [OR] other than glucocorticoids [AND] and immunosuppressants
 - recipients of simultaneous pancreas kidney transplantation, pancreatic islet transplantation, multiple PTAs, other transplants
 - BMI [NOT] <18.5 [OR] ≥ 30 kg/m2
