Inclusion Criteria:
 - Prescription of a Narval CC mandibular advancement device [AND] 
 - Age ≥ 18 years [AND] 
 - Ability to understand the study information [AND] and information on usage of personal data [AND] 
 - Signed and dated informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] with central sleep apnea (central AI > 5/hour) [OR] 
 - Patients [AND] with loose teeth [AND] and severe parodontitis [OR] 
 - Patients [AND] with completely missing teeth [OR] removable tooth replacement [OR] or with a teeth health insufficient to retain an MAD [OR] 
 - Patients [AND] with missing molars [OR] 
 - Patients [AND] with maximum protrusion of [NOT] less than 5mm
