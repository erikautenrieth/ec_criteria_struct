Inclusion Criteria:
 1. The patient [AND] should be completely edentulous for at least 6 months [OR] and able to speak and write in Arabic.
 2. First class jaw relation.
 3. Adequate bone for an implant length of at least 10 mm [AND] diameter of at least 2.75 mm.

Exclusion Criteria:
1) Patients [AND] who received radiotherapy to the head or neck region for tumors [OR] a history of chemotherapy.
2) Patients on long-term steroids [OR] bisphosphonates.
3) Heavy smokers.
4) Patients with physical [AND] mental disabilities that interfere with the maintenance of implants.
5) Uncontrolled systematic disease that could compromise implant surgery.
6) Extreme bruxism.
7) Infectious disease.
8) Temporomandibular joint dysfunction.
