<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients with recent stroke/TIA [AND] severe stenosis of intracranial ICA [OR] MCA [AND] impaired CVR within previous three months [AND] not before 3 weeks after acute stroke. This is to differentiate between patients with a long-standing fixed-stenosis [AND] from patients with partially recanalized intracranial artery (masquerading as severe stenosis) [OR].
 2. Age >21 years

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients with atrial fibrillation/arrhythmias [OR].
 2. Within 2 weeks of cardiac catheterization [OR] arterial puncture at femoral puncture site [OR].
 3. Decompensated heart failure [AND] usually class 3 [OR] 4 [OR].
 4. LV EF <30% [OR].
 5. Moderate [OR] severe AR [OR].
 6. Persistent [AND] uncontrolled hypertension (BP persistently >160/100 mmHg) [OR].
 7. Bleeding diathesis [OR].
 8. Active thrombophlebitis/venous disease of lower limbs [OR].
 9. Severe lower extremity vaso-occlusive disease [OR].
 10. Presence of a documented aortic aneurysm/dissection requiring surgical repair [OR].
 11. Pregnancy [OR].
