Inclusion Criteria:
 - Receiving HHD [OR] ICHDF for more than 3 months [AND] less than 36 months.
 - Haemoglobin [AND] equal to or greater than 100 g/L at enrolment.
 - Willing [AND] physically able to undertake the study assessments/tests.
 - Willing to provide blood for storage [AND] future analysis.
 - Able to give informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Living donor transplant [OR] change to peritoneal dialysis planned.
 - Physical assessments contraindicated for the following clinical reasons:
   - [NOT] Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS) within the last 3 months (chest pain, ECG changes or typical biomarker elevation).
   - [NOT] Any current uncontrolled cardiac dysrhythmias causing symptoms (chest pain, palpitations, syncope or dizziness).
   - [NOT] Symptomatic aortic stenosis.
   - [NOT] New York Heart Association grade IV Heart failure.
   - [NOT] Severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.
   - [NOT] Acute pulmonary embolus or pulmonary infarction in the last 3 months.
   - [NOT] Current acute myocarditis or pericarditis.
   - [NOT] Suspected or known dissecting aneurysm.
   - [NOT] Acute systemic infection, accompanied by fever, body aches or swollen lymph glands.
 - Pregnancy.
 - Life expectancy of [NOT] less than twelve months.
