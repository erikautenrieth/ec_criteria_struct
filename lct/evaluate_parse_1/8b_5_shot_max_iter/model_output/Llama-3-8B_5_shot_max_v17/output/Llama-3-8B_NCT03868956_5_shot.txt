Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age [AND] >= 18 years
 - Known history of objectively documented deep vein thrombosis of the lower limb [OR] with [OR] without pulmonary embolism
 - Out-patients [AND] referred for clinically suspected acute recurrent ipsilateral DVT of the lower limb [OR] i.e. the occurrence of new symptoms and signs of DVT [OR] or the increase of symptoms and signs in patients with post-thrombotic syndrome
 - Patients [AND] covered by social security [OR] or equivalent regimen
 - Signed and dated informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Known current [NOT] pregnancy
 - Any condition [AND] which, in the opinion of the investigator, may prevent him from performing the colour doppler ultrasound test [OR] plaster cast [OR] inaccessible vein segment after abdominal [OR] pelvic surgery [OR] or other causes that may lead to a technically inadequate CDUS
 - Delay from onset of symptoms to inclusion of more than 10 [OR] days
 - Therapeutic anticoagulation for more than 48 [OR] hours in the two days prior to consent
 - Prophylactic anticoagulation for more than 48 [OR] hours in the two days prior to consent
 - Clinical symptoms of pulmonary embolism
 - Life expectancy less than 3 [OR] months
 - Patient [AND] unable to adhere to protocol [OR] follow-up visits and contacts
 - Participants under legal guardianship [OR] incapacitation
 - Patient already enrolled in a deep vein thrombosis (DVT) diagnostic research
