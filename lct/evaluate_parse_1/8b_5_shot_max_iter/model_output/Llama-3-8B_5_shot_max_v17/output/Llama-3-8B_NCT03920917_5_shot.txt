Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient [AND] with paroxysmal atrial fibrillation [OR] who is scheduled for ablation procedure [AND] ≥20 [AND] ≤80 years of age
 - Left atrium size [AND] < 45mm
 - Paroxysmal atrial fibrillation [AND] that is recurrence during antiarrhythmic drug treatment [OR] is not able to use an antiarrhythmic drug.
 - Patient [AND] who is indicated for anticoagulation therapy [AND] (for prevention of cerebral infarction)

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] with persistent [OR] permanent atrial fibrillation
 - Atrial fibrillation [AND] associated with severe cardiac malformation [OR] a structural heart disease that is hemodynamically affected
 - Patients [AND] with severe renal impairment [OR] CT imaging difficulty using contrast media
 - Patients [AND] with a past history of radiofrequency ablation for atrial fibrillation [OR] other cardiac surgery
 - Patients [AND] with active internal bleeding
 - Patients [AND] with contraindications for anticoagulation therapy [AND] (for prevention of cerebral infarction) [AND] antiarrhythmic drugs
 - Patients [AND] with valvular atrial fibrillation [AND] (mitral stenosis >grade 2, mechanical valve, mitral valvuloplasty)
 - Patients [AND] with a severe comorbid disease
 - Patients [AND] with expected survival < 1 year
 - Patients [AND] who are drug addicts [OR] alcoholics
 - Patients [AND] who cannot read the consent form [AND] (illiterates, foreigners, etc.)
 - Other patients [AND] who are judged by the principal or sub-investigator to be ineligible for participation in this clinical study
