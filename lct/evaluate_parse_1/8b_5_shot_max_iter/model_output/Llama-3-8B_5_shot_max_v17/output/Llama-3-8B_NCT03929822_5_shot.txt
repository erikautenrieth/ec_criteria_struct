Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
  - Women [AND] called back from a screening mammography by either FFDM [OR] tomosynthesis with soft tissue abnormalities including masses, [OR] asymmetries, [OR] focal asymmetries [OR] or architectural distortion with [OR] without calcifications. </EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
  - Age [NOT] <30 years old
  - Screening mammography with only calcifications abnormalities
  - Male patients
  - Pregnant [OR] lactating patients
  - Patients with any allergy to iodinated contrast
  - Patients with eGFR [NOT] < 45
  - Patients that may be treated with radioactive iodine
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
