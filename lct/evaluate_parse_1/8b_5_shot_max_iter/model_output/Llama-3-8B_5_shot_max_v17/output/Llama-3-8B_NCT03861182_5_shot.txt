<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:

Adult Healthy Volunteers:
- Over 18 years of age [AND] apparently healthy

Neonates:
- Aged between 1h of life [AND] and 28 days of life [OR] born beyond 33 Week of Amenorrhea + 1day [AND] and 2kg of birth weight [AND] hospitalized in the medical-surgical centre of Pediatrics of the hospital of Strasbourg [AND] for whom a blood sample was prescribed as part of their routine care

Exclusion Criteria:

Adult Healthy Volunteers:
- Systemic inflammatory disease [OR] transient inflammatory state [OR] any drug that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h preceding the sampling [NOT] free of any medication

Neonates:
- Risk of anemia [NOT] less than 7g/DL [OR] current anticoagulant treatment
