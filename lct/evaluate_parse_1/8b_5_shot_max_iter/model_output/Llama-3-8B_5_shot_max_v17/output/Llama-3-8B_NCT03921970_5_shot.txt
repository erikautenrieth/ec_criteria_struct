Inclusion Criteria:
 - American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) classification [AND] I-II
 - Scheduled for laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy [AND] under general anesthesia
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Bleeding [NOT] diathesis
 - Receiving [NOT] anticoagulant treatment
 - Known [NOT] local anesthetics [AND] opioid allergy
 - Infection [OR] of the skin at the site of the needle puncture
 - Pregnancy [OR] lactation
 - Patients [AND] who do not accept the procedure
