Inclusion Criteria:
 - All patients [AND] who are assigned to a clinically indicated chest X-ray [OR] by the emergency department of Vienna General Hospital
 - Ability to provide informed consent [AND] informed consent after detailed patient briefing
Exclusion Criteria:
 - The emergency department's assessment of a critical clinical condition [AND] oppose an examination with both modalities (ULDCT of the chest, [OR] chest X-ray)
 - Assigned to chest X-ray [AND] as follow-up
 - Women [AND] with positive ß-HCG-test
