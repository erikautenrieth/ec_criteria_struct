Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adults [AND] between age 18 [OR] and 75
 - Participants [AND] without a history of gastrointestinal disorders (control group)
 - Participants [AND] with functional dyspepsia (FD group)
 - Participants [AND] with moderate-to-severe irritable bowel syndrome (defined by IBS symptom severity scores (IBS-SSS) greater than 250) (IBS group)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - History of myocardial infarction [OR] supplemental oxygen requirement [OR] or diabetes
 - History of chronic gastrointestinal symptoms [NOT] for healthy control population only
 - History of gastric surgery
 - Psychosis [OR] or altered cognitive status
 - History of head injury [OR] metal in the skull [OR] stroke [OR] or a history of seizures
 - Implantable devices, such as a pacemaker [OR] or nerve stimulator
 - Current use of the following medications [OR] or use of substances which are known to lower the seizure threshold: amitriptyline (Elavil) [OR] nortriptyline (Pamelor) [OR] imipramine (Tofranil) [OR] doxepin (Sinequan) [OR] clozapine (Clozaril) [OR] chlorpromazine (Thorazine) [OR] amphetamines [OR] or methamphetamine [OR] Ecstasy [OR] Ketamine [OR] Angel Dust/phencyclidine (PCP) [OR] cocaine [OR] or 3 or more alcoholic drinks per day
