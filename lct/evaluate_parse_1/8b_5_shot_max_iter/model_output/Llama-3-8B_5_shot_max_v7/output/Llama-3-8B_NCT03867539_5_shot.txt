Inclusion Criteria:
 - Carpal tunnel syndrome diagnosed either by physical exam [OR] EMG
 - Age >18.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Allergy to bupivacaine [NOT] free
 - Pre-operative opioid usage within the last six months [NOT] prevented
 - Patients [AND] who are allergic to Percocet [OR] acetaminophen
 - Patients [AND] who are taking tricyclic antidepressants (interaction with bupivacaine) [NOT] possible
 - Vulnerable populations (prisoners [OR] or pregnant women) [NOT] included
