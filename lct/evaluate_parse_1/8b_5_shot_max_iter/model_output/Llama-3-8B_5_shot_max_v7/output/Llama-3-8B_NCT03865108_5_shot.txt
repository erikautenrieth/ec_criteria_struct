Inclusion Criteria:
  - Singleton gestation [AND] 
       - Twin gestation reduced to singleton either spontaneously [OR] or therapeutically, is eligible unless the reduction occurred after 13 weeks 6 days project gestational age [NOT].

Exclusion Criteria:
