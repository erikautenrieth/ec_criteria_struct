Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of primary knee osteoarthritis according to American College of Radiology [AND] being radiographically K-L grade 2-3 [AND] presence of symptoms for at least 3 months [AND] patients who have been planned a conventional physiotherapy programme because of knee osteoarthritis [AND] presence of functional capacity for outpatient physical therapy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [NOT] with secondary knee osteoarthritis [OR] history of lower extremity [OR] or spine surgery [OR] history of intraarticular injection in last 1 month [OR] patients with neurological disease [AND] that will affect muscle strength, balance [AND] and coordination
