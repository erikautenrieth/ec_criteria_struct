Inclusion Criteria:
 - Chronic (more than three months) ESBL colonization of the same strain [AND] as determined by [OR] species, phenotype [AND] and antibiogram.
 - Patients must be able to swallow sachets [AND] must be able to speak [AND] understand Swedish [AND] must have a permanent residence in Sweden [AND] must be able to sign informed consent in Swedish.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Immunosuppression [NOT] (i.e. chemotheraphy [OR] treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors).
 - In patient care [OR] immunodeficiency.
 - Psychiatric disorder [OR] alcohol [OR] substance abuse.
 - Dementia [OR] invasive catheters.
