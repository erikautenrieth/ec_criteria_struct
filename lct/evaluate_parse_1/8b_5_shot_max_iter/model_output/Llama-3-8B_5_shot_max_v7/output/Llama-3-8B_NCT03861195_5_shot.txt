Inclusion Criteria:
 - age ≥18 years
 - BMI ≤40 kg/m2
 - indication for hysterectomy for benign disease [OR] prophylactic surgery due to hereditary cancer
 - indication for radical hysterectomy [AND] and/or pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for cervical cancer
 - indication for modified radical hysterectomy [AND] and/or pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for endometrial cancer
 - size of uterus [AND] vagina allows for retrieval by the vaginal route in cancer patients
 - written informed consent [AND] obtained.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - known extensive intra-abdominal adhesions [AND] preventing safe laparoscopic access
 - anaesthesiological contraindications to laparoscopy [OR] preventing safe anaesthesia
 - women with pacemaker [OR] other implants where electrosurgery is [NOT] to be avoided
 - women with known defects of the hemostasis [OR] increasing risk of bleeding
 - pregnancy [OR] requiring termination of pregnancy
 - other internal [OR] anatomical criteria that preclude a minimal invasive approach [OR] preventing safe surgery
 - inability to understand patient information [OR] preventing informed consent
