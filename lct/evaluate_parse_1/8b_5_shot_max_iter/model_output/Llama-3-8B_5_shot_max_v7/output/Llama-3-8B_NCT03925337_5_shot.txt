Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients age: ≥ 22 years [AND] 
 - Patients presenting for routine colonoscopy for screening [OR] and/or surveillance purposes.
 - Willingness to undergo two withdrawals [AND] with [OR] without the use of computer-aided software while undergoing conventional colonoscopy with sedation
 - Ability to provide written, informed consent [AND] and understand the responsibilities of trial participation
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Minors aged < 22 years [NOT]
 - People with [NOT] diminished cognitive capacity
 - Patients undergoing diagnostic colonoscopy [OR] (e.g. as an evaluation for active gastrointestinal bleed, referring collectively to the stomach and the small and large intestine).
 - Patients with incomplete colonoscopies [OR] (those where endoscopists did not successfully intubate the cecum due to technical difficulties [OR] or poor bowel preparation)
 - Patients that have standard contraindications to colonoscopy [NOT] in general (e.g. documented acute diverticulitis, fulminant colitis [AND] and known or suspected perforation).
 - Patients with inflammatory bowel disease [NOT]
 - Patients with any polypoid/ulcerated lesion > 2 cm [NOT] concerning for invasive cancer on endoscopy
 - Patients referred for endoscopic mucosal resection (EMR) [NOT], which is a procedure to remove early-stage cancer [AND] and precancerous growths from the lining of the digestive tract.
