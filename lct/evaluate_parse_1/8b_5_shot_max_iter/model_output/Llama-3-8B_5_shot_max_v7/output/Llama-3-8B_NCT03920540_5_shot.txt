Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients diagnosed with Hunter syndrome [AND] male at the age of ≥ 5 [AND] adequate abilities (including 6-MWT) to participate in this study in the opinion of the investigator [AND] voluntarily signed written informed consent to participation in this study [AND] consent to contraception.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Prior treatment with iduronate-2-sulfatase ERT [OR] history of bronchotomy, bone marrow transplantation, or cord blood transplantation [OR] known hypersensitivity reactions to any of the components of the investigational product [OR] prior or planned administration of other investigational products within 30 days before treatment with the investigational product in this study or during this study [OR] unable to perform 6-MWT [OR] female [NOT] excluded.
