Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient requiring an electroneuromyogram whose indication [AND] allows the application of a standard protocol of examination.
 - Patient that has given his/her [OR] free and informed consent.
 - Patient that signed the consent form.
 - Patient with an [AND] health insurance plan.
 - The patient is at least 18 years old ([≥]).
 - The patient is under 80 years old ([≤]).
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patient with symptoms of neuropathy during the screening [OR] not.
 - Patient whose Neurological severity score (NSS) score is [NOT] ≥ 3.
 - Patient whose neurological examination is [NOT] abnormal and suggestive of neuropathy.
 - Patient presenting a pathology that [NOT] could cause neuropathy (e.g. diabetes, renal failure, etc.).
 - Patient already participating in a category 1 research study ([NOT] Jardé law).
 - Patient in an exclusion period determined by another study [OR] not.
 - Patient under the protection of justice, under guardianship [OR] or under curatorship.
 - Patient refusing to sign the consent form [OR] not.
 - Patient for which it is [NOT] impossible to provide information about the study.
 - Pregnant patient, parturient, [OR] breastfeeding.
