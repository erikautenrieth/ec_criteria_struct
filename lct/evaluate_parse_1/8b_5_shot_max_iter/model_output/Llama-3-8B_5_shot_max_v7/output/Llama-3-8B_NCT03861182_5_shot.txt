<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:

Adult Healthy Volunteers:

* Over 18 years of age [AND] with no exclusion criteria

Neonates:

* Aged between 1h of life [OR] and 28 days of life
* Born beyond 33 Week of Amenorrhea [AND] + 1day [AND] and 2kg of birth weight
* Hospitalized in the medical-surgical centre of Pediatrics of the hospital of Strasbourg
* For whom a blood sample was prescribed as part of their routine care

Exclusion Criteria:

Adult Healthy Volunteers:

* Systemic inflammatory disease [OR] transient inflammatory state
* Any drug that modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h preceding the sampling [NOT]

Neonates:

* Risk of anemia < 7g/DL [OR] current anticoagulant treatment
