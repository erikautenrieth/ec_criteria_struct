Inclusion Criteria:
 - Presence of erectile dysfunction

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Presence of [NOT] uncontrolled diabetes mellitus,
 - Presence of [NOT] uncontrolled hypertension,
 - Presence of [NOT] uncontrolled lipid metabolism disorders,
 - Presence of [NOT] neurological diseases,
 - Presence of [NOT] haematological diseases,
 - Urinary tract infection [OR] or
 - Diagnosis of any malignancies [OR]
 - Chronic kidney failure [OR]
 - Presence of [NOT] metabolic syndrome,
 - Presence of [NOT] psychiatric diseases and/or medical treatment,
 - Smoking [OR]
 - History of pelvic surgery [OR] cardiac surgery [OR] pelvic radiotherapy
