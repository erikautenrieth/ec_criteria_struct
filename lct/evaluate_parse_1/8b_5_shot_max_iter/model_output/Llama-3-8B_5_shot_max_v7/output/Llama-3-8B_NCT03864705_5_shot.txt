<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Type 2 diabetes mellitus [AND] on metformin therapy for at least 3 months

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] type 1 diabetes mellitus
  - [NOT] uncontrolled hypertension
  - [NOT] significant liver or kidney disease
  - [NOT] active malignancy
  - [NOT] recent (within 6 months) major surgery or trauma
  - [NOT] active infection
  - [NOT] pregnancy or breastfeeding
  - [NOT] recent (within 30 days) use of any medication that may affect glucose metabolism
  - [NOT] known allergy to metformin or other sulfonylureas
  - [NOT] significant cardiac disease or history of myocardial infarction
  - [NOT] uncontrolled hyperthyroidism

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
