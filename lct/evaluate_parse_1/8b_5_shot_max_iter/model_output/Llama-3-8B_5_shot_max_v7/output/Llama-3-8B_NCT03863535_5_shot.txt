Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
Type II diabetic patients, 18 years and older, were included if the participants had [AND] 
  1. severe non-PDR in either eyes [OR] with/without diabetic macular edema;
  2. ETDRS letters-measured best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) ranging from 20/40 to 20/320 Snellen equivalent;     
  3. no previous treatment (of any type) in either eye.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
  1. history of prior laser treatment [OR] or vitrectomy in the study eye;
  2. history of thromboembolic event - including myocardial infarction [OR] or cerebral vascular accident;
  3. major surgery within the prior 6 months [OR] or planned within the next 28 days;
  4. history of glaucoma [OR] or ocular hypertension;
  5. loss of vision as a result of [NOT] other causes;
  6. history of systemic corticosteroid therapy within the last 3 months;
  7. severe systemic disease [NOT] other than diabetes mellitus;
  8. known coagulation abnormalities [OR] or current use of anticoagulative medication [OR] other than aspirin
  9. any condition that could [NOT] affect followup or documentation (including preretinal or vitreous hemorrhage).
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
