Inclusion Criteria:
 - 1. Histologically confirmed pancreatic adenocarcinoma [AND] 2. Locally advanced [OR] or metastatic PC [AND] 3. Chemotherapy-naïve [AND] 4. ECOG performance status of 0 [OR] or 1 [AND] 5. An adequate bone marrow [AND] liver function [AND] kidney function
Exclusion Criteria:
 - 1. Age [NOT] <80 years [AND] 2. Brain metastasis [AND] 3. With other malignancies [AND] 4. Chronic diarrhea [OR] cardiac disease [OR] pregnancy [OR] breast feeding
