Inclusion Criteria:
 - Male participants who are between the ages of 21 [AND] 65 years (inclusive) on the day of signing informed consent [OR]
 - Diagnosis of active rheumatoid arthritis, [OR] psoriatic arthritis, [OR] ankylosing spondylitis [OR] or, non-radiographic axial spondyloarthritis for at least 12 weeks prior to screening, meeting the corresponding specific disease classification criteria as specified in the protocol

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Previously documented problems with male reproductive health [NOT]
 - Prior diagnosis of male infertility [NOT]
 - Use of any prohibited concomitant medication as outlined by protocol [NOT]

Note: Other protocol-defined Inclusion/Exclusion criteria may apply.
