Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients diagnosed with Hunter syndrome [AND] 
  - Male at the age of ≥ 5 [OR] 
  - Adequate abilities (including 6-MWT) to participate in this study in the opinion of the investigator [AND] 
  - Voluntarily signed written informed consent to participation in this study [AND] 
  - Consent to contraception

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Prior treatment with iduronate-2-sulfatase ERT [OR] 
  - History of bronchotomy [OR] bone marrow transplantation [OR] cord blood transplantation [AND] 
  - Known hypersensitivity reactions to any of the components of the investigational product [OR] 
  - Prior or planned administration of other investigational products within 30 days before treatment with the investigational product in this study [OR] during this study [AND] 
  - Unable to perform 6-MWT [OR] 
  - Female [NOT]
