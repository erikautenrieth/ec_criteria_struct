Inclusion Criteria:
 - American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) classification [AND] I-II
 - Scheduled for laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy [AND] under general anesthesia
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Bleeding diathesis
 - Receiving anticoagulant treatment [OR] or receiving anticoagulant therapy
 - Known local anesthetics [AND] and opioid allergy
 - Infection of the skin at the site of the needle puncture [OR] or infection of the skin at the site of the needle prick
 - Pregnancy [OR] or lactation
 - Patients [AND] who do not accept the procedure [OR] refuse the procedure
