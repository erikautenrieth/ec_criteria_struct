Inclusion Criteria:
 - Being older than 18 [AND] having a diagnosis of HIV [AND] presenting at least one of the following symptoms during the preceding two weeks: [OR] sleep problems, [OR] fatigue, [OR] pain, [OR] anxiety [OR] or symptoms of depression [AND] understanding and speaking French [AND] being able to follow the instructions to learn the relaxation technique.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Those [NOT] reporting any chronic or acute diseases, [OR] food allergies, [OR] smoking [OR] or alcohol abuse
 - Use of illicit drugs; [OR] regular consumption of medications, [OR] micronutrient supplements, [OR] or both
 - Vegetarians, [OR] or those unable to eat meat, [AND] are also excluded since the foods for the study contain meat
 - Unable to refrain from taking medications during the study period
