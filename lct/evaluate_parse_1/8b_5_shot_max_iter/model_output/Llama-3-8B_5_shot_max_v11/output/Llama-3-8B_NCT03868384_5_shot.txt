Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with hypertension [AND] taking antihypertensive drugs more than 1 year

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Secondary hypertension [OR] hypotensive emergency [OR] and urgency
 - Severe heart failure (NYHA III [OR] and IV)
 - Angina [OR] with 6 months
 - Myocardial infarction [OR] within 6 months
 - Peripheral artery disease [OR] within 6 months
 - Significant arrhythmia ([OR] ventricular tachycardia [OR] and fibrillation, [OR] atrial fibrillation, [OR] atrial flutter)
 - Pregnancy [NOT] allowed
 - Night labor [OR] shift worker [NOT] allowed
 - Those who are currently participating in other clinical studies [NOT] allowed
 - Those who have taken other clinical trial drugs [NOT] within the past month
 - According to the opinion of the researcher, those who have [OR] may have a disease that may interfere with the completion of the study [NOT] allowed
 - History of drug [OR] alcohol dependence [NOT] within 6 months
