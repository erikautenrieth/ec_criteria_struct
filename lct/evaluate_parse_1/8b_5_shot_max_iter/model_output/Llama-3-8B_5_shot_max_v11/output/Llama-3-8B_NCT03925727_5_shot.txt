Inclusion Criteria:
 - Subject-reported history of dry eye disease in both eyes [AND] for at least 6 months;
 - History of use of artificial tear eye drops [AND] for dry eye symptoms;
 - Total score of ≥40 [OR] on SANDE;
 - TFBUT [AND];
 - Corneal fluorescein staining [AND];
 - Lissamine green conjunctival staining [AND];
 - Schirmer's test score [AND].

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Have [NOT] participated in a previous tavilermide (MIM-D3) study;
 - Have clinically significant slit lamp findings [OR] at Visit 1;
 - Have a history of lacrimal duct obstruction [AND] within 12 months of Visit 1;
 - Have an uncontrolled systemic disease [OR];
 - Be a woman [AND] who is pregnant, nursing [OR] or planning a pregnancy;
 - Be a woman [AND] of childbearing potential [AND] who is [NOT] using an acceptable means of birth control;
 - Have a condition [OR] or be in a situation which the investigator feels may put the subject at significant risk [OR] may confound the study results [OR] or may interfere significantly with the subject's participation in the study;
 - Be currently enrolled in an investigational drug [OR] device study [OR] have used an investigational drug [OR] device within 45 days prior to Visit 1.
