Inclusion Criteria:
  - All patients who are assigned to a clinically indicated chest X-ray by the emergency department of Vienna General Hospital [AND] ability to provide informed consent [AND] informed consent after detailed patient briefing

Exclusion Criteria:
  - The emergency department's assessment of a critical clinical condition [OR] opposes an examination with both modalities (ULDCT of the chest, chest X-ray)
  - Assigned to chest X-ray as follow-up [OR] women with positive [NOT] ß-HCG-test
