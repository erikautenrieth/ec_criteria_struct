Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Skeletal maturity [AND] 
  2. AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures [OR] 
  3. Wound primarily closeable [AND] (no flap [OR] or delay in closure due to contamination needed) [AND] 
  4. Palpable pedal pulses [AND] (no vascular injury sustained) [AND] 
  5. Presentation within 24 hours from injury [OR] 

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Current injury is a pathologic fracture [NOT] 
  2. Sustained bilateral tibia fracture [OR] 
  3. Sustained comminuted femur fracture [OR] 
  4. Sustained severe Traumatic Brain Injury (GCS<12) *** [OR] 
  5. Sustained severe spinal cord injury (lower extremity paresis/paralysis) [OR] 
  6. Sustained severe burns (>10% total body surface area (TBSA) [OR] or >5% TBSA with full thickness [OR] or circumferential injury) [OR] 
  7. Prior ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery [OR] 
  8. Prior or current lower limb deformity [OR] or abnormality [OR] 
  9. Unable to complete follow-up visits [NOT]
