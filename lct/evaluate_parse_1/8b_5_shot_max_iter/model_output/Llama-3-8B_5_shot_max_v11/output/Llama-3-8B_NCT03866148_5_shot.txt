Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] with moderate to severe sleep apnoea who require CPAP as standard care [OR] (AHI >15)
 - Patients between the age of 18 [AND] and 75

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] with a diagnosis of atrial fibrillation [OR] atrial flutter [OR] and/or ventricular tachycardia (VT)
 - Patients [AND] with an ILR already in-situ [OR] an established indication for ILR
 - Patients [AND] with a palliative diagnosis [NOT] i.e. life expectancy less than 3 years
 - Patients [AND] who [NOT] lack capacity
