Inclusion Criteria:
  - Major Depressive Disorder according to Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (DSM-V) [AND]
  - > 25 years [AND]
  - Sufficient understanding of the German language

Exclusion Criteria:
  - < 25 years [NOT]
  - Concomitant diagnosis of past or present psychotic disorder [OR]
  - Concomitant diagnosis of past or present bipolar disorder [OR]
  - First degree relative with a psychotic disorder [OR]
  - Unable or unwilling to discontinue antidepressant medication [OR]
  - Pregnancy or breastfeeding [OR]
  - Known hypersensitivity to LSD [OR]
  - Somatic disorders including central nervous system (CNS) involvement [OR]
  - Known or suspected non-compliance, drug or alcohol abuse [OR]
  - Metal implants [OR]
  - Weight < 42 kg [OR]
  - Suicide risk or very likely to require psychiatric hospitalisation
