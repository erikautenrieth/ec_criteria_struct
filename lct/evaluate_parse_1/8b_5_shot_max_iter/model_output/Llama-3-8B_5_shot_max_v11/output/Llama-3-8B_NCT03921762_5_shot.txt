Inclusion Criteria:
  - > 50 years old [AND]
  - Bilateral age-related cataract [OR]
  - Qualify for bilateral implantation within 1 month [AND]
  - No previous refractive or ocular surgery [NOT]
  - Available IOL dioptre range: 18 to 27D [OR]
  - Expected postoperative astigmatism ≤ 0.75D [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Pupil > 4 mm [OR]
  - Occupation requiring night-time driving [OR] or any occupation incompatible with multifocality [NOT]
  - Acute or chronic disease or illness that would increase risk or confound study results [e.g. diabetes mellitus (with retinopathy), immunocompromised, glaucoma, epiretinal membrane, cornea guttata, etc….] [NOT] found to be relevant by the investigators [AND]
  - Amblyopia [OR] strabismus [OR]
  - Extremely shallow anterior chamber [OR]
  - Capsule or zonular abnormalities that may affect postoperative centration or tilt of the lens [e.g. pseudoexfoliation syndrome, uveitis, Marfan's syndrome] [NOT]
  - Pupil abnormalities (non-reactive, tonic pupils, abnormally shaped pupils or pupils that do not dilate under mesopic/ scotopic conditions) [OR]
  - Instability to place the intraocular lens safely at the location planned [OR]
  - Pregnancy (pregnancy test will be taken pre-operatively in women of reproductive age) [NOT]
