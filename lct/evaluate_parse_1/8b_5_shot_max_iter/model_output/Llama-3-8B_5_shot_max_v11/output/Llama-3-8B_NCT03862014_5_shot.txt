<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [AND] parents of the patients who accept to participate [AND] sign the informed consent
  - Patients whose molar teeth are diagnosed as molar incisor hypomineralization by EAPD [OR] 
  - Teeth that are fully erupted [AND] 
  - Patients who have at least two permanent first molars that need fissure sealant [OR] 
  - Teeth that have white-yellow-brown discoloration that indicate high-caries risk [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Teeth that are previously restored [OR] have caries [NOT]
  - Patients who have orthodontic treatment [OR] 
  - Patients who are not cooperative for the dental procedure [OR] 
  - Teeth that have dental flourosis [OR] enamel malformation according to the specific syndromes [NOT]

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
