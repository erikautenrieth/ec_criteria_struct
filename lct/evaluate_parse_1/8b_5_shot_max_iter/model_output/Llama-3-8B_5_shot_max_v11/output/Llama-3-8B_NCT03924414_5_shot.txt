Inclusion Criteria:
 - Men [AND] women age 65 years [OR] older
 - Current PD diagnosis [AND] with symptoms severity at Hoehn & Yahr (H&Y) stage [OR] 1-4 based on an expert assessment (movement disorders neurologist report [OR] or telemedicine evaluation)
 - Willing [AND] able to continue in follow-up for at least 2 years
 - Willing [AND] able to provide informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - History [OR] of hip fracture
 - Any use [OR] of a bisphosphonate drug within the last 12 months
 - Use [OR] of any other osteoporosis treatment (such as SERMs [OR] and denosumab) within the last 6 months
 - Tooth extraction [OR] or invasive dental procedures within the past 30 days [OR] planned/scheduled extraction/procedure in the next 12 months
 - Non-ambulatory [OR], i.e., unable to walk without assistance of another person.
 - Undergoing kidney dialysis
 - A diagnosis [OR] of multiple myeloma [OR] Paget's disease
 - Unable [OR] to speak [OR] read English sufficiently to complete informed consent
 - Any other criteria [OR] which would make the patient unsuitable to participate in this study as determined by the study staff (e.g., an uncontrolled drug [OR] and/or alcohol addiction)
