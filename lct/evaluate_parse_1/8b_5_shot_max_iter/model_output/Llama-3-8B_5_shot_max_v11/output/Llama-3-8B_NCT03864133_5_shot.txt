Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adults [AND] age 55-90 years [AND] with visual significant cataracts [OR] in one [OR] both eyes.
 - Healthy individuals [AND] able to tolerate outpatient cataract surgery [AND] under local anesthesia [AND] via either phacoemulsification [OR] and/or femtosecond assisted cataract surgery. Well-controlled diabetes [OR] hypertension will be included.
 - Females [AND] of childbearing potential [AND] must agree to use a reliable method of birth control [AND] while participating in this study. Reliable methods of birth control are: abstinence [OR] oral contraceptives [OR] intrauterine device (IUD) [OR] DepoProvera [OR] tubal ligation [OR] vasectomy of the partner (with confirmed negative sperm counts) [AND] in monogamous relationship (same partner). An acceptable, although less reliable, method involves the careful use of condoms [AND] spermicidal foam or gel [AND] or cervical cap or sponge. A pregnancy test is required at least 10 days from the last normal menstrual period, if the patient is a sexually active female of childbearing potential.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Allergy [AND] to Phenylephrine [OR] or NSAIDs.
 - Inability [AND] to sit steady [AND] and upright for the Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT).
 - Complications [AND] during surgery, including posterior capsular rupture [OR] vitreous loss [OR] zonular dialysis [OR] or iris trauma.
 - Macular thickness above 300 microns at baseline [NOT] excluded.
 - Currently taking a prostaglandin analogue [NOT] excluded.
 - Presence [AND] of an epiretinal membrane on the preoperative OCT [NOT] excluded.
 - Retained lens fragment post-operatively [NOT] excluded.
 - Inability [AND] to return for follow appointments [NOT] excluded.
 - Female patients who are pregnant [OR] lactating [OR] planning to become pregnant during the course of treatment [NOT] excluded.
