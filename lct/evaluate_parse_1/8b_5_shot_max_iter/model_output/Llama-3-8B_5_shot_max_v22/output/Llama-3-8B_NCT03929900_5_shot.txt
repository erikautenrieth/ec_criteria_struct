Inclusion Criteria:
  - Age 45-79 years [AND] 
  - Estimated glomerular filtration rate 20-60mL/min/1.73m2 [OR] based on CKD-EPI(Cr) equation [AND] 
  - Non-dialysis dependent (NDD) [AND] 

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease [OR] 
  - Unable [OR] unwilling to give consent [AND] 
  - Previously received chronic dialysis [AND] 
  - Previous [OR] any organ transplant [AND] 
  - Patients who diagnosed heart failure with NYHA class 3 [OR] 4 [AND] 
  - Known liver cirrhosis (Child-pugh class 2 [OR] 3) [AND] 
  - Pregnant women [OR] 
  - Single kidney due to trauma [OR] donation [AND] 
  - Patients who received immunosuppressive agent within the past 1 year [AND] 
  - Patients who diagnosed with glomerulonephritis through kidney biopsy [AND]
