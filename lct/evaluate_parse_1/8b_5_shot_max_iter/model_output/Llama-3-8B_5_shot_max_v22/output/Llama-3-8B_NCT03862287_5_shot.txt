Inclusion Criteria:
  - ASA I-III [AND] male [OR] female patients aged between 18 [AND] 80 years old, [AND] who will receive spinal anesthesia for surgeries lasting less than one hour.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [NOT] younger than 18 years old.
  - ASA physical status [OR] >3.
  - Immunosuppression [OR] high risk of infection.
  - Contraindications to receiving spinal anesthesia (e.g. coagulation impairment) [OR].
  - Patients with psychosis [OR].
  - Patients with preexisting back pain [OR].
  - Patients with cognitive impairment [OR].
  - Allergies to local anesthetics, analgesics [OR] any medication used in the study [OR].
  - Patients with chronic regular opioid usage [OR].
  - Presence of preexisting neurological symptoms [OR].

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
