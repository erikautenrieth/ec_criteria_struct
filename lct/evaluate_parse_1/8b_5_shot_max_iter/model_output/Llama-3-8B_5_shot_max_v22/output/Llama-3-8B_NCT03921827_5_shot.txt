Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients with recent stroke/TIA [AND] and severe stenosis of intracranial ICA [OR] MCA [AND] impaired CVR within previous three months [NOT] before 3 weeks after acute stroke. This is to differentiate between patients with a long-standing fixed-stenosis [OR] from patients with partially recanalized intracranial artery (masquerading as severe stenosis).
  2. Age [OR] >21 years

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Patients with atrial fibrillation/arrhythmias [OR] atrial flutter.
  2. Within [OR] 2 weeks of cardiac catheterization [OR] arterial puncture at femoral puncture site.
  3. Decompensated heart failure [AND] usually class [OR] 3 [OR] 4.
  4. LV EF [OR] <30%.
  5. Moderate [OR] or severe AR.
  6. Persistent [OR] and uncontrolled hypertension (BP persistently [OR] >160/100 mmHg).
  7. Bleeding diathesis [OR] coagulopathy.
  8. Active thrombophlebitis [OR] venous disease of lower limbs.
  9. Severe lower extremity vaso-occlusive disease [OR] peripheral artery disease.
  10. Presence of a documented aortic aneurysm [OR] dissection requiring surgical repair.
  11. Pregnancy [OR] or lactation.
