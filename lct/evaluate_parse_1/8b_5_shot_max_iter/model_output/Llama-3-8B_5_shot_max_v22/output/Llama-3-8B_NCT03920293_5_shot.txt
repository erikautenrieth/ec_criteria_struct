Inclusion Criteria:

1. Diagnosed with Myasthenia Gravis [AND] at least 6 months (180 days) prior to the date of the Screening Visit [AND] as confirmed by specific criteria.
2. Myasthenia Gravis Foundation of America Clinical Classification Class II [OR] IV at screening.
3. MG-ADL profile must be ≥ 6 [AND] at screening [AND] randomization (Day 1).
4. Vaccinated against meningococcal infections [OR] within 3 years prior to, [OR] at the time of, initiating study drug to reduce the risk of meningococcal infection (N meningitidis).

Exclusion Criteria:

Medical Conditions

1. Any active [OR] untreated thymoma. History of thymic carcinoma [OR] thymic malignancy unless deemed cured by adequate treatment with no evidence of recurrence for ≥ 5 years before screening.
2. History of thymectomy [OR] within the 12 months prior to screening.
3. History of N meningitidis infection.
4. Use of the following [OR] within the time period specified below:
   - IV immunoglobulin [OR] within 4 weeks of randomization.
   - Use of plasma exchange [OR] within 4 weeks of randomization.
   - Use of rituximab [OR] within 6 months of screening.
5. Participants [AND] who have received previous treatment with complement inhibitors (for example, eculizumab).
