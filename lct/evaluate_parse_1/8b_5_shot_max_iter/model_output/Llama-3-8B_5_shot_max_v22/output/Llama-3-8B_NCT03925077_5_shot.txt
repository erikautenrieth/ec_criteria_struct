Inclusion Criteria:
  1. demonstrate readiness to physical activity by completing the online Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire [OR] (PAR-Q);
  2. have a SCI resulting in incomplete [OR] or complete (C5 and below) paraplegia [OR] or tetraplegia;
  3. have broadband internet access [AND] with a reliable connection;
  4. between the ages of 18 [AND] to 65 years;
  5. have the ability [AND] to converse [OR] and read English.

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. significant visual impairment [AND] that prevents seeing a computer screen [OR] to follow a home exercise program;
  2. active pressure ulcer [OR] with open wounds;
  3. any contraindications [AND] to exercise based on the American College of Sports Medicine guidelines [AND] that would prevent participation;
  4. significant hearing impairment [AND] impeding ability [OR] to hear music [OR] to engage in exercise.
