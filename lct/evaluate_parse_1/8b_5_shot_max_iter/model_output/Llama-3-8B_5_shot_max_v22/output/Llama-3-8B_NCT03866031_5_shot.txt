Inclusion Criteria:

1. The patient should be completely edentulous [AND] for at least 6 months.
2. First class jaw relation [OR] adequate bone for an implant length of at least 10 mm [AND] diameter of at least 2.75 mm.
3. The patients should be able to speak [AND] write in Arabic.

Exclusion Criteria:

1. Patients who received radiotherapy to the head [OR] neck region for tumors [AND] have a history of chemotherapy.
2. Patients on long-term steroids [OR] bisphosphonates.
3. Heavy smokers [OR] patients with physical [AND] mental disabilities that interfere with the maintenance of implants.
4. Uncontrolled systematic disease that could compromise implant surgery [OR] extreme bruxism.
5. Patients with infectious disease [OR] temporomandibular joint dysfunction.
