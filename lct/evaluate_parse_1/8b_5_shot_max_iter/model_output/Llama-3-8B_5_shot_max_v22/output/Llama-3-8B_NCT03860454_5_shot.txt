<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - LMNA+ cases with pathogenic LMNA mutations [AND] for LMNA+ and heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust [OR] 
  - DCMWT cases: patients with heart muscle failure but with wild-type lamin gene [AND] Heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust [OR] 
  - HV (controls): matched to cases

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Needle-phobia [AND] that would preclude blood-letting [OR] 
  - Participants unwilling to consent [OR] 
  - Patients [AND] that have a conventional contraindication for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) [OR] 
  - Patients [AND] that have had a blood transfusion within the last month [AND] patients having haemodialysis will be excluded
