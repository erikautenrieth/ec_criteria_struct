<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Type 2 diabetes mellitus on metformin therapy for at least 3 months [AND] with a HbA1c level between 7.0% and 10.0% [OR] with a history of microvascular complications

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with type 1 diabetes mellitus [OR] with a history of severe hypoglycemia [OR] with a history of diabetic ketoacidosis [OR] with a history of pancreatitis [OR] with a history of gastrointestinal disease that may interfere with the absorption of study medications
  -  Patients who are currently taking other anti-diabetic medications [OR] who have a history of taking other anti-diabetic medications [OR] who are planning to start other anti-diabetic medications during the study
  -  Patients with a history of cardiovascular disease [OR] with a history of cerebrovascular disease [OR] with a history of peripheral arterial disease [OR] with a history of heart failure [OR] with a history of hypertension that is not well controlled [OR] with a history of hyperlipidemia that is not well controlled
  -  Patients who are unable to provide informed consent [OR] who are unable to follow study procedures [OR] who are unable to return for follow-up visits [OR] who are unable to provide blood samples for testing [OR] who are unable to provide urine samples for testing
