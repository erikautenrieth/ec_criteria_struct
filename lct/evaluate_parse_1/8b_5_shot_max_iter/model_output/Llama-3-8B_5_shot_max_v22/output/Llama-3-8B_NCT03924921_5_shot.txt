<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Being older than 18 [AND] 
  -  have a diagnosis of HIV [AND] 
  -  present at least one of the following symptoms during the preceding two weeks: [OR] sleep problems, [OR] fatigue, [OR] pain, [OR] anxiety [OR] or symptoms of depression;
  -  understand and speak French [AND] 
  -  be able to follow the instructions to learn the relaxation technique.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Those reporting any chronic or acute diseases [OR] food allergies [OR] smoking [OR] alcohol abuse;
  -  Use of illicit drugs [OR] regular consumption of medications [OR] micronutrient supplements [OR] both;
  -  Vegetarians [OR] those unable to eat meat [OR] are also excluded since the foods for the study contain meat;
  -  Unable to refrain from taking medications during the study period [NOT] allowed.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
