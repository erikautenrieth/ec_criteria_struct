<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients diagnosed with Hunter syndrome [AND] 
  - Male at the age of ≥ 5 [OR] 
  - Adequate abilities (including 6-MWT) to participate in this study [AND] in the opinion of the investigator.
  - Voluntarily signed written informed consent to participation in this study [AND] 
  - Consent to contraception [OR]

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Prior treatment with iduronate-2-sulfatase ERT [OR]
  - History of bronchotomy, bone marrow transplantation, or cord blood transplantation [OR]
  - Known hypersensitivity reactions to any of the components of the investigational product [NOT]
  - Prior or planned administration of other investigational products within 30 days before treatment with the investigational product in this study [OR] during this study.
  - Unable to perform 6-MWT [OR]
  - Female [NOT]
