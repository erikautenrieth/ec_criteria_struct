Inclusion Criteria:
  - All adults undergoing cardiac surgery with CPB will be considered for enrollment.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Factors that affect immune function or AKI assessment are the basis for exclusion criteria:
    - [OR] Concurrent disease associated with immunosuppression including [AND] malignancy, [OR] chronic infection (e.g. HIV, Hepatitis C), [OR] organ transplant [AND] immunosuppressant medications
    - [OR] Documented acute infection with the past [AND] 1 month (e.g. pneumonia, urinary tract infection)
    - [OR] Prednisone [OR] or other steroid use currently [OR] within the past one month
    - [OR] AKI at the time of surgery
    - [OR] ESRD requiring renal replacement therapy
    - [OR] Estimated GFR <45 mL/min (as judged by the Chronic Kidney Disease Epidemiology Collaboration (CKD-EPI) equation)
