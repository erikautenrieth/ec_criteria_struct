Inclusion Criteria:
  - Diagnosis of CD for at least 3 months prior to baseline [AND] confirmed diagnosis of moderate to severe CD as assessed by SF, AP score, and SES-CD
  - Demonstrated intolerance, loss of response or inadequate response to conventional or to biologic therapy for CD
  - If female, subject must meet the contraception recommendations [OR] if not, a reliable form of birth control must be used throughout the study

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Have a current diagnosis of ulcerative colitis [OR] inflammatory bowel disease-unclassified (IBD-U) (formerly known as indeterminate colitis) [OR] abdominal or perianal abscess [OR] short bowel syndrome
  - Have a stoma [OR] ostomy
  - Have had a bowel resection within 6 months [OR] any kind of intra-abdominal or extra abdominal surgery within 3 months of baseline
  - Have ever received any monoclonal antibodies binding IL-23 [NOT] including those that are currently in use or have been discontinued within the past 6 months
