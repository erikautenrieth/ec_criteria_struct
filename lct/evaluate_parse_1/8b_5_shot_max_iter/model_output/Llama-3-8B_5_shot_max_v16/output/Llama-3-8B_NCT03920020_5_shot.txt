Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of primary knee osteoarthritis according to American College of Radiology [AND] 
 - Being radiographically K-L grade 2-3 [OR] 3
 - Presence of symptoms for at least 3 months [AND] 
 - Patients who have been planned a conventional physiotherapy programme because of knee osteoarthritis [AND] 
 - Presence of functional capacity for outpatient physical therapy [AND]
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with [NOT] seconder knee osteoarthritis
 - History of lower extremity [OR] spine surgery
 - History of intraarticular injection in last 1 month [OR] 
 - Patients with neurological disease that will [NOT] affect muscle strength, balance and coordination
