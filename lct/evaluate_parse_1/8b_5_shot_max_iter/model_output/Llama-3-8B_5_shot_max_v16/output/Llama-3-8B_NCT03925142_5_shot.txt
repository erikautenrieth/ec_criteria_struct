Inclusion Criteria:
 - Male [OR] female
 - 30-69 years old
 - BMI 25-37 kg/m2
 - Total cholesterol <260 mg/dL
 - Low-density lipoprotein cholesterol <190 mg/dL
 - Glucose <110 mg/dL
 - Systolic/diastolic blood pressure < 140/90
 - Body weight stable for 3 months prior (±3 kg) [AND] stable physical activity regimen 3 months prior [AND] medication use stable for 6 months prior
 - Non-smoking [AND] not drinking more than 2 alcoholic drinks per day
 - Non-diabetic [AND] not lactose-intolerant [AND] not acutely ill
 - Non-pregnant and not lactating [AND] willing and able to consume the prescribed diets (lacto-ovo vegetarian and omnivorous)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - <30 [OR] >69 years old
 - BMI <25.0 [OR] >37 kg/m2
 - Fasting serum total cholesterol > 260 mg/dL [OR] low-density lipoprotein cholesterol >189 mg/dL
 - Glucose >110 mg/dL [OR] systolic/diastolic blood pressure > 140/90
 - Body weight stable for <3 months prior (±3 kg) [OR] stable physical activity regimen < 3 months prior
 - Medication use unstable for 6 months prior [AND] using medications
 - Smoking [OR] drinking more than 2 alcoholic drinks per day
 - Diabetic [OR] have renal failure [OR] have liver failure [OR] have cardiovascular events (myocardial infarction or stroke) during the 6 months prior to the study
 - Pregnant [OR] lactating
