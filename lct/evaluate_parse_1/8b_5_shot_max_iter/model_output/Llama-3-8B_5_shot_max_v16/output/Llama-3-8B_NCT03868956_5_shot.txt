Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age [AND] >= 18 years
 - Known history of objectively documented deep vein thrombosis of the lower limb [OR] (with [OR] or without pulmonary embolism)
 - Out-patients referred for clinically suspected acute recurrent ipsilateral DVT of the lower limb [AND] i.e. the occurrence of new symptoms and signs of DVT [OR] or the increase of symptoms and signs in patients with post-thrombotic syndrome
 - Patients [AND] covered by social security [OR] or equivalent regimen
 - Signed and dated informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Known current pregnancy
 - Any condition [AND] which, in the opinion of the investigator [AND] may prevent him from performing the colour doppler ultrasound test [OR] (plaster cast, inaccessible vein segment after abdominal or pelvic surgery, [OR] or other causes that may lead to a technically inadequate CDUS)
 - Delay from onset of symptoms to inclusion of more than 10 days
 - Therapeutic anticoagulation for more than 48 hours [OR] in the two days prior to consent
 - Prophylactic anticoagulation for more than 48 hours [OR] in the two days prior to consent
 - Clinical symptoms of pulmonary embolism
 - Life expectancy less than 3 months
 - Patient [AND] unable to adhere to protocol [OR] or follow-up visits and contacts
 - Participants [AND] under legal guardianship [OR] or incapacitation
 - Patient [AND] already enrolled in a deep vein thrombosis (DVT) diagnostic research
