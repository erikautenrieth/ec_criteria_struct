Inclusion Criteria:
 - age 15 to 21 [AND] 
 - be able to read and understand English [OR] as determined by having the individual read a brief, eighth grade reading level paragraph [AND] 
 - able to begin the intervention within six weeks of their infant's birth [AND] 
 - be able to participate in an intervention over two weeks, requiring approximately a 12-hour time commitment: either in-person on two Saturdays for six hours each [OR] or a self-paced content of videos and resource materials over two weeks [AND] 
 - own or have access to a device with internet access [AND] 
 - have a working e-mail address.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Any condition [NOT] that might prevent the father's infant from reaching normal developmental milestones will participation, including congenital malformations [OR] or prematurity requiring prolonged/ ongoing infant hospitalization.
