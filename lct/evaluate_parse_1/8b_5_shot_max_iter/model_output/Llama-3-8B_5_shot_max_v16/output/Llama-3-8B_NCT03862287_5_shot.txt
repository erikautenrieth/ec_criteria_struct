Inclusion Criteria:
 - ASA I-III [AND] male and female patients [AND] aged between 18 [AND] to 80 years old, who will receive spinal anesthesia [AND] for surgeries lasting less than one hour.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] younger than 18 years old [OR] older than 80 years old
 - ASA physical status >3
 - Immunosuppression [OR] high risk of infection
 - Contraindications to receiving spinal anesthesia (e.g. coagulation impairment) [OR] any other contraindication
 - Patients [AND] with psychosis
 - Patients [AND] with preexisting back pain
 - Patients [AND] with cognitive impairment
 - Allergies [OR] to local anesthetics, [OR] analgesics, [OR] any medication used in the study
 - Patients [AND] with chronic regular opioid usage
 - Presence [OR] of preexisting neurological symptoms
