Inclusion Criteria:
 - Male [OR] Female ≥ 60 years old
 - MMSE score between 4 [AND] and 26 [OR] / 30
 - Diagnosis of minor [OR] to major NeuroCognitive Disorder [AND] of the Alzheimer type [OR] multiple etiology;
 - Subject [AND] without a major hearing [OR] or visual impairment;
 - Subject [AND] not presenting any contraindications to the practice of adapted physical activity;
 - Subject [AND] affiliated to Social Security System;
 - Signing informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - < 60 years [NOT]
 - Medical indications: Recent [OR] or unstable cardiovascular events: ECG changes, unstable angina, uncontrolled arrhythmia, 3rd degree BAV, acute heart failure [NOT]
 - MMSE score < 4 [NOT]
 - Subject already engaged in appropriate physical activity [NOT]
 - Subject with a perceptual disorder (AMD, deafness...) [NOT]
 - Subject with a motor disability [NOT]
 - Subject under guardianship, curatorship [OR] or safeguard of justice [NOT]
 - Persons deprived of their liberty (administrative [OR] or judicial) [NOT]
