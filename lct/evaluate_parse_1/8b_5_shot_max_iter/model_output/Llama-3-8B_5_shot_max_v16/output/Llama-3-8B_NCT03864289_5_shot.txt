Inclusion Criteria:
 - Children with ASD [OR] high risk for autism;
 - Han ethnicity;
 - Parents were at least middle school educated [AND] and agreed to do at least 20 hours per week of planned practice activities with the child in daily life.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Children with [NOT] Syndromic ASD caused by known genetic defects [OR] or inherited metabolic diseases, such as Rett Syndrome, Fragile X Syndrome, Angelman Syndrome, Prader-Willi Syndrome, tuberous sclerosis;
 - Visual, auditory [OR] and physical disabilities;
 - Complication with other [NOT] serious chronic diseases, such as liver [OR] and kidney failure, connective tissue diseases, tumors.
