Inclusion Criteria:
 - Prescription of a Narval CC mandibular advancement device
 - Age ≥ 18 years
 - Ability to understand the study information [AND] and information on usage of personal data
 - Signed and dated informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] who have central sleep apnea (central AI > 5/hour) [OR] patients with loose teeth [AND] and severe parodontitis
 - Patients [AND] who have completely missing teeth [OR] removable tooth replacement, [OR] or who have a teeth health insufficient to retain an MAD [OR] patients with missing molars [OR] patients with maximum protrusion of less than 5mm
