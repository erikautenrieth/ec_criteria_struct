Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 - Skeletal maturity [AND] 
 - AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures [OR] 
 - Wound primarily closeable (no flap or delay in closure due to contamination needed) [AND] 
 - Palpable pedal pulses (no vascular injury sustained) [OR] 
 - Presentation within 24 hours from injury [AND] 
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 - Current injury is a [NOT] pathologic fracture [OR] 
 - Sustained bilateral tibia fracture [OR] 
 - Sustained comminuted femur fracture [OR] 
 - Sustained severe Traumatic Brain Injury (GCS<12) *** [OR] 
 - Sustained severe spinal cord injury (lower extremity paresis/paralysis) [OR] 
 - Sustained severe burns (>10% total body surface area (TBSA) or >5% TBSA with full thickness or circumferential injury) [OR] 
 - Prior ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery [OR] 
 - Prior or current lower limb deformity or abnormality [OR] 
 - Unable to complete follow-up visits [OR] 
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
