Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
All adults undergoing cardiac surgery with CPB will be considered for enrollment.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
Factors that affect immune function or AKI assessment are the basis for exclusion criteria:
[OR] Concurrent disease associated with immunosuppression including malignancy, [OR] chronic infection (e.g., HIV, Hepatitis C), [OR] organ transplant and immunosuppressant medications
[OR] Documented acute infection with the past 1 month (e.g., pneumonia, urinary tract infection)
[OR] Prednisone [OR] or other steroid use currently [OR] or within the past one month
[OR] AKI at the time of surgery
[OR] ESRD requiring renal replacement therapy
[OR] Estimated GFR <45 mL/min (as judged by the Chronic Kidney Disease Epidemiology Collaboration (CKD-EPI) equation)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
