Inclusion Criteria:
 - BMI 45-60 kg/m2
 - Failure of at least one weight loss medication [OR]
 - Previous Gastric Bypass Surgery lead to less than 50% of excess weight lost by 2 years after surgery [AND] with significant loss of control over eating
 - Subject is able to attend all scheduled clinic appointments on their own [OR] with a caregiver [AND] provide voluntary, decisionally capable, and appropriately informed consent
 - Premenopausal women must agree to use acceptable methods of birth control [AND] participants provide voluntary, decisionally capable, and appropriately informed consent
 - Subject is able to comply w/ all testing and follow-up requirements defined by the study protocol [AND] participant has no immediate plan for relocation beyond 6 hours of the study site
 - Participant has no immediate plan for relocation beyond 6 hours of the study site [AND] proficiency with the English language

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Subject has an implanted medical device that delivers electrical energy to the brain [NOT]
 - Subject has an implantable cardiac pacemaker, defibrillator, [OR] neurostimulator [NOT]
 - Subject is likely to require repeat MR imaging after implant of the RNS Neurostimulator and Leads [NOT]
 - Subject is unable to fit into CT scanner (500lb upper weight limit for CT scanner) [NOT]
 - Inability to provide informed consent to treatment [NOT]
