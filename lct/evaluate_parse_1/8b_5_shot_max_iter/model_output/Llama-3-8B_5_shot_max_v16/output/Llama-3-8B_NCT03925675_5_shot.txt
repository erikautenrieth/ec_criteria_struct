Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. Patients evaluated for residual or recurrent gliomas after initial tumor surgery, external beam radiation [OR] and / or chemotherapy with temozolamide.
 2. Male [AND] and female
 3. Ages 18 [AND] to 70
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. Patient diagnosis with glioma [NOT] before surgery, external beam radiation [OR] and /or chemotherapy with temozolamide.
 2. Women who were [NOT] pregnant, [NOT] breast feeding, [OR] possibly pregnant.
 3. Patients with [NOT] hepatic [OR] renal dysfunction.
 4. Patients with MRI contraindications (i.e. pacemakers, [OR] non-MR compatible devices).
 5. Patients with a history of drug hypersensitivity [NOT] to 18F-Fluciclovine.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
