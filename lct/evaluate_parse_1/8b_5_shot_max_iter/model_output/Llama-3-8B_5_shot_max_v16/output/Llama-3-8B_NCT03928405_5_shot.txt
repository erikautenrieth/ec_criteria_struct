 - [NOT] lack of communication difficulties [OR] problems
 - Volunteering to participate in the study
 - Cerebrovascular event, [NOT]

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients diagnosed with dementia [AND] (infection, vascular, hematological diseases), patients who [NOT] could not be contacted during the follow-up period [OR] who did not come to control, cardiac [OR] cerebrovascular event,
 - Endocrine disorder, fluid-electrolyte imbalance [OR] infection

 - Patients with delirium [OR] depression
 - Those with protective sensation loss [NOT] (those with Semmes-Weinstein Monofilament Thickness 4.56 [OR] above)
