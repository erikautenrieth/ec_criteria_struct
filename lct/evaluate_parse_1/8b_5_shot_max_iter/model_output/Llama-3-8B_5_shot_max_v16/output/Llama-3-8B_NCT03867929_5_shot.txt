Inclusion Criteria:
 - Presence of erectile dysfunction

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Presence of uncontrolled [OR] diabetes mellitus
 - Presence of uncontrolled [OR] hypertension
 - Presence of uncontrolled [OR] lipid metabolism disorders
 - Presence of [NOT] neurological diseases
 - Presence of [NOT] haematological diseases
 - Urinary tract infection
 - Diagnosis of [NOT] any malignancies
 - Chronic kidney failure
 - Presence of [NOT] metabolic syndrome
 - Presence of [NOT] psychiatric diseases [AND] or medical treatment
 - Smoking
 - History of pelvic surgery, [OR] cardiac surgery [OR] pelvic radiotherapy
