Inclusion Criteria:
  - Age [AND] >= 18 years
  - Known history of objectively documented deep vein thrombosis of the lower limb [OR] with or without pulmonary embolism
  - Out-patients referred for clinically suspected acute recurrent ipsilateral DVT of the lower limb [AND] i.e. the occurrence of new symptoms and signs of DVT [OR] or the increase of symptoms and signs in patients with post-thrombotic syndrome
  - Patients [AND] covered by social security [OR] equivalent regimen
  - Signed and dated informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Known current pregnancy [NOT]
  - Any condition [AND] which, in the opinion of the investigator, may prevent him from performing the colour doppler ultrasound test [OR] plaster cast, inaccessible vein segment after abdominal or pelvic surgery, or other causes that may lead to a technically inadequate CDUS
  - Delay from onset of symptoms to inclusion of more than 10 days [NOT]
  - Therapeutic anticoagulation for more than 48 hours in the two days prior to consent [NOT]
  - Prophylactic anticoagulation for more than 48 hours in the two days prior to consent [NOT]
  - Clinical symptoms of pulmonary embolism [NOT]
  - Life expectancy less than 3 months [NOT]
  - Patient [AND] unable to adhere to protocol or follow-up visits and contacts
  - Participants under legal guardianship or incapacitation [NOT]
  - Patient already enrolled in a deep vein thrombosis (DVT) diagnostic research [NOT]

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
