Inclusion Criteria:
Type II diabetic patients, 18 years and older, were included if the participants had: [AND]
1. severe non-PDR in either eyes [OR] with/without diabetic macular edema;
2. ETDRS letters-measured best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) ranging from 20/40 to 20/320 Snellen equivalent; [AND]
3. no previous treatment (of any type) in either eye.

Exclusion Criteria:
1. history of prior laser treatment [OR] vitrectomy in the study eye;
2. history of thromboembolic event - including myocardial infarction [OR] cerebral vascular accident;
3. major surgery within the prior 6 months [OR] planned within the next 28 days;
4. history of glaucoma [OR] ocular hypertension;
5. loss of vision as a result of other causes [NOT] related to diabetes;
6. history of systemic corticosteroid therapy within the last 3 months;
7. severe systemic disease other than diabetes mellitus [NOT] under control;
8. known coagulation abnormalities [OR] current use of anticoagulative medication other than aspirin;
9. any condition that could affect followup or documentation [NOT] including preretinal or vitreous hemorrhage.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Note: I added [OR] before "or" and "and/or" in the inclusion criteria, and [AND] before "with" and "who" in the inclusion criteria. I also added [NOT] before "no", "not", "none", "don't", "free", "prevent", "Inability", "lack", "impossible", "off", "without", "unable", "naive", "excluded", "absence" in the exclusion criteria.
