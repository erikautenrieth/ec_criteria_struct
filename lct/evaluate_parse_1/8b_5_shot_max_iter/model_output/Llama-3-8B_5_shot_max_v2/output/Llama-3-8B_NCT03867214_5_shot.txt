<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Adult patients who plan to undergo simultaneous primary total knee arthroplasty on bilateral knee joint with a diagnosis of osteoarthritis [OR] aseptic bone necrosis, [AND] but not of rheumatoid arthritis;
  - All patients who have normal preoperative platelet count [AND] normal prothrombin time [AND] normal partial thromboplastin time [AND] normal international normalized ratio;
  - The use of only balanced electrolyte solutions [OR] and/or albumin for plasma volume restitution.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Allergy to tranexamic acid [NOT];
  - Receiving warfarin [OR] heparin; had a history of hemophilia [OR] deep venous thrombosis [OR] pulmonary embolism [OR] or renal impairment [OR] or were pregnant;
  - Patients with any cardiovascular problems [AND] such as myocardiac infarction history [OR] atrial fibrillation [OR] angina;
  - Patients with thromboembolic disorders [OR] or those exhibiting a deteriorating general condition;
  - Preoperative anemia [AND] (a hemoglobin value of <11 g/dL [OR] in females and <12 g/dL [OR] in males), refusal of blood products;
  - Preoperative use of anticoagulant therapy within five days before surgery [AND] fibrinolytic disorders requiring intraoperative antifibrinolytic treatment [OR] coagulopathy [AND] (as identified by a preoperative platelet count of <150,000/mm3 [OR] an international normalized ratio of >1.4 [OR] or a prolonged partial thromboplastin time [>1.4 times normal]).
