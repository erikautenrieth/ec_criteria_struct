Inclusion Criteria:

1. Histologically or cytologically confirmed non-small cell lung cancer [AND] with a ECOG score of performance status [OR] 0-1.
2. Locally advanced or recurrent metastatic NSCLC that has never received systemic treatment [AND] with at least 1 measurable lesion that has not been previously irradiated [OR] according to RECIST1.1 criteria.
3. Prior to enrollment, a central laboratory testing report has confirmed that the tumor has one of two common EGFR mutations positive [OR] that are sensitive to EGFR-TKI therapy, accompanied with [OR] or not accompanied with other EGFR mutation, with the exception of exon 20 insertion [AND].

Exclusion Criteria:

1. Previously received systemic treatment for locally advanced or recurrent metastatic cancer [NOT].
2. Primary T790M mutation-positive patient [NOT].
3. Previous interstitial lung disease, drug-induced interstitial lung disease, radiation pneumonia requiring hormonal therapy, or any clinically proven active interstitial lung disease [NOT].
4. Known active infections such as hepatitis B, hepatitis C, and human immunodeficiency virus [NOT].
5. Local radiation therapy is carried out within 1 week [OR] more than 30% bone marrow radiation therapy or extensive radiation therapy is performed within 4 weeks [NOT].
6. ≤ 4 weeks from major surgery [OR] ≤ 2 weeks from minor surgery [NOT].
