Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Age [AND] ≥18 years [AND] ≤ 90 years
  2. Tumor diagnosed as Glioblastoma Multiforme [OR] on MRI with distinct ring-like pattern of contrast enhancement with thick irregular walls [AND] and a core area reduced signal [OR] suggestive of tumour necrosis as assessed by the surgeon
  3. Tumors situated in [OR] near eloquent areas; motor cortex, sensory cortex, subcortical pyramidal tract [OR] or speech areas as indicated on MRI (Sawaya Grading II [AND] II)
  4. The tumor is [AND] suitable for resection (according to neurosurgeon)
  5. Karnofsky performance scale [AND] 80 [OR] more
  6. Written [AND] Informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Tumors of the cerebellum [OR] brain stem [OR] midline
  2. Multifocal contrast enhancing lesions
  3. Substantial non-contrast enhancing tumor areas [OR] suggesting low grade gliomas with malignant transformation
  4. Medical reasons [OR] precluding MRI (eg, pacemaker)
  5. [NOT] Inability to give consent because of [OR] language barrier
  6. Psychiatric history
  7. Previous brain tumour surgery
  8. Previous low-grade glioma
  9. Second primary malignancy within the past 5 years [OR] with the exception of adequately treated in situ carcinoma of any organ [OR] basal cell carcinoma of the skin
  10. Severe aphasia [OR] dysphasia
