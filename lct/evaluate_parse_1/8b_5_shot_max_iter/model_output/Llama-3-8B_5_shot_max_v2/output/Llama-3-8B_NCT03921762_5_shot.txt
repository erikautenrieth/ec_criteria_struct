Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] > 50 years old
  - Bilateral age-related cataract
  - [AND] Qualify for bilateral implantation within 1 month
  - [AND] No previous refractive or ocular surgery
  - [AND] Available IOL dioptre range: 18 to 27D
  - [AND] Expected postoperative astigmatism ≤ 0.75D

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [OR] Pupil > 4 mm
  - [OR] Occupation requiring night-time driving [OR] or any occupation incompatible with multifocality
  - [AND] Acute [OR] or chronic disease [OR] or illness that would increase risk [OR] or confound study results [e.g. [NOT] diabetes mellitus (with retinopathy), [NOT] immunocompromised, [NOT] glaucoma, [NOT] epiretinal membrane, [NOT] cornea guttata, etc….] found to be relevant by the investigators
  - [OR] Amblyopia [OR] strabismus
  - [OR] Extremely shallow anterior chamber
  - [OR] Capsule [OR] or zonular abnormalities that may affect postoperative centration [OR] or tilt of the lens (e.g. pseudoexfoliation syndrome, uveitis, Marfan's syndrome)
  - [OR] Pupil abnormalities (non-reactive, tonic pupils, abnormally shaped pupils [OR] or pupils that do not dilate under mesopic/ scotopic conditions)
  - [OR] Instability to place the intraocular lens safely at the location planned
  - [OR] Pregnancy (pregnancy test will be taken pre-operatively in women of reproductive age)
