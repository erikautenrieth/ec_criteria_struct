<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Subjects with adhesive capsulitis diagnosis by physical examination [AND] (Compared with intact side, there is more than 25% limitation in at least two directions of shoulder range of motion)
  - Subjects have complaints for at least 1 month [OR] more
  - [NOT] pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding

Exclusion Criteria:
  - History of bilateral adhesive capsulitis
  - History of shoulder trauma, fracture, shoulder surgery, calcific tendinopathy, glenohumeral osteoarthritis, inflammatory rheumatic diseases, tumor, infection [OR] any other condition that may affect shoulder mobility
  - History of corticosteroid injection in the shoulder during the last 3 months [OR] any other medication that may affect shoulder mobility
  - History of recent lung, breast or bypass surgery [OR] any other surgery that may affect shoulder mobility
  - History of cervical radiculopathy [OR] brachial plexus lesion [OR] any other condition that may affect shoulder mobility
  - History of neuromuscular disease [OR] any other condition that may affect shoulder mobility
  - History of physical therapy program for the same shoulder in the last 6 months [OR] any other therapy that may affect shoulder mobility
