<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Diagnosed with sciatica by GP [AND] 
  - NRS leg pain of 6 [OR] more on a 10-point NRS scale
  - Minimum duration of symptoms of 3 weeks [AND] maximum duration of 8 weeks

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Age under 18 years [NOT] 
  - Condition preventing [OR] to receive transforaminal epidural injection
  - Severe scoliosis [AND] 
  - Transforaminal epidural injection received in 6 months [OR] before randomization date
  - Surgery for sciatica at the same level [AND] 
  - Surgery for sciatica at another level within one year [OR] before inclusion
  - Pregnancy [AND] 

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
