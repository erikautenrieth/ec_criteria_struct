<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Dysfunctional lipids, blood pressure [AND] glycemic control determined at a screening visit:
       -  Triglycerides > 150 mg/dL [OR] HDL <50 in females, [OR] <40 mg/dL in males
       -  Systolic blood pressure ≥130 mmHg [OR] diastolic blood pressure ≥80 mmHg [OR] (or on hypertension medication)
       -  HbA1c ≥5.7% [OR] higher
  -  [NOT] No diagnosis or treatment for cancer in the past year
  -  [NOT] No diagnosis of mild cognitive impairment [OR] dementia in the past year
  -  Ability to perform daily exercise, including aerobic activity [AND] resistance exercise
  -  [NOT] Non-smoker in the past year

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Positive exercise stress test
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
