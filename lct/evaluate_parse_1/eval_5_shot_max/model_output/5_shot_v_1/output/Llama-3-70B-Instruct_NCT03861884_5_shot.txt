Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Ability to give informed consent.
  2. Either pathological, [OR] genetic [OR] imaging biomarker evidence to suggest Fronto-temporal Lobar Degeneration, [OR] Alzheimer's Disease [OR] or Vascular Cognitive Impairment, [OR] or defined clinical diagnoses.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] Ability to give informed consent due to lack of mental capacity to consent to study involvement.
  2. [NOT] Speaking English before age 5 years.
  3. Having a learning disability.
  4. MRI contraindications
