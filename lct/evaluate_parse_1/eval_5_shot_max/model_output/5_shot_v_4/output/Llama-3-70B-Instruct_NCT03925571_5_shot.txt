Inclusion Criteria:
 - Majors aged between 18 [AND] 55 years, [AND] with an appropriate hearing [AND] able to use Music-Care© (selection of the playlist via internet access),
 - Needing treatment in odontology - periodontal surgery (coronary elongation [OR] or sanitation surgery [OR] or muco-gingival surgery) [OR] or implantary surgery (placement of an unit implant [OR] or two implant in the same localization),
 - Oral [AND] dated consent,
 - Agreeing to be contacted via phone at J+1 [AND] to fill follow-up study documents,
 - Profiting of social security [OR] or CMU.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [AND] with a long-term taking of anxiolytic [OR] or analgesic,
 - Regular consumer of soft drug (cannabis) [OR] or hard drug (opioid),
 - Patient [AND] with medical history of psychiatry (mental harm), hearing problem, cognitive [AND] behavioural impairment (claustrophobia), sensory disabilities,
 - Patient [AND] with problems of hemostasis,
 - Majors [AND] under trusteeship [OR] or guardianship,
 - Pregnant women [OR] or lactating,
 - Minors,
 - Protected person, deprived of freedom [OR] or under justice safeguard,
 - Profiting of a medical help from government (AME),
 - [NOT] contactable after care,
 - Patient [AND] with hepatic impairment,
 - Patient [AND] with dry mouth,
 - Patient relevant a suboptimal hearing incompatible with musicotherapy [OR] or inability to use Music-care®.
