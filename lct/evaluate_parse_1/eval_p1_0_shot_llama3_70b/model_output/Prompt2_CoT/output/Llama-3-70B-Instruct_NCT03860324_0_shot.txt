Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Surgical plan [AND] total hip replacement
  -  Signing of consent form [AND] to participate in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient [NOT] refusal
  -  BMI > 40 kg/m2
  -  Surgical plan [AND] revision of hip replacement
  -  Patient [NOT] able [NOT] to quantify pain level
  -  Chronic kidney disease [AND] with a Glomerular Filtration Rate < 50ml/min
  -  Previously medicated [AND] with opioids
  -  Patient [NOT] able [NOT] to perform the surgery [AND] with spinal block
  -  Allergy [AND] to local anesthetics
  -  Infection [AND] in the site of the Erector Spinae Plane [OR] Fascia Iliaca block
  -  Allergy [OR] contraindication [AND] to the use of morphine
