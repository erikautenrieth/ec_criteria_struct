<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  All newly registered patients with smear-positive recurrent pulmonary TB
  -  Adults as well as children (no age limit)
  -  Able [AND] and willing to provide written informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients transferred to a health facility [NOT] not supported by Damien Foundation will be excluded. This includes patients diagnosed with HIV/TB-coinfection.
