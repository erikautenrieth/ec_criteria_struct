<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adults determined to be candidates for venous sinus stenting

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients [NOT] unable to provide informed consent
  -  Patients [NOT] unable to comply with follow-up visits
  -  Patients [AND] with contraindications to dual antiplatelet therapy
  -  Patients [AND] with known allergies to aspirin, [OR] clopidogrel, [OR] or contrast dye
  -  Patients [AND] with active intracranial hemorrhage [OR] or other bleeding diathesis
  -  Pregnant [OR] or lactating women
  -  Patients [AND] with severe renal insufficiency (GFR < 30 mL/min)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
