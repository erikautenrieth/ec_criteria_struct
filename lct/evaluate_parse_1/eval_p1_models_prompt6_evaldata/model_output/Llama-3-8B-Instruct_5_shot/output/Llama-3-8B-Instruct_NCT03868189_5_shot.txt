<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patient requiring an electroneuromyogram whose indication [AND] allows the application of a standard protocol of examination.
  - Patient that has given his/her [NOT] free and informed consent.
  - Patient that signed the consent form.
  - Patient with an health insurance plan.
  - The patient is at least 18 years old [OR] ≥.
  - The patient is under 80 years old [OR] ≤.

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patient with symptoms of neuropathy during the screening [OR].
  - Patient whose Neurological severity score (NSS) score is [NOT] ≥ 3.
  - Patient whose neurological examination is [NOT] abnormal and suggestive of neuropathy.
  - Patient presenting a pathology that could cause neuropathy (e.g. diabetes, renal failure, etc.) [OR].
  - Patient already participating in a category 1 research study (Jardé law) [OR].
  - Patient in an exclusion period determined by another study [OR].
  - Patient under the protection of justice, under guardianship [OR] or under curatorship.
  - Patient refusing to sign the consent form [OR].
  - Patient for which it is [NOT] impossible to provide information about the study.
  - Pregnant patient, parturient [OR] or breastfeeding.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
