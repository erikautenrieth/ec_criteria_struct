Inclusion Criteria:
 1. [AND] Signed informed consent [AND] with the study
 2. [AND] Men [OR] women [AND] >18 years of age
 3. [AND] Patients ≥1 [AND] ≤6 months after type I myocardial infarction
 4. [AND] Physical inactivity before MI defined as the absence of moderate to vigorous exercise for at least 30 minutes 5 [OR] or more days a week
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. [NOT] Heart failure NYHA IIIB-IV
 2. Planned coronary revascularization
 3. Planned major surgery within the next 12 months
 4. [NOT] Inability to walk for any reason
 5. Comorbidities that would preclude adherence to the rehabilitation program (e.g. arthrosis, active malignancy, major depression [OR] or other significant psychiatric disorder, cognitive impairment) - at the discretion of the physician
 6. [NOT] Life expectancy less than 12 months
 7. [NOT] Pregnancy
 8. [NOT] Inability to operate the smart-watch
