Inclusion Criteria:
 - BMI [AND] 45-60 kg/m2
 - Failure [AND] of at least one weight loss medication
 - Previous Gastric Bypass Surgery [AND] lead to less than 50% of excess weight lost by 2 years after surgery
 - Significant Loss [AND] of Control over eating
 - Subject [AND] is able to attend all scheduled clinic appointments on their own [OR] with a caregiver.
 - Premenopausal women [AND] must agree to use acceptable methods of birth control.
 - Participants provide voluntary, decisionally capable, [AND] appropriately informed consent.
 - Subject [AND] is able to comply [AND] w/ all testing [AND] and follow-up requirements defined by the study protocol.
 - Participant [AND] has [NOT] no immediate plan for relocation beyond 6 hours of the study site.
 - Proficiency [AND] with the English language.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Subject has an implanted medical device that delivers electrical energy to the brain.
 - Subject has an implantable cardiac pacemaker, defibrillator, [OR] neurostimulator.
 - Subject [AND] is likely to require repeat MR imaging after implant of the RNS Neurostimulator [AND] and Leads.
 - Subject [AND] is unable to fit into CT scanner (500lb upper weight limit for CT scanner).
 - [NOT] Inability to provide informed consent to treatment.
