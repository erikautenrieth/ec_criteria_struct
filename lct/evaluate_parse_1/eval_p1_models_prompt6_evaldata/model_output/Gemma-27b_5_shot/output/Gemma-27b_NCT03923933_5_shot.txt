Inclusion Criteria:
 -  glomerular filtration rate less than 30 ml / min / 1.73m [AND] without replacement therapy [AND] dialysis [OR] hemodialysis [AND] volume overload [AND] at least 100 ml per day of residual diuresis [AND] use of a loop diuretic for at least one month
Exclusion Criteria:
 -  Allergies [NOT] not known to diuretics [OR] patients with severe infections [OR] patients with hemodynamic instability [OR] amputees [OR] patients with cognitive impairment [OR] patients with acute renal failure [OR] patients with graft loss
