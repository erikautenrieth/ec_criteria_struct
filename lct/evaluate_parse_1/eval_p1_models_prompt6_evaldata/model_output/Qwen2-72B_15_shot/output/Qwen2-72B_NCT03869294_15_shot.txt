Inclusion Criteria:
 - Histologically confirmed pancreatic adenocarcinoma
 - Locally advanced [OR] or metastatic PC
 - Chemotherapy-naïve
 - ECOG performance status of 0 [OR] or 1
 - An adequate bone marrow, liver function [AND] and kidney function
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Age ≥80 years
 - Brain metastasis
 - [NOT] With other malignancies
 - Chronic diarrhea, cardiac disease, pregnancy [OR] or breast feeding
