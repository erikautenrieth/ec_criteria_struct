  Inclusion Criteria:

* Age 18-90 [AND]
* Acute ischemic or hemorrhagic stroke (within 30 days) [AND]
* Consent to participate and to anonymous processing of data [AND]

Additional Inclusion Criteria for Sub-project Neurophysiological Markers:

* Signing of informed consent for the participation in the sub-project [AND]

Additional Inclusion Criteria for Epigenetic subproject:

* Signing of informed consent for participation in the subproject [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:

* Stroke occurred more than 30 days after the transfer to intensive rehabilitation [NOT]

Additional Exclusion Criteria for Epigenetic subproject (for retrospective analysis):

* History of major psychiatric disorder or recent exacerbation [NOT]
* Global aphasia [NOT]
* Refusal of the patient to participate in the study [NOT]
