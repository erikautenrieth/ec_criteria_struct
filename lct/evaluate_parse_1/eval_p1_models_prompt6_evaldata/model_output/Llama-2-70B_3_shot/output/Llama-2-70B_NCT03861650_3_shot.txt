  Inclusion Criteria:

1. Patients free from any disease affecting bone formation [AND] of both sexes were included in the study.
2. Patients younger than 25 years old [AND]
3. Patients included in the study [OR] post traumatic patients, hemifacial microsomia, Treacher Collins [OR] post ankylotic mandibular deformity.
4. Distraction range [AND] between 15-20 mm.

Exclusion Criteria:

1. The presence of uncontrolled systemic disease [NOT].
2. Previous radiotherapy [OR] on head and neck region.
3. Chemotherapy [OR] within the past 12-month period.
4. An active infection [NOT] at the distraction site.
