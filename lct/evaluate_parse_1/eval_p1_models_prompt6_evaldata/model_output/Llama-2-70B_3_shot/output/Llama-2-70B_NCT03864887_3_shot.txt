  Inclusion Criteria:

* Patients who have had brain death or serious brain trauma [AND]
* Healthy people who are interested in optical assessment of hemodynamic parameters [OR]

Exclusion Criteria:

* Patients who are in pregnancy or have plans to conceive [NOT]
* Patients who have vertebra surgery or have plans for surgery [NOT]
* Patients who are inappropriate to join this trial judged by the radiologists or specialists [NOT]
	+ AIDS [OR]
	+ Active Hepatitis [OR]
	+ Tuberculosis [OR]
	+ Syphilis [OR]
	+ Patients who regularly take anticoagulants [OR]
	+ Antiplatelet drugs [OR]
