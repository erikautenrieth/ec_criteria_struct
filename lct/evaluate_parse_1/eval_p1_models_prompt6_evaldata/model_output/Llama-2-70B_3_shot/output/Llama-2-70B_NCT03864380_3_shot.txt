  Inclusion Criteria:
 - Person registered at the 2019 Paris Marathon
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Ophthalmological disorders [AND] making access to the fundus impossible for both eyes [NOT]

Note: The [AND] operator is used to indicate that both conditions must be met for the participant to be excluded. The [NOT] operator is used to indicate that the participant is excluded if they have ophthalmological disorders that make access to the fundus impossible for both eyes.
