  Inclusion Criteria:

* Patient requiring a first total knee prosthesis (scheduled between Tuesday and Friday) [AND]
* Patient that has given his/her free and informed consent and signed the consent form [AND]
* The patient must be a member or beneficiary of a health insurance plan [AND]
* The patient is at least 18 years old (≥) and under 90 years old (<)

Exclusion Criteria:

* The subject is in a period of exclusion determined by a previous study (therapeutic study) [OR]
* The subject refuses to sign the consent [OR]
* It is impossible to give the subject informed information [OR]
* The patient is under safeguard of justice or state guardianship [OR]
* The patient is pregnant or breastfeeding [OR]
* The patient is allergic to latex bandages [OR]
* The subject has contralateral amputation, pressure ulcers, severe Obstructive Arteriopathy of Lower Limbs, lymphoedema or thromboembolic event of the lower limb (ipsilateral and / or contralateral / superficial or deep or detected by postoperative Doppler) [OR]
* The subject has chronic or active wounds or any cause that hinders normal rehabilitation or the application of a bandage [OR]
* patient with a stroke / Disabling Neurological Pathology, Chronic Immunological Pathology / Systemic Disease [OR]
* patient with Hepatic insufficiency [OR]
* patient with Venous insufficiency (stages 3 and 4) [OR]
* The patient is on dialysis [OR]
* The subject has cognitive or behavioral disorders (Parkinson's disease, cerebellar syndrome, opposition, agitation, dementia) [OR]
* The subject has a history of knee surgery
