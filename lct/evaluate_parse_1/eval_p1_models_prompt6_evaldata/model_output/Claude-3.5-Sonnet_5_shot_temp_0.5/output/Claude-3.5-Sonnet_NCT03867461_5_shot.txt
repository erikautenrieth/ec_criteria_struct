<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Adults determined to be candidates for venous sinus stenting

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Age < 18 years
  -  Pregnancy
  -  [NOT] Unable to provide informed consent
  -  [NOT] Inability to undergo MRI [OR] or CT venogram
  -  [NOT] Inability to undergo lumbar puncture
  -  [NOT] Inability to undergo cerebral angiogram [OR] or venous sinus stenting
  -  Contraindication to dual antiplatelet therapy
  -  Severe coagulopathy [OR] or thrombocytopenia
  -  Active infection
  -  [NOT] Unavailable for follow-up
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
