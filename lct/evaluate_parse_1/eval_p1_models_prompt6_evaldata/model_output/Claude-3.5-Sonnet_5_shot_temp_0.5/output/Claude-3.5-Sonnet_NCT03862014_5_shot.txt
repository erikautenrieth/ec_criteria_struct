  -  Patients [AND] and parents of the patients who accept to participate [AND] and sign the informed consent

  -  Teeth that are previously restored [OR] or have caries
  -  Patients who have orthodontic treatment
  -  Patients who are [NOT] not cooperative for the dental procedure
