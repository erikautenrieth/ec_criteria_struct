Inclusion Criteria:
 - All patients over 18 years of age undergoing gastric resection surgery due to cancer [AND] within or [NOT] not of an intensified recovery program (Eras) with any level of compliance with the protocol (from 0-100%).
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients undergoing emergency surgery
 - Endoscopic procedures
 - [NOT] Non-oncological gastric surgery
 - Patients who refuse to participate
