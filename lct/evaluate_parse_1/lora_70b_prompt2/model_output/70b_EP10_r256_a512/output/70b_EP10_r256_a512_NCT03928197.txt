Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age between 25 years and 60 years
 - For the volunteers with venous insufficiency: clearly visual venous insufficiency (Ceap 1 and 2)
 - BMI between 24 and 30
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Volunteers using medication (Exception for contraception)
 - Volunteers having comorbidities
 - Pregnant women
 - Women [AND] who menstruate on the test date
 - Volunteers with an implanted electrical device (IPG, [OR] Pacemaker)
