Inclusion Criteria:
 - Severe CopD (Fev1 [forced expiratory volume in 1 second] / FVC [forced vital capacity] < 70% et FeV1 < 50%)
 - Informed consent
 - Affiliation to a social security scheme
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Effort oxygen therapy
 - Cardiovascular, [OR] neuromuscular [OR] or musculoskeletal disorders that can provide significant dyspnea [OR] or limit exercise
 - Legal incapacity
 - Low or [NOT] no cooperation anticipated
