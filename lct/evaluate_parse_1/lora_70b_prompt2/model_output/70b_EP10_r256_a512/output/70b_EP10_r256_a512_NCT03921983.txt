Inclusion Criteria:
 - COPD diagnostic
 - Both sex
 - 18 < Age <80 years old
 - LTot- group : PaO2 ≥ 65 mmHg [AND] and SaO2≥ 92%
 - LTot+ group: long term oxygenotherapy prescribed for more than 3 months, with daily use between 12 and 15 hours
Patients in both groups will be matched for age (± 5 years), sex and physical activity
estimated by GPAQ questionnaire (± 15%)
Mets.min/week
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Recent cardiorespiratory exacerbation <6 weeks
 - Pulmonary rehabilitation program during the last 2 months
 - Continuous Ltot (24 hours) [OR] or ofambulation O2 therapy alone
 - Anticoagulant drugs
 - Hematocrit outside of normal range (35-50%)
