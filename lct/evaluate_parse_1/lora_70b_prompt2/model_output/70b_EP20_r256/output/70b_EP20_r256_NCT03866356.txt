Inclusion Criteria:
 - The inclusion criteria were being female, ≥ 18 years, having an initial diagnosis of SI, being literate, having [NOT] no sensory disorder affecting communication, and agreeing to participate in the research
Exclusion Criteria:
 - The exclusion criteria were pregnancy, presence of a urinary tract infection [OR] and previous treatment [OR] for SI. The removal criteria were patient [NOT] non-compliance with the protocol, [NOT] failure in contacting the patient and the patient's wish to withdraw from the study.
