Inclusion Criteria:
 - Screened to be generally healthy [AND] as determined by standard medical assessment on physical [AND] mental health
 - Tanner stage 2 based on breast development for girls, [AND] and pubic hair in boys. Girls will have to be premenarcheal
 - Having obtained his/her informed consent [AND] and parents or legal guardians consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - History of serious medical conditions such as intestinal [OR] or severe metabolic diseases, disorders such as diabetes, [OR] renal, [OR] hepatic [OR] or pancreatic diseases, disorders, ulcer, [OR] hyperthyroidism, [OR] malignance, [OR] chronic malnutrition, [OR] anaemia
 - History [OR] or on therapy with medication known to interfere with bone metabolism such as steroids, [OR] hormones, [OR] diuretics, [OR] cortisone [OR] or anti-seizure medication
 - Taking regularly any nutritional supplements [AND] unwilling to stop for the study
