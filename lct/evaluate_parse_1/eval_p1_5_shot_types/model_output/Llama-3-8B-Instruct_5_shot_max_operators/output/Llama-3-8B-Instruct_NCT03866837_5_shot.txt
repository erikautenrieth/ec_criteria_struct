Inclusion Criteria:
 - vaginal delivery
 - an infant age of 6 months [OR] (±3 weeks)
 - mother [AND] ≥15 years of age
 - infant [AND] still breastfeeding
 - anticipated residence in the area for the study duration.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] inability to provide informed consent
 - hemoglobin < 70 g/L
 - Z scores for weight-for-age (WAZ) [OR] or weight-for-height (WHZ) <3,
 - [OR] any maternal [OR] infant chronic illness
 - administration of any infant vitamin [OR] mineral supplements for the past 2 months
 - [OR] any history of infant antibiotic treatment.
