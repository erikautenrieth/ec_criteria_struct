Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age ≥18 years [AND] and ≤ 90 years
 - Tumor diagnosed as Glioblastoma Multiforme on MRI with distinct ring-like pattern of contrast enhancement [AND] with thick irregular walls [AND] and a core area reduced signal suggestive of tumour necrosis as assessed by the surgeon
 - Tumors situated in or near eloquent areas; motor cortex, sensory cortex, subcortical pyramidal tract [OR] or speech areas as indicated on MRI (Sawaya Grading II [AND] II)
 - The tumor is suitable for resection [AND] according to neurosurgeon
 - Karnofsky performance scale 80 [OR] more
 - Written Informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Tumors of the cerebellum, brain stem [OR] or midline
 - Multifocal contrast enhancing lesions
 - Substantial non-contrast enhancing tumor areas suggesting low grade gliomas with malignant transformation
 - Medical reasons precluding MRI [AND] eg, pacemaker
 - Inability to give consent because of [OR] language barrier
 - Psychiatric history
 - Previous brain tumour surgery
 - Previous low-grade glioma
 - Second primary malignancy within the past 5 years [AND] with the exception of adequately treated in situ carcinoma of any organ [OR] basal cell carcinoma of the skin
 - Severe aphasia [OR] dysphasia
