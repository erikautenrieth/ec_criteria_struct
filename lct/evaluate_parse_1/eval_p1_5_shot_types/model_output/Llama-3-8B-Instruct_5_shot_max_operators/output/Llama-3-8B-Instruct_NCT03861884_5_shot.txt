Inclusion Criteria:
 - Ability to give informed consent.
 - Either pathological, genetic or imaging biomarker evidence [OR] to suggest Fronto-temporal Lobar Degeneration, Alzheimer's Disease [OR] or Vascular Cognitive Impairment, [OR] or defined clinical diagnoses.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] lack of mental capacity to consent to study involvement.
 - Not speaking English before age 5 years.
 - Learning disability.
 - MRI contraindications.
