Inclusion Criteria:
 - BMI [AND] 45-60 kg/m2 [OR]
 - Failure of at least one weight loss medication [AND]
 - Previous Gastric Bypass Surgery [AND] lead to less than 50% of excess weight lost by 2 years after surgery [AND]
 - Significant Loss of Control over eating [AND]
 - Subject [AND] is able to attend all scheduled clinic appointments on their own [OR] with a caregiver [AND]
 - Premenopausal women [AND] must agree to use acceptable methods of birth control [AND]
 - Participants [AND] provide voluntary, decisionally capable, and appropriately informed consent [AND]
 - Subject [AND] is able to comply w/ all testing and follow-up requirements defined by the study protocol [AND]
 - Participant [AND] has no immediate plan for relocation beyond 6 hours of the study site [AND]
 - Proficiency with the English language [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Subject [AND] has an implanted medical device that delivers electrical energy to the brain [NOT]
 - Subject [AND] has an implantable cardiac pacemaker, defibrillator, or neurostimulator [NOT]
 - Subject [AND] is likely to require repeat MR imaging after implant of the RNS Neurostimulator and Leads [NOT]
 - Subject [AND] is unable to fit into CT scanner (500lb upper weight limit for CT scanner) [NOT]
 - Inability to provide informed consent to treatment [NOT]
