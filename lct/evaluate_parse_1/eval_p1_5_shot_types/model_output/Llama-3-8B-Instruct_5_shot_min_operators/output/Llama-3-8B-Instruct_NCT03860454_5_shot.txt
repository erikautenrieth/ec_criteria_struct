<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
  - LMNA+ cases with pathogenic LMNA mutations [AND] for LMNA+ and heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of LMNA+ patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust [OR] DCMWT cases: patients with heart muscle failure but with wild-type lamin gene. Heart myocardial samples from the explanted hearts of DCMWT patients who are scheduled to undergo clinically indicated heart transplantation at the Papworth Hospital NHS Trust [OR] HV (controls): matched to cases.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Needle-phobia that would preclude blood-letting
  - Participants unwilling to consent
  - Patients that have a conventional contraindication for cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) [OR] Patients that have had a blood transfusion within the last month [OR] patients having haemodialysis will be excluded.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
