Inclusion Criteria:
 - Receiving HHD or ICHDF for more than 3 months [AND] and less than 36 months.
 - Haemoglobin equal to or greater than 100 g/L [OR] at enrolment.
 - Willing [AND] and physically able to undertake the study assessments/tests
 - Willing to provide blood for storage [AND] and future analysis
 - Able to give informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] Living donor transplant or change to peritoneal dialysis planned
 - Physical assessments contraindicated for the following clinical reasons
       - Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS) within the last 3 months [OR] (chest pain, ECG changes or typical biomarker elevation).
       - [OR] Any current uncontrolled cardiac dysrhythmias causing symptoms (chest pain, palpitations, syncope or dizziness)
       - [OR] Symptomatic aortic stenosis
       - [OR] New York Heart Association grade IV Heart failure
       - [OR] Severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease
       - [OR] Acute pulmonary embolus or pulmonary infarction in the last 3 months
       - [OR] Current acute myocarditis or pericarditis
       - [OR] Suspected or known dissecting aneurysm
       - [OR] Acute systemic infection, accompanied by fever, body aches or swollen lymph glands
 - [NOT] Pregnancy
 - [NOT] Life expectancy of less than twelve months
