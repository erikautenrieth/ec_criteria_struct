Inclusion Criteria:
 - Type 2 diabetes mellitus [AND] on metformin therapy for at least 3 months

Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] Type 1 diabetes mellitus
 - [OR] other forms of diabetes mellitus (e.g. gestational, secondary to pancreatitis, etc.)
 - [NOT] currently taking other oral antidiabetic medications
 - [OR] insulin therapy
 - [NOT] having a history of pancreatitis
 - [OR] other gastrointestinal disorders that may affect glucose absorption
