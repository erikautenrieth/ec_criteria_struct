Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
  - All adults undergoing cardiac surgery with CPB will be considered for enrollment.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
  - Factors that affect immune function [OR] AKI assessment are the basis for exclusion criteria:
    - Concurrent disease associated with immunosuppression [AND] including malignancy, chronic infection [OR] e.g., HIV, Hepatitis C, organ transplant and immunosuppressant medications
    - [NOT] Documented acute infection with the past 1 month [OR] e.g., pneumonia, urinary tract infection
    - Prednisone [OR] or other steroid use currently [OR] within the past one month
    - AKI at the time of surgery
    - ESRD requiring renal replacement therapy
    - Estimated GFR <45 mL/min [AND] as judged by the Chronic Kidney Disease Epidemiology Collaboration (CKD-EPI) equation
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
