Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patient requiring an electroneuromyogram [AND] whose indication [OR] allows the application of a standard protocol of examination.
 - Patient [AND] that has given his/her [NOT] free and informed consent.
 - Patient [AND] that signed the consent form.
 - Patient [AND] with an health insurance plan.
 - The patient is [AND] at least 18 years old ([≥]).
 - The patient is [AND] under 80 years old ([≤]).
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patient [NOT] with symptoms of neuropathy during the screening.
 - Patient [NOT] whose Neurological severity score (NSS) score is [≥] 3.
 - Patient [NOT] whose neurological examination is [NOT] abnormal and suggestive of neuropathy.
 - Patient [NOT] presenting a pathology that could cause neuropathy (e.g. diabetes, renal failure, etc.).
 - Patient [NOT] already participating in a category 1 research study (Jardé law).
 - Patient [NOT] in an exclusion period determined by another study.
 - Patient [NOT] under the protection of justice, under guardianship or under curatorship.
 - Patient [NOT] refusing to sign the consent form.
 - Patient [NOT] for which it is [NOT] impossible to provide information about the study.
 - Patient [NOT] pregnant, parturient, or breastfeeding.
