Inclusion Criteria:
 - Having mild [OR] moderate severity of AD
 - Lack of communication difficulties [AND] or problems
 - Volunteering to participate in the study
 - [NOT] Cerebrovascular event
 - Having disturbing the balance and coordination of neurological disease [AND] such as Parkinson [OR] MS
 - Mini Mental Test Score [AND] 18 to 24 points from being scored
 - Being 65-80 years old
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients diagnosed with [NOT] dementia (infection, vascular, hematological diseases), patients who could not be contacted during the follow-up period [OR] who did not come to control
 - Patients with endocrine disorder [OR] fluid-electrolyte imbalance [OR] infection
 - Patients with malignancy [AND] malignancy, chemotherapy [AND] radiotherapy
 - Patients with delirium [OR] depression
 - Those with protective sensation loss [NOT] (those with Semmes-Weinstein Monofilament Thickness 4.56 [AND] above)
 - Patients with lower [OR] upper extremity amputations at any level
