Inclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. age > 18 years [AND];
 2. confirmed diagnosis of prediabetes or T2D (documented A1c of ≥ 5.7%, 2-hour post-load glucose of >140, or FBG of >100) [OR];
 3. a family member or peer willing to participate and attend all sessions as a social influencer [AND];
 4. proficiency in English of at least one member of each dyad [OR];
 5. willingness to provide written consent [AND];
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
 <EDITED_CRITERIA>
 1. [NOT] type 1 diabetes or diabetes secondary to other conditions (e.g. steroid-induced, pancreatic insufficiency, or chemotherapy-induced);
 2. malignancy or life-threatening illness with life expectancy of <5 years [OR];
 3. end-stage disease or serious illness that prohibits participation (e.g. end-stage renal disease or class IV congestive heart failure) [OR];
 4. [NOT] inability to perform unsupervised physical activity;
 5. pregnancy [OR];
 6. diagnosed cognitive deficits or limited decision-making capacity [OR];
 7. alcohol or substance abuse [OR];
 8. homelessness or no fixed address [OR];
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
