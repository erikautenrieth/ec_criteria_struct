  - Patients with a soft tissue sarcoma [AND] of the thigh

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] History of clinically significant medical conditions including: Cardiovascular: Atrial Fibrillation, Ventricular Fibrillation, Significant Coronary Artery Disease [OR] Hepatic: Viral or Autoimmune Hepatitis, Cirrhosis of the Liver, Liver Metabolism Disorders [OR] Renal: Any form of renal impairment
