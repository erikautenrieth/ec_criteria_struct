<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Being older than 18 [OR]
 - have a diagnosis of HIV
 - present at least one of the following symptoms during the preceding two weeks: sleep problems [OR] fatigue [OR] pain [OR] anxiety [OR] symptoms of depression
 - [AND] understand and speak French
 - [AND] be able to follow the instructions to learn the relaxation technique

Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] be able to provide informed consent
 - [OR] have a medical condition that would interfere with participation in the study
 - [OR] be currently receiving treatment for a mental health condition
 - [OR] have a history of substance abuse
 - [OR] be unable to attend the study sessions
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
