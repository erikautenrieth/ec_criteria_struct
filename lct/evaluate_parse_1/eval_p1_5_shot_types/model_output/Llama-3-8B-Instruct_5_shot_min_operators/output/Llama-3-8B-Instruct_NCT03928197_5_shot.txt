 - Age between 25 years [AND] and 60 years
 - For the volunteers [AND] with venous insufficiency: clearly visual venous insufficiency [OR] (CEAP 1 [OR] and 2)
 - BMI between 24 [AND] and 30
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Volunteers [NOT] using medication (exception for contraception)
 - Volunteers [NOT] having comorbidities
 - Pregnant women
