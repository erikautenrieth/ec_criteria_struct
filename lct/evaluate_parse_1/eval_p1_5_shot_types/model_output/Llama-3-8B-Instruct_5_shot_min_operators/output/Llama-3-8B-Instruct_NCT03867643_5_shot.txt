Inclusion Criteria:
 - Children who are 6-12 years old [OR] in Chongqing
 - After birth, three injections of Hep B were inoculated in time according to the procedure of 0, 1 and 6 months [AND] (The vaccination certificate record)
 - [NOT] No HBV booster vaccine given since primary HBV immunization
 - Children or guardian [AND] knew and agreed to accept the study
 - [AND] Follow - up study and blood collection after vaccination can be accepted

Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] Unwilling or impossible to participate in this research project;
 - Have a history of allergy [OR] have had a serious vaccine reaction
 - Have immune impairment diseases [AND] appear immune impairment;
 - Immunosuppressive treatment [OR] receive any injection or oral administration of cortisone or cancer chemotherapy;
 - [OR] Any kind of vaccine or any kind of observation drug has been vaccinated in the past four weeks;
 - [OR] Any acute disease or other infection requiring antibiotic or antiviral treatment in the past four weeks;
 - Fever symptoms occurred in the past week [AND] (axillary temperature ≥ 38 ℃ )
 - Blood transfusion experience;
 - [OR] Has a history of more serious infectious diseases (five types of hepatitis, AIDS, syphilis, gonorrhea, etc. );
 - Hepatitis B infection or carriers [AND] (lineal relationship);
 - Abnormal physical examination
