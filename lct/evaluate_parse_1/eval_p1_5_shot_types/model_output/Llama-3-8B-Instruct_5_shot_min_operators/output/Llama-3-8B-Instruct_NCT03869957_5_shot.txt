Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients admitted in the non-critical areas of the Instituto Nacional de Ciencias Médicas y Nutrición Salvador Zubirán (INCMNSZ) [AND] with a nutritional risk between January 2019 and July 2020 [OR] will be considered eligible.
 - Patients [AND] with recent diagnosis of IF type II (an evolution >28 days) [AND] originate from various gastrointestinal or systemic diseases (short bowel, intestinal fistula, intestinal dysmotility, mechanical obstruction, and extensive small bowel mucosal disease).
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients [NOT] with contraindications for PN
 - Patients [NOT] with known allergies to the components of the PN formula
 - Severe liver [OR] or renal insufficiency
 - Uncontrolled diabetes mellitus
 - Certain acute and life-threatening conditions
 - Immunological diseases (such as autoimmune diseases, human immunodeficiency virus infection, cancer, etc.)
 - Those that take immunosuppressant medications
 - Severe hemorrhagic disorders
 - Pregnant [OR] or lactating
