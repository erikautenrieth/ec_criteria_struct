<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
Female subjects between 18 [AND] 65 years of age currently taking spironolactone for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris [OR] female subjects between 18 [AND] 65 years of age currently taking oral antibiotics for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris [AND] subjects able to complete an online survey in English

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] subjects less than 18 years of age or older than 65 years of age [OR] subjects that have ever been diagnosed with any mood disorder by a clinician prior to starting spironolactone (history of mood disorder or current mood disorder) [OR] subjects taking hormonal contraception (including but not limited to oral contraceptive pills) for a duration of less than 6 months [OR] subjects that have ever been diagnosed with any type of sexual dysfunction prior to starting spironolactone [OR] subjects not able to complete an online survey in English [OR] subjects taking oral isotretinoin for a diagnosis of acne [OR] subjects that are currently pregnant
