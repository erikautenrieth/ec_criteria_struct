Inclusion Criteria:
 1. the patient [AND] should be completely edentulous for at least 6 months [OR] and/or first class jaw relation.
 2. adequate bone for an implant length of at least 10 mm [AND] and diameter of at least 2.75 mm.
 3. the patients [AND] should be able to speak [AND] write in Arabic.

Exclusion Criteria:
 1) patients [NOT] who received radiotherapy to the head or neck region for tumors.
 2) a history of chemotherapy.
 3) patients [NOT] on long-term steroids, or bisphosphonates.
 4) heavy smokers.
 5) patients [NOT] with physical [OR] mental disabilities that interfere with the maintenance of implants.
 6) uncontrolled systematic disease that could compromise implant surgery.
 7) extreme bruxism.
 8) infectious disease.
 9) temporomandibular joint dysfunction.
