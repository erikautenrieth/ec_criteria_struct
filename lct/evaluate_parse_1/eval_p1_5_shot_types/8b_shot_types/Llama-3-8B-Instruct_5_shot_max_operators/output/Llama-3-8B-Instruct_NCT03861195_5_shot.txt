Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age [AND] ≥18 years
 - BMI [AND] ≤40 kg/m2
 - Indication for hysterectomy for benign disease [OR] prophylactic surgery due to hereditary cancer
 - Indication for radical hysterectomy [AND] and/or pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for cervical cancer
 - Indication for modified radical hysterectomy [AND] and/or pelvic-/para-aortic (sentinel-node) lymphadenectomy for endometrial cancer
 - Size of uterus [AND] and vagina allows for retrieval by the vaginal route in cancer patients
 - Written informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Known [NOT] extensive intra-abdominal adhesions
 - Anaesthesiological contraindications to laparoscopy
 - Women with pacemaker [OR] other implants where electrosurgery is to be avoided
 - Women with known [NOT] defects of the hemostasis
 - Pregnancy
 - Other internal [OR] anatomical criteria that preclude a minimal invasive approach
 - Inability to understand patient information
