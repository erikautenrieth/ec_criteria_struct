Inclusion Criteria:
 - Burn patient treated as outpatient [AND] with a burn surface area > or = 5% of the total body surface area [AND] mainly 2nd degree burn
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Psychiatric antecedents [OR] claustrophobia
 - Heavy hearing [OR] visual impairment
 - Face burn [OR] conjunctivitis
 - [NOT] consent not obtained
