Inclusion Criteria:
 - Men [AND] and women age 65 years [OR] older
 - Current PD diagnosis with symptoms severity at Hoehn & Yahr (H&Y) stage 1-4 [AND] based on an expert assessment (movement disorders neurologist report [OR] or telemedicine evaluation)
 - Willing [AND] and able to continue in follow-up for at least 2 years
 - Willing [AND] and able to provide informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
 - History of hip fracture
 - Any use of a bisphosphonate drug within the last 12 months
 - Use of any other osteoporosis treatment (such as SERMs [OR] and denosumab) within the last 6 months
 - Tooth extraction [OR] or invasive dental procedures within the past 30 days [OR] planned/scheduled extraction/procedure in the next 12 months
 - Non-ambulatory, i.e., [NOT] able to walk without assistance of another person
 - Undergoing kidney dialysis
 - A diagnosis of multiple myeloma [OR] Paget's disease
 - Unable to speak [OR] read English sufficiently to complete informed consent
 - Any other criteria, which would make the patient [NOT] suitable to participate in this study as determined by the study staff (e.g., an uncontrolled drug [OR] and/or alcohol addiction)
