Inclusion Criteria:
 - Carpal tunnel syndrome diagnosed either by physical exam [OR] EMG
 - Age >18.

Exclusion Criteria:
 - Allergy to bupivacaine
 - Pre-operative opioid usage within the last six months
 - Patients [AND] who are allergic to Percocet [OR] acetaminophen
 - Patients [AND] who are taking tricyclic antidepressants [AND] (interaction with bupivacaine)
 - Vulnerable populations [NOT] (prisoners [OR] or pregnant women)
