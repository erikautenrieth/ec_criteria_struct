Inclusion Criteria:
 - Non syndromic patients
 - Medically fit for Surgery
 - Patient with Primary, complete cleft lip
 - Patient's age [AND] younger than six months
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patient with syndromic cleft lip
 - Previous operated cases
 - Incomplete cleft lip
 - Patient [NOT] older than six months
 - Patients [OR] with any systemic condition
