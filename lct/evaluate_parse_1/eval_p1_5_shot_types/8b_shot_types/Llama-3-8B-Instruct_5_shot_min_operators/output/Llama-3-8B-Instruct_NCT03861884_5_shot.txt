Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Ability to give informed consent.
 2. [OR] pathological, genetic or imaging biomarker evidence to suggest Fronto-temporal Lobar Degeneration, Alzheimer's Disease or Vascular Cognitive Impairment, [OR] defined clinical diagnoses.

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. [NOT] Lack of mental capacity to consent to study involvement.
 2. Not speaking English [OR] before age 5 years.
 3. Learning disability.
 4. MRI contraindications.
