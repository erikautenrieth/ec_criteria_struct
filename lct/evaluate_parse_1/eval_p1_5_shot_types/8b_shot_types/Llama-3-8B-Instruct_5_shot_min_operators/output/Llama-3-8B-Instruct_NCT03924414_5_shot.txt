Inclusion Criteria:
  - Men [AND] and women age 65 years [OR] older
  - Current PD diagnosis with symptoms severity at Hoehn & Yahr (H&Y) stage 1-4 [AND] based on an expert assessment (movement disorders neurologist report or telemedicine evaluation)
  - Willing [AND] and able to continue in follow-up for at least 2 years
  - Willing [AND] and able to provide informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] History of hip fracture
  - Any use of a bisphosphonate drug [OR] within the last 12 months
  - Use of any other osteoporosis treatment (such as SERMs and denosumab) [OR] within the last 6 months
  - Tooth extraction [OR] or invasive dental procedures [OR] within the past 30 days or planned/scheduled extraction/procedure [OR] in the next 12 months
  - Non-ambulatory, i.e., [NOT] unable to walk without assistance of another person
  - Undergoing kidney dialysis
  - A diagnosis of multiple myeloma [OR] Paget's disease
  - [NOT] Unable to speak or read English sufficiently to complete informed consent
  - Any other criteria, which would make the patient [NOT] unsuitable to participate in this study as determined by the study staff (e.g., an uncontrolled drug and/or alcohol addiction)
