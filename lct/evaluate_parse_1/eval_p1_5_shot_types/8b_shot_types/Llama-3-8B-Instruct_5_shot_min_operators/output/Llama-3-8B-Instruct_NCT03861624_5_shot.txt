Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Skeletal maturity
  2. AO/OTA 42 open tibia fractures
  3. Wound [AND] primarily closeable [OR] (no flap or delay in closure due to contamination needed)
  4. Palpable pedal pulses [AND] (no vascular injury sustained)
  5. Presentation [AND] within 24 hours from injury

Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] current injury is a pathologic fracture
  2. Sustained bilateral tibia fracture
  3. Sustained comminuted femur fracture
  4. Sustained severe Traumatic Brain Injury (GCS<12) ***
  5. Sustained severe spinal cord injury (lower extremity paresis/paralysis)
  6. Sustained severe burns (>10% total body surface area (TBSA) [OR] or >5% TBSA with full thickness or circumferential injury)
  7. Prior ipsilateral leg injury requiring surgery
  8. Prior or current lower limb deformity or abnormality
  9. [NOT] unable to complete follow-up visits
