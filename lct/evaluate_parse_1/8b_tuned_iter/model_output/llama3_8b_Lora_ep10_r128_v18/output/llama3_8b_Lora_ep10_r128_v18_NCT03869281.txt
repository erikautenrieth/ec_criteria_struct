Inclusion Criteria (PTA group)
 - T1D patients [AND] who received their first PTA, [OR] or second PTA only if their first graft was explanted within a week, between 2 January 2005 and 31 December 2017
 - age ≥18 [AND] and < 65 years
 - willingness to participate in this observational study
Inclusion Criteria (control group)
 - Type 1 diabetes
 - age ≥18 years [AND] and < 65 years
 - willingness to participate in this observational study
 - minimum disease duration of 5 years
 - brittle diabetes, as defined by the occurrence of frequent acute complications (ketoacidosis [OR] or severe hypoglycaemic episodes requiring third-part assistance [OR] or unawareness [AND] despite well-conducted intensive insulin therapy)
 - presence of at least one diabetes-related complication (either neuropathy, [OR] retinopathy, [OR] subclinical nephropathy).
Exclusion Criteria (both groups)
 - conditions other than T1D known to affect bone health (i.e., [NOT] non-compensated hypothyroidism, [OR] hyperthyroidism, [OR] hyperparathyroidism, [OR] inflammatory bowel disease, [OR] malignancy, [OR] rheumatoid arthritis, [OR] hypogonadism, severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, [OR] hepatic insufficiency, alcohol intake ≥ 3 units/day ),
 - use of drugs that can affect bone metabolism (e.g., bisphosphonates, [OR] anticonvulsants, [OR] hormone replacement therapy) other than glucocorticoids [OR] and immunosuppressants
 - recipients of simultaneous pancreas kidney transplantation, [OR] pancreatic islet transplantation, [OR] multiple PTAs, other transplants
 - BMI <18.5 [OR] or ≥ 30 kg/m2
