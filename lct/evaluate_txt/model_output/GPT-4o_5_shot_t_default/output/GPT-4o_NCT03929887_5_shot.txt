<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Diagnosis of glomerulonephritis (MCD, [OR] FSGS, [OR] MN, [OR] IgAN, [OR] RPGN, [OR] lupus nephritis [AND] and etc.) on first diagnostic kidney biopsy, [AND] as per specified pathology definitions
 - First diagnostic kidney biopsy since 2010
 - Willingness to comply with study requirements, [OR] including intention to fully participate in protocol-specified follow-up at a clinical study site
 - Informed consent [AND] and, where age appropriate, informed assent (clinical data, [OR] human derived materials including genomic data)

Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] Unwillingness to give informed consent
 - [NOT] Not enough kidney tissue to confirm a diagnosis (adequate tissue: more than 2 cores of kidney tissues (10 x 1.2 mm) [AND] including over 10-15 glomerulus each)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
