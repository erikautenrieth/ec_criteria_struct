<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Be a patient in the Bellevue Hospital Center ED.
 - Be ≥18 years old.
 - Screen positive for homelessness risk using a homelessness risk screening tool (HRST).
 - Screen positive for unhealthy alcohol [OR] or drug use.
 - [AND] Able to provide consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Medically (e.g., critically ill) [OR] or psychiatrically unstable.
 - [NOT] Unable to provide informed consent for other reason (e.g., cognitive deficit, profound intoxication).
 - Incarcerated [OR] or in police custody.
 - [NOT] Unable to understand [AND] and speak English.
 - Lives outside NYC [OR] and/or cannot give a NYC ZIP code.
 - [NOT] Already homeless (residing in a shelter [OR] or on the streets).
 - [NOT] Does not have a telephone where can be reached for follow-up.
 - [AND] Has already received specialized peer navigator/addiction social worker services during current ED visit.
 - [AND] Has already participated in the study.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
