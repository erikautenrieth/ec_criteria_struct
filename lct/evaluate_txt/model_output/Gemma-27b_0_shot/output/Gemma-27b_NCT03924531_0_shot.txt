Inclusion Criteria:
  - over 21 years of age [AND] able to understand [AND] speak English [AND] self-report of having difficulty with a healthy diet [AND] engaging in regular physical activity.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - On chemotherapy for cancer treatment [OR] Current substance abuse disorder (i.e., drugs, alcohol) [OR] Current diagnoses of Post-traumatic stress disorders, severe anxiety, [OR] severe depression [OR] On hemodialysis [OR] Pregnant. (Pregnant women may develop Preeclampsia, which is a pregnancy complication characterized by high blood pressure)
