Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Primary liver cancer patients [AND] ICD 10 is C22.0 [AND] Barcelona stage (BCLC stage) 0-A.
  2. At least 20 years of age.
  3. [NOT] Mental illness.
  4. Patients [AND] can communicate in Mandarin [OR] Taiwanese.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] Know himself condition.
  2. [NOT] Conscious patients.
  3. Patients with liver cancer resection [OR] partial liver resection were performed within 3 months.
