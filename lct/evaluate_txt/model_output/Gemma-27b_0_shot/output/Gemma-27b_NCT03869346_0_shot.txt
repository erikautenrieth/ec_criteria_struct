Inclusion Criteria:
  - American Society of Anesthesiologists GradeⅠ [OR] Ⅱ [AND] Patients undergo elective surgery for benign Reproductive diseases [OR] breast disease. (including laparoscopic ovarian cyst removal, laparoscopic tubal ligation, laparoscopic tubal drainage, laparoscopic salpingoplasty, etc.)
  - Aged 18-45 years
  - Body Mass Index 18-25kg/m2, [OR] body weight 40-65 kilograms
  - Agreed to participate the research
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Difficult airway
  - upper respiratory tract infection within 2 weeks
  - history of allergy [OR] long-term use of propofol [AND] opioids
  - self-rating anxiety scale before operation indicates anxiety
  - pregnancy [OR] lactation.
