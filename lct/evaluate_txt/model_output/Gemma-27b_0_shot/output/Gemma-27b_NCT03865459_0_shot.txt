Inclusion Criteria:
  -  age > 18 years
  -  female gender
  -  rigid cystoscopy for the first time
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  presence [AND] any contraindication [OR] cystoscopy (such as lidocaine allergy, urinary tract infection, [OR] urethra-related anatomical disorders)
  -  any analgesic used 24 hours before cystoscopy
  -  manipulations such as Double-J stent placement [OR] removal, [OR] bladder biopsy.
