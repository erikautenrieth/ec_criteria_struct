Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients presenting acute myocardial infarction [AND] hemodynamic stability at the diagnosis
  - Patients undergoing coronary artery bypass [OR] in Dijon University hospital
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients with Acute pulmonary oedema
  - Patients with cardiorespiratory arrest before coronary angiography
  - [NOT]hemodynamically unstable patient at diagnosis
  - patients requiring combined surgery
  - patients with unstable angina
