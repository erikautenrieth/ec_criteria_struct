Inclusion Criteria:
  - Meets diagnostic criteria for fibromyalgia
  - Has insomnia
  - Have internet access [AND] a device that can access the web-based program.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Active sleep disorder [OR] which is [NOT] treated (obstructive sleep apnea, obesity hypoventilation syndrome)
  - Active psychiatric disorder (Bipolar affective disorder, anxiety, depression, schizophrenia) [OR] which is [NOT] optimally managed.
  - Chronic fatigue syndrome
  - Morbid obesity
