Inclusion Criteria:
Persons [AND] with
  - stroke [OR] cerebrovascular disease
  - traumatic brain injury
  - multiple sclerosis
  - spinal cord injuries [OR] (including spinal cord lesions i.e. myelopathies etc.)
Exclusion Criteria:
Persons [AND] with
  - heterotopic ossifications,
  - chronic administration of drugs, [AND] which promote fat increase
  - chronic administration of drugs, [AND] which promote bone [OR] muscle loss
  - co-existing other diseases such as: endocrinopathies, muscular dystrophies, [NOT] non-alcoholic fatty liver disease.
