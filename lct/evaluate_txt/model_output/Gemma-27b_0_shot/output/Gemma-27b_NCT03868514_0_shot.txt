Inclusion Criteria:
Age [≥ 18]
[AND] Indications of breast reconstruction: histologically confirmed breast cancer, precancerous lesions (DCIS, LCIS), mutation carrier with increased breast cancer risk, strong family history (lifetime risk > 15%)
[AND] The patient is capable to realise the nature, aims [AND] possible consequences of the clinical trial (MPG §20.2.1)
[AND] Patient information has been provided [AND] all written consents of the patient are available
Exclusion Criteria:
Metastatic breast cancer
[OR] Patient with known contraindications against mesh-assisted [OR] plastic-reconstructive breast surgery according to the instruction for use
[OR] Patient is kept in an institution under judicial [OR] official orders (MPG §20.3)
[OR] Participate in another operative clinical trial, if it relates to the area of reconstructive breast surgery [AND] [OR] influences the primary endpoint of the clinical trial
