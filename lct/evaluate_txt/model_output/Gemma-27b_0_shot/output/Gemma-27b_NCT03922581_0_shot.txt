Inclusion Criteria:
  -  ability to provide informed consent
  -  willingness to participate in study
  -  self-identity as African American
  -  history of childhood [OR] adult trauma exposure [AND] 3 total criterion A traumas
  -  positive PTSD screen [AND] PC-PTSD ≥ 3
  -  positive MDD screen [AND] PHQ-9 ≥ 5
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  presence of intellectual disability, bipolar, [OR] psychotic disorder
  -  presence of current substance use disorder [AND] past 1 month [AND] see cut off scores in psychological measures below
  -  active suicidality
