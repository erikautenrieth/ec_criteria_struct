Inclusion Criteria:
  -  All individuals affected by NMDs [AND] attending our Centre for Home Mechanical Ventilation between January 2015 [AND] December 2018 [AND] who required daytime MPV as an extension of nocturnal NIV
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients [WITH] [NOT] need for daytime ventilation
  -  patients [WITH] [NOT] effective NIV during sleep as assessed by the measurement of TcCO2>49mmHg (S)
  -  patients [WITH] uncontrollable oral leaks [OR] episode of acute respiratory infection during the trials
