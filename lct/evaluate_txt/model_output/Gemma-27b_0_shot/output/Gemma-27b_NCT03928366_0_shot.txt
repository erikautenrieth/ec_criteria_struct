Exclusion Criteria:

Allergy to propofol [OR] remifentanil [OR] to some of its excipients. Body mass index (BMI) <18 [OR] > 30 kg / m2. Pregnancy. Airway [OR] ventilation criteria hard. [NOT]Absence of accompanying adult at the end of the study.
