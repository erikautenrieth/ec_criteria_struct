Inclusion Criteria:Adolescents
  1. Age 12-18
  2. [AND] currently meets Diagnostic and Statistical Manual-5 criteria for Anorexia Nervosa
  3. [AND] medically stable for outpatient treatment
  4. [AND] fluent in English
  5. [AND] [NOT] co-morbid condition that would exclude participation
  6. [AND] medical clearance from primary care physician [AND] permission to speak to Primary Care Physician about clinical issues
  7. [AND] biological parent [OR] primary caregiver willing to engage in treatment [AND] who live with the adolescent
Inclusion Criteria:Parents
  1. Age >18
  2. [AND] child with a diagnoses of AN
  3. [AND] both parents willing to participate
  4. [AND] fluent in English
  5. [AND] [NOT] co-morbid condition that would exclude participation
Exclusion Criteria: Adolescent
  1. Adolescent outside age range
  2. [OR] adolescent adopted
  3. [OR] pregnant adolescent
  4. [AND] presence of: pervasive developmental disability, psychosis, bipolar disorder, substance abuse, autism spectrum disorder, [OR] intellectual disability
  5. [AND] presence of: a brain disorder [OR] injury (such as TBI) that could impact the ability to engage in treatment
  6. [AND] use of anti-psychotic medication
  7. [AND] concurrent psychosocial therapy
Exclusion Criteria: Parents
  1. [AND] presence of: pervasive developmental disability, psychosis, bipolar disorder, substance abuse, autism spectrum disorder, [OR] intellectual disability.
  2. [AND] presence of: a brain disorder [OR] injury (such as TBI) that could impact the ability to engage in treatment
  3. [AND] use of anti-psychotic medication
