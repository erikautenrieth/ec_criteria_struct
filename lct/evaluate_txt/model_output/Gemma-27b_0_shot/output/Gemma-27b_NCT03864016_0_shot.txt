Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Person [AND] who has given written consent
  -  Adult patient
  -  Patient undergoing coronary artery bypass surgery [AND] with CEC, [OR] without valve surgery, by sternotomy.
  -  ASA <4.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Person [NOT] affiliated to the national health insurance system
  -  Person subject to legal protection (curatorship, guardianship)
  -  Person [AND] who has been deemed mentally incompetent
  -  Pregnant, parturient [OR] breastfeeding woman
  -  Adult [NOT] able [OR] unwilling to provide consent
  -  Patient [AND] with preoperative cognitive dysfunction (MMS <13) (APPENDIX 6),
  -  Patient [AND] with morphine intolerance,
  -  Patient [AND] on long-term opioid treatment,
  -  Emergency surgery,
  -  Eye disease, corneal injury, [OR] wearing contact lenses, [AND] a neurological disease that can influence the pupillary reflex,
  -  Refusal to participate
