Inclusion Criteria:
  - Woman ≥ 18 years old [AND] undergoing laparoscopic surgery [OR] endometriosis [OR] uterine myoma's [OR] hysterectomy [OR] colpopexy.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Woman < 18 years old
  - Males
  - Pregnancy
  - Conditions associated with chronic pain, such as peripheral neuropathy, pathology of the vertebral column [AND] osteo-articular disease.
  - Conditions causing acute pain e.g. abdominal trauma
