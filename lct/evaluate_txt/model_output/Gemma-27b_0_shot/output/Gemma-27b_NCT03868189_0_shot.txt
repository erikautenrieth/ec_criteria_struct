Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient [AND] requiring an electroneuromyogram [AND] whose indication allows the application of a standard protocol of examination.
  -  Patient [AND] [NOT] free [AND] informed consent.
  -  Patient [AND] signed the consent form.
  -  Patient [AND] an health insurance plan.
  -  The patient is at least 18 years old (≥).
  -  [AND] the patient is under 80 years old (≤).
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patient [OR] symptoms of neuropathy during the screening.
  -  Patient [OR] Neurological severity score (NSS) score is ≥ 3.
  -  Patient [OR] neurological examination is abnormal [AND] suggestive of neuropathy.
  -  Patient presenting a pathology that could cause neuropathy (e.g. diabetes, renal failure, etc.).
  -  Patient already participating in a category 1 research study (Jardé law).
  -  Patient in an exclusion period determined by another study.
  -  Patient under the protection of justice, under guardianship [OR] under curatorship.
  -  Patient refusing to sign the consent form.
  -  Patient for which it is [NOT] possible to provide information about the study.
  -  Pregnant patient, parturient, [OR] breastfeeding.
