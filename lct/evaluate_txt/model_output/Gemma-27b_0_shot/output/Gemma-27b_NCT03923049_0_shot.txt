Inclusion Criteria:
1. Subjects older than 18 years of age [AND] with high suspicion of cardiac sarcoidosis [OR] biopsy proven extracardiac sarcoidosis [OR] positive HRS criteria will be enrolled.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Coronary artery disease
  2. Insulin dependent diabetes
  3. Claustrophobia
  4. Pregnancy [OR] nursing
  5. Presence of pacemaker [OR] automatic implantable cardioverter-defibrillator
  6. [NOT] Impaired renal function (estimated glomerular filtration rate <45 ml/min/1.73 m2) will be excluded.
  7. [NOT] Inability to undergo PET/MRI due to any other condition.
