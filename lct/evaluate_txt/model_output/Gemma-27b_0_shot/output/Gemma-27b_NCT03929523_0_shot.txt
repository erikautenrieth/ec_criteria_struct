Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Provide written informed consent prior to the performance of any study specific procedure
  -  [AND] Affiliated to the French social security system
  -  [AND] Recipient age ≥ 18 years
  -  [AND] Patients undergoing primary liver transplantation.
  -  [AND] Candidate for a first elective liver transplantation, whatever the indication, with a liver graft harvested from a brain-dead ECD defined by the presence of at least one of the following criteria:
       -  Donor age > 65 years
       -  [OR] Intensive care unit stay > 7 days
       -  [OR] BMI > 30
       -  [OR] Proven macro-steatosis biopsy ≥ 30%
       -  [OR] Natremia > 155 mmol/L at any time
       -  [OR] AST > 150 IU/mL at any time
       -  [OR] ALT > 170 IU/mL at any time.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Fulminant hepatic failure
  -  [OR] Retransplantation
  -  [OR] Split liver transplantation
  -  [OR] Living donor liver transplantation
  -  [OR] Grafts donated after cardiac arrest (DCD grafts)
  -  [OR] Domino transplantation
  -  [OR] Combined liver transplant
  -  [OR] Unexpected medical contraindication to liver transplantation
  -  [OR] Patient participating in other interventional research, excluding routine care research (old regulation) [AND] category 2 research [NOT] interfering with primary endpoint analysis
  -  [OR] Patient under legal protection
  -  [OR] Patient deprived of liberty by a judicial [OR] administrative decision
  -  [OR] Patient refusing to participate in the study
  -  [OR] Pregnant [OR] lactating women
  -  [OR] [NOT] Inability to understand information concerning the protocol
