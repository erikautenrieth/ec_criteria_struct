Inclusion criteria:
  1. Patients [AND] with locally advanced [OR] metastatic renal cell carcinoma
  2. [AND] Whose first-line treatment included at least one anti-angiogenic agent (tyrosine kinase inhibitor [OR] monoclonal antibodies) [AND] / [OR] an mTOR inhibitor;
  3. [AND] Whatever was the overall ECOG-PS score (Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group Performance Status) at treatment initiation time
  4. [AND] With first-line treatment carried out between 2007 [AND] June 2016.
Exclusion criteria:
  1. Patient previously treated [AND] with only one cytokine (Interferon α2a, high-dose interleukin-2)
  2. [NOT] Refusal of processing of personal data
