Inclusion Criteria:
  1. Women 18 years of age [OR] older at enrollment
  2. [AND] has an established viable singleton pregnancy
  3. [AND] is recommended for routine GDM screening between 24-28 weeks gestation
  4. [AND] has confirmed gestational age of 24-28 weeks at the Screening Visit
  5. [AND] is willing [AND] able to provide documentation of informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. Has diabetes before pregnancy [OR] has already been diagnosed with GDM
  2. Has contraindications for drinking oral glucose solution up to 100 g of sugar
  3. Is taking metformin for any reason
  4. Has any concomitant illness, disease [OR] condition that, in the clinical judgment of the investigator, is likely to [NOT] prevent the subject from complying with any aspect of the protocol, [OR] that may put the subject at unacceptable risk
