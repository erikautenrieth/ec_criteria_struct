Inclusion Criteria:
  - Adults [AND] over 50 years old [AND] with degenerative scoliosis (n=20) [AND] with pain (>2/10). [OR] Other inclusion criteria are: (1) curves 15°-45°.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Curves over 50° are surgical candidates, [AND] will be excluded. [OR] Patients who have had cortisone [OR] anesthetic injections within the last 3 months, [OR] surgery [OR] presenting contra-indications to exercises (fractures, cancer, severe cardio-vascular co-morbidities, [OR] osteopenia) will be excluded.
