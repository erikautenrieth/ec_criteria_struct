Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [WITH] Spondyloarthritis (SpA) meeting the criteria of Amor [OR] Assessement of spondyloarthritis International Society (ASAS)
  - Indication of standard radiography for the follow-up of the patients.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] the presence of a contraindication to the performance of conventional radiography (CR) [OR] EOS imaging
  - [NOT] any disease affecting cognition
  - Patients who have had surgery on the spine [OR] hip.
