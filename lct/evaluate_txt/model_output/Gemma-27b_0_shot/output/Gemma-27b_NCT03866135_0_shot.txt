Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients diagnosed as AS according to the modified New York diagnostic criteria [AND] were included
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Patients previously diagnosed with OP [OR]
  - Patients previously treated with OP [OR]
  - Patients with bone fractures previously associated with OP [OR]
  - Those using drugs that affect bone metabolism, metabolic bone diseases (Paget's disease, osteomalacia, hyperparathyroidism) [OR]
  - Diabetes mellitus [OR]
  - Hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism [OR]
  - Patients with kidney, liver [AND] intestinal diseases [OR]
  - Those using corticosteroids
