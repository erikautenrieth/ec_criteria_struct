Inclusion Criteria:
  -  [AND] All pregnant women undergoing a C/S [OR] in on the hospitals during the study period will be eligible to participate in this study.
Exclusion Criteria:
The following conditions will [NOT] be considered as outcome, unless they present with
systemic repercussion due to infection. For example:
  -  Vaginosis, candidiasis
  -  Lower tract urinary infection
  -  Fungal infections of the skin (athlete's foot, jockitch, ringworm, [AND] yeast infections)
  -  Otitis
  -  Pharyngitis
  -  Herpes simplex, Herpes Zoster (Shingles)
  -  Uncomplicated chronic infection
  -  Sexually transmitted infections (Gonorrhoea, Syphilis, Trichomonas, Chlamydia, Hepatitis, HIV)
  -  Tuberculosis
  -  Bacterial colonization (presence of microorganisms [WITHOUT] clinical signs/symptoms)
  -  Known vaginal, urethral [AND] [OR] rectal GBS colonization
  -  Asymptomatic bacteriuria
  -  Known [OR] oropharyngeal colonization
  -  [NOT]Infectious hypothermia/hyperthermia (e.g. related to epidural, thyroid storm, prostaglandin administration) during hospital stay
