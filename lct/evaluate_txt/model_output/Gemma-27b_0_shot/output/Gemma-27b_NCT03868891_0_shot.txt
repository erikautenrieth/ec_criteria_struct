Inclusion Criteria:
  - 6-17 years old

  - [AND] patulous ET [OR] pathologically low ET opening [OR] closing pressures
  - [AND] pregnancy
  - [AND] [NOT] able [OR] unwilling to perform the tests [AND] exercises outlined in the study
