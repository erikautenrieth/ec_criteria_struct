Inclusion Criteria:
  -  30-60 years
  -  [OR]Increased fasting blood glucose from 6.1 to 7.0 mmol / L [OR]
  -  [OR]Decreased glucose tolerance of 7 , 8 to 11.1 mmol / L [OR]
  -  [OR]Glycated hemoglobin with values between 5.7 [AND] 6.4%
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT]Use hypoglycemic, hypolipidemic drugs,
  -  [NOT]Use dietary supplements (vitamins, minerals, bioflavonoids, prebiotics, probiotics [OR] other bioactive compounds),
  -  [NOT]Exercise intensely (more than 10 hours per week)
  -  History of cardiovascular diseases , diabetes mellitus, liver [AND] kidney disease.
