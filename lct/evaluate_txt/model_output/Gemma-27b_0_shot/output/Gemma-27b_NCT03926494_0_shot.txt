Inclusion Criteria:
  - All comatose patients [AND] Glasgow coma score <8 [AND] due to carbon monoxide poisoning, treated by hyperbaric oxygen therapy in a tertiary hospital in Île de France area;
  - Aged ≥ 18 years;
  - Hospitalized in hyperbaric medicine unity [OR] Raymond Poincaré hospital in Garches.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT] Pregnant woman;
  - Patient present clinical signes of stroke [AND] [NOT] CO detection.
In case of unusable data, patient will be excluded from study.
