  -  The subject must meet all of the following inclusion criteria [AND] participate in this study.
       1. Evidence of lung nodule on chest radiograph [OR] Computed Tomography regardless of underlying aetiology
       2. Age > 21 years old
       3. Ability to provide informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Subjects [WHO] [NOT] radiographic evidence of lung nodules [OR] [WHO] [NOT] willing/unable to provide informed consent
