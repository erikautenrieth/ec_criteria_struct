Inclusion Criteria:
  1. [AND] are aged 13-17, years [AND] a high school student-athlete [AND] attends school where an athletic trainer can supervise the exercise protocol;
  2. [AND] have sustained a concussion within 2-7 days of clinic presentation [AND] diagnosed by a study physician;
  3. [AND] demonstrate symptom exacerbation during a graded treadmill exercise test [AND] cannot complete the test;
  4. [AND] are currently participating in a school [OR] club sport;
  5. [AND] are English speaking [AND] capable of giving assent
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [AND] have a reported history of neurological condition [OR] disorder including but [NOT] limited to brain surgery, special education, seizure disorder, speech pathology, previous diagnosis of Post-Concussion Syndrome (PCS),
  2. [AND] are unwilling to exercise,
  3. [AND] have focal neurologic deficit that would represent risk for walking/running on treadmill,
  4. [AND] exhibit an [NOT] ability to exercise due to injury, known heart disease, [OR] increased cardiac risk,
  5. [AND] have experienced more than 3 previous concussions,
  6. [AND] have suffered a substantial comorbidity (eg,cervical injury),
  7. [AND] currently taking β-blockers, calcium channel blockers, [OR] prophylactic headache medications (e.g., amitriptyline, topiramate), [OR]
  8. [AND] have a resting blood pressure of > 140/90.
  9. [AND] If the potential participant [OR] parent/guardian is unable to fully understand the study protocol [OR] study risks due to injury [OR] developmental delay, the potential participant will be excluded from the study.
