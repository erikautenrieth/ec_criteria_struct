Inclusion Criteria:
  - Patients [AND] with chronic suppurative otitis media [AND] with total [OR] subtotal tympanic membrane perforation [AND] with limited anterior remnant.
  - dry perforation for at least 3 months.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Recurrent perforation after previous myringoplasty.
  - Ossicular disruption [OR] fixation.
  - [NOT]immune-compromised patients including uncontrolled diabetics, chronic liver [AND] kidney diseases.
