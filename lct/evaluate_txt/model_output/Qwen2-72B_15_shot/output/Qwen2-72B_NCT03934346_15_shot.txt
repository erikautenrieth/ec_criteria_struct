Inclusion Criteria:
 - Apparently healthy men
 - Body mass index between 18 [OR] and 30 kg/m2
 - Willing to eat only the foods provided by the study [AND] for a 2 week period
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Those reporting any chronic [OR] or acute diseases, food allergies, smoking [OR] or alcohol abuse
 - Use of illicit drugs; regular consumption of medications, micronutrient supplements, [OR] or both
 - Vegetarians, [OR] or those unable to eat meat, are also excluded since the foods for the study contain meat
 - [NOT] Unable to refrain from taking medications during the study period
