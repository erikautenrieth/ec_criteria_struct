<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] Patient with a diagnosis of advanced cancer [OR] metastatic [OR] recurrent incurable solid tumors
  - [AND] Presence of fatigue of >= 4/10 on Edmonton Symptom Assessment System (ESAS) Fatigue item (0-10 severity scale)
  - [AND] Patient should describe fatigue as being present for a minimum of 2 weeks prior to screening
  - [AND] Uncontrolled pain; patient is on opioids for the treatment of cancer pain, he/she must have had no major dose change (> 25%) for at least 48 hours prior to study entry [AND] Change in opioid dose after study entry is allowed
  - [AND] Patient must be willing to engage in telephone follow up with research staff
  - [AND] Patient must have telephone access to be contacted by the research staff
  - [AND] Hemoglobin level of >= 9 g/dL [AND] Patient may receive packed red blood cell (PRBC) transfusion so as to have hemoglobin level of >= 9 g/dL so at participate in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
  - [AND] [NOT] Surgery [OR] pain relieving procedures within 2 weeks of entry into the study [OR] during the study period
  - [AND] [NOT] Patients with history of substance abuse (Cut down, Annoyed, Guilty, Eye opener [CAGE] >= 2+) [OR] cognitively impaired (MD Anderson Symptom [MDAS] > 7)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
