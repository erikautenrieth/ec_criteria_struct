Inclusion Criteria:
[AND] Type II diabetic patients [AND] 18 years and older [AND] were included if the participants had:
  1. [AND] severe non-PDR in either eyes [OR] with diabetic macular edema;
  2. [AND] ETDRS letters-measured best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) ranging from 20/40 to 20/320 Snellen equivalent;     
  3. [AND] no previous treatment (of any type) in either eye.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [AND] history of prior laser treatment [OR] vitrectomy in the study eye;
  2. [AND] history of thromboembolic event [AND] including myocardial infarction [OR] cerebral vascular accident;
  3. [AND] major surgery within the prior 6 months [OR] planned within the next 28 days;
  4. [AND] history of glaucoma [OR] ocular hypertension;
  5. [AND] loss of vision as a result of other causes;
  6. [AND] history of systemic corticosteroid therapy within the last 3 months;
  7. [AND] severe systemic disease other than diabetes mellitus;
  8. [AND] known coagulation abnormalities [OR] current use of anticoagulative medication other than aspirin
  9. [AND] any condition that could affect followup [OR] documentation (including preretinal [OR] vitreous hemorrhage).
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
