<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  - [AND]Diagnosis of primary knee osteoarthritis according to American College of Radiology
  - [AND]Being radiographically K-L grade 2-3
  - [AND]Presence of symptoms for at least 3 months
  - [AND]Patients who have been planned a conventional physiotherapy programme because of knee osteoarthritis
  - [AND]Presence of functional capacity for outpatient physical therapy
Exclusion Criteria:
  - [NOT]Patients with seconder knee osteoarthritis
  - [NOT]History of lower extremity[OR]spine surgery
  - [NOT]History of intraarticular injection in last 1 month
  - [NOT]Patients with neurological disease that will affect muscle strength[OR]balance[OR]coordination
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
