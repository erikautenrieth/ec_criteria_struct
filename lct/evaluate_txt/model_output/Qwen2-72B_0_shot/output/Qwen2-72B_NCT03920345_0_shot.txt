<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
[AND] Subject age 18 - 85 years [AND] SIJ arthropathy [OR] chronic lower back pain (CLBP) [AND] Patient to undergo endoscopic electrothermic ablation of spinal levels S1-S3 [OR] The L4-L5 [OR] L5-S1 facet joint can be included in the ablation per doctor discretion [AND] Correct spinal levels (ranging L4-S3) to be treated have been confirmed by [AND] diagnostic SIJ injection [AND] followed by [AND] diagnostic Medial/Lateral Branch Block at the applicable levels [AND] The subject is likely to follow standard of care post-operative follow-up for at least 24 months.

Exclusion Criteria:
[AND] Patients receiving additional invasive back surgery after the study treatment [AND] Inability to complete follow-up visits [OR] required questionnaires [AND] Non-compliant patients [AND] Difficult [OR] impossible communication with the patient [AND] Breastfeeding [OR] pregnant [OR] patients who plan a pregnancy while participating in the study [AND] Systemic neurological disorders with mobility limitations (e.g. advanced Parkinson's disease [OR] multiple sclerosis) [AND] Patients with incompatibilities [OR] known limitations that make participation impossible [AND] Patients after stabilization with implants on thoracic [OR] lumbar spine [AND] Inability to provide informed consent without a legally authorized representative.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
