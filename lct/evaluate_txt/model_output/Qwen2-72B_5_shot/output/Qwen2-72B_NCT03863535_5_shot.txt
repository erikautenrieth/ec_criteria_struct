Inclusion Criteria:
Type II diabetic patients, 18 years and older, were included [IF] the participants had:
  1. severe non-PDR in either eyes [WITH] with/without diabetic macular edema;
  2. ETDRS letters-measured best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) ranging from 20/40 [TO] to 20/320 Snellen equivalent;     
  3. [NOT] no previous treatment (of any type) in either eye.
Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [NOT] history of prior laser treatment [OR] or vitrectomy in the study eye;
  2. [NOT] history of thromboembolic event [INCLUDING] - including myocardial infarction [OR] or cerebral vascular accident;
  3. [NOT] major surgery within the prior 6 months [OR] or planned within the next 28 days;
  4. [NOT] history of glaucoma [OR] or ocular hypertension;
  5. [NOT] loss of vision as a result of other causes;
  6. [NOT] history of systemic corticosteroid therapy within the last 3 months;
  7. [NOT] severe systemic disease other than diabetes mellitus;
  8. [NOT] known coagulation abnormalities [OR] or current use of anticoagulative medication other than aspirin
  9. [NOT] any condition that could affect followup [OR] or documentation (including preretinal [OR] or vitreous hemorrhage).
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
