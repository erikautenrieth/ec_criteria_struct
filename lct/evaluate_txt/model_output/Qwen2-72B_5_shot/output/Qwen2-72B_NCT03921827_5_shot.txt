<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients [WITH] recent stroke/TIA [AND] severe stenosis of intracranial ICA [OR] or MCA [AND] impaired CVR within previous three months [BUT] not before 3 weeks after acute stroke. This is to differentiate between patients [WITH] a long-standing fixed-stenosis [FROM] patients [WITH] partially recanalized intracranial artery (masquerading as severe stenosis).
 2. Age >21 years
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients [WITH] atrial fibrillation[OR]/ arrhythmias.
 2. Within 2 weeks [OF] cardiac catheterization [OR] or arterial puncture at femoral puncture site.
 3. Decompensated heart failure [USUALLY] class 3 [OR] or 4
 4. LV EF <30%
 5. Moderate [OR] or severe AR
 6. Persistent [AND] uncontrolled hypertension (BP persistently >160/100 mmHg)
 7. Bleeding diathesis
 8. Active thrombophlebitis[OR]/ venous disease of lower limbs
 9. Severe lower extremity vaso-occlusive disease
 10. Presence of a documented aortic aneurysm[OR]/ dissection requiring surgical repair
 11. Pregnancy
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
