<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
 - Major Depressive Disorder according to Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (DSM-V) [AND]
 - > 25 years [AND]
 - Sufficient understanding of the German language

Exclusion Criteria:
 - < 25 years [OR]
 - Concomitant diagnosis of past [OR] or present psychotic disorder [OR]
 - Concomitant diagnosis of past [OR] or present bipolar disorder [OR]
 - First degree relative with a psychotic disorder [OR]
 - Unable [OR] or unwilling to discontinue antidepressant medication [OR]
 - Pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding [OR]
 - Known hypersensitivity to LSD [OR]
 - Somatic disorders including central nervous system (CNS) involvement [OR]
 - Known [OR] or suspected non-compliance, drug [OR] or alcohol abuse [OR]
 - Metal implants [OR]
 - Weight < 42 kg [OR]
 - Suicide risk [OR] or very likely to require psychiatric hospitalisation
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
