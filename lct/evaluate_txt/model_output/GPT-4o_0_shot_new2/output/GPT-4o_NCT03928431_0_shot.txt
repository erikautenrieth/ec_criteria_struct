Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Infants of healthy mothers [AND] uncomplicated pregnancies at term[OR] mothers of any ethnic [OR] social background [AND] can speak[OR] read[OR] understand Swedish to the extent [AND] they can consent in Swedish. An additional inclusion criterion for mothers in the CS groups is vaginal pH ≤ 4 at the time of birth.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Infants of mothers [AND] pre-eclampsia [OR] complicated pregnancies[OR] mothers <18 years [OR] >40 years of age[OR] genital herpes simplex[OR] genital warts[OR] HIV[OR] Hepatitis B [OR] complications during delivery[OR] infants delivered prior to 37 weeks of gestation. Additional exclusion criteria for mothers in the CS group: Mothers positive to Group B Streptococcus[OR] Group A Streptococcus[OR] bacterial vaginosis[OR] vaginal pH > 4 at the time of birth.
