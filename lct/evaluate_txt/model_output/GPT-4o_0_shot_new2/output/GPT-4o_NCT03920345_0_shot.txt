<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Subject age 18 - 85 years[OR];
  -  SIJ arthropathy[AND] chronic lower back pain (CLBP)[OR];
  -  Patient to undergo endoscopic electrothermic ablation of spinal levels S1-S3. The L4-L5[OR] L5-S1 facet joint can be included in the ablation per doctor discretion[OR].
  -  Correct spinal levels (ranging L4-S3) to be treated have been confirmed by 1) diagnostic SIJ injection[OR] followed by 2) diagnostic Medial[OR]Lateral Branch Block at the applicable levels[OR].
  -  The subject is likely to follow standard of care post-operative follow-up for at least 24 months[OR].

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients receiving additional invasive back surgery after the study treatment[OR].
  -  [NOT]Inability to complete follow-up visits[OR] required questionnaires[OR].
  -  Non-compliant patients[OR]
  -  Difficult[OR][NOT]impossible communication [AND] the patient[OR]
  -  Breastfeeding,[OR] pregnant[OR] patients [AND] plan a pregnancy while participating [AND] the study[OR]
  -  Systemic neurological disorders [AND] mobility limitations (e.g. advanced Parkinson's disease[OR] multiple sclerosis)[OR]
  -  Patients [AND] incompatibilities[OR] known limitations [AND] make participation [NOT]impossible[OR]
  -  Patients after stabilization[OR]implants on thoracic[OR] lumbar spine[OR].
  -  [NOT]Inability to provide informed consent [NOT]without a legally authorized representative[OR].
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
