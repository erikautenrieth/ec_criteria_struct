<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
- Over 18 years of age
Neonates:
  -  Aged between 1h of life [OR] 28 days of life
  -  Born beyond 33 Week of Amenorrhea [OR] 1day [OR] 2kg of birth weight
  -  Hospitalized [AND] the medical-surgical centre of Pediatrics [OR] the hospital of Strasbourg
  -  For whom a blood sample was prescribed [AND] part of their routine care
Exclusion Criteria:
Adult Healthy Volunteers :
  -  Systemic inflammatory disease
  -  Transient inflammatory state
  -  Any drug [AND] modifies the coagulation cascade during the 48h preceding the sampling
Neonates:
  -  Risk [OR] anemia < 7g/DL
  -  Current anticoagulant treatment
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
