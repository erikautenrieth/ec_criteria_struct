Inclusion Criteria:
Female subjects between 18 [AND] and 65 years of age currently taking spironolactone for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris
OR
Female subjects between 18 [AND] and 65 years of age currently taking oral antibiotics for a diagnosis of acne vulgaris
AND Subjects able to complete an online survey in English
Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects less than 18 years of age [OR] or older than 65 years of age Subjects [AND] that have ever been diagnosed [AND] with any mood disorder by a clinician prior to starting spironolactone (history of mood disorder [OR] or current mood disorder) Subjects taking hormonal contraception (including [AND] but [NOT] not limited to oral contraceptive pills) for a duration of less than 6 months Subjects [AND] that have ever been diagnosed [AND] with any type of sexual dysfunction prior to starting spironolactone Subjects [NOT] not able to complete an online survey in English Subjects taking oral isotretinoin for a diagnosis of acne Subjects [AND] that are currently pregnant
