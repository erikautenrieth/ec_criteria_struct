  Inclusion Criteria:

* Patient requiring an electroneuromyogram whose indication allows the application of a standard protocol of examination [AND] Patient that has given his/her free and informed consent [AND] Patient that signed the consent form [AND] Patient with an health insurance plan [AND] The patient is at least 18 years old (≥) [AND] The patient is under 80 years old (≤)

Exclusion Criteria:

* Patient with symptoms of neuropathy during the screening [OR] Patient whose Neurological severity score (NSS) score is ≥ 3 [OR] Patient whose neurological examination is abnormal and suggestive of neuropathy [OR] Patient presenting a pathology that could cause neuropathy (e.g. diabetes, renal failure, etc.) [OR] Patient already participating in a category 1 research study (Jardé law) [OR] Patient in an exclusion period determined by another study [OR] Patient under the protection of justice, under guardianship or under curatorship [OR] Patient refusing to sign the consent form [OR] Patient for which it is impossible to provide information about the study [OR] Pregnant patient, parturient, or breastfeeding.
