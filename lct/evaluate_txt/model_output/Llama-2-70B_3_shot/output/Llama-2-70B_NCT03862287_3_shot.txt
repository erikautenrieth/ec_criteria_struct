  Inclusion Criteria:
  -  ASA I-III male and female patients aged between 18 to 80 years old [AND] who will receive spinal anesthesia for surgeries lasting less than one hour.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients younger than 18 years old [OR] ASA physical status >3 [OR] Immunosuppression [OR] high risk of infection [OR] Contraindications to receiving spinal anesthesia (e.g. coagulation impairment) [OR] Patients with psychosis [OR] Patients with preexisting back pain [OR] Patients with cognitive impairment [OR] Allergies to local anesthetics [OR] analgesics [OR] any medication used in the study [OR] Patients with chronic regular opioid usage [OR] Presence of preexisting neurological symptoms.
