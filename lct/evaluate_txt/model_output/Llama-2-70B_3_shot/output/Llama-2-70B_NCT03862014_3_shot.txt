  Inclusion Criteria:

* Patients and parents of the patients who accept to participate and sign the informed consent [AND]
* Patients whose molar teeth are diagnosed as molar incisor hypomineralization by EAPD [AND]
* Teeth that are fully erupted [AND]
* Patients who have at least two permanent first molars that need fissure sealant [AND]
* Teeth that have white-yellow-brown discoloration that indicate high-caries risk [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:

* Teeth that are previously restored or have caries [NOT]
* Patients who have orthodontic treatment [NOT]
* Patients who are not cooperative for the dental procedure [NOT]
* Teeth that have dental flourosis or enamel malformation according to the specific syndromes [NOT]
