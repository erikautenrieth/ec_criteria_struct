  Inclusion Criteria:

1. Patient with paroxysmal atrial fibrillation who is scheduled for ablation procedure and ≥20 and ≤80 years of age [AND]
2. Left atrium size < 45mm [AND]
3. Paroxysmal atrial fibrillation that is recurrence during antiarrhythmic drug treatment [OR] is not able to use an antiarrhythmic drug [AND]
4. Patient who is indicated for anticoagulation therapy (for prevention of cerebral infarction) [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:

1. Patients with persistent or permanent atrial fibrillation [NOT]
2. Atrial fibrillation associated with severe cardiac malformation [OR] structural heart disease that is hemodynamically affected [NOT]
3. Patients with severe renal impairment [OR] CT imaging difficulty using contrast media [NOT]
4. Patients with a past history of radiofrequency ablation for atrial fibrillation [OR] other cardiac surgery [NOT]
5. Patients with active internal bleeding [NOT]
6. Patients with contraindications for anticoagulation therapy (for prevention of cerebral infarction) [AND] antiarrhythmic drugs [NOT]
7. Patients with valvular atrial fibrillation (mitral stenosis >grade 2, mechanical valve, mitral valvuloplasty) [NOT]
8. Patients with a severe comorbid disease [NOT]
9. Expected survival < 1 year [NOT]
10. Drug addicts [OR] alcoholics [NOT]
11. Patients who cannot read the consent form (illiterates, foreigners, etc.) [NOT]
12. Other patients who are judged by the principal or sub-investigator to be ineligible for participation in this clinical study [NOT]
