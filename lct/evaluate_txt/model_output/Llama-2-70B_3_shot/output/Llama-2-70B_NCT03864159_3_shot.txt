  Inclusion Criteria:
  - Age between 18 to 25 years [AND]
  - Amateur basketball players [OR]
  - Belong to the Celeste club [AND]
  - Not having any injury to the hamstring musculature at the time of the study [NOT]

Exclusion Criteria:
  - At the time of the study they are included in a program of individualized strength training in lower limbs [OR]
  - Subjects with alterations in venous return [OR] or congenital coagulopathies [OR]
  - Subjects who present hypermobility [OR]
  - Not signed the informed consent document [NOT]
