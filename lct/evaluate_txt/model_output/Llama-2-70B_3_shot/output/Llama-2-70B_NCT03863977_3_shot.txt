  Inclusion Criteria:

* Patients' age from 18 to 60 years old [AND]
* physical status ASA I-II (by the American Society of Anesthesiologists) [AND]
* Elective unilateral open inguinal hernia repair [AND]
* Under spinal anesthesia

Exclusion Criteria:

* age < 18 or > 60 [NOT]
* American society of anesthesiologist (ASA) class III-IV [NOT]
* known allergy to amino amide local anesthetics [NOT]
* skin infection and diseases at site of injection [NOT]
* coagulation disorders [NOT]
* patients with body mass index (BMI) > 35 kg/m2 [NOT]
* scrotal hernias [NOT]
