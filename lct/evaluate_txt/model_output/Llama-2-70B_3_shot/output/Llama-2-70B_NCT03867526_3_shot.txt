  Inclusion Criteria:

* Informed consent will be obtained from the patient or the parents before any study-related procedures. [AND]
* Patients of both genders older than 6 months and younger than 80 years [OR]
* The patient has a diagnosis of Wilson disease [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:

* No informed consent from the patient or the parents before any study-related procedures [NOT]
* Patients of both genders younger than 6 months and older than 80 years [NOT]
* No diagnosis of Wilson disease [NOT]
