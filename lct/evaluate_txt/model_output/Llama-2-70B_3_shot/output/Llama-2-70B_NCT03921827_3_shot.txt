  Inclusion Criteria:

1. Patients with recent stroke/TIA and severe stenosis of intracranial ICA or MCA and impaired CVR within previous three months but not before 3 weeks after acute stroke [AND] Age >21 years.

Exclusion Criteria:

1. Patients with atrial fibrillation/arrhythmias [OR] Within 2 weeks of cardiac catheterization or arterial puncture at femoral puncture site.

2. Decompensated heart failure [AND] usually class 3 or 4.

3. LV EF <30% [OR] Moderate or severe AR.

4. Persistent and uncontrolled hypertension (BP persistently >160/100 mmHg) [OR] Bleeding diathesis.

5. Active thrombophlebitis/venous disease of lower limbs [OR] Severe lower extremity vaso-occlusive disease.

6. Presence of a documented aortic aneurysm/dissection requiring surgical repair.

7. Pregnancy.
