  Inclusion Criteria:

* (1) Gestational age 35 to 39 weeks [AND]
* (2) Adults [AND]
* (3) Primipara and their spouses [AND]
* (4) Having routine prenatal visits [AND] plan to have the birth in the medical center [AND]
* (5) Able to communicate with Mandarin or Taiwanese [AND]
* (6) Willing to participate in this study [AND] give written informed-consent forms [AND]

Exclusion Criteria:

* (1) Having fetus with abnormality [NOT]
* (2) High-risk pregnancy [NOT]
* (3) Cannot room-in due to newborn's medical conditions [NOT]
