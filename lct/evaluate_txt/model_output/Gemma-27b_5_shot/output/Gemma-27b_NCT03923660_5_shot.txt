Inclusion Criteria:
 -  Severe COPD [AND] (FEV1 [forced expiratory volume in 1 second] / FVC [forced vital capacity] < 70% [AND] FEV1 < 50%)
 -  Informed consent
 -  Affiliation [AND] to a social security scheme
Exclusion Criteria:
 -  Effort oxygen therapy
 -  Cardiovascular, neuromuscular [OR] or musculoskeletal disorders that can provide significant dyspnea [OR] or limit exercise
 -  Legal incapacity
 -  Low [OR] [NOT] no cooperation anticipated
