Inclusion Criteria:
 -  Age between 25 years [AND] and 60 years
 -  For the volunteers with venous insuffiency: clearly visual venous insufficiency (CEAP 1 [AND] and 2)
 -  BMI between 24 [AND] and 30
Exclusion Criteria:
 -  Volunteers using medication [OR] (exception for contraception)
 -  Volunteers having comorbidities
 -  Pregnant women
 -  Women who menstruate on the test date
 -  Volunteers with an implanted elektronical device (IPG, Pacemaker)
