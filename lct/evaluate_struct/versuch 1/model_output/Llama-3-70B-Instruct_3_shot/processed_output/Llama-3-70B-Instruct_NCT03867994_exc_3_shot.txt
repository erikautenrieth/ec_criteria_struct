- Patients suffering from ST-segment elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI) [AND]
- Patients need for immediate cardiac catheterization [AND]
- Elevated liver enzymes (Aspartate aminotransferase (AST) and Alanine aminotransferase (ALT) three times the upper limit of normal). [AND]
- Active infection. [AND]
- Any contraindication to carvedilol, or atorvastatin. [AND]
- Patients on regular use of vitamins, minerals. [AND]
- Using medication with antioxidant properties e.g., beta-carotene, vitamin E, vitamin C, selenium, theophylline or N-acetyl cysteine 7 days prior to CC. [AND]
- Hemodynamically unstable patients (defined as abnormal or unstable blood pressure, especially hypotension (blood pressure less than 90/60 mm Hg). [AND]
- Patients who required dialysis. [AND]
- Pregnancy.
