- Women who are considered high-risk based on a greater than 25% lifetime risk of breast cancer as per the IBIS (International Breast Cancer Intervention Study) Breast Cancer Risk Evaluation Tool [AND]
- Pathology that is felt to be discordant with imaging [AND]
- Extensive calcifications extending more than 20 mm and/or masses greater than 30 mm [AND]
- The following lesions will be excluded based on their increased risk of upstaging; [AND]
lesions (other than fibroadenomas, phyllodes and papillomas) that are associated with a palpable mass, [OR]
ADH (atypical ductal hyperplasia) with high-risk features (ADH with zonal necrosis, significant cytological atypia or more than 2 foci), [OR]
LCIS (lobular carcinoma in situ) with high-risk features (associated with ADH, pleomorphic LCIS, zonal necrosis and > 4 foci) or discordant with imaging, [OR]
papilloma with atypia, [OR]
and spindle cell lesion (especially if there is atypia). [AND]
- Lesions that are suspicious for borderline or malignant Phyllodes, [OR]
DCIS (ductal carcinoma in situ), [OR]
invasive mammary carcinoma, [OR]
or encapsulated papillary carcinoma
