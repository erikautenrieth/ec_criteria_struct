Inclusion Criteria:
 - All infants [OR] or fetuses [AND] with microcephaly, (defined as head circumference ≤-3SD according to INTERGROWTH standards for gestational age and sex, detected during the mother's pregnancy, [OR] or at the end of the mother's pregnancy at one of the participating maternities)
 - and with abnormal ultrasound [OR] and/or clinical examination findings for newborns.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Infants/fetuses [AND] with microcephaly whose mothers are under the age of 18 years,
 - or inability or refusal of mothers/ guardians to give informed consent.
