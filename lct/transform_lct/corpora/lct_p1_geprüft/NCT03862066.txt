Inclusion Criteria:
 - Newly diagnosed histologically proven locoregional oral squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC) [NOT] without evidence of distant metastases. OSCC [OR] includes the subsites of oral tongue, [OR] floor of mouth, [OR] gingiva, [OR] retromolar trigone [OR] and buccal mucosa OR Recurrent [OR] or persistent histologically proven locoregional OSCC that was initially treated with surgery alone.
 - must be eligible for surgical resection
 - greater than 18 years of age
Exclusion Criteria:
 - prior immunotherapy [OR] or treatment with another anti PD-1 agent besides nivolumab
 - prior chemotherapy including cetuximab [OR] or radiation therapy
 - concomitant malignancies except cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma [OR] or basal cell carcinoma
 - unresectable primary tumor [OR] or regional disease [OR] or distant metastases
