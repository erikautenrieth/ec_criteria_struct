Inclusion Criteria:
 - Male  participants [AND] who are healthy as determined by medical evaluation including a detailed medical history, full physical examination, including blood pressure (BP) [AND] and pulse rate (PR) measurement, 12 lead ECG, and clinical laboratory tests.
 - Body mass index (BMI) of 17.5 to 30.5 kg/m2; and a total body weight >50 kg (110 lb).
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Known immunodeficiency disorder, including positive serology for human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) at screening, [OR] or a first degree relative with a hereditary immunodeficiency.
 - Infection with hepatitis B [OR] or hepatitis C viruses.
 - Participants with selected acute or chronic infections [OR] or infection history.
 - Participants have a known present [OR] or a history of malignancy other than a successfully treated [OR] or excised non metastatic basal cell [OR] or squamous cell cancer of the skin.
 - History of alcohol abuse [OR] or binge drinking [OR] and/or any other illicit drug use [OR] or dependence within 6 months of Screening.
 - Use of tobacco/ [OR] nicotine containing products within 3 months prior to dosing or positive urine cotinine test.
