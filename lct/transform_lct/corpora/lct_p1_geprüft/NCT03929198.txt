Inclusion Criteria:
 - Dysfunctional lipids, [OR] blood pressure [AND] and glycemic control determined at a screening visit:
 - Triglycerides > 150 mg/dL [OR] OR HDL <50 in females, <40 mg/dL in males
 - Systolic blood pressure ≥130 mmHg [OR] OR diastolic blood pressure  ≥80 mmHg ([OR] or on hypertension medication)
 - HbA1c ≥5.7% or higher
 - [NOT] No diagnosis or treatment for cancer in the past year
 - [NOT] No diagnosis of mild cognitive impairment [OR] or dementia in the past year
 - Ability to perform daily exercise, including aerobic activity and resistance exercise
 - [NOT] Non-smoker in the past year
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Positive exercise stress test
