Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients aged from 18 to 80 years
 - Obese (BMI ≥ 30kg/m²)
 - Referred by GPs to the medical analysis laboratory for usual biological assessment
 - Patients with [NOT] no respiratory follow-up already in place
 - Bicarbonate - Concentration ≥ 27 mmol/L
 - STOP-BANG score ≥ 3
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Acute disease [OR] or recently diagnosed chronic disease (< 2 months)
 - Hospitalization for respiratory, [OR] metabolic [OR] or cardiovascular event (< 2 months)
 - Renal insufficiency stage 4 or 5, [OR] or autoimmune disease, [OR] or viral hepatitis, [OR] or cirrhosis
 - Pregnant women
 - Patients already included in an interventional study (end of the study < 1 month)
