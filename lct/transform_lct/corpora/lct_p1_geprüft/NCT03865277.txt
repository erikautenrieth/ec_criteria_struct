Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age: older than 18 years
 - WHO (ECOG) performance status 0-2
 - Histological proven HNSCC
 - HPV negative tumors [OR] or HPV positive tumors
 - Stage III, IVA [OR] or IVB HNSCC according to UICC and AJCC guidelines
 - Tumor [OR] classified as irresectable [OR] or patient inoperable or patient [NOT] refused surgery
 - Tumor extension [AND] and localization suitable for radiochemotherapy with curative intent
 - Simultaneous standard chemotherapy with cisplatin applicable ([NOT] no contra-indications)
 - Dental examination [AND] and -treatment before start of therapy
 - For women [AND] with childbearing potential [OR] and men [AND] in reproductive ages adequate contraception.
 - Ability of subject to understand character and individual consequences of the clinical trial
 - Written informed consent (must be available before enrolment in the trial)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Refusal of the patients to take part in the trial
 - Presence of distant metastases (UICC stage IVC)
 - Previous radiotherapy in the head [OR] and neck region
 - Second malignancy that is likely to require treatment during the trial intervention or follow-up period or that, in the opinion of the physician, has a considerable risk of recurrence [OR] or metastases within the follow-up period
 - Serious disease [OR] or medical condition [AND] with life expectancy of less than one year
 - Participation in competing interventional trial on cancer treatment
 - Patients who are not suitable for radiochemotherapy
 - Pregnant [OR] or lactating women
 - Patients [NOT] not able to understand the character and individual consequences of the trial
 - Nasopharyngeal Carcinomas
