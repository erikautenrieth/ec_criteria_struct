Inclusion Criteria:
 - Signed informed consent
 - At least 19 years of age
 - Known [OR] or suspected  infection
 - [AND] AND one or more of the following organ dysfunctions judged due to sepsis:
 1. Cardiovascular- refractory hypotension  (a systolic blood pressure (SBP) < 90 mm Hg [OR] or mean arterial pressure (MAP) < 60 mm Hg [AND] despite an IV fluid challenge of at least 30 ml/kg fluids), [OR] or use of vasopressor(s) to maintain SAP  > 90 mm Hg [OR] or MAP > 60 mm Hg, [OR] or;
 2. Respiratory: PaO2/ [OR] FiO2  < 300 [OR] or PaO2/ [OR] FiO2 < 200 if lung is the only organ dysfunction [OR] or SaO2: [OR] FiO2 150
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Known pregnancy
 - Underlying severe congestive heart failure (New York Heart Association (NYHA) IV), severe COPD (need for chronic oxygen [OR] or mechanical ventilation), severe liver disease (Child-Pugh Class C), cancer requiring chemotherapy, [OR] or transplantation (bone marrow, [OR] heart, [OR] lung, [OR] liver, [OR] pancreas, [OR] or small bowel) in the past 6 months [OR] or likely within the next 6 months
 - Previous episode of sepsis during that hospital admission
 - Absolute Neutrophil Count < 500/mm³
 - CD4 count < 50/mm³
 - Treating physician deems aggressive care unsuitable (i.e. [NOT] no commitment to active care)
 - Participation in another interventional drug study within previous 1 month
 - Allergic to the study drug [OR] or any of its components
 - Lactation
 - Have signed a Do No Resuscitate (DNR) Form
