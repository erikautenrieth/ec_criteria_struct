Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Provision of written informed consent
 2. Age > or = to 65 years
 3. Hip fracture location within the femoral neck, [OR] intertrochanteric, and subtrochanteric regions
 4. Indication for one of the following surgical interventions: hemiarthroplasty, [OR] total hip replacement, sliding plate [OR] and screw fixation, [OR] or intramedullary fixation
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Indication for closed reduction [OR] or percutaneous screw
 2. Allergy to TXA
 3. Cerebrovascular accident/ [OR] stroke, active coronary disease/ [OR] myocardial infarction, [OR] or deep vein thrombosis/ [OR] pulmonary emboli within one (1) month of the fracture
 4. Presence of hypercoaguable disorder
