Inclusion Criteria:
 - Male [OR] and Female; 18 years of age or older
 - Confirmed diagnosis of Type 2 diabetes mellitus
 - Willing to use CGM [OR] and BGM
 - A1c result ≥8.0% [AND] and ≤12.0%
 - Receive their diabetes care from Premier Medical Associates [OR] or the Palo Alto Medical Foundation
 - Own a Smartphone with a data plan and be the primary user of that smartphone
 - Smartphone must use a supported Android OS or iOS
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding
 - Use of an insulin pump
 - Any condition [OR] or situation that the Sponsor or Investigator determines as [NOT] inappropriate for study inclusion
