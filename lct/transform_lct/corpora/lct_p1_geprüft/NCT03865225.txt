Inclusion Criteria:
 - male [OR] and female subjects aged between 18 and 90
 - written and oral informed consent
 - evidence of an ischaemic lesion on cranial computed tomography scan [OR] or magnetic resonance imaging
Exclusion Criteria:
 - intake of any tricyclic antidepressant within the last 14 days
 - atrial fibrillation
 - prior diagnosed autonomic neuropathy
 - malignant cerebral infarct [OR] or indication for treatment at intensive care unit
 - aphasia [OR] or cognitive deficit with resulted [NOT] inability to participate in Heart rate variability-biofeedback training
 - respiratory insufficiency
 - blindness, [OR] deafness [OR] or other physical limitations with resulted [NOT] inability to participate in heart rate variability-biofeedback training
