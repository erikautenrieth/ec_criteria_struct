Inclusion Criteria:
 - Be 60 or older, and in the case of women, they must be postmenopausal ([NOT] interruption of menstruation for more than one year).
 - Have [NOT] not participated in any systematic exercise program for at least six months prior to the start of this project.
 - To have a body mass index (BMI) between 22 and 30 kg / m2.
 - Are [NOT] not cardiopathic, [OR] diabetic [OR] or have any other metabolic disease.
 - Do [NOT] not present joint problems [OR] or any other injury that [NOT] prevents the proper execution of the exercises of the training routine.
 - [NOT] Non-smoker [OR] or have quit smoking for at least five years ago.
 - Release of the doctor (medical certificate) to perform the physical exercises.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Do [NOT] not participate in at least 20 training sessions [OR] or [NOT] miss more than three consecutive training sessions.
