Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adults (age 30 to 75 years).
 - Diagnosis of primary hypertension based on ABPM (mean 24-h systolic BP ≥130 mmHg [OR] or mean 24-h diastolic BP ≥80 mmHg).
 - [NOT] No current use of antihypertensive medication.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Low life expectancy.
 - Other indications for the use of diuretics.
 - Intolerance [OR] or contraindications to the study drugs.
 - Cardiovascular disease (heart failure, [OR] myocardial infarction [OR] or stroke).
 - Secondary hypertension.
 - Chronic kidney disease [OR] and / or abnormal renal function (creatinine >1.5 mg/dL).
 - Hyperkalemia (serum potassium >5.5 mEq/L).
 - Gout.
 - Previous antihypertensive treatment with more than one drug.
 - Systolic blood pressure ≥160 mmHg [OR] or diastolic blood pressure ≥100 mmHg measured through office blood pressure.
 - Pregnancy [OR] or prospective pregnancy during the study.
 - Lactating women.
