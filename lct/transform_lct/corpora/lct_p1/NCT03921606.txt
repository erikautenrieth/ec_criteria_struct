Inclusion Criteria:
 - aged 18 years or above,
 - able to speak Cantonese [OR] and read Chinese
 - do [NOT] not [AND] intend to quit smoking (pre-contemplation stage), but are willing to promote their health
 - use a smartphone and can use an instant messaging tool (e.g., WhatsApp/WeChat)
 - willing to receive health promotion advice and interact with our interventionist via WhatsApp/WeChat on a smartphone throughout the study period
Exclusion Criteria:
 - inability to give informed consent or participate in our intervention due to impaired mental status, [OR] cognitive impairment or communication barriers
 - participate in another smoking cessation programme or service
