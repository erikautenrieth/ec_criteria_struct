Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Age greater than or equal to 21 years old
 2. Clinical suspicion of malignancy based on signs, symptoms, imaging studies, [OR] and/or [NOT] non-surgical biopsies of any of the following cancer types: bladder, [OR] breast, [OR] colorectal, [OR] kidney, [OR] liver, [OR] lung, [OR] pancreas [OR] or prostate.
 3. Candidate for subsequent medical [OR] and/or surgical work-up to complete organ-specific cancer diagnosis inclusive of pathologic staging as specified by the College of American Pathology (CAP).
 4. Provision of informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Age less than 21 years old
 2. Any prior cancer [AND] diagnosis, without or with any treatment (e.g., medical, [OR] surgical, [OR] or radiation oncology) within prior six months.
 3. Any surgery, i.e., invasive procedure performed in the operating room with any level of anesthesia, within prior month.
 4. Any [NOT] non-cancer systemic therapy associated with molecularly targeted immune modulation, e.g., monoclonal antibodies.
 5. Any concurrent [OR] or prior pregnancy within previous twelve months.
 6. Any history of organ tissue transplantation
 7. Any history of blood product transfusion within prior month.
 8. Any major trauma within prior six months, e.g., burn injury, [OR] extremity crush injury, [OR] motor vehicle accident, etc.
 9. Unable to provide informed consent.
