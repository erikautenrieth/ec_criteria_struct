Inclusion Criteria:
 - Have a previous history of elbow joint fracture [OR] or dislocation that conform to its manifestations and signs. And it is diagnosed by X-ray test. HO must accord with the relative diagnostic criteria5 and have imaging evidences of heterotopic ossification.
 - Have complete clinical and imaging dates.
 - The age＞18 and no gender limitation.
 - The patient is being a relatively stable physical [AND] and mental state [NOT] without acute injection [OR] or diseases.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Have a history of diabetes mellitus [OR] or impaired blood glucose, [OR] metabolic syndrome [OR] and gout [OR] or renal dysfunction (creatinine ≥ 1.5 mg/dL) that apparently influence glucose, [OR] lipid [OR] and protein metabolic of the body.
 - Have a history of taking drugs that can influence uric acid metabolic including gout suppressant, [OR] diuretic, small-dose aspirin [OR] (75~300mg/d) or large-dose aspirin (＞3g/d), anti-tuberculosis drugs, [OR] immunosuppressor, [OR] nicotinic acid and anti-tumor drugs such as cyclophosphamide and so on.
