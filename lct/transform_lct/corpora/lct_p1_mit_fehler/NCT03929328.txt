Inclusion Criteria:
 - Adult patients aged 18 years or more, both gender
 - Receiving endotracheal intubation [AND] and mechanical ventilation for more than 12 hours
 - Recovery from the precipitating illness
 - Weaning readiness according to the following criteria :
 1. Respiratory criteria :
 1. PaO2: [AND] FIO2 >150 with FIO2 ≤0.4, PEEP <8 cm H2O
 2. Arterial pH >7.35
 3. Rapid shallow breathing index (RSBI) < 105
 4. Maximum inspiratory pressure (MIP) < -20 cm H20
 2. Clinical criteria :
 1. [NOT] Absence of electrocardiographic signs of myocardial ischemia
 2. [NOT] No [OR] vasoactive drugs, or vital signs [AND] are stable with using vasoactive drugs
 3. Heart rate <140/min,
 4. Hemoglobin >8 g/dL
 5. Temperature <38°C
 6. [NOT] No [OR] need for sedatives, or mental status [AND] are stable with sedatives
 7. Presence of respiratory stimulus, [AND] and appropriate spontaneous cough
 8. [NOT] Absence of excessive tracheobronchial secretions
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Tracheostomy status
 - Decision to [NOT] stop life-supportive therapies before enrollment
