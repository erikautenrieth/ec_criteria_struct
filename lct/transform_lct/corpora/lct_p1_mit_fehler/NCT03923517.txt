Inclusion Criteria:
 - informal carer of a person with YOD who has been diagnosed with either Frontal Temporal Dementia (FTD) [OR] or Alzheimer's Disease (AD) 12 months ago or less;
 - the carer has face-to-face contact with the person with YOD at least twice a weak;
 - the carer has a working computer and internet access in his home;
 - the carer is available for the intervention duration of approximately eight weeks;
 - the carer is fluent in German;
 - signed consent form.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - the carer has face-to-face contact with the person with YOD less than twice a week;
 - the person with YOD the carer is caring for has a different dementia cause other than FTD [OR] or AD [OR] or the diagnosis is older than 12 months;
 - the carer does not have a working computer and internet access at home or is not confident to use them;
 - the carer is [NOT] not available for the approximately eight week intervention duration;
 - the carer is [NOT] not fluent in German;
 - the carer has a significant health problem [OR] or disability, which precludes him/her from participating in the study;
 - no signed informed consent.
