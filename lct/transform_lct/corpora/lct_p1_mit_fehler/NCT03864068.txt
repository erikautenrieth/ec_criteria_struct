Inclusion Criteria:
 - Women [AND] with chronic anovulation [OR] or oligomenorrhea defined as spontaneous intermenstrual periods [OR] of greater than or equal to 45 days or a total of less than or equal to 8 menses per year.
 - Women [AND] with Hyperandrogenism defined as a total testosterone [OR] greater than 50 ng/dL or a free androgen index greater than 10.
 - Women [AND] with Polycystic Ovaries on Ultrasound defined as either 12 or more follicles measuring [OR] 2-9 mm in diameter or increased ovarian volume greater than 10 cm.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Women [AND] with Hyperprolactinemia defined as 2 prolactin levels at least one week apart greater than 30 ng/mL.
 - Women [AND] with known 21-hydroxylase deficiency [OR] or other enzyme deficiency leading to the congenital adrenal hyperplasia.
 - Women [AND] with elevated FSH levels greater than 10 mIU/mL.
 - Women [AND] with uncorrected thyroid disease as per ASRM guidelines for nonpregnant subjects (TSH less than 0.45 mIU/mL [OR] or greater than 4.5 MIU/mL).
 - Women [AND] with a suspected adrenal [OR] or ovarian tumor secreting androgens
 - Women [AND] with Cushing's syndrome
 - Women on confounding medications which affect ovarian function including metformin, [OR] hormonal contraceptives [OR] or other medications for type 2 diabetes
 - Women [AND] with medical conditions that are contraindications [OR] to OTC inositol or previous allergic reactions to the supplement [OR] or to the placebo maltodextrin [OR] or inulin.
