Inclusion Criteria:
 - -Subjects presented with melasma.
 - Age 20-40 years old.
 - Melasma persisting for more than 6 months that has failed to respond to conventional treatment with hydroquinone [OR] or other topical lightening agents.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - -History of photosensitivity, [OR] keloids, [OR] hypertrophic scarring [OR] and post- inflammatory hyperpigmentation.
 - Pregnancy [OR] and lactation.
 - Subjects with local inflammatory skin disorder [OR] or active herpes infection at the site of procedure.
 - Subjects with history of medical diseases which contraindicate cryosurgery such as; cold intolerance, [OR] cold urticaria, [OR] Raynaud`s disease [AND] and history of allergic reactions to cryosurgery.
