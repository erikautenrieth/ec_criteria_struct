Inclusion Criteria:
 - age of 18 years or younger
 - both sexes are included
 - a clinical [OR] or radiological finding of mastoiditis in the patient's file
 - Documentation of CSVT in an imaging study, either CT [OR] or MRI.
Patient's exclusion criteria
 - age above 18 years
 - [NOT] lack of a clinical [OR] or radiological finding of mastoiditis in the patient's file
 - [NOT] Lack of documentation of CSVT in an imaging study, either CT [OR] or MRI.
 - previous unprovoked venous thromboembolism event.
