Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with chronic suppurative otitis media [AND] with total [OR] or subtotal tympanic membrane perforation [AND] with limited anterior remnant.
 - dry perforation for at least 3 months.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Recurrent perforation after previous myringoplasty.
 - Ossicular disruption [OR] or fixation.
 - immune-compromised patients including uncontrolled diabetics, chronic liver [OR] and kidney diseases.
