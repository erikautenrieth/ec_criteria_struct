Patients will [NOT] not be included in the study if one or more of the following criteria
applies:
- Patient is pregnant
- Patient is breast feeding
- Patient with recent (< 1 week) gastrointestinal disorders with diarrhea as major symptom
- Patient with any serious active infection
- Patient who has any other life-threatening illness [OR] or organ system dysfunction, which in the opinion of the investigator would either compromise patient safety or interfere with the evaluation of the test radiopharmaceutical
- Patient who [NOT] cannot communicate reliably with the investigator
- Patient who is unlikely to cooperate with the requirements of the study
- Patient who is unwilling [OR] and/or unable to give informed consent
- Patient at increased risk of death from a pre-existing concurrent illness
- Patient who participated already in this study.
