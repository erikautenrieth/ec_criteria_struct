The following exclusion criteria apply to all subjects:
1. For individuals undergoing blister [OR] or skin biopsy procedures, history of keloid formation.
2. Current [OR] or prior (within 3 months) anticoagulant [OR] or anti-platelet therapy (other than aspirin [OR] or non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs [NSAIDs]).
3. Current [OR] or prior (within 3 months) use of immunomodulatory drugs (e.g., chemotherapy, [OR] steroids), except if approved by the principal investigator.
4. Pregnancy.
5. Any condition that, in the opinion of the investigator, contraindicates participation in the study.
