Inclusion Criteria:
 - Male [OR] or female, 18 to 65 years of age.
 - Able to provide informed consent.
 - Present to Stanford Emergency Department as a trauma [AND] with a major operative lower extremity injury
 - Glasgow coma scale of 15 within 24 hours after admission [OR] or extubation
 - Negative urinary toxicology screen for illicit substances;
 - Negative pregnancy test if female [AND] and less then 60 y/o;
 - [NOT] No [OR] suspicion for a head injury and/or negative head CT scan for intracranial hemorrhage [OR] or injury based on standard of care
 - [NOT] No history of seizure disorder [OR] or other neurological disorders.
 - All patients included must screen positive for PHQ-9 [AND] score >4 (positive symptoms of depression) and CES-T score <36 (poor coping self-efficacy).
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Incarceration,
 - Pregnant females,
 - Prior psychotic disorder,
 - Current use of anti-depressant [OR] or anti-psychotic medications,
 - Prior-admission opioid use within 30 days (patients will be screened for recent prescription opioid use using the CURES report),
 - Heavy alcohol use,
 - Lesional neurological disorder [OR] or brain implant [OR] or intracranial ferromagnetic material,
 - Seizure disorder
