Inclusion Criteria:
 - age > 18 years
 - female gender
 - rigid cystoscopy for the first time
Exclusion Criteria:
 - presence of any contraindication for cystoscopy (such as lidocaine allergy, urinary tract infection, [OR] and urethra-related anatomical disorders)
 - any analgesic used 24 hours before cystoscopy
 - manipulations [OR] such as Double-J stent placement [OR] or removal, and/or bladder biopsy.
