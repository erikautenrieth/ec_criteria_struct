Inclusion Criteria:
 - All participants were included if their age more than or equal to 18 years. Body mass index < 30 kg/m2, total cholesterol < 200mg/dl, serum triglycerides [AND] < 200 mg/dl and random blood glucose level < 126 mg/dl.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Participants had acute [OR] or chronic metabolic disorders (liver, [OR] kidney, [OR] thyroid, [OR] endocrine [OR] or gastrointestinal disorders).
 - Females [AND] with irregular menstrual cycle or on estrogen therapy [OR] (7).
 - Participants with organic cerebral lesion that was detected on cranial computed tomography (CT) [OR] or cranial magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).
