Inclusion Criteria:
 - ASA physical status 1 and 2, underwent gynicological laparotomic surgery under general anesthesia, followed up in PACU(postoperative care unit)
Exclusion Criteria:
 - ASA 3 or 4, mental retardation, [OR] psychiatric disease, history of malignant hyperthermia in patient [OR] or her family, neurological disease, [OR] morbid obesity, history of asthma [AND] and follow-up in the intensive care unit.
