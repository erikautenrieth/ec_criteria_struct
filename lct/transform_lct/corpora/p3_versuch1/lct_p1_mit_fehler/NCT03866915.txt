Inclusion Criteria:
 - All patients older than 18 years undergoing elective surgery [AND] that receive a preoperative visit by anesthesiologists.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients undergoing emergency surgery
 - Patients under 18 years of age
 - Patients who refuse to participate
 - Patients [NOT] unable to perform the 6MWT
 - Claudication [AND] of the lower extremities and [NOT] inability to maintain a constant pace of walking on level ground
