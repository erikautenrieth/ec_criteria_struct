Inclusion Criteria:
 - Women over age [AND] 18 with a core biopsy proven borderline [OR] or high-risk breast lesion that requires further excision for management based on the surgeon's assessment
 - Informed consent must be obtained.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Women [AND] who are considered high-risk based on a greater than 25% lifetime risk of breast cancer as per the IBIS (International Breast Cancer Intervention Study) Breast Cancer Risk Evaluation Tool
 - Pathology that is felt to be discordant with imaging
 - Extensive calcifications [OR] extending more than 20 mm and/or masses greater than 30 mm
 - The following lesions will be [NOT] excluded based on their increased risk of upstaging; lesions (other than fibroadenomas, [OR] phyllodes [OR] and papillomas) that are associated with a palpable mass, [OR] ADH [AND] (atypical ductal hyperplasia) with high-risk features (ADH [AND] with zonal necrosis, significant cytological atypia [OR] or more than 2 foci), LCIS [AND] (lobular carcinoma in situ) with high-risk features [OR] (associated with ADH, [OR] pleomorphic LCIS, [OR] zonal necrosis [OR] and > 4 foci) or discordant with imaging, [OR] papilloma [OR] with atypia, and spindle cell lesion (especially if there is atypia).
 - Lesions that are suspicious for borderline [OR] or malignant Phyllodes, [OR] DCIS (ductal carcinoma in situ), invasive mammary carcinoma, [OR] or encapsulated papillary carcinoma
 - Any lesion that either the radiologist, pathologist [OR] or surgeon feels is [NOT] not amenable to VAPE [OR] or surgical excision
