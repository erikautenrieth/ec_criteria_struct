Inclusion Criteria:
 - All adult patients (aged ≥18 years) undergoing elective bariatric surgery and by any approach (Includes surgery with laparoscopic, assisted laparoscopic approach [OR] or open approach) in a participating hospital during the 30-day cohort [AND] period with a planned overnight stay.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients undergoing emergency surgery
 - Endoscopic procedures
 - Patients under 18 years of age
 - Patients with previous bariatric surgery [AND] who undergo revision surgery
 - Patients who refuse to participate
