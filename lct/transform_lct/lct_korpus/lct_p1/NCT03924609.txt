Inclusion Criteria:
A. Histologically proven breast cancer confirmed by biopsy [OR] or pathological examination of the resected tumor.
B. Histologically confirmed breast cancer patients, fulfilling any of the following:
 1. Multiple bone metastases indicated by bone scan
 2. Bone scan positive, [AND] and proven by other imaging examinations, including computed tomography (CT), magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) [OR] and X-ray.
 3. Bone scan positive, [AND] with clinical symptoms, including bone pain, [OR] pathologic fracture, [OR] spinal cord compression and so on.
 4. Bone metastases indicated by PET-CT
 5. Histologically proven bone metastases
Exclusion Criteria:
A. [NOT] No bone metastases confirmed by pathological examination 
B. Bone metastases secondary to other malignant tumor other than breast cancer 
C. Secondary primary tumor
