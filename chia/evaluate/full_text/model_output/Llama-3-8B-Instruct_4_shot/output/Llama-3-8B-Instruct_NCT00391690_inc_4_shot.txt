Patients with histologically confirmed diagnosis of prostate cancer [AND] who have not yet developed bone metastases. 
Prostate cancer patients with a rise in PSA [AND] under hormone therapy. 
PSA criteria: 
Patients who have undergone prostatectomy: any rise in PSA [OR] or 
Patients without prostatectomy: 2 consecutive rises in PSA levels [AND] relative to a previous reference value, separated by one month. The first measurement must occur one month after the reference value [AND] must be above the reference value. The second confirmatory measurement taken one month after the first measurement must be greater than the first measurement. 
Previous chemotherapy [OR] or radiotherapy must have been performed [AND] ≥ 8 weeks prior to study entry. 
Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) score of 0 [OR], 1 [OR] or 2 [AND] (patients that spend less than 50% of time in bed during the day) 
Adequate liver function - serum total bilirubin concentration [NOT] less than 1.5 x upper limit of normal value 
Age: ≥ 18 years 
Patient has given written informed consent [AND] prior to any study-specific procedures. Patients with psychiatric [OR] or addictive disorders which prevent them from giving their informed consent [NOT] must not enter the study.
