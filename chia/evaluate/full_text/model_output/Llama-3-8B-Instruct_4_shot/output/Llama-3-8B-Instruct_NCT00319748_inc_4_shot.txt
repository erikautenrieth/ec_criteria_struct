Adequate performance status: 
Breast - Karnofsky score > 50 [AND]; 
Ovarian, endometrial or cervical - Gynecologic Oncology Group (GOG) performance score ≤2 [OR]. 
If female [AND] and of childbearing potential, are willing to use adequate contraception (hormonal, barrier method, abstinence) prior to study entry [AND] for the duration of study participation. 
Normal organ function within 14 days of study entry [AND]. 
Diagnosis of one of the following malignancies: 
Metastatic breast cancer (BR) [OR] 
Metastatic ovarian cancer (OV) [OR] 
Metastatic endometrial cancer (EM) [OR] 
Metastatic cervical cancer (CX) [OR]. 
Measurable metastatic disease (>1cm) [AND] in at least one site other than bone-only [AND]. 
Progression on or failure to respond to at least one previous chemotherapy regimen for metastatic disease [AND]. 
Progression on prior therapy with a hormonal agent [IF] estrogen receptor or progesterone receptor positive, and/or with trastuzumab [IF] HER2-neu positive. [IF] patient has progressed through hormone or trastuzumab therapy only, must have received one chemotherapy regimen [AND]. 
Measurable metastatic disease as defined by Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) [AND]. 
Primary tumor must have been diagnosed histologically as either epithelial ovarian cancer, fallopian tube cancer, or primary peritoneal cancer (not borderline or low malignant potential epithelial carcinoma) [AND]. 
Subjects must have failed at least two previous chemotherapy regimens [AND]. Paclitaxel must have been a component of one or both regimens [AND] and cisplatin or carboplatin must have been a component of one or both regimens [AND]. 
Measurable metastatic disease [AND]. 
Histologically proven recurrent or persistent endometrial cancer that is not amenable to curative treatment with surgery and/or radiation therapy [AND] has failed 2 previous treatment regimens [AND]. 
Measurable metastatic disease [AND]. 
Histologically proven recurrent or persistent squamous cell carcinoma, adenosquamous carcinoma, or adenocarcinoma of the cervix that is not amenable to curative treatment with surgery and/or radiation therapy [AND] has failed 2 previous treatment regimens [AND].
