<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Patients residing in the following clinical states will be considered: 
A. Rising PSA: Patients with a history of localized disease who have undergone definitive radiation [AND] surgery. These patients must demonstrate progression of disease biochemically as outlined below [AND] may not have radiographically evident disease. 
B. Non-castrate metastatic: Patients must present with radiographic evidence of metastatic disease at the time of diagnosis [OR] after treatment for localized disease. These patients must show newly detected disease [OR] progressing disease in bone [OR] in soft tissue. Biochemical progression is defined as: minimum no. of determinations: 3 [AND] Interval: >2 weeks [AND] Minimal Baseline PSA value (ng/ml): 2 [AND] Minimal % increase in range of values: 50%. 
Diagnosis of prostate adenocarcinoma histologically confirmed at MSKCC [AND] 
Patient must have level of serum testosterone above the lower limit of normal [AND] 
Karnofskcy performance status (KPS) >_70% [AND] 
Patients must have adequate organ function as defined by the following laboratory criteria: 
WBC >_3500/mm3 [AND] platelet count >_100,000/mm3 [AND]. 
Bilirubin <2.0 mg/dl [OR] SGOT <3.0 X the upper limit of normal [AND]. 
Creatinine <_1.6 mg/dl [OR] creatinine clearance >_60 cc/min [AND]. 
Prior hormonal therapy is allowed as: 
1. Neoadjuvant treatment prior to radiation therapy [OR] radical prostatectomy, provided that the total duration of exposure does not exceed 10 months [AND]. 
2. One cycle of intermittent therapy up to a maximum exposure of 10 months [AND]. 
Patients must be at least 18 years of age [AND] 
Patients must have signed an informed consent document stating that they understand the investigational nature of the proposed treatment [AND] [NOT] have any medical condition that would make them unsuitable for the study.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
