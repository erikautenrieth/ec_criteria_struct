<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Women must be [OR] postmenopausal (i.e.12 months without menstrual period) [OR] surgically sterile, [NOT] i.e. women of child bearing potential are not allowed to be included into the study. In cases of doubt [AND] a pregnancy test should be performed. (NB -post menopausal women currently receiving hormone replacement [AND] are permissible) 
Acute myocardial infarction < 12 h defined as: 
1. [AND] Angina or equivalent symptoms > 20 min [AND] 
2. [AND] ST elevation in 2 contiguous ECG leads (= 2 mm precordial lead, = 1 mm limb lead). This ECG recording serves as baseline ECG, i.e. ECG I. 
[AND] Planned primary percutaneous coronary intervention 
[AND] The subject has given written informed, dated consent to participate in the study
