<EDITED_CRITERIA>
1. Congestive heart failure; 
2. [OR] CABG or Percutaneous Coronary Intervention (PCI) procedure; 
3. [OR] Planned need for major surgery (e.g. valve surgery or resection of aortic or left ventricular aneurysm, carotid end-arterectomy, abdominal aortic aneurysm surgery etc.); 
4. [OR] Congenital heart disease; 
5. [OR] Transmural myocardial infarction within the previous seven days [AND] CK has not returned to normal; 
6. [OR] Chest pain lasting longer than 30 minutes within 12 hours pre-procedure [AND] CK enzymes positive (≥ 2x the normal upper limit); 
7. [OR] History of any cerebrovascular accident; 
8. [OR] Left main stenosis of 50% or more; 
9. [OR] Intention to treat more than 1 totally occluded major epicardial vessel; 
10. [OR] Single vessel (single territory) disease [NOT] including patients with multi-vessel disease.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
