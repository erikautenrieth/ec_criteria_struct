<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Patients with a histologically [OR] cytologically proven diagnosis of NSCLC 
[AND] Unresectable (locally advanced) stage IIIa [OR] IIIb disease 
[AND] Initial radiotherapy field of treatment to encompass greater than or equal to 30% of the esophagus 
[AND] Life expectancy greater than or equal to 6 months 
[AND] Estimated weight loss less than or equal to 10% in the 3 months before study randomization 
[AND] Measurable disease 
[AND] 18 years of age or older 
[AND] Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 - 2 
[AND] Hemoglobin (hgb) greater than or equal to 10 g/dL without transfusional support [OR] growth factor use in the 4 weeks before study randomization 
[AND] Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) greater than or equal to 1.5 x 10^9/L without growth factor use in the 2 weeks before study randomization 
[AND] Platelet count greater than or equal to 100 x 10^9/L 
[AND] Serum bilirubin less than or equal to 1.5 x institutional upper limit of normal (ULN) 
[AND] Serum creatinine less than or equal to 2.0 mg/dL [OR] (Note: Patients with a serum creatinine greater than or equal to 1.4 and less than or equal to 2.0 mg/dL must demonstrate a 24-hour urinary creatinine clearance greater than or equal to 50 mL/min) 
[AND] Females of childbearing potential: negative serum [OR] urine pregnancy test 
[AND] Patient must give written informed consent before participating in any study-specific procedure, randomization, [OR] receiving investigational product. 
[AND] Patients with reproductive capability must agree to practice adequate contraception methods.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
