<EDITED_CRITERIA>
1. Have dementia [OR] delirium (as determined by the palliative care specialist) at study entry.
2. Are pregnant [AND] not planning to terminate the pregnancy during the study period.
3. Have been taking corticosteroids for longer than 48 hours [OR] have a history of corticosteroid use within the past 30 days.
4. Have pulmonary edema [OR] ascites [OR] pitting edema on clinical examination.
5. Are unable to walk [AND] have a history of falls or near-falls in the past 30 days.
6. Have a history of serious adverse gastrointestinal events (i.e., bleeding [OR] perforation) [OR] history of a coagulopathy [OR] current anti-coagulant use.
7. Have an ALT/AST>3x upper limit of normal [NOT] due to a known liver disease.
8. Patients on methotrexate [AND] have not had a dose adjustment in the past 30 days.
9. Patients taking melatonin receptor agonists (such as Rozerem® [ramelteon]) [AND] have not had a dose adjustment in the past 30 days.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
