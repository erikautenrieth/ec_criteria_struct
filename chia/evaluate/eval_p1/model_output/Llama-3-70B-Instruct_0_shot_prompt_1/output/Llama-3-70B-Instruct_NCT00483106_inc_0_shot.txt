<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:

1. Male or female patients aged 18-65 years [AND] with a diagnosis of Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) according to the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, 5th Edition (DSM-5) criteria.
2. Patients must have a history of ADHD symptoms [AND] have been treated with a stable dose of medication for at least 3 months prior to screening.
3. Patients must have a body mass index (BMI) ≥ 18.5 kg/m² [AND] ≤ 40 kg/m².
4. Patients must be able to understand [AND] provide written informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:

1. Patients with a history of any other psychiatric disorder [OR] neurological disorder that may interfere with the diagnosis of ADHD.
2. Patients with a history of substance abuse [OR] dependence within the past 6 months.
3. Patients with a history of seizure disorder [OR] have been treated with an anticonvulsant medication within the past 6 months.
4. Patients with a history of cardiovascular disease [OR] have been treated with a medication for cardiovascular disease within the past 6 months.
5. Patients who have taken an investigational drug within the past 30 days [OR] have participated in a clinical trial within the past 30 days.
6. Patients who are pregnant [OR] breastfeeding [OR] planning to become pregnant during the study period.
7. Patients with a known allergy [OR] intolerance to the study medication.
8. Patients who are unable to comply with the study protocol [NOT] willing to adhere to the study procedures.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
