<EDITED_CRITERIA>
1. Are referred to the Cachexia Clinic with involuntary weight loss of >5% of their premorbid weight within the previous 6 months AND
2. Are 18 years of age or older AND
3. Have a Karnofsky performance score of 60 or higher AND
4. Can maintain oral food intake during the study AND
5. Can understand the study procedures and can sign an informed consent form AND
6. Are NOT currently taking melatonin AND
7. Are taking megestrol acetate AND continue to lose weight despite at least 2 weeks of therapy AND
8. Have a calculated creatinine clearance of >/= 60 cc/min.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
