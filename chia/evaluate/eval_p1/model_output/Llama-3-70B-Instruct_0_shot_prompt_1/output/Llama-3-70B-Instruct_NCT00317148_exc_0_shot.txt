Body mass index (BMI) of 35 kg/m2 or more. 
Significant metabolic and endocrine diseases [AND] 
Diagnosis of cancer [OR]

Palpable fibroids or uterine prolapse: Grade 2 or 3 [OR] 
[NOT] Cigarette smoking
