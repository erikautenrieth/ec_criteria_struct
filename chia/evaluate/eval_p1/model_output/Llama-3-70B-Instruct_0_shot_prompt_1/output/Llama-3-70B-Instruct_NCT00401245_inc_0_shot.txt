<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Generally healthy, postmenopausal woman who seeks treatment for hot flushes [AND] 
Meets 1 of the following: 
At least 12 months of spontaneous amenorrhea [OR] 
At least 6 months of spontaneous amenorrhea with serum follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) levels > 40 mIU/mL [OR] 
At least 6 weeks postsurgical bilateral oophorectomy (with or without hysterectomy) [OR] 
Hysterectomized without bilateral oophorectomy [AND] 
with serum FSH levels >40 mIU/mL.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
