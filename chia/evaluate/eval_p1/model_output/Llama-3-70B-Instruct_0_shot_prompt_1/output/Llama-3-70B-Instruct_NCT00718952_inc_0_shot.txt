<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Subjects aged 12-65 [AND] 
Confirmed idiopathic pulmonary hypertension [OR] connective tissue disease associated pulmonary hypertension [OR] congenital heart disease(with Eisenmenger syndrome) associated pulmonary hypertension [AND] 
Baseline 6-minutes walking distance 150m-550m [AND] 
WHO pulmonary hypertension function II-III [AND] non-responder to calcium channel blockers [AND] 
Documented written informed consent.
