<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:

1. Male or female subjects 6-17 years of age [AND] a diagnosis of Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) according to the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, 5th Edition (DSM-5) criteria.
2. Subjects must have a history of ADHD symptoms for at least 6 months prior to screening [AND] a positive response to ADHD treatment in the past.
3. Subjects must have a score of ≥ 24 on the ADHD Rating Scale-IV (ADHD-RS-IV) [AND] a score of ≥ 2 on the Clinical Global Impressions-Severity of Illness Scale (CGI-S).

Exclusion Criteria:

1. Subjects with a current diagnosis of bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, or other psychotic disorders [OR] a history of these conditions.
2. Subjects with a current diagnosis of major depressive disorder [OR] a history of suicidal ideation or behavior.
3. Subjects with a history of seizure disorder [OR] a history of stroke or transient ischemic attack.
4. Subjects with a known history of substance abuse [OR] dependence within the past 6 months.
5. Subjects with a significant medical condition that could impact the safety of the subject or the efficacy of the study medication, such as uncontrolled hypertension, diabetes, or cardiac disease [NOT] adequately controlled with medication.
6. Subjects who have taken certain medications, including stimulants, antidepressants, or antipsychotics, within 30 days of screening [OR] who have taken certain medications, including monoamine oxidase inhibitors, within 14 days of screening.
7. Subjects who have a history of allergic reaction to the study medication [OR] its excipients.
8. Subjects who are unable to comply with the study procedures [NOT] willing to provide informed consent.
9. Subjects who have participated in another clinical trial within 30 days of screening [NOT] who are currently participating in another clinical trial.
10. Subjects who are pregnant [OR] breastfeeding [OR] who plan to become pregnant during the study period.
