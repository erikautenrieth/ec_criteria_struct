<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Eligible patients must be between 6 and 12 years of age [AND] have a diagnosis of Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) according to the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, 5th Edition (DSM-5) [AND] have a Clinical Global Impression-Severity (CGI-S) score of 4 or higher at screening. 

Exclusion criteria include a history of bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, [OR] other psychotic disorders [OR] a history of seizure disorder, except febrile seizures [OR] a history of significant neurological disorders, including stroke, brain tumor, [OR] spinal cord injury. 

Patients are also excluded if they have a known allergy or intolerance to study medication [OR] are taking medications that are contraindicated with the study medication [OR] have a history of substance abuse or dependence within the past 6 months [OR] have a positive drug screen at screening. 

Additionally, patients are excluded if they have a significant medical condition that, in the opinion of the investigator, would interfere with the patient's ability to participate in the study [OR] have a planned surgery or hospitalization during the study period [OR] are pregnant or lactating [OR] have a body mass index (BMI) > 40 kg/m². 

Patients who are unable to swallow capsules [NOT] able to comply with the study procedures [NOT] able to provide informed consent will also be excluded.
