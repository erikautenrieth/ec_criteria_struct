<EDITED_CRITERIA>
1. Are referred to the Cachexia Clinic with involuntary weight loss of >5% of their premorbid weight within the previous 6 months. 
2. Are 18 years of age [OR] older 
3. Have a Karnofsky performance score of 60 [OR] higher. 
4. Can maintain oral food intake during the study [AND] can understand the study procedures [AND] can sign an informed consent form. 
5. Are [NOT] currently taking melatonin. 
6. Are taking megestrol acetate [AND] continue to lose weight despite at least 2 weeks of therapy. 
7. Have a calculated creatinine clearance of >/= 60 cc/min.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
