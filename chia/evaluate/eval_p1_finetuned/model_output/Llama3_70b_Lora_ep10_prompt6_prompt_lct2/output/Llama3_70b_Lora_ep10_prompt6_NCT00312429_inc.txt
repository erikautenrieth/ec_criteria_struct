Inclusion Criteria:
- Diagnosis reviewed at transplant center and confirmed to fit the criterion for high risk blood disease [OR] or cancer, as defined for the study 
- Estimated life expectancy of at least 6 weeks following study entry 
- Cancer and Leukemia Group B (CALGB) performance status less than or equal to 2 
- White blood cell count, [OR] platelet, [OR] hematocrit, [OR] tuberculosis, [OR] aspartate aminotransferase (AST), alanine aminotransferase (ALT), alkaline phosphatase, [OR] creatinine, [OR] and HIV test results reviewed by transplant center 
- Multiple gated acquisition (MUGA), echocardiogram, [OR] cardiac MRI, [OR] and/or pulmonary function tests (PFT) performed and reviewed by transplant center (for individuals with an ejection fraction [OR] and diffusing capacity [DLCO] of 40-50%, the appropriate cardiology [OR] or pulmonary consultations should be considered if the individual has severe heart [OR] or lung disease at the initiation of therapy) 
- Sufficient number of umbilical cord blood units available for transplantation 
- If female, willing to use contraception throughout the study
