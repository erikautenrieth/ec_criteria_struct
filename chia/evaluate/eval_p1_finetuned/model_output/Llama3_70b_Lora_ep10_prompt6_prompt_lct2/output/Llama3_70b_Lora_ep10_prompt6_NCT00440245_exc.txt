Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Age 18-40 years.
  -  Former smokers
  -  Normotensive
  -  Normal spirometry
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Current smoker
  -  HbA1C >6.5%
  -  pregnant and nursing women
  -  Acute illness in the previous 4 weeks
  -  Allergy or intolerance to the investigational product
  -  Chronic disease (cardiovascular, metabolic, respiratory, gastrointestinal, liver, kidney, neurological, musculoskeletal, autoimmune, endocrine, hematological or cancer)
  -  Medication that requires prescription
  -  Laboratory values outside the reference ranges (see additional lab tests)
