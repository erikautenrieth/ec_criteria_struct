Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Have dementia [OR] or delirium (as determined by the palliative care specialist) at study entry. 
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Are pregnant 
 2. Have been taking corticosteroids for longer than 48 hours. 
 3. Have pulmonary edema, [OR] ascites [OR] or pitting edema on clinical examination. 
 4. Are [NOT] unable to walk. 
 5. Have a history of serious adverse gastrointestinal events (i.e., bleeding [OR] or perforation),history of a coagulopathy [OR] or current anti-coagulant use. 
 6. Have an ALT/ [OR] AST>3x upper limit of normal. 
 7. Patients on methotrexate. 
 8. Patients taking melatonin receptor agonists (such as Rozerem® [ramelteon]).
