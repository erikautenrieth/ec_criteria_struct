Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Evidence of decompensated liver disease (Childs B-C), hepato-cellular carcinoma, [OR] pre-existing severe depression [OR] or other psychiatric disease, significant cardiac disease, significant renal disease, [OR] seizure disorders [OR] or severe retinopathy.
  -  received telbivudine as the antiviral therapy [OR] or have received more than one NA in the past.
  -  received interferon [OR] or peginterferon treatment in the past.
  -  received antiviral therapy for any systemic anti-viral, [OR] anti-neoplastic [OR] or immuno-modulatory treatment (including supraphysiologic doses of steroids [OR] and radiation) within the past 6 months.
  -  Positive test at screening for anti-HIV, [OR] anti-HCV.
  -  Patients who are expected to need systemic antiviral therapy other than that provided by the study at any time during their participation in the study are also [NOT] excluded. Exception: patients who have had a limited (<=7 days) course of acyclovir for herpetic lesions more than 1 month prior to the first administration of test drug are [NOT] not [NOT] excluded.
  -  Serum total bilirubin > 3 times the upper limit of normal at screening.
  -  History [OR] or other evidence of bleeding from esophageal varices [OR] or other conditions consistent with decompensated liver disease.
  -  History [OR] or other evidence of a medical condition associated with chronic liver disease other than HBV (e.g., hemochromatosis, [OR] autoimmune hepatitis, [OR] metabolic liver diseases including Wilson's [OR] and alpha1-antitrypsin deficiency, [OR] alcoholic liver disease, [OR] toxin exposures, [OR] thalassemia).
  -  Women [AND] with ongoing pregnancy [OR] or [AND] who are breast feeding.
  -  Neutrophil count <1500 cells/mm3 [OR] or platelet count <90,000 cells/mm3 at screening.
  -  Hemoglobin < 11.5 g/dL for females [AND] and < 12.5 g/dL for men at screening.
  -  Serum creatinine level >120 umol/ml for men [AND] and >105 umol/ml for women at screening.
  -  History of severe psychiatric disease, especially depression. Severe psychiatric disease is defined as major depression [OR] or psychosis, a period of treatment with an antidepressant medication [OR] or major tranquilizer at therapeutic doses for depression [OR] or psychosis for at least 3 months, a suicidal attempt, hospitalization for psychiatric disease, [OR] or a period of disability due to a psychiatric disease.
  -  History of immunologically mediated disease (e.g., inflammatory bowel disease, [OR] idiopathic thrombocytopenic purpura, [OR] lupus erythematosus, [OR] autoimmune hemolytic anemia, [OR] scleroderma, severe psoriasis, [OR] rheumatoid arthritis).
  -  History [OR] or other evidence of chronic pulmonary disease associated with functional limitation. Severe cardiac disease (e.g., NYHA Functional Class III or IV, myocardial infarction within 6 months, ventricular tachyarrhythmias requiring ongoing treatment, unstable angina [OR] or other significant cardiovascular diseases).
  -  History of a severe seizure disorder [OR] or current anticonvulsant use.
  -  Evidence of an active [OR] or suspected cancer [OR] or a history of malignancy [AND] where the risk of recurrence is >=20% within 2 years. Patients with a lesion suspicious of hepatic malignancy on a screening imaging study will only be eligible if the likelihood of carcinoma is <=10% following an appropriate evaluation.
  -  History of having received any systemic anti-neoplastic [OR] (including radiation) or immunomodulatory treatment (including systemic corticosteroids) <=6 months prior to the first dose of study drug [OR] or the expectation that such treatment will be needed at any time during the study.
  -  Major organ transplantation.
  -  Thyroid disease [AND] with thyroid function poorly controlled on prescribed medications. Patients with abnormal thyroid stimulating hormone [OR] or T4 concentrations, [AND] with elevation of antibodies to thyroid peroxidase [AND] and any clinical manifestations of thyroid disease are [NOT] excluded.
  -  History [OR] or other evidence of severe retinopathy (e.g. CMV retinitis, [OR] macula degeneration) [OR] or clinically relevant ophthalmological disorder due to diabetes mellitus [OR] or hypertension
  -  [NOT] Inability [OR] or [NOT] unwillingness to provide informed consent or abide by the requirements of the study.
  -  History [OR] or other evidence of severe illness [OR] or any other conditions which would make the patient, in the opinion of the investigator, [NOT] unsuitable for the study.
  -  Patients with a value of alpha-fetoprotein >100 ng/mL are [NOT] excluded, unless stability (less than 10% increase) has been documented over at least the previous 3 months.
  -  Evidence of drug [OR] and/or alcohol abuse (20g/day for women [AND] & 30g/day for men).
  -  Patients included in another trial [OR] or having been given investigational drugs within 12 weeks prior to screening
  -  Any known history of hypersensitivity to interferon.
