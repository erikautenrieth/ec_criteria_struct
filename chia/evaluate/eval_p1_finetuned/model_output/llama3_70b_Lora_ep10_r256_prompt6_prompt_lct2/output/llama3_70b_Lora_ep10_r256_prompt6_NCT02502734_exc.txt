Inclusion Criteria:
A history of life-threatening asthma defined for this protocol as an asthma episode that required intubation, [OR] hypercapnea requiring [NOT] non-invasive ventilatory support, [OR] respiratory arrest, [OR] hypoxic seizures [OR] or asthma-related syncopal episode(s). 
Subjects with a history of asthma exacerbation requiring the use of systemic corticosteroids (tablets, [OR] suspension, [OR] or injection) for at least 3 days [OR] or a depot corticosteroid injection [OR] or emergency room attendance [OR] (within 3 months) or requiring hospitalization for asthma (within 6 months) prior to screening. 
Significant, [NOT] non-reversible active pulmonary disease (e.g. cystic fibrosis, [OR] bronchiectasis, [OR] tuberculosis). 
Culture-documented [OR] or suspected bacterial [OR] or viral infection of the upper [OR] or lower respiratory tract, [OR] sinus [OR] or middle ear that is [NOT] not resolved within 4 weeks of Visit 1 [AND] and led to a change in asthma management [OR] or, in the opinion of the Investigator, is expected to affect the subject's asthma status [OR] or the subject's ability to participate in the study. 
Any fracture in the leg to be measured within 6 months prior to the screening visit. 
Any metabolic disorders [OR] or other diseases that may impact on normal growth patterns. 
[NOT] No major surgery requiring general anaesthesia for at least 3 months prior to the screening visit. 
 [NOT] No febrile illnesses [AND] with temperature >39 degree celsius for more than five consecutive days within the week preceding the Screening Visit. 
Any significant abnormality [OR] or medical condition identified at the screening medical assessment (including serious psychological disorder) that in the Investigator's opinion, [NOT] preclude entry into the study due to risk to the subject [OR] or that may interfere with the outcome of the study. 
Clinical visual evidence of candidiasis at Visit 1 (Screening). 
Use of any of the prohibited medications listed in protocol. 
Strenuous physical exercise within 3 hours of Visit 1 (Screening) 
Drug allergies: Any adverse reaction including immediate [OR] or delayed hypersensitivity to any intranasal, [OR] inhaled, [OR] or systemic corticosteroid therapy. Known [OR] or suspected sensitivity to the constituents of the ELLIPTA Inhaler (i.e., lactose, [OR] FF). 
Milk Protein Allergy: History of severe milk protein allergy. 
The subject has participated in a clinical trial [AND] and has received an investigational product within the following time period prior to the first dosing day in the current study: 30 days, 5 half-lives [OR] or twice the duration of the biological effect of the investigational product (whichever is longer). 
Exposure to more than 4 investigational medicinal products within 12 months prior to the first dosing day. 
[NOT] Unable to use the ELLIPTA inhaler [AND] and peak flow meter correctly. 
An affiliation with the Investigator site: the parents/guardians [OR] or child is an immediate family member of the participating Investigator, sub-Investigator, study coordinator, [OR] or employee of the participating Investigator. 
The Parent [OR] or Guardian has a history of psychiatric disease, [OR] intellectual deficiency, [OR] substance abuse [OR] or other condition (e.g. [NOT] inability to read, comprehend [OR] or write) which may affect: validity of consent to participate in the study; adequate supervision of the subject during the study; compliance of subject with study medication [AND] and study procedures (e.g. completion of daily diary, [OR] attending scheduled clinic visits); subject safety [AND] and well-being. 
Children in care: Children [AND] who are wards of the government [OR] or state are [NOT] not eligible for participation in this study.
