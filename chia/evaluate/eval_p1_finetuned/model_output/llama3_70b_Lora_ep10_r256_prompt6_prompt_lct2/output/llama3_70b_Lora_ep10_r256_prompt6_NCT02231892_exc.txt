Inclusion Criteria:
1. Personal history of stroke, [OR] brain lesions, [OR] previous neurosurgery, any personal history of seizure [OR] or fainting episode of unknown cause, [OR] or head trauma resulting in loss of consciousness, lasting over 30 minutes [OR] or with sequela lasting longer than two days.
Justification: Stroke [OR] or head trauma can lower the seizure threshold, and are therefore contra-indications for TMS. Fainting episodes [OR] or syncope of unknown cause could indicate an undiagnosed condition associated with seizures.
Screening tool: TMS adult safety questionnaire, Medical History.
2. First-degree family history of any neurological disorder with a potentially hereditary basis, including migraines, [OR] epilepsy, [OR] or multiple sclerosis.
1. Justification: Neurological disorders can lower the seizure threshold, and are therefore contra-indications for TMS. First-degree family history of certain neurological disorders with a hereditary component increases the risk of the subject having an undiagnosed condition that is associated with lowered seizure threshold.
2. Screening tool: TMS adult safety screening, Medical History.
3. Cardiac pacemakers, [OR] neural stimulators, [OR] implantable defibrillator, [OR] implanted medication pumps, [OR] intracardiac lines, [OR] or acute, unstable cardiac disease, [AND] with intracranial implants (e.g. aneurysm clips, [OR] shunts, [OR] stimulators, [OR] cochlear implants, [OR] or electrodes) or any other metal object within [OR] or near the head that [NOT] precludes MRI scanning.
1. Justification: Any metal around the head is a contraindication for both MRI [AND] and TMS, as both methods involve exposure to a relatively strong magnetic field.
2. Screening tool: TMS adult safety screening, MRI safety screening, Medical History.
4. Noise-induced hearing loss [OR] or tinnitus.
1. Justification: individuals with noise-induced hearing problems may be particularly vulnerable to the acoustic noise generated by TMS [AND] and MRI equipment.
2. Screening tools: TMS adult safety screening.
5. Current use (any use in the past 4 weeks, chronic use within 6 past six months) of any investigational drug [OR] or of any medications with psychotropic, [OR] anti [OR] or pro-convulsive action.
1. Justification: The use of certain medications [OR] or drugs can lower seizure threshold and is therefore contra-indicated for TMS.
2. Screening tools: MRI safety screening questionnaire, Medical history, Medical Assessments: Urine toxicology analyzes for presence of a broad range of prescription [OR] and nonprescription drugs.
6. Lifetime history of major depressive disorder, [OR] schizophrenia, [OR] bipolar disorder, [OR] mania, [OR] or hypomania.
1. Justification: The population of interest here is a healthy control population [NOT] with [NOT] no psychiatric disorders. In subjects with depression, [OR] bipolar disorder, [OR] mania [OR] or hypomania, there is a small chance that TMS can trigger (hypo)manic symptoms.
2. Screening tools: SCID Screen Patient Questionnaire. Potential diagnoses will be further evaluated by a counsellor.
7. Meet current DSM V criteria for moderate to severe substance use disorder (excluding nicotine), smoke daily, [OR] or urine toxicology positive for any illicit substance inconsistent with history given.
1. Justification: The population of interest here is a healthy control population [NOT] with [NOT] no substance use disorder. Current use of illicit substances could impact on seizure threshold and is therefore contra-indicated for TMS.
2. Screening tools: SCID Screen Patient Questionnaire. Potential diagnoses will be further evaluated by a counsellor, Drug Use Survey (DUS), Substance Use Disorder Evaluation, Medical Assessments: urine qualitative drug screen is performed for methadone, [OR] benzodiazepines, [OR] cocaine, [OR] amphetamine/ [OR] methamphetamine, [OR] opiates, [OR] barbiturates, [OR] and tetrahydrocannabinol.
8. Have met DSM V criteria for moderate to severe substance use disorder (excluding nicotine, alcohol [OR] and cannabis) in the past, [OR] or have met DSM V criteria for moderate to severe substance use disorder for cannabis [OR] or alcohol in the past 5 years.
1. Justification: the population of interest here is a healthy control population [NOT] with [NOT] no present [OR] or past substance use disorder.
2. Screening tools: SCID Screen Patient Questionnaire. Potential diagnoses will be further evaluated by a counselor. Drug Use Survey (DUS), Substance Use Disorder Evaluation.
9. History of myocardial infarction, [OR] angina, [OR] congestive heart failure, [OR] cardiomyopathy, [OR] stroke [OR] or transient ischemic attack, [OR] or any heart condition currently under medical care.
1. Justifications: the risk of TMS for individuals with a heart condition is unknown.
2. Screening tool: physical assessment (EKG), medical history.
10. Pregnant women [OR] or women [AND] with reproductive potential [AND] who are sexually active [AND] and [NOT] not using an acceptable form of contraception.
1. Justification: it is unknown whether TMS poses a risk to fetuses.
2. Screening tool: Medical assessments (urine pregnancy test) at the beginning of each visit that involves TMS [OR] or MRI.
11. History of learning disability [OR] or current ADHD
1. Justification: Subjects should be able to perform cognitive tasks to a high degree of accuracy, both in the MRI scanner and outside the scanner. Subjects with ADHD/ [OR] LD may engage different neural circuitry even if they can perform the tasks.
2. Screening tool: Wechsler Abbreviated Scale of Intelligence, Medical history, Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale.
12. Participation in an rTMS session less than two weeks ago.
1. Justification: in order to limit exposure to TMS, we will [NOT] not enroll subjects who have received TMS less than two weeks ago.
2. Screening tool: TMS safety screening questionnaire.
