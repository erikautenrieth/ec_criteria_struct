Inclusion Criteria:
Histologically confirmed locally advanced gastric (primary endpoint includes proximal [OR] and mid-body stomach) [OR] or esophagogastric adenocarcinoma; distal gastric (antral) adenocarcinomas are eligible for enrolment but will [NOT] not be included in the primary analysis
Locally advanced disease as determined by endoscopic ultrasound (EUS) stage > primary tumor (T) 3 [OR] and/or any T, lymph nodes (N)+ disease [NOT] without metastatic disease (Mx)
All patients must have diagnostic laparoscopy [AND] with diagnostic washings for cytology; both cytology positive [OR] and negative patients are eligible for enrolment, but only cytology negative patients will be included in the primary analyses; gross peritoneal disease is [NOT] not eligible
Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status =< 1
Eligible for surgery with curative intent
Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) >= 1250/ul
Hemoglobin >= 9 g/dL
Platelets >= 100,000/ul
Total bilirubin < 1.5 x upper limit of normal
Serum glutamic oxaloacetic transaminase (SGOT) [OR] and serum glutamate pyruvate transaminase (SGPT) < 2.5 x upper limit of normal for patients [NOT] without liver metastases [OR] OR SGOT [OR] and SGPT < 5 x upper limit of normal for patients [AND] with liver metastases
Creatinine =< 1.5 x upper limit of normal
Measurable [OR] or [NOT] non-measurable disease by Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumor (RECIST) 1.1 will be allowed
Women [AND] of child-bearing potential [AND] and men must agree to use adequate contraception (hormonal [OR] or barrier method of birth control; abstinence) prior to study entry and for the duration of study participation, up until 30 days after final study treatment; should a woman become pregnant [OR] or suspect that she is pregnant while participating in this study, she should inform her treating physician immediately
Patients taking substrates, [OR] inhibitors, [OR] or inducers of cytochrome P450, family 3, subfamily A, polypeptide 4 (CYP3A4) should be encouraged to switch to alternative drugs whenever possible, given the potential for drug-drug interactions with irinotecan
Signed informed consent
