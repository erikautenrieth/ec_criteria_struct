Inclusion Criteria:
 1. bilateral AT 
 2. insertional AT 
 3. local steroid injection within 6 weeks [OR] or physical therapy within 4 weeks 
 4. [NOT] inability to comply with follow-up criteria 
 5. history of surgery on the Achilles tendon [OR] or systemic diseases (general inflammatory diseases such as rheumatologic disorders [OR] and diabetes) 
 6. daily use of opioids for pain
 7. anticoagulation [OR] or immunosuppressive therapy 
 8. intent to use NSAIDs [OR] or steroids 
 9. self-reported pregnancy
