Inclusion Criteria:
Subjects who have systemic infection 
Subjects who have human Immunodeficiency virus (HIV), [OR] hepatitis B virus (HBV), [OR] and hepatitis C virus (HCV) 
Subjects who need to take the medicine which is [NOT] prohibited during this study 
Subjects who have asthma 
Subjects who [NOT] can [NOT] not [NOT] stop treatment with topical steroids [OR] (group 1~5), oral antibiotics, [OR] whole body photochemotherapy, [OR] immunosuppressive drug within 4 weeks before the treatment visit 
Pregnant, [OR] breast-feeding women [OR] or women [AND] who plan to become pregnant during this study (Females [AND] of childbearing potential must have a negative urine pregnancy test) 
Subjects who currently participate in other clinical trial [OR] or participated in other clinical trial within 30 days 
Subjects who had a serious adverse events during stem cell therapy
Subjects who had a hypersensitivity to antibiotics [OR] or antimycotics 
Subjects who creatinine value is more than two times of the upper limit of the normal range at screening test 
Subjects who aspartate transaminase/ [OR] alkaline transaminase (AST/ [OR] ALT) value is more than three times of the upper limit of the normal range at screening test 
Subjects who have any other condition which the investigator judges would make patients [NOT] unsuitable for study participation
