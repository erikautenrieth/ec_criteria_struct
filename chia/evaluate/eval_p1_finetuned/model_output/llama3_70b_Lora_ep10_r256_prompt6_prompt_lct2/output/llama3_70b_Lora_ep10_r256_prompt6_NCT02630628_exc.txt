Inclusion Criteria:
Renal disease [NOT] unrelated to SLE (e.g. diabetes mellitus, other glomerular [OR] or tubulointerstitial disease, [OR] renovascular disease), [OR] or transplanted kidney.
Estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR by MDRD) =20 mL/min per 1.73 m2 [OR] or serum creatinine >300 micromol/L (3.39 mg/dL) at screening.
Renal biopsy showing cellular [OR] or fibrocellular crescent in more than 25% of glomeruli.
CNS [OR] or other severe organ manifestation of lupus that necessitate aggressive immunosuppressive therapy on its own.
Co-morbidities that require corticosteroid therapy (e.g. asthma, [OR] inflammatory bowel disease).
Treatment with prednisolone [OR] (or prednisone, [OR] or equivalent) at >20 mg/D for over 4 weeks within the past 3 months.
Treatment with MMF at >1.5 g/D for over 4 weeks within the past 3 months.
Known hypersensitivity [OR] or intolerability to prednisolone [OR] (or prednisone, [OR] or equivalent), TAC, [OR] or MMF at a dose of 1.25 g or below per day.
Subjects who are already on treatment with TAC, [OR] cyclosporine [OR] or any other calcineurin inhibitor for over 4 weeks within the past 12 months.
Treatment with cyclophosphamide, [OR] leflunomide, [OR] or methotrexate for over 2 weeks, [OR] or use of biological agent(s) regardless of duration, within the past 6 months (Note: prior use of azathioprine, [OR] mizoribine, [OR] intravenous immunoglobulins [OR] and anti-malarials is allowed).
Uncontrolled hypertension [AND] with systolic BP >160 mmHg [OR] or diastolic BP >95 mmHg.
Women [AND] who are pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding.
Women [AND] with childbearing potential [OR] or their male partners, [AND] who refuse to use an effective birth control method
