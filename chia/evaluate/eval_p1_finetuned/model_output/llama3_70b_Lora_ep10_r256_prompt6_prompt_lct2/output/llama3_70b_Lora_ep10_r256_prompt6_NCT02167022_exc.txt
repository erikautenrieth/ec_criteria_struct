Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Diagnosis: Diagnosis of CP secondary to neuronal migration.
Exclusion Criteria:
 2. Co-morbidities: Medical conditions that may [NOT] prevent the administration of rehabilitation therapies at the intensity required by the study, [OR] or that may compromise the study ability to maintain blindness, [OR] or that have a co-morbidity [NOT] not typically associated with CP (i.e. cancer, [OR] cystic fibrosis).
 3. Co-interventions: Anticipated pharmacological intervention [OR] or procedure [OR] or participation in other studies that may interfere with this study.
