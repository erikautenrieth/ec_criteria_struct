Early Syphilis Cases Determined to Be Serofast at 6 Months after Initial Treatment
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Confirmed diagnosis of early syphilis (primary, secondary or early latent syphilis)
  -  Age ≥18 years
  -  Received initial treatment with either benzathine penicillin G (BPG) 2.4 million units intramuscularly (IM) or doxycycline 100 mg orally twice daily for 14 days
  -  Serologic follow-up at 6 months after initial treatment
  -  Negative Treponema pallidum particle agglutination (TPPA) test at 6 months after initial treatment
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Confirmed diagnosis of neurosyphilis, ocular syphilis or syphilitic hepatitis
  -  Any concurrent sexually transmitted disease (STD)
  -  Any medical or psychiatric condition that might confound the study results
  -  Previous treatment for syphilis
  -  Previous history of any allergic reaction to penicillin or doxycycline
  -  Pregnant or breastfeeding women
  -  Women of child-bearing potential
  -  Subjects with a history of drug use
  -  Subjects who are using any kind of authentic e-device (e.g. Fitbit, Garmin, etc.) that tracks physical activity, sleep or other health related metrics.
