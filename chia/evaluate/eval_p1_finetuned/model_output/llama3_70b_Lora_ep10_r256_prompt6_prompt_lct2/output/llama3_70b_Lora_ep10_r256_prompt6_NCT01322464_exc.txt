Inclusion Criteria:
Subjects were [NOT] not to have a history [OR] or presence of significant cardiovascular, [OR] pulmonary, [OR] hepatic, [OR] renal, [OR] haematologic, [OR] gastrointestinal, [OR] endocrine, [OR] immunologic, [OR] dermatologic, [OR] neurologic, [OR] or psychiatric disease. 
Subjects were [NOT] not to have any history [OR] or presence [OR] or family history of schizophrenia, other psychotic illness, severe personality disorder, [OR] depression, [OR] or other significant psychiatric disorder. 
Subjects were [NOT] not to have a postural drop of 20 mmHg or more in systolic blood pressure at screening. 
Subjects were [NOT] not to have participated in a previous clinical trial within 90 days prior to study initiation. 
Subjects were [NOT] not to have donated plasma within 90 days prior to study initiation. 
Subjects were [NOT] not to have donated blood within 90 days prior to study initiation. 
Subjects were [NOT] not to have had an abnormal diet [OR] or substantial changes in eating habits within 30 days prior to study initiation. 
Subjects were [NOT] not to have had treatment with any known enzyme-altering agents (barbiturates, [OR] phenothiazines, [OR] cimetidine etc.) within 30 days prior to or during the study. 
Subjects were to have [NOT] no history of known hypersensitivity [OR] or idiosyncratic reaction to the study drug [OR] or related compounds. 
Subjects were [NOT] not to use any prescription medication within 14 days prior to or during the study. 
Subjects were [NOT] not to use any over-the-counter medication within 7 days prior to or during the study. 
Subjects were [NOT] not to have a history of alcohol [OR] or drug abuse within 2 years prior to the study (subjects with a history of previous use of cannabis were [NOT] not excluded unless they had used cannabis [OR] or cannabinoid based medicine within 30 days prior to study drug administration [OR] or were unwilling to abstain for the duration of the study).
