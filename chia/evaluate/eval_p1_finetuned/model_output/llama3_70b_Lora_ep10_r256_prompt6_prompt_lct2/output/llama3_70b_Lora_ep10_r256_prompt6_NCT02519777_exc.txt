Inclusion Criteria:
Major depressive disorder [AND] with psychotic features
Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI) [AND] with a clear impact on activities of daily living
Developmental delay, [OR] intellectual deficit, [OR] and/or severe educational disability resulting in some dependence for activities of daily living
Ongoing substance use disorder [AND] with significant impact on activities of daily living. Difficult [OR] or [NOT] impossible to determine whether cognitive [OR] or functional decline is due to substance use [OR] or HIV, [OR] or both
Evidence of intoxication [OR] or withdrawal during the screening evaluation
Central nervous system (CNS) infections [OR] or opportunistic conditions: brain abscess (bacterial, [OR] mycobacterial, [OR] fungal [OR] or Toxoplasma), meningitis [AND] with persistent neurologic impairment, primary CNS lymphoma, [OR] progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy (PML), [OR] or another structural brain lesion [AND] with neurological sequelae
Other CNS conditions: [NOT] non-opportunistic primary [OR] or metastatic brain tumors, uncontrolled seizure disorder, [OR] progressive multiple sclerosis, [OR] stroke [AND] with neurological sequelae, [OR] or dementia due to causes other than HIV (eg, Alzheimer's disease)
Constitutional illness (eg, persistent unexplained fever, [OR] diarrhea, significant weight loss, [OR] disabling weakness) within 30 days of screening
Known [NOT] untreated B12 deficiency [OR] or malnutrition (body mass index [BMI] less than 18) at screening
Evidence of current hepatitis C virus infection (HCV) (ie, HCV antibody [Ab] positive within 90 days prior to study entry unless also shown to be plasma HCV RNA negative within the same time period)
Unstable [OR] and advanced liver disease (as defined by the presence of at least one of the following: ascites, [OR] encephalopathy, [OR] coagulopathy, [OR] hypoalbuminemia, [OR] esophageal [OR] or gastric varices, [OR] or persistent jaundice)
Prior [OR] or current use of any CCR5 antagonist (such as MVC [OR] and cenicriviroc [CVC]) [AND] and integrase inhibitor (such as RAL, [OR] DTG, [OR] and elvitegravir [EVG])
Current use of any medication, including antiretrovirals, [NOT] prohibited in the study (refer to the A5324 protocol-specific web page [PSWP] for the prohibited medications)
Breastfeeding
Presence of an AIDS-defining opportunistic infection within 6 months prior to entry. Note: Refer to the A5324 Manual of Operations (MOPS) for the list of AIDS-defining opportunistic infections.
Active syphilis [OR] or treatment for syphilis within 90 days prior to study entry. NOTE: Active syphilis is defined as four-fold increase in serum rapid plasma reagin (RPR) [OR] or venereal disease research laboratory (VDRL) tests in an individual [AND] with past syphilis, [OR] or newly reactive serum RPR [OR] or VDRL [AND] with a reactive confirmatory test (enzyme immunoassays [EIA] [OR] or chemiluminescent assay [CIA], T. pallidum particle agglutination [TP-PA], [OR] or fluorescent treponemal antibody absorbed [FTA-ABS]).
Known allergy/ [OR] sensitivity [OR] or any hypersensitivity to components of study drugs [OR] or their formulation
