• currently taking haloperidol, [OR] artane, [OR] Phenergan (Promethazine), [OR] chlorpromazine, [OR] erythromycin, [OR] Azithromycin, [OR] clarithromycin, [OR] Ketoconazole, [OR] fluconazole, [OR] mefloquine (as prophylaxis), [OR] lumefantrine (in Coartem), [OR] quinine, [OR] Septrin
