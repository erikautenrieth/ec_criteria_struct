Inclusion Criteria:
Cardio-vascular pathologies, evoluting [AND] and uncontrolled, (severe HTA), cardiac deficiency, severe angor, severe arrhythmia. 
Infectious pathologies evoluting and requiring antibiotherapy. 
Patients HIV+. 
Transplanted patients [OR] or patients suffering from severe auto-immune disease. 
Psychiatric troubles that do [NOT] not allow the protocol follow-up. 
Pregnant [OR] or breast-feeding women. 
[NOT] No contraception.
