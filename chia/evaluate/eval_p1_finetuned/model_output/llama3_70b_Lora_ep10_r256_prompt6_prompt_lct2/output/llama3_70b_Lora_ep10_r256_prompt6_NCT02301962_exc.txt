Inclusion Criteria:
History [OR] or known presence of central nervous system metastases.
History of another malignancy except: Malignancy treated [AND] with curative intent [AND] and with [NOT] no known active disease present for >=5 years prior to enrolment [AND] and felt to be at low risk for recurrence by the treating physician; Adequately treated [NOT] non-melanomatous skin cancer [OR] or lentigo maligna [NOT] without evidence of disease; Adequately treated cervical carcinoma in situ [NOT] without evidence of disease; Prostatic intraepithelial neoplasia [NOT] without evidence of prostate cancer.
Known immediate [OR] or delayed hypersensitivity reaction [OR] or idiosyncrasy to drugs chemically related to panitumumab [OR] or excipients that contraindicates their participation.
Prior anti-epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFr) antibody therapy (e.g., panitumumab [OR] or cetuximab) [OR] or treatment with small molecule EGFr inhibitors (e.g., gefitinib, [OR] erlotinib, [OR] lapatinib).
Antitumor therapy (e.g., chemotherapy, [OR] hormonal therapy, [OR] immunotherapy, [OR] antibody therapy, [OR] radiotherapy), [OR] or investigational agent [OR] or therapy <=30 days before first dose of study treatment [OR] or [NOT] not recovered from any acute toxicity.
Other investigational procedure <=30 days before study entry.
History of interstitial lung disease (ILD) e.g., interstitial pneumonitis, [OR] pulmonary fibrosis [OR] or evidence of ILD on baseline chest computer tomography.
Subject previously enrolled to this study.
History of keratitis, [OR] ulcerative keratitis [OR] or severe dry eye.
Major surgery (e.g., requiring general anesthesia) <=30 days before first dose of study treatment. Subjects must have recovered from any surgery related toxicities.
Minor surgical procedure (e.g., open biopsy) <=7 days before first dose of study treatment, [OR] or [NOT] not yet recovered from prior minor surgery Note: uncomplicated placement of vascular access device, [OR] fine needle aspiration, [OR] thoracocentesis [OR] or paracentesis >=3 days prior to first dose of study treatment is acceptable.
Clinically significant cardiovascular disease (including myocardial infarction, unstable angina, symptomatic congestive heart failure, serious uncontrolled cardiac arrhythmia) <=6 months prior to enrolment.
History of any medical [OR] or psychiatric condition [OR] or laboratory abnormality that in the opinion of the investigator may increase the risk associated with the study participation [OR] or investigational product administration, compliance with the study procedures [OR] or may interfere with the interpretation of the results.
Unstable pulmonary embolism, [OR] deep vein thrombosis, [OR] or other significant arterial/ [OR] venous thromboembolic event <=30 days before first dose of study treatment. If on anticoagulation, subject must be on stable therapeutic dose prior to first dose of study treatment.
Subject [AND] who is pregnant [OR] or breast feeding, [OR] or planning to become pregnant during treatment [AND] and within 2 months after the discontinuation of study treatment.
Known positive test(s) for human immunodeficiency virus infection (testing is not required in the absence of clinical suspicion).
Active infection requiring systemic treatment [OR] or any uncontrolled infection <=14 days prior to first dose of study treatment (with the exception of uncomplicated urinary tract infection [OR] or upper respiratory tract infection).
Subject has any kind of disorder that compromises the ability of the subject to give written informed consent [AND] and/or to comply with study procedures or is unwilling or unable to comply with study requirements.
