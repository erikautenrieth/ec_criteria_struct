Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with idiopathic granulomatous mastitis
  -  Age >18 years
  -  Patients with a palpable breast mass
  -  Patients with imaging findings consistent with granulomatous mastitis
  -  Patients with histological confirmation of granulomatous mastitis
  -  Patients who have not received any prior treatment for granulomatous mastitis
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with known etiological factors for granulomatous mastitis such as tuberculosis, sarcoidosis, Wegener's granulomatosis, and Crohn's disease
  -  Patients with atypical histological features
  -  Patients with co-existing breast cancer
  -  Patients with previous irradiation to the affected breast
  -  Patients with a history of cardiac or autoimmune diseases
  -  Pregnant patients
  -  Patients with known hypersensitivity to any of the drugs used in the study
  -  Patients with impaired liver functions
  -  Patients with impaired kidney functions
  -  Patients with blood dyscrasias
  -  Patients with active infection
  -  Patients with diabetes mellitus
  -  Patients with hypothyroidism
  -  Patients with hypertension
  -  Patients with arteriosclerosis
  -  Patients with heart failure
  -  Patients with a history of myocardial infarction
