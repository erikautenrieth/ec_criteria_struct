Inclusion Criteria:
  -  PBC diagnosis ≥ 6 months prior to screening
  -  Evidence of liver disease (ALT/AST above ULN or total bilirubin above ULN)
  -  On a stable dose of ursodiol for at least 3 months prior to screening
  -  On a stable dose of immunosuppressant (if approved for use in PBC) for at least 3 months prior to screening
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. A medical condition, other than PBC, that in the investigator's opinion would [NOT] preclude full participation in the study [OR] or confound its results (e.g., cancer on active treatment) 
 2. AST [OR] or ALT > 3 × ULN 
 3. Total bilirubin > 2 × ULN 
 4. Auto-immune hepatitis 
 5. Primary sclerosing cholangitis 
 6. Known history of alpha-1-Antitrypsin deficiency 
 7. Known history of chronic viral hepatitis 
 8. Creatine kinase above ULN 
 9. Serum creatinine above ULN 
 10. For females, pregnancy [OR] or breast-feeding 
 11. Use of colchicine, [OR] methotrexate, [OR] azathioprine, [OR] or systemic steroids in the two months preceding screening 
 12. Current use of fibrates, including fenofibrates, [OR] or simvastatin 
 13. Use of an experimental treatment for PBC 
 14. Use of experimental [OR] or unapproved immunosuppressant 
 15. Any other condition(s) that would compromise the safety of the subject [OR] or compromise the quality of the clinical study, as judged by the Investigator
