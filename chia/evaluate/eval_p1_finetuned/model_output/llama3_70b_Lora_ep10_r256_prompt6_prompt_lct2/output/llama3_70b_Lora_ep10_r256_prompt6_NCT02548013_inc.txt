Inclusion Criteria:
 1. PPROM [AND] with gestational age between 27 to 34 weeks 
 2. Cephalic presentation 
 3. Clear amniotic fluid 
 4. Oral temperature > 38 C 
 5. Near distance from the hospital (the patient can reach hospital within one hour ) 
 6. Home environment safe [AND] and amenable to rest, availability of family support such as a sister [OR] or mother [AND] who will help the patient at home. 
 7. Maternal [AND] and fetal condition remain stable after hospitalization for 72 hours
