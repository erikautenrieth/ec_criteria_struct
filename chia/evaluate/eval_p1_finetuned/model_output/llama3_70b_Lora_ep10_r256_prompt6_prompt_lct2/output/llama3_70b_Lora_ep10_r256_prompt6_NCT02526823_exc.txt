Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with severe complications [OR] or severe infection;
Invasion of central nervous system;
Patients with severe heart disease history, including ventricular tachycardia (VT), [OR] atrial fibrillation (AF), [OR] heart block, [OR] myocardial infarction (MI), [OR] congestive heart failure (CHF), [OR] coronary heart disease patients needed therapy;
patients with severe allergic constitution, [OR] or those who are allergic to [OR] or intolerant of drug composition in chemotherapy regimens; [AND] with other malignant tumors in the past 5 years;
patients received doxorubicin therapy, total cumulative dose of adriamycin was more than 300 mg/m2, total cumulative dose of epirubicin was more than 450 mg/m2;
Patients participate in other clinical studies;
Other patients who are [NOT] not suitable for the study.
