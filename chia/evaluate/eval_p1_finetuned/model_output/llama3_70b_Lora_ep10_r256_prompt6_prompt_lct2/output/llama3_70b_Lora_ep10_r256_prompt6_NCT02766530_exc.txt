Inclusion Criteria:
Estimated GFR (eGFR) < 60 mL/min/1.73 m2 [AND] and blood glucose > 135 mg/dl; Past [OR] or present history of acute renal failure, [OR] renal dialysis, [OR] diabetes mellitus.
Exclusion Criteria:
Women [AND] who received metallic fixation, [OR] coronary artery stent in recent 3 months; [OR] or women [AND] who received mechanical valve replacement that is [NOT] not compatible with MR magnet; [OR] or women [AND] with aneurysmal clips, [OR] pacemakers.
Past history of claustrophobia.
Women [AND] who are pregnant [OR] or [AND] who are planning to be pregnant, [OR] or women [AND] who are lactating (though the possibility in our target population should be very low)
Past history of breast cancer within recent 5 years before the currently diagnosed breast cancer.
Women [AND] who received chemotherapy for other disease entity in recent 1 year.
Women [AND] who cannot cooperate with the examinations.
