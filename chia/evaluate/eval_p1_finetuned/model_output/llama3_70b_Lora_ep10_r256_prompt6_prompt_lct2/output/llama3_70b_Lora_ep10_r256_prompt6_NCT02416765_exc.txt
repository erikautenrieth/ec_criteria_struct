Inclusion Criteria:
1. Clinically significant microvascular complications: nephropathy (estimated glomerular filtration rate below 40 ml/min), [OR] neuropathy (especially diagnosed gastroparesis) [OR] or severe proliferative retinopathy as judged by the investigator. 
2. Recent (< 3 months) acute macrovascular event e.g. acute coronary syndrome [OR] or cardiac surgery. 
3. Ongoing pregnancy. 
4. Severe hypoglycemic episode within 1 month of screening. 
5. Agents affecting gastric emptying (Motilium®, [OR] Prandase®, [OR] Victoza®, [OR] Byetta® [OR] and Symlin®) as well as oral anti-diabetic agents (Metformin, [OR] SGLT-2 inhibitors [OR] and DPP-4 inhibitors) if [NOT] not at a stable dose for 3 months. Otherwise, these medications are acceptable and will be kept stable during the entire protocol. 
6. Oral steroids unless patients present a low stable dose (e.g. 10 mg or less of prednisone per day [OR] or physiological doses, less than 35 mg/day, of hydrocortisone Cortef®). Inhale steroids at stable dose in the last month are acceptable. 
7. Other serious medical illness likely to interfere with study participation [OR] or with the ability to complete the trial by the judgment of the investigator (e.g. unstable psychiatric condition). 
8. [NOT] Failure to comply with team's recommendations (e.g. [NOT] not willing to change pump parameters, follow algorithm's suggestions, etc). 
9. Living [OR] or planned travel [NOT] outside Montreal (> 1h of driving) area during closed-loop procedures.
