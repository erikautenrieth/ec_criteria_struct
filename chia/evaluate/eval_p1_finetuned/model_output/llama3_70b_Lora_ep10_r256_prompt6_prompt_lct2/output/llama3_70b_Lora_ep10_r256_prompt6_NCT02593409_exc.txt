Inclusion Criteria:
HIV infection at screening
participation in previous [OR] or concurrent HIV vaccine trials
lactating, [OR] pregnant [OR] or planning pregnancy
renal function impairment (serum creatinine >1.5 mg/dl), Fanconi syndrome
abnormal liver function tests (AST/ [OR] ALT > 43 U/L), liver disease, [OR] viral hepatitis, [OR] hepatitis B virus (HBV) infection
serum phosphorus <2.2mg/dl, [OR] osteoporosis
known sensitivity to components of the Truvada® formulation
any immunosuppressive treatment, such as systemic corticosteroids
assumption of medication that interacts with Truvada®
high likelihood of poor adherence to PREP [AND] and clinic attendance
any condition that in the opinion of the attending physician could endanger the health of the participant [OR] or render her unsuitable to participate in the trial
