Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis of idiopathic Parkinson's disease that is optimally treated (motor fluctuations <20% of subject's awake time). Subjects may be on levodopa therapy [AND] but must be stable at the time of entry into the study
Sexually active (i.e. =1 attempt/week) males, 40 - 64 years of age (inclusive) at time of screening
Diagnosis of moderate erectile dysfunction (defined according to the NIH Consensus Development Panel on Impotence) for more than 6 months [AND] and demonstrating and incomplete response to tadalafil alone
Subject demonstrating an IIEF-5 drug-free baseline score that is = 10 [AND] but = 16, and an IIEF-5 tadalafil-alone baseline score that is = 18
Subject in a stable heterosexual relationship for at least 6 months. (2)
Subject motivated to seek treatment for erectile dysfunction.
Subject with a total serum testosterone level = 300 ng/dL, with or without supplementation
Hoehn and Yahr Scale score of 1 - 3
Patient able to consent and comply with protocol requirements
Exclusion Criteria:
History of myocardial infarction, severe cerebrovascular disease, uncontrolled hypertension, or any cardiac or respiratory disease that would preclude the use of sildenafil/tadalafil
History of severe renal or hepatic disease
History of psychiatric disease, including schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, drug abuse, or personality disorders
History of gastrointestinal disease or disorder that would preclude the absorption of study drug
Uncontrolled diabetes mellitus
Hematological or biochemical values at baseline outside the reference ranges considered as clinically significant
Use of any other PDE5 inhibitor besides tadalafil within 1 week of the first study visit
Use of any other investigational drug within 1 week of the first study visit
Allergy to any ingredient of the study drug
Subjects with a history of priapism, penile prosthesis, or penile implant
Subjects with a history of deep vein thrombosis, pulmonary embolism, or retinal vascular disease
Subjects with a known sensitivity for any of the excipients of the study drug
Subjects with a history of cardiac dysrhythmia, including QTc prolongation
Subjects with a history of neurological disease, including multiple sclerosis, Parkinson's disease, or dementia
Subjects with a history of significant trauma to the pelvis or urinary tract
Subjects with a history of urinary retention or bladder pathology
Subjects with a history of prostate cancer or prostate surgery
Subjects with a known allergy to tadalafil or sildenafil
Subjects with any medical or psychiatric condition that, in the opinion of the Investigator, would preclude safe and/or successful completion of the study
