Inclusion Criteria:
Women must be postmenopausal (i.e.12 months [NOT] without menstrual period), [OR] or surgically sterile, i.e. women [AND] of child bearing potential are [NOT] not allowed to be included into the study. In cases of doubt a pregnancy test should be performed. (NB -post menopausal women currently receiving hormone replacement are permissible) 
Acute myocardial infarction < 12 h defined as: 
1. Angina [OR] or equivalent symptoms > 20 min [AND] and 
2. ST elevation in 2 contiguous ECG leads (= 2 mm precordial lead, = 1 mm limb lead). This ECG recording serves as baseline ECG, i.e. ECG I. 
Planned primary percutaneous coronary intervention 
The subject has given written informed, dated consent to participate in the study
