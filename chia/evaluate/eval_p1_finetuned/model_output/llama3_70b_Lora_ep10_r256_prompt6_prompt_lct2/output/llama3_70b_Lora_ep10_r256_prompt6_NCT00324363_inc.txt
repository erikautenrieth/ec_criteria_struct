Inclusion Criteria:
Treated with a stable dose of one of the following for at least 3 months prior to screening: * >=1000 mg/day immediate-release metformin; [OR] or metformin >=1000 mg/day [AND] and sulfonylurea; [OR] or sulfonylurea/ [OR] metformin combination therapy. 
HbA1c between 7.1% and 11.0%, inclusive. 
Body Mass Index (BMI) >21 kg/m^2 [AND] and <35 kg/m^2.
