Inclusion Criteria:
1. Patient has an allergy to nickel.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Patient has a diagnosis of bowel obstruction, [OR] bowel strangulation, [OR] peritonitis, [OR] bowel perforation, local [OR] or systemic infection, [OR] ischemic bowel, [OR] carcinomatosis [OR] or extensively spread inflammatory bowel disease.
 2. Patient is participating in another clinical trial which may affect this study's outcomes.
 3. Patient has been taking regular steroid medication.
 4. Patient has contraindications to general anesthesia.
 5. Patient has preexisting sphincter problems [OR] or evidence of extensive local disease in the pelvis.
