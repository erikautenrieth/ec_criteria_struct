Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with second primary cancer, except:adequately treated [NOT] non-melanoma skin cancer, curatively treated in-situ cancer of the cervix, [OR] or other solid tumor curatively treated [AND] with [NOT] no evidence of disease for <= 5 years.
Exclusion Criteria:
Has known active central nervous system(CNS) metastases
Has an active infection requiring systemic therapy
Pregnancy [OR] or breast feeding
Patients with cardiac problem
Any previous treatment with sunitinib
