Inclusion Criteria:
score level D on the SIGAM mobility grade 
have experienced 1 or more falls in the last month before the study 
have a residual limb length which does [NOT] not allow for seven inches clearance of bracket attachment for the PowerFoot 
the residual limb must be stable in volume ([NOT] no change in socket [OR] or socket padding in last 6 months) [AND] and [NOT] without pain that [NOT] limits function 
the sound-side (contralateral) lower extremity must be [NOT] free of impediments that affect gait, [OR] range of motion, [OR] or limb muscle activity 
Any diagnosed cardiovascular, [OR] pulmonary, [OR] neurological, [OR] and/ or orthopedic conditions that would interfere with subject participation
