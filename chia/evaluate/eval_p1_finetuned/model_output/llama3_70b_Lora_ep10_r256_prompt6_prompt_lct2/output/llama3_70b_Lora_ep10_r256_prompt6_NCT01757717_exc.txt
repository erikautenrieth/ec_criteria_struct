Inclusion Criteria:
Patients who may receive therapeutically effective doses via an external beam approach to the lesion of interest as specified by MSKCC Radiation Oncology Department dose constraint criteria. 
Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with kyphoplasty cement [OR] or hardware that would [NOT] preclude effective catheter placement. 
Patients with paraspinal extension of disease [AND] with visceral involvement. 
Abnormal complete blood count. Any of the following: 
Platelet count < 75,000/ml 
Hb level < 9gm/dl 
WBC < 3.5/ml 
Abnormal coagulation profile: INR > 2.5 [OR] and/or PTT > 80 
Patients who are on anticoagulation medication that may [NOT] not be safely held for the procedure (≥ 5 days for antiplatelet agents [OR] and warfarin; ≥ 24 hours for low-molecular weight heparin formulations) will be [NOT] excluded. 
Contraindications to general anesthesia
