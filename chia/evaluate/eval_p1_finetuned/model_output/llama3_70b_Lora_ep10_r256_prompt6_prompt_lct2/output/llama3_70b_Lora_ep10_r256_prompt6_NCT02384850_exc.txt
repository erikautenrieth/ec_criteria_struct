Inclusion Criteria:
 - For all phases:
 1. Histologically confirmed diagnosis of mCRC that is locally recurrent or metastatic.
 2. For phase 2 and 3: Previous treatment with FOLFIRI or FOLFOX for metastatic CRC.
 3. For phase 2 and 3: Previous treatment with one or two anti-EGFR monoclonal antibodies (mAbs) for metastatic CRC.
 4. For all phases: Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status 0-2.
 5. For all phases: Estimated creatinine clearance ≥ 50 mL/min.
 6. For all phases: Total bilirubin level ≤ 1.5 times the upper limit of normal.
 7. For all phases: AST and ALT levels ≤ 3.0 times the upper limit of normal.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - For all phases:
 1. For phase 1: Prior treatment with Lonsurf®.
 2. Clinically significant cardiac disease.
 3. Active, uncontrolled serious infection.
 4. Symptomatic CNS metastasis.
 5. Unresolved toxicities from prior therapy.
 6. For all phases: Pregnant or lactating women. Documentation of a negative pregnancy test must be available for women of childbearing potential. Woman of childbearing potential are premenopausal women with intact reproductive organs and women less than two years after menopause.
 7. For all phases: Lab values that in the opinion of the primary physician would prevent safe participation in the trial.
 8. For phase 2 and 3: Previous treatment with regorafenib.
 9. For all phases: Use of any concurrent anticancer therapy.
 10. For all phases: Use of any medication that is known to affect lfting of the intestinal barrier function.
 11. For all phases: No other concurrent uncontrolled medical conditions.
 12. For phase 2 and 3: prior treatment with 2 or more lines of chemotherapy.
