Inclusion Criteria:
Overlap syndrome with Primary Sclerosing Cholangitis (PSC) [OR] or Primary Biliary Cholangitis (PBC) (Paris criteria, strong positive Anti-Mitochondrial Antibodies (AMA), past liver biopsy [OR] or cholangiographic findings compatible with PBC [OR] or PSC).
Presentation with acute liver failure, defined as presence of hepatic encephalopathy [AND] and coagulopathy (INR > 1.5)
Current treatment with prednisone/ [OR] prednisolone [AND] and/or immunosuppressive medication for an indication other than autoimmune hepatitis
Current systemic infection
Other clinically significant medical conditions that could interfere with the trial
If female [AND] of childbearing potential: known pregnancy, [OR] or unwilling to practice anticontraceptive measures.
History of [NOT] noncompliance with medical regimens, or patients who are considered to be potentially unreliable or unable to participate
Mental instability [OR] or incompetence, such that the validity of informed consent or compliance with the trial is uncertain
