Inclusion Criteria:
• singleton pregnancy
 • 34-42 weeks of gestation
 • high risk pregnancies: DM, or GDM, or hypertension, or preeclampsia
Exclusion Criteria:
a disease that might affect hepatic [OR] or renal function, contraindications to opioid analgesics, fetal growth retardation, signs of fetal asphyxia by cardiotocography, [OR] meconium stained amniotic fluid [OR] or placental insufficiency. The subjects should [NOT] not have received fentanyl during the previous 14 days.
