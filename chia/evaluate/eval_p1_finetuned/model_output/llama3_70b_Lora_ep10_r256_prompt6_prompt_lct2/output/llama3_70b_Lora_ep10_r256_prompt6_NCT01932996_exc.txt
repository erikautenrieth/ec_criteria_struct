Inclusion Criteria:
Use of smoking cessation medications [OR] or interventions in last 30 days
Exclusion Criteria:
Unstable medical illness that requires immediate medical care
AUDIT score of < 5 [OR] or > 26
Pregnancy [OR] or other Nicotine Replacement Therapy (NRT) contraindications
Current history [OR] or in past 6 months of psychotic disorder [OR] or major depressive disorders that is [NOT] not stable on treatment for past 3 months
Cognitive impairment
