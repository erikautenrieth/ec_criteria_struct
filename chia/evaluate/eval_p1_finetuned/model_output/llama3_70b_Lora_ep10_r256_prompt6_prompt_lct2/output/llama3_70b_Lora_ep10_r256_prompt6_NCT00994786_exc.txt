Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients with any other primary DSM-IV psychiatric diagnosis in addition to Obsessive Compulsive Disorder.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who currently fulfil criteria for DSM-IV eating disorder, [OR] body dysmorphic disorder, current alcohol [OR] or substance abuse, [OR] or who have a lifetime history of bipolar disorder. Patients with a history of Schizophrenia [OR] and other psychotic disorders, [OR] Delirium, [OR] Dementia, [OR] and Amnestic [OR] and other cognitive disorders.
 - Subjects with a concurrent Axis II Cluster A Personality Disorder
Borderline [OR] or Antisocial Personality Disorder.
 - Subjects who based on history [OR] or mental status examination have a significant risk of committing suicide, in the investigator's opinion.
 - Subjects with a history of more than three adequate trials with an SSRI.
 - Subjects who have had an adequate trial of pregabalin.
 - Subjects who have initiated psychotherapy in the last 4 months prior to the first visit.
 - Subjects who, during the course of the study, would be likely to require treatment with prohibited concomitant therapy.
 - Prior use of [OR] or a known allergy [OR] or hypersensitivity to pregabalin.
 - Subjects who have participated in any clinical trial within 30 days prior to entering the study, [OR] or in a clinical trial involving a psychotropic medication within the 6 months prior to entering the study.
 - Any subject who has been taking benzodiazepines before entering the study [AND] who: 1) [NOT] cannot tolerate being [NOT] free of benzodiazepines for 4 weeks, [OR] or 2) has signs [OR] or symptoms of benzodiazepine withdrawal [OR] or rebound at the end of those 4 weeks. Should a patient entering the study, [AND] who is currently on benzodiazepines develop discontinuation symptoms with discontinuation of their benzodiazepine, we will treat these symptoms with a more gradual benzodiazepine taper. Study will be delayed until the patient is able to tolerate the [NOT] discontinuation for 4 weeks.
 - Patients with a current seizure disorder, [OR] organic brain disorder [OR] or a history of seizure disorders (except for febrile seizures in childhood).
 - Patients with thyroid pathology, [AND] the treatment of which has [NOT] not been stabilized for at least three months.
 - Patients on neuroleptic drugs in the two months prior to study entry [OR] or cognitive behavioural therapy specific to OCD within four weeks of study entry
 - Pregnant [OR] or lactating females, [OR] or if sexually active [AND] and of childbearing potential, [NOT] not using adequate methods of birth control.
 - Patients with a history [OR] or evidence of a medical condition that would expose them to an increased risk of a significant adverse event [OR] or interfere with assessments of safety [OR] and efficacy during the trial.
 - Patients receiving psychotropics of any kind, including betablockers [OR] and other anticonvulsants. Sleep medication such as oral chloral-hydrate [OR] or zopiclone are acceptable.
 - Patients using any herbal psychoactive treatments, e.g. St John's Wort, [OR] Valerian, [OR] Kava Kava, [OR] L-tryptophan.
 - Patients with any condition [OR] or on any therapy that, in the investigator's opinion, [OR] or as indicated in the pregabalin product label, may pose a risk to the subject.
 - Patients who have had a major life event in the past three months, which in the judgement of the investigator is influencing their current condition.
 - Patients having clinically significant abnormal laboratory, [OR] or ECG findings [NOT] not resolved by further examinations.
