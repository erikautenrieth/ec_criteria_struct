Inclusion Criteria:
Obstructed outlet syndrome (objectified by defeacography)
Irritable bowel syndrome (Rome-IV criteria for irritable bowel syndrome)
Congenital [OR] or organic bowel pathology
Rectal prolapse
Anatomical limitations [NOT] preventing placement of an electrode
Skin [OR] and perineal disease [AND] with risk of infection
Previous large bowel/ [OR] rectal surgery
Stoma
Coexisting neurological disease
Significant psychological co-morbidity as assessed subjectively by the investigator
Being [OR] or attempting to become pregnant during study follow-up
