Inclusion Criteria:
1. Subject is a post-menopausal woman, defined as either; six (6) months [OR] or more (immediately prior to screening visit) [NOT] without a menstrual period, [OR] or prior hysterectomy [OR] and/or oophorectomy 
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Subject is pregnant [OR] or lactating [OR] or is attempting [OR] or expecting to become pregnant during the study 
 2. Women [AND] with abnormally high liver enzymes [OR] or liver disease. (ALT [OR] or AST exceeding 2.0 x ULN [AND] AND total bilirubin exceeding 1.5 x ULN at screening [AND] and confirmed on repeat). 
 3. Received an investigational drug in the 30 days prior to the screening for this study 
 4. Women [AND] with a history of PCOS 
 5. Concurrent use of any testosterone, [OR] progestin, [OR] androgen, [OR] estrogen, [OR] anabolic steroids, [OR] DHEA [OR] or hormonal products for at least 2 weeks prior to screening [AND] and during the study. 
 6. Use of oral contraceptives in the preceding 2 weeks. Use of Depo-Provera® in the preceding 10 months. 
 7. Has an IUD in place 
 8. Women currently using narcotics 
 9. Women currently taking spironolactone 
 10. Infectious disease screen is positive for HIV [OR] or Hepatitis A, [OR] B [OR] or C. 
 11. Clinically significant abnormal findings on screening examination [OR] or any condition which in the opinion of the investigator would interfere with the participant's ability to comply with the study instructions [OR] or endanger the participant if she took part in the study
