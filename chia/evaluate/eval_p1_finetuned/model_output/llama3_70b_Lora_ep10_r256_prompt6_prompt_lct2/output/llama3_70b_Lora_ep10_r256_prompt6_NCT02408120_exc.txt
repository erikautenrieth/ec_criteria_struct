Inclusion Criteria:
  -  1. Ages of 18 and above
  -  2. Patients with three GCTs or more
  -  3. Patients with GCTs in the past or present
  -  4. Patients with a previous diagnosis of any cancer or presence of any tumor
  -  5. Patients with hereditary syndromes such as NF1, NF2, Schwannomatosis, Juvenile Polyposis coli, Peutz-Jeghers syndrome, Juvenile Granulosa Cell Tumor of the ovary, Testicular Cancer, or any other genetic abnormalities
  -  6. Patients who are able to give informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  1. Patients who are unwilling or unable to give informed consent
  -  2. Patients who are unable to undergo genetic testing
  -  3. Patients with a history of a bone graft or any other tissue graft
  -  4. Patients with a history of a previous diagnosis of any cancer or presence of any tumor other than a GCT
  -  5. Patients who are using any kind of authentic or counterfeit hormonal therapy
  -  6. Patients who are using any kind of dietary supplements
  -  7. Patients with a history of a serious medical condition
  -  8. Patients who are unable to comply with study procedures
