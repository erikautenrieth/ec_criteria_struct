Inclusion Criteria:
  -  18-55
  -  Males [OR] and females
  -  Subjects of normal intelligence
  -  Subjects with a current DSM-IV diagnosis of obsessive-compulsive disorder
Exclusion Criteria:
Any other primary DSM-IV diagnosis; DSM-IV criteria for body dysmorphic disorder, [OR] bipolar affective disorder, [OR] schizophrenia, [OR] psychotic disorder, current alcohol/ [OR] substance abuse.
A previous adequate trial of topiramate
Comorbid major depressive disorder diagnosis which predates OCD diagnosis
Cognitive behavioural therapy [OR] or additional psychotherapy in past four months
Allergy [OR] or hypersensitivity to topiramate
BMI < 20
History of kidney stones
