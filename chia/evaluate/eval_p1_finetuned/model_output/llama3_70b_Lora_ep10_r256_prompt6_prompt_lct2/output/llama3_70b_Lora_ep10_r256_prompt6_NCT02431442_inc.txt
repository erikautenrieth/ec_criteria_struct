Inclusion Criteria:
Able to provide voluntary, written informed consent with comprehension of all aspects of the protocol, prior to any study procedures. 
Healthy obese male [OR] and female volunteers aged 18 to 55 years, inclusive. Heterozygous subjects may be 18 to 65 years inclusive. 
In good general health, [NOT] without significant medical history, [OR] physical examination findings, [OR] or clinical laboratory abnormalities. 
Body Mass Index of 30-40 kg/m2, inclusive. Heterozygous subjects may have a broader BMI range; to be eligible heterozygous subjects may have a BMI 27 -55 kg/ m2, inclusive. 
Stable body weight during the previous 6 months, based on Investigator judgment. 
Blood pressure <140/90 mmHg at Screening [AND] and D-1. Measurement may be repeated within 24 hours, based on Investigator judgment. 
Females must [NOT] not be pregnant [AND] and must have a negative serum pregnancy test result at the Screening Visit [AND] and Day -1. 
Females of childbearing potential must agree to be abstinent [OR] or else use any two of the following medically acceptable forms of contraception from the Screening Period through the Final Study Visit: hormonal, [OR] condom [AND] with spermicidal jelly, [OR] diaphragm [OR] or cervical cap [AND] with spermicidal jelly, [OR] or IUD. Hormonal contraception must have started at least 3 months prior to screening. A female whose male partner has had a vasectomy must agree to use one additional form of medically acceptable contraception. Subjects must agree to practice the above birth control methods for 30 days from the final visit as a safety precaution. 
Females of [NOT] non-childbearing potential, defined as surgically sterile (status post hysterectomy, [OR] bilateral oophorectomy, [OR] or bilateral tubal ligation) [OR] or post-menopausal for at least 12 months (and confirmed with a screening FSH level in the post-menopausal range), do [NOT] not require contraception during the study. 
Males [AND] with female partners of childbearing potential must agree to use two medically acceptable forms of contraception as described above, with one of the two forms being condom [AND] with spermicide, from the Screening Period through the Final Study Visit. Males [AND] with female partners of childbearing potential [AND] who themselves are surgically sterile (status post vasectomy) must agree to use condoms [AND] with spermicide over the same period of time. Male subjects must agree to practice the above birth control methods for 30 days from the final visit as a safety precaution.
