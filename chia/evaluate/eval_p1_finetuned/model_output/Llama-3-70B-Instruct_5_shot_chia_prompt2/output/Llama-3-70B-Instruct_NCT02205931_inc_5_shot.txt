<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Age between 1 month [AND] 24 months of age ([NOT] not beyond second birthday at baseline).
Diagnosis of epilepsy [AND] confirmed.
At least an average of 4 seizures/week in baseline period.
Failed response to previous trial of two anti-epileptic drugs. In the case of infantile spasms this could include a trial of corticosteroids.
Children with written informed consent [AND] from parent/guardian.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
