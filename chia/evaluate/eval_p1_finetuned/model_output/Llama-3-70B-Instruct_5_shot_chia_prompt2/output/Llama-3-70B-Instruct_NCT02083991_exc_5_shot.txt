<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Diabetes mellitus [OR] plasma glucose >11,1 at admission.
Receiving steroids [AND] at the time of transplantation [OR] likely to need steroids after transplantation.
Multiorgan transplants [AND]/or previously transplanted [AND] with any other organ than kidney.
Panel reacting antibodies(PRA) >25% in most recent test [OR] considered to be of high risk for rejection which requires an enhanced immunosuppression.
[NOT] Renal transplants from HLA-identical sibling.
Hypersensitivity to [OR], or disability to take immunosuppressive drugs.
Blood group(ABO)-incompatible transplants.
Unlikely to comply [AND] with the study requirements.
Transplant from donor positive [AND] for HIV, HBsAg, Hepatitis C.
Female of childbearing potential planning [OR]/being pregnant [OR] unwilling to use contraception.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
