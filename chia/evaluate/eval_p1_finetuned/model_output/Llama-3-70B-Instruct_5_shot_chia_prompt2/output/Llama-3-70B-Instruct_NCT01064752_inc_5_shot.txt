3. Predicted adherence [AND] to the medication. 
4. Capable [AND] of providing informed consent. 
5. > 18 years old 
6. CD4 cell counts >150 cells/μL (though likely most, if [NOT] all, will be >250 cells/μL). 
7. When available, subjects will be screened [AND] for stability of blood CD4 [AND] HIV RNA levels.
