<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Patients with a diagnosis of hypertension, defined as systolic blood pressure ≥140 mmHg [OR] diastolic blood pressure ≥90 mmHg
Patients with a history of myocardial infarction [AND] who are clinically stable
Patients with a history of stroke [AND] who are clinically stable
Patients with a history of transient ischemic attack [AND] who are clinically stable
Patients with a history of coronary artery disease [AND] who are clinically stable
Patients with a history of peripheral artery disease [AND] who are clinically stable
Patients with a history of heart failure [AND] who are clinically stable
Patients with a history of diabetes mellitus [AND] who are clinically stable
Patients with a history of dyslipidemia [AND] who are clinically stable
Patients with a history of smoking [AND] who are clinically stable
[NOT] Patients with a history of cancer within the past 5 years
[NOT] Patients with a history of liver disease
[NOT] Patients with a history of kidney disease
[NOT] Patients with a history of bleeding disorders
[NOT] Patients who are pregnant [OR] breastfeeding
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
