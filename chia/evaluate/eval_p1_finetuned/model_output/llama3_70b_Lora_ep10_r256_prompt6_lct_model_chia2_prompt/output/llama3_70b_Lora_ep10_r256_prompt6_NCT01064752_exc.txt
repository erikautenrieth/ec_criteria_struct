Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Taking a tetracycline within 6 months [OR] or history of adverse reaction to minocycline [OR] or another tetracycline.
 2. Enhanced risk from lumbar puncture, including documented [OR] or suspected cerebral mass lesion predisposing to brain herniation [OR] or bleeding diathesis.
 3. Pregnancy [OR] or expectation of pregnancy during the study.
 4. Active opportunistic infection [OR] or active neurological disease that might confound evaluation.
 5. ADC Stage > 1.
 6. Hemoglobin < 10 Gms/dL.
 7. BUN [OR] or creatine above the normal limits.
 8. Taking other drugs known to reduce the metabolism of minocycline and thus increase the probability of toxicity.
