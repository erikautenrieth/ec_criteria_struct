Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients with stable (Canadian Cardiovascular Society 1, 2, 3 or 4) [OR] or unstable (Braunwald class IB, [OR] IC, [OR] IIB, [OR] IIC, [OR] IIIB, [OR] IIIC) angina pectoris [OR] and ischemia, [OR] or patients with atypical chest pain [OR] or even those [AND] who are asymptomatic [AND] provided they have documented myocardial ischaemia (e.g. treadmill exercise test, [OR] radionuclide scintigraphy, [OR] stress echocardiography, [OR] Holter tape); 
 2. Patients [AND] who are eligible for coronary revascularization (angioplasty [OR] or CABG); 
 3. At least 2 lesions (located in different vessels [AND] and in different territories) potentially amenable to stent implantation; 
 4. de novo native vessels; 
 5. Multivessel disease [AND] with at least one significant stenosis in LAD [AND] and with treatment of the lesion in another major epicardial coronary artery. A two-vessel disease [OR] or a three-vessel disease may be viewed as a combination of a side branch [AND] and a main epicardial vessel provided they supply different territories; left anterior descending, [OR] left circumflex [OR] and right coronary artery); 
 6. Total occluded vessels. One total occluded major epicardial vessel [OR] or side branch can be included [AND] and targeted as long as one other major vessel has a significant stenosis amenable for SA, provided the age of occlusion is less than one month e.g. recent instability, [OR] infarction [AND] with ECG changes in the area subtended by the occluded vessel. Patients with total occluded vessels of unknown duration [OR] or existing longer than one month [AND] and a reference over 1.50 mm should [NOT] not be included, [NOT] not even as a third [OR] or fourth vessel to be dilated; 
 7. Significant stenosis has been defined as a stenosis of more than 50% in luminal diameter (in at least one view, on visual interpretation [OR] or preferably by QCA); 
 8. Left ventricular ejection fraction should be at least 30%.
