Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Psychiatric disorders other than insomnia, [OR] PTSD [OR] and specific phobias; including bipolar [OR] and psychotic disorders [AND] and meeting criteria for DSM-5 moderate alcohol [OR] or drug use disorders within the past year.
  -  Diagnosis of a sleep disorder other than insomnia including PSG findings of apnea/ [OR] hypopnea [OR] or periodic limb movement indices > 10/hour;
  -  Medical conditions that require consistent use of medication [OR] or compromise sleep;
  -  History of moderate to severe traumatic brain injury [OR] or mild traumatic brain injury [AND] with ongoing post-concussive symptoms;
  -  Suicidal ideation [AND] with intent to act [OR] or with specific plan [AND] and intent in the past 6 months (Type 4 - 5 ideation on the Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale) [OR] or a concerning history of prior suicidal behavior.
  -  Caffeine use exceeding 5 cups of coffee per day [OR] or its equivalent;
  -  Habitual bedtimes after 3 AM, habitual rise times after 10 AM, [OR] or habitual napping > 1hour/day;
  -  Pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding, [OR] or expecting to conceive while in study;
  -  Positive urine toxicology.
