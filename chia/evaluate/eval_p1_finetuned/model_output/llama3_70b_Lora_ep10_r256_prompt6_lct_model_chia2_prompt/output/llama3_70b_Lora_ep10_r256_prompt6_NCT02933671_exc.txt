Inclusion Criteria:
ASA 4 [OR] or 5
revision hip arthroplasty
diagnosis of chronic pain
daily chronic opioid use (over 3 months of continuous opioid use)
[NOT] inability to communicate pain scores [OR] or need for analgesia
acute hip fracture
Infection at the site of block placement
Age under 18 years old [OR] or greater than 75 years old
Pregnant women
Intolerance/ [OR] allergy to local anesthetics
Weight <50 kg
Suspected, [OR] or known addiction to [OR] or abuse of illicit drug(s), [OR] prescription medicine(s), [OR] or alcohol within the past 2 years.
Uncontrolled anxiety, [OR] schizophrenia, [OR] or other psychiatric disorder that, in the opinion of the investigator, may interfere with study assessments [OR] or compliance
Current [OR] or historical evidence of any clinically significant disease [OR] or condition that, in the opinion of the investigator, may increase the risk of surgery [OR] or complicate the subject's postoperative course.
