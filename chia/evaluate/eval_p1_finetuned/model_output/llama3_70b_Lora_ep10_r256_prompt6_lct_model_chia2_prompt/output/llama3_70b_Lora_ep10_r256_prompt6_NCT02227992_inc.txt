Inclusion Criteria:
Paediatric subjects aged =28 days (= 1 month) [AND] to <18 years, requiring [NOT] non-emergent open hepatic, [OR] abdominal, [OR] retroperitoneal, [OR] pelvic [OR] or thoracic ( [NOT] non-cardiac) surgical procedures. i) The first 36 subjects to be enrolled will be subjects aged =1 years [AND] to <18 years. ii) The next 4 subjects to be enrolled will be subjects aged =28 days [AND] to <1 year.
The subject's parent/legal guardian must be willing to give permission for the subject to participate in the trial, and provide written informed consent for the subject. In addition, assent must be obtained from paediatric subjects who possess the intellectual and emotional ability to comprehend the concepts involved in the trial.
