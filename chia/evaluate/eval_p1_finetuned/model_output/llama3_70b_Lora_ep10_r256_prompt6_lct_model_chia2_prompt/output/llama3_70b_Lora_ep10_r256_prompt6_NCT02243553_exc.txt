Inclusion Criteria:
1. History [OR] or presence of allergy to the study drugs [OR] or their components [OR] or drugs of their class, [OR] or a history of drug [OR] or other allergy that, in the opinion of the physician responsible, contraindicates their participation 
2. Any finding of the medical examination (including blood pressure, [OR] pulse rate [OR] and electrocardiogram) deviating from normal [AND] and of clinical relevance 
3. History [OR] or diagnosis of any significant medical conditions: Including but not limited to gastrointestinal, [OR] hepatic, [OR] renal, [OR] respiratory, [OR] cardiovascular, [OR] metabolic, [OR] immunologic, [OR] hematological, [OR] psychiatric, [OR] neurological, [OR] oncological [OR] or hormonal disorders 
4. Known elevated liver enzymes in past clinical trials [AND] with any compound (experimental [OR] or marketed) 
5. Clinically relevant laboratory abnormalities (e.g. Hgb<11g/dL, [OR] Hct<30g/dL, total cholesterol >240mg/dL, [OR] triglycerides >500mg/dL, [OR] fasting glucose >130mg/dL, liver function tests >2.5x upper limit of normal, baseline international normalized ratio >1.2) 
6. History of evidence of clinically significant hepatic, [OR] cardiac, [OR] pulmonary, [OR] endocrine, [OR] immunological, [OR] gastrointestinal, [OR] hematological, [OR] vascular [OR] or collagen disease 
7. History of alcohol abuse [OR] or use of any illicit drugs 
8. [NOT] Unable to abstain from more than one beer [OR] or alcohol equivalent per day for the duration of the study 
9. Use of tobacco products [OR] and/or history of smoking within the past 2 months 
10. Pregnant [OR] or breast feeding 
11. Sexually active women [AND] of childbearing age [AND] who do [NOT] not use an acceptable barrier method of birth control 
12. Hypersensitivity to caffeine, [OR] warfarin, [OR] vitamin K, [OR] omeprazole, [OR] dextromethorphan, [OR] midazolam, [OR] tipranavir, [OR] ritonavir [OR] or their excipients 
13. Concomitant treatment with other experimental compounds 
14. Concomitant administration of any prescription [OR] or over the counter medications known to alter P450 enzyme [OR] or P-gp activity 
15. Concomitant administration of any prescription [OR] or over the counter medications known to be highly dependent on P450 [OR] or P-gp for clearance for which elevated plasma concentrations are known to be associated with serious toxicity 
16. Concomitant administration of any food product known to alter P450 enzyme [OR] or P-gp activity such as grapefruit juice, [OR] Seville oranges 
17. Concomitant administration of any drug that could affect bleeding (e.g., aspirin, [OR] clopidogrel, [OR] ticlopidine, [OR] warfarin, [OR] heparin, [OR] low-molecular weight heparin) 
18. Concomitant administration of oral contraceptives (may be included with 7-day washout period) 
19. Concomitant administration of any herbal medications 
20. Inadequate venous access 
21. Renal [OR] or hepatic insufficiency 
22. Clinically unacceptable result at the screening physical examination 
23. Use of investigational medications within 30 days before study entry 
24. HIV-positive 
25. Body Mass Index (BMI) > 30 kg/m²
