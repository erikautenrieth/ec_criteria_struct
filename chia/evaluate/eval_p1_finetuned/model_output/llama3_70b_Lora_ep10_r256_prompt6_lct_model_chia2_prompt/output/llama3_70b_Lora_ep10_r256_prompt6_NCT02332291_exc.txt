Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Current diagnosis of major depressive disorder
  -  Daily access to smartphone or tablet with internet connection for the study duration
  -  Willing and able to complete MRI, mood, and neuropsychological testing
  -  IQ > 70
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Current or past diagnoses of other Axis I psychiatric disorders, except for generalized anxiety disorder (GAD) symptoms occurring during a depressive episode
  -  History of alcohol [OR] or drug dependence [OR] or abuse in the last three years
  -  History of developmental disorder [OR] or IQ score < 70
  -  Presence of acute suicidality
  -  Acute grief (< 1 month)
  -  Current [OR] or past psychosis
  -  Primary neurological disorder, including but not limited to dementia, [OR] stroke, [OR] brain tumors, [OR] epilepsy, [OR] Parkinson's disease, [OR] or demyelinating diseases
  -  MRI contraindications
  -  Any physical [OR] or intellectual disability adversely affecting ability to complete assessments
  -  Electroconvulsive therapy in last 6 months
  -  Use of antidepressant medications [OR] or other psychotropic medications in the last 4 weeks ([OR] or the last 6 weeks for fluoxetine). Occasional use of benzodiazepines [OR] or [NOT] non-benzodiazepine sedatives (such as zolpidem, [OR] eszopiclone, [OR] or zaleplon) during this period is allowable.
  -  A failed therapeutic trial of escitalopram in the current depressive episode (defined as at least 6 weeks of treatment at a daily dose of 10mg or higher)
  -  Known allergy [OR] or hypersensitivity to escitalopram [OR] or bupropion
  -  Current [OR] or planned psychotherapy
