Inclusion Criteria:
The treating physician has chosen Ventavis as a suitable long-term treatment for the patient 
Patient with primary pulmonary hypertension (i.e. Idiopathic Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension [OR] or Familial Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension) [AND] and classified as NYHA functional class III (NYHA = New York Heart Association) 
 [NOT] No prior treatment with Ventavis [OR] or other active treatments for primary pulmonary hypertension within 6 weeks of date of study inclusion (unless otherwise advised by Bayer Schering Pharma)
