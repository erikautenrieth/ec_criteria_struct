Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Expected performance of PCI < 60 minutes from diagnosis (qualifying ECG) [OR] or [NOT] inability to arrive at the catheterisation laboratory within 3 hours
Previous CABG
Left bundle branch block [OR] or ventricular pacing
Patients with cardiogenic shock - Killip Class 4
Patients with a body weight < 55 kg (known or estimated)
Uncontrolled hypertension, defined as sustained blood pressure = 180/110 mm Hg (systolic BP = 180 mm Hg [OR] and/or diastolic BP = 110 mm Hg) prior to randomisation
Known prior stroke [OR] or TIA
Recent administration of any i.v. [OR] or s.c. anticoagulation within 12 hours, including unfractionated heparin, [OR] enoxaparin, [OR] and/or bivalirudin [OR] or current use of oral anticoagulation (i.e. warfarin [OR] or a NOACs)
Active bleeding [OR] or known bleeding disorder/ [OR] diathesis
Known history of central nervous system damage (i.e. neoplasm, [OR] aneurysm, [OR] intracranial [OR] or spinal surgery) [OR] or recent trauma to the head [OR] or cranium (i.e. < 3 months)
Major surgery, [OR] biopsy of a parenchymal organ, [OR] or significant trauma within the past 2 months (this includes any trauma associated with the current myocardial infarction)
Clinical diagnosis associated with increased risk of bleeding including known active peptic ulceration [OR] and/or neoplasm [AND] with increased bleeding risk
Prolonged cardiopulmonary resuscitation (> 2 minutes) within the past 2 weeks
Known acute pericarditis [OR] and/or subacute bacterial endocarditis
Known acute pancreatitis [OR] or known severe hepatic dysfunction, including hepatic failure, [OR] cirrhosis, [OR] portal hypertension [OR] (oesophageal varices) [OR] and active hepatitis
Dementia
 Known severe renal insufficiency
Previous enrolment in this study [OR] or treatment with an investigational drug [OR] or device under another study protocol in the past 7 days
Known allergic reactions to tenecteplase, [OR] clopidogrel, [OR] enoxaparin [OR] and aspirin
Inability to follow the protocol and comply with follow-up requirements or any other reason that the investigator feels would place the patient at increased risk if the investigational therapy is initiated.
