Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age 18-29
 - Current diagnosis of MDD/persistent depressive disorder or HDRS greater than or equal to 12.  - Daily access to smartphone or tablet with internet connection for the study duration
 - Willing and able to complete MRI, mood, and neuropsychological testing.  - IQ > 85
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Lacks English-speaking ability as determined by self-report and clinical evaluation
 2. Evidence of schizophrenia, schizoaffective disorder, psychosis, bipolar I and II disorder
 3. Active suicidal ideation, intent, or plan, or past attempt within 1 year
 4. Severe depression (HDRS > 30)
 5. Neurological disorder (epilepsy, multiple sclerosis, traumatic brain injury, clinical stroke)
 6. Use of medications known to have negative impact on cognition (benzodiazepines and lorazepam equivalents > 1mg daily, narcotics, anticholinergics)
 7. History of alcohol or drug abuse or dependence within past year
 8. Acute, severe, unstable medical illness. For cancer, acutely ill patients (including those with metastases) are excluded, but past history of successfully treated cancer does not result in exclusion
 9. Regular online brain training or regular player of non-fluency verbal games, defined as doing these procedures at a frequency of twice weekly or greater during the year prior to screening
 10. History of psychiatric hospitalization within past 5 years
 11. A positive urine toxicology test for illegal substances
 12. A positive pregnancy test
 13. History of claustrophobia
 14. Inability to complete the MRI, mood, or neuropsychological testing
 15. A history of a seizure not induced by drug withdrawal, or a significant brain injury or skull defect
 16. Use of medications that are known to have a negative impact on fMRI
 17. A history of motion sickness
 18. A history of anxiolytic and/or antidepressant medications
 19. A history of obsessive-compulsive disorder
 20. A history of post-traumatic stress disorder
 21. A history of attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder
 22. A history of autism spectrum disorder
 23. A history of intellectual disability
 24. A history of a personality disorder as a current focus of treatment
 25. ECT within 3 months prior to screening
 26. Inappropriate for ECT, or poor response to ECT within the past 5 years
 27. Incompetent to provide consent
