Inclusion Criteria:
Unstable angina;
Myocardial infarction [OR] and heart surgery up to three months before the survey;
Chronic respiratory diseases;
Hemodynamic instability;
Trauma recent face, nausea [OR] and vomiting.
Exclusion Criteria:
Orthopedic [OR] and neurological diseases that may [NOT] preclude the achievement of the cardiopulmonary test [AND] and Cardiac Rehabilitation exercises;
Psychological [OR] and / or cognitive impairments that restrict them to respond to questionnaires;
