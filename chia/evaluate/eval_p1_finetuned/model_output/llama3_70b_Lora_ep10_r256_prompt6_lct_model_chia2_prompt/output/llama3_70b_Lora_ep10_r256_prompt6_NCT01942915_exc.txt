Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients with C class by child-pugh score 
 2. Patients in the acute phase of severe hepatitis 
 3. Patients have been diagnosed with cancer of the liver 
 4. Patients with severe cardiopulmonary cerebral disease, [OR] and in the failure state 
 5. Patients in Highly allergic constitution 
 6. Patients with moderately severe mental disease       
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients with severe cardiopulmonary cerebral disease, [OR] and in the failure state 
 2. Patients in Highly allergic constitution 
 3. Patients with moderately severe mental disease       
 4. Patients with severe hepatitis
 5. Patients with liver cancer
 6. Patients with C class by child-pugh score
