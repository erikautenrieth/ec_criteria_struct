Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Early syphilis cases (primary, secondary and early latent syphilis) diagnosed in the routine clinical practice of the Institute of Dermatology and Venereology, Zhongshan Hospital, Fudan University, Shanghai, China;
  -  Cases determined to be serofast (i.e. maintaining a certain level of seroreactivity) at 6 months after initial treatment;
  -  Cases with a complete clinical and serological follow-up at 6 months after initial treatment.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Cases with a history of prior syphilis infection;
  -  Cases with a history of taking drugs that are known to be effective against T. pallidum in the past 3 months;
  -  Cases with a history of blood transfusion in the past month;
  -  Pregnant women;
  -  HIV positive cases.
