Inclusion Criteria:
- Generally healthy, postmenopausal woman [AND] who seeks treatment for hot flushes.
Meets 1 of the following: At least 12 months of spontaneous amenorrhea; At least 6 months of spontaneous amenorrhea [AND] with serum follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) levels > 40 mIU/mL; At least 6 weeks postsurgical bilateral oophorectomy (with or without hysterectomy). Hysterectomized [AND] without bilateral oophorectomy [AND] and with serum FSH levels >40 mIU/mL.
