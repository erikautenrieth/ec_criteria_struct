Inclusion Criteria:
 Administration of the licensed MF59-containing vaccines, e.g. Fluad™ [OR] or Addigrip™ [OR] or virosome-based influenza vaccines such as Inflexal V™, InfectoVac Flu™ [OR] or Invivac™ during the 2006-2007 influenza season. 
Exclusion Criteria:
 Administration of licensed vaccines within 2 weeks (for inactivated vaccines) [OR] or 4 weeks (for live vaccines) prior to enrolment in this study. 
Planned administration of a vaccine [NOT] not foreseen by the study protocol up to 30 days after the second vaccination with H5N1 vaccine. 
Chronic administration (defined as more than 14 days) of immunosuppressants [OR] or other immune-modifying drugs within six months prior to the first administration of the study vaccine. 
Any confirmed [OR] or suspected immunosuppressive [OR] or immunodeficient condition, based on medical history and physical examination (no laboratory testing required). 
History of chronic alcohol consumption [OR] and/or drug abuse. 
History of hypersensitivity to vaccines. 
History of allergic disease [OR] or reactions likely to be exacerbated by any component of the vaccine (including egg [OR] and thiomersal allergy). 
Acute clinically significant pulmonary, [OR] cardiovascular, [OR] hepatic [OR] or renal functional abnormality, as determined by physical examination [OR] or laboratory screening tests. 
Acute disease at the time of enrolment. 
Serious chronic disease including any medically significant chronic pulmonary, [OR] cardiovascular, [OR] renal, [OR] neurological, [OR] psychiatric [OR] or metabolic disorder, as determined by medical history and physical examination. 
Administration of immunoglobulins [OR] and/or any blood products within the three months preceding the first vaccination [OR] or during the study. 
Use of any investigational [OR] or non-registered product (drug [OR] or vaccine) other than the study vaccine(s) within 30 days prior to the first vaccination, [OR] or planned use during the study period. 
Any condition which, in the opinion of the investigator, [NOT] prevents the subject from participation in the study.
