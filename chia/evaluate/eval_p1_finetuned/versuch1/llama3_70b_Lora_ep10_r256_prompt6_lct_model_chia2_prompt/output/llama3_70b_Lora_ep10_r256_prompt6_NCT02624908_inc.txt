Inclusion Criteria:
use of basal-bolus insulin
onset of diabetes after age 30
BMI less than 35
eGFR at least 60 ml/mn
Hb A1c 7.0-10.0%
willingness to perform home glucose monitoring
willingness to transmit glucose and medication information weekly
Exclusion Criteria:
history of cardiovascular, gastrointestinal, renal [OR] and endocrinological diseases
 treatment with drugs that affect blood glucose
history of cognitive [OR] and psychiatric [OR] diseases
alcohol [OR] and drug abuse
pregnancy [OR] and breastfeeding
concomitant diseases that [NOT] prevent participation in the study
