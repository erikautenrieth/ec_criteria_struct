Inclusion Criteria:
  -  1. Prior treatment with gemcitabine, [OR] carboplatin (except in the adjuvant setting), [OR] or Iniparib. 
  -  2. Past [OR] or current history of neoplasm other than the entry diagnosis, [AND] with the exception of treated [NOT] non-melanoma skin cancer [OR] or carcinoma in-situ of any primary site, [OR] or invasive cancers treated definitively, [AND] with treatment ending >5 years previously [AND] and [NOT] no evidence of recurrences. 
  -  3. A history of cardiac disease, as defined by: 
Malignant hypertension 
Unstable angina 
 Congestive heart failure 
 Myocardial infarction within the previous 6 months 
Symptomatic, unstable [OR] or uncontrolled, cardiac arrhythmias. Patients [AND] who have stable, rate-controlled atrial fibrillation are eligible for study enrollment. 
  -  4. Active brain metastases. Patients [AND] with treated brain metastases are eligible, if (1) radiation therapy was completed at least 2 weeks prior to study entry; (2) follow-up scan shows [NOT] no disease progression; and (3) patient does [NOT] not require steroids. 
  -  5. Women [AND] who are pregnant [OR] or lactating. 
  -  6. Any serious, active infection (> Grade 2) at the time of treatment. 
  -  7. A serious underlying medical condition that would impair the ability of the patient to receive protocol treatment. 
  -  8. A major surgical procedure, [OR] or significant traumatic injury ≤28 days of beginning treatment, [OR] or anticipation of the need for major surgery during the course of the study. 
  -  9. Uncontrolled [OR] or intercurrent illness including, that in the opinion of the investigator may increase the risks associated with study participation or administration of the investigational products, [OR] or that may interfere with the interpretation of the results. 
  -  10. History of any medical [OR] or psychiatric condition [OR] or laboratory abnormality that, in the opinion of the investigator, may increase the risks associated with the study participation or administration of the investigational products, [OR] or that may interfere with the interpretation of the results. 
  -  11. Known [OR] or suspected allergy/ [OR] hypersensitivity to any agent given in the course of this trial. 
The above information is not intended to contain all considerations relevant to a patient's potential participation in a clinical trial.
