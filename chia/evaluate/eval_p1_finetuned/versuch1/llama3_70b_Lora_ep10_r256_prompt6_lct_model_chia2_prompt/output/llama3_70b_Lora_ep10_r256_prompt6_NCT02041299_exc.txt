Inclusion Criteria:
Thalassemia syndromes;
Myelodysplastic syndrome (MDS) [OR] or myelofibrosis;
Diamond Blackfan anemia;
Primary bone marrow failure;
Baseline LIC >30 mg/g dw (measured by MRI);
 [NOT] Unable [OR] or [NOT] unwilling to undergo a 7 day washout period if currently being treated with deferiprone [OR] or deferoxamine [OR] or deferasirox;
Previous discontinuation of treatment with deferiprone [OR] or deferoxamine due to adverse events;
History [OR] or presence of hypersensitivity [OR] or idiosyncratic reaction to deferiprone [OR] or deferoxamine;
Treated with hydroxyurea within 30 days;
History of malignancy;
Evidence of abnormal liver function (serum ALT level(s) > 5 times upper limit of normal at screening [OR] or creatinine levels >2 times upper limit of normal at screening);
 A serious, unstable illness, as judged by the Investigator, during the past 3 months before screening/baseline visit including but not limited to: hepatic, [OR] renal, [OR] gastro-enterologic, [OR] respiratory, [OR] cardiovascular, [OR] endocrinologic, [OR] neurologic [OR] or immunologic disease;
Clinically significant abnormal 12-lead ECG findings;
 Cardiac MRI T2* <10ms;
 Myocardial infarction, [OR] cardiac arrest [OR] or cardiac failure within 1 year before screening/baseline visit;
 [NOT] Unable to undergo MRI
Presence of metallic objects such as artificial joints, [OR] inner ear (cochlear) implants, [OR] brain aneurysm clips, [OR] pacemakers, [OR] and metallic foreign bodies in the eye [OR] or other body areas that would [NOT] prevent use of MRI imaging
