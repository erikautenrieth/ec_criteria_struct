Inclusion Criteria:
Informed consent obtained prior to any screening procedure 
Caucasian male [OR] or female patient 
At least 18 years of age 
Weight at least 45 kg 
Patient with moderate [OR] or severe chronic atopic dermatitis 
Good general health ascertained by medical history, physical examination [AND] and laboratory determinations, showing [NOT] no signs of clinically significant findings, except chronic atopic dermatitis 
Negative pregnancy test (premenopausal female patient) at screening [AND] and use of adequate contraceptive measures (both male [OR] and female patients) throughout the study [AND] and 30 days after the last cis-UCA dose
