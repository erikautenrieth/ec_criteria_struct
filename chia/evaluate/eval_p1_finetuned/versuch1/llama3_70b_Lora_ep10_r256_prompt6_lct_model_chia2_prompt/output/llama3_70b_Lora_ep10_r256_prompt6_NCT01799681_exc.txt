Inclusion Criteria:
  -  diagnosed with PD;
  -  aged 40-80 years;
  -  scored 24 or above on the Folstein Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE);
  -  able to give written, informed consent;
  -  able to walk with full capability on lower extremity passive range of motion;
  -  able to walk for at least 50 feet with full capability on lower extremity passive range of motion;
  -  able to get up and down from the floor with no external support (arm rests OK) in one attempt;
  -  has a score of 27 or above on the Berg Balance Scale;
  -  has a score of 0.5 or above on the Schwab and England Activities of Daily Living Scale;
  -  has a score of 20 or above on the Folstein Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE);
  -  able to walk with full capability on lower extremity passive range of motion;
  -  able to walk for at least 50 feet with full capability on lower extremity passive range of motion;
  -  able to get up and down from the floor with no external support (arm rests OK) in one attempt;
  -  has a score of 27 or above on the Berg Balance Scale;
  -  has a score of 0.5 or above on the Schwab and England Activities of Daily Living Scale;
  -  has a score of 20 or above on the Folstein Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE);
Exclusion Criteria:
 - any neurological conditions other than PD; 
 - significant musculoskeletal [OR] or cardiopulmonary diseases; 
 - other disorders that may affect balance [OR] or locomotion; 
 - taken any structured behavioral [OR] or exercise programs in the past 3 months 
 - or they are receiving regular physical rehabilitation at present; 
 - unstable condition on anti-parkinsonian medications; 
 - surgical interventions for PD; 
 - communication [OR] or cognitive deficits [AND] with mini-mental state examination, (MMSE) <24/30 (Folstein et al., 1975); 
 - a history of more than two falls in the previous 12 months.
