Inclusion Criteria:
1. The subject is a pregnant [OR] or lactating female. 
2. The subject has pre-existing sustained supine hypertension greater than 180mmHg systolic [OR] and 110mmHg diastolic BP [OR] or had these measurements at the Screening Visit. Sustained is defined as persistently greater at 2 separate measurements at least 5 minutes apart [AND] with the subject supine [AND] and at rest for the 5 minutes. 
3. Subjects taking concomitant medications of interest are [NOT] excluded unless those medications are reviewed and discussed with the Medical Monitor [OR] or Study Physician and documented prior to enrolling the subject. If agreement is reached between the Investigator and Sponsor for the subject to continue in the study, all allowed medications should be maintained at a constant dose throughout the study. 
4. The Principal Investigator deems any clinical laboratory test (at the Screening Visit) abnormality to be clinically significant 
5. The subject has participated in other studies of investigational drugs [OR] or devices within 30 days prior to enrollment in this study (other than Study SPD426-406). 
6. Current [OR] or relevant history of physical [OR] or psychiatric illness, any medical disorder that may require treatment [OR] or make the subject unlikely to fully comply with the requirements of the study [OR] or complete the study, [OR] or any condition that presents undue risk from the investigational product [OR] or study procedures. 
7. The subject has a concurrent chronic [OR] or acute illness, [OR] disability, [OR] or other condition (including significant unexpected laboratory [OR] or electrocardiogram [ECG] findings) that might confound the results of the tests [OR] and/or measurements administered in this study, [OR] or that might have increased the risk to the subject. 
8. Known [OR] or suspected intolerance [OR] or hypersensitivity to the investigational product(s), closely-related compounds, [OR] or any of the stated ingredients. 
9. Prior enrollment failure [OR] or randomization in this study. 
10. History of alcohol abuse [OR] or other substance abuse within the last year.
