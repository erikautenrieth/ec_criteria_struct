Acute Coronary Syndrome group: 40 patients with type 1 myocardial infarction within 21 days prior to the imaging visit [AND] and invasive coronary angiography [AND] with angiographic evidence of at least a 50% stenosis in one or more coronary arteries. Only patients undergoing PCI will be included in the study.
Stable Ischemic Heart Disease group: 40 patients [AND] who have undergone invasive coronary angiography within 21 days prior to the imaging visit, [AND] with history of typical angina prior to the angiogram, [NOT] but [NOT] no prior myocardial infarction [OR] or coronary revascularization.
