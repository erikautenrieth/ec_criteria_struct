Inclusion Criteria:
18 years of age or older 
Histologically confirmed diagnosis of melanoma, [OR] breast cancer [OR] or gynecologic cancer at MSKCC 
Have one of the following disease histories: 
Newly-diagnosed [OR] or recurrent (local, [OR] regional, [OR] metastatic) malignant melanoma [OR] or breast cancer patients in whom SLN mapping is indicated 
Residual clinically [OR] or radiographically evident tumor, including primary cutaneous [OR] and mucosal melanomas 
Prior radiation therapy, [OR] chemotherapy, [OR] or surgery in patients requiring flap reconstruction in the head [OR] and neck region. 
Newly diagnosed patients with previous excisional biopsy. [OR] OR 
Newly-diagnosed gynecologic cancer patients in whom SLN mapping [OR] and surgical excision is indicated [OR] OR 
Normal baseline cardiac function based upon pre-operative evaluation 
At the discretion of the operating surgeon, ANC>1000/mcl [AND] and platelets>100,000/mcl. 
At the discretion of the operating surgeon, Bilirubin level of < 2.0 mg/dl [AND] in the [NOT] absence of a history of Gilbert's disease [OR] (or pattern consistent with Gilbert's). 
For melanoma patients, If patients have a history of malignancy other than melanoma, [OR] and other skin cancers in the past five years, their inclusion is up to the discretion of the physician. 
All patients of childbearing [OR] and child-creating age must be using an acceptable form of birth control 
Women [AND] who are pre-menopausal must have a negative serum pregnancy test
