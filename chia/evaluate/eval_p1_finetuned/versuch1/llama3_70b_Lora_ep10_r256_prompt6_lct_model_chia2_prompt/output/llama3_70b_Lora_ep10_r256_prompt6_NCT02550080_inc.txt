Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosed with cutaneous vasculitis, [OR] urticaria, [OR] psoriasis, [OR] acne, [OR] bullous skin diseases, [OR] sterile pustulosis, [OR] leprosy, [OR] pneumocystis pneumonia [OR] and any other patients [AND] who need dapsone administration.
Subjects are dapsone- [NOT] naive.
All subjects must have a clinical need for treatment [AND] with dapsone that precedes the decision to participate in the study.
All subjects are willing to complete the 6-weeks period clinical trial.
All subjects are written informed consent.
