Inclusion Criteria:
Severe Pre-Eclampsia defined by
  -  Symptoms of central nervous system dysfunction (New onset cerebral [OR] or visual disturbance, such as: Photopsia, [OR] scotomata, [OR] cortical blindness, [OR] retinal vasospasm)
  -  Severe headache
  -  Fatigue
  -  Nausea [OR] and vomiting
  -  Abdominal pain
  -  Severe blood pressure elevation Systolic blood pressure ≥160 mmHg [OR] or diastolic blood pressure ≥110 mmHg on two occasions at least four hours apart while the patient is on bedrest (unless the patient is on antihypertensive therapy)
  -  Thrombocytopenia (<100,000 platelets/microL)
  -  Renal failure (creatinine >1.1 mg/dL [OR] or doubling of serum creatinine concentration in the absence of other renal disease)
  -  Pulmonary edema
     1. Patients with more than one of the above criteria
     2. Patients with any contraindication to Magnesium Sulfate including severe renal impairment or myasthenia gravis.
Exclusion Criteria:
- Patients with more than one contraindication to Magnesium Sulfate
