Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Age 18 years or older 
 2. Diagnosis of venous leg ulcer(s), as clinically determined by the investigator by a positive venous reflux test (venous refilling <20 seconds) using Doppler ultrasound [AND] for at least 4 weeks prior to screening day, which have [NOT] not adequately responded to conventional ulcer therapy. 
 3. Designated venous leg ulcer meets the following criteria at both the screening [AND] and baseline visits. If the patient has multiple ulcers, at least one ulcer must meet the following criteria at both the screening [AND] and baseline visits: 
 1. Present for at least 4 weeks 
 2. CEAP Classification Stage 6 
 3. Surface ulcer [AND] with an area > 15cm2 post debridement 
 4. Viable, granulating wound (investigator discretion) 
 4. Ulcers that extend through the epidermis [AND] but [NOT] not through the muscle, [OR] tendon, [OR] or bone (Stage II [OR] or III ulcers as defined by the IAET). 
 5. Female patients of childbearing potential must have a negative pregnancy test at screening [AND] and must agree to use hormonal contraceptive, [OR] intrauterine device, [OR] diaphragm [AND] with spermicide, [OR] condom [AND] with spermicide, [OR] or abstinence throughout until 2 weeks after the last administration of study drug 
 6. Signed informed consent
