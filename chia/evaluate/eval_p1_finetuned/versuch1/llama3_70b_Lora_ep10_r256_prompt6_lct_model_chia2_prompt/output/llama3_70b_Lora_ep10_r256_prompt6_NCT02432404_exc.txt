Inclusion Criteria:
Current pregnancy
Desire/intent to become pregnant over the course of the study
Women [AND] who are less than 6 weeks postpartum
Contraindications to hormonal contraceptive use per package insert, including history of deep vein thrombosis, [OR] smoking [AND] in women older than 35 years
Current IUD
Unable to comprehend consent material because of language barrier [OR] or psychological difficulty
