Inclusion Criteria:
Established pre-existing diabetes (including unrecognised diabetes defined as a fasting plasma glucose = 7.0mmol/L [OR] and/ or HbA1c = 48mmol/mol); Contraindications to metformin therapy (creatinine = 130µmol/L/ alanine transaminase = 2.0 x upper limit normal/ previous intolerance to metformin)
Exclusion Criteria:
Planned continued antenatal care/ [OR] delivery at centre [NOT] not included in trial
Planned fast for cultural/ [OR] religious reasons e.g. Ramadan
