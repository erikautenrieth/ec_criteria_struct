Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients with AML arising from myelodysplastic syndromes (including CMML) or myeloproliferative neoplasms (secondary AML, ts-AML)
  -  Patients with AML with specific genetic abnormalities (e.g. AML with t(9;22) (q34;q11) [BCR-ABL], AML with t(4;11) (q21;q23) [AF4/MLL], AML with t(1;9) (q34;q34) [BAALC], AML with inv(16) (p13;q22) [CBFB-MYH11], AML with t(16;16) (p13;q22) [FUS-ERG])
  -  Patients with high-risk AML (presence of higher-risk features: MRD ≥ 10% at diagnosis, or age < 6 month and white blood cell (WBC) count ≥ 300×109/L with translocations t(9;22) (q34;q11) [BCR-ABL], t(4;11) (q21;q23) [AF4/MLL], t(1;9) (q34;q34) [BAALC], inv(16) (p13;q22) [CBFB-MYH11] or t(16;16) (p13;q22) [FUS-ERG])
  -  Salvage chemotherapy regimen for high-risk AML
  -  Patients who are able to receive the salvage chemotherapy regimen
  -  Patients who are able to undergo HSCT within 100 days of enrollment
  -  Donor willing and able to undergo HSCT
  -  No contraindication to HSCT
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients who are not able to receive the salvage chemotherapy regimen
  -  Patients who are not able to undergo HSCT
  -  No donor available
  -  Contraindication to HSCT
  -  Active CNS Leukemia.
  -  Known HIV infection or known hepatitis B or hepatitis C infection (with a detectable viral load).
  -  Participant has received strong and/or moderate CYP3A inducers within 7 days prior to the initiation of study treatment.
  -  Have received prior radiation to maximally tolerated levels to any critical normal organ.
  -  Clinically significant cardiac disease.
  -  Active, uncontrolled serious infection.
  -  Have other non-myeloid malignancy within 2 years of entry (with exceptions).
  -  Psychiatric disorder that would preclude study participation
  -  Previous solid organ transplant (prior treatment with SCT is allowed but not if patient has GVHD or is still receiving immunosuppression/GVHD therapy).
