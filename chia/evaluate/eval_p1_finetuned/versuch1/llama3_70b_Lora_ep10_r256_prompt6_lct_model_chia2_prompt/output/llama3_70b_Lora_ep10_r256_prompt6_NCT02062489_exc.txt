Inclusion Criteria:
  -  The patients are diagnosed as early breast cancer, and they are indicated for adjuvant chemotherapy.
  -  The patients are aged from 18 to 65 years old.
  -  The patients are postoperative, and they have received the first course of chemotherapy.
  -  The patients are suitable for general surgery and one-lung ventilation.
  -  The patients have the American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) physical status I or II.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  The patients have other cancers at the same time [OR] or have the history of other cancers except controlled skin basal cell carcinoma [OR] or skin squamous cell carcinoma [OR] or carcinoma in situ of cervix uterus;
  -  The patients have active infections that were [NOT] not suitable for chemotherapy;
  -  The patients have severe [NOT] non-cancerous diseases.
  -  The patients have history of neoadjuvant hormone therapy.
  -  The patients have bilateral breast cancers [OR] or DCIS [OR] or metastatic breast cancers.
  -  The patients are undergoing current administration of anti-cancer therapies, [OR] or are attending other clinical trials.
  -  The patients are pregnant [OR] or lactational, [OR] or they refuse to practice contraception during the whole trial.
  -  The patients are in some special conditions that they can't understand the written informed consent, such as they are demented [OR] or hawkish.
  -  The patients have allergic history [OR] or contraindication of tamoxifen.
