Inclusion Criteria:
Vaccination against pneumococcal infection in anamnesis;
Application of preparations of immune globulin [OR] or blood transfusion within last three months prior to clinical studies;
Prolonged use (more than 14 days) immunosuppressants [OR] or other immunosuppressive drugs within 6 months prior to the start of the study;
Any confirmed [OR] or suspected immunosuppressive [OR] or immunodeficient condition, including HIV infection;
A history [OR] or currently hematologic [OR] and other cancers;
A positive reaction for HIV infection, [OR] viral hepatitis B [OR] and hepatitis C;
The presence of respiratory, [OR] cardio-vascular insufficiency, impaired liver [OR] and kidney function, established during a physical examination at visit number 1;
Pronounced congenital defects [OR] or serious chronic diseases in the acute stage, including any clinically important exacerbation of chronic diseases of the liver, [OR] kidney, [OR] cardiovascular, [OR] nervous system, [OR] mental diseases [OR] or metabolic disorders, confirmed by the history [OR] or objective examination (pulmonary: cystic fibrosis, [OR] lung abscess, [OR] empyema, active tuberculosis; extra-pulmonary: congestive heart failure, [OR] malabsorption, chronic renal [OR] and hepatic failure, [OR] cirrhosis, [OR] malignancy, [OR] immunodeficiency, [OR] cirrhosis of the liver);
Severe allergic reactions in anamnesis [OR] of autoimmune disease;
The presence of acute infectious [OR] and/or communicable illnesses within 1 month prior to study;
History of chronic alcohol abuse [OR] and/or drug use;
Exacerbation of chronic diseases;
Breastfeeding;
Pregnancy;
Participation in any other clinical study within the last 3 months.
