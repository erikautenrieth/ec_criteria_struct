Inclusion Criteria:
Age = 18 years [AND] and NYHA (New York Heart Association) functional class II, III and IV

Exclusion Criteria:
Anaphylactic reaction to a previous dose of TIV(trivalent influenza vaccine)
Known IgE( Immunoglobulin E)-mediated hypersensitivity to eggs manifested as hives, [OR] swelling of the mouth [OR] and throat, [OR] difficulty in breathing, [OR] hypotension, [OR] or shock
Guillain-Barré syndrome within eight weeks of a previous influenza vaccine
 Anaphylactic reaction to neomycin
Patients who have had influenza vaccine in two of the three previous years
