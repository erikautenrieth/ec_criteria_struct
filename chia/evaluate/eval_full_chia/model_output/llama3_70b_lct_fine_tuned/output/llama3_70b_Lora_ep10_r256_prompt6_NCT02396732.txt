Inclusion Criteria:
Age 18 years or older
Blunt [OR] or penetrating trauma
Requires VTE thromboprophylaxis
High-risk for VTE

Exclusion Criteria:
Presence of VTE upon admission
Pregnant [OR] or nursing
Inability to give informed consent by patient or healthcare proxy
Contraindication to enoxaparin
Contraindication to aspirin
Epidural [OR] or subdural hematoma
Presence, [OR] or removal within the last 12 hours, of an epidural [OR] or spinal catheter, [OR] or recent (within the last 12 hours) epidural [OR] or spinal anesthesia/ [OR] procedures
