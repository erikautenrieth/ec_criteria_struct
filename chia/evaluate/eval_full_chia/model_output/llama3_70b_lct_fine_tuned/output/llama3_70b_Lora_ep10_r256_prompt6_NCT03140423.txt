Inclusion Criteria:
Inclusion criteria includes all U.S. HCA hospitals with an adult ICU;
Note: Unit of randomization is the hospital, but the participants are hospital adult ICUs
All patients within adult ICUs are included, including rare patients <18 years [OR] and >=12 years.

Exclusion Criteria:
Exclusion criteria includes ICUs [AND] with an average length of stay of less than 2 days;
HCA hospitals that are [NOT] not able to transfer [OR] or merge data into the centralized data warehouse for the baseline [AND] and intervention periods of the study are also [NOT] excluded.
