Inclusion Criteria:
An average defecation frequency (DF) of <3 per week based on a 3-week defecation diary (patient-reported)
Meet at least one other criterion of the Rome-IV criteria for idiopathic constipation based on the 3-week defecation diary (1)
Refractory to conservative treatment
Age: 14-80 years
Straining during =25% of defecations
Lumpy [OR] or hard stools in =25% of defecations
 Sensation of [NOT] incomplete evacuation for =25% of defecations
Sensation of anorectal obstruction/ [OR] blockage for =25% of defecations
Manual manoeuvres to facilitate =25% of defecations

Exclusion Criteria:
Obstructed outlet syndrome (objectified by defeacography)
IBS (Rome-IV criteria for irritable bowel syndrome)
Congenital [OR] or organic bowel pathology
Rectal prolapse
Anatomical limitations [NOT] preventing placement of an electrode
Skin [OR] and perineal disease with risk of infection
Previous large bowel/ [OR] rectal surgery
Stoma
Coexisting neurological disease
Significant psychological co-morbidity as assessed subjectively by the investigator
Being [OR] or attempting to become pregnant during study follow-up
