Inclusion Criteria:
Patients receiving once daily dosing of methylprednisolone [OR] or prednisone in a dose of 10 mg/day or greater
Hyperglycemic (Glucose level > 126 mg/dL)
 Diabetic [OR] and [NOT] nondiabetic patients
Expected duration of hospital stay [AND] and time on steroids >= 3 days
Patient of appropriate caregiver able to give Informed Consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with 2 or more doses of methylprednisolone/ [OR] prednisone per day
Steroids other than methylprednisolone [OR] or prednisone
Pregnancy
estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) < 45 ml/min/1.73 m2
