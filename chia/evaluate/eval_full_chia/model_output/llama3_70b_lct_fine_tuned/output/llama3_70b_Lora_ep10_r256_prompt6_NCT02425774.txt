Inclusion Criteria:
patients undergoing partial [OR] or full resection of the pancreas due to a benign [OR] or malignant tumor

Exclusion Criteria:
adjuvant radiotherapy
evident intra-abdominal inflammation (diagnosed by imaging [OR] and/or laboratory results, including an abscess [OR] or cholecystitis)
chronic pancreatitis
pancreatic polypeptide producing endocrine tumor
American Society of Anesthesiologists physical-health status classification (ASA-PS)>3
Poorly regulated diabetes (>200 mg/dl (=11 mmol/l))
