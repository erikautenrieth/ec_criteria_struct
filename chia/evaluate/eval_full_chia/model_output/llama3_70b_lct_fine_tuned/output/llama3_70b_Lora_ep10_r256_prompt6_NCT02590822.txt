Inclusion Criteria:
Capacity to provide informed consent before any trial-related activities
Established T2DM (=3months)
HbA1c = 9% if on triple therapy [OR] or = 10% on diet [OR] & exercise [OR] or monotherapy [OR] or dual therapy
Current glucose lowering therapy either mono, [OR] dual [OR] or triple of any combination of metformin, [OR] sulphonylurea, [OR] DPP-IV inhibitor, [OR] GLP-1 therapy [OR] or an SGLT2 [OR] +/- diet [OR] and exercise
Poorly managed diet controlled diabetes (with HbA1c > 6.5%, [NOT] not currently taking any glucose lowering therapy, meeting BMI inclusion range)
Body mass index > 30Kg/m2 [OR] or > 27.5 Kg/m2 (South Asian),
Diagnosis of T2DM before the age of 60 years of age
Age =18 [AND] and = 65 years

Exclusion Criteria:
• Diabetes duration >12 years
Currently taking more than three glucose lowering therapies
 Weight-loss of >5kg in the preceding 6 months
 Stage 4 [OR] or 5 chronic kidney disease (eGFR< 30ml/min/1.73 m2),
 Current therapy with Insulin, [OR] thiazolidinediones, [OR] steroids [OR] or atypical antipsychotic medication
 [NOT] Untreated thyroid disease
 Known macrovascular disease including coronary artery disease, [OR] stroke/ [OR] TIA [OR] or peripheral vascular disease
 Presence of arrhythmia (including atrial fibrillation, [OR] atrial flutter, [OR] or 2nd or 3rd degree atrioventricular block)
 Known heart failure
 Other clinically relevant heart disease
 [NOT] Inability to exercise [OR] or undertake a MRP
 Absolute contraindication to CMR
Cardiovascular symptoms (angina, [OR] limiting dyspnoea during normal physical activity)
 Inflammatory condition e.g. Connective tissue disorder, [OR] Rheumatoid arthritis
