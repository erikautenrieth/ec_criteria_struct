Inclusion Criteria:
Moderate [OR] to severe rheumatoid arthritis
Taking methotrexate [NOT] without adequate control of symptoms
Have at least one cardiovascular risk factor (eg, current smoker, [OR] high blood pressure, [OR] high cholesterol levels, [OR] diabetes mellitus, history of heart attack, family history of coronary heart disease, [OR] extra-articular RA disease)

Exclusion Criteria:
Current [OR] or recent infection
Clinically significant laboratory abnormalities
Pregnancy
