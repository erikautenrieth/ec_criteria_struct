Inclusion Criteria:
Phase I: Patients must have histologically confirmed R/R NHL [OR] or HL (defined by WHO criteria). Patients with chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL) [OR] and small lymphocytic lymphoma (SLL) are eligible. In addition, patients with NHL [NOT] other than diffuse large B cell lymphomas (DLBCL) must have received at least 2 prior therapies. Patients with DLBCL [OR] and HL will be eligible if there is [NOT] no available standard therapy. 
Phase II: Patients must have histologically confirmed R/R NHL (as defined by WHO criteria). Patients with NHL [NOT] other than diffuse large B cell lymphomas (DLBCL) must have received at least 2 prior therapies. Patients with DLBCL will be eligible if there is [NOT] no available standard therapy. 
Must have received front line chemotherapy. [NOT] No upper limit for the number of prior therapies 
Evaluable Disease in the Phase I, [AND] and measurable disease in the Phase II 
Age > 18 years 
ECOG performance status < 2 
Patients must have adequate organ [AND] and marrow function 
Adequate Contraception 
Ability to understand [AND] and the willingness to sign a written informed consent document 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Prior Therapy Exposure to chemotherapy [OR] or radiotherapy within 2 weeks prior to entering the study [OR] or those [AND] who have [NOT] not recovered from adverse events due to agents administered more than 2 weeks earlier. Systemic steroids that have [NOT] not been stabilized (≥ 5 days) to the equivalent of ≤10 mg/day prednisone prior to the start of the study drugs. [NOT] No other investigational agents are allowed. 
2. History of allergic reactions to TGR-1202 [OR] or carfilzomib 
3. Uncontrolled inter-current illness 
4. Pregnant women 
5. Nursing women 
6. Current malignancy [OR] or history of a prior malignancy 
7. Patient known to be Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV)-positive 
8. Active Hepatitis A, [OR] Hepatitis B, [OR] or Hepatitis C infection
