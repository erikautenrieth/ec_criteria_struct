Inclusion Criteria:
Informed consent of parent(s) or legal guardian; informed consent or assent of subject as applicable.
Male [OR] or female children between the ages of 10 and 35 years [AND] with congenital heart disease [AND] that has been palliated with a Fontan circulation.
Ability of perform a maximal exercise test as defined by a respiratory exchange ratio (RER) greater than 1.0 at the time of maximal exercise

Exclusion Criteria:
The use of beta blockers within 2 months of randomization
Patients actively listed for transplantation at time of entry into the study [OR] or anticipated to undergo heart transplantation, [OR] interventional catheterization, [OR] or corrective cardiac surgery during the 7 months following entry into the study
Sustained [OR] or symptomatic ventricular dysrhythmias uncontrolled by drug therapy [OR] or the use of an implantable defibrillator, [OR] and/or significant cardiac conduction defects, e.g., 2nd degree [OR] or 3rd degree AV block, [OR] or sick sinus syndrome, unless a functioning pacemaker is in place
Uncorrected obstructive [OR] or severe regurgitant valve disease, [OR] nondilated cardiomyopathy, [OR] or significant systemic ventricular outflow obstruction
Known renovascular hypertension [OR] or evidence of pulmonary hypertension (pulmonary vascular resistance > 6 Wood units) unresponsive to vasodilator agents such as oxygen, [OR] nitroprusside, [OR] or nitric oxide
History [OR] or current clinical evidence of moderate- [OR] to-severe fixed obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] or severe reactive airway diseases (e.g., asthma) requiring hospitalization within the past 2 years [OR] or patient currently using long-term inhaled bronchodilators
Renal, [OR] hepatic, [OR] gastrointestinal, [OR] or biliary disorder that could impair absorption, [OR] metabolism [OR] or excretion of orally administered medication
Concurrent terminal illness [OR] or other severe disease (e.g., active neoplasm) [OR] or other significant laboratory value(s) which, in the opinion of the investigator, could [NOT] preclude participation or survival
Endocrine disorders such as primary aldosteronism, [OR] pheochromocytoma, [OR] hyper- [OR] or hypothyroidism, [OR] insulin-dependent diabetes mellitus
Unwillingness [OR] or [NOT] inability to cooperate, or for the parents or guardians to give consent, or for the child to give assent, or any condition of sufficient severity to [NOT] impair cooperation in the study
Pregnancy [OR] or possible pregnancy at time of randomization, [OR] or female [AND] of child bearing potential [AND] who are lactating, [OR] or sexually active [AND] and [NOT] not taking adequate contraceptive precautions (e.g., intrauterine device [OR] or oral contraceptives for 3 months prior to entry into the study)
Use of an investigational drug within 30 days of randomization, [OR] or within 5 half-lives of the investigational drug (the longer period will apply)
History of drug sensitivity [OR] or allergic reaction to alpha-blockers [OR] or beta-blockers
Use of any of the following medications within two weeks of randomization: MAO inhibitors, [OR] Calcium channel blockers, [OR] alpha blockers, [OR] beta blockers, [OR] disopyramide, [OR] flecainide, [OR] encainide, [OR] moricizine, [OR] propafenone, [OR] sotalol, [OR] or beta adrenergic agonists
Hospital admission for protein losing enteropathy [OR] or plastic bronchitis within 3 months of randomization
Active [OR] and/or chronic protein losing enteropathy [OR] or plastic bronchitis (on inhaled medication to control the plastic bronchitis).
Hypoalbuminemia defined as serum albumin <2.0g/dL
Renal dysfunction defined as serum creatinine >2.0mg/dL
 Hepatic dysfunction defined as serum AST [OR] and/or ALT> 3 times upper limit of normal (approximately 120 IU/L however, will vary depending on age),
 Significant anemia [OR] or polycythemia defined as hemoglobin >18gm/dL [OR] or hemoglobin <7gm/dL
 Severely elevated serum BNP defined as BNP>300pg/ml
