Inclusion Criteria:
Age ≥ 18 years (Age ≥ 12 years for patients with bone sarcomas). 
Histologically confirmed diagnosis of unresectable, [OR] recurrent, [OR] and/or metastatic high grade soft-tissue [OR] or bone sarcoma of one of the following subtypes: soft tissue sarcomas (leiomyosarcoma, poorly differentiated/ [OR] de-differentiated liposarcoma, high grade pleomorphic undifferentiated sarcoma/ [OR] MFH [OR] and synovial sarcoma), [OR] and bone sarcomas (Ewing sarcoma, [OR] osteosarcoma, [OR] and chondrosarcoma [de-differentiated [OR] or mesenchymal]). 
ECOG Performance Status of 0 or 1. 
At least one site of measurable disease on CT/ [OR] MRI scans as defined by RECIST 1.1. Baseline imaging must be performed within 30 days of dosing. 
At least one site of accessible disease for pre- [OR] and post-treatment core biopsies for at least 20 patients per arm on the expansion cohorts. 
Patients may have received 1-3 prior systemic therapies in the metastatic setting. 
Adequate organ function within 14 days of dosing 
Must be willing to provide and have available archival tissue for PD-L1 testing. 
Written, voluntary informed consent. 
Fertile men [OR] and women [AND] of childbearing potential must agree to use an effective method of birth control from providing signed consent and for 120 days after last study drug administration. Women [AND] of childbearing potential include pre-menopausal women [OR] and women [AND] within the first 2 years of the onset of menopause. Women [AND] of childbearing potential must have a negative pregnancy test ≤ 72 hours prior to Day 1 of study. 
Effective methods of birth control include: surgically sterile, barrier device (condom, [OR] diaphragm), contraceptive coil, [OR] intrauterine device (IUD), [OR] and abstinence. 
Life expectancy of >12 weeks. 
Patients with central nervous system disease are eligible for enrollment if they have received prior radiotherapy [OR] or surgery to sites of CNS metastatic disease [AND] and are [NOT] without evidence of clinical progression for at least 4 weeks prior to screening, have [NOT] no evidence of new [OR] or enlarging brain metastases, [AND] and are [NOT] off steroids for at least 7 days before first dose of pembrolizumab. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Prior systemic therapy targeting PD-1: PD-L1 axis. 
Patients who are curable by conventional multidisciplinary management. 
Patients with severe [OR] and/or uncontrolled concurrent medical disease that in the opinion of the investigator could cause unacceptable safety risks [OR] or compromise compliance with the protocol. 
Patients who have received wide field radiotherapy ≤ 4 weeks [OR] or limited field radiation for palliation < 2 weeks prior to screening [OR] or who have [NOT] not recovered adequately from side effects of such therapy. 
Patients who have active infections requiring therapy. 
Patients that are known to be positive for Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) (HIV 1/2 antibodies), active Hepatitis B (HBsAg reactive), [OR] or Hepatitis C (HCV RNA [qualitative] is detected); patients with negative Hepatitis C antibody testing may [NOT] not need RNA testing. 
Patients that have a known psychiatric [OR] or substance abuse disorder that would interfere with cooperation with the requirements of the trial. 
Patients who received systemic anti-cancer treatment prior to the first dose of study drug within the following time frames: 
Patients with active autoimmune disease [OR] or a documented history of autoimmune disease [OR] or syndrome that requires systemic steroids [OR] or immunosuppressive agents. Patients with vitiligo [OR] or resolved childhood asthma/ [OR] atopy would be exception to this rule. Patients that require inhaled steroids [OR] or local steroid injections would [NOT] not be excluded from the study. Patients with hypothyroidism [NOT] not from autoimmune disease that is stable on hormone replacement will [NOT] not be excluded from the study. 
Women [AND] who are pregnant [OR] or nursing/ [OR] breastfeeding. 
Known hypersensitivity to pembrolizumab [OR] or another mAb. 
 Has a history of ([NOT] non-infectious) pneumonitis that required steroids [OR] or current pneumonitis. 
Patients with [NOT] untreated central nervous system disease. Patients with controlled treated CNS lesions [AND] who have undergone surgery [OR] or stereotactic radiosurgery [AND] and stable for 4 weeks are eligible. 
Inability to comply with protocol required procedures. 
Patients with medical conditions that require chronic systemic corticosteroid therapy [OR] or require any other form of immunosuppressive medication. However, patients using physiologic replacement doses of hydrocortisone, [OR] or its equivalent, will be considered eligible for this study: up to 20 mg hydrocortisone [OR] (or 5 mg of prednisone) in the morning and 10 mg hydrocortisone [OR] (or 2.5 mg prednisone) in the evening. 
Patients with the risk factors for bowel obstruction [OR] or bowel perforation (examples include but are not limited to a history of acute diverticulitis, [OR] intra-abdominal abscess, [OR] abdominal carcinomatosis). 
Patients who have received a live vaccine within 30 days prior to the first dose of trial treatment.
