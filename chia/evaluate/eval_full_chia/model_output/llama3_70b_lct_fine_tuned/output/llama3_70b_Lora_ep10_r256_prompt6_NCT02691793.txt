RET fusion positive [OR] or FGFR2 fusion/ [OR] other FGFR mutation Refractory solid tumor [OR] and/or specific sensitivity to Sunitinib by Avatar scan that has progressed following standard therapy [OR] or that has [NOT] not responded to standard therapy [OR] or for which there is [NOT] no standard therapy.
