Inclusion Criteria:
outpatients aged 18-70 years
confirmed diagnosis of H. pylori infection by at least one of the following methods: 13C-urea breath test, [OR] histology, [OR] rapid urease test [OR] or bacterial culture
an intention of H. pylori eradication treatment and have written inform consent
ability to read short messages on the mobile phone

Exclusion Criteria:
advanced chronic disease that would [NOT] not allow the patient to complete the treatment [OR] or follow-up [OR] or attend visits
allergy to any of the drugs used in this study
previous Helicobacter Pylori eradication treatment
pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding (female participants with childbearing potential were required to use medically accepted contraception for the duration of the study)
taking antibiotics [OR] or PPIs [OR] or bismuth salts within four weeks
previous gastrointestinal surgery
