ASA I & II, Nulliparous [OR] and Multiparous, Spontaneous/ [OR] Induced/ [OR] Augmented Labor, Early active labor (cervix <5 cm (if known)), Pain (VPS) > 3, 18-45 years of age

Exclusion Criteria:
<37 weeks gestation, H/o Cesarean Section, Multiple Gestation, Pre-eclampsia, Narcotics within 3 hours prior to labor epidural placement, Chronic Pain (as defined by chronic opiate consumption), Women [AND] who are participating in another study that will impact protocol
