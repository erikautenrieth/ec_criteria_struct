Inclusion Criteria:
Subject's ability to lay in a supine position [AND] with their hands at their sides during CVP measurements 
A consent form signed by the patient or patient's representative 
Subjects that are age 18-90 
Subjects that have an indwelling CICC [AND] and are transitioning to a PICC for long-term IV access 
CICC placed in the internal jugular vein [OR] or subclavian vein position 

Exclusion Criteria:
Inability to obtain consent 
Subjects under 18 years of age 
Non-English speaking subjects 
Subjects that are [NOT] unable to lay flat due to pulmonary complications, [OR] increased intracranial pressure (ICP), [OR] or unstable spinal cord injuries 
Subjects with known cardiac abnormalities (atrial septal defects [OR] or ventricular septal defects, severe tricuspid valve disease, severe pulmonary hypertension, [OR] Ejection fraction < 15%) 
Prisoners 
Subjects with known upper extremity deep vein thromboses (subclavian [OR] or distal) 
Subjects with [NOT] non-functional CICC [OR] or PICC distal ports 
Subjects with femoral CICCs 
Pregnant women
