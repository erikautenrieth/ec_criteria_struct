Inclusion Criteria:
18-year or older patients
Patient hospitalized in neuro-critical care for:
Arachnoid hemorrhage
Intra parenchymatous hematoma
stroke
Acute brain Severe injury
Post-operative complication of an act of neurosurgery [OR] or programmed neuroradiology
Sedation [AND] and mechanical ventilation planned > 2 days
Monitoring of intracranial temperature [AND] and pressure by intraparenchymal sensor (Sophysa®)
Brain temperature > 38.5°C for more than 30 minutes

Exclusion Criteria:
Known hypersensitivity to paracetamol [OR] or mannitol (excipient with known effect)
Severe hepatocellular insufficiency (ASAT [OR] or ALAT > 5N, [OR] or bilirubin > 2N)
 Pharmacological intervention (administration of corticosteroids, [OR] NSAIDs [OR] or paracetamol) [OR] or physical intervention (external cooling technique) that may influence temperature in the last 6 hours.
Pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding women
Previous participation in this study
