Inclusion Criteria:
Adult patients up to age 75 years, undergoing elective, ambulatory, arthroscopic rotator cuff repair.

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnancy, [OR] coagulopathy, [OR] allergy to bupivacaine, [OR] renal failure, [OR] hepatic insufficiency, [OR] and/or [NOT] inappropriate candidate for usual therapy (specifically, if [NOT] unable to receive the usual preoperative interscalene nerve block: preexisting nerve injury on side of surgery, refusal of nerve block, infection at site of nerve block).
