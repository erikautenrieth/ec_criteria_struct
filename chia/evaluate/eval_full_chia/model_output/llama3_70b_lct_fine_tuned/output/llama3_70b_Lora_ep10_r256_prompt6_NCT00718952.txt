Inclusion Criteria:
Subjects aged 12-65. 
Confirmed idiopathic pulmonary hypertension, [OR] connective tissue disease associated pulmonary hypertension, [OR] congenital heart disease(with Eisenmenger syndrome) associated pulmonary hypertension. 
Baseline 6-minutes walking distance 150m-550m. 
WHO pulmonary hypertension function II-III [AND] with [NOT] non-responder to calcium channel blockers. 
Documented written informed consent. 

Exclusion Criteria:
The other types of pulmonary hypertension. 
 Subjects who refuse to subscribe written informed consents or can't cooperate with the trial well. 
Subjects with serious acute [OR] or chronic disease involved liver, [OR] kidney, [OR] and brain [OR] or have to use potent CYP3A4-inhibitor [OR] or nitrate to treat the underlying diseases. 
Subjects who are currently treated with sildenafil for PAH [OR] or taking sildenafil [OR] or tadalafil. 
Other contraindications in package insert.
