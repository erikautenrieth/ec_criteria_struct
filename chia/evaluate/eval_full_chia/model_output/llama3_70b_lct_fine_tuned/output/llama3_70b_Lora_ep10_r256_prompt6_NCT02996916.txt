Inclusion Criteria:
Written informed consent obtained
Male [OR] and female subjects aged 20 years or older at informed consent
Essential hypertension [AND] who had [NOT] never received angiotensin II receptor antagonists [OR] and calcium channel blockers

Exclusion Criteria:
Secondary hypertension [OR] or malignant hypertension
Diabetes mellitus
 History [OR] or evidence of a stroke
 Hepatic [OR] or hematologic abnormality
 Mild Cognitive Impairment [OR] or Dementia
 Serum potassium level = 5.5 mEq/L
 Serum creatinine level = 3.0 mg/dL
 Acute [OR] or chronic disease
 Allergy to any drugs
 Pregnancy
