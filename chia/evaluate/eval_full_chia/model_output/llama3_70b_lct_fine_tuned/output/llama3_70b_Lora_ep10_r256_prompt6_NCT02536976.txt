Inclusion Criteria:
Aged 25-80 at screening. Subjects older than 80 will be allowed at the discretion of the PI.
Ambulatory (defined as able to ambulate at least 10 meters, with or without assistance).
Clinical Diagnosis of PD based on the United Kingdom Brain Bank diagnostic criteria for PD.
At least 8 micturitions per 24 hours [AND] and
At least 3 urgency episodes per 3-day diary.
A MoCA score between 19 and 28 (inclusive) at screening. For those on cognitive enhancers (donepezil, [OR] rivastigmine, [OR] memantine, [OR] galantamine) a MoCA score between 19 and 29 (inclusive) at screening.
Provide informed consent to participate in the study and understand that they may withdraw their consent at any time without prejudice to their future medical care.
Be cognitively capable, in the opinion of investigator, to understand and provide such informed consent.
Be cognitively capable to complete the required questionnaires and assessments, OR have a care partner who is willing and capable to assist them in the completion of these tasks.
Be on a stable regimen of antiparkinson's medications at least 30 days prior to screening, [AND] and be expected to remain on a stable dose for the duration of the study.
If taking cognitive enhancers (donepezil, [OR] rivastigmine, [OR] memantine, [OR] galantamine), must be on stable dose at least 30 days prior to screening, [AND] and be expected to remain on a stable dose for the duration of the study.

Exclusion Criteria:
Known [OR] or suspected alcohol [OR] or substance abuse in the preceding 12 months.
Women [AND] who are pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding.
 Women [AND] of childbearing potential (WOCP) [AND] who are [NOT] not using at least one method of contraception.
Patients with severe renal impairment (CLcr = 29 mL/min, [OR] or eGFR = 29 mL/min/1.73 m2), [OR] or moderate [OR] or severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh classes B [OR] or C).
 Patients with bladder outlet obstruction (BOO) that, in the opinion of the study urologist, would expose them to risk of urinary retention during treatment with mirabegron.
 Patients treated with drugs metabolized by the CYP2D6 pathway.
 Patients with supine systolic blood pressure (SBP) = 180 mm Hg, [OR] or diastolic blood pressure (DBP) = 110 mm Hg.
 Clinically significant, uncontrolled cardiac arrhythmia, unstable angina, [OR] congestive heart failure (NYHA Class 3 [OR] or 4), [OR] or history of myocardial infarction in the preceding 2 years.
 History of cancer in the preceding 2 years other than successfully treated, [NOT] non-metastatic, squamous cell [OR] or basal cell carcinoma, [OR] or cervical cancer in situ.
 Any major urological procedure in the preceding 90 days.
 Any major surgical procedure in the preceding 30 days.
 Previously treated with mirabegron within 60 days prior to the baseline visit (Visit 2), [OR] or previously having [NOT] failed treatment with mirabegron regardless of duration [OR] and timing of treatment.
 Current [OR] or previous, within the 60 days preceding the baseline visit (Visit 2), treatment with antimuscarinic agents for OAB symptoms; [AND] and, willingness to [NOT] not use antimuscarinic agents for the duration of the study.
 Currently receiving any other investigational drug [OR] or having received an investigational drug within the 60 days preceding the baseline visit (Visit 2).
 Any condition [OR] or laboratory test result, which, in the opinion of the Investigator [OR] or the Study Urologist, might result in an increased risk to the patient, [OR] or would affect their participation in the study.
 Any patient who, in the opinion of the Investigator, is [NOT] not a good candidate for the study [OR] or will [NOT] not be able to follow study procedures.
