Inclusion Criteria:
The group of patients who participated in the study included adults aged at least 19 years among the atraumatic CA outpatients [AND] who came to the ER [AND] and received CPR.

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant women [AND] and young children aged <18 years;
Patients with underlying disease cases [NOT] without the possibility of resuscitation (e.g., terminal cancer);
 Patients with do-not-resuscitate (DNR) status;
Death by excessive bleeding (e.g., abdominal main artery rupture);
Patients who have experienced in-hospital CA;
Patients previously treated with steroid, [OR] anti-cancer medicine, [OR] or immunosuppression treatment before CA;
Patients already been registered with other studies; or
Patients from whom informed consent cannot be obtained
