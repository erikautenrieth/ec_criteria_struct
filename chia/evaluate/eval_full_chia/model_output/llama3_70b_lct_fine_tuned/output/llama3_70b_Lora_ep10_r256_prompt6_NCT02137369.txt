Inclusion Criteria:
Men [OR] or women aged 18-60 years.
Primary psychiatric diagnosis of Major Depressive Disorder, [NOT] without psychotic features, confirmed via SCID-IV structured diagnostic interview.
Screening Hamilton Depression Rating Scale (HAMD) = 18; [AND] and Baseline HAMD = 15.
If the patient is a woman [AND] of child-bearing potential, she must agree to use an acceptable form of birth control for duration of study participation.
Able to understand and provide informed consent for participation.

Exclusion Criteria:
Lifetime history of Bipolar Disorder, [OR] Dementia, [OR] Autism Spectrum Disorder, [OR] Schizophrenia, [OR] or any other Psychotic Disorder.
Psychotic symptoms occurring at any time during the current major depressive episode.
Current (past 12 months) diagnosis of Panic disorder, [OR] Obsessive Compulsive Disorder, [OR] Posttraumatic Stress Disorder, [OR] Anorexia Nervosa, [OR] or Bulimia Nervosa.
Alcohol [OR] or Drug Dependence within 12 months [OR] or Abuse within 3 months (excluding nicotine [OR] and caffeine) of baseline visit, as assessed by history [OR] and urine drug screen.
Clinical evidence of a severe Personality Disorder, as assessed by the study psychiatrist, which would impede participation or completion of the trial.
Known neurological disorders [OR] or documented serious head injury.
Serious [OR] and unstable medical illnesses including cardiovascular disease [OR] and cancer.
Active medical conditions [AND] with known mood changes (endocrine, [OR] autoimmune disorders).
Current diabetes mellitus.
For women, pregnancy, [OR] lactation, [OR] or unwillingness to comply with birth control requirements.
Use of any of the following treatments [OR] or any other alternative therapy within 2 weeks of the pre-treatment PET scan that may have beneficial effects on mood, including St John's Wort, [OR] S-adenosyl methionine (SAMe), n-3 fatty acids, [OR] or light therapy.
Use of antidepressant medication within 1 month of the pre-treatment PET scan (within 5 weeks for fluoxetine [OR] and protryptyline).
Failure to achieve a much improved status (i.e. equivalent to >50% symptom reduction) with any lifetime treatment course of CBT (defined as a minimum of 4 sessions of a specified manual-driven therapy by a CBT-trained therapist) [OR] or escitalopram (defined as a minimum of 6 weeks of at least 10 mg/day).
Clinically significant active suicidal ideation [OR] or self-injurious behavior necessitating immediate treatment, as determined by the investigator.
Received electroconvulsive therapy in the past 6 months [OR] or during the current depressive episode.
Currently responding to medication treatment, [NOT] without clinical reasons to change.
Current treatment with weekly individual [OR] or group psychotherapy of any type targeted at depressive symptoms.
QTc >500 milliseconds on EKG at screening.
Contraindications for MRI, including, but not limited to pacemaker, [OR] aneurysm clips, [OR] neurostimulators, [OR] cochlear implants, [OR] metal in eyes, [OR] steel worker, [OR] intra-uterine devices for birth control.
Maintenance [OR] or prophylactic therapy for stable medical conditions.
Hypnotic medication prescribed [OR] or approved by the study physician, (up to a three doses per week) for insomnia, as long if [NOT] not the night before a PET/ [OR] MRI or clinic ratings visit. Antipsychotic medications, whether prescribed for sleep [OR] or other indications, are prohibited.
