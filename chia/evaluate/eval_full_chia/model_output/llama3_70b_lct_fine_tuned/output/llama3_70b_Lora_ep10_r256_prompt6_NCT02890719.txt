Inclusion Criteria:
Age between 18 and 78 year-old. 
Previous liver transplantation(more than 6 month). 
Genotype 1 [OR] and 4 infection. 
Hepatitis C recurrence defined by the presence of abnormal liver function test, positive HCV-RNA, histological signs of hepatitis C recurrence. 
Viral load ≥10000UI/mL. 
Immunosuppression with tacrolimus [OR] and/or mycophenolate (Prednisone use is allowed at low dose, ≤10 mg/d). 
Treatment [NOT] naïve [OR] or treatment experienced (Peg-RBV [OR] or triple therapy). 

Exclusion Criteria:
Genotype 2, [OR] 3, [OR] 5 [OR] or 6 infection. 
Decompensated cirrhosis defined by the presence of actual [OR] or previous history of clinical decompensation including ascites, [OR] hepatic encephalopathy, [OR] variceal bleeding [OR] or spontaneous bacterial peritonitis, [OR] or a Child-Pugh B [OR] or C. 
Hepatocellular carcinoma after liver transplantation. 
Total bilirubin > 3 mg/dL. 
Immunosuppression with cyclosporine [OR] or an mTOR inhibitor (everolimus [OR] or sirolimus). 
Severe extrahepatic diseases: cardiovascular, [OR] respiratory, [OR] cerebrovascular [OR] and poorly controlled diabetes. 
Platelets < 75 x 109 cells/L. 
Neutrophil count < 0.5 x 109 cells/L. 
Hemoglobin < 9 g/dL. 
Albumin < 3g/dL. 
HIV infection. 
Hepatitis B infection. 
Active intake of toxic amounts of alcohol [OR] or recreational drugs. 
Females [AND] who are pregnant, [OR] become to be pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding [OR] or males [AND] whose partners are pregnant, [OR] become to be pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding. 
Intake of disallowed medications including(but not limited to): 
1. Antibiotics: clarithromycin, [OR] erythromycin, [OR] telithromycin, [OR] nafcillin, [OR] rifampin 
2. Antifungals: itraconazole, [OR] ketoconazole, [OR] voriconazole 
3. Antihypertensives: nifedipine 
4. Anticonvulsants: carbamazepine, [OR] phenytoin, [OR] phenobarbital 
5. Bosentan 
6. Modafinil 
7. St.Jonh's Wort 
8. Immunosuppressants: cyclosporin, [OR] everolimus, [OR] sirolimus 
9. Diabetes agents: glibenclamide, [OR] glyburide 
10. Lipid lowering agents: gemfibrozil 
11. Eltrombopag 
12. Lapatinib 
13. HIV medications: efavirenz, [OR] etravirine, all ritonavir boosted [OR] and unboosted HIV protease inhibitors 
14. Statins: simvastatin, [OR] fluvastatin, [OR] rosuvastatin at doses greater than 10 mg/d, atorvastatin at doses greater than 10 mg/d.
