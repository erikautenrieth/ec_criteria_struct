Inclusion Criteria:
Adult men [AND] who have sex with men, [AND] and transgender women
Unaware of HIV status at enrollment in follow-up cohort
High risk for HIV infection
Willing to test for HIV
 [NOT] No prior ART, including prior administration of pre- and post-exposure prophylaxis in the last 30 days
Willing to provide informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Prior receipt of investigational anti-HIV vaccine
Ongoing therapy with any of the following: Systemic corticosteroids. Short course less than or equal to 21 days of corticosteroids is allowed; Systemic chemotherapeutic agents; Nephrotoxic systemic agents, including aminoglycosides, [OR] amphotericin B, [OR] cidofovir, [OR] cisplatin, [OR] foscarnet, [OR] pentamidine; Immunomodulatory treatments including Interleukin-2; Investigational agents
Known allergy/sensitivity [OR] or any hypersensitivity to components of study drugs (ART) [OR] or their formulations
 Active drug [OR] or alcohol use [OR] or dependence that would interfere with adherence to study requirements
Serious medical [OR] or psychiatric illness that would interfere with the ability to adhere to study requirements
Chronic [OR] or acute hepatitis B infection
Use of female hormonal products based on estrogen [OR] or derivatives
