Inclusion Criteria:
Healthy postmenopausal women [AND] with 50 or more moderate to severe hot flushes. 
Women between 40 to 70 years of age. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Body mass index (BMI) of 35 kg/m2 or more. 
Significant metabolic [OR] and endocrine diseases. 
Diagnosis of cancer. 
Use of steroids [OR] or drugs that interfere with the metabolism of estrogen. 
Use of any systemic estrogen, [OR] progestin, [OR] or DHEA in the eight weeks prior to randomization. 
Use of alternative therapies [OR] or natural products to treat postmenopausal symptoms in the four weeks prior to randomization. 
Palpable fibroids [OR] or uterine prolapse: Grade 2 or 3. 
Cigarette smoking
