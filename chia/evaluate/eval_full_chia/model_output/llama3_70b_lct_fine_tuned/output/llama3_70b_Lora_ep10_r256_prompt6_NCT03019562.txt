Inclusion Criteria:
19-65 years of age
ASA physical status classification I or II
Scheduled for total hip replacement surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
Allergic to study drugs
Patient with asthma [OR] or COPD, patient who is severely respiratory depressed
Renal [OR] of hepatic insufficiency
Epileptic status
Intracranial lesion associated with increased intracranial pressure
Acute abdomen, patient who has diagnosed paralytic ileus [OR] or suspicious ileus
Pregnant [OR] or lactating women
