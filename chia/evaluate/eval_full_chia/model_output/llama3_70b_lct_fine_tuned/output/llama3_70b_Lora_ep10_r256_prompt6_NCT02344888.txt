Inclusion Criteria:
Infertile lean women [AND] with PCOS as defined by the Rotterdam criteria.
CC resistance (defined as [NOT] failure of ovulation after receiving 150 mg/day of CC for 5 consecutive days per cycle, for at least 3 consecutive cycles).

Exclusion Criteria:
Age < 20 [OR] or > 35 years.
Body mass index (BMI) < 18.5 kg/m2 [OR] or > 25 kg/m2.
Presence of any infertility factor other than anovulatory PCOS.
Previous history of ovarian surgery [OR] or surgical removal of one ovary.
 Previous exposure to cytotoxic drugs [OR] or pelvic irradiation.
Oral hypoglycemic [OR] or hormonal therapy either currently [OR] or in the preceding 3 months.
Metabolic [OR] or hormonal abnormalities
