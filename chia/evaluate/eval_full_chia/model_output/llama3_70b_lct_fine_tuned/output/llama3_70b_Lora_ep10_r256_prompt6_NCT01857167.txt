Inclusion Criteria:
1. Fasting glucose > 7.0 [OR] or have diabetes medication; 
2. Male, 35-80 years; female, postmenopausal to 80 years; 
3. Agree to participant in the trial. 

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Deny to sign the informed consent; 
2. type 1 diabetes; 
3. Family history of hypertriglyceridemia [OR] or fasting triglyceride>4.56 mmol/L; 
4. Have severe liver disease, [OR] kidney disease [OR] or cancer; 
5. Participating in the other clinical trial within 30 days; 
6. Other diseases [OR] or conditions, for which the doctor of the patients do [NOT] not agree his or her participating.
