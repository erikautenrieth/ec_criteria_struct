Inclusion Criteria:
Pregnant gestational age >= 28 weeks
Systolic blood pressure >=160 mm Hg [OR] OR a diastolic blood pressure of >=110 mm Hg measured twice more than 15 minutes apart
Able to swallow pills
>= 18 years

Exclusion Criteria:
Indication for emergent cesarean [OR] or known fetal anomaly
Anti-hypertensive therapy received in the past 12 hours
History of eclampsia [OR] or other adverse CNS complication (e.g., stroke [OR] or PRES) in this pregnancy
Actively wheezing at time of enrollment [OR] or history of asthma complications
 Known coronary artery disease [OR] or type I DM [AND] with microvascular complications [OR] or signs of heart failure [OR] or clinical dissection of the aorta
