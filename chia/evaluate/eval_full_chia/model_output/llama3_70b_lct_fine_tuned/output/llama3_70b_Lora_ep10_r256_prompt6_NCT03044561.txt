Inclusion Criteria:
(1) cases of infertility, older than 20 years of age [AND] and [NOT] not older than 40 years.
 (2) Body mass index (BMI):20-29.
 (3) women have experienced two or more implantation failure attributed to inadequate endometrial development.
Exclusion Criteria:
 (1) Uterine abnormalities (e.g. septate, [OR] bicornuate [OR] and fibroid uterus, [OR] Asherman Syndrome).
Concurrent use of organic nitrites [OR] and nitrates.
Severe hepatic impairment.
Severe renal impairment.
Hypotension.
Recent stroke [OR] or heart attack.
