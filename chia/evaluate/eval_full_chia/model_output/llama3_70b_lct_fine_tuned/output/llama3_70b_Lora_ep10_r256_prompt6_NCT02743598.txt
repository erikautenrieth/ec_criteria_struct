Inclusion Criteria:
HIV controlled on therapy for at least 12 weeks
Viral load < 200 copies
BMI >27 to 45
Diagnosis of DM type 2 [AND] with A1-C >7 to 15
Participants must be willing to comply with all study related procedures

Exclusion Criteria:
Personal [OR] or family history of pancreatitis
Medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) [OR] or Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia Syndrome Type 2 (MEN 2)
Gastroparesis
Allergy to liraglutide [OR] or any of the active ingredients in liraglutide [OR] or other GLP-1 analogue
Weight loss drugs other than metformin
Type 1 diabetes mellitus [OR] or diabetic ketoacidosis
Known major cognitive deficit dementia, history of head trauma [AND] with loss of consciousness >30 min, history of stroke, current central nervous system (CNS) disorder such as seizures [OR] or opportunistic CNS infection
Renal insufficiency defined as creatinine clearance < 60 mL/min
Active opportunistic infections
Pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding
Unstable cardiovascular disease [AND] with hospitalization within 1 year for acute coronary syndrome
 Decompensated heart failure
 Substance abuse
 Active alcohol [OR] or opioid substitution therapy
Serious [OR] or unstable medical [OR] or psychological conditions that would compromise the subject's safety for successful participation
