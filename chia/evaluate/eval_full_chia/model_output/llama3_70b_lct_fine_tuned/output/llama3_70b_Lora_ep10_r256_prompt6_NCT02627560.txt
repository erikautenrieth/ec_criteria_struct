Inclusion Criteria:
breast cancer
undergoing unilateral mastectomy [AND] with or without axillary node dissection
received adequate oral and written information about the study and signed an informed-consent form

Exclusion Criteria:
pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding
known thromboembolic disease [OR] or with high risk of thromboembolism, warranting extra anticoagulation in connection with the procedure
known allergy to tranexamic acid/ [OR] Cyklokapron®
