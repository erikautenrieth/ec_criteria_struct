Inclusion Criteria:
Subjects over the age of 18 years [AND] who agree informed consent [AND] and [AND] who have at least one polyp of eligible size (6-10mm) 

Exclusion Criteria:
Anticoagulant therapy during the past 1 week of the procedure 
Known coagulopathy 
History of liver cirrhosis, [OR] chronic kidney disease, [OR] malignancy, [OR] inflammatory bowel disease, significant infectious disease, [OR] polyposis syndrome
