Inclusion Criteria:
Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus patients
Patient who had been diagnosed within the previous 12 months [AND] with HbA1c levels of 8.0-12.0%, did [NOT] not have a medical history related to diabetes, [AND] and did [NOT] not display proliferative retinopathy

Exclusion Criteria:
The use of weight-lowering drugs, any investigational blood-glucose [OR] or lipid-lowering agent (other than statins [OR] or ezetimibe) within the past 3 months
Previous treatment with systemic corticosteroids [OR] or a change in dosage of thyroid hormones in the previous 6 weeks
The use of insulin within the 3 months prior to screening
