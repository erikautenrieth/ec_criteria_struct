Inclusion Criteria:
patients aged >18, <75, left ventricle ejection fraction (LVEF) >50%, multivessel coronary disease detected by coronarography, indication to receive a CABG, stable CAD. All diabetics [OR] and [NOT] non diabetics.

Exclusion Criteria:
acute myocardial infarction, [OR] heart failure, [OR] neoplastic disease, chronic diseases that may affect the inflammatory profile both systemic [OR] and epicardial (cancer, chronic intestinal inflammation, [OR] hepatitis, [OR] AIDS); life expectancy < 6 months, previous CABG [OR] and/or other open heart surgery intervention, acute coronary syndrome
