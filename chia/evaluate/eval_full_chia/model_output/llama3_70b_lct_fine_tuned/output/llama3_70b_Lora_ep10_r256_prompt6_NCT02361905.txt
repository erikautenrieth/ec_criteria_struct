Inclusion Criteria:
hypoechoic uterine leiomyoma (echogenicity <3),
intramural leiomyomas with an ultrasonographic size <20 cm [AND] but >4cm,
indication to surgery (symptoms of menometrorrhagia,
menstrual disorder,
infertility,
pelvic pain [OR] or pelvic pressure

Exclusion Criteria:
submucosal leiomyoma,
endometrial hyperplasia [AND] with atypia,
history of uterine surgery
