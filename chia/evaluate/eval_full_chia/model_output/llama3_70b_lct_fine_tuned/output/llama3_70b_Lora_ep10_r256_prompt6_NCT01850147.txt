Inclusion Criteria:
Histologic [OR] or cytologic diagnosis of stage IIIB/ [OR] IV NSCLC 
ECOG PS: 0,1 
Unidimensional [OR] or bi-dimensional measurable disease 
Receive prior treatment including first-line platinum-based chemotherapy, standard second-line chemotherapy [AND] and 1 EGF/ [OR] EGFR inhibitor 
Evidence of disease progression 
Life expectancy >12 weeks 
Neutrophils > 1.5 109/l, Platelets > 100 109/l, Hemoglobin > 9g/dl, Total bilirubin < 1.5 UNL, AST (SGOT) [AND] and ALT (SGPT) < 2.5 UNL, Alkaline phosphatases < 5 UNL, Creatinine < 1 UNL 

Exclusion Criteria:
Pre-existing hemoptysis [AND] of a severity > grade 3 by NCI CTCAE criteria within 4 weeks prior to study entry 
Uncontrolled hypertension 
CHF, [OR] angina [OR] or arrhythmias 
LVEF < 1 UNL 
Existing a second malignancy within 5 years 
Infected with HIV
