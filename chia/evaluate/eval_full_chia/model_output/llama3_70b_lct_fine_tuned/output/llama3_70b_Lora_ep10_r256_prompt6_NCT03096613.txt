Inclusion Criteria:
Aged 18 years or older, male [OR] or female.
Systolic heart failure [AND] with New York Heart Association (NYHA) class II-III.
 Left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) less than 40% by echocardiography during screening [AND] and randomization.
SCH (TSH: upper limits of normal (ULN) -10mIU/L, [AND] and FT4 level within reference range).
Having received standard HF therapy for at least 2 weeks, having reached target dose [OR] or max tolerable dose.
Provided informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
Acute heart failure [OR] or acute exacerbation of chronic heart failure within the past 2 weeks.
 Scheduled cardiac resynchronization therapy [OR] or heart transplantation.
 History of malignant tumor [OR] or life expectancy under 12 months.
 Already on medications that may affect thyroid function (L-T4, [OR] carbimazole, [OR] propylthiouracil, [OR] amiodarone, [OR] lithium).
 Pregnancy [OR] and lactation period.
 Participation in another clinical trial within the past 30 days.
 Contraindication [OR] or intolerance to evidence-based therapy for CHF, such as beta-blocker, [OR] angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor [OR] or angiotensin receptor blocker.
 Known hypersensitivity to the trial treatment(s) [OR] or diluents (when applicable), including placebo [OR] or other comparator drug(s).
 Untreated adrenal insufficiency.
 Untreated pituitary insufficiency.
 Untreated thyrotoxicosis.
 Treatment with levothyroxine must [NOT] not be initiated in patients with acute myocardial infarction, [OR] acute myocarditis, [OR] or acute pancarditis.
 Severe renal dysfunction (eGFR=30 ml/min/1.73 m2).
 Significant hepatic impairment (Serum GPT > 120 U/L).
 Any disorder which, in the opinion of the investigator, might jeopardise subject's safety [OR] or compliance with the protocol.
