Inclusion Criteria:
age > 17 [AND] and < 60 years;
American Society of Anesthesiology (ASA) I-III;
admitted for living donor renal transplantation.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with a history of drug abuse;
preoperative history of schizophrenia, [OR] epilepsy, [OR] parkinsonism, use of cholinesterase inhibitor, [NOT] inability to communicate in the preoperative period (coma, profound dementia, [OR] or language barrier).
