Inclusion Criteria:
Symptomatic patients with heart failure (men [OR] and women) aged >18 years,
Functional class II, III or IV by the New York Heart Association (NYHA)
Left ventricular ejection fraction <35%
Ischemic [OR] and [NOT] nonischemic etiology
Type B natriuretic peptide (BNP) >150 pg/ml ([OR] or pro-BNP [N-terminal-proBNP] = 600 pg / ml) [OR] or if the patient was hospitalized for cardiac decompensation within the preceding 12 months, BNP >100 pg/ml ([OR] or N-terminal-proBNP = 400 pg / ml)

Exclusion Criteria:
History of hypersensitivity [OR] or allergy to any of the study drugs, drugs of similar chemical classes, ACE inhibitors (ACEIs), angiotensin II receptor blockers (ARBs), [OR] or neprilysin inhibitors, as well as known [OR] or suspected contraindications to the study drugs.
Previous history of intolerance to recommended target doses of ACEIs [OR] or ARBs.
 Known history of angioedema.
 Requirement for treatment with both ACEIs [OR] and ARBs.
 Current acute decompensated heart failure ([OR] exacerbation of chronic heart failure manifested by signs [OR] and symptoms that may require intravenous therapy).
 Symptomatic hypotension.
 Estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) <30%.
 Serum potassium >5.4 mmol/L.
 Acute coronary syndrome, [OR] stroke, [OR] transient ischaemic attack, [OR] cardiac, [OR] carotid, [OR] or other major cardiovascular surgery, [OR] percutaneous coronary intervention, [OR] or carotid angioplasty within the 3 months.
 Coronary [OR] or carotid artery disease likely to require surgical [OR] or percutaneous intervention within the 6 months.
 Implantation of a cardiac resynchronization therapy (CRT) device within 3 months [OR] or intent to implant a CRT.
 History of heart transplant [OR] or on a transplant list [OR] or with left ventricular (LV) assistance device.
 History of severe pulmonary disease.
 Diagnosis of peripartum- [OR] or chemotherapy-induced cardiomyopathy within the 12 months.
 Documented [NOT] untreated ventricular arrhythmia [AND] with syncopal episodes within the 3 months.
 Symptomatic bradycardia [OR] or second- or third-degree atrioventricular block [NOT] without a pacemaker.
 Presence of haemodynamically significant mitral [OR] and/or aortic valve disease, except mitral regurgitation secondary to LV dilatation.
 Presence of other haemodynamically significant obstructive lesions of the LV outflow tract, including aortic [OR] and subaortic stenosis.
 Any surgical [OR] or medical condition which might significantly alter the absorption, [OR] distribution, [OR] metabolism, [OR] or excretion of study drugs, including, but not limited to, any of the following: History of active inflammatory bowel disease during the 12 months. Active duodenal [OR] or gastric ulcers during the 3 months. Evidence of hepatic disease as determined by any one of the following: aspartate aminotransferase [OR] or alanine aminotransferase values exceeding 2x upper limit of normal, history of hepatic encephalopathy, history of oesophageal varices, [OR] or history of porto-caval shunt. Current treatment with cholestyramine [OR] or colestipol resins.
 Presence of any other disease [AND] with a life expectancy of <5 years.
