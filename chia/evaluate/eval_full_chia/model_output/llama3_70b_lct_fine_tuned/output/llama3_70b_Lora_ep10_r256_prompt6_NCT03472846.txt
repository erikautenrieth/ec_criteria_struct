Inclusion Criteria:
Postmenopausal women
Age 60-80 years
T-score according to DXA: <-2.5
indication for osteoporosis therapy according to international guidelines

Exclusion Criteria:
Diabetes mellitus type 1
renal insufficiency III-V °
Cirrhosis hepatis (Child B or higher)
Chronic alcohol abuse
rheumatic disease (RA, [OR] SpA, [OR] SLE)
Malignancies (<5 years)
Eating Disorder (anorexia nervosa, [OR] bulimia)
bone-specific pretreatment (DMAB, [OR] TPTD, [OR] strontium ranelate, [OR] SERMs) Bisphosphonate treatment is allowed
