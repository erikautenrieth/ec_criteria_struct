Inclusion Criteria:
Females 18-65 years old [AND] who undergoing colposcopic directed biopsy

Exclusion Criteria:
A known allergy to Celecoxib, [OR] aspirin [OR] or another NSAID.
Active peptic ulceration [OR] or gastrointestinal bleeding.
Inflammatory bowel disease.
Congestive heart failure (NYHA II-IV).
 Established ischemic heart disease, [OR] peripheral arterial disease [OR] and/or cerebrovascular disease.
 History of neurologic deficit.
 Known hepatic [OR] or renal impairment.
 Pregnancy.
 Breast-feeding.
 Post-hysterectomy.
 Bleeding disorders.
 Drug abuse.
 Cervical [OR] and vaginal infection.
