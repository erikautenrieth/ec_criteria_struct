Inclusion Criteria:
Cerebral palsy of any types caused by Neonatal Jaundice 

Exclusion Criteria:
Epilepsy 
Hydrocephalus [AND] with ventricular drain 
Coagulation disorders 
Allergy to anesthetic agents 
Severe health conditions such as cancer, [OR] failure of heart, [OR] lung, [OR] liver [OR] or kidney 
Active infections
