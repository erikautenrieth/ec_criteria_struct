Inclusion Criteria:
18 years of age and older,
diagnosis of stage II adhesive capsulitis as determined by clinical examination of the treating physician, [AND] and
[NOT] absence of abnormal findings on X-ray.

Exclusion Criteria:
allergy to Doxycycline [OR] or Methylprednisolone,
pregnancy,
diagnosis,
Inflammatory arthritis [OR] or diabetes,
secondary adhesive capsulitis (history of significant trauma, [OR] rotator cuff tear injury, [OR] stroke)
evidence of arthritis on x-ray,
current infectious disease, [AND] and
any previous treatment for the for adhesive capsulitis of the affected shoulder.
