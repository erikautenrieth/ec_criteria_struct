Inclusion Criteria:
Pregnant women [AND] with APS diagnosed according to the revised classification criteria for APS in 2006 in Sydney, Australia
Early pregnancy body weight is 50-90 Kg

Exclusion Criteria:
Women [AND] with systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE)
Women [AND] with active thromboembolic disorders
Women [AND] with history of previous thromboembolic disorders
