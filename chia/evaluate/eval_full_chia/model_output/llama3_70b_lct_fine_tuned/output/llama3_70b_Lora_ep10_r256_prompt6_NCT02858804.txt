Inclusion Criteria:
age=65 years
diagnosis with mantle cell lymphoma
Ann Arbor stage II,III or IV
ECOG=1 [OR] or if ECOG=2 [AND] but recover after pretreatment.

Exclusion Criteria:
with centre neural system involvement
serious complications such as uncontrolled diabetes, [OR] gastric ulcer [OR] or other serious angiocardiopathy determined by the physician
HIV positive [OR] or active HBV infection [OR] or other uncontrolled systematic infection
clinical central nervous dysfunction
serious surgery within 30 days
pregnancy [OR] or baby nursing period [OR] or un-contracepted child bearing period woman.
