Inclusion Criteria:
Age: > or = 16 years 
Weight: more than 40 Kg 
Autoimmune Hemolytic anemia [AND] with clinical [OR] and biochemical evidence of hemolysis refractory to treatment, in relapse [OR] or steroids dependant 
Idiopathic thrombocytopenic purpura [AND] with platelet counts < 50,000, refractory to treatment, in relapse [OR] or steroids dependant 

Exclusion Criteria:
Current viral [OR] or bacterial infection. 
Positive serology for HIV, [OR] HCV, [OR] HBV.
