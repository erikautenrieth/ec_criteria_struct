Inclusion Criteria:
Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS) (ROME III criteria): subtype with diarrhea [OR] or mixed form
age 18-65 years

Exclusion Criteria:
IBS subtype with constipation
medication: antidepressants [OR] or H1-receptor antagonists
pregnancy, [OR] breast feeding
co-morbidity: severe kidney- [OR] and/or liver disease [OR] or other gastrointestinal diseases
