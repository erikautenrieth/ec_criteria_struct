Inclusion Criteria:
Female 
Age ≥18 years 
Patients with a lesion > 0.5 cm in largest diameter size, initially scored BI-RADS® 3, 4a, 4b [OR] or 4c in B-mode ultrasound 
Informed consent about histological examination (core cut biopsy (CCB), vacuum-assisted biopsy (VAB), fine needle aspiration (FNA) [OR] or surgery) has already been given in the course of clinical routine 
Signed informed consent of study participation 

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant [OR] or lactating women 
Women [AND] with breast implants on the same side as the lesion 
Women [AND] that underwent local radiation [OR] or chemotherapy within the last 12 months 
Women [AND] with history of breast cancer [OR] or breast surgery in the same quadrant 
Lesions in [OR] or close to scar tissue (< 1cm) 
Skin lesions [OR] or lesions that have been biopsied previously 
Lesion larger than 4 cm in the longest dimension 
 [NOT] No lesion should be included when more than 50% of the lesion is further down than 4 cm beneath the skin level.
