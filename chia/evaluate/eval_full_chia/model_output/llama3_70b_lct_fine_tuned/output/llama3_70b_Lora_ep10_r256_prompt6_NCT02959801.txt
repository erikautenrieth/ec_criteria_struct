Inclusion Criteria:
proven acute deep venous thrombosis, less than 21 days [AND] and who were referred to the interventional radiology department.

Exclusion Criteria:
presence of subacute [OR] or chronic DVT more than 21 days in duration, [NOT] inability to lie in the prone position required for intervention, terminal systemic disease requiring palliative treatment, active bleeding (from a gastric/ [OR] duodenal ulcer [OR] or the cerebrovascular system), a haemorrhagic stroke within the previous year, an impaired bleeding-clotting profile, [OR] and any haemophilic disorder, [OR] or pregnancy.
