Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosed with Beta-Thalassemia Major [AND] and receiving regular blood transfusion [AND] and on iron chelating therapy.
Weight: equal to or over 35 kg.
Normal renal function.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with renal impairment (serum creatinine more than twice the upper limit of normal).
Patients with heart failure.
 Patients with sepsis [OR] or active infection.
Patients with diabetes mellitus (either primary [OR] or secondary to thalassemia).
regular consumption of medication with potential hepatotoxicity.
 regular herbal medicine [OR] or antioxidant supplementation.
patients with gastrointestinal conditions [NOT] preventing adsorption of oral medication.
