Inclusion Criteria:
Males [OR] and females [AND] who are at least 18 years of age at time of enrollment. 
Subject understands the investigational nature of the study and provides written, informed consent. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects taking any lipid modification therapy, including but not limited to statins, [OR] fibrates [OR] and bile acid sequestrants. 
Subjects taking fish oil [OR] or any other supplements, which in the investigator s opinion may interfere with the study. 
Subjects with acute liver disease [OR] or active peptic ulcer disease. 
Subjects with elevated uric acid levels greater than 10 mg/dL [OR] or gout 
Pregnancy [OR] or women currently breastfeeding. 
Female subjects taking hormonal contraceptives [OR] or hormone replacement therapy may be included in this study only if they have been on a stable dose for at least 3 months. 
BMI less than 18.5 
Subjects with weight that varies greater than 20% over the past 3 months. 
Subjects taking the following medications for at least six weeks, which may interfere with the study, will be [NOT] excluded: BAS, [OR] antibiotics, [OR] anticoagulants, [OR] anticonvulsants, [OR] antiarrhythmic, [OR] Cyclosporine, [OR] Mycophenolate [OR] and Synthroid. Subjects with chronic diarrhea, [OR] gastric bypass [OR] or lap band procedures, [OR] ostomies, [OR] bowel motility problems, [OR] or other conditions that could affect intestinal fat absorption. 
Subjects initiating new medications [OR] or patients on multiple medications may also be [NOT] excluded. 
Inability to swallow capsules 
Patients with a history of type I [OR] or type II diabetes [OR] or HbA1c greater than 6.5%. 
Volunteers may also be [NOT] excluded, if in the opinion of the study investigators, they have some other condition [OR] or disorder that may adversely affect the outcome of the study [OR] or the safety of the volunteer.
