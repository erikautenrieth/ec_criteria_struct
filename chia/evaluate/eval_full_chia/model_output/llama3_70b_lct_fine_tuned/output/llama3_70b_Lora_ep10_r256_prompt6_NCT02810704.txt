Inclusion Criteria:
Males [OR] and females 21 years of age or older;
Undergoing elective primary, [OR] resurfacing arthroplasty, [OR] revision, [OR] or second stage re-implantation total hip replacement;
 Undergoing elective primary, [OR] revision, [OR] or second stage re-implantation total [OR] or uni compartmental knee replacement;
Patient has necessary mental capacity to participate and is able to comply with study protocol requirements;
Patient is willing and able to give informed consent; and
Patient is willing to be randomized and participate.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients undergoing bilateral hip [OR] or knee replacement;
 Patients undergoing total hip [OR] or knee replacement [AND] who have been enrolled in this study for a prior hip [OR] or knee replacement;
 Patients who are concurrently enrolled in another active interventional clinical trial testing a drug [OR] or intervention known [OR] or believed to interact with aspirin, [OR] warfarin, [OR] or rivaroxaban;
 Patients who have a contraindication to two or more of the three study prophylaxis regimens;
 Women [AND] who are pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding, as well as those of reproductive potential unless there is a negative urine pregnancy test on the day of surgery;
 Patients on chronic (longer than the prior 6 months) anticoagulation other than with antiplatelet medications;
 Patients with documented gastrointestinal, [OR] cerebral, [OR] or other hemorrhage within 3 months of the operation;
 Patients with a known diagnosis of defective hemostasis [AND] and past history of clinical bleeding requiring transfusion [OR] and treatment;
 Patients who have had an operative procedure involving the eye, [OR] ear, [OR] or central nervous system within one month;
 Patients with severe uncontrolled hypertension [AND] with systolic BP > 220mmHg [OR] or diastolic BP > 120mmHg;
 Patients with an absolute body weight of less than 41 kilograms (90.4 lbs) at baseline visit;
 Vulnerable patient populations including prisoners [OR] and institutionalized individuals.
