Inclusion Criteria:
Highly active RMS as defined by:
One relapse in the previous year [AND] and at least 1 T1 Gadolinium (Gd)+ lesion [OR] or 9 or more T2 lesions, while on therapy with other disease modifying drugs (DMDs)
Two or more relapses in the previous year, whether on DMD treatment [OR] or [NOT] not.
Expanded Disability Status Scale (EDSS) score less than equals to (<=) 5.0.
Other protocol defined inclusion criteria could apply.

Exclusion Criteria:
Previous exposure to drugs such as fingolimod, [OR] natalizumab, [OR] alemtuzumab, [OR] mitoxantrone [OR] and ocrelizumab.
Positive hepatitis C [OR] or hepatitis B surface antigen test [OR] and/or hepatits B core antibody test for immunoglobulin G (IgG) [OR] and/or immunoglobulin M (IgM).
Current [OR] or previous history of immune deficiency disorders including a positive human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) result.
 Currently receiving immunosuppressive [OR] or myelosuppressive therapy with, for example, monoclonal antibodies, [OR] methotrexate, [OR] cyclophosphamide, [OR] cyclosporine [OR] or azathioprine, [OR] or chronic use of corticosteroids.
History of tuberculosis, presence of active tuberculosis, [OR] or latent tuberculosis
Evidence [OR] or suspect of Progressive Multifocal Leukoencephalopathy (PML) in Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI).
Active malignancy [OR] or history of malignancy.
Other protocol defined exclusion criteria could apply.
