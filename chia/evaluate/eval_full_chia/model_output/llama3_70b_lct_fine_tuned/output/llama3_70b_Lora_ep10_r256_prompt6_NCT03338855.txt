Inclusion Criteria:
Patients are able to provide signed and dated written informed consent prior to any study specific procedures.
Women [AND] are post-menopausal (defined as at least 1 year post cessation of menses) [AND] and aged = 45 [AND] and = 70 years. Males are aged = 40 years [AND] and = 70 years. Patients should have suitable veins for cannulation [OR] or repeated venipuncture.
Patients are diagnosed with T2DM for at least the last 6 months.
Patients are on [NOT] no other anti-diabetic drug treatment, [OR] or on stable maximum 3000 mg daily dose metformin treatment [OR] and/or on stable dose of a DPPIV inhibitor treatment for at least the last 3 months5. HbA1c levels =6.0% (=42 mmol/mol) [AND] and =9.0% (75 mmol/mol).
Have a body mass index (BMI) = 35 kg/m2.

Exclusion Criteria:
Involvement in the planning and conduct of the study (applies to both AstraZeneca staff and staff at third party vendor or at the investigational sites).
Previous enrolment in the present study [OR] or participation in another clinical study with an investigational product during the last 3 months [OR] or as judged by the Investigator.
History [OR] of or presence of any clinically significant disease [OR] or disorder including a recent (< 3 months) cardiovascular event which, in the opinion of the Investigator, may either put the patient at risk because of participation in the study [OR] or influence the results [OR] or the patient's ability to participate in the study.
Clinical diagnosis of Type 1 diabetes, [OR] maturity onset diabetes of the young, [OR] secondary diabetes [OR] or diabetes insipidus.
Unstable/ [OR] rapidly progressing renal disease [OR] or estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate < 60 mL/min (Cockcroft-Gault formula).
Clinically significant out of range values of serum levels of either alanine aminotransferase (ALT), [OR] aspartate aminotransferase (AST) [OR] or alkaline phosphatase (ALP) in the Investigator's opinion.
Contraindications to dapagliflozin according to the local label.
Use of antidiabetic drugs other than metformin within 3 months prior to screening.
Weight gain [OR] or loss > 5 kg in the last 3 months, ongoing weight-loss diet (hypocaloric diet) [OR] or use of weight loss agents.
History of drug abuse [OR] or alcohol abuse in the past 12 months.
Any clinically significant abnormalities in clinical chemistry, [OR] hematology [OR] or urinalysis [OR] or other condition the Investigator believes would interfere with the patient's ability to provide informed consent, comply with study instructions, [OR] or which might confound the interpretation of the study results [OR] or put the patient at undue risk.
Plasma donation within one month of screening [OR] or any blood donation/ [OR] blood loss > 500 mL within 3 months prior to screening [OR] or during the study.
Anemia defined as Hemoglobin (Hb) < 115 g/L (7.1 mM) in women [AND] and < 120 g/L (7.5 mM) in men.
Use of anti-coagulant treatment such as heparin, [OR] warfarin, [OR] platelet inhibitors, [OR] thrombin [OR] and factor X inhibitors.
Use of medication such as oral glucocorticoids, [OR] anti-estrogens [OR] or other medications that are known to markedly influence insulin sensitivity.
Use of loop diuretics.
Regular smoking [OR] and other regular nicotine use.
Central nervous system aneurysm clip
Implanted neural stimulator
 Implanted cardiac pacemaker [OR] of defibrillator
Cochlear implant
Metal containing corpora aliena in the eye [OR] or brain.
Patients, who do [NOT] not want to be informed about unexpected medical findings, [OR] or do [NOT] not wish that their physician be informed about coincidental findings, cannot participate in the study.
