Inclusion Criteria:
Men [OR] or women aged 18 years or older
Patients presenting for CMR with the clinical diagnosis of hypertrophic cardiomyopathy based on left ventricular wall thickness of at least =15 mm in the [NOT] absence of any other cardiac [OR] or systemic cause of hypertrophy
Patients presenting for CMR with the clinical diagnosis of idiopathic dilated cardiomyopathy based upon left ventricular ejection fraction =40%, LV end-diastolic diameter =55 mm [OR] or left ventricular end-systolic diameter =45 mm, [AND] and the [NOT] absence of coronary stenoses on angiography.
Patients presenting for CMR evaluation of chest pain [AND] but [NOT] without evidence of obstructive coronary artery disease either by coronary angiography [OR] or stress testing.

Exclusion Criteria:
Decompensated heart failure [OR] or hemodynamic instability
Prior coronary revascularization (PCI [OR] or CABG) [OR] or myocardial infarction (as evidenced by previously elevated CPK-MB [OR] or troponin levels)
Accelerating angina [OR] or unstable angina
Inability to physically tolerate MRI [OR] or implanted objects that are MRI incompatible
Inability to provide written informed consent obtained at time of study enrollment.
Severe claustrophobia
Advanced heart block [OR] or sinus node dysfunction
Hypersensitivity [OR] or allergic reaction to regadenoson [OR] or adenosine
Hypotension
Active bronchospasm [OR] or history of hospitalization due to bronchospasm
History of seizures
Recent cerebrovascular accident
Use of dipyridamole within the last 5 days
Contraindication to aminophylline
Severe renal insufficiency [AND] with estimated glomerular filtration rate <30 ml/min/ 1.73 m2
Pregnant [OR] or nursing
