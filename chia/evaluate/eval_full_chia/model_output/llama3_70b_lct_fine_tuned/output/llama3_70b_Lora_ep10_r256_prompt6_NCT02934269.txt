Inclusion Criteria:
Healthy male [OR] and/or female subjects between the ages of 18 and 55 years, [AND] and a body mass index (BMI) of ≥ 18 [AND] and ≤ 33 kg/m2 [AND] with body weight ≥ 50 [AND] and ≤ 90 kg at screening. 
Females must have been surgically sterilized (hysterectomy, [OR] bilateral oophorectomy, [OR] or bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy; proper documentation required) at least 6 months before screening, [OR] or be postmenopausal (defined as 24 consecutive months [NOT] without menses before screening, [AND] with a follicle-stimulating hormone [FSH] level of > 40 IU/L at screening). 

Exclusion Criteria:
Exposure/ [OR] treatment to an investigational (new chemical entity) [OR] or marketed drug [OR] or biologic within 30 days preceding the first dose administration, [OR] or five half-lives of that investigational drug [OR] or biologic, if known (whichever is longer). 
Donation blood [OR] or serum within 8 weeks before the first dose administration to a blood bank [OR] or blood donation center. 
History of alcohol [OR] or drug abuse (as defined by the current version of the DSM) within 2 years before the first dose administration, [OR] or positive alcohol [OR] or drug screen. 
Vaccination within 30 days prior to the first dose administration [OR] or has plans to receive a vaccination during the course of the study (including the follow phone call on Day 105).
