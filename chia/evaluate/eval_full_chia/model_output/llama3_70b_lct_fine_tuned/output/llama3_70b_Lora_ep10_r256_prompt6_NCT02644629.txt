Inclusion Criteria:
Age 18-65
Diagnosis of MDD (Major Depressive Disorder), made or affirmed by a senior psychiatrist in Shalvata
MADRS score > 20
Treated with conventional anti-depressant, administered within a formal psychiatric clinic [OR] or by a certified psychiatrist.

Exclusion Criteria:
Active [OR] or past psychotic disorder, including a history of psychotic affective state
Mental Retardation [OR] or Autistic Spectrum Disorder
Prominent personality disorder
Cardiac [OR] or neurologic active medical condition, including past CVA/ [OR] TIA (Cardiovascular Accident/ [OR] Transient Ischemic Attack) or any other unstable medical condition.
Chronic nasal congestion
Active [OR] or recent drug [OR] or alcohol abuse
Substantial suicidality in a patient requiring admission [AND] but refuses to do so, and signs an "against medical advice" release form as part of clinical evaluation, [AND] and does [NOT] not answer the terms for involuntary admission.
