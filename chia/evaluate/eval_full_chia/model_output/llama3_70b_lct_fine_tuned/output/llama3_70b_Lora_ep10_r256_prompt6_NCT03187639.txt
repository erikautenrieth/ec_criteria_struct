Inclusion Criteria:
Aged over 18
Primary symptom of chest pain
 [NOT] No contraindication to CTA
Willing and able to provide written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Atrial fibrillation [OR] of new onset [OR] or when rate control has been difficult
Known bigemini/ [OR] trigeminy
 Prior CABG surgery
Allergic to contrast
Advanced renal impairment
Significant valve disease (severe aortic stenosis [OR] or regurgitation; severe mitral regurgitation)
Life expectancy <12 months
Inclusion in another trial [NOT] without prior agreement with CI
