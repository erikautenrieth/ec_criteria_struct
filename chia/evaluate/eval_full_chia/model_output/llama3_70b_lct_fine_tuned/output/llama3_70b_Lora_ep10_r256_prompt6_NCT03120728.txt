Inclusion Criteria:
Healthy, women ages 18 to 39yo [AND] with BMI <30
Regular menstrual cycles [AND] with duration between 24-35 days
Completion of screening visit where ovulation will be assessed [AND] with blood draw for progesterone level (must be 5ng/mL or greater)
 [NOT] Not seeking pregnancy during the study period
Use of a [NOT] non-hormonal form of contraception, such as: sterilization (tubal ligation, [OR] Essure), copper IUD (intrauterine device), [OR] barrier methods [OR] or abstinence
Must speak English [OR] or Spanish

Exclusion Criteria:
Currently pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding
Severe pelvic organ prolapse [OR] or prolapse to any degree that may [NOT] prevent retention of the vaginal ring after insertion
Use of oral contraceptive pills, [OR] patches, [OR] implants [OR] or hormonal intrauterine contraception in the month prior to screening
Use of depo medroxyprogesterone within 6 months of screening
Use of medications that interact with contraceptive steroid hormones: anti-epileptic medications, [OR] rifampin, [OR] rifabutin, [OR] fosamprenavir, etc
Medical condition [AND] with safety deemed to be category 3 or 4 when using a combined hormonal contraceptive, as determined by the Center for Disease Control Medical Eligibility Criteria: current [OR] or past history of breast cancer, severe decompensated cirrhosis, history of deep vein thrombosis [OR] or pulmonary embolus, diabetes [AND] with nephropathy/ [OR] retinopathy/ [OR] neuropathy [OR] or other vascular disease diagnosed more than 20 years ago, current symptomatic gallbladder disease, hypertension, [OR] ischemic heart disease, known thrombogenic mutations, [OR] hepatocellular adenoma, [OR] malignant hepatoma, multiple risk factors for atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, multiple sclerosis [AND] with prolonged immobility, history of peripartum cardiomyopathy, cigarette smoking [AND] and =35yo, history of complicated solid organ transplant, history of stroke, history of superficial venous thrombosis [NOT] not associated with catheter, systemic lupus erythematosus [AND] with positive antiphospholipid antibodies, valvular heart disease complicated by pulmonary hypertension [OR] or atrial fibrillation [OR] or bacterial endocarditis, [OR] and acute viral hepatitis
