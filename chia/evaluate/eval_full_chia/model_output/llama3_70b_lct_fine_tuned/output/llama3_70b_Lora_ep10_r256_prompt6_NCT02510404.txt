Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Diagnosis of primary immunodeficiency [AND] with established plan to undergo myeloablative [OR] or [NOT] non-myeloablative allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplant for treatment thereof [OR] or diagnosis of a form of primary immunodeficiency [AND] for which hematopoietic stem cell transplantation is [NOT] not indicated. 
 2. Active infection with EBV, [OR] CMV, [OR] and/or Adenovirus, [NOT] unable to be successfully controlled with standard therapy. 
 3. Steroids less than 0.5 mg/kg/day prednisone 
 4. Karnofsky/ [OR] Lansky score of ≥ 50 
 5. ANC greater than 500/µL. 
 6. Bilirubin <2x, AST <3x, Serum creatinine <2x upper limit of normal, [AND] Hgb >8.0 
 7. Pulse oximetry of > 90% on room air 
 8. Negative pregnancy test (if female [AND] of childbearing potential) 
 9. Patient or parent/guardian capable of providing informed consent. 

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Patients with other uncontrolled infections (see 2.3.2 for definitions) 
 2. Patients [AND] who received ATG, [OR] Campath, [OR] or other T cell immunosuppressive monoclonal antibodies in the last 28 days 
 3. Received donor lymphocyte infusion in last 28 days 
 4. Diagnosis of Omenn's syndrome [OR] or MHC class I deficiency 
 5. Active [AND] and uncontrolled malignancy 
 6. Pregnant [OR] or lactating 
 7. [NOT] Unable to wean steroids to ≤0.5 mg/kg/day prednisone. 
 8. Patients with Grade 3 hyperbilirubinemia
