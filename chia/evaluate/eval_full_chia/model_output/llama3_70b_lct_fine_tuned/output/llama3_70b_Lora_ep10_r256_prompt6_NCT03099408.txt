Inclusion Criteria:
Women be at least 18 years of age
Have symptoms of vaginal odor [OR] and or/ [OR] discharge
Meet the clinical (Amsel) criteria for BV
Willing to participate in research

Exclusion Criteria:
Presence of another vaginal infection [OR] or STD
Allergy to metronidazole
Pregnant [OR] or nursing
Use of oral [OR] or intravaginal antibiotics within the past 2 weeks
HIV [OR] or other chronic disease
Inability to keep return appointments
Contraindications for Lactobacillus Vaginal Suppositories(those [NOT] without sexual history)
