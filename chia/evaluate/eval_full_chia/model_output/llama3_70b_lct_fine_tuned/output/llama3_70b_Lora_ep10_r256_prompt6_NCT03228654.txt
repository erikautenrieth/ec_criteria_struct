Inclusion Criteria:
uterine size <12 weeks.
presence of benign cause for the hysterectomy e.g. fibroid uterus, [OR] perimenopausal beeding [NOT] not responding to medical treatment [OR] or complex endometrial hyperplasia [NOT] without atypia.
[NOT] Absence of significant scarring in the pelvis from previous surgeries.

Exclusion Criteria:
Suspected [OR] or known gynecological malignancy.
uterine size >12 weeks.
Endometriosis
Presence of adnexal mass.
cervix flushed with the vagina.
presence of significant scarring in the pelvic area from previous surgery.
