Inclusion Criteria:
Patients aged =50 years [AND] with DM2 [AND] and symptomatic PAD diagnosed clinically (according to Fontaine criteria, stage IIa [OR] or IIb [OR] and III) [AND] and by measuring the <U+0391><U+0392><U+0399>.

Exclusion Criteria:
Congestive heart failure, history of ventricular tachycardia, [OR] ventricular fibrillation [OR] or multifocal ventricular extrasystoles [OR] or QTc prolongation.
Patients with atrial fibrillation taking any anticoagulant therapy [OR] or patients with a history of cardioembolic ischemic stroke [OR] or hemorrhagic stroke.
Patients with a history (= 12 months) of acute coronary syndrome receiving dual antiplatelet therapy, [OR] or patients receiving monotherapy with aspirin.
Patients with hepatic impairment (child-Pugh staging, calibration = 5) [OR] or renal impairment (creatinine clearance = 30ml / min), recent peptic ulcer, a history of hypersensitivity to cilostazol, [OR] cancer patients undergoing treatment.
