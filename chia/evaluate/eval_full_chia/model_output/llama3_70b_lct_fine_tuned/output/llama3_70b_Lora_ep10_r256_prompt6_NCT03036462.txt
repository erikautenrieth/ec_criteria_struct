Inclusion Criteria:
Patients aged at least 18 years
Patients with chronic heart failure present for at least 12 months
Confirmed presence of iron deficiency
Serum haemoglobin of 9.5 to 14.0 g/dL

Exclusion Criteria:
Hypersensitivity to the active substance, to FCM [OR] or any of its excipients
Known serious hypersensitivity to other parenteral iron products
 Anaemia [NOT] not attributed to iron deficiency, e.g. other microcytic anaemia
Evidence of iron overload [OR] or disturbances in the utilisation of iron
