Inclusion Criteria:
Clinical [OR] and radiologic diagnosis of primary knee osteoarthritis (Kellgren & Lawrence I, II or III); 
Capability to understand the Informed Consent Form; 
Chronic pain  for at least 3 months prior to inclusion, measured by VAS. (VAS 4 or above); 
[NOT] Absence of skin injures, [OR] infections [OR] or tumor in the target knee; 
Availability to comply with the visits. 

Exclusion Criteria:
History of spinal cord stenosis [OR] or clinical symptoms of lumbar radiculopathy; 
History [OR] or onset neurological diseases; 
Generalized pain [OR] or fibromyalgia; 
 [NOT] Inability to walk; 
History of knee surgery in the target knee; 
Secondary causes of osteoarthritis; 
Use of statins [OR] and quinolones in the previous year; 
Uncontrolled [OR] and ongoing psychiatric diseases; 
Invasive knee treatments [AND] with hyaluronic acid infusion, [OR] corticosteroids [OR] and anaesthetics, in the target knee, up to 6 months previous to study inclusion.
