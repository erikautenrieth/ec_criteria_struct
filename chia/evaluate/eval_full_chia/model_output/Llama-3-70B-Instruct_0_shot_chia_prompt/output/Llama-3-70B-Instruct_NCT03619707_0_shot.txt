<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Normal uterine cavity [AND] Normal Hormonal investigation: TSH [OR] PRL [OR] FBS
Frozen embryo transfer cycles: at least 2 embryos
Primary [OR] secondary infertility: tubal occlusion [OR] male factor [OR] unexplained [OR] endometriosis [OR] ovarian factors…
Body mass index (BMI) =18 [AND] =30 kg/m2

Exclusion Criteria:
Preexisting untreated medical condition [AND] (thyroid disease [OR] diabetes mellitus [OR] hypertension [OR] pulmonary conditions [OR] cardiac condition…)
History of [AND] three or more consecutively failed In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) cycles after embryo transfer
History of [AND] three or more miscarriages
Previous allergy reactions [AND] to progesterone products [NOT] treated
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
