Inclusion Criteria:
Male [OR] female
Age 18 [AND] to 65 years
Diagnosed [AND] with spinal cord injury between 3 days [AND] 4 weeks
American Spinal Injury Association Impairment Scale A [OR] B
Informed consent [AND] for inclusion into the database is obtained

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] survivable injury
