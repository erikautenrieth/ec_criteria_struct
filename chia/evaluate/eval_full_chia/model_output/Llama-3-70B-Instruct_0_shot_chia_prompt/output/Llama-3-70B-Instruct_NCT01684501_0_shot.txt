Inclusion Criteria:
weigh more than 200 lbs [AND] are high level ambulators corresponding to levels E [OR] F of the Special Interest Group of Amputee Medicine (SIGAM) mobility grade [AND] have the ability to follow multi-step commands.

Exclusion Criteria:
score level D on the SIGAM mobility grade [OR] have experienced 1 [OR] more falls in the last month before the study [OR] have a residual limb length which does [NOT] allow for seven inches clearance of bracket attachment for the PowerFoot [OR] the residual limb must be stable in volume ([NOT] having a change in socket [OR] socket padding in last 6 months) [AND] without pain that limits function [OR] the sound-side (contralateral) lower extremity must be [NOT] free of impediments that affect gait, range of motion, [OR] limb muscle activity [OR] Any diagnosed cardiovascular, pulmonary, neurological, [OR] orthopedic conditions that would interfere with subject participation
