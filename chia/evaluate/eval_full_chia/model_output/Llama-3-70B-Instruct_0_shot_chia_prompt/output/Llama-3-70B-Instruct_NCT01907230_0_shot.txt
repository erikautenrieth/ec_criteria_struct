Inclusion Criteria:
Age : from 20 [AND] to 90 y/o.
HBsAg-positive for more than 6 months [AND] HBV DNA < 2000 IU/ml (Subgroup 1) [OR] HBsAg-negative but anti-HBc positive [AND] HBV DNA < 2000 IU/ml (Subgroup 2).
Inflammatory arthritis patients who plan to treat [AND] biological agents, including Humira [OR] Enbrel [OR] Simponi [OR] Orencia [OR] Mabthera [OR] Actemra; [AND] as first line biologic treatment is indicated.

Exclusion Criteria:
HCV [OR] HIV [OR] HDV coinfection.
HCC [OR] other malignancy within 3 years.
Decompensated liver cirrhosis (CTP score = 7).
Uremia patients under hemodialysis [OR] continuous ambulatory peritoneal dialysis [OR] patients with Ccr < 50 mL/min
Pregnant [OR] breastfeeding women.
Women of child-bearing potential (WOCBP) who are [NOT] willing [OR] unable to use an acceptable method of contraception to avoid pregnancy throughout the study [AND] for up to 4 weeks after the last dose of study drug.
