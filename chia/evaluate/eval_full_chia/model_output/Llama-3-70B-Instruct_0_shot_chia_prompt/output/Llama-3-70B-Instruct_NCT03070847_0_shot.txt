<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
age > 18 y.o. [AND] American Society of Anesthesiologists Physical Status Classification (ASA) 1-2 [AND] signed informed consent form [AND] after reading the information about the study [AND] talking with one of the investigators

Exclusion Criteria:
pregnancy [OR] known allergies [AND] for tranexamic acid [OR] any other substance in Exacyl [OR] deep vein thrombosis [OR] Hormone Replacement Therapy [OR] oral contraceptive usage [OR] anticoagulants usage [OR] obesity - BMI (body mass index) >30 kg/m2 [OR] renal disease, [AND] as glomerular filtration rate (GFR) <60 ml/min/1,73 m*m [OR] seizures [OR] epilepsy [AND] in the past
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
