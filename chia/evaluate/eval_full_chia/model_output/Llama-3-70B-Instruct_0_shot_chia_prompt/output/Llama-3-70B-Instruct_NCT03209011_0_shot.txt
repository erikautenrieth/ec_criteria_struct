<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
HBsAg [AND] HBeAg positive for more than 6 months, HBV DNA detectable [AND] ALT level abnormal lasted for three months [AND] at least 190 IU/L [OR] liver puncture biopsy demonstrated apparent inflammation, [NOT] treated before enrolled.

Exclusion Criteria:
Active consumption of alcohol [OR] drugs
Co-infection [AND] human immunodeficiency virus, hepatitis C virus, [OR] hepatitis D virus
History of autoimmune hepatitis [AND]
Psychiatric disease
Evidence of neoplastic diseases of the liver
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
