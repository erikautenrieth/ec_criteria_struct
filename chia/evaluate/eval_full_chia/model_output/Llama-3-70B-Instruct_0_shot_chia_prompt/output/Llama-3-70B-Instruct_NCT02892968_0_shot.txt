<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
At the cluster level, ED physicians practicing [AND] at a participating site will be eligible. 
At the patient level, all hip fractures seen [AND] by a participating ED physician will be eligible 

Exclusion Criteria:
ED physicians who work casually (less than 0.25 Full Time Equivalent) 
ED Physicians who are routinely using U/S guided RA [OR] decline participation in the trial. 
Patients' age less than 65 years; 
Patients who are delirious [AND] on initial assessment by ED physician [OR] severe dementia 
Patients with communication problems ([AND] critically ill, [OR] unconscious, [OR] language barrier despite use of secure telephone-based translation service) 
Patients with allergies [AND] to narcotics [OR] local anesthetic; [OR] anticoagulant use (e.g. warfarin, [OR] dabigatran, [OR] rivaroxaban). 
Patients with hip fractures [NOT] requiring surgery (e.g. greater trochanter avulsion) will also be excluded.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
