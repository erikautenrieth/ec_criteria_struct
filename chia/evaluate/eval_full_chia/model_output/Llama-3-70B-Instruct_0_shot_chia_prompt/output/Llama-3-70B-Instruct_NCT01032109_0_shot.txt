<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
[AND] choroidal neovascularization caused by age-related macula degeneration 
[NOT] previous treatment 
[AND] a follow-up at least 12 months 
[AND] a baseline visual acuity ranging from a letter score of 0 [OR] 70 on the Early Treatment Diabetic Retinopathy Study chart 

Exclusion Criteria:
choroidal neovascularization caused by [NOT] age-related macula degeneration [OR] other eye diseases 
ocular surgery within the past 3 mouths [AND] history of uveitis 
history of uveitis [AND] 
intraocular pressure higher than 25 mmHg [OR] glaucoma 
history of systemic [OR] ocular thromboembolic events.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
