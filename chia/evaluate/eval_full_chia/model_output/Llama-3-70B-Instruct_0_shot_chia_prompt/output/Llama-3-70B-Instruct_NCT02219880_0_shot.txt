Inclusion Criteria:
Aged between 18-70 years
Meets the Diagnostic [AND] Statistical Manual (DSM) IV [AND] DSM-V diagnostic criteria for generalised anxiety disorder (GAD) based on structured interview (Mini International Neuropsychiatric Interview-Plus 6 [MINI-Plus 6]. Note that while the MINI-Plus 6 uses the DSM-IV criteria, the same criteria are used in the DSM-V).
Presents [AND] with anxiety (Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale = 18) at the time of study entry
Fluent [AND] in written [AND] spoken English
Provides a signed copy of the consent form
[NOT] Primary diagnosis other than GAD
[NOT] Presentation of moderate [OR] severe depressive symptoms (Montgomery-Asberg Rating Scale: MADRS = 18 at time of study entry [OR] = 24 at any time during study)
[NOT] Presentation of suicidal ideation (= 3 on MADRS suicidal thoughts domain at time of study entry [OR] at any time during study)
[NOT] Current diagnosis of bipolar disorder [OR] schizophrenia on structured interview (MINI Plus)
[NOT] Current substance/alcohol use disorder on structured interview (MINI Plus)
[NOT] Currently taking an antidepressant, mood stabiliser, antipsychotic, anticonvulsant, warfarin [OR] thyroxin, [OR] current regular use (more than 2 days per week) of a benzodiazepine [OR] opioid-based analgesic
[NOT] Current use of a psychotropic nutraceutical (e.g. St John's wort)
[NOT] Previous intolerance to kava
[NOT] Three [OR] more failed trials of pharmacotherapy for the current GAD episode
[NOT] Recently commenced psychotherapy (within four weeks of study entry)
[NOT] Known [OR] suspected clinically unstable systemic medical disorder
[NOT] Diagnosed hepato-biliary disease/inflammation
[NOT] Elevated liver enzymes at baseline blood test
[NOT] Pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding, [OR] trying to conceive
[NOT] Not using medically approved contraception (including abstinence) if female [AND] of childbearing age
[NOT] Unable to participate in all scheduled visits, treatment plan, [OR] other trial procedures according to the protocol (except for the optional genetic component)
