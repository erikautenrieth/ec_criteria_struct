<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with osteoarthritis of the hip secondary [OR] to degeneration, inflammatory arthritis, gouty arthritis, acetabular dysplasia [OR] osteonecrosis of the femoral head, [AND] undergoing primary unilateral minimally invasive THA
Age > 18 years [AND] < 90 years
Failure of medical treatment [OR] rehabilitation.
Hemoglobin > 11g/dl,
[NOT] use of non-steroid anti-inflammatory agent one week before operation

Exclusion Criteria:
Preoperative Hemoglobin < 11 g/dl
History of infection [OR] intraarticular fracture of the affective hip
Renal function deficiency (GFR < 30 ml/min/1.73m2)
Elevated liver enzyme (aspartate transaminase (AST) [OR] alanine transaminase(ALT) level are more than twice normal range) [AND] history of liver cirrhosis, [AND] impaired liver function(elevated total bilirubin level) [AND] coagulopathy (including long-term use anticoagulant)
History of deep vein thrombosis, [OR] ischemic heart disease [OR] stroke
Contraindications of tranexamic acid, [OR] floseal, [OR] rivaroxaban
Allergy to tranexamic acid, [OR] floseal, [OR] rivaroxaban, [OR] the excipients
History of heparin-induced thrombocytopenia (HIT)
Coagulopathy [OR] bleeding tendency caused by organ dysfunction, such as cirrhosis, [OR] bone marrow suppression etc.
Patient who have active bleeding disorder, such as intracranial hemorrhage, [OR] upper gastrointestinal bleeding, [OR] hematuria.
Patients [AND] with known allergies to materials of bovine origin.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
