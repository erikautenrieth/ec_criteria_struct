<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
ASA I-III patients scheduled for elective [AND] one [OR] two level minimally invasive lumbar fusions

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] requiring surgery for neoplastic processes
[NOT] Allergy to acetaminophen
Liver dysfunction [AND] elevated Liver Function Tests (LFTs)
Alcohol [OR] drug dependency
Mental retardation
[NOT] Less than 50 kg of weight
[NOT] Pregnant women
Patients [AND] requiring long-acting opioid pain management (including fentanyl patch, oxycontin, etc) for over 3 weeks immediately prior to surgery
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
