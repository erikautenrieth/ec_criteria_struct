<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
normotensive
forced expiratory volume in 1s : forced vital capacity ratio > 0.75
[NOT] medical history of cardiovascular [AND] respiratory disease
[NOT] taking medications [OTHER THAN] oral contraceptives
[NOT] free from sleep apnea
body mass index less than 30 kg/m2

Exclusion Criteria:
history of hypertension [AND]
known impaired renal function
liver disease
heart failure
myocardial infarction
coronary artery disease
smoked [WITHIN] the past year
apnea hypopnea index > 5 events per hour
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
