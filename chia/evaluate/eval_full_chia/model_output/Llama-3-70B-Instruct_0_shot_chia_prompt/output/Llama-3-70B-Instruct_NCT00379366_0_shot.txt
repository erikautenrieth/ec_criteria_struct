Inclusion Criteria:
[AND] over 18 years 
[AND] successful angioplasty (residual stenosis < 30%) [AND] on a significant stenosis (maximal systolic speed 3 times > from basal maximal systolic speed, stenosis > 70% on angiography) [OR] on the venous-prosthesis anastomosis [OR] on the venous segment 5 cm after the anastomosis of a prosthetic haemodialysis vascular access (at least 1 month old) 
[AND] social security affiliation 
[AND] signed informed consent 

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] contra-indications of radiotherapy 
[NOT] angioplasty [AND] with stenting
