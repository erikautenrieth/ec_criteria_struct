<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients presenting for elective posterior spinal fusion surgery [AND] (lower thoracic, lumbar, sacral)
Ages 18 [AND] 80

Exclusion Criteria:
Preoperative renal failure [AND] (defined as a serum creatinine > 2.0 mg/dL.)
American Society of Anesthesiologists Physical Status [OR] IV [OR] V
Pulmonary disease [AND] necessitating home oxygen therapy
[NOT] Allergy to methadone [OR] hydromorphone [OR] ketamine
Preoperative recent history of opioid [OR] alcohol abuse
Significant liver disease
[NOT] Inability to use a PCA device [OR] speak the English language
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
