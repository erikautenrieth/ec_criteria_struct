<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Elective open abdominal hysterectomy [AND] with midline incision, age > 18 years [AND] American Society of Anesthesiologist classification score (ASA classification) [AND] 1-3.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patient [AND] with a chronic pain condition, major unexpected surgical complication, unexpected prolonged intubation, patient refusal, local anesthetic allergy, any contraindication [AND] to regional anesthesia, greater than 2 attempts [AND] by resident [AND] greater than 1 attempt [AND] by staff anesthesiologist [AND] for TAP block.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
