<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosed Iron deficiency anemia.
H-pylori positive cases.
Second trimester pregnancy.

Exclusion Criteria:
Severe Iron deficiency anemia ([AND] hemoglobin < 8.0 g/dL).
Parasitic worm infection [OR] e.g. schistosomiasis [OR] hook worm by stool analysis.
Any cases giving clinical symptoms of gastritis [AND] e.g. nausea [OR] vomiting [OR] dull aching pain [OR] soreness in the epigastrium.
Cases [AND] history of gastric ulcer diagnosed by upper endoscopy.
Cases [AND] complaining of hematemesis.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
