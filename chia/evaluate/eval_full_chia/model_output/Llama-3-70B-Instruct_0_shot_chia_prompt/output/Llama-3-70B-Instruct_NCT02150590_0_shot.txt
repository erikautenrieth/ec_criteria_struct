<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) [AND] GOLD grade 2-3
residents [AND] at low altitude (<800 m)

Exclusion Criteria:
unstable condition [OR] COPD exacerbation
mild (GOLD 1) [OR] very severe COPD (GOLD 4)
requirement [AND] for oxygen therapy [AND] at low altitude residence
hypoventilation
pulmonary hypertension
more than mild [OR] unstable cardiovascular disease
use [AND] of drugs [AND] that affect respiratory center drive
internal, neurologic [OR] psychiatric disease [AND] that interfere [AND] with protocol compliance [AND] including current heavy smoking (>20 cigarettes per day) [OR] inability [NOT] to perform 6 min walk test.
previous intolerance [NOT] to moderate altitude (<2600m).
exposure [NOT] to altitudes >1500m [AND] for >2 days [AND] within the last 4 weeks [AND] before the study.
pregnant [OR] nursing patients
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
