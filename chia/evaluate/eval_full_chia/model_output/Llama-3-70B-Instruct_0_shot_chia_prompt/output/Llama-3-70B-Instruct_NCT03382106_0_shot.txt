Inclusion Criteria:
Between the age of 25 [AND] 65 at baseline
Be willing [AND] to participate in a smoking cessation program

>5 pack-year history of smoking [AND]
Global Initiative for Chronic Obstructive Lung Disease (GOLD) 0: FEV1=0.80 [AND] FEV1/FVC>0.70 Forced Expiratory Volume in 1 second (FEV1), Forced Vital Capacity (FVC)

Women only: Cannot be pregnant [OR] nursing at baseline [OR] plan to become pregnant during the course of the study [NOT]
Body Mass Index (BMI) > 32 [NOT]
Weight > 220 pounds [NOT]
Allergies [AND] to shell fish, seafood, eggs [OR] iodine [NOT]
Heart disease, kidney disease [OR] diabetes [NOT]
Diagnosis of asthma [NOT]
Any metal in [OR] on the body (that cannot be removed) between the nose [AND] the abdomen [NOT]
Any major organ system disease (by judgment of the study medical team) [NOT]
A glomerular filtration rate of 60 cc per minute or less [NOT]
Nitroglycerin usage [OR] nitrates [AND] use of phosphodiesterase 5 (PDE5) inhibitors [NOT]
