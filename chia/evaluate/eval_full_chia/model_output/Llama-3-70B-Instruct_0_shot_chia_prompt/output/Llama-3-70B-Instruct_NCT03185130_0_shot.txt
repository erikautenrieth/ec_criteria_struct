<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Age [AND] 10 to 65 years
Temperature [AND] less than 100.4 F
Normal neurologic exam [AND] normal mental status

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant
Meningeal signs [AND] are present
Acute angle closure glaucoma [AND] is suspected
Head trauma [AND] within the previous two weeks
Lumbar puncture [AND] within the previous two weeks
Thunderclap onset [AND] of the headache
Known allergy [AND] to one of the study drugs
History of intracranial hypertension[AND]
Is [AND] a prisoner
Patient declined [AND] informed consent
Non-English speaking patient [OR] parent/guardian for pediatric patients
Attending provider excludes patient
Severe Dehydration
[NOT] Temperature less than 100.4 F
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
