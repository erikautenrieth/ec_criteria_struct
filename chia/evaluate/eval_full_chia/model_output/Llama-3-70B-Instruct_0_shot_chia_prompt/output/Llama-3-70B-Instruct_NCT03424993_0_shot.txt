<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Habitual dietary sodium intake > 3400mg per day

Exclusion Criteria:
[AND] Abnormal resting ECG
[AND] Current abnormal blood panel (assessed [AND] comprehensive metabolic panel, [OR] lipid panel [AND] complete blood count).
[AND] Hypertension ([AND] currently taking anti-hypertensive medications [OR] resting blood pressure >140/90 mmHg)
[AND] Medical history of cardiovascular disease, [OR] malignant cancer, [OR] diabetes [OR] kidney disease
[AND] Obesity ([AND] Body Mass Index > 30)
[AND] Current pregnancy
[AND] [NOT] able to provide consent
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
