<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Cytologically proven acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL) [AND] no relapse of a previously unrecognized ALL
Patients must meet one of the following risk criteria:
Standard-risk (SR) group meeting all of the following criteria:
Blasts < 1,000/µL in peripheral blood (PB) on day 8 [AND] aged 1 to < 6 years [AND] initial WBC < 20,000/µL [AND] M1 (5%) [OR] M2 (= 5% to < 25%) blasts in bone marrow on day 15; [AND] M1 marrow on day 33.
Aged < 1 [OR] = 6 years [AND] WBC = 20,000/µL [AND] blasts < 1,000/µL in PB on day 8 [AND] M1 [OR] M2 marrow on day 15 [AND] M3 (= 25%) marrow on day 15 [OR] meets SR criteria but M3 marrow on day 15 [AND] *M1 marrow on day 33.
Meets IR criteria [AND] M3 marrow on day 15 [NOT] SR [AND] M3 on day 15 [AND] blasts = 1,000/µL in PB on day 8 [AND] M2 [OR] M3 marrow on day 33 [AND] translocation t(9;22) [BCR/ABL+] (Philadelphia chromosome-positive) [OR] t(4;11) [MLL/AF4+].

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Down syndrome
[NOT] other major disease that prohibits study treatment (e.g., severe congenital heart disease)
[NOT] requiring significant therapy modification owing to study therapy associated complications
[NOT] complications due to other interventions
[NOT] one with missing data that are needed for the differential diagnosis, [OR] for selection of the proper therapy arm
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
