<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Non-ST segment elevation acute coronary syndrome

Exclusion Criteria:
Left main disease
Known [AND] hypersensitivity [OR] contraindication to any of the following medications: Heparin [OR] aspirin [OR] clopidogrel [OR] sirolimus [OR] siptagliptin [OR] statin
Congestive heart failure ([AND] patients with LVEF <30% [OR] cardiogenic shock)
Uncontrolled myocardial ischemia ([AND] repeated chest pain [OR] dyspnea after revascularization)
Uncontrolled ventricular arrhythmia
History of malignancy [AND] chemotherapy
Serious hematologic disease ([AND] e.g. CML [OR] MDS)
Current infectious disease [AND] needs antibiotics therapy
Creatinine level >1.5 mg/dL [OR] dependence on dialysis
[NOT] Other severe concurrent illness ([AND] e.g. active infection [OR] malignancy).
Life expectancy of less than one year
Pregnancy [OR] women with potential childbearing
Type I DM
Treatment with insulin
History of pancreatitis
Who [NOT] can read the informed consent form ([AND] e.g. illiteracy [OR] foreigner)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
