<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Systemically healthy adults.
Minimum of 24 permanent teeth [AND] no gingivitis (Community Periodontal Index score = 0) [AND] no periodontitis (Community Periodontal Index score = 0) [AND] [NOT] absence of untreated caries.

Exclusion Criteria:
Smoker [OR] former smoker.
Presence of dental prostheses [OR] presence of orthodontic devices.
Antibiotic treatment [OR] routine use of oral antiseptics in the previous 3 months.
Presence of any systemic disease that could alter the production [OR] composition of saliva.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
