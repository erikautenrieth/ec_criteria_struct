<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
English [OR] Spanish speaking*
Emergency Department patient
Aged 18-80
Have had >4 emergency department visits [AND] within 12 months for 2 consecutive 12-month periods. Period of time can be extended [AND] by up to 6 months if incarcerated [OR] institutionalized for ≥ 6 months.
Meet Diagnostic [AND] Statistical Manual version IV (DSM-IV) criteria for alcohol dependence [OR] & DSM-V criteria for alcohol use disorder, severe.
Have ≥2 days/week of heavy drinking (>4 drinks/day)
Capable of giving informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Active opioid dependence
[NOT] Acute [OR] chronic pain requiring opioid treatment
[NOT] Acute liver injury (liver aminotransferase concentrations >5 times the upper limit of normal)
[NOT] Health condition considered unsafe for inclusion (at discretion of PI [AND] / [OR] attending physician)
[NOT] Lack of capacity [OR] willingness to consent
[NOT] Currently prescribed pharmacotherapy for alcohol dependence ( [NOT] including treatment of acute alcohol withdrawal syndrome)
[NOT] Previous significant adverse reaction to naltrexone [OR] diluent
[NOT] Pregnant, nursing, [OR] not using effective methods of birth control
[NOT] Prisoners (as defined by Office of Human Research Protection) at the time of enrollment ARE [NOT] ELIGIBLE for study entry. However, subjects who become prisoners after being enrolled will be included [AND] not be withdrawn from the study. Patients on parole [OR] probation are eligible for enrollment.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
