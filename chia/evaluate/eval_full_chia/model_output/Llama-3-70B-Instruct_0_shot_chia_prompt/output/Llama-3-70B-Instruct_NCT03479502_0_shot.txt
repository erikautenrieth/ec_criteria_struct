<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
18 years of age [AND] older,
diagnosis of stage II adhesive capsulitis as determined by clinical examination of the treating physician, [AND]
absence of abnormal findings on X-ray.

Exclusion Criteria:
allergy to Doxycycline [OR] Methylprednisolone,
pregnancy,
diagnosis,
Inflammatory arthritis [OR] diabetes,
secondary adhesive capsulitis (history of significant trauma [AND] rotator cuff tear injury [OR] stroke)
[NOT] evidence of arthritis on x-ray,
current infectious disease, [AND]
[NOT] any previous treatment for the for adhesive capsulitis of the affected shoulder.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
