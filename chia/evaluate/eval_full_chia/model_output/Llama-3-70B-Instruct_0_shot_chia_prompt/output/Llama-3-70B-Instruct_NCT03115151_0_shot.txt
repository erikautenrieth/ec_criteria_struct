<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Adult subjects aged 18 years [OR] older
Scheduled for elective posterior lumbar spinal fusion surgery between 1 [AND] 3 levels

Exclusion Criteria:
Baseline cognitive deficits sufficient to make objective pain self-assessments unreliable [AND] in the estimation of the Study Investigators.
[NOT] Immunocompromised subject
Coagulopathy
Severe liver [AND] renal dysfunction
Preoperative neurological deficits
The dura damage during surgery
[NOT] Inability to follow directions [OR] comprehend the English language.
Females who are pregnant [AND] determined by positive pregnancy test on [OR] before the day of surgery.
Prisoners.
Patient refusal to provide informed consent.
Allergy to amide local anesthetics (lidocaine, bupivacaine, ropivacaine) [OR] opioid (fentanyl).
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
