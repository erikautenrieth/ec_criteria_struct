<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
biopsy proven NASH [AND] Type 2 DM [AND] HbA1c :>6.5% [AND] BMI < 45kg/m2 [AND] Any anti-diabetic agent [OR] except SGLT2 inhibitors, TZDs(thiazolidinediones) [OR] DPP4(Dipeptidyl peptidase4) inhibitors [OR] GLP1 RAs(Glucagon-like Peptide 1-Receptor Agonists)

Exclusion Criteria:
eGFR <45 ml/min [OR] structural [AND] functional urogenital abnormalities, that predispose [AND] urogenital infections [OR] Investigational product use in the last 6 months [OR] SGLT2 inhibitor, TZD, DPP4 inhibitor [OR] GLP1 RA use within the past 6 months [OR] DKA(Diabetic Ketoacidosis) [OR] HHS(Hyperosmoloar Hyperglycaemic Syndrome) within the last 6 months [OR] Pregnancy [OR] Presence of major contraindications [AND] magnetic resonance imaging (cardiac pacemakers [OR] claustrophobia [OR] foreign bodies [OR] implanted medical devices with ferromagnetic properties) [OR] Liver cirrhosis [OR] Type 1 diabetes [OR] Severe uncorrected insulin insufficiency [OR] Significant alcohol intake [OR] HIV infection [OR] Use of Traditional Chinese Medication [OR] alternative therapies [OR] Coexisting causes of chronic liver disease - chronic viral hepatitis(B [OR] C), autoimmune liver disease, hemochromatosis, Wilson's etc. [OR] Use of medications associated with steatosis eg. Methotrexate [OR] anticonvulsants [OR] antiretroviral therapy etc. [OR] history of stroke [OR] Steroid therapy [OR] Endogenous Cushing's [OR] Familial hypertriglyceridemia [OR] [NOT] absence of any of the above conditions
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
