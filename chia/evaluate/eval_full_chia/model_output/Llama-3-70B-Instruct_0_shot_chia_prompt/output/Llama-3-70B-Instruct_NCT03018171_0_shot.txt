<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Written maternal informed consent
Singleton pregnancy
Gestational age = 37 weeks [AND] BMI < 30 [AND] fetus in cephalic presentation [AND] ASA I

Exclusion Criteria:
Suspect [OR] certainty of fetal malformation [OR] Presence of conditions such as preeclampsia [OR] multiparity [OR] preterm labor [OR] History of adverse reaction to a-2 adrenergic agonists [AND] Nicotine addiction [AND] Chronic use of opioid
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
