Inclusion Criteria:
Accepted for CABG surgery [AND] treatment with Ticagrelor within 48 hours

Exclusion Criteria:
Anticoagulation therapy [OR] prior CABG [OR] active bleeding [OR] at high risk of bleeding [OR] severe liver disease [OR] severe renal disease [OR] hypersensitivity to ticagrelor [OR] history of intracranial hemorrhage
