<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
age > 18 years
critical limb ischemia [AND] (Rutherford class 4-6)
angiographic stenosis > 50% [OR] occlusion of at least one tibial vessel of at least 40mm [AND] for which an interventional treatment is scheduled

Exclusion Criteria:
need for major amputation known [AND] before intervention
[NOT] allergy to Paclitaxel
contraindication [AND] for combined antiplatelet treatment
life expectancy < 1 year
hypersensitivity [OR] contraindication to one of the study drugs
[NOT] lack of consent
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
