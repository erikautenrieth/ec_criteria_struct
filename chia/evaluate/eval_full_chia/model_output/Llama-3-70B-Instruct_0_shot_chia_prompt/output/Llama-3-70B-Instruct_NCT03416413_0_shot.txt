<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Adults [AND] over 18 years of age
Symptomatic GSV [OR] SSV vein reflux > 0.5 seconds on colour Duplex
Varicose vein tributary [AND] requiring treatment

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Current DVT
[NOT] Recurrent varicose veins
Arterial disease [AND] (ABPI<0.8)
Vein diameter < 3mm
Preference [AND] for one of the treatment options
Patient [AND] who are unwilling to participate
[NOT] Inability [OR] unwillingness to complete questionnaires
[NOT] Inability to attend follow-up appointments
Patient [AND] currently included in a study of varicose vein treatment
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
