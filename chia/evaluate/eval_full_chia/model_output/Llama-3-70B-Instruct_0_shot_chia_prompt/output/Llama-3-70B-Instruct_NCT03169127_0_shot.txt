<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Need of lower third molar surgeries

Exclusion Criteria:
[AND] Presence of systemic diseases;
[AND] Presence of local inflammation [OR] infection;
Any history of allergic reaction [AND] to local anesthetics, gastrointestinal bleeding [OR] ulceration;
[AND] Cardiovascular, kidney [OR] hepatic diseases;
Patients who are making use of antidepressants, diuretics [OR] anticoagulants;
[AND] Asthma [AND] allergy to aspirin, ibuprofen [OR] any other nonsteroidal antiinflammatory drug;
[AND] Regular use of any nonsteroidal antiinflammatory drug,
[AND] Pregnancy [OR] breast feeding.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
