<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
[AND] 35-75 years old;
[AND] diagnosed as type 2 diabetes according to the criteria of the World Health Organization in 1999.

Exclusion Criteria:
[OR] type 1 diabetes, [OR] specific types of diabetes, [OR] gestational diabetes [OR] pregestational diabetes;
[AND] acute cardiovascular [OR] cerebrovascular accidents within past 3 months;
[AND] severe hepatic [OR] renal dysfunction;
[AND] malignant tumor;
[AND] allergic history [OR] contraindication for any drugs in trials;
[AND] taking part in other clinical trials;
[AND] obviously poor compliance.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
