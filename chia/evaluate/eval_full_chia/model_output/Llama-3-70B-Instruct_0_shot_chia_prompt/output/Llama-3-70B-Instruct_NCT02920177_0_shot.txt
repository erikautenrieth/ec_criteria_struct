<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with symptomatic FAI
Clinical [AND] radiographic evidence of FAI
Patients [AND] able to provide consent to study participation
Completion [AND] of 6 weeks of physical therapy program

Exclusion Criteria:
Established Osteoarthritis (Kellgren-Lawrence > 3)
Minimum joint space > 2 mm [OR] as measured on AP radiograph
Hip dysplasia (center edge angle < 20° [OR] on AP radiograph)
Patients [AND] with clinically significant cardiovascular, renal, hepatic, endocrine disease, cancer [OR] diabetes
Patients [AND] with ongoing infection [OR] including HIV [OR] Hepatitis
Patient [AND] with history of osteomyelitis/septic arthritis
Anticoagulation therapy
Patients [AND] who are pregnant [OR] breast feeding
Patients [AND] with systemic, rheumatic [OR] inflammatory disease of the knee [OR] chondrocalcinosis, hemochromatosis, inflammatory arthritis, arthropathy of the knee associated with juxta-articular Paget's disease of the femur [OR] tibia, hemophilic arthropathy, infectious arthritis, Charcot's knee joint, villonodular synovitis, [OR] synovial chondromatosis
Patients [AND] taking immunosuppressant medication
Patients [AND] with abnormal hematology [OR] serum chemistry lab results
Patients [AND] receiving injection to treatment knee within 2 months of study enrollment
BMI greater than 35 [OR] less than 20
Patients [NOT] able to provide consent to study participation
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
