<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Histopathological verification of glioblastoma multiforme (GBM: WHO grade IV) [AND] in remission (Group A) [OR] with active disease (Group B). 
CMV-positive GBM [AND] CMV seropositive 
Life expectancy 6 weeks [OR] greater 
Karnofsky/Lansky score 50 [OR] greater 
Patient [OR] parent/guardian capable of providing informed consent 
Bilirubin less than 1.5x upper limit of normal [AND] AST less than 3x upper limit of normal [AND] serum creatinine less than 1.5x normal [AND] Hgb 8.0 g/dL [OR] greater 
Pulse oximetry of 90% [OR] greater on room air 
Sexually active patients must be willing to utilize one of the more effective birth control methods [AND] for 6 months after the CTL infusion. The male partner [AND] should use a condom. 
Patients [AND] should have been off other investigational antineoplastic therapy [AND] for one month prior to entry in this study. 
Informed consent explained to [AND] understood by [AND] signed by patient/guardian. Patient/guardian [AND] given copy of informed consent. 

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Severe intercurrent infection 
[NOT] Known HIV positivity 
[NOT] Pregnant [OR] lactating 
History of hypersensitivity reactions [AND] to murine protein-containing products.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
