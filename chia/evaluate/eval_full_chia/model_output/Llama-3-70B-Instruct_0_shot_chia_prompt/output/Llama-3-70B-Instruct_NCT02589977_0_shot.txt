Inclusion Criteria:
estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) > 60 ml/min
preserved left ventricular ejection fraction (>= 50%) [AND] on echocardiography
HEALTHY: normal cardiac structure [AND] function [AND] on echocardiography, BP < 140/90

Exclusion Criteria:
Exclusion Criteria: coronary artery disease [OR] diabetes mellitus [OR] contraindications to cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (CMR) [OR] weight >350 lbs [OR] [NOT] ability to lie flat for imaging [OR] anemia [OR] contraindications to regadenoson [OR] aminophylline
HEALTHY: [NOT] known cardiovascular disease [OR] cardiac risk factors [OR] use of cardiac medications
HYPERTENSIVE: [NOT] known cardiovascular disease [OR] risk factors aside from hypertension [OR] use of cardiac medications
HFpEF: [NOT] prior history of LVEF below 50% [OR] acute decompensated HF [OR] moderate [OR] greater valvular disease [OR] significant cardiac arrhythmias [OR] pericardial disease [OR] congenital heart disease [OR] primary pulmonary hypertension
