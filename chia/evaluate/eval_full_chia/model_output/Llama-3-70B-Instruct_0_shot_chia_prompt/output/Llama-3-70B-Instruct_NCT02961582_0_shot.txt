<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
An average defecation frequency (DF) of <3 per week based on a 3-week defecation diary (patient-reported) 
[AND] Meet at least one other criterion of the Rome-IV criteria for idiopathic constipation based on the 3-week defecation diary (1) 
[AND] Refractory to conservative treatment 
[AND] Age: 14-80 years 
[AND] Straining during [OR] =25% of defecations 
[AND] Lumpy [OR] hard stools in [OR] =25% of defecations 
[AND] Sensation of incomplete evacuation for [OR] =25% of defecations 
[AND] Sensation of anorectal obstruction/blockage for [OR] =25% of defecations 
[AND] Manual manoeuvres to facilitate [OR] =25% of defecations

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Obstructed outlet syndrome (objectified by defeacography) 
[NOT] Irritable bowel syndrome (Rome-IV criteria for irritable bowel syndrome) 
[NOT] Congenital [OR] organic bowel pathology 
[NOT] Rectal prolapse 
[NOT] Anatomical limitations preventing placement of an electrode 
[NOT] Skin [AND] perineal disease with risk of infection 
[NOT] Previous large bowel/rectal surgery 
[NOT] Stoma 
[NOT] Coexisting neurological disease 
[NOT] Significant psychological co-morbidity as assessed subjectively by the investigator 
[NOT] Being [OR] attempting to become pregnant during study follow-up
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
