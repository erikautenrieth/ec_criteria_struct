<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
The diagnosis of chronic bronchitis [AND] The diagnosis of community-acquired pneumoniae [OR] FEV1 value = 30-80% [AND] The diagnosis of mild-severe acute exacerbation of chronic bronchitis (AECB) [AND] Oxygen saturation < 90%

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding [OR] Allergy [AND] against to penicillin [OR] cephalosporins [AND] Renal impairment [OR] Active hepatic disease [OR] Antibiotic use [NOT] except study drugs [OR] Immunosuppressive therapy [NOT] before 6 months of study initiation [OR] Use of probenecid like drugs
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
