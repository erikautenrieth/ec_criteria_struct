<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
First single stroke [AND] ischaemic [OR] haemorrhagic responsible [AND] of an hemiplegia
Stroke since less than 2 month
A sufficient [AND] understood
A spasticity : a Tardieu score upper [OR] equal to 2 on at least one of the following muscle-[AND]triceps surae, flexors of fingers, [OR] of wrist [AND] of elbow
A [NOT] free consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Previous [AND] antispastic drugs
Contraindication [AND] for baclofen [OR] toxin
Antecedent [AND] of epileptic seizure
Psychiatric [AND] antecedent
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
