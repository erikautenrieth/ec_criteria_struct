<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Moderate to severe COPD ([AND] post-bronchodilator forced expiratory volume in 1 s (FEV1) 30-79%predicted);
Resting functional residual capacity (FRC) >120% predicted;
Clinically stable [AND] on stable triple therapy [AND] with an ICS/LABA [OR] tiotropium;
Symptomatic: Baseline Dyspnea Index =8 [AND] answer "in the morning" when asked about what time of day their COPD symptoms are worst.

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] A diagnosis of sleep disordered breathing;
[NOT] Nocturnal oxygen therapy.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
