<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Moderate [AND] severe rheumatoid arthritis
Taking methotrexate [AND] without adequate control of symptoms
Have at least one cardiovascular risk factor (eg, current smoker [OR] high blood pressure [OR] high cholesterol levels [OR] diabetes mellitus [OR] history of heart attack [AND] family history of coronary heart disease [OR] extra-articular RA disease)

Exclusion Criteria:
Current [OR] recent infection
Clinically significant laboratory abnormalities
Pregnancy [OR] [NOT] able to avoid pregnancy during the study
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
