Inclusion Criteria:
Provision of written informed consent [AND] (by patient or appropriate designee according to local regulations) prior to any study specific procedures.
Aged 18 years [OR] older, male [OR] female.
History of stable angina pectoris [AND] with angiographic evidence of CAD (diameter stenosis = 50%) [AND] in major, i.e., left main, left anterior descending, left circumflex, [OR] and right coronary arteries.
History of previous myocardial infarction (MI) [AND] history of coronary revascularization, i.e., percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI) [OR] coronary artery bypass graft (CABG), [NOT] including the elective PCI during the index hospitalization
Documented history of type 2 diabetes mellitus.
Post-procedural residual diameter stenosis of the treated lesions < 20% [AND] in patients with stent implantation [OR] < 50% [AND] in those with balloon angioplasty
Post-procedural thrombolysis in myocardial infarction (TIMI) grade 3 flow [AND] in treated vessels
Negative cardiac troponin test [AND] before the index elective PCI.
Taking Clopidogrel 75 mg daily dose [AND] for at least 7 days [OR] taking Clopidogrel 75 mg daily dose [AND] for less than 7 days [AND] but with 300 to 600 mg Clopidogrel loading dose before PCI.
Taking acetylsalicylic acid (ASA) 100 mg daily treatment [AND] for at least 7 days [OR] taking ASA 100 mg daily dose [AND] for less than 7 days [AND] but with 300 mg ASA loading dose before PCI.
have a negative urine [OR] blood pregnancy test [AND] at enrolment [AND] prior to randomization;
currently be using a hormonal contraceptive [AND] agree to continue its use [AND] in addition to using double-barrier local contraception (i.e., intra-uterine device [AND] plus spermicidal [AND] condom for male partner) [AND] from screening through study completion.

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Applicable
