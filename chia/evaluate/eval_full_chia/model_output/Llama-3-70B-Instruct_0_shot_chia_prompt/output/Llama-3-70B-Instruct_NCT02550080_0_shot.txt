<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosed [AND] cutaneous vasculitis [OR] urticaria [OR] psoriasis [OR] acne [OR] bullous skin diseases [OR] sterile pustulosis [OR] leprosy [OR] pneumocystis pneumonia [OR] any other patients who need dapsone administration.
Subjects are [NOT] dapsone-experienced.
All subjects must have a clinical need for treatment [AND] dapsone that precedes the decision to participate in the study.
All subjects are willing to complete the 6-weeks period clinical trial.
All subjects have given written informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
Has previously received [AND] Dapsone therapy.
The subject [OR] any of their healthcare providers is aware of the subjects HLA type.
Has been diagnosed [AND] Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase deficiency [OR] methemoglobin reductase deficiency.
Satisfies any contraindications [OR] restrictions to Dapsone therapy as listed in the product labels.
Current severe illness, including heart failure [OR] liver failure [OR] renal failure [OR] major organ allograft [OR] malignancy requiring parenteral chemotherapy that can [NOT] be discontinued for the duration of the trial, [OR] any other conditions which, in the opinion of the Investigator, would make the patient unsuitable for the study.
Any laboratory abnormality at Screening which, in the opinion of the Investigator, should preclude the subject's participation in the study [alanine aminotransferase (ALT) [OR] glutamic oxaloacetic transaminase(ALT) [OR] et al].
Pregnant women [OR] women who are breastfeeding.
Subject is, in the opinion of the Investigator, [NOT] able to complete the 6 week Observation period [AND] the EPT assessments as required.
A positive result for HLA-B*1301 in those subjects randomised to the genetic screening arm.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
