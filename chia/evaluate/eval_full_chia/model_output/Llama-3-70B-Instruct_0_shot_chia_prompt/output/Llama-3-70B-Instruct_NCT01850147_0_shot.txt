<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Histologic [OR] cytologic diagnosis of stage IIIB/IV NSCLC 
ECOG PS: 0 [OR] 1 
Unidimensional [OR] bi-dimensional measurable disease 
Receive prior treatment [AND] including first-line platinum-based chemotherapy, standard second-line chemotherapy [AND] 1 EGF/EGFR inhibitor 
Evidence of disease progression 
Life expectancy >12 weeks 
Neutrophils > 1.5 109/l [AND] Platelets > 100 109/l [AND] Hemoglobin > 9g/dl [AND] Total bilirubin < 1.5 UNL [AND] AST (SGOT) [AND] ALT (SGPT) < 2.5 UNL [AND] Alkaline phosphatases < 5 UNL [AND] Creatinine < 1 UNL 

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Pre-existing hemoptysis of a severity > grade 3 by NCI CTCAE criteria within 4 weeks prior to study entry 
[NOT] Uncontrolled hypertension 
[NOT] CHF, angina [OR] arrhythmias 
LVEF < 1 UNL 
[NOT] Existing a second malignancy within 5 years 
[NOT] Infected with HIV
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
