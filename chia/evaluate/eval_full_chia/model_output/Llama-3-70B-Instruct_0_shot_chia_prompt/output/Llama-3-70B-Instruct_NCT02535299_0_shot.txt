<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes according to WHO criteria [AND] glycated hemoglobin (HbA1c) was more than 10%;
Seronegative for antibodies against insulin [OR] islet cells [OR] glutamic acid decarboxylase (GAD);

Exclusion Criteria:
Type 1 diabetes mellitus [OR] presence of autoimmune diabetes indicated by antibodies to insulin [OR] islet cells [OR] GAD;
Gestational diabetes;
patients [NOT] having heart [OR] liver [OR] renal function impairment;[NOT] presence of severe infections [OR] cerebrovascular disease;
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
