<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients undergoing ambulatory hand surgery [AND] for carpal tunnel [OR] trigger finger, under local anesthesia [AND] with [OR] without sedation.

Exclusion Criteria:
ASA > 3;
Coagulopathy;
Renal disease [OR] Liver disease,
History of recent gastro-intestinal bleeding [AND] 
Pregnancy.
Diagnosis of chronic pain [AND] currently taking opioid pain medication [OR] with a history of drug abuse.
Patients [NOT] with a self-described allergy to ASA, acetaminophen, NSAIDS [AND] codeine.
All patients receiving a brachial plexus block [AND] for anesthesia [AND]/or analgesia.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
