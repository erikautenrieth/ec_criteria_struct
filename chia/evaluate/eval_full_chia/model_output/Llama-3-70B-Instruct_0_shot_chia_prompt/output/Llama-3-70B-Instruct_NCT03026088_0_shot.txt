Inclusion Criteria:
18-80 year, male [OR] female.
Chronic Heart failure subjects [AND] with medical history of cardiac disease [OR] other related cardiovascular disease.
Left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) less than [OR] equal to (=<) 40 percent (%).
New York Heart Association (NYHA) class of II - IV
NYHA II : Slight limitation of physical activity. Comfortable at rest, but ordinary physical activity results in undue breathlessness, fatigue [OR] palpitation.
NYHA III:Marked limitation of physical activity. Comfortable at rest, but less than ordinary activity causes undue breathlessness, fatigue [OR] palpitation.
NYHA IV:Unable to carry on any physical activity without discomfort. Symptoms at rest can be present. If any physical activity is undertaken, discomfort increased.
Signed Informed Consent Form (ICF).

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Acute coronary syndrome (ACS) within 3 months.
[NOT] Under beta-blocker treatment for the last 2 weeks.
[NOT] Under other medicine treatment which may affect heart rate, like Non-dihydropyridine calcium channel blockers (NDHP-CCBs) [OR] ivabradine for the last 2 weeks; [NOT] Under Digoxin treatment [more than (>) 0.125 milligram (mg)].
[NOT] Uncontrolled Diabetes [hemoglobin A1c, (HbA1c) >7.5%].
[NOT] Severe [OR] uncontrolled hypertension [resting Systolic Blood Pressure (SBP) >180 millimeters of mercury (mmHg), [OR] resting Diastolic Blood Pressure (DBP) >110mmHg at screening period].
[NOT] Severe hypotension [resting SBP less than (<) 90mmHg, [OR] resting DBP<50mmHg].
[NOT] Resting heart rate <60 beat per minute (bpm).
Any contradiction to Bisoprolol according to label, [AND] including:
[NOT] Acute heart failure [OR] during episodes of heart failure decompensation requiring intravenous inotropic therapy.
[NOT] Cardiogenic shock.
[NOT] Atrioventricular block of second [OR] third degree (without a pacemaker).
[NOT] Sick sinus syndrome.
[NOT] Sinoatrial block.
[NOT] Slowed heart rate, causing symptoms (symptomatic bradycardia),
[NOT] Decreased blood pressure, causing symptoms (symptomatic hypotension),
[NOT] Severe bronchial asthma [OR] severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.
[NOT] Sever forms of peripheral arterial occlusive disease [AND] Raynaud's syndrome.
[NOT] Untreated phaeochromocytoma.
[NOT] Metabolic acidosis.
[NOT] Hypersensitivity to bisoprolol [OR] to any of the excipients.
[NOT] Severe Arrhythmia including atrial fibrillation, atrial flutter, ventricular fibrillation, ventricular flutter [OR] ventricular tachycardia.
[NOT] Significant valvular heart disease, congenital heart disease, pulmonary heart disease [OR] perinatal heart disease.
[NOT] Acute pulmonary edema.
[NOT] Severe hepatic dysfunction, defined as:
Serum Alanine Aminotransferase (ALT) > triple the upper limit of the normal range; [AND]/or
Serum Aspartate Aminotransferase (AST) > triple the upper limit of the normal value range [AND]/or
[NOT] Severe renal dysfunction, defined as:
Serum creatinine > twice the upper limit of the normal range
[NOT] Chronic Kidney Disease (glomerular filtration rate <45 Milliliter per minute).
[NOT] Hyperthyroidism [OR] hypothyroidism.
[NOT] Severe infectious disease, example (eg) Human Immunodeficiency Virus positive [OR] active tuberculosis.
[NOT] Severe autoimmune disease, e.g. lupus erythematosus, multiple sclerosis.
[NOT] Severe respiratory, digestive, hematological disease (including Anemia of Hb < 100 gram per litre) [OR] tumor.
[NOT] Known to be hypersensitivity to Bisoprolol, [OR] any of the excipient.
[NOT] Substance [OR] alcohol abuse.
[NOT] Received heart transplantation [OR] pacemaker implantation; revascularization treatment within 3 months; [OR] plan to receive above treatment in 6 months.
[NOT] Currently undertaking other treatment that may affect the safety [AND]/or efficacy evaluation, e.g. beta receptors agonists, et cetera.
[NOT] No legal ability [OR] legal ability is limited.
[NOT] Subjects unlikely to cooperate in the study [OR] with inability [OR] unwillingness to give informed consent.
[NOT] Child-bearing period women without effective contraceptive measures, pregnancy [AND] lactation.
[NOT] Participation in another clinical trial within the past 90 days.
[NOT] Other significant condition that in the Investigator's opinion would exclude the subject from the trial.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
