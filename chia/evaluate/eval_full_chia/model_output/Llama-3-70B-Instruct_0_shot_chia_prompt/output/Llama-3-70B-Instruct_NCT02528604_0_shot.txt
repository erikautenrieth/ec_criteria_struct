<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with symptomatic persistent atrial fibrillation of less than 1-year duration.
Patients must be over 65 years old.
Patients [AND] give informed consent prior to participating in this study.

Exclusion Criteria:
Paroxysmal atrial fibrillation.
Long-standing persistent [OR] permanent atrial fibrillation.
Previous pacemaker implantation.
Previous atrial ablation.
Patient [NOT] able to take warfarin [OR] other oral anti-coagulant medication.
Patient [AND] suffering [AND] with unstable angina in last one week.
Patient [AND] has had a myocardial infarction within last two months.
Patient [AND] expecting [OR] has had major cardiac surgery within last two months.
Patient [AND] participating in a conflicting study.
Patient [NOT] able to perform exercise testing.
Patient [AND] mentally incapacitated [AND] cannot consent [OR] comply with follow-up.
Patient [AND] has New York Heart Association (NYHA) class III [OR] IV heart failure.
Patient [AND] has left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) less than 35% [NOT] secondary to tachycardia.
Pregnancy.
Patient [AND] suffers [AND] with other cardiac rhythm disorders.
Recent coronary artery intervention [OR] other factors suggesting clinical instability (ECG, clinical [OR] laboratory findings).
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
