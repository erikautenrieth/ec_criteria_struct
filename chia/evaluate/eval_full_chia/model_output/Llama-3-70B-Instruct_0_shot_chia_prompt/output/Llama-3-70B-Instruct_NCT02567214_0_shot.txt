<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Age > 50 years
Smoking history > 10 packs/year
FEV1 30 - 79% of predicted [AND] FEV1/FVC < 70% (GOLD 2-3)
FRC > 120 % predicted
Borg dyspnea score > 3 [AND] during the 3-min constant rate shuttle walking test at V3

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Respiratory exacerbation [NOT] within the 2 months preceding the study
[NOT] Current diagnostic of asthma
[NOT] Significant O2 desaturation (SpO2 < 85%) [OR] at rest [OR] during exercise
[NOT] Presence of another pathology that could influence exercise tolerance
[NOT] Use of home oxygen
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
