Inclusion Criteria:
Has given written informed consent [AND] before any study-related activity is performed
Advanced hormone-dependent prostate cancer [AND] for which androgen deprivation therapy is indicated, [AND] independently from this trial, Firmagon® is intended to be used [AND] for treatment
Age greater than [OR] equal to 18 years [AND] less than 80 years
Advanced hormone-dependent prostate cancer [NOT] with any other clinically significant disorder
Easten Cooperative Oncology Group score = 2
PSA = 2 ng/mL [AND] at screening
Life expectancy of at least 12 months [AND] as per the investigator's judgement

Exclusion Criteria:
Previous [OR] concurrent hormonal management of prostate cancer
Contraindication [AND] for prescription of Firmagon®
Concurrent treatment [AND] with a 5-a-reductase inhibitor
Considered [AND] as a candidate for curative therapy
History of severe untreated asthma [AND] anaphylactic reactions [OR] severe urticaria [AND] angioedema
QTc interval over 450 msec [OR] risk factors for torsades de pointes [OR] on Class IA [AND] Class III anti arrhythmic medications
Cancer [NOT] within the last 5 years except prostate cancer [AND] surgically removed basal [OR] squamous cell carcinoma of the skin
Known [OR] suspected hepatic, symptomatic biliary disease (this includes moderate [OR] severe chronic hepatic impairment)
Patients [AND] with clinically significant laboratory abnormalities [OR] disorders other than prostate cancer
Patient [AND] with Hepatitis B Virus (HBV) [OR] Hepatitis C Virus (HCV) [OR] Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) infections
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
