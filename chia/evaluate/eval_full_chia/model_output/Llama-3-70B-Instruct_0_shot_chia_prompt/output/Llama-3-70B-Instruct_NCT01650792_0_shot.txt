<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis of heart failure according to Framingham criteria [AND] informed consent [AND] age 18 years [OR] above

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] history of an untreated malignancy [NOT] except local skin cancers
Ischemic stroke [AND] determined using the Questionnaire for Verifying Stroke-Free Status (QVSFS)
Patients [AND] on renal dialysis [OR] with end-stage hepatic dysfunction
Acute infection/inflammation [AND] Temperature > 101.5 F [OR] WBC> 15, 000
Inability [NOT] to obtain informed consent from patient [OR] next of kin
Anticoagulant use [AND] warfarin [OR] heparin
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
