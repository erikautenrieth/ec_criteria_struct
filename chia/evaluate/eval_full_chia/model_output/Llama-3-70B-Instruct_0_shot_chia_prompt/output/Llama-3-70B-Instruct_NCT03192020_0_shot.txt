<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
patients [AND] with =20° passive extension deficit (PED) in metacarpophalangeal (MP) [OR] proximal interphalangeal (PIP) joint, [OR] TPED of =30° in MP [AND] PIP joints of finger/fingers II-V
age > 18 years
palpable cord
provision of informed consent
ability [AND] to fill the Finnish versions of questionnaires.

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] recurrent contracture in the finger [AND] to be treated
neurologic condition [AND] causing the loss of function of the finger [AND] to be treated
contraindication [AND] for collagenase clostridium histolyticym (Xiapex/Xiaflex ®)
pregnant [OR] breast feeding
TPED > 135° (Tubiana stage 4) in finger [AND] to be treated
rheumatoid arthritis
previous fracture in finger [AND] to be treated, which affects range of motion of MP [OR] PIP joint
age > 80 years
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
