<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
medical indication [AND] for induction of labor
18 years of age
signed informed consent
cephalic presentation
[NOT] PROM
37+0 - 42+0 weeks of gestation
Bishop-Score = 6
[NOT] contra-indication [AND] for medical induction of labor
[NOT] clinical signs of infection

Exclusion Criteria:
fetal anomalies
contra-indications [AND] for medical induction of labor
placental pathologies
St.p. surgery [AND] with opening the uterine cavity (incl. caesarean section)
PROM
multiple gestations
< 37-0 weeks of gestation
St.p. cervical tear
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
