<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Symptomatic primary knee osteoarthritis [AND] with failed conservative treatment at least 3 months
Kellgren-Lawrence grade I [OR] II [OR] III
Gave informed consent
Can do questionnaires

Exclusion Criteria:
Severe deformity ([OR] varus [OR] values from mechanical axis more than 5 degrees)
[NOT] Allergy to hyaluronic acid
Pain on hip [OR] ankle
Post-traumatic [OR] post surgery of lower extremity
Post infection of knee
Previous hyaluronic acid injection within 6 months
Pregnancy [OR] lactation
Underlying Rheumatoid arthritis [OR] stroke [OR] malignancy [OR] venous occlusion
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
