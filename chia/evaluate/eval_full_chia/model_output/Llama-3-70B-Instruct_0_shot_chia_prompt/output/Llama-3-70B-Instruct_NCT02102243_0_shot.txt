<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Normotensive controls
Stage I (140-159 [OR] 90-99 mmHg) untreated subjects [AND] with essential hypertension
Patients [AND] with PA [AND] stage I (140-159 [OR] 90-99 mmHg) hypertension

Exclusion Criteria:
Congestive heart failure [OR] coronary artery disease
Blood pressure averaging > 159 [OR] 99 mmHg
Serum creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL
Diabetes mellitus [OR] other systemic illness
Left ventricular hypertrophy [AND] by echocardiography [OR] ECG
Pregnancy
Hypersensitivity [AND] to spironolactone, chlorthalidone, amlodipine, human recombinant insulin [OR] Definity
Any [NOT] history of substance abuse ([NOT] other than tobacco)
History of gouty arthritis
Patients [AND] with right-to-left, bi-directional, [OR] transient right-to-left cardiac shunts
Hypersensitivity [AND] to perflutren, blood, blood products [OR] albumin
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
