<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Aged over 18
Primary symptom of chest pain [AND] no contraindication to CTA
Willing [AND] able to provide written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Atrial fibrillation of new onset [OR] when rate control has been difficult
Known bigemini/trigeminy
Prior CABG surgery
[NOT] allergic to contrast
Advanced renal impairment
Significant valve disease ([AND] severe aortic stenosis [OR] regurgitation; [AND] severe mitral regurgitation)
Life expectancy <12 months
[NOT] inclusion in another trial [AND] without prior agreement with CI
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
