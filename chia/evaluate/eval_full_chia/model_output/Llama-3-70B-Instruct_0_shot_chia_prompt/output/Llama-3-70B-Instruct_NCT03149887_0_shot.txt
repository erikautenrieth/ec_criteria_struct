<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Adult patients up to age 75 years [AND] undergoing elective [OR] ambulatory [OR] arthroscopic rotator cuff repair.

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnancy [OR] coagulopathy [OR] allergy to bupivacaine [OR] renal failure [OR] hepatic insufficiency [OR] inappropriate candidate for usual therapy (specifically, if [NOT] able to receive the usual preoperative interscalene nerve block: history of preexisting nerve injury on side of surgery [OR] refusal of nerve block [OR] infection at site of nerve block).
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
