<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Children 7-17 [AND] with moderate [OR] severe pain [AND] requiring around the clock treatment [AND] with an opioid analgesic.
Be an experienced opioid user, defined as any subject treated [AND] with opioid therapy, equivalent [OR] equal to >20 mg per day of morphine, [AND] for a period of 3 consecutive days immediately prior to first day of dosing.

Exclusion Criteria:
Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS) [AND] for suicidal ideation [AND] behavior in past year.
[NOT] Hypersensitivity to morphine, [OR] naltrexone.
A life expectancy (assessed by investigator) of less than 6 months [OR] is [NOT] capable of taking medication orally.
Undergone surgery [AND] within 3 days prior to the first day of dosing.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
