Inclusion Criteria:
White Brazilian of European descent
Fulfillment of the Diagnostic [AND] Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, Fourth Edition, (DSM-IV) diagnostic criteria for ADHD
Eligibility [AND] to immediate-release MPH (IR-MPH) treatment

Exclusion Criteria:
Contraindication [AND] for IR-MPH use
Current stimulant treatment
Evidence of a clinically significant neurological disease that might affect cognition (e.g., delirium [OR] dementia [OR] epilepsy [OR] head trauma [OR] multiple sclerosis)
Current [OR] past history of psychosis
Estimated intelligence quotient score lower than 70
[NOT] Having a contraindication for IR-MPH use
