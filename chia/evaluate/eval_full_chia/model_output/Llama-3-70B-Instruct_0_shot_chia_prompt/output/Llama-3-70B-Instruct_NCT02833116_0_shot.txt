<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Unilateral leg pain secondary to lateral stenosis [OR] disc protrusion [OR] herniated disc.
Age between 18 [AND] 80 years.
Moderate [AND] severe pain (NVS>4).
Right proficient oral [AND] written language.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] high intracranial pressure.
Patients [AND] Multiple Sclerosis.
Patients [AND] Guillain-Barré syndrome [AND] radiculopathy of vascular origin.
Patients [AND] history of previous lumbar surgery.
Patients [AND] pregnant [OR] lactating.
Patients [AND] allergy [OR] intolerance to any of the drugs used.
Patients [AND] severe cognitive impairment.
Patients [AND] intrathecal injectio radiculalgia.
Patients [AND] poorly controlled major psychiatric pathology.
Patients [AND] type I diabetes [OR] poorly controlled type II diabetes (Hb1Ac>8.5).
Patients [AND] glaucoma.
Patients [AND] caudal equine syndrome.
Patients [AND] pre-treatment [AND] steroid injections [OR] local anesthetics.
Patients [AND] central canal stenosis.
Patients [AND] chronic treatment [AND] oral corticosteroids [NOT] without stabilized pattern.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
