<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
CPPE [AND] evidence of septated pleural effusion on pleural ultrasonography [OR] chest CT scan [AND] empyema.

Exclusion Criteria:
age <18 years;
Pregnancy;
[NOT] ability to give informed written consent;
previous thoracic surgery [OR] thrombolytic therapy for pleural infection;
medical thoracoscopy cannot be performed [AND] within 48 hours;
[NOT] ability to tolerate procedure due to hemodynamic instability [OR] severe hypoxemia;
[NOT] ability to correct coagulopathy;
presence of a homogeneously echogenic effusion on pleural US;
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
