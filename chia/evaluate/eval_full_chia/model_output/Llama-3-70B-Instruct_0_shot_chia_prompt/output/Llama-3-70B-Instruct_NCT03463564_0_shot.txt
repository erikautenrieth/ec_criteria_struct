<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
T1DM for at least 12 months [AND] persistent HbA1c levels = 7.5% (58 mmol/mol) despite optimized education therapy, [AND] recurrent severe hypoglycemic episodes [OR] high glucose variability [AND] willingness to wear the insulin pump.

Exclusion Criteria:
previous use of insulin pump [OR] pregnancy [OR] planning to become pregnant in the next 2 years, [OR] lack of ability to use the study devices [OR] history of severe chronic diseases [AND] recent [OR] concomitant use of corticosteroids [OR] drug abuse [OR] alcohol abuse [OR] psychiatric complaints that interfere with the correct use of the devices.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
