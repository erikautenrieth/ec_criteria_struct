<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Term singleton infants (>37 weeks gestational age) 
[AND] Birth weight > 2500g 
[AND] Healthy at inclusion 
[AND] Breastfed exclusively [OR] predominantly (>50% meals) at inclusion 
[AND] [NOT] previous iron supplementation 
[AND] [NOT] previous blood transfusion 
[AND] Informed consent given

Exclusion Criteria:
preterm delivery (<37 weeks of gestation) 
[OR] birth weight < 2500 g 
[OR] multiple pregnancy 
[OR] major illness [OR] congenital anomaly 
[OR] being <50% breastfed at the time of inclusion 
[OR] food allergy 
[OR] anaemia (Hb <105 g/L [10.5 g/dL]) at inclusion 
[OR] [NOT] lack of informed consent
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
