<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
BV positive by Nugent score [AND] HIV negative [AND] Capable of providing written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Currently pregnant [OR] using a reliable contraception (e.g. injectables [OR] intrauterine devices [OR] implant [OR] oral contraceptive pills) [AND] Desiring pregnancy in the next year [AND] History of tubal ligation [AND] hysterectomy [AND] Contraindication to progestin-only contraceptives [AND] [NOT] Able to comprehend consent material because of language barrier [OR] psychological difficulty
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
