<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Willing [AND] to receive three doses of an FDA-approved Hepatitis B vaccine
Volunteer chronically infected with HCV (as demonstrated by serology [OR] viral load laboratory studies)
Healthy volunteer [AND] without significant medical problems

Exclusion Criteria:
Received any vaccine within a month prior to study vaccine
Positive serum antibody against Hep B surface antigen [OR] core Hep B core antigen
HIV positive
For HCV-negative, healthy volunteers: History of HCV infection [OR] positive HCV antibody test
Participation in another clinical study of an investigational product currently [OR] within the past 90 days, [OR] expected participation during this study
In the opinion of the investigator, the volunteer is unlikely [AND] to comply with the study protocol
Any clinically significant abnormality [OR] medical history [OR] physical examination including history of immunodeficiency [OR] autoimmune disease (in addition [AND] to HCV infection, for HCV group)
Currently taking systemic steroids [OR] other immunomodulatory medications including anticancer medications [OR] antiviral medications
Any clinically significant acute [OR] chronic medical condition requiring care by a primary care provider (e.g., diabetes, coronary artery disease, rheumatologic illness, malignancy, substance abuse) that, in the opinion of the investigator, would preclude participation
[NOT] Able to continue participation for 156 weeks
History of previous Hepatitis B vaccination(s)
Male [OR] female < 18 [AND] > 62 years of age
Is pregnant [OR] lactating
History of Hepatitis B infection
Clinical, laboratory, [OR] biopsy evidence of cirrhosis
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
