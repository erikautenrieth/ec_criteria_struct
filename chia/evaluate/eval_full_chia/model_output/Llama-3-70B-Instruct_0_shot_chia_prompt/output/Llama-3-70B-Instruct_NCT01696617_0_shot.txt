<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Age : 18-65
Patients [AND] with major depressive disorder according to DSM-IV criteria [AND] that have lasted >8 weeks
MADRS total score of 18 [OR] higher
Patients who responded inadequately (a score of >18 on the MADRS) [AND] to first-line antidepressant treatment of 4 week duration
Current use of standard antidepressant treatment [AND] in monotherapy [OR] combination of 2 antidepressants : escitalopram (10 - 20mg/d) [OR] fluoxetine(20 - 40mg/d) [OR] paroxetine CR(25 - 50mg/d) [OR] sertraline(100 - 150mg/d) [OR] mirtazapine (15 - 45mg/d) [OR] duloxetine (30 - 60mg/d) [OR] venlafaxine ER(150-225mg/d)

Exclusion Criteria:
Past [AND] history of hypersensitivity to aripiprazole
Primary diagnosis of MDD [AND] with psychotic feature [OR] bipolar disorder [OR] schizophrenia [OR] schizoaffective disorder [OR] other psychotic disorder [OR] anxiety disorder [OR] a history of alcohol/ drug abuse within the past 12 months [OR] a diagnosis of dementia
Clinically significant current Axis II (DSM-IV-TR) diagnosis
A significant risk of suicide corroborated by a score of =5 on item 10(suicidal thoughts) on the MADRS scale [OR] by clinical judgment of the investigator
Pregnancy [OR] in breast-feeding
Presence of a serious medical illness [AND] including cardiac [OR] hepatic [OR] renal [OR] respiratory [OR] endocrinologic [OR] neurologic [OR] hematologic disease [OR] physical disorder judged to significantly affect central nervous system function
Patients taking antipsychotics [OR] mood stabilizer [OR] any psychotropic medications [NOT] besides antidepressants [OR] except benzodiazepines [OR] beta blockers [OR] hypnotics
Patients [AND] with past treatment failures of aripiprazole
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
