<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
all adult patients [AND] with a nasal [OR] facial skin/soft tissue defect [AND] requiring reconstruction [AND] limited to [OR] including a full-thickness skin graft

Exclusion Criteria:
current [OR] recent (within one week of surgery) systemic antibiotic use, [AND] intolerance to both clindamycin [AND] cephalexin, [AND] discovery of a persistent cutaneous malignancy at the site of the defect following the reconstructive procedure [AND] previous reconstruction at the site of the skin/soft-tissue defect, [AND] [NOT] absence of any of the above conditions.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
