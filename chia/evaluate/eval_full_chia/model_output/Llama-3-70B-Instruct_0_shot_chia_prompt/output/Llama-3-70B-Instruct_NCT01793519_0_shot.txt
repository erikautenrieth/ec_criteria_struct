Inclusion Criteria:
Age greater than [OR] equal to 18 years
Have RA, as defined by the 1987 revised American College of Rheumatology criteria
In sustained clinical remission for the last 6 months while receiving treatment [AND] with either etanercept, infliximab, [OR] adalimumab, [AND] greater than [OR] equal to 1 DMARD (methotrexate, hydroxychloroquine, sulfasalazine, leflunomide, minocycline, cyclosporine, azathioprine, gold, penicillamine). If there is [NOT] visit 6 months before enrollment, the nearest visit in the 6-12 month period before enrollment should be considered [AND] have a DAS28 less than 2.6.

Exclusion Criteria:
Had dose increase of anti-TNF agent [OR] DMARD in the last 6 months
Had change of anti-TNF agent [OR] DMARD in the last 6 months
Treated currently [AND] with golimumab [OR] certolizumab
Treated [AND] with greater than 10 mg of prednisone (or equivalent) daily in the last 6 months
Treated [AND] with greater than 5 mg of prednisone (or equivalent) daily in the last 3 months
Treated [AND] with intramuscular [OR] intravenous corticosteroids in the last 6 months for RA activity
Treated [AND] with anakinra, abatacept, [OR] tocilizumab in the last 6 months
Treated [AND] with rituximab in the last 12 months
Treated [AND] with an investigational RA drug in the last 6 months
Pregnant (or anticipate pregnancy during the study period) [OR] lactating women
[NOT] Absence of documentation in the medical record of clinical remission for the last 6 months
Unwilling [AND] to discontinue anti-TNF agent
[NOT] Absence of documentation of negative tuberculin skin test, negative QuantiFERON-TB Gold test, [OR] treatment for latent tuberculosis prior to starting treatment with the anti-TNF agent
Treatment of solid malignancy [OR] non-melanoma skin cancer within the past 5 years, [OR] any history of melanoma [OR] hematologic [OR] lymphoproliferative malignancy
[NOT] Absence of documentation of age-appropriate cancer screening at the time of randomization
[NOT] Absence of documentation of negative hepatitis B serologies, [NOT] absence of completion of treatment for chronic hepatitis B, [OR] [NOT] absence of suppressive antiviral treatment
