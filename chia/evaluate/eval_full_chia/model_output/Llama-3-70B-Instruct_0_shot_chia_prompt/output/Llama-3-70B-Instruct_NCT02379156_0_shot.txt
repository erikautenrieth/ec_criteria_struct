<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Duration of SCI [AND] =1 year;
Level of SCI [AND] C3-T1, AIS A [OR] B;
Age [AND] between 18 [AND] 65 years.

Exclusion Criteria:
Evidence of sympathetic integrity below the lesion level by the skin axon-reflex vasodilatation (SkARV) test;
[NOT] Known allergies to midodrine hydrochloride;
PMH of diagnosed heart, kidney, peripheral vascular, [OR] cerebral vascular disease, [OR] diabetes mellitus;
Hypertension (BP>140/90 mmHg);
[NOT] Untreated thyroid disease;
Acute illness [OR] infection;
Current smoker;
Pregnancy.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
