<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Diabetes mellitus [AND] Foot ulcer at the malleoli area between 0,25 cm² [AND] 5,0 cm² [OR] Foot ulcer duration more than 6 weeks [AND] Ankle-brachial index above 0,40 [OR] presence of palpable pulses in arteria dorsalis pedes [OR] arteria tibialis posterior [AND] informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Hemoglobin concentration under 6.5 mmol/l [AND] screening [NOT] HBA1c more than 108 mmol/l [AND] Non-compliant with blood-letting [AND] Clinically infected ulcer [AND] Patient planned for [OR] has had a revascularization procedure in the affected leg within the last 8 weeks [AND] The ulcer have been treated with growth factors in the last 8 weeks [AND] History of deep venous insufficiency [AND] chronic venous leg ulcer [OR] stasis dermatitis [AND] Breast-feeding women [OR] fertile women [NOT] agreeing to use an effective method of contraception [AND] Participation in another clinical ulcer-healing study within the last 4 weeks [AND] Patient has previously been randomized in this study [AND] Judgement by the investigator that the patient is [NOT] able to participate in the study
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
