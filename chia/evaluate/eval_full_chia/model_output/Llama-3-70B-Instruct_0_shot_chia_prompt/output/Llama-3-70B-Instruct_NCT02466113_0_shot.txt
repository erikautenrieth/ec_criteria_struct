<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
The informed consent [AND] has been obtained from the patient. 
[AND] With confirmed diagnosis of stage II colon cancer. 
[AND] With moderate/good ECOG health rating (PS): 0-1 score. 
The patient [NOT] receive anti-cancer treatment before primary surgery. 
The patient [AND] receive radical operation for colon cancer [AND] with negative margin.

Exclusion Criteria:
[AND] With severe comorbidities, such as cardiovascular disease [OR] chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] diabetes mellitus [OR] chronic renal dysfunction. 
[AND] With bad compliance [OR] contraindication to enrollment. 
[AND] Pregnant woman [OR] lactating woman. 
[AND] With contraindication to receive adjuvant chemotherapy.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
