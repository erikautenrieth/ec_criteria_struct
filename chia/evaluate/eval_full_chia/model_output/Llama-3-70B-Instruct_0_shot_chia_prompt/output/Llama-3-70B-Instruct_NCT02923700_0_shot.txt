<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
patients affected by mono-lateral symptomatic knee articular degenerative pathology [AND] with history of chronic (for at least 4 months) pain [OR] swelling;
imaging findings of degenerative changes of the joint ([AND] osteoarthritis [OR] chondropathy) [AND] with Kellgren Lawrence Score from 0 [TO] 3 at X-ray evaluation.

Exclusion Criteria:
age > 80 years;
Kellgren-Lawrence score at X-ray evaluation > 3;
major axial deviation ([AND] varus >5° [OR] valgus > 5°),
systemic disorders such as diabetes, rheumatoid arthritis, haematological diseases ([AND] coagulopathy), severe cardiovascular diseases, infections, immunodepression;
patients [AND] in therapy [AND] with anticoagulants [OR] antiaggregants;
use of NSAIDs [AND] in the 5 days before blood donation;
patients [AND] with Hb values < 11 g/dl [AND] platelet values < 150,000/mmc;
patients [NOT] having any of the above conditions.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
