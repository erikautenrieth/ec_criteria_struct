<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Women (18-75 years) [AND] with suspected UTI
[AND] at least two symptoms of UTI ([OR] dysuria, [OR] urgency of micturition, [OR] frequency, [OR] lower abdominal pain)
[AND] Written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] signs of complicated UTI ([AND] e.g. temperature > 38°C, [AND] loin tenderness)
[NOT] conditions that may lead to complicated infections ([AND] i.e. renal diseases, [AND] patients with urinary catheter)
[NOT] pregnancy/[OR] breastfeeding
[NOT] current self-medication with UU preparations ([AND] e.g. z.B. Cystinol®, [OR] Uvalysat®, [OR] Arctuvan®)
[NOT] antibiotic use in the last 7 days
[NOT] previous UTI in the past 2 weeks
history of pyelonephritis [AND]
[NOT] contraindications for trial drugs
[NOT] serious diseases
[NOT] inability to understand trial Information
[NOT] current participation in another clinical trial [OR] participation in another clinical trial within the last 4 weeks
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
