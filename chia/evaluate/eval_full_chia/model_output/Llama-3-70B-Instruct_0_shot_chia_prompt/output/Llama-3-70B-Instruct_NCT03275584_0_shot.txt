<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Adult patient being referred [AND] clinically indicated positron emission tomography myocardial perfusion imaging at the Centre hospitalier de l'Université de Montréal

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant women
Patient [AND] claustrophobic [AND] unable to undergo the examination
Breastfeeding women [NOT] willing to temporarily stop breastfeeding
Patient [WITH] contra-indication to: dipyridamole [OR] aminophylline [OR] dobutamine [OR] exercise stress test (depending on the method of cardiovascular stress test chosen)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
