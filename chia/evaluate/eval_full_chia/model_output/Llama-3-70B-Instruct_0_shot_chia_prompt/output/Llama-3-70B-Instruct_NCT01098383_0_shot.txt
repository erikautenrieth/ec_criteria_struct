<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
A formal diagnosis of Autism [OR] Pervasive Developmental Disorder not otherwise specified (PDD-NOS), given by a child neurologist [AND] who has made the diagnosis.
Age: 10-18 years.
A signed parental consent form [AND] that is complete [AND] fully informed.

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] an underlying infectious disease
[NOT] chromosomal abnormality
[NOT] metabolic disorder
[NOT] specific brain related disorder (such as tuberous sclerosis)
history of fetal cytomegalovirus infection [AND] that is documented
birth asphyxia [AND] that is severe
a history of major head injury [AND] that is documented
a chronic use of non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs, (NSAID) [AND] that is ongoing
known brain damage [AND] that is significant
Epilepsy [AND] that is uncontrolled
Abnormal Electro-cardiogram (ECG) [AND] that is significant
Epileptiform EEG [AND] that is abnormal
Use of psychostimulants, anti-depressants, neuroleptics [OR] anti-convulsive agents within the past month [AND] that is ongoing.
Lack of cooperation in the screening phase [AND] that is significant.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
