Inclusion Criteria:
Age >18 years old
1cm squared surface area
Venous incompetence confirmed by [AND] clinical assessment [AND] duplex ultrasound scan
[NOT] evidence of arterial disease ([AND] Arterial Duplex [OR] Ankle Brachial Pressure Index >0.9)
Patients able [AND] complete trial procedures
Patients with a life expectancy of greater than 1 year

Exclusion Criteria:
Age less than 18 years
Significant arterial disease ([AND] Ankle Brachial Pressure Index <0•9 [OR] evidence on Arterial Duplex)
Acute Deep Vein Thrombosis
Patient [NOT] able [OR] unwilling to have high compression (30mmHg minimum)
Patients with dexterity insufficiency of hands
Patients with peripheral neuropathy
Leg ulcers of another underlying cause
Leg ulcers of greater than 1 year duration
Patients [NOT] able [OR] unwilling to provide written, informed consent
