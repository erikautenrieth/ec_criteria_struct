Idiopathic Parkinson's disease (Hughes AJ et al. 2001)
Patients [AND] with motor fluctuations
Chronic Insomnia disorder criteria according [AND] to the criteria of DMS-V (American Psychiatric Association, 2013) [AND] insomnia severity index > 15
Able [AND] to use independently the device required for treatment by apomorphine
Collection of written informed consent (legal obligation for any project under the public health law [AND] bioethics laws [OR] / [OR] CNIL).
Affiliate [AND] to social security [OR] beneficiary of such a regime

Atypical Parkinsonian Syndromes
Parkinson's disease [AND] with hallucinations
Parkinson's disease [AND] with impulse Control disorder (ICD)

Another obvious severe disease explaining insomnia
Exclusion for monitoring difficulties ([NOT] mutation, [NOT] insufficient motivation, [NOT] priority associated pathology in care)
Patient [NOT] willing to accept a pump
Patient [NOT] accepting polysomnography [AND] multiple sleep latency test

antiemetic neuroleptics

Hypersensitivity [AND] to apomorphine [OR] one of the excipients

Intellectual Disability
