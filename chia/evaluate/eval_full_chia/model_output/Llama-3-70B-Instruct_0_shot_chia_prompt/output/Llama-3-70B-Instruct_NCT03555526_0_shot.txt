Inclusion Criteria:
H pylori infection [AND] failed after at least two eradication therapies
aged 20 years [OR] greater
willingness [AND] to receive rescue therapy

Exclusion Criteria:
aged less than 20 years
history of [AND] gastric resection surgery
history of [AND] allergy to study drugs
pregnancy [OR] lactating women
severe underlying illness, such as end stage renal disease [OR] decompensated liver cirrhosis [OR] non-curative malignancy
