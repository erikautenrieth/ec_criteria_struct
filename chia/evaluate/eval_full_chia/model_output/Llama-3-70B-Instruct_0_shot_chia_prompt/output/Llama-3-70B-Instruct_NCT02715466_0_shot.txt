Inclusion Criteria:
Male [OR] female patients [AND] = 18 [AND] = 85 years of age
Women of child bearing potential [AND] must test negative on standard pregnancy test (urine [OR] serum)
Patients [AND] with body weight = 55 kg [AND] = 140 kg [AND] body mass index (BMI) = 18 kg/m2
Patients diagnosed [AND] severe sepsis / septic shock at admission on Intensive Care Unit [AND] who can be enrolled within 90 min after admission [OR] patients diagnosed [AND] severe sepsis / septic shock during Intensive Care Unit stay [AND] who can be enrolled within 90 min after diagnosis
Patients [AND] where antibiotic therapy has already been started (prior to randomization)
Patient [AND] who are fluid responsive. Fluid responsiveness is defined as increase of > 10% in mean arterial pressure (MAP) after passive leg raising (PLR)
Signed informed consent [AND] by patient, legal representative [OR] authorized person [OR] deferred consent

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Administration of HES, dextrane solutions [OR] > 500 ml of Gelatin solutions within the 24 h prior to randomization
Death expected within the next 48 h ([NOT] moribund patients as defined by ASA = class V)
Patients [AND] whose medical condition does preclude the PLR manoeuvre
Patients [AND] for whom the need of pressure infusions are expected
Requirement [AND] for renal support (either continuous [OR] discontinuous techniques, including intermittent haemodialysis, haemofiltration [AND] haemodiafiltration)
Patients [AND] receiving therapeutic heparin medication due to chronic coagulation disease [AND] anticoagulation medication (i.e. partial thromboplastin time > 60 sec)
Acutely burned patients
Contraindications [AND] according to summary of product characteristics of investigational test [AND] reference product
[NOT] Simultaneous participation in another interventional clinical trial (drugs [OR] medical devices studies)
