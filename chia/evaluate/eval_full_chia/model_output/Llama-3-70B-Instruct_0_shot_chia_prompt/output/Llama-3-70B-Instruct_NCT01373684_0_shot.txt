<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Chronic hepatitis B ([AND] HBsAg positive > 6 months)
HBeAg negative [AND] within six months prior to initiation of peginterferon alfa-2a
HBV DNA < 200 IU/ml [AND] during nucleos(t)ide analogue (except Telbivudine) treatment [AND] within one month prior to initiation of peginterferon alfa-2a
Compensated liver disease
Age > 18 years
Written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Treatment [AND] with any investigational drug [OR] within 30 days of entry to this protocol
Current treatment [AND] with Telbivudine
Severe hepatitis activity [AND] as documented by ALT>10 x ULN
History of decompensated cirrhosis ([AND] defined as jaundice [AND] in the presence of cirrhosis, ascites, bleeding gastric [OR] esophageal varices [OR] encephalopathy)
Pre-existent neutropenia ([AND] neutrophils <1,500/mm3) [OR] thrombocytopenia ([AND] platelets < 90,000/mm3)
Co-infection [AND] with hepatitis C virus, hepatitis D virus [OR] human immunodeficiency virus (HIV)
Other acquired [OR] inherited causes of liver disease: alcoholic liver disease, obesity induced liver disease, drug related liver disease, auto-immune hepatitis, hemochromatosis, Wilson's disease [OR] alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency
Alpha fetoprotein > 50 ng/ml
Hyper- [OR] hypothyroidism ([AND] subjects requiring medication to maintain TSH levels in the normal range are eligible if all other inclusion/exclusion criteria are met)
Immune suppressive treatment [AND] within the previous 6 months
Contra-indications [AND] for alfa-interferon therapy like suspected hypersensitivity to interferon [OR] Peginterferon [OR] any known pre-existing medical condition that could interfere [AND] with the patient's participation in [AND] completion of the study.
[NOT] Pregnancy, breast-feeding
Other significant medical illness that might interfere [AND] with this study: significant pulmonary dysfunction [AND] in the previous 6 months, malignancy other than skin basocellular carcinoma [AND] in previous 5 years, immunodeficiency syndromes ([AND] e.g. HIV positivity, auto-immune diseases, organ transplants other than cornea [AND] hair transplant)
Any medical condition requiring, [OR] likely to require chronic systemic administration of steroids, during the course of the study
Substance abuse, such as alcohol (>80 g/day), I.V. drugs [AND] inhaled drugs in the past 2 years.
Any other condition which [AND] in the opinion of the investigator would make the patient unsuitable for enrollment, [OR] could interfere [AND] with the patient participating in [AND] completing the study
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
