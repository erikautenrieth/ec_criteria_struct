<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
1. Are referred [AND] to the Cachexia Clinic [AND] with involuntary weight loss of >5% of their premorbid weight [AND] within the previous 6 months. 
2. Are 18 years of age [OR] older 
3. Have a Karnofsky performance score of 60 [OR] higher. 
4. Can maintain oral food intake [AND] during the study 
5. Can understand the study procedures [AND] can sign an informed consent form. 
6. Are [NOT] currently taking melatonin. 
7. Are taking megestrol acetate [AND] continue to lose weight [AND] despite at least 2 weeks of therapy. 
8. Have a calculated creatinine clearance of >/= 60 cc/min. 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Have dementia [OR] delirium (as determined by the palliative care specialist) [AND] at study entry. 
2. Are pregnant 
3. Have been taking corticosteroids [AND] for longer than 48 hours. 
4. Have pulmonary edema [OR] ascites [OR] pitting edema [AND] on clinical examination. 
5. Are [NOT] able to walk. 
6. Have a history of serious adverse gastrointestinal events (i.e., bleeding [OR] perforation),[AND] history of a coagulopathy [OR] current anti-coagulant use. 
7. Have an ALT/AST>3x upper limit of normal. 
8. Patients [AND] on methotrexate. 
9. Patients taking melatonin receptor agonists (such as Rozerem® [ramelteon]).
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
