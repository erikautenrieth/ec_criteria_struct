<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Perimenopausal women complaining of abnormal uterine bleeding ([OR] menorrhagia, [OR] metrorrhagia, [OR] polymenorrhoea [OR] polymenorrhagia) [AND] without local gynecological cause.
Failure of medical treatment [AND] for at least 3 months.

Exclusion Criteria:
Age [NOT] >= 45 [AND] [NOT] <= 55 years.
[AND] Blood disorders [OR] coagulopathy.
Diagnosed [OR] suspected local gynecologic lesion ([OR] polyp, [OR] adenomyosis, [OR] myoma, [OR] malignancy [OR] cervical pathology).
[AND] Use intrauterine contraceptive device.
[AND] Pregnancy related conditions.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
