<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Adult women [AND] at least 18 years of age
Elective Female Pelvic Medicine [AND] Reconstructive Surgery [OR] Gynecologic Minimally Invasive surgeries [AND] including hysterectomy, suburethral sling [OR] pelvic organ prolapse repair [AND] that require cystoscopy.

Exclusion Criteria:
Surgeries [AND] that include: intradetrusor Botox [OR] vaginal mesh excision [OR] fistula repair
Pregnancy
History of nephrolithiasis [AND]
[NOT] Allergy to study medications
Congenital urogenital anomaly
Neurogenic bladder
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
