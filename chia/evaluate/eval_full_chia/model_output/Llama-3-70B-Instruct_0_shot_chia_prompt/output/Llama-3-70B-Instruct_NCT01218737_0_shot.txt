<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patient is indicated to have an ocular refractive surgery performed [AND] (myopia [OR] astigmatism [OR] hypermetropy) by the Lasik method. 
Patient presents a normal eye fundus. 
Patient has intraocular pressure (IOP) ≤ 20 mmHg. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Surgery [AND]/[OR] previous ocular pathology ([AND] presence of scar/[AND] change in the cornea, glaucoma, retinopathies, etc.). 
Patient has diabetes [OR] is immunodepressed. 
Any systemic infection during the study. 
Signs/[AND]/[OR] symptoms of ocular inflammation/[AND] infection (bacterial, viral, fungal, caused by Chlamydia, by Mycobacterium, Acanthamoeba [OR] of allergic etiology). 
[NOT] Have used any systemic [OR] topical antibiotics for ocular infection in the previous 14 days. 
Patient has known hypersensitivity to any of the components of the formulations used in the study.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
