Inclusion Criteria:
ST-segment elevation acute myocardial infarction patients [AND] during the first 12 hours of symptom onset;
Intention [AND] to perform primary percutaneous coronary intervention;
Signed informed consent;
Patient eligible [AND] for transradial [OR] transfemoral primary percutaneous coronary intervention, being pre-requisites: (a) familiarity [AND] of the operator [AND] with the radial [OR] femoral techniques using vascular closure devices, (b) agreement [AND] of the operator [AND] to use the access route determined by the randomization process.

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Less than 18 years of age;
[NOT] Pregnancy;
Chronic use [AND] of vitamin K antagonists [OR] direct thrombin inhibitors, [OR] oral Xa-factor antagonists;
Hypersensitivity [AND] to antiplatelet [AND] / [OR] anticoagulant drugs;
Active bleeding [OR] high bleeding risk ([AND] severe liver failure, [OR] active peptic ulcer, [OR] creatinine clearance < 30 mL/min, [OR] platelets count < 100.000 mm3);
[NOT] Uncontrolled systemic hypertension;
Cardiogenic shock;
History of myocardial revascularization surgery [AND] with = 1 internal mammary [OR] radial artery graft;
Documented chronic peripheral arterial disease [AND] preventing the use of the femoral technique;
Severe concomitant disease [AND] with life expectancy below 12 months;
Participation [AND] in drug [OR] devices investigative clinical trials [AND] in the last 30 days;
Medical, geographic [OR] social conditions [AND] impairing the participation [AND] in the study [OR] inability [AND] to understand [AND] sign the informed consent term.
