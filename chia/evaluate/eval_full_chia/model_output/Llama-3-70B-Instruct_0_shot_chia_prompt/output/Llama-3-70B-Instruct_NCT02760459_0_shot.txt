<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Age > 40 years [AND] 45
Primary knee osteoarthritis diagnosed using the American College of Rheumatology criteria [AND] 46
Undergoing elective [AND] primary [AND] unilateral total knee arthroplasty
American Society of Anesthesiology (ASA) physical status class 1-3
BMI < 40 kg/m2

Exclusion Criteria:
History of active rheumatic diseases
History of previous musculoskeletal injury of the same knee [AND] excluding patients [NOT] with secondary knee osteoarthritis
History of previous surgery on the same knee
History of adverse effects from medications [AND] to be used in this study
Contraindication [AND] to spinal anesthesia
History of psychiatric disorders [OR] cognitive impairment
Contraindication [AND] to corticosteroid agents
Poorly controlled diabetes mellitus [AND] HbA1C > 7.5
Poorly controlled hypertension
History of ischemic heart disease [OR] peripheral arterial disease [OR] cerebrovascular disease
Hepatic insufficiency [AND] Child-Pugh score > 5
Renal insufficiency [AND] Creatinine clearance < 30 mL/min
History of cataracts [OR] glaucoma [OR] ocular hypertension
History of steroid [OR] immunosuppressive drug use [AND] within 6 months of surgery
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
