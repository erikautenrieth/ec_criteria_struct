<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Women aged 25-75 years old.
Women [AND] with recently diagnosed breast cancer [AND] who will receive NAC [AND] to reduce tumor burden before surgery. ([AND] including locally advanced breast cancer (LABC) [AND] according to clinical assessment; [OR] tumor size > 2cm, [AND] that is, at least T2 in TNM staging).

Exclusion Criteria:
Estimated GFR (eGFR) < 60 mL/min/1.73 m2 [AND] blood glucose > 135 mg/dl; 
Past [AND] history of acute renal failure, renal dialysis, diabetes mellitus.
Women [AND] who received metallic fixation, coronary artery stent in recent 3 months; [OR] women [AND] who received mechanical valve replacement [AND] that is [NOT] compatible with MR magnet; [OR] women [AND] with aneurysmal clips, pacemakers.
Past [AND] history of claustrophobia.
Women [AND] who are pregnant [OR] who are planning to be pregnant, [OR] who are lactating ([AND] though the possibility in our target population should be very low)
Past [AND] history of breast cancer within recent 5 years before the currently diagnosed breast cancer.
Women [AND] who received chemotherapy for other disease entity in recent 1 year.
Women [AND] who [NOT] can cooperate with the examinations.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
