Inclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with either Relapsing-remitting MS (RRMS) [OR] Secondary progressive MS (SPMS) [OR] Primary progressive MS (PPMS) by McDonald 2010 criteria.
Patients [AND] defined by subtype based on 2013 updated phenotypic criteria.
[AND] prospectively with an EDSS change of at least 1.0 points over the last two years, [OR] retrospectively, with any significant change in motor function over at least one year, unrelated to relapse.
[AND] 55 years of age [OR] older at time of randomization;
[NOT] evidence of recent new inflammatory disease activity (inactive by the Lublin criteria16) [AND] [NOT] new relapse for at least five years [AND] [NOT] new MRI lesion for at least three years
[AND] taking one of the following DMTs: interferon ß-1a, [OR] interferon ß-1b, [OR] glatiramer acetate, [OR] natalizumab, [OR] fingolimod, [OR] dimethyl fumarate, [OR] teriflunomide; continuously for no less than 5 years.
[AND] taking most recent DMT continuously* for no less than two years.
[AND] willing to be randomized per this protocol; each patient will be questioned as to their willingness to stay in the trial regardless of the group to which group they are randomized.
[AND] willing to follow the protocol
[AND] continuously will be defined as no less than 75% of all prescribed doses, [AND] [NOT] time of greater than four weeks from last intended dose to have missed a dose (8 weeks for natalizumab, i.e. one missed dose).

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] any MS relapse in the last five years, as determined at the screen visit by the PI
[NOT] any new [OR] definitely enlarging T2/FLAIR lesion [OR] new gadolinium-enhancing lesion within the past three years (at least two scans separated by at least three years must be reviewed) on brain or spine MRI scan. Lesions must be 3mm or larger to be exclusionary.
[NOT] significant (as defined by the PI) intolerance of presently-used DMT
[NOT] use of alemtuzumab, [OR] mitoxantrone, [OR] cyclophosphamide, [OR] methotrexate, [OR] cyclosporine, [OR] rituximab
[NOT] prior use of any experimental agent used as a DMT for MS in the last five years
[NOT] uncontrolled hypertension, [OR] uncontrolled diabetes, [OR] uncontrolled asthma, [OR] uncontrolled depression
[NOT] cancers other than basal cell skin cancers within the last 5 years
[NOT] unable to give informed consent [OR] follow the protocol
[NOT] unable to undergo brain MRI
[NOT] unwilling to be randomized per this protocol
