<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
E.coli in blood culture 
AND identical isolate in urine sample (>= 1.000 CFU) [OR] relevant clinical signs of UTI

Exclusion Criteria:
[AND] Bacterial infection origin from another organ (e.g. pneumonia)
[AND] Severe sepsis with multiorgan failure
[AND] Perinephritic abscess
[AND] Pyonephrosis requiring drainage
[AND] Allergy to pivmecillinam
[AND] E.coli isolate resistant to pivmecillinam
[AND] Pregnancy/[OR] breastfeeding
[AND] Severe neutropenia
[AND] Prostatitis
[AND] Severe kidney failure (eGFR<15 ml/min)
[AND] Using valproate
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
