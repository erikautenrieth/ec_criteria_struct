<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Completed "ALO-IIT-012(PEAK study)" [AND] without major protocol deviations.
Male [OR] female, 19 years [AND] to 75 years.
Female [AND] with childbearing potential [AND] who has a negative urine pregnancy test result at study start [AND] willing to continue practice appropriate birth control during the entire duration of study
Subjects completed PEAK can be included [AND] within 30 days after End Of the Study
Subjects completed PEAK can be included [AND] if their treatment is the same as randomized [AND] even after 30 days of End Of the Study.

Exclusion Criteria:
eGFR(Epidermal growth factor receptor) < 50mL/min
AST(aspartate aminotransferase)/ALT(alanine aminotransaminase) >2.5 upper limit of normal
Pregnant [OR] lactating women
Subject [AND] who the investigator deems inappropriate to participate in this study
Patients [AND] with a history of bladder cancer [AND] patients with active bladder cancer
Patients [AND] with uninvestigated macroscopic hematuria
Patients [AND] with cardiac failure [OR] a history of cardiac failure (New York Heart Association [NYHA] Stages 3 [AND] to 4)
Patients [AND] with genetic problems [AND] such as galactose intolerance, Lapp lactase deficiency [OR] glucose-galactose malabsorption, since this study drug contains lactose
[NOT] Patients with ability to participate in this study
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
