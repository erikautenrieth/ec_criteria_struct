<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Primary periampullary tumor
R0 [OR] R1 resection
Chronic pancreatitis [AND] requiring pancreatoduodenectomy

Exclusion Criteria:
Metastatic tumor
Locally unresectable tumor
Previous gastric resection
ASA IV [OR] V
Age under 18 years
Preoperative complete parenteral [OR] enteral feeding
Immunosuppressive therapy [AND] before operation
Severe malnutrition
Lack [AND] of the patient's consent [AND] for the trial participation, feeding tube insertion [OR] epidural analgesia
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
