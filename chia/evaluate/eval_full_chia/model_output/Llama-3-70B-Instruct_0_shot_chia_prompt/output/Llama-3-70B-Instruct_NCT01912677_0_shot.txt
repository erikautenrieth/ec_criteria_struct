<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Pregnant [AND] gestational age >= 28 weeks
Systolic blood pressure >=160 mm Hg [OR] a diastolic blood pressure of >=110 mm Hg measured twice more than 15 minutes apart
Able [AND] to swallow pills
>= 18 years

Exclusion Criteria:
Indication [AND] for emergent cesarean [OR] known fetal anomaly
Anti-hypertensive therapy received [AND] in the past 12 hours
History of eclampsia [AND] or other adverse CNS complication (e.g., stroke [OR] PRES) in this pregnancy
Actively wheezing [AND] at time of enrollment [OR] history of asthma complications
[NOT] Known coronary artery disease [OR] type I DM [AND] with microvascular complications [OR] signs of heart failure [OR] clinical dissection of the aorta
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
