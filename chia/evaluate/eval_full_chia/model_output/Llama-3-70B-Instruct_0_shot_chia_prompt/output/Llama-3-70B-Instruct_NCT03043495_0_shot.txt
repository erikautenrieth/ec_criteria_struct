<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients undergoing surgeries [AND] in the upper limb (arm [OR] forearm [OR] hand)

Exclusion Criteria:
Coagulopathies [AND] with prothrombin concentration less than 60% [OR] INR more than 1.5)
[NOT] ability to postpone anti-coagulation medications.
Infection [OR] injury [OR] a lesion at the block site.
Suspected cervical vertebral column injury [AND] necessitating using a neck collar.
A compromised lung [AND] on the contralateral side of the block (Pneumothorax [OR] hemothorax [OR] Pneumonectomy).
Traumatic vascular injuries [OR] operative interventions (Surgical harvesting) [AND] involving arteries of the upper limb [AND] on the operative side.
Patients [AND] with communication difficulties.
Hypersensitivity [AND] to local anesthetics [AND]/[OR] Dexamethasone.
Patients [AND] on perioperative intravenous (IV) steroids.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
