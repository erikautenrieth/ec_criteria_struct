<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Type 2 Diabetes
Hypertension
Estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) > 30 ml/min
Use of Ace Inh [AND] ARB for control of blood pressure [AND] who are willing to be placed on alternate drug(s) [OR] in the washout period for blood pressure control

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnancy
Patients [AND] chronic kidney disease stage [AND] eGFR < 30 ml/min (CKD stage IV [OR] V)
Nephrotic range proteinuria (urinary protein > 3.5 gm/day)
History [AND] of renal transplantation
History [AND] of multiple myeloma
Known history [AND] of hypersensitivity reaction [OR] intolerability to Ace Inh [OR] ARB.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
