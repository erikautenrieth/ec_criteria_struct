<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Scheduled to undergo revision total knee arthroplasty

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Inability to provide informed consent [OR] to comply with study assessments (e.g. due to cognitive impairment [OR] geographic distance).
Age = 17.
[NOT] Allergy to povidone iodine.
Any condition [AND] requiring antibiotics 14 days prior to arriving [AND] for surgery.
Patients [AND] with chronic immunosuppression (such as HIV/AIDS).
[NOT] Unable to adhere to follow up schedule [AND] treatment.
Patients scheduled to undergo revision total knee arthroplasty [AND] for infectious reasons.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
