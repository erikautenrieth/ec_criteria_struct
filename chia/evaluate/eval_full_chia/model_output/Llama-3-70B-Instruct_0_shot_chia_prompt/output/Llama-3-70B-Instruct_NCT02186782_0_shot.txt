<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Infertile women [AND] with eugonadotrophic anovulation/[OR] oligoovulation.
Unexplained infertility.

Exclusion Criteria:
Age < 20 [OR] > 35 years.
Body mass index (BMI) < 18.5 kg/m2 [OR] > 25 kg/m2.
Presence of any infertility factor [NOT] anovulation/[OR] oligoovulation.
Previous [AND] history of ovarian surgery [OR] surgical removal of one ovary.
Previous exposure [AND] to cytotoxic drugs [OR] pelvic irradiation.
Metabolic [OR] hormonal abnormalities.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
