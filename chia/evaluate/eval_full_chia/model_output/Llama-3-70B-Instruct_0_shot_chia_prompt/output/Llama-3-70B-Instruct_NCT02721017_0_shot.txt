<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
scheduled for Nuss procedure [AND] for pectus excavatum correction
at least 13 years old [AND] at the time of the procedure

Exclusion Criteria:
age less than 13 years [AND] at time of procedure
use of pain medication prior [AND] to procedure
pectus carinatum [OR] Poland's syndrome [OR] any chest wall anomaly [NOT] other than pectus excavatum
previous repair of pectus excavatum [AND] by any technique
previous thoracic surgery
congenital heart disease
bleeding dyscrasia
major anesthetic risk factors [OR] history of previous problem [AND] with anesthesia
pregnancy
[NOT] ability to communicate in English
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
