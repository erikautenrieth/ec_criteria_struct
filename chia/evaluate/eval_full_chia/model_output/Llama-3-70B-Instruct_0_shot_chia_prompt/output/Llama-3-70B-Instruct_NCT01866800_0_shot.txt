Inclusion Criteria:
Subject is 65 years old [AND] who is able [AND] willing to give an informed consent.
Patients undergoing planned trans-femoral TAVI.
Calculated eGFR below 60ml/min/1.73m2 (MDRD)

Exclusion Criteria:
History of acute coronary syndrome [AND] in the past 30 days.
History of congesting heart failure [AND] with left ventricular ejection fraction <30% [OR] exacerbation [AND] in the past 30 days.
Current dialysis treatment.
[NOT] Known furosemide hypersensitivity.
Contraindications [AND] to placement of a Foley catheter [AND] in the bladder.
