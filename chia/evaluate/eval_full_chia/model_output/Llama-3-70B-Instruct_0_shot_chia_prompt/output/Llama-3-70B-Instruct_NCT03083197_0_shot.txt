<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
Age = 15 years old
Hospitalization [AND] with acute undifferentiated fever (temperature > 37.5 C, tympanic) =14 days [OR] patients admitted to hospital with a history of fever [AND] 14 days who subsequently develop fever within 24 hours of admission
Clinically suspected scrub typhus: defined as acute undifferentiated fever [AND] [NOT] no clear focus of infection [AND] negative malaria blood smear [OR] negative malaria RDT. Patients may have one [OR] none [OR] a combination of other clinical findings such as eschar, rash, lymphadenopathy, headache, myalgia, cough, nausea [AND] abdominal discomfort.
A positive scrub typhus RDT (Scrub Typhus IgM RDT, InBios International, Seattle, WA, USA) [OR] positive PCR-based detection of O. tsutsugamushi DNA from the admission blood sample
Written informed consent [OR] written informed assent as required
Able [AND] to take oral medication

Exclusion Criteria:
Known hypersensitivity [AND] to tetracycline, doxycycline [OR] azithromycin
Administration of doxycycline, azithromycin, chloramphenicol, rifampicin, [OR] tetracycline during the preceding 7 days
Pregnancy [OR] breast-feeding
Patients [AND] with myasthenia gravis [OR] systemic lupus erythematosus
Patients [AND] with an established infection (diagnostic test required) e.g. acute malaria, dengue, leptospirosis, typhoid, Japanese encephalitis etc.
Current TB [OR] TB treatment in = 6 months (contain active antibiotics against Orientia spp.)
Current HAART use [AND] for HIV, long term use of immunosuppressants (e.g. steroids, chemotherapy, TNF-inhibitors [AND] related agents)
Patients [AND] with severe disease whom the clinical team feel their condition necessitates the need for additional scrub typhus treatment beyond the allocated antibiotic treatment assigned at randomization (e.g. IV chloramphenicol [AND] / [OR] PO/NG rifampicin)

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
