<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients undergoing a high tibial osteotomy (HTO) [AND] patients undergoing tibial tubercle osteotomy (TTO) [OR] with medial patello-femoral ligament (MPFL) reconstruction.

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Legally competent [AND] [NOT] mentally impaired (e.g., minors, Alzheimer's subjects, dementia, etc.)
[NOT] Older than [OR] equal to 18 years of age
Any patient considered a vulnerable subject
[NOT] Free of bleeding [OR] clotting disorder
Preoperative anticoagulation therapy
Abnormal coagulation profile
Renal disorder [OR] insufficiency
Sickle cell disease
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
