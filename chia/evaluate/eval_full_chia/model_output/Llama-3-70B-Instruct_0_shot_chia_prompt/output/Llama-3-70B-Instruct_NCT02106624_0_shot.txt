<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
need mechanical ventilation for more than 2 days [AND] mean blood pressure more than 60mmHg [AND] predicted ICU stay more than 7 days [AND] tolerance of parenteral [OR] enteral nutrition

Exclusion Criteria:
irreversible status of primary disease
any [NOT] reversible status of primary disease
[NOT] history of malnutrition before enrollment [AND] history of steroid cortisol administration
severe liver dysfunction (Child-Pugh Score C)
pregnancy
refuse to enrollment
re-admission to ICU [AND] has been enrolled during former admission to ICU
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
