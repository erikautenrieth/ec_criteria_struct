<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patient [AND] with spontaneous intracranial hemorrhage [OR] traumatic intracranial hemorrhage [OR] patient [AND] requiring neurological surgery
Coagulation disorder defined [AND] by PT less than 60%

Exclusion Criteria:
Concomitant use [AND] with oral anticoagulant drugs
Acquired deficiency of coagulation factors [AND] whose treatment is established
Hypersensitivity [AND] to a PCC
History of thrombocytopenia [AND] induced by heparin
Disseminated intravascular coagulation
[NOT] Extracranial active bleeding
Hypersensitivity [AND] to vitamin K
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
