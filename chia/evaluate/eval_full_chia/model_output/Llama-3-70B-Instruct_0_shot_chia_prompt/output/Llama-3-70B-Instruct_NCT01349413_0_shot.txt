<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with functional dyspepsia that fulfill Rome III criteria [AND] with inadequate relief of dyspeptic symptoms
Age >18
Provision [AND] of written consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Presence [AND] of organic pathology identified by upper endoscopy [OR] other investigations
Presence [AND] of sliding hiatus hernia as defined by flap valve grade IV disruption of morphology at gastro-esophageal junction
Concurrent medications that affect gastrointestinal motility
Presence [AND] of acid reflux [OR] heartburn symptoms of more than twice a month
History of gastric surgery [AND]
H. pylori infection
Use [AND] of PPI [OR] NSAID in the past 4 weeks
Pregnancy
Known [AND] hypersensitivity to PPI
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
