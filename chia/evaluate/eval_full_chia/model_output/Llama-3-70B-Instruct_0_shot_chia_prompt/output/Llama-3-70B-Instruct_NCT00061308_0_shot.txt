Inclusion Criteria:
Have had one prior platinum-based chemotherapy regimen [AND] for the treatment of primary disease. 
At least 4 weeks since last surgery [OR] radiation therapy. 
Must have had a treatment-free interval of greater than 6 months following response [AND] to platinum. 
ECOG performance status of 0,1 [OR] 2. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Women of child-bearing potential [AND] that [NOT] do practice adequate contraception. 
Pregnant [OR] lactating. 
Received more than one primary chemotherapy regimen. 
Concomitant [OR] previous malignancies [NOT] with the exception of adequately treated basal cell [OR] squamous cell skin cancer, in situ cervical cancer, incidental carcinoid, [OR] other cancer from which the patient has been disease free for 5 years. 
Active uncontrolled infection [AND] requiring antibiotics. 
Concurrent severe medical problems unrelated [AND] to the malignancy which would limit full compliance [AND] with the study. 
Received radiation [AND] to more than 10% of bone. 
Prior treatment [AND] with topotecan [OR] gemcitabine. 
Hypersensitivity [AND] to camptothecin [OR] nucleoside analogues. 
Use of an investigational agent [AND] within 30 days.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
