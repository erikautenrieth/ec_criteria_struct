Inclusion Criteria:
Pregnant women [AND] with abdomen discumfort [AND] ultrasound diagnosis of polyhydramnios (AFI>25cm) 
Single [OR] twin pregnancies 

Exclusion Criteria:
Multiple pregnancy (more than 3 fetuses) 
Maternal [AND] history of placental abruptio 
Fetus [AND] with IUGR 
Pregnancy [AND] complicated with pre-eclampsia 
[NOT] Unability to give informed consent
