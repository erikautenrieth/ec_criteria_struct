<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] older than 18 years
Classification of the American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA I-III)
[NOT] cognitive deficits
Signed informed consent prior [AND] to surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
Emergency surgery
Pregnancy [OR] lactation
Immune disorders
Kidney [OR] liver disease [OR] advanced-stage cardiopulmonary
Patient refusal [AND] to participate in the study
Patients under 18 years [OR] inability [AND] to consent
Associated neuromuscular disorders, contraindication [AND] for the use of rocuronium/ sugammadex, allergy [OR] hypersensitivity [AND] to rocuronium / sugammadex
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
