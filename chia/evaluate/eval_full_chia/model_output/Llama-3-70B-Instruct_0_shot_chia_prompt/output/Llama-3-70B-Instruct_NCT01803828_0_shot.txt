<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
age 35-75 years;
Diagnosis of Type 2 Diabetes from at least 3 years;
HbA1c < 10% [AND] normal blood pressure [OR] controlled hypertension;
BMI < 40;

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] congenital [OR] valvular cardiomyopathy;
[NOT] ischemic heart disease;
[NOT] endocrine diseases: male hypogonadism [OR] hyperthyroidism [OR] adrenal diseases [OR] pituitary diseases;
[NOT] proliferative retinopathy [OR] autonomic neuropathy;
[NOT] contraindications to sildenafil use [OR] CMR imaging;
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
