<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosed epileptic patients of either sex [AND] with age between 10-19 yrs (<19yrs), coming to the medicine Out Patient [OR] In Patient Departments [AND] undergoing AED therapy for more than 6 months. 
Epileptics [AND] with high homocysteine levels i.e. > 10.9 µmol/L ([NOT] normal homocysteine levels are 4.3-9.9 µmol/L for male [AND] 3.3-7.2 µmol/L for female adolescent) [AND] a high homocysteine concentration is deemed as at least 11.4 µmol/L for male [AND] at least 10.4 µmol/L for female. Gender mean of high homocysteine concentration is 10.9 µmol/L) [5] 

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnancy [AND] lactation 
Patients [AND] with diabetes, Ischemic heart disease (IHD) [OR] stroke [OR] malignancy [OR] psychiatric diseases are excluded from study. 
The patients [AND] receiving vitamin supplements [OR] who had clinical evidence for an acute illness, renal dysfunction, thyroid dysfunction, chronic inflammatory diseases, inborn errors of homocysteine, cobalamin [OR] folate metabolism, [OR] any other condition known to interfere with homocysteine metabolism will be excluded 
Patients [AND] who are already involved in any other trial. 
Patients [NOT] willing to fill consent/ assent form are also excluded from study.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
