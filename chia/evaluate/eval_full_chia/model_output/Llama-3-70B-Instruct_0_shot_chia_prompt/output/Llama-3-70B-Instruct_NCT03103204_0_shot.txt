<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Moderate [AND] to advanced generalized chronic periodontitis
Body mass index: > 18.5 kg/m2
Minimum of 12 natural teeth
Smokers [OR] non-smokers [OR] former-smokers

Exclusion Criteria:
Systemic diseases ([AND] diabetes [OR] renal diseases [OR] rheumatic diseases [OR] osteoporosis [OR] cardiovascular diseases)
Pregnant [AND] lactating women
HIV/AIDS
[NOT] periodontal treatment in the last year (before baseline appointment)
Medication: [NOT] Immunosuppressive drugs [OR] antibiotics in the past three months (before baseline appointment)
[NOT] orthodontic appliance
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
