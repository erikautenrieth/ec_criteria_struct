Inclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] underwent percutaneous coronary intervention [AND] with drug-eluting stent;

Exclusion Criteria:
Taking other drugs which can influence the lipid profile (eg. Niacin [OR] Fibrates);
Serum creatinine level > 2.0 mg/dL
Serum aspartate transaminase > 3 times upper limit of normal
Serum alanine transaminase > 3 times upper limit of normal
[NOT] Having anaphylactic reaction [AND] for Rosuvastatin;
[NOT] Having the other contraindications [AND] for Rosuvastatin;
[NOT] Having plan [AND] to be pregnant;
[NOT] Having life expectancy less than 1 year
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
