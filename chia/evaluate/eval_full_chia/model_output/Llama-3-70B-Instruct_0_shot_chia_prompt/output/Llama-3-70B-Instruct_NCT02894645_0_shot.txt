<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Confirmed diagnosis of non-Burkitt B-lineage ALL
[AND] 1 to 17 years of age ([OR] before 18th birthday)
Renal function [AND] within normal range for age
Liver function [AND] within normal range for age
Able [AND] to participate in the full 2 years of treatment

Exclusion Criteria:
Age [NOT] less than one year [OR] age greater than/[OR] equals to 18 years
Previous treatment [AND] with cytotoxic agents [OR] high-dose steroids
Mixed phenotype acute leukemia (MPAL)
ALL [AND] as secondary malignancy
Abnormal renal [OR] liver function
Doubtful compliance [OR] unable [NOT] to afford full course of therapy
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
