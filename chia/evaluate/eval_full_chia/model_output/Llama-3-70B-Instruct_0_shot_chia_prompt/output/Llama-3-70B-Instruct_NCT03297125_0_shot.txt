Inclusion Criteria:
Newly diagnosed glioblastoma (GBM) [AND] WHO grade IV.

Exclusion Criteria:
Optune compliance < 75% [AND] they would be excluded from the final analyses.
History of craniectomy [AND] significant skull defect [OR] contraindication to Optune.
Active implantable medical device (i.e. DBS [OR] spinal cord stimulator [OR] pacemaker [OR] defibrillator [OR] vagus nerve stimulator [OR] programmable shunt).
Karnofsky Performance Status (KPS) < 60 [AND] [NOT] ≥ 60.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
