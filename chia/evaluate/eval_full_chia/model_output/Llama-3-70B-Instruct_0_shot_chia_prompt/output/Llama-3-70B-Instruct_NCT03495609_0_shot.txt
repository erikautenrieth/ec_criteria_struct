Inclusion Criteria:
premenopausal women [AND] BRCA1 carrier

Exclusion Criteria:
History of allergic reaction to compounds of similar chemical [OR] biologic composition to hCG [AND] receiving medication that could interfere with the study protocol objectives ([AND] hormonal contraceptives [OR] androgens [OR] prednisone [OR] thyroid hormones [OR] insulin)
[NOT] previous treatment with follicle stimulating hormone for assisted reproduction
[NOT] uncontrolled intercurrent illness
[NOT] Heart disease
[NOT] Severe cognitive decline
[NOT] Psychiatric desease
[NOT] HIV positive
[NOT] Hepatitis B [OR] C infection
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
