<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
A diagnosis of VTE [AND] in outpatient clinic [OR] as discharge diagnosis after hospitalization.
A claimed prescription of a NOAC from a Danish pharmacy [AND] within 14 days of discharge [OR] outpatient clinic visit.

Exclusion Criteria:
A prescription of a NOAC [AND] within 90 days prior to hospitalization [OR] outpatient clinic visit for VTE.
Patients [AND] with NOAC preference [NOT] apart from preference consistent with current cluster randomized NOAC.
Other contraindications [AND] mentioned in the "Summary of Product Characteristics" [AND] for the respective NOAC.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
