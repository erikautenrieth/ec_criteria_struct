Inclusion Criteria:
Adult patient (male [OR] female) requiring emergency endotracheal intubation [AND] who must have emergency endotracheal intubation.

Exclusion Criteria:
Children (<18 years old).
Women who are known [AND] pregnant.
Any patient who has been previously randomized [AND] in the EvK Trial.
Patients who require endotracheal intubation [NOT] with sedative medication. For example, patients in full cardiac arrest.
Patients with a known allergy [AND] to ketamine [OR] etomidate.
Any individual wearing a MedAlert bracelet indicating [AND] that he/she has formally opted out [AND] of the EvK Trial.
