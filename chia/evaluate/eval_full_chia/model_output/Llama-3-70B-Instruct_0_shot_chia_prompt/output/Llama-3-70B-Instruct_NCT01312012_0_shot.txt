Inclusion Criteria:
pregnant women [AND] in 30 [OR] 32 weeks of gestation, [AND] with positive HBsAg [AND] HBeAg, serum viral load above 8log10 copies per mL

Exclusion Criteria:
major systemic disease
Pregnant woman [AND] infection of human immunodeficiency virus [OR] hepatitis C virus
Pregnant woman [AND] is receiving any drug [AND] with antiviral activity [OR] any form of drug therapy [AND] for hepatitis B virus
Pregnant woman [AND] whose ultrasonographic examination reveals congenital anomaly of the fetus
Pregnant woman [AND] whose amniocentesis reveals any genetic abnormality
