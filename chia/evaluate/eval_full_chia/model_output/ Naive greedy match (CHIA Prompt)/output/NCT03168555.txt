planned elective cholecystectomy

Exclusion Criteria:

known chronic diarrheal disease (celiac disease, [OR] lactose malabsorption, [OR] Inflammatory bowel diseases, [OR] incl microscopic colitis)

chronic cholecystitis

suspected obstructive choledocholithiasis
icterus
