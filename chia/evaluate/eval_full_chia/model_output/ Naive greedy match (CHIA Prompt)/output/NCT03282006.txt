Inclusion Criteria:
E.coli in blood culture
AND identical isolate in urine sample (>= 1.000 CFU) OR relevant clinical signs [AND] of UTI

Exclusion Criteria:
Bacterial infection origin from another organ (e.g. pneumonia)
Severe sepsis [AND] with multiorgan failure
Perinephritic abscess
Pyonephrosis [AND] requiring drainage
Allergy [AND] to pivmecillinam
E.coli isolate resistant [AND] to pivmecillinam
Pregnancy/ [OR] breastfeeding
Severe neutropenia
Prostatitis
Severe kidney failure (eGFR<15 ml/ [OR] min)
Using valproate
