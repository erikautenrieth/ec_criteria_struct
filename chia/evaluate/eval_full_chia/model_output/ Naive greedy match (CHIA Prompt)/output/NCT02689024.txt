Inclusion Criteria:
adult patients aged = 55 years [AND] with
a radiographically confirmed hip fracture

Exclusion Criteria:
multiple injuries (polytrauma patients)
previous adverse reaction [OR] or known allergy [AND] to local anaesthetics [OR] or opioids [OR] or paracetamol
skin infection in proximity [AND] of injection site
delirious state at presentation in the ED
