Inclusion Criteria:
need mechanical ventilation [AND] for more than 2 days
mean blood pressure more than 60mmHg
predicted ICU stay more than 7 days
tolerance [AND] of parenteral [OR] or enteral nutrition

Exclusion Criteria:
irreversible status [AND] of primary disease
any history [AND] of malnutrition before enrollment
history [AND] of steroid cortisol administration
severe liver dysfunction (Child-Pugh Score C)
pregnancy
refuse [AND] to enrollment
re-admission [AND] to ICU [AND] and has been enrolled [AND] during former admission [AND] to ICU
