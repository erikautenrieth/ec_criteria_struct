Moderate [AND] to severe COPD (post-bronchodilator forced expiratory volume in 1 s (FEV1) 30-79%predicted);
Resting functional residual capacity (FRC) >120% predicted;
Clinically stable [AND] and [AND] on stable triple therapy [AND] with an ICS/ [OR] LABA [AND] and tiotropium;
Symptomatic: Baseline Dyspnea Index =8 [AND] and answer "in the morning" when asked about what time [AND] of day their COPD symptoms [AND] are worst.

Exclusion Criteria:
A diagnosis [AND] of sleep disordered breathing;
