Inclusion Criteria:
Patients underwent percutaneous coronary intervention [AND] with drug-eluting stent;

Exclusion Criteria:
Taking other drugs which can influence the lipid profile (eg. Niacin, [OR] Fibrates;
Serum creatinine level > 2.0 mg/ [OR] dL
Serum aspartate transaminase > 3 times upper limit [AND] of normal
Serum alanine transaminase > 3 times upper limit [AND] of normal
Having anaphylactic reaction [AND] for Rosuvastatin;
Having the other contraindications [AND] for Rosuvastatin;
Having plan [AND] to be pregnant;
Having life expectancy less than 1 year
