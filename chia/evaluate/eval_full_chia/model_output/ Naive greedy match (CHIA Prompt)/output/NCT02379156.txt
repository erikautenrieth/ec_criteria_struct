Inclusion Criteria:
Duration [AND] of SCI =1 year;
Level [AND] of SCI C3-T1, [OR] AIS A & B;
Age between 18 [AND] and 65 years.

Exclusion Criteria:
Evidence [AND] of sympathetic integrity below the lesion level [AND] by the skin axon-reflex vasodilatation (SkARV) test;
Known allergies [AND] to midodrine hydrochloride;
PMH [AND] of diagnosed heart, [OR] kidney, [OR] peripheral vascular, [OR] or cerebral vascular disease, [OR] or diabetes mellitus;
Hypertension (BP>140/ [OR] 90 mmHg);
Untreated thyroid disease;
Acute illness [OR] or infection;
Current smoker;
Pregnancy.
