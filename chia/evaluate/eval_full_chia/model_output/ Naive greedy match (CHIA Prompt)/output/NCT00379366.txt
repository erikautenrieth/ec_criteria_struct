Inclusion Criteria:
over 18 years 
successful angioplasty (residual stenosis < 30%) [AND] on a significant stenosis (maximal systolic speed 3 times > from basal maximal systolic speed, [OR] stenosis > 70% [AND] on angiography) [AND] on the venous-prosthesis anastomosis [OR] or [AND] on the venous segment 5 cm after the anastomosis [AND] of a prosthetic haemodialysis vascular access (at least 1 month old) 
social security affiliation 
signed informed consent 

Exclusion Criteria:
contra-indications [AND] of radiotherapy 
angioplasty [AND] with stenting
