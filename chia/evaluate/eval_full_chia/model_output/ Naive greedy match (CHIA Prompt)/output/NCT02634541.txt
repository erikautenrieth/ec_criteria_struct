Inclusion Criteria:
Axial spondyloarthritis (ASAS criteria) [AND] and radiologic sacroiliitis [AND] as detected either [AND] by MRI [OR] or X-ray.

Exclusion Criteria:
Psoriasis [OR] or psoriasis arthropathy
Inflammatory bowel disease
Unwillingness [AND] to participate in the study [AND] with additional imaging protocols
Expected life-span less than <1 year
Diabetes ([AND] to improve the PET imaging quality)
Probable noncompliance
Pregnancy
Age <18 years [OR] or >75 years
Contraindication [AND] for adalimumab
Methotrexate used [AND] within the previous 6 months
A biologic medicine used [AND] within the previous 6 months
