Inclusion Criteria:
H pylori infection failed after at least two eradication therapies
aged 20 years [OR] or greater
willingness [AND] to receive rescue therapy

Exclusion Criteria:
aged less than 20 years
history [AND] of gastric resection surgery
history [AND] of allergy [AND] to study drugs
pregnancy [OR] or lactating women
severe underlying illness, [OR] such [AND] as end stage renal disease, [OR] decompensated liver cirrhosis, [OR] or non-curative malignancy
