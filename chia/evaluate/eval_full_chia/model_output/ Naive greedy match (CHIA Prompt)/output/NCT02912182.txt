Inclusion Criteria:
definite unilateral vestibulopathy
[NOT] no pathological HINTS (examination criteria in acute vestibular syndrome)
capable [AND] of making their own decisions

Exclusion Criteria:
tinnitus [OR] or hearing loss [AND] with same debut [AND] as vertigo
history [AND] of bleeding peptic ulcer
glaucoma
pregnancy [OR] or non-acceptance [AND] to use anticonception measures [AND] during 13 days after debut
high blood pressure >180 systolic, [OR] 105, [OR] diastolic
ketoacidosis [AND] with a Base Excess >=2
psychic disorder ([NOT] not [AND] including mild depression)
serious infection (neutropenia, [OR] tuberculosis)
chronic otitis
history [AND] of vertiginous disease; Ménière, [OR] Vertiginous migraine, [OR] atypical BPPV
