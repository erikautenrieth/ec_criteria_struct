Inclusion Criteria:
18-50 ages
Singleton pregnancy
Cervical length <=25mm between 18(0) [AND] and 23(6) weeks

Exclusion Criteria:
Multiple pregnancy
Prior spontaneous preterm birth [OR] or second trimester losses between 16(0) [AND] and 36(6) weeks
Cerclage in situ
Painful regular uterine contraction [AND] and/ [OR] or preterm labor
Ruptured membranes
Major fetal defects
Active vaginal bleeding
Placenda previa [AND] and/ [OR] or accreta
Cervical dilation >1.5 cm [AND] and/ [OR] or visible membranes [AND] by pelvic exam
Suspicion [AND] of chorioamnionitis
