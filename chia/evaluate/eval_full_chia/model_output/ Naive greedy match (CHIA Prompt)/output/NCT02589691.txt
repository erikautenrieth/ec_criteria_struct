Inclusion Criteria:
age <2 years
indication [AND] of general anesthesia [AND] with tracheal intubation
inhalational induction scheduled
written informed consent [AND] of both parents

Exclusion Criteria:
contra-indication [AND] to inhalational induction (full stomach)
contra-indication [AND] to the use [AND] of rocuronium
American Society [AND] of Anesthesiologists score (ASA) III [OR] or IV
intracranial surgery
parental refusal
[NOT] absence [AND] of affiliation [AND] to social security
