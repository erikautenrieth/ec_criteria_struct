1. Presence [AND] of bacteremia [AND] due solely [AND] to: 
S. aureus [AND] on at least 1 blood culture [AND] within 72 hours [AND] of beginning study drug (Cohort A) OR 
MRSA [AND] on a baseline blood culture [AND] and [AND] on at least 1 additional blood culture after at least 72 hours [AND] of vancomycin [AND] and/ [OR] or daptomycin treatment (Cohort B).

1. For subjects in Cohort A: previous therapy [AND] for more than 48 hours [AND] with any parenteral antibiotic [AND] with activity against S. aureus [AND] within 72 hours [AND] of positive blood culture results.

4. Known left-sided endocarditis [OR] or prosthetic heart valve. 
5. Osteomyelitis [OR] or prosthetic joint infection except new onset nonhardware-associated vertebral osteomyelitis.

7. Evidence [AND] of significant hepatic, [OR] hematologic, [OR] or immunologic impairment.
