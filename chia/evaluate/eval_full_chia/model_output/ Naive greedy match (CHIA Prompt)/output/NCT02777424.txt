Inclusion Criteria:
Patient [AND] with spontaneous intracranial hemorrhage [OR] or traumatic intracranial hemorrhage [OR] or patient [AND] requiring neurological surgery
Coagulation disorder defined [AND] by PT less than 60%

Exclusion Criteria:
Concomitant use [AND] with oral anticoagulant drugs
Acquired deficiency [AND] of coagulation factors whose treatment is established
Hypersensitivity [AND] to a PCC
History [AND] of thrombocytopenia induced [AND] by heparin
Disseminated intravascular coagulation
Extracranial active bleeding
Hypersensitivity [AND] to vitamin K
