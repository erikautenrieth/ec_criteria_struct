Inclusion Criteria:
Mild male factor infertility [OR] or unexplained infertility.

Exclusion Criteria:
Advanced male factor infertility.
Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) [AND] as defined [AND] by the Rotterdam criteria.
Endometriosis.
Tubal disease.
Uterine abnormalities [OR] or myoma.
Previous uterine surgery.
Metabolic [OR] or hormonal abnormalities.
