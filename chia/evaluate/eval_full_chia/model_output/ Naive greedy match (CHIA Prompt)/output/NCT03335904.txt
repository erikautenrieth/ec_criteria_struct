Inclusion Criteria:
normotensive
forced expiratory volume in 1s : forced vital capacity ratio > 0.75
[NOT] no medical history [AND] of cardiovascular [AND] and respiratory disease
[NOT] not taking medications [NOT] other than oral contraceptives
[NOT] free from sleep apnea
body mass index less than 30 kg/ [OR] m2

Exclusion Criteria:
history [AND] of hypertension
known impaired renal function
liver disease
heart failure
myocardial infarction
coronary artery disease
smoked [AND] within the past year
apnea hypopnea index > 5 events per hour
