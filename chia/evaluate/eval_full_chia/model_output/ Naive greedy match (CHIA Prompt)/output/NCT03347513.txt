Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosed Iron deficiency anemia.
H-pylori positive cases.
Second trimester pregnancy.

Exclusion Criteria:
Severe Iron deficiency anemia (hemoglobin < 8.0 g/ [OR] dL).
Parasitic worm infection e.g. schistosomiasis, [AND] and hook worm [AND] by stool analysis.
Any cases giving clinical symptoms [AND] of gastritis e.g. nausea, [OR] vomiting, [OR] dull aching pain [OR] or soreness in the epigastrium.
Cases [AND] with history [AND] of gastric ulcer diagnosed [AND] by upper endoscopy.
Cases complaining [AND] of hematemesis.
