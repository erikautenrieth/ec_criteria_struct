Inclusion Criteria:
Type I diabetic patients
Parturients presented [AND] for Cesarean section

Exclusion Criteria:
Contraindications [AND] for spinal anesthesia (like bleeding diathesis [OR] or regional infection at site [AND] of neuroaxial block)
Known allergy [AND] to Granisetron [OR] or local anaesthetic (heavy bupivacaine, [OR] Marcaine Spinal 0.5% Heavy, [OR] 5mg/ [OR] ml, [OR] AstraZeneca ampule)
Pregnancy induced hypertension
Congenital [OR] or rheumatic heart diseases
Antepartum haemorrhage
Fetal destress [OR] or gestational age < 36 week
