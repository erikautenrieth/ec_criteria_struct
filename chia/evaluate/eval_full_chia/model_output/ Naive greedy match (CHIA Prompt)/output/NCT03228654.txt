Inclusion Criteria:
uterine size <12 weeks.
presence [AND] of benign cause [AND] for the hysterectomy e.g. fibroid uterus, [OR] perimenopausal beeding [NOT] not responding [AND] to medical treatment [OR] or complex endometrial hyperplasia [NOT] without atypia.
Absence [AND] of significant scarring in the pelvis from previous surgeries.

Exclusion Criteria:
Suspected [OR] or known gynecological malignancy.
uterine size >12 weeks.
Endometriosis
Presence [AND] of adnexal mass.
cervix flushed [AND] with the vagina.
presence [AND] of significant scarring in the pelvic area from previous surgery.
