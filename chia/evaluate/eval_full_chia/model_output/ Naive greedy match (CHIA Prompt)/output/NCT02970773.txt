Inclusion Criteria:
Motor complete tetraplegia [AND] for at least 3 months
Age from 18 [AND] to 74 years
Body mass index (BMI) from 18 [AND] to 35kg/ [OR] m2
Informed consent [AND] as documented [AND] by signature

Any anti-coagulation therapy (apart from rivaroxaban [AND] for second objective)

Bleeding disorder
