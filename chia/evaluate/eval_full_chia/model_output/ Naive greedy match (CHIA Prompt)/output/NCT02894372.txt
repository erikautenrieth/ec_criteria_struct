Inclusion Criteria:
Patients after throat surgeries: tonsillectomy, [OR] adenotonsillectomy, [OR] uvulopalatoplasty, [OR] uvulopalatopharyngoplasty 
Patients [AND] with acute throat diseases: pharyngitis, [OR] tonsillitis, [OR] pharyngotonsillitis 

Exclusion Criteria:
Purulent infection 
Refusal [AND] to participate 
Allergy [AND] to tested material
