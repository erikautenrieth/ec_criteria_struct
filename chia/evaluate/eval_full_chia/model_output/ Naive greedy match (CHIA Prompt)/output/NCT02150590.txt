Inclusion Criteria:
chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), [OR] GOLD grade 2-3
residents at low altitude (<800 m)

Exclusion Criteria:
unstable condition, [OR] COPD exacerbation
mild (GOLD 1) [OR] or very severe COPD (GOLD 4)
requirement [AND] for oxygen therapy at low altitude residence
hypoventilation
pulmonary hypertension
more than mild [OR] or unstable cardiovascular disease
use [AND] of drugs [AND] that affect respiratory center drive
internal, [OR] neurologic [OR] or psychiatric disease [AND] that interfere [AND] with protocol compliance [AND] including current heavy smoking (>20 cigarettes per day), [OR] inability [AND] to perform 6 min walk test.
previous intolerance [AND] to moderate altitude (<2600m).
exposure [AND] to altitudes >1500m [AND] for >2 days [AND] within the last 4 weeks before the study.
pregnant [OR] or nursing patients
