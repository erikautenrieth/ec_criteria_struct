Inclusion Criteria:
Elective open abdominal hysterectomy [AND] with midline incision, [OR] age > 18 years, [OR] American Society [AND] of Anesthesiologist classification score (ASA classification) 1-3.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patient [AND] with a chronic pain condition, [OR] major unexpected surgical complication, [OR] unexpected prolonged intubation, [OR] patient refusal, [OR] local anesthetic allergy, [OR] any contraindication [AND] to regional anesthesia, [OR] greater than 2 attempts [AND] by resident [AND] and greater than 1 attempt [AND] by staff anesthesiologist [AND] for TAP block.
