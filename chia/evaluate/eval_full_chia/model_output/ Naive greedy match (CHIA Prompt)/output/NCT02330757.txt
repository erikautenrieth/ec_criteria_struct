Inclusion Criteria:
Women [NOT] without PCOS [AND] as defined [AND] by the Rotterdam criteria.
Presence [AND] of at least 2 cryopreserved good quality cleavage-stage embryo (good quality cleavage-stage embryos display stage-specific cell division, [AND] have blastomeres [AND] of fairly equal size [AND] with few [AND] to [NOT] no cytoplasmic fragments).

Exclusion Criteria:
PCOS [OR] or polycystic ovary [AND] on ultrasound scan.
Moderate [OR] or severe endometriosis.
Hydrosalpinx.
Uterine abnormalities [OR] or myoma.
Previous uterine surgery.
