Inclusion Criteria:
Informed consent
Diagnosis [AND] of type 2 diabetes (HbA1c > 48 mmol/ [OR] mol)
Age older than 30 years

Exclusion Criteria:
Type 1 diabetes
Treatment [AND] with insulin
Body weight > 140 kg
HbA1c > 75 mmol/ [OR] mol
Treatment [AND] with GLP-1 analogues, [OR] Dipeptidyl peptidase-4 inhibitors, [OR] or glitazones
Chronic kidney disease
Hepatic disease
Pancreatitis
Inflammatory bowel disease
Osteoporosis
Family [OR] or personal history [AND] of medullary thyroid carcinoma
Treatment [AND] with glucocorticoids
Hormone replacement therapy
Diabetic gastroparesis
Pregnancy [OR] or lactation
