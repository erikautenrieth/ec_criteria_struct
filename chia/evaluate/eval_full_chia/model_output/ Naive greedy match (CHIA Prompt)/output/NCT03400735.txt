Inclusion Criteria:
The diagnosis [AND] of chronic bronchitis
The diagnosis [AND] of community-acquired pneumoniae
FEV1 value = 30-80%
The diagnosis [AND] of mild-severe acute exacerbation [AND] of chronic bronchitis (AECB)
Oxygen saturation < 90%

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding
Allergy against [AND] to penicillin [OR] or cephalosporins
Renal impairment
Active hepatic disease
Antibiotic use except study drugs
Immunosuppressive therapy before 6 months [AND] of study initiation
Use [AND] of probenecid like drugs
