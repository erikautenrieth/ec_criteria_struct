Inclusion Criteria:
Adolescent (10-21 years) undergoing spinal fusion [AND] for idiopathic scoliosis, [OR] spondylolisthesis [OR] or Scheuermann kyphosis.
Posterior spinal fusion
No contraindication [AND] for Pregabalin use
ASA I-III
Written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Other spinal pathology [OR] or other associated medical condition
Major neurologic developmental delay
Need [AND] for anterior surgery [OR] or [AND] for vertebral column resection.
Preoperative opioid use
[NOT] Inability [AND] to use PCA
