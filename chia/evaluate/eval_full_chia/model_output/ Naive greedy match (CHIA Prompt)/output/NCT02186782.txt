Inclusion Criteria:
Infertile women [AND] with eugonadotrophic anovulation/ [OR] oligoovulation.
Unexplained infertility.

Exclusion Criteria:
Age < 20 [OR] or > 35 years.
Body mass index (BMI) < 18.5 kg/ [OR] m2 [OR] or > 25 kg/ [OR] m2.
Presence [AND] of any infertility factor [NOT] other than anovulation/ [OR] oligoovulation.
Previous history [AND] of ovarian surgery [OR] or surgical removal [AND] of one ovary.
Previous exposure [AND] to cytotoxic drugs [OR] or pelvic irradiation.
Metabolic [OR] or hormonal abnormalities.
