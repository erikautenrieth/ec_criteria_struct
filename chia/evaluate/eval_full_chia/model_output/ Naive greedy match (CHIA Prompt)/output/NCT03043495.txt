Inclusion Criteria:
Patients undergoing surgeries in the upper limb (arm, [OR] forearm [OR] or hand)

Exclusion Criteria:
Coagulopathies ([AND] with prothrombin concentration less than 60% [OR] or INR more than 1.5)
In-ability [AND] to postpone anti-coagulation medications.
Infection [OR] or injury [OR] or a lesion at the block site.
Suspected cervical vertebral column injury necessitating using a neck collar.
A compromised lung [AND] on the contralateral side [AND] of the block (Pneumothorax, [OR] hemothorax [OR] or Pneumonectomy).
Traumatic vascular injuries [OR] or operative interventions (Surgical harvesting) involving arteries [AND] of the upper limb [AND] on the operative side.
Patients [AND] with communication difficulties.
Hypersensitivity [AND] to local anesthetics [AND] and/ [OR] or Dexamethasone.
Patients [AND] on perioperative intravenous (IV) steroids.
