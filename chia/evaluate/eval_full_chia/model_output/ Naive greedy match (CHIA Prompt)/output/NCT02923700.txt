Inclusion Criteria:
patients affected [AND] by mono-lateral symptomatic knee articular degenerative pathology [AND] with history [AND] of chronic ([AND] for at least 4 months) pain [OR] or swelling;
imaging findings [AND] of degenerative changes [AND] of the joint (osteoarthritis [OR] or chondropathy [AND] with Kellgren Lawrence Score from 0 [AND] to 3 at X-ray evaluation).

Exclusion Criteria:
age > 80 years;
Kellgren-Lawrence score at X-ray evaluation > 3;
major axial deviation (varus >5° , [OR] valgus > 5°), [OR] systemic disorders such [AND] as diabetes, [OR] rheumatoid arthritis, [OR] haematological diseases (coagulopathy), [OR] severe cardiovascular diseases, [OR] infections, [OR] immunodepression;
patients in therapy [AND] with anticoagulants [OR] or antiaggregants;
use [AND] of NSAIDs in the 5 days before blood donation;
patients [AND] with Hb values < 11 g/ [OR] dl [AND] and platelet values < 150,000/ [OR] mmc.
