Inclusion Criteria:
Accepted [AND] for CABG surgery
Treatment [AND] with Ticagrelor [AND] within 48 hours

Exclusion Criteria:
Anticoagulation therapy
Prior CABG.
Active bleeding [OR] or at high risk [AND] of bleeding
Severe liver [OR] or renal disease.
Hypersensitivity [AND] to ticagrelor
History [AND] of intracranial hemorrhage
