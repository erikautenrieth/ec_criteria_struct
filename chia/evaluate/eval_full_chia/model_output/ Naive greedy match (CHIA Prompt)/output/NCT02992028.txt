Inclusion Criteria:
Rotator cuff tear patients undergoing arthroscopic rotator cuff tear

Exclusion Criteria:
age <45 [OR] or >80
allergies [AND] to medications used in the study
history [AND] of renal diseases, [OR] a coagulation abnormality, [OR] a hepatic disease, [OR] or drug abuse
definite radiographic evidence [AND] of osteoarthritis [AND] of the glenohumeral joint
inflammatory arthritis [AND] including rheumatoid arthritis
a history [AND] of acute trauma
systemic conditions associated [AND] with chronic pain
a history [AND] of infection
an inability [AND] to understand the questionnaires
