Inclusion Criteria:
Moderate [AND] to advanced generalized chronic periodontitis
Body mass index: > 18.5 kg/ [OR] m2
Minimum [AND] of 12 natural teeth
Smokers, [OR] non-smokers [OR] or former-smokers

Exclusion Criteria:
Systemic diseases (diabetes, [OR] renal diseases, [OR] rheumatic diseases, [OR] osteoporosis [AND] and cardiovascular diseases)
Pregnant [AND] and lactating women
HIV/ [OR] AIDS
periodontal treatment in the last year (before baseline appointment)
Medication: Immunosuppressive drugs, [OR] antibiotics in the past three months (before baseline appointment) )
orthodontic appliance
