Inclusion Criteria:
obese : weight [AND] for height > median + 3 standard deviations
simple obesity

Exclusion Criteria:
pathological obesity
chronic diseases e.g. cerebral palsy, [OR] metabolic disease, [OR] etc.
diseases [AND] of red blood cells
[AND] on medication e.g. steroid, [OR] multivitamins, [OR] thiamine-containing vitamins, [OR] diuretic drugs
hemodialysis [OR] or peritoneal dialysis
bariatric surgery
