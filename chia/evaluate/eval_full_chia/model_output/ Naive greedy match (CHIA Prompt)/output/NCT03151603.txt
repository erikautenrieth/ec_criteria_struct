Inclusion Criteria:
Women (18-75 years) [AND] with suspected UTI
at least two symptoms [AND] of UTI (dysuria, [OR] urgency [AND] of micturition, [OR] frequency, [OR] lower abdominal pain)
Written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
signs [AND] of complicated UTI (e. g. temperature > 38°C, [OR] loin tenderness)
conditions [AND] that [AND] may lead [AND] to complicated infections (i.e. renal diseases, [OR] patients [AND] with urinary catheter)
pregnancy/ [OR] breastfeeding
current self-medication [AND] with UU preparations e.g. z.B. Cystinol®, [OR] Uvalysat®, [OR] Arctuvan®
antibiotic use in the last 7 days
previous UTI in the past 2 weeks
history [AND] of pyelonephritis
contraindications [AND] for trial drugs
serious diseases
inability [AND] to understand trial Information
current participation in another clinical trial [OR] or participation in another clinical trial [AND] within the last 4 weeks
