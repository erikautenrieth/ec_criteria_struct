Inclusion Criteria:
histologically diagnosed primary classical osteosarcoma in extremities
staging IIB
MRI showing [NOT] no skip lesion
receive standard neo-adjuvant chemotherapy, [OR] adjuvant chemotherapy,[AND] and standard surgical treatment

Exclusion Criteria:
a history [AND] of non-standard treatment(chemotherapy [OR] or surgery)
secondary osteosarcoma [OR] or well-differentiated parosteal osteosarcoma
evident dysfunction [AND] of cardia,liver [AND] and kidney, [OR] or pregnant women [OR] or women [AND] during lactation
