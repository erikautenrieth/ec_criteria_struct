Inclusion Criteria:
Moderate [AND] to severe rheumatoid arthritis
Taking methotrexate [NOT] without adequate control [AND] of symptoms
Have at least one cardiovascular risk factor (eg, [OR] current smoker, [OR] high blood pressure, [OR] high cholesterol levels, [OR] diabetes mellitus, [OR] history [AND] of heart attack, [OR] family history [AND] of coronary heart disease, [OR] extra-articular RA disease)

Exclusion Criteria:
Current [OR] or recent infection
Clinically significant laboratory abnormalities
Pregnancy
