Inclusion Criteria:
age>18 years
critical limb ischemia (Rutherford class 4-6)
angiographic stenosis>50% [OR] or occlusion [AND] of at least one tibial vessel [AND] of at least 40mm [AND] for which an interventional treatment is scheduled

Exclusion Criteria:
need [AND] for major amputation known before intervention
allergy [AND] to Paclitaxel
contraindication [AND] for combined antiplatelet treatment
life expectancy <1 year
hypersensitivity [OR] or contraindication [AND] to one [AND] of the study drugs
lack [AND] of consent
