Inclusion Criteria:
Primary periampullary tumor
R0, [OR] R1 resection
Chronic pancreatitis [AND] requiring pancreatoduodenectomy

Exclusion Criteria:
Metastatic tumor
Locally unresectable tumor
Previous gastric resection
ASA IV-V
Age under 18 years
Preoperative complete parenteral [OR] or enteral feeding
Immunosuppressive therapy before operation
Severe malnutrition
Lack [AND] of the patient's consent [AND] for the trial participation, [OR] feeding tube insertion [OR] or epidural analgesia
