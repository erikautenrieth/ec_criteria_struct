Inclusion Criteria:
aged between 3 - 36 months
[AND] having primary corrective heart surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
recent surgery (< 3 months)
previous chemotherapy
previous transfusion [AND] of blood products
neurodevelopmental disorders ([AND] including Trisomy 21)
supplemental oxygen requirement (< 3 months)
asthma [AND] requiring regular therapy
obstructive sleep apnea
the presence [AND] of concurrent infection [OR] or inflammation
a known allergy [AND] to dexmedetomidine hydrochloride
