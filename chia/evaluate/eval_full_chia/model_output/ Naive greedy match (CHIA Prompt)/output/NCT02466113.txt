Inclusion Criteria:

With confirmed diagnosis [AND] of stage II colon cancer. 
With moderate/ [OR] good ECOG health rating (PS): 0-1 score. 
The patient receive [NOT] no anti-cancer treatment before primary surgery. 
The patient receive radical operation [AND] for colon cancer [AND] with negative margin. 

Exclusion Criteria:
With severe comorbidities, [OR] such [AND] as cardiovascular disease, [OR] chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, [OR] diabetes mellitus, [AND] and chronic renal dysfunction. 
With bad compliance [OR] or contraindication [AND] to enrollment.
