<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Age 18-65 years
History of snake bite [AND] features of local envenomation [AND] with/ [OR] without systemic features
Less than 24 hours since bite, [AND]
[NOT] prior antibiotic treatment

Exclusion Criteria:
Upper limb bites
Multiple (> 1) bites
Wound manipulation
Extensive local necrosis [OR] blebs
Seriously-ill patients [AND] hypotension/[OR] capillary leak/[OR] life threatening bleeding.
Suspected cobra bite, [OR]
Pregnant/[OR] breast-feeding women
