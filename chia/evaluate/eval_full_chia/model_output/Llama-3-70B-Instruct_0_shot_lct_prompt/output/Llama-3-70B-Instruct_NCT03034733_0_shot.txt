<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
primary total knee replacement surgery [AND] ASA (american society of anesthesiologists) class 1[OR]2[OR]3

Exclusion Criteria:
severe coronary artery disease [OR] heart failure [OR] kidney failure
insulin-dependent DM (diabetes mellitus) [OR] poorly controlled type II DM
gastric[OR]duodenal ulcer
allergy[OR]contra-indication for any drug used in the study
[NOT] corticosteroid use during last 3 months
preoperative use of opioid drugs ([NOT] excl. codeine, tramadol)
neuropathy[OR]sensory impairment of lower limbs
[NOT] lack of co-operation, e.g. [NOT] inability to use a PCA (patient controlled analgesia)-device
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
