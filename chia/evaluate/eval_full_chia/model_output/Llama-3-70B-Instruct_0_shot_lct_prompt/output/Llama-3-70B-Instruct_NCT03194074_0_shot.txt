<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients scheduled for laser laryngeal surgery under general anesthesia [AND] with either Propofol [OR] desflurane based technique.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with cardiac, pulmonary, hepatic, [OR] renal dysfunction, epilepsy, [OR] uncontrolled hypertension, [OR] those taking medications that influence the central nervous system, are excluded from the study. Patients who show obvious alteration of mental status, [OR] refuse to participate, are also excluded from the study.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
