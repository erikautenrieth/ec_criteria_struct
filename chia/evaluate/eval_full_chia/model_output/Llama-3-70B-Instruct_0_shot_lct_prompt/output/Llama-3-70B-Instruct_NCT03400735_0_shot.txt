<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
The diagnosis of chronic bronchitis
The diagnosis of community-acquired pneumoniae
FEV1 value = 30 [OR] 80%
The diagnosis of mild [AND] severe acute exacerbation of chronic bronchitis (AECB)
Oxygen saturation < 90%

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding
Allergy [AND] against to penicillin [OR] cephalosporins
Renal impairment
Active hepatic disease
[NOT] Antibiotic use except study drugs
Immunosuppressive therapy before 6 months of study initiation
Use of probenecid like drugs
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
