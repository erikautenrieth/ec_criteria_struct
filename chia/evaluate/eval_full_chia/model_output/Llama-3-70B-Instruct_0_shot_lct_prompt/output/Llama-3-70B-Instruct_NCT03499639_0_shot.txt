<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
patients were 18 years old [OR] more,
[NOT] naive to HCV treatment,
HCV genotype 4,
compensated liver disease [AND] no decompensated liver cirrhosis.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with combined HCV/HBV co-infection
hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC)
decompensated liver cirrhosis (Child-Pugh score above 6)
[NOT] genotype 4
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
