<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Age greater than 18 [AND] Planned thoracoscopy with low probability(by surgeon estimate) of conversion to open procedure 

Exclusion Criteria:
Age less than 18 [OR] Clinical [OR] laboratory evidence of systemic infection [OR] Current pregnancy as assessed by preoperative urine HCG test [OR] Serious, uncontrolled, non-malignant illness [OR] Malignant illness requiring systemic chemotherapy in the last 6 months [OR] Documented allergy to oxycodone, morphine sulfate [OR] acetaminophen [OR] Contraindication to peripheral nerve blockade [OR] general anesthesia including: 
1. patient refusal [OR] 
2. active infection at site of planned block [OR] 
3. documented allergy to any local [OR] general anesthetic medications [OR] 
4. significant coagulopathy( prothrombin time >15 seconds, INR>1.5 [OR] 
5. pre-existing neuropathy [AND] medical conditions [OR] deformities which would compromise block [OR] anesthetic safety [OR] 
Planned pleurodesis [OR] 
Current use of high dose inhaled [OR] systemic steroids [OR] 
Current use of Amiodarone (Cordarone) [OR] 
Morbid obesity (BMI=40kg/m2) [OR] 
Patients [AND] clinically significant mental health issues such as psychosis requiring treatment with antipsychotic medications [OR] 
Patients [NOT] able to consent [OR] 
Patients [AND] active infections requiring antibiotics within one month of registration [OR] 
Participation in other clinical trials that may interfere with this study
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
