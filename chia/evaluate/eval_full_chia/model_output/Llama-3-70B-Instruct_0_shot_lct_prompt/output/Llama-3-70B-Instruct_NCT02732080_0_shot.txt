Inclusion Criteria:
Patients presenting [AND] with ST-elevation acute myocardial infarction (STEMI) within 12 hours of their symptom onset [AND] in whom TIMI-3 flow was established [AND] in infarct related artery (IRA) after balloon angioplasty [OR] thrombectomy.

Exclusion Criteria:
Recanalized (TIMI I-III flow) IRA at coronary angiography.
Patients [AND] in whom TIMI-3 flow was [NOT] able to be established after wire crossing, balloon angioplasty [OR] thrombectomy.
STEMI due to bypass-graft occlusion
Patients [AND] with severe heart failure [OR] cardiogenic shock
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
