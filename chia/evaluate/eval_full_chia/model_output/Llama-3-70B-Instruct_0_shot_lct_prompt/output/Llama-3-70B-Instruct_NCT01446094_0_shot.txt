<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Aged 18 years [OR] older
Scheduled for invasive coronary angiography

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Ability to give informed consent
Possible pregnancy (confirmed by urine test)
Women [AND] who are breastfeeding
Severe claustrophobia
[NOT] Ability to lie flat for 20-30 minutes (the anticipated amount of time to complete the MRI procedure)
Individuals [AND] with cochlear implants
Individuals [AND] with non-MRI compatible aneurysm clips
Potential contraindications to regadenoson use due to:
Contraindication to administration of Gadolinium (Gd) based contrast agents (GBCA)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
