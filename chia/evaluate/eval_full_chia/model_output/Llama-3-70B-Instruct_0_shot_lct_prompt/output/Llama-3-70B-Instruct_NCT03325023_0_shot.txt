<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Written consent for participation in the clinical trial
Age 18 to 45 years [AND] Irregular menstruation (> 35 days) [OR] secondary amenorrhea > 3 months

Exclusion Criteria:
Ovarian cancer [OR] adrenal gland tumor [OR] endometrial cancer [OR] cervical cancer [OR] breast cancer
Congenital adrenal hyperplasia (17-OH-progesterone > 2.5 ng / mL)
Clinically diagnosed Cushing's disease [OR] acromegaly [OR] gigantism
Type I [OR] II diabetes
Unexplained bleeding from the genital tract
[NOT] Hormone treatment within the last 2 months
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
