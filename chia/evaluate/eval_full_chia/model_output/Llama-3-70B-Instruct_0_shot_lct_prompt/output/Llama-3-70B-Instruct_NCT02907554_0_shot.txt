<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Male [AND] females aged 18 to 70 years
Brain death
Male [AND] females aged 18 to 70 years
Indication of kidney transplantation
Informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Contra-indication for multiorgan procurement ([OR] infections, cancer, etc)
Preexistent chronic renal failure
Refusal for organ procurement by the donor ([AND] confirmed by the French national register [OR] reported by the next-of-kin)
[NOT] Need for a double kidney transplantation
[NOT] Need for a multiorgan transplantation
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
