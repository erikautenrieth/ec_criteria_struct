current diagnosis of narcolepsy [AND] cataplexy [OR] healthy control

Exclusion Criteria:
use of any sedative hypnotics [OR] tranquilizers [OR] anticonvulsants [OR] antihistamines (except [NOT] non-sedating) [OR] benzodiazepines [OR] clonidine [OR] any medication known to affect dopamine at start of baseline period
[NOT] having significant unstable [OR] uncontrolled medical/psychiatric disease
[NOT] having significant history of head trauma/surgery [OR] seizure disorder
radiation exposure exceeding 20mSv in last 12 months
[NOT] being pregnant
[NOT] having substance abuse/dependence (including alcohol)
[NOT] having sleep apnea [OR] being shift workers
[NOT] being on a sodium-restricted diet
[NOT] having ever taken Xyrem / sodium oxybate / GHB at any time
[NOT] having claustrophobia
[NOT] having metal implants / objects in the body that may interfere with MRI
[NOT] having succinic semialdehyde dehydrogenase deficiency
