<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Women (18-75 years) [AND] with suspected UTI
at least two symptoms of UTI (dysuria, [OR] urgency of micturition, [OR] frequency, [OR] lower abdominal pain)
Written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
signs of complicated UTI (e. g. temperature > 38°C, [OR] loin tenderness)
conditions that may lead to complicated infections (i.e. renal diseases, [OR] patients with urinary catheter)
pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding
current self-medication with UU preparations e.g. z.B. Cystinol®, [OR] Uvalysat®, [OR] Arctuvan®
[NOT] antibiotic use in the last 7 days
[NOT] previous UTI in the past 2 weeks
history of pyelonephritis
contraindications for trial drugs
serious diseases
[NOT] inability to understand trial Information
[NOT] current participation in another clinical trial [OR] participation in another clinical trial within the last 4 weeks
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
