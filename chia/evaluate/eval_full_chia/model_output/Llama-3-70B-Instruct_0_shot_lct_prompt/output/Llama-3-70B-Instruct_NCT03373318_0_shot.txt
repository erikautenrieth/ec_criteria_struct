Inclusion Criteria:
Adult patients (> 18 years) scheduled for cardiopulmonary bypass surgery [AND] with Glomerular Filtration Rate (GFR) greater than [OR] equal to 60 [AND] left ventricular ejection fraction greater than [OR] equal to 40%

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients who [NOT] meet the inclusion criteria [AND] those who have a history of allergic reactions to human albumin [OR] those who have received iodinated contrast during the 7 days prior to surgery [OR] pregnant women, will be excluded from the study.
