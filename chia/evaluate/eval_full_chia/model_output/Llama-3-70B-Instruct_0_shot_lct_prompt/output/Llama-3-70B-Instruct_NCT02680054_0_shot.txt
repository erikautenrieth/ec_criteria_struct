<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis of Type 1 diabetes [AND] for at least a year
On multiple daily insulin injections, including basal long-acting insulin [AND] rapid-acting insulin before each meal.
HbA1c < 75 mmol/mol (9.0%)
Participant [OR] parent/legal guardian willing [AND] able to give informed consent for participation in the study.
Family have a freezer [AND] in which to safely store the test meals.
In the Investigator's opinion, is able [AND] willing to comply with all trial requirements.

Exclusion Criteria:
HbA1c greater than 75 mmol/mol (9.0%)
Child [NOT] willing to agree to second insulin injection at a meal-time
Untreated coeliac disease [OR] other concomitant condition likely to affect BG control
Food allergies, [OR] other than controlled Coeliac Disease
Vegetarians, [OR] vegans [OR] patients with religious dietary restrictions, [NOT] as the standard meal contains meat
Participant taking any glucose-containing medication concurrently [AND] [NOT] free from such medication
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
