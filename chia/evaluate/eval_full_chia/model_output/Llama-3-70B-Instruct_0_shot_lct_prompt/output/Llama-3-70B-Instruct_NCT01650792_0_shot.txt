<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis of heart failure according to Framingham criteria
[AND] Informed consent
Age 18 years [OR] above

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with a history of an [NOT] untreated malignancy (except local skin cancers)
Ischemic stroke (determined using the Questionnaire for Verifying Stroke-Free Status (QVSFS)
Patients [AND] on renal dialysis [OR] with end-stage hepatic dysfunction
Acute infection/inflammation (Temperature > 101.5 F [OR] WBC> 15, 000)
[NOT] Ability to obtain informed consent from patient [OR] next of kin
Anticoagulant use (warfarin [OR] heparin)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
