<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Adult patient (male [OR] female) requiring emergency endotracheal intubation [AND] who is in need of sedation.

Exclusion Criteria:
Children (<18 years old).
Women [AND] who are known to be pregnant.
Any patient [AND] who has been previously randomized in the EvK Trial.
Patients [AND] who require endotracheal intubation [NOT] with sedative medication. For example, patients [AND] in full cardiac arrest.
Patients [AND] with a known allergy to ketamine [OR] etomidate.
Any individual [AND] wearing a MedAlert bracelet indicating that he/she has formally opted out of the EvK Trial.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
