<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
American Society of Anesthesiologist physical status 1 [OR] 2 [OR] 3
Scheduled for elective video-assisted thoracic surgery
[AND] Able to operate a patient-controlled analgesia device (PCA)

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] History of morphine allergy
[NOT] History of bupivacaine allergy
[NOT] Contraindication for ketamine infusion
[NOT] Contraindication for thoracic paravertebral block
Anticipated postoperative positive pressure ventilation
Body mass index more than 35
Any known psychiatric disorder
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
