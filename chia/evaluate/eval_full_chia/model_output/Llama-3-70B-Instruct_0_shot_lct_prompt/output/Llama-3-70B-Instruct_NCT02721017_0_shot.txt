<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
scheduled for Nuss procedure for pectus excavatum correction [AND] at least 13 years old at the time of the procedure

Exclusion Criteria:
age less than 13 years at time of procedure [OR] use of pain medication prior to procedure [OR] pectus carinatum [OR] Poland's syndrome [OR] any chest wall anomaly other than pectus excavatum [AND] previous repair of pectus excavatum by any technique [OR] previous thoracic surgery [OR] congenital heart disease [OR] bleeding dyscrasia [OR] major anesthetic risk factors [OR] history of previous problem with anesthesia [OR] pregnancy [OR] [NOT] ability to communicate in English
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
