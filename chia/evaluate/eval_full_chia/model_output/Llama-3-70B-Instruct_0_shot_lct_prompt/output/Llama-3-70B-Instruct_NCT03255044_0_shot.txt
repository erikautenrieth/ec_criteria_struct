<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
older than 18 years [AND] of both sexes
diagnosed [AND] with stable chronic heart failure NYHA class II [OR] III
ejection fraction < 40 % [AND] as assessed by 2D echocardiography
who have been optimized [AND] on Guideline Directed treatment for heart failure for at least a month prior to enrolling.

Exclusion Criteria:
Known hypersensitivity [AND] to statin
Treatment [AND] with statins during the past month prior to study.
Serum creatinine > 3 mg/dl
Significant liver disease: liver enzymes 2.5 [OR] folds the upper normal limit
Malignancy
Pregnancy [OR] lactation
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
