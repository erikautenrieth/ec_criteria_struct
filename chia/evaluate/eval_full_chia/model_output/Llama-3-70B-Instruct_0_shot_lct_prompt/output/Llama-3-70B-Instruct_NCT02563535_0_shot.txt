<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
age > 18 years
critical limb ischemia ([AND] Rutherford class 4-6)
angiographic stenosis > 50% [OR] occlusion of at least one tibial vessel of at least 40mm for which an interventional treatment is scheduled

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] need for major amputation known before intervention
allergy to Paclitaxel
contraindication for combined antiplatelet treatment
life expectancy < 1 year
hypersensitivity [OR] contraindication to one of the study drugs
[NOT] lack of consent
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
