<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Duration of SCI = 1 year;
Level of SCI C3-T1, AIS A [AND] B;
Age between 18 [AND] 65 years.

Exclusion Criteria:
Evidence of sympathetic integrity below the lesion level by the skin axon-reflex vasodilatation (SkARV) test;
Known allergies to midodrine hydrochloride;
PMH of diagnosed heart [OR] kidney [OR] peripheral vascular [OR] cerebral vascular disease [OR] diabetes mellitus;
Hypertension (BP > 140/90 mmHg);
[NOT] Treated thyroid disease;
Acute illness [OR] infection;
Current smoker;
Pregnancy.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
