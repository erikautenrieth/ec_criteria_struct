<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Adults over 18 years of age
Symptomatic GSV [OR] SSV vein reflux > 0.5 seconds on colour Duplex
Varicose vein tributary [AND] requiring treatment

Exclusion Criteria:
Current DVT
Recurrent varicose veins
Arterial disease ([AND] ABPI<0.8)
Vein diameter < 3mm
Preference for one of the treatment options
Patient [AND] who are unwilling to participate
[NOT] Ability or [NOT] willingness to complete questionnaires
[NOT] Ability to attend follow-up appointments
Patient currently included [AND] in a study of varicose vein treatment
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
