<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
HBsAg-positive for more than 6 months ([OR] HBeAg-positive [OR] HBeAg-negative).
Age > 20 y/o.
[AND] Under lamivudine/adefovir treatment for more than 1 year due to previous lamivudine resistance (LAM-R), [AND] current HBV DNA is undetectable (< 20 IU/ml) during enrollment.

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] HCV, [OR] HIV, [OR] HDV coinfection.
[NOT] Uncontrolled HCC, [OR] malignancy [OR] decompensated liver cirrhosis (CTP score = 7).
[NOT] Uremia patients [OR] Creatinine = 2 mg/dl.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
