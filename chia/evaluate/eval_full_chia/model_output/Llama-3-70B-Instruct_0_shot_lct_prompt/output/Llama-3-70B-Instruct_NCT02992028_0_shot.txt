<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Rotator cuff tear patients [AND] undergoing arthroscopic rotator cuff tear

Exclusion Criteria:
age <45 [OR] >80
allergies to medications used in the study
history of renal diseases [OR] a coagulation abnormality [OR] a hepatic disease [OR] drug abuse
definite radiographic evidence of osteoarthritis of the glenohumeral joint
inflammatory arthritis [AND] including rheumatoid arthritis
a history of acute trauma
systemic conditions [AND] associated with chronic pain
a history of infection
[NOT] ability to understand the questionnaires
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
