<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Blood culture-proven typhoid fever (S. typhi [OR] S. paratyphi) 
Signed informed consent [AND] to participate in the study.

Exclusion Criteria:
Allergy [AND] to ceftriaxone [OR] macrolides 
Major typhoid fever-associated complications 
[NOT] Ability to swallow oral medication 
Underlying illness 
Pregnancy 
Lactation 
Treatment within the past 4 days [AND] with an antibiotic that may be effective against typhoid fever
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
