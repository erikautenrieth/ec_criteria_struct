<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients older than 18 years 
[AND] Classification of the American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA I-III)
[NOT] cognitive deficits
[AND] Signed informed consent prior to surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
Emergency surgery
Pregnancy [OR] lactation
Immune disorders
Kidney [OR] liver disease [OR] advanced-stage cardiopulmonary
Patient refusal to participate in the study
Patients under 18 years [OR] [NOT] ability to consent
Associated neuromuscular disorders, [OR] contraindication for the use of rocuronium [OR] sugammadex, [OR] allergy [OR] hypersensitivity to rocuronium [OR] sugammadex
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
