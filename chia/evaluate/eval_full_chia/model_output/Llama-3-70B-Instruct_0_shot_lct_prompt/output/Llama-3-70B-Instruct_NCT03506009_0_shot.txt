<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
18-80 years old;
Diagnosis of posterior circulation ischemic stroke;
Time from onset to treatment [AND]=6 hours;
NIHSS: 4-25;
Signed informed consent by patient self [OR] legally authorized representatives.

Exclusion Criteria:
mRS=2;
History of stroke within 3 months;
History of intracranial hemorrhage;
Suspected subarachnoid hemorrhage;
Intracranial tumour [OR] vascular malformation [OR] arterial aneurysm;
Major surgery within 1 month;
Systolic pressure [AND]=180 mmHg [OR] diastolic pressure [AND]=110 mmHg;
Platelet count [NOT]> 105/mm3;
Heparin therapy [OR] oral anticoagulation therapy within 48 hours;
Abnormal APTT;
Thrombin [OR] Xa factor inhibitor;
Severe disease with a life expectancy of [NOT]> 3 months;
Blood glucose [NOT]> 50 mg/dL (2.7mmol/L);
Patients who have received any other investigational drug [OR] device within 3 months;
Pregnancy;
Researchers consider patients [NOT] appropriate to participate in the registry.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
