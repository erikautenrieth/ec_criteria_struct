end diastolic diameter >60 mm [OR] an ejection fraction <50% [AND] written informed consent [AND] age >18 years

Exclusion Criteria:
previous unusual response to esmolol [AND] inclusion in other randomized studies [OR] esmolol administration in the previous 30 days [OR] emergency operation
