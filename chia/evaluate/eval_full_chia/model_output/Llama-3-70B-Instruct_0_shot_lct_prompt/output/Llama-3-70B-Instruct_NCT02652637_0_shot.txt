Inclusion Criteria:
Patients undergoing colon resection

Exclusion Criteria:
Emergency surgery needed [AND] 
Bowel obstruction [OR] 
Colonoscopy scheduled to be undertaken peroperatively [OR] 
Other reason indicating mechanical preparation [OR] contradicting it
[NOT] Allergy to used drugs (PEG [OR] neomycin [OR] metronidazole)
