<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
A formal diagnosis of Autism [OR] Pervasive Developmental Disorder not otherwise specified (PDD-NOS), given by a child neurologist [AND] who has a formal diagnosis.
Age: 10-18 years.
A signed parental consent form [AND] is obtained.

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] an underlying infectious disease
[NOT] chromosomal abnormality
[NOT] metabolic disorder
[NOT] specific brain related disorder (such as tuberous sclerosis)
[NOT] history of fetal cytomegalovirus infection
[NOT] birth asphyxia
[NOT] a history of major head injury
[NOT] a chronic use of non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs, (NSAID)
[NOT] known brain damage
[NOT] Epilepsy
[NOT] Abnormal Electro-cardiogram (ECG)
[NOT] Epileptiform EEG
[NOT] Use of psychostimulants, anti-depressants, neuroleptics [OR] anti-convulsive agents within the past month.
[NOT] Lack of cooperation in the screening phase
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
