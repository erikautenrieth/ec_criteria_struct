<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
medical indication for induction of labor
[AND] 18 years of age
[AND] signed informed consent
[AND] cephalic presentation
[AND] [NOT] PROM
[AND] 37+0 - 42+0 weeks of gestation
[AND] Bishop-Score = 6
[AND] [NOT] contra-indication for medical induction of labor
[AND] [NOT] clinical signs of infection

Exclusion Criteria:
fetal anomalies
[OR] contra-indications for medical induction of labor
[OR] placental pathologies
[OR] St.p. surgery [AND] with opening the uterine cavity (incl. caesarean section)
[OR] PROM
[OR] multiple gestations
[OR] < 37-0 weeks of gestation
[OR] St.p. cervical tear
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
