<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with STEMI undergoing primary PPCI
Age > 18 years old

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Ability to provide written informed consent
Known history of prior intracranial bleeding
On treatment [AND] with a P2Y12 receptor antagonist (ticlopidine, clopidogrel, prasugrel, ticagrelor) in the prior 10 days
Known allergies [OR] to aspirin, ticagrelor [OR] cangrelor
On treatment [AND] with oral anticoagulant
Treatment [AND] with glycoprotein IIb/IIIa inhibitors
Fibrinolytics [OR] within 24 hours
Active bleeding
High risk of bleeding
Known platelet count <80x106/mL
Known hemoglobin <10 g/dL
Intubated patients (prior to randomization)
Known creatinine clearance <30 mL/minute [OR] on hemodialysis
Known severe hepatic dysfunction
Patients [AND] with sick sinus syndrome (SSS) [OR] high degree AV block without pacemaker protection
Current treatment [AND] with drugs interfering [AND] with CYP3A4 metabolism (to avoid interaction with ticagrelor): Ketoconazole, itraconazole, voriconazole, clarithromycin, nefazodone, ritonavir, saquinavir, nelfinavir, indinavir, atazanavir, [OR] telithromizycin
Pregnant [OR] lactating females.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
