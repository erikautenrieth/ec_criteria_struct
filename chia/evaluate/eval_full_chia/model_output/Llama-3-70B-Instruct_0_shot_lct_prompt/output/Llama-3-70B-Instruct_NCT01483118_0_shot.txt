<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients aged greater than 18 years of age [AND] ability to understand [AND] willingness to comply with the study protocol [AND] written informed consent [AND] patients meeting the Rotterdam PCOS workshop criteria for polycystic ovary syndrome, defined by oligomenorrhea [OR] amenorrhea [AND] at least one of the following two signs: clinical [OR] biochemical evidence of hyperandrogenism [OR] ultrasound finding of polycystic appearing ovaries.

Exclusion Criteria:
Current pregnancy [OR] lactation [AND] liver disease [OR] elevated liver enzymes [AND] established diagnosis of diabetes mellitus [AND] abnormal serum glucose levels either at fasting [OR] after the 2-hr oral glucose tolerance test meeting criteria for the diagnosis of diabetes mellitus according to the American Diabetes Association [AND] insulin sensitizing treatment within 3 months prior to [OR] during the eight week study period [AND] hormonal treatment involving estrogen [OR] progesterone 3 months prior to [OR] during the study period, [NOT] with the exception of medroxyprogesterone acetate for withdrawal bleeding [AND] systemic [OR] inhaled corticosteroids [AND] known hypersensitive reaction to cinnamon [AND] patients [AND] seizure disorders, [OR] known cardiovascular disease, [OR] cerebrovascular disease [AND] body mass index (BMI)range [NOT] 20-50 (excluding all women [AND] BMI under 20 [OR] over 50).
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
