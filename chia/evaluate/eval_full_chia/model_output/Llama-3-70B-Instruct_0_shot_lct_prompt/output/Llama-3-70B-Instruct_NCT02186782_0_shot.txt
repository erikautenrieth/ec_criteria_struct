<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Infertile women [AND] with eugonadotrophic anovulation/oligoovulation.
Unexplained infertility.

Exclusion Criteria:
Age < 20 [OR] > 35 years.
Body mass index (BMI) < 18.5 kg/m2 [OR] > 25 kg/m2.
Presence of any infertility factor [AND] other than anovulation/oligoovulation.
Previous history of ovarian surgery [OR] surgical removal of one ovary.
Previous exposure to cytotoxic drugs [OR] pelvic irradiation.
[NOT] Absence of metabolic [OR] hormonal abnormalities.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
