<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Subjects [AND] age 21 [OR] older
Subjects [AND] with healthy eyes
Subjects [AND] who have previously undergone LASIK surgery
Subjects [AND] with residual refractive error.

Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects [NOT] under the age of 21.
Subjects [AND] with excessively thin corneas.
Subjects [AND] with topographic evidence of keratoconus.
Subjects [AND] with ectatic eye disorders.
Subjects [AND] with autoimmune diseases.
Subjects [AND] who are pregnant [OR] nursing.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
