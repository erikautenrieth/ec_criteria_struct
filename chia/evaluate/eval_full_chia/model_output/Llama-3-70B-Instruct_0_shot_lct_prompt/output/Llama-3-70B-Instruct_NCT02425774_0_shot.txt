<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
patients undergoing partial [OR] full resection of the pancreas due to a benign [OR] malignant tumor

Exclusion Criteria:
adjuvant radiotherapy
evident intra-abdominal inflammation ([AND] diagnosed by imaging [OR] laboratory results, including an abscess [OR] cholecystitis)
chronic pancreatitis
pancreatic polypeptide producing endocrine tumor
American Society of Anesthesiologists physical-health status classification (ASA-PS) > 3
[NOT] poorly regulated diabetes (>200 mg/dl (=11 mmol/l))
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
