<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
F; age 18 [AND] 70
American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) I [OR] II;
breast cancer ( DIN 2 [OR] 3, [OR] LIN 2 [OR] 3 sec. Tavassoli) scheduled for nipple-sparing mastectomy, simple mastectomy, skin-sparing mastectomy, skin-reducing mastectomy [OR] lymphnode biopsy [AND] axillary dissection;
immediate sub-pectoral prosthetic reconstruction;
signed informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
preexisting pectoral, axillar, thoracic homolateral pain
habitual opioid consumption;
drug-alcoholics addiction ;
ICU postoperative recovery;
kidney failure (creatinin > 2 g/dl, creatinin <clearance 30 ml/h) [OR] hepatic failure (cholinesterase < 2000 UI);
cardiac arrhythmias [OR];
Epilepsy;
Psychiatric, cognitive disorders, mental retardation;
Coagulopathies (INR > 2, activated partial thromboplastin time - aPTT>44 sec);
platelet count less than 100.000/mm3;
BMI > 30;
[NOT] allergies to study drugs.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
