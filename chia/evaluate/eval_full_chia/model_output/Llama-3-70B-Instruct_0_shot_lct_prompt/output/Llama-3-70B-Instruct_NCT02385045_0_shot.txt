<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
• All patients attending for a routine diagnostic endoscopic procedure at St Mary's Hospital NHS Trust for dyspepsia [AND] abdominal pain 

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients attending for a therapeutic endoscopic procedure, e.g. variceal banding [OR] stent insertion [OR] balloon dilatation. 
Patients [AND] a known diagnosis, e.g. upper gastrointestinal cancer 
Patients previously treated [AND] HP eradication therapy 
Patients who had taken PPI [OR] H2 receptor antagonists [OR] antibiotics within 4 weeks 
Patients [AND] acute gastrointestinal bleeding 
Patients who'd had previous gastric surgery 
Patients [AND] chronic liver disease 
Patients [AND] abnormal coagulation [OR] any other contra-indication to use of standard biopsy in routine diagnostic endoscopic procedures 
Patients who are [NOT] able [OR] unwilling to give informed consent 
Patients under the age of 18 years
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
