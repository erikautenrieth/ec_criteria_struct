<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Born at University of New Mexico Hospital
Greater than 34 weeks gestation
Primary in-utero drug exposure was opioids [OR] other than buprenorphine
Maternal [AND] infant urine drug screen positive for methadone [OR] and/or opioids on admission

Exclusion Criteria:
Born prior to 34 weeks
Neonatal intensive care unit admission
Serious medical comorbidities
Primary substance exposure in-utero was buprenorphine [OR] was [NOT] opioids
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
