<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
An average defecation frequency (DF) of <3 per week based on a 3-week defecation diary (patient-reported) [AND] meet at least one other criterion of the Rome-IV criteria for idiopathic constipation based on the 3-week defecation diary (1) [AND] refractory to conservative treatment [AND] age: 14-80 years [AND] straining during =25% of defecations [OR] lumpy or hard stools in =25% of defecations [OR] sensation of incomplete evacuation for =25% of defecations [OR] sensation of anorectal obstruction/blockage for =25% of defecations [OR] manual manoeuvres to facilitate =25% of defecations

Exclusion Criteria:
Obstructed outlet syndrome (objectified by defeacography) [OR] irritable bowel syndrome (Rome-IV criteria for irritable bowel syndrome) [OR] congenital [OR] organic bowel pathology [OR] rectal prolapse [OR] anatomical limitations preventing placement of an electrode [OR] skin and perineal disease with risk of infection [OR] previous large bowel/rectal surgery [OR] stoma [OR] coexisting neurological disease [OR] significant psychological co-morbidity as assessed subjectively by the investigator [OR] being [OR] attempting to become pregnant during study follow-up [NOT] having any of the above conditions
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
