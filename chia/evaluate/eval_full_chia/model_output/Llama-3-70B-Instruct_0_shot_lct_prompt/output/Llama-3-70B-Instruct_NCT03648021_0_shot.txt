Inclusion Criteria:
18-year [OR] older patients
Patient hospitalized in neuro-critical care for [AND] one [OR] more of the following:
Arachnymoid hemorrhage
Intra parenchymatous hematoma
stroke
Acute brain Severe injury
Post-operative complication of an act of neurosurgery [OR] programmed neuroradiology
Sedation [AND] mechanical ventilation planned > 2 days
Monitoring of intracranial temperature [AND] pressure by intraparenchymal sensor (Sophysa®)
Brain temperature > 38.5°C for more than 30 minutes

Exclusion Criteria:
Known hypersensitivity to paracetamol [OR] mannitol (excipient with known effect)
Severe hepatocellular insufficiency ([AND] ASAT [OR] ALAT > 5N, [OR] bilirubin > 2N)
Pharmacological intervention ([AND] administration of corticosteroids, NSAIDs [OR] paracetamol) [OR] physical intervention ([AND] external cooling technique) that may influence temperature in the last 6 hours.
[NOT] Pregnant [OR] breastfeeding women
[NOT] Previous participation in this study
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
