<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Healthy patients [AND] age 18 [OR] older
Breech presentation
Singleton gestation [AND] scheduled for ECV [AND] desiring CSE.

Exclusion Criteria:
Refusal
Contraindication to neuraxial ([OR] coagulopathy [OR] anticoagulant use [OR] local infection [OR] sepsis etc) [AND] [NOT] rupture of membranes.
Drop-out: Patients may choose to drop-out of the study at any time. The physicians involved in this study may choose to end a patient's involvement in the study at their discretion.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
