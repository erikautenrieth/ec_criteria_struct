<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Subject is 65 years old [AND] who is able [AND] willing to give an informed consent.
Patients undergoing planned trans-femoral TAVI.
Calculated eGFR below 60ml/min/1.73m2 (MDRD)

Exclusion Criteria:
History of acute coronary syndrome in the past 30 days.
History of congesting heart failure [AND] left ventricular ejection fraction <30% [OR] exacerbation in the past 30 days.
[NOT] Current dialysis treatment.
[NOT] Known furosemide hypersensitivity.
Contraindications to placement of a Foley catheter in the bladder.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
