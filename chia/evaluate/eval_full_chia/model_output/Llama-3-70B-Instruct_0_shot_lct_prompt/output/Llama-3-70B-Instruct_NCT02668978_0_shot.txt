<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients over the age of 18 years [AND] who are able to give their informed consent
Lobar [OR] sublobar resections
Open [OR] video-assisted thoracoscopic [OR] robotic surgeries
Diagnostic [OR] therapeutic procedures

Exclusion Criteria:
Traumatic pulmonary contusion [OR] laceration
Lung reduction surgery
Planned removal of more than 10 lung lesions
Pneumonectomy
[NOT] Known hypersensitivity to bovine protein
[NOT] Known hypersensitivity to Brilliant Blue FCF (E133)
[NOT] Presence of active infection
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
