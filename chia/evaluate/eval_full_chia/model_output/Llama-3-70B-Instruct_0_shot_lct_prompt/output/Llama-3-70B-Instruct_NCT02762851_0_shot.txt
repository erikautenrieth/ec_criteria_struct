<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Age = 18 years [AND] NYHA (New York Heart Association) functional class II [OR] III [OR] IV

Exclusion Criteria:
Anaphylactic reaction to a previous dose of TIV(trivalent influenza vaccine)
Known IgE( Immunoglobulin E)-mediated hypersensitivity to eggs manifested as hives [OR] swelling of the mouth [OR] throat, difficulty in breathing [OR] hypotension [OR] shock
Guillain-Barré syndrome within eight weeks of a previous influenza vaccine
Anaphylactic reaction to neomycin
Patients [WHO] have had influenza vaccine in two of the three previous years
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
