<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Subjects undergoing burn excision surgery for standard of care purposes
Male [OR] female >= 18 years of age
Subject [AND] subject's medical decision maker agrees to participate in this study [AND] provides informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects [AND] a history of hypercoagulopathy [OR] deep vein thrombosis (DVT) [OR] pulmonary embolism
[NOT] Renal function, [NOT] renal impairment
Subjects [AND] known hypersensitivity to tranexamic acid
Consecutive fibrinolytic states [AND] coagulopathy
[NOT] History of convulsions
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
