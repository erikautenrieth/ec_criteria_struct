<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
[AND] 18 years [OR] older
Chest radiograph showing new opacities.
Cough
Production of sputum
Temp >38,0 °C [OR] <36,0 °C
Audible abnormalities by chest examination compatible with pneumonia
Leukocytosis (>10.000 cells/mm3) [OR] leftward shift (>10%) [OR] leucopenia (<4000 cells/mm3)
C-reactive protein > 15 mg/l (three fold higher than the upper limit of normal)

Exclusion Criteria:
[AND] Immunocompromised patients:
Patients [AND] with a known congenital [OR] acquired immunodeficiency.
Patients [AND] who received chemotherapy less than 6 weeks ago.
Patients [AND] who received corticosteroids in the last 6 weeks.
Patients [AND] who received immunosuppressive medication in the last 6 weeks (e.g. cyclosporin, cyclophosphamide, azathioprine).
Patients [AND] with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [AND] who are on systemic corticosteroids.
Patients [AND] who require intensive care unit treatment.
Patients [AND] with tropical worm infection.
Patients [AND] with dexamethasone intolerance.
[NOT] Pregnant [AND] breastfeeding women.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
