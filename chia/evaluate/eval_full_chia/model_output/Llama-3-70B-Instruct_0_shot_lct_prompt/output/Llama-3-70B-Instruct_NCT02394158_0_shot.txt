<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Singleton pregnancy;
8-22 weeks gestation [AND] Previous pregnancy complicated by gestational diabetes

Exclusion Criteria:
Established pre-existing diabetes ([OR] including unrecognised diabetes defined as a fasting plasma glucose = 7.0mmol/L [OR] HbA1c = 48mmol/mol); [NOT] Contraindications to metformin therapy ([OR] creatinine = 130µmol/L [OR] alanine transaminase = 2.0 x upper limit normal [OR] previous intolerance to metformin)
[NOT] Planned continued antenatal care [OR] delivery at centre [NOT] included in trial
[NOT] Planned fast for cultural [OR] religious reasons e.g. Ramadan
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
