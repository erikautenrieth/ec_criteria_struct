Inclusion Criteria:
Type 2 diabetes
Age 18 [AND] 75 years
Anti-GAD antibodies negative (Glutamic Acid Decarboxylase)
C-peptide levels = 1.5 ng/mL
Fasting blood glucose > 126 mg/dl
HbA1c 8.0 [OR] 10.5 %
BMI 25.0 [OR] 45.0 kg/m2
Previous therapy [AND] BBIT (basal insulin [AND] at least once daily bolus insulin)

Exclusion Criteria:
Use of any oral antidiabetic treatment except for metformin (i.e., sulphonylureas [OR] DPP-IV inhibitors [OR] thiazolidinediones [OR] SGLT-2 inhibitors (Sodium dependent glucose transporter) [OR] GLP-1 analogues (glucagone like peptide)) within the last three months prior to Screening
[NOT] Repeated episodes of severe hypoglycaemia within the last six months prior to Screening
[NOT] History of diabetic ketoacidosis [OR] precoma diabetica [OR] diabetic coma
Treatment [AND] any other investigational drug within the last three months before Screening
[NOT] Acute infections within the last four weeks prior to Screening
[NOT] Recurrent urogenital infections
[NOT] History of pancreatitis
Anamnestic history of hypersensitivity [AND] the study drugs [OR] to drugs with similar chemical structures
History of severe [OR] multiple allergies
Concomitant participation [AND] other clinical trials
[NOT] Type 1 diabetes
Cardiovascular disease [AND] Clinically relevant ventricular tachycardia [OR] ventricular fibrillation [OR] 3rd degree AV block [OR] Torsades de Pointes [OR] treatment [AND] antiarrhythmic drugs. Percutaneous coronary intervention within the past 6 months. Any of the following within the past 6 months: myocardial infarction (MI) [OR] coronary artery bypass surgery; unstable angina; [OR] stroke.
[NOT] Malignancy including leukemia [AND] lymphoma within the last 5y.
[NOT] Liver disease such as cirrhosis [OR] chronic active hepatitis.
[NOT] Significant renal dysfunction (see also exclusion criteria laboratory abnormalities).
State [AND] kidney transplantation
Endocrine disease:
[NOT] Systolic blood pressure outside the range of 100-160 mmHg [OR] diastolic blood pressure above 95 mmHg at Screening
[NOT] History of active substance abuse (including alcohol > 40g/day) within the past 2 years.
[NOT] Pregnancy [OR] childbearing potential without adequate contraception
Present therapy [AND] systemic steroids
Presence of psychiatric disorder [OR] intake of anti-depressive [OR] anti-psychotic agents with the exception of benzodiazepines [AND] SSRIs/SNRI's (selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor)
[NOT] Potentially unreliable subjects, [AND] those judged by the investigator to be unsuitable for the study.
Contraindications for Magnetic resonance (MR) scanning such as persons [AND] cardiac pacemaker [AND] implants out of metal [OR] claustrophobia
