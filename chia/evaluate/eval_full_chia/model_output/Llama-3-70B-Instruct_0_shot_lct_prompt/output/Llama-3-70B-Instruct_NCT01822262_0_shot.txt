<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Clinical diagnosis of calculous cholecystitis.

Exclusion Criteria:
Gallbladder's wall >3mm [OR] atrophied gallbladder [OR] gallstone [AND] obstruct the Hartmann's pouch.
Abdominal ultrasound display the contractibility of gallbladder is poor.
The aged patients [AND] who has bad heart [AND] lung function.
Patients [AND] who has acute cholecystitis [OR] pancreatitis [OR] pancreaticobiliary diseases, especially choledocholithiasis.
Pregnant [OR] lactational women.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
