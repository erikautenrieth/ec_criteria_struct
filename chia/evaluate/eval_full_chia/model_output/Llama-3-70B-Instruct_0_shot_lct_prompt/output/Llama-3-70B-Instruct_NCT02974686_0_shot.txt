Inclusion Criteria:
Kidney transplant recipients at Washington University/Barnes-Jewish Hospital 
[AND] Experiencing GI toxicity from MPA as determined by the treating physician within 12 months post-renal transplant 
[AND] On standard immunosuppression with tacrolimus [OR] prednisone

Exclusion Criteria:
Dual organ [OR] kidney after another solid organ transplant
Presence of a preexisting significant GI condition that [NOT] have a presumed causal relationship with MPA
Evidence of any GI disorder induced by an infection [OR] underlying medical condition [OR] concomitant medication other than MPA
eGFR<40 ml/min at time of possible conversion
Proteinuria >1 gram/day at time of possible conversion
Hemoglobin <10 g/dL
WBC <3 K/cumm
Platelets <100 K/cumm
Wound healing issues at time of possible conversion (eg, wound dehiscence [OR] wound infection [OR] incisional hernia [OR] lymphocele [OR] seroma)
Elevated total cholesterol (>350 mg/dL) [OR] triglycerides (>500 ng/dL) at time of possible conversion
Hypersensitivity to everolimus [OR] sirolimus [OR] other rapamycin deriviatives
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
