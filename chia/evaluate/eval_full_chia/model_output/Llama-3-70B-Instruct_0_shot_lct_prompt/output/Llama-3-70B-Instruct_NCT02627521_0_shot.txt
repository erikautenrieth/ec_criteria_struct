<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Accepted for CABG surgery [AND] Treatment with Ticagrelor within 48 hours

Exclusion Criteria:
Anticoagulation therapy [OR] Prior CABG [OR] Active bleeding [OR] at high risk of bleeding [OR] Severe liver disease [OR] renal disease [OR] Hypersensitivity to ticagrelor [OR] History of intracranial hemorrhage
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
