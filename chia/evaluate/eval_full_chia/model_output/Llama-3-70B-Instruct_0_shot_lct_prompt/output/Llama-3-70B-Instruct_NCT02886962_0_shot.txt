Inclusion Criteria:
Adult patients (= 18 years)
Patient [AND] on hemodialysis treatment for at least 1 month
Patient [AND] with a history of [OR] presenting a new episode of atrial fibrillation (either permanent [OR] paroxysmal).
Patient [AND] with a CHADS2VASC score =2
Patient [AND] with high risk of bleeding as defined by (1) HASBLED score =3 [OR] (2) HASBLED = CHADS2VASC score [OR] (3) recent history of severe bleeding (type 3a, 3b, 3c) [OR] particularly cerebral [OR] gastrointestinal [OR] (4) prior recurrent (>2) history of falls.
Patient [AND] capable of understanding information about the study [AND] of giving his/her consent
Patient [AND] informed of the preliminary medical exam results
Patient [AND] with healthcare insurance
Written consent signed

Exclusion Criteria:
Formal indication to oral anticoagulation beside atrial fibrillation (mechanic heart valves [OR] recurrent thrombophlebitis [OR] antiphospholipid syndrome)
Life expectancy < 6 months (e.g., terminal cancer) [NOT] expected to live longer
Live donor transplantation scheduled within 6 months
Pregnancy (ß-HCG blood-based assay) [OR] nursing (lactating) women
Women of child bearing potential, [NOT] using an effective method of birth control
Patient [NOT] under legal guardianship
Patients [NOT] under law protection
Known hypersensibility to coumadin [OR] indoine derivatives [OR] to any excipients ([NOT] CI to oral AVK)
Severe liver failure ([NOT] CI to oral AVK)
