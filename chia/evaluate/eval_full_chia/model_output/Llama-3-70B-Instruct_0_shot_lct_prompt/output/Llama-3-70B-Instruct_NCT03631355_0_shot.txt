<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients undergoing a high tibial osteotomy (HTO)
Patients undergoing tibial tubercle osteotomy (TTO) [AND] with [OR] without medial patello-femoral ligament (MPFL) reconstruction

Exclusion Criteria:
Legally incompetent [OR] mentally impaired (e.g., minors, Alzheimer's subjects, dementia, etc.)
[NOT] Older than 18 years of age
Any patient considered a vulnerable subject
[NOT] Having bleeding [OR] clotting disorder
Preoperative anticoagulation therapy
Abnormal coagulation profile
Renal disorder [OR] insufficiency
Sickle cell disease
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
