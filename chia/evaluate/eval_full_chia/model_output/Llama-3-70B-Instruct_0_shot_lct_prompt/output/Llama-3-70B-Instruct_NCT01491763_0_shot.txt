<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with Ph (BCR/[OR] ABL) positive de novo < 55 years old (it is advisable to include patients over 55 years [AND] LAL07OPH protocol).
Performance status 0-2 (Appendix B) may include patients [AND] with performance status > 2 attributable to LAL.
Patients [NOT] without functional impairment of organs: liver function: total bilirubin, AST, ALT, alfa-GT [OR] alkaline phosphatase less than 3 times the upper limit of normal laboratory renal function: serum creatinine < 2 mg/dL [OR] clearance creatinine > 30 ml/min (except renal function attributable to LAL) cardiac function (Appendix B) normal: ventricular EF > 50%, [NOT] absence of severe chronic respiratory disease. In the event that alterations are secondary to the disease it is at the discretion of the investigator to determine if the patient can be included in the trial.

Exclusion Criteria:
Any other variety of LAL
Patients [AND] with a history of coronary artery disease, valvular [OR] hypertensive heart disease
Patients [AND] with chronic liver disease
Patients [AND] with chronic respiratory failure
Renal failure [NOT] due to LAL
Patients [AND] with positive HIV status
[NOT] no serious neurological abnormalities due to LAL
Impact on overall severe (grade 3 [OR] 4 of the WHO scale) [NOT] attributable to the LAL
Pregnant [OR] breastfeeding
initial blast crisis CML
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
