<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Major subjects of over 40 years (mean age of Meniere's disease 40 [OR] 50 years)
[AND] Informed consent signed
[AND] Medical examination performed prior to participation in research
Patients [NOT] with history of inner ear disease
[AND] Recipient of a French social security scheme

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [NOT] of age 18 [OR] more
Patients on a legal protection regime type [AND] guardianship
Respiratory pathologies, cardiovascular, renal, diabetes
Claustrophobia
Contraindications to exposure to a magnetic field
Contraindications to injecting Dotarem ®
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
