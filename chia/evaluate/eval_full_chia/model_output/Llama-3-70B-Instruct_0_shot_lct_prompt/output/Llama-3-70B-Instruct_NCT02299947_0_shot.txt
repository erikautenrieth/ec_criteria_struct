Inclusion Criteria:
Elective surgery for thoracic aneurysm

Exclusion Criteria:
Prior trombosis [OR] myocardial infarction, congenital coagulation disorder, use of anti-coagulants [AND] prior to surgery, prior thoracic surgery, pregnancy, pre-operative fibrinogen concentration <1g/L [AND] [NOT] >1g/L
