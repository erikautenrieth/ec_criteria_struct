Inclusion Criteria:
Adult patients (age = 18)
Diagnosed by preoperative imaging modalities to have a brain tumor [OR] metastatic brain tumors [OR] vascular lesions (aneurysm [OR] arteriovenous malformation [OR] arteriovenous fistula) [AND] requiring surgical intervention.
The patient is determined by a board certified neurosurgeon to have a tumor [OR] vascular lesion that would take up fluorescein
Patient [OR] legally authorized representative provides written informed consent [AND] to enroll in this study

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Applicable
