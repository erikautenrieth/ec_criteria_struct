<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Clinical suspicion of Morton neuroma confirmed [AND] in ultrasound scan
Symptoms present more than six months
The thickness of the nerve must be at least 2 mm [OR] / in short axis [AND] at least 5 mm [OR] / in the longitudinal axis.

Exclusion Criteria:
Contraindication for the use of corticosteroids [OR] local anesthetics
Presence of inflammatory arthropathy [OR] neuropathy
Skin lesions [AND] in the area
[NOT] absence of diabetes mellitus
Infiltration [OR] previous surgery in the area
[NOT] refusal to participate in the study
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
