<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Pathologic confirmation of lung adenocarcinoma [AND] with measurable disease, defined as at least one lesion that can be accurately measured in at least one dimension (longest diameter to be recorded on CT); Patients must have previously untreated locally advanced [OR] metastatic NSCLC; Patients must have lung cancer [AND] with a documented EGFR activating mutation (exon 19 deletion, L858R).

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Prior chemotherapy [OR] Prior treatment with gefitinib, erlotinib, [OR] other drugs that target EGFR Patients must [NOT] be receiving any other investigational agents Any evidence of interstitial lung disease
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
