<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Scheduled to undergo revision total knee arthroplasty

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Ability to provide informed consent [OR] to comply with study assessments (e.g. due to cognitive impairment [OR] geographic distance).
Age = 17.
Allergy to povidone iodine.
Any condition requiring antibiotics 14 days prior to arriving for surgery.
Patients [AND] chronic immunosuppression (such as HIV/AIDS).
[NOT] Able to adhere to follow up schedule [AND] treatment.
Patients scheduled to undergo revision total knee arthroplasty for infectious reasons.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
