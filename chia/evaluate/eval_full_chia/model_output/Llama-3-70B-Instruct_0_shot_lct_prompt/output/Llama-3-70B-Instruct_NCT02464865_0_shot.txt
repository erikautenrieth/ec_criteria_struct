<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
obese : weight for height > median + 3 standard deviations
simple obesity

Exclusion Criteria:
pathological obesity
chronic diseases [AND] e.g. cerebral palsy [OR] metabolic disease [OR] etc.
diseases [AND] of red blood cells
[NOT] on medication [OR] e.g. steroid [OR] multivitamins [OR] thiamine-containing vitamins [OR] diuretic drugs
[NOT] hemodialysis [OR] peritoneal dialysis
[NOT] bariatric surgery
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
