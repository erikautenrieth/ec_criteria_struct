<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Has given written informed consent before any study-related activity is performed
[AND] Advanced hormone-dependent prostate cancer for which [AND] androgen deprivation therapy is indicated, [AND] independently from this trial, Firmagon® is intended to be used for treatment
Age greater than [OR] equal to 18 years [AND] less than 80 years
[AND] Advanced hormone-dependent prostate cancer [NOT] without any other clinically significant disorder
Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group score = 2
PSA = 2 ng/mL at screening
Life expectancy of at least 12 months as per the investigator's judgement

Exclusion Criteria:
Previous [OR] concurrent hormonal management of prostate cancer
Contraindication for prescription of Firmagon®
Concurrent treatment with a 5-a-reductase inhibitor
Considered as a candidate for curative therapy
History of severe untreated asthma, anaphylactic reactions [OR] severe urticaria [OR] angioedema
QTc interval over 450 msec [OR] risk factors for torsades de pointes [OR] on Class IA [OR] Class III anti arrhythmic medications
Cancer within the last 5 years except prostate cancer [AND] surgically removed basal [OR] squamous cell carcinoma of the skin
Known [OR] suspected hepatic, symptomatic biliary disease (this includes moderate [OR] severe chronic hepatic impairment)
Patients with clinically significant laboratory abnormalities / disorders other than prostate cancer
Patient with Hepatitis B Virus (HBV) [OR] Hepatitis C Virus (HCV) [OR] Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) infections
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
