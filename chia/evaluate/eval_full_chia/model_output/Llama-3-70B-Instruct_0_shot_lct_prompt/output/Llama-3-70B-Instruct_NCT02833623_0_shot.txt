<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
outpatients aged 18-70 years
confirmed diagnosis of H. pylori infection by at least one of the following methods: 13C-urea breath test [OR] histology [OR] rapid urease test [OR] bacterial culture
[AND] an intention of H. pylori eradication treatment [AND] have written inform consent
[AND] ability to read short messages on the mobile phone

Exclusion Criteria:
advanced chronic disease [AND] that would [NOT] allow the patient to complete the treatment [OR] follow-up [OR] attend visits
allergy to any of the drugs used in this study
previous Helicobacter Pylori eradication treatment
pregnancy [OR] breastfeeding ([AND] female participants with childbearing potential were required to use medically accepted contraception for the duration of the study)
taking antibiotics [OR] PPIs [OR] bismuth salts within four weeks
previous gastrointestinal surgery
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
