<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
submucosal,
intramural [OR] subserosal leiomyomas,
symptoms of menometrorrhagia,
menstrual disorder,
infertility,
pelvic pain

Exclusion Criteria:
endometrial hyperplasia [AND] with atypia,
estrogen-progestin therapy in the 2 months before enrollment,
autoimmune diseases,
chronic [OR] metabolic [OR] systemic [OR] endocrine disorders, including hyperandrogenism [OR] hyperprolactinemia [OR] diabetes mellitus [OR] thyroid disease,
[NOT] hypogonadotropic hypogonadism,
majors clinical conditions
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
