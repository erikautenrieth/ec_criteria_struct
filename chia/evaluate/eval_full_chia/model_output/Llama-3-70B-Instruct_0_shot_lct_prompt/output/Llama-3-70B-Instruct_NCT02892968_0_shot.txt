<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
At the cluster level, ED physicians practicing at a participating site will be eligible. 
At the patient level, all hip fractures seen by a participating ED physician will be eligible 

Exclusion Criteria:
ED physicians [AND] who work casually (less than 0.25 Full Time Equivalent) 
ED Physicians [AND] who are routinely using U/S guided RA for hip fracture patients, [OR] decline participation in the trial. 
Patients' age [NOT] greater than or equal to 65 years; 
Patients [AND] who are delirious on initial assessment by ED physician [OR] severe dementia 
Patients [AND] with communication problems (critically ill, unconscious, language barrier [NOT] despite use of secure telephone-based translation service) 
Patients [AND] with allergies to narcotics [OR] local anesthetic; [OR] anticoagulant use (e.g. warfarin, dabigatran, rivaroxaban). 
Patients [AND] with hip fractures [NOT] requiring surgery (e.g. greater trochanter avulsion) will also be excluded.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
