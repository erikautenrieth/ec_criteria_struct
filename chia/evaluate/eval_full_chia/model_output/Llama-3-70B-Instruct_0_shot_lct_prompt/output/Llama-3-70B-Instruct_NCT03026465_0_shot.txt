Inclusion Criteria:
Patients older than 18 years
Ischemic symptoms [OR] evidence of myocardial ischemia ([AND] inducible [OR] spontaneous) in the presence of >50% de novo stenosis located in native coronary vessels

Exclusion Criteria:
Target lesion located in the left main stem
STEMI
Restenosis
Cardiogenic shock
Malignancies [OR] other comorbid conditions [AND] life expectancy less than 12 months [OR] that may result in protocol noncompliance
[NOT] Known allergy to the study medications (probucol [OR] sirolimus [OR] zotarolimus)
[NOT] Pregnancy (present [OR] suspected [OR] planned)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
