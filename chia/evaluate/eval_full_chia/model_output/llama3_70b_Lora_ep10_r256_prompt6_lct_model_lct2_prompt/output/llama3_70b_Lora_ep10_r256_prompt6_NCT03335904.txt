Inclusion Criteria:
normotensive
forced expiratory volume in 1s : forced vital capacity ratio > 0.75
[NOT] no medical history of cardiovascular [OR] and respiratory disease
 [NOT] not taking medications other than oral contraceptives
 [NOT] free from sleep apnea
body mass index less than 30 kg/m2

Exclusion Criteria:
history of hypertension
known impaired renal function
liver disease
heart failure
myocardial infarction
coronary artery disease
smoked within the past year
apnea hypopnea index > 5 events per hour
