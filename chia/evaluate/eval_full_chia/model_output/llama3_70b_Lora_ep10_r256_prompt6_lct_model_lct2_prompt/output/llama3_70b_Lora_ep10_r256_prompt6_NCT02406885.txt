Inclusion Criteria:
Men [OR] or women, 18 to 65 years old [AND] with a BMI of 35 kg/m2 or greater [AND] who will be undergoing bariatric surgery (VSG [OR] and RYGB)
Signed written informed consent
Women [AND] of childbearing potential (WOCBP) must have a negative serum [OR] or urine pregnancy test (minimum sensitivity 25 IU/L or equivalent units of HCG) within 24 hours prior to the start of study drug
Women must [NOT] not be breastfeeding

Exclusion Criteria:
History of documented clotting/ [OR] coagulation disorder
History of cancer (within the last year)
 Any diagnosis requiring anti-coagulation
History of hypersensitivity reaction to apixaban
 Active clinically significant bleeding
Creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL
Participants currently receiving any type of anticoagulation [OR] or blood thinning medications, including heparin, [OR] low molecular weight heparins, [OR] Plavix, [OR] aspirin, [OR] NSAIDS
 Combined P-glycoprotein [AND] and strong cytochrome P450 (CYP) 3A4 inhibitor
 Combined P-glycoprotein [AND] and moderate CYP 3A4 inhibitor
 Combined P-glycoprotein inducer [AND] and strong CYP 3A4 inducer
 Inducers of p-glycoprotein
 Strong inducers of CYP 3A4
