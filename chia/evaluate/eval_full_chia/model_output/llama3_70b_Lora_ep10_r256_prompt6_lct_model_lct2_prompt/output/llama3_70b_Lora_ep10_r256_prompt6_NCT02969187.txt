Inclusion Criteria:
Fulfills NIH criteria for bariatric surgery
Planned operation of laparoscopic Roux-en Y gastric bypass (LRYGB) [OR] or laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy (LSG) as primary bariatric procedure

Exclusion Criteria:
BMI <35 [OR] and > 60 kg/m2
Inability to walk (bed-bound [OR] or wheelchair dependence)
open abdominal surgeries except simple appendectomy [OR] and common OB/GYN procedures in the pelvis (hysterectomy, [OR] C-section, [OR] and oophorectomy, [OR] tubal ligation)
laparoscopic bowel [OR] or solid organ resection except laparoscopic cholecystectomy
ventral hernia repair with mesh
Preoperative chronic opiate use for chronic pain defined as opiate usage at least 60 mg/day of morphine equivalent for = 3 months (as defined by International Association for the Study of Pain22) in the one year period prior to the bariatric surgery
The American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) score > 3
History of hypersensitivity [OR] or adverse reaction to bupivacaine [OR] or narcotics
 [NOT] Inability to speak English
ventral hernia repair
Cholecystectomy
hiatal hernia repair [AND] with posterior cruroplasty
extensive lysis of adhesions
other procedures that mandate addition of "trocar(s)" [OR] or "feeding tube"
