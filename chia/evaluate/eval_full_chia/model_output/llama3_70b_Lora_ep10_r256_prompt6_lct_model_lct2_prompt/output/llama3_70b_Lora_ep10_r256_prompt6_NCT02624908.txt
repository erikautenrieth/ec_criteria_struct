Inclusion Criteria:
use of basal-bolus insulin
onset of diabetes after age 30
BMI less than 35
eGFR at least 60 ml/mn
Hb A1c 7.0-10.0%
willingness to perform home glucose monitoring
willingness to transmit glucose and medication information weekly

Exclusion Criteria:
Type 1 diabetes
Known peripheral artery disease
Liver enzymes equal or more than 1.5 times the upper limit of normal
Chronic heart failure NYHA class III or IV
Current haemodialysis [OR] or peritoneal dialysis
End stage liver disease, defined as acute [OR] or chronic liver disease [AND] and recent history of one of the following: ascites, [OR] encephalopathy, [OR] variceal bleeding, [OR] bilirubin equal or greater than 2.0 mg/dL, albumin equal or less than 3.5 g/ dL, [OR] prothrombin time greater or equal to 4 seconds, [OR] INR greater than or equal to 1.7 [OR] or prior liver transplant
Known [OR] or suspected hypersensitivity to trial products [OR] or related products
Female [AND] of child-bearing potential [AND] who is pregnant, [OR] breast-feeding [OR] or intends to become pregnant [OR] or is [NOT] not using adequate contraceptive methods as required by law [OR] or local practice.
Expected simultaneous participation in any other clinical trial of an investigational medicinal product.
Receipt of any investigational medicinal product within 30 days before randomization
Current [OR] or past (within the last 5 years) malignant neoplasms (except basal cell [OR] and squamous cell skin carcinoma)
 Any condition that in the investigator's opinion would make the subject [NOT] unable to adhere to the trial visit schedule [AND] and procedures
 Known history of [NOT] non-compliance to treatment.
