Inclusion Criteria:
Patients scheduled for thyroidectomy with general anesthesia in the University of Chile Clinical Hospital

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients ASA III y IV
Chronic pain history
Drug [OR] and alcohol abuse
Chronic use of opioid [OR] and sedatives
Neuropsychiatric illness
NSAID [OR] and other analgesics used the 48 hours previous to the surgery
CMI > 30
