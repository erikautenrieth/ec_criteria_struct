Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosed epileptic patients of either sex [AND] with age between 10-19 yrs (<19yrs), coming to the medicine Out Patient /In Patient Departments [AND] and undergoing AED therapy for more than 6 months. 
Epileptics [AND] with high homocysteine levels i.e. > 10.9 µmol/L (Normal homocysteine levels are 4.3-9.9 µmol/L for male [AND] and 3.3-7.2 µmol/L for female adolescent [AND] and a high homocysteine concentration is deaned as at least 11.4 µmol/L for male [AND] and at least 10.4 µmol/L for female. Gender mean of high homocysteine concentration is 10.9 µmol/L) [5] 

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnancy [AND] and lactation 
Patients with diabetes, [OR] Ischemic heart disease (IHD), [OR] stroke, [OR] malignancy [OR] and psychiatric diseases are [NOT] excluded from study. 
The patients receiving vitamin supplements [OR] or who had clinical evidence for an acute illness, [OR] renal dysfunction, [OR] thyroid dysfunction, [OR] chronic inflammatory diseases, [OR] inborn errors of homocysteine, [OR] cobalamin [OR] or folate metabolism, [OR] or any other condition known to interfere with homocysteine metabolism will be [NOT] excluded 
Patients who are already involved in any other trial. 
Patients [NOT] not willing to fill consent/ assent form are also [NOT] excluded from study.
