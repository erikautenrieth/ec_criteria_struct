Inclusion Criteria:
Kidney transplant recipients at Washington University/ [OR] Barnes-Jewish Hospital
Experiencing GI toxicity from MPA as determined by the treating physician within 12 months post-renal transplant
On standard immunosuppression [AND] with tacrolimus [AND] and prednisone

Exclusion Criteria:
Dual organ [OR] or kidney after another solid organ transplant
Presence of a preexisting significant GI condition that does [NOT] not have a presumed causal relationship with MPA
Evidence of any GI disorder induced by an infection, underlying medical condition, [OR] or concomitant medication other than MPA
eGFR<40 ml/min at time of possible conversion
Proteinuria >1 gram/day at time of possible conversion
Hemoglobin <10 g/dL
WBC <3 K/cumm
 Platelets <100 K/cumm
Wound healing issues at time of possible conversion (eg, wound dehiscence, [OR] wound infection, [OR] incisional hernia, [OR] lymphocele, [OR] seroma)
Elevated total cholesterol  (>350 mg/dL) [OR] and/or triglycerides (>500 ng/dL) at time of possible conversion
Hypersensitivity to everolimus, [OR] sirolimus, [OR] or other rapamycin deriviatives
