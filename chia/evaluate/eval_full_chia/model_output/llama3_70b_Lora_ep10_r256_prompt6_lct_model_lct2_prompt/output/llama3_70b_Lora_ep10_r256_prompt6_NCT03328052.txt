Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with a clinical diagnosis of depression [AND] who in the judgement of their physician require medication management may be eligible for enrollment. A score of 10 or more on the PHQ-9 instrument will be required for enrollment.
Some practices utilize the PHQ-2 [AND] and PHQ-9 are part of routine screening for depression. If the tests are performed routinely, they do not need to be repeated for study eligibility, and may be performed prior to informed consent for this study. If, however, the PHQ-9 is [NOT] not routinely performed, informed consent must be performed prior to administration. Patients with a score below 10 will be considered screen failures and will [NOT] not be enrolled or offered the MYnd testing.
Patients with [NOT] non-psychotic comorbid conditions may be included.
Patients must be either medication treatment [NOT] naïve [OR] for behavioral illnesses [OR] or have [NOT] no active medication treatments for at least 1 month prior to enrollment. Prohibited medications at the time of enrollment will include stimulants, [OR] benzodiazepines [OR] and THC. Prior therapy with these agents is permitted with a washout of >30 days.
Patients must have private medical insurance coverage through Horizon Blue Cross Blue Shield. This is limited to insured commercial members, including HMO, and excluding, for the avoidance of doubt, members of self-insured customers [OR] or Medicare [OR] or Medicaid programs.

Exclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis of a psychotic disorder.
History of, [OR] or current, open head brain trauma. Candidates with any metal, [OR] shrapnel [OR] or other similar objects in the head that could affect the QEEG
History of: craniotomy, [OR] cerebral metastases, [OR] cerebrovascular accident; current diagnosis of seizure disorder, [OR] schizophrenia, [OR] schizo-affective disorder, [OR] dementia, [OR] mental retardation, [OR] or major depression [AND] with psychotic features; [OR] or use of depot neuroleptics in last 12 months.
Uncontrolled thyroid disorders.
Known pregnancy [OR] and/or lactation, [OR] or intent to become pregnant during this study.
Chronic [OR] or acute pain requiring prescription pain medication(s) (narcotic [OR] or synthetic narcotic)
Participation in any other therapeutic drug study within 60 days preceding inclusion.
