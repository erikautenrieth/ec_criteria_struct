Inclusion Criteria:
The cirrhotic malnourished patients [AND] who were diagnosed as liver cancer preoperatively [AND] and underwent hepatectomy were consecutively enrolled.

Exclusion Criteria:
Contraindication for hepatectomy, including gastrointestinal hemorrhage, severe hemorrhagic disorders, explicit acute [NOT] nonspecific infectious lesion, overt ascites, [OR] Child-Pugh Score C, [OR] indocyanine green retention rate at 15min (ICGR15)＞30%(12), serum hepatitis B virus (HBV)-DNA＞126 copies/ml [AND] and serum alanine aminotransferase (ALT) ＞ 2×ULN, serum triglycerides＞2.0 mmol/L, circulatory shock, [OR] stroke, [OR] acute myocardial infarction, [OR] renal failure, [OR] coma of unknown cause 
Pregnancy 
Age of＜18y [OR] or＞75y 
Performed intraoperative ablation 
Unresectable tumor during operation 
Allergic reactions against fish [OR] or egg proteins
