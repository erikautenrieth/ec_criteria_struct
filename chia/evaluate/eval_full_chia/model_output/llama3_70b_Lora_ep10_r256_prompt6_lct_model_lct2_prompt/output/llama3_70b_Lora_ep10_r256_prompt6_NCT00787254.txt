Inclusion Criteria:
The patient was on nonsteroid anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID) treatment on the day when consent was obtained, [AND] and requires the long-term continuous treatment even after treatment with the investigational drug is started. 
The patient was confirmed to have a history of gastric ulcer [OR] or duodenal ulcer. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Endoscopically confirmed gastric [OR] and/or duodenal ulcers on Day 1. 
Endoscopically confirmed active upper gastrointestinal hemorrhage on Day 1. 
Current [OR] or past history of aspirin-induced asthma [OR] or hypersensitivity to NSAIDs. 
Past [OR] or planned surgery affecting gastric acid secretion. 
Clinically significant hepatic [OR] or renal disorder. 
Serious cardiac dysfunction, [OR] hypertension, [OR] or hematological disorder.
