Inclusion Criteria:
1. Male [OR] or female, 18-75 years old. 
2. Has a diagnosis of WHO Group 1 PAH. 
3. Right heart catheterization performed at Screening [AND] with results that are: 
1. Mean pulmonary arterial pressure ≥25 mmHg (at rest) [AND] and 
2. Pulmonary venous hypertension (measured as pulmonary capillary wedge pressure (PCWP) ≤15 mmHg. If PCWP is [NOT] not available, then mean left atrial pressure [OR] or left ventricular end-diastolic pressure ≤15 mmHg [NOT] in the [NOT] absence of left atrial obstruction. [AND] and 
3. Pulmonary vascular resistance (PVR) ≥300 dyn•s/cm5 (3.75 Wood units) 
4. Has WHO/ [OR] NYHA-FC of II or III. 
5. Be on stable dose of at least one of the following PAH-specific therapies: endothelin receptor antagonist, [OR] an agent acting on the nitric oxide pathway [OR] (phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitor [OR] or soluble guanylate cyclase stimulator), [OR] and/or a prostacyclin [OR] or prostacyclin analog. 
6. Has a 6-minute walk distance that is ≥150 [AND] and ≤500 meters. 
7. Have a ventilation-perfusion scan that rules [NOT] out thromboembolic disease. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Exclusions Related to Cardiovascular Disease 
1. History of uncontrolled hypertension 
2. Persistent hypotension at Screening. 
3. Evidence [OR] or history of left-sided heart disease [OR] and/or clinically significant cardiac disease in which pulmonary hypertension is more likely WHO Group 2. 
4. Acute decompensated heart failure within 1 month of Screening. 
5. Recent initiation (<8 weeks from Screening) [OR] or planned initiation of cardiopulmonary rehabilitation exercise program. 
Exclusions Related to Pulmonary Disease 
6. Newly diagnosed [AND] with PAH [AND] and [NOT] not on PAH-specific therapy. 
7. Pulmonary hypertension due to: 
1. Uncorrected congenital systemic-to-pulmonary shunt. 
2. Pulmonary veno-occlusive disease [OR] and/or pulmonary capillary hemangiomatosis 
3. Persistent pulmonary hypertension of the newborn 
4. WHO clinical classification Groups 2-5 
8. Evidence of significant airway [OR] and/or parenchymal lung disease. 
9. Chronic infection related to tuberculosis [OR] or fungal [OR] or mycobacterial disease. 
Exclusions Based on Other Medical Conditions
10. Chronic infections including, but not limited to tuberculosis (TB), [OR] hepatitis B virus (HBV) [OR] or hepatitis C virus (HCV). 
11. History of portal hypertension [OR] or chronic liver disease, including positive serology for infection with HCV [OR] and/or HBV. 
12. Evidence of active infection requiring intravenous [OR] or oral antibiotics within 4 weeks of Screening. 
13. Body mass index ≥35.0 at Screening. 
14. History of obstructive sleep apnea. 
15. History of malignancy [OR] within the last 5 years, except [NOT] nonmelanoma skin cancer [OR] and cervical carcinoma in situ treated with curative intent. 
16. Neuropsychiatric disorders/ [OR] symptoms [OR] or psychological conditions. 
17. Pregnancy [OR] or breast-feeding 
18. Prior treatment with B cell [OR] or lymphocyte-depleting agents (eg, rituximab, [OR] Campath) 
Exclusions Based on Concomitant Medication Use
19. Concurrent regular use of another leukotriene pathway inhibitor, including over-the-counter medications [OR] or herbal remedies. 
Exclusions Based on Laboratory Values
20. Significant/ [OR] chronic renal insufficiency. 
21. Transaminases (alanine transaminase, [OR] aspartate transaminase) levels >3 × upper limit of normal (ULN) [AND] and/or bilirubin level >2 × ULN. 
22. Absolute neutrophil count <1500 mm3. 
23. Hemoglobin concentration <9 g/dL at Screening. 
24. Hepatic dysfunction as defined by Child-Pugh Class B [OR] or C
