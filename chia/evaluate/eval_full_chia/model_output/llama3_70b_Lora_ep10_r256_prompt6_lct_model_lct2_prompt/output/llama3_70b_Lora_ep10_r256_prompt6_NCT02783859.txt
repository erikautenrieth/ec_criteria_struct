Inclusion Criteria:
Hospitalised children aged 3-mo to 5-yrs (in Darwin, children have to be Indigenous)
Have features of severe pneumonia on admission (temperature >37.5 celsius [OR] or a history of fever at home [OR] or observed at the referring clinic, age-adjusted tachypnoea  [respiratory rate>50 if <12-months; respiratory rate>40 if >12-months] [AND] with chest wall recession [OR] and/or oxygen saturation <92% in air), [AND] and consolidation on chest X-ray as diagnosed by the treating clinician
After 1-3 days of IV antibiotics, [NOT] are afebrile, [AND] with improved respiratory symptoms [AND] and signs, oxygen saturation>90% in air [AND] and are ready to be switched to oral amoxicillin-clavulanate, [AND] and
Have symptoms of no longer than 7 days at point of hospitalisation.

Exclusion Criteria:
Current wheeze
Underlying chronic illness other than asthma (e.g. bronchiectasis, [OR] cyanotic congenital heart disease [OR] or cardiac failure, [OR] neuromuscular disorders, [OR] immunodeficiency) that could potentially influence the current illness
Severe malnutrition (weight-for-height Z-score <-3)
Complicated (effusion, [OR] empyema [OR] or abscess) pneumonia, including tuberculosis
Extra-pulmonary infection requiring antibiotic therapy (e.g. meningitis)
 Beta-lactam allergy
Previously enrolled
Lack a mobile phone and/or [NOT] unable to return for follow-up clinic visits during the next 24 months
