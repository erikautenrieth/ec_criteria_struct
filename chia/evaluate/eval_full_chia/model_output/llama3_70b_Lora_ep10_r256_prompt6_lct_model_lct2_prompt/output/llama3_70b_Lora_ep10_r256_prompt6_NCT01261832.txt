Inclusion Criteria:
Acute Myocardial Infarction Undergoing Primary percutaneous coronary intervention.

Exclusion Criteria:
The patient has a known hypersensitivity [OR] or contraindication to any of the following medications: Heparin, [OR] Aspirin, [OR] Clopidogrel, [OR] Cilostazol
Uncontrolled hypertension
History of bleeding diathesis [OR] or known coagulopathy (including heparin-induced thrombocytopenia), [OR] or refuses blood transfusions.
Baseline hemogram with Hb<10g/dL [OR] or PLT count<100,000/μL
Patients already taking warfarin, [OR] cilostazol [OR] or any other type of anti-platelet agents except aspirin [OR] and clopidogrel
Gastrointestinal [OR] or genitourinary bleeding within the prior 3 months, [OR] or major surgery within 2 months.
Pregnancy
