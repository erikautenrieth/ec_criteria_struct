Inclusion Criteria:
Patients scheduled for laser laryngeal surgery under general anesthesia with either Propofol [OR] or desflurane based technique.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with cardiac, [OR] pulmonary, [OR] hepatic, [OR] or renal dysfunction, [OR] epilepsy, [OR] or uncontrolled hypertension, [OR] or those taking medications that influence the central nervous system, are [NOT] excluded from the study. Patients who show obvious alteration of mental status, [OR] or refuse to participate, are also [NOT] excluded from the study.
