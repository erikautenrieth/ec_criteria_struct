Inclusion Criteria:
1. HIV infection [AND] with plasma [AND] and CSF HIV RNA concentrations (using Roche Amplicor assay) > 1,000 copies/ mL (available after baseline LP). 
2. [NOT] Off antiretroviral therapy (ART) for > 6 weeks before the study [AND] and [NOT] no plans to begin treatment for the study duration. (The decision of whether or not a subject takes antiretroviral therapy will be made by the subject in consultation with his/her primary care provider prior to screening for this study.) 
3. Predicted adherence to the medication. 
4. Capable of providing informed consent. 
5. > 18 years old 
6. CD4 cell counts >150 cells/μL (though likely most, if not all, will be >250 cells/μL). 
7. When available, subjects will be screened for stability of blood CD4 [AND] and HIV RNA levels. 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Taking a tetracycline within 6 months [OR] or history of adverse reaction to minocycline [OR] or another tetracycline. 
2. Enhanced risk from lumbar puncture, including documented [OR] or suspected cerebral mass lesion predisposing to brain herniation [OR] or bleeding diathesis. 
3. Pregnancy [OR] or expectation of pregnancy during the study. 
4. Active opportunistic infection [OR] or active neurological disease that might confound evaluation. 
5. ADC Stage > 1. 
6. Hemoglobin < 10 Gms/dL. 
7. BUN [OR] or creatine above the normal limits. 
8. Taking other drugs known to reduce the metabolism of minocycline [AND] and thus increase the probability of toxicity.
