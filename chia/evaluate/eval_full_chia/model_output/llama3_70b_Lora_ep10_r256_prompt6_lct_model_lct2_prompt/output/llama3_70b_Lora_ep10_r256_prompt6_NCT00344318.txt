Inclusion Criteria:
Male [OR] or female between, and including, 6-12 weeks (42 to 90 days) of age at the time of the first vaccination. 
Subjects for whom the investigator believes that their parents/guardians can and will comply with the requirements of the protocol 
Written informed consent obtained from the parent or guardian of the subject. 
Free of obvious health problems as established by medical history and clinical examination before entering into the study. 
Born after a gestation period between 36 and 42 weeks. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Use of any investigational [OR] or non-registered product (drug [OR] or vaccine) other than the study vaccine(s) within 30 days preceding the first dose of study vaccine, [OR] or planned use during the study period 
Chronic administration (defined as more than 14 days) of immunosuppressants [OR] or other immune-modifying drugs within six months prior to the first vaccine dose. 
Planned administration/ [OR] administration of a vaccine [NOT] not foreseen by the study protocol during the period starting one month before each dose of vaccine(s) [AND] and ending 7 days after dose 1 [AND] and dose 2 [OR] or 1 month after dose 3. 
Previous vaccination against diphtheria, [OR] tetanus, [OR] pertussis, [OR] polio, [OR] hepatitis B, [OR] Haemophilus influenzae type b, [OR] and/or S. pneumoniae with the exception of vaccines where the first dose can be given within the first two weeks of life according to the national recommendations 
History of or intercurrent diphtheria, [OR] tetanus, [OR] pertussis, [OR] hepatitis B, [OR] polio, [OR] and Haemophilus influenzae type b diseases. 
History of allergic disease [OR] or reactions likely to be exacerbated by any component of the vaccines. 
 History of seizures (this criterion does [NOT] not apply to subjects [AND] who have had a single, uncomplicated febrile convulsion in the past) [OR] or neurological disease. 
Acute disease at the time of enrolment 
Any confirmed [OR] or suspected immunosuppressive [OR] or immunodeficient condition based on medical history [AND] and physical 
A family history of congenital [OR] or hereditary immunodeficiency. 
Major congenital defects [OR] or serious chronic illness. 
Administration of immunoglobulins [OR] and/or any blood products since birth [OR] or planned administration during the active phase of the study.
