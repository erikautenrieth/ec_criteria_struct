Inclusion Criteria:
Male [OR] or female subjects aged =18 to =65 years
General good health as established by medical history [AND] and physical examination
 Written informed consent
Females [AND] of childbearing potential must agree to use an efficacious hormonal [OR] or barrier method of birth control during the study. Abstinence is acceptable.
Available for all visits scheduled in this study.

Exclusion Criteria:
Primary groups: Vaccination against typhoid fever within 5 years before dosing.
History of clinical typhoid fever, clinical paratyphoid A [OR] or B fever.
Immunization with any other vaccine (oral [OR] or parenteral) within 4 weeks prior to study start [OR] or planned vaccination during the study
Current intake of antibiotics [OR] or end of antibiotic therapy <8 days before first IMP administration
Chronic (longer than 14 days) administration of immunosuppressants [OR] or other immune-modifying drugs within 6 months before the first dose of investigational vaccine; oral corticosteroids in dosages of =0.5 mg/kg/d prednisolone [OR] or equivalent are [NOT] excluded; inhaled [OR] or topical steroids are allowed
