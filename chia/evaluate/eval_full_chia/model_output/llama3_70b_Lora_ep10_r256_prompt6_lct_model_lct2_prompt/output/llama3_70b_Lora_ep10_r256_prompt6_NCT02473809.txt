Inclusion Criteria:
Informed consent
Diagnosis of type 2 diabetes (HbA1c > 48 mmol/mol)
Age older than 30 years

Exclusion Criteria:
Type 1 diabetes
Treatment with insulin
Body weight > 140 kg
HbA1c > 75 mmol/mol
Treatment with GLP-1 analogues, [OR] Dipeptidyl peptidase-4 inhibitors, [OR] or glitazones
Chronic kidney disease
Hbatic disease
Pancreatitis
Inflammatory bowel disease
Osteoporosis
Family [OR] or personal history of medullary thyroid carcinoma
Treatment with glucocorticoids
Hormone replacement therapy
Diabetic gastroparesis
Pregnancy [OR] or lactation
