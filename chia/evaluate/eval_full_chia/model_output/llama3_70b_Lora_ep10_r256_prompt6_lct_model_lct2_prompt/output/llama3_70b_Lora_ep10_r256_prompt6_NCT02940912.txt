Inclusion Criteria:
Idiopathic Parkinson's disease ( Hughes AJ et al. 2001)
Patients with motor fluctuations
Chronic Insomnia disorder criteria according to the criteria of DMS- V ( American Psychiatric Association, 2013) [AND] and insomnia severity index > 15
Able to use independently the device required for treatment by apomorphine
Collection of written informed consent (legal obligation for any project under the public health law, bioethics laws and / or CNIL).
Affiliate to social security or beneficiary of such a regime

Exclusion Criteria:
Atypical Parkinsonian Syndromes
Parkinson's disease [AND] with hallucinations
Parkinson's disease [AND] with impulse Control disorder (ICD)
Parkinson's disease already treated with APOMORPHINE pump [OR] or justifying the use of the pump continuously day [AND] and night
Another obvious severe disease explaining insomnia
Exclusion for monitoring difficulties (mutation, [NOT] insufficient motivation, priority associated pathology in care)
Patient unwilling to accept a pump
Patient [NOT] not accepting polysomnography [OR] and multiple sleep latency test
Patient with health problems [OR] or a skin disease [NOT] precluding continuous subcutaneous infusion
Female parturient [OR] or nursing
Cardiac dysrhythmia [NOT] precluding treatment with domperidone [OR] or apomorphine (increased QTc = 440 ms in men, QTc = 450 ms in women)
antiemetic neuroleptics
Tetrabenazine
Excessive alcohol consumption
Hypersensitivity to apomorphine [OR] or one of the excipients
Respiratory Depression
 Hepatic impairment
Intellectual Disability
Dementia
