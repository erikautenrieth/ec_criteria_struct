Inclusion Criteria:
1. Age ≥ 18 years 
2. Patient with breast cancer, histologically proven, metastatic [OR] or locally advanced 
3. Patient treated by Eribulin between January and October 2014 (for the retrospective part) [OR] or between November 2014 and September 2015 (for the prospective part). 
4. Patient with at least an assessment of the response to Eribulin 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Presence of other neoplasia 
2. Man
