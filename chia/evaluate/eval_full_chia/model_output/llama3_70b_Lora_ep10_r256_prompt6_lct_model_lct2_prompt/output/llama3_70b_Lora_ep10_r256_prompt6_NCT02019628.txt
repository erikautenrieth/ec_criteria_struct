Inclusion Criteria:
1. Women [OR] and men ages 18 years and over. 
2. Interest in participating in a novel nutritional supplement program. 
3. Willingness to follow recommendations. 

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Currently enrolled in another research trial for investigative nutritional [OR] or other therapies thought to have an impact on immune system functioning. 
 2. Unable to consent to the study. 
 3. Women [AND] who are pregnant [OR] or are attempting conception, especially in the presence of a history of recurrent spontaneous abortion. 
 4. Other medical complications that might [NOT] preclude one from participating in the study, i.e., recent heart attack [OR] or stroke [OR] or chronic kidney disease. 
 5. Currently taking immunomodulatory medication, i.e. interferon. 
 6. Currently taking other medications thought to have an impact on immune system functioning, i.e., chemotherapeutic agents. 
 7. Known allergy to rice, [OR] rice bran, [OR] or related food products. 
 8. Known allergy to mushrooms [OR] or related food products. 
 9. History of malignancies related to the NK cell line, including: NK cell leukemias [OR] and T-cell large granular lymphocyte leukemias, [OR] NK-cell lymphoproliferative disease [OR] of granular lymphocytes, [OR] and NK cell lymphomas, e.g., nasal [OR] and nasal-like NK/T-cell lymphomas. 
 10. Current smoker.
