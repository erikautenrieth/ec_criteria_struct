Inclusion Criteria:
Women [AND] with singleton pregnancy.
History of preterm labor [OR] and/or midtrimester miscarriage in a previous pregnancy.
Cervical length of 15-25 mm by transvaginal sonography (TVS) at 16-24 weeks of gestation.

Exclusion Criteria:
Age < 20 [OR] or > 35 years.
Congenital uterine malformation.
Multifetal pregnancy.
Known major fetal structural [OR] or chromosomal abnormality.
 Known allergy [OR] or contraindication (relative [OR] or absolute) to progesterone therapy.
 Presence of contraindication to cervical cerclage.
 Medical conditions complicating pregnancy.
 Vaginal bleeding.
