Inclusion Criteria:
Patient over 18 years weighing between 65 and 85 Kg
Referred for STEMI within 6 hours from beginning of chest pain [OR] or stable coronary artery disease requiring a loading dose of Prasugrel [OR] or Ticagrelor according to the international recommendations.
 [NOT] No previous treatment with Clopidogrel, [OR] Prasugrel [OR] or Ticagrelor.
Patient fasting for at least 6 hours.
Affiliate or receiving a social security system.
Written informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
Allergy [OR] or contraindication to paracetamol, [OR] Prasugrel [OR] or Ticagrelor
Paracetamol ingestion in the previous 48 hours
Patient treated with drugs supposed to alter gastric emptying times (calcium antagonists, Alimentary tract treatments, [OR] opioid analgesics, [OR] tricyclic antidepressants, [OR] antibiotics).
Conditions [OR] or pathologies supposed to alter gastric emptying times (Thyroid dysfunction, chronic renal failure, [OR] Parkinson's disease, [OR] scleroderma, [OR] amyloidosis, any gastrointestinal disease, any [NOT] not cured malignancy, [OR] and any advanced psychiatric [OR] or neurological disease).
Presence of vomiting
Cardiogenic shock, [OR] ventricular arrhythmia [OR] or resuscitated cardiac arrest
Hepatic insufficiency
Severe respiratory disease
Pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding women
