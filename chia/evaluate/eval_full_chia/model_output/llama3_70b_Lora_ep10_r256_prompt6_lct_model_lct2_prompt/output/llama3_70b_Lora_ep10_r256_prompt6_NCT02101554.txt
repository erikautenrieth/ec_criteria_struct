Inclusion Criteria:
Children 7-17 [AND] with moderate [OR] to severe pain requiring around the clock treatment with an opioid analgesic.
Be an experienced opioid user, defined as any subject treated with opioid therapy, equivalent or equal to >20 mg per day of morphine, for a period of 3 consecutive days immediately prior to first day of dosing.

Exclusion Criteria:
Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS) for suicidal ideation [OR] and behavior in past year.
Hypersensitivity to morphine, [OR] naltrexone.
A life expectancy (assessed by investigator) of less than 6 months [OR] or is [NOT] no longer capable of taking medication orally.
Undergone surgery within 3 days prior to the first day of dosing.
