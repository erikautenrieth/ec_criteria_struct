Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Cognitive impairment from mild to moderate degree defined by a Clinical Deterioration Rating (CDR) score range between 0.5 and 2.0. 
 2. Evidence on brain MRI of white matter hyperintensities (leukoaraiosis of moderate or severe degree according to the modified Fazekas visual scale [OR] and/or presence of lacunar infarcts). 
 3. Consent to participation in the study. 

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. [NOT] Absence of objectionable cognitive impairment [OR] or presence of dementia of severe degree defined by CDR score > 2.0. 
 2. Unavailability of brain MRI (in case of absolute contraindications, the use of cranial CT is allowed). 
 3. Expected poor compliance with the study protocol. 
 4. Past diagnosis of major depression, [OR] schizophrenia, [OR] major anxiety syndrome, [OR] or manic- depressive illness. 
 5. Diagnosis of degenerative cognitive impairment based on clinical [OR] and/or neuroradiological findings (i.e., patients with prevailing memory impairment, [OR] or with medial temporal atrophy on brain MRI [NOT] in [NOT] absence of evident vascular abnormalities; i.e., Alzheimer disease as defined using the National Institute of Neurological and Communicative Disorders and Stroke/ [OR] Alzheimer's Disease and Related Disorders Association criteria, [OR] Parkinson disease, [OR] Huntington disease, [OR] frontotemporal dementia). 
 6. Diagnosis of cognitive impairment from other causes (i.e., vitamine B12 [OR] and folic acid deficiency, [OR] thyroid disorders, [OR] metabolic diseases, [OR] head trauma, [OR] tumor [OR] or infections of the central nervous system, [OR] normal pressure hydrocephalus). 
 7. Medical conditions expected to progress, [OR] recur, [OR] or change to such a degree to interfere with the assessment of the clinical [OR] and mental status. 
 8. Clinically relevant cardiac [OR] or pulmonary insufficiency. 
 9. Relevant electrocardiograph abnormalities; bradycardia (50 bpm) [OR] or tachycardia (120 bpm) under resting conditions. 
 10. Myocardial infarction within the past 6 months. 
 11. Stroke still requiring neurological rehabilitation. 
 12. Severe/ [NOT] untreated blood pressure (systolic 180 mm Hg, diastolic 95 mm Hg). 
 13. Clinically relevant liver function impairment. 
 14. Insulin-dependent diabetes mellitus. 
 15. Idiopathic epilepsy [OR] and anti-epileptic treatment. 
 16. Severe anemia (Hb <10 mg/dL). 
 17. Severe gastrointestinal disease. 
 18. Cancer. 
 19. Known intolerance to study drugs. 
 20. Coexistent serious illnesses that would imply a drop-out before the end of the trial.
