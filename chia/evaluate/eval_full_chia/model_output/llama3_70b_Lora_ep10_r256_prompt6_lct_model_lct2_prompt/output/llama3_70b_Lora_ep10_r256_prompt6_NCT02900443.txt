Inclusion Criteria:
Probable [OR] or definite diagnosis of autoimmune hepatitis according to the International Autoimmune Hepatitis Study Group criteria
First presentation of AIH requiring treatment according to the current EASL guidelines
Age = 18 years
Must provide informed consent and agree to comply with the trial protocol

Exclusion Criteria:
Overlap syndrome [AND] with Primary Sclerosing Cholangitis (PSC) [OR] or Primary Biliary Cholangitis (PBC) (Paris criteria, strong positive Anti-Mitochondrial Antibodies (AMA), past liver biopsy [OR] or cholangiographic findings compatible with PBC [OR] or PSC).
Presentation with acute liver failure, defined as presence of hepatic encephalopathy [OR] and coagulopathy (INR > 1.5)
Current treatment with prednisone/ [OR] prednisolone [OR] and/or immunosuppressive medication for an indication other than autoimmune hepatitis
Current systemic infection
Other clinically significant medical conditions that could interfere with the trial
If female [AND] of childbearing potential: known pregnancy, [OR] or unwilling to practice anticontraceptive measures.
History of [NOT] noncompliance with medical regimens, [OR] or patients who are considered to be potentially unreliable [OR] or unable to participate
Mental instability [OR] or incompetence, such that the validity of informed consent or compliance with the trial is uncertain
