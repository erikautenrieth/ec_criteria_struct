Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Male [OR] or female of any race, at least 18 years of age at Visit 1 Screening. 
 2. Has provided verbal and written informed consent. 
 3. Be able and willing to follow instructions, including participation in all study assessments and visits. 
 4. Currently being treated for glaucoma using at least two medications, [AND] and be willing to continue on the same regime. 
 5. Suffers from at least two of the symptoms in the GLIA™ Glaucoma Medication Ocular Side Effect Symptoms Questionnaire at a severity of 2 (moderate) or more. 
 6. If a woman of childbearing potential, have a negative urine pregnancy test at Visit 1 [AND] and be using an adequate method of birth control throughout the study period. 

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Comorbidity with other severe [OR] or chronic eye conditions that in the judgment of the investigator will interfere with study assessments, such as corneal opacities [OR] and scars, [OR] dystrophies, [OR] epithelial scarring, [OR] infections, [OR] blood clots, etc. 
 2. Best corrected visual acuity (BCVA) at baseline <20/200. 
 3. Has a condition [OR] or history that, in the opinion of the investigator, may interfere significantly with the subject's participation in the study. 
 4. A woman [AND] who is pregnant, [OR] nursing an infant, [OR] or planning a pregnancy. 
 5. Has a known adverse reaction [OR] and/or sensitivity to the study drug [OR] or its components. 
 6. Routine use (more than twice a week) of a chlorinated swimming pool. 
 7. Unwilling [OR] or [NOT] unable to cease using the following medications during the study period: Topical ocular cyclosporine (e.g. Restasis®), anti-histamines, [OR] antipsychotics, [OR] or eye gels. 
 8. Currently enrolled in an investigational drug [OR] or device study [OR] or have used an investigational drug [OR] or device within 30 days prior to Visit 1.
