Inclusion Criteria:
Patients presenting for abdominal myomectomy [AND] with documented uterine fibroids on pelvic imaging (pelvic ultrasound [OR] or MRI) within in last 12 months
Age = 18 years [AND] and = 50 years
Pre-operative hemoglobin >8 g/dl
Willing to have buccal administration of misoprostol [OR] or a placebo at least one hour pre-procedure.
Ability to understand and the willingness to sign a written informed consent.
Admissible medical/ [OR] surgical history
Can be previously treated with Depo-Lupron, [OR] Depo-Provera, [OR] or Oral Contraceptive pills
Intraoperative use of vasopressin [OR] and uterine tourniquet is permissible
Can have had prior Cesarean delivery

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients who have had a prior abdominal myomectomy
Post-menopausal women
Patients with known bleeding/ [OR] clotting disorders
Patients with a history of gynecologic malignancy
History of allergic reactions attributed to compounds of similar chemical [OR] or biologic composition to misoprostol
Any cases converted to abdominal hysterectomy [OR] or other additional elective surgical procedures performed at time of abdominal myomectomy will be [NOT] excluded from data analysis
Uncontrolled intercurrent illness including, but not limited to, ongoing [OR] or active infection, symptomatic congestive heart failure, unstable angina pectoris, [OR] cardiac arrhythmia, [OR] or psychiatric illness/ [OR] social situations that would limit compliance with study requirements.
