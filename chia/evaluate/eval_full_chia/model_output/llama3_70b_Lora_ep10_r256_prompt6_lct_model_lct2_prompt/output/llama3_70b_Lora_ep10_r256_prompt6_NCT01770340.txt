Inclusion Criteria:
Localized intermediate-risk [OR] or high-risk prostate cancer cT3
Gleason score = 7 (3+4 [OR] and/or 4+3) [OR] and/or
PSA = 20 ng/ml
intact preoperative erectile function [AND] with an IIEF = 21 (IIEF-6).

Exclusion Criteria:
IIEF < 21
Operations in the past 6 months which could [NOT] limit the erectile function
Erectile dysfunction in the history [OR] or current medication for erectile dysfunction
Current involvement in another comparable study.
