Inclusion Criteria:
all adult patients with a nasal [OR] or facial skin/ [OR] soft tissue defect requiring reconstruction limited to or including a full-thickness skin graft

Exclusion Criteria:
current [OR] or recent (within one week of surgery) systemic antibiotic use, intolerance to both clindamycin [OR] and cephalexin, discovery of a persistent cutaneous malignancy at the site of the defect following the reconstructive procedure and previous reconstruction at the site of the skin/ [OR] soft-tissue defect.
