Inclusion Criteria:
Pathologic confirmation of lung adenocarcinoma [AND] with measurable disease, defined as at least one lesion that can be accurately measured in at least one dimension (longest diameter to be recorded on CT); Patients must have previously [NOT] untreated locally advanced [OR] or metastatic NSCLC; Patients must have lung cancer [AND] with a documented EGFR activating mutation (exon 19 deletion, [OR] L858R).

Exclusion Criteria:
Prior chemotherapy Prior treatment with gefitinib, [OR] erlotinib, [OR] or other drugs that target EGFR Patients must [NOT] not be receiving any other investigational agents Any evidence of interstitial lung disease
