Inclusion Criteria:
Age = 18 years
American Society of Anesthesiologists Classification I-III
Normal cognitive function in order to sign written, informed consent and to understand trial protocol
Agreement to the trial protocol, including the randomized manner

Exclusion Criteria:
Allergy to LA
Infection in [OR] or near insertion site of the peripheral nerve catheter
Anatomical abnormalities [NOT] preventing successful peripheral catheter insertion
Habitual use of opioids
Pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding (disproved by a negative pregnancy test before trial inclusion)
