Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with biopsy-proven metastatic carcinoid tumors [OR] or other neuroendocrine tumors (Islet cell, [OR] Gastrinomas [OR] and VIPomas) [AND] with at least one measurable lesion (other than bone) that has either [NOT] not been previously irradiated [OR] or if previously irradiated has demonstrated progression since the radiation therapy 
The patient has [NOT] no major impairment of renal [OR] or hepatic function, as defined by the following laboratory parameters: total bilirubin <1.5 X ULN; AST, [OR] ALT<2.5X ULN (<5 X ULN if liver metastases are present) 
Patients on Sandostatin Lar (long acting somatostatin analogue) must be on a stable dose for 30 days prior to study entry [AND] and short acting somatostatin analogues must be judged to be on a clinically stable dose by the investigator prior to study entry 
Must have a life expectancy of greater than three (3) months 
Karnofsky Performance Status > 60 
Female patients must have a negative serum pregnancy test at screening. ([NOT] Not applicable to patients with bilateral oophorectomy [OR] and/or hysterectomy [OR] or to those patients who are postmenopausal.) 

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with symptomatic CNS metastases [OR] or leptomeningeal involvement 
Patients with known brain metastases, [OR] unless these metastases have been treated [OR] and/or have been stable for at least six months prior to study start. Subjects with a history of brain metastases must have a head CT [AND] with contrast to document either response [OR] or progression. 
Patients with bone metastases as the only site(s) of measurable disease 
Patients with hepatic artery chemoembolization within the last 6 months ([OR] one month if there are other sites of measurable disease) 
Patients who have been previously treated with radioactive directed therapies 
Patients who have been previously treated with epothilone 
Patients with any peripheral neuropathy [OR] or unresolved diarrhea greater than Grade 1 
Patients with severe cardiac insufficiency patients taking Coumadin [OR] or other warfarin-containing agents with the exception of low dose warfarin (1 mg or less) for the maintenance of in-dwelling lines [OR] or ports 
Patients taking any experimental therapies history of another malignancy within 5 years prior to study entry except curatively treated [NOT] non-melanoma skin cancer, [OR] prostate cancer, [OR] or cervical cancer in situ 
Patients with active [OR] or suspected acute [OR] or chronic uncontrolled infection including abcesses [OR] or fistulae 
Patients with a medical [OR] or psychiatric illness that would [NOT] preclude study or informed consent and/or history of [NOT] noncompliance to medical regimens [OR] or [NOT] inability [OR] or [NOT] unwillingness to return for all scheduled visits 
HIV+ patients 
Pregnant [OR] or lactating females.
