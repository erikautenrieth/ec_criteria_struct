Inclusion Criteria:
patients with renal cancer coming to the laparoscopic radical nephrectomy

Exclusion Criteria:
age under 18y [OR] or over 85y
diabetes type 1 [AND] with complications
[NOT] no co-operation [OR] or inadequate finnish language skills
persistent pain for other reason
severe hepatic insufficiency [OR] or paracetamol (acetaminophen) is contraindicated for other reason
any type of steroid in regular use
oxycodone contraindicated
medications changing notably paracetamol (acetaminophen) [OR] and/or ropivacaine metabolism in regular use
