Inclusion Criteria:
Histologically [OR] or cytologically confirmed adenocarcinoma of the breast [AND] with locally advanced [OR] or metastatic disease, [AND] and a candidate for chemotherapy. 
Human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (HER2)-positive. 
 [NOT] No prior chemotherapy [AND] for their metastatic breast cancer (MBC). 
Measurable disease. 
Age ≥ 18 years. 
For women [AND] of childbearing potential [AND] and men [AND] with partners of childbearing potential, agreement to use a highly effective, [NOT] non-hormonal form of contraception [OR] or 2 effective forms of [NOT] non-hormonal contraception by the patient and/or partner. Contraception use must continue for the duration of study treatment [AND] and for at least 6 months after the last dose of study treatment. Male patients [AND] whose partners are pregnant should use condoms for the duration of the study. 

Exclusion Criteria:
History of any chemotherapy [AND] for MBC. 
An interval of < 6 months from the completion of cytotoxic chemotherapy in the neo-adjuvant [OR] or adjuvant setting until the time of metastatic diagnosis. 
Trastuzumab ≤ 21 days prior to randomization. 
Hormone therapy < 7 days prior to randomization. 
Current peripheral neuropathy of Grade ≥ 3. 
History of other malignancy within the last 5 years, except for appropriately treated carcinoma in situ of the cervix, [NOT] non-melanoma skin carcinoma, [OR] Stage I uterine cancer, [OR] or other cancers with a similar outcome as those previously mentioned. 
Previous radiotherapy for the treatment of unresectable, locally advanced [OR] or metastatic breast cancer is [NOT] not allowed if more than 25% of marrow-bearing bone has been irradiated [OR] or the last fraction of radiotherapy has been administered within approximately 3 weeks prior to randomization. 
Brain metastases [AND] that are [NOT] untreated, symptomatic, [OR] or require therapy to control symptoms [OR] or any radiation, [OR] surgery, [OR] or other therapy to control symptoms from brain metastases within 2 months prior to randomization. 
History of exposure to the following cumulative doses of anthracyclines: Doxorubicin [OR] or liposomal doxorubicin > 500 mg/m^2; epirubicin > 900 mg/m^2; mitoxantrone > 120mg/m^2 [OR] and idarubicin > 90 mg/m^2. 
Current unstable angina. 
History of symptomatic congestive heart failure, [OR] or ventricular arrhythmia requiring treatment. 
History of myocardial infarction within 6 months prior to randomization. 
Left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) below 50% within approximately 28 days prior to randomization. 
History of decreased LVEF [OR] or symptomatic congestive heart failure (CHF) [AND] with previous adjuvant trastuzumab treatment. 
Cardiac troponin I ≥ 0.2 ng/mL within 28 days of randomization. 
Severe dyspnea at rest because of complications of advanced malignancy [OR] or requiring current continuous oxygen therapy. 
Current severe, uncontrolled systemic disease (eg, clinically significant cardiovascular, [OR] pulmonary, [OR] or metabolic disease; wound healing disorders; [OR] ulcers; [OR] or bone fractures). 
Major surgical procedure [OR] or significant traumatic injury within approximately 28 days prior to randomization [OR] or anticipation of the need for major surgery during the course of study treatment. 
Current pregnancy [OR] or lactation. 
History of receiving any investigational treatment within approximately 28 days prior to randomization. 
Current known infection with human immunodeficiency virus (HIV), active hepatitis B [OR] and/or hepatitis C virus. 
History of intolerance (including Grade 3-4 infusion reaction) [OR] or hypersensitivity to trastuzumab, [OR] murine proteins, [OR] or docetaxel. 
Known hypersensitivity to any of the study drugs, including the excipients, [OR] or any drugs formulated in polysorbate 80. 
Assessed by the investigator to be unable or unwilling to comply with the requirements of the protocol.
