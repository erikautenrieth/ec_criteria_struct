Inclusion Criteria:
For both groups: 
Patients aged from 18 to 65 years old. 
Both genders eligible for study. 
Female participants must use a contraceptive method. 
Feasibility of patch testing. 
Participants must be able to understand and sign the Informed Consent, and comply with all aspects of the protocol. 
Patients must be registered in a social security system or with a health insurance coverage 
 First group: allergic patients 
Patients with allergic contact dermatitis to para-phenylenediamine (PPD) based on a history of PPD contact dermatitis [OR] and positive PPD patch tests. 
  Second group : healthy volunteers 
 [NOT] No history of PPD allergic contact dermatitis, [AND] with a negative PPD patch test. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant [OR] or lactating women. 
Evolutive skin disease on the testing zone (lower back). 
Patients with a clinically significant disease (chronic, [OR] recurrent [OR] or active). 
Systemic corticotherapy [OR] or immunosuppressive treatment during the previous month, [OR] or local corticoid treatment the week before the patch testing. 
Local [OR] or systemic drug use which interacts with the outcome measures. 
Exposure to sun [OR] or UV radiations, 15 days before the patch testing. 
Patients deprived of their civic rights, in custody, [OR] or subject to a tutorial, judiciary [OR] or administrative decision. 
Patients subject to a protection measure. 
Patients in a critical medical situation. 
Patients with a personal situation judged by the investigator as unlikely to be compatible with optimal participation in the study, [OR] or which could constitute a risk for the patient. 
Linguistic barrier [OR] or psychological profile [NOT] preventing the patient from signing the consent form. 
Patient still in an exclusion period following the participation in another clinical trial. 
Patients having earned more than 4500€ in indemnities for participation in clinical trials during the previous 12 months, including this study.
