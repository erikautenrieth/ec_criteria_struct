Inclusion Criteria:
Confirmed diagnosis (clinical [OR] and histological features) of Hailey Hailey [OR] or Darier diseases. 
Moderate to very severe lesions located in large folds 
Patient aged 18 ans or more 
Patient with health coverage 
Patient who have signed the consent form 
Patient proficient into filling out the questionnaires. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Hypersensibility to toxin [OR] or excipients 
 Myastheny 
Deglutition's problems 
Past medical history of dysphagia [OR] or aspiration pneumonia 
Pregnancy (positive B-HCG test performed a maxima 72h before) [OR] or breastfeeding 
Mental, physical incapacity to fill in the questionnaires 
Guardianship patients 
Skin infections at the inclusion visit 
Application in the last 7 days at the site of injection of local treatments (apart emollients [OR] or antiseptics) [OR] or injections of botulism toxin [OR] or dynamic phototherapy [OR] or laser in the last 6 months. 
Systemic treatment with aminosides in the last 15 days 
Inclusion in another study in the last 2 months.
