Inclusion Criteria:
Patients aged greater than 18 years of age 
Ability to understand and willingness to comply with the study protocol 
Written informed consent 
Patients meeting the Rotterdam PCOS workshop criteria for polycystic ovary syndrome, defined by oligomenorrhea [OR] or amenorrhea [AND] and at least one of the following two signs: clinical [OR] or biochemical evidence of hyperandrogenism [OR] or ultrasound finding of polycystic appearing ovaries. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Current pregnancy [OR] or lactation 
Liver disease [OR] or elevated liver enzymes 
 Established diagnosis of diabetes mellitus 
Abnormal serum glucose levels either at fasting [OR] or after the 2-hr oral glucose tolerance test meeting criteria for the diagnosis of diabetes mellitus according to the American Diabetes Association. 
Insulin sensitizing treatment within 3 months prior to or during the eight week study period. 
Hormonal treatment involving estrogen [OR] or progesterone 3 months prior to or during the study period, with the exception of medroxyprogesterone acetate for withdrawal bleeding. 
Systemic [OR] or inhaled corticosteroids. 
Known hypersensitive reaction to cinnamon. 
Patients with seizure disorders, known cardiovascular disease, [OR] or cerebrovascular disease. 
Body mass index (BMI)range 20-50 (excluding all women [AND] with BMI under 20 [OR] or over 50).
