Inclusion Criteria:
Duration of SCI =1 year;
Level of SCI C3-T1, AIS A & B;
Age between 18 and 65 years.

Exclusion Criteria:
Evidence of sympathetic integrity below the lesion level by the skin axon-reflex vasodilatation (SkARV) test;
 Known allergies to midodrine hydrochloride;
 PMH of diagnosed heart, [OR] kidney, [OR] peripheral vascular, [OR] or cerebral vascular disease, [OR] or diabetes mellitus;
 Hypertension (BP>140/90 mmHg);
 [NOT] Untreated thyroid disease;
 Acute illness [OR] or infection;
 Current smoker;
 Pregnancy.
