Inclusion Criteria:
age 35-75 years;
Diagnosis of Type 2 Diabetes from at least 3 years;
HbA1c < 10%;
normal blood pressure [OR] or controlled hypertension;
BMI < 40;

Exclusion Criteria:
congenital [OR] or valvular cardiomyopathy;
ischaemic heart disease;
endocrine diseases: male hypogonadism, [OR] hyperthyroidism, [OR] adrenal diseases, [OR] pituitary diseases
proliferative retinopathy [OR] or autonomic neuropathy;
contraindications to sildenafil use [OR] or CMR imaging;
