Inclusion Criteria:
Men [OR] and women aged 18-45 years.
Diagnosis of functional dyspepsia, based on the Rome IV criteria (2016).
GIS score of at least 6.
Negative H. pylori test.
Availability of a signed patient information sheet (Informed Consent form) for participation in the clinical trial.
Patients who agree to use an effective method of contraception throughout the clinical trial.

Exclusion Criteria:
Organic diseases of the digestive system (gastro-oesophageal reflux disease (GERD), ulcer, chronic pancreatitis, [OR] cholelithiasis, [OR] fatty liver disease, [OR] hepatitis, [OR] cirrhosis of liver, etc.).
Diagnosis of other functional diseases of the digestive system, such as dyskinesia of cystic duct [OR] or gallbladder, [OR] irritable bowel syndrome, etc.
Discontinuation of proton pump inhibitors, [OR] propulsives, [OR] antispasmodics, [OR] antacids, [OR] or bismuth preparations less than 7 days prior to randomization.
H. Pylori eradication within 2 months before study entry.
Intestinal infection within 2 months before study entry.
Known history of/suspected malignant neoplasm of various sites.
Prior diagnosis of a class IV cardiovascular disease (according to the New York Heart Association, 1964), hypothyroidism, [OR] diabetes mellitus, chronic kidney disease (С3-5), [OR] or disease of liver [AND] with portal hypertension [OR] and/or severe decompensation (Child-Pugh score > 6).
Other severe coexisting morbidity which, in the investigator's opinion, can [NOT] prevent the patient from participating in the study.
Allergy/ [OR] intolerance to any of the components of medications used in the treatment.
Pregnancy, [OR] breast-feeding.
Patients who, from investigator's point of view, will [NOT] fail to comply with the observation requirements of the trial [OR] or with the dosing regimen of the investigational drugs.
Planned hospitalization during the study period, for any diagnostic [OR] or treatment procedures.
Drug addiction, [OR] alcohol use in the amount over 2 units of alcohol a day, mental diseases.
Intake of medicines listed in the section 'Prohibited concomitant treatment' for 1 month prior to the enrollment in the trial.
Participation in other clinical trials within 3 months to the enrollment in this study.
Patient is related to the research staff of the clinical investigative site [AND] who are directly involved in the trial [OR] or is the immediate family member of the investigator. The immediate family members include husband/ [OR] wife, parents, children [OR] or brothers (or sisters), regardless of whether they are natural [OR] or adopted.
Patient works for OOO "NPF "MATERIA MEDICA HOLDING" (i.e., is the company's employee, temporary contract worker [OR] or appointed official responsible for carrying out the research or their immediate family).
