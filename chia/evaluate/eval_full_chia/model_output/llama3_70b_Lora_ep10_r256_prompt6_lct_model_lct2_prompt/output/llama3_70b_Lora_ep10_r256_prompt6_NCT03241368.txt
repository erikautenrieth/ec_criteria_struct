Inclusion Criteria:
Subject has provided informed consent. 
Subject is ≥ 18 years of age 
Subject is willing [AND] and able to comply with all aspects of treatment [AND] and evaluation schedule. 
Subject has known CD [AND] and a recent history (within last 2 years) of mucosal disease (diagnosis based on radiologic, [OR] endoscopic, [OR] or histological evidence). 

Exclusion Criteria:
Subject has indeterminate, [OR] ulcerative, [OR] antibiotic-associated colitis. 
Subject has stool positive for ova [OR] and parasite [OR] and for Clostridium difficule toxins within 3 months prior to enrollment. 
Subject with other known infectious cause of abdominal symptoms. 
 Subject with clinical evidence of renal disease [AND] with the past 6 months, defined as estimated glomerular filtration rate (GFR) outside the normal reference range. 
Subject with known history of intestinal obstruction [OR] or current obstructive symptoms, such as severe abdominal pain [AND] with accompanying nausea [OR] or vomiting, based on investigator judgment. 
Subject with a diagnosis of gastroparesis [OR] or small bowel [OR] or large bowel dysmotility. 
Subjects with a history of small bowel [OR] or colonic resection. 
Subject with any current condition believed to have an increased risk of capsule retention such as suspected [OR] or known bowel obstruction, [OR] stricture, [OR] or fistula. 
Subject has used non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs including aspirin, two times per week, during the 4 weeks preceding enrollment. Low dose aspirin regimens (< 100 mg daily) are acceptable and [NOT] not exclusionary. 
Subject suffers from any condition, such as swallowing problems, that [NOT] precludes compliance with study [AND] and/or device instructions. 
Subject with cardiac pacemaker [OR] or other implanted electromedical device. 
Subject has an allergy [OR] or other known contraindication to the medications used in the study. 
Subject is pregnant (documented by a positive pregnancy test) [OR] or is actively breast-feeding. 
Subject is considered to be a part of a vulnerable population (eg. prisoners [OR] or those [NOT] without sufficient mental capacity). 
Subject has a known contraindication to MRE [OR] or IC. 
Subject has participated in a drug [OR] or device research study within 30 days of enrollment that may interfere with the subject's safety [OR] or ability to participate in the study.
