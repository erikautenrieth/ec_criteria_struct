Inclusion Criteria:
Patients scheduled for dental extraction [AND] and treated with edoxaban, [OR] apixaban, [OR] rivaroxaban [OR] or dabigatran
 [NOT] Not having taken the direct oral anticoagulant on the day of the extraction
Provision of signed and dated informed consent form
Stated willingness to comply with all study procedures and availability for the duration of the study

Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects with any condition that, as judged by the Investigator, would place the subject at increased risk of harm if he/she participated in the study.
Pregnancy [OR] or lactation
Known allergic reaction to tranexamic acid
