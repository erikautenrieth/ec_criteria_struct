Inclusion Criteria:
Adults = 18 years of age
Admitted to any ICU [AND] and receiving invasive mechanical ventilation
Anticipated ventilation of =72 hours at the time of screening, as per the ICU physician.

Exclusion Criteria:
Invasively mechanically ventilated >72 hours at the time of screening;
Patients at potential increased risk of iatrogenic probiotic infection (see Section 2.6 for detailed explanation) including specific immunocompromised populations (HIV <200 CD4 cells/µL, those receiving chronic immunosuppressive medications (e.g., azathioprine, [OR] cyclosporine, [OR] cyclophosphamide, [OR] tacrolimus, [OR] methotrexate, [OR] mycofenolate, Anti-IL2), previous transplantation (including stem cell) at any time, malignancy requiring chemotherapy in the last 3 months, [OR] neutropenia [absolute neutrophil count < 500]). However, patients receiving corticosteroids previously [OR] or presently [OR] or projected to receive corticosteroids are [NOT] not excluded;
Patients at risk for endovascular infection (previously documented rheumatic heart disease, [OR] congenital valve disease, [OR] surgically repaired congenital heart disease, [OR] unrepaired cyanotic congenital heart disease, any intracardiac repair with prosthetic material [mechanical [OR] or bio-prosthetic cardiac valves], previous [OR] or current endocarditis, permanent endovascular devices (e.g., endovascular grafts [e.g., aortic aneurysm repair, [OR] stents involving large arteries such as aorta, [OR] femorals [OR] and carotids], inferior vena cava filters, [OR] dialysis vascular grafts), tunnelled ([NOT] not short-term) hemodialysis catheters, [OR] pacemakers [OR] or defibrillators. Patients with temporary central venous catheters, [OR] central venous dialysis catheters [OR] or peripherally inserted central catheters (PICCs) are [NOT] not excluded [AND] and patients with coronary artery stents, [OR] coronary artery bypass grafts (CABG) [OR] or neurovascular coils are [NOT] not excluded; patients with mitral valve prolapse [OR] or bicuspid aortic valve are [NOT] not excluded providing they have [NOT] no other exclusion criteria;
Patients with a primary diagnosis of severe acute pancreatitis, [NOT] without reference to a Ranson score [Ranson 1974]). However, patients with mild [OR] or moderate pancreatitis are [NOT] not excluded;
Patients with percutaneous gastric [OR] or jejunal feeding tubes already in situ as per Health Canada guidance;
Strict contraindication [OR] or [NOT] inability to receive enteral medications;
Intent to withdraw advanced life support as per the ICU physician;
Previous enrolment in this [OR] or current enrolment in a potentially confounding trial
