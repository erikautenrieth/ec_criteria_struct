Inclusion Criteria:
Symptomatic primary knee osteoarthritis [AND] with failed conservative treatment at least 3 months
Kellgren-Lawrence grade I-III
Gave informed consent
Can do questionnaires

Exclusion Criteria:
Severe deformity (varus [OR] or values from mechanical axis more than 5 degrees
Allergy to hyaluronic acid
Pain on hip [OR] or ankle
Post-traumatic [OR] or post surgery of lower extremity
 Post infection of knee
Previous hyaluronic acid injection within 6 months
Pregnancy [OR] or lactation
Underlying Rheumatoid arthritis, [OR] stroke, [OR] malignancy, [OR] venous occlusion
