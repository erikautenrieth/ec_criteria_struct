Inclusion Criteria:
Inclusion Criteria Patients: Fulfilling the diagnostic criteria of schizophrenia [OR] or schizoaffective disorder according to ICD-10 (International Classification of Diseases version 10) [OR] or DSM-IV/V (Diagnostic and Statistical Manual version 4 /5), Age 18-45 years, [NOT] Never treated with antipsychotic compounds [OR] or central nervous system (CNS) stimulants, Legally competent
Inclusion criteria controls: Matching patients on age (+/- 2 years), sex and parental socioeconomic status, Age 18-45 years, [NOT] No psychiatric [OR] or physical disease.

Exclusion Criteria:
Exclusion Criteria patients: Substance abuse on a daily basis during the last 3 month [OR] or patients fulfilling the criteria of ongoing substance abuse due to ICD-10/ [OR] DSM-IV/V, Treatment with antidepressant during the last 30 days, Head injury [AND] with more than 5 minutes of unconsciousness, Patients involuntarily admitted [OR] or treated, Components of metal implanted by operation, [OR] Pacemaker, [OR] Pregnancy, [OR] Severe physical illness
Exclusion criteria controls: First degree relatives [AND] with psychiatric disease, Substance abuse during the last 3 month [OR] or positive screening of drugs in urine-sample, Head injury [AND] with more than 5 minutes of unconsciousness, Components of metal implanted by operation, [OR] Pacemaker, [OR] Pregnancy, [OR] Severe physical illness
