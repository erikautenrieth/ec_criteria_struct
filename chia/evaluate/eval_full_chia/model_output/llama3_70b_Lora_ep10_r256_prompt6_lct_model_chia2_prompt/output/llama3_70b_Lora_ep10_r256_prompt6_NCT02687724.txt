Inclusion Criteria:
Patients = 18 years of age
Subjects must be able and willing to give written informed consent and to comply with the requirements of this study protocol
Established diagnosis of UC [AND] and moderate- [OR] to-severe disease activity, defined as a Mayo score of 6-12, [AND] with an endoscopic subscore =2.
Patients had an inadequate response to, [OR] or had failed to tolerate, 1 or more of the following conventional therapies: oral 5-aminosalicylates, [OR] oral corticosteroids, [OR] azathioprine (AZA), [OR] and/or 6-mercaptopurine (6MP); [OR] or corticosteroid dependent (ie, an [NOT] inability to taper corticosteroids [NOT] without recurrence of UC symptoms).
Patients concurrently treated with oral 5-aminosalicylates [OR] or corticosteroids were to receive a stable dose for at least 2 weeks before baseline, [AND] and patients receiving AZA [OR] and/or 6MP were to receive a stable dose for at least 4 weeks before baseline. Patients were required to maintain stable doses of their concomitant UC medications during the study.
Female subjects of child bearing potential must be willing to ensure that they [OR] or their partner use effective contraception during the study [AND] and for 6 months thereafter OR
Surgical sterilized female patients [AND] with documentation of prior hysterectomy, [OR] tubal ligation [OR] or complete bilateral oophorectomy OR
Postmenopausal women [AND] with postmenopausal defined as permanent cessation >1 year of previously occurring menses.
Female subjects' serum pregnancy test performed at the screening visit [AND] and urine pregnancy test performed at the baseline visit must be negative.
Subjects have following investigations within 1 month prior to enrolment.
Routine bloods including U&E, [OR] FBC, [OR] LFTs, [OR] inflammatory markers (CRP) [OR] and albumin will be measured.
Medical history, concomitant medications
Intradermal reaction to Tuberculin (PPD skin test) [OR] or Mycobacterium tuberculosis antigenspecific interferon-gamma release assay (IGRA)
TB screening: chest X-Ray unless performed in the last 6 months
Stool examination for enteric pathogens including Clostridium difficile
Inclusion/exclusion criteria
Informed consent
May score (including sigmoidoscopy unless performed in previous 3 months)
Patient's weight [AND] and height [AND] and abdominal circumference

Exclusion Criteria:
Female subjects [AND] who are pregnant [OR] or breast-feeding [OR] or considering becoming pregnant during the study
Patients aged <18 years of age
Patients who cannot give informed consent,
Pregnant patients [OR] or those  who are breastfeeding will be deemed ineligible.
Prior treatment with any anti-TNF agent
Contra-indication to use of GLM (Hypersensitivity to the active substance [OR] or to any of the excipients; Active tuberculosis (TB), acute [OR] or chronic Hepatitis B infection [OR] or other severe infections such as sepsis [OR] and/or opportunistic infections including HIV infection; Moderate [OR] or severe heart failure (NYHA class III/IV)
Have symptoms [OR] or signs suggestive of current active [OR] or latent TB upon medical history, physical examination [OR] and/or chest radiograph, [OR] or positive Mycobacterium tuberculosis antigen-specific interferon-gamma release assay (IGRA)
Patients with a history of, [OR] or at imminent risk for, colectomy; [AND] who required gastrointestinal surgery within 2 months before screening;
History of colonic mucosal dysplasia [OR] or adenomatous colonic polyps that were [NOT] not removed
Screening stool study positive for enteric pathogens [OR] or Clostridium difficile toxin.
Oral corticosteroids at a dose >40 mg prednisone [OR] or its equivalent per day; receipt of cyclosporine, [OR] tacrolimus, [OR] sirolimus, [OR] or mycophenolate mofetil within 8 weeks before the first study agent injection; [OR] or use of an investigational agent within 5 half-lives of that agent before the first study agent injection.
Patients in recent receipt of live vaccinations within 4 weeks prior to enrolment
