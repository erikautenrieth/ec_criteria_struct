Inclusion Criteria:
= 20years of age;
diagnosis of stroke (>6months);
sedentary [OR] or insufficiently active;
have a writing medical permission to participate in the training program.

Exclusion Criteria:
cognitive impairment (Mini-Mental Status Examination score: illiterate 13 points; elementary and middle school 18 points; and high-school 26 points; [OR] or [NOT] inability to respond to verbal command);
[NOT] inability to walk independently for at least 10 minutes, [AND] with or [NOT] without walking devices;
pain [OR] or other disorders precluding their participation.
