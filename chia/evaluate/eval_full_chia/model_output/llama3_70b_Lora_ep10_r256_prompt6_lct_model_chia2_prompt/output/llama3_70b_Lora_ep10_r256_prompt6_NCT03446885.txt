Inclusion Criteria:
diagnosis of ADHD
parental permission and/or teen consent/assent as appropriate
between 16-25 years of age
IQ greater than or equal to 70
permit or license to drive
ability to read and understand English

Exclusion Criteria:
any medical condition that would contraindicate use of stimulant medication
any prior adverse response to lisdexamfetamine dimesylate [OR] or other stimulant medication
use of concurrent, [NOT] non-stimulant psychoactive medication
diagnosis of schizophrenia [OR] or presence of thought disorder symptoms
autism spectrum disorder
