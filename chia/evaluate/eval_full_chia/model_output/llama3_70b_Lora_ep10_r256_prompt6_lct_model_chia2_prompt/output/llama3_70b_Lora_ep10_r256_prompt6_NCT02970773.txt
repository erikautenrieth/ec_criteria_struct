Inclusion Criteria:
Motor complete tetraplegia for at least 3 months
Age from 18 to 74 years
Body mass index (BMI) from 18 to 35kg/m2
Informed consent as documented by signature

Exclusion Criteria:
Any anti-coagulation therapy (apart from rivaroxaban for second objective)
Hypersensitivity [OR] or allergy to factor Xa inhibitors
Acute bacterial endocarditis
Bleeding disorder
Clinically relevant active bleeding
Gastrointestinal ulcer [OR] or tumor
Hepatic dysfunction [AND] with increased bleeding risk
Renal failure [OR] / patients undergoing dialysis
Pregnancy [OR] and breast feeding
Gastrectomy, [OR] biliopancreatic diversion, [OR] resection [OR] or re-routing of small intestines
Feeding tube
Recent blood donation
Abnormalities of laboratory values: alanine-aminotransferase (ALAT), aspartate-aminotransferase (ASAT), gamma-glutamyl transferase (gammaGT), alkalic phosphatase (AP), bilirubin, amylase, [OR] lipase, [OR] cystatin C, creatinine, white blood cell count, [OR] haemoglobin, [OR] platelet count, [OR] prothrombin time, [OR] aPTT, [OR] fibrinogen, [OR] thrombin time, factors II,V,VII [OR] and X
Use of therapeutic [OR] or recreational drugs influencing plasmatic coagulation
