Inclusion Criteria:
planned sequential both-sided lower third molar extraction (split-mouth) [AND] with osteotomy (with or without upper molar extraction in local anesthesia)
able to understand the study and the NRS scale

Exclusion Criteria:
simultaneous both sided extraction [OR] or only upper third molar extraction
general anesthesia
known [OR] or presumed abnormal coagulation status
known [OR] or presumed liver [OR] or renal dysfunction
contraindication against metamizole known [OR] or suspected (known [OR] or suspected allergy against novalgin [OR] or other pyrazolones, anaphylactic reaction against NSAIDS, [OR] decreased bone marrow function [OR] or hematopoesis, [OR] hepatic porphyria, [OR] glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase deficiency, [OR] and pregnancy/ [OR] breastfeeding)
contraindication against ibuprofen (known [OR] or suspected allergy against ibuprofen, anaphylactic reaction against Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAID), active [OR] or recurrent stomach [OR] or duodenal ulcera [OR] or bleeding, severe liver [OR] or renal insufficiency, inflammatory bowel syndrome, [OR] and pregnancy/ [OR] breastfeeding)
pregnancy [OR] and breast feeding mothers
