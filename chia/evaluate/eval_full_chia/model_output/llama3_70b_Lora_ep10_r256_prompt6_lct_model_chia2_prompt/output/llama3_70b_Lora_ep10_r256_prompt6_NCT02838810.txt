Inclusion Criteria:
CHB patients [AND] who had received single NAs for more than 12 months.
Hepatitis B e antigen (HBeAg)-negative.
Hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg) positive [AND] and <1000 IU/mL.
Hepatitis B virus DNA <100 IU/mL.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with liver cirrhosis, [OR] Hepatocellular Carcinoma [OR] or AFP >2 ULN [OR] or other malignancies.
Patients with other factors causing liver diseases.
Pregnant [OR] and lactating women.
Patients with concomitant HIV infection [OR] or congenital immune deficiency diseases.
Patients with diabetes, [OR] autoimmune diseases.
Patients with important organ dysfunctions.
Patients with serious complications (e.g., infection, [OR] hepatic encephalopathy, [OR] hepatorenal syndrome, [OR] gastrointestinal bleeding.)
Patients who receive antineoplastic [OR] or immunomodulatory therapy in the past 12 months.
Patients with a previous use of IFN anti hepatitis B virus treatment [OR] or have NAs drug resistance.
Patients [AND] who [NOT] can't come back to clinic for follow-up on schedule.
