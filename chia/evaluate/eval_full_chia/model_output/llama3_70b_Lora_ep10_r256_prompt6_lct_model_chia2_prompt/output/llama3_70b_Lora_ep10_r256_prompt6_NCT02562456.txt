Inclusion Criteria:
Children aging between 3 and 6 years
presenting good health conditions
whose parents or legal guardians accept and sign the consent form
with at least one occlusal [OR] or occlusal proximal caries lesion in primary molars
only occlusal [OR] and/or occlusal-proximal surfaces [AND] with caries lesions [AND] with dentin involvement

Exclusion Criteria:
severe behavioral issues
presence of fistula [OR] or abscess near the selected tooth
 presence of pulp exposure in the selected tooth
presence of mobility in the selected tooth
