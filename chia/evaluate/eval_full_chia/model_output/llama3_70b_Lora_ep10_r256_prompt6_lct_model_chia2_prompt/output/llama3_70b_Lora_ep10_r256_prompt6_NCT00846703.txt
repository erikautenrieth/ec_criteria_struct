Inclusion Criteria:
Cytologically proven acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL)
 [NOT] No relapse of a previously unrecognized ALL
Patients must meet one of the following risk criteria:
Standard-risk (SR) group meeting all of the following criteria:
Blasts < 1,000/µL in peripheral blood (PB) on day 8
Aged 1 to < 6 years
Initial WBC < 20,000/µL
M1 (5%) [OR] or M2 (= 5% to < 25%) blasts in bone marrow on day 15;
 M1 marrow on day 33.
Aged < 1 [OR] or = 6 years [OR] and/or WBC = 20,000/µL
Blasts < 1,000/µL in PB on day 8
 M1 [OR] or M2 marrow on day 15
 M3 (= 25%) marrow on day 15 [OR] OR meets SR criteria [AND] but M3 marrow on day 15 [AND] and *M1 marrow on day 33.
Meets IR criteria [AND] and M3 marrow on day 15 ([NOT] not SR [AND] and M3 on day 15)
Blasts = 1,000/µL in PB on day 8
 M2 [OR] or M3 marrow on day 33
Translocation t(9;22) [BCR/ABL+] (Philadelphia chromosome-positive) [OR] or t(4;11) [MLL/AF4+].

Exclusion Criteria:
 [NOT] No Down syndrome
 [NOT] No other major disease that [NOT] prohibits study treatment (e.g., severe congenital heart disease)
 [NOT] Not requiring significant therapy modification owing to study therapy associated complications
 [NOT] No complications due to other interventions
 [NOT] No one with missing data that are needed for the differential diagnosis, [OR] or for selection of the proper therapy arm
