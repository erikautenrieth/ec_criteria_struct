Inclusion Criteria:
Has given written informed consent.
Male [OR] or female outpatients aged at least 18 years [AND] and [NOT] not more than 45 years.
Has a diagnosis of major depressive disorder by Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, Fourth Edition (DSM-IV) criteria.
Current HAMD-17 score = 20 [AND] and the duration of the index episode is greater than or equal to four weeks.

Exclusion Criteria:
Currently enrolled in, [OR] or discontinued within the last 30 days from, a clinical trial involving an off-label use of an investigational drug.
 Current Axis I primary psychiatric diagnosis other than major depressive disorder.
Organic mental disease, including mental retardation.
History of clinically significant disease, including any cardiovascular, [OR] hepatic, [OR] renal, [OR] respiratory, [OR] hematologic, [OR] endocrinologic, [OR] or neurologic disease, [OR] or clinically significant laboratory abnormality that is [NOT] not stabilized [OR] or is anticipated to require treatment during the study.
Subjects receiving an investigational agent (including different formulation [OR] and generic agents of investigational drug) in the previous 3 months prior to screening.
Women [AND] in pregnancy [OR] or lactation, [OR] or female [AND] of child bearing potential [NOT] without appropriate birth control measures.
Use of antipsychotics [OR] or mood stabilizers within 5 days prior to screening.
Has received depot antipsychotic medication within one cycle prior to screening.
Known allergy [OR] or [NOT] lack of response to mirtazapine.
 Has received ECT [OR] or MECT within 3 months prior to screening.
History of anticholinergic drug allergy [OR] or complications (allergic reaction, skin rash, [OR] urticaria [OR] and other allergic reactions which caused by drugs).
Smokers.
 Significant risk of suicidal [OR] and/or self-harm behaviors
