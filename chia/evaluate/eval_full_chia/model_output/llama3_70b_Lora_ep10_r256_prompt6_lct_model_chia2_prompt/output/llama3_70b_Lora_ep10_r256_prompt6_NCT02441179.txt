Inclusion Criteria:
1. Patients ≥ 18 years-old from "Instituto Teletón Santiago" [OR] and "Hospital Clínico Mutual de seguridad". 
2. C5 to T12 spinal cord injury, classified as ISNCSCI grades C and D 
3. Traumatic [OR] and [NOT] non-traumatic, [NOT] non-progressive lesions 
4. Onset > 6 months 
5. Ability to ambulate [AND] with or [NOT] without assistive devices 
6. Ability to follow verbal [OR] or visual commands 
7. Signed informed consent 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Orthopedic injuries that are unstable 
2. Osteoporosis [AND] with high risk of pathological fracture 
3. Cutaneous lesions [OR] and/or pressure ulcers 
4. Joint contractures 
5. Cardiopulmonary diseases 
6. Body weight exceeding 150 Kg
