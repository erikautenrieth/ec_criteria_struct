Inclusion Criteria:
All patients (excluding neonates) requiring one or more allogeneic RBC transfusions for the treatment of anemia will be included.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients that do [NOT] not have a valid Ontario Health Insurance Plan (OHIP) number at time of first transfusion
Patients that require emergent release of a RBC transfusion and in whom emergency randomization could [NOT] not be completed
Patients with complex antibody profile in which it is [NOT] impossible to match RBC units
