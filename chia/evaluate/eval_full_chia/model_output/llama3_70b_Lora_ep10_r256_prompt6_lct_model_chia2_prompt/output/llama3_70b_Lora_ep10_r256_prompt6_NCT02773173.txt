Inclusion Criteria:
Patients older than 18 years
Classification of the American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA I-III)
 [NOT] No cognitive deficits
Signed informed consent prior to surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
Emergency surgery
Pregnancy [OR] or lactation
Immune disorders
Kidney [OR] or liver disease [OR] or advanced-stage cardiopulmonary
Patient refusal to participate in the study
Patients under 18 years or inability to consent
Associated neuromuscular disorders, contraindication for the use of rocuronium/ [OR] sugammadex, allergy [OR] or hypersensitivity to rocuronium [OR] / sugammadex
