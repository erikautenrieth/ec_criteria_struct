Inclusion Criteria:
Signed Informed Consent Form
Patients having physical [AND] and mental ability to participate in the study
Patients of both sexes aged 35 to 65 years
Presence of documented ST-elevation myocardial infarction confirmed by ECG, [AND] as well as troponin I [AND] and CK-MB levels.
Presence of hemodynamically relevant stenosis of one artery (i.e., the infarct-related artery) confirmed by coronary angiography (CAG), [AND] with the occlusion of other arteries [NOT] not exceeding 30%.
