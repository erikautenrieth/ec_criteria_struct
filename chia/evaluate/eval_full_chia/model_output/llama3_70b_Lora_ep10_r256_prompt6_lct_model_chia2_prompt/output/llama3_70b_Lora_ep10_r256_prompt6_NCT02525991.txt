Inclusion Criteria:
Male [OR] and female patients between the ages of 18-65 years, inclusive
Patients (or legal representative) willing and able to provide written Informed Consent Form.
Psychiatric patients already diagnosed of schizophrenia [OR] or bipolar disorder, according to the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders- IV, Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders- V or International Code of Disease criteria.
Patients with an on-going agitation episode, [OR] or with a previous one within the 6 months prior to screening, attended and managed in the hospital setting.
 Previously treated with ADASUVE® [AND] with a positive outcome (responders) according to (CGI-I) scale (defined as having a CGI-I score of 1 or 2 at 2 hours after administration of the inhalation)
Patients [NOT] free of active respiratory disease such as acute respiratory signs/ [OR] symptoms (e.g., wheezing) [OR] or with active airways disease (asthma, [OR] chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] or emphysema).
Requirement of family [OR] or other caregiver support at study investigator criteria (defined as a patient's relative [OR] or caregiver (male [OR] or female) = 80 year old, [AND] who spend = 3 consecutive hours with patient, [AND] with good physical [AND] and psychological health status [AND] and [NOT] without physical limitations, reading and writing educational level and able to understand and follow the study procedures).
Availability of patient's medical records data about the previous treatment with ADASUVE® at hospital setting.
If a female [AND] is of childbearing potential [AND] and sexually active (except if female [AND] is surgically sterile [OR] or post-menopausal [AND] with history of [NOT] no menses for at least 24 months), patient must be [NOT] non-lactating [AND] and [NOT] non-pregnant (with a negative pregnancy test result at baseline visit) [AND] and have to agree to use a medically acceptable and effective birth control method throughout the study [AND] and for one week following the end of the study.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patient diagnosed with dementia.
Patients with serious [AND] and unstable illnesses including current hepatic, [OR] renal, [OR] gastroenterologic, [OR] respiratory, [OR] cardiovascular (including ischemic heart disease [OR] and congestive heart failure), [OR] endocrinologic, [OR] neurologic (including stroke, [OR] transient ischemic attack, [OR] subarachnoidal bleeding, [OR] brain tumor, [OR] encephalopathy, [OR] and meningitis).
 Patients with a history of allergic reactions to loxapine [OR] or amoxapine
 Patients who have received an investigational drug within 30 days prior to the current agitation episode must be [NOT] excluded.
 Patients who are considered by the investigator, for any reason, to be [NOT] unable to self-administer the inhalation device.
