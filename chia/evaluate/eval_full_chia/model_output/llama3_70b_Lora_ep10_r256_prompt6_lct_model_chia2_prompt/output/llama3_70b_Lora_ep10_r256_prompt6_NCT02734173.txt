Inclusion Criteria:
HCV RNA evidence of HCV infection
Documented history of chronic HCV RNA infection [AND] with Genotype 1
Able to provide informed consent
Available for ongoing follow-up if required

Exclusion Criteria:
<18 years old
Evidence of decompensated liver disease
HOMA IR< 2.0
HIV seropositivity
Chronic HBV/ [OR] HIV infection
Use of immune suppressing medications
Active malignancy
