Inclusion Criteria:
Adults (18 and older) [AND] with physiologically confirmed SA [OR] or mild-moderate asthma [AND] and followed by an asthma specialist for at least 6 months. 
Must agree to have regular clinic visits (minimum 3-4 per year for SA, 1-2 for mild-moderate asthma). 
Must have good compliance with medications Patients with asthma [OR] and COPD. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Malignancy [OR] and other significant medical conditions that will impact follow up within this program. 
Those less than 18 years of age. 
Concomitant interstitial lung disease, [OR] sarcoidosis, other significant lung disease. 
Those who have had a transplant. 
Significant travel with work. 
Unable to make appointments (every three to six months over 2 years). 
Those residing in another country [OR] or planned absence for more than one month.
