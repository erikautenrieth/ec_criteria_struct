written consent

Exclusion Criteria:
rearthroplasty
ASA IV-V
inadequate spoken finnish for reliable pain assessment
Dementia [OR] or otherwise impaired cognition
contraindication for any medication [OR] or substance used in survey protocol

clinical suspicion that subject can [NOT] not use PCA adequately
history of substance abuse [OR] or current excessive use of alcohol
preoperative use of either pregabalin, [OR] gabapentin [OR] or strong opiates
