Inclusion Criteria:
Healthy patients
aged 3-13 years
Level I [OR] or level II on the American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) physical status classification system (as determined by the anesthesiologist)
obstructive sleep apnea [OR] or recurrent throat infections
undergoing elective tonsillectomy [AND] with or [NOT] without adenoidectomy
Parents who agree to complete documentation and follow up at 14 days post-operation.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients Level III or greater on the American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) physical status classification system (as determined by the anesthesiologist)
Patients with chronic conditions that would [NOT] limit our ability to develop the study according to objectives, such as neurodevelopmental conditions [NOT] preventing patients from understanding the Oucher tool
Hepatic [OR] or renal disease
cardiac disease
active infection
diabetes mellitus
sickle cell disease
known coagulation disorders
pre- operative treatment with anti-emetics, [OR] steroids, [OR] or analgesics
Acetaminophen allergy [OR] or already receiving acetaminophen within 24 h of surgery
Complicating health factors [NOT] precluding the use of opioids [OR] or acetaminophen
any other factors which would interfere with pain assessment [OR] and management
Patients weighing more than 30 kg that would exceed maximum dexamethasone dose
Patients who live [NOT] without a home telephone
patient living [NOT] without parental supervision.
