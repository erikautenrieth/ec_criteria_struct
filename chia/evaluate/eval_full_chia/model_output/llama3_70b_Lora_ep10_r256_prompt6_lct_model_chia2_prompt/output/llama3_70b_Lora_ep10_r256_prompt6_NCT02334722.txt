Inclusion Criteria:
Adult (>18 years of age and older) patients [AND] who have or will have undergone surgical resection [OR] or biopsy of a supratentorial brain tumor [AND] and are able to consent for themselves.
Able to be randomized prior to or up to 48 hours after surgery.

Exclusion Criteria:
 [NOT] No known history of seizure activity.
Pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding.
Renal dysfunction (CrCl < 30ml/min).
Becks Depression Inventory (BDI) =14
Allergy to levetiracetam.
