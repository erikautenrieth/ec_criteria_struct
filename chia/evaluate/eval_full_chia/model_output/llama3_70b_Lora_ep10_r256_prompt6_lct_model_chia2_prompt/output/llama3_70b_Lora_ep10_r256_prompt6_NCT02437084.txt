Inclusion Criteria:
Healthy adults 30- 65 years old,
BMI 25-35 kg/m2,
nondiabetic as defined by fasting plasma glucose <126 mg/dL
Lipids: one group with an LDL =/>130 [AND] and Triglycerides < 150 mg/dL The 2nd group will have and LDL=/>130 mg/dL [AND] and Triglycerides =/>150 mg/dL [AND] but less than 400 mg/dL.

Exclusion Criteria:
Less than 30 yrs of age [OR] or > 65 yrs of age
Any significant co-morbidities, such as active heart, [OR] kidney, [OR] or liver diseases, accelerated [OR] or malignant hypertension, [OR] heart failure, severe anemia.
