Inclusion Criteria:
Subject or subject's legally acceptable representative has provided informed consent.
Male [OR] or female >=18 years of age.
Histologically [OR] or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of adenocarcinoma of the colon [OR] or rectum.
 Wild-type KRAS (without mutation in exon 2 [codons 12 and 13], exon 3 [codons 59 and 61], and exon 4 [codons 117 and 146]) [AND] and wild-type NRAS (without mutation in exon 2 [codons 12 and 13], exon 3 [codons 59 and 61], and exon 4 [codons 117 and 146]) tumor status.
Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0, 1 or 2.
 Measurable [OR] or [NOT] non-measurable disease per RECIST Version 1.1.
 Must have failed after fluoropyrimidine-, [OR] oxaliplatin-, [OR] and irinotecan-containing chemotherapy regimens [AND] for metastatic disease. Failure is defined as either disease progression (clinical [OR] or radiological) [OR] or intolerance to the regimen. Metastatic relapse within 6 months after completing adjuvant chemotherapy [AND] (with either an irinotecan [OR] or oxaliplatin containing regimen) will also be considered as treatment failure of a prior regimen [AND] for metastatic disease. Laboratory: Adequate baseline organ function defined by (<=7 days prior to first dose of study treatment).
Hematologic function, as follows: Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) >=1.5 x 10^9/Liter (L), Platelet count >=75 x 10^9/L, Hemoglobin >=8.0 gram/deciliter (g/dL).
Renal function, as follows: Creatinine <=1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN).
Hepatic function, as follows: Aspartate aminotransferase (AST) <=3 x ULN, Alanine aminotransferase (ALT) <=3 x ULN, Total Bilirubin <=1.5 x ULN.
Metabolic function, as follows: Serum Magnesium [AND] within normal limits. Serum Calcium [AND] within normal limits. Serum Potassium [AND] within normal limits.
All prior treatment related toxicities common terminology criteria for adverse events (CTCAE) version 4.03 <=Grade 1 at the time of enrollment.
Women [AND] of childbearing potential must have a negative serum pregnancy test within 7 days of first dose of study treatment [AND] and agree to use adequate contraception, during the study [AND] and for 2 months following the last dose of study treatment. Men [AND] with a female partner [AND] of childbearing potential must have either had a prior vasectomy [OR] or agree to use adequate contraception, from time of signing informed consent until 5 months after the last dose of study treatment.

Exclusion Criteria:
History [OR] or known presence of central nervous system metastases.
 History of another malignancy except: Malignancy treated [AND] with curative intent [AND] and [NOT] with [NOT] no known active disease present for >=5 years prior to enrolment [AND] and felt to be at low risk for recurrence by the treating physician; Adequately treated [NOT] non-melanomatous skin cancer [OR] or lentigo maligna [NOT] without evidence of disease; Adequately treated cervical carcinoma in situ [NOT] without evidence of disease; Prostatic intraepithelial neoplasia [NOT] without evidence of prostate cancer.
Known immediate [OR] or delayed hypersensitivity reaction [OR] or idiosyncrasy to drugs chemically related to panitumumab [OR] or excipients that contraindicates their participation.
 Prior anti-epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFr) antibody therapy (e.g., panitumumab [OR] or cetuximab) [OR] or treatment with small molecule EGFr inhibitors (e.g., gefitinib, [OR] erlotinib, [OR] lapatinib).
 Antitumor therapy (e.g., chemotherapy, [OR] hormonal therapy, [OR] immunotherapy, [OR] antibody therapy, [OR] radiotherapy), [OR] or investigational agent [OR] or therapy <=30 days before first dose of study treatment [OR] or [NOT] not recovered from any acute toxicity.
 Other investigational procedure <=30 days before study entry.
 History of interstitial lung disease (ILD) e.g., interstitial pneumonitis, [OR] pulmonary fibrosis [OR] or evidence of ILD on baseline chest computer tomography.
 Subject previously enrolled to this study.
 History of keratitis, [OR] ulcerative keratitis [OR] or severe dry eye.
 Major surgery (e.g., requiring general anesthesia) <=30 days before first dose of study treatment. Subjects must have recovered from any surgery related toxicities.
 Minor surgical procedure (e.g., open biopsy) <=7 days before first dose of study treatment, [OR] or [NOT] not yet recovered from prior minor surgery Note: uncomplicated placement of vascular access device, [OR] fine needle aspiration, [OR] thoracocentesis [OR] or paracentesis >=3 days prior to first dose of study treatment is acceptable.
 Clinically significant cardiovascular disease (including myocardial infarction, unstable angina, symptomatic congestive heart failure, serious uncontrolled cardiac arrhythmia) <=6 months prior to enrolment.
 History of any medical [OR] or psychiatric condition [OR] or laboratory abnormality that in the opinion of the investigator may increase the risk associated with the study participation [OR] or investigational product administration, compliance with the study procedures [OR] or may interfere with the interpretation of the results.
 Unstable pulmonary embolism, [OR] deep vein thrombosis, [OR] or other significant arterial/ [OR] venous thromboembolic event <=30 days before first dose of study treatment. If on anticoagulation, subject must be on stable therapeutic dose prior to first dose of study treatment.
 Subject who is pregnant [OR] or breast feeding, [OR] or planning to become pregnant during treatment [AND] and within 2 months after the discontinuation of study treatment.
 Known positive test(s) for human immunodeficiency virus infection (testing is not required in the absence of clinical suspicion).
 Active infection requiring systemic treatment [OR] or any uncontrolled infection <=14 days prior to first dose of study treatment (with the exception of uncomplicated urinary tract infection [OR] or upper respiratory tract infection).
 Subject has any kind of disorder that compromises the ability of the subject to give written informed consent [OR] and/or to comply with study procedures or is unwilling or unable to comply with study requirements.
