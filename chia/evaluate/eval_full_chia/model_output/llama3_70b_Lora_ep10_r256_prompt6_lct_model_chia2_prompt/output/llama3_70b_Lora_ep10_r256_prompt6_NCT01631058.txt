Inclusion Criteria:
All renal ([OR] only) male [OR] and female recipients aged = 60, years undergoing kidney transplantation from a living [OR] or deceased donor, including Expanded Criteria Donors (ECD).
Panel Reactive Antibody (PRA) < 30%.
Patients who consented to participate in the study by signing the informed consent form before the transplant surgery to the 1st post-operative day).

Exclusion Criteria:
Allergy to any of proposed medications
Patients with any active infection including HBV, [OR] HCV [OR] and HIV.
