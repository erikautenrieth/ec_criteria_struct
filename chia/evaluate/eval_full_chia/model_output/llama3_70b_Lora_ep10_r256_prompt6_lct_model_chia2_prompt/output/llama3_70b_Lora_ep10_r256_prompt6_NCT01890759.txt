Inclusion Criteria:
Male [OR] and female subjects aged 9 to 17 months on the day of inclusion 
Informed consent form has been signed and dated by the parent(s) or other legally acceptable representative(s) (if applicable) 
Subject and parent/legally acceptable representative (if applicable) able to attend all scheduled visits and to comply with all trial procedures. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Participation in the 4 weeks preceding inclusion [OR] or planned participation during the present trial period in another clinical trial investigating a vaccine, [OR] drug, [OR] medical device, [OR] or medical procedure. 
Receipt of any vaccine in the 4 weeks preceding each trial vaccination [OR] or planned receipt of any vaccine in the 4 weeks following each trial vaccination, except for: 
(i) influenza vaccination, which may be received at least 2 weeks before study vaccines. 
(ii) measles (M) [OR] or measles, mumps, [OR] rubella (MMR) routine vaccination, which can be administered concomitantly with the first dose of study vaccine as per routine immunization schedule 
(iii) for subjects enrolled at Indian sites: oral poliomyelitis vaccine (OPV) received during National Immunization Days (NIDs) [OR] and supplementary immunization activity days (SIADs) 
Previous vaccination against meningococcal disease with either the study vaccine [OR] or another meningococcal vaccine 
Receipt of immune globulins, [OR] blood [OR] or blood-derived products in the past 3 months 
Known [OR] or suspected congenital [OR] or acquired immunodeficiency; [OR] or receipt of immunosuppressive therapy, such as anti-cancer chemotherapy [OR] or radiation therapy, within the preceding 6 months; [OR] or long-term systemic corticosteroid therapy (prednisone [OR] or equivalent for more than 2 consecutive weeks within the past 3 months) 
History of meningococcal diseases, confirmed either clinically, [OR] serologically, [OR] or microbiologically 
At high risk, in the opinion of the Investigator, for meningococcal disease during the trial 
Known [OR] or suspected systemic hypersensitivity to any of the vaccine components, [OR] or history of a life-threatening reaction to the vaccine used in the trial [OR] or to a vaccine containing any of the same substances 
Known thrombocytopenia, contraindicating intramuscular vaccination 
Bleeding disorder, [OR] or receipt of anticoagulants in the 3 weeks preceding inclusion, contraindicating intramuscular vaccination 
In an emergency setting, [OR] or hospitalized involuntarily 
Chronic illness that, in the opinion of the investigator, is at a stage where it might interfere with trial conduct [OR] or completion 
For subjects enrolled at Indian sites: Moderate [OR] or severe acute illness/ [OR] infection (according to investigator judgment) on the day of vaccination [OR] or febrile illness (temperature ≥ 38.0°C). 
For subjects enrolled at Russian sites: Acute disease of any severity on the day of vaccination [OR] or febrile illness (axillary temperature ≥ 37.0°C). 
A prospective subject should [NOT] not be included in the study until the condition has resolved [OR] or the febrile event has subsided. 
Receipt of oral [OR] or injectable antibiotic therapy within 72 hours prior to the first blood draw 
Identified as a natural [OR] or adopted child of the Investigator or employee with direct involvement in the proposed study 
Personal history of Guillain-Barré Syndrome.
