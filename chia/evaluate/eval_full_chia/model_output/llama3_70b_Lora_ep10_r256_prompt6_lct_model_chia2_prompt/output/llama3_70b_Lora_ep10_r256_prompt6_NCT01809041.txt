Inclusion Criteria:
major elective gastrointestinal, [OR] gynecological, [OR] prostate [OR] or bladder surgery patients [AND] who are = 60 years old.
the surgery is laparoscopic surgery [AND] and is expected to last for = 2 hours under general anesthesia [AND] and the patient will stay in hospital for at least 7 days after surgery.
[NOT] lack of serious hearing [AND] and vision impairment [AND] and be able to read so that neurobehavioral tests can be performed.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients are [NOT] not expected to be alive for longer than 3 months.
Mini-mental State Examination (MMSE) [18] score = 23.
history of dementia, [OR] psychiatric illness [OR] or any diseases of central nervous system.
current use of sedatives [OR] or antidepressant.
alcoholism [OR] and drug dependence.
patients previously included in this study (for patients who have second intra-abdominal surgery during the study period).
difficult to follow up or patients with poor compliance.
uncontrolled hypertension (> 180/100 mmHg)
