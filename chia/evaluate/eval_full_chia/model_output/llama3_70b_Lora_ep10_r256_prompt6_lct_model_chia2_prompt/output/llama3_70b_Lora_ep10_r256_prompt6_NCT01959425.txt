Inclusion Criteria:
Successful cardiac ablation for AF
Documented freedom from AF recurrence (symptomatic [OR] or asymptomatic arrhythmic recurrences lasting longer than 30 seconds) 3 months after successful cardiac ablation (AF recurrence during 3-month blanking period is [NOT] excluded).
Patient must have been on a commercially approved anticoagulation therapy for at least two (2) months prior to randomization in the OAT Study.
CHADS2 score = 2 [OR] or CHA2DS2-VASc score (=3)
Left ventricular ejection fraction > 25%
 LA size < 65
High risk for thromboembolic events (i.e., CHADS2 score = 2 [OR] or CHA2DS2-VASc score = 3) [AND] and require OAT before undergoing cardiac ablation
Able [AND] and willing to comply with all pre- and follow-up testing and requirements
Signed informed consent form
Age 18 years or older

Exclusion Criteria:
OAT required for reasons [NOT] not related to AF (i.e., prosthetic valve, [OR] PV stenosis, previous pulmonary embolism, presence of spontaneous echo contrast [SEC] at standard echo performed at 3-months follow-up).
Any cardiac surgery within the past 60 days (2 months) [OR] or valvular cardiac surgical procedure at any time (i.e., ventriculotomy, [OR] atriotomy, [OR] and valve repair [OR] or replacement [AND] and presence of a prosthetic valve)
Previous myocardial infarction (MI) [OR] or a percutaneous coronary intervention PCI within the past 3 months
Awaiting cardiac transplantation [OR] or other cardiac surgery within the next 365 days (12 months)
Documented left atrial thrombus
Significant pulmonary disease, (e.g., restrictive pulmonary disease, [OR] constrictive [OR] or COPD) [OR] or any other disease [OR] or malfunction of the lungs [OR] or respiratory system that produces chronic symptoms
Significant medical problem that in the opinion of the investigator would [NOT] preclude enrollment in this study
Women [AND] who are pregnant (as evidenced by pregnancy test if pre-menopausal)
Acute illness [OR] or active systemic infection [OR] or sepsis
Unstable angina
Contraindication to anticoagulation (i.e., heparin, [OR] warfarin [OR] or another commercially available anticoagulation medication)
History of blood clotting [OR] or bleeding abnormalities
Life expectancy less than 360 days (12 months)
Uncontrolled Heart Failure [OR] or NYHA Class III or IV heart failure
Enrollment in a clinical study evaluating another device [OR] or drug, within the past 6 months
Unable [OR] or unwilling to comply with protocol requirements
