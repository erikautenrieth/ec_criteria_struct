Inclusion Criteria:
1. Subjects must have recurrent [OR] or persistent platinum-resistant epithelial ovarian, [OR] fallopian tube, [OR] or primary peritoneal carcinoma [AND] with measureable disease (as defined by RECIST 1.1.) after first [OR] or second line platinum-based chemotherapy, [AND] for which treatment with PLD is indicated. Platinum-based therapy is defined as treatment with carboplatin, [OR] cisplatin [OR] or another organoplatinum compound. Platinum-resistant is defined as having a platinum- [NOT] free interval (PFI) of < 12 months after first- [OR] or second-line platinum-based chemotherapy, [OR] or having disease progression while receiving second-line platinum-based chemotherapy. 
Subjects are allowed to have received, but are [NOT] not required to have received: 
one additional cytotoxic regimen [OR] and/or PARP inhibitor for management of recurrent [OR] or persistent disease. 
biologic therapy (e.g., bevacizumab) as part of their primary treatment regimen [OR] or part of their treatment for management of recurrent [OR] or persistent disease. 
2. Histologic documentation of the original primary tumor. 
3. Documented radiographic disease progression < 12 months after the last dose of first- [OR] or second-line platinum-based chemotherapy. 
4. Subjects in Phase 2 must have disease amenable to biopsy [AND] and must be willing to undergo pre- [AND] and post-treatment tumor biopsies. Optional for Phase 1. 
Note: archival tissue will be requested for all subjects preferably from primary tumor site prior to cancer treatment; however, archival tissue is [NOT] not a requirement for study entry. 
5. ECOG performance status of 0 or 1. 
6. Laboratory parameters for vital functions should be in the normal range. Laboratory abnormalities that are [NOT] not clinically significant are generally permitted, except for the following laboratory parameters, which must be within the ranges specified, regardless of clinical significance: 
Hemoglobin: ≥ 9 g/dL 
Neutrophil count: ≥ 1.5 x 109/L 
Platelet count: ≥ 100,000/mm3 
Serum creatinine, ≤ 1.5x Institutional Upper Limit of Normal (ULN), [OR] or Creatinine Clearance ≥ 50 mL/min (by Cockcroft-Gault formula) 
Serum bilirubin: ≤ 1.2 mg/dL 
AST/ [OR] ALT: ≤ 2.5 x ULN 
Alkaline phosphatase: ≤ 2.5 x ULN 
7. Age ≥18 years. 
8. Able and willing to give valid written informed consent. 
9. Body weight > 30 kg

1. Prior exposure to doxorubicin, [OR] PLD [OR] or any other anthracycline, [OR] motolimod [OR] and other TLR agonists, [OR] MEDI4736 [OR] or checkpoint inhibitors, such as anti-CTLA4 [OR] and anti-PD1/ [OR] anti-PD-L1 antibodies. 
2. Subjects with platinum-refractory disease, defined as disease progression while receiving first line platinum-based therapy. 
3. Clinically significant persistent immune-related adverse events following prior therapy. 
4. Subjects with history [OR] or evidence upon physical examination of CNS disease, including primary brain tumor, [OR] seizures [NOT] not controlled with standard medical therapy, any brain metastases, [OR] or, within six months prior to Day 1 of this study, history of cerebrovascular accident (CVA, [OR] stroke), transient ischemic attack (TIA) [OR] or subarachnoid hemorrhage. 
5. Subjects with clinically significant cardiovascular disease. This includes: 
1. Resisted hypertension 
2. Myocardial infarction [OR] or unstable angina within 6 months prior to Day 1 of the study. 
3. History of serious ventricular arrhythmia (i.e., ventricular tachycardia [OR] or ventricular fibrillation) [OR] or cardiac arrhythmias requiring anti-arrhythmic medications, except for atrial fibrillation that is well controlled with anti-arrhythmic medication. 
4. Baseline ejection fraction ≤ 50% as assessed by echocardiogram [OR] or MUGA. 
5. New York Heart Association (NYHA) Class II or higher congestive heart failure. 
6. Grade 2 or higher peripheral ischemia, except for brief (< 24 hrs) episodes of ischemia managed [NOT] non-surgically [AND] and [NOT] without permanent deficit.

Active, [OR] suspected [OR] or prior documented autoimmune disease (including inflammatory bowel disease, [OR] celiac disease, [OR] Wegner's granulomatosis, active Hashimoto's thyroiditis, [OR] rheumatoid arthritis, [OR] lupus, [OR] scleroderma [OR] and its variants, multiple sclerosis, [OR] myasthenia gravis).
