Inclusion Criteria:
Aged at least 18 years with an ability and willingness to give written informed consent. 
Body mass index 25-35 kg/m2 
Users of at least 2 cups of caffeinated coffee per day [AND] who are willing to be randomized to any of the interventions. 
[NOT] Non-smoking 

Exclusion Criteria:
Any condition/ [OR] illness that may affect the study outcomes or would make participation potentially harmful such as pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding, [OR] diabetes mellitus, [OR] heart disease, [OR] stroke, [OR] hypertension, [OR] malabsorption syndromes, [OR] GERD, a history of ulcer, according to a detailed medical history. 
Abnormal hepatic function (liver function test > twice the normal range), abnormal renal function (creatinine > 1.1 mg/dl), fasting plasma glucose in the diabetic range (>/= 126 mg/dl), [OR] or blood pressure > 140/90 mmHg. 
Present alcoholism [OR] or drug abuse [OR] or use of medications that could interfere with the treatment including bronchodilators, [OR] quinolone antibiotics, [OR] monoamine oxidase inhibitors, [OR] anxiolytics, [OR] ranitidine, [OR] corticosteroids, [OR] growth hormone, [OR] antihypertensives.
