Inclusion Criteria:
Age of = 18 years of age and able to give written informed consent;
Active chronic central serous chorioretinopathy (cCSC);
Subjective visual loss > 6 weeks, interpreted as onset of active disease;
Foveal subretinal fluid (SRF), on optical coherence tomography (OCT), at Baseline Examination;
 =1 ill-defined hyperfluorescent leakage areas on fluorescein angiography (FA) [AND] with retinal pigment epithelial window defect(s) that are compatible with cCSC;
 Hyperfluorescent areas on indocyanine green angiography (ICGA).

Exclusion Criteria:
Any previous treatments for active CSC;
 Previous prescription of mineralocorticoid receptor antagonists, [OR] for cCSC [OR] or for other diseases;
 Current treatment with corticosteroids (topical [OR] or systemic), corticosteroid use within 3 months before possible start of trial treatment, [OR] or anticipated start of corticosteroid treatment within the first 2 years from the start of the trial period;
 Evidence of another diagnosis that can explain serous SRF [OR] or visual loss;
 Best-corrected visual acuity < 20/200 (Snellen equivalent);
 Profound chorioretinal atrophy in central macular area on ophthalmoscopy [AND] and OCT;
 Myopia > 6D;
 Visual loss [OR] and/or serous detachment on OCT < 6 weeks;
 Continuous [OR] and/or progressive visual loss > 18 months [OR] or serous detachment on OCT > 18 months;
 [NOT] No hyperfluorescence on ICGA;
 Intraretinal edema on OCT;
 (relative) Contraindications for FA [OR] or ICGA;
 (relative) Contraindications for photodynamic treatment (pregnancy, [OR] porphyria, severely disturbed liver function). Pregnancy will [NOT] not be routinely tested in female patients, but the possibility of pregnancy will be discussed during screening
 (relative) Known contraindications for initiation of eplerenone treatment (hyperkalemia, [OR] abnormal renal clearance, severe hepatic insufficiency (Child-Pugh C), type 2 diabetes mellitus [AND] with microalbuminuria, concomitant use of potassium supplements, [OR] potassium-sparing diuretics, [OR] strong CYP3A4 inhibitors, [OR] or the combination of an ACE-inhibitor [AND] and an angiotensin receptor blocking agent). Pregnancy will [NOT] not be routinely tested in female patients, but the possibility of pregnancy will be discussed during screening;
 Soft drusen in treated eye [OR] or fellow eye, signs of choroidal neovascularization on ophthalmoscopy [OR] and/or FA/ [OR] ICGA of the study eye.
