Inclusion Criteria:
Normal uterine cavity
Normal Hormonal investigation: TSH, [OR] PRL, [OR] FBS
Frozen embryo transfer cycles: at least 2 embryos
Primary [OR] or secondary infertility: tubal occlusion, [OR] male factor, [OR] unexplained, [OR] endometriosis, [OR] ovarian factors…
Body mass index (BMI) =18 to =30 kg/m2

Exclusion Criteria:
Preexisting untreated medical condition (thyroid disease, [OR] diabetes mellitus, [OR] hypertension, [OR] pulmonary conditions, [OR] cardiac condition…)
History of three or more consecutively failed In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) cycles after embryo transfer
History of three or more miscarriages
Previous allergy reactions to progesterone products
