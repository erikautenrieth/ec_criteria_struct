Inclusion Criteria:
 1. Males [OR] and females ≥ 18 years old. 
 2. Clinical diagnosis of type 1 diabetes for at least one year. 
 3. The subject will have been on insulin pump therapy for at least 3 months [AND] and currently using a fast actin insulin analog (Lispro, [OR] Aspart [OR] or Guilisine). 
 4. Last (less than 3 months) HbA1c ≤ 10%. 
 5. Currently using carbohydrate counting as the meal insulin dose strategy. 

Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Clinically significant microvascular complications: nephropathy (estimated glomerular filtration rate below 40 ml/min), [OR] neuropathy [OR] (especially diagnosed gastroparesis) [OR] or severe proliferative retinopathy as judged by the investigator. 
 2. Recent (< 3 months) acute macrovascular event e.g. acute coronary syndrome [OR] or cardiac surgery. 
 3. Ongoing pregnancy. 
 4. Severe hypoglycemic episode within 1 month of screening. 
 5. Agents affecting gastric emptying  (Motilium®, [OR] Prandase®, [OR] Victoza®, [OR] Byetta® [OR] and Symlin®) [AND] as well as oral anti-diabetic agents  (Metformin, [OR] SGLT-2 inhibitors [OR] and DPP-4 inhibitors) if [NOT] not at a stable dose for 3 months. Otherwise, these medications are acceptable and will be kept stable during the entire protocol. 
 6. Oral steroids unless patients present a low stable dose (e.g. 10 mg or less of prednisone per day [OR] or physiological doses, less than 35 mg/day, of hydrocortisone Cortef®). Inhale steroids at stable dose in the last month are acceptable. 
 7. Other serious medical illness likely to interfere with study participation [OR] or with the ability to complete the trial by the judgment of the investigator (e.g. unstable psychiatric condition). 
 8. [NOT] Failure to comply with team's recommendations (e.g. [NOT] not willing to change pump parameters, follow algorithm's suggestions, etc). 
 9. Living [OR] or planned travel [NOT] outside Montreal (> 1h of driving) area during closed-loop procedures.
