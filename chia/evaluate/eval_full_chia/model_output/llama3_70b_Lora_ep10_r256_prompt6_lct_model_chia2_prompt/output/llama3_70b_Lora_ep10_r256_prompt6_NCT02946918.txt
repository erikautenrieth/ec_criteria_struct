Inclusion Criteria:
Age > 18 years
Presumed AJCC (American Joint Committee on Cancer) tumor Stage I [OR] or II
Planned total [OR] or near-total thyroidectomy
Planned goal TSH suppression 0.1-0.5 mU/L for at least 18 weeks postoperatively
Normal serum TSH within 12 months preceding surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
AJCC Stage III or greater
Undifferentiated, [OR] Anaplastic [OR] or Medullary Thyroid Cancer
Planned postoperative TSH goal other than 0.1-0.5 mU/L
History of gastrointestinal malabsorption [OR] or gastric bypass surgery
Pregnancy
Use of medications that alter the absorption [OR] or metabolism of levothyroxine
Prior use of levothyroxine
