Inclusion Criteria:
. Inclusion criteria are American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) physical status I-III, age between 18 and 70 years [AND] and body mass index (BMI) between 20 and 35 kg/m2.

Exclusion Criteria:
Exclusion criteria are pregnancy, patients with contraindications to regional anesthesia, allergy to LAs, patients taking opioids regularly due to chronic pain, use of anticoagulation drugs other than acetylsalicylic acid [OR] or dipyridamole, atrioventricular block, [OR] diabetes.
