Inclusion Criteria:
Eligible patients must meet the following criteria to be enrolled in the study: 
1. Newly diagnosed, stage IV squamous cell lung cancer. This includes patients [AND] who present with disseminated metastases, [OR] and those [AND] with a malignant pleural [OR] or pericardial effusion (i.e., formerly stage IIIB in the 6th TNM staging system). 
2. Patients [AND] who have received prior adjuvant therapy [AND] for early-stage lung cancer are eligible if at least 12 months have elapsed from that treatment. 
3. Histologically confirmed squamous cell bronchogenic carcinoma. Patients whose tumors contain mixed [NOT] non-small cell histologies are eligible, as long as squamous carcinoma is the predominant histology. Mixed tumors [AND] with small cell anaplastic elements are [NOT] not eligible. Cytologic specimens obtained by brushings, [OR] washings, [OR] or needle aspiration of the defined lesion are acceptable. 
4. Patients [AND] who have received palliative radiation therapy [AND] for symptomatic metastases must have completed treatment >14 days prior the initiation of the study treatment. 
5. Presence of evaluable (measureable [OR] or [NOT] non-measurable) disease. 
6. ECOG Performance Status of 0 or 1. 
7. Laboratory values as follows: 
Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) >1,500/microL [AND] and platelets >100,000/microL (≤72 hours prior to initial treatment). 
Hemoglobin >9 g/dL (Note: Patients may be transfused [OR] or receive erythropoietin to maintain [OR] or exceed this level).

Alanine aminotransferase (ALT) [AND] and aspartate aminotransferase (AST) ≤2.5 times the upper limit of normal if [NOT] no liver involvement [OR] or ≤5 times the upper limit of normal [AND] with liver involvement.

10. Ability to understand the nature of this study, give written informed consent, and comply with study requirements. 
11. Patients entering this study must be willing to provide tissue from a previous tumor biopsy (if available) for correlative testing. If tissue is [NOT] not available, a patient will still be eligible for enrollment into the study.
