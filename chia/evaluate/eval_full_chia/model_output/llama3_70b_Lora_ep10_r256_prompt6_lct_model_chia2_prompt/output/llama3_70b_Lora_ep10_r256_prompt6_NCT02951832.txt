Inclusion Criteria:
Women aged 20-49; 
Having a regular menstrual cycle of which the menstrual period is between day 3-7, [AND] and the period between day 25-35; 
Excluding internal [OR] and surgical disease (after having variety of physical examination such as electrocardiogram/ [OR] hepatic [OR] and renal function/ [OR] blood routine [OR] and urine routine). 

Exclusion Criteria:
Having experienced severe allergies, [OR] trauma history [OR] and/or operation history within 3 months; 
With a history of mental illness [OR] and/or family history of mental illness; 
Limb disabled; 
Taking medicine within one month; 
Suffering major events [OR] or having mood swings.
