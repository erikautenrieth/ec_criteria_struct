Inclusion Criteria:
Age between 20 and 80 years
Patients undergoing percutaneous coronary intervention [AND] and need to take dual antiplatelet therapy continuously at least 12weeks
Modified Lanza Score grade 0-1 measured by upper gastrointestinal endoscopy
mild gastrointestinal symptom
Creatinen in blood = 3mg/dl
BUN = 50mg/dl
 Birilubin = 3mg/dl
 AST [AND] and ALT = 80U/L

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant [OR] or breast feeding
History of Stomach [OR] or esophagus surgery
Peptic ulcer [OR] or reflux esophagitis
Zollinger-Ellison syndrome [OR] or primary esophageal motility disorders
Malignant tumor
Bleeding tendency [OR] or coagulopathy
Contraindication of ALBIS
Long term use of aspirin [OR] or P2Y12 receptor antagonist within 1month
Patients who tool medicine such as PPI, [OR] APA, [OR] H2blocker, [OR] Muscarine receptor antagonist, [OR] anti-gastic agent, [OR] antacid, [OR] anticaogulant, [OR] Bisphosphonate agents, [OR] Cytotoxic drug, [OR] NSAID, [OR] adrenal cortex hormone agents (topical treatment is allowed)
Terminal patient
