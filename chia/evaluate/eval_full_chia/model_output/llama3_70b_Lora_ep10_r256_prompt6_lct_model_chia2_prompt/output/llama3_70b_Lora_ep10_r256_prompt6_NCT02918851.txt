Inclusion Criteria:
Habitual exerciser defined as = 30 minutes of at least moderate [OR] or high intensity exercise = 3 times per week. After consent, and at the subsequent screening visit, a VO2 max test will be performed, and subjects with a low value (< 35 mL/kg/min) will be [NOT] excluded (screen failure). Based on our previous experience, we anticipate that <10% of the subjects will fall into this category
Men: (0.006012 x H3) + (14.6 x W) + 604 = TBV
Women: (0.005835 x H3) + (15 x W) + 183 = TBV [H=height in inches; W=weight in pounds]
Has access to transportation to visit the blood collection facility and to return to Stony Brook for all study visits.

Exclusion Criteria:
Any significant acute [OR] or chronic medical illness [OR] or problem, including, but not limited to, diabetes, [OR] hypertension, [OR] cardiac disease, [OR] asthma, [OR] chronic obstructive lung disease
Current [OR] or recent (last 60 days) tobacco [OR] or nicotine use
History of sickle cell trait [OR] or disease [OR] or any other acquired [OR] or hereditary hematological abnormality
History of fainting [OR] or other significant adverse reaction during phlebotomy [OR] or donation of blood
Known prolonged QTc (or evidence of such at screening) on electrocardiogram defined as >470 ms
 Known [OR] or suspected illicit drug [OR] or alcohol abuse
 Known [OR] or suspected HIV, [OR] Hepatitis B, [OR] or Hepatitis C infection
 History of thrombophilia [OR] or anticoagulant therapy
 Pregnancy
 Obesity defined as BMI>30
 Recent history of blood donation: a) Single whole blood unit donation within the past 8 weeks; b) Double RBC donation by apheresis within the past 16 weeks; [OR] or c) Plasma donation by apheresis within the past 4 weeks
 Inadequate RBC mass based on TBV <4500 ml (above) [OR] or screening Hb <14 g/dL
