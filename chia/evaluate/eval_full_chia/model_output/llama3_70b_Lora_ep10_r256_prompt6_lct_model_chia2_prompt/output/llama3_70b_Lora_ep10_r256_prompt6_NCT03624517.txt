Inclusion Criteria:
Adult males [OR] and females [AND] who are 18 years of age or older.
Evidence [OR] or suspicion of upper gastrointestinal bleed (GIB)
Patient with known [OR] or suspected cirrhosis
Upper GIB secondary to bleeding esophageal varices as show by esophageal endoscopy, requiring endoscopic band ligation (EBL) at presentation
Willing and able to provide informed consent for study, or have a Legally authorized representative (LAR) provide consent if the patient is unable to do so

Exclusion Criteria:
Known upper gastrointestinal malignancy
Bleeding [OR] from gastric varices, [AND] with or [NOT] without esophageal varices
Use of any other endoscopic method to [NOT] stop GI bleeding beyond endoscopic band ligation
Variceal bleeding in the last 90 days
History of transjugular, [OR] intrahepatic, [OR] portosystemic shunt (TIPS) [OR] or vascular decompression surgery
Pregnant females
Incarcerated individuals
Myocardial infarct, [OR] cerebrovascular accident, [OR] sepsis, [OR] respiratory failure, [OR] or severe intercurrent illness within the previous 6 weeks
 [NOT] Non-cirrhotic portal hypertension causing esophageal varices
Known [OR] or suspected allergy to octreotide
