Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with craniotomy [AND] for supratentorial tumors under general anesthesia
American Society of Anaesthesiologists (ASA) 2 [AND] and stable ASA 3 patients
Elective surgery
Patients with Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS) 15/15

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with a history of allergy [OR] or hypersensitivity to tramadol.
History of epilepsy [OR] or convulsions due to any reason.
Chronic usage of analgesic drugs.
 Patients using monoamine oxidase inhibitors.
 Patients with clinical signs of raised ICP.
 Obesity (women [AND] with a body mass index >35 kg/m2 [OR] or men [AND] with a body mass index >42 kg/m2)
 Language barrier.
 Patients taking B-blockers [OR] or Ca channel blockers.
 Patients above 65 years of age ( Physiology difference)
