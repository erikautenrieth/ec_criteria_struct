Inclusion Criteria:
a crown-rump length = 6mm [AND] and [NOT] no cardiac activity [OR] OR
 a crown-rump length <6mm [AND] and [NOT] no fetal growth at least one week later [OR] OR
At least one week after diagnosis [OR] OR a discrepancy of at least one week between crown-rump length and calendar gestational age
Intra-uterine pregnancy
Women aged above 16 years
Hemodynamic stable patient
 [NOT] No signs of infection
 [NOT] No signs of incomplete abortion
 [NOT] No contraindications for mifepristone [OR] or misoprostol

Exclusion Criteria:
Patient does [NOT] not meet inclusion criteria, discovered after randomization
Inability to give informed consent
 Known clotting disorder [OR] or use of anticoagulants
 Known risk factors for, [OR] or presence of, a cardiovascular disease
 Language barrier
