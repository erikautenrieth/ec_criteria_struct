Inclusion Criteria:
Understand and communicate with the investigator, comply with the requirements of the study and give a written, signed and dated informed consent
Male [OR] or [NOT] non-pregnant, [NOT] non-lactating female patients at least 18 years of age
Diagnosis of moderate to severe Ankylosing Spondylitis (AS) [AND] with prior documented radiologic evidence fulfilling the Modified New York criteria for AS
Active AS assessed by total Bath Ankylosing Spondylitis Disease Activity index (BASDAI) = 4 (0-10) at baseline
Spinal pain as measured by BASDAI question #2 = 4 cm (0-10 cm) at baseline
 Total back pain as measured by visual analog scale (VAS) = 40 mm (0-100 mm) at baseline
Patients should have been on [NOT] non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) at the maximum tolerated dose for at least 4 weeks prior to their Baseline Visit, [AND] with an inadequate response [OR] or for less than 4 weeks if [NOT] withdrawn for intolerance, [OR] toxicity [OR] or contraindications
Stable dose of NSAIDs including Cyclooxygenase-1 (COX-1) [OR] or Cyclooxygenase-2 (COX-2) inhibitors for at least 2 weeks before their Baseline Visit
Patients who have been on a tumor necrosis factor alpha (TNFa) inhibitor ([NOT] not more than one) must have experienced an inadequate response to previous [OR] or current treatment given at an approved dose for at least 3 months prior to baseline [OR] or had been [NOT] intolerant upon administration of an anti-TNFa agent
Total ankylosis of the spine
Use of other investigational drugs within 5 half-lives of enrollment, [OR] or within 4 weeks before the Baseline Visit, whichever is longer.
History of hypersensitivity to any of the study drugs [OR] or its excipients [OR] or to drugs of similar chemical classes.
Chest x-ray, computerized tomography (CT) scan, [OR] or chest magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) with evidence of ongoing infectious [OR] or malignant process, obtained within 3 months prior to screening and evaluated by a qualified physician.
Previous exposure to secukinumab [OR] or any other biologic drug directly targeting Interleukin-17 (IL-17), [OR] Interleukin-12/23 (IL-12/23), [OR] or the IL-17 receptor, [OR] or any other biologic immunomodulating agent, except those targeting TNFa
Patients who have taken more than one anti-TNFa agent
Any intramuscular [OR] or intravenous corticosteroid injection within 2 weeks before baseline
Any therapy by intra-articular injections (e.g. corticosteroid) within 4 weeks before baseline
Previous treatment with any cell-depleting therapies
Patients taking high potency opioid analgesics (e.g., methadone, [OR] hydromorphone, [OR] morphine)

NA
