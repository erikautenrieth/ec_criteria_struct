Inclusion Criteria:
Written informed consent
Adult patients (older than 18 years of age), male [OR] and female, [AND] with chronic [NOT] non-cancer [OR] and cancer pain (at least 3 months in duration)
Patients experiencing an average weekly pain intensity score greater than 4 on a 11 points NRS
Subject agreed to follow the protocol
Naïve cannabis patients [AND] with chronic [NOT] non-cancer [OR] and cancer pain ([NOT] not used cannabis in any presentation in the last 12 weeks)
Patients receiving opioids [OR] and other concomitant pain medications should have a stable dose for the last 15 days.
Normal cognitive status according to MiniCog
Normal liver function (defined as aspartate aminotransferase 10-40 U/L [AND] and alanine aminotransferase 7-56 U/L)
Normal renal function (defined as serum creatinine level <133 µmol/L [AND] and Estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (eGFR) greater than or equal to 60)
Negative result on ßhuman chorionic gonadotropin pregnancy test (if applicable)
Ability to read and respond to questions in French [OR] or English.
A male volunteer [AND] with sexual partners [AND] who are pregnant, possibly pregnant, [OR] or who could become pregnant must be surgically sterile [OR] or agrees to use one of the accepted contraceptive regimens from first drug administration until 3 months after the last drug administration.

Exclusion Criteria:
Acute pain (less than 3 months in duration)
Previous serious adverse event [OR] or hypersensitivity to cannabis [OR] or cannabinoids
 [NOT] Inability to understand and comply with the instructions of the study
Presence of significant cardiac disease (history of unstable ischemic heart disease, [OR] heart failure, severe [AND] and uncontrolled hypertension) that, in the opinion of the investigator, would put the patient at risk of a clinically significant arrhythmia [OR] or myocardial infarction
Current substance use disorder according to the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders Fifth Edition (DSM 5)
Life-time history of dependence on cannabis [OR] or diagnosis of cannabis use disorder (CUD) according to the DSM 5
 Life-time history of DSM 5 schizophrenia, [OR] bipolar disorder, [OR] or previous psychosis [AND] with or intolerance to cannabinoids
 Current [OR] or history of suicidal ideation
Pregnant, [OR] breast-feeding [OR] or female patients [AND] of child-bearing potential [AND] and male patients [AND] whose partner is of child-bearing potential, unless willing to ensure that they [OR] or their partner use effective contraception
Hepatic impairment (aspartate aminotransferase more than three times normal) [OR] or renal function impairment (serum creatinine level >133 µmol/L, Estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (eGFR) <60)
Cognitive impairment according to MiniCog
The patient is currently using [OR] or has used cannabinoid based medications within 90 days of study entry [AND] and is unwilling to [NOT] abstain for the duration of the study
Positive urine drug screen for cannabinoids [OR] and other potential abuse substances (e.g. alcohol, [OR] cocaine, [OR] amphetamines [OR] and methamphetamines, [OR] unprescribed opioids)
Participation in another clinical trial within 30 days of enrolment in our trial
