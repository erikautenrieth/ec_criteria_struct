Inclusion Criteria:
Female patients older than 18 years.
Patients who agree to participate in the study.
Those that meet the ACR 1990 [OR] and 2010 criteria for Fibromyalgia.
 [NOT] No previous use of vitamin D.
Patients diagnosed with primary [OR] or secondary fibromyalgia.

Exclusion Criteria:
Those subjects with previous use of vitamin D.
 Known subjects with renal, [OR] liver, [OR] calcium metabolism disorders, [OR] malabsorption disorders, known neoplasms.
Subjects with serum calcium levels equal to or greater than 10.2 mg / dl.
