Inclusion Criteria:
Women [AND] who are in their first 5 years of menopause 
Have a T-score between -1 and -2.49 at the femoral neck, [OR] total hip, [OR] or L1-L4 spine 
Be 19 years of age or older 
Have their health care provider's permission to enroll in the study. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Have osteoporosis 
Have a 10 yr probability of hip fracture >3% [OR] or major fracture >20% based on results of the FRAX tool 
Currently take bisphosphonates, [OR] estrogen replacement therapy, [OR] glucocorticosteroids, [OR] or other drugs affecting bone 
 Currently participate in a resistance training [OR] or high impact weight bearing exercise program two or more times weekly 
Weigh >300 lbs 
Have abnormal results for the following laboratory tests: serum 25(OH)D; serum creatinine; serum calcium; PTH; TSH 
Have Paget's disease, [OR] heart disease, uncontrolled hypertension, [OR] renal disease, [OR] or other concomitant conditions that [NOT] prohibit participation in exercises, [OR] risedronate therapy, [OR] or use of CaD supplements.
