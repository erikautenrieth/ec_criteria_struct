Inclusion Criteria:
Subjects aged 18 to 80 years old
Overweight [OR] or obesity (BMI =25 kg/m2)
 Previous diagnosis of type 2 diabetes, fulfilling at least one of the following criteria: 1) current treatment with oral antidiabetic drugs [OR] and/or insulin; 2) a fasting glucose value above 126 mg/dl on at least 2 occasions; 3) blood glucose level at 2 hours after an oral glucose tolerance test is equal to or more than 200 mg/dl; [OR] or 4) a glycated hemoglobin (HbA1c) level > 6.5 %
Clinical diagnosis of diabetic nephropathy, [AND] with a urinary albumin/ [OR] creatinine ratio >30 mg/g [AND] and an estimated glomerular filtration rate more than 20 ml/min per 1.73 m2.
Treatment with stable doses of angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors, [OR] angiotensin II receptor blockers [OR] or anti-aldosterone agents in the last four weeks.

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Non diabetic nephropathy (confirmed by biopsy).
Dialysis [OR] for acute renal failure within the 6 previous months.
Evidence in the clinic history of relevant bilateral stenosis of renal artery (> 75%)
Urinary albumin/ [OR] creatinine ratio higher than 3000 mg/g, at the baseline visit.
Systolic blood pressure = 180 mmHg [OR] or diastolic blood pressure = 110 mm Hg at the baseline visit.
Stroke, [OR] transient ischemic attack, [OR] acute coronary syndrome, [OR] or hospitalization for heart failure worsening, within the previous 30 days.
Professional drivers, risk profession [OR] or respiratory failure.
Severe daytime sleepiness (Epworth sleepiness scale >18)
Concomitant treatment with high doses of acetylsalicylic acid (> 500 mg/day) [OR] or continuous treatment with non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs
Previous treatment with CPAP
Participation in another clinical trial within the 30 days prior to randomization.
