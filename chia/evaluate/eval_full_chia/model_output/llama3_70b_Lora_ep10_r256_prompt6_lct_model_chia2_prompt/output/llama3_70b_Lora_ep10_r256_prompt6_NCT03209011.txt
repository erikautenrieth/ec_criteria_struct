Inclusion Criteria:
HBsAg [AND] and HBeAg positive for more than 6 months, HBV DNA detectable [AND] with ALT level abnormal lasted for three months and at least time190 IU/L [OR] or liver puncture biopsy demonstrated apparent inflammation, [NOT] never treated before enrolled.

Exclusion Criteria:
Active consumption of alcohol [OR] and/or drugs
Co-infection with human immunodeficiency virus, [OR] hepatitis C virus, [OR] or hepatitis D virus
History of autoimmune hepatitis
Psychiatric disease
Evidence of neoplastic diseases of the liver
