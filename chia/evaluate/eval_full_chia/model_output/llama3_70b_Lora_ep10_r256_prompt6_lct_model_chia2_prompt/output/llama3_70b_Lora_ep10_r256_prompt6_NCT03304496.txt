Inclusion Criteria:
Men [OR] and women older than 18 years, scheduled consecutively to perform a coronary procedure in the department of hemodynamics of the National Institute of Cardiology "Ignacio Chavez".
Patients may have any of the following indications for cardiac catheterization: Thoracic pain under study. Stable chronic coronary disease. Acute myocardial infarction [AND] with ST segment elevation, [NOT] not perfused ([NOT] without timely reperfusion therapy) with less than 4 weeks of evolution. Acute myocardial infarction [AND] with ST-segment elevation, successful thrombolytic therapy, which will undergo drug-invasive therapy. Acute myocardial infarction [NOT] without ST segment elevation. Unstable angina. Any acute coronary syndrome, to intervene [NOT] non-infarct-related artery. Disease of any heart valve. Myocarditis [OR] or pericarditis. Dilated cardiomyopathy. Patients in renal [OR] or cardiac transplantation protocol for any etiology. Congenital heart disease that requires knowing the coronary anatomy prior to surgical correction.
The planned procedure can be any of the following: For diagnostic purposes (coronary angiography only, left catheterization, left [OR] and right catheterization). For therapeutic purposes: percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI), [AND] with or [NOT] without stent placement.
A priori access must be right [OR] or left radial artery.
Radial arterial pulse may be present [OR] or [NOT] absent by palpation.
Modified Allen [OR] or Barbeau test should be positive (presence of collateral palmar flow).

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant.
Not have informed consent for the present clinical trial, or do not fully understand the meaning of informed consent.
With acute myocardial infarction [AND] with ST segment elevation in the first 12 hours from the onset of symptoms.
 With any acute coronary syndrome complicated [AND] with acute pulmonary edema, [OR] cardiogenic shock [OR] and / or malignant ventricular arrhythmias.
In which a cardiac catheterization is planned a priori to be performed via femoral, [OR] brachial [OR] or ulnar.
Patients in whom first attempt of arterial puncture is performed by 2nd year interventional cardiology fellow [OR] or by physician in charge.
Participating in another clinical trial.
Be allergic [OR] or have contraindications to nitroglycerin [OR] or other nitrates.
 Any phosphodiesterase 5 inhibitor (sildenafil, [OR] tadalafil, [OR] avanafil, [OR] vardenafil) has been taken within 72 hours prior to the study.
