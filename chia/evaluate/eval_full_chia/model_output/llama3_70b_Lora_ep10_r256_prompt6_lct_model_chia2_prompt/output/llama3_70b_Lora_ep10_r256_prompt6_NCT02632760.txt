Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with anaemia (males Hb <130 g/L, females <120 g/L) undergoing elective cardiac surgery, [AND] and available to receive trial drug 1- 10 weeks prior to surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnancy
Known hypersensitivity to study drug (ferric carboxymaltose [OR] or equivalent) or its excipients
 Known [OR] or suspected haemoglobinopathy/ [OR] thalassaemia
Bone marrow disease
 Haemochromatosis
 Renal dialysis
 Erythropoietin [OR] or IV iron in the previous 4 weeks
