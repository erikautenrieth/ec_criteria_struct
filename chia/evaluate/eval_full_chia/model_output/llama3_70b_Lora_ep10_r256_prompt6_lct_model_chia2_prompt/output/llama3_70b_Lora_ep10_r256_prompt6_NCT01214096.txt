Inclusion Criteria:
1. Age: 18-75 years old, [NOT] no limitation in gender; 
2. Left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) ≤ 40% (ECHO); 
3. Patients with chronic heart failure (NYHA class II or III); 
4. In the past one month, the clinical condition (including history, clinical symptoms and signs) was relatively stable; 
5. Patients on standard treatment of chronic heart failure at the target dose [OR] or maximum tolerance dose for over 1 month, [OR] or unchanged dose in last 1 month; 
6. Understand and sign the informed consent form; 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Atrial fibrillation; 
2. Subject underwent cardiac pacemaker treatment; 
3. Subject underwent metal graft treatment; 
4. Claustrophobia; 
5. Acute myocardial infarction, [OR] cardiac ischemia indicated by 6-minute walk test, [OR] hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, [OR] constrictive pericarditis, significant valve disease [OR] or congenital heart disease, severe pulmonary hypertension; 
6. Ischemic heart failure [NOT] without the revascularization [OR] or undergone the revascularization within last 6 months; 
7. Subject underwent cardiac surgery [OR] or cerebrovascular events within the previous six months; 
8. Subjects who plan to have cardiac transplantation; 
9. Severe hepatic [OR] and renal insufficiency (serum creatinine>2.0 mg /dl, AST [OR] or ALT is five times higher than the upper limit of normal range); 
10. Subject needs mechanical ventilation; 
11. Systolic blood pressure < 90mmHg, [OR] or > 160mmHg; 
12. Chronic heart failure complicated with acute hemodynamic disturbance [OR] or acute decompensation within last 1 month; 
13. Mobitz Type II or III° atrial ventricular block，severe ventricular arrhythmia (polymorphic [OR] and frequent premature ventricular beats, frequent [NOT] non-sustained ventricular tachycardia); 
14. Serum potassium<3.2mmol/L, [OR] or>5.5mmol/L; 
15. Female subject is pregnant [OR] or plan to become pregnant 
16. Childbearing-aged female subject [AND] who is unmarried [OR] or dose [NOT] not bear child; 
17. Subject with life expectancy less than 6 months as assessed by investigators; 
18. Subject participated in any other clinical trial within the previous three months; 
19. Subject with previous history of tumor, [OR] or current tumor patient, [OR] or subject with pre-cancerous disease manifested by pathological examination (such as ductal carcinoma in situ [OR] or cervical epithelial dysplasia) 
20. Examinations (physical examination, [OR] X-ray examination, [OR] type-B ultrasonic detection [OR] or other methods) reveal that the subject has malignant mass, [OR] gland hyperplasia [OR] or adenoma with endocrine activity, [OR] or impact on heart, [OR] or endocrine function (such as pheochromocytoma, [OR] thyroid enlargement); 
21. The Investigator deemed for whatever reason that the subject is [NOT] not likely to complete the study [OR] or comply with the study procedures (due to administration [OR] or any other reason).
