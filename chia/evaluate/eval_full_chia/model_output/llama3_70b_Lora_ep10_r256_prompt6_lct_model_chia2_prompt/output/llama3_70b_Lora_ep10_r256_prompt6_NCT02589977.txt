Inclusion Criteria:
estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) > 60 ml/min
preserved left ventricular ejection fraction (>= 50%) on echocardiography
HEALTHY: normal cardiac structure [AND] and function on echocardiography, BP < 140/90
HYPERTENSIVE: history of BP >140/90, 1 or more antihypertensive medications, LV ejection fraction (LVEF) at least 50%, current BP < 160/90
HFpEF: physician-confirmed diagnosis of HF, symptomatic HF, LVEF at least 50%, elevated LV filling pressure by catheterization, [OR] echocardiographic criteria [OR] or B-type-natriuretic peptide > 100, current BP < 160/90

Exclusion Criteria:
Exclusion Criteria: coronary artery disease, [OR] diabetes mellitus, contraindications to cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (CMR), weight >350 lbs, [NOT] inability to lie flat for imaging, [OR] anemia, contraindications to regadenoson [OR] or aminophylline
HEALTHY: known cardiovascular disease, [OR] cardiac risk factors [OR] or use of cardiac medications
HYPERTENSIVE: known cardiovascular disease [OR] or risk factors aside from hypertension [OR] or use of cardiac medications
 HFpEF: prior history of LVEF below 50%, acute decompensated HF, [OR] moderate [OR] or greater valvular disease, significant cardiac arrhythmias, [OR] pericardial disease, [OR] congenital heart disease, [OR] primary pulmonary hypertension
