Inclusion Criteria:
Age=18 years [AND] and =80 years;
Patients with [NOT] non-cystic fibrosis bronchiectasis diagnosed by high-resolution CT;
 Are sensitive to amikacin;
 Acute exacerbation of bronchiectasis;
 Capable of the completion of bronchoscopy, [OR] alveolar lavage, [OR] pulmonary function testing etc;
 Willing to join in and sign the informed consent form.

Exclusion Criteria:
Active bleeding [NOT] without control;
 Receiving nasal [OR] or facial surgery recently;
 With severe cardio-pulmonary dysfunction, such as left heart failure, unstable arrhythmia, etc.
 With other respiratory diseases: such as active pulmonary tuberculosis, [OR] non-tuberculosis mycobacteria (NTM) pulmonary disease, [OR] pulmonary aspergillosis, etc.
 Be allergic to amikacin
