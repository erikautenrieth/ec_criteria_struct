Inclusion Criteria:
Males [OR] and females of 18 years of age or older at the time of the vaccination
Severe chronic kidney disease (Stage 4 and 5)

Exclusion Criteria:
immunization with PPV23 within the last year
any confirmed [OR] or suspected immunodeficiency condition, including human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) infection, [OR] haematological malignancy, [OR] or a congenital immunodeficiency
history of allergic disease [OR] or reactions likely to be exacerbated by any component of the vaccine
history of allergic disease likely to be stimulated by the vaccination
history [OR] or records of immunosuppressive therapy (with the exception of topical corticosteroids) for more than 14 days [AND] and within 6 months of vaccination
history [OR] or evidence of administration of immunoglobulins [OR] and/or any blood products during the study period [OR] or within the three months preceding the study vaccine
use of any other investigational [OR] or non-registered drug [OR] or vaccine during the study period [OR] or within 30 days preceding the study vaccine
administration of a vaccine during the period starting one month before the dose of vaccine [AND] and ending one month after
pregnancy
