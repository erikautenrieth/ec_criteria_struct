Inclusion Criteria:
Male [OR] and/or female patients from 30-80 years of age [AND] with a diagnosis of Type 2 diabetes (WHO criteria). 
Incipient [OR] and established diabetic nephropathy (urinary albumin excretion ≥ 100 mg/day [AND] but ≤ 2000 mg/day). 
Glomerular filtration rate (GFR) ≥ 40 ml/min (estimated using Modification of Diet in Renal Disease (MDRD) formula) in the last 4 months. 
Female patients must be postmenopausal [OR] or must have had a bilateral oophorectomy [OR] or must have been surgically sterilized [OR] or hysterectomized at least 6 months prior to screening. 
To be eligible patients must fulfill the following criteria: Patients on ongoing hypertensive therapy must have a blood pressure ≥ 135/85 mm Hg [AND] but lower than 170/105 mm Hg at baseline (Day -1) [AND] AND patients must be on stable antihypertensive medications for at least 8 weeks prior to baseline (Day -1).; Newly diagnosed hypertensive patients must have a blood pressure ≥ 135/85 mm Hg [AND] but lower than 170/105 mm Hg at baseline (Day -1). 
Patients must be on stable hypoglycemic medications for at least 8 weeks prior to Visit 2 ( Day -1). 
Patients must be willing [AND] and medically able to [NOT] discontinue all Angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor (ACEI), Angiotensin receptor blocker (ARB), aldosterone receptor antagonist [OR] and potassium sparing diuretic medications for the duration of the study. 
Oral body temperature within the range 35.0-37.5 °C 
Able to provide written informed consent prior to study participation.. 
Able to communicate well with the investigator and comply with the requirements of the study. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Severe Hypertension Grade 3 WHO classification (Mean Sitting Diastolic Blood Pressure (MSDBP) 110 mmHg [OR] and/or Mean Sitting Systolic Blood Pressure MSSBP 180 mmHg) 
Acetylsalicyclic acid (ASA) treatment >1g/day [OR] or regular use of Non steroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAIDs) 
Kidney disease [NOT] not caused by diabetes [OR] or hypertension 
Serum potassium < 3.5 [OR] or > 5.1 mEq/L 
GFR < 40 ml/min/1.73 m2 as measured by the MDRD formula 
Serum albumin < 2.0 mg/dL 
History of hypertensive encephalopathy [OR] or cerebrovascular accident at any time prior to Visit1. 
Current diagnosis of heart failure (New York Heart Association (NYHA) Class II-IV) 
History of myocardial infarction, unstable angina pectoris, [OR] coronary bypass surgery, [OR] or any percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI) during the 6 months prior to Visit 1 
Second [OR] or third degree heart block [NOT] without a pacemaker 
Concurrent potentially life threatening arrhythmia [OR] or symptomatic arrhythmia 
Clinically significant valvular heart disease 
Type 1 diabetes mellitus 
Uncontrolled Type II diabetes mellitus (Hemaglobin subtype A1C (HbA1C) >11 %) 
History of malignancy including leukemia [OR] and lymphoma (but [NOT] not basal cell skin carcinoma) within the past five years 
Participation in any clinical investigation within 4 weeks prior to dosing [OR] or longer if required by local regulation. 
Donation [OR] or loss of 400 mL or more of blood within 8 weeks prior to dosing. 
Significant illness within the two weeks prior to dosing. 
Any surgical [OR] or medical condition which might significantly alter the absorption, [OR] distribution, [OR] metabolism, [OR] or excretion of study drugs including, but [NOT] not limited to, any of the following: 
History of major gastrointestinal tract surgery such as gastrectomy, [OR] gastroenterostomy, [OR] or bowel resection

Any history of pancreatic injury, [OR] pancreatitis [OR] or evidence of impaired pancreatic function/ [OR] injury as indicated by abnormal lipase [OR] or amylase Evidence of hepatic disease, a history of hepatic encephalopathy, [OR] a history of esophageal varices, [OR] or a history of portocaval shunt 
Current treatment with cholestyramine [OR] or cholestipol resins
