Inclusion Criteria:
Be categorized as American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) Physical Status Class 1, 2, or 3.
Have a planned [NOT] non-emergent surgical procedure [OR] or clinical situation (e.g., intubation) that requires moderate [OR] or deep NMB [AND] with either rocuronium [OR] or vecuronium.
Have a planned surgical procedure [OR] or clinical situation that would allow objective neuromuscular monitoring techniques to be applied [AND] with access to the arm for neuromuscular transmission monitoring.
Age between 2 to <17 years at Visit 2.
If female, may participate if she is [NOT] not pregnant, [NOT] not breastfeeding, [AND] and at least one of the following: 1) [NOT] Not a woman of childbearing potential (WOCBP); [OR] or 2) A WOCBP [AND] who agrees to follow the study contraceptive guidance during the treatment period and for at least 7 days after the last dose of study treatment.

Exclusion Criteria:
Has any clinically significant condition [OR] or situation (eg, anatomical malformation that complicates intubation) other than the condition being studied that, in the opinion of the investigator, would interfere with the trial evaluations or optimal participation in the trial.
 Has a neuromuscular disorder that may affect NMB [OR] and/or trial assessments.
 Is dialysis-dependent [OR] or has (or is suspected of having) severe renal insufficiency (defined as estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) <30 ml/min).
 Has or is suspected of having a family [OR] or personal history of malignant hyperthermia.
 Has [OR] or is suspected of having an allergy to study treatments [OR] or its/their excipients, to opioids/ [OR] opiates, muscle relaxants [OR] or their excipients, [OR] or other medication(s) used during general anesthesia.
 Has received [OR] or is planned to receive toremifene [OR] and/or fusidic acid via IV administration within 24 hours before [OR] or within 24 hours after administration of study treatment.
 Has been previously treated with sugammadex [OR] or has participated in a sugammadex clinical trial.
 Is currently participating in [OR] or has participated in an interventional clinical trial with an investigational compound [OR] or device within 30 days of signing the informed consent/assent for this current trial.
