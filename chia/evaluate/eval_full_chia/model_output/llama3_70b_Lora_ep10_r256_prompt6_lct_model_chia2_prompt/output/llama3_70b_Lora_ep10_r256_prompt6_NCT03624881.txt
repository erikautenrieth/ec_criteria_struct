Inclusion Criteria:
Symptomatic paroxysmal AF [AND] who had at least one AF episode electrocardiographically documented within one (1) year prior to enrollment. Documentation may include electrocardiogram (ECG); Transtelephonic monitoring (TTM), Holter monitor [OR] or telemetry strip
Failed at least one antiarrhythmic drug (AAD) (Class I [OR] or III antiarrhythmic drugs) as evidenced by recurrent symptomatic AF, [OR] or intolerable to the AAD
Age 18 years or older
Signed Patient Informed Consent Form (ICF)
Able [AND] and willing to comply with all pre-, post-, and follow-up testing and requirements

Exclusion Criteria:
Previous surgical [OR] or catheter ablation for atrial fibrillation
Previous cardiac surgery (including CABG) within the past 6 months (180 days)
Valvular cardiac surgical/ [OR] percutaneous procedure (i.e., ventriculotomy, [OR] atriotomy, [OR] and valve repair [OR] or replacement [AND] and presence of a prosthetic valve)
Any carotid stenting [OR] or endarterectomy
Documented LA thrombus on imaging
LA size > 50 mm (parasternal long axis view)
LVEF < 40%
Contraindication to anticoagulation (heparin [OR] or warfarin)
History of blood clotting [OR] or bleeding abnormalities
PCI/ [OR] MI within the past 2 months (60 days)
 Documented thromboembolic event (including TIA) within the past 12 months (365 days)
Rheumatic Heart Disease
Uncontrolled heart failure [OR] or NYHA function class III or IV
Severe mitral regurgitation (Regurgitant volume = 60 mL/beat, Regurgitant fraction = 50%, [OR] and/or Effective regurgitant orifice area = 0.40cm2)
Awaiting cardiac transplantation [OR] or other cardiac surgery within the next 12 months (365 days)
Unstable angina
Acute illness [OR] or active systemic infection [OR] or sepsis
AF secondary to electrolyte imbalance, [OR] thyroid disease, [OR] or reversible [OR] or [NOT] non-cardiac cause.
Presence of implanted ICD/ [OR] CRT-D.
Significant pulmonary disease, (e.g., restrictive pulmonary disease, [OR] constrictive [OR] or chronic obstructive pulmonary disease) [OR] or any other disease [OR] or malfunction of the lungs [OR] or respiratory system that produces chronic symptoms.
Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD; active requiring significant intervention [NOT] not including OTC medication)
Significant congenital anomaly [OR] or medical problem that in the opinion of the investigator would [NOT] preclude enrollment in this study.
Women [AND] who are pregnant (as evidenced by pregnancy test if pre-menopausal)
Concurrent enrollment in an investigational study evaluating another device, [OR] biologic, [OR] or drug.
Presence of intracardiac thrombus, [OR] myxoma, [OR] tumor, [OR] interatrial baffle [OR] or patch [OR] or other abnormality that [NOT] precludes vascular access, [OR] or manipulation of the catheter.
Life expectancy less than 12 months
