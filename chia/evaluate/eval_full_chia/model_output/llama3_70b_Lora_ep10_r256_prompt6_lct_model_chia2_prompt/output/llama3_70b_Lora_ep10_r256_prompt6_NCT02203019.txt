Inclusion Criteria:
Men [OR] and women 18-89 years old
with the diagnosis of sepsis (as specified below) within the previous 24 hours
who require mechanical ventilation, [AND] and
provide informed consent either personally or by an authorized representative.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with documented allergies to propofol, [OR] dexmedetomidine, [OR] fentanyl, [OR] eggs [OR] or egg products, [OR] or soy [OR] or soy products.
A heart rate less than 50 beats/minute [OR] or grade 2 [OR] or 3 AV heart block
Mean arterial pressure less than 55 mmHg [AND] despite appropriate fluid resuscitation [AND] and vasopressor support.
Current triglyceride level > 400 mg/dl
