Inclusion Criteria:
Liver cirrhosis
Age between 18 and 70 years
Esophageal varices [AND] with high bleeding risk: more than F2 [AND] and red color sign
 [NOT] No previous history of upper gastrointestinal bleeding
 [NOT] No previous history of endoscopic, [OR] radiologic, [OR] or surgical therapy for varices [OR] or ascites
Do [NOT] not take beta-blocker, [OR] ACE inhibitor, [OR] or nitrate
Child-Pugh score <12

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with systolic blood pressure <100 mmHg [OR] or basal heart rate <60/min
Portal vein thrombosis
Uncontrolled ascites [OR] or hepatic encephalopathy
Severe coagulation disorder: prothrombin time <40% [OR] (or INR >1.7) or platelet count <30,000/mm3
 Medium [OR] or large sized gastric [OR] or duodenal varices
Coexisting malignancy
 Severe cardiovascular disorder, [OR] renal failure, [OR] peritonitis, [OR] sepsis
Severe erosive esophagitis, severe esophageal stricture, active gastric [OR] or duodenal ulcer
Contraindication to beta-blocker
Pregnancy
Refusal to give consent to participate in the trial
