Inclusion Criteria:
Phase 1 Specific Patient at least 18yrs of age [AND] with histologically confirmed CLL/ [OR] SLL [OR] or B-cell Non-Hodgkin lymphoma (DLBCL, [OR] FL, [OR] MCL, [OR] MZL, [OR] lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma). 
Phase 2a Inclusion 
Histological evidence: FL Grade 1-3A/ [OR] iNHL, [AND] with relapsed [OR] or refractory disease (iNHL includes LPL/ [OR] WM, [OR] MZL); aNHL, defined as DLBCL, [OR] FL Grade 3B, [OR] MCL, [OR] and transformed NHL [AND] with relapsed disease; CLL/ [OR] SLL, [OR] PTCL, [OR] or CTCL (with MF/ [OR] SS) [AND] with relapsed [OR] or refractory. 
Received BCR [OR] and/or BCL2 inhibitors were intolerant [OR] or had relapsed/ [OR] refractory disease afterwards. 
Prior treatment for lymphoid malignancy [AND] for progressive /refractory disease 
≥ 1 prior regimen (min 2 cycles) with antibody conjugate, [OR] cytotoxic chemotherapy, [OR] or TKI alone [OR] or in combination. 
Measureable disease defined as: ≥ 1 lesion ≥ 1.5 cm single dimension via CT, [OR] CT/PET [AND] with nodal [OR] or mass lesions; Quantifiable circulating tumor cells; [OR] or for Waldenström's macroglobulinemia presence of IgM [AND] l > 2X ULN; For CTCL: mSWAT > 0 
Ability to provide diagnostic reports 
General Inclusion 
ECOG Score of 0 or 1. 
Hematologic ANC > 1000/uL [AND] and platelet > 75,000/uL, 
Serum creatinine of < 1.5 ULN [OR] or calculated CrCl of > 50 mL/min 
Bilirubin < 20.0mg/dL (if Gilberts then < 2.5 mg/dL) [AND] and AST/ [OR] AST < 2.5 ULN 

Exclusion Criteria:
Richter's syndrome, [OR] Burkitt's lymphoma, [OR] or Burkitt-like Lymphoma (transformed DLBCL from Follicular NHL are eligible). 
Prior transplant [AND] with stem cell infusion 90 days [OR] or active graft-versus-host treatment within 8 weeks of Day 1. 
Prior therapy with SYK inhibitors. 
Chronic treatment with strong CYP3A4 inhibitor/ [OR] inducer, acid reducing agent, [OR] Proton pump inhibitors 
Known lymphomatous involvement of the CNS. 
Persistent, unresolved NCI CTCAE v4.0 ≥ Grade 2, previous drug-related toxicity (except alopecia, [OR] erectile impotence, [OR] hot flashes, [OR] libido, [OR] neuropathy). 
Prior monoclonal antibody, [OR] radioimmunoconjugate, [OR] antibody drug conjugate, [OR] phototherapy, [OR] radiotherapy, [OR] chemotherapy, [OR] immunotherapy, [OR] immunosuppressive therapy, [OR] or any test agent within 3 weeks [OR] or for alemtuzumab 8 weeks of Day 1. 
For CTCL: (TSEBT) within 12 weeks, [OR] or initiation of topical steroid, [OR] nitrogen mustard, [OR] or topical retinoid within 2 weeks. (Stable topical ≥ 4 weeks prior to Day 1 allowed). 
Known carrier [OR] or infection for HIV/ [OR] Hep B [OR] or C. HCV ab+ must be PCR-. HBV ab+ must be HBsAg- [OR] or undetectable DNA 
Active infection requiring systemic treatment, 
Significant GI disease, previous major gastric/ [OR] bowel surgery, [OR] difficulty swallowing [OR] or malabsorption syndrome. 
Major surgery within 4 weeks 
Previous malignancies within 2 yrs. unless relapse risk is small (< 5%). 
Current use of systemic steroids >20 mg QD prednisone (or equivalent) 
Breastfeeding [OR] or pregnant (intention to become) females [OR] or participation in other clinical trials
