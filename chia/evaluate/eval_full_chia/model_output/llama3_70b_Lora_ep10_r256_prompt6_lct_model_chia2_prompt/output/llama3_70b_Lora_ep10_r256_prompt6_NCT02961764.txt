Inclusion Criteria:
Presents to the Emergency Department (ED) and meets the clinical definition for Acute Bacterial Skin and Skin Structure Infections (ABSSSI)
Known or suspected gram-positive infection.

Exclusion Criteria:
Known or suspected gram-negative infections, [OR] anaerobic infections, [OR] or fungemia
Known or suspected infections that are severe, life threatening [OR] or are [NOT] not included in the ABSSSI Food and Drug Administration (FDA) guidance
Injection drug users [AND] with a fever
Severe neurological disorder leading to immobility [OR] or confined to a wheelchair
Bilateral Lower extremity involvement of the suspected infection.
