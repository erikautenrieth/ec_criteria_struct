Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with de novo stenotic lesions [AND] who are suitable for coronary stenting [AND] with drug-eluting stent

Exclusion Criteria:
1. High risk profiles for ischemic adverse events such as A. ST-segment elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI) B. Patients with cardiogenic shock [OR] or concomitant severe decompensated heart failure C. Myocardial infarction [OR] or stent thrombosis in spite of the maintenance of antiplatelet therapy D. Restenosis in stented segments [OR] or previous sites of balloon angioplasty 2. Patients who [NOT] cannot follow allocated DAPT schedule due to the planned surgery [OR] or elective procedure within 3 months after the stenting 3. Recent history of major surgery [OR] or evident events of gastrointestinal bleeding within 1 month from the procedure 4. Patients on anticoagulation therapy [AND] with warfarin [OR] or other anticoagulants 5. Life expectancy less than 1 year (such as malignancies [OR] or other chronic systemic diseases) 6. Pregnant women 7. Past history of allergy [OR] or other contraindications for the following medications/materials: aspirin, [OR] clopidogrel, [OR] heparin, [OR] cobalt chromium, [OR] sirolimus
