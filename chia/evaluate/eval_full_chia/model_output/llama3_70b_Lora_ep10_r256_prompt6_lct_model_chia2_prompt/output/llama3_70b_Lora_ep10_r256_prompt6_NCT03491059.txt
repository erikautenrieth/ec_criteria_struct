Inclusion Criteria:
males [OR] and females greater than or equal to 18 years of age
current regular user of e-cigarettes (use at least once daily for the past 30 days) [AND] with nicotine strength > 6mg/ml
health medical history
abstinent from any tobacco/ [OR] nicotine use for 4 hours prior to imaging

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] not a regular user of e-cigarettes
pregnant [OR] or lactating (only excluded from imaging study)
prisoner
incapable of giving informed consent
 [NOT] unable to lie flat on the scanner for extended periods of time
unstable medical condition like heart disease, uncontrolled hypertension, [OR] thyroid disease, [OR] diabetes, [OR] renal [OR] or liver impairment, [OR] or glaucoma
prostatic hypertrophy, [OR] stroke, [OR] or ulcer in past year
psychiatric conditions such as schizophrenia, [OR] adult ADHD, [OR] or bipolar disorder
current [OR] or regular use of psychiatric medications such as tranquilizers, [OR] antipsychotics, [OR] and/or antidepressants
use of medications that are inducers of CYP2A6 (a nicotine metabolizing enzyme) such as rifampicin, [OR] dexamethasone, [OR] phenobarbital, [OR] and other anti-convulsant drugs
 [NOT] unable to communicate in English
current use of smokeless tobacco, [OR] tobacco cigarettes (5 and fewer a day)
occasional use of pipes is permitted if subject abstains for the week prior to the study
older than 80 years
