Inclusion Criteria:
Supracondylar fracture
Age 2-17 years
American Society of Anesthesiologists Status 1 -3
Scheduled for closed reduction [AND] with percutaneous pinning under general anesthesia

Exclusion Criteria:
Pulseless extremity
Compromised neurologic status on exam (specifically assessment of radial, [OR] ulnar, [OR] and median nerve)
Known allergy to local anesthetics (7) [NOT] Not scheduled for closed reduction [AND] with percutaneous pinning under general anesthesia
Bleeding diathesis
American Society of Anesthesiologist (ASA) status 4 or higher.
Sleep apnea by polysomnography
