Inclusion Criteria:
Any patient age 4-16 years [AND] with sickle cell disease [AND] who presents the Pediatric ER [AND] with acute sickle cell pain crisis [AND] with a pain of 6/10 or higher

Exclusion Criteria:
Patient with fever (38C [OR] or 100.4F)
Patient less than age 4 years
 Patient greater than age 16 years
 Patient with hypersensitivity/ [OR] allergy to either morphine, [OR] NSAIDs, [OR] or acetaminophen
 Patient received acetaminophen within the past 4 hours
 Patient with known liver disease [OR] or renal disease
 Patient [NOT] not requiring IV morphine (pain score 5/10 or less)
 Patient enrolled in the study within the past 72 hours
