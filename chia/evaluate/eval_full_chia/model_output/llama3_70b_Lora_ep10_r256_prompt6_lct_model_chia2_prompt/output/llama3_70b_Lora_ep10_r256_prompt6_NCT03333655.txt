Inclusion Criteria:
Response assessment of complete response (CR), partial response (PR), long stable disease (SD) for >3 months with a cancer immunotherapy treatment [AND] for metastatic cancer [OR] or hematologic malignancies either through a marketed CPI [OR] or through participation in a Roche/Genentech CPI clinical trial.
Availability of tumor biopsy material extracted and preserved by the investigating site.

Exclusion Criteria:
Participants taking CPI combination therapies [AND] with chemotherapy are [NOT] not permitted.
Pregnant, [OR] lactating, [OR] or intending to become pregnant during the study.
