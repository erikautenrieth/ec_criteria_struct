Inclusion Criteria:
Between the ages of 21-60
Right-handed
Capable of giving informed consent and complying with study procedures
Reports drinking a minimum of 5 standard drinks for men [OR] or 4 standard drinks for women on at least 4 days per week on average over the past 28 days
Meets DSM-V criteria for current Alcohol Use Disorder
Seeking treatment for Alcohol Use Disorder
Agree to [NOT] not seek additional treatment, apart from Alcoholics Anonymous
Willing to attempt to [NOT] abstain from alcohol completely for the duration of the study
Willing to be hospitalized on a research unit for 24 hours, longer if detoxification is needed.

Exclusion Criteria:
Risk of severe alcohol withdrawal (e.g. history of seizures [OR] or delirium tremens)
 Current Moderate [OR] or Severe Substance Use Disorder, other than Alcohol, [OR] Nicotine [OR] or Caffeine Use Disorders
Lifetime history of Bipolar Disorder, [OR] Schizophrenia [OR] or Schizoaffective Disorder
 Any current psychiatric disorder, other than Alcohol Use Disorder, that, in the judgment of the investigator, will require treatment that will interfere with study participation.
Current severe depression (HAM-D >24) [OR] or anxiety (HAM-A >24)
Significant suicide [OR] or violence risk
 Currently taking any psychotropic medications
 Legally mandated to participate in treatment
 History of prior treatment with disulfiram
 Sufficiently socially unstable as to [NOT] preclude participation (e.g. homeless)
 Contraindications to disulfiram treatment (liver disease, [OR] kidney disease, [OR] cardiac disease, [OR] seizure disorder, [OR] hypothyroidism, [OR] diabetes mellitus, [OR] pregnancy [OR] or lactation, allergy to disulfiram [OR] or thiuran derivatives)
 Neurological [OR] or medical conditions that would interfere with MRI scanning (e.g. history of stroke, [OR] seizure, [OR] brain tumor, [OR] brain infection, [OR] traumatic brain injury, [OR] multiple sclerosis, [OR] dementia, [OR] metal device in body, [OR] pregnancy, [OR] claustrophobia, [OR] color blindness, severe hearing impairment, [OR] weight>300 lbs., [OR] wheelchair-bound)
 Currently taking medications containing alcohol, [OR] metronidazole, [OR] isoniazid, [OR] paraldehyde, [OR] phenytoin, [OR] warfarin, [OR] or theophylline.
 Significant alcohol withdrawal (CIWA>8) at screening, after confirming a blood alcohol level of zero.
