Inclusion Criteria:
Patient has been fully informed and has signed an IRB approved informed consent form within 7 days (Day 7-13) prior to POD 15 [AND] and is willing and able to follow study procedure
Patient is a primary liver transplant recipient
Patient is 20 to 70 years of age
Patient should be clearly conscious, fully understand and able to answer questionnaire

Exclusion Criteria:
Patient has previously received [OR] or is receiving an organ transplant other than a liver.
Patient currently requires dialysis
Recipient [OR] or donor is known to be seropositive for human immunodeficiency virus (HIV)
Patient has received a liver transplant from a [NOT] non-heart beating donor
Patient who is HCV negative has received an HCV positive (HCV RNA by PCR [OR] or HCV antibody) donor liver
Patient who is HbsAg negative has received an HbsAg positive (HBV DNA by PCR [OR] or HBV antibody) donor liver
Patient has received a liver transplant from a decrease donor > 70 years of age
Patient has a current malignancy [OR] or a history of malignancy (within the past 5 years), except hepatocellular carcinoma within UCSF criteria [OR] and basal [OR] or [NOT] non-metastatic squamous cell carcinoma of skin that has been treated successfully.
Patient is hemodynamically unstable on POD 15
