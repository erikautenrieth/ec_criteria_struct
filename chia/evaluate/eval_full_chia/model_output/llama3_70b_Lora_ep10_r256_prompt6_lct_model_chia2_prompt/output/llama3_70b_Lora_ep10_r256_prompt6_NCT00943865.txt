Inclusion Criteria:
men [OR] and women 30-55 years [AND] with BMI 30-40 [AND] and waist 95 cm or more 
normal OGTT 
normal treadmill stress test 
plus 2 of 4: 
1. low serum levels of HDL cholesterol (<40 mg⁄dL for men [OR] or < 50 mg ⁄dL for women); 
2. hypertriglyceridemia (triglyceride levels of 150 mg⁄dL or greater); 
3. impaired glucose homeostasis (fasting plasma glucose concentration of 110 mg⁄dL or greater [OR] or glucose of 140 mg⁄dL or greater after OGTT [OR] or 
4. hypertension (systolic blood pressure ≥ 140 [OR] or diastolic blood pressure ≥90 mmHg [OR] or treatment with antihypertensive drugs). 

Exclusion Criteria:
diabetes 
ischemic heart disease [OR] or any abnormality on treadmill stress test 
inflammatory [OR] or chronic disorder 
pregnancy 
lactation 
creatinine level of 1,5 mg/dL or more 
gastrointestinal problems [OR] or musculoskeletal disorders that would [NOT] prevent them to follow the test diets [OR] or exercise interventions 
liver dysfunction [AND] with a factor of at least 3 above the upper limit of normal in AST [OR] and ALT levels 
thyroid dysfunction, [AND] with serum TSH out of normal limits 
use of immunosuppressive drugs, [OR] corticosteroids [OR] or anorexigen
