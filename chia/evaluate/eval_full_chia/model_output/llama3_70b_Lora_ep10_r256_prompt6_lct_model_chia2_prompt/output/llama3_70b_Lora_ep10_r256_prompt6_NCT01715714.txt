Inclusion Criteria:
Patients on chronic statin treatment (>30 days) scheduled for isolated CABG, including on- or off-pump [OR] or repeat (redo's) revascularisation procedures
Stable [OR] or unstable angina, including [NOT] non ST-segment-elevation acute coronary syndrome (NSTE-ACS)
Age = 18 years
Written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Any concomitant cardiovascular procedure to CABG (i.e. valve, [OR] aortic [OR] or carotid surgery)
Acute ST-segment-elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI)
 NSTE-ACS [AND] with cardiogenic shock warranting emergent salvage surgery within 12 hrs from hospital admission
History of atrial fibrillation [OR] or muscle disease (myopathy)
Current renal (creatinine>2x upper limit of normal (ULN), dialysis, kidney transplant) [OR] or hepatic dysfunction (AST/ [OR] ALT>2x ULN, liver transplant [OR] or neoplasm)
 [NOT] Inability of oral drug intake
