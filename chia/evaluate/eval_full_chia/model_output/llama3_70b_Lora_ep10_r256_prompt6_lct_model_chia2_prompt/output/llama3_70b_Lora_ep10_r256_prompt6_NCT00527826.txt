Inclusion Criteria:
Subject must have a diagnosis of COPD based on the American Thoracic Society (ATS)/ European Respiratory Society (ERS) criteria. 
Male [OR] or female subjects, aged >=40 years. Females must be of [NOT] Non Child Bearing Potential. The definition of [NOT] Non Child Bearing Potential is as following: Females, regardless of their age, [AND] with functioning ovaries [AND] and who have a current documented tubal ligation [OR] or hysterectomy, [OR] or females [AND] who are post-menopausal. 
Have diagnosed COPD stage III [OR] or IV according to GOLD criteria: a baseline post-bronchodilator Forced Expiratory Volume, measured at 1 second (FEV1) <50% of predicted normal [AND] and a baseline post- bronchodilator FEV1/ [OR] Inspiratory Vital Capacity (IVC) ratio <70%. 
Have experienced at least 2 moderate [OR] or severe COPD exacerbations leading to medical consultation (requiring oral corticosteroids [OR] or increasing dosage of oral corticosteroids [OR] and/or antibiotics [OR] or hospitalization) within the 12 months preceding Visit 1. 
Have stable COPD medication within 4 weeks prior to Visit 1 ([NOT] no new medication added [AND] and [NOT] no dosage changes in medication). 
Current [OR] or ex-smokers [AND] with a smoking history of at least 10 pack years (number of pack years = [number of cigarettes per day / 20] x number of years smoked, e.g., 20 cigarettes per day for 10 years, [OR] or 10 cigarettes per day for 20 years). 
Are currently managed at home (outpatients), [AND] are ambulatory [AND] and able to travel to the clinic. Subjects can be treated with all relevant COPD medication. This includes vaccines, [OR] inhaled short-acting beta-2-agonists [AND] as needed, short-acting [OR] or long-acting anticholinergics (tiotropium), systemic beta-2-agonists, [OR] theophylline, [OR] mucolytics, [OR] antioxidants, [OR] beta-1-agonists (for cardiovascular indication), [NOT] non-invasive ventilation, [OR] long term oxygen therapy [AND] and can have Cor Pulmonale. 
A signed and dated written informed consent is obtained prior to participation. 
Able to comply with the requirements of the protocol and be available for study visits over 52 weeks. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Known other respiratory disorders [OR] or signs for other respiratory disorders (e.g. asthma, [OR] lung cancer, [OR] sarcoidosis, [OR] tuberculosis, [OR] lung fibrosis, [OR] cystic fibrosis, [OR] bronchoectasis). 
Known history of significant inflammatory disease, other than COPD (e.g. rheumatoid arthritis [OR] and systemic lupus erythematosus). 
 Known to be severely alpha-1-antitrypsin deficient (PI SZ [OR] or ZZ) 
 Having undergone lung surgery (e.g. lung resection including lung volume reduction surgery, [OR] lung transplant) [OR] or subjects scheduled for surgery. 
Concurrent medication from Visit 1 and for the duration of the study with any of the prohibited medications: monoamine oxidase inhibitors [OR] and tricyclic antidepressants, [OR] and ritonavir (a highly potent cytochrome P450 3A4 inhibitor). 
Subjects receiving chronic [OR] or prophylactic antibiotic therapy. 
Serious, uncontrolled disease (including serious psychological disorders) likely to interfere with the study [OR] or impact on subject safety. 
Have, in the opinion of the investigator, evidence of alcohol, [OR] drug [OR] or solvent abuse. 
History of depression. 
History [OR] or presence of clinically significant drug sensitivity [OR] or clinically significant allergic reaction to corticosteroids [OR] or salmeterol. 
Moderate [OR] or severe COPD exacerbation (requiring corticosteroids [OR] or increased dosage of corticosteroids [OR] and/or antibiotics [OR] or hospitalization) within the 4 weeks prior to Visit 1 
Lower respiratory tract infection within the 4 weeks prior to Visit 1. 
Pregnant [OR] or lactating female [OR] and female of childbearing potential.
