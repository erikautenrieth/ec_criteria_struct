Inclusion Criteria:
Type 2 diabetic patients
Age = 50
Glycemic control: HbA1c = 10.0%
10 = Beck Depression Inventory (BDI) <30 points
Participants who can undergo contraception in case of being in childbearing period
Understands the study procedure, alternatives, and risks and voluntarily agrees to participate by giving written informed concent

Exclusion Criteria:
Type 1 diabetes, [OR] Secondary diabetes, [OR] gestational diabetes
Ongoing dementia treatment [OR] or anti-depressive disorder medication
Uncontrolled psychiatric disorder
BDI = 30 points
Heavy alcoholics
Underlying chronic liver disease (hemochromatosis, [OR] liver cell carcinoma, [OR] autoimmune liver disease, [OR] liver cirrhosis, chronic viral hepatitis)
Allergy [OR] or hypersensitivity to target medication [OR] or any of its components
Renal failure, [OR] moderate [OR] or severe renal impairment (estimated glomerular filtration rate < 30 mL/min/1.73 m2), [OR] or ongoing dialysis
Abnormal liver function (AST/ [OR] ALT > x3 upper normal limit)
History of alcohol [OR] or drug abuse in the previous 3 months
Premenopausal women [AND] who are nursing [OR] or pregnant
Human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) [OR] or human immunodeficiency virus (AIDS)
chronic pancreatitis [OR] or pancreatic cancer
