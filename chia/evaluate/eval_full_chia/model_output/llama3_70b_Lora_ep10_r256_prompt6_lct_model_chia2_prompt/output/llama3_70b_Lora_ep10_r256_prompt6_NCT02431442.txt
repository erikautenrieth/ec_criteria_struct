Inclusion Criteria:

Healthy obese male [OR] and female volunteers aged 18 to 55 years, inclusive. Heterozygous subjects may be 18 to 65 years inclusive. 
In good general health, [NOT] without significant medical history, [OR] physical examination findings, [OR] or clinical laboratory abnormalities. 
Body Mass Index of 30-40 kg/m2, inclusive. Heterozygous subjects may have a broader BMI range; to be eligible heterozygous subjects may have a BMI 27 -55 kg/ m2, inclusive. 
Stable body weight during the previous 6 months, based on Investigator judgment. 
Blood pressure <140/90 mmHg at Screening [AND] and D-1. Measurement may be repeated within 24 hours, based on Investigator judgment.

Females [AND] of [NOT] non-childbearing potential, defined as surgically sterile (status post hysterectomy, [OR] bilateral oophorectomy, [OR] or bilateral tubal ligation) [OR] or post-menopausal [AND] for at least 12 months (and confirmed with a screening FSH level in the post-menopausal range), do [NOT] not require contraception during the study. 
Males [AND] with female partners [AND] of childbearing potential must agree to use two medically acceptable forms of contraception as described above, with one of the two forms being condom [AND] with spermicide, from the Screening Period through the Final Study Visit. Males [AND] with female partners [AND] of childbearing potential [AND] who themselves are surgically sterile (status post vasectomy) must agree to use condoms [AND] with spermicide over the same period of time. Male subjects must agree to practice the above birth control methods for 30 days from the final visit as a safety precaution.
