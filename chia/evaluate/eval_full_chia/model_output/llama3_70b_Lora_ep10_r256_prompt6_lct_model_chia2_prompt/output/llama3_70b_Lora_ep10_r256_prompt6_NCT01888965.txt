Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with a confirmed diagnosis of: 
1. Stage 4 colon cancer either s/p metastasectomy [OR] or post-initial chemotherapy [OR] or maintenance "standard of care", either involving 5-fluorouracil/ [OR] leucovorin (5-FU/ [OR] LV) alone [OR] or continual bevacizumab alone. Patients in maintenance cohort must have had 2 consecutive CT scans showing stable disease [AND] and [NOT] not be experiencing significant prior treatment-related toxicity above Grade 1. 
2. Pancreas cancer, either s/p resection [OR] and adjuvant chemotherapy [OR] or locally advanced pancreas cancer s/p chemotherapy [OR] and radiation. Initial chemotherapy [OR] or radiation therapy may have been stopped between 2 weeks and 2 months prior to study start, and patients must have recovered from prior treatment related toxicity to grade 1 or less. 
Prior surgery, including tumor resection [OR] or metastasectomy must have been performed at least 4 weeks prior to study enrollment. 
 [NOT] No concomitant anti-cancer treatment is allowed 
Age >/= 18 years 
Performance status of 0-1 
Adequate hepatic, [OR] bone marrow, [OR] and renal function 
Partial thromboplastin time (PTT) must be </= 1.5 x upper normal limit of institution's normal range and INR (International Normalized Ratio) < 1.5. 
Life expectancy >/= 4 months for maintenance cohorts [AND] and >/= 6 months for adjuvant cohorts 
Women [AND] of childbearing potential must have a negative serum pregnancy test within 14 days prior to initiation of treatment [AND] and must [NOT] not be lactating. 
Subject is capable of understanding and complying with protocol demands and able to sign and date the informed consent 

Exclusion Criteria:
Women [AND] of child-bearing potential, [AND] who are biologically able to conceive, [NOT] not employing two forms of highly effective contraception [OR] or who are pregnant. 
Women [AND] who are breast-feeding 
Fertile males unwilling to use contraception 
Patients with brain metastases [OR] or any history of brain metastases 
Patients who have undergone major surgery (e.g., intra-thoracic, [OR] -abdominal, [OR] or -pelvic) </= 4 weeks prior to starting study treatment [OR] or who have [NOT] not recovered from such therapy 
Patients with a history of pulmonary embolism, [OR] or [NOT] untreated deep vein thrombosis within the past 6 months 
Impairment of gastrointestinal (GI) function [OR] or GI disease that may significantly alter the absorption of dovitinib 
The subject has had another active malignancy within the past 5 years except for cervical cancer [OR] in situ, [OR] in situ carcinoma of the bladder [OR] or [NOT] non-melanoma carcinoma of the skin. 
Patients who have received the last administration of an anticancer therapy including chemotherapy, [OR] immunotherapy, [OR] hormonal therapy [OR] and monoclonal antibodies </= 2 weeks prior to starting the study drug, [OR] or who have [NOT] not recovered from the side effects of such therapy 
Cirrhosis, chronic active hepatitis [OR] or chronic persistent hepatitis 
Patients who are currently receiving prasugrel 
 [NOT] No concurrent use of isoniazid, [OR] labetolol, [OR] trovafloxacin, [OR] tolcapone, [OR] and felbamate 
 [NOT] No concurrent use of other investigational drugs [OR] or antineoplastic therapies. 
Patients with impaired cardiac function [OR] or clinically significant cardiac diseases.
