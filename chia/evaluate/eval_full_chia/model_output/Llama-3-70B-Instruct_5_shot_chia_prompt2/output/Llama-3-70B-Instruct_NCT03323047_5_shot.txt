Inclusion Criteria:
Healthy patients
aged 3-13 years
Level I [OR] level II on the American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) physical status classification system (as determined by the anesthesiologist)
obstructive sleep apnea [OR] recurrent throat infections
undergoing elective tonsillectomy [AND] with or without adenoidectomy
Parents who agree [AND] to complete documentation [AND] follow up at 14 days post-operation.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients Level III [OR] greater on the American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) physical status classification system (as determined by the anesthesiologist)
Patients with chronic conditions [AND] that would limit our ability to develop the study according to objectives, such as neurodevelopmental conditions [AND] preventing patients from understanding the Oucher tool
Hepatic [OR] renal disease
cardiac disease
active infection
diabetes mellitus
sickle cell disease
known coagulation disorders
pre- operative treatment [AND] with anti-emetics [OR], steroids [OR], or analgesics
Acetaminophen allergy [OR] already receiving acetaminophen within 24 h of surgery
Complicating health factors [AND] precluding the use of opioids [OR] acetaminophen
any other factors [AND] which would interfere with pain assessment [AND] management
Patients weighing more than 30 kg [AND] that would exceed maximum dexamethasone dose
Patients who live [NOT] without a home telephone
patient living [NOT] without parental supervision.
