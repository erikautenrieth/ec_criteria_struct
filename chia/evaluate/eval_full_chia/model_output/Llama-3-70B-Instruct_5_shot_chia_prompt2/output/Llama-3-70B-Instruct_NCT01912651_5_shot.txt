Inclusion Criteria:
all adult patients with a nasal [OR] or facial skin/soft tissue defect requiring reconstruction limited to [OR] including a full-thickness skin graft

Exclusion Criteria:
current [OR] recent (within one week of surgery) systemic antibiotic use, 
intolerance [AND] to both clindamycin [AND] cephalexin, 
discovery of a persistent cutaneous malignancy at the site of the defect following the reconstructive procedure 
[AND] previous reconstruction at the site of the skin/soft-tissue defect.
