Inclusion Criteria:
Age [AND] between 20 and 40
Normal menstrual cycles: 25-34 days
Oligomenorrhea/amenorrhea [OR] or polycystic syndrome (defined according to the Rotterdam criteria 2004)
BMI >18 [AND] <35 kg/m2

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with >14 follicles [AND] on day of trigger
Previous hyperresponse [AND] with OHSS development
Previous low response ([NOT] more than 3 oocytes) on a high dose of FSH stimulation
Endocrine disorders
