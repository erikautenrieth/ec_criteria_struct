Inclusion Criteria:
Estimated glomerular filtration rate [AND] =20 mL/min [AND] <60 mL/min
Hgb [AND] =8.5 g/dL [AND] =11.5 g/dL
Serum ferritin [AND] =500 ng/mL [AND] transferrin saturation (TSAT) [AND] =25%
Serum intact parathyroid hormone [AND] =600 pg/mL

Exclusion Criteria:
Serum phosphate <3.0 mg/dL
Intravenous (IV) iron [AND] administered within 4 weeks prior to Screening
Erythropoiesis-stimulating agents (ESA) [AND] administered within 4 weeks prior to Screening
Blood transfusion [AND] within 4 weeks prior to Screening
