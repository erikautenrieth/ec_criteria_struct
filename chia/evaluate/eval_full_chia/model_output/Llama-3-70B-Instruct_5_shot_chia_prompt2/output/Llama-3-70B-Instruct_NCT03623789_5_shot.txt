Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with osteoarthritis of the hip secondary to degeneration [OR], inflammatory arthritis [OR], gouty arthritis [OR], acetabular dysplasia [OR] or osteonecrosis of the femoral head, and undergoing primary unilateral minimally invasive THA
Age > 18 years [AND] < 90 years
Failure of medical treatment [OR] rehabilitation.
Hemoglobin > 11g/dl,
[NOT] No use of non-steroid anti-inflammatory agent one week before operation

Exclusion Criteria:
Preoperative Hemoglobin < 11 g/dl
History of infection [OR] intraarticular fracture of the affective hip
Renal function deficiency (GFR < 30 ml/min/1.73m2)
Elevated liver enzyme (aspartate transaminase (AST) [OR] alanine transaminase(ALT) level are more than twice normal range), history of liver cirrhosis [OR], impaired liver function(elevated total bilirubin level) [AND] coagulopathy (including long-term use anticoagulant)
History of deep vein thrombosis [OR], ischemic heart disease [OR] stroke
Contraindications of tranexamic acid [OR], floseal [OR], or rivaroxaban
Allergy to tranexamic acid [OR], floseal [OR], rivaroxaban [OR], or the excipients
History of heparin-induced thrombocytopenia (HIT)
Coagulopathy [OR] bleeding tendency caused by organ dysfunction, such as cirrhosis [OR], bone marrow suppression etc.
Patient who have active bleeding disorder, such as intracranial hemorrhage [OR], upper gastrointestinal bleeding [OR], hematuria.
Patients with known allergies to materials of bovine origin.
