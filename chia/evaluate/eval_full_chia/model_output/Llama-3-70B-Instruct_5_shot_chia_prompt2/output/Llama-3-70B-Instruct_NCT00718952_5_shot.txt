Inclusion Criteria:
Subjects aged 12-65. 
Confirmed idiopathic pulmonary hypertension [OR] connective tissue disease associated pulmonary hypertension [OR] congenital heart disease(with Eisenmenger syndrome) associated pulmonary hypertension. 
Baseline 6-minutes walking distance 150m-550m. 
WHO pulmonary hypertension function II-III [AND] with non-responder to calcium channel blockers. 
Documented written informed consent. 

Exclusion Criteria:
The other types of pulmonary hypertension. 
Subjects who refuse to subscribe written informed consents [OR] can't cooperate with the trial well. 
Subjects with serious acute [OR] chronic disease involved liver [OR], kidney [OR], and brain [OR] have to use potent CYP3A4-inhibitor [OR] nitrate to treat the underlying diseases. 
Subjects who are currently treated with sildenafil for PAH [OR] taking sildenafil [OR] tadalafil. 
Other contraindications [AND] in package insert.
