Inclusion Criteria:
Pathologic confirmation [AND] of lung adenocarcinoma with measurable disease, defined as at least one lesion that can be accurately measured in at least one dimension (longest diameter to be recorded on CT); Patients must have previously untreated locally advanced [OR] metastatic NSCLC; Patients must have lung cancer with a documented EGFR activating mutation (exon 19 deletion [OR] L858R).

Exclusion Criteria:
Prior chemotherapy
Prior treatment with gefitinib [OR] erlotinib [OR] or other drugs that target EGFR
Patients must [NOT] be receiving any other investigational agents
Any evidence of interstitial lung disease
