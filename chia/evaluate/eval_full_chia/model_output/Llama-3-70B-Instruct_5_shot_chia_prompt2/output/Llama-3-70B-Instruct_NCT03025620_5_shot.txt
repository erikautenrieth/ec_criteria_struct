Inclusion Criteria:
Elderly patients over 65 years old [AND] exhibiting clinical indices of cardiovascular disease 
Male [OR] female 
Subjects who were hospitalized [AND] in the Geriatric Unit of the Emile Roux Hospital (AP-HP) 
MMSE (Mini Mental State Examination)score > or = 15 
Supervision available [AND] for study medication 
Able to ingest [AND] oral diet 

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [NOT] able to understand the objectives of the dietary intervention 
Patients in paliative care 
Patients receiving [AND] supplement diets
