Inclusion Criteria:
age <2 years
indication of general anesthesia [AND] with tracheal intubation
inhalational induction scheduled
written informed consent [AND] of both parents

Exclusion Criteria:
contra-indication to inhalational induction (full stomach)
contra-indication [AND] to the use of rocuronium
American Society of Anesthesiologists score (ASA) III [OR] IV
intracranial surgery
parental refusal
absence [AND] of affiliation to social security
