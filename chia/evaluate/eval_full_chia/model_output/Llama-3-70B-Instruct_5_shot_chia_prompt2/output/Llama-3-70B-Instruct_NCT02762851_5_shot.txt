Inclusion Criteria:
Age = 18 years [AND] NYHA (New York Heart Association) functional class II [OR] III [OR] and IV

Exclusion Criteria:
Anaphylactic reaction [AND] to a previous dose of TIV(trivalent influenza vaccine)
Known IgE( Immunoglobulin E)-mediated hypersensitivity [AND] to eggs manifested as hives [OR], swelling of the mouth and throat [OR], difficulty in breathing [OR], hypotension [OR], or shock
Guillain-Barré syndrome [AND] within eight weeks of a previous influenza vaccine
Anaphylactic reaction [AND] to neomycin
Patients who have had influenza vaccine [AND] in two of the three previous years
