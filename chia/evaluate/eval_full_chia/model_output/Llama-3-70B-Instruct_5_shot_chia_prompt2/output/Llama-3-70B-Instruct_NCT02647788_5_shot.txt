Inclusion Criteria:
Patients undergoing ambulatory hand surgery for carpal tunnel [AND] trigger finger, under local anesthesia [AND] with or without sedation.

Exclusion Criteria:
ASA> 3;
Coagulopathy;
Renal disease [OR], Liver disease,
History of recent gastro-intestinal bleeding
Pregnancy.
Diagnosis of chronic pain [AND] currently taking opioid pain medication [OR] with a history of drug abuse.
Patients with a self-described allergy [AND] to ASA [OR], acetaminophen [OR], NSAIDS [OR] and codeine.
All patients receiving a brachial plexus block for anesthesia [AND] and/or analgesia
