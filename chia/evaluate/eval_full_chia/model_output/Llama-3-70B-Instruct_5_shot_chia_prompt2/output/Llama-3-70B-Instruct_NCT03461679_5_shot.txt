Inclusion Criteria:
Patients undergoing total knee arthroplasty [AND] under spinal anaesthesia
45y or older
ASA 1-3
BMI 18-35

Exclusion Criteria:
Unable to consent
Chronic opioid consumption
Allergy [AND] to study medication
Lower limb surgery [AND] preceding year
Unable to complete baseline testing [OR] pre-existing neurological deficit
Contraindication [AND] to spinal anaesthesia
