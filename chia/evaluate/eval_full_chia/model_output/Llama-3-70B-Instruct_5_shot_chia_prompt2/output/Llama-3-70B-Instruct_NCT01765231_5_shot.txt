Inclusion Criteria:
treatment-naive patients with lymphoma
HBsAg negative [AND] HBcAb positive [AND] hepatitis B virus DNA negative at baseline
treated with chemotherapy [OR] and/or immunosuppressive therapy
life expectancy of more than 3 months

Exclusion Criteria:
younger than 18 years old
HBsAg positive [OR] HBcAb negative [OR] hepatitis B virus DNA positive at baseline
pregnant [OR] lactating women
