Inclusion Criteria:
Normal uterine cavity
Normal Hormonal investigation: TSH [AND] PRL [AND] FBS
Frozen embryo transfer cycles: at least 2 embryos
Primary [OR] secondary infertility: tubal occlusion [OR], male factor [OR], unexplained [OR], endometriosis [OR], ovarian factors…
Body mass index (BMI) =18 [AND] =30 kg/m2

Exclusion Criteria:
Preexisting [AND] untreated medical condition (thyroid disease [OR], diabetes mellitus [OR], hypertension [OR], pulmonary conditions [OR], cardiac condition…)
History of three [OR] more consecutively failed In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) cycles after embryo transfer
History of three [OR] more miscarriages
Previous allergy reactions [AND] to progesterone products
