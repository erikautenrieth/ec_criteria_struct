Inclusion Criteria:
Patients scheduled for laser laryngeal surgery under general anesthesia with either Propofol [OR] or desflurane based technique.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with cardiac [OR], pulmonary [OR], hepatic [OR], or renal dysfunction, epilepsy [OR], or uncontrolled hypertension, or those taking medications that influence the central nervous system, are excluded from the study. Patients who show obvious alteration of mental status [OR], or refuse to participate, are also excluded from the study.
