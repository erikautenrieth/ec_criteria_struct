Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis of DSM 5 Anxiety Disorder
Stable medical history [AND] general health
On stable anti-parkinsonian therapy [AND] for 2 weeks before enrollment

Exclusion Criteria:
Unstable medical disease [OR] comorbid psychiatric disease
Dementia
Subjects with less than one year duration of Parkinson's
Current treatment [AND] with a dopamine agonist
