Inclusion Criteria:
Arrestees examined by a physician during detention in police cells
aged 18 or older
smoking at least 10 cigarettes per day
giving written consent [AND] to participate in the study
health status compatible [AND] with detention in police cells

Exclusion Criteria:
Lack of understanding of the study
contra-indication [OR] to nicotine replacement therapy
health status [NOT] compatible with detention in police cells
serious mental disorder
usual place of residence outside Seine-Saint-Denis
