Inclusion Criteria:
Women seeking medication abortion [AND] through 70 days gestation
Eligible for Mifeprex(r) [AND] at a study clinical site
English [OR] Spanish speaking
Willing [AND] able to participate in the study, [AND] including willing to go to the study pharmacy to obtain mifepristone

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Not pregnant
[NOT] Not seeking medication abortion
Under the age of 15
Contraindications [AND] for medication abortion
