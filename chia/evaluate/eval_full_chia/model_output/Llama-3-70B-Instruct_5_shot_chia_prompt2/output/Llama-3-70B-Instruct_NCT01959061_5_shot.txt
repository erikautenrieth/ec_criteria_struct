Inclusion Criteria:
Histologically [AND] confirmed colorectal adenocarcinoma
Disease limited to the liver [AND] Unresectable disease by surgery [OR] or other local therapies
Age >18 years
ECOG performance status 0-2,[AND] Child pugh A [OR] or B
Expected survival = 3 months
Adequate hematological [AND], hepatic [AND], and renal function

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant [OR] or lactating women
Patients with severe organ dysfunction [OR] or failure
With severe cardiovascular disease [OR], or mental
Extraliver metastases
