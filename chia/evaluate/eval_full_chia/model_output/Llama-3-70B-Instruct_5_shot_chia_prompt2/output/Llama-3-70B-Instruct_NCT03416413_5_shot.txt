Inclusion Criteria:
Adults over 18 years of age
Symptomatic GSV [OR] SSV vein reflux > 0.5 seconds on colour Duplex
Varicose vein tributary requiring treatment

Exclusion Criteria:
Current DVT
Recurrent varicose veins
Arterial disease (ABPI<0.8)
Vein diameter < 3mm
Preference for one of the treatment options
Patient who are unwilling [AND] to participate
Inability [OR] unwillingness to complete questionnaires
Inability [OR] to attend follow-up appointments
Patient currently included [AND] in a study of varicose vein treatment
