Inclusion Criteria:
1. Women and men ages 18 years and over.
2. Interest [AND] in participating in a novel nutritional supplement program.
3. Willingness [AND] to follow recommendations.

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Currently enrolled in another research trial for investigative nutritional [OR] other therapies thought to have an impact on immune system functioning.
2. Unable [AND] to consent to the study.
3. Women who are pregnant [OR] or are attempting conception, especially in the presence of a history of recurrent spontaneous abortion.
4. Other medical complications that might preclude one from participating in the study, i.e., recent heart attack [OR] or stroke [OR] chronic kidney disease.
5. Currently taking immunomodulatory medication, i.e. interferon.
6. Currently taking other medications thought to have an impact on immune system functioning, i.e., chemotherapeutic agents.
7. Known allergy [AND] to rice, rice bran, or related food products.
8. Known allergy [AND] to mushrooms [OR] or related food products.
9. History of malignancies related to the NK cell line, including: NK cell leukemias [AND] T-cell large granular lymphocyte leukemias, NK-cell lymphoproliferative disease of granular lymphocytes, [AND] NK cell lymphomas, e.g., nasal [AND] nasal-like NK/T-cell lymphomas.
10. Current smoker.
