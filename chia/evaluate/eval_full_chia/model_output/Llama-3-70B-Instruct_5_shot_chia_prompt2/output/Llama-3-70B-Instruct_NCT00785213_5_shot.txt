Inclusion Criteria:
Healthy adults 18-45 years of age 
Non-smoking 
Non-pregnant ([NOT] post-menopausal [OR] surgically sterile [OR] using effective contraceptive measures) 
Body mass index (BMI) less than or equal to 32 
Medically healthy [AND] on the basis of medical history [AND] physical examination 
Hemoglobin > or = to 11.5g/dL 
Completion of the screening process [AND] within 28 days prior to dosing 
Provision of voluntary written informed consent 

Exclusion Criteria:
Recent participation ([AND] within 28 days) in other research studies 
Recent significant blood donation [OR] or plasma donation 
Pregnant [OR] or lactating 
Test positive at screening for human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) [OR], hepatitis B surface antigen (HbsAg) [OR], or hepatitis C virus (HCV) 
Recent (2-year) history [OR] or evidence of alcoholism [OR] or drug abuse 
History [OR] or presence of significant cardiovascular [OR], pulmonary [OR], hepatic [OR], gallbladder or biliary tract [OR], renal [OR], hematologic [OR], gastrointestinal [OR], endocrine [OR], immunologic [OR], dermatologic [OR], neurologic [OR], or psychiatric disease 
Subjects who have used any drugs [OR] or substances known to inhibit [OR] or induce cytochrome (CYP) P450 enzymes [AND] or P-glycoprotein (P-gp) within 28 days prior to the first dose [AND] throughout the study 
Drug allergies [AND] to quinine sulfate [OR] or rosiglitazone
