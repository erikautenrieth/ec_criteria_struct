Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis of diabetes mellitus according to World Health Organization criteria ( treatment with insulin [OR] an oral hypoglycemic agent, twice random glucose measurements major than 200 mg/dl, [OR] or a fasting glucose major than 140 mg/dl)
Ulcer located on the legs [OR] feet, stage III [OR] IV (Wagner Classification System)
The subject agrees to comply with study protocol requirements [AND] all follow up visit requirements.

Exclusion Criteria:
Uncontrolled diabetes
Ulcer infection
Non-diabetic ulcers [AND] Orthopedic [OR] neuromuscular pathologic conditions
