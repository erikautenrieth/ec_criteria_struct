Inclusion Criteria:
Males [OR] females above the age of 18
Patients undergoing laparoscopic [OR] or robotic colorectal resections

Exclusion Criteria:
History of constipation
Pre-existing use of narcotics [OR] opioids
Pre-existing renal [OR] or hepatic failure
Mental illness [OR] mental retardation [OR] or inability to participate in informed consent due to mental status
Pre-existing dementia
Allergy to any protocol medication
Emergency operation
Subjects who are incarcerated [OR] or wards of the state
Minors
Subjects with inflammatory bowel disease [OR] active colitis [OR] or pre-existing intra-abdominal inflammation. Diverticulitis [NOT] without active infection/inflammation will not be excluded.
