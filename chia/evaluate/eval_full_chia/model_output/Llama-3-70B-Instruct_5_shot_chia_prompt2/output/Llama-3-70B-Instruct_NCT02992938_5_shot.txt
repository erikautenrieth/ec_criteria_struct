Inclusion Criteria:
Patients scheduled for thyroidectomy [AND] with general anesthesia in the University of Chile Clinical Hospital

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients ASA III [OR] IV
Chronic pain history
Drug [AND] alcohol abuse
Chronic use of opioid [AND] sedatives
Neuropsychiatric illness
NSAID [OR] other analgesics used the 48 hours previous to the surgery
CMI > 30
