Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis of heart failure according to Framingham criteria
Informed consent
Age 18 years or above

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with a history of an untreated malignancy (except [NOT] local skin cancers)
Ischemic stroke (determined using the Questionnaire for Verifying Stroke-Free Status (QVSFS)
Patients on renal dialysis [OR] or with end-stage hepatic dysfunction
Acute infection [OR] inflammation (Temperature > 101.5 F, [AND] and/or WBC> 15, 000)
Inability to obtain informed consent from patient [OR] next of kin
Anticoagulant use (warfarin [OR] or heparin)
