Inclusion Criteria:
Over 18 years of age; Systemically healthy [AND] Non-smoking; [AND]
With good oral hygiene; [AND]
Absent irreversible pulpal alteration; [AND]
With the presence of a non-carious cervical lesion (LCNCs) that needs to be restored. This lesion should be non-carious [AND] non-retentive, with at least 1 mm [AND] up to 3 mm depth, should involve both enamel [AND] dentin of vital teeth without mobility, [AND] present hypersensitivity;
Presence a natural tooth of the same position of the restored tooth, but in the opposite arch of the same jaw to be considered for the positive control;
Periodontal parameters : Depth Probing (PS), Visible Plaque Index (IPV), Gingival Index (GI) [AND] Probing Bleed Index (SS). The normal included were: PS = 1 to 3 mm [AND] GI = 0 [AND] IPV = score 0 [AND] e SS = score 0.

Exclusion Criteria:
With severe systemic alteration;
In the use of antibiotics [OR] anti-inflammatories in the last three months;
With periodontium with periodontal parameters different from those established in the inclusion criteria.
Individuals with clinical signs of parafunctional habits;
Smoking;
Individuals who have performed other restorations in the last 12 months;
Pregnant women [OR] infants;
Periodontal sites that presented bleeding during crevicular fluid collection [OR] sites that prevent proper collection of clinical parameters.
