<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Generally healthy, postmenopausal woman [AND] who seeks treatment for hot flushes. 
Meets 1 of the following: At least 12 months of spontaneous amenorrhea; At least 6 months of spontaneous amenorrhea [AND] with serum follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) levels > 40 mIU/mL; At least 6 weeks postsurgical bilateral oophorectomy ([OR] with [OR] without hysterectomy). Hysterectomized [AND] without bilateral oophorectomy [AND] with serum FSH levels >40 mIU/mL. 

Exclusion Criteria:
History of a seizure disorder [AND] other than a single childhood febrile seizure. 
History [AND] or presence of clinically important hepatic [OR] or renal disease [OR] or other medical disease. 
Presence [OR] or recent history of major depressive disorder [OR], bipolar disorder [OR], psychotic disorder [OR], or generalized anxiety disorder requiring therapy.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
