Inclusion Criteria:
Recently diagnosed type 2 diabetic patients.
Fasting plasma glucose [AND] between 200-300 mg/dl [AND] (A1C level [AND] between 7% [AND] 10%).
Those who age [AND] between 30 [AND] 80 years old [AND] can inject insulin by themselves.

Exclusion Criteria:
Previous treated [AND] with anti-diabetic medication
Pregnant [OR] nursing women.
Impaired liver function (ALT > 120 U/L)
Impaired renal function (Serum creatinine >1.5 mg/dL [AND] in male, >1.4 mg/dL [AND] in female )
Recently suffered [OR] from MI [OR] CVA.
Patients are acute intercurrent illness.
2-hour C-peptide level < 1.8 ng/mL.
