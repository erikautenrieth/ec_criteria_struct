Inclusion Criteria:
Patients over the age of 18 years who are able to give their informed consent
Lobar [AND] or sublobar resections
Open [OR], video-assisted thoracoscopic [OR] or robotic surgeries
Diagnostic [OR] or therapeutic procedures

Exclusion Criteria:
Traumatic pulmonary contusion [OR] or laceration
Lung reduction surgery
Planned removal of more than 10 lung lesions
Pneumonectomy
Known hypersensitivity [AND] to bovine protein
Known hypersensitivity [AND] to Brilliant Blue FCF (E133)
Presence of active infection
