Inclusion Criteria:
18 years or older
Type 1 [OR] 2 diabetes
PDR patients requiring surgical intervention [AND] for complications of vitreous hemorrhage [OR] or traction retinal detachment [AND] pre-operative IVC treatment.
women postmenopausal [AND] for 12 months before the study, [OR] surgically sterile, [OR] not pregnant [AND] on effective contraception.

Exclusion Criteria:
previous retinal vein occlusion.
any intraocular surgery [AND] within the previous 12 months.
myopia of > or = to 8 diopters.
active ocular [OR] periocular infection
treatment with an investigational agent [AND] for any condition 60 days prior to enrollment.
evidence of severe cardiac disease.
clinically significant peripheral vascular disease ([NOT] previous surgery, [NOT] amputation, [OR] symptoms of claudication)
uncontrolled hypertension ([NOT] treated systolic blood pressure > 155 mmHg [OR] diastolic blood pressure > 95 mmHg)
stroke within the preceding 12 months.
