Inclusion Criteria:
Patients undergoing surgeries in the upper limb (arm, forearm [OR] or hand)

Exclusion Criteria:
Coagulopathies (with prothrombin concentration less than 60% [OR] INR more than 1.5)
In-ability [AND] to postpone anti-coagulation medications.
Infection [OR] or injury [OR] or a lesion at the block site.
Suspected cervical vertebral column injury necessitating using a neck collar.
A compromised lung on the contralateral side of the block (Pneumothorax [OR] hemothorax [OR] Pneumonectomy).
Traumatic vascular injuries [OR] operative interventions (Surgical harvesting) involving arteries of the upper limb on the operative side.
Patients with communication difficulties.
Hypersensitivity [AND] to local anesthetics [AND] and/or Dexamethasone.
Patients on perioperative intravenous (IV) steroids.
