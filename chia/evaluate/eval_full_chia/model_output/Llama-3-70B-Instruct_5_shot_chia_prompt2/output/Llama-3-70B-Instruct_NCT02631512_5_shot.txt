Inclusion Criteria:
Type I [OR] II diabetes mellitus.
Target ulcer area [AND] between 0.5 and 5 sqcm, [AND] and more than 4 weeks old.
Ankle-brachial pressure index [AND] above 0.7.

Exclusion Criteria:
Ulcers due to non-diabetic etiology.
Uncontrolled diabetes defined as HbA1c [AND] above 70 mmol/mol [AND] insufficient nutritional status.
Ulcers older than 1 year.
Any of gangrene [OR], osteomyelitis [OR], cellulitis [OR], or Charcot osteoarthropathy.
