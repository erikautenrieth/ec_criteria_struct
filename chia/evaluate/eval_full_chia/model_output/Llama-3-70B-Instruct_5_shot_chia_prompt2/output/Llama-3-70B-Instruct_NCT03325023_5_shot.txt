Inclusion Criteria:
Written consent [AND] for participation in the clinical trial
Age 18 to 45 years [AND] Irregular menstruation (> 35 days) [OR] or secondary amenorrhea> 3 months

Exclusion Criteria:
Ovarian cancer [OR] adrenal gland tumor [OR] endometrial cancer [OR] cervical cancer [OR] breast cancer
Congenital adrenal hyperplasia (17-OH-progesterone> 2.5 ng / mL)
Clinically diagnosed Cushing's disease [OR] acromegaly [OR] gigantism
Type I [OR] II diabetes
Unexplained bleeding [AND] from the genital tract
Hormone treatment [NOT] within the last 2 months
