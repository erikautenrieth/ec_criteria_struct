Inclusion Criteria:
Any patients that will be submitted to phacoemulsification surgery [AND] in the Hospital de Clinicas of State University of Campinas (BRAZIL)
Patients over 18 years old
Patients who are able to perform SD-OCT
Patients who sign the consent form
Exclusion Criteria:
Diabetic patients
Patients with any macular changes prior to the surgery (epiretinal membranes [OR] age macular disease [OR] macular edema...)
Patients who had any complication during phacoemulsification surgery
