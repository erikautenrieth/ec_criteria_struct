Inclusion Criteria:
Adult patients
Kidney transplant recipients
Patients treated by a calcineurin inhibitor [AND] mycophenolic acid
Viremia >= 3 log UI/ml
Patients who have given written informed consent
Negative pregnancy test (blood ß-HCG dosage)

Exclusion Criteria:
Known [AND] proved BKV nephropathy
Hypersensitivity to everolimus [OR], sirolimus [OR] or excipient
Concomitant treatment by leflunomide [OR], cidofovir [OR], sirolimus [OR], Millepertuis (Hypericum Perforatum)
Pregnant [OR] or lactating women
Women of child bearing potential [NOT] unless they are using a birth control method
