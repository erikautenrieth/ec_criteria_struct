Inclusion Criteria:
Term singleton infants (>37 weeks gestational age)
Birth weight > 2500g
Healthy [AND] at inclusion
Breastfed exclusively [OR] predominantly (>50% meals) at inclusion
[NOT] No previous iron supplementation
[NOT] No previous blood transfusion
Informed consent given

Exclusion Criteria:
preterm delivery (<37 weeks of gestation)
birth weight < 2500 g
multiple pregnancy
major illness [OR] congenital anomaly
being <50% breastfed at the time of inclusion
food allergy
anaemia (Hb <105 g/L [10.5 g/dL]) at inclusion
lack of informed consent
