Inclusion Criteria:
age [AND] over 40
composite head [AND] neck tumor resection
treated hypertension
hypertension medications taken [AND] on morning of surgery (except diuretics)

Exclusion Criteria:
patient refusal
age [NOT] less than 40 [OR] over 80 years
combined surgical procedures
emergency surgery
Left ventricular ejection fraction [NOT] less than 50 per cent
calculated creatinine clearance [NOT] less than 60 mL per minute
