Inclusion Criteria:
Have had one prior platinum-based chemotherapy regimen [AND] for the treatment of primary disease. 
At least 4 weeks since last surgery [OR] radiation therapy. 
Must have had a treatment-free interval [AND] of greater than 6 months following response to platinum. 
ECOG performance status [AND] of 0,1, [OR] or 2. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Women of child-bearing potential that [NOT] do not practice adequate contraception. 
Pregnant [OR] or lactating. 
Received more than one primary chemotherapy regimen.
Concomitant [OR] or previous malignancies [NOT] with the exception of adequately treated basal cell [OR] or squamous cell skin cancer, in situ cervical cancer, incidental carcinoid, [OR] or other cancer from which the patient has been disease free for 5 years. 
Active uncontrolled infection [AND] requiring antibiotics. 
Concurrent severe medical problems [AND] unrelated to the malignancy which would limit full compliance with the study. 
Received radiation [AND] to more than 10% of bone. 
Prior treatment [AND] with topotecan [OR] or gemcitabine. 
Hypersensitivity [AND] to camptothecin [OR] or nucleoside analogues. 
Use of an investigational agent [AND] within 30 days.
