Inclusion Criteria:
Subjects undergoing burn excision surgery for standard of care purposes
Male [OR] female >= 18 years of age
Subject [OR] subject's medical decision maker agrees to participate in this study [AND] provides informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects with a history of hypercoagulopathy [OR], deep vein thrombosis (DVT) [OR], pulmonary embolism
Renal impairment
Subjects with known hypersensitivity [AND] to tranexamic acid
Consecutive fibrinolytic states [AND] to coagulopathy
History of convulsions
