Inclusion Criteria:
Chronic hepatitis B (HBsAg positive > 6 months)
HBeAg negative [AND] within six months prior to initiation of peginterferon alfa-2a
HBV DNA < 200 IU/ml [AND] during nucleos(t)ide analogue (except Telbivudine) treatment within one month prior to initiation of peginterferon alfa-2a
Compensated liver disease
Age > 18 years
Written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Treatment with any investigational drug within 30 days of entry to this protocol
Current treatment [AND] with Telbivudine
Severe hepatitis activity [AND] as documented by ALT>10 x ULN
History of decompensated cirrhosis (defined as jaundice [AND] in the presence of cirrhosis, ascites, bleeding gastric [OR] or esophageal varices [OR] encephalopathy)
Pre-existent neutropenia (neutrophils <1,500/mm3) [OR] or thrombocytopenia (platelets < 90,000/mm3)
Co-infection [AND] with hepatitis C virus, hepatitis D virus [OR] or human immunodeficiency virus (HIV)
Other acquired [OR] or inherited causes of liver disease: alcoholic liver disease, obesity induced liver disease, drug related liver disease, auto-immune hepatitis, hemochromatosis, Wilson's disease [OR] or alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency
Alpha fetoprotein > 50 ng/ml
Hyper- [OR] or hypothyroidism (subjects requiring medication to maintain TSH levels in the normal range are eligible if all other inclusion/exclusion criteria are met)
Immune suppressive treatment within the previous 6 months
Contra-indications for alfa-interferon therapy like suspected hypersensitivity to interferon [OR] or Peginterferon [OR] or any known pre-existing medical condition that could interfere with the patient's participation in and completion of the study.
Pregnancy [OR], breast-feeding
Other significant medical illness that might interfere with this study: significant pulmonary dysfunction in the previous 6 months, malignancy other than skin basocellular carcinoma in previous 5 years, immunodeficiency syndromes (e.g. HIV positivity, auto-immune diseases, organ transplants other than cornea and hair transplant)
Any medical condition requiring, or likely to require chronic systemic administration of steroids, during the course of the study
Substance abuse, such as alcohol (>80 g/day) [OR], I.V. drugs [OR] and inhaled drugs in the past 2 years.
Any other condition which in the opinion of the investigator would make the patient unsuitable for enrollment, or could interfere with the patient participating in and completing the study
