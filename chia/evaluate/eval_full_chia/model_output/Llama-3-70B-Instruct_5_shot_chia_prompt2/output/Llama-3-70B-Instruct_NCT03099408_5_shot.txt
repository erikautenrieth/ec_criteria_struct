Inclusion Criteria:
Women [AND] be at least 18 years of age
Have symptoms of vaginal odor [OR] and/or discharge
Meet the clinical (Amsel) criteria for BV
Willing [AND] to participate in research

Exclusion Criteria:
Presence of another vaginal infection [OR] STD
Allergy [AND] to metronidazole
Pregnant [OR] or nursing
Use of oral [OR] intravaginal antibiotics within the past 2 weeks
HIV [OR] other chronic disease
Inability [AND] to keep return appointments
Contraindications [AND] for Lactobacillus Vaginal Suppositories(those [NOT] without sexual history)
