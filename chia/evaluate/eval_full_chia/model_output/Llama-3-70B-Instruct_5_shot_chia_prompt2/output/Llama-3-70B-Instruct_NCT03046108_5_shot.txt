Inclusion Criteria:
Clinical suspicion of Morton neuroma confirmed [AND] in ultrasound scan
Symptoms present more than six months
The thickness of the nerve must be at least 2 mm [AND] in short axis and at least 5 mm [AND] in the longitudinal axis.

Exclusion Criteria:
Contraindication [AND] for the use of corticosteroids [OR] local anesthetics
Presence of inflammatory arthropathy [OR] neuropathy
Skin lesions [AND] in the area
diabetes mellitus
Infiltration [OR] previous surgery in the area
Refusal [AND] to participate in the study
