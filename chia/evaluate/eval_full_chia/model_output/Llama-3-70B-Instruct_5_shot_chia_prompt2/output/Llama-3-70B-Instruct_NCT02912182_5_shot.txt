Inclusion Criteria:
definite unilateral vestibulopathy
no pathological HINTS ([AND] examination criteria in acute vestibular syndrome)
capable of making their own decisions

Exclusion Criteria:
tinnitus [OR] hearing loss with same debut as vertigo
history of bleeding peptic ulcer
glaucoma
pregnancy [OR] non-acceptance to use anticonception measures during 13 days after debut
high blood pressure >180 systolic [AND], 105, diastolic
ketoacidosis with a Base Excess >=2
psychic disorder ([NOT] including mild depression)
serious infection ([OR] neutropenia [OR], tuberculosis)
chronic otitis
history of vertiginous disease; Ménière [OR], Vertiginous migraine [OR], atypical BPPV
