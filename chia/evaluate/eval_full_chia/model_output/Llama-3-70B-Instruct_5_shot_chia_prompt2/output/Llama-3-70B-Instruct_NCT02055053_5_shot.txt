Inclusion Criteria:
Age 18 or older [AND] with unilateral [OR] bilateral inguinal herna for laparoscopic repair
American Society of Anesthesiology (ASA) Class I [OR] II

Exclusion Criteria:
Conversion from laparoscopic to open surgery
History of Chronic pain [OR] ongoing treatment for chronic pain
Age less than 18 yrs
Allergy to local anesthetics
