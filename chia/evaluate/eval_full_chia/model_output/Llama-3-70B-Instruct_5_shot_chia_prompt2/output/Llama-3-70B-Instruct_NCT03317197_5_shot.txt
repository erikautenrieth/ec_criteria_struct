Inclusion Criteria:
The group of patients who participated in the study included adults aged at least 19 years [AND] among the atraumatic CA outpatients who came to the ER [AND] received CPR.

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant women [OR] young children aged <18 years;
Patients with underlying disease cases [AND] [NOT] without the possibility of resuscitation (e.g., terminal cancer);
Patients with do-not-resuscitate (DNR) status;
Death by excessive bleeding (e.g., abdominal main artery rupture);
Patients who have experienced in-hospital CA;
Patients previously treated with steroid [OR] anti-cancer medicine [OR] immunosuppression treatment before CA;
Patients already been registered with other studies;
or
Patients from whom informed consent cannot be obtained
