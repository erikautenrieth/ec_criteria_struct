Inclusion Criteria:
Written informed consent
Age =18 years
Has undergone first time isolated CABG due to an episode of acute coronary syndrome (STEMI [OR] NSTEMI [OR] unstable angina) within 6 weeks before surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
Previously enrolled in this study (i.e. patient now at repeat encounter)
Concomitant surgical procedure [AND] other than CABG
Anticoagulant treatment [AND] after the operation (e.g. warfarin [OR], direct thrombin inhibitors (dabigatran) [OR], FXa inhibitors (rivaroxaban [OR], apixaban [OR], heparin [OR], low-molecular weight heparin [OR], fondaparinux)
Discharge from the operating hospital [AND] to an ICU at another hospital
Pregnancy [OR] or lactation
Known intolerance [OR] or contraindication to ticagrelor [OR] or ASA
Any disorder that may interfere with drug absorption
Any condition [NOT] other than coronary artery disease with a life expectancy <12 months
Known chronic liver disease [OR], renal disease requiring dialysis [OR] or bleeding disorder
Atrioventricular block II [OR] and III in patients [NOT] without pacemaker
Any other indication for dual antiplatelet therapy, i.e. recent stent implantation
Debilitating stroke within 90 days before inclusion
Previous intracranial bleeding
Treatment with immunosuppressants (e.g. cyclosporine [OR] and tacrolimus)
Treatment with strong CYP3A4-inhibitors (e.g. ketoconazole [OR], clarithromycin [OR], nefazodone [OR], ritonavir [OR] or atazanavir)
Any condition that in the opinion of the investigator may interfere with adherence to trial protocol
