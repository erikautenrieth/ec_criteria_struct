Inclusion Criteria:
scheduled for Nuss procedure [AND] for pectus excavatum correction
at least 13 years old [AND] at the time of the procedure

Exclusion Criteria:
age less than 13 years at time of procedure
use of pain medication prior to procedure
pectus carinatum [OR] Poland's syndrome [OR] or any chest wall anomaly [NOT] other than pectus excavatum
previous repair of pectus excavatum by any technique
previous thoracic surgery
congenital heart disease
bleeding dyscrasia
major anesthetic risk factors [OR] history of previous problem with anesthesia
pregnancy
inability to communicate [AND] in English
