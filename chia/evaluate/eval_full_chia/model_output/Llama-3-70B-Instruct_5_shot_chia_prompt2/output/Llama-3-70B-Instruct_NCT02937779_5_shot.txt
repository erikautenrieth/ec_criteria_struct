Inclusion Criteria:
>= 18 years old the day of inclusion
[NOT] Pregnancy
Positive HBs Ag
Informed consent obtained [AND] with information sheet given [AND] explained [AND] the consent form signed by the participant [AND] of the project investigator at the latest the day of the inclusion

Exclusion Criteria:
Women refusing HBs Ag test
HIV co-infection
HCV co-infection
HBV treatment ongoing at the day of inclusion
Creatinine clearance < 30 mL/min
Severe gravidic disease present at inclusion [AND] involving life threatening to the mother [OR] and/or the child
Evidence of pre-existing fetal anomalies [AND] incompatible with the child's life
Imminent child's birth defined as cervix dilatation up to 7 centimeters
Intention to deliver in a maternity [NOT] linked to the study
Any concomitant medical condition that, according to the clinical site investigator would contraindicate participation in the study.
Concurrent participation in any other clinical trial [NOT] without written agreement of the two study teams
