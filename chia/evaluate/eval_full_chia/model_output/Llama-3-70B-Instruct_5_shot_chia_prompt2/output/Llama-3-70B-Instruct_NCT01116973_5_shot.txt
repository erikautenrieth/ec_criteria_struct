Inclusion Criteria:
Subject's ability [AND] to lay in a supine position with their hands at their sides during CVP measurements 
A consent form signed [AND] by the patient or patient's representative 
Subjects that are age 18-90 
Subjects that have an indwelling CICC [AND] are transitioning to a PICC for long-term IV access 
CICC placed in the internal jugular vein [OR] or subclavian vein position 

Exclusion Criteria:
Inability [AND] to obtain consent 
Subjects under 18 years of age 
Non-English speaking subjects 
Subjects that are unable [AND] to lay flat due to pulmonary complications [OR], increased intracranial pressure (ICP) [OR], or unstable spinal cord injuries 
Subjects with known cardiac abnormalities ([AND] atrial septal defects [OR] ventricular septal defects [OR], severe tricuspid valve disease [OR], severe pulmonary hypertension [OR], Ejection fraction < 15%) 
Prisoners 
Subjects with known upper extremity deep vein thromboses ([AND] subclavian [OR] or distal) 
Subjects with non-functional CICC [OR] or PICC distal ports 
Subjects with femoral CICCs 
Pregnant women
