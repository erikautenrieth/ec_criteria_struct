Inclusion Criteria:
patients with renal cancer coming to the laparoscopic radical nephrectomy

Exclusion Criteria:
age under 18y [OR] over 85y
diabetes type 1 [AND] with complications
[NOT] no co-operation [OR] inadequate finnish language skills
persistent pain [AND] for other reason
severe hepatic insufficiency [OR] paracetamol (acetaminophen) is contraindicated for other reason
any type of steroid [AND] in regular use
oxycodone contraindicated
medications changing [AND] notably paracetamol (acetaminophen) [AND] and/or ropivacaine metabolism [AND] in regular use
