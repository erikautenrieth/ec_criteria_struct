Inclusion Criteria:
acute myocardial infarction [OR] 
ischemic cardiomyopathy [AND] with [OR] without previous myocardial infarction [OR] 
dilated cardiomyopathy due to valvular heart disease [OR], hypertensive heart disease [OR], history of myocarditis [AND] (no active myocardial infection present) 

Exclusion Criteria:
none, all patients meeting the inclusion criteria will be eligible.
