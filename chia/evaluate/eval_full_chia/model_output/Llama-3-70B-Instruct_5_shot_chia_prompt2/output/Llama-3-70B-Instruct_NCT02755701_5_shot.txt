Inclusion Criteria:
Age = 19 [AND] = 70 years;
Presence of liver cirrhosis
Serum albumin level = 3.5g/dl, [AND] ultrasound [OR] CT scan confirmed ascites (=Grade 1)
[NOT] No administration of diuretics [AND] BCAA within the past 1 week
Voluntary consent [AND] to take part in this trial

Exclusion Criteria:
Child-Pugh score > 12
Having been diagnosed as HCC within the past 5 years
Serum creatinine > 1.5mg/dl
Serum bilirubin > 5.0mg/dl
Presence of such complications as SBP, [OR] or hepatic encephalopathy(West Haven grade = 3)
Patients who experienced organ failure [AND] by acute exacerbation of liver cirrhosis within the past 1 month
Presence of serious cardiac [OR] or respiratory disease
Contraindicated [AND] to either diuretics [OR] BCAA
Having commenced anti-viral treatment [AND] against hepatitis C, B within the past 1 month
Pregnant [OR] or lactating women
Chronic alcohol taker
Woman patients who do [NOT] agree to the contraception from baseline to 12 month
Unsuitable patients judged by investigator
Patients participating in another clinical trial within 1 month
