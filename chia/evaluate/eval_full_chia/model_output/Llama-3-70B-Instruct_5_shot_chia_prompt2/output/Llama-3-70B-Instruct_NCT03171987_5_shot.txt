Inclusion Criteria:
All subjects underwent a detailed history [AND] and systemic physical examination including neurologic [AND] and musculoskeletal evaluations. To rule out any confounding etiologies, basic diagnostic laboratory tests including complete blood count [AND] and acute phase reactants (erythrocyte sedimentation rate [AND] and C-reactive protein) were performed. The patients diagnosed as having acute non-specific low back pain according to history [AND] and physical examinations were invited to participate and will be informed about the purpose [AND] and course of the study.
A primary complaint of pain in the area between the 12th rib [AND] and buttock crease without leg pain
Female [OR] or male, 20 - 80 years of age
Low back pain of less than six weeks' duration; [AND] and at least moderate pain intensity (NRS<U+2267>4)

Exclusion Criteria:
Known [OR] or suspected serious spinal pathology [AND] and spinal implants
Lumbar spinal surgery within the preceding six months
Serious comorbidities [AND] preventing prescription of paracetamol
Alternative treatment for low back pain in previous two weeks
Chronic neurological lesion
Chronic musculoskeletal lesion
Active cancer
Pregnancy
Use of pain medication (except paracetamol) within 3 days
Treatment site has active skin lesion [OR] or inflammation
Known allergy to skin patch
