Inclusion Criteria:
Age = 18 years
American Society of Anesthesiologists Classification I-III
Normal cognitive function [AND] in order to sign written, informed consent [AND] to understand trial protocol
Agreement [AND] to the trial protocol, [AND] including the randomized manner

Exclusion Criteria:
Allergy [AND] to LA
Infection [AND] in or near insertion site of the peripheral nerve catheter
Anatomical abnormalities [AND] preventing successful peripheral catheter insertion
Habitual use of opioids
Pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding (disproved by a negative pregnancy test before trial inclusion)
