Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with heart transplantation
Patient with coronary artery disease
Age [AND] between 18 [AND] 80 years

Exclusion Criteria:
Renal insufficiency (> 265 µmol/l)
Incapability [AND] to give informed consent
Cardiogenic shock [AND] of patient with KILLIP III [OR] IV
pregnant [OR] breast feeding females
insufficient contraception [AND] (only for substudy 3)
