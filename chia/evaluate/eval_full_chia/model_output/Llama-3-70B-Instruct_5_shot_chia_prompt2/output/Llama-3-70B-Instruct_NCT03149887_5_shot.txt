Inclusion Criteria:
Adult patients up to age 75 years, undergoing elective, ambulatory, arthroscopic rotator cuff repair.

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnancy [OR] coagulopathy [OR] allergy to bupivacaine [OR] renal failure [OR] hepatic insufficiency [OR] and/or inappropriate candidate for usual therapy (specifically, if unable to receive the usual preoperative interscalene nerve block: preexisting nerve injury [AND] on side of surgery [OR] refusal of nerve block [OR] infection [AND] at site of nerve block).
