Inclusion Criteria:
Age : from 20 to 90 y/o.
HBsAg-positive for more than 6 months [AND] HBV DNA < 2000 IU/ml (Subgroup 1) [OR] HBsAg-negative [AND] but anti-HBc positive [AND] with HBV DNA < 2000 IU/ml (Subgroup 2).
Inflammatory arthritis patients who plan to treat with biological agents, including Humira [OR] or Enbrel [OR] or Simponi [OR] or Orencia [OR] or Mabthera [OR] or Actemra; as first line biologic treatment is indicated.

Exclusion Criteria:
HCV [OR] HIV [OR] or HDV coinfection.
HCC [OR] or other malignancy within 3 years.
Decompensated liver cirrhosis (CTP score = 7).
Uremia patients under hemodialysis [OR] or continuous ambulatory peritoneal dialysis [OR] or patients with Ccr < 50 mL/min
Pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding women.
Women of child-bearing potential (WOCBP) who are unwilling [OR] or unable to use an acceptable method of contraception to avoid pregnancy throughout the study [AND] for up to 4 weeks after the last dose of study drug.
