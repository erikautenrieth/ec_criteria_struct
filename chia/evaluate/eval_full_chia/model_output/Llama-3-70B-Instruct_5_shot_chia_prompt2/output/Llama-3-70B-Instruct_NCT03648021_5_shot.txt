Inclusion Criteria:
18-year or older patients
Patient hospitalized in neuro-critical care for:
Arachnoid hemorrhage [OR] 
Intra parenchymatous hematoma [OR] 
stroke [OR] 
Acute brain Severe injury [OR] 
Post-operative complication of an act of neurosurgery [OR] or programmed neuroradiology
Sedation [AND] mechanical ventilation planned > 2 days
Monitoring of intracranial temperature [AND] pressure by intraparenchymal sensor (Sophysa®)
Brain temperature > 38.5°C for more than 30 minutes

Exclusion Criteria:
Known hypersensitivity to paracetamol [OR] or mannitol (excipient with known effect)
Severe hepatocellular insufficiency (ASAT [OR] or ALAT > 5N, [OR] or bilirubin > 2N)
Pharmacological intervention (administration of corticosteroids [OR], NSAIDs [OR] or paracetamol) [OR] physical intervention (external cooling technique) that may influence temperature in the last 6 hours.
Pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding women
Previous participation in this study
