Inclusion Criteria:
Age = 15 years old
Hospitalization with acute undifferentiated fever (temperature > 37.5 C, tympanic) =14 days [OR] patients admitted to hospital with a history of fever = 14 days who subsequently develop fever within 24 hours of admission
Clinically suspected scrub typhus: defined as acute undifferentiated fever [AND] with no clear focus of infection [AND] negative malaria blood smear [AND] and/or negative malaria RDT. Patients may have one, none, or a combination of other clinical findings such as eschar, rash, lymphadenopathy, headache, myalgia, cough, nausea [AND] abdominal discomfort.
A positive scrub typhus RDT (Scrub Typhus IgM RDT, InBios International, Seattle, WA, USA) [OR] and/or positive PCR-based detection of O. tsutsugamushi DNA from the admission blood sample
Written informed consent [AND] and/or, written informed assent as required
Able to take oral medication

Exclusion Criteria:
Known hypersensitivity to tetracycline [OR], doxycycline [OR] or azithromycin
Administration of doxycycline [OR], azithromycin [OR], chloramphenicol [OR], rifampicin [OR], or tetracycline during the preceding 7 days
Pregnancy [OR] or breast-feeding
Patients with myasthenia gravis [OR] or systemic lupus erythematosus
Patients with an established infection (diagnostic test required) e.g. acute malaria [OR], dengue [OR], leptospirosis [OR], typhoid [OR], Japanese encephalitis etc.
Current TB [OR] or TB treatment in = 6 months (contain active antibiotics against Orientia spp.)
Current HAART use for HIV [OR], long term use of immunosuppressants (e.g. steroids [OR], chemotherapy [OR], TNF-inhibitors [OR] and related agents)
Patients with severe disease whom the clinical team feel their condition necessitates the need for additional scrub typhus treatment beyond the allocated antibiotic treatment assigned at randomization (e.g. IV chloramphenicol [AND] and/or PO/NG rifampicin)
