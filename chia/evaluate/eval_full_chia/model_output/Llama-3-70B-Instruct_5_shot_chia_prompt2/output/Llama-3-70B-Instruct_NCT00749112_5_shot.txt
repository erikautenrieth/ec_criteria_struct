Inclusion Criteria:
Age: > or = 16 years 
Weight: more than 40 Kg 
Autoimmune Hemolytic anemia [AND] with clinical [AND] biochemical evidence of hemolysis refractory to treatment, [OR] in relapse [OR] steroids dependant 
Idiopathic thrombocytopenic purpura [AND] with platelet counts < 50,000, refractory to treatment, [OR] in relapse [OR] steroids dependant 

Exclusion Criteria:
Current viral [OR] bacterial infection. 
Positive serology for HIV [OR], HCV [OR], HBV.
