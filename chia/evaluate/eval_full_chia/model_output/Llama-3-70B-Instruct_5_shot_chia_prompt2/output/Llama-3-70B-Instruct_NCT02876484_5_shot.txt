Inclusion Criteria:
Uncomplicated RYGB performed minimum 3 months prior to the study.
Fasting plasma glucose < 7,0 mM [AND] HbA1c < 48 mmol/mol 3 months after RYGB

Exclusion Criteria:
Fasting plasma glucose > 7,0 mM [OR] HbA1c > 48 mmol/mol 3 months after RYGB.
Dysregulated thyroid diseases [OR] use of antithyroid treatment.
Late diabetic complications [OR] as retinopathy, renal insufficiency, neuropathy [OR] or previous pancreatitis.
Complications to RYGB [AND] Documented reactive hypoglycaemia, severe dumping (vomiting, diarrhea, severe abdominal pain after food intake).
