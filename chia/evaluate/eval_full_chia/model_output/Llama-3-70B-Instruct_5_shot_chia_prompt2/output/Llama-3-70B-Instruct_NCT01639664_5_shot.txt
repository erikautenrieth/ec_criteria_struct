Inclusion Criteria:
All patients admitted to the ICU [AND] in septic shock
All patients that develop septic shock [AND] while in the ICU

Exclusion Criteria:
Age less than 14 years
Pregnancy
Estimated life expectancy (due to comorbidities) less than 90 days
Presence of relative [OR] or absolute contraindications to CPFA
Admission from an other ICU [AND] where the patient remained for more than 24 hours
Absence [AND] of informed consent
