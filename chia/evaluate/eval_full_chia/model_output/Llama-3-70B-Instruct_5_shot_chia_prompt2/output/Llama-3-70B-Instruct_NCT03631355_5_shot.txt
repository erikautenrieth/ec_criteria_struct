Inclusion Criteria:
Patients undergoing a high tibial osteotomy (HTO)
Patients undergoing tibial tubercle osteotomy (TTO) [AND] with [OR] or without medial patello-femoral ligament (MPFL) reconstruction

Exclusion Criteria:
Legally incompetent [OR] or mentally impaired (e.g., minors, Alzheimer's subjects, dementia, etc.)
Younger than 18 years of age
Any patient considered a vulnerable subject
Have bleeding [OR] or clotting disorder
Preoperative anticoagulation therapy
Abnormal coagulation profile
Renal disorder [OR] or insufficiency
Sickle cell disease
