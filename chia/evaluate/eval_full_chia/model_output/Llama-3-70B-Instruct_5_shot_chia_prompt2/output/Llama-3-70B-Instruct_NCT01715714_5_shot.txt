Inclusion Criteria:
Patients on chronic statin treatment (>30 days) [AND] scheduled for isolated CABG, including on- [OR] off-pump [OR] repeat (redo's) revascularisation procedures
Stable [OR] unstable angina, including non ST-segment-elevation acute coronary syndrome (NSTE-ACS)
Age = 18 years
Written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Any concomitant cardiovascular procedure [AND] to CABG (i.e. valve, [OR] aortic [OR] carotid surgery)
Acute ST-segment-elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI)
NSTE-ACS with cardiogenic shock [AND] warranting emergent salvage surgery within 12 hrs from hospital admission
History of atrial fibrillation [OR] muscle disease (myopathy)
Current renal (creatinine>2x upper limit of normal (ULN), [OR] dialysis, [OR] kidney transplant) [OR] hepatic dysfunction (AST/ALT>2x ULN, [OR] liver transplant [OR] neoplasm)
Inability of oral drug intake
