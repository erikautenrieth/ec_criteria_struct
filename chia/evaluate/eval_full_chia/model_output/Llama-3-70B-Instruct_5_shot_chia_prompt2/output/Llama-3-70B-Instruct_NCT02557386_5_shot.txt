Inclusion Criteria:
Male sex
ASA status I [OR] II
BMI [AND] between 20 and 34 kg/m2
Cruciate ligament of the knee reconstructive surgery
No contraindications [AND] to general and regional anesthesia

Exclusion Criteria:
Chronic pain more than 3 months
Drug abuse
Chronic use of analgesic drugs (more than 3 months)
Psychiatric illness
Peripheral neuropathy
Drug allergy
Severe gastroesophageal reflux disease
