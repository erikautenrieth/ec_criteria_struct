Inclusion Criteria:
Clinical diagnosis of allergic rhinitis based on sneeze attacks [AND] runny/blocked/itchy nose [AND] in the absence of a common cold during the previous 12 months. 
History of positive skin prick test [OR] or blood radio-allergosorbent test (RAST) to grass [AND] or ragweed pollen 

Exclusion Criteria:
Ongoing allergen immunotherapy 
upper respiratory tract infection 
Pregnancy 
Clinical history of lactose-intolerance [OR] or allergies to cow-milk
