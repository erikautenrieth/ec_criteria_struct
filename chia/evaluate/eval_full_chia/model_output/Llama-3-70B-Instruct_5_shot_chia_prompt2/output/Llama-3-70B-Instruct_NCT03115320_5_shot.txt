Inclusion Criteria:
- Patient with IVF cycle [AND] therefore having frozen-thawed embryos
Regular menstruation cycle
Patient's willingness [AND] to participate in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
- Irregular menstrual cycle [AND] demanding preparing endometrium with hormones for frozen-thawed embryo
[NOT] No frozen embryos after IVF cycle
Allergy to Pregnyl® [OR] or some of its ingredients in the medication [OR] other contraindications due to Pregnyl®
