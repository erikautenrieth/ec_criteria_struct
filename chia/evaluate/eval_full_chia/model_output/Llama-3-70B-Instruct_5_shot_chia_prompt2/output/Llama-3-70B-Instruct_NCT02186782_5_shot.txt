Inclusion Criteria:
Infertile women with eugonadotrophic anovulation [OR] oligoovulation.
Unexplained infertility.

Exclusion Criteria:
Age < 20 [OR] > 35 years.
Body mass index (BMI) < 18.5 kg/m2 [OR] > 25 kg/m2.
Presence of any infertility factor [NOT] other than anovulation [AND] oligoovulation.
Previous history of ovarian surgery [OR] or surgical removal of one ovary.
Previous exposure to cytotoxic drugs [OR] or pelvic irradiation.
Metabolic [OR] or hormonal abnormalities.
