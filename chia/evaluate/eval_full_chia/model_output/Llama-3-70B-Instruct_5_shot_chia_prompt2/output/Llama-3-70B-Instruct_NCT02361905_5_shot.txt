Inclusion Criteria:
hypoechoic uterine leiomyoma (echogenicity <3),
intramural leiomyomas with an ultrasonographic size <20 cm [AND] >4cm,
indication to surgery ([AND] symptoms of menometrorrhagia, [OR] menstrual disorder, [OR] infertility, [OR] pelvic pain [OR] pelvic pressure)

Exclusion Criteria:
submucosal leiomyoma,
endometrial hyperplasia [AND] with atypia,
history of uterine surgery
