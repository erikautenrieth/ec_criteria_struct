<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Pregnant patients [AND] who require a scheduled [OR] non-urgent cesarean birth
Patient [AND] able to receive neuraxial analgesia
Patient [AND] able to give verbal [AND] written consent for both cesarean birth [AND] study

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] requiring emergent cesarean birth
Patients [AND] allergic to lidocaine [OR] adhesive
Patients [AND] who have already received an epidural during this admission [OR] requiring general anesthesia for cesarean birth
Patients [AND] using chronic oral neuromodulators
Patients [AND] with cardiac disease [OR] using anti-arrhythmic agents
Patients [AND] with fibromyalgia [OR] chronic pain syndromes such as rheumatoid arthritis, osteoarthritis, [OR] lupus
[NOT] Daily narcotic [OR] opiate use for greater than the 2 months prior to enrollment in the study.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
