<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Recently diagnosed type 2 diabetic patients.
Fasting plasma glucose between 200-300 mg/dl [AND] (A1C level between 7% [OR] 10%).
Those who age between 30 [OR] 80 years old [AND] can inject insulin by themselves.

Exclusion Criteria:
Previous treated [AND] anti-diabetic medication
Pregnant [OR] nursing women.
Impaired liver function (ALT > 120 U/L)
Impaired renal function ([NOT] Serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 mg/dL in male, [NOT] Serum creatinine ≤ 1.4 mg/dL in female )
Recently suffered from MI [OR] CVA.
Patients [AND] acute intercurrent illness.
2-hour C-peptide level < 1.8 ng/mL [OR] absent.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
