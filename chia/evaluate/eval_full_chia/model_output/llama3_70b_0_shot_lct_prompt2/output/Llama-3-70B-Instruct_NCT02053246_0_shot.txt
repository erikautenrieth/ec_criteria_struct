<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Adults ([AND] = 18 years of age) [AND] with World Health Organization Group 2 Pulmonary Hypertension ([AND] Mean pulmonary artery pressure [OR] = 25 mmHg [AND] pulmonary capillary wedge pressure [OR] = 15 mmHg)
New York Heart Association class II-[OR]IV symptoms
Left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) [OR] = 45%

Exclusion Criteria:
Other causes of heart failure [NOT] diastolic dysfunction, such as restrictive cardiomyopathy [OR] infiltrative cardiomyopathy
Women [AND] who are pregnant [OR] nursing
Liver cirrhosis,
Primary valvular disease
Acute coronary syndrome
Causes of PH [NOT] that of heart failure, such as: chronic thromboembolic PH [OR] sickle-cell disease [OR] sarcoidosis
Severe bradycardia [OR] greater than 1st degree heart block
Decompensated heart failure
Current use of a third generation beta-blocker ([AND] nebivolol [OR] carvedilol [OR] labetalol) [OR] high dose of any beta-blockers ([AND] greater than 100 mg daily of metoprolol [OR] equivalent)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
