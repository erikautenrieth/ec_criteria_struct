<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Histologically confirmed PD-L1 status defined NSCLC [AND] biopsy must be within 70 days of first treatment with pembrolizumab. 
ECOG performance status 2. 
Life expectancy > 12 weeks. 
Uni-dimensionally measurable disease according to Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumours (RECIST) v1.1 
Computerised Tomography (CT) scan of chest [OR] abdomen within 28 days of starting pembrolizumab. 
Adequate haematological function: 
Platelet count ≥100 x 109 /L [AND] neutrophils ≥1.5 x 109/L [AND] haemoglobin ≥ 9g/dL. 
Adequate hepatic function: 
Serum bilirubin ≤1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN) [AND] serum transaminases ≤2.5 x ULN. 
Adequate renal function: Creatinine clearance <1.5 times ULN [AND] concurrent with creatinine clearance >50 ml/min. 
Provision of signed [AND] dated, written informed consent prior to any study specific procedures, sampling [AND] analyses. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Untreated symptomatic brain [OR] leptomeningeal metastatic disease. 
Medical [OR] psychiatric conditions comprising informed consent. 
Any medical condition which in the opinion of the investigator would compromise the ability of the patient to participate in the trial [OR] which would jeopardise compliance with the protocol. 
[NOT] Radiotherapy within 4 weeks of trial entry. 
Active autoimmune disease that has required systemic treatment in past 2 years [NOT] free of treatment. 
Chronic usage of steroids [OR] other immunosuppressant medication. 
Previous history of pneumonitis. 
Any evidence of clinical autoimmunity [NOT] absence of autoimmunity.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
