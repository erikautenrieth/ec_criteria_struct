<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Treated [AND] with a stable dose of one of the following for at least 3 months prior to screening: * >=1000 mg/day immediate-release metformin; [OR] metformin >=1000 mg/day [AND] sulfonylurea; [OR] sulfonylurea/metformin combination therapy. 
HbA1c between 7.1% [AND] 11.0%, inclusive. 
Body Mass Index (BMI) >21 kg/m^2 [AND] <35 kg/m^2. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Have participated [OR] in this study previously, [OR] any other study using exenatide [OR] GLP-1 analogs. 
Have participated [OR] in an interventional, medical, surgical, [OR] pharmaceutical study within 30 days of screening. 
[NOT] Have characteristics contraindicating metformin [OR] sulfonylurea use. 
[NOT] Have been treated [AND] with exogenous insulin for more than 1 week within the 3 months prior to screening. 
[NOT] Have used drugs for weight loss within 1 month of screening.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
