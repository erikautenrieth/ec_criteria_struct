<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Over 18 years of age [AND] Systemically healthy [AND] Non-smoking;
[AND] With good oral hygiene;
[AND] Absent [NOT] irreversible pulpal alteration;
[AND] With the presence of a non-carious cervical lesion (LCNCs) that needs to be restored. This lesion should be non-carious, non-retentive, with at least 1 mm [AND] up to 3 mm depth, should involve both enamel [AND] dentin of vital teeth without mobility, [AND] present hypersensitivity;
[AND] Presence a natural tooth of the same position of the restored tooth, but in the opposite arch of the same jaw to be considered for the positive control;
[AND] Periodontal parameters : Depth Probing (PS), Visible Plaque Index (IPV), Gingival Index (GI) [AND] Probing Bleed Index (SS). The normal included were: PS = 1 to 3 mm, GI = 0, IPV = score 0 [AND] SS = score 0.

Exclusion Criteria:
With severe systemic alteration;
[OR] In the use of antibiotics [AND] anti-inflammatories in the last three months;
[OR] With periodontium with periodontal parameters different from those established in the inclusion criteria;
[OR] Individuals with clinical signs of parafunctional habits;
[OR] Smoking;
[OR] Individuals who have performed other restorations in the last 12 months;
[OR] Pregnant women [OR] infants;
[OR] Periodontal sites that presented bleeding during crevicular fluid collection [OR] sites that prevent proper collection of clinical parameters.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
