Drug-eluting stent implantation within 3 months prior to TAVI procedure [OR] 
Bare-metal stent implantation within 1 month prior to TAVI procedure [OR] 
[NOT] Allergy [OR] intolerance to (N)OAC [OR] clopidogrel.
