<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Uncomplicated RYGB performed [AND] minimum 3 months prior to the study.
Fasting glucose < 7,0 mM [OR] HbA1c < 48 mmol/mol 3 months after RYGB

Exclusion Criteria:
Fasting plasma glucose > 7,0 mM [OR] HbA1c > 48 mmol/mol 3 months after RYGB
[NOT] Dysregulated thyroid diseases [OR] use of antithyroid treatment.
Late diabetic complications [AND] retinopathy [OR] renal insufficiency [OR] neuropathy [OR] previous pancreatitis.
Complications to RYGB [AND] Documented reactive hypoglycaemia [OR] severe dumping (vomiting [OR] diarrhea [OR] severe abdominal pain after food intake)
[NOT] Cholecystectomy.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
