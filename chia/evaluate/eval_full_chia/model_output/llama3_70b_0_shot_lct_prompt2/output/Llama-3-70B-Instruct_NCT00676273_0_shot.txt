<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Are at least 18 years of age [AND] Demonstrate a positive cough stress test during complex multi-channel urodynamic testing [AND] Demonstrate impact of stress urinary incontinence on quality of life questionnaire [AND] Are able to comprehend [AND] sign a written informed consent [AND] Understand [AND] are willing to comply with the study requirements, including agreeing to be available for the follow-up evaluations [AND] Are psychologically stable [AND] suitable for interventions determined by the investigator [AND] Are ambulatory [AND] able to use a toilet independently

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients: 
[NOT] Who are pregnant [OR] planning to become pregnant during the study [OR] in the future 
[AND] With a elevated post-void residual (defined as PVR > 100cc) 
[AND] With a bleeding condition [OR] on anti-coagulant therapy 
[AND] With immunosuppression (i.e. HIV [OR] lymphoma) 
[AND] With multiple sclerosis [OR] other progressive neurological disease 
[AND] With evidence of a local [OR] systemic infection, including urinary tract infection 
[AND] With evidence of intrinsic sphincter deficiency as defined by a maximal urethral closure pressure of <20 cm H2O 
[AND] Previous sub-urethral sling 
[AND] Predominant overactive bladder symptoms
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
