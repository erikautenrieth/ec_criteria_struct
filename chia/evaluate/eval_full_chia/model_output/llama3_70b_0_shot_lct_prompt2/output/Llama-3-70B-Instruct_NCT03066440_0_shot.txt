Inclusion Criteria:
Age [AND] between 0 [OR] 18 years
Venous pH [AND] less than 7.25
Ketonuria as confirmed on urine point-of-care testing [OR] urinalysis
Hyperglycemia ([AND] Serum glucose > 200 mg/dl)
Serum bicarbonate < 15 mmol/L
PICU admission

Exclusion Criteria:
Age [AND] > 18 Years
Physician discretion
Septic [OR] hypovolemic shock
Signs of life-threatening cerebral edema [OR] multi-organ failure upon presentation to the emergency room [OR] pediatric intensive care unit
Enrollment time [AND] more than 1 hr since arrival to emergency room [OR] PICU
[NOT] Pregnancy
