Inclusion Criteria:
Children [AND] between the ages of 4-18 [AND] with incomplete ASIA C [OR] D spinal cord injuries [AND] at least 12 months before study enrolment 
Non-ambulatory [OR] 'exercise only' ambulators [AND] with [OR] without assistive devices 
Normal motor [AND] cognitive development up to time of injury 
Medical Stability 

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Other neuromuscular disease 
Contraindication to weight bearing on lower extremities 
Pressure sores where harness would be applied 
Uncontrollable hypotension when upright 
Lower limb contractures impeding range of motion necessary for ambulation 
Prior enrolment in a BWATT program 
[NOT] Unable to commit to intervention for duration of protocol
