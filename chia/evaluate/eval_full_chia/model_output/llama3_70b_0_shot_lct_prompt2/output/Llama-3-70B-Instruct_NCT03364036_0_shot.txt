Inclusion Criteria:
Highly active RMS as defined by:
One relapse in the previous year [AND] at least 1 T1 Gadolinium (Gd)+ lesion [OR] 9 [OR] more T2 lesions, while on therapy [AND] with other disease modifying drugs (DMDs)
Two [OR] more relapses in the previous year, whether on DMD treatment [OR] not.
Expanded Disability Status Scale (EDSS) score less than [OR] equals to (<=) 5.0.
Other protocol defined inclusion criteria could apply.

Exclusion Criteria:
Previous exposure to drugs such as fingolimod, natalizumab, alemtuzumab, mitoxantrone [OR] ocrelizumab.
Positive hepatitis C [OR] hepatitis B surface antigen test [OR] hepatits B core antibody test for immunoglobulin G (IgG) [OR] immunoglobulin M (IgM).
Current [OR] previous history of immune deficiency disorders including a positive human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) result.
Currently receiving immunosuppressive [OR] myelosuppressive therapy [AND] with, for example, monoclonal antibodies, methotrexate, cyclophosphamide, cyclosporine [OR] azathioprine, [OR] chronic use of corticosteroids.
History of tuberculosis [OR] presence of active tuberculosis [OR] latent tuberculosis
Evidence [OR] suspect of Progressive Multifocal Leukoencephalopathy (PML) in Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI).
Active malignancy [OR] history of malignancy.
[NOT] Other protocol defined exclusion criteria could apply.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
