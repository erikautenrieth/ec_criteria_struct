<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Overweight [AND] obese PCOS patients [AND] who have newly diagnosed IGR;
PCOS diagnosis based on 2003 Rotterdam criteria
Overweight [OR] obesity diagnostic criteria according to WHO-WPR
Impaired glucose regulation diagnostic criteria according to 1998 WHO diagnostic criteria.

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Except for serious complications ([AND] cardiovascular events [AND] recent significant liver, kidney [OR] lung disease within 3 months)
high blood pressure (>160/100mmHg)
active infection
secondary diabetes
pregnancy
alcohol abuse
allergic to GLP-1 receptor agonist
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
