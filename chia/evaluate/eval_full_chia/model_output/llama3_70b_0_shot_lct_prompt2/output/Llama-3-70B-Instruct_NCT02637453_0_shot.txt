<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
[NOT] response to more than one antiarrhythmic drug [OR] unwilling to receive long-term drug treatment.
Can provide informed consent form expressing willingness to participate in the study [AND] comply with follow-up tests [AND] evaluation procedures.
Aged 18-80 years.

Exclusion Criteria:
[AND] acute diseases, such as acute phase after myocardial infarction (within 3 months) [OR] within 3 months after acute heart failure [OR] new cerebral infarction;
[AND] in the list of heart transplantation;
Expected survival less than 1 year;
[AND] other hemorrhagic diseases [AND] anticoagulant therapy is [NOT] allowed;
Thrombosis in left atrium;
[AND] Heart failure, New York Heart Association(NYHA) III/IV [OR] eject fraction(EF)<40%;
Patients [AND] uncontrolled cancer;
[AND] significant hepatic [OR] renal impairment ([AND] alanine transaminase(ALT) [OR] Aspartate transaminase(AST) >2 times upper limit of normal) [OR] creatinine clearance rate(CCr)<50%;
Previous catheter radiofrequency ablation for AF [OR] cardiac surgery;
Pregnant [OR] lactating women [OR] women who plan to become pregnant [OR] women of child bearing age [NOT] using reliable contraceptive measures.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
