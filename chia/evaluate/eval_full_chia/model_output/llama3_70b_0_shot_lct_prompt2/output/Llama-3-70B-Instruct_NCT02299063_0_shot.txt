<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
aged between 3 - 36 months [AND] having primary corrective heart surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
recent surgery (< 3 months) [OR] previous chemotherapy [OR] previous transfusion of blood products [OR] neurodevelopmental disorders (including Trisomy 21) [OR] supplemental oxygen requirement (< 3 months) [OR] asthma requiring regular therapy [OR] obstructive sleep apnea [OR] the presence of concurrent infection [OR] inflammation [OR] a known allergy to dexmedetomidine hydrochloride
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
