<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Age=18 years [AND] =80 years;
Patients [AND] who have non-cystic fibrosis bronchiectasis diagnosed by high-resolution CT;
Are sensitive to amikacin;
Acute exacerbation of bronchiectasis;
Capable of the completion of bronchoscopy, alveolar lavage, pulmonary function testing, etc;
Willing to join in [AND] sign the informed consent form.

Exclusion Criteria:
Active bleeding [NOT] without control;
Receiving nasal [OR] facial surgery recently;
[AND] With severe cardio-pulmonary dysfunction, such as left heart failure, unstable arrhythmia, etc.
[AND] With other respiratory diseases: such as active pulmonary tuberculosis, non-tuberculosis mycobacteria (NTM) pulmonary disease, pulmonary aspergillosis, etc.
[AND] Be allergic to amikacin
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
