<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Cerebral palsy of any types caused by Neonatal Jaundice 

Exclusion Criteria:
Epilepsy [AND] Hydrocephalus [AND] with ventricular drain 
Coagulation disorders 
Allergy [AND] to anesthetic agents 
Severe health conditions such as cancer [OR] failure of heart [OR] lung [OR] liver [OR] kidney 
Active [NOT] absence of infections
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
