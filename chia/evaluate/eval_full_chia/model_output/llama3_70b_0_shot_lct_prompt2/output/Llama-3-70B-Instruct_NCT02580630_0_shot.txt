Inclusion Criteria:
Midsubstance pain in the achilles tendon [AND] Symptoms for at least 3 months [AND] Ultrasound scanning at the first visit shows thickness of the achilles tendon above 7 mm [OR] 20% thicker than the contralateral [AND] Patient can read [AND] understand danish

Exclusion Criteria:
Earlier operations in the foot [AND] leg, that is judged to complicate training [OR] known arthritis [OR] known diabetes [OR] Leg ulcerations [OR] infections in the foot [OR] Judged [NOT] able to comply with the training protocol [OR] Daily use of pain killers [OR] Glucocorticosteroid injection to the diseased achilles tendon within the last 6 months [OR] Earlier allergic reactions to glucocorticosteroid [OR] local anesthetic [OR] Pregnancy [OR] planning to become pregnant [OR] BMI above 30
