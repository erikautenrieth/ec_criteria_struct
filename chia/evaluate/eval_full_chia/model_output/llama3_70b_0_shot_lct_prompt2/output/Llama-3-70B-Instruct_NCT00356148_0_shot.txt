<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Women at any age [AND] with early stage breast cancer (stage I-II) [AND] American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) score of I-II.

Exclusion Criteria:
Ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS; stage 0 cancer) [OR] Advanced [OR] distant metastatic stage, 
Receiving any neoadjuvant therapy, 
[NOT] Having a history of receiving any antibiotics within prior 3 months, 
[NOT] Having a history of immunodeficiency, 
[NOT] Having a remote infection, 
[NOT] Having a history of reaction to study antibiotics, 
[NOT] Denial of signing the consent form.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
