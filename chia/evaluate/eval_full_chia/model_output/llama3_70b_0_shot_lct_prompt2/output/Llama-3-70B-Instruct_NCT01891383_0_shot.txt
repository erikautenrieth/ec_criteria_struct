<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Cases ([AND] with a history of TBI): 
1. Ages 50-95 years 
2. History of traumatic brain injury of sufficient severity to have resulted in medical attention ([AND] ascertained via the Ohio State University TBI Identification Questionnaire—OSU TBI-ID, [AND] based on DoD/VA criteria) 
3. Residence in AFRH-Washington D.C. [OR] the Veterans Home of California-Yountville 
4. MMSE score ≥ 20 
5. Capacity to provide consent to participate in research ([AND] assessment made by study physician) 
6. Ability to read [AND] write English 
Controls ([NOT] with a history of TBI): 
1. Ages 50-95 years 
2. [NOT] history of traumatic brain injury of sufficient severity to have resulted in medical attention ([AND] ascertained via the Ohio State University TBI Identification Questionnaire—OSU TBI-ID) 
3. Residence in AFRH-Washington [OR] the Veterans Home of California-Yountville 
4. MMSE score ≥ 20 
5. Capacity to provide consent [OR] assent to participate in research 
6. Ability to read [AND] write English - 

Exclusion Criteria:
Cases ([AND] with a history of TBI): 
1. History of penetrating brain injury 
2. History of disabling neurological [OR] psychiatric condition such as epilepsy ([NOT] besides posttraumatic epilepsy), multiple sclerosis, cortical stroke, hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy, encephalitis, [OR] schizophrenia 
Controls ([NOT] with a history of TBI): 
History of disabling neurological [OR] psychiatric condition such as epilepsy, multiple sclerosis, cortical stroke, hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy, encephalitis, [OR] schizophrenia
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
