<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis of uncomplicated gastroschisis
Gestational age >33 weeks at time of delivery
Weight >1900g at time of delivery
Transfer of patient [AND] Riley Hospital for Children prior to any abdominal surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
Neurological Congenital malformations [OR] those known to impair intestinal motility
Additional congenital gastrointestinal abnormalities requiring surgical intervention
Congenital Cyanotic heart disease
Surgical Closure of abdominal wall defect [AND] prosthetic material (e.g. prosthetic [OR] bio-prosthetic mesh)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
