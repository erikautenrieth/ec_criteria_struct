<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
age =18 [AND] <75 years;
patients [AND] with stable coronary artery disease referred to PCI [AND] in an artery suitable for IVUS pullback;
signed informed consent [AND] before PCI.

Exclusion Criteria:
cardiac [OR] non-cardiac illness [AND] with life expectancy of less than two years;
[NOT] able to advance the IVUS catheter through the culprit lesion;
acute coronary syndrome
congestive heart failure NYHA III-IV
diabetes mellitus
chronic kidney disease
previous PCI [AND] in the target vessel
heavily calcified vessels
allergy [AND] to metformin
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
