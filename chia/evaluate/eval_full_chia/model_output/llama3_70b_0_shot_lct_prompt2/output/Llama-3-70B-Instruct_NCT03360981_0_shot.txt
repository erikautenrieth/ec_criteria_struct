<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
patients aged >18 [AND] <75, left ventricle ejection fraction (LVEF) >50% [AND] multivessel coronary disease detected by coronarography [AND] indication to receive a CABG [AND] stable CAD. All diabetics [OR] non diabetics.

Exclusion Criteria:
acute myocardial infarction [OR] heart failure [OR] neoplastic disease [OR] chronic diseases that may affect the inflammatory profile both systemic [OR] epicardial (cancer [OR] chronic intestinal inflammation [OR] hepatitis [OR] AIDS); life expectancy < 6 months [OR] previous CABG [AND]/or other open heart surgery intervention [OR] acute coronary syndrome.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
