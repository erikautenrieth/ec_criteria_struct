<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Motor complete tetraplegia for at least 3 months
Age from 18 to 74 years
Body mass index (BMI) from 18 to 35kg/m2
[AND] Informed consent as documented by signature

Exclusion Criteria:
Any anti-coagulation therapy [OR] apart from rivaroxaban for second objective
[NOT] Hypersensitivity [OR] allergy to factor Xa inhibitors
Acute bacterial endocarditis
Bleeding disorder
Clinically relevant active bleeding
Gastrointestinal ulcer [OR] tumor
Hepatic dysfunction [AND] with increased bleeding risk
Renal failure [OR] patients undergoing dialysis
Pregnancy [AND] breast feeding
Gastrectomy [OR] biliopancreatic diversion [OR] resection [OR] re-routing of small intestines
Feeding tube
Recent blood donation
Abnormalities of laboratory values: alanine-aminotransferase (ALAT) [OR] aspartate-aminotransferase (ASAT) [OR] gamma-glutamyl transferase (gammaGT) [OR] alkalic phosphatase (AP) [OR] bilirubin [OR] amylase [OR] lipase [OR] cystatin C [OR] creatinine [OR] white blood cell count [OR] haemoglobin [OR] platelet count [OR] prothrombin time [OR] aPTT [OR] fibrinogen [OR] thrombin time [OR] factors II [OR] V [OR] VII [OR] X
Use of therapeutic [OR] recreational drugs influencing plasmatic coagulation
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
