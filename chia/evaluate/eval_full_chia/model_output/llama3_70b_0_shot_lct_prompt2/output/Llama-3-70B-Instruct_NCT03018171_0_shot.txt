<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Written maternal informed consent
Singleton pregnancy
Gestational age = 37 weeks [OR], 
ASA I
BMI < 30
fetus in cephalic presentation

Exclusion Criteria:
Suspect [OR] certainty of fetal malformation,
Presence of conditions such as preeclampsia [OR] multiparity [OR] preterm labor
[NOT] History of adverse reaction to a-2 adrenergic agonists
[NOT] Nicotine addiction
[NOT] Chronic use of opioid
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
