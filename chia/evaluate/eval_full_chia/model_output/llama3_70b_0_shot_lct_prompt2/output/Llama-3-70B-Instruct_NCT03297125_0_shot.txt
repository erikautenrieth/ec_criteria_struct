<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Newly diagnosed glioblastoma (GBM) [AND] WHO grade IV.

Exclusion Criteria:
Optune compliance < 75% [OR] they would be excluded from the final analyses.
History of craniectomy [OR] significant skull defect ([AND] contraindication to Optune).
Active implantable medical device ([AND] i.e. DBS [OR] spinal cord stimulator [OR] pacemaker [OR] defibrillator [OR] vagus nerve stimulator [OR] programmable shunt).
[NOT] Karnofsky Performance Status (KPS) >= 60.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
