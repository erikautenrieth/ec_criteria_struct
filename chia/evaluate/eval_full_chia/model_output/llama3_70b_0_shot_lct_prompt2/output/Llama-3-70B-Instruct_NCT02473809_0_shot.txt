<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Informed consent [AND] Diagnosis of type 2 diabetes (HbA1c > 48 mmol/mol) [AND] Age older than 30 years

Exclusion Criteria:
Type 1 diabetes [OR] Treatment with insulin [OR] Body weight > 140 kg [OR] HbA1c > 75 mmol/mol [OR] Treatment with GLP-1 analogues, Dipeptidyl peptidase-4 inhibitors [OR] glitazones [OR] Chronic kidney disease [OR] Hepatic disease [OR] Pancreatitis [OR] Inflammatory bowel disease [OR] Osteoporosis [OR] Family [OR] personal history of medullary thyroid carcinoma [OR] Treatment with glucocorticoids [OR] Hormone replacement therapy [OR] Diabetic gastroparesis [OR] Pregnancy [OR] lactation
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
