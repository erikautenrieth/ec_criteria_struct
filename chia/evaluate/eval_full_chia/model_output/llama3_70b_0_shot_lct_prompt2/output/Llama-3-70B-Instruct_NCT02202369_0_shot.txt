<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Subjects undergoing a single level lumbar decompression [AND] fusion
> 18 years of age [AND] < 70 years of age
The subject is willing [AND] able to understand, sign [AND] date the study specific patient informed consent [AND] HIPAA authorization to volunteer participation in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] liver disease (documented liver function test abnormality)
Patients [AND] renal disease (documented glomerular filtration rate < 60mL/min/1.73m2)
Patients [AND] a baseline (pre-operative) opioid use greater than 30 mg of morphine equivalents/day
Patients [AND] active alcohol dependence
Patients [AND] active illicit drug dependence
Patients [NOT] > 18 years of age [AND] [NOT] < 70 years of age
Patients allergic to any medication given in either arm (list medications)
Patients who have a seizure disorder
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
