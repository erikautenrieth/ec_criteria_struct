Inclusion Criteria:
Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus patients [AND] who had been diagnosed within the previous 12 months [AND] with HbA1c levels of 8.0-12.0% [AND] did [NOT] have a medical history related to diabetes [AND] did [NOT] display proliferative retinopathy

Exclusion Criteria:
The use of weight-lowering drugs [OR] any investigational blood-glucose [OR] lipid-lowering agent (other than statins [OR] ezetimibe) within the past 3 months
Previous treatment [AND] with systemic corticosteroids [OR] a change in dosage of thyroid hormones in the previous 6 weeks
The use of insulin within the 3 months prior to screening
Others
