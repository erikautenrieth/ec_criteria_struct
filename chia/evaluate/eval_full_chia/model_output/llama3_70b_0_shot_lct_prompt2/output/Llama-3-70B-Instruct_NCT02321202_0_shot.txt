Inclusion Criteria:
The cirrhotic malnourished patients [AND] who were diagnosed as liver cancer preoperatively [AND] underwent hepatectomy were consecutively enrolled.

Exclusion Criteria:
Contraindication for hepatectomy, including gastrointestinal hemorrhage [OR] severe hemorrhagic disorders [OR] explicit acute nonspecific infectious lesion [OR] overt ascites [OR] Child-Pugh Score C [OR] indocyanine green retention rate at 15min (ICGR15)＞30%(12) [OR] serum hepatitis B virus (HBV)-DNA＞126 copies/ml [AND] serum alanine aminotransferase (ALT) ＞ 2×ULN [OR] serum triglycerides＞2.0 mmol/L [OR] circulatory shock [OR] stroke [OR] acute myocardial infarction [OR] renal failure [OR] coma of unknown cause 
[NOT] Pregnancy 
Age of＜18y [OR] ＞75y 
Performed intraoperative ablation 
Unresectable tumor during operation 
[NOT] Allergic reactions against fish [OR] egg proteins
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
