<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
. Inclusion criteria are American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) physical status I [AND] II [OR] III, age between 18 [AND] 70 years [AND] body mass index (BMI) between 20 [AND] 35 kg/m2.

Exclusion Criteria:
Exclusion criteria are pregnancy, patients [AND] contraindications to regional anesthesia, allergy to LAs, patients taking opioids regularly due to chronic pain, use of anticoagulation drugs other than acetylsalicylic acid [OR] dipyridamole, atrioventricular block, diabetes, [NOT] having any other medical condition.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
