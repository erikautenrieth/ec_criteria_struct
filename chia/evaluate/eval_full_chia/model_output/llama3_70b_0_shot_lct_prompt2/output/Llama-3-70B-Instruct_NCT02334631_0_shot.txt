Inclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] undergoing small bowel video capsule endoscopy

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with a contraindication to VCE ([OR] small bowel strictures [OR] oropharyngeal dysphagia [OR] pregnancy [OR] patients [AND] who are [NOT] surgical candidates)

Inpatient procedures [AND] for active GI bleeding
