Inclusion Criteria:
All patients admitted to the Duke CICU, [AND] who require intubation [AND] sedation for mechanical ventilation that is expected to be >24 hours in duration will be included, unless they meet the specified exclusion criteria.
Patients intubated within one hour prior to care transition to the CICU will also be screened for inclusion.

Exclusion Criteria:
Exclusion criteria include patients [AND] following resuscitation from cardiac arrest who are treated on the cooling protocol,
patients [AND] who have suffered a neurologic event (seizure [OR] stroke) [OR] who have baseline dementia, both of which could limit delirium assessment,
patients [AND] with child class B [OR] C liver disease,
patients [AND] with known allergy to study medications.
