<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with craniotomy for supratentorial tumors under general anesthesia
American Society of Anaesthesiologists (ASA) 2 [AND] stable ASA 3 patients
Elective surgery
Patients [AND] with Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS) 15/15

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with a history of allergy [OR] hypersensitivity to tramadol.
History of epilepsy [OR] convulsions due to any reason.
Chronic usage of analgesic drugs.
Patients [AND] using monoamine oxidase inhibitors.
Patients [AND] with clinical signs of raised ICP.
Obesity (women [AND] with a body mass index >35 kg/m2 [OR] men [AND] with a body mass index >42 kg/m2)
[NOT] Language barrier.
Patients [AND] taking B-blockers [OR] Ca channel blockers.
Patients [NOT] above 65 years of age ( Physiology difference)
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
