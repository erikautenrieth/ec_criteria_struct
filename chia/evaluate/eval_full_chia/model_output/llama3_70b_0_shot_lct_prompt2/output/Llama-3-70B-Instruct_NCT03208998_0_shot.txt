<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
HBsAg [AND] HBeAg positive for more than 6 months, HBV DNA detectable [AND] with ALT level abnormal lasted for three months [AND] at least time 190 IU/L [OR] liver puncture biopsy demonstrated apparent inflammation, [NOT] ever treated before enrolled.

Exclusion Criteria:
Active consumption of alcohol [OR] and/or drugs
Co-infection [AND] with human immunodeficiency virus [OR] hepatitis C virus [OR] hepatitis D virus
History of autoimmune hepatitis
Psychiatric disease
Evidence of neoplastic diseases of the liver
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
