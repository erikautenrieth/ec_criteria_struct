<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
patients affected by mono-lateral symptomatic knee articular degenerative pathology [AND] with history of chronic (for at least 4 months) pain [OR] swelling;
imaging findings of degenerative changes of the joint (osteoarthritis [OR] chondropathy) [AND] with Kellgren Lawrence Score from 0 to 3 at X-ray evaluation.

Exclusion Criteria:
age > 80 years;
Kellgren-Lawrence score at X-ray evaluation > 3;
major axial deviation (varus >5° [OR] valgus > 5°),
systemic disorders such as diabetes [OR] rheumatoid arthritis [OR] haematological diseases (coagulopathy) [OR] severe cardiovascular diseases [OR] infections [OR] immunodepression;
patients [NOT] in therapy without anticoagulants [OR] antiaggregants;
use of NSAIDs in the 5 days before blood donation;
patients [NOT] with Hb values ≥ 11 g/dl [AND] platelet values ≥ 150,000/mmc.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
