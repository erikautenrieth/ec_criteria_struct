<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Age 18-65
scheduled to receive ISB [AND] general anesthesia as a day surgery patient for rotator cuff repair [OR] acromioplasty, as a part of planned routine care

Exclusion Criteria:
Allergy [OR] sensitivity [OR] absolute contraindications to any of the medications involved in the study
preexisting CNS depression [OR] taking regularly medication that cause CNS depression
preexisting cognitive deficits [OR] dementia [OR] delirium
severe respiratory comorbidities (e.g. chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR] pneumonia [OR] respiratory failure)
sleep disordered breathing (diagnosed OSA [OR] obesity hypoventilation syndrome)
pregnancy [OR] breast feeding
history of chronic pain [OR] regular (at least once daily) opioid use preoperatively
renal impairment - CrCl =60 mL/minute
[NOT] fluent in English to be able to participate in the study process, including consent [AND] phone interview
Body Mass Index >35
[NOT] ability to take oral medication.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
