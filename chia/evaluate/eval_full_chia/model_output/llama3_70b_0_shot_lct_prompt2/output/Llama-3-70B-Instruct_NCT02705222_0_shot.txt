<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Perimenopausal women [AND] complaining of abnormal uterine bleeding (menorrhagia, metrorrhagia, polymenorrhoea [OR] polymenorrhagia) [NOT] without local gynecological cause.
Failure of medical treatment for at least 3 months.

Exclusion Criteria:
Age < 45 [OR] > 55 years.
Blood disorders [OR] coagulopathy.
Diagnosed [OR] suspected local gynecologic lesion (polyp [OR] adenomyosis [OR] myoma [OR] malignancy [OR] cervical pathology).
[NOT] Use intrauterine contraceptive device.
Pregnancy related conditions.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
