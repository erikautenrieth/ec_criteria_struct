<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patient [AND] has been fully informed [AND] has signed an IRB approved informed consent form within 7 days (Day 7-13) prior to POD 15 [AND] is willing [AND] able to follow study procedure
Patient [AND] is a primary liver transplant recipient
Patient [AND] is 20 to 70 years of age
Patient [AND] should be clearly conscious, fully understand [AND] able to answer questionnaire

Exclusion Criteria:
Patient [OR] has previously received [OR] is receiving an organ transplant other than a liver
Patient [AND] currently requires dialysis
Recipient [OR] donor is known to be seropositive for human immunodeficiency virus (HIV)
Patient [AND] has received a liver transplant from a non-heart beating donor
Patient who is HCV negative [AND] has received an HCV positive (HCV RNA by PCR [OR] HCV antibody) donor liver
Patient who is HbsAg negative [AND] has received an HbsAg positive (HBV DNA by PCR [OR] HBV antibody) donor liver
Patient [AND] has received a liver transplant from a deceased donor > 70 years of age
Patient [NOT] has a current malignancy [OR] a history of malignancy (within the past 5 years), except hepatocellular carcinoma within UCSF Criteria [AND] basal [OR] non-metastatic squamous cell carcinoma of skin that has been treated successfully
Patient [AND] is hemodynamically unstable on POD 15
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
