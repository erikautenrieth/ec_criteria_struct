<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Above 18 years of age
Symptomatic, permanent AF of at least three months duration
Resting heart rate =80 bpm
[AND] Signed informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Congestive heart failure
Ischemic heart disease
Hypotension (Systolic blood pressure <100 mmHg)
Treatment [OR] with class I [OR] III antiarrhythmic drugs
[NOT] Severe hepatic [OR] renal failure
Pregnancy [OR] lactation
[NOT] Hypersensitivity [OR] contradictions to study drugs
Atrio-ventricular conduction disturbances
Thyrotoxicosis
Life limiting disease [OR] substance abuse which may affect participation
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
