<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Female subjects aged >/= 18 years [AND] of reproductive age.
Capacity to give consent for study participation, [AND] after being adequately informed of the aims, benefits, risks, time [OR] motion of the study.

Exclusion Criteria:
Participation in another clinical trial.
Known [OR] suspected (or history of) malignancy [OR] chronic illness.
Serious organic [OR] mental disease diagnosed by a psychiatrist (e.g., major depression currently treated with antidepressant medication) suspected on the basis of the medical history [OR] clinical examination.
[NOT] Conditions that may affect the compliance to the study.
[NOT] Contraindications to therapy with the study drug [OR] hypersensitivity to the study drug (active ingredient [OR] excipients of the formulation).
