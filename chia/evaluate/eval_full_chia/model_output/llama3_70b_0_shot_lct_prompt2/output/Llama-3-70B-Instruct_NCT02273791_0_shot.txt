<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Women [AND] with PCOS as defined by the Rotterdam criteria.
Presence of at least 2 cryopreserved good quality cleavage-stage embryo (good quality cleavage-stage embryos display stage-specific cell division, have blastomeres of fairly equal size [AND] with few [OR] no cytoplasmic fragments).

Exclusion Criteria:
Moderate [OR] severe endometriosis.
Hydrosalpinx.
Uterine abnormalities [OR] myoma.
Previous uterine surgery.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
