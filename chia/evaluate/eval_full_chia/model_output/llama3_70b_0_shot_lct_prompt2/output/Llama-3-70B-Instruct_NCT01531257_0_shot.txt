<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
1. Male [AND] female recipients of all races, ≥18 years of age. 
2. Patients undergoing primary [OR] subsequent deceased-donor [OR] living donor kidney transplantation. 
3. Subject [AND]/or guardian must be able to provide informed consent. 
4. Subject [AND]/or guardian must be able to comply with the study protocol. 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Need for combined organ transplantation with an extra-renal organ [OR] islet cell transplant. 
2. Recipients of previous non-renal solid organ [OR] islet cell transplantation. 
3. Infection with HIV. 
4. [NOT] Ability [OR] willingness of a participant [AND]/or guardian to provide informed consent
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
