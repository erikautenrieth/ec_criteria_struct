<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
18 years [OR] older
Type 1 [OR] 2 diabetes
PDR patients requiring surgical intervention for complications of vitreous hemorrhage [OR] traction retinal detachment [AND] pre-operative IVC treatment.
women postmenopausal for 12 months before the study, surgically sterile, [OR] not pregnant [AND] on effective contraception.

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] previous retinal vein occlusion.
[NOT] any intraocular surgery within the previous 12 months.
myopia of > [OR] = to 8 diopters.
[NOT] active ocular [OR] periocular infection
[NOT] treatment with an investigational agent for any condition 60 days prior to enrollment.
[NOT] evidence of severe cardiac disease.
[NOT] clinically significant peripheral vascular disease ([AND] previous surgery, [OR] amputation, [OR] symptoms of claudication)
[NOT] uncontrolled hypertension ([AND] treated systolic blood pressure > 155 mmHg [OR] diastolic blood pressure > 95 mmHg)
[NOT] stroke within the preceding 12 months.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
