<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Current diagnosis of otolaryngeal cancer [AND] undergoing surgery [AND] with general anesthesia
Competent [AND] to provide informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Emergency surgery
Monitored Anesthesia Care (i.e., regional anesthesia alone [AND] without plans for general anesthesia)
Surgery involving the eye [OR] eyebrow [OR] forehead [OR] frontal scalp near the sensor placement
Poor health literacy
Allergy [OR] have experienced any drug reaction to ketamine
Pregnant [OR] lactating
Currently in active alcohol withdrawal [NOT] able to participate
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
