Inclusion Criteria:
Methadone-maintained cocaine-dependent patients [AND] use between 1g to 2g a day [OR] 1 to 3 times a week

Exclusion Criteria:
use more than 2g a day [OR] 5 times a week to everyday 
Subjects who are diagnosed as suffering from psychotic illness according to DSM-IV (Axis 1)22 [OR] with a history of CNS disease [OR] a history of infection that might affect CNS (HIV [OR] syphilis [OR] cytomegalovirus [OR] herpes) [OR] a history of head injury with loss of consciousness [OR] pregnant women [OR] [NOT] excluded.
