<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Infection [AND] with Plasmodium falciparum [OR] P. vivax either alone [OR] mixed
Age >12 months
Weight >5kg
Living [AND] in the study clusters

Exclusion Criteria:
General danger signs [OR] symptoms of severe malaria
Anaemia, defined as Hb <[NOT] 9g/dl
G6PD deficiency (as determined by FST)
Pregnant women as determined by Urine ß-HCG pregnancy test
Known hypersensitivity to any of the drugs given
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
