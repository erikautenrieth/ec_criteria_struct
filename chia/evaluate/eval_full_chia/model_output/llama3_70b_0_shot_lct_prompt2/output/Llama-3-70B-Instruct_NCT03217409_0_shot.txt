<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Subjects [AND] = 19 [OR] = 75 years of age
Subjects [AND] undergoing treatment for type 2 diabetes
Subjects [AND] undergoing treatment of statin for hypercholesterolemia
Fasting LDL-C [AND] = 250mg/dL at the screening visit
Fasting LDL-C [AND] =70mg/dL [OR] = 160mg/dL at the randomization visit
Fasting TG<500mg/dL

Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects [AND] with hypersensitivity reaction to Statin [AND] Ezetimibe
Subjects [AND] with severe kidney disease
Subjects [AND] with HIV positive result at the screening
Subjects [AND] pregnant [OR] breast-feeding
Subjects [AND] with taking any medication affecting level of LDL (Fenofibrate, Omega 3 fatty acid, etc.)
Subjects [AND] Insulin-treated
Subjects [NOT] meeting other exclusions applied
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
