Inclusion Criteria:
Lobectomy [OR] pneumonectomy
Esophagectomy
Radical (total) cystectomy
Pancreatectomy
Partial hepatectomy
Hyperthermic intraperitoneal chemotherapy (HIPEC)
Gastrectomy (subtotal [OR] total)
Cholecystectomy [OR] bile duct resection

Exclusion Criteria:
Age less than 18 years
American Society of Anesthesiologist Class 5
Projected life expectancy less than 30 days
[NOT] Known [AND] suspected hypersensitivity to either propofol, e.g. egg [OR] soy allergy, [OR] volatile general anesthetic agents
[NOT] Known [AND] suspected history of malignant hyperthermia
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
