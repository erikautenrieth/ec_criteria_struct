<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
35-75 years old;
diagnosed as type 2 diabetes [AND] according to the criteria of the World Health Organization in 1999.

Exclusion Criteria:
type 1 diabetes [OR] specific types of diabetes [OR] gestational diabetes [OR] pregestational diabetes;
acute cardiovascular [OR] cerebrovascular accidents within past 3 months;
severe hepatic [OR] renal dysfunction;
malignant tumor;
allergic history [OR] contraindication for any drugs in trials;
taking part in other clinical trials;
[NOT] obviously poor compliance.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
