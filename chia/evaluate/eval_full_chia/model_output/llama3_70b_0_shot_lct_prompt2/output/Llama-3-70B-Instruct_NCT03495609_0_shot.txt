<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
premenopausal women [AND] BRCA1 carrier

Exclusion Criteria:
History of allergic reaction to compounds of similar chemical [OR] biologic composition to hCG
receiving medication that could interfere with the study protocol objectives ([AND] hormonal contraceptives [OR] androgens [OR] prednisone [OR] thyroid hormones [OR] insulin)
previous treatment with follicle stimulating hormone for assisted reproduction
[NOT] controlled intercurrent illness
Heart disease
Severe cognitive decline
Psychiatric disease
HIV positive
Hepatitis B [OR] C infection
