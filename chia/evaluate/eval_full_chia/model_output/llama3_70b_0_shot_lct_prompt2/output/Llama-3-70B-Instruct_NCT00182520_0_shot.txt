<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Outpatient [AND] with primary DSM- IV OCD
Completion of a 14-week open label trial of one [OR] the following SRI's: fluoxetine 80 mg/day, paroxetine 60 mg/day, fluvoxamine 300 mg/day, clomipramine 250 mg/day, sertraline 200 mg/day, citalopram 60 mg/day, escitalopram 30 mg/day [AND] demonstrating a [NOT] full response to SRI treatment ([AND] CGI-I of 3 [OR] 4, Y-BOCS reduction of < 35%)
Stable ([AND] 8 wks [OR] longer) concurrent medications including benzodiazepines, sedative hypnotics, antipsychotics, [AND] antidepressants.

Exclusion Criteria:
Any other primary DSM-IV diagnosis; DSM-IV criteria for body dysmorphic disorder, bipolar affective disorder, schizophrenia, psychotic disorder, current alcohol/substance abuse.
A previous adequate trial of topiramate
Comorbid major depressive disorder diagnosis which predates OCD diagnosis
Cognitive behavioural therapy [OR] additional psychotherapy in past four months
[NOT] Allergy [OR] hypersensitivity to topiramate
BMI < 20
History of kidney stones
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
