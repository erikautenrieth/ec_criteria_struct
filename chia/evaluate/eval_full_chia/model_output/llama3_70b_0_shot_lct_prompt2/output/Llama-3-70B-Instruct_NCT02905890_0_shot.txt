<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
BV positive by Nugent score [AND] HIV negative [AND] Capable of providing written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Currently pregnant [OR] using a reliable contraception (e.g. injectables [OR] intrauterine devices [OR] implant [OR] oral contraceptive pills)
Desiring pregnancy in the next year
History of tubal ligation [OR] hysterectomy
Contraindication to progestin-only contraceptives
[NOT] Able to comprehend consent material because of language barrier [OR] psychological difficulty
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
