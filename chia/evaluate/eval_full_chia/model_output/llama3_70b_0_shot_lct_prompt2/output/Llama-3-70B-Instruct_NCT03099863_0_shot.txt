Adult women [AND] at least 18 years of age
Elective Female Pelvic Medicine [AND] Reconstructive Surgery [OR] Gynecologic Minimally Invasive surgeries, including hysterectomy, suburethral sling [OR] pelvic organ prolapse repair that require cystoscopy.

Exclusion Criteria:
Surgeries that include: intradetrusor Botox [OR] vaginal mesh excision [OR] fistula repair
Pregnancy
History of nephrolithiasis
Allergy to study medications
[NOT] Congenital urogenital anomaly
Neurogenic bladder
