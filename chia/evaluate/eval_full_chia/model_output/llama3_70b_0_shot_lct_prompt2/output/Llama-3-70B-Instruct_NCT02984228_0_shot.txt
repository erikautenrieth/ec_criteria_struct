Inclusion Criteria:
English speaking/literate [AND] Age 18-100 years [AND] Visual analog score pain >= 5 [AND] Greater than [OR] equal to 3 months of pain after onset of symptoms that has failed conservative treatments [AND] Confirmation of glenohumeral OA via imaging [AND] Transient relief of symptoms after diagnostic intra-articular injection into the glenohumeral joint

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] English speaking/literate [OR] Painful active, concurrent cervical spine conditions [OR] Current non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID) use [OR] History of taking coumadin [OR] similar anticoagulant, [AND] having a known coagulopathy, bleeding dyscrasia, [OR] platelet count < 150,000/cubic mm [OR] Allergic reaction to poultry [OR] previous viscosupplementation [OR] Involved in workers' compensation [OR] active litigation involving affected shoulder [OR] [NOT] able to refrain from NSAID use for 5 days prior to [AND] 6 weeks after injection [OR] History of corticosteroid injection to affected shoulder within the last 3 months [OR] History of viscosupplementation [OR] platelet-rich plasma to affected shoulder within the last 6 months [OR] Presence of acute fracture [OR] History of shoulder tumor [OR] Known uncontrolled systemic illness ([AND] uncontrolled diabetes, human immunodeficiency virus, vasculitis, autoimmune/inflammatory disease) [OR] Psychiatric [AND] somatoform disorders
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
