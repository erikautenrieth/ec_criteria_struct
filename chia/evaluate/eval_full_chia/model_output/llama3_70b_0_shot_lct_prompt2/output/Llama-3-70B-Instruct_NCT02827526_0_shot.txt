<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients presenting for elective posterior spinal fusion surgery (lower thoracic, lumbar, sacral) [AND] ages 18-80

Exclusion Criteria:
Preoperative renal failure (defined as a serum creatinine > 2.0 mg/dL) [OR] American Society of Anesthesiologists Physical Status IV [OR] V
Pulmonary disease necessitating home oxygen therapy
Allergy to methadone [OR] hydromorphone [OR] ketamine
Preoperative recent history of opioid [OR] alcohol abuse
Significant liver disease
[NOT] Ability to use a PCA device [AND] speak the English language
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
