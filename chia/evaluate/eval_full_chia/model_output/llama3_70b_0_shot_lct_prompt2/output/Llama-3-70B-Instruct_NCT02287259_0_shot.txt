<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
[major depressive episode in type2 bipolar disorder [OR] bipolar disorder NOS.] (MADRS more than 20 point)
[AND] 18years to 65years
[AND] subjects [AND] who sign the informed consent document

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] have Diabetes [AND] abnormal metabolism of sugar
[NOT] noticed as bipolar disorder
[AND] have an organic brain disease
[AND] pregnant [OR] breastfeeding women
[NOT] have heart disease
[AND] have actively suicidal thought(Suicidal ideation score of MADRS is 6)
[AND] who are judged by the investigator to should be excluded from the study
