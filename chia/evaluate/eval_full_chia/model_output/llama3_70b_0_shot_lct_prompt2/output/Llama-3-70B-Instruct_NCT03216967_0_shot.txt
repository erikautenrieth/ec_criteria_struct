<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Adult patients
Kidney transplant recipients
Patients [AND] treated by a calcineurin inhibitor [OR] mycophenolic acid
Viremia >= 3 log UI/ml
Patients who have given written informed consent
Negative pregnancy test (blood ß-HCG dosage)

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Known proved BKV nephropathy
Hypersensitivity to everolimus [OR] sirolimus [OR] excipient
Concomitant treatment by leflunomide [OR] cidofovir [OR] sirolimus [OR] Millepertuis (Hypericum Perforatum)
Pregnant [OR] lactating women
Women of child bearing potential [NOT] using a birth control method
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
