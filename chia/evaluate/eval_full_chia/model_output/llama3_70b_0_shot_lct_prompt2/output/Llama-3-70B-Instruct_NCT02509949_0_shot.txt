Inclusion Criteria:
age > 17 [AND] < 60 years;
American Society of Anesthesiology (ASA) I [OR] II [OR] III;
admitted for living donor renal transplantation.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] a history of drug abuse;
preoperative history of schizophrenia [OR] epilepsy [OR] parkinsonism [OR] use of cholinesterase inhibitor [OR] [NOT] ability to communicate in the preoperative period ([NOT] coma [OR] [NOT] profound dementia [OR] [NOT] language barrier).
