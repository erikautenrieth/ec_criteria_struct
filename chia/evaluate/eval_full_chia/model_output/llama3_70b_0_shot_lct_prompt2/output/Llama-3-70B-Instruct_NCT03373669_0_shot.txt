<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Age =1 year, stratified into different age groups [AND] Living in the Waya Clinic Catchment Area [AND] Good health condition, [NOT] without clinically significant medical history (by participant [OR] guardian, in case of minor) [AND] [NOT] pregnant for female subjects [AND] Available to participate for the study duration, including all planned follow-up visits for up to 9 months from screening [AND] Signed informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Presence of a significant medical [OR] psychiatric condition (Examples include: Diagnosis [AND] treatment of tuberculosis (TB) [OR] HIV; renal insufficiency; hepatic disease; oral [OR] parenteral medication known to affect the immune function, such as corticosteroids, other immunosuppressant drugs; [OR] behavioural [OR] memory issues) [OR] Ever having received oral cholera vaccine [OR] Receipt of an investigational product (within 30 days before vaccination) [OR] History of diarrhoea in 7 days prior to first dose of vaccine (defined as =3 unformed loose stools in 24 hours) [OR] History of chronic diarrhea (lasting for more than 2 weeks in the past 6 months) [OR] Current use of laxatives, antacids, [OR] other agents to lower stomach acidity? [OR] Planning to become pregnant in the next 2 years.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
