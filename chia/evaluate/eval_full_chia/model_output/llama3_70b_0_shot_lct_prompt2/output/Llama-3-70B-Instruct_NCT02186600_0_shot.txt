Inclusion Criteria:
Women [AND] who are in their first 5 years of menopause 
Have a T score between -1 [AND] -2.49 at the femoral neck [OR] total hip [OR] L1-L4 spine

Have their health care provider's permission [AND] to enroll in the study.

Have osteoporosis

Currently take bisphosphonates [OR] estrogen replacement therapy [OR] glucocorticosteroids [OR] other drugs affecting bone 
Currently participate in a resistance training [OR] high impact weight bearing exercise program two [OR] more times weekly 
[NOT] weigh <300 lbs 
Have abnormal results for the following laboratory tests: serum 25(OH)D [OR] serum creatinine [OR] serum calcium [OR] PTH [OR] TSH 
Have Paget's disease [OR] heart disease [OR] uncontrolled hypertension [OR] renal disease [OR] other concomitant conditions that [NOT] prohibit participation in exercises [OR] risedronate therapy [OR] use of CaD supplements.
