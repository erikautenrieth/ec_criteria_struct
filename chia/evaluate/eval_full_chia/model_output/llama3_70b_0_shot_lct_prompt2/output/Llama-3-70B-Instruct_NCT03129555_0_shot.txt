Inclusion Criteria:
A diagnosis of VTE [AND] in outpatient clinic [OR] as discharge diagnosis after hospitalization.
A claimed prescription of a NOAC from a Danish pharmacy within 14 days of discharge [OR] outpatient clinic visit.

Exclusion Criteria:
A prescription of a NOAC within 90 days prior to hospitalization [OR] outpatient clinic visit for VTE.
Patients [AND] with NOAC preference apart from preference consistent [AND] with current cluster randomized NOAC.
Other contraindications mentioned [AND] in the "Summary of Product Characteristics" for the respective NOAC [AND] [NOT] consistent with the treatment.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
