<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
Primary B-NHL [OR] PTCL (ALK+ anaplastic large cell lymphoma [AND] NK(natural killer cell )/T cell lymphoma were excluded) [OR] HL patients confirmed by histopathology;
Ages =18 years old [AND] < 80 years old;
ECOG (Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group)score: 0-2
At least one measurable lesion;
Expected survival time=3 months;
Liver function: transaminase=2.5× upper limit of normal value [AND] bilirubin=1.5×upper limit of normal value;
Renal function: serum creatinine is 44-133 mmol/L;
Routine blood test:WBC=3.0×109/L [AND] Neutrophils=1.5×109/L [AND] Hb=100g/L [AND] Platelet=80×109/L; LVEF=50%;
New York Heart Association (NYHA) heart function classification is I-II grade
[AND] signed informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] severe complications [OR] severe infection;
Invasion of central nervous system;
Patients [AND] severe heart disease history, including ventricular tachycardia (VT) [OR] atrial fibrillation (AF) [OR] heart block [OR] myocardial infarction (MI) [OR] congestive heart failure (CHF) [OR] coronary heart disease patients needed therapy;
patients [AND] severe allergic constitution [OR] those who are allergic to [OR] intolerant of drug composition in chemotherapy regimens; [AND] with other malignant tumors in the past 5 years;
patients [NOT] received doxorubicin therapy, total cumulative dose of adriamycin was more than 300 mg/m2 [OR] total cumulative dose of epirubicin was more than 450 mg/m2;
Patients [NOT] participate in other clinical studies;
Other patients who are [NOT] suitable for the study.

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
