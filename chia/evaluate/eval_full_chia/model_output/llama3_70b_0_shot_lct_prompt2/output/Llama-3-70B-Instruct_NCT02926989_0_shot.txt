<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Acutely ill hospitalised children [AND] need for intravenous fluid therapy

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] An initial plasma sodium concentration of lower than 130 mmol/L [OR] an initial plasma sodium concentration of higher than 150 mmol/L [OR] an initial plasma potassium concentration of lower than 3.0 mmol/L [OR] need for 10% glucose solution [OR] diabetes [OR] diabetes insipidus [OR] diabetic ketoacidosis [OR] renal disease [AND] that needs dialysis [OR] protocol-determined chemotherapy hydration [OR] severe liver disease [OR] inborn errors of metabolism [AND] that need protocol-determined fluid therapy
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
