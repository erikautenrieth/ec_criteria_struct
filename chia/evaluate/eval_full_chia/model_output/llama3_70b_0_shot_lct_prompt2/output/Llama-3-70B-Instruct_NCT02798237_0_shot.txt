<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
= 20 years of age;
diagnosis of stroke (>6months) [AND] sedentary [OR] insufficiently active;
have a writing medical permission to participate in the training program.

Exclusion Criteria:
cognitive impairment (Mini-Mental Status Examination score: illiterate 13 points; elementary [OR] middle school 18 points; [AND] high-school 26 points; [OR] inability to respond to verbal command);
[NOT] ability to walk independently for at least 10 minutes, [AND] with [OR] without walking devices;
pain [OR] other disorders precluding their participation.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
