<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis of diabetes mellitus
Best corrected visual acuity 20/[OR]32 - 20/[OR]320
Diabetic macular edema [AND] involving the center of the macula
Optical coherence tomography central subfield thickness of at least 250 microns

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] History of anti-vascular endothelial growth factor treatment in the past 12 months
[NOT] Any diabetic macular edema treatment in the past 4 months
Heart attack [OR] stroke [OR] transient ischemic attack [OR] acute congestive heart failure within 4 months
