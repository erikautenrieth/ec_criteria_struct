<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Adult Patients [AND] with Overt Hepatic Encephalopathy.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with active GIT bleeding.
Patients [AND] with history of bowel obstruction [OR] perforation.
Patients [AND] with history of allergy to PEG.
Treatment [AND] with rifaximin [OR] neomycin in the previous 7 days.
Patients [AND] with major psychiatric illness.
Patients [AND] receiving benzodiazepines [AND] narcotics.
Patients [AND] with compromised renal.
Patients [AND] receiving medications highly bound to plasma proteins, eg. Warfarin.
Patients [AND] who are Pregnant [OR] lactating women.
Patients [AND] with Fulminant hepatic failure.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
