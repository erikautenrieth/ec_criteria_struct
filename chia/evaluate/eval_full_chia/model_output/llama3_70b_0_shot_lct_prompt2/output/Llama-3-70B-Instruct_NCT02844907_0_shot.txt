<EDITED_CRITERIA>

Inclusion Criteria:
Body Mass Index (BMI) = 35 kg/m2 [AND] HbA1c = 5.7% [AND] Ability to speak [AND] understand English

Exclusion Criteria:
Rheumatoid arthritis [OR] Diabetes [OR] immediate family history of diabetes
Coronary artery disease [OR] Congestive heart failure
Pulmonary disorders, including COPD [OR] asthma
Malabsorptive GI disease, such as celiac disease [OR] gastric bypass
[NOT] Significant hepatic disease
Renal insufficiency (eGFR < 60 mL/kg/min)
Anemia (hematocrit < 34%) as measured at screening visit
Pregnant females
Consumption of daily medications that alter glucose metabolism [OR] GI function (glucocorticoids, psychotropics, narcotics, metoclopramide)
Consumption [OR] injection of insulin
Apparent sensitivity to any of the study peptides as determined by the skin test
Diagnosis [OR] h/o PTSD, depression, substance use, mental health problems, sleep disorders, HPA disruption [OR] TBI

</EDITED_CRITERIA>
