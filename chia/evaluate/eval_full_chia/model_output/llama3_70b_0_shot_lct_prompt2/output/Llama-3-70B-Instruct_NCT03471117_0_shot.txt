<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
CKD patients classified as Stage 3 [AND] 4 of National Kidney Foundation Classification [AND] with estimated glomerular filtration rate (GFR) between 15 [OR] 59 mL/min/1.73 m2 according to the Modification of Diet in Renal Disease (MDRD) formula based on serum creatinine, age, gender, [AND] race.
Men [OR] women 35 to 70 years of age

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Allergy to Glitazones
Myocardial infarction
Heart failure
Angina
History of kidney stones
Liver disease (abnormal liver enzymes)
Anemia (hemoglobin <8 g/dl)
Cancer [AND] current treatment
Previous organ transplantation
Immunosuppressant therapy
Human immunodeficiency virus infection
Pregnancy [OR] lactating
Current tobacco use
Dilantin [AND] oral contraceptive usage due to potential drug interaction [AND] glitazones
Self-identified history of hypoglycemia
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
