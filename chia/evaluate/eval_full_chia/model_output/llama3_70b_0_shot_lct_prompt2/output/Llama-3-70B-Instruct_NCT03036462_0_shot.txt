<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Patients aged at least 18 years
Patients [AND] with chronic heart failure present for at least 12 months
Confirmed presence of iron deficiency
Serum haemoglobin of 9.5 [OR] 14.0 g/dL

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Hypersensitivity to the active substance, [OR] to FCM [OR] any of its excipients
Known serious hypersensitivity to other parenteral iron products
Anaemia [NOT] attributed to iron deficiency, e.g. other microcytic anaemia
Evidence of iron overload [OR] disturbances in the utilisation of iron
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
