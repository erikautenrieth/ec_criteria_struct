<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
CHB patients [AND] who had received NAs for more than 12 months.
Hepatitis B e antigen (HBeAg)-negative [AND] anti-HBeAg positive.
Hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg) positive [AND] <1500 IU/mL.
Hepatitis B virus DNA [NOT] detectable(Roche Cobas).

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with liver cirrhosis, Hepatocellular Carcinoma [OR] other malignancies.
Patients [AND] with other factors causing liver diseases.
Pregnant [AND] lactating women.
Patients [AND] with concomitant HIV infection [OR] congenital immune deficiency diseases.
Patients [AND] with diabetes, autoimmune diseases.
Patients [AND] with important organ dysfunctions.
Patients [AND] with serious complications (e.g., infection, hepatic encephalopathy, hepatorenal syndrome, gastrointestinal bleeding).
Patients [AND] who receive antineoplastic [OR] immunomodulatory therapy in the past 12 months.
Patients [AND] who [NOT] can come back to clinic for follow-up on schedule.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
