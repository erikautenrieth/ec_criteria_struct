Inclusion Criteria:
Has a cataract [AND] is expected to undergo clear corneal cataract surgery [AND] with phacoemulsification [AND] implantation of a posterior chamber intraocular lens 
Has a potential post-operative pinhole corrected Snellen VA of at least 20/200 [OR] better in both eyes 

Exclusion Criteria:
Any intraocular inflammation in the study eye present during the screening slit lamp examination 
Score greater than "0" [AND] on the Ocular Pain Assessment in the study eye at Screening 
Any intraocular inflammation in the study eye present during the screening slit lamp examination
