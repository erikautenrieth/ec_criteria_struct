Inclusion Criteria:
submucosal [OR] intramural [OR] subserosal leiomyomas,
symptoms of menometrorrhagia [OR] menstrual disorder [OR] infertility [OR] pelvic pain

Exclusion Criteria:
endometrial hyperplasia [AND] with atypia,
estrogen-progestin therapy in the 2 months before enrollment,
autoimmune diseases,
chronic [OR] metabolic [OR] systemic [OR] and endocrine disorders, including hyperandrogenism [OR] hyperprolactinemia [OR] diabetes mellitus [OR] and thyroid disease,
hypogonadotropic hypogonadism,
majors clinical conditions
