Inclusion Criteria:
patients aged >18 [AND] <75, left ventricle ejection fraction (LVEF) >50%, multivessel coronary disease detected by coronarography, indication to receive a CABG, stable CAD. All diabetics [OR] and non diabetics.

Exclusion Criteria:
acute myocardial infarction [OR] heart failure [OR] neoplastic disease [OR] chronic diseases that may affect the inflammatory profile both systemic [AND] epicardial (cancer [OR] chronic intestinal inflammation [OR] hepatitis [OR] AIDS); life expectancy < 6 months, previous CABG [AND] and/or other open heart surgery intervention, acute coronary syndrome
