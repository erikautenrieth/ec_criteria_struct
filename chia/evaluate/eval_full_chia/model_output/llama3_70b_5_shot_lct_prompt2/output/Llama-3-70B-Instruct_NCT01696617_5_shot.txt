Inclusion Criteria:
Age : 18-65
Patients with major depressive disorder according to DSM-IV criteria that have lasted >8 weeks
MADRS total score of 18 or higher
Patients who responded inadequately (a score of >18 on the MADRS) to first-line antidepressant treatment of 4 week duration
Current use of standard antidepressant treatment in monotherapy [OR] combination of 2 antidepressants : escitalopram (10 - 20mg/d) [OR], fluoxetine(20 - 40mg/d) [OR], paroxetine CR(25 - 50mg/d) [OR], sertraline(100 - 150mg/d) [OR], mirtazapine (15 - 45mg/d) [OR], duloxetine (30 - 60mg/d) [OR] or venlafaxine ER(150-225mg/d)

Exclusion Criteria:
Past history of hypersensitivity [AND] to aripiprazole
Primary diagnosis of MDD with psychotic feature [OR], bipolar disorder [OR], schizophrenia [OR], schizoaffective disorder [OR], other psychotic disorder [OR] or anxiety disorder [OR], a history of alcohol/ drug abuse within the past 12 months [OR], or a diagnosis of dementia
Clinically significant current Axis II (DSM-IV-TR) diagnosis
A significant risk of suicide corroborated by a score of =5 on item 10(suicidal thoughts) on the MADRS scale [OR] by clinical judgment of the investigator
Pregnancy [OR] or in breast-feeding
Presence of a serious medical illness including cardiac [OR], hepatic [OR], renal [OR], respiratory [OR], endocrinologic [OR], neurologic [OR], or hematologic disease [OR] physical disorder judged to significantly affect central nervous system function
Patients taking antipsychotics [OR], mood stabilizer [OR] or any psychotropic medications besides antidepressants, except benzodiazepines [OR] or beta blockers [OR] hypnotics
Patients with past treatment failures of aripiprazole
