Inclusion Criteria:
histologically [AND] diagnosed primary classical osteosarcoma in extremities
staging IIB
MRI showing [NOT] no skip lesion
receive standard neo-adjuvant chemotherapy [AND] adjuvant chemotherapy [AND] and standard surgical treatment

Exclusion Criteria:
a history of non-standard treatment(chemotherapy [OR] or surgery)
secondary osteosarcoma [OR] or well-differentiated parosteal osteosarcoma
evident dysfunction of cardia [OR] liver [OR] and kidney [OR], or pregnant women [OR] or women during lactation
