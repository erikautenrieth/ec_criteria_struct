Inclusion Criteria:
subjects older than 35 years
diagnosis of moderate [OR] to very severe COPD (FEV1 <80% predicted), according to the GesEPOC criteria, established at least 3 months
current [OR] former smoker with an accumulated consumption >10 packs x year
hospital admission [AND] for COPD exacerbation

Exclusion Criteria:
osteoarticular [OR], neuromuscular [OR] or cognitive limitation that prevents ambulation
previous diagnosis of active neoplastic disease
institutionalized patients [OR] alcohol consumption >60 g/day
patient belonging to another health sector in the Community of Madrid [OR] or other community
participation in another study within 6 months prior
