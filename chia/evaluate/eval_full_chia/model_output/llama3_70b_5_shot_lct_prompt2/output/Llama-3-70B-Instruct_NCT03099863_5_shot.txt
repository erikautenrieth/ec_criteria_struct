Inclusion Criteria:
Adult women at least 18 years of age
Elective Female Pelvic Medicine and Reconstructive Surgery [OR] Gynecologic Minimally Invasive surgeries including hysterectomy [OR], suburethral sling [OR], and pelvic organ prolapse repair that require cystoscopy.
Exclusion Criteria:
Surgeries that include: intradetrusor Botox [OR], vaginal mesh excision [OR], and fistula repair
Pregnancy
History of nephrolithiasis
Allergy to study medications
Congenital urogenital anomaly
Neurogenic bladder
