Inclusion Criteria:
Only patients with atrial fibrillation [AND] above 18 years [AND] with TTR <50% based on the last three values of INR will be included in this study.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients will not be included if they have reached a stable dose of warfarin [OR] liver dysfunction [OR] alcoholism [OR] use of another anticoagulant [OR] use of chemotherapy [OR] or if they do [NOT] meet the inclusion criteria
