Inclusion Criteria:
Duration of SCI =1 year;
Level of SCI C3-T1, [AND] AIS A [OR] B;
Age between 18 [AND] 65 years.

Exclusion Criteria:
Evidence of sympathetic integrity below the lesion level by the skin axon-reflex vasodilatation (SkARV) test;
Known allergies to midodrine hydrochloride;
PMH of diagnosed heart disease [OR], kidney disease [OR], peripheral vascular disease [OR], or cerebral vascular disease [OR], or diabetes mellitus;
Hypertension (BP>140/90 mmHg);
Untreated thyroid disease;
Acute illness [OR] infection;
Current smoker;
Pregnancy.
