Inclusion Criteria:
Male [OR] Female.
No age restriction.
Diagnosed with an infection [AND] related stone.
Medically fit [AND] for definitive surgical management of stone.
Life expectancy greater than one year.
Stone free [AND] after definitive surgical therapy defined as fragments less than 3mm.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with medical comorbidities [AND] preventing them from definitive surgical therapy.
Patients with persistent stone burden [AND] following definitive surgical therapy.
