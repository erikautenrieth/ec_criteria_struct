Inclusion Criteria:
Patient harboring a GRE [OR] CRE bacteria
Colonization confirmed by our microbiology department, [AND] including at least 3 positives swabs in the last month

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant woman [OR] breastfeeding
immunosuppression [AND] including AIDS, corticosteroids over 60mg/day
ongoing antibiotic treatment [AND] at the day of inclusion
impossibility [AND] to obtain a signed consent form.
