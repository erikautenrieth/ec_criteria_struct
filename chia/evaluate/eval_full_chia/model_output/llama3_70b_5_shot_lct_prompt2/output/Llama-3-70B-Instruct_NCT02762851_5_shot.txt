Inclusion Criteria:
Age = 18 years [AND] NYHA (New York Heart Association) functional class II [OR] III [OR] and IV

Exclusion Criteria:
Anaphylactic reaction to a previous dose of TIV(trivalent influenza vaccine)
Known IgE( Immunoglobulin E)-mediated hypersensitivity to eggs manifested as hives [OR] swelling of the mouth and throat [OR] difficulty in breathing [OR] hypotension [OR] or shock
Guillain-Barré syndrome within eight weeks of a previous influenza vaccine
Anaphylactic reaction to neomycin
Patients who have had influenza vaccine in two of the three previous years
