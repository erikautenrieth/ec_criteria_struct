Inclusion Criteria:
age =18 at screening
[NOT] not intending to move away from the clinic's catchment area for the next 2 years
HIV-1 antibody negative
reports commercial sex work
contact information is provided
written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
HIV infection at screening
participation in previous [OR] or concurrent HIV vaccine trials
lactating [OR], pregnant [OR] or planning pregnancy
renal function impairment (serum creatinine >1.5 mg/dl) [OR], Fanconi syndrome
abnormal liver function tests (AST/ALT > 43 U/L) [OR], liver disease [OR], viral hepatitis [OR], hepatitis B virus (HBV) infection
serum phosphorus <2.2mg/dl [OR], osteoporosis
known sensitivity to components of the Truvada® formulation
any immunosuppressive treatment [AND], such as systemic corticosteroids
assumption of medication that interacts [AND] with Truvada®
high likelihood of poor adherence to PREP [AND] clinic attendance
any condition that in the opinion of the attending physician could endanger the health of the participant [OR] render her unsuitable to participate in the trial
