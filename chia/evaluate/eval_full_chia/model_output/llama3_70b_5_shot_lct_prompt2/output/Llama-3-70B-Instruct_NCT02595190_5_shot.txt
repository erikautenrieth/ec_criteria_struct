Inclusion Criteria:
1. Diagnosed with symptomatic sacral perineurial cysts(e.g., lumbosacral [OR] or perineal pain, fecal [OR] or urinary functions change, sexual function change, lower limb radiation pain, muscle abate, paresthesia, etc)
2. Visual analog scale more than or equal to 4
3. Signed the informed consent
4. Years, range 18-60
5. Self-rating anxiety scale (SAS) [AND] self-rating depression scale (SDS) scores < 50
6. [NOT] No Congenital, Mental [OR] and other Nervous system diseases
7. [NOT] No Serious Cardiac, Pulmonary, Hepatic [OR] and Nephritic disease
8. [NOT] No history of drug allergy
9. [NOT] No pain(including dysmenorrhea) [OR] or drug use (e.g., antipyretics, sleeping pills) within the last month
10. MRI finding of sacral perineurial cysts, but without any clinical symptoms, included in the negative control group
11. MRI finding healthy volunteers don't have sacral perineurial cysts, included in the negative control group blank control group

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Patients with lumbar common diseases(e.g., Lumbar disc [OR], Lumbar spinal stenosis [OR], Lumbar slippage, etc)
2. Researchers think that Patients with disease may be interference results(e.g., Spinal deformity [OR], spine fracture [OR], ankylosing spondylitis [OR], spinal tuberculosis [OR] and spinal infection [OR], spinal tumor [OR], pelvic inflammatory disease [OR] and other disease of department of gynaecology, etc)
3. Patients with other nervous system diseases(e.g., cerebral tumor [OR], neurinoma [OR], trigeminal neuralgia,etc)
4. Patients with Magnetic resonance imaging contraindication,including claustrophobic syndrome patients
5. Patients with recent (less than 3 years) use chemical drugs [OR] or have obvious psychological problems
6. In the past 2 months involved in other drugs [OR] or devices clinical trials
