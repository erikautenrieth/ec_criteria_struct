Inclusion Criteria:
Born at University of New Mexico Hospital
Greater than 34 weeks gestation
Primary in-utero drug exposure was opioids [AND] [NOT] other than buprenorphine
Maternal [OR] or infant urine drug screen positive for methadone [AND] and/or opioids on admission

Exclusion Criteria:
Born prior to 34 weeks
Neonatal intensive care unit admission
Serious medical comorbidities
Primary substance exposure in-utero was buprenorphine [OR] or was [NOT] opioids
