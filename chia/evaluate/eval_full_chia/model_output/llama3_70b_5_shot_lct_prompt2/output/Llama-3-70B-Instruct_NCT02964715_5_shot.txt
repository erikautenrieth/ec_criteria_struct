Inclusion Criteria:
biopsy proven NASH
Type 2 DM
HbA1c :>6.5%
BMI < 45kg/m2
Any anti-diabetic agent [NOT] except SGLT2 inhibitors [OR], TZDs(thiazolidinediones) [OR], DPP4(Dipeptidyl peptidase4) inhibitors [OR] and GLP1 RAs(Glucagon-like Peptide 1-Receptor Agonists)

Exclusion Criteria:
eGFR <45 ml/min
structural [AND] functional urogenital abnormalities, that predispose for urogenital infections
Investigational product use in the last 6 months
SGLT2 inhibitor [OR], TZD [OR], DPP4 inhibitor [OR] and GLP1 RA use within the past 6 months
DKA(Diabetic Ketoacidosis) [OR] or HHS(Hyperosmoloar Hyperglycaemic Syndrome) within the last 6 months
Pregnancy
Presence of major contraindications to magnetic resonance imaging (cardiac pacemakers [OR], claustrophobia [OR], foreign bodies [OR] and implanted medical devices with ferromagnetic properties).
Liver cirrhosis
Type 1 diabetes
Severe uncorrected insulin insufficiency
Significant alcohol intake
HIV infection
Use of Traditional Chinese Medication [OR] or alternative therapies
Coexisting causes of chronic liver disease - chronic viral hepatitis(B [OR] & C) [OR], autoimmune liver disease [OR], hemochromatosis [OR], Wilson's etc.
Use of medications associated with steatosis eg. Methotrexate [OR], anticonvulsants [OR], antiretroviral therapy etc.
h/o stroke
Steroid therapy
Endogenous Cushing's
Familial hypertriglyceridemia
