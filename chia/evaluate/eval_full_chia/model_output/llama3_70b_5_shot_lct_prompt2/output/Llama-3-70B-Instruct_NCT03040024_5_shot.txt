Inclusion Criteria:
Current diagnosis of otolaryngeal cancer [AND] undergoing surgery with general anesthesia
Competent to provide informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Emergency surgery
Monitored Anesthesia Care (i.e., regional anesthesia alone [AND] without plans for general anesthesia)
Surgery involving the eye [OR], eyebrow [OR], forehead [OR], or frontal scalp near the sensor placement
Poor health literacy
Allergy [OR], or have experienced any drug reaction to ketamine
Pregnant [OR] or lactating
Currently in active alcohol withdrawal
