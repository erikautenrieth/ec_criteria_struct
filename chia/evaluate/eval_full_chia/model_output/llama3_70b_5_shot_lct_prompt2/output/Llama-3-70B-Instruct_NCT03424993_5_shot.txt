Inclusion Criteria:
Habitual dietary sodium intake > 3400mg per day

Exclusion Criteria:
Abnormal resting ECG
Current abnormal blood panel (assessed by comprehensive metabolic panel [AND] lipid panel [AND] complete blood count).
Hypertension (currently taking anti-hypertensive medications [OR] resting blood pressure >140/90 mmHg)
Medical history of cardiovascular disease [OR] malignant cancer [OR] diabetes [OR] kidney disease
Obesity (Body Mass Index > 30)
Current pregnancy
Unable to provide consent
