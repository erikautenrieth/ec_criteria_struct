Inclusion Criteria:
3 -15 years old
Clinical symptoms suggestive of pharyngitis [AND] with MC Isaac score =3
Rapid-antigen detection test (RADT) positive for GAS-
Signed informed parental/patient consent form

Exclusion Criteria:
Hypersensitivity to B-lactams
concomitant disease [AND] which must be treated with antibiotics
chronic disease-[AND] Immunocompromised
Antibiotics within 72 h
history of ARF [OR], scarlet fever [OR], impetigo [OR], acute glomerulonephritis
Family history of ARF
Complicated pharyngitis
