Inclusion Criteria:
Primary total hip arthroplasty (THA)

Exclusion Criteria:
Revision cases
Uncontrolled bleeding tendency (prothrombin conc. [NOT] Less than 70%)
History of deep venous thrombosis
Sever liver impairment [AND] (liver failure)
Sever renal impairment (S. creatinine [AND] more than 3)
