Inclusion Criteria:
Patients aged between 40 [AND] 60 years old.
With Child score B [OR] C
Presented for elective gastrointestinal endoscopy

Exclusion Criteria:
Emergent condition like hematemesis.
Patients with moderate [AND] to severe hepatic encephalopathy.
Patients with hepatopulmonary syndrome.
Patients with known [OR] or suspected hypersensitivity to the used medication were also excluded from the study.
