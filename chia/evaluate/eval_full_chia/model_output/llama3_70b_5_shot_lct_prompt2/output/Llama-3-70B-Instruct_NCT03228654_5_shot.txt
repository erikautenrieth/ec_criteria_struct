Inclusion Criteria:
uterine size <12 weeks.
presence of benign cause for the hysterectomy e.g. fibroid uterus [OR] perimenopausal beeding [NOT] responding to medical treatment [OR] complex endometrial hyperplasia [AND] without atypia.
Absence of significant scarring [AND] in the pelvis from previous surgeries.

Exclusion Criteria:
Suspected [OR] or known gynecological malignancy.
uterine size >12 weeks.
Endometriosis
Presence of adnexal mass.
cervix flushed [AND] with the vagina.
presence of significant scarring [AND] in the pelvic area from previous surgery.
