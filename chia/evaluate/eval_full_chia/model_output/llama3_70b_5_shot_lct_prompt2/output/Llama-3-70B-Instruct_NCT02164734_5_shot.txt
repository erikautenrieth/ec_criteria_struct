Inclusion Criteria:
Mild-to-moderate RDS;
Postnatal age 2 [AND] to 48 hours;
Gestational age 27 0/7 [AND] to 36 6/7 weeks;
Treated with nasal CPAP modalities = 5 cm H2O [AND] FiO2 between 0.30 [AND] 0.60 for at least 2 hours to maintain SpO2 90-95%;
Informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Weight < 800 g;
Airway anomalies;
Pulmonary air leaks;
Craniofacial [OR] or cardiothoracic malformations
