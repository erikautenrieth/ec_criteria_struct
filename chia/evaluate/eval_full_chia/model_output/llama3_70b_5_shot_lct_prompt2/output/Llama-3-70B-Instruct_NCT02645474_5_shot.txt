adult patients
ASA class 1 [OR] 2 [OR] 3 patients
patients scheduled for elective breast mastectomy [OR] quadrantectomy

Exclusion Criteria:
patients' refusal
contraindication to regional anaesthesia ([AND] coagulopathies [OR] concurrent anticoagulant therapy [OR] allergy to local anaesthetics [OR] infection at puncture site)
