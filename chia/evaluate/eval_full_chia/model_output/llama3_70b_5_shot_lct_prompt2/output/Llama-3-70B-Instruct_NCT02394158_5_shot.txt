Inclusion Criteria:
Singleton pregnancy;
8-22 weeks gestation
Previous pregnancy complicated by gestational diabetes

Exclusion Criteria:
Established pre-existing diabetes (including unrecognised diabetes defined as a fasting plasma glucose = 7.0mmol/L [AND] or HbA1c = 48mmol/mol); Contraindications to metformin therapy (creatinine = 130µmol/L [OR] alanine transaminase = 2.0 x upper limit normal [OR] previous intolerance to metformin)
Planned continued antenatal care [AND] / delivery at centre [NOT] included in trial
Planned fast for cultural [OR] / religious reasons e.g. Ramadan
