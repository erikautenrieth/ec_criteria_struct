Inclusion Criteria:
HbA1c > 13.0 %
No treatment [AND] with insulin [OR] oral agents for 6 months
20 = Age < 80 years

Exclusion Criteria:
Contraindication [AND] to sitagliptin [OR] or metformin [OR] or thiazolidinedione
Pregnant [OR] or breast feeding women
Type 1 diabetes [OR] gestational diabetes [OR] or secondary forms of diabetes
[NOT] Not appropriate for oral antidiabetic agent
Medication which affect glycemic control
Disease which affect efficacy [AND] safety of drugs
Any major illness (Liver disease [OR] Renal failure [OR] Heart disease [OR] Cancer [OR] etc)
