Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with functional dyspepsia that fulfill Rome III criteria [AND] with inadequate relief of dyspeptic symptoms
Age >18
Provision of written consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Presence of organic pathology identified by upper endoscopy [OR] or other investigations
Presence of sliding hiatus hernia as defined by flap valve grade IV disruption of morphology at gastro-esophageal junction
Concurrent medications that affect gastrointestinal motility
Presence of acid reflux [OR] or heartburn symptoms of more than twice a month
History of gastric surgery
H. pylori infection
Use of PPI [OR] or NSAID in the past 4 weeks
Pregnancy
Known hypersensitivity [AND] to PPI
