Inclusion Criteria:
Female 
Age ≥18 years 
Patients with a lesion > 0.5 cm [AND] in largest diameter size, initially scored BI-RADS® 3 [OR] 4a [OR] 4b [OR] 4c in B-mode ultrasound 
Informed consent [AND] about histological examination (core cut biopsy (CCB) [OR] vacuum-assisted biopsy (VAB) [OR] fine needle aspiration (FNA) [OR] surgery) has already been given in the course of clinical routine 
Signed informed consent [AND] of study participation 

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant [OR] lactating women 
Women with breast implants [AND] on the same side as the lesion 
Women that underwent local radiation [OR] chemotherapy within the last 12 months 
Women with history of breast cancer [OR] breast surgery in the same quadrant 
Lesions in [OR] close to scar tissue (< 1cm) 
Skin lesions [OR] lesions that have been biopsied previously 
Lesion larger than 4 cm [AND] in the longest dimension 
[NOT] No lesion should be included when more than 50% of the lesion is further down than 4 cm beneath the skin level.
