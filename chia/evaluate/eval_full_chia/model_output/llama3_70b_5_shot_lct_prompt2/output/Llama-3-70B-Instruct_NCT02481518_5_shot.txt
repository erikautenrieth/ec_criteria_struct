Inclusion Criteria:
Age > 18 years
Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group score 0-2
First Diagnosed Head [AND] neck cancer and plan for treatment with cisplatin
Serum creatinine =1.5 mg/dl [OR] or eGFR=60(ml/min/1.73 m2)

Exclusion Criteria:
Prior treatment with cisplatin before randomization
Uncontrolled [AND] concurrent disease
Pregnancy
