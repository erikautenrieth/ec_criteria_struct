Inclusion Criteria:
All renal (only) male [OR] female recipients aged = 60, years undergoing kidney transplantation from a living [OR] deceased donor, including Expanded Criteria Donors (ECD).
Panel Reactive Antibody (PRA) < 30%.
Patients who consented [AND] to participate in the study by signing the informed consent form before the transplant surgery to the 1st post-operative day).

Exclusion Criteria:
Allergy [AND] to any of proposed medications
Patients with any active infection [AND] including HBV [OR], HCV [OR] and HIV.
