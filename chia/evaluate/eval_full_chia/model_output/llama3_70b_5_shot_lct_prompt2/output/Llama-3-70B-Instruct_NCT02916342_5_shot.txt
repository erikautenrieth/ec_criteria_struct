Inclusion Criteria:
ASA physical status I-III;
18-85 years of age, inclusive;
surgery less than 3 hours.

Exclusion Criteria:
indication for catheter insertion;
contraindications to brachial plexus block (e.g., allergy [OR] to local anaesthetics, malignancy [OR] or infection in the area);
existing neurological deficit [AND] in the area to be blocked;
pregnancy;
history of neck surgery [OR] or radiotherapy;
severe respiratory disease;
chest deformity;
inability to understand [AND] the informed consent [AND] demands of the study;
patient refusal.
