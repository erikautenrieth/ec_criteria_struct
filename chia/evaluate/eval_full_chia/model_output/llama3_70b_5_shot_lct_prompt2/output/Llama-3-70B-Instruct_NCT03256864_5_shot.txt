Inclusion Criteria:
Liver Transplant Recipients have received liver transplantations for at least 6+1 months prior to enrollment
Liver Transplant Recipients have [NOT] no acute rejection episodes within 3 months prior to the enrollment and are clinically stable
Liver Transplant Recipients have been treated with twice-daily regimen of tacrolimus(TAC) [AND] plus everolimus(EVR) and TAC [AND] EVR trough levels have stayed within targeted ranges for at least 6 weeks prior to enrollment
Provide written informed consent prior to inclusion.
Liver transplant recipients who are 18-65 years of age [AND] of a primary liver transplant
Allograft functioning at an acceptable level as defined by the AST, ALT, Total Bilirubin levels =3 times ULN prior to enrollment.
Abbreviated MDRD eGFR = 30 mL/min/1.73m2. 
Exclusion Criteria:
Patients who are recipients of multiple solid organ [OR] or islet cell tissue transplants, or have previously received an organ [OR] or tissue transplant. Patients who have a combined liver-kidney transplant.
History of malignancy of any organ system (other than localized basal cell carcinoma of the skin), treated [OR] or untreated, within the past 5 years, regardless of whether there is evidence of local recurrence [OR] or metastases.
Existence of any surgical, medical [OR] or mental conditions, other than the current transplantation, which, in the opinion of the investigator, might interfere with the objectives of the study.
Pregnant [OR] or nursing (lactating) women.
