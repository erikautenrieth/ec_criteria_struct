Inclusion Criteria:
aged 18 or older,
have undergone oro-tracheal intubation for a coma (Glasgow Coma Score below [OR] equal to 8),
with mechanical ventilation initiated in the first 48 hours following hospital admission

Exclusion Criteria:
pregnancy,
patients under legal custody,
patients without health insurance,
patients included in another interventional clinical study involving infections [OR] or antibiotics and having the same primary parameter,
moribund patients,
situation in which the procalcitonin concentration could be increased [NOT] without correlation to an infectious process (poly-traumatised patients [OR], surgical interventions within the last 4 days [OR], cardiorespiratory arrest [OR], administration of anti-thymocyte globulin [OR], immunodepressed patients (bone marrow transplant patients [OR], patients with severe neutropenia)),
patients with an absolute indication for administration of antibiotics at the moment of ICU admission (meningitis [OR], pneumonia) or a chronic infection for which long-term antibiotic treatment is necessary (endocarditis [OR], osteo-articular infections [OR], mediastinitis [OR], deep abscesses [OR], pneumocystis infection [OR], toxoplasmosis [OR], tuberculosis)
patients with haemodynamic instability of septic origin [OR] a respiratory insufficiency (defined by a ratio Pa02/Fi02 = 200 mmHg [AND] PEP = 5 cmH2O)
