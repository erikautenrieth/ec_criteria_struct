Inclusion Criteria:
Adult Patients with Overt Hepatic Encephalopathy.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with active GIT bleeding.
Patients with history of bowel obstruction [OR] perforation.
Patients with history of allergy [AND] to PEG.
Treatment with rifaximin [OR] or neomycin in the previous 7 days.
Patients with major psychiatric illness.
Patients receiving benzodiazepines [AND] or narcotics.
Patients with compromised renal.
Patients receiving medications highly bound to plasma proteins [AND] eg. Warfarin.
Pregnant [OR] or lactating women.
Fulminant hepatic failure.
