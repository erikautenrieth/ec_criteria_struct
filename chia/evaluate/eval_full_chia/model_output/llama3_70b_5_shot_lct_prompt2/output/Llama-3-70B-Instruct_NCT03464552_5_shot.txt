Inclusion Criteria:
Females 18-65 years old who undergoing colposcopic directed biopsy

Exclusion Criteria:
A known allergy [AND] to Celecoxib, aspirin [OR] or another NSAID.
Active peptic ulceration [OR] or gastrointestinal bleeding.
Inflammatory bowel disease.
Congestive heart failure (NYHA II-IV).
Established ischemic heart disease [OR] peripheral arterial disease [OR] and/or cerebrovascular disease.
History of neurologic deficit.
Known hepatic [OR] renal impairment.
Pregnancy.
Breast-feeding.
Post-hysterectomy.
Bleeding disorders.
Drug abuse.
Cervical [AND] vaginal infection.
