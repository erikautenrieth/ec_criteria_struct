Inclusion Criteria:
Healthy, women ages 18 to 39yo with BMI <30
Regular menstrual cycles with duration between 24-35 days
Completion of screening visit where ovulation will be assessed with blood draw for progesterone level (must be 5ng/mL [OR] greater)
Not seeking pregnancy during the study period
Use of a non-hormonal form of contraception, such as: sterilization (tubal ligation, Essure) [OR], copper IUD (intrauterine device) [OR], barrier methods [OR] or abstinence
Must speak English [OR] Spanish

Exclusion Criteria:
Currently pregnant [OR] breastfeeding
Severe pelvic organ prolapse [OR] prolapse to any degree that may prevent retention of the vaginal ring after insertion
Use of oral contraceptive pills [OR], patches [OR], implants [OR] or hormonal intrauterine contraception in the month prior to screening
Use of depo medroxyprogesterone within 6 months of screening
Use of medications that interact with contraceptive steroid hormones: anti-epileptic medications [OR], rifampin [OR], rifabutin [OR], fosamprenavir [OR], etc
Medical condition with safety deemed to be category 3 [OR] 4 when using a combined hormonal contraceptive, as determined by the Center for Disease Control Medical Eligibility Criteria: current [OR] past history of breast cancer [OR], severe decompensated cirrhosis [OR], history of deep vein thrombosis [OR] pulmonary embolus [OR], diabetes with nephropathy/retinopathy/neuropathy [OR] other vascular disease diagnosed more than 20 years ago [OR], current symptomatic gallbladder disease [OR], hypertension [OR], ischemic heart disease [OR], known thrombogenic mutations [OR], hepatocellular adenoma [OR], malignant hepatoma [OR], multiple risk factors for atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease [OR], multiple sclerosis with prolonged immobility [OR], history of peripartum cardiomyopathy [OR], cigarette smoking [AND] =35yo [OR], history of complicated solid organ transplant [OR], history of stroke [OR], history of superficial venous thrombosis not associated with catheter [OR], systemic lupus erythematosus with positive antiphospholipid antibodies [OR], valvular heart disease complicated by pulmonary hypertension [OR] atrial fibrillation [OR] bacterial endocarditis [OR], and acute viral hepatitis
