Inclusion Criteria:
Having been diagnosed with primary OCD as defined by the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (DSM-IV-) criteria;Cleaning [OR] or checking as primary OCD symptoms
Yale-Brown Obsessive-Compulsive Scale (Y-BOCS) score of = 16
Never receiving adequate treatment [OR] stop receiving treatment for at least 8 weeks
Having an education degree of high school [OR] above
Accepting [AND] to participate in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
Having significant medical illnesses that would interfere [AND] with the conduct of the study
Clinically significant abnormal laboratory finding
Having comorbid psychiatric conditions according to the criteria set forth in the DSM-IV(administered by the Mini-International Neuropsychiatric Interview (MINI))
The current OCD symptoms are too severe that the patient cannot finish the evaluation [OR] receive the ERP
Being currently at risk [AND] for suicide
Being pregnant [OR] or having the intention to be pregnant before the end of the study
A history of having inadequate response [AND] to adequate SSRIs [OR] CBT treatment
Subjects who are unable [AND] to undergo the MRI
