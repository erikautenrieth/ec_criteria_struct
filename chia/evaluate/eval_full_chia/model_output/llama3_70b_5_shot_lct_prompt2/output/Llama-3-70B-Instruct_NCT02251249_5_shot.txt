Inclusion Criteria:
Patient over 18 years [AND] weighing between 65 [AND] 85 Kg
Referred for STEMI within 6 hours from beginning of chest pain [OR] stable coronary artery disease requiring a loading dose of Prasugrel [OR] Ticagrelor according to the international recommendations.
[NOT] No previous treatment with Clopidogrel [OR] Prasugrel [OR] Ticagrelor.
Patient fasting for at least 6 hours.
Affiliate [OR] receiving a social security system.
Written informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
Allergy [OR] contraindication to paracetamol [OR] Prasugrel [OR] Ticagrelor
Paracetamol ingestion in the previous 48 hours
Patient treated with drugs supposed to alter gastric emptying times (calcium antagonists [OR] Alimentary tract treatments [OR] opioid analgesics [OR] tricyclic antidepressants [OR] antibiotics).
Conditions [OR] pathologies supposed to alter gastric emptying times (Thyroid dysfunction [OR] chronic renal failure [OR] Parkinson's disease [OR] scleroderma [OR] amyloidosis [OR] any gastrointestinal disease [OR] any not cured malignancy [OR] and any advanced psychiatric [OR] neurological disease).
Presence of vomiting
Cardiogenic shock [OR] ventricular arrhythmia [OR] resuscitated cardiac arrest
Hepatic insufficiency
Severe respiratory disease
Pregnant [OR] breastfeeding women
