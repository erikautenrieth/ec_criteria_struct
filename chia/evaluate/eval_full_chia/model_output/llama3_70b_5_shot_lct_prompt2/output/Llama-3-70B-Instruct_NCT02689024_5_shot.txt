Inclusion Criteria:
adult patients aged = 55 years [AND] with
a radiographically confirmed hip fracture

Exclusion Criteria:
multiple injuries [OR] (polytrauma patients)
previous adverse reaction [OR] or known allergy to local anaesthetics [OR] opioids [OR] paracetamol
skin infection [AND] in proximity of injection site
delirious state [AND] at presentation in the ED
