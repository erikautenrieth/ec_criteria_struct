Inclusion Criteria:
Diabetes mellitus 
Foot ulcer at the malleoli area [AND] between 0,25 cm² and 5,0 cm² 
Foot ulcer duration [AND] more than 6 weeks 
Ankle-brachial index above 0,40 [OR] presence of palpable pulses in arteria dorsalis pedes [AND] and/or arteria tibialis posterior 
informed consent 

Exclusion Criteria:
Hemoglobin concentration under 6.5 mmol/l [NOT] at screening 
HBA1c more than 108 mmol/l 
Non-compliant [AND] with blood-letting 
Clinically infected ulcer 
Patient planned for [OR] or has had a revascularization procedure in the affected leg within the last 8 weeks 
The ulcer have been treated [AND] with growth factors in the last 8 weeks 
History of deep venous insufficiency [OR] chronic venous leg ulcer [OR] stasis dermatitis 
Breast-feeding women [OR] or fertile women [NOT] agreeing to use an effective method of contraception 
Participation in another clinical ulcer-healing study within the last 4 weeks 
Patient has previously been randomized in this study 
Judgement by the investigator that the patient is [NOT] able to participate in the study
