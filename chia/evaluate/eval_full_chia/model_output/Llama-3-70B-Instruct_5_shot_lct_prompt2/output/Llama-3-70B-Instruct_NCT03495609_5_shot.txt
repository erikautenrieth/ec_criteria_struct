Inclusion Criteria:
premenopausal women
BRCA1 carrier

Exclusion Criteria:
History of allergic reaction to compounds of similar chemical [OR] biologic composition to hCG
receiving medication that could interfere with the study protocol objectives (hormonal contraceptives [OR], androgens [OR], prednisone [OR], thyroid hormones [OR], insulin)
previous treatment with follicle stimulating hormone for assisted reproduction
uncontrolled intercurrent illness
Heart disease
Severe cognitive decline
Psychiatric desease
HIV positive
Hepatitis B [OR] C infection
