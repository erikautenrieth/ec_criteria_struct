Inclusion Criteria:
Liver cirrhosis
Age between 18 [AND] 70 years
Esophageal varices with high bleeding risk: more than F2 [AND] red color sign
[NOT] No previous history of upper gastrointestinal bleeding
[NOT] No previous history of endoscopic, radiologic, or surgical therapy for varices or ascites
Do [NOT] take beta-blocker [OR], ACE inhibitor [OR], or nitrate
Child-Pugh score <12

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with systolic blood pressure <100 mmHg [OR] basal heart rate <60/min
Portal vein thrombosis
Uncontrolled ascites [OR] hepatic encephalopathy
Severe coagulation disorder: prothrombin time <40% [OR] (or INR >1.7) [OR] platelet count <30,000/mm3
Medium [OR] large sized gastric or duodenal varices
Coexisting malignancy
Severe cardiovascular disorder [OR], renal failure [OR], peritonitis [OR], sepsis
Severe erosive esophagitis [OR], severe esophageal stricture [OR], active gastric [OR] duodenal ulcer
Contraindication to beta-blocker
Pregnancy
Refusal to give consent to participate in the trial
