Inclusion Criteria:
Subjects = 19 [OR] = 75 years of age
Subjects undergoing treatment [AND] for type 2 diabetes
Subjects undergoing treatment [AND] of statin [AND] for hypercholesterolemia
Fasting LDL-C = 250mg/dL at the screening visit
Fasting LDL-C =70mg/dL [OR] = 160mg/dL at the randomization visit
Fasting TG<500mg/dL

Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects with hypersensitivity reaction [AND] to Statin [AND] Ezetimibe
Subjects with severe kidney disease
Subjects with HIV positive result at the screening
Pregnant [OR] breast-feeding subjects
Subjects with taking any medication affecting level of LDL (Fenofibrate, Omega 3 fatty acid etc.)
Insulin-treated Subjects
Other exclusions applied
