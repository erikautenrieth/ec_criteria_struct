Inclusion Criteria:
Written informed consent prior to beginning specific protocol procedures, including expected cooperation of the patients for the treatment and follow-up, [AND] willingness and ability to complete collection of data via wearable device and study mobile must be obtained and documented according to the local regulatory requirements.
Female [OR] male patients.
Age = 18 years old.
Metastatic invasive hormone receptor positive [AND] HER2 negative breast cancer (histologically confirmed).
Patients who in the opinion of the treating physician are candidates suitable for randomization for mono-chemotherapy treatment, that has either an approved label in Europe [OR] and/or is supported by guidelines for the treatment of first-line advanced BC, which are based on evidence on safety and efficacy in this setting.
Symptomatic [OR] or asymptomatic metastatic breast cancer.
Resolution of all acute toxic effects of prior anti-cancer therapy or surgical procedures to NCI CTCAE version 4.0 grade = 1 (except alopecia [OR] or other toxicities [NOT] considered a safety risk for the patient at investigator's discretion).
Life-expectancy > 6 months.
For female patients: The patients need to be either A) of non-childbearing potential (documented postmenopausal [OR] post hysterectomy) B) childbearing potential with negative serum [OR] or urinary pregnancy test (in this case patients need to use highly effective non-hormonal contraceptive methods).
