Inclusion Criteria:
Adult patient being referred for clinically indicated positron emission tomography myocardial perfusion imaging at the Centre hospitalier de l'Université de Montréal

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant women
Claustrophobic patient [AND] unable to undergo the examination
Breastfeeding women [AND] unwilling to temporarily stop breastfeeding
Patient with contra-indication to: dipyridamole [OR] aminophylline [OR] dobutamine [OR] exercise stress test (depending on the method of cardiovascular stress test chosen)
