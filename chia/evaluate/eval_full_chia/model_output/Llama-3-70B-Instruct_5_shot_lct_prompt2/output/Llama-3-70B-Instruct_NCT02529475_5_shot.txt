Inclusion Criteria:
Major subjects of over 40 years (mean age of Meniere's disease 40 to 50 years)
Informed consent [AND] signed
Medical examination [AND] performed prior to participation in research
Patients [NOT] without history of inner ear disease
Recipient of a French social security scheme

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] minors
Patients on a legal protection regime [AND] type guardianship
Respiratory pathologies [OR], cardiovascular [OR], renal [OR], diabetes
Claustrophobia
Contraindications [AND] to exposure to a magnetic field
Contraindications [AND] to injecting Dotarem ®
