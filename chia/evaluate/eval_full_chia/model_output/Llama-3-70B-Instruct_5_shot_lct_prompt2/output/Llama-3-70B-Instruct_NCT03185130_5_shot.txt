Inclusion Criteria:
Age 10 to 65 years
Temperature [AND] less than 100.4 F
Normal neurologic exam [AND] normal mental status

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant
Meningeal signs [AND] are present
Acute angle closure glaucoma [AND] is suspected
Head trauma within the previous two weeks
Lumbar puncture within the previous two weeks
Thunderclap onset of the headache
Known allergy to one of the study drugs
History of intracranial hypertension
Is a prisoner
Patient declined [NOT] informed consent
Non-English speaking patient [OR] or parent/guardian for pediatric patients
Attending provider excludes patient
Severe Dehydration
