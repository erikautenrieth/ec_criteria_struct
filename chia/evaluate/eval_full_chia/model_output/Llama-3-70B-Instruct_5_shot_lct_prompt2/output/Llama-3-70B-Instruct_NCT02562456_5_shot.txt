Inclusion Criteria:
Children aging between 3 and 6 years
presenting good health conditions
whose parents [OR] legal guardians accept [AND] sign the consent form
with at least one occlusal [OR] occlusal proximal caries lesion in primary molars
only occlusal [AND] and/or occlusal-proximal surfaces with caries lesions [AND] with dentin involvement

Exclusion Criteria:
severe behavioral issues
presence of fistula [OR] or abscess near the selected tooth
presence of pulp exposure [AND] in the selected tooth
presence of mobility [AND] in the selected tooth
