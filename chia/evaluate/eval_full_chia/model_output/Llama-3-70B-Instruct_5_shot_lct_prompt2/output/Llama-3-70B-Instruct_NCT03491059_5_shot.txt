Inclusion Criteria:
males [AND] females greater than or equal to 18 years of age
current regular user of e-cigarettes (use at least once daily for the past 30 days) with nicotine strength > 6mg/ml
health [AND] medical history
abstinent from any tobacco/nicotine use for 4 hours prior to imaging

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] a regular user of e-cigarettes
pregnant [OR] lactating (only excluded from imaging study)
prisoner
incapable of giving informed consent
unable to lie flat on the scanner for extended periods of time
unstable medical condition like heart disease [OR], uncontrolled hypertension [OR], thyroid disease [OR], diabetes [OR], renal [OR] or liver impairment [OR], or glaucoma
prostatic hypertrophy [OR], stroke [OR], or ulcer in past year
psychiatric conditions such as schizophrenia [OR], adult ADHD [OR], or bipolar disorder
current [OR] regular use of psychiatric medications such as tranquilizers [OR], antipsychotics [OR], and/or antidepressants
use of medications that are inducers of CYP2A6 (a nicotine metabolizing enzyme) such as rifampicin [OR], dexamethasone [OR], phenobarbital [OR], and other anti-convulsant drugs
unable to communicate in English
current use of smokeless tobacco [OR], tobacco cigarettes (5 and fewer a day)
[NOT] occasional use of pipes is permitted if subject abstains for the week prior to the study
older than 80 years
