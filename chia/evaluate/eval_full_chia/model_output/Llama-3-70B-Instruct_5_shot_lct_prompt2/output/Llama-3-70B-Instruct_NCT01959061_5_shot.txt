Inclusion Criteria:
Histologically confirmed colorectal adenocarcinoma
Disease limited to the liver [AND] Unresectable disease by surgery [OR] or other local therapies
Age >18 years
ECOG performance status 0-2 [AND] Child pugh A [OR] or B
Expected survival = 3 months
Adequate hematological [AND] hepatic [AND] renal function

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant [OR] lactating women
Patients with severe organ dysfunction [OR] failure
With severe cardiovascular disease [OR] mental
Extraliver metastases
