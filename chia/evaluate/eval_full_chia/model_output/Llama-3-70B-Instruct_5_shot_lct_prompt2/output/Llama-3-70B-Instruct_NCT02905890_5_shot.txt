Inclusion Criteria:
BV positive by Nugent score
HIV negative
Capable of providing written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Currently pregnant [OR] using a reliable contraception (e.g. injectables [OR], intrauterine devices [OR], implant [OR], oral contraceptive pills)
Desiring pregnancy in the next year
History of tubal ligation [OR] or hysterectomy
Contraindication to progestin-only contraceptives
Unable to comprehend consent material because of language barrier [OR] psychological difficulty
