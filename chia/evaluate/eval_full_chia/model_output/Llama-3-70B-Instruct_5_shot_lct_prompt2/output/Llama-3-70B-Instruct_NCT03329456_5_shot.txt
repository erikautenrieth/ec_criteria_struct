Inclusion Criteria:
. Inclusion criteria are American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) physical status I-III [AND] age between 18 [AND] 70 years [AND] body mass index (BMI) between 20 [AND] 35 kg/m2.

Exclusion Criteria:
Exclusion criteria are pregnancy [OR] patients with contraindications to regional anesthesia [OR] allergy to LAs [OR] patients taking opioids regularly due to chronic pain [OR] use of anticoagulation drugs [NOT] other than acetylsalicylic acid [OR] dipyridamole [OR] atrioventricular block [OR] diabetes.
