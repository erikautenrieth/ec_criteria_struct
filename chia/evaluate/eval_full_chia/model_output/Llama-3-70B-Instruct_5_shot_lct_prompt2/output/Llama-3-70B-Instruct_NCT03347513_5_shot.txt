Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosed Iron deficiency anemia.
H-pylori positive cases.
Second trimester pregnancy.

Exclusion Criteria:
Severe Iron deficiency anemia (hemoglobin < 8.0 g/dL).
Parasitic worm infection [AND] e.g. schistosomiasis, [OR] and hook worm by stool analysis.
Any cases giving clinical symptoms of gastritis [AND] e.g. nausea, [OR] vomiting, [OR] dull aching pain [OR] or soreness in the epigastrium.
Cases with history of gastric ulcer diagnosed by upper endoscopy.
Cases complaining of hematemesis.
