Inclusion Criteria:
Any allogeneic stem cell transplant recipient [AND] = 14 years of age [AND] = 60 years of age
Bilirubin/ SGOT/SGPT < 5 × upper normal limits.
Creatinine < 2 × upper normal limits.
Ejection fraction = 50% [AND] no severe arrhythmia.
Estimated life expectancy = 6 months.
Patients' CMV-DNA = 1000cp/ml [AND] in treatment group [AND] being negative in prophylactic group.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients receiving prednisone = 1mg/kg/d [AND] for the treatment of acute GVHD [OR] mild, severe chronic GVHD.
Recipient < 14years of age
Donor is sero-positive [OR] in HBV/HCV/HIV [OR] RPR.
