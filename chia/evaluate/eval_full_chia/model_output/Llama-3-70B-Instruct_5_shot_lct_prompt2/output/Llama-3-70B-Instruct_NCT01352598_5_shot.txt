Inclusion Criteria:
Patient age >= 18 years
Zubrod performance status of 0-3
T1-3 N0 M0 adenocarcinoma of the prostate
Prostate volume = 100 cc
Signed study-specific consent form
Exclusion Criteria:
Extension of local tumor to involve adjacent organs other than seminal vesicles (T4)
Prostate volume > 100 cc
Nodal involvement
Metastatic disease
Prior pelvic radiotherapy [AND] except as part of combination therapy for prostate cancer
History of scleroderma
Patients with psychiatric [OR] or addictive disorder that would preclude obtaining informed consent
