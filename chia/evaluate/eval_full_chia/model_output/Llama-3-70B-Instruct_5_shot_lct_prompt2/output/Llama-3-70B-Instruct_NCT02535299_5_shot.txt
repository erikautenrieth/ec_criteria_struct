Inclusion Criteria:
Newly dignosised type 2 diabetes according to WHO criteria [AND] glycated hemoglobin (HbA1c) was more than 10%;
Seronegative for antibodies against insulin [AND] islet cells [AND] glutamic acid decarboxylase (GAD);

Exclusion Criteria:
Type 1 diabetes mellitus,[OR] presence of autoimmune diabetes indicated by antibodies to insulin [OR] islet cells [OR] and GAD;
Gestational diabetes;
patients with heart [OR] liver [OR] renal function impairment;[OR] presence of severe infections [OR] cerebrovascular disease;
