Inclusion Criteria:
Age 18 or older.
Patients diagnosed with acromegaly [AND] with GH-secreting pituitary adenoma on sellar MRI, meeting the biochemical criteria outlined above (refer to 1. Diagnosis of acromegaly) [AND] with typical acromegalic features.
[NOT] No prior use of somatostatin analogues.
Adequate hepatic [AND] renal function
Provision of a signed written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Severe co-morbid illness such as untreatable other malignancy [OR] and/or active infections.
Pregnant [OR] lactating women
Hypersensitivity to Sandostatin [OR] any component of the formulation.
