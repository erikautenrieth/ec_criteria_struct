Inclusion Criteria:
Patients older than 18 years
Ischemic symptoms [OR] evidence of myocardial ischemia (inducible [OR] or spontaneous) in the presence of >50% de novo stenosis located in native coronary vessels

Exclusion Criteria:
Target lesion located in the left main stem
STEMI
Restenosis
Cardiogenic shock
Malignancies [OR] other comorbid conditions with life expectancy less than 12 months [OR] that may result in protocol noncompliance
Known allergy [AND] to the study medications (probucol [OR], sirolimus [OR], zotarolimus)
Pregnancy (present [OR], suspected [OR], or planned)
