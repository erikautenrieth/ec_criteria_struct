Inclusion Criteria:
South Australian secondary school students [AND] in years 10, 11, and 12 in 2017
Written parental consent [AND] for those under the age of 18
Written student consent [OR] assent for those under the age of 18 (or if 18 years old and older consent for themselves)
Available at school [AND] for at least the first pharyngeal swab [AND] willing to comply with study procedures

Exclusion Criteria:
Previous anaphylaxis [AND] following any component of Bexsero vaccine
Previous receipt of meningococcal B vaccine (Bexsero)
Known pregnancy
