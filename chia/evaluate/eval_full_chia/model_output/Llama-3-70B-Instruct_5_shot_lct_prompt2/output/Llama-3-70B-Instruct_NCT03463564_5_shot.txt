Inclusion Criteria:
T1DM for at least 12 months
persistent HbA1c levels = 7.5% (58 mmol/mol) despite optimized education therapy,
recurrent severe hypoglycemic episodes [OR] or high glucose variability
willingness [AND] to wear the insulin pump

Exclusion Criteria:
previous use of insulin pump
pregnancy [OR] or planning to become pregnant in the next 2 years,
lack of ability [AND] to use the study devices
history of severe chronic diseases
recent [OR] or concomitant use of corticosteroids
drug [OR] or alcohol abuse
psychiatric complaints that interfere [AND] with the correct use of the devices
