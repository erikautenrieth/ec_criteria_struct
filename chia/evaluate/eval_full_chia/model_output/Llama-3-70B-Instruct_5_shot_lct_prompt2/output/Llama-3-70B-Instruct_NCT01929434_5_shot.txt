Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with diagnosis of cerebral palsy. 
Patients' curator must be able to give voluntary consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
Intracranial infection. 
Severe respiratory [AND] circulatory system diseases. 
Hematologic malignancies. 
Positive serological tests such as AIDS [OR] hepatitis B virus [OR] hepatitis C virus [OR] syphilis （antigen [OR] antibody）. 
Tumors. 
Genetic [AND] metabolic diseases.
