Inclusion Criteria:
Age 35-70 years old
Fasting blood glucose 100-125 mg/dL

Exclusion Criteria:
A1C >7.0%
2hr glucose during OGTT >200 mg/dL
Total cholesterol >280 mg/dL
Previous diabetic history [AND] coronary artery disease
Allergy to rosuvastatin [OR] parvastatin
Baseline ALT more than 3 times UNL
Serum Cr > 2.0 mg/dL
Pregnancy [OR] breast feeding [OR] plan to be pregnant woman.
