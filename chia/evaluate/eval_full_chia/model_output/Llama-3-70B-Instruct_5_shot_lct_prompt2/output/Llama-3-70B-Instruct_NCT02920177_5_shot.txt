Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with symptomatic FAI
Clinical [AND] radiographic evidence of FAI
Patients able to provide consent [AND] to study participation
Completion of 6 weeks of physical therapy program

Exclusion Criteria:
Established Osteoarthritis (Kellgren-Lawrence > 3)
Minimum joint space > 2 mm as measured on AP radiograph
Hip dysplasia (center edge angle < 20° on AP radiograph)
Patients with clinically significant cardiovascular [OR], renal [OR], hepatic [OR], endocrine disease [OR], cancer [OR] or diabetes
Patients with ongoing infection [AND] including HIV [OR] and Hepatitis
Patient with history of osteomyelitis [OR] septic arthritis
Anticoagulation therapy
Patients who are pregnant [OR] or breast feeding
Patients with systemic [OR], rheumatic [OR] or inflammatory disease of the knee [OR] chondrocalcinosis [OR], hemochromatosis [OR], inflammatory arthritis [OR], arthropathy of the knee associated with juxta-articular Paget's disease of the femur [OR] or tibia [OR], hemophilic arthropathy [OR], infectious arthritis [OR], Charcot's knee joint [OR], villonodular synovitis [OR], and synovial chondromatosis
Patients taking immunosuppressant medication
Patients with abnormal hematology [OR] or serum chemistry lab results
Patients receiving injection to treatment knee within 2 months of study enrollment
BMI greater than 35 [OR] less than 20
