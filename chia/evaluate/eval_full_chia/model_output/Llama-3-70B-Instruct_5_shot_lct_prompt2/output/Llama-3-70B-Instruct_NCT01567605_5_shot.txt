Inclusion Criteria:
traumatic spinal cord injury at least one year ago
regular bowel care routine [AND] (at least four weeks)

Exclusion Criteria:
cauda equina [OR] or conus lesion
currently use ventilator
colostomy [OR], or do [NOT] perform regular bowel care for any reason
any skin breakdown [AND] (pressure sores)
do [NOT] speak English
are under 19 years old
are pregnant [OR] or think you might be pregnant
medical/psychiatric condition [OR] or substance abuse that is likely to affect your ability to complete this study
currently using medications containing lidocaine
allergy to lidocaine
