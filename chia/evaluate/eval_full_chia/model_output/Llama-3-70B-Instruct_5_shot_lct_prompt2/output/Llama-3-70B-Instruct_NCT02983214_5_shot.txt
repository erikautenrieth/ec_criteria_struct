Inclusion Criteria:
Patients aged =50 years [AND] with DM2 [AND] symptomatic PAD diagnosed clinically (according to Fontaine criteria, stage IIa [OR] IIb [OR] III) [AND] by measuring the <U+0391><U+0392><U+0399>.

Exclusion Criteria:
Congestive heart failure [OR] history of ventricular tachycardia [OR] ventricular fibrillation [OR] multifocal ventricular extrasystoles [OR] QTc prolongation.
Patients with atrial fibrillation [AND] taking any anticoagulant therapy [OR] patients with a history of cardioembolic ischemic stroke [OR] hemorrhagic stroke.
Patients with a history (= 12 months) of acute coronary syndrome [AND] receiving dual antiplatelet therapy [OR] patients receiving monotherapy with aspirin.
Patients with hepatic impairment (child-Pugh staging, calibration = 5) [OR] renal impairment (creatinine clearance = 30ml / min) [OR] recent peptic ulcer [OR] a history of hypersensitivity to cilostazol [OR] cancer patients undergoing treatment.
