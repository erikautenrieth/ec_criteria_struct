Inclusion Criteria:
Patients scheduled for supine-positioned elective craniotomy for supratentorial malignant [OR] and non-malignant brain tumors 3 cm or larger (measured as the largest diameter in any plane on MR images)
ASA (American Society of Anesthesiologist) status 1-3 (27)
Written informed consent [AND] from participating patients

Exclusion Criteria:
Age younger than 18 yrs. [OR] older than 75 yrs.
Pregnancy [OR] or nursing (negative pregnancy blood test)
History of allergic reactions [AND] to phenylephrine [OR] or ephedrine
eGFR < 60ml/min/1.73m2
