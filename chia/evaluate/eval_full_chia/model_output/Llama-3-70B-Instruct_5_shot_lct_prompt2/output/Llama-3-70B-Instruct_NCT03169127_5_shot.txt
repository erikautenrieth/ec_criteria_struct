Inclusion Criteria:
Need of lower third molar surgeries

Exclusion Criteria:
Presence of systemic diseases;
Presence of local inflammation [AND] or infection;
Any history of allergic reaction to local anesthetics [OR] gastrointestinal bleeding [OR] ulceration;
Cardiovascular [OR] kidney [OR] hepatic diseases;
Patients who are making use of antidepressants [OR] diuretics [OR] anticoagulants;
Asthma [AND] allergy to aspirin [OR] ibuprofen [OR] any other nonsteroidal antiinflammatory drug;
Regular use of any nonsteroidal antiinflammatory drug,
Pregnancy [OR] breast feeding.
