Inclusion Criteria:
Age 18-65 years
History of snake bite with features of local envenomation [AND] with/without systemic features
Less than 24 hours since bite, [AND]
No prior antibiotic treatment

Exclusion Criteria:
Upper limb bites
Multiple (> 1) bites
Wound manipulation
Extensive local necrosis [OR] or blebs
Seriously-ill patients with hypotension [OR] capillary leak [OR] life threatening bleeding.
Suspected cobra bite, [OR]
Pregnant [OR] breast-feeding women
