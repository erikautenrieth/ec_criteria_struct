Inclusion Criteria:
Age = 65 years with one additional stroke risk factor (hypertension [OR] diabetes [OR] heart failure [OR] history of [OR] left ventricular ejection fraction <0.40), previous stroke [OR] or transient ischemic attack).
Atrial fibrillation [AND] not on oral anticoagulation (OAC) therapy but eligible
Atrial fibrillation on sub-optimal OAC

Exclusion Criteria:
Uncontrolled hypertension (defined as average SBP = 160 mmHg [2 readings taken at time of screening]).
End stage renal disease (CrCl < 15 ml/min)
Valvular Heart Disease [AND] including those with prosthetic valve, mitral stenosis (moderate to severe) [OR] or valve repair.
Excess alcohol intake (males: = 28 units/week [OR] females: = 21 units/week. One unit of alcohol = 8 oz beer [OR] 1 oz hard liquor [OR] 4 oz wine).
Intracranial bleed at any point.
History of "Major Bleeding" at any point (defined as overt bleeding at a critical site [AND] including intracranial, intraspinal, intraocular, pericardial, or retroperitoneal; [OR] or bleed requiring hospitalization).
Foreshortened life-expectancy [OR] or severe comorbidities precluding study follow-up period
Unable to read/[NOT] understand English
Severe cognitive impairment (defined as score = 5 on the Short Portable Mental Status Questionnaire)
