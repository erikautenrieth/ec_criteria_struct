Inclusion Criteria:
Primary periampullary tumor
R0 [OR] R1 resection
Chronic pancreatitis requiring pancreatoduodenectomy

Exclusion Criteria:
Metastatic tumor
Locally unresectable tumor
Previous gastric resection
ASA IV [OR] V
Age under 18 years
Preoperative complete parenteral [OR] enteral feeding
Immunosuppressive therapy before operation
Severe malnutrition
Lack of the patient's consent [AND] for the trial participation [AND], feeding tube insertion [AND] or epidural analgesia
