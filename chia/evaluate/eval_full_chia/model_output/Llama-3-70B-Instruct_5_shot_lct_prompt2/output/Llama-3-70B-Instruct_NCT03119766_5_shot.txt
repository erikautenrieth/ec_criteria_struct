Inclusion Criteria:
Men [AND] women aged 18-45 years.
Diagnosis of functional dyspepsia, based on the Rome IV criteria (2016).
GIS score of at least 6.
Negative H. pylori test.
Availability of a signed patient information sheet (Informed Consent form) for participation in the clinical trial.
Patients who agree to use an effective method of contraception throughout the clinical trial.

Exclusion Criteria:
Organic diseases of the digestive system (gastro-oesophageal reflux disease (GERD) [OR], ulcer [OR], chronic pancreatitis [OR], cholelithiasis [OR], fatty liver disease [OR], hepatitis [OR], cirrhosis of liver [OR], etc.).
Diagnosis of other functional diseases of the digestive system, such as dyskinesia of cystic duct [OR] or gallbladder, irritable bowel syndrome, etc.
Discontinuation of proton pump inhibitors [OR], propulsives [OR], antispasmodics [OR], antacids [OR], or bismuth preparations less than 7 days prior to randomization.
H. Pylori eradication within 2 months before study entry.
Intestinal infection within 2 months before study entry.
Known history of [OR] suspected malignant neoplasm of various sites.
Prior diagnosis of a class IV cardiovascular disease (according to the New York Heart Association, 1964) [OR], hypothyroidism [OR], diabetes mellitus [OR], chronic kidney disease (С3-5) [OR], or disease of liver with portal hypertension [AND] and/or severe decompensation (Child-Pugh score > 6).
Other severe coexisting morbidity which, in the investigator's opinion, can prevent the patient from participating in the study.
Allergy [OR] intolerance to any of the components of medications used in the treatment.
Pregnancy [OR], breast-feeding.
Patients who, from investigator's point of view, will fail to comply with the observation requirements of the trial [OR] with the dosing regimen of the investigational drugs.
Planned hospitalization during the study period, for any diagnostic [OR] treatment procedures.
Drug addiction [OR], alcohol use in the amount over 2 units of alcohol a day [OR], mental diseases.
Intake of medicines listed in the section 'Prohibited concomitant treatment' for 1 month prior to the enrollment in the trial.
Participation in other clinical trials within 3 months to the enrollment in this study.
Patient is related to the research staff of the clinical investigative site who are directly involved in the trial [OR] is the immediate family member of the investigator. The immediate family members include husband/wife [OR], parents [OR], children [OR] or brothers (or sisters), regardless of whether they are natural or adopted.
Patient works for OOO "NPF "MATERIA MEDICA HOLDING" (i.e., is the company's employee [OR], temporary contract worker [OR] appointed official responsible for carrying out the research [OR] their immediate family).
