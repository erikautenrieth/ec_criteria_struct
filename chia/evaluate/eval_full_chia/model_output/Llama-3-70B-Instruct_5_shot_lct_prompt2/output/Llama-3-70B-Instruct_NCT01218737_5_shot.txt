Inclusion Criteria:
Patient is indicated to have an ocular refractive surgery performed (myopia [OR] astigmatism [OR] hypermetropy) by the Lasik method. 
Patient presents a normal eye fundus. 
Patient has intraocular pressure (IOP) ≤ 20 mmHg. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Surgery [AND]/or previous ocular pathology (presence of scar/change in the cornea, glaucoma, retinopathies, etc.). 
Patient has diabetes [OR] is immunodepressed. 
Any systemic infection during the study. 
Signs [AND]/or symptoms of ocular inflammation/infection (bacterial [OR] viral [OR] fungal [OR] caused by Chlamydia [OR] by Mycobacterium [OR] Acanthamoeba [OR] or of allergic etiology). 
Have used any systemic [OR] topical antibiotics for ocular infection in the previous 14 days. 
Patient has known hypersensitivity [AND] to any of the components of the formulations used in the study.
