Inclusion Criteria:
current diagnosis of narcolepsy with cataplexy [OR] healthy control

Exclusion Criteria:
use of any sedative hypnotics [OR], tranquilizers [OR], anticonvulsants [OR], antihistamines (except non-sedating) [OR], benzodiazepines [OR], clonidine [OR] or any medication known to affect dopamine at start of baseline period
significant [AND] unstable [OR] uncontrolled medical/psychiatric disease
significant history of head trauma/surgery [OR] seizure disorder
radiation exposure exceeding 20mSv in last 12 months
pregnancy
substance abuse/dependence (including alcohol)
have sleep apnea [OR], or are shift workers
on a sodium-restricted diet
has ever taken Xyrem [OR] sodium oxybate [OR] GHB at any time
claustrophobia
metal implants [OR] objects in the body that may interfere with MRI
succinic semialdehyde dehydrogenase deficiency
