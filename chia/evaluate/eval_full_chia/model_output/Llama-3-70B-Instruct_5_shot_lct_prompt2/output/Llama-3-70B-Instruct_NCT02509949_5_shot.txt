Inclusion Criteria:
age > 17 [AND] < 60 years;
American Society of Anesthesiology (ASA) I-III;
admitted for living donor renal transplantation.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with a history of drug abuse;
preoperative history of schizophrenia [OR] epilepsy [OR] parkinsonism [OR] use of cholinesterase inhibitor [OR] inability to communicate in the preoperative period ([OR] coma [OR] profound dementia [OR] language barrier).
