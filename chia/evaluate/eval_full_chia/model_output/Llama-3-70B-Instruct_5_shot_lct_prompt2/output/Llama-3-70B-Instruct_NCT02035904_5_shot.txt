Inclusion Criteria:
F; age 18 to 70
American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) I [OR] II;
breast cancer ( DIN 2 [OR] 3, [OR] LIN 2 [OR] 3 sec. Tavassoli) scheduled for nipple-sparing mastectomy [OR], simple mastectomy [OR], skin-sparing mastectomy [OR], skin-reducing mastectomy [AND] c, lymphnode biopsy [AND] axillary dissection;
immediate sub-pectoral prosthetic reconstruction;
signed informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
preexisting pectoral [OR], axillar [OR], thoracic homolateral pain
habitual opioid consumption;
drug-alcoholics addiction ;
ICU postoperative recovery;
kidney failure (creatinin > 2 g/dl [OR], creatinin <clearance 30 ml/h) [AND] or hepatic failure (cholinesterase < 2000 UI);
cardiac arrhythmias [OR];
Epilepsy;
Psychiatric [OR], cognitive disorders [OR], mental retardation;
Coagulopathies (INR > 2 [OR], activated partial thromboplastin time - aPTT>44 sec);
platelet count less than 100.000/mm3;
BMI > 30;
Allergies to study drugs.
