Inclusion Criteria:
Septic shock patients [AND] despite early goal directed therapy
Agree to participate [AND] this study

Exclusion Criteria:
Age < 18
Pregnancy
Bradycardia (HR < 55bpm)
Systolic Blood Pressure < 80 mmHg [OR] Mean arterial pressure < 50 mmHg on maximal support
Death [NOT] imminent
Unlikely to survive 90 days
Acute liver failure
Dementia
High-grade block [AND] in the absence of a functioning pacemaker.
