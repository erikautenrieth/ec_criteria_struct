Inclusion Criteria:
Need of lower third molar surgeries

Exclusion Criteria:
Presence of systemic diseases;
Presence of local inflammation [AND] and/ [OR] or infection;
Any history of allergic reaction to local anesthetics, [OR] gastrointestinal bleeding [OR] or ulceration;
Cardiovascular, [OR] kidney [OR] or hepatic diseases;
Patients [AND] who are making use of antidepressants, [OR] diuretics [OR] or anticoagulants;
Asthma [AND] and allergy to aspirin, [OR] ibuprofen [OR] or any other nonsteroidal antiinflammatory drug;
Regular use of any nonsteroidal antiinflammatory drug, [OR] Pregnancy [OR] or breast feeding.
