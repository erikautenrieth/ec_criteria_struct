Inclusion Criteria:
At least two of the following additional criteria
At least 70 yrs old
Female gender
Diabetes
Creatinine clearance <60mL/ [OR] min
History of gastro-intestinal [OR] or other organ bleeding
Baseline anemia
Current treatment [AND] with glycoproteins IIb-IIIa inhibitors

Exclusion Criteria:
Intolerance [OR] or allergy to ASA, [OR] clopidogrel [OR] or ticlopidine precluding treatment for 12 months
Concurrent participation in other investigational study
Femoral sheath (artery)
