Inclusion Criteria:
18 years old [OR] or older.
ADHD is diagnosed according to Diagnostic [AND] and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, [OR] fifth edition (DSM-5 criteria).
Substance Use Disorder is diagnosed according to DSM-5 criteria.
Qb-score 1.3 [OR] or higher on at least one of the weighted summary parameters QbActivity, [OR] QbInattention [OR] or QbImpulsivity on the QbTest.
Participants are given their written informed consent to participate in the study.

Exclusion Criteria:
Affected by alcohol [OR] or drugs during the last month.
Untreated severe comorbid psychiatric [OR] or somatic illness.
Bloodpressure 150/ [OR] 95 [OR] or higher.
Irregular pulse, [OR] or pulse 100 [OR] or higher.
No counter indications according to the Medikinet pill.
Concurrent clinical diagnosis [AND] that significantly could affect test performance.
Concurrent prescription of medicines for ADHD [OR] or medicines [AND] that significantly could affect test performance.
