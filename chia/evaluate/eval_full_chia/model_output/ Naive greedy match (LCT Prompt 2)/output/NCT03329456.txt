Inclusion Criteria:
. Inclusion criteria are American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) physical status I-III, [OR] age between 18 [AND] and 70 years [AND] and body mass index (BMI) between 20 [AND] and 35 kg/ [OR] m2.

Exclusion Criteria:
Exclusion criteria are pregnancy, [OR] patients [AND] with contraindications to regional anesthesia, [OR] allergy to LAs, [OR] patients taking opioids regularly due to chronic pain, [OR] use of anticoagulation drugs other than acetylsalicylic acid [OR] or dipyridamole, [OR] atrioventricular block, [OR] diabetes.
