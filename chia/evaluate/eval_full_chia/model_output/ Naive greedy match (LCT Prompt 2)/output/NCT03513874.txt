Inclusion Criteria:
Type 1 diabetes according to ADA criterias <5 years.
Age= 18 years [AND] and less than 70 years.
Non-obese: defined as BMI less than 28 kg/ [OR] m2
Positive for at least one of the anti-islet autoantibodies: GADA, [OR] IA2A, [OR] ZnT8A
Fasting [OR] or postprandial plasma C-peptide more than 100 pmol/ [OR] L
Written informed consent from the patient [OR] or family representative.

Exclusion Criteria:
History of any malignancy [OR] or other severe diseases
Female patients [AND] who are pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding before [OR] or during the three-year follow-up
Poor compliance [OR] or refusal to participate.
