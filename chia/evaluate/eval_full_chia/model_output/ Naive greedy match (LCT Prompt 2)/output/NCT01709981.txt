Inclusion Criteria:
Patients must be more than 18 years of age [AND] and referred for coronary angiography

Exclusion Criteria:
Plan for diagnostic-only coronary angiography
On colchicine chronically
History of intolerance to colchicine
Glomerular filtration rate <30mL/ [OR] minute [OR] or on dialysis
Active malignancy [OR] or infection
History of myelodysplasia
High-dose statin load <24 hours prior to procedure
Use of oral steroids [OR] or non-steroidal anti-inflammatory agents other than aspirin within 72 hours [OR] or 3 times the agent's half-life (whichever is longer)
Use of strong CYP3A4/ [OR] P-glycoprotein inhibitors (specifically ritonavir, [OR] ketoconazole, [OR] clarithromycin, [OR] cyclosporine, [OR] diltiazem [AND] and verapamil)
Unable to consent
Participating in a competing study
