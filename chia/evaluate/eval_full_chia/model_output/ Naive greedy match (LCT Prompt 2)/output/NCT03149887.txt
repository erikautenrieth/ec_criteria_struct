Inclusion Criteria:
Adult patients up to age 75 years, [OR] undergoing elective, [OR] ambulatory, [OR] arthroscopic rotator cuff repair.

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnancy, [OR] coagulopathy, [OR] allergy to bupivacaine, [OR] renal failure, [OR] hepatic insufficiency, [AND] and/ [OR] or inappropriate candidate for usual therapy (specifically, [OR] if [NOT] unable to receive the usual preoperative interscalene nerve block: preexisting nerve injury on side of surgery, [OR] refusal of nerve block, [OR] infection at site of nerve block).
