Inclusion Criteria:
Midsubstance pain in the achilles tendon
Symptoms for at least 3 months
Ultrasound scanning at the first visit shows thickness of the achilles tendon above 7 mm [OR] or 20% thicker than the contralateral.
Patient can read [AND] and understand danish

Exclusion Criteria:
Earlier operations in the foot [AND] and leg, [AND] that is judged to complicate training
known arthritis.
known diabetes
Leg ulcerations [OR] or infections in the foot.
Judged [NOT] unable to comply [AND] with the training protocol.
Daily use of pain killers
Glucocorticosteroid injection to the diseased achilles tendon within the last 6 months.
Earlier allergic reactions to glucocorticosteroid [OR] or local anesthetic.
Pregnancy [OR] or planning to become pregnant
BMI above 30.
