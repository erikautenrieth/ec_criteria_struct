Inclusion Criteria:
Healthy patients
aged 3-13 years
Level I [OR] or level II on the American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) physical status classification system (as determined by the anesthesiologist)
obstructive sleep apnea [OR] or recurrent throat infections
undergoing elective tonsillectomy [AND] with [OR] or [NOT] without adenoidectomy
Parents [AND] who agree to complete documentation [AND] and follow up at 14 days post-operation.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients Level III [OR] or greater on the American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) physical status classification system (as determined by the anesthesiologist)
Patients [AND] with chronic conditions [AND] that would limit our ability to develop the study according to objectives, [OR] such as neurodevelopmental conditions preventing patients from understanding the Oucher tool
Hepatic [OR] or renal disease
cardiac disease
active infection
diabetes mellitus
sickle cell disease
known coagulation disorders
pre- operative treatment [AND] with anti-emetics, [OR] steroids, [OR] or analgesics
Acetaminophen allergy [OR] or already receiving acetaminophen within 24 h of surgery
Complicating health factors precluding the use of opioids [OR] or acetaminophen
any other factors which would interfere [AND] with pain assessment [AND] and management
Patients weighing more than 30 kg [AND] that would exceed maximum dexamethasone dose
Patients [AND] who live [NOT] without a home telephone
patient living [NOT] without parental supervision.
