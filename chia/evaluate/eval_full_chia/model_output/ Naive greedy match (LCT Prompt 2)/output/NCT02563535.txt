Inclusion Criteria:
age>18 years
critical limb ischemia (Rutherford class 4-6)
angiographic stenosis>50% [OR] or occlusion of at least one tibial vessel of at least 40mm for which an interventional treatment is scheduled

Exclusion Criteria:
need for major amputation known before intervention
allergy to Paclitaxel
contraindication for combined antiplatelet treatment
life expectancy <1 year
hypersensitivity [OR] or contraindication to one of the study drugs
[NOT] lack of consent
