Inclusion Criteria:
Informed consent as documented by signature (see informed consent form)
Primary ITP according to the definition of Rodeghiero et al. (52) [AND] and a platelet count of <30x109/ [OR] l
Age range: 18-45 years
Previously treated patients, [AND] with failure [OR] or intolerance to first-line therapy, [OR] or relapse after first-line therapy, [OR] i.e. corticosteroids, [OR] intravenous immunoglobulin (IVIG), [OR] or anti-D immunoglobulins

Exclusion Criteria:
Adults older than 45 [AND] and children younger than 18 years
Platelet count higher than 30x109/ [OR] l at time of screening
Suspicion of secondary ITP
Positive family history for ITP
Presence [OR] or history of autoimmune disease as judged by the investigator
Hepatosplenomegaly
Presence [OR] or history of relevant hepatic disease as judged by the investigator
Presence [OR] or history of thromboembolic disease as judged by the investigator
Patients [AND] with splenectomy
Women [AND] who are pregnant [OR] or breast feeding
Intention to become pregnant during the course of the study
Lack of safe double contraception (see 7.1)
Any vaccination 2 weeks prior start of the study
Drugs [AND] with a known impact on the immune system [OR] or on platelet function must be recorded [AND] and an exclusion of the study should be discussed [AND] with the study center
Known [OR] or suspected non-compliance, [OR] drug [OR] or alcohol abuse
[NOT] Inability to follow the procedures of the study, [OR] e.g. due to language problems, [OR] psychological disorders, [OR] dementia of the study subject
Participation in another study [AND] with investigational drug within the 30 days preceding [AND] and during the present study
Previous enrolment into the current study
Previous treatment [AND] with romiplostim [OR] or eltrombopag
Hypersensitivity to the active substance [OR] or to any of the excipients [OR] or to E. coli derived proteins
Enrolment of the investigator, [OR] his/ [OR] her family members, [OR] employees [AND] and other dependent persons
