Inclusion Criteria:
Type of subject: outpatient. 
Informed consent: Subjects must give their signed [AND] and dated written informed consent to participate. 
Gender: Male [OR] or female. Female subjects must be post-menopausal [OR] or using a highly effective method for avoidance of pregnancy. The decision to include [OR] or exclude women of childbearing potential may be made at the discretion of the investigator in accordance [AND] with local practice in relation to adequate contraception. 
Age: >=40 [AND] and <=80 years of age at Screening (Visit 1). 
Tobacco use: Subjects [AND] with a current [OR] or prior history of >=10 pack-years of cigarette smoking at screening (Visit 1). Previous smokers are defined as those [AND] who have stopped smoking for at least 6 months prior to Visit 1. 
Airflow Obstruction: 
Subjects [AND] with a measured post-albuterol/ [OR] salbutamol forced expiratory volume in 1 second (FEV1)/ [OR] (forced vital capacity)FVC ratio of <=0.70 at Screening (Visit 1). 
Subjects [AND] with a measured post-albuterol/ [OR] salbutamol FEV1 >=50 [AND] and <=70% of predicted normal values calculated using NHANES III reference equations [Hankinson, [OR] 1999; Hankinson, [OR] 2010] at Screening (Visit 1). 
Post-bronchodilator spirometry will be performed approximately 15 minutes after the subject has self-administered 4 inhalations (i.e., [OR] total 400mcg) of albuterol/ [OR] salbutamol via a metered dose inhaler (MDI )[AND] with a valved-holding chamber. The FEV1/ [OR] FVC ratio [AND] and FEV1 percent predicted values will be calculated. 
Symptoms of COPD: Subjects must score 2 [OR] or higher on the modified Medical Research Council Dyspnea scale (Visit 1) 
Cardiovascular disease: 
For patients >= 40 years of age: any one of the following: 
Established (i.e. by clinical signs [OR] or imaging studies) coronary artery disease (CAD) Established (i.e. by clinical signs [OR] or imaging studies) peripheral vascular disease (PVD) Previous stroke Previous MI Diabetes mellitus [AND] with target organ disease OR 
For patients >=60 years of age: any 2 of the following: 
Being treated for hypercholesterolemia Being treated for hypertension Being treated for diabetes mellitus Being treated for peripheral vascular disease 

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnancy: Women [AND] who are pregnant [OR] or lactating. 
Asthma: Subjects [AND] with a current diagnosis of asthma. (Subjects [AND] with a prior history of asthma are eligible if they also have a current diagnosis of COPD). 
alpha 1-antitrypsin deficiency: Subjects [AND] with known alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency as the underlying cause of COPD. 
Other respiratory disorders: Subjects [AND] with active tuberculosis, [OR] lung cancer, [OR] bronchiectasis, [OR] sarcoidosis, [OR] pulmonary fibrosis, [OR] pulmonary hypertension, [OR] interstitial lung diseases [OR] or other active pulmonary diseases. 
Lung resection [OR] or transplantation: Subjects [AND] with lung volume reduction surgery within the 12 months prior to Screening [OR] or [AND] having had a lung transplant. 
A moderate/ [OR] severe COPD exacerbation [AND] that has [NOT] not resolved at least 14 days prior to Visit 1 [AND] and at least 30 days following the last dose of oral corticosteroids (if applicable). 
Current severe heart failure (New York Heart Association class IV). Subjects will also be [NOT] excluded if they have a known ejection fraction of <30% [OR] or if they have an implantable cardioverter defibrillator (ICD). 
Other diseases/ [OR] abnormalities: Any life-threatening condition [AND] with life expectancy <3 years, [OR] other than vascular disease [OR] or COPD, [AND] that might [NOT] prevent the subject from completing the study. 
End stage chronic renal disease: Subjects will be [NOT] excluded if on renal replacement therapy (hemodialysis [OR] or peritoneal). 
Drug/ [OR] food allergy: Subjects [AND] with a history of hypersensitivity to any of the study medications (e.g. beta-agonists, [OR] corticosteroid) [OR] or components of the inhalation powder (e.g. lactose, [OR] magnesium stearate). In addition, [OR] patients [AND] with a history of severe milk protein allergy [AND] that, [OR] in the opinion of the study physician, [OR] contraindicates the subject's participation will also be [NOT] excluded. 
Drug/ [OR] alcohol abuse: Subjects [AND] with a known [OR] or suspected history of alcohol [OR] or drug abuse within the last 2 years. 
Oxygen therapy: Subjects receiving treatment [AND] with long-term oxygen therapy (LTOT) [OR] or nocturnal oxygen therapy required for greater than 12 hours a day. Oxygen prn use (i.e. <=12 hours per day) is [NOT] not exclusionary. 
Questionable validity of consent: Subjects [AND] with a history of psychiatric disease, [OR] intellectual deficiency, [OR] poor motivation [OR] or other conditions [AND] that will limit the validity of informed consent to participate in the study [OR] or the potential compliance to study procedures. 
Affiliation [AND] with investigator site: Study investigators, [OR] sub-investigators, [OR] study coordinators, [OR] employees of a participating investigator [OR] or immediate family members of the aforementioned are [NOT] excluded from participating in this study.
