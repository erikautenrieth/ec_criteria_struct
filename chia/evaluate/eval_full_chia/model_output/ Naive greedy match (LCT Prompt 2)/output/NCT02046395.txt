Inclusion Criteria:
Type 2 Diabetes
Hypertension
Estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) > 30 ml/ [OR] min
Use of Ace Inh [AND] and ARB for control of blood pressure [AND] who are willing to be placed on alternate drug(s) in the washout period for blood pressure control

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnancy
Patients [AND] with chronic kidney disease stage [AND] with eGFR < 30 ml/ [OR] min (CKD stage IV [AND] and V)
Nephrotic range proteinuria (urinary protein > 3.5 gm/ [OR] day)
History [OR] or renal transplantation
History of multiple myeloma
Known history of hypersensitivity reaction [OR] or intolerability to Ace Inh [OR] or ARB.
