Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis reviewed at transplant center [AND] and confirmed to fit the criterion for high risk blood disease [OR] or cancer, [OR] as defined for the study 
Estimated life expectancy of at least 6 weeks following study entry 
Cancer [AND] and Leukemia Group B (CALGB) performance status less than [OR] or equal to 2 
White blood cell count, [OR] platelet, [OR] hematocrit, [OR] tuberculosis, [OR] aspartate aminotransferase (AST), [OR] alanine aminotransferase (ALT), [OR] alkaline phosphatase, [OR] creatinine, [AND] and HIV test results reviewed by transplant center 
Multiple gated acquisition (MUGA), [OR] echocardiogram, [OR] cardiac MRI, [AND] and/ [OR] or pulmonary function tests (PFT) performed [AND] and reviewed by transplant center (for individuals [AND] with an ejection fraction [AND] and diffusing capacity [DLCO] of 40-50%, [OR] the appropriate cardiology [OR] or pulmonary consultations should be considered if the individual has severe heart [OR] or lung disease at the initiation of therapy)

Exclusion Criteria:

Diagnosed [AND] with a medical [OR] or psychiatric illness [AND] that may interfere [AND] with study participation
