Inclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with Ph (BCR/ [OR] ABL) positive de novo < 55 years old (it is advisable to include patients over 55 years LAL07OPH protocol).
Performance status 0-2 (Appendix B) may include patients [AND] with performance status > 2 attributable to LAL.
Patients [NOT] without functional impairment of organs: liver function: total bilirubin, [OR] AST, [OR] ALT, [OR] alfa-GT [AND] and alkaline phosphatase less than 3 times the upper limit of normal laboratory renal function: serum creatinine < 2 mg/ [OR] dL [OR] or clearance creatinine > 30 ml/ [OR] min (except renal function attributable to LAL) cardiac function (Appendix B) normal: ventricular EF > 50%, [NOT] absence of severe chronic respiratory disease. In the event [AND] that alterations are secondary to the disease is at the discretion of the investigator to determine if the patient can be included in the trial.

Exclusion Criteria:
Any other variety of LAL
Patients [AND] with a history of coronary artery disease, [OR] valvular [OR] or hypertensive heart disease
Patients [AND] with chronic liver disease
Patients [AND] with chronic respiratory failure
Renal failure [NOT] not due to LAL
Patients [AND] with positive HIV status
No serious neurological abnormalities due to LAL
Impact on overall severe (grade 3 [OR] or 4 of the WHO scale) [NOT] not attributable to the LAL
Pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding
initial blast crisis CML
