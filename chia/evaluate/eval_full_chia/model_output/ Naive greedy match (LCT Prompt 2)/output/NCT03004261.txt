Inclusion Criteria:
Any allogeneic stem cell transplant recipient = 14 years of age [AND] and = 60 years of age
Bilirubin/ [OR] SGOT/ [OR] SGPT < 5 × upper normal limits.
Creatinine < 2 × upper normal limits.
Ejection fraction = 50%, [NOT] no severe arrhythmia.
Estimated life expectancy = 6 months.
Patients' CMV-DNA = 1000cp/ [OR] ml in treatment group [AND] and being negative in prophylactic group.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients receiving prednisone = 1mg/ [OR] kg/ [OR] d for the treatment of acute GVHD [OR] or mild, [OR] severe chronic GVHD.
Recipient < 14years of age
Donor is sero-positive in HBV/ [OR] HCV/ [OR] HIV [OR] or RPR.
