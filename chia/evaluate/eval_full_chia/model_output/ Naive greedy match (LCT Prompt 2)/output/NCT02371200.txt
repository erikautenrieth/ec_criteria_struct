Inclusion Criteria:
1. Subject has a history of GTC seizures, [OR] either primary GTC [OR] or partial onset seizures [AND] with secondary generalization. 
2. Is being admitted to a hospital for routine vEEG monitoring related to seizures. 
3. Male [OR] or female between the ages of 2-99. 
4. Has an upper arm circumference which is adequate for proper fit of the EMG monitor (at least 14cm). 
5. If female [AND] and of childbearing potential, [OR] has a negative pregnancy test. 
6. Can understand [AND] and sign written informed consent, [OR] or will have a parent [OR] or a legally authorized representative (LAR) [AND] who can do so, [OR] prior to the performance of any study assessments. 
7. Subject [AND] and/ [OR] or Primary Caregiver must be competent to follow all study procedures. 
8. Is able to read, [OR] speak, [AND] and understand English. 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Does [NOT] not have a documented history of generalized seizures. 
2. Has [NOT] not had a GTC seizure within the last year AND is [NOT] not expected to have a reduction of anti-epileptic drugs during their hospital admission. 
3. Intracranial EEG electrodes are being used 
4. The subject's upper arm circumference [NOT] not adequate for proper fit of the EMG monitor (less than 14cm). 
5. Pregnant female. 
6. Subject/ [OR] Caregiver is [NOT] unable to provide consent.
