Inclusion Criteria:
Patient is indicated to have an ocular refractive surgery performed (myopia, [OR] astigmatism, [OR] hypermetropy) by the Lasik method. 
Patient presents a normal eye fundus. 
Patient has intraocular pressure (IOP) ≤ 20 mmHg. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Surgery [AND] and/ [OR] or previous ocular pathology (presence of scar/ [OR] change in the cornea, [OR] glaucoma, [OR] retinopathies, [OR] etc.). 
Patient has diabetes [OR] or is immunodepressed. 
Any systemic infection during the study. 
Signs [AND] and/ [OR] or symptoms of ocular inflammation/ [OR] infection (bacterial, [OR] viral, [OR] fungal, [OR] caused by Chlamydia, [OR] by Mycobacterium, [OR] Acanthamoeba [OR] or of allergic etiology). 
Have used any systemic [OR] or topical antibiotics for ocular infection in the previous 14 days. 
Patient has known hypersensitivity to any of the components of the formulations used in the study.
