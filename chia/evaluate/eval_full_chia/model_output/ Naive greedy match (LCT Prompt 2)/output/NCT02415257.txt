Inclusion Criteria:
Vestibular schwannoma advised to surgical treatment
No measurable remaining vestibular function

Exclusion Criteria:
impaired decision making
neurofibromatosis
signs for central dysfunction
remaining vestibular function
Patients are advised [NOT] not to participate in the gentamicin arm if
hearing is better than 30 deciBel (dB) in pure tone average (500, [OR] 1000, [OR] 2000, [OR] 3-4000 Hz) [AND] and speech discrimination better than 70%
the neurosurgeon aim at hearing preservation surgery [AND] and do [NOT] not want to risk gentamicin associated hearing loss
