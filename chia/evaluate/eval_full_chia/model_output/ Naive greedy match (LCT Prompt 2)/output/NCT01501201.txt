Inclusion Criteria:
Type 2 diabetes mellitus [AND] with HbA1c > 7.5 %
Body mass index > 35 [AND] and < 50 kg/ [OR] m2
Candidate for Gastric By-Pass
Treatment [AND] with GLP1 (glucagon-like peptide) analogue [OR] or insulin

Exclusion Criteria:
Contraindication to bariatric surgery
Pregnancy
Affiliation of health care assurance
Psychiatric disorders
