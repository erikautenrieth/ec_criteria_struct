Inclusion Criteria:
Before participate in the study, [OR] patients must understand the treatment plan [AND] and willing to participate in the study. Patients must have signed an approved informed consent.
Histopathologic confirmed squamous cell carcinoma of head [AND] and neck ,including oral cavity, [OR] oropharynx, [OR] larynx, [OR] or hypopharynx.
Ages=65 years,Not limited to gender.
ECOG performance status =2.
Patients [AND] with surgical contraindication [OR] or reject to surgery.
Postoperative TNM(primary tumor,regional nodes,metastasis) staging III~IV, [OR] positive surgical margin.
[NOT] without evidence of distant metastases.
No contraindication to chemoradiotherapy.
Life expectancy > 3 months.
Available Organ function: white blood cell=3.5×109/ [OR] L, [OR] Neutrophils =1.5×109/ [OR] L, [OR] Hemoglobin =80g/ [OR] L, [OR] Blood platelet>100×109/ [OR] L; Alanine aminotransferase (ALT) [AND] and Aspartate aminotransferase (AST)= 2.5 upper limit of normal(ULN); Total bilirubin (TBIL) <1.5 ULN;serum creatinine=1.5 ULN; creatinine clearance of = 50ml/ [OR] min

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with a history of any other malignancy.
Concomitant treatment [AND] with any other anticancer therapy.
