Inclusion Criteria:
Singleton pregnancy;
8-22 weeks gestation
Previous pregnancy complicated by gestational diabetes

Exclusion Criteria:
Established pre-existing diabetes (including unrecognised diabetes defined as a fasting plasma glucose = 7.0mmol/ [OR] L [AND] and/ [OR] or HbA1c = 48mmol/ [OR] mol); Contraindications to metformin therapy (creatinine = 130µmol/ [OR] L/ [OR] alanine transaminase = 2.0 x upper limit normal/ [OR] previous intolerance to metformin)
Planned continued antenatal care/ [OR] delivery at centre [NOT] not included in trial
Planned fast for cultural/ [OR] religious reasons e.g. Ramadan
