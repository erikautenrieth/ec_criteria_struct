Inclusion Criteria:
Patient aged 18 years [OR] or older. 
Patient suffering from mild to moderate active proctitis [OR] or distal proctosigmoiditis (MAYO score ≥ 3 [AND] and ≤ 10) at inclusion based on clinical [AND] and endoscopic findings within 6 months before study inclusion. 
Patient [AND] with evidence of endoscopic active proctitis [OR] or distal proctosigmoiditis (Montreal classification E1 [OR] or E2 defined by an involvement [NOT] not exceeding 25 cm from the anal margin) within 6 months before study inclusion. 
Treatment of the current flare [AND] with Pentasa® to induce a remission initiated by the patient, [OR] the general practitioner [OR] or the gastroenterologist, [OR] during the inclusion visit [OR] or during the week before the inclusion visit. 
Patient [AND] having received oral [AND] and written information on the study, [NOT] without any objections for the use of his/ [OR] her personal data, [AND] and [AND] having signed a written Informed Consent Form. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Patient included in an interventional study assessing treatment for active proctitis [OR] or distal proctosigmoiditis. 
Patient [AND] with left sided, [OR] colitis [OR] or pancolitis. 
Patient [AND] with severe proctitis (MAYO score ≥ 11 at inclusion). 
Patient previously treated [AND] with biologics. 
Patient treated [AND] with immunosuppressive within 1 month before study inclusion. 
Patient treated [AND] with corticosteroids within 2 weeks before study inclusion.
