Inclusion Criteria:
ASA I-III patients scheduled for elective one [OR] or two level minimally invasive lumbar fusions

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients requiring surgery for neoplastic processes
Allergy to acetaminophen
Liver dysfunction [AND] and elevated Liver Function Tests (LFTs)
Alcohol [OR] or drug dependency
Mental retardation
Less than 50 kg of weight
regnant women
Patients requiring long-acting opioid pain management (including fentanyl patch, [OR] oxycontin, [OR] etc) for over 3 weeks immediately prior to surgery
