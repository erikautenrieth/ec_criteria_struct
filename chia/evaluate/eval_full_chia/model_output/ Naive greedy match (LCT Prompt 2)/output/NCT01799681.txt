Inclusion Criteria:
diagnosed [AND] with PD by a neurologist (Fahn [AND] and Elton, [OR] 1987); 
aged 30 to 85 years; 
at modified Hoehn [AND] and Yahr (H&Y) stage 1.5 to 3 (Hoehn [AND] and Yahr ,1967; Goetz et al., [OR] 2004); 
able [AND] and willing to give written consent for participation in the study; 
living at home in the community; 
able to walk independently for 30 metres [AND] with [OR] or [NOT] without an assistive device. 

Exclusion Criteria:
any neurological conditions other than PD; 
significant musculoskeletal [OR] or cardiopulmonary diseases; 
other disorders [AND] that may affect balance [OR] or locomotion; 
taken any structured behavioral [OR] or exercise programs in the past 3 months [OR] or they are receiving regular physical rehabilitation at present; 
unstable condition on anti-parkinsonian medications; 
surgical interventions for PD; 
communication [OR] or cognitive deficits [AND] with mini-mental state examination, [OR] (MMSE) <24/ [OR] 30 (Folstein et al., [OR] 1975); 
a history of more than two falls in the previous 12 months.
