Inclusion Criteria:
• All patients attending for a routine diagnostic endoscopic procedure at St Mary's Hospital NHS Trust for dyspepsia [AND] and abdominal pain 

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients attending for a therapeutic endoscopic procedure e.g. variceal banding, [OR] stent insertion, [OR] balloon dilatation. 
Patients [AND] with a known diagnosis e.g. upper gastrointestinal cancer 
Patients previously treated [AND] with HP eradication therapy 
Patients [AND] who had taken PPI, [OR] H2 receptor antagonists [AND] and antibiotics within 4 weeks 
Patients [AND] with acute gastrointestinal bleeding 
Patients [AND] who'd had previous gastric surgery 
Patients [AND] with chronic liver disease 
Patients [AND] with abnormal coagulation [OR] or any other contra-indication to use of standard biopsy in routine diagnostic endoscopic procedures 
Patients [AND] who are [NOT] unable [OR] or unwilling to give informed consent 
Patients under the age of 18 years
