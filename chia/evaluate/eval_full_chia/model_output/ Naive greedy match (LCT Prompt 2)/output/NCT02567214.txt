Inclusion Criteria:
Age > 50 years
Smoking history > 10 packs/ [OR] year
FEV1 30 - 79% of predicted [AND] and FEV1/ [OR] FVC < 70% (GOLD 2-3)
FRC > 120 % predicted
Borg dyspnea score > 3 during the 3-min constant rate shuttle walking test at V3

Exclusion Criteria:
Respiratory exacerbation within the 2 months preceding the study
Current diagnostic of asthma
Significant O2 desaturation (SpO2 < 85%) at rest [OR] or during exercise
Presence of another pathology [AND] that could influence exercise tolerance
Use of home oxygen
