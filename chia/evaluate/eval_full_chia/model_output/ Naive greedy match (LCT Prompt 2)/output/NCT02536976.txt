Inclusion Criteria:
Aged 25-80 at screening. Subjects older than 80 will be allowed at the discretion of the PI.
Ambulatory (defined as able to ambulate at least 10 meters, [AND] with [OR] or [NOT] without assistance).
Clinical Diagnosis of PD based on the United Kingdom Brain Bank diagnostic criteria for PD.
At least 8 micturitions per 24 hours [AND] and
At least 3 urgency episodes per 3-day diary.
A MoCA score between 19 [AND] and 28 (inclusive) at screening. For those on cognitive enhancers (donepezil, [OR] rivastigmine, [OR] memantine, [OR] galantamine) a MoCA score between 19 [AND] and 29 (inclusive) at screening.
Provide informed consent to participate in the study [AND] and understand [AND] that they may withdraw their consent at any time [NOT] without prejudice to their future medical care.
Be cognitively capable, [OR] in the opinion of investigator, [OR] to understand [AND] and provide such informed consent.
Be cognitively capable to complete the required questionnaires [AND] and assessments, [OR] OR have a care partner [AND] who is willing [AND] and capable to assist them in the completion of these tasks.
Be on a stable regimen of antiparkinson's medications at least 30 days prior to screening, [AND] and be expected to remain on a stable dose for the duration of the study.
If taking cognitive enhancers (donepezil, [OR] rivastigmine, [OR] memantine, [OR] galantamine), [OR] must be on stable dose at least 30 days prior to screening, [AND] and be expected to remain on a stable dose for the duration of the study.

Exclusion Criteria:
Known [OR] or suspected alcohol [OR] or substance abuse in the preceding 12 months.
Women [AND] who are pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding.
Women of childbearing potential (WOCP) [AND] who are [NOT] not using at least one method of contraception.
Patients [AND] with severe renal impairment (CLcr = 29 mL/ [OR] min, [OR] or eGFR = 29 mL/ [OR] min/ [OR] 1.73 m2), [OR] or moderate [OR] or severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh classes B [OR] or C).
Patients [AND] with bladder outlet obstruction (BOO) [AND] that, [OR] in the opinion of the study urologist, [OR] would expose them to risk of urinary retention during treatment [AND] with mirabegron.
Patients treated [AND] with drugs metabolized by the CYP2D6 pathway.
Patients [AND] with supine systolic blood pressure (SBP) = 180 mm Hg, [OR] or diastolic blood pressure (DBP) = 110 mm Hg.
Clinically significant, [OR] uncontrolled cardiac arrhythmia, [OR] unstable angina, [OR] congestive heart failure (NYHA Class 3 [OR] or 4), [OR] or history of myocardial infarction in the preceding 2 years.
History of cancer in the preceding 2 years other than successfully treated, [OR] non-metastatic, [OR] squamous cell [OR] or basal cell carcinoma, [OR] or cervical cancer in situ.
Any major urological procedure in the preceding 90 days.
Any major surgical procedure in the preceding 30 days.
Previously treated [AND] with mirabegron within 60 days prior to the baseline visit (Visit 2), [OR] or previously [AND] having failed treatment [AND] with mirabegron regardless of duration [AND] and timing of treatment.

Currently receiving any other investigational drug [OR] or [AND] having received an investigational drug within the 60 days preceding the baseline visit (Visit 2).
Any condition [OR] or laboratory test result, [OR] which, [OR] in the opinion of the Investigator [OR] or the Study Urologist, [OR] might result in an increased risk to the patient, [OR] or would affect their participation in the study.
Any patient [AND] who, [OR] in the opinion of the Investigator, [OR] is [NOT] not a good candidate for the study [OR] or will [NOT] not be able to follow study procedures.
