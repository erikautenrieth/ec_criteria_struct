Inclusion Criteria:
Over 18 years of age; Systemically healthy; Non-smoking;
With good oral hygiene;
Absent irreversible pulpal alteration;
With the presence of a non-carious cervical lesion (LCNCs) [AND] that needs to be restored. This lesion should be non-carious, [OR] non-retentive, [AND] with at least 1 mm [AND] and up to 3 mm depth, [OR] should involve both enamel [AND] and dentin of vital teeth [NOT] without mobility, [AND] and present hypersensitivity;
Presence a natural tooth of the same position of the restored tooth, [AND] but in the opposite arch of the same jaw to be considered for the positive control;
Periodontal parameters : Depth Probing (PS), [OR] Visible Plaque Index (IPV), [OR] Gingival Index (GI) [AND] and Probing Bleed Index (SS). The normal included were: PS = 1 to 3 mm, [OR] GI = 0, [OR] IPV = score 0 e SS = score 0.

Exclusion Criteria:
With severe systemic alteration;
In the use of antibiotics [AND] and anti-inflammatories in the last three months;
With periodontium [AND] with periodontal parameters different from those established in the inclusion criteria.
Individuals [AND] with clinical signs of parafunctional habits;
Smoking;
Individuals [AND] who have performed other restorations in the last 12 months;
Pregnant women [AND] and infants;
Periodontal sites [AND] that presented bleeding during crevicular fluid collection [OR] or sites [AND] that [NOT] prevent proper collection of clinical parameters.
