Inclusion Criteria:
No response to more than one antiarrhythmic drug, [OR] or unwilling to receive long-term drug treatment.
Can provide informed consent form expressing willingness to participate in the study [AND] and comply [AND] with follow-up tests [AND] and evaluation procedures.
Aged 18-80 years.

Exclusion Criteria:
With acute diseases, [OR] such as acute phase after myocardial infarction (within 3 months), [OR] within 3 months after acute heart failure [OR] or new cerebral infarction;
In the list of heart transplantation;
Expected survival less than 1 year;
With other hemorrhagic diseases [AND] and anticoagulant therapy is [NOT] not allowed;
Thrombosis in left atrium;
Heart failure, [OR] New York Heart Association(NYHA) III/ [OR] IV [OR] or eject fraction(EF)<40%;
Patients [AND] with uncontrolled cancer;
Significant hepatic [OR] or renal impairment ( [AND] and/ [OR] or alanine transaminase(ALT) [OR] or Aspartate transaminase(AST) >2 times upper limit of normal, [OR] creatinine clearance rate(CCr)<50%);
Previous catheter radiofrequency ablation for AF [OR] or cardiac surgery;
Pregnant [AND] and lactating women, [OR] women [AND] who plan to become pregnant, [OR] or women of child bearing age [NOT] not using reliable contraceptive measures.
