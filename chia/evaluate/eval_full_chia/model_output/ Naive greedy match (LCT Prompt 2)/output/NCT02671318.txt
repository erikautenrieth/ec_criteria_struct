Inclusion Criteria:
Adult kidney transplant recipients > 18 y.o.
Kidney Transplant recipients, [OR] after the first episode of cytomegalovirus infection, [OR] using the current immunosuppressive regimen: azathioprine [OR] or mycophenolate, [OR] tacrolimus [AND] and prednisone.

Exclusion Criteria:
Re-transplant;
Patients [AND] with any panel reactive antibody (PRA) equal to [OR] or above 50%, [OR] class I [OR] or class II;
Acute rejection episode in the last 30 days, [OR] or episode > 2A in the Banff criteria;
GFR (MDRD) < 40 ml/ [OR] min;
Proteinuria > 0,5 g/ [OR] l;
Hemoglobin < 10 g/ [OR] l [AND] and/ [OR] or leucocytes < 4000 cels/ [OR] mm3 [AND] and/ [OR] or platelets < 150.000 cels/ [OR] mm3;
Triglycerides > 500 mg/ [OR] dl [AND] with [OR] or [NOT] without use of fibrate;
Cholesterol total > 300 mg/ [OR] dl [AND] with [OR] or [NOT] without use of statin;
Hepatic abnormalities;
Significant periphery edema;
Pulmonary abnormalities [OR] or breast x-ray abnormalities;
Hyper sensibility to sirolimus formula;
