Inclusion Criteria:
Subjects = 19 [OR] or = 75 years of age
Subjects undergoing treatment for type 2 diabetes
Subjects undergoing treatment of statin for hypercholesterolemia
Fasting LDL-C = 250mg/ [OR] dL at the screening visit
Fasting LDL-C =70mg/ [OR] dL [OR] or = 160mg/ [OR] dL at the randomization visit
Fasting TG<500mg/ [OR] dL

Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects [AND] with hypersensitivity reaction to Statin [AND] and Ezetimibe
Subjects [AND] with severe kidney disease
Subjects [AND] with HIV positive result at the screening
Pregnant [OR] or breast-feeding subjects
Subjects [AND] with taking any medication affecting level of LDL (Fenofibrate, [OR] Omega 3 fatty aicd etc.)
Insulin-treated Subjects
Other exclusions applied
