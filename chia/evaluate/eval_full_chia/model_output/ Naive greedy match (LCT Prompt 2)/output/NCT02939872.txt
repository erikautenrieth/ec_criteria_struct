Inclusion Criteria:
Age 19 [AND] and more
On dual [OR] or triple antiplatelet therapy [AND] and between 12months [AND] and 14months from Bioresorbable Vascular Scaffold implantation
No history of death, [OR] serious myocardial infarction, [OR] stroke, [OR] repeat revascularization, [OR] or major bleeding

Exclusion Criteria:
Contraindication to antiplatelet therapy
Need to continue clopidogrel due to stroke, [OR] peripheral disease, [OR] significant carotid disease [OR] or recent acute coronary syndrome
Major bleeding history [OR] or bleeding diathesis
Pregnancy
