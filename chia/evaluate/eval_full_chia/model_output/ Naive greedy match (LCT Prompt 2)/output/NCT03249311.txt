Inclusion Criteria:
Male participants between 18 [AND] and 40 years-old
Written informed consent signed by the participant

Exclusion Criteria:
Lifetime personal history of diagnosis of major depressive disorder according to the DSM-V (American Psychiatric Association, [OR] 2013) using the Structured Clinical Interview for DSM-V Axis I Disorders, [OR] Research Version, [OR] Non-patient Edition (SCID-5-RV for DSM-V; First et al., [OR] 2015)
A history of suicidal ideation [AND] and behaviour, [OR] including self-harm [AND] and/ [OR] or harm to others.
A history of substance abuse [AND] and/ [OR] or dependence.
A positive drug screen for illicit drugs
Substantial alcohol use
Current use of Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs), [OR] including the antibiotic linezolid [AND] and the thiazine dye methylthioninium chloride (methylene blue)
Current use of serotonin-precursors (such as L-tryptophan, [OR] oxitriptan)
Current use of serotonergic drugs (triptans, [OR] certain tricyclic antidepressants, [OR] lithium, [OR] tramadol, [OR] St. John's Wort)
Concomitant use of NSAIDS, [OR] ASA, [AND] and other anticoagulants.
Current use of Thioridazine
Current use of CYP1A2 Inhibitors
Current use of Triptans (5HT1 Agonists)
Blood pressure greater than 140/ [OR] 90 [AND] and/ [OR] or a pulse rate greater than 90 bpm
Recent history of myocardial infarction, [OR] cerebrovascular accident, [OR] cardiac arrhythmias, [OR] or unstable heart disease.
Evidence of significant physical illness contraindicating the use of levomilnacipran [AND] and duloxetine found on the physical exam [OR] or in the laboratory data obtained during the first week of the study
Current use of medication [AND] that may affect voiding (ie- anticholinergics)
History of obstructive urinary disorders [AND] and dysuria, [OR] prostatic hypertrophy, [OR] prostatitis, [AND] and other lower urinary tract obstructive disorders.
History of Stevens-Johnson Syndrome [AND] and Erythema multiforme.
Diabetes Type I [AND] and II
Fructose intolerance, [OR] glucose-galactose malabsorption [OR] or sucrose-isomaltase insufficiency.
Hepatic Impairment
Uncontrolled narrow-angle glaucoma
Severe renal impairment
History of seizure disorder
Anatomically narrow ocular angles.
Osteoporosis [OR] or major risk for bone fractures.
