Inclusion Criteria:
Patients 18 years of age [OR] or older [AND] with >3 unformed stools/ [OR] 24 hours [AND] with positive stool test for C. difficile.
Patients receiving = 1 high [OR] or medium risk antibiotic for treatment of an infection other than CDI, [OR] for an anticipated duration of = 5 days from the time of enrollment.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with severe-complicated disease [AND] that would compromise oral therapy (hypotenstion [OR] or shock, [OR] ileus [OR] or bowel obstruction, [OR] megacolon).
Patients [AND] with an allergy to oral vancomycin [OR] or fidaxomicin.
Patients anticipated to receive metronidazole after enrollment.
Patients [AND] who already received oral vancomycin [OR] or metronidazole (either oral [OR] or intravenous) for > 24 hours within the preceding 72 hours at the time of enrollment.
Patients anticipated to receive adjunctive C. difficile therapy (rifaxamin, [OR] nitazoxanide, [OR] tigecycline) after enrollment.
