Inclusion Criteria:
Written informed consent
Age =18 years
Has undergone first time isolated CABG due to an episode of acute coronary syndrome (STEMI, [OR] NSTEMI, [OR] unstable angina) within 6 weeks before surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
Previously enrolled in this study (i.e. patient now at repeat encounter)
Concomitant surgical procedure other than CABG
Anticoagulant treatment after the operation (e.g. warfarin, [OR] direct thrombin inhibitors (dabigatran), [OR] FXa inhibitors (rivaroxaban, [OR] apixaban, [OR] heparin, [OR] low-molecular weight heparin, [OR] fondaparinux)
Discharge from the operating hospital to an ICU at another hospital
Pregnancy [OR] or lactation
Known intolerance [OR] or contraindication to ticagrelor [OR] or ASA
Any disorder [AND] that may interfere [AND] with drug absorption
Any condition other than coronary artery disease [AND] with a life expectancy <12 months
Known chronic liver disease, [OR] renal disease requiring dialysis [OR] or bleeding disorder
Atrioventricular block II [AND] and III in patients [NOT] without pacemaker
Any other indication for dual antiplatelet therapy, [OR] i.e. recent stent implantation
Debilitating stroke within 90 days before inclusion
Previous intracranial bleeding
Treatment [AND] with immunosuppressants (e.g. cyclosporine [AND] and tacrolimus)
Treatment [AND] with strong CYP3A4-inhibitors (e.g. ketoconazole, [OR] clarithromycin, [OR] nefazodone, [OR] ritonavir [OR] or atazanavir)
Any condition [AND] that in the opinion of the investigator may interfere [AND] with adherence to trial protocol
