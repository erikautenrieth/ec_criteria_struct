1. Individuals scheduled for undergoing colonoscopy at the Endoscopy Center of Wuxi people's Hospital in China 
2. Greater than the age of 18 
3. Individuals living [AND] with other family members 
4. Outpatients 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. History of colorectal surgery 
2. Suspected [OR] or known digestive tract obstruction, [OR] stricture, [OR] or perforation 
3. Serious status of illness, [OR] such as severe renal failure whose creatinine clearance<30 ml/ [OR] min, [OR] New York Heart Association grade III [OR] or grade IV congestive heart failure, [OR] or hemodynamic instability, [OR] etc.
