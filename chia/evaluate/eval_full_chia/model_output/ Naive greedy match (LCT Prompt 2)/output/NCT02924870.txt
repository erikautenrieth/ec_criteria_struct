Inclusion Criteria:
subjects older than 35 years
diagnosis of moderate to very severe COPD (FEV1 <80% predicted), [OR] according to the GesEPOC criteria, [OR] established at least 3 months
current [OR] or former smoker [AND] with an accumulated consumption >10 packs x year
hospital admission for COPD exacerbation

Exclusion Criteria:
osteoarticular, [OR] neuromuscular [OR] or cognitive limitation [AND] that prevents ambulation
previous diagnosis of active neoplastic disease
institutionalized patients; alcohol consumption >60 g/ [OR] day
patient belonging to another health sector in the Community of Madrid [OR] or other community
participation in another study within 6 months prior.
