Inclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with symptomatic persistent atrial fibrillation of less than 1-year duration.
Patients must be over 65 years old.
Patients give informed consent prior to participating in this study.

Exclusion Criteria:
Paroxysmal atrial fibrillation.
Long-standing persistent [OR] or permanent atrial fibrillation.
Previous pacemaker implantation.
Previous atrial ablation.
Patient is [NOT] unable to take warfarin [OR] or other oral anti-coagulant medication.
Patient is suffering [AND] with unstable angina in last one week.
Patient has had a myocardial infarction within last two months.
Patient is expecting [OR] or has had major cardiac surgery within last two months.
Patient is participating in a conflicting study.
Patient is [NOT] unable to perform exercise testing.
Patient is mentally incapacitated [AND] and cannot consent [OR] or comply [AND] with follow-up.
Patient has New York Heart Association (NYHA) class III/ [OR] IV heart failure.
Patient has left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) less than 35% [NOT] not secondary to tachycardia.
Pregnancy.
Patient suffers [AND] with other cardiac rhythm disorders.
Recent coronary artery intervention [OR] or other factors suggesting clinical instability (ECG, [OR] clinical [OR] or laboratory findings).
