Inclusion Criteria:
Women aged 25-75 years old.
Women [AND] with recently diagnosed breast cancer [AND] and [AND] who will receive NAC to reduce tumor burden before surgery. (including locally advanced breast cancer (LABC) according to clinical assessment; [OR] or tumor size > 2cm, [AND] that is, [OR] at least T2 in TNM staging).

Exclusion Criteria:
Estimated GFR (eGFR) < 60 mL/ [OR] min/ [OR] 1.73 m2 [AND] and blood glucose > 135 mg/ [OR] dl; Past [OR] or present history of acute renal failure, [OR] renal dialysis, [OR] diabetes mellitus.
Women [AND] who received metallic fixation, [OR] coronary artery stent in recent 3 months; [OR] or women [AND] who received mechanical valve replacement [AND] that is [NOT] not compatible [AND] with MR magnet; [OR] or women [AND] with aneurysmal clips, [OR] pacemakers.
Past history of claustrophobia.
Women [AND] who are pregnant [OR] or [AND] who are planning to be pregnant, [OR] or [AND] who are lactating (though the possibility in our target population should be very low)
Past history of breast cancer within recent 5 years before the currently diagnosed breast cancer.
Women [AND] who received chemotherapy for other disease entity in recent 1 year.
Women [AND] who cannot cooperate [AND] with the examinations.
