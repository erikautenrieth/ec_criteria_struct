Inclusion Criteria:
Age: 18 to75 years old;
Pathologically diagnosed [AND] with advanced gastric cancer (including adenocarcinoma of the gastroesophageal junction) [AND] with measurable metastases outside the stomach (measuring = 10mm on spiral CT scan, [OR] satisfying the criteria in RECIST 1.1);
Failure of prior therapy (during [OR] or after treatment) in patients [AND] who have received at least two prior chemotherapy regimens;
ECOG PS of 0-2;
HB = 90g / [OR] L
ANC = 1.5 × 109 / [OR] L
PLT = 80 × 109 / [OR] L
Bilirubin <1.25 times the upper limit of normal (ULN)
ALT [AND] and AST <2.5 × ULN; liver metastases, [OR] if any, [OR] the ALT [AND] and AST<5 × ULN
Serum Cr = 1 × ULN endogenous creatinine clearance>50ml/ [OR] min (Cockcroft-Gault formula)
An expected survival of = 3 months;
Patient received apatinib treatment regimen at investigators' discretion;
Patient has to voluntarily join the study [AND] and sign the Informed Consent Form for the study;
Pregnancy test (serum [OR] or urine) has to be performed for woman of childbearing age within 7 days before enrolment [AND] and the test result must be negative. They shall take appropriate methods for contraception during the study until the 8th week post the last administration of study drug. For men, [OR] (previous surgical sterilization accepted), [OR] shall agree to take appropriate methods of contraception during the study until the 8th week post the last administration of study drug.

Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects [AND] with poor-controlled arterial hypertension (systolic blood pressure> 140 mmHg [AND] and diastolic blood pressure > 90 mm Hg) [AND] despite standard medical management; Coronary heart disease greater than ClassII; II-level arrhythmia (including QT interval prolongation, [OR] for man = 450 ms, [OR] for woman = 470 ms) together [AND] with Class II cardiac dysfunction;
Factors [AND] that could have an effect on oral medication (such as inability to swallow, [OR] chronic diarrhea [AND] and intestinal obstruction);
Subjects [AND] with high gastrointestinal bleeding risk, [OR] including the following conditions: local active ulcer lesions [AND] with positive fecal occult blood test (++); history of black stool, [OR] or vomiting blood in the past 3 months;unresected primary lesion in stomach [AND] with positive fecal occult blood test (+), [OR] ulcerated gastric carcinoma [AND] with massive alimentary tract bleeding risk judged by PIs based on gastric endoscopy result;
Abnormal Coagulation (INR>1.5<U+3001>APTT>1.5 UNL), [AND] with tendency of bleed;
Associated [AND] with CNS (central nervous system) metastases;
Pregnant [OR] or lactating women;
Other conditions regimented at investigators' discretion.
