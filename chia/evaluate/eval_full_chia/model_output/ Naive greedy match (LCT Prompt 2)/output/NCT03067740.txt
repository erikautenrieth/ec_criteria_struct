Inclusion Criteria:
Patients are of American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) physical status I [AND] and II, [OR] aged 8-14 years old, [OR] of both gender, [AND] with suspected acute appendicitis scheduled for laparoscopic appendicectomy.

Exclusion Criteria:
The diagnosis of developmental delay, [OR] attention deficit disorder, [OR] chronic pain, [OR] psychiatric illness, [OR] previous open abdominal surgery, [OR] the presence of a gastrostomy, [OR] ventricular-peritoneal shunt [OR] or other abdominal prosthesis, [OR] immunosuppression, [AND] and those allergic to any of the medications.
