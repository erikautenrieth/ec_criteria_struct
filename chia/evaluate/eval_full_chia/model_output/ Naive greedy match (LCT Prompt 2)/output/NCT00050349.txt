Inclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with biopsy-proven metastatic carcinoid tumors [OR] or other neuroendocrine tumors (Islet cell, [OR] Gastrinomas [AND] and VIPomas) [AND] with at least one measurable lesion (other than bone) [AND] that has either [NOT] not been previously irradiated [OR] or if previously irradiated has demonstrated progression since the radiation therapy 
The patient has [NOT] no major impairment of renal [OR] or hepatic function, [OR] as defined by the following laboratory parameters: total bilirubin <1.5 X ULN; AST, [OR] ALT<2.5X ULN (<5 X ULN if liver metastases are present) 
Patients on Sandostatin Lar (long acting somatostatin analogue) must be on a stable dose for 30 days prior to study entry [AND] and short acting somatostatin analogues must be judged to be on a clinically stable dose by the investigator prior to study entry 
Must have a life expectancy of greater than three (3) months 
Karnofsky Performance Status > 60 
Female patients must have a negative serum pregnancy test at screening. (Not applicable to patients [AND] with bilateral oophorectomy [AND] and/ [OR] or hysterectomy [OR] or to those patients [AND] who are postmenopausal.) 

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with symptomatic CNS metastases [OR] or leptomeningeal involvement 
Patients [AND] with known brain metastases, [OR] unless these metastases have been treated [AND] and/ [OR] or have been stable for at least six months prior to study start. Subjects [AND] with a history of brain metastases must have a head CT [AND] with contrast to document either response [OR] or progression. 
Patients [AND] with bone metastases as the only site(s) of measurable disease 
Patients [AND] with hepatic artery chemoembolization within the last 6 months (one month if there are other sites of measurable disease) 
Patients [AND] who have been previously treated [AND] with radioactive directed therapies 
Patients [AND] who have been previously treated [AND] with epothilone 
Patients [AND] with any peripheral neuropathy [OR] or unresolved diarrhea greater than Grade 1 
Patients [AND] with severe cardiac insufficiency patients taking Coumadin [OR] or other warfarin-containing agents [AND] with the exception of low dose warfarin (1 mg [OR] or less) for the maintenance of in-dwelling lines [OR] or ports 
Patients taking any experimental therapies history of another malignancy within 5 years prior to study entry except curatively treated non-melanoma skin cancer, [OR] prostate cancer, [OR] or cervical cancer in situ 
Patients [AND] with active [OR] or suspected acute [OR] or chronic uncontrolled infection including abcesses [OR] or fistulae 
Patients [AND] with a medical [OR] or psychiatric illness [AND] that would preclude study [OR] or informed consent [AND] and/ [OR] or history of noncompliance to medical regimens [OR] or inability [OR] or unwillingness to return for all scheduled visits 
HIV+ patients 
Pregnant [OR] or lactating females.
