colorectal cancer above to 12 cm from the anal verge
unresectable synchronous metastases
[NOT] no contraindications for chemotherapy
[NOT] absence of peritoneal carcinomatosis, [OR] central nervous system o bone metastasis.
performance status ECOG = 2 (Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group)
uncontrolled concomitant medical conditions [AND] that may compromise to chemotherapy
significant symptomatic cardiac disease
[NOT] not pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding

Exclusion Criteria:

Other neoplastic disease in the 5 previous years, [OR] except squamous [OR] or basal cell skin carcinoma [OR] or cervical "in situ" carcinoma
Significant heart disease (chronic congestive heart failure, [OR] symptomatic coronary disease) [OR] or myocardial infarction in the previous 6 months
Peripheral neuropathy
Patients [AND] who do [NOT] not give informed consent
