Inclusion Criteria:
Adult (age 18 years [AND] and older)
Patients [AND] with end-stage renal disease(ESRD)/ [OR] chronic kidney disease(CKD)stage 5

Exclusion Criteria:
Diabetes Mellitus
Acute coronary syndrome in the past 6 months
Cardiac arrhythmias (2nd [AND] and 3rd degree heart block [OR] or premature ventricular complexes in Lown classes 4 [OR] or 5)
Symptoms suggestive of obstructive [OR] or central sleep apnea ([AND] with a score of > 10 on Epworth sleepiness scale)
Patients taking Clonidine
Body mass index (BMI) > 34
Patients [NOT] unable to give consent
Pregnant women
Patients [AND] with leg injury involving nerve damage
Patients taking anticoagulant medication
Patients [AND] with significant bleeding disorder [OR] or liver disorder
Hemoglobin <1.05 g/ [OR] dl at the time of initiation of therapy
patients [AND] with unilateral [OR] or bilateral nephrectomy
Planned kidney transplant in the next 4 months
Life expectancy under 6 months
Oliguria (urine output less than 400 ml per day)
