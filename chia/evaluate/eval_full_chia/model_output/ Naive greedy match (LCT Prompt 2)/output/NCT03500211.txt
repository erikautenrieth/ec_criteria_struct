Inclusion Criteria:
Pregnant patients [AND] who require a scheduled [OR] or non-urgent cesarean birth
Patient able to receive neuraxial analgesia
Patient able to give verbal [AND] and written consent for both cesarean birth [AND] and study

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients requiring emergent cesarean birth
Patients allergic to lidocaine [OR] or adhesive
Patients [AND] who have already received an epidural during this admission [OR] or requiring general anesthesia for cesarean birth
Patients using chronic oral neuromodulators
Patients [AND] with cardiac disease [OR] or using anti-arrhythmic agents
Patients [AND] with fibromyalgia [OR] or chronic pain syndromes such as rheumatoid arthritis, [OR] osteoarthritis, [OR] or lupus.
Daily narcotic [OR] or opiate use for greater than the 2 months prior to enrollment in the study.
