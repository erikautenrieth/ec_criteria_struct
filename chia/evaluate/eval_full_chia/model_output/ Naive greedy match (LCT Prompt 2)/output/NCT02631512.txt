Inclusion Criteria:
Type I [OR] or II diabetes mellitus.
Target ulcer area between 0.5 [AND] and 5 sqcm, [AND] and more than 4 weeks old.
Ankle-brachial pressure index above 0.7.

Exclusion Criteria:
Ulcers due to non-diabetic etiology.
Uncontrolled diabetes defined as HbA1c above 70 mmol/ [OR] mol [AND] and insufficient nutritional status.
Ulcers older than 1 year.
Any of gangrene, [OR] osteomyelitis, [OR] cellulitis, [OR] or Charcot osteoarthropathy.
