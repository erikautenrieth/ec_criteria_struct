Inclusion Criteria:
Treated [AND] with a stable dose of one of the following for at least 3 months prior to screening: * >=1000 mg/ [OR] day immediate-release metformin; [OR] or metformin >=1000 mg/ [OR] day [AND] and sulfonylurea; [OR] or sulfonylurea/ [OR] metformin combination therapy. 
HbA1c between 7.1% [AND] and 11.0%, [OR] inclusive. 
Body Mass Index (BMI) >21 kg/ [OR] m^2 [AND] and <35 kg/ [OR] m^2. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Have participated in this study previously, [OR] or any other study using exenatide [OR] or GLP-1 analogs. 
Have participated in an interventional, [OR] medical, [OR] surgical, [OR] or pharmaceutical study within 30 days of screening. 
Have characteristics contraindicating metformin [OR] or sulfonylurea use. 
Have been treated [AND] with exogenous insulin for more than 1 week within the 3 months prior to screening. 
Have used drugs for weight loss within 1 month of screening.
