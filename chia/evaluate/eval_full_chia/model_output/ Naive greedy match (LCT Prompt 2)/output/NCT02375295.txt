Inclusion Criteria:
Male [OR] or Female.
No age restriction.
Diagnosed [AND] with an infection related stone.
Medically fit for definitive surgical management of stone.
Life expectancy greater than one year.
Stone [NOT] free after definitive surgical therapy defined as fragments less than 3mm.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with medical comorbidities preventing them from definitive surgical therapy.
Patients [AND] with persistent stone burden following definitive surgical therapy.
