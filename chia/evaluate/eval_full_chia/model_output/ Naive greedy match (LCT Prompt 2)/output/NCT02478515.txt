Signed informed consent form
Macula edema secondary to BRVO
BCVA of 77 to 20 letters assessed [AND] with the use of ETDRS charts
CRT <U+2267>250µm

Exclusion Criteria:
Previous treatment [AND] with anti-VEGF drugs [OR] or corticosteroid [OR] or grid laser photocoagulation (study eye)
History of vitrectomy surgery, [OR] submacular surgery, [OR] or other surgical intervention for RVO
Ocular disorders in the study eye [AND] that may confound interpretation of study results
BCVA over 77 letters between screening [AND] and Day 0
The pregnant [OR] or lactating woman
