Inclusion Criteria:
Male [OR] or female =18 years of age at Visit 1
Documentation of a CF diagnosis
Enrolled in the Cystic Fibrosis Foundation National Patient Registry (CFFNPR) prior to Visit 1 (US sites only)
At the time of Visit 1, [OR] there is a plan to initiate IV antibiotics for a pulmonary exacerbation
Performed spirometry at Visit 1 [AND] and Visit 2 [AND] and willing to perform spirometry at Visit 3
Completed the CRISS questionnaire at Visit 1 [AND] and Visit 2 [AND] and willing to complete the Cystic Fibrosis Respiratory Symptoms Diary (CFRSD) questionnaire at Visit 3
Willing to adhere to a specific treatment duration determined by initial response to treatment [AND] and subsequent randomization
Willing to return for follow up Visit 3
Written informed consent obtained from the subject [OR] or subject's legal representative

Exclusion Criteria:
Previous randomization in this study
Treatment [AND] with IV antibiotics in the 6 weeks prior to Visit 1
Admission to the intensive care unit for current pulmonary exacerbation in the two weeks prior to Visit 2, [OR] unless admission was due to a desensitization protocol
Pneumothorax in the two weeks prior to Visit 2
Primary diagnosis for current hospitalization is unrelated to worsening lower respiratory symptoms (e.g., [OR] pulmonary clean out, [OR] distal intestinal obstruction syndrome (DIOS), [OR] sinusitis)
Massive hemoptysis defined as > 250 cc in a 24 hour period [OR] or 100 cc/ [OR] day over 4 consecutive days occurring in the two weeks prior to Visit 2
Current pulmonary exacerbation thought to be due to allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis (ABPA)
At Visit 1, [OR] receiving ongoing treatment [AND] with a duration of more than 2 weeks [AND] with prednisone equivalent to >10mg/ [OR] day
History of solid organ transplantation
Receiving antimicrobial therapy to treat non-tuberculous mycobacterium (e.g., [OR] M. abscessus, [OR] M. avium complex) in the two weeks prior to Visit 2
