Inclusion Criteria:
Veteran receiving care within the Veterans Health Administration healthcare system
Age 18 years
Physician diagnosis of chronic heart failure, [OR] American Heart Association Stage C-D
LVEF <45%
No change in active cardiac medications for 4 weeks prior to randomization
Ability to provide informed consent
Moderate to severe central [OR] or mixed central [AND] and obstructive sleep apnea, [OR] defined as an apnea-hypopnea index (AHI) 15 events per hour, [AND] with a central AHI >5 events/ [OR] hour

Exclusion Criteria:
Hospitalization for acute decompensated HF within previous 30 days
Hospitalization for myocardial infarction [OR] or cardiac surgery within previous 90 days
Presence of a left ventricular assist device
History of heart transplantation
Poorly controlled hypertension (>170/ [OR] >110)
Poorly controlled diabetes (HbA1c > 9.0)
Severe renal failure [AND] with estimated glomerular filtration rate <30 ml/ [OR] min
Prior stroke [AND] with functional impairment [OR] or other severe, [OR] uncontrolled medical problems [AND] that may impair ability to participate in the study exams, [OR] based on medical history [AND] and review of medical records
Severe chronic insomnia, [AND] with reported usual sleep duration <4 hours
Severe daytime sleepiness, [OR] defined as Epworth Sleepiness Scale score 18 [OR] or higher [OR] or a report of falling asleep driving during the previous year, [AND] and deemed a safety risk by study physician
Awake resting oxyhemoglobin saturation <89%
Pregnancy
Smoking by subject [OR] or other person in the subject's bedroom, [OR] or other open flame in bedroom
Current use of a positive airway pressure device (including continuous [OR] or bi-level positive airway pressure [OR] or adaptive servo-ventilation) [OR] or supplemental oxygen therapy
