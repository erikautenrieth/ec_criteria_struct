Inclusion Criteria:
Adult (=18 years)
Presence of intracranial aneurysm ([AND] with [OR] or [NOT] without rupture)
Treating surgeon has recommended surgical repair of the aneurysm

Exclusion Criteria:
History of seizures within last 10 years
History of epilepsy
History of prior stroke
Currently prescribed medication [AND] with anti-epileptic activity (keppra, [OR] dilantin, [OR] tegretol, [OR] lamictal, [OR] topamax, [OR] etc.)
Brain tumor
Pregnant [OR] or nursing woman
Known levetiracetam allergy
