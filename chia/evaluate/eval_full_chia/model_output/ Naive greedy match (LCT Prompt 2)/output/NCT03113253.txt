Inclusion Criteria:
Subjects undergoing burn excision surgery for standard of care purposes
Male [OR] or female >= 18 years of age
Subject [OR] or subject's medical decision maker agrees to participate in this study [AND] and provides informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects [AND] with a history of hypercoagulopathy, [OR] deep vein thrombosis (DVT), [OR] pulmonary embolism
Renal impairment
Subjects [AND] with known hypersensitivity to tranexamic acid
Consecutive fibrinolytic states to coagulopathy
History of convulsions
