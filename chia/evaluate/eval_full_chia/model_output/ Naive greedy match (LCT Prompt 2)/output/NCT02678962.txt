Inclusion Criteria:
Age from 40 to 80 years old, [OR] either gender;
Patients [AND] with bilateral age related cataracts, [OR] require bilateral cataract phacoemulsification combined Intraocular Lens implantation;
Willing to undergo second eye surgery within 7 days after first eye surgery;
The potential postoperative visual acuity of 20/ [OR] 40 [OR] or better in both eyes;
Preoperative measurement of corneal astigmatism indicate the subjects are suitable for multifocal intraocular lenses implantation;
Capability to understand the informed consent [AND] and willing [AND] and able to attend study

Exclusion Criteria:
Preexisting ocular diseases [OR] or conditions other than age related cataracts, [OR] have contraindications for cataract surgery;
Preexisting systemic diseases [OR] or conditions [AND] that may confound the results of the study;
Previous ocular surgery history [OR] or ocular trauma [AND] that may confound the results of the study;
Require combined surgery [AND] that may confound the results of the study;
Previous participation in other clinical trial within 30 days of this study start;
Systemic [OR] or ocular medications [AND] that may confound the outcome of the intervention
Pregnant, [OR] lactating, [OR] or planning to become pregnant during the course of the trial;
