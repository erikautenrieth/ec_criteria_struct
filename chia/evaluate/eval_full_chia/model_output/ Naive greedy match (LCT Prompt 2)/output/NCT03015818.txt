Inclusion Criteria:
age > 18
written informed consent
SVD defined on echocardiography by an alteration of bioprosthesis leaflets function [AND] with a mean transvalvular gradient > 20 mmHg [AND] and maximal velocity = 3 m/ [OR] s [AND] and effective orifice area =1.2 cm², [AND] and/ [OR] or an aortic regurgitation more [OR] or equal to grade 2 on 4.

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Inability to give informed consent
Pregnancy
Concurrent antibiotherapy
Certain infectious endocarditis
Concurrent anti-inflammatory therapy, [OR] including corticosteroid therapy
