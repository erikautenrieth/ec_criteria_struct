Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis of schizophrenia [OR] or schizoaffective disorder 
If entering the study as an inpatient, [OR] hospitalization was recent 
Currently receiving treatment [AND] with an atypical antipsychotic [AND] and continuation on the medication has been recommended 
Assumes primary responsibility for taking medication 
Currently living in a stable environment 

Exclusion Criteria:
History of significant head trauma, [OR] seizure disorder, [OR] or mental retardation 
History of alcohol [OR] or drug abuse [OR] or dependence within 1 month prior to study entry 
History of violence within 6 months prior to study entry
