Inclusion Criteria:
History of three [OR] or more consecutively failed In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) cycles after embryo transfer.
Normal uterine cavity (as assessed by hysteroscopy [OR] or HSG).
Normal hormonal investigation: TSH, [OR] PRL, [OR] FBS.
Normal acquired/ [OR] inherited thrombophilia profile: LAC, [OR] ACA IgG/ [OR] IgM, [OR] Prot S, [OR] Antithrombin III, [OR] beta-2 glycoprotein, [OR] Factors V, [OR] II, [OR] MTHFR.
Normal semen analysis [AND] and mild/ [OR] moderate male factor (Total motile sperm count > 5 million/ [OR] ml [AND] and/ [OR] or normal WHO morphology >20%.
Patient provides written informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
Evidence of low ovarian reserve by at least one of the following: AMH = 1,5 ng/ [OR] mL [AND] and/ [OR] or basal CD 3 FSH = 10 mIU/ [OR] mL [AND] and/ [OR] or basal CD 3 Estradiol = 60 ng/ [OR] mL [AND] and/ [OR] or previous egg collection yield = 3 oocytes.

Severe male factor infertility (Total motile sperm count < 5 million/ [OR] ml [AND] and/ [OR] or normal WHO morphology <20%).
Hypersensitivity to Heparin [OR] or its derivatives.
Acquired thrombophilia.
Active hemorrhage [OR] or increased risk of bleeding due to impairment of homeostasis.
Severe impairment of liver [OR] or pancreatic function.
Severe renal insufficiency (Creatinine Clearance < 30 ml/ [OR] min).
Injuries to [OR] or operations on the central nervous system, [OR] eyes [AND] and ears within the last 2 months.
Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (DIC) attributable to heparin-induced thrombocytopenia.
Acute bacterial endocarditis [AND] and endocarditis lenta.
Any organic lesion [AND] with high risk of bleeding (e.g.: active peptic ulcer, [OR] hemorrhagic stroke, [OR] cerebral aneurysm [OR] or cerebral neoplasms).
