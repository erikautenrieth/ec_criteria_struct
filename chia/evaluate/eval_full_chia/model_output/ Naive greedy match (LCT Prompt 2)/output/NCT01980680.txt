Inclusion Criteria:
Age between 20 [AND] and 40
Normal menstrual cycles: 25-34 days
Oligomenorrhea/ [OR] amenorrhea [OR] or polycystic syndrome (defined according to the Rotterdam criteria 2004)
BMI >18 [AND] and <35 kg/ [OR] m2

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with >14 follicles on day of trigger
Previous hyperresponse [AND] with OHSS development
Previous low response (less than 3 oocytes on a high dose of FSH stimulation)
Endocrine disorders
