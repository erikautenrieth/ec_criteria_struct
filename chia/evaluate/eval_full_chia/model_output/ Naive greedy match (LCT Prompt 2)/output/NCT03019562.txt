Inclusion Criteria:
19-65 years of age
ASA physical status classification I [OR] or II
Scheduled for total hip replacement surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
Allergic to study drugs
Patient [AND] with asthma [OR] or COPD, [OR] patient [AND] who is severely respiratory depressed
Renal of hepatic insufficiency
Epileptic status
Intracranial lesion associated [AND] with increased intracranial pressure
Acute abdomen, [OR] patient [AND] who has diagnosed paralytic ileus [OR] or suspicious ileus
Pregnant [OR] or lactating women
