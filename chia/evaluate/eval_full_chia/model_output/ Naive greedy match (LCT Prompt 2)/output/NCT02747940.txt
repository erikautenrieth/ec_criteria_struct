Inclusion Criteria:
Control: devoid of any systemic [OR] or neurological diseases
Chronic migraine: by ICHD-III (International Classification of Headache Disorder) criteria
Fibromyalgia: by ACR (American College of Rheumatology) 2010 criteria

Exclusion Criteria:
history of major systemic illness, [OR] including uncontrolled hypertension, [OR] diabetes, [OR] chronic renal insufficiency, [OR] autoimmune diseases [OR] or malignancies
history of neurological disorders which might affect sensation such as previous stroke [OR] or peripheral neuropathy
history of substance abuse (except painkillers)
heavy smokers ([AND] with a daily consumption >20 cigarettes)
pregnancy [OR] or lactation
any contraindication for magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)
[AND] and any obvious infection [OR] or inflammation over a period of at least 1 month before the study.
