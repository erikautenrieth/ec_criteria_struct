Inclusion Criteria:
Between the ages of 21-60
Right-handed
Capable of giving informed consent [AND] and complying [AND] with study procedures
Reports drinking a minimum of 5 standard drinks for men [OR] or 4 standard drinks for women on at least 4 days per week on average over the past 28 days
Meets DSM-V criteria for current Alcohol Use Disorder
Seeking treatment for Alcohol Use Disorder
Agree to [NOT] not seek additional treatment, [OR] apart from Alcoholics Anonymous
Willing to attempt to abstain from alcohol completely for the duration of the study
Willing to be hospitalized on a research unit for 24 hours, [OR] longer if detoxification is needed.

Exclusion Criteria:
Risk of severe alcohol withdrawal (e.g. history of seizures [OR] or delirium tremens)
Current Moderate [OR] or Severe Substance Use Disorder, [OR] other than Alcohol, [OR] Nicotine [OR] or Caffeine Use Disorders
Lifetime history of Bipolar Disorder, [OR] Schizophrenia [OR] or Schizoaffective Disorder
Any current psychiatric disorder, [OR] other than Alcohol Use Disorder, [AND] that, [OR] in the judgment of the investigator, [OR] will require treatment [AND] that will interfere [AND] with study participation.
Current severe depression (HAM-D >24) [OR] or anxiety (HAM-A >24)
Significant suicide [OR] or violence risk
Currently taking any psychotropic medications
Legally mandated to participate in treatment
History of prior treatment [AND] with disulfiram
Sufficiently socially unstable as to preclude participation (e.g. homeless)
Contraindications to disulfiram treatment (liver disease, [OR] kidney disease, [OR] cardiac disease, [OR] seizure disorder, [OR] hypothyroidism, [OR] diabetes mellitus, [OR] pregnancy [OR] or lactation, [OR] allergy to disulfiram [OR] or thiuran derivatives)
Neurological [OR] or medical conditions [AND] that would interfere [AND] with MRI scanning (e.g. history of stroke, [OR] seizure, [OR] brain tumor, [OR] brain infection, [OR] traumatic brain injury, [OR] multiple sclerosis, [OR] dementia, [OR] metal device in body, [OR] pregnancy, [OR] claustrophobia, [OR] color blindness, [OR] severe hearing impairment, [OR] weight>300 lbs., [OR] wheelchair-bound)
Currently taking medications containing alcohol, [OR] metronidazole, [OR] isoniazid, [OR] paraldehyde, [OR] phenytoin, [OR] warfarin, [OR] or theophylline.
