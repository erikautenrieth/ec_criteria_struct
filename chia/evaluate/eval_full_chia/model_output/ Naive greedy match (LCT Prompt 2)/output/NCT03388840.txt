Inclusion Criteria:
male patients [AND] with androgenetic alopecia between 18 years [AND] and 60 years

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with Non-androgenetic causes of hair loss.
Female patients [AND] with androgenetic alopecia.
Patients [AND] who received anti-hair loss treatment within the past six months.
Patients [AND] with history of bleeding disorders [OR] or on anticoagulant therapy.
Patients [AND] with history of chronic liver disease, [OR] cancer [OR] or connective tissue disorders.
Patients [AND] with current scalp infection.
