Inclusion Criteria:
Consecutive 30 female patients presenting to our clinic for brow lifting [AND] with botulinum toxin will be randomized to receive one of the two injection techniques

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with previous periorbital/ [OR] forehead surgery
Patients [AND] who plucked the upper eyebrow margin
Patients [AND] with eyebrow tatoos
Patients [AND] with upper face botulinum toxin injection in the past 12 months
Patients [AND] with resorbable upper face fillers injection in the past 12 months
Patients [AND] with previous permanent upper face fillers injection
Pregnant patients
Lactating patients
Patients [AND] with preexisting neuromuscular conditions (myasthenia gravis, [OR] Eaton Lambert syndrome)
Patients using medication [AND] that could potentiate the effect of botulinum (ex: aminoglycoside antibiotics)
Patients [AND] with sensitivity to botulinum toxin [OR] or human albumin
