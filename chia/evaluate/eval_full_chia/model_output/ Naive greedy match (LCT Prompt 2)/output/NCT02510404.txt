Inclusion Criteria:
1. Diagnosis of primary immunodeficiency [AND] with established plan to undergo myeloablative [OR] or non-myeloablative allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplant for treatment thereof [OR] or diagnosis of a form of primary immunodeficiency for which hematopoietic stem cell transplantation is [NOT] not indicated. 
2. Active infection [AND] with EBV, [OR] CMV, [AND] and/ [OR] or Adenovirus, [NOT] unable to be successfully controlled [AND] with standard therapy. 
3. Steroids less than 0.5 mg/ [OR] kg/ [OR] day prednisone 
4. Karnofsky/ [OR] Lansky score of ≥ 50 
5. ANC greater than 500/ [OR] µL. 
6. Bilirubin <2x, [OR] AST <3x, [OR] Serum creatinine <2x upper limit of normal, [OR] Hgb >8.0 
7. Pulse oximetry of > 90% on room air 
8. Negative pregnancy test (if female of childbearing potential) 
9. Patient [OR] or parent/ [OR] guardian capable of providing informed consent. 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Patients [AND] with other uncontrolled infections (see 2.3.2 for definitions) 
2. Patients [AND] who received ATG, [OR] Campath, [OR] or other T cell immunosuppressive monoclonal antibodies in the last 28 days 
3. Received donor lymphocyte infusion in last 28 days 
4. Diagnosis of Omenn's syndrome [OR] or MHC class I deficiency 
5. Active [AND] and uncontrolled malignancy 
6. Pregnant [OR] or lactating 
7. Unable to wean steroids to ≤0.5 mg/ [OR] kg/ [OR] day prednisone. 
8. Patients [AND] with Grade 3 hyperbilirubinemia
