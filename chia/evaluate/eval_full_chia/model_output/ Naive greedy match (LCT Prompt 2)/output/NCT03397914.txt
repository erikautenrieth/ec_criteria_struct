Inclusion Criteria:
Age between one year [AND] and 18 years
Sepsis due to MDR [OR] or minimally susceptible gram-negative bacteria
History of MDR gram-negative infection [OR] or sepsis due to organisms sensitive to colistin.
Culture result consistent [AND] with MDR gram negative for this febrile neutropenic episode.

Exclusion Criteria:
Age less than one year [OR] or over 18 years
Patients [AND] with renal impairment
Colistin use less than 72 hours
