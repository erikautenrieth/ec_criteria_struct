Inclusion Criteria:
18 years of age [AND] and older, [OR] diagnosis of stage II adhesive capsulitis as determined by clinical examination of the treating physician, [AND] and
[NOT] absence of abnormal findings on X-ray.

Exclusion Criteria:
allergy to Doxycycline [OR] or Methylprednisolone, [OR] pregnancy, [OR] diagnosis, [OR] Inflammatory arthritis [OR] or diabetes, [OR] secondary adhesive capsulitis (history of significant trauma, [OR] rotator cuff tear injury, [OR] stroke)
evidence of arthritis on x-ray, [OR] current infectious disease, [AND] and
any previous treatment for the for adhesive capsulitis of the affected shoulder.
