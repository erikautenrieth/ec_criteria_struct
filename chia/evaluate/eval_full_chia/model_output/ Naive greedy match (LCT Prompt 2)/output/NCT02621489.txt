Inclusion Criteria:
Patients eligible for PCI [AND] with application of DES, [OR] due to ACS.
Patients [AND] with known [OR] or newly diagnosed T2D (type 2 diabetes is diagnosed according to current WHO criteria [OR] or by the use of anti-diabetic drugs)
Male [AND] and female subjects 18-80 years.
HbA1c (accordingly to IFCC) 47 mmol/ [OR] mol - 110 mmol/ [OR] mol.
Signed informed consent form.

Exclusion Criteria:
Type 1 diabetes (autoantibody positive).
Any history of receiving GLP-1 analogues [OR] or dipeptidyl peptidase inhibitors within 6 months
Known severe heart failure, [OR] classified as NYHA 4.
Active myocarditis; malfunctioning artificial heart valve.
History of ventricular tachycardia within 3 months before study entry; second- [OR] or third-degree atrioventricular block.
Supine systolic blood pressure <85 mm Hg [OR] or >200 mm Hg at screening.
Primary renal impairment, [OR] creatinine clearance < 45 ml/ [OR] min if treated [AND] with metformin.
Uncorrected hypokalemia [OR] or hyperkalemia (potassium <3.5 mmol/ [OR] l [OR] or >5.5 mmol/ [OR] l).
Significant anemia (Hb < 90 g/ [OR] l)
Severe gastrointestinal disease, [OR] including gastroparesis. As judged by the Investigator.
Body mass index (BMI) > 45 kg/ [OR] m2.
Malignant neoplasm requiring chemotherapy, [OR] surgery, [OR] radiation [OR] or palliative therapy in the previous 5 years. Patients [AND] with intraepithelial squamous cell carcinoma of the skin treated [AND] with topical 5FU [AND] and subjects [AND] with basal cell skin cancer are allowed to enter the trial.
Females of child bearing potential [AND] who are pregnant, [OR] breast-feeding [OR] or intend to become pregnant.
Current drug [AND] and alcohol abuse.
History of acute [OR] or chronic pancreatitis
Subjects considered by the Investigator to be unsuitable for the study.
