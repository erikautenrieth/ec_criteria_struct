Inclusion Criteria:
Adult Patients [AND] with Overt Hepatic Encephalopathy.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with active GIT bleeding.
Patients [AND] with history of bowel obstruction, [OR] perforation.
Patients [AND] with history of allergy to PEG.
Treatment [AND] with rifaximin [OR] or neomycin in the previous 7 days.
Patients [AND] with major psychiatric illness.
Patients receiving benzodiazepines [AND] and narcotics.
Patients [AND] with compromised renal.
Patients receiving medications highly bound to plasma proteins eg. Warfarin.
Pregnant [OR] or lactating women.
Fulminant hepatic failure.
