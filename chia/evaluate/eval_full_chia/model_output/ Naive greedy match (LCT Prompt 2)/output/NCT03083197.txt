Inclusion Criteria:
Age = 15 years old
Hospitalization [AND] with acute undifferentiated fever (temperature > 37.5 C, [OR] tympanic) =14 days [OR] or patients admitted to hospital [AND] with a history of fever = 14 days [AND] who subsequently develop fever within 24 hours of admission
Clinically suspected scrub typhus: defined as acute undifferentiated fever [AND] with [NOT] no clear focus of infection [AND] and negative malaria blood smear [AND] and/ [OR] or negative malaria RDT. Patients may have one, [NOT] none, [OR] or a combination of other clinical findings such as eschar, [OR] rash, [OR] lymphadenopathy, [OR] headache, [OR] myalgia, [OR] cough, [OR] nausea [AND] and abdominal discomfort.
A positive scrub typhus RDT (Scrub Typhus IgM RDT, [OR] InBios International, [OR] Seattle, [OR] WA, [OR] USA) [AND] and/ [OR] or positive PCR-based detection of O. tsutsugamushi DNA from the admission blood sample
Written informed consent [AND] and/ [OR] or, [OR] written informed assent as required
Able to take oral medication

Exclusion Criteria:
Known hypersensitivity to tetracycline, [OR] doxycycline [OR] or azithromycin
Administration of doxycycline, [OR] azithromycin, [OR] chloramphenicol, [OR] rifampicin, [OR] or tetracycline during the preceding 7 days
Pregnancy [OR] or breast-feeding
Patients [AND] with myasthenia gravis [OR] or systemic lupus erythematosus
Patients [AND] with an established infection (diagnostic test required) e.g. acute malaria, [OR] dengue, [OR] leptospirosis, [OR] typhoid, [OR] Japanese encephalitis etc.
Current TB [OR] or TB treatment in = 6 months (contain active antibiotics against Orientia spp.)
Current HAART use for HIV, [OR] long term use of immunosuppressants (e.g. steroids, [OR] chemotherapy, [OR] TNF-inhibitors [AND] and related agents)
Patients [AND] with severe disease whom the clinical team feel their condition necessitates the need for additional scrub typhus treatment beyond the allocated antibiotic treatment assigned at randomization (e.g. IV chloramphenicol [AND] and/ [OR] or PO/ [OR] NG rifampicin)
