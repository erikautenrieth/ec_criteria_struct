Methadone-maintained cocaine-dependent patients use between 1g to 2g a day; 1 to 3 times a week 

Exclusion Criteria:
use more than 2g a day; 5 times a week to everyday 
Subjects [AND] who are diagnosed as suffering from psychotic illness according to DSM-IV (Axis 1)22, [OR] or [AND] with a history of CNS disease, [OR] a history of infection [AND] that might affect CNS (HIV, [OR] syphilis, [OR] cytomegalovirus, [OR] herpes), [OR] or a history of head injury [AND] with loss of consciousness,pregnant women.
