Inclusion Criteria:
Previous diagnoses of COPD [AND] and HF under optimized clinical treatment as judged by the accompanying physician
Reduced left ventricular ejection fraction (<50%)
Non-reversible airway obstruction (post-bronchodilator FEV1/ [OR] FVC < 0.7 [AND] and FEV1 < 80 %)
Respiratory muscle weakness (Pi,max < 70cmH2O)
Persistent dyspnea on daily life (Baseline Dyspnea Index focal score < [OR] or= 8).

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Inability to perform exercise tests
Diagnosed psychiatric [OR] or cognitive disorders
Progressive neurological [OR] or neuromuscular disorders [AND] having a major impact on exercise capacity
