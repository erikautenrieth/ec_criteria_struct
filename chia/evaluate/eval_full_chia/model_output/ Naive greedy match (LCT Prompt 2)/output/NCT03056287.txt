Inclusion Criteria:
1) age 50-70 
2) stroke within the past 6 to 60 months, [OR] 3) major depressive disorder (PHQ-9 > 10) [AND] and diagnosed using the Structured Clinical Interview for Depression (SCID) according to the Diagnostic [AND] and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, [OR] Fourth Edition (DSM-IV), [OR] 4) residual paresis in the lower extremity (Fugl-Meyer LE motor score <34), [OR] 5) ability to walk [NOT] without assistance [AND] and [NOT] without an AFO on the treadmill ≥ 30 seconds at speeds ranging from 0.2-0.8 m/ [OR] s, [OR] 6) [NOT] no antidepressant medications [OR] or clinically able to discontinue medications, [OR] 7) HRSD question #9 regarding suicide <2, [OR] 8) provision of informed consent. In addition, [OR] all subjects [AND] who meet criteria for the training portion must complete an exercise tolerance test [AND] and be cleared for participation by the study cardiologist. 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Unable to ambulate at least 150 feet prior to stroke, [OR] or experienced intermittent claudication while walking; 
2. history of congestive heart failure, [OR] unstable cardiac arrhythmias, [OR] hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, [OR] severe aortic stenosis, [OR] angina [OR] or dyspnea at rest [OR] or during ADL's; 
3. History of oxygen dependence; 
4. Preexisting neurological disorders, [OR] dementia [OR] or previous stroke; 
5. History of major head trauma; 
6. Legal blindness [OR] or severe visual impairment; 
7. history of psychosis [OR] or other Axis I disorder [AND] that is primary; 
8. Life expectancy <1 yr.; 
9. Severe arthritis [OR] or other problems [AND] that limit passive range of motion; 
10. History of DVT [OR] or pulmonary embolism within 6 months; 
11. Uncontrolled diabetes [AND] with recent weight loss, [OR] diabetic coma, [OR] or frequent insulin reactions; 
12. Severe hypertension [AND] with systolic >200 mmHg [AND] and diastolic >110 mmHg at rest; 
13. attempt of suicide in the last 2 years [OR] or at suicidal risk assessed by SCID interview; 
14. Previous [OR] or current enrollment in a clinical trial to enhance motor recovery; 15) currently exercising ≥ 2 times per week (≥20 minutes); 
16) Presence of non-MR compatible implants, [OR] pregnancy [OR] or severe claustrophobia.
