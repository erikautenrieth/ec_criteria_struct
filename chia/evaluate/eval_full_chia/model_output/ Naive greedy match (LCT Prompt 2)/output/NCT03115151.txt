Inclusion Criteria:
Adult subjects aged 18 years [OR] or older
Scheduled for elective posterior lumbar spinal fusion surgery between 1 [AND] and 3 levels

Exclusion Criteria:
Baseline cognitive deficits sufficient to make objective pain self-assessments unreliable in the estimation of the Study Investigators.
Immunocompromised subject
Coagulopathy
Severe liver [AND] and renal dysfunction
Preoperative neurological deficits
The dura damage during surgery
[NOT] Inability to follow directions [OR] or comprehend the English language.
Females [AND] who are pregnant as determined by positive pregnancy test on [OR] or before the day of surgery.
Prisoners.
Patient refusal to provide informed consent.
Allergy to amide local anesthetics (lidocaine, [OR] bupivacaine, [OR] ropivacaine) [OR] or opioid (fentanyl).
