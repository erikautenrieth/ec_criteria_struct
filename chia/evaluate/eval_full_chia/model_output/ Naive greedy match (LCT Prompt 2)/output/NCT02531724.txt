Inclusion Criteria:
Patients in the cardiothoracic intensive care after cardiac surgery [AND] with cardiopulmonary bypass
Acute kidney injury, [OR] defined as increase in S-creatinine 50% [OR] or 27 mol/ [OR] L
Normal S-creatinine before surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
Ongoing treatment [AND] with inotropic drugs ([NOT] not norepinephrine)
Central venous oxygen saturation (ScvO2) < 60% [AND] despite optimization of hematocrit [AND] and volume status
Need of renal replacement therapy
Ongoing bleeding
Patient [OR] or next of kin does [NOT] not consent [AND] with study participation
