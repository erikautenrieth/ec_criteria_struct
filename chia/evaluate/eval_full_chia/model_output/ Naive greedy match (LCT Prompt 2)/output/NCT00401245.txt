Inclusion Criteria:
Generally healthy, [OR] postmenopausal woman [AND] who seeks treatment for hot flushes. 
Meets 1 of the following: At least 12 months of spontaneous amenorrhea; At least 6 months of spontaneous amenorrhea [AND] with serum follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) levels > 40 mIU/ [OR] mL; At least 6 weeks postsurgical bilateral oophorectomy ([AND] with [OR] or [NOT] without hysterectomy). Hysterectomized [NOT] without bilateral oophorectomy [AND] and [AND] with serum FSH levels >40 mIU/ [OR] mL. 

Exclusion Criteria:
History of a seizure disorder other than a single childhood febrile seizure. 
History [OR] or presence of clinically important hepatic [OR] or renal disease [OR] or other medical disease. 
Presence [OR] or recent history of major depressive disorder, [OR] bipolar disorder, [OR] psychotic disorder, [OR] or generalized anxiety disorder requiring therapy.
