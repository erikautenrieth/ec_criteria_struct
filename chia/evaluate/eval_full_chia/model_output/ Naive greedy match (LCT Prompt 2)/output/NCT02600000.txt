Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis of Heart Failure;
Lower left ventricular ejection fraction 45% (LVEF <45%) assessed by simple [AND] and recent echocardiogram;
Functional Class II [AND] and III by the New York Heart Association (NYHA)
Clinically stable;
Ex-smokers over five years;
Maximal inspiratory pressure (MIP) <70% of predicted;
Forced expiratory volume/ [OR] Forced vital capacity (FEV1 / [OR] FVC) > 70% of predicted;

Exclusion Criteria:
Unstable angina;
Myocardial infarction [AND] and heart surgery up to three months before the survey;
Chronic respiratory diseases;
Hemodynamic instability;
Trauma recent face, [OR] nausea [AND] and vomiting.
Orthopedic [AND] and neurological diseases [AND] that may preclude the achievement of the cardiopulmonary test [AND] and Cardiac Rehabilitation exercises;
Psychological [AND] and / [OR] or cognitive impairments [AND] that restrict them to respond to questionnaires;
