Inclusion Criteria:
18 years [OR] or older
Type 1 [OR] or 2 diabetes
PDR patients requiring surgical intervention for complications of vitreous hemorrhage [OR] or traction retinal detachment [AND] and pre-operative IVC treatment.
women postmenopausal for 12 months before the study, [OR] surgically sterile, [OR] or [NOT] not pregnant [AND] and on effective contraception.

Exclusion Criteria:
previous retinal vein occlusion.
any intraocular surgery within the previous 12 months.
myopia of > [OR] or = to 8 diopters.
active ocular [OR] or periocular infection
treatment [AND] with an investigational agent for any condition 60 days prior to enrollment.
evidence of severe cardiac disease.
clinically significant peripheral vascular disease (previous surgery, [OR] amputation, [OR] or symptoms of claudication)
uncontrolled hypertension (treated systolic blood pressure > 155 mmHg [OR] or diastolic blood pressure > 95 mmHg)
stroke within the preceding 12 months.
