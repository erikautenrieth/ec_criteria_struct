Inclusion Criteria:
1. Women 18 to 40 years of age inclusive [AND] who can give written informed consent 
2. Available for all visits [AND] and consent to follow all procedures scheduled for the study 
3. Agree to daily application of gel [AND] and monitoring as per Daily Monitored Adherence (DMA) method 
4. Healthy [AND] and self-reported sexually active 
5. HIV-negative as determined by a HIV rapid test at time of enrollment 
6. On a stable form of contraception [AND] and willing to continue on this stable method of contraception, [OR] OR, [OR] Have undergone surgical sterilisation at least 3 months prior to enrollment 
7. In the [NOT] absence of the use of exogenous hormone(s), [OR] have a self-reported regular menstrual cycle defined as [AND] having a minimum of 21 days [AND] and a maximum of 36 days between menses 
8. Upon pelvic/ [OR] speculum examination [AND] and colposcopy at the time of enrollment, [OR] the cervix [AND] and vagina appear normal as determined by the investigator 
9. Asymptomatic for genital infections at the time of enrollment 
10. Willing to refrain from use of vaginal products [OR] or objects within 14 days prior to enrollment [AND] and for the duration of the study 
11. Willing to answer acceptability [AND] and adherence questionnaires throughout the study 
12. Willing to refrain from participation in any other research study for the duration of this study 
13. Willing to provide adequate locator information for study retention purposes [AND] and be reachable per local standard procedures 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Currently pregnant [OR] or last pregnancy outcome within 3 months prior to enrolment 
2. Currently breast-feeding 
3. Participated in any other research study within 60 days prior to screening 
4. Previously participated in any HIV vaccine study 
5. Untreated urogenital infections (either symptomatic [OR] or asymptomatic) within 2 weeks prior to enrollment 
6. Presence of abnormal physical finding on the vulva, [OR] vaginal walls [OR] or cervix during pelvic/ [OR] speculum examination [AND] and/ [OR] or colposcopy 
7. History of significant urogenital [OR] or uterine prolapse, [OR] undiagnosed vaginal bleeding, [OR] urethral obstruction 
8. Pap smear result at screening [AND] that requires cryotherapy, [OR] biopsy, [OR] treatment (other than for infection), [OR] or further evaluation 
9. Any Grade 2, [OR] 3 [OR] or 4 baseline haematology, [OR] chemistry [OR] or urinalysis laboratory abnormality according to the DAIDS Table for Grading Adverse Experiences 
10. Unexplained, [OR] undiagnosed abnormal bleeding per vagina, [OR] bleeding per vagina during [OR] or following vaginal intercourse, [OR] or gynaecologic surgery within 90 days prior to enrollment 
11. Any history of anaphylaxis [OR] or severe allergy resulting in angioedema; [OR] or a history of sensitivity/ [OR] allergy to latex 
12. Any serious acute, [OR] chronic [OR] or progressive disease 
13. Any condition(s) [AND] that, [OR] in the opinion of the investigator, [OR] might interfere [AND] with adherence to study requirements [OR] or evaluation of the study objectives
