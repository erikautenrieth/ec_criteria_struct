Inclusion Criteria:
Adult patients
Kidney transplant recipients
Patients treated by a calcineurin inhibitor [AND] and mycophenolic acid
Viremia >= 3 log UI/ [OR] ml
Patients [AND] who have given written informed consent
Negative pregnancy test (blood ß-HCG dosage)

Exclusion Criteria:
Known proved BKV nephropathy
Hypersensitivity to everolimus, [OR] sirolimus [OR] or excipient
Concomitant treatment by leflunomide, [OR] cidofovir, [OR] sirolimus, [OR] Millepertuis (Hypericum Perforatum)
Pregnant [OR] or lactating women
Women of child bearing potential unless they are using a birth control method
