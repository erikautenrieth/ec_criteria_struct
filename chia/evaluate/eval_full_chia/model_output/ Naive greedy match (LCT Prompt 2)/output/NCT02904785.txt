Inclusion Criteria:
Clinical [AND] and radiologic diagnosis of primary knee osteoarthritis (Kellgren & Lawrence I, [OR] II [OR] or III); 
Capability to understand the Informed Consent Form; 
Chronic pain for at least 3 months prior to inclusion, [OR] measured by VAS. (VAS 4 [OR] or above); 
Absence of skin injures, [OR] infections [OR] or tumor in the target knee; 
Availability to comply [AND] with the visits. 

Exclusion Criteria:
History of spinal cord stenosis [OR] or clinical symptoms of lumbar radiculopathy; 
History [OR] or onset neurological diseases; 
Generalized pain [OR] or fibromyalgia; 
[NOT] Inability to walk; 
History of knee surgery in the target knee; 
Secondary causes of osteoarthritis; 
Use of statins [AND] and quinolones in the previous year; 
Uncontrolled [AND] and ongoing psychiatric diseases; 
Invasive knee treatments [AND] with hyaluronic acid infusion, [OR] corticosteroids [AND] and anaesthetics, [OR] in the target knee, [OR] up to 6 months previous to study inclusion.
