Inclusion Criteria:
Advanced solid tumor malignancy (during expansion at the maximum tolerated dose, [OR] entry will be limited to patients wtih adenocarcinoma of the colon [OR] or rectum) 
Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) 0 [OR] or 1 

Exclusion Criteria:
Prior treatment [AND] with more than 6 cycles of traditional alkylating agent-based chemotherapy regimens 
Prior treatment [AND] with more than 2 cycles of carboplating-based chemotherapy regimens 
For colorectal cancer patients in the expanded cohorts, [OR] prior treatment [AND] with more than 2 systemic chemotherapy regimens in the metastatic setting
