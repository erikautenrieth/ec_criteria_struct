Inclusion Criteria:
1. Speak, [OR] read, [AND] and understand English [OR] or Spanish [AND] and is willing [AND] and able to provide written informed consent on an IRB-approved form prior to the initiation of any study procedures; 
2. Healthy, [OR] premenopausal female age 18-47; 
3. History of menstrual events [AND] that occur in regular cycles 
4. Agreement [NOT] not to attempt to become pregnant 
5. Agrees to use double-barrier contraception during the study [AND] and for 30 days after discontinuation of study medication. Acceptable double-barrier methods are: male condom [AND] with spermicide; male condom [AND] with diaphragm; diaphragm containing spermicide [AND] plus additional intra-vaginal spermicide; 
6. Has a negative pregnancy test at the Screening visit. An exception for the pregnancy test requirement will be granted for subjects reporting surgical sterilization in medical history 
7. Normal laboratory values [OR] or clinically insignificant findings at screening as determined by the Investigator; 
8. Subject is willing to remain in the clinic overnight for PK assessment on Days 0 [AND] and 8 
9. Ability to complete the study procedures in compliance [AND] with the protocol. 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Subject is a post-menopausal woman, [OR] defined as either; six (6) months [OR] or more (immediately prior to screening visit) [NOT] without a menstrual period, [OR] or prior hysterectomy [AND] and/ [OR] or oophorectomy 
2. Subject is pregnant [OR] or lactating [OR] or is attempting [OR] or expecting to become pregnant during the study 
3. Women [AND] with abnormally high liver enzymes [OR] or liver disease. (ALT [OR] or AST exceeding 2.0 x ULN AND total bilirubin exceeding 1.5 x ULN at screening [AND] and confirmed on repeat). 
4. Received an investigational drug in the 30 days prior to the screening for this study 
5. Women [AND] with a history of PCOS 
6. Concurrent use of any testosterone, [OR] progestin, [OR] androgen, [OR] estrogen, [OR] anabolic steroids, [OR] DHEA [OR] or hormonal products for at least 2 weeks prior to screening [AND] and during the study. 
7. Use of oral contraceptives in the preceding 2 weeks. Use of Depo-Provera® in the preceding 10 months. 
8. Has an IUD in place 
9. Women currently using narcotics 
10. Women currently taking spironolactone 
11. Infectious disease screen is positive for HIV [OR] or Hepatitis A, [OR] B [OR] or C. 
12. Clinically significant abnormal findings on screening examination [OR] or any condition which in the opinion of the investigator would interfere [AND] with the participant's ability to comply [AND] with the study instructions [OR] or endanger the participant if she took part in the study
