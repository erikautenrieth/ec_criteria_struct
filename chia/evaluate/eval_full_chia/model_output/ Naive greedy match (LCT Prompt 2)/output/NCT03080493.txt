Inclusion Criteria:
15 weeks 0 days gestational age - 23 weeks 5 days gestational age at time of dilator insertion
Able to read [AND] and write in English
Active cell phone [AND] with text messaging capability
Ride home from dilator insertion clinic appointment

Exclusion Criteria:
Current use of gabapentin [OR] or pregabalin
Allergy to gabapentin, [OR] acetaminophen, [OR] codeine, [OR] or ibuprofen
Self reported renal disease (severe impaired renal function)
Self reported current [OR] or chronic narcotic use (typical daily use)
Women [AND] with any issue [AND] that, [OR] in the opinion of the investigator, [OR] would interfere [AND] with study participation [OR] or generating accurate study data
