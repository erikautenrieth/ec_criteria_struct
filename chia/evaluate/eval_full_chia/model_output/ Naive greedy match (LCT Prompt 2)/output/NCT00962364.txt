Inclusion Criteria:
acute myocardial infarction [OR] or 
ischemic cardiomyopathy [AND] with [OR] or [NOT] without previous myocardial infarction [OR] or 
dilated cardiomyopathy due to valvular heart disease, [OR] hypertensive heart disease, [OR] history of myocarditis ([NOT] no active myocardial infection present) 

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] none, [OR] all patients meeting the inclusion criteria will be eligible.
