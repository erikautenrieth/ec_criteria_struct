Inclusion Criteria:
Adult acute myeloid leukemia 
Age: ≥18 [AND] and ≤ 60 
Clinical condition of the patient allows to carry out induction therapy: ECOG performance status: ≤ 2 [AND] and the Hematopoietic Cell Transplant-Co-morbidity Index (HCT-I): ≤3 
Informed consent to participate in the study (ICF signed) 
The second early induction start criteria is [AND] in addition to the listed above, [OR] the percentage of the blasts on the level >10% on 7th day. 

Exclusion Criteria:
No informed consent for participation in the study, [OR] mental illness, [OR] which [NOT] don't allow to obtain informed consent [AND] and conduct the treatment according to the protocol 
Pregnancy 
HIV infection 
Active cancer 
Active hepatitis virus infection
