Inclusion Criteria:
age > 18 y.o.
American Society of Anesthesiologists Physical Status Classification (ASA) 1-2
signed informed consent form after reading the information about the study [AND] and talking [AND] with one of the investigators

Exclusion Criteria:
pregnancy
known allergies for tranexamic acid [OR] or any other substance in Exacyl
deep vein thrombosis
Hormone Replacement Therapy [OR] or oral contraceptive usage
anticoagulants usage
obesity - BMI (body mass index) >30 kg/ [OR] m2
renal disease, [OR] as glomerular filtration rate (GFR) <60 ml/ [OR] min/ [OR] 1,73 m*m
seizures [OR] or epilepsy in the past
