Inclusion Criteria:
>= 18 years old the day of inclusion
Pregnancy
Positive HBs Ag
Informed consent obtained [AND] with information sheet given [AND] and explained [AND] and the consent form signed by the participant of the project investigator at the latest the day of the inclusion

Exclusion Criteria:
Women refusing HBs Ag test
HIV co-infection
HCV co-infection
HBV treatment ongoing at the day of inclusion
Creatinine clearance < 30 mL/ [OR] min
Severe gravidic disease present at inclusion involving life threatening to the mother [AND] and/ [OR] or the child
Evidence of pre-existing fetal anomalies incompatible [AND] with the child's life
Imminent child's birth defined as cervix dilatation up to 7 centimeters
Intention to deliver in a maternity [NOT] not linked to the study
Any concomitant medical condition [AND] that, [OR] according to the clinical site investigator would contraindicate participation in the study.
Concurrent participation in any other clinical trial [NOT] without written agreement of the two study teams
