Inclusion Criteria:
Physically healthy adults age 18-55 [AND] who meet DSM-5 criteria for insomnia [AND] and Criterion A (exposure to a traumatic event) for PTSD. The index trauma must have occurred within the past 5 years [AND] and at least 3 months before enrolling, [AND] and insomnia symptoms must have started [OR] or worsened after the exposure to the index trauma

Exclusion Criteria:
Psychiatric disorders other than insomnia, [OR] PTSD [AND] and specific phobias; including bipolar [AND] and psychotic disorders [AND] and meeting criteria for DSM-5 moderate alcohol [OR] or drug use disorders within the past year.
Diagnosis of a sleep disorder other than insomnia including PSG findings of apnea/ [OR] hypopnea [OR] or periodic limb movement indices > 10/ [OR] hour;
Medical conditions [AND] that require consistent use of medication [OR] or compromise sleep;
History of moderate to severe traumatic brain injury [OR] or mild traumatic brain injury [AND] with ongoing post-concussive symptoms;
Suicidal ideation [AND] with intent to act [OR] or [AND] with specific plan [AND] and intent in the past 6 months (Type 4 - 5 ideation on the Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale) [OR] or a concerning history of prior suicidal behavior.
Caffeine use exceeding 5 cups of coffee per day [OR] or its equivalent;
Habitual bedtimes after 3 AM, [OR] habitual rise times after 10 AM, [OR] or habitual napping > 1hour/ [OR] day;
Pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding, [OR] or expecting to conceive while in study;
Positive urine toxicology.
