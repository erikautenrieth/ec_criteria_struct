Inclusion Criteria:
Adult participants [AND] with low [OR] or intermediate-1 risk MDS
No previous treatment [AND] with hematopoietic growth factors within 3 months prior to screening
Symptomatic anemia (hemoglobin <10 g/ [OR] dL) as determined by investigator
Serum erythropoietin <500 milliunits/ [OR] milliliter (mU/ [OR] mL) within 14 days prior to the first dose of study treatment
Require [NOT] no red blood cell transfusion [OR] or dependent on <4 units within 8 weeks prior to screening
Clinically stable for at least 1 month prior to entry into the study
For female participants of childbearing potential [AND] and male participants [AND] with partners of childbearing potential, [OR] agreement (by participants [AND] and/ [OR] or partner) to use highly effective form(s) of contraception

Exclusion Criteria:
Contraindications [AND] and/ [OR] or known hypersensitivity to the active substance [AND] and/ [OR] or any of the excipients of epoetin beta treatment
Poorly controlled hypertension as assessed by the investigator
History of Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML) [OR] or high risk for AML
Administration of another investigational drug within 1 month before screening [OR] or planned during the study period
Previously documented evidence of Pure Red Cell Aplasia (PRCA)
