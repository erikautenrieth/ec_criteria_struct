Inclusion Criteria:
Adult men [AND] and women (=18 years).
Receptors of a first kidney transplant from an incompatible HLA living donor (at least 1 mismatch HLA at any antigenic level).
AB0 compatible transplant.
Patients [AND] with a calculated PRA of 0% by solid phase technique [AND] and [NOT] absence of anti-HLA class I [AND] and class II antibodies by single antigen test (Luminex®).
Patients [AND] who agree to participate in the Trial by signing the Specific Informed Consent of this study.
Potentially fertile women should use high reliability contraceptive methods (Pearl-Index <1) in order to avoid pregnancy during the entire duration of the study [AND] and up to 6 weeks after the end of their treatment [AND] with Mycophenolate Mofetil (MMF). Potentially Fertile Women include any woman [AND] who has experienced menarche [AND] and [AND] who has [NOT] not undergone successful surgical sterilization (hysterectomy, [OR] bilateral tubal ligation [OR] or bilateral oophorectomy) [OR] or [AND] who is [NOT] not post-menopausal (defined as amenorrhea = 12 consecutive months, [OR] or women [AND] who are receiving hormone replacement therapy [AND] with a documented level of follicle stimulating hormone (FSH)> 35 mlU / [OR] ml). Potentially fertile women must have a pregnancy test [AND] with a negative result in the 72 hours prior to the start of the trial.
Sexually active males (including vasectomized males) [AND] who are being treated [AND] with MMF must accept the use of barrier contraceptive methods during MMF treatment [AND] and for 90 days thereafter. Potentially fertile partners of these patients should use a reliable contraceptive method during the same period, [OR] in order to minimize the risk of pregnancy.
Patients must agree [NOT] not to donate blood during treatment [AND] with MMF [AND] and during the 6 subsequent weeks. Males should [NOT] not make a sperm donation during MMF treatment [AND] and up to 90 days after completion.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with a calculated PRA higher than 0% per solid phase [AND] and / [OR] or anti-HLA class I [AND] and / [OR] or class II antibodies detectable by single antigen test (Luminex®).
Positive result of Cross Match.
Patients [AND] who receive a graft from a cadaver donor.
Identical HLA patients
Patients [AND] who have undergone a previous solid organ transplant (including kidney transplant) [OR] or [AND] who are going to receive another solid organ transplant concomitantly.
Glomerular primary focal [AND] and segmental sclerosis
Atypical hemolytic uremic syndrome (aHUS) / [OR] thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura syndrome.
Patients [AND] with chronic infection [AND] with Hepatitis B virus (HBV) [AND] and / [OR] or active infection [AND] with Hepatitis C virus (positive PCR result) at the time of transplant.
Patients [AND] with infection [AND] with the known Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV).
Patients [AND] with active systemic infection [AND] that requires the continued administration of antibiotics.
Patients [AND] with any neoplasm except localized skin cancer [AND] and [AND] who is receiving adequate treatment.
Patients [AND] with severe anemia (hemoglobin <6g / [OR] dl), [OR] leukopenia (WBC <2500 / [OR] mm3) [AND] and / [OR] or thrombocytopenia (platelets <80,000 / [OR] mm3).
Patients [AND] who are hemodynamically unstable even if they have hemoglobin levels> 6g / [OR] dL.
Patients [AND] with intestinal pathology [OR] or severe diarrhea [AND] that may decrease absorption according to medical criteria.
Patients [AND] with known hypersensitivity to any of the drugs used in this study.
Patients [AND] who have received any investigational drug in the 30 days prior to their inclusion in this study.
Potentially fertile women [AND] who do [NOT] not agree to use reliable contraceptive measures during the trial, [AND] who are pregnant, [OR] breastfeeding [OR] or [AND] who present a positive pregnancy test at the time of their inclusion in the study.
Patients [AND] who are legally detained in an official institution.
