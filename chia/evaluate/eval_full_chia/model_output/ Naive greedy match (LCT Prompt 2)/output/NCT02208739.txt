Inclusion Criteria:
Patients should have at least 12 teeth present 
Patients [AND] with Moderate to Advanced Chronic periodontitis 
Patients [AND] with 2 [OR] or more interproximal sites ([NOT] not on same tooth) [AND] with probing pocket depths of 5mm [OR] or more [AND] and 2 [OR] or more interproximal sites ([NOT] not on same tooth)of probing attachment loss of 4mm [OR] or more which bled on probing. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] who had history of systemic antibiotic usage over the previous 4 months 
Patients [AND] who were pregnant 
Patients [AND] who had received non-surgical periodontal treatment within the past 6 months 
Patients [AND] who had received surgical periodontal treatment within the past 12 months 
Patients [AND] who were smokers 
Patients [AND] with a history of stroke [OR] or an acute cardiovascular event over the previous 12 months.
