Inclusion Criteria:
Histologically confirmed PD-L1 status defined NSCLC. Biopsy must be within 70 days of first treatment [AND] with pembrolizumab. 
ECOG performance status 2. 
Life expectancy > 12 weeks. 
Uni-dimensionally measurable disease according to Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumours (RECIST) v1.1 
Computerised Tomography (CT) scan of chest [AND] and abdomen within 28 days of starting pembrolizumab. 
Adequate haematological function: 
Platelet count ≥100 x 109 / [OR] L. 
Neutrophils ≥1.5 x 109/ [OR] L. 
Haemoglobin ≥ 9g/ [OR] dL. 
Adequate hepatic function: 
Serum bilirubin ≤1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN). 
Serum transaminases ≤2.5 x ULN. 
Adequate renal function: Creatinine clearance <1.5 times ULN concurrent [AND] with creatinine clearance >50 ml/ [OR] min. 
Provision of signed [AND] and dated, [OR] written informed consent prior to any study specific procedures, [OR] sampling [AND] and analyses. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Untreated symptomatic brain [OR] or leptomeningeal metastatic disease. 
Medical [OR] or psychiatric conditions comprising informed consent. 
Any medical condition which in the opinion of the investigator would compromise the ability of the patient to participate in the trial [OR] or which would jeopardise compliance [AND] with the protocol. 
Radiotherapy within 4 weeks of trial entry. 
Active autoimmune disease [AND] that has required systemic treatment in past 2 years 
Chronic usage of steroids [OR] or other immunosuppressant medication. 
Previous history of pneumonitis. 
Any evidence of clinical autoimmunity.
