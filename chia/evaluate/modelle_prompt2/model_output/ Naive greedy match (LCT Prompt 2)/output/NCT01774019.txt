Inclusion Criteria:
Age 18 [OR] or older
Willing [AND] and able to comply [AND] with the study procedures [AND] and provide written informed consent to participate in the study
Diagnosis of probable pancreatic cancer, [OR] distal common bile duct (CBD) cholangiocarcinoma [AND] and other periampullary cancers (histology [NOT] not required)
Biliary obstructive symptoms [OR] or signs
Bilirubin level at/ [OR] above 100 umol per liter (5.8 mg/ [OR] dL)
Distal biliary obstruction consistent [AND] with pancreatic cancer, [OR] distal CBD cholangiocarcinoma [OR] or other periampullary malignancy
Location of distal biliary obstruction is such [AND] that it would allow the proximal end of a stent to be positioned at least 2cm from the hilum
Patients deemed as resectable by pancreatic protocol CT [OR] or MRI
Surgical candidate per pancreatobiliary surgeon after multi-disciplinary discussion
Surgery intent within 4 weeks
Endoscopic [AND] and surgical treatment to be provided by same team

Exclusion Criteria:
Biliary strictures caused by confirmed benign tumors
Biliary strictures caused by malignancies other than pancreatic cancer, [OR] distal CBD cholangiocarcinoma [AND] and other periampullary cancers
Surgically altered biliary tract anatomy, [NOT] not including prior cholecystectomy
Neoadjuvant chemotherapy for current malignancy
Palliative indication due to reasons other than surgical candidate status
Previous biliary drainage by ERCP/ [OR] PTC
Patients for whom endoscopic techniques are contraindicated
Participation in another investigational trial within 90 days
Pregnancy
