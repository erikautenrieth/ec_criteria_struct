Inclusion Criteria:
Adults patients aged 18 to 85 years
Diagnosed [AND] with Major Depressive Disorder, [OR] unipolar [OR] or bipolar depression
Undergoing ECT for treatment of their symptoms
Currently residing in Manitoba

Exclusion Criteria:
Relative contraindications to ECT therapy (recent MI [OR] or CVA, [OR] increased intracranial pressure, [OR] intracranial mass lesion, [OR] intracranial aneurysm, [OR] epilepsy, [OR] known cardiac arrhythmia, [OR] pheochromocytoma, [OR] pregnancy)
Contraindications to etomidate (sepsis, [OR] primary [OR] or secondary adrenal insufficiency, [OR] porphyria)
DSM-V diagnosis of a lifetime history of psychotic spectrum disorder
Drug [OR] or alcohol dependence, [OR] or abuse within the past 3 months, [OR] soy-bean oil allergy
