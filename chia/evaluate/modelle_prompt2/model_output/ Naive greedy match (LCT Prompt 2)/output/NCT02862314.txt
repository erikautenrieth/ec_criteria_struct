aged 18 [OR] or older, [OR] have undergone oro-tracheal intubation for a coma (Glasgow Coma Score below [OR] or equal to 8), [AND] with mechanical ventilation initiated in the first 48 hours following hospital admission

pregnancy, [OR] patients under legal custody, [OR] patients [NOT] without health insurance, [OR] patients included in another interventional clinical study involving infections [OR] or antibiotics [AND] and [AND] having the same primary parameter, [OR] moribund patients, [OR] situation in which the procalcitonin concentration could be increased [NOT] without correlation to an infectious process (poly-traumatised patients, [OR] surgical interventions within the last 4 days, [OR] cardiorespiratory arrest, [OR] administration of anti-thymocyte globulin, [OR] immunodepressed patients (bone marrow transplant patients, [OR] patients [AND] with severe neutropenia), [OR] patients [AND] with an absolute indication for administration of antibiotics at the moment of ICU admission (meningitis, [OR] pneumonia) [OR] or a chronic infection for which long-term antibiotic treatment is necessary (endocarditis, [OR] osteo-articular infections, [OR] mediastinitis, [OR] deep abscesses, [OR] pneumocystis infection, [OR] toxoplasmosis, [OR] tuberculosis)
