Inclusion Criteria:
Patients presenting [AND] with ST-elevation acute myocardial infarction (STEMI) within 12 hours of their symptom onset in whom TIMI-3 flow was established in infarct related artery (IRA) after balloon angioplasty [OR] or thrombectomy.

Exclusion Criteria:
Recanalized (TIMI I-III flow) IRA at coronary angiography.
Patients in whom TIMI-3 flow was [NOT] not able to be established after wire crossing, [OR] balloon angioplasty [OR] or thrombectomy.
STEMI due to bypass-graft occlusion
Severe heart failure [OR] or cardiogenic shock
