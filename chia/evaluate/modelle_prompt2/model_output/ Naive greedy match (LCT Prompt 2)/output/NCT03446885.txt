Inclusion Criteria:
diagnosis of ADHD
parental permission [AND] and/ [OR] or teen consent/ [OR] assent as appropriate
between 16-25 years of age
IQ greater than [OR] or equal to 70
permit [OR] or license to drive
ability to read [AND] and understand English

Exclusion Criteria:
any medical condition [AND] that would contraindicate use of stimulant medication
any prior adverse response to lisdexamfetamine dimesylate [OR] or other stimulant medication
use of concurrent,non-stimulant psychoactive medication
diagnosis of schizophrenia [OR] or presence of thought disorder symptoms
autism spectrum disorder
