Inclusion Criteria:
All renal (only) male [AND] and female recipients aged = 60, [OR] years undergoing kidney transplantation from a living [OR] or deceased donor, [OR] including Expanded Criteria Donors (ECD).
Panel Reactive Antibody (PRA) < 30%.
Patients [AND] who consented to participate in the study by signing the informed consent form before the transplant surgery to the 1st post-operative day).

Exclusion Criteria:
Allergy to any of proposed medications
Patients [AND] with any active infection including HBV, [OR] HCV [AND] and HIV.
