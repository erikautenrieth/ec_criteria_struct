Inclusion Criteria:
Patients scheduled for thyroidectomy [AND] with general anesthesia in the University of Chile Clinical Hospital

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients ASA III y IV
Chronic pain history
Drug [AND] and alcohol abuse
Chronic use of opioid [AND] and sedatives
Neuropsychiatric illness
NSAID [AND] and other analgesics used the 48 hours previous to the surgery
CMI > 30
