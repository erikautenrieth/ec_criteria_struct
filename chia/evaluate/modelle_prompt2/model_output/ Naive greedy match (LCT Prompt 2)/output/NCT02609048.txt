Positive Anti-Mitochondrial Antibodies (AMA) titers (>1/ [OR] 40 on immunofluorescence [OR] or M2 positive by enzyme linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA) [OR] or positive PBC-specific antinuclear antibodies
