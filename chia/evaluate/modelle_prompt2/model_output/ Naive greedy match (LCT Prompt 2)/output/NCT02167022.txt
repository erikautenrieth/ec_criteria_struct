Inclusion Criteria:
1. Age: 12 to 36 months of age (The diagnosis of CP is often uncertain under the age of 12 months. The cutoff at 36 months is to have a population of young children when the brain is most "plastic" [AND] and most susceptible to reorganization). 
2. Diagnosis: Diagnosis of spastic CP confirmed by a pediatric neurologist [OR] or pediatric rehabilitation specialist. 
3. Etiology: The insult to the central nervous system [AND] that caused the motor dysfunction must have occurred during gestation [OR] or within one year after birth independent of gestational age. 
4. Disease severity level: Gross Motor Function Classification System (GMFCS) levels I, [OR] II [AND] and III. 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Diagnosis: Diagnosis of CP secondary to neuronal migration. 
2. Co-morbidities: Medical conditions [AND] that may [NOT] prevent the administration of rehabilitation therapies at the intensity required by the study, [OR] or [AND] that may compromise the study ability to maintain blindness, [OR] or [AND] that have a co-morbidity [NOT] not typically associated [AND] with CP (i.e. cancer, [OR] cystic fibrosis). 
3. Co-interventions: Anticipated pharmacological intervention [OR] or procedure [OR] or participation in other studies [AND] that may interfere [AND] with this study.
