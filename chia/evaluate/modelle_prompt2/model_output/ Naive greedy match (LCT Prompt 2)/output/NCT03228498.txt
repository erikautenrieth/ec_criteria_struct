Inclusion Criteria:
1. Cognitive impairment from mild to moderate degree defined by a Clinical Deterioration Rating (CDR) score range between 0.5 [AND] and 2.0. 
2. Evidence on brain MRI of white matter hyperintensities (leukoaraiosis of moderate [OR] or severe degree according to the modified Fazekas visual scale [AND] and/ [OR] or presence of lacunar infarcts). 
3. Consent to participation in the study. 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Absence of objectionable cognitive impairment [OR] or presence of dementia of severe degree defined by CDR score > 2.0. 
2. Unavailability of brain MRI (in case of absolute contraindications, [OR] the use of cranial CT is allowed). 
3. Expected poor compliance [AND] with the study protocol. 
4. Past diagnosis of major depression, [OR] schizophrenia, [OR] major anxiety syndrome, [OR] or manic- depressive illness. 
5. Diagnosis of degenerative cognitive impairment based on clinical [AND] and/ [OR] or neuroradiological findings (i.e., [OR] patients [AND] with prevailing memory impairment, [OR] or [AND] with medial temporal atrophy on brain MRI in [NOT] absence of evident vascular abnormalities; i.e., [OR] Alzheimer disease as defined using the National Institute of Neurological [AND] and Communicative Disorders [AND] and Stroke/ [OR] Alzheimer's Disease [AND] and Related Disorders Association criteria, [OR] Parkinson disease, [OR] Huntington disease, [OR] frontotemporal dementia). 
6. Diagnosis of cognitive impairment from other causes (i.e., [OR] vitamine B12 [AND] and folic acid deficiency, [OR] thyroid disorders, [OR] metabolic diseases, [OR] head trauma, [OR] tumor [OR] or infections of the central nervous system, [OR] normal pressure hydrocephalus). 
7. Medical conditions expected to progress, [OR] recur, [OR] or change to such a degree to interfere [AND] with the assessment of the clinical [AND] and mental status. 
8. Clinically relevant cardiac [OR] or pulmonary insufficiency. 
9. Relevant electrocardiograph abnormalities; bradycardia (50 bpm) [OR] or tachycardia (120 bpm) under resting conditions. 
10. Myocardial infarction within the past 6 months. 
11. Stroke still requiring neurological rehabilitation. 
12. Severe/ [OR] untreated blood pressure (systolic 180 mm Hg, [OR] diastolic 95 mm Hg). 
13. Clinically relevant liver function impairment. 
14. Insulin-dependent diabetes mellitus. 
15. Idiopathic epilepsy [AND] and anti-epileptic treatment. 
16. Severe anemia (Hb <10 mg/ [OR] dL). 
17. Severe gastrointestinal disease. 
18. Cancer. 
19. Known intolerance to study drugs. 
20. Coexistent serious illnesses [AND] that would imply a drop-out before the end of the trial.
