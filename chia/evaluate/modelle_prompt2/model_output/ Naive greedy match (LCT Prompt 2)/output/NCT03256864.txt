Inclusion Criteria:
Liver Transplant Recipients have received liver transplantations for at least 6+1 months prior to enrollment
Liver Transplant Recipients have [NOT] no acute rejection episodes within 3 months prior to the enrollment [AND] and are clinically stable
Liver Transplant Recipients have been treated [AND] with twice-daily regimen of tacrolimus(TAC) [AND] plus everolimus(EVR) [AND] and TAC [AND] and EVR trough levels have stayed within targeted ranges for at least 6 weeks prior to enrollment
Provide written informed consent prior to inclusion.
Liver transplant recipients [AND] who are 18-65 years of age of a primary liver transplant
Allograft functioning at an acceptable level as defined by the AST, [OR] ALT, [OR] Total Bilirubin levels =3 times ULN prior to enrollment.
Abbreviated MDRD eGFR = 30 mL/ [OR] min/ [OR] 1.73m2. 
Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] who are recipients of multiple solid organ [OR] or islet cell tissue transplants, [OR] or have previously received an organ [OR] or tissue transplant. Patients [AND] who have a combined liver-kidney transplant.
History of malignancy of any organ system (other than localized basal cell carcinoma of the skin), [OR] treated [OR] or untreated, [OR] within the past 5 years, [OR] regardless of whether there is evidence of local recurrence [OR] or metastases.
Existence of any surgical, [OR] medical [OR] or mental conditions, [OR] other than the current transplantation, [OR] which, [OR] in the opinion of the investigator, [OR] might interfere [AND] with the objectives of the study.
Pregnant [OR] or nursing (lactating) women.
