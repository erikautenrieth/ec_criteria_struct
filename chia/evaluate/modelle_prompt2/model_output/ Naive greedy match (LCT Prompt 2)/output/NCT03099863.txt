Inclusion Criteria:
Adult women at least 18 years of age
Elective Female Pelvic Medicine [AND] and Reconstructive Surgery [OR] or Gynecologic Minimally Invasive surgeries including hysterectomy, [OR] suburethral sling, [AND] and pelvic organ prolapse repair [AND] that require cystoscopy.

Exclusion Criteria:
Surgeries [AND] that include: intradetrusor Botox, [OR] vaginal mesh excision, [AND] and fistula repair
Pregnancy
History of nephrolithiasis
Allergy to study medications
Congenital urogenital anomaly
Neurogenic bladder
