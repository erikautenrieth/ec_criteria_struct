Inclusion Criteria:
Signed informed consent form (ICF)
Age 18 to 55 years old (inclusive) as of the date the ICF is signed
Diagnosis of MS according to the McDonald criteria 2010 [AND] and cranial MRI scan demonstrating white matter lesions attributable to MS within 10 years before Screening
Onset of MS symptoms (as determined by a neurologist, [OR] either at present [OR] or retrospectively) within 10 years of the date the ICF is signed
EDSS score 0.0 to 5.0 (inclusive) at Screening
Patients [AND] with (highly) active RRMS disease course indicated to receive alemtuzumab according to the following conditions (at least 1 out of 3 conditions has to be fulfilled): 1. =2 MS relapses within 24 months, [OR] 2. clinical (=1 relapse) [OR] or MRI (new gadolinium enhancing lesions) disease activity under therapy [AND] with other diseasemodifying therapies, [OR] 3. severe relapse [AND] with high disease activity (=9 T2 hyperintense Lesions [AND] and =1 gadolinium enhancing lesion) on MRI.
Completion of all vaccinations required by the applicable immunization guidelines published by "ständige Impfkommission" (STIKO)
History of chickenpox [OR] or positive test for antibodies against varicella zoster virus (VZV)

Exclusion Criteria:
Participation in another clinical trial at present [OR] or within 4 weeks of study entry. There may be exceptions at the discretion of the Investigator.
Has any progressive form of MS
Hypersensitivity to the active substance, [OR] or to any of the excipients of Lemtrada®
Medical, [OR] psychiatric, [OR] cognitive, [OR] or other conditions [AND] that, [OR] in the Investigator's opinion, [OR] compromise the patient's ability to understand the patient information, [OR] to give informed consent, [OR] to comply [AND] with the trial protocol, [OR] or to complete the study
Any disability acquired from trauma [OR] or another illness [AND] that could interfere [AND] with evaluation of disability due to MS
Major systemic disease [OR] or other illness [AND] that would, [OR] in the opinion of the Investigator, [OR] compromise patient safety [OR] or interfere [AND] with the interpretation of study results, [OR] e.g., [OR] current peptic ulcer disease [OR] or other conditions [AND] that may predispose to hemorrhage
Known bleeding disorder (e.g,. dysfibrinogenemia, [OR] factor IX deficiency, [OR] hemophilia, [OR] Von Willebrand's disease, [OR] disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC), [OR] fibrinogen deficiency, [OR] or clotting factor deficiency)
Significant autoimmune disease including [AND] but [NOT] not limited to immune cytopenias, [OR] rheumatoid arthritis, [OR] systemic lupus erythematosus, [OR] other connective tissue disorders, [OR] vasculitis, [OR] inflammatory bowel disease, [OR] severe psoriasis
History of malignancy, [OR] except basal skin cell carcinoma
Major psychiatric disorder [AND] that is [NOT] not adequately controlled by treatment
Epileptic seizures [AND] that are [NOT] not adequately controlled by Treatment
Active infection, [OR] e.g., [OR] deep-tissue infection, [AND] that the Investigator considers sufficiently serious to preclude study participation
In the Investigator's opinion, [OR] is at high risk for infection (e.g., [OR] indwelling catheter, [OR] dysphagia [AND] with aspiration, [OR] decubitus ulcer, [OR] history of prior aspiration pneumonia [OR] or recurrent urinary tract infection)
Seropositivity for human immunodeficiency virus (HIV)
Infection [AND] with hepatitis C Virus
Past [OR] or present hepatitis B infection (positive hepatitis B serology)
Active infection [AND] with human cytomegaly virus (HCMV), [OR] Epstein-Barr virus (EBV), [OR] varicella-zoster virus (VZV)
Latent tuberculosis unless effective anti-tuberculosis therapy has been completed, [OR] or active tuberculosis.
Invasive fungal infections in history [AND] and at present
Cervical cytology other than PAP I [OR] or PAP II (Papanicolaou) [OR] or cervical high risk human papillomavirus (HPV) positivity
Any other illness [OR] or infection (latent [OR] or active) [AND] that, [OR] in the Investigator's opinion, [OR] could be exacerbated by study medication
Differential blood count < lower limit of normal (LLN) at Screening
Confirmed platelet count < the LLN of the evaluating laboratory at Screening [OR] or documented at <100,000/ [OR] µL within the past year on a sample [NOT] without platelet clumping
Presence (i.e., [OR] above the ULN) of anti-thyroid stimulating hormone receptor antibodies (anti-TSHR) [AND] and anti-thyroid peroxidase antibody (anti-TPO)
Vaccination less than 6 weeks prior to treatment [AND] with Lemtrada.
Treatment [AND] with antineoplastic [OR] or immunosuppressive drugs within 8 weeks prior to study inclusion
Intolerance of pulsed corticosteroids, [OR] especially a history of steroid psychosis
[NOT] Inability to undergo MRI [AND] with gadolinium administration
Of childbearing potential [AND] with a positive serum pregnancy test, [OR] pregnant [OR] or lactating
Female patients of childbearing potential: Unwilling to agree to use a reliable [AND] and acceptable contraceptive method (Pearl index <1) throughout the study period. These methods include: hormone releasing intrauterine device (IUD), [OR] hormonal-based contraception, [OR] surgical sterilization, [OR] abstinence, [OR] or double-barrier contraception (condom [AND] and occlusive cap [diaphragm [OR] or cervical cap combined [AND] with spermicide]).
