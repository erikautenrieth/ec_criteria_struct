Active chronic central serous chorioretinopathy (cCSC);

Foveal subretinal fluid (SRF), [OR] on optical coherence tomography (OCT), [OR] at Baseline Examination;

Continuous [AND] and/ [OR] or progressive visual loss > 18 months [OR] or serous detachment on OCT > 18 months;
No hyperfluorescence on ICGA;

(relative) Contraindications for FA [OR] or ICGA;
