Inclusion Criteria:
Habitual dietary sodium intake > 3400mg per day

Exclusion Criteria:
Abnormal resting ECG
Current abnormal blood panel (assessed by comprehensive metabolic panel, [OR] lipid panel [AND] and complete blood count).
Hypertension (currently taking anti-hypertensive medications [OR] or resting blood pressure >140/ [OR] 90 mmHg)
Medical history of cardiovascular disease, [OR] malignant cancer, [OR] diabetes [OR] or kidney disease
Obesity (Body Mass Index > 30)
Current pregnancy
Unable to provide consent
