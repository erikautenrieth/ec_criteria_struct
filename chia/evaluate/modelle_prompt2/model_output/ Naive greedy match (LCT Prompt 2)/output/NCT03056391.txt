1. Patient age ≥ 12 years

3. Temperature >38C on admission [OR] or fever during the preceding 48 hours 
4. Enrolled within 18 hours of commencing antimalarial treatment 
5. Written informed consent from patient [OR] or attending relative able to [AND] and willing to give informed consent. Consent form [AND] and information sheets will be translated into Malay [AND] and copies provided to the patient.

1. Patient [OR] or relatives [NOT] unable [OR] or unwilling to give informed consent 
2. Contraindication [OR] or allergy to paracetamol [OR] or artesunate therapy 
3. Known cirrhosis, [OR] or >6 standard alcoholic drinks/ [OR] day
