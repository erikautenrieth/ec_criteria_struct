Inclusion Criteria:
Age >/ [OR] =18 years at screening
Patients [AND] with relapsing forms of multiple sclerosis (RMS) [AND] with active disease defined by clinical [OR] or imaging features: (i) at least one clinical relapse over a 6-month period prior to screening; (ii) AND/ [OR] OR at least one T1 gadolinium-enhancing lesion [OR] or new [AND] and/ [OR] or enlarging T2 lesion as detected by brain Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) performed over a 3 months period prior to screening [AND] with [NOT] no change of Disease-Modifying Treatment(s) (DMT) compared to a previous MRI performed within 24 months before screening
For women of childbearing potential: agreement to use an acceptable birth control method during the treatment period [AND] and for at least 12 months after the last dose of ocrelizumab
Participants should be beneficiary of healthcare coverage under the social security system

Exclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis of primary progressive MS
[NOT] Inability to complete an MRI (contraindications for MRI include [AND] but are [NOT] not restricted to weight =140 kg, [OR] pacemaker, [OR] cochlear implants, [OR] presence of foreign substances in the eye, [OR] intracranial vascular clips, [OR] surgery within 6 weeks of entry into the study, [OR] coronary stent implanted within 8 weeks prior to the time of the intended MRI, [OR] etc…)
Gadolinium intolerance
History of ischemic cerebrovascular disorders (e.g., [OR] stroke, [OR] transient ischemic attack) [OR] or ischemia of the spinal cord
History [OR] or known presence of central nervous system (CNS) [OR] or spinal cord tumor (e.g., [OR] meningioma, [OR] glioma)
History [OR] or known presence of potential metabolic causes of myelopathy (e.g., [OR] untreated vitamin B12 deficiency)
History [OR] or known presence of infectious causes of myelopathy (e.g., [OR] syphilis, [OR] Lyme disease, [OR] human T-lymphotropic virus 1 (HTLV-1), [OR] herpes zoster myelopathy)
History of genetically inherited progressive CNS degenerative disorder (e.g., [OR] hereditary paraparesis; MELAS [mitochondrial myopathy, [OR] encephalopathy, [OR] lactic acidosis, [OR] stroke] syndrome)
Neuromyelitis optica
History [OR] or known presence of systemic autoimmune disorders potentially causing progressive neurologic disease (e.g., [OR] lupus, [OR] anti-phospholipid antibody syndrome, [OR] Sjogren's syndrome, [OR] Behçet's disease, [OR] sarcoidosis)
History of severe, [OR] clinically significant brain [OR] or spinal cord trauma (e.g., [OR] cerebral contusion, [OR] spinal cord compression)
Vulnerable patients (Patient referred to in Articles L. 1121-5 to L. 1121-8 [AND] and L. 1122-1-2 of the French Public Health Code)
