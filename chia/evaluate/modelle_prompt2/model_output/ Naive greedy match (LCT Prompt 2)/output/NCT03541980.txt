Exclusion Criteria:
Patient [AND] with fever (38C [OR] or 100.4F)
Patient less than age 4 years
Patient greater than age 16 years
Patient [AND] with hypersensitivity/ [OR] allergy to either morphine, [OR] NSAIDs, [OR] or acetaminophen
Patient received acetaminophen within the past 4 hours
Patient [AND] with known liver disease [OR] or renal disease
Patient [NOT] not requiring IV morphine (pain score 5/ [OR] 10 [OR] or less)
Patient enrolled in the study within the past 72 hours
