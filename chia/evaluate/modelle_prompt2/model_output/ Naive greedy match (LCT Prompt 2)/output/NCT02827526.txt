Inclusion Criteria:
Patients presenting for elective posterior spinal fusion surgery (lower thoracic, [OR] lumbar, [OR] sacral)
Ages 18-80

Exclusion Criteria:
Preoperative renal failure (defined as a serum creatinine > 2.0 mg/ [OR] dL.)
American Society of Anesthesiologists Physical Status IV [OR] or V
Pulmonary disease necessitating home oxygen therapy
Allergy to methadone, [OR] hydromorphone, [OR] or ketamine
Preoperative recent history of opioid [OR] or alcohol abuse
Significant liver disease
[NOT] Inability to use a PCA device [OR] or speak the English language
