Inclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with symptomatic FAI
Clinical [AND] and radiographic evidence of FAI
Patients able to provide consent to study participation
Completion of 6 weeks of physical therapy program

Exclusion Criteria:
Established Osteoarthritis (Kellgren-Lawrence > 3)
Minimum joint space > 2 mm as measured on AP radiograph
Hip dysplasia (center edge angle < 20° on AP radiograph)
Patients [AND] with clinically significant cardiovascular, [OR] renal, [OR] hepatic, [OR] endocrine disease, [OR] cancer [OR] or diabetes
Patients [AND] with ongoing infection including HIV [AND] and Hepatitis
Patient [AND] with history of osteomyelitis/ [OR] septic arthritis
Anticoagulation therapy
Patients [AND] who are pregnant [OR] or breast feeding
Patients [AND] with systemic, [OR] rheumatic [OR] or inflammatory disease of the knee [OR] or chondrocalcinosis, [OR] hemochromatosis, [OR] inflammatory arthritis, [OR] arthropathy of the knee associated [AND] with juxta-articular Paget's disease of the femur [OR] or tibia, [OR] hemophilic arthropathy, [OR] infectious arthritis, [OR] Charcot's knee joint, [OR] villonodular synovitis, [AND] and synovial chondromatosis
Patients taking immunosuppressant medication
Patients [AND] with abnormal hematology [OR] or serum chemistry lab results
Patients receiving injection to treatment knee within 2 months of study enrollment
BMI greater than 35 [OR] or less than 20
