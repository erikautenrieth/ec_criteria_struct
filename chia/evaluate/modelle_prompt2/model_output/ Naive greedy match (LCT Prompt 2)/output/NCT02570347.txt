Inclusion Criteria:
Age 18-65 years
History of snake bite [AND] with features of local envenomation [AND] with/ [NOT] without systemic features
Less than 24 hours since bite, [OR] AND
No prior antibiotic treatment

Exclusion Criteria:
Upper limb bites
Multiple (> 1) bites
Wound manipulation
Extensive local necrosis [OR] or blebs
Seriously-ill patients [AND] with hypotension/ [OR] capillary leak/ [OR] life threatening bleeding.
Suspected cobra bite, [OR] OR
Pregnant/ [OR] breast-feeding women
