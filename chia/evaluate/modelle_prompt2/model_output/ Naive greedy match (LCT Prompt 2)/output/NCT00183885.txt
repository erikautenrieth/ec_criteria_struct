Inclusion Criteria:
Unresectable, [OR] histologically confirmed hepatocellular carcinoma [AND] with evident disease limited to liver. 
Tissue from tumor must be available. This may be paraffin embedded tissue from previous biopsy/ [OR] resection [OR] or if it is [NOT] not available, [OR] a repeat biopsy must be performed. The requirement for biopsy may be waived if alpha-fetoprotein is greater than 500 ng/ [OR] mL [AND] and in the investigators opinion [NOT] not explained by a concurrent hepatic inflammatory process. 
Patients must agree to have a 20 cc blood sample drawn [AND] in addition to routine labs [AND] with each cycle of chemotherapy. 
Patients must have measurable disease. If prior radiation therapy was administered, [OR] measurable disease must be outside the radiation field. 
Patients must have a Zubrod performance status of 0-2. 
Patients must have a predicted life expectancy of at least 12 weeks. 
Patients must have a pre-treatment granulocyte count (i.e., [OR] segmented neutrophils + bands) of greater than [OR] or equal to 1,500/ [OR] mm3, [OR] a hemoglobin level of greater than [OR] or equal to 9 gm/ [OR] dl, [AND] and platelet count greater than [OR] or equal to 50,000/ [OR] mm3. The granulocyte requirement may be waived if in the investigator's opinion the lower count reflects hypersplenism [AND] with adequate bone marrow reserves. 
Patients must have adequate renal function as documented by a calculated creatinine clearance ≥ 60. 
Patients must have adequate hepatic function as documented by a serum bilirubin less than [OR] or equal to 2x the institutional upper limit of normal, [OR] regardless of whether patients have liver involvement secondary to tumor. Patients may [NOT] not have ascites [OR] or the ascites must be responsive to diuretics. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] who have received prior chemotherapy for unresectable disease 
Patients [AND] with any active [OR] or uncontrolled infection, [OR] including known HIV infection. (Patients [AND] with active hepatitis B will be placed on lamivudine. Patients [AND] with active hepatitis C will be eligible if liver tests qualify (5.1.9) 
Patients [AND] with psychiatric disorders [AND] that would interfere [AND] with consent [OR] or follow-up. Pregnant [OR] or lactating women. Men [AND] and women of reproductive potential may [NOT] not participate unless they have agreed to use an effective contraceptive method. 
Patients [AND] with any other severe concurrent disease, [OR] which in the judgment of the investigator, [OR] would make the patient inappropriate for entry into this study.
