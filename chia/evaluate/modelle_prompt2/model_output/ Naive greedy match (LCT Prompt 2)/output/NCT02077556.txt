Inclusion Criteria:
De novo kidney transplants
20 - 65 years old
aspartate aminotransferase/ [OR] alanine aminotransferase within 2 times the upper limit of normal range

Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnancy
Tuberculosis
Hepatitis B [OR] or C carrier status
Human immunodeficiency virus-positive status
Retransplantation [OR] or multiorgan transplantation
History of rheumatoid arthritis
Use of drugs [AND] that might have enhanced [OR] or inhibited CYP3A4 [OR] or P-gp activity
