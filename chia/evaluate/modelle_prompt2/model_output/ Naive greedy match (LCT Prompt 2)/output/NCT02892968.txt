Inclusion Criteria:
At the cluster level, [OR] ED physicians practicing at a participating site will be eligible. 
At the patient level, [OR] all hip fractures seen by a participating ED physician will be eligible 

Exclusion Criteria:
ED physicians [AND] who work casually (less than 0.25 Full Time Equivalent) 
ED Physicians [AND] who are routinely using U/ [OR] S guided RA for hip fracture patients, [OR] or decline participation in the trial. 
Patients' age less than 65 years; 
Patients [AND] who are delirious on initial assessment by ED physician [OR] or severe dementia 
Patients [AND] with communication problems (critically ill, [OR] unconscious, [OR] language barrier [AND] despite use of secure telephone-based translation service) 
Patients [AND] with allergies to narcotics [OR] or local anesthetic; [OR] or anticoagulant use (e.g. warfarin, [OR] dabigatran, [OR] rivaroxaban). 
Patients [AND] with hip fractures [NOT] not requiring surgery (e.g. greater trochanter avulsion) will also be [NOT] excluded.
