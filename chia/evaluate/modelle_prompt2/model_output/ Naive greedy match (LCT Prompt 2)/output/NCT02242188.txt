Inclusion Criteria:
Term singleton infants (>37 weeks gestational age)
Birth weight > 2500g
Healthy at inclusion
Breastfed exclusively [OR] or predominantly (>50% meals) at inclusion
No previous iron supplementation
No previous blood transfusion
Informed consent given

Exclusion Criteria:
preterm delivery (<37 weeks of gestation)
birth weight < 2500 g
multiple pregnancy
major illness [OR] or congenital anomaly
being <50% breastfed at the time of inclusion
food allergy
anaemia (Hb <105 g/ [OR] L [10.5 g/ [OR] dL]) at inclusion, [NOT] lack of informed consent
