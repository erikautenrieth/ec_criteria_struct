Inclusion Criteria:
Participants [AND] having H. pylori related chronic gastritis [AND] with/ [NOT] without peptic ulcers [AND] who are aged greater than 20 years old [AND] and are willing to received eradication therapy.

Exclusion Criteria:
pregnant [OR] or nursing woman
serious concomitant illness [AND] and malignant tumor of any kind
history of hypersensitivity to test drugs
serious bleeding during the course of the ulcer
previous gastric surgery
receiving bismuth salts, [OR] PPIs, [OR] or antibiotics in the previous month.
