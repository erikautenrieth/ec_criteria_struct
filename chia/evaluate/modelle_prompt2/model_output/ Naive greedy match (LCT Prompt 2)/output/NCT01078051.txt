Inclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with angina [OR] or silent ischemia [AND] and documented ischemia
Patients [AND] who are eligible for intracoronary stenting
Age > 18 years
De novo lesion CTO
Reference vessel size 2.5 mm by visual estimation
At least one CTO lesions located in proximal [OR] or mid epicardial coronary artery. (If the patient has two CTO lesions, [OR] one CTO lesion should be located in proximal [OR] or mid epicardial coronary artery)
Angiographically defined total occlusion over 3 months
If [NOT] no definite symptom [AND] with total occlusion, [OR] two experienced operators decide CTO in consideration of angiographical morphology (degree of calcification, [OR] bridging collaterals, [OR] non-tapered stump, [OR] angiographic filling from collaterals)

NA
