Inclusion Criteria:
Male [OR] or female patients = 18 [AND] and = 85 years of age
Women of child bearing potential must test negative on standard pregnancy test (urine [OR] or serum)
Patients [AND] with body weight = 55 kg [AND] and = 140 kg [AND] and body mass index (BMI) = 18 kg/ [OR] m2
Patients diagnosed severe sepsis / [OR] septic shock at admission on Intensive Care Unit [AND] who can be enrolled within 90 min after admission OR patients diagnosed severe sepsis / [OR] septic shock during Intensive Care Unit stay [AND] who can be enrolled within 90 min after diagnosis
Patients where antibiotic therapy has already been started (prior to randomization)
Patient [AND] who are fluid responsive. Fluid responsiveness is defined as increase of > 10% in mean arterial pressure (MAP) after passive leg raising (PLR)
Signed informed consent by patient, [OR] legal representative [OR] or authorized person [OR] or deferred consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Administration of HES, [OR] dextrane solutions [OR] or > 500 ml of Gelatin solutions within the 24 h prior to randomization
Death expected within the next 48 h (moribund patients as defined by ASA = class V)
Patients whose medical condition does preclude the PLR manoeuvre
Patients for whom the need of pressure infusions are expected
Requirement for renal support (either continuous [OR] or discontinuous techniques, [OR] including intermittent haemodialysis, [OR] haemofiltration [AND] and haemodiafiltration)
Patients receiving therapeutic heparin medication due to chronic coagulation disease / [OR] anticoagulation medication (i.e. partial thromboplastin time > 60 sec)
Acutely burned patients
Contraindications according to summary of product characteristics of investigational test [AND] and reference product
Simultaneous participation in another interventional clinical trial (drugs [OR] or medical devices studies)
