Inclusion Criteria:
All patients admitted at the Gynecological emergency Unit at Hospital de Clínicas de Porto Alegre scheduled for uterine evacuation [AND] with <12 weeks of gestation.

Exclusion Criteria:
patients [AND] who do [NOT] not wish to participate in the project;
patients [AND] with ectopic pregnancy;
patients [AND] with comorbidities (heart failure congestive, [OR] chronic obstructive pulmonary disease);
patients [AND] with hypovolemic shock;
patients [AND] with cervical incompetence;
patients [AND] with infected miscarriage/ [OR] abortion (presence of fever, [OR] pus from the cervix, [OR] leukocytosis [> 14000]);
patients [AND] with twin pregnancy;
patients [AND] with Marfan syndrome;
patients allergic to misoprostol;
patients [AND] with coagulopathy;
patients [AND] with opening of cervical internal os (4 mm of dilatation at the time of consultation);
patients [AND] with previous surgery of the cervix (conization);
patients [AND] with concomitant use of IUDs.
