Inclusion Criteria:
Age 35-70 years old
Fasting blood glucose 100-125 mg/ [OR] dL

Exclusion Criteria:
A1C >7.0%
2hr glucose during OGTT >200 mg/ [OR] dL
Total cholesterol >280 mg/ [OR] dL
Previous diabetic history, [OR] coronary artery disease
Allergy to rosuvastatin [OR] or parvastatin
Baseline ALT more than 3 times UNL
Serum Cr > 2.0 mg/ [OR] dL
Pregnancy, [OR] breast feeding [OR] or plan to be pregnant woman.
