Inclusion Criteria:
1. Patients [AND] with stable (Canadian Cardiovascular Society 1, [OR] 2, [OR] 3 [OR] or 4) [OR] or unstable (Braunwald class IB, [OR] IC, [OR] IIB, [OR] IIC, [OR] IIIB, [OR] IIIC) angina pectoris [AND] and ischemia, [OR] or patients [AND] with atypical chest pain [OR] or even those [AND] who are asymptomatic provided they have documented myocardial ischaemia (e.g. treadmill exercise test, [OR] radionuclide scintigraphy, [OR] stress echocardiography, [OR] Holter tape);

8. Left ventricular ejection fraction should be at least 30%. 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Congestive heart failure; 
2. CABG [OR] or Percutaneous Coronary Intervention (PCI) procedure; 
3. Planned need for major surgery (e.g. valve surgery [OR] or resection of aortic [OR] or left ventricular aneurysm, [OR] carotid end-arterectomy, [OR] abdominal aortic aneurysm surgery etc.); 
4. Congenital heart disease; 
5. Transmural myocardial infarction within the previous seven days [AND] and CK has [NOT] not returned to normal; 
6. Chest pain lasting longer than 30 minutes within 12 hours pre-procedure, [OR] if CK enzymes positive (≥ 2x the normal upper limit). 
7. History of any cerebrovascular accident; 
8. Left main stenosis of 50% [OR] or more; 
9. Intention to treat more than 1 totally occluded major epicardial vessel; 
10. Single vessel (single territory) disease.
