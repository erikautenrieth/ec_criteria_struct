Inclusion Criteria:
Has a cataract [AND] and is expected to undergo clear corneal cataract surgery [AND] with phacoemulsification [AND] and implantation of a posterior chamber intraocular lens 
Has a potential post-operative pinhole corrected Snellen VA of at least 20/ [OR] 200 [OR] or better in both eyes 

Exclusion Criteria:
Any intraocular inflammation in the study eye present during the screening slit lamp examination 
Score greater than "0" on the Ocular Pain Assessment in the study eye at Screening 
Any intraocular inflammation in the study eye present during the screening slit lamp examination
