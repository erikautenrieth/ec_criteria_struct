Inclusion Criteria:
Patients aged 19 [OR] or older
Patients [AND] who have submitted a written consent to participate in the clinical trial
De novo lesion
Patients scheduled for elective intervention to treat ischemic cardiovascular disease

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with any contraindications [OR] or hypersensitivity related to antiplatelet therapy
Patients [AND] with Acute Myocardial Infarction (ST elevation myocardial infarction, [OR] Non ST elevation myocardial infarction)
Patients [AND] who are anticipated to receive treatment [OR] or surgery [AND] that may require desisting the administration of antiplatelet therapy for 2 weeks [OR] or longer during the period of the clinical trial
Chronic total occlusion (CTO) lesions, [OR] in-stent restenosis (ISR)
Patients experiencing cardiogenic shock
Women [AND] who are breastfeeding, [OR] pregnant, [OR] or desiring pregnancy
Patients [AND] with findings of hemorrhage
Patients [AND] with a life expectancy of less than 1 year
Patients [AND] who have received a drug-eluting stent (DES) procedure within the past 6 months
Any other patients judged by the investigator to be unsuitable for the trial
