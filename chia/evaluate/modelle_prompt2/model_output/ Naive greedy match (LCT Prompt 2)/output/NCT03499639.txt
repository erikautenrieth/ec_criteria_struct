Inclusion Criteria:
patients were 18 years old [OR] or more, [NOT] naive to HCV treatment, [OR] HCV genotype 4, [OR] compensated liver disease.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with combined HCV/ [OR] HBV co-infection
hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC)
decompensated liver cirrhosis (Child-Pugh score above 6)
non-genotype 4
