Inclusion Criteria:
1. Males [AND] and females ≥ 18 years old. 
2. Clinical diagnosis of type 1 diabetes for at least one year. 
3. The subject will have been on insulin pump therapy for at least 3 months [AND] and currently using a fast actin insulin analog (Lispro, [OR] Aspart [OR] or Guilisine). 
4. Last (less than 3 months) HbA1c ≤ 10%. 
5. Currently using carbohydrate counting as the meal insulin dose strategy. 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. Clinically significant microvascular complications: nephropathy (estimated glomerular filtration rate below 40 ml/ [OR] min), [OR] neuropathy (especially diagnosed gastroparesis) [OR] or severe proliferative retinopathy as judged by the investigator. 
2. Recent (< 3 months) acute macrovascular event e.g. acute coronary syndrome [OR] or cardiac surgery. 
3. Ongoing pregnancy. 
4. Severe hypoglycemic episode within 1 month of screening. 
5. Agents affecting gastric emptying (Motilium®, [OR] Prandase®, [OR] Victoza®, [OR] Byetta® [AND] and Symlin®) as well as oral anti-diabetic agents (Metformin, [OR] SGLT-2 inhibitors [AND] and DPP-4 inhibitors) if [NOT] not at a stable dose for 3 months. Otherwise, [OR] these medications are acceptable [AND] and will be kept stable during the entire protocol. 
6. Oral steroids unless patients present a low stable dose (e.g. 10 mg [OR] or less of prednisone per day [OR] or physiological doses, [OR] less than 35 mg/ [OR] day, [OR] of hydrocortisone Cortef®). Inhale steroids at stable dose in the last month are acceptable. 
7. Other serious medical illness likely to interfere [AND] with study participation [OR] or [AND] with the ability to complete the trial by the judgment of the investigator (e.g. unstable psychiatric condition). 
8. Failure to comply [AND] with team's recommendations (e.g. [NOT] not willing to change pump parameters, [OR] follow algorithm's suggestions, [OR] etc). 
9. Living [OR] or planned travel outside Montreal (> 1h of driving) area during closed-loop procedures.
