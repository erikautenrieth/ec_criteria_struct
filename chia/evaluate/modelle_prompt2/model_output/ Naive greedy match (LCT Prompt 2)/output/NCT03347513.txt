Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosed Iron deficiency anemia.
H-pylori positive cases.
Second trimester pregnancy.

Exclusion Criteria:
Severe Iron deficiency anemia (hemoglobin < 8.0 g/ [OR] dL).
Parasitic worm infection e.g. schistosomiasis, [AND] and hook worm by stool analysis.
Any cases giving clinical symptoms of gastritis e.g. nausea, [OR] vomiting, [OR] dull aching pain [OR] or soreness in the epigastrium.
Cases [AND] with history of gastric ulcer diagnosed by upper endoscopy.
Cases complaining of hematemesis.
