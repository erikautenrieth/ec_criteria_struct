Inclusion Criteria:
successful left atrial appendage occlusion [AND] with Amulet device within 37 days prior to randomization.
treatment [AND] with dual antiplatelet therapy (clopidogrel [AND] and acetylsalicylic acid) between left atrial appendage closure [AND] and randomization
participant's age 18 years [OR] or older at the time of signing the informed consent form
participant is willing to follow all study procedures; especially randomized antiplatelet treatment regimen [AND] and follow-up visits [AND] with transesophageal echocardiography when applicable
participant is willing to sign the study informed consent form

Exclusion Criteria:
indications to dual antiplatelet therapy other than atrial fibrillation [OR] or left atrial appendage occlusion at the time of enrollment [OR] or predicted appearance of such indications within the duration of the trial (eg. coronary artery disease)
indications to anticoagulation at the time of enrollment [OR] or predicted appearance of such indications within the duration of the trial (eg. pulmonary embolism)
known allergy to clopidogrel [OR] or acetylsalicylic acid precluding its administration as specified by the protocol
any known inborn [OR] or acquired coagulation disorders
poor tolerance of [OR] or technical difficulties [AND] with performing transesophageal echocardiography
peridevice leak >5mm on transesophageal echocardiography study preceding enrollment
left atrial thrombus on transesophageal echocardiography study performed after successful left atrial appendage closure [AND] but before enrollment
life expectancy of less than 18months
participation in other clinical studies [AND] with experimental therapies at the time of enrollment [AND] and preceding 3 months
chronic kidney disease stage IV [AND] and V
women [AND] who are pregnant [OR] or breast feeding; women of childbearing potential [AND] who do [NOT] not consent to apply at least to methods of contraception. This criterion does [NOT] not apply to postmenopausal women
