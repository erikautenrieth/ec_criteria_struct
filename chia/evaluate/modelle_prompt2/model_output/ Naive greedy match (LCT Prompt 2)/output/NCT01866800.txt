Inclusion Criteria:
Subject is 65 years old [AND] who is able [AND] and willing to give an informed consent.
Patients undergoing planned trans-femoral TAVI.
Calculated eGFR below 60ml/ [OR] min/ [OR] 1.73m2 (MDRD)

Exclusion Criteria:
History of acute coronary syndrome in the past 30 days.
History of congesting heart failure [AND] with left ventricular ejection fraction <30% [OR] or exacerbation in the past 30 days.
Current dialysis treatment.
Known furosemide hypersensitivity.
Contraindications to placement of a Foley catheter in the bladder.
