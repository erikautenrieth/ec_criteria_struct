Competent to provide informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Emergency surgery
Monitored Anesthesia Care (i.e., [OR] regional anesthesia alone [NOT] without plans for general anesthesia)

Poor health literacy
Allergy, [OR] or have experienced any drug reaction to ketamine
Pregnant [OR] or lactating
