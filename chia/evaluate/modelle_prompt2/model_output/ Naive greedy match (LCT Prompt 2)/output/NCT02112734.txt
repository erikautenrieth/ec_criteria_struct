Inclusion Criteria:
Healthy, [OR] term, [OR] breastfeeding infants [AND] who will be predominately breastfed for at least 6-months. This will be determined by answering yes/ [NOT] no to question 'do you intend to breastfeed until your infant is at least 6 months of age.'

Exclusion Criteria:
Infants [AND] who have already received postnatal vitamin D supplementation
prematurity (<37 weeks)/ [OR] low birthweight <2500 g
poor health due to a current [OR] or past significant disease state [OR] or congenital abnormality.
