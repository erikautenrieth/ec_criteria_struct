Inclusion Criteria:
CHB patients [AND] who had received single NAs for more than 12 months.
Hepatitis B e antigen (HBeAg)-negative.
Hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg) positive [AND] and <1000 IU/ [OR] mL.
Hepatitis B virus DNA <100 IU/ [OR] mL.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with liver cirrhosis, [OR] Hepatocellular Carcinoma [OR] or AFP >2 ULN [OR] or other malignancies.
Patients [AND] with other factors causing liver diseases.
Pregnant [AND] and lactating women.
Patients [AND] with concomitant HIV infection [OR] or congenital immune deficiency diseases.
Patients [AND] with diabetes, [OR] autoimmune diseases.
Patients [AND] with important organ dysfunctions.
Patients [AND] with serious complications (e.g., [OR] infection, [OR] hepatic encephalopathy, [OR] hepatorenal syndrome, [OR] gastrointestinal bleeding.)
Patients [AND] who receive antineoplastic [OR] or immunomodulatory therapy in the past 12 months.
Patients [AND] with a previous use of IFN anti hepatitis B virus treatment [OR] or have NAs drug resistance.
Patients [AND] who can't come back to clinic for follow-up on schedule.
