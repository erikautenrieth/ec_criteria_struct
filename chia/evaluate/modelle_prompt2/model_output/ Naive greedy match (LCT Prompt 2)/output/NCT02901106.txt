Inclusion Criteria:
patient 18 years old [AND] and more
[AND] with multiple sclerosis according to the criteria of Mac Donald 2010 : relapsing-remitting (RR), [OR] secondary-progressive (SP) [OR] or primary-progressive (PP)
for which treatment [AND] with dimethyl-fumarate has been prescribed
followed at the Rothschild Foundation in the Neurology Department
[AND] having given written consent to participation in the study

Exclusion Criteria:
pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding woman
patient [AND] with a measure of legal protection
subject unaffiliated insurance
