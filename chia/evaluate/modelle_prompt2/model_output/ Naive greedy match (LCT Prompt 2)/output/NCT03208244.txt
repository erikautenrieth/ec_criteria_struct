Inclusion Criteria:
Recipient is Age = 18 years
Serum ALT within normal limits [AND] with [NOT] no history of liver disease
Lack of sensitization (i.e. PRA < 20%) [AND] that would be expected to result in a high likelihood of needing aggressive immunosuppression to treat rejection

Exclusion Criteria:
Sensitization (i.e. PRA >20%)
Any liver disease in recipient
Albumin < 3g/ [OR] dl [OR] or platelet count < 75 x 103/ [OR] mL
Need for dual organ transplant
