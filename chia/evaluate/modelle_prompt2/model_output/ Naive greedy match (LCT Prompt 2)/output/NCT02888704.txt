Inclusion Criteria:
Of either gender, [OR] aged ≥19 [AND] and ≤70 years 
Atopic dermatitis subjects [AND] who are coincident [AND] with Hanifin [AND] and Rajka diagnosis criteria 
Subacute [AND] and chronic atopic subjects [AND] who have atopic dermatitis symptoms continually at least 6 months 
Subjects [AND] with over moderate atopic dermatitis (SCORAD score > 20) 
Subjects [AND] who understand [AND] and voluntarily sign an informed consent form 

Exclusion Criteria:
Subjects [AND] who have systemic infection 
Subjects [AND] who have human Immunodeficiency virus (HIV), [OR] hepatitis B virus (HBV), [AND] and hepatitis C virus (HCV) 
Subjects [AND] who need to take the medicine which is prohibited during this study 
Subjects [AND] who have asthma 
Subjects [AND] who can [NOT] not stop treatment [AND] with topical steroids (group 1~5), [OR] oral antibiotics, [OR] whole body photochemotherapy, [OR] immunosuppressive drug within 4 weeks before the treatment visit 
Pregnant, [OR] breast-feeding women [OR] or women [AND] who plan to become pregnant during this study (Females of childbearing potential must have a negative urine pregnancy test) 
Subjects [AND] who currently participate in other clinical trial [OR] or participated in other clinical trial within 30 days 
Subjects [AND] who had a serious adverse events during stem cell therapy 
Subjects [AND] who had a hypersensitivity to antibiotics [OR] or antimycotics 
Subjects [AND] who creatinine value is more than two times of the upper limit of the normal range at screening test 
Subjects [AND] who aspartate transaminase/ [OR] alkaline transaminase (AST/ [OR] ALT) value is more than three times of the upper limit of the normal range at screening test 
Subjects [AND] who have any other condition which the investigator judges would make patients unsuitable for study participation
