Inclusion Criteria:
Cohort 1: Recurrent [OR] or refractory medulloblastoma in which current standard treatment approaches have failed; biopsy is [NOT] not required for recurrent disease. 
Cohort 2: Newly-diagnosed high-grade glioma (World Health Organization [WHO] grade 3 [OR] or 4) 
Life expectancy ≥ 3 months 

Exclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis of brainstem glioma 
Concurrent administration of any other anti-tumor therapy 
Pre-existing uncontrolled diarrhea
