Inclusion Criteria:
Clinical diagnosis of calculous cholecystitis.

Exclusion Criteria:
Gallbladder's wall >3mm, [OR] atrophied gallbladder,gallstone obstruct the Hartmann's pouch.
Abdominal ultrasound display the contractibility of gallbladder is poor.
The aged patients [AND] with bad heart [AND] and lung function.
Patients [AND] who has acute cholecystitis,pancreatitis,pancreaticobiliary diseases, [OR] especially choledocholithiasis.
Pregnant [OR] or lactational women.
