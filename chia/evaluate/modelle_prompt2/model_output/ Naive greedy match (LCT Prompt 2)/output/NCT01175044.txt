Inclusion Criteria:
Scheduled to undergo revision total knee arthroplasty

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Inability to provide informed consent [OR] or to comply [AND] with study assessments (e.g. due to cognitive impairment [OR] or geographic distance).
Age = 17.
Allergy to povidone iodine.
Any condition requiring antibiotics 14 days prior to arriving for surgery.
Patients [AND] with chronic immunosuppression (such as HIV/ [OR] AIDS).
Unable to adhere to follow up schedule [AND] and treatment.
Patients scheduled to undergo revision total knee arthroplasty for infectious reasons.
