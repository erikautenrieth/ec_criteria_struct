Inclusion Criteria:
Histologic [OR] or cytologic diagnosis of stage IIIB/ [OR] IV NSCLC 
ECOG PS: 0,1 
Unidimensional [OR] or bi-dimensional measurable disease 
Receive prior treatment including first-line platinum-based chemotherapy, [OR] standard second-line chemotherapy [AND] and 1 EGF/ [OR] EGFR inhibitor 
Evidence of disease progression 
Life expectancy >12 weeks 
Neutrophils > 1.5 109/ [OR] l, [OR] Platelets > 100 109/ [OR] l, [OR] Hemoglobin > 9g/ [OR] dl, [OR] Total bilirubin < 1.5 UNL, [OR] AST (SGOT) [AND] and ALT (SGPT) < 2.5 UNL, [OR] Alkaline phosphatases < 5 UNL, [OR] Creatinine < 1 UNL 

Exclusion Criteria:
Pre-existing hemoptysis of a severity > grade 3 by NCI CTCAE criteria within 4 weeks prior to study entry 
Uncontrolled hypertension 
CHF, [OR] angina [OR] or arrhythmias 
LVEF < 1 UNL 
Existing a second malignancy within 5 years 
Infected [AND] with HIV
