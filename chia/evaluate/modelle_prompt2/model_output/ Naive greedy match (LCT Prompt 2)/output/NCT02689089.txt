Inclusion Criteria:
Males [OR] or non-pregnant, [OR] non-nursing females between the ages of 2-65 years
LTBI diagnosis as per Canadian TB Standards using either the Tuberculin Skin Test (TST) [OR] or the Interferon Gamma Release Assay (IGRA)
Children 2-5 years [AND] with negative TSTs [AND] who have been in close contact [AND] with a case of active TB disease recently
Able [AND] and willing to provide fully informed consent [OR] or parent/ [OR] guardian able to provide consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Suspected [OR] or confirmed active TB disease
Known allergies to any of the study medications by participant self-report
have a positive pregnancy test at screening, [OR] or
are [NOT] not willing to use a reliable method of barrier contraception during the study, [OR] or
are breastfeeding
hormonal contraception
HIV infected participants [AND] who are on anti-retroviral drugs
other drugs [AND] that interact [AND] with 3HP (see Table 1)
Known contact [AND] with an INH [OR] or rifampin resistant case
Weight < 10 kg
Evidence of possible liver damage defined by an aspartate transaminase (AST) level [AND] that is more than 3x the upper limit of normal in an asymptomatic patient
Porphyria reported by patient
[NOT] Inability to adhere to protocol.
Patients may be [NOT] excluded from the study for other reasons, [OR] at the investigator's discretion [AND] with detailed documentation.
