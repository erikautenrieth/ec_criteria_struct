Inclusion Criteria:
Elderly patients over 65 years old exhibiting clinical indices of cardiovascular disease 
Male [OR] or female 
Subjects [AND] who were hospitalized in the Geriatric Unit of the Emile Roux Hospital (AP-HP) 
MMSE (Mini Mental State Examination)score > [OR] or = 15 
Supervision available for study medication 
Able to ingest oral diet 

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [NOT] unable to understand the objectives of the dietary intervention 
Patients in paliative care 
Patients receiving supplement diets
