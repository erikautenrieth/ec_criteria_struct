Inclusion Criteria:
traumatic spinal cord injury at least one year ago
regular bowel care routine (at least four weeks)

Exclusion Criteria:
cauda equina [OR] or conus lesion
currently use ventilator
colostomy, [OR] or do [NOT] not perform regular bowel care for any reason
any skin breakdown (pressure sores)
do [NOT] not speak English
are under 19 years old
are pregnant [OR] or think you might be pregnant
medical/ [OR] psychiatric condition [OR] or substance abuse [AND] that is likely to affect your ability to complete this study
currently using medications containing lidocaine
allergy to lidocaine
