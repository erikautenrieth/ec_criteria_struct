Inclusion Criteria:
18-80 year, [OR] male [OR] or female.
Chronic Heart failure subjects [AND] with medical history of cardiac disease [OR] or other related cardiovascular disease.
Left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) less than [OR] or equal to (=<) 40 percent (%).
New York Heart Association (NYHA) class of II - IV
NYHA II : Slight limitation of physical activity. Comfortable at rest, [AND] but ordinary physical activity results in undue breathlessness, [OR] fatigue [OR] or palpitation.
NYHA III:Marked limitation of physical activity. Comfortable at rest, [AND] but less than ordinary activity causes undue breathlessness, [OR] fatigue [OR] or palpitation.
NYHA IV:Unable to carry on any physical activity [NOT] without discomfort. Symptoms at rest can be present. If any physical activity is undertaken, [OR] discomfort increased.

Severe hypotension [resting SBP less than (<) 90mmHg, [OR] or resting DBP<50mmHg].
Resting heart rate <60 beat per minute (bpm).

Acute heart failure [OR] or during episodes of heart failure decompensation requiring intravenous inotropic therapy.

Atrioventricular block of second [OR] or third degree ([NOT] without a pacemaker).
