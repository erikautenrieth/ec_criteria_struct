Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis of dyslipidemia: The existence of a previous clinical diagnostic of dyslipidemia associated [AND] with lipid-lowering therapy. It is also considered patients [AND] who have an altered analytical, [OR] using the following cutoffs: total cholesterol = 200 mg / [OR] dl, [OR] triglycerides = 180 mg / [OR] dl, [OR] HDL-cholesterol = 40 mg / [OR] dl [OR] or LDL-cholesterol = 150 mg / [OR] dl. Lipid-lowering treatment [AND] and diet, [OR] stable in the last month.
A concentration of LDL-cholesterol above 100 mg / [OR] dl, [OR] in the month prior to inclusion.
An apnea-hypopnea index between 5-30 h-1

Exclusion Criteria:
Apnea-hypopnea index of less than 5 h-1 [OR] or greater than 30 h-1.
Predominance of central apneas [AND] and hypopneas, [OR] defined as more than 25% of all respiratory events.
Professional drivers, [OR] risk profession [OR] or respiratory failure (according to criteria of the clinical pathway for diagnosis [AND] and treatment of sleep-disordered breathing).
Very excessive daytime sleepiness (Epworth Sleepiness Scale> 18).
Morbid obesity (BMI> 40 kg / [OR] m2).
Prior treatment [AND] with CPAP.
