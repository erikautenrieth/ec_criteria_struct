Inclusion Criteria:
Age =1 year, [OR] stratified into different age groups
Living in the Waya Clinic Catchment Area
Good health condition, [NOT] without clinically significant medical history (by participant [OR] or guardian, [OR] in case of minor)
Not pregnant for female subjects.
Available to participate for the study duration, [OR] including all planned follow-up visits for up to 9 months from screening.
Signed informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
Presence of a significant medical [OR] or psychiatric condition (Examples include: Diagnosis [AND] and treatment of tuberculosis (TB) [OR] or HIV; renal insufficiency; hepatic disease; oral [OR] or parenteral medication known to affect the immune function, [OR] such as corticosteroids, [OR] other immunosuppressant drugs; [OR] or behavioural [OR] or memory issues)
Ever [AND] having received oral cholera vaccine.
Receipt of an investigational product (within 30 days before vaccination).
History of diarrhoea in 7 days prior to first dose of vaccine (defined as =3 unformed loose stools in 24 hours).
History of chronic diarrhea (lasting for more than 2 weeks in the past 6 months)
Current use of laxatives, [OR] antacids, [OR] or other agents to lower stomach acidity?
Planning to become pregnant in the next 2 years.
