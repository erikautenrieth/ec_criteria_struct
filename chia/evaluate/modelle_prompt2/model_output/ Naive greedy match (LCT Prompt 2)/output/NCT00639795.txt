Inclusion Criteria:
Age greater than 18 
Planned thoracoscopy [AND] with low probability(by surgeon estimate) of conversion to open procedure 

Exclusion Criteria:
Age less than 18 
Clinical [OR] or laboratory evidence of systemic infection 
Current pregnancy as assessed by preoperative urine HCG test 
Serious, [OR] uncontrolled, [OR] non-malignant illness 
Malignant illness requiring systemic chemotherapy in the last 6 months 
Documented allergy to oxycodone, [OR] morphine sulfate [OR] or acetaminophen 
Contraindication to peripheral nerve blockade [OR] or general anesthesia including: 
1. patient refusal 
2. active infection at site of planned block 
3. documented allergy to any local [OR] or general anesthetic medications 
4. significant coagulopathy( prothrombin time >15 seconds, [OR] INR>1.5 
5. pre-existing neuropathy [AND] and medical conditions [OR] or deformities which would compromise block [OR] or anesthetic safety 
Planned pleurodesis 
Current use of high dose inhaled [OR] or systemic steroids 
Current use of Amiodarone (Cordarone) 
Morbid obesity (BMI=40kg/ [OR] m2) 
Patients [AND] with clinically significant mental health issues such as psychosis requiring treatment [AND] with antipsychotic medications. 
Patients [NOT] unable to consent 
Patients [AND] with active infections requiring antibiotics within one month of registration 
Participation in other clinical trials [AND] that may interfere [AND] with this study
