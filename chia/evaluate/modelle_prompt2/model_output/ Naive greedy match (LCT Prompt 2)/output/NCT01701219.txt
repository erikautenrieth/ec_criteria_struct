Inclusion Criteria:
1. Presence of bacteremia due solely to: 
S. aureus on at least 1 blood culture within 72 hours of beginning study drug (Cohort A) OR 
MRSA on a baseline blood culture [AND] and on at least 1 additional blood culture after at least 72 hours of vancomycin [AND] and/ [OR] or daptomycin treatment (Cohort B). 
2. Male [OR] or female ≥ 18 years of age. 
3. If female of childbearing potential must be willing to practice sexual abstinence [OR] or dual methods of contraception during treatment [AND] and for at least 30 days after the last dose of study drug. 
4. Expectation of survival for at least 2 months. 

Exclusion Criteria:
1. For subjects in Cohort A: previous therapy for more than 48 hours [AND] with any parenteral antibiotic [AND] with activity against S. aureus within 72 hours of positive blood culture results. 
2. For subjects in Cohort B: previous therapy for more than 48 hours [AND] with any parenteral antibiotic [AND] with activity against MRSA, [OR] except vancomycin [AND] and/ [OR] or daptomycin, [OR] within 72 hours of positive blood culture results confirming persistence. 
3. Previous episode of S. aureus bacteremia within 3 months. 
4. Known left-sided endocarditis [OR] or prosthetic heart valve. 
5. Osteomyelitis [OR] or prosthetic joint infection except new onset nonhardware-associated vertebral osteomyelitis. 
6. History of any hypersensitivity [OR] or allergic reaction to any β-lactam antibacterial agent. 
7. Evidence of significant hepatic, [OR] hematologic, [OR] or immunologic impairment. 
8. Pregnant [OR] or nursing females.
