Inclusion Criteria:
20-70 yrs of age
ASA(American Society of Anesthesiologists) physical status class I [OR] or II
Scheduled for gynecological laparoscopic surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
Allergic to study drugs
Antiemetics [OR] or steroids use within 24 hrs prior to surgery
Dependence upon opioids
Insulin dependent Diabetes Mellitus
Cardiovascular [OR] or pulmonary disease
Renal [OR] or hepatic insufficiency
BMI>=35kg/ [OR] m2
History of motion sickness [OR] or PONV
Cigarette smoker
Conversion to open laparotomy from laparoscopic surgery
Pregnants
