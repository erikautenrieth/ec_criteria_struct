Inclusion Criteria:
All patients (excluding neonates) requiring one [OR] or more allogeneic RBC transfusions for the treatment of anemia will be included.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] that do [NOT] not have a valid Ontario Health Insurance Plan (OHIP) number at time of first transfusion
Patients [AND] that require emergent release of a RBC transfusion [AND] and in whom emergency randomization could [NOT] not be completed
Patients [AND] with complex antibody profile in which it is [NOT] impossible to match RBC units
