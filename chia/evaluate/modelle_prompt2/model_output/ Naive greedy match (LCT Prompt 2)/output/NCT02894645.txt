Inclusion Criteria:
Confirmed diagnosis of non-Burkitt B-lineage ALL
1 to 17 years of age (before 18th birthday)
Renal function within normal range for age
Liver function within normal range for age
Able to participate in the full 2 years of treatment

Exclusion Criteria:
Age less than one year [OR] or age greater than/ [OR] equals to 18 years
Previous treatment [AND] with cytotoxic agents [OR] or high-dose steroids
Mixed phenotype acute leukemia (MPAL)
ALL as secondary malignancy
Abnormal renal [OR] or liver function
Doubtful compliance [OR] or [NOT] unable to afford full course of therapy
