Inclusion Criteria:
submucosal, [OR] intramural [OR] or subserosal leiomyomas, [OR] symptoms of menometrorrhagia, [OR] menstrual disorder, [OR] infertility, [OR] pelvic pain

Exclusion Criteria:
endometrial hyperplasia [AND] with atypia, [OR] estrogen-progestin therapy in the 2 months before enrollment, [OR] autoimmune diseases, [OR] chronic, [OR] metabolic, [OR] systemic [AND] and endocrine disorders, [OR] including hyperandrogenism, [OR] hyperprolactinemia, [OR] diabetes mellitus [AND] and thyroid disease, [OR] hypogonadotropic hypogonadism, [OR] majors clinical conditions
