Inclusion Criteria:
Acute Coronary Syndrome group: 40 patients [AND] with type 1 myocardial infarction within 21 days prior to the imaging visit [AND] and invasive coronary angiography [AND] with angiographic evidence of at least a 50% stenosis in one [OR] or more coronary arteries. Only patients undergoing PCI will be included in the study.
Stable Ischemic Heart Disease group: 40 patients [AND] who have undergone invasive coronary angiography within 21 days prior to the imaging visit, [AND] with history of typical angina prior to the angiogram, [AND] but [NOT] no prior myocardial infarction [OR] or coronary revascularization.
have [NOT] no prior CAD associated event ([NOT] no prior myocardial infarction, [OR] acute coronary syndrome, [OR] coronary angiogram, [OR] or PCI), [OR] have CAC between 10 to <1000, [AND] and
match to patients in the ACS group by gender, [OR] age by decile, [AND] and CAC category (using CAC categories of 10 to <100, [OR] 100 to <400, [OR] 400 to <1000).

Exclusion Criteria:
Age < 18 years
Creatinine > 1.5 mg/ [OR] dL
History of severe allergy to Iodine contrast agents
Pregnancy
Active atrial fibrillation
Multiple premature ventricular [OR] or atrial contractions
Ejection fraction <35%
Class III congestive heart failure
