Inclusion Criteria:
Patients older than 18 years
Ischemic symptoms [OR] or evidence of myocardial ischemia (inducible [OR] or spontaneous) in the presence of >50% de novo stenosis located in native coronary vessels

Exclusion Criteria:
Target lesion located in the left main stem
STEMI
Restenosis
Cardiogenic shock
Malignancies [OR] or other comorbid conditions [AND] with life expectancy less than 12 months [OR] or [AND] that may result in protocol noncompliance
Known allergy to the study medications (probucol, [OR] sirolimus, [OR] zotarolimus)
Pregnancy (present, [OR] suspected, [OR] or planned)
