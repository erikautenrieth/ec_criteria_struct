Inclusion Criteria:
Prior Myocardial Infarction [AND] and
Sustained monomorphic VT documented on 12-lead ECG [OR] or rhythm strip terminated by pharmacologic means [OR] or DC cardioversion
=3 episodes of VT treated [AND] with antitachycardia pacing (ATP), [OR] at least one of which was symptomatic
= 5 episodes of VT treated [AND] with antitachycardia pacing (ATP) regardless of symptoms
=1 appropriate ICD shocks, [OR] =3 VT episodes within 24 hours

Exclusion Criteria:
Unable [OR] or unwilling to provide informed consent.
Active ischemia (acute thrombus diagnosed by coronary angiography, [OR] or dynamic ST segment changes demonstrated on ECG) [OR] or another reversible cause of VT (e.g. drug-induced arrhythmia), [OR] had recent acute coronary syndrome within 30 days, [OR] coronary revascularization (<90 days bypass surgery, [OR] <30 days percutaneous coronary intervention), [OR] or have CCS functional class IV angina. Note [AND] that biomarker level elevation alone after ventricular arrhythmias does [NOT] not denote acute coronary syndrome [OR] or active ischemia.
Are ineligible to take the antiarrhythmic drug to which they would be assigned due to allergy, [OR] intolerance [OR] or contraindication
Are known to have protruding left ventricular thrombus [OR] or mechanical aortic [AND] and mitral valves
Have had a prior catheter ablation procedure for VT
Are in renal failure (Creatinine clearance <15 mL/ [OR] min), [OR] have NYHA Functional class IV heart failure, [OR] or a systemic illness likely to limit survival to <1 year
Have had recent ST elevation myocardial infarction [OR] or non-ST elevation MI (< 30 days); note [AND] that biomarker elevation alone after ventricular arrhythmias does [NOT] not denote MI.
Are pregnant.
