Inclusion Criteria:
Patients older than 18 years
Classification of the American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA I-III)
No cognitive deficits
Signed informed consent prior to surgery

Exclusion Criteria:
Emergency surgery
Pregnancy [OR] or lactation
Immune disorders
Kidney [OR] or liver disease [OR] or advanced-stage cardiopulmonary
Patient refusal to participate in the study
Patients under 18 years [OR] or inability to consent
Associated neuromuscular disorders, [OR] contraindication for the use of rocuronium/ [OR] sugammadex, [OR] allergy [OR] or hypersensitivity to rocuronium / [OR] sugammadex
