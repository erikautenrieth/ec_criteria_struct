Inclusion Criteria:
Aged 18 years [OR] or older
Previously taken one [OR] or more statins
Withdrawn from statins because of perceived side effects
Developed side effects within 2 weeks of initiation
Clinical indication for statins for primary [OR] or secondary prevention of cardiovascular disease [OR] or dyslipidaemia, [OR] on either [NOT] no medication [OR] or non-statin lipid lowering therapy (e.g, [OR] ezetimibe)

Exclusion Criteria:
History of neuropathy
Regularly taking prescribed analgesia
History of a chronic pain condition
History of severe mental illness (as their experience of symptoms may already be altered)
Current use of fibrates (because of the risk of interaction [AND] with statins [AND] but will [NOT] not exclude participants taking ezetimibe).
Severe previous reaction [OR] or reaction considered immunological, [OR] such as anaphylaxis, [OR] facial swelling, [OR] severe rash, [OR] muscle ache [AND] with rise in serum creatine kinase, [OR] inflammatory myopathy, [OR] rhabdomyolysis [OR] or liver function abnormalities (aspartate transaminase (AST) [OR] or alanine transaminase (ALT) greater than 3 times upper limit [OR] or normal).
Side-effects taking longer than 2 weeks to develop (because in such participants much longer blocks of treatment would be required, [OR] if the present study is positive such studies will be planned for the future)*.
History of statin intolerance [AND] with drug interaction to antiretroviral drugs.
History of statin intolerance to any other drug.
Pregnant [OR] or breast feeding.
Side effects taking longer than 2 weeks to present.
In clinical judgement of study doctor, [OR] participant should [NOT] not participate.
