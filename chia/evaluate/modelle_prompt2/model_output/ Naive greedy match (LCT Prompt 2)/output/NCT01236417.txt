Inclusion Criteria:
Post menopausal women [AND] with a history of estrogen positive breast cancer [AND] who are receiving aromatase inhibitors for at least one month. 
Patients must complain of mild to moderate arthralgia. 
Ability to understand [AND] and sign informed consent. 
Patients meet criteria for low to moderate risk for moderate exercise based oon the ACSM guidelines. 

Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Inability to comply [AND] with study requirements. 
Metastatic breast cancer. 
Patients [AND] with orthopedic [OR] or neuromuscular disorders [AND] that preclude participation in exercise. 
Rheumatoid arthritis. 
History of MI, [OR] angina [OR] or congestive heart failure. 
Pregnant [OR] or lactating females. 
Patients [AND] that are high risk for moderate exercise based on ACSM risk classification. 
Patients [AND] who exceed minimal physical activity recommendations from the US Surgeon General's Report: Accumulation of 30 minutes [OR] or more of moderate physical activity on most days of the week. 
Morbidly obese [AND] with BMI ≥ 40
