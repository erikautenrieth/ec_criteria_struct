Inclusion Criteria:
35-75 years old;
diagnosed as type 2 diabetes according to the criteria of the World Health Organization in 1999.

Exclusion Criteria:
type 1 diabetes,specific types of diabetes,gestational diabetes [OR] or pregestational diabetes;
acute cardiovascular [OR] or cerebrovascular accidents within past 3 months;
severe hepatic [OR] or renal dysfunction;
malignant tumor;
allergic history [OR] or contraindication for any drugs in trials;
taking part in other clinical trials;
obviously poor compliance.
