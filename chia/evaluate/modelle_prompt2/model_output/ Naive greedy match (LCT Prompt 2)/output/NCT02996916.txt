Inclusion Criteria:
Written informed consent obtained
Male [AND] and female subjects aged 20 years [OR] or older at informed consent
Essential hypertension [AND] who had never received angiotensin II receptor antagonists [AND] and calcium channel blockers

Exclusion Criteria:
Secondary hypertension [OR] or malignant hypertension
Diabetes mellitus
History [OR] or evidence of a stroke
Hepatic [OR] or hematologic abnormality
Mild Cognitive Impairment [OR] or Dementia
Serum potassium level = 5.5 mEq/ [OR] L
Serum creatinine level = 3.0 mg/ [OR] dL
Acute [OR] or chronic disease
Allergy to any drugs
Pregnancy
