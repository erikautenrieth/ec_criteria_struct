Inclusion Criteria:
CKD patients classified as Stage 3 [AND] and 4 of National Kidney Foundation Classification [AND] with estimated glomerular filtration rate (GFR) between 15 [AND] and 59 mL/ [OR] min/ [OR] 1.73 m2 according to the Modification of Diet in Renal Disease (MDRD) formula based on serum creatinine, [OR] age, [OR] gender, [AND] and race.
Men [AND] and women 35 to 70 years of age

Exclusion Criteria:
Allergy to Glitazones
Myocardial infarction
Heart failure
Angina
History of kidney stones
Liver disease (abnormal liver enzymes)
Anemia (hemoglobin <8 g/ [OR] dl)
Cancer [AND] with current treatment
Previous organ transplantation
Immunosuppressant therapy
Human immunodeficiency virus infection
Pregnancy [OR] or lactating
Current tobacco use
Dilantin [AND] and oral contraceptive usage due to potential drug interaction [AND] with glitazones
Self-identified history of hypoglycemia
