Inclusion Criteria:
All patients admitted to the Duke CICU, [AND] who require intubation [AND] and sedation for mechanical ventilation [AND] that is expected to be >24 hours in duration will be included, [OR] unless they meet the specified exclusion criteria.
Patients intubated within one hour prior to care transition to the CICU will also be screened for inclusion.

Exclusion Criteria:
Exclusion criteria include patients following resuscitation from cardiac arrest [AND] who are treated on the cooling protocol
patients [AND] who have suffered a neurologic event (seizure, [OR] stroke) [OR] or [AND] who have baseline dementia, [OR] both of which could limit delirium assessment
patients [AND] with child class B [AND] and C liver disease
patients [AND] with known allergy to study medications.
