Patients aged =50 years [AND] with DM2 [AND] and symptomatic PAD diagnosed clinically (according to Fontaine criteria, [OR] stage IIa [OR] or IIb [AND] and III) [AND] and by measuring the <U+0391><U+0392><U+0399>.

Exclusion Criteria:

Patients [AND] with atrial fibrillation taking any anticoagulant therapy [OR] or patients [AND] with a history of cardioembolic ischemic stroke [OR] or hemorrhagic stroke.
Patients [AND] with a history (= 12 months) of acute coronary syndrome receiving dual antiplatelet therapy, [OR] or patients receiving monotherapy [AND] with aspirin.
Patients [AND] with hepatic impairment (child-Pugh staging, [OR] calibration = 5) [OR] or renal impairment (creatinine clearance = 30ml / [OR] min), [OR] recent peptic ulcer, [OR] a history of hypersensitivity to cilostazol, [OR] cancer patients undergoing treatment.
