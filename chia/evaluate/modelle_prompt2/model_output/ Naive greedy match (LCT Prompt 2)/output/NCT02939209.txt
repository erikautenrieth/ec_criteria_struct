Inclusion Criteria:
Age 18-65
scheduled to receive ISB [AND] and general anesthesia as a day surgery patient for rotator cuff repair [AND] and acromioplasty, [OR] as a part of planned routine care

Exclusion Criteria:
Allergy, [OR] sensitivity, [OR] or absolute contraindications to any of the medications involved in the study
preexisting CNS depression, [OR] or taking regularly medication [AND] that cause CNS depression
preexisting cognitive deficits, [OR] dementia, [OR] or delirium
severe respiratory comorbidities (e.g. chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, [OR] pneumonia, [OR] respiratory failure)
sleep disordered breathing (diagnosed OSA, [OR] obesity hypoventilation syndrome)
pregnancy [AND] and breast feeding
history of chronic pain [OR] or regular (at least once daily) opioid use preoperatively
renal impairment - CrCl =60 mL/ [OR] minute
[NOT] not fluent in English to be able to participate in the study process, [OR] including consent [AND] and phone interview
Body Mass Index >35
inability to take oral medication.
