Inclusion Criteria:
DSM-IV [OR] or DSM-5 diagnosis of schizophrenia [OR] or schizoaffective disorder
Male [OR] or Female
Age: 18 to 65 years
Caucasian [OR] or Non-Caucasian
Smoke at least 10 cigarettes daily
Urine cotinine level ? 100 ng/ [OR] ml (NicAlert(r) reading ? 3)
Agrees to wear a head mounted display (HMD) for up to 45 minutes
Able to complete the Evaluation to Sign Consent (ESC) [AND] with minimum score of 80%

Exclusion Criteria:
History of organic brain disease
DSM-IV diagnosis of Alcohol [OR] or Substance Dependence within the last six months (except nicotine) [OR] or DSM-5 diagnosis of Substance Use Disorder in the last six months (except nicotine)
DSM-IV diagnosis of Alcohol [OR] or Substance Abuse within the last one month (except nicotine) [OR] or DSM-5 diagnosis of Substance Use Disorder in the last six months (except nicotine)
Pregnancy [OR] or lactation
Severe liver dysfunction (LFT 3X upper limit of normal)
Previous known hypersensitivity to tetracyclines
Current treatment [AND] with tetracycline [OR] or derivative
Treatment [AND] with oral contraceptives (unless a second form of birth control is used [AND] and documented)
Treatment [AND] with cholestyramine [OR] or colestipol
Treatment [AND] with Urinary alkalinizers (e.g., [OR] sodium lactate, [OR] potassium citrate)
Treatment [AND] with warfarin
Treatment [AND] with bupropion, [OR] varenicline, [OR] or nicotine replacement products in the month prior to study inclusion
Less than two months treatment of adjunctive medications AND less than one month on same dose: beta blockers, [OR] antidepressants, [OR] mood stabilizers, [OR] antianxiety medications.
Medical condition whose pathology [OR] or treatment would significantly increase the risk associated [AND] with the proposed protocol.
History of head injury, [OR] seizures, [OR] or stroke
Positive urine toxicology screen for substances of non-therapeutic use prior to craving assessments
