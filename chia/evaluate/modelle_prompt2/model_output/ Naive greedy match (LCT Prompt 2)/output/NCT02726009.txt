Inclusion Criteria:
Has given written informed consent before any study-related activity is performed
Advanced hormone-dependent prostate cancer for which androgen deprivation therapy is indicated, [AND] and independently from this trial, [OR] Firmagon® is intended to be used for treatment
Age greater than [OR] or equal to 18 years [AND] and less than 80 years
Advanced hormone-dependent prostate cancer [NOT] without any other clinically significant disorder
Easten Cooperative Oncology Group score = 2
PSA = 2 ng/ [OR] mL at screening
Life expectancy of at least 12 months as per the investigator's judgement

Exclusion Criteria:
Previous [OR] or concurrent hormonal management of prostate cancer
Contraindication for prescription of Firmagon®
Concurrent treatment [AND] with a 5-a-reductase inhibitor
Considered as a candidate for curative therapy
History of severe untreated asthma, [OR] anaphylactic reactions [OR] or severe urticaria [AND] and/ [OR] or angioedema
QTc interval over 450 msec [OR] or risk factors for torsades de pointes [OR] or on Class IA [AND] and Class III anti arrhythmic medications
Cancer within the last 5 years except prostate cancer [AND] and surgically removed basal [OR] or squamous cell carcinoma of the skin
Known [OR] or suspected hepatic, [OR] symptomatic biliary disease (this includes moderate to severe chronic hepatic impairment)
Patients [AND] with clinically significant laboratory abnormalities / [OR] disorders other than prostate cancer
Patient [AND] with Hepatitis B Virus (HBV), [OR] Hepatitis C Virus (HCV) [AND] and Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) infections
