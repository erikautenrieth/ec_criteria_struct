Inclusion Criteria:
37 weeks gestation [OR] or greater
Living, [OR] singleton fetus
No major fetal malformations
Cephalic presentation
No prior uterine scar
Intact fetal membranes
Qualifies for prostaglandin administration according to current Parkland protocol
Have a cervical dilation of 2 centimeters [OR] or less, [OR] measured at the level of the internal os
Have an indication for induction [OR] or attempted induction of labor according to Parkland protocol

Exclusion Criteria:
latex allergy
non-reassuring fetal status
HIV
active herpes outbreak
Prior uterine scar
Contraindication to prostaglandins according to current Parkland protocol
Contraindication to vaginal delivery
