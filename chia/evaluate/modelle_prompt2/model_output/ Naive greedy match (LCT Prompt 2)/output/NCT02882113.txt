Inclusion Criteria:
19 years old [AND] and above.
Patients [AND] who previously have received a liver transplant over the last six months [AND] and within last three years.
Patients [AND] who are on Tacrolimus immunosuppressive therapy twice a day for at least two weeks.
Patients [AND] who have normal liver function [AND] and renal function.
Patients [AND] who have been monitored [NOT] without complication such as acute rejection.
Patients willing to sign his/ [OR] her consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] who have Tacrolimus trough level resulted as 2 ng/ [OR] mg at the baseline.
Patients [AND] who are on steroid therapy due to positive result of acute rejection test before the baseline.
Patients [AND] who have received a transplant besides liver.
Patients [AND] who are allergic to IP [OR] or macrolide compounds.
Patients [AND] who are on cyclosporine, [OR] bosentan, [OR] or potassium sparing diuretic.
Patients [AND] with genetic diseases such as galactose intolerance, [OR] Lapp lactase deficiency, [OR] or glucose-galactose malabsorption.
Pregnant [OR] or lactating women.
Patients [NOT] not willing to adhere to study procedures/ [OR] treatments.
