Inclusion Criteria:
patients aged >18, [OR] <75, [OR] left ventricle ejection fraction (LVEF) >50%, [OR] multivessel coronary disease detected by coronarography, [OR] indication to receive a CABG, [OR] stable CAD. All diabetics [AND] and non diabetics.

Exclusion Criteria:
acute myocardial infarction, [OR] heart failure, [OR] neoplastic disease, [OR] chronic diseases [AND] that may affect the inflammatory profile both systemic [AND] and epicardial (cancer, [OR] chronic intestinal inflammation, [OR] hepatitis, [OR] AIDS); life expectancy < 6 months, [OR] previous CABG [AND] and/ [OR] or other open heart surgery intervention, [OR] acute coronary syndrome
