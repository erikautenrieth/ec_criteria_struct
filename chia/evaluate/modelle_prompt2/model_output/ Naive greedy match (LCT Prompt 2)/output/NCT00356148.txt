Inclusion Criteria:
Women at any age [AND] with early stage breast cancer (stage I-II) [AND] and American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) score of I-II. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS; stage 0 cancer), [OR] Advanced [OR] or distant metastatic stage, [OR] Receiving any neoadjuvant therapy, [OR] History of receiving any antibiotics within prior 3 months, [OR] History of immunodeficiency, [OR] Having a remote infection, [OR] History of reaction to study antibiotics, [OR] Denial of signing the consent form.
