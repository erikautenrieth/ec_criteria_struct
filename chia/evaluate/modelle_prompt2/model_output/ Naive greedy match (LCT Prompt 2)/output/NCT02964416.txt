Inclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with craniotomy for supratentorial tumors under general anesthesia
American Society of Anaesthesiologists (ASA) 2 [AND] and stable ASA 3 patients
Elective surgery
Patients [AND] with Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS) 15/ [OR] 15

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with a history of allergy [OR] or hypersensitivity to tramadol.
History of epilepsy [OR] or convulsions due to any reason.
Chronic usage of analgesic drugs.
Patients using monoamine oxidase inhibitors.
Patients [AND] with clinical signs of raised ICP.
Obesity (women [AND] with a body mass index >35 kg/ [OR] m2 [OR] or men [AND] with a body mass index >42 kg/ [OR] m2)
Language barrier.
Patients taking B-blockers [OR] or Ca channel blockers.
Patients above 65 years of age ( Physiology difference)
