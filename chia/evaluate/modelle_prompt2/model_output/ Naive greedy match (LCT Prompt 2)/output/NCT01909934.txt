Inclusion Criteria:
Male [OR] or female patients age 18 years [OR] or older, [AND] with relapsed [OR] or refractory sALCL [AND] who have previously received at least 1 multiagent chemotherapy
Bidimensional measurable disease
An Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 [OR] or 1
Female patients [AND] who are postmenopausal for at least 1 year before the screening visit, [OR] surgically sterile, [OR] or agree to practice 2 effective methods of contraception, [OR] at the same time, [OR] from the time of signing the informed consent form through 30 days after the last dose of study drug, [OR] or agree to practice true abstinence
Male patients [AND] who agree to practice effective barrier contraception during the entire study treatment period through 6 months after the last dose of study drug [OR] or agree to practice true abstinence
Clinical laboratory values as specified in the study protocol

Exclusion Criteria:
Previous treatment [AND] with brentuximab vedotin.
Previously received an allogeneic transplant.
Patients [AND] with current diagnosis of primary cutaneous ALCL (patients whose ALCL has transformed to sALCL are eligible).
Known cerebral/ [OR] meningeal disease including signs [OR] or symptoms of progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy (PML)
Female patients [AND] who are lactating [AND] and breastfeeding [OR] or pregnant
Known human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) positive
Known hepatitis B surface antigen-positive, [OR] or known [OR] or suspected active hepatitis C infection
