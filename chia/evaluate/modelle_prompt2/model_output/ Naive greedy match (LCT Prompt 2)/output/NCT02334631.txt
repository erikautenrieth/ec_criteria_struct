Inclusion Criteria:
Patients undergoing small bowel video capsule endoscopy

Exclusion Criteria:
Patients [AND] with a contraindication to VCE (small bowel strictures, [OR] oropharyngeal dysphagia, [OR] pregnancy, [OR] patients [AND] who are [NOT] not surgical candidates)
Endoscopic insertion of video capsule endoscope
Inpatient procedures for active GI bleeding
Patients [AND] with fluid restriction [OR] or [AND] who are [NOT] unable to drink up to 900 ml of fluid within 10 minutes prior to the VCE
