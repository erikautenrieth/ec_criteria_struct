Inclusion Criteria:
HIV-1 infected males [OR] or females 
over 18 years of age 
signed informed consent 
currently receiving a stable antiretroviral regimen comprising of: 
two [OR] or more licensed NRTIs 
one licensed NNRTI [OR] or boosted protease inhibitor 
[NOT] no previous protease inhibitor resistance documented on HIV-1 genotypic resistance testing 
failure of current antiretroviral regimen due to: 
toxicity, [OR] intolerance [OR] or virological failure if receiving an NNRTI containing regimen at screening 
toxicity [OR] or intolerance if receiving a boosted-protease inhibitor regimen at screening ([AND] with plasma HIV RNA < 400 copies/ [OR] mL at screening) 
willing to modify antiretroviral therapy, [OR] in accordance [AND] with the randomisation assignment 
[NOT] no previous exposure to etravirine 
subjects in good health upon medical history, [OR] physical exam, [AND] and laboratory testing in the opinion of the investigator 
have [NOT] no serologic evidence of active HBV infection evidenced by negative hepatitis B surface antigen 
female subjects [AND] who are heterosexually active [AND] and of childbearing potential (i.e., [NOT] not surgically sterile [OR] or at least two years post menopausal) must practice contraception as follows from screening through completion of the study: 
barrier contraceptives (condom, [OR] diaphragm [AND] with spermicide) 
IUD [OR] or Depo PLUS a barrier contraceptive 
female subjects of childbearing potential must have a negative pregnancy test. 

Exclusion Criteria:
current alcohol abuse [OR] or drug dependence 
pregnancy 
active opportunistic infection [OR] or significant co-morbidities 
current prohibited concomitant medication 
a likelihood of diminished response to any of the study treatment arms, [OR] in the opinion of the investigator, [OR] based on HIV genotypic resistance testing
