Inclusion Criteria:
Overweight [AND] and obese PCOS patients [AND] with newly diagnosed IGR;
PCOS diagnosis based on 2003 Rotterdam criteria
Overweight / [OR] obesity diagnostic criteria according to WHO-WPR
Impaired glucose regulation diagnostic criteria according to 1998 WHO diagnostic criteria.

Exclusion Criteria:
Except for serious complications (cardiovascular events [AND] and recent significant liver, [OR] kidney [OR] or lung disease within 3 months)
high blood pressure (>160/ [OR] 100mmHg)
active infection
secondary diabetes
pregnancy
alcohol abuse
allergic to GLP-1 receptor agonist
