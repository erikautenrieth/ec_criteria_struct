Inclusion Criteria:
Participants must have completed 3 cycles of a bortezomib-based induction regimen (as defined by current NCCN guidelines) [AND] and have [NOT] no evidence of disease progression as defined by IMWG criteria.
Participants [AND] with light chain [AND] and [NOT] free light chain (FLC) only may be enrolled if they meet all the criteria for a diagnosis of MM.
Participants must be considered by their physician eligible to receiving the IRD regimen.
Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status [AND] and/ [OR] or other performance status 0, [OR] 1, [OR] or 2 at time of enrollment.

Exclusion Criteria:
Failure to have fully recovered ([AND] that is, [OR] less than [OR] or equal to [<=] Grade 1 toxicity) from the reversible effects of prior chemotherapy.
Major surgery within 14 days before enrollment.
Radiotherapy within 14 days before enrollment (if the involved field is small, [OR] 7 days will be considered a sufficient interval between treatment [AND] and administration of the ixazomib.)
Central nervous system involvement.
Infection requiring systemic antibiotic therapy [OR] or other serious infection within 14 days before study enrollment.
Evidence of current uncontrolled cardiovascular conditions, [OR] including uncontrolled hypertension, [OR] uncontrolled cardiac arrhythmias, [OR] symptomatic congestive heart failure, [OR] unstable angina, [OR] or myocardial infarction within the past 6 months.
Systemic treatment, [OR] within 14 days before the first dose of ixazomib, [AND] with strong cytochrome P450 3A (CYP3A) inducers (rifampin, [OR] rifapentine, [OR] rifabutin, [OR] carbamazepine, [OR] phenytoin, [OR] phenobarbital), [OR] or use of Ginkgo biloba [OR] or St. John's wort.
Ongoing [OR] or active systemic infection, [OR] active hepatitis B [OR] or C virus infection, [OR] or known human immunodeficiency virus positive.
Diagnosed [OR] or treated for another malignancy within 2 years before study enrollment [OR] or previously diagnosed [AND] with another malignancy [AND] and have any evidence of residual disease. Participants [AND] with non-melanoma skin cancer [OR] or carcinoma in situ of any type are [NOT] not [NOT] excluded if they have undergone complete resection.
Has greater than [OR] or equal to (>=) Grade 2 peripheral neuropathy, [OR] or Grade 1 [AND] with pain on clinical examination during the screening period.
PD on first-line therapy.
Participation in other interventional clinical trials, [OR] including those [AND] with other investigational agents [NOT] not included in this trial, [OR] within 30 days of the start of this trial [AND] and throughout the duration of this trial. Non-interventional trials ([AND] that is, [OR] observational trials) are permitted at any time point.
