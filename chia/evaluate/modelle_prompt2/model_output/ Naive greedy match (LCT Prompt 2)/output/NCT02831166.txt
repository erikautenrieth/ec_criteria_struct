Inclusion Criteria:
ST-segment elevation acute myocardial infarction patients during the first 12 hours of sympton onset;
Intention to perform primary percutaneous coronary intervention;
Signed informed consent;
Patient eligible for transradial [AND] and transfemoral primary percutaneous coronary intervention, [OR] being pre-requisites: (a) familiarity of the operator [AND] with the radial [AND] and femoral techniques using vascular closure devices, [OR] (b) agreement of the operator to use the access route determined by the randomization process.

Exclusion Criteria:
Less than 18 years of age;
Pregnancy;
Chronic use of vitamin K antagonists [OR] or direct thrombin inhibitors, [OR] or oral Xa-factor antagonists;
Hypersensitivity to antiplatelet [AND] and/ [OR] or anticoagulant drugs;
Active bleeding [OR] or high bleeding risk (severe liver failure, [OR] active peptic ulcer, [OR] creatinine clearance < 30 mL/ [OR] min, [OR] platelets count < 100.000 mm3);
Uncontrolled systemic hypertension;
Cardiogenic shock;
Previous myocardial revascularization surgery [AND] with = 1 internal mammary [OR] or radial artery graft;
Documented chronic peripheral arterial disease preventing the use of the femoral technique;
Severe concomitant disease [AND] with life expectancy below 12 months;
Participation in drug [OR] or devices investigative clinical trials in the last 30 days;
Medical, [OR] geographic [OR] or social conditions impairing the participation in the study [OR] or inability to understand [AND] and sign the informed consent term.
