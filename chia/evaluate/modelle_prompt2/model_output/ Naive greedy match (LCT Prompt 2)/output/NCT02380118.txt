Inclusion Criteria:
Accident & Emergency Department patients, [OR] requiring parenteral drug sedation (as determined by an emergency clinician) will be enrolled.

Exclusion Criteria:
known hypersensitivity [OR] or contraindication to the study drugs
reversible aetiology for agitation (e.g. hypotension, [OR] hypoxia, [OR] hypoglycaemia)
known pregnancy
acute alcohol withdrawal
patients aged>75 years.
