Inclusion Criteria:
18 - 64 years old
Able to give consent
unprotected sex (in past 6 months) [AND] with 1 [OR] or more men of unknown HIV status
evaluated for an STI within 6 months prior to screening
sex in last 6 months [AND] with an HIV-infected partner
IDU [AND] with report of using previously used [OR] or shared needles in past 6 months [OR] or has been in a methadone, [OR] buprenorphine, [OR] or suboxone treatment program in past 6 months [OR] or engaging in high-risk sexual behaviors
individuals engaging in transactional sex (i.e sex for money, [OR] drugs, [OR] or housing)
Infrequently uses condoms during sex [AND] with 1 [OR] or more partners of unknown HIV status [AND] who are known to be at substantial risk of HIV infection (IDU [OR] or bisexual male partner)
CrCl = 60 ml/ [OR] min
HIV- uninfected women desiring PrEP

Exclusion Criteria:
Active alcohol [OR] or drug use [OR] or dependence which may interfere [AND] with adherence to study requirements
HIV-infected at screening [OR] or enrollment
Estimated CrCl < 60 mL/ [OR] min
Past participation in an HIV vaccine study
Positive Hepatitis B surface antigen test
Underlying medical condition [AND] with survival unlikely during follow-up period
Any condition [AND] that in the opinion of study staff would make participation in the study unsafe [OR] or interfere [AND] with achieving study objectives
Pregnant [OR] or breast feeding
Actively trying to achieve pregnancy
