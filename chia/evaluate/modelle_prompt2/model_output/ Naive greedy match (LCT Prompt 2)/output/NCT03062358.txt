Inclusion Criteria:
Has a HCC diagnosis confirmed by radiology, [OR] histology, [OR] or cytology (fibrolamellar, [AND] and mixed hepatocellular/ [OR] cholangiocarcinoma subtypes are [NOT] not eligible) 
Has Barcelona Clinic Liver Cancer (BCLC) Stage C disease [OR] or BCLC Stage B disease [NOT] not amenable to locoregional therapy [OR] or refractory to locoregional therapy [AND] and [NOT] not amenable to a curative treatment approach 
Has a Child-Pugh A liver score within 7 days prior to first dose of study medication 
Has a life expectancy of >3 months 
Has at least one measurable lesion based on RECIST version 1.1 as determined by investigator 
Has Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 [OR] or 1 performed within 7 days prior to receiving the first dose of study medication 
Has documented objective radiographic progression during [OR] or after treatment [AND] with sorafenib [OR] or oxaliplatin-based chemotherapy, [OR] or else intolerance to sorafenib [OR] or oxaliplatin-based chemotherapy 
Female participants of childbearing potential must have a negative urine [OR] or serum pregnancy test within 72 hours prior to receiving the first dose of study therapy 
Female [AND] and male participants of reproductive potential must agree to use adequate contraception starting from the first dose of study medication, [OR] throughout the study period, [AND] and for up to 120 days after the last dose of study medication 

Exclusion Criteria:
Is currently participating [OR] or has participated in a study [AND] with an investigational agent [OR] or using an investigational device within 4 weeks of the first dose of study medication 
Has received sorafenib [OR] or oxaliplatin-based chemotherapy within 14 days of first dose of study medication 
Has had esophageal [OR] or gastric variceal bleeding within the last 6 months 
Has clinically apparent ascites on physical examination 
Has portal vein invasion at the main portal branch (Vp4), [OR] inferior vena cava, [OR] or cardiac involvement of HCC based on imaging 
Has had clinically diagnosed hepatic encephalopathy in the last 6 months 
Has had a solid organ [OR] or hematologic transplant 
Has had prior systemic therapy for HCC in the advanced (incurable) setting other than sorafenib [OR] or oxaliplatin-based chemotherapy, [OR] prior to start of study medication 
Has an active autoimmune disease [AND] that has required systemic treatment in the past 2 years. Replacement therapy is [NOT] not considered a form of systemic treatment. 
Has a diagnosis of immunodeficiency [OR] or is receiving systemic steroid therapy [OR] or any other form of immunosuppressive therapy within 7 days prior to the first dose of study medication 
Has received locoregional therapy to liver (transcatheter chemoembolization [TACE], [OR] transcatheter embolization [TAE], [OR] hepatic arterial infusion [HAI], [OR] radiation, [OR] radioembolization, [OR] or ablation) [OR] or other site within 4 weeks prior to the first dose of study medication 
Has had major surgery to liver [OR] or other site within 4 weeks prior to the first dose of study medication 
Has had a minor surgery ≤7 days prior to the first dose of study medication 
Has [NOT] not recovered adequately (i.e., [OR] Grade ≤1 [OR] or baseline) from the toxicity [AND] and/ [OR] or complications from any intervention prior to study start 
Has a diagnosed additional malignancy within 3 years prior to first dose of study medication [AND] with the exception of curatively treated basal cell carcinoma of the skin, [OR] squamous cell carcinoma of the skin [AND] and/ [OR] or curatively resected in situ cancers 
Has a known history of, [OR] or any evidence of, [OR] central nervous system (CNS) metastases [AND] and/ [OR] or carcinomatous meningitis 
Has a history of (non-infectious) pneumonitis [AND] that required steroids [OR] or current pneumonitis 
Has an active infection requiring systemic therapy 
Is pregnant [OR] or breast feeding [OR] or expecting to conceive [OR] or father starting from the first dose of study medication, [OR] throughout the study period, [AND] and for up to 120 days after the last dose of study medication 
Has received prior immunotherapy [AND] with an anti-Programmed Cell Death Receptor 1 (PD-1), [OR] Programmed Cell Death Receptor Ligand 1 (anti-PD-L1), [OR] or anti- Programmed Cell Death Receptor Ligand 2 (PD-L2) [OR] or has previously participated in clinical studies [AND] with pembrolizumab 
Has a known history of human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) 
Has untreated active Hepatitis B 
Has hepatitis C in which participants received therapy for HCV <4 weeks prior to receiving pembrolizumab 
Has received a live vaccine within 30 days prior to the first dose of study therapy
