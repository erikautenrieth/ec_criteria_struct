Inclusion Criteria:
Blood culture-proven typhoid fever (S. typhi [OR] or S. paratyphi) 
Signed informed consent to participate in the study. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Allergy to ceftriaxone [OR] or macrolides 
Major typhoid fever-associated complications 
[NOT] Inability to swallow oral medication 
Underlying illness 
Pregnancy 
Lactation 
Treatment within the past 4 days [AND] with an antibiotic [AND] that may be effective against typhoid fever
