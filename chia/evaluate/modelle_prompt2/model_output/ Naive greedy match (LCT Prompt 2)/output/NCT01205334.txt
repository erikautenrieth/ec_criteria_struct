Inclusion Criteria:
Histopathological verification of glioblastoma multiforme (GBM: WHO grade IV) in remission (Group A) [OR] or [AND] with active disease (Group B). 
CMV-positive GBM 
CMV seropositive 
Life expectancy 6 weeks [OR] or greater 
Karnofsky/ [OR] Lansky score 50 [OR] or greater 
Patient [OR] or parent/ [OR] guardian capable of providing informed consent 
Bilirubin less than 1.5x upper limit of normal, [OR] AST less than 3x upper limit of normal, [OR] serum creatinine less than 1.5x normal [AND] and Hgb 8.0 g/ [OR] dL [OR] or greater 
Pulse oximetry of 90% [OR] or greater on room air 
Sexually active patients must be willing to utilize one of the more effective birth control methods for 6 months after the CTL infusion. The male partner should use a condom. 
Patients should have been [NOT] off other investigational antineoplastic therapy for one month prior to entry in this study. 
Informed consent explained to, [OR] understood by [AND] and signed by patient/ [OR] guardian. Patient/ [OR] guardian given copy of informed consent. 

Exclusion Criteria:
Severe intercurrent infection 
Known HIV positivity 
Pregnant [OR] or lactating 
History of hypersensitivity reactions to murine protein-containing products.
