Here is the eligibility criteria with the logical operators [AND], [OR], and [NOT] inserted:

Eligible patients must meet the following criteria to be enrolled in the study: 

1. Newly diagnosed, stage IV squamous cell lung cancer [AND] this includes patients who present with disseminated metastases [OR] or those with a malignant pleural [OR] or pericardial effusion (i.e., formerly stage IIIB in the 6th TNM staging system). 
2. Patients who have received prior adjuvant therapy for early-stage lung cancer are eligible [AND] if at least 12 months have elapsed from that treatment [NOT]. 
3. Histologically confirmed squamous cell bronchogenic carcinoma [AND] patients whose tumors contain mixed non-small cell histologies are eligible [AND] as long as squamous carcinoma is the predominant histology [NOT]. Mixed tumors with small cell anaplastic elements are not eligible [NOT]. Cytologic specimens obtained by brushings [OR] washings [OR] or needle aspiration of the defined lesion are acceptable. 
4. Patients with previous radiotherapy as definitive therapy for locally advanced non-small cell lung cancer are eligible [AND] as long as the recurrence is outside the original radiation therapy port [NOT]. Radiation therapy must have been completed >4 weeks prior to the initiation of study treatment [NOT]. Patients who have received chemo/radiation for locally advanced NSCLC are not eligible [NOT]. Patients who have received palliative radiation therapy for symptomatic metastases must have completed treatment >14 days prior the initiation of the study treatment [NOT]. 
5. Presence of evaluable (measureable [OR] or non-measurable) disease. 
6. ECOG Performance Status of 0 [OR] 1. 
7. Laboratory values as follows: 
Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) >1,500/microL [AND] platelets >100,000/microL (≤72 hours prior to initial treatment) [NOT]. 
Hemoglobin >9 g/dL [NOT]. (Note: Patients may be transfused [OR] receive erythropoietin to maintain or exceed this level). 
Bilirubin < ULN [NOT]. 
Alanine aminotransferase (ALT) and aspartate aminotransferase (AST) ≤2.5 times the upper limit of normal if no liver involvement [OR] ≤5 times the upper limit of normal with liver involvement [NOT]. 
Creatinine <2.0 mg/dL [OR] creatinine clearance >40 mL/min (as calculated by the Cockcroft-Gault method [NOT]. 
8. Women of childbearing potential must have a negative serum pregnancy test performed within 7 days prior to start of treatment [NOT]. Women of childbearing potential [OR] men with partners of childbearing potential must use effective birth control measures during treatment [AND] at least 6 months after the last dose of the study treatment [NOT]. If a woman becomes pregnant [OR] suspects she is pregnant while participating in this study, she must agree to inform her treating physician immediately [NOT]. Sexually active men must agree to use a medically acceptable form of birth control during treatment [AND] at least 6 months after the last dose [NOT]. If a female partner becomes pregnant during the course of the study the treating physician should be informed immediately [NOT]. 
9. >18 years of age [NOT]. 
10. Ability to understand the nature of this study [AND] give written informed consent [AND] comply with study requirements [NOT]. 
11. Patients entering this study must be willing to provide tissue from a previous tumor biopsy (if available) for correlative testing [NOT]. An exception to this is when the national/local regulations prohibits some of the key activities of this research like the export of samples to third countries [OR] storage of coded samples [OR] global gene expression profiling without a pre-specified list of target genes [NOT]. If tissue is not available [NOT], a patient will still be eligible for enrollment into the study [NOT].
