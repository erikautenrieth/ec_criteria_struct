Type of subject: outpatient [AND]. 
Informed consent: Subjects must give their signed and dated written informed consent to participate [AND]. 
Gender: Male [OR] or female [AND]. Female subjects must be post-menopausal [OR] or using a highly effective method for avoidance of pregnancy [AND]. The decision to include or exclude women of childbearing potential may be made at the discretion of the investigator in accordance with local practice in relation to adequate contraception [NOT]. 
Age: >=40 [AND] <=80 years of age at Screening (Visit 1) [AND]. 
Tobacco use: Subjects with a current or prior history of >=10 pack-years of cigarette smoking at screening (Visit 1) [AND]. Previous smokers are defined as those who have stopped smoking for at least 6 months prior to Visit 1 [AND]. 
Airflow Obstruction: 
Subjects with a measured post-albuterol/salbutamol forced expiratory volume in 1 second (FEV1)/(forced vital capacity)FVC ratio of <=0.70 [OR] or >=50 and <=70% of predicted normal values calculated using NHANES III reference equations [Hankinson, 1999; Hankinson, 2010] at Screening (Visit 1) [AND]. 
Post-bronchodilator spirometry will be performed approximately 15 minutes after the subject has self-administered 4 inhalations (i.e., total 400mcg) of albuterol/salbutamol via a metered dose inhaler (MDI) with a valved-holding chamber [AND]. The FEV1/FVC ratio and FEV1 percent predicted values will be calculated [AND]. 
Symptoms of COPD: Subjects must score 2 [OR] higher on the modified Medical Research Council Dyspnea scale (Visit 1) [AND]. 
Cardiovascular disease: 
For patients >= 40 years of age: any one of the following: 
Established (i.e. by clinical signs or imaging studies) coronary artery disease (CAD) [OR] Established (i.e. by clinical signs or imaging studies) peripheral vascular disease (PVD) [OR] Previous stroke [OR] Previous MI [OR] Diabetes mellitus with target organ disease [OR] [AND]. 
For patients >=60 years of age: any 2 of the following: 
Being treated for hypercholesterolemia [OR] Being treated for hypertension [OR] Being treated for diabetes mellitus [OR] Being treated for peripheral vascular disease [OR] [AND].
