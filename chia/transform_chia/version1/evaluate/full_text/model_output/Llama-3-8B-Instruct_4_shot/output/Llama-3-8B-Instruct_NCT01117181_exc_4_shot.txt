Meets criteria for Major Depressive Episode [AND] by Diagnostic Statistical Manual of Mental Disorder - IV (TR) criteria. 
Clinically significant agitation /aggression [AND] for which either 1) the frequency of agitation /aggression as assessed by the NPI is 'Very frequently' [OR] 2) the frequency of agitation /aggression as assessed by the NPI is 'Frequently' [AND] the severity of the agitation as assessed by the NPI is 'Moderate' [OR] 'Marked'. 
Clinically significant delusions [AND] for which either 1) the frequency of delusions as assessed by the NPI is 'Very frequently' [OR] 2) the frequency of delusions as assessed by the NPI is 'Frequently' [AND] the severity of the delusions as assessed by the NPI is 'Moderate' [OR] 'Marked'. 
Clinically significant hallucinations [AND] for which either 1) the frequency of hallucinations as assessed by the NPI is 'Very frequently' [OR] 2) the frequency of hallucinations as assessed by the NPI is 'Frequently' [AND] the severity of the hallucinations as assessed by the NPI is 'Moderate' [OR] 'Marked'. 
Treatment with psychotropic medications in the 2 weeks prior to randomization [NOT] with the exception of approved treatments for dementia (ChEIs and memantine) [OR] selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor antidepressants [OR] trazodone (if used as an aid to facilitate sleep and not as an antidepressant); other psychotropics (with the exclusion of antipsychotics) [NOT] if stable for 3 months, may be allowed only with Steering Committee approval on a case by case basis. Note that antipsychotics are [NOT] allowed. 
Treatment with methylphenidate is [NOT] contraindicated in the opinion of the study physician. 
Failure of treatment with methylphenidate [AND] in the past for apathy after convincing evidence of an adequate trial as judged by study physician. 
Treatment with a medication that would prohibit the safe concurrent use of methylphenidate [AND] such as monoamine oxidase inhibitors [OR] tricyclic antidepressants. 
Need for acute psychiatric hospitalization [OR] is suicidal. 
Uncontrolled hypertension [AND] (medication non-compliance [OR] past 3 months with a diastolic reading of 105 as verified by compartment pressure of the rectus sheath (CPRS)). 
Symptomatic coronary artery disease deemed to be significant by study physician at the time of screening. 
Lack of appetite that results in significant unintentional weight loss [AND] as determined by the study physician in the last three months. 
Significant communicative impairments. 
Current participation in a clinical trial [OR] in any study that may add significant burden [OR] affect study outcomes. 
Hyperthyroidism [OR] advanced arteriosclerosis [OR] symptomatic cardiovascular disease [OR] serious structural cardiac abnormalities [OR] cardiomyopathy [OR] serious heart rhythm abnormalities [OR] or a family history of sudden death [OR] death related to heart problems. 
Glaucoma [OR] pheochromocytoma [OR] known or suspected hypersensitivity to methylphenidate [OR] its excipients. 
Central Nervous System (CNS) abnormalities [AND] (e.g., cerebral aneurysm) [AND] or other vascular abnormalities such as vasculitis [OR] pre-existing stroke, motor tics [OR] a family history or diagnosis of Tourette's syndrome, seizures (convulsions, epilepsy) [OR] or abnormal EEGs. 
Any condition that, in the opinion of the study physician, makes it medically inappropriate [OR] risky for the patient to enroll in the trial.
