Inclusion Criteria:
Midsubstance pain in the achilles tendon
Symptoms for at least 3 months
Ultrasound scanning [AND] at the first visit shows thickness of the achilles tendon above 7 mm [OR] or 20% thicker than the contralateral.
Patient can read and understand danish
Exclusion Criteria:
Earlier operations in the foot and leg, that is judged to complicate training
known arthritis.
known diabetes
Leg ulcerations [OR] or infections in the foot.
Judged unable to comply with the training protocol.
Daily use of pain killers
Glucocorticosteroid  injection [AND] to the diseased achilles tendon [AND] within the last 6 months.
Earlier allergic reactions to glucocorticosteroid [OR] or local anesthetic.
Pregnancy [OR] or planning to become pregnant
BMI above 30.
