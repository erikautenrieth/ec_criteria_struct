Inclusion Criteria:
Age : 18-65
Patients with major depressive disorder [AND] according to DSM-IV criteria that have lasted >8 weeks
MADRS total score of 18 or higher
Patients who responded inadequately  (a score of >18 on the MADRS) [AND] to first-line antidepressant treatment of 4 week duration
Current use of standard antidepressant  treatment in monotherapy [OR] or combination of 2 antidepressants : escitalopram (10 - 20mg/d), fluoxetine(20 - 40mg/d), paroxetine CR(25 - 50mg/d), sertraline(100 - 150mg/d), mirtazapine (15 - 45mg/d), duloxetine (30 - 60mg/d) or venlafaxine ER(150-225mg/d)
Exclusion Criteria:
Past history of hypersensitivity [AND] to aripiprazole
Primary diagnosis of MDD  with psychotic feature [OR], bipolar disorder [OR], schizophrenia [OR], schizoaffective disorder [OR], other psychotic disorder [OR] or anxiety disorder [OR], a history of alcohol [OR]/ drug abuse  within the past 12 months [OR], or a diagnosis of dementia
Clinically significant current Axis II (DSM-IV-TR) diagnosis
A significant risk of suicide [AND] corroborated by a score of =5 on item 10(suicidal thoughts) on the MADRS scale or by clinical judgment of the investigator
Pregnancy or in breast-feeding
Presence of a serious medical illness including cardiac, hepatic, renal, respiratory, endocrinologic, neurologic, [OR] or hematologic disease or physical disorder judged to significantly affect central nervous system function
Patients taking antipsychotics [OR], mood stabilizer [OR] or any psychotropic medications [NOT] besides antidepressants, [NOT] except benzodiazepines [OR] or beta blockers [OR] or hypnotics
Patients with past treatment failures of aripiprazole
