Inclusion Criteria:
Adults (= 18 years of age) with World Health Organization Group 2 Pulmonary Hypertension (Mean pulmonary artery pressure = 25 mmHg and pulmonary capillary wedge pressure = 15 mmHg)
New York Heart Association [AND] class II-IV symptoms
Left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) = 45%
Exclusion Criteria:
Other causes of heart failure [AND] [NOT] other than diastolic dysfunction, such as restrictive cardiomyopathy [OR] or infiltrative cardiomyopathy
Women who are pregnant [OR] or nursing
Liver cirrhosis,
Primary valvular disease
Acute coronary syndrome
Causes of PH [AND] [NOT] other than that of heart failure, such as: chronic thromboembolic PH [OR], sickle-cell disease [OR], or sarcoidosis
Severe bradycardia [OR] or greater than 1st degree heart block
Decompensated heart failure
Current use of a third generation beta-blocker  (nebivolol [OR], carvedilol [OR], or labetalol) or high dose of any beta-blockers (greater than 100 mg daily of metoprolol [OR], or equivalent)
