Inclusion Criteria:
A positive history of chronic claudication,
Exercise-limiting claudication established by history and direct observation during a screening walking test administered by the evaluating vascular surgeon,
Arterial occlusive disease [AND] per ankle Brachial index measurements [OR] and/or other imaging modalities,
Stable blood pressure regimen, stable lipid regimen, stable diabetes regimen and risk factor control for 6 weeks.
Exclusion Criteria:
Rest pain [OR] or tissue loss [AND] due to PAD (Fontaine stage III [OR] and IV),
acute lower extremity ischemic event secondary to [OR] thromboembolic disease or acute trauma,
Walking capacity [AND] significantly limited by conditions [NOT] other than claudication including leg [OR] (joint [OR]/musculoskeletal [OR], neurologic) and systemic (heart [OR], lung disease) pathology,
Current use of either ACE inhibitors [OR] or angiotensin II receptor blockers,
Chronic kidney disease [AND] with estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate < 30 ml/min/1.73 m2,
History of bilateral severe renal artery stenosis [OR] and 7) History of angioedema related to previous ACE-inhibitor treatment [OR] or known hypersensitivity to ramipril [OR] or other ACE inhibitors.
