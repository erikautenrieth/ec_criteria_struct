Inclusion Criteria:
healthy parturients with uncomplicated, single gestation pregnancies, full term (38-42 weeks of gestation) pregnancy, agreed to participate
Exclusion Criteria:
a disease [OR] that might affect hepatic [OR] or renal function, contraindications to opioid analgesics [OR], fetal growth retardation [OR], signs of fetal asphyxia [OR] by cardiotocography, meconium stained amniotic fluid [OR] or placental insufficiency. The subjects should [NOT] not have received fentanyl during the previous 14 days.
