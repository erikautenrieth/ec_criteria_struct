Inclusion Criteria:
Signed informed consent form
Macula edema  secondary [AND] to BRVO
BCVA of 77 to 20 letters assessed with the use of ETDRS charts
CRT <U+2267>250µm
Exclusion Criteria:
Previous treatment with anti-VEGF drugs [OR] or corticosteroid [OR] or grid laser photocoagulation (study eye)
History of vitrectomy surgery [OR], submacular surgery [OR], or other surgical intervention [AND] for RVO
Ocular disorders in the study eye that may confound interpretation of study results
BCVA over 77 letters between screening and Day 0
The pregnant or lactating woman
