Inclusion Criteria:
Male [OR] or Female.
No age restriction.
Diagnosed with an infection related stone.
Medically fit for definitive surgical management [AND] of stone.
Life expectancy greater than one year.
[NOT] Stone free after definitive surgical therapy defined as fragments less than 3mm.
Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with medical comorbidities [AND] preventing them from definitive surgical therapy.
Patients with persistent stone burden [AND] following definitive surgical therapy.
