Inclusion Criteria:
Children receiving amoxicilline-clavulanic acid (50-90 mg/kg/day, twice daily) due to acute otitis media [OR] or acute sinusitis
Exclusion Criteria:
Receiving antibiotic [OR] and/or probiotic, 8 weeks before the study
Chronic gastrointestinal system disorders
Congenital anomalies
Chronic diseases
Chemotherapy [OR] and radiotherapy
Pregnancy
