Inclusion Criteria:
adults capable of providing consent
have a diagnosis of locally advanced [OR] or metastatic melanoma
Exclusion Criteria:
are allergic [AND] to influenza vaccination
have received influenza vaccination within the past 6 months
require prednisone [OR], methotrexate [OR], or other immunosuppressing medications
have HIV infection
have a history of solid organ [OR] or bone marrow transplant
require combination immunotherapy
are on other studies requiring blood draws that might exceed 450 mL total during the period of the influenza vaccine study
