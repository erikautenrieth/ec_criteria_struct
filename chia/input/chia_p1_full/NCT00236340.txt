Inclusion Criteria:
Pregnant women with abdomen discumfort [AND] and ultrasound  diagnosis of polyhydramnios (AFI>25cm)
Single [OR] or twin [AND] pregnancies
Exclusion Criteria:
Multiple pregnancy (more than 3 fetuses)
Maternal history of [AND] placental abruptio
Fetus [AND] with IUGR
Pregnancy complicated with pre-eclampsia
[NOT] Unability to give informed consent
