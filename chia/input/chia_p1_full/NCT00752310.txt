Inclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Non-smoking [OR], or smoking no more than 10 cigarettes [OR], or 2 cigars [OR], or 2 pipes per day for at least 3 months prior to selection
Normal weight  as defined by a Body Mass Index (BMI [OR], weight in kg divided by the square of height in meters) of 18.0 to 30.0 kg/m2, extremes included
Able to comply with protocol requirements. Healthy [AND] on the basis of a medical evaluation [AND] that reveals the [NOT] absence of any clinically relevant abnormality [AND] and includes a physical examination, medical history, electrocardiogram (ECG), vital signs, and the results of blood biochemistry, blood coagulation, and hematology tests and a urinalysis carried out at screening.
Exclusion Criteria:
No positive HIV 1 [OR] or HIV 2 test at screening
no history of significant skin disease such as, but not limited to rash [OR] or eruptions [OR], drug allergies [OR], food allergy [OR], dermatitis [OR], eczema [OR], psoriasis [OR], or urticaria
no history of allergy to drugs such as, but not limited to, sulphonamides [OR] and penicillins
no previously demonstrated clinically significant allergy [OR] or hypersensitivity [AND] to any of the excipients of the investigational medication administered in this trial
no female subject of childbearing potential [NOT] without use of effective nonhormonal birth control [OR] methods, or [NOT] not willing to continue practicing these birth control methods for at least 30 days after the end of the treatment period
