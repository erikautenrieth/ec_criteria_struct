Inclusion Criteria:
singleton, term pregnancy
currently on buprenorphine maintenance therapy
scheduled for elective CD [AND] under spinal anesthesia
Exclusion Criteria:
use illicit drugs [OR] or relapse during the last trimester of pregnancy
positive drug screen at the time of delivery
allergies [AND] to any medications used in the study
taking prescribed gabapentin at the time of admission [AND] for CD
contraindications to neuraxial anesthesia [OR] or require general anesthesia [AND] for CD
designated ASA physical status 4 or above
