Inclusion Criteria:
patients who underwent successful TAVI
with any approved TAVI device
via transfemoral access
with use of any of the approved vascular closure devices
provided written informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
no consent
periprocedural complications requiring continuation of heparin [OR] or administration of protamine sulfate
alergy  to fish [OR], protamine [OR], protamine derivates [OR], history of Humulin N [OR], Novolin N [OR], Novolin NPH [OR], Gensulin N [OR], SciLin N [OR], NPH Iletin II [OR] and isophane insulin intake
