Inclusion Criteria:
Subjects who, in the opinion of the investigator, can and will comply with the requirements of the protocol.
Written informed consent obtained from the subject prior to performing any study specific procedure.
A male [OR] or female between, and including, 18 and 50 years of age at the time of the first study visit.
Healthy subjects as established by medical history [AND]  and clinical examination before entering into the study. Healthy subjects with no medical conditions that, in the opinion of the investigator, prevents the subject from participating in the study.
Subjects must weigh at least 110 pounds (50 kg), but [NOT] not to present obesity (BMI < 32kg/m2).
Female subjects of [NOT] non-childbearing potential may be enrolled in the study. Non-childbearing potential is defined as pre-menarche [OR], current bilateral tubal [OR] ligation or occlusion [OR], hysterectomy [OR], bilateral ovariectomy [OR] or post-menopause.
has practiced adequate contraception for 30 days prior to vaccination, and
has a negative pregnancy test on the day of vaccination and
has agreed to continue adequate contraception during the entire treatment period  and for 1 month [OR], after completion of the vaccination series.
Exclusion Criteria:
Progressive [OR], unstable [OR] or uncontrolled clinical conditions.
Hypersensitivity [OR], including allergy, to any component of vaccines [OR], medicinal products [OR] or medical equipment whose use is foreseen in this study.
Clinical conditions representing a contraindication to intramuscular vaccination [OR] and blood draws.
Clinical conditions.
Systemic administration of corticosteroids (PO [OR]/IV [OR]/IM) within 90 days prior to informed consent.
Administration of antineoplastic [OR] and immunomodulating agents [OR] or radiotherapy within 90 days prior to informed consent.
Received immunoglobulins [OR] or any blood products within 180 days prior to informed consent.
Received an investigational [OR] or non-registered medicinal product within 30 days prior to informed consent.
Any other clinical condition that, in the opinion of the investigator, might pose additional risk to the subject due to participation in the study.
Any history of meningococcal vaccination [OR] or meningococcal [OR] and gonorrhoea diseases.
Enrolment in any activity requiring a blood donation greater than 50 mL during the period starting 30 days before the first study visit [OR] (Day -83, Day -60 or Day -30) or for the duration of the study period.
Administration of long-acting immune-modifying drugs at any time during the study period
Subjects with blood disorders.
Subjects with a history of difficulty in providing blood samples
Any antibiotic intake 7 days prior to blood collection.
Subjects who donated >450 mL of blood within 60 days prior to any blood collection visits.
Subjects who lost >200 mL during a single apheresis [OR] or who lost red blood cells on more than one occasion during apheresis within the previous 60 days.
Concurrently participating in another clinical study, at any time during the study period, in which the subject has been or will be exposed to an investigational [OR] or a non-investigational vaccine [OR]/product
Ongoing anaemia  as indicated by haemoglobin values below the lower limit of the laboratory-specified reference range. If the finger prick method demonstrates an anaemia [OR], no further protocol procedures will be performed, [AND]  and the subject will be referred for appropriate medical management. The subject may participate in this study following therapy and evidence that the anaemia has been resolved.
History of any reaction [OR] or hypersensitivity likely to be exacerbated by any component of the vaccines.
Pregnant [OR] or lactating female.
Female planning to become pregnant [OR] or planning to discontinue contraceptive precautions.
Any confirmed [OR] or suspected immunosuppressive [OR] or immunodeficiency condition based on medical history [OR] and physical examination
Family history of congenital [OR] or hereditary immunodeficiency.
Serious chronic illness.
History of chronic alcohol consumption [OR] and/or drug abuse.
