Inclusion Criteria:
Idiopathic Parkinson's disease ( Hughes AJ et al. 2001)
Patients with motor fluctuations
Chronic Insomnia disorder [AND] criteria according to the criteria of DMS- V ( American Psychiatric Association, 2013) and insomnia severity index > 15
Able to use independently the device [AND] required for treatment by apomorphine
Collection of written informed consent (legal obligation for any project under the public health law , bioethics laws and / or CNIL) .
Affiliate to social security [OR] or beneficiary of such a regime
Exclusion Criteria:
Atypical Parkinsonian Syndromes
Parkinson's disease [AND] with hallucinations
Parkinson's disease [AND] with impulse Control disorder (ICD)
Parkinson's disease  already treated [AND] with APOMORPHINE pump or justifying the use of the pump continuously day and night
Another obvious severe disease [AND] explaining insomnia
Exclusion for monitoring difficulties (mutation, insufficient motivation, priority associated pathology in care)
Patient unwilling to accept a pump
Patient not accepting polysomnography and multiple sleep latency test
Patient with health problems [OR] or a skin disease [AND] [NOT] precluding continuous subcutaneous infusion
Female parturient [OR] or nursing
Cardiac dysrhythmia [NOT] precluding treatment [AND] with domperidone [OR] or apomorphine (increased QTc = 440 ms in men [OR], QTc = 450 ms [AND] in women)
antiemetic neuroleptics
Tetrabenazine
Excessive alcohol consumption
Hypersensitivity to apomorphine [OR] or one of the excipients
Respiratory Depression
Hepatic impairment
Intellectual Disability
Dementia
