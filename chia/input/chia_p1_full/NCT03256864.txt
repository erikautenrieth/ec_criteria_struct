Inclusion Criteria:
Liver Transplant Recipients have received liver transplantations for at least 6+1 months prior to enrollment
Liver Transplant Recipients have [NOT] no acute rejection episodes within 3 months prior to the enrollment and are clinically stable
Liver Transplant Recipients have been treated with twice-daily regimen of tacrolimus(TAC) plus everolimus(EVR) and TAC and EVR trough levels have stayed within targeted ranges for at least 6 weeks prior to enrollment
Provide written informed consent prior to inclusion.
Liver transplant recipients who are 18-65 years of age of a primary liver transplant
Allograft functioning at an acceptable level as defined by the AST, ALT, Total Bilirubin levels =3 times ULN prior to enrollment.
Abbreviated MDRD eGFR = 30 mL/min/1.73m2.
Exclusion Criteria:
Patients who are recipients of multiple solid organ [OR] or islet cell tissue transplants [OR], or have previously received an organ [OR] or tissue transplant [OR]. Patients who have a combined liver-kidney transplant.
History of malignancy of any organ system ([NOT] other than localized basal cell carcinoma of the skin), treated [OR] or untreated, within the past 5 years, regardless of whether there is evidence of local recurrence or metastases.
Existence of any surgical [OR], medical [OR] or mental conditions [AND], [NOT] other than the current transplantation, which, in the opinion of the investigator, might interfere with the objectives of the study.
Pregnant or nursing (lactating) women.
