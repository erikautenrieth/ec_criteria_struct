Inclusion Criteria:
Infection with Plasmodium falciparum [OR] or P. vivax either alone or mixed
Age >12 months
Weight >5kg
Living in the study clusters
Exclusion Criteria:
General danger signs or symptoms of severe malaria
Anaemia, defined [AND] as Hb <9g/dl
G6PD deficiency (as determined by FST)
Pregnant women as determined by Urine ß-HCG pregnancy test
Known hypersensitivity [AND] to any of the drugs given
