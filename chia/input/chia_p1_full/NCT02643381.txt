Inclusion Criteria:
Adult patient (male [OR] or female) requiring emergency endotracheal intubation.
Exclusion Criteria:
Children (<18 years old).
Women who are known to be pregnant.
Any patient who has been previously randomized in the EvK Trial.
Patients who require endotracheal intubation [AND] [NOT] without sedative medication. For example, patients in full cardiac arrest.
Patients with a known allergy to ketamine [OR] or etomidate.
Any individual wearing a MedAlert bracelet indicating that he/she has formally opted out of the EvK Trial.
