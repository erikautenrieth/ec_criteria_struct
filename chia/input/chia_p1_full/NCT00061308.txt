Inclusion Criteria:
Have had one prior platinum-based chemotherapy regimen [AND] for the treatment of primary disease.
At least 4 weeks since last surgery [OR] or radiation therapy.
Must have had a treatment-free interval of greater than 6 months following response to platinum.
ECOG performance status of 0 [OR],1 [OR], or 2.
Exclusion Criteria:
Women of child-bearing potential [AND] that [NOT] do not practice adequate contraception.
Pregnant [OR] or lactating.
Received more than one primary chemotherapy regimen.
Concomitant [OR] or previous malignancies with the [NOT] exception of adequately treated basal cell [OR] or squamous cell skin cancer [OR], in situ cervical cancer [OR], incidental carcinoid [OR], or other cancer from which the patient has been disease free for 5 years.
Active uncontrolled infection [AND] requiring antibiotics.
Concurrent severe medical problems unrelated to the malignancy which would limit full compliance with the study.
Received radiation [AND] to more than 10% of bone.
Prior treatment with topotecan [OR] or gemcitabine.
Hypersensitivity to camptothecin [OR] or nucleoside analogues.
Use of an investigational agent within 30 days.
