Inclusion Criteria:
Female
Age ≥18 years
Patients with a lesion > 0.5 cm  [AND] in largest diameter size, initially scored BI-RADS® 3, 4a, 4b or 4c [AND]  in B-mode ultrasound
Informed consent about histological examination (core cut biopsy (CCB) [OR], vacuum-assisted biopsy (VAB) [OR], fine needle aspiration (FNA) [OR] or surgery) has already been given in the course of clinical routine
Signed informed consent of study participation
Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant [OR] or lactating women
Women with breast implants on the same side as the lesion
Women that underwent local radiation [OR] or chemotherapy within the last 12 months
Women with history of breast cancer [OR] or breast surgery in the same quadrant
Lesions in or close to scar tissue (< 1cm)
Skin lesions [OR] or lesions that have been biopsied previously
Lesion larger [AND] than 4 cm in the longest dimension
No lesion [AND] should be included when more than 50% of the lesion is further down than 4 cm beneath the skin level.
