Inclusion Criteria:
primary total knee replacement surgery
ASA (american society of anesthesiologists) class 1-3
Exclusion Criteria:
severe coronary artery disease [OR], heart failure [OR], kidney failure
insulin-dependent DM (diabetes mellitus), poorly controlled type II DM
gastric [OR]/duodenal ulcer
allergy [OR]/contra-indication [AND] for any drug used in the study
corticosteroid use during last 3 months
preoperative use of opioid drugs ([NOT] excl. codeine [OR], tramadol)
neuropathy [OR]/sensory impairment of lower limbs
lack of co-operation, e.g. inability to use [AND]  a PCA (patient controlled analgesia)-device
