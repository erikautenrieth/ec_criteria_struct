Inclusion Criteria:
Blood culture-proven  [AND] typhoid fever (S. typhi [OR] or S. paratyphi)
Signed informed consent to participate in the study.
Exclusion Criteria:
Allergy to ceftriaxone [OR] or macrolides
Major typhoid  [AND] fever-associated complications
Inability to swallow oral medication
Underlying illness
Pregnancy
Lactation
Treatment within the past 4 days [AND] with an antibiotic [AND] that may be effective against typhoid fever
