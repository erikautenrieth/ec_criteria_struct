Inclusion Criteria:
Good general health
Older than the age of legal consent (i.e. 18 years old)
Sonographic [AND] diagnosis of ovarian endometrioma with diameter at least 4cm on 2 separate scans at least 6 weeks apart
[NOT] No contraindication to use of progesterone [OR] or combined oral contraceptive pills
[NOT] Not attempting to conceive either at the time of study entry [OR] or for at least 2 years after surgery
Willing and able to participate after the study has been explained
Exclusion Criteria:
Operative findings [AND] [NOT] not suggestive of endometriotic cyst
Contraindications to progestogens [OR] or oral contraceptive pills
Unwillingness to tolerate menstrual irregularity
Planning pregnancy within 2 years of study
Cannot understand English, Cantonese or Putonghua
