Inclusion Criteria:
Children 6 month to 14 years who will be presented to the pediatric emergency [OR] or attended by emergency medical service who have active seizure and had [NOT] no intravenous access would be eligible for the study.
Exclusion Criteria:
Cardiac arrest
Head trauma
Drowning
Congenital heart disease
Inborn errors of metabolism
Electrolyte imbalance (hypocalcaemia [OR], hyponatremia [OR] and hypoglycemia)
Hemodynamic instability
Allergy [AND] to benzodiazepines
Focal seizures [AND] with preserved level of consciousness
