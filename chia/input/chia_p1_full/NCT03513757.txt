Inclusion Criteria:
All children scheduled for outpatient MRI scans with expected duration of scan between 30 minutes and 75 minutes.
Exclusion Criteria:
Inpatient status [OR], airway abnormalities [OR], allergy  to any study medications [OR], eggs [OR] and soy [OR], and mitochondrial disorders.
All subjects with any cardiac disease [OR] or history of cardiac arrhythmias will be excluded.
