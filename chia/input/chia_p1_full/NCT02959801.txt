Inclusion Criteria:
proven acute deep venous thrombosis, less than 21 days and who were referred to the interventional radiology department.
Exclusion Criteria:
presence of subacute [OR] or chronic DVT  more than 21 days in duration [OR], inability to lie in the prone position  required for intervention [OR], terminal systemic disease requiring palliative treatment [OR] , active bleeding (from a gastric [OR]/duodenal ulcer [OR] or the cerebrovascular system) [OR] , a haemorrhagic stroke [OR] within the previous year, an impaired bleeding-clotting profile [OR], and any haemophilic disorder [OR], or pregnancy.
