Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with chronic heart failure [AND] of New York Heart Association Class II or III, a left ventricular ejection fraction of = 40% for patients in NYHA class II [OR] or = 45% for patients in NYHA class III, a hemoglobin level at the screening visit between 9.5-13.5 g/dl, and iron deficiency, which is defined as serum ferritin level < 100µg/l [OR] or between 100 and 299 µg/l, when transferring saturation is < 20%.
Age =18 years
Obtained informed consent
Stable pharmacological therapy [AND] during the last 4 weeks ([NOT] with the exception of diuretics)
Exclusion Criteria:
Hemochromatosis, iron overload, defined as TSAT > 45%
Known hypersensitivity [AND] to Ferinject®.
Known active infection [OR], CRP>20 mg/L, clinically significant bleeding [OR], active malignancy.
Chronic liver disease [OR] and/or screening alanine transaminase (ALT) [OR] or aspartate transaminase (AST) above three times the upper limit of the normal range.
Immunosuppressive therapy [OR] or renal dialysis (current [OR] or planned within the next 6 months).
History of erythropoietin [OR], i. v. [OR] or oral iron therapy [OR], and blood transfusion in previous 12 weeks [OR] and/or such therapy planned within the next 6 months.
Unstable angina pectoris as judged by the investigator, clinically significant uncorrected valvular disease [OR] or left ventricular outflow obstruction [OR], obstructive cardiomyopathy [OR], poorly controlled fast atrial [OR] fibrillation or flutter [OR], poorly controlled symptomatic brady- [OR] or tachyarrhythmias.
Acute myocardial infarction [OR] or acute coronary syndrome [OR], transient ischemic attack [OR] or stroke within the last 3 months.
Coronary-artery bypass graft [OR], percutaneous intervention  (e.g. cardiac, cerebrovascular, aortic; diagnostic catheters are allowed) [OR] or major surgery, including thoracic [OR] and cardiac surgery, within the last 3 months.
Participation in a CHF training program.
Known HIV/AIDS.
Inability to fully comprehend and/or perform study procedures in the investigator's opinion.
Vitamin B12 [OR] and/or serum folate deficiency according to the laboratory (re-screening is possible after substitution therapy).
Pregnancy [OR] or lactation.
Participation in another clinical trial within previous 30 days and/or anticipated participation in another trial during this study.
Anticoagulation
