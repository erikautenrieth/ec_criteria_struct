Inclusion Criteria:
Patients undergoing small bowel video capsule endoscopy
Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with a contraindication [AND] to VCE (small bowel strictures [OR], oropharyngeal dysphagia [OR], pregnancy [OR], patients who are [NOT] not surgical candidates)
Endoscopic insertion [AND] of video capsule endoscope
Inpatient procedures [AND] for active GI bleeding
Patients with fluid restriction [OR] or who are unable to drink up to 900 ml of fluid within 10 minutes prior to the VCE
