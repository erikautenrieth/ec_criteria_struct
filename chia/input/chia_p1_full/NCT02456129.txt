Inclusion Criteria:
Body mass index (BMI): 18 ≤ BMI ≤ 32 kg/m²
Postmenopausal state revealed by:
Medical history, if applicable (natural menopause at least 12 months prior to first study drug administration [OR]; or surgical menopause  by bilateral ovariectomy at least 3 months prior to first study drug administration), [AND] in addition: in women < 65 years old [OR], follicle stimulating hormone (FSH) > 40 IU/L
Exclusion Criteria:
Incompletely cured pre-existing diseases for which it can be assumed that the absorption, distribution, metabolism, elimination or effects of the study drugs will not be normal
Known [OR] or suspected liver diseases
Clinically relevant findings(e.g. blood pressure [OR], electrocardiogram(ECG) [OR]; physical [OR] and gynecological examination [OR], laboratory examination)
