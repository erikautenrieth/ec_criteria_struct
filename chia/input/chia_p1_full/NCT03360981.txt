Inclusion Criteria:
patients aged  >18 [OR], <75, left ventricle ejection fraction  (LVEF) >50% [OR], multivessel coronary disease [AND] detected by coronarography [OR], indication to receive a CABG [OR], stable CAD [OR]. All diabetics [OR] and non diabetics.
Exclusion Criteria:
