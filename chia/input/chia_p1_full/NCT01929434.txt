Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with diagnosis of cerebral palsy.
Patients' curator must be able to give voluntary consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
Intracranial infection.
Severe respiratory [OR] and circulatory system diseases.
Hematologic malignancies.
Positive serological tests such as AIDS [OR], hepatitis B virus [OR], hepatitis C virus [OR] and syphilis （antigen or antibody）.
Tumors.
Genetic [OR] and metabolic diseases.
