Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with anaemia (males Hb <130 g/L [OR], females <120 g/L) undergoing elective cardiac surgery, and available to receive trial drug 1- 10 weeks prior to surgery
Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnancy
Known hypersensitivity [AND] to study drug (ferric carboxymaltose or equivalent) or its excipients
Known [OR] or suspected haemoglobinopathy [OR]/thalassaemia
Bone marrow disease
Haemochromatosis
Renal dialysis
Erythropoietin [OR] or IV iron in the previous 4 weeks
