Inclusion Criteria:
Fulfills NIH criteria [AND] for bariatric surgery
Planned operation of laparoscopic Roux-en Y gastric bypass (LRYGB) [OR] or laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy (LSG) as primary bariatric procedure
Exclusion Criteria:
BMI <35 and > 60 kg/m2
Inability to walk (bed-bound [OR] or wheelchair dependence)
open abdominal surgeries [NOT] except simple appendectomy [OR] and common OB/GYN procedures in the pelvis (hysterectomy [OR], C-section [OR], and oophorectomy [OR], tubal ligation)
laparoscopic bowel [OR] or solid organ resection [AND] [NOT] except laparoscopic cholecystectomy
ventral hernia  repair [AND] with mesh
Preoperative chronic opiate use [AND] for chronic pain defined as opiate usage at least 60 mg/day of morphine equivalent for = 3 months (as defined by International Association for the Study of Pain22) [AND] in the one year period prior to the bariatric surgery 
The American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) score > 3
History of hypersensitivity [OR] or adverse reaction to bupivacaine [OR] or narcotics
Inability to speak English
ventral hernia [AND] repair
Cholecystectomy
hiatal hernia [AND] repair [AND] with posterior cruroplasty
extensive lysis of adhesions
other procedures that mandate addition of "trocar(s)" or "feeding tube"
Addition of trocar(s)  [OR] or conversion of surgery [AND] to hand-assisted [OR] or open
