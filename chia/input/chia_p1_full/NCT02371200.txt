Inclusion Criteria:
1. Subject has a history of GTC seizures, either primary GTC [OR] or partial onset seizures [AND] with secondary generalization.
2. Is being admitted to a hospital [AND] for routine vEEG monitoring [AND] related to seizures.
3. Male [OR] or female between the ages of 2-99.
4. Has an upper arm circumference which is adequate for proper fit of the EMG monitor (at least 14cm).
5. If female and of childbearing potential [AND], has a negative pregnancy test.
6. Can understand and sign written informed consent, or will have a parent or a legally authorized representative (LAR) who can do so, prior to the performance of any study assessments.
7. Subject and/or Primary Caregiver must be competent to follow all study procedures.
8. Is able to read, speak, and understand English.
Exclusion Criteria:
1. Does [NOT] not have a documented history of generalized seizures.
2. Has [NOT] not had a GTC seizure within the last year AND is [NOT] not expected to have a reduction of anti-epileptic drugs [AND] during their hospital admission.
3. Intracranial EEG electrodes are being used
4. The subject's upper arm circumference [NOT] not adequate for proper fit of the EMG monitor (less than 14cm).
5. Pregnant female.
6. Subject/Caregiver is unable to provide consent.
