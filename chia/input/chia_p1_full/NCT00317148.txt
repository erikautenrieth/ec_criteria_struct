Inclusion Criteria:
Healthy postmenopausal women with 50 or more [AND] moderate to severe hot flushes.
Women between 40 to 70 years of age.
Exclusion Criteria:
Body mass index (BMI) of 35 kg/m2 or more.
Significant metabolic [OR] and endocrine diseases.
Diagnosis of cancer.
Use of steroids [OR] or drugs that interfere with the metabolism of estrogen.
Use of any systemic  estrogen [OR], progestin [OR], or DHEA in the eight weeks prior to randomization.
Use of alternative therapies [OR] or natural products [AND] to treat postmenopausal symptoms in the four weeks prior to randomization.
Palpable fibroids [OR] or uterine prolapse [AND]: Grade 2 or 3.
Cigarette smoking
