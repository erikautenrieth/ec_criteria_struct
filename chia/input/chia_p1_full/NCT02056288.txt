Inclusion Criteria:
Supracondylar fracture
Age 2-17 years
American Society of Anesthesiologists Status 1 -3
Scheduled for closed reduction with percutaneous pinning [AND] under general anesthesia
Exclusion Criteria:
Pulseless extremity
Compromised neurologic status on exam (specifically assessment of radial [OR], ulnar, and median nerve )
Known allergy [AND] to local anesthetics (7) [NOT] Not scheduled for closed reduction with percutaneous pinning [AND] under general anesthesia
Bleeding diathesis
American Society of Anesthesiologist (ASA) status 4 or higher.
Sleep apnea [AND] by polysomnography
