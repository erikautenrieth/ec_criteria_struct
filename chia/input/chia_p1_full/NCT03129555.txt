Inclusion Criteria:
A diagnosis of VTE in outpatient clinic [OR] or as discharge diagnosis after hospitalization.
A claimed prescription [AND] of a NOAC from a Danish pharmacy within 14 days of discharge [OR] or outpatient clinic visit.
Exclusion Criteria:
A prescription of a NOAC within 90 days prior to hospitalization [OR] or outpatient clinic visit [AND] for VTE.
Patients with NOAC preference apart from preference consistent with current cluster randomized NOAC.
Other contraindications [AND] mentioned in the "Summary of Product Characteristics" for the respective NOAC.
