Inclusion Criteria:
Cases (with a history of TBI):
1. Ages 50-95 years
2. History of traumatic brain injury [AND] of sufficient severity to have resulted in medical attention (ascertained via the Ohio State University TBI Identification Questionnaire—OSU TBI-ID, and based on DoD/VA criteria)
3. Residence in AFRH-Washington D.C. [OR] or the Veterans Home of California-Yountville
4. MMSE score ≥ 20
5. Capacity to provide consent to participate in research (assessment made by study physician)
6. Ability to read and write English
Controls (without a history of TBI):
1. Ages 50-95 years
2. [NOT] No history of traumatic brain injury of sufficient severity to have resulted in medical attention (ascertained via the Ohio State University TBI Identification Questionnaire—OSU TBI-ID)
3. Residence in AFRH-Washington [OR] or the Veterans Home of California-Yountville
4. MMSE score ≥ 20
5. Capacity to provide consent or assent to participate in research
6. Ability to read and write English -
Exclusion Criteria:
Cases (with a history of TBI):
1. History of penetrating brain injury
2. History of disabling neurological [OR] or psychiatric condition  such as epilepsy ([NOT] besides posttraumatic epilepsy) [OR] , multiple sclerosis [OR], cortical stroke [OR], hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy [OR], encephalitis [OR], or schizophrenia
Controls (without a history of TBI):
History of disabling neurological or psychiatric condition such as epilepsy [OR], multiple sclerosis [OR], cortical stroke [OR], hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy [OR], encephalitis [OR], or schizophrenia
