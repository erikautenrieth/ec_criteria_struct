Inclusion Criteria:
Age 19 and more
On dual [OR] or triple antiplatelet therapy and between 12months and 14months [AND] from Bioresorbable Vascular Scaffold  implantation
[NOT] No history of death [OR], serious myocardial infarction [OR], stroke [OR], repeat revascularization [OR], or major bleeding
Exclusion Criteria:
Contraindication [AND] to antiplatelet therapy
Need to continue clopidogrel due to stroke [OR], peripheral disease [OR], significant carotid disease [OR] or recent acute coronary syndrome
Major bleeding  history [OR] or bleeding diathesis
Pregnancy
