Inclusion Criteria:
18 years of age and older,
diagnosis of stage II adhesive capsulitis as determined by clinical examination of the treating physician, and
[NOT] absence of abnormal findings [AND] on X-ray.
Exclusion Criteria:
allergy to Doxycycline [OR] or Methylprednisolone,
pregnancy,
diagnosis,
Inflammatory arthritis [OR] or diabetes,
secondary adhesive capsulitis (history of significant trauma [OR], rotator cuff tear injury [OR], stroke)
evidence of arthritis [AND] on x-ray,
current infectious disease, and
any previous treatment [AND] for the for adhesive capsulitis of the affected shoulder.
