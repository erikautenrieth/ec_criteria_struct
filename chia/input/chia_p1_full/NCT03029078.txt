Inclusion Criteria:
Patient harboring a GRE [OR] or CRE bacteria
Colonization  confirmed [AND] by our microbiology department, including at least 3 positives swabs in the last month
Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant woman [OR]  or breastfeeding
immunosuppression including AIDS [OR], corticosteroids over 60mg/day
ongoing antibiotic  treatment [AND] at the day of inclusion
impossibility to obtain a signed consent form.
