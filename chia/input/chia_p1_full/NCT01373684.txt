Inclusion Criteria:
Chronic hepatitis B [AND] (HBsAg positive > 6 months)
HBeAg negative within six months prior to initiation of peginterferon alfa-2a
HBV DNA < 200 IU/ml during nucleos(t)ide analogue ([NOT] except Telbivudine) treatment [AND] within one month prior to initiation of peginterferon alfa-2a
Compensated liver disease
Age > 18 years
Written informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
Treatment with any investigational drug within 30 days of entry to this protocol
Current treatment with Telbivudine
Severe hepatitis activity [AND] as documented by ALT>10 x ULN
History of decompensated cirrhosis (defined as jaundice in the presence of cirrhosis, ascites, bleeding gastric [OR] or esophageal varices [OR] or encephalopathy)
Pre-existent neutropenia (neutrophils <1,500/mm3) [OR] or thrombocytopenia [AND] (platelets < 90,000/mm3)
Co-infection with hepatitis C virus [OR], hepatitis D virus [OR] or human immunodeficiency virus (HIV)
Other acquired [OR] or inherited causes of liver disease: alcoholic liver disease [OR], obesity induced liver disease [OR], drug related liver disease [OR], auto-immune hepatitis [OR], hemochromatosis [OR], Wilson's disease [OR] or alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency
Alpha fetoprotein > 50 ng/ml
Hyper- [OR] or hypothyroidism (subjects requiring medication to maintain TSH levels in the normal range are eligible if all other inclusion/exclusion criteria are met)
Immune suppressive treatment within the previous 6 months
Contra-indications [AND] for alfa-interferon therapy like suspected hypersensitivity to interferon [OR] or Peginterferon or any known pre-existing medical condition that could interfere with the patient's participation in and completion of the study.
Pregnancy, breast-feeding
Other significant medical illness that might interfere with this study: significant pulmonary dysfunction [OR] in the previous 6 months, malignancy [NOT] other than skin basocellular carcinoma in previous 5 years [OR], immunodeficiency syndromes (e.g. HIV positivity [OR], auto-immune diseases [OR], organ transplants [NOT] other than cornea [OR] and hair transplant)
Any medical condition requiring, or likely to require chronic systemic administration of steroids, during the course of the study
Substance abuse, such as alcohol (>80 g/day) [OR], I.V. drugs [OR] and inhaled drugs in the past 2 years.
Any other condition which in the opinion of the investigator would make the patient unsuitable for enrollment, or could interfere with the patient participating in and completing the study
