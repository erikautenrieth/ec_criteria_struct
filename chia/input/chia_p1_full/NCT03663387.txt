Inclusion Criteria:
Male [OR] and female subjects between 40-85 years old will be enrolled. Younger subjects are not included as the risk for brain amyloid lesions is too low
All subjects will speak English  as their first language [OR] or demonstrate proficiency in English (defined as reaching a scaled score of > 11 on the WAIS vocabulary test).
All subjects will have normal cognition at baseline: [AND] a Clinical Dementia Rating CDR=0, Global Deterioration Scale GDS<2.
All subjects will be in good general health and able to participate in the LP and imaging exams. This determination is made by the study neurologist and reviewed at a consensus meeting for each subject.
Exclusion Criteria:
Uncontrolled hypertension [OR] or metabolic disease
Neurodegenerative disorders (i.e. Parkinson disease [OR]. LBD [OR], or FTD).
Dementia [OR] or Mild cognitive impairment at baseline
Long life major depression. [AND] Baseline scores =16 on the 17-item Hamilton Depression Scale at baseline.
Long-life DSM-IV axis 1 disorders.
Mental retardation.
Substance abuse.
Concurrent  medication limiting validity [AND] of neuropsychological tests [OR] or imaging.
Anti-depressants with anti-cholinergic properties
Monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOi)
Regular use of narcotic analgesics (>2 doses per week).
Use of neuroleptics
Use of anti-dementia medications  (Aricept [OR], Exelon [OR], Razadyne) and memantine [OR] (Namenda)) [OR] or anti-Parkinsonian medications (Sinemet [OR], amantadine [OR], bromocriptine [OR], pergolide [OR], selegeline).
Individuals taking over the counter memory enhancing or protecting medications (e.g. ginkgo biloba [OR], vitamins) are not excluded.
Implanted medical devices that are incompatible with MRI imaging.
Radiation exposures exceeding annual Rad Worker limits.
Heart failure [AND] stage D as defined by American Heart Association (7).
Chronic kidney disease [AND] in stages = 4, as defined per National Kidney Foundation (8).
Brain tumor [OR] and other neoplastic disorders outside the brain where disease itself [OR] or its treatment (radiation [OR], chemotherapy) is likely to affect brain structure [OR]  or function.
Stroke [AND] when meeting criteria for total anterior [OR], partial anterior [OR] or posterior circulation infarct [AND] according to the Oxford Community Stroke Project classification. Patients with clinically silent of lacunar strokes and transient ischemic attacks will not be excluded.
Significant head trauma.
Hydrocephalus.
Hostility [OR] or refusal to cooperate
