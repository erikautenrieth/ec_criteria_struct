Inclusion Criteria:
Outpatient [AND] with primary DSM- IV OCD
Completion of a 14-week open label trial of one the following SRI's: fluoxetine 80 mg/day [OR], paroxetine 60 mg/day [OR], fluvoxamine 300 mg/day [OR], clomipramine 250 mg/day [OR], sertraline 200 mg/day [OR], citalopram 60 mg/day [OR], escitalopram 30 mg/day [AND] and demonstrating a non [OR] or partial responses to  SRI treatment (CGI-I of 3 [OR] or 4, Y-BOCS reduction of < 35%)
Stable (8 wks or longer) concurrent medications including benzodiazepines [OR], sedative hypnotics [OR], antipsychotics [OR], and antidepressants.
Exclusion Criteria:
Any other primary DSM-IV diagnosis; DSM-IV criteria for body dysmorphic disorder [OR], bipolar affective disorder [OR], schizophrenia [OR], psychotic disorder, [OR] current alcohol [OR]/substance abuse.
A previous adequate trial of topiramate
Comorbid major depressive disorder diagnosis which predates OCD diagnosis
Cognitive behavioural therapy [OR] or additional psychotherapy in past four months
Allergy [OR] or hypersensitivity [AND] to topiramate
BMI < 20
History of kidney stones
