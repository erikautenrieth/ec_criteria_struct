Inclusion Criteria:
elective procedure
weight over 40 kg
American Society of Anesthesiology class I-III
first upper GI endoscopy procedure
finnish [OR] or/and swedish speaking
Exclusion Criteria:
amide [OR] and/or esther local anaesthetic allergy
paraben [AND] allergy
Child-Pugh grade B [OR]/C [AND] liver failure
renal insufficiency (calculated glomerular filtration rate under 60 ml/min/1.73 m2 according to Cockcroft-Gault scale )
dementia
those presenting with swallowing problem
chronic pain condition
chronic use of pain medication
pregnancy
lactation
