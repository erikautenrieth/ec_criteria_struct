Inclusion Criteria:
Women at any age with early stage breast cancer (stage I-II) [AND] and American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) score of I-II.
Exclusion Criteria:
Ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS [OR]; [AND] stage 0 cancer),
Advanced [OR] or distant metastatic stage,
Receiving any neoadjuvant therapy,
History of [AND] receiving any antibiotics within prior 3 months,
History of [AND] immunodeficiency,
Having a remote infection,
History of [AND] reaction [AND] to study antibiotics,
[NOT] Denial of signing the consent form.
