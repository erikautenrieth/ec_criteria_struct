Inclusion Criteria:
Male [OR] or female >40 and <70 years old.
Has a body mass index >27 and <47 kg/m2.
[NOT] Not diagnosed with Type 2 diabetes.
[NOT] Not currently engaged in > 60 min/wk of exercise
Meet at least 3 of 5 National Cholesterol Education Adult Treatment Panel III
Increased waist circumference (=102 cm in men [OR]; =88 cm in women)
Elevated triglycerides (=150 mg/dl), or on medication for treating the condition
Reduced HDL-cholesterol (<40mg/dl in men [OR], <50 mg/dl in women), or on medication for treating the condition
High blood pressure [AND] (=130 mmHg systolic [OR] or =85mmHg diastolic), or on medication for treating the condition
Elevated fasting glucose [AND] (=100 mg/dl), or on medication for treating the condition
Exclusion Criteria:
Morbidly obese patients (BMI >47 kg/m2) [OR] and overweight [OR]/lean patients (BMI <27 kg/m2)
Evidence of type 1 diabetes [OR] and diabetics requiring insulin therapy.
Subjects who have [NOT] not been weight stable (>2 kg weight change in past 3 months)
Subjects who have been recently active (>30 min of moderate/high intensity exercise, 2 times/week).
Subjects who are smokers [OR] or who have quit smoking <5 years ago
Subjects prescribed metformin [OR] or have taken metformin within 1 year.
Subjects with abnormal estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR).
Hypertriglyceridemic  (>400 mg/dl) [OR] and hypercholesterolemic (>260 mg/dl) subjects
Hypertensive (>160/100 mmHg)
Subjects currently taking medications that affect heart rate and rhythm (i.e. Ca++ channel blockers [OR], nitrates [OR], alpha- [OR] or beta-blockers).
Subjects with a history of significant metabolic [OR], cardiac [OR], congestive heart failure [OR], cerebrovascular [OR], hematological [OR], pulmonary [OR], gastrointestinal [OR], liver [OR], renal [OR], or endocrine disease [OR] or cancer that in the investigator's opinion would interfere with or alter the outcome measures, or impact subject safety.
Pregnant [OR] (as evidenced by positive pregnancy test) or nursing women
Subjects with contraindications to participation in an exercise training program
Currently taking active weight suppression medication (e.g. phentermine [OR],orlistat [OR], lorcaserin [OR], naltrexone-bupropion in combination [OR], liraglutide [OR], benzephetamine [OR], diethylpropion [OR], phendimetrazine)
Known hypersensitivity [AND] to perflutren (contained in Definity)
