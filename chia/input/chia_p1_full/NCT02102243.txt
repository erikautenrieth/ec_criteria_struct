Inclusion Criteria:
Normotensive controls
Stage I (140-159/90-99 mmHg) untreated subjects with essential hypertension
Patients with PA and stage I (140-159/90-99 mmHg) hypertension
Exclusion Criteria:
Congestive heart failure [OR] or coronary artery disease
Blood pressure averaging > 159/99 mmHg
Serum creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL
Diabetes mellitus [OR] or other systemic illness
Left ventricular hypertrophy [AND] by echocardiography or ECG
Pregnancy
Hypersensitivity to spironolactone [OR], chlorthalidone [OR], amlodipine [OR], human recombinant insulin or Definity
Any history of substance abuse ([NOT] other than tobacco)
History of gouty arthritis
Patients with right-to-left, [OR] bi-directional [OR], or transient right-to-left cardiac shunts
Hypersensitivity to perflutren [OR], blood [OR], blood products [OR] or albumin
