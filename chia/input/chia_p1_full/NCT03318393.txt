Inclusion Criteria:
Age 1 day to less than 18 years
Cared for in the pediatric intensive care unit [OR] or pediatric cardiac intensive care unit
receiving venovenous [OR] or venoarterial ECMO
Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with known [OR] or suspected heparin induced thrombocytopenia prior to consent
Patients with hepatic failure defined as coagulopathy [AND] with elevated transaminases more than three times normal values
Patients with plan to decannulate from ECMO within 48 hours
Known [OR] or suspected pregnant women
Previous enrollment in this study
Primary language spoken that is not English or Spanish
