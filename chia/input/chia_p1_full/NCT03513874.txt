Inclusion Criteria:
Type 1 diabetes according to ADA criterias <5 years.
Age= 18 years and less than 70 years.
[NOT] Non-obese: defined as BMI less than 28 kg/m2
Positive for at least one of the anti-islet autoantibodies: GADA [OR], IA2A [OR], ZnT8A
Fasting [OR] or postprandial plasma C-peptide more than 100 pmol/L
Written informed consent from the patient or family representative.
Exclusion Criteria:
History of any malignancy [OR] or other severe diseases
Female patients who are pregnant or breastfeeding before or during the three-year follow-up
Poor compliance [OR] or refusal to participate.
