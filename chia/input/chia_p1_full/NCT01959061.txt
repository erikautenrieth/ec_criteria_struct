Inclusion Criteria:
Histologically confirmed colorectal adenocarcinoma
Disease limited to the liver [OR] Unresectable disease by surgery [OR] or other local therapies
Age >18 years
ECOG performance status 0-2,Child pugh A [OR] or B
Expected survival = 3 months
Adequate hematological [OR], hepatic [OR], and renal function
Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant [OR] or lactating women
Patients with severe organ  dysfunction [OR] or failure
With severe cardiovascular disease [OR], or mental
Extraliver metastases
