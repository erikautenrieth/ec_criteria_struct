Inclusion Criteria:
1. Diagnosis of primary immunodeficiency [OR] with established plan to undergo myeloablative or non-myeloablative allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplant [OR] for treatment thereof or diagnosis of a form of primary immunodeficiency [AND] for which [NOT] hematopoietic stem cell transplantation is not indicated.
2. Active infection with EBV [OR], CMV [OR], and/or Adenovirus [AND], unable to be successfully controlled with standard therapy.
3. Steroids less than 0.5 mg/kg/day prednisone
4. Karnofsky/Lansky score of ≥ 50
5. ANC greater than 500/µL.
6. Bilirubin <2x, AST <3x, Serum creatinine <2x upper limit of normal, Hgb >8.0
7. Pulse oximetry of > 90% on room air
8. Negative pregnancy test [AND] (if female of childbearing potential)
9. Patient or parent/guardian capable of providing informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
1. Patients with other uncontrolled infections (see 2.3.2 for definitions)
2. Patients who received ATG [OR], Campath [OR], or other T cell immunosuppressive monoclonal antibodies in the last 28 days
3. Received donor lymphocyte infusion in last 28 days
4. Diagnosis of Omenn's syndrome [OR] or MHC class I deficiency
5. Active and uncontrolled malignancy
6. Pregnant [OR] or lactating
7. [NOT] Unable to wean  steroids [AND] to ≤0.5 mg/kg/day prednisone.
8. Patients with Grade 3 hyperbilirubinemia
