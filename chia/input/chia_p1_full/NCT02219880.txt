Inclusion Criteria:
Aged between 18-70 years
Meets the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual (DSM) IV and DSM-V diagnostic criteria [AND] for generalised anxiety disorder (GAD) [AND]  based on structured interview (Mini International Neuropsychiatric Interview-Plus 6 [MINI-Plus 6]. Note that while the MINI-Plus 6 uses the DSM-IV criteria, the same criteria are used in the DSM-V).
Presents with anxiety [AND] (Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale = 18) at the time of study entry
Fluent in written and spoken English
Provides a signed copy of the consent form
Primary diagnosis [AND] [NOT] other than GAD
Presentation of moderate to severe depressive symptoms (Montgomery-Asberg Rating Scale: MADRS = 18 [OR] at time of study entry or = 24 at any time during study)
Presentation of suicidal ideation (= 3 on MADRS suicidal thoughts domain at time of study entry [OR] or at any time during study)
Current diagnosis of bipolar disorder [OR] or schizophrenia on structured interview (MINI Plus)
Current substance [OR]/alcohol use disorder on structured interview (MINI Plus) Page 21 of 39 Commercial-in-Confidence
Currently taking  an antidepressant [OR], mood stabiliser [OR], antipsychotic [OR], anticonvulsant [OR], warfarin [OR] or thyroxin [OR], or current regular use (more than 2 days per week) of a benzodiazepine [OR] or opioid-based analgesic
Current use [AND] of a psychotropic nutraceutical (e.g. St John's wort)
Previous intolerance [AND] to kava
Three or more failed trials of pharmacotherapy [AND] for the current GAD episode
Recently commenced psychotherapy (within four weeks of study entry)
Known [OR] or suspected clinically unstable systemic medical disorder
Diagnosed hepato-biliary disease [OR]/inflammation
Elevated liver enzymes [AND] at baseline blood test
Pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding [OR], or trying to conceive
[NOT] Not using medically approved contraception (including abstinence) if female and of childbearing age
Unable to participate in all scheduled visits [OR], treatment plan [OR], or other trial procedures according to the protocol ([NOT] except for the optional genetic component)
