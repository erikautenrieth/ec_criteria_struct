Pathologically [AND] proven unresectable adenocarcinoma of stomach
With uni-dimensionally measurable disease (at least longest diameter 2 cm [AND] on conventional CT scan [OR], x-ray [OR] or physical examination [OR], or 1cm on spiral CT scan)
Age 18 to 70 years old
Estimated life expectancy of more than 3 months

Adequate kidney function (serum creatinine < 1.5 mg/dL)
Adequate liver function (serum total bilirubin  < 2 times  [AND] the upper normal limit (UNL); serum transaminases levels <3 times [OR] [<5 times for patients with liver metastasis] UNL)
[NOT] No prior chemotherapy but prior [NOT] adjuvant chemotherapy finished at least 6 months before enrollment was allowed. ([NOT] but, prior [NOT] adjuvant chemotherapy with capecitabine [OR] or S-1 [OR] or camptothecin analogues was excluded)
[NOT] No prior radiation therapy for at least 4 weeks before enrollment in the study
Exclusion Criteria:
[NOT] Other tumor [AND] type than adenocarcinoma

Gastric outlet obstruction [OR] or intestinal obstruction

The patient has bony lesions as the sole evaluable disease.

Other serious illness [OR] or medical conditions

History of significant neurologic [OR] or psychiatric disorders including dementia [OR] or seizures
