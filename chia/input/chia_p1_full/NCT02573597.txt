Inclusion Criteria:
ASA I & II, Nulliparous and Multiparous, Spontaneous [OR]/Induced [OR]/Augmented Labor, Early active labor (cervix <5 cm (if known)), Pain (VPS) > 3, 18-45 years of age
Exclusion Criteria:
<37 weeks gestation, H/o Cesarean Section [OR], Multiple Gestation [OR], Pre-eclampsia [OR], Narcotics [OR] within 3 hours prior to labor epidural placement, Chronic Pain (as defined by chronic opiate consumption), Women who are participating in another study that will impact protocol
