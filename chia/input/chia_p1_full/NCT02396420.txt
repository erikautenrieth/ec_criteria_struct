Inclusion Criteria:
Patient has provided signed informed consent
Patient is aged greater than or equal to 40 and less than or equal to 89 years of age
Patient has a prostate size [AND] between 90g and 200g, as determined by MRI
Patient has experienced lower urinary tract symptoms (LUTS) for at least 6 months prior to study enrollment
Patient has an IPSS score of at least 13 at baseline
Patient is either: refractory to medical treatment [OR], contraindicated to medical treatment [OR], OR refuses medical treatment
Patient either: refuses surgical treatment [OR] OR is contraindicated for surgical treatment
Patient meets ONE of the following criteria: baseline PSA < 4.0ng/mL [OR] (no prostate biopsy required) OR baseline PSA >/= 4 ng/mL AND a negative prostate biopsy (minimum 12 core biopsy) within the prior 12 months
Exclusion Criteria:
History of prostate [OR], bladder [OR], or rectal cancer
History of transurethral resection of the prostate (TURP) [OR], open prostate surgery [OR], or radiofrequency [OR] or microwave therapies
History of open bladder, rectosigmoid colon, or other pelvic surgery
Patient is unwilling to discontinue alpha blockers 1 month after study treatment
Patient is unwilling to discontinue 5-alph reductase inhibitors 1 month after study treatment
Neurogenic bladder [OR] or other neurologic disorder impacting bladder function such as Parkinson's disease [OR], multiple sclerosis [OR], cerebral vascular accident [OR] or diabetes
Any other confounding bladder [OR] or urethral pathology, including urethral stricture [OR], bladder neck contracture [OR], or bladder atonia
Active prostatitis [OR] or urinary tract infection
Cystolithiasis within the past 3 months
Serum creatinine > 1.7mg/dL
Inability to discontinue oral anticoagulant 2-5 days prior to study treatment
Coagulation disturbances [NOT] not normalized [AND] by medical treatment
Iodinated contrast [AND] allergy that, in the opinion of the Investigator, cannot be adequately premedicated
Gelatin [AND] allergy
Known severe peripheral vascular disease [OR] or major iliac arterial occlusive disease
Interest in future fertility
Clinically significant cardiac arrhythmia [OR] or other cardiac disease [OR] (including congestive heart failure), uncontrolled diabetes mellitus [OR], clinically significant respiratory disease [OR], or known immunosuppression
Other condition that the Investigator believes puts the patient at risk for a complication during the procedure
