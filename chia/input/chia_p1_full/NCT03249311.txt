Inclusion Criteria:
Male participants between 18 and 40 years-old
Written informed consent signed by the participant
Exclusion Criteria:
Lifetime personal history of diagnosis of major depressive disorder [AND] according to the DSM-V (American Psychiatric Association, 2013) using the Structured Clinical Interview for DSM-V Axis I Disorders, Research Version, Non-patient Edition (SCID-5-RV for DSM-V; First et al., 2015)
A history of suicidal ideation [OR] and behaviour [OR], including self-harm [OR] and/or harm to others.
A history of substance abuse [OR] and/or dependence.
A positive drug screen for illicit drugs
Substantial alcohol use
Current use of Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs), including the antibiotic linezolid [OR] and the thiazine dye methylthioninium chloride (methylene blue)
Current use of serotonin-precursors (such as L-tryptophan [OR], oxitriptan)
Current use of serotonergic drugs (triptans [OR], certain tricyclic antidepressant [OR]s, lithium [OR], tramadol [OR], St. John's Wort)
Concomitant use of NSAIDS [OR], ASA [OR], and other anticoagulants.
Current use of Thioridazine
Current use of CYP1A2 Inhibitors
Current use of Triptans (5HT1 Agonists)
Blood pressure greater than 140/90 [OR]  and/or a pulse rate greater than 90 bpm
Recent history of myocardial infarction [OR], cerebrovascular accident [OR], cardiac arrhythmias [OR], or unstable heart disease.
Evidence of significant physical illness contraindicating the use of levomilnacipran [AND]  and duloxetine found on the physical exam [OR] or in the laboratory data obtained during the first week of the study
Current use of medication [AND] that may affect voiding (ie- anticholinergics)
History of obstructive urinary disorders [OR] and dysuria [OR], prostatic hypertrophy [OR], prostatitis [OR], and other lower urinary tract obstructive disorders.
History of Stevens-Johnson Syndrome [OR] and Erythema multiforme.
Diabetes Type  I [OR] and II
Fructose intolerance [OR], glucose-galactose malabsorption [OR] or sucrose-isomaltase insufficiency.
Hepatic Impairment
Uncontrolled narrow-angle glaucoma
Severe renal impairment
History of seizure disorder
Anatomically narrow ocular angles.
Osteoporosis [OR] or major risk for bone fractures.
