Inclusion Criteria:
18-50 ages
Singleton pregnancy
Cervical length <=25mm between 18(0) and 23(6) weeks
Exclusion Criteria:
Multiple pregnancy
Prior spontaneous preterm birth [OR] or second trimester losses between 16(0) and 36(6) weeks
Cerclage in situ
Painful regular uterine contraction [OR] and/or preterm labor
Ruptured membranes
Major fetal defects
Active vaginal bleeding
Placenda previa [OR] and/or accreta
Cervical dilation  >1.5 cm [OR] and/or visible membranes [AND] by pelvic exam
Suspicion of chorioamnionitis
