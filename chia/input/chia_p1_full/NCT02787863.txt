Inclusion Criteria:
Individuals of both sexes from 18 years with a diagnosis of community-acquired pneumonia [OR], COPD [OR] or Bronchial Asthma;
The presence of signed and dated informed consent to participate in a clinical study;
The ability to perform the requirements of the Protocol;
For women of childbearing age is a negative result of a pregnancy test before vaccination.
community-acquired pneumonia: the presence of radiologically confirmed infiltration of the lung tissue [OR]; the presence of at least two of the following clinical signs: acute fever [OR] early in the disease (temperature > 38.0°C), cough with sputum [OR], the physical signs [AND] of pneumonia (focus of crepitate [OR] and/or fine bubble rales [OR], bronchial breathing hard [OR], shortening of percussion sounds [OR]), leukocytosis > 10*10 9 /l [OR] and/or stab shift > 10%; the occurrence of the disease outside the hospital and the organized groups (such as nursing homes, sanatoriums, etc.).
COPD: dyspnea [OR]: progressive (worsens over time), increases with exertion, persistent [OR]; chronic cough  (may appear sporadically and may be unproductive); chronic expectoration [OR]; the impact of risk factors [OR] in the medical history (Smoking [OR], occupational dust pollutants and chemicals); widespread wheeze on auscultation of the chest [OR] and/or distant wheezing in the chest [OR]; family history of COPD [OR]; spirometric [AND] data confirming the presence of fixed bronchial obstruction.
Exclusion Criteria:
Vaccination [AND] against pneumococcal infection in anamnesis;
Application of preparations of immune globulin [OR] or blood transfusion within last three months prior to clinical studies;
Prolonged use (more than 14 days) immunosuppressants [OR] or other immunosuppressive drugs within 6 months prior to the start of the study;
Any confirmed or suspected immunosuppressive [OR] or immunodeficient condition, including HIV infection;
A history or currently hematologic and other cancers;
A positive reaction for HIV infection  [OR] , viral hepatitis B and hepatitis C;
The presence of respiratory [OR], cardio-vascular insufficiency [OR], impaired [OR] liver and kidney function, established during a physical examination at visit number 1;
Pronounced congenital defects [OR] or serious chronic diseases in the acute stage, including any clinically important exacerbation of chronic diseases of the liver  [OR], kidney, cardiovascular, nervous system [OR], mental diseases [OR] or metabolic disorders, confirmed by the history or objective examination (pulmonary: cystic fibrosis [OR], lung abscess [OR], empyema [OR], active tuberculosis [OR]; extra-pulmonary: congestive heart failure [OR], malabsorption [OR], chronic renal and hepatic failure [OR], cirrhosis [OR], malignancy [OR], immunodeficiency [OR], cirrhosis of the liver);
Severe allergic reactions in anamnesis of autoimmune disease;
The presence of acute infectious and/or communicable illnesses within 1 month prior to study;
History of chronic alcohol abuse [OR] and/or drug use;
Exacerbation [AND] of chronic diseases;
Breastfeeding;
Pregnancy;
Participation in any other clinical study within the last 3 months.
