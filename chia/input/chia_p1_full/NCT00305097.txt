Inclusion Criteria:
Aged at least 18 years with an ability and willingness to give written informed consent.
Body mass index 25-35 kg/m2
Users of at least 2 cups of caffeinated coffee per day who are willing to be randomized to any of the interventions.
Non-smoking
Exclusion Criteria:
Any condition/illness that [OR] may affect the study outcomes or would make participation potentially harmful such as pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding [OR], diabetes mellitus [OR], heart disease [OR], stroke [OR], hypertension [OR], malabsorption syndromes [OR], GERD [OR], a history of [AND] ulcer, according to a detailed medical history.
Abnormal hepatic function [OR] (liver function test > twice the normal range), abnormal renal function  (creatinine > 1.1 mg/dl) [OR], fasting plasma glucose  in the diabetic range (>/= 126 mg/dl) [OR], or blood pressure > 140/90 mmHg.
Present alcoholism [OR] or drug abuse [OR] or use of medications that could interfere with the treatment including bronchodilators, quinolone antibiotics [OR], monoamine oxidase inhibitors [OR], anxiolytics [OR], ranitidine [OR], corticosteroids [OR], growth hormone [OR], antihypertensives.
