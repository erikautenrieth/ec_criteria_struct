Inclusion Criteria:
(1)= 45 years old;
(2)A diagnosis [OR] or previous diagnosis of essential hypertension [OR], including anyone currently taking antihypertensive drugs; or for those who have [NOT] not taken antihypertensive drugs within the last 2 weeks, two consecutive examinations were conducted at least one day apart, and both sitting blood pressure (mean value of 3 measurements) met the following criteria: diastolic blood pressure (DBP) =90 mmHg  [OR] or systolic blood pressure (SBP) =140 mmHg (the second blood pressure was measured at V1);
(3)If a study participant is a woman of childbearing age, she agrees to use a reliable contraceptive method during the trial;
(4)Voluntarily participates and has signed an informed consent form.
(1)Completed MTHFR C677T gene polymorphism detection  in run-in period [OR] or MTHFR C677T genotype already known in advance;
(2)Exhibited good tolerance to enalapril and good overall medication compliance (>80%) in run-in period [OR] or previously exhibited good tolerance and adherence to ACEI drugs in previous medication history.
(3)Voluntarily continues to participate in this study.
Exclusion Criteria:
(1)Women who are pregnant [OR] and/or lactating [OR]; or women who intend to conceive within a year;
(2)History of allergies to enalapril [OR], folic acid [OR] or other components of the compound drug;
(3)History of adverse reactions [OR] or intolerance to enalapril [OR] or other ACE inhibitors [OR], or drugs or supplements containing folic acid;
(4)Diagnosis [OR] or suspicion of secondary hypertension;
(5)Known serious medical conditions, including: Cardiovascular: patients with clinically diagnosed cardiac dysfunction (NYHA class III and above) [OR], hypertrophic obstructive cardiomyopathy [OR], clinically significant valvular heart disease [OR], acute coronary syndrome [OR] within the last 3 months, or percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI) [OR], or coronary artery bypass graft (CABG) [OR]; or abnormal pre-enrollment ECG test results with clinically significant arrhythmias [OR] (atrial flutter [OR], atrial fibrillation [OR], grade [OR] II-III atrioventricular block, etc.); Digestive: a previous diagnosis of various types of viral hepatitis [OR] that are still in the active phase; abnormal pre-enrollment liver function test [OR] results (ALT [OR], AST [OR], GGT [OR], TBIL [OR], or DBIL 3 times higher than normal, ALB = 30g/L); gastrectomy [OR] and/or gastrojejunostomy [OR]; gastrointestinal dysfunction [OR]; Urinary: pre-enrollment serum creatinine greater than 200umol/L [OR]; clinical diagnosis of renal artery stenosis [OR], isolated kidney [OR], kidney transplantation [OR] and/or other diseases; Endocrine: type 1 diabetes [OR] or uncontrolled type 2 diabetes [OR] (fasting blood glucose above 11.1 mmol/L at pre-enrollment); previous diagnosis of hyperthyroidism [OR] and failure to correct; Respiratory: pulmonary heart disease [OR]; chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR]; Neuropsychiatric: recent transient ischemic attack [OR] or stroke (within the last 3 months) [OR]; peripheral [OR] or severe autonomic dysfunction [OR]; mental [OR] or nervous system dysfunction [OR], inability to express desire [OR]; known drug [OR] or alcohol dependence [OR]; Malignancy [OR], malnutrition [OR], hematopoietic disorders and other serious diseases.
(6)Significant signs of abnormalities [AND] as seen in laboratory tests or physical characteristics, which, at the discretion of the investigators, indicates that the patient is experiencing a serious illness or, may affect the observation and evaluation of the drug's efficacy or adverse events, or renders the patient unsuitable for participating in this study;
(7)Patients currently taking folate [OR], B12 [OR], or B6 [OR], or any compounds containing them, who express an inability [OR] or a refusal to stop usage;
(8)Regular usage  of folic acid supplements [OR] or compounds containing folic acid in the past 3 months;
(9)Participation in a clinical trial [AND] for a drug that has not yet been officially approved for marketing within one month prior to the first visit.
