Inclusion Criteria:
3-17 years
weight </= 100kg
scheduled for urologic [OR] or orthopedic procedure [AND] necessitating intrathecal morphine
ability to use verbal [OR] or pictorial pain assessment tools and techniques
informed consent and (if applicable) assent
Exclusion Criteria:
Inability to use verbal [OR] or pictorial pain scoring scales
hypersensitivity [AND] to selective 5-HT receptor antagonists
diagnosed congenital long QT syndrome
severe hepatic impairment
pregnancy [OR] or nursing mothers
