Inclusion Criteria:
obese : weight for height > median + 3 standard deviations
simple obesity
Exclusion Criteria:
pathological obesity
chronic diseases e.g. cerebral palsy [OR], metabolic disease, etc.
diseases of red blood cells
on medication e.g. steroid [OR], multivitamins [OR], thiamine-containing vitamins [OR], diuretic drugs
hemodialysis [OR] or peritoneal dialysis
bariatric surgery
