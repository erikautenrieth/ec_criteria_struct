Inclusion Criteria:
Male [OR] and females aged 18 to 70 years
Brain death
Male and females aged 18 to 70 years
Indication of kidney transplantation
Informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
Contra-indication [AND] for multiorgan procurement (infections [OR], cancer, etc)
Preexistent chronic renal failure.
Refusal [AND] for organ procurement by the donor (confirmed by the French national register [OR] or reported by the next-of-kin).
Need for a double kidney transplantation.
Need for a multiorgan transplantation
