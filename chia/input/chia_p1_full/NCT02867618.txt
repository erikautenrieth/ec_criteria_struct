Inclusion Criteria:
Phase I: Patients must have histologically [AND] confirmed R/R  NHL [OR] or HL (defined [AND] by WHO criteria). Patients with chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL) [OR] and small lymphocytic lymphoma (SLL) are eligible. In addition, patients with NHL [NOT] other than diffuse large B cell lymphomas (DLBCL) [AND] must have received at least 2 prior therapies. Patients with DLBCL [OR] and HL [AND] will be eligible if there is [NOT] no available standard therapy.
Phase II: Patients must have histologically confirmed R/R NHL (as defined by WHO criteria). Patients with NHL [AND] [NOT] other than diffuse large B cell lymphomas (DLBCL) [AND] must have received at least 2 prior therapies. Patients with DLBCL [AND] will be eligible if there is [NOT] no available standard therapy.
Must have received front line chemotherapy. No upper limit for the number of prior therapies
Evaluable Disease in the Phase I [OR], and measurable disease in the Phase II
Age > 18 years
ECOG performance status < 2
Patients must have adequate organ and marrow function
Adequate Contraception
Ability to understand and the willingness to sign a written informed consent document
Exclusion Criteria:
1. Prior Therapy Exposure to chemotherapy [OR] or radiotherapy [OR] within 2 weeks prior to entering the study or those who have [NOT] not recovered [AND] from adverse events [AND] due to agents administered more than 2 weeks earlier. Systemic steroids that have [NOT] not been stabilized (≥ 5 days) [AND] to the equivalent of ≤10 mg/day prednisone prior to the start of the study drugs. No other investigational agents are allowed.
2. History of allergic reactions to TGR-1202 [OR] or carfilzomib
3. Uncontrolled inter-current illness
4. Pregnant [AND] women
5. Nursing [AND] women
6. Current malignancy [OR] or history of a prior malignancy
7. Patient known to be Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV)-positive
8. Active Hepatitis A [OR], Hepatitis B [OR], or Hepatitis C infection
