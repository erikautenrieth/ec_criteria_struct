Inclusion Criteria:
(1) cases of infertility, older than 20 years of age and not older than 40 years.
(2) Body mass index (BMI):20-29.
(3) women have experienced two or more implantation  failure attributed [AND] to inadequate endometrial development.
Exclusion Criteria:
(1) Uterine abnormalities (e.g. septate [OR], bicornuate [OR] and fibroid uterus [OR], Asherman Syndrome).
Concurrent use of organic nitrites and nitrates.
Severe hepatic impairment.
Severe renal impairment.
Hypotension.
Recent stroke [OR] or heart attack.
