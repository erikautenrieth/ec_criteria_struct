Inclusion Criteria:
Understand and communicate with the investigator, comply with the requirements of the study and give a written, signed and dated informed consent
Male or non-pregnant [OR], non-lactating female patients at least 18 years of age
Diagnosis of moderate to severe Ankylosing Spondylitis (AS) [AND] with prior documented radiologic evidence [AND] fulfilling the Modified New York criteria for AS
Active AS assessed [AND] by total Bath Ankylosing Spondylitis Disease Activity index (BASDAI) = 4 (0-10) at baseline
Spinal pain as measured [AND] by BASDAI question #2 = 4 cm (0-10 cm) at baseline
Total back pain [AND] as measured by visual analog scale (VAS) = 40 mm (0-100 mm) at baseline
Patients should have been on non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) at the maximum tolerated dose for at least 4 weeks prior to their Baseline Visit, [AND] with an inadequate response  or for less than 4 weeks if withdrawn for [OR] intolerance [OR], toxicity or contraindications
Stable dose of NSAIDs [OR] including Cyclooxygenase-1 (COX-1) or Cyclooxygenase-2 (COX-2) inhibitors [OR] for at least 2 weeks before their Baseline Visit
Patients who have been on a tumor necrosis factor alpha (TNFa) inhibitor (not more than one) must have experienced an inadequate response [OR] to previous [OR] or current treatment given at an approved dose for at least 3 months prior to baseline or had been intolerant [AND] upon administration of an anti-TNFa agent
Total ankylosis of the spine
Use of other investigational drugs within 5 half-lives of enrollment, or within 4 weeks before the Baseline Visit, whichever is longer.
History of hypersensitivity to any of the study drugs [OR] or its excipients or to drugs of similar chemical classes.
Chest x-ray [OR], computerized tomography (CT) scan [OR], or chest magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) with evidence of ongoing infectious [OR] or malignant process, obtained within 3 months prior to screening and evaluated by a qualified physician.
Previous exposure to secukinumab [OR] or any other biologic drug [AND] directly targeting Interleukin-17 (IL-17) [OR], Interleukin-12/23 (IL-12/23) [OR], or the IL-17 receptor [OR], or any other biologic immunomodulating agent [AND], [NOT] except those targeting TNFa
Patients who have taken more than one anti-TNFa agent
Any intramuscular [OR] or intravenous corticosteroid injection within 2 weeks before baseline
Any therapy by intra-articular injections [AND] (e.g. corticosteroid) within 4 weeks before baseline
Previous treatment with any cell-depleting therapies
Patients taking high potency opioid analgesics (e.g., methadone [OR], hydromorphone [OR], morphine)

NA
