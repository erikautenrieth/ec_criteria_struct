Inclusion Criteria:
Inpatient [OR] or outpatient age 8-19 years inclusive; participants must live with a parent, guardian, or caregiver;
Fluent in English;
Diagnosed or told by a clinician that they have any of the following bipolar spectrum disorders (BSD): bipolar I [OR], bipolar II [OR], unspecified bipolar and related disorders [OR], Disruptive Mood Dysregulation Disorder [OR] (DMDD), cyclothymic disorder [OR], other specified bipolar and related disorders [OR], as well as mood disorder not otherwise specified (if diagnosed in the past as per DSM-IV);
Body mass index >85%ile for age and sex by standard growth charts;
Received a new or ongoing prescription for at least one SGA (i.e., olanzapine [OR], clozapine [OR], risperidone [OR], quetiapine [OR], aripiprazole [OR], ziprasidone [OR], iloperidone [OR], lurasidone [OR], paliperidone [OR], brexpiprazole [OR] or cariprazine) that is not prescribed as a PRN medication;
Exclusion Criteria:
Patients will be excluded if they have had exposure to a total daily dose of MET 1000 mg bid for at least 2 weeks in the past 3 months;
Patients will be excluded if they could not tolerate  MET [AND] during the recommended titration schedule outlined in the protocol;
Major neurological or medical illnesses that affect weight gain (e.g., unstable thyroid disease) [OR] or require a systemic medication that might impact weight or glucose regulation (e.g., diabetes mellitus [OR] [insulin] [OR], chronic renal failure [OR] [steroids]);
Fasting glucose = 126 mg/dL on 2 occasions during screening indicating need for prompt treatment;
If lab results are available in the last 6 months, then a serum creatinine =1.3 mg/dL on 2 occasions during screening and/or follow-up, indicating potential impairment of renal functioning;
Pregnant or breast feeding;
Children and caregivers who are unable to complete assessments for any reason;
