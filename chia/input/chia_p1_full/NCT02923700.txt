Inclusion Criteria:
patients affected by mono-lateral symptomatic knee articular degenerative pathology with history of chronic (for at least 4 months) pain [OR] or swelling;
imaging findings of degenerative changes [AND]  of the joint (osteoarthritis [OR] or chondropathy with Kellgren Lawrence Score [AND] from 0 to 3 at X-ray evaluation).
Exclusion Criteria:
age > 80 years;
Kellgren-Lawrence score [AND] at X-ray evaluation > 3;
major axial deviation (varus >5° [OR], valgus > 5°),
systemic disorders such as diabetes [OR], rheumatoid arthritis [OR], haematological diseases (coagulopathy) [OR] , severe cardiovascular diseases [OR], infections [OR], immunodepression;
patients in therapy with anticoagulants [OR] or antiaggregants;
use of NSAIDs in the 5 days before blood donation;
patients with Hb values < 11 g/dl [OR]  and platelet values < 150,000/mmc.
