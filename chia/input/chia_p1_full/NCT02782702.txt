Inclusion Criteria:
Confirmed diagnosis (clinical and histological  features) [AND] of Hailey Hailey [OR] or Darier diseases.
Moderate to very severe lesions located in large folds
Patient aged 18 ans or more
Patient with health coverage
Patient who have signed the consent form
Patient proficient into filling out the questionnaires.
Exclusion Criteria:
Hypersensibility to toxin [OR] or excipients
Myastheny
Deglutition's problems
Past medical history of dysphagia [OR] or aspiration pneumonia
Pregnancy  (positive B-HCG test performed a maxima 72h before) [OR] or breastfeeding
Mental [OR], physical incapacity [AND] to fill in the questionnaires
Guardianship patients
Skin infections [AND] at the inclusion visit 
Application in the last 7 days at the site of injection of local treatments ([NOT] apart emollients [OR] or antiseptics) or injections [OR] of botulism toxin or dynamic phototherapy [OR] or laser in the last 6 months.
Systemic treatment [AND] with aminosides in the last 15 days
Inclusion in another study in the last 2 months.
