Inclusion Criteria:
Age = 65 years [AND] with one additional stroke  risk factor (hypertension [OR], diabetes [OR], heart failure history of [OR] or left ventricular ejection fraction <0.40), previous stroke [OR] or transient ischemic attack).
Atrial fibrillation and not [AND] on oral anticoagulation (OAC) therapy but eligible
Atrial fibrillation on sub-optimal OAC
Exclusion Criteria:
Uncontrolled hypertension (defined as average SBP = 160 mmHg [2 readings taken at time of screening]).
End stage renal disease (CrCl < 15 ml/min)
Valvular Heart Disease  including those with prosthetic valve [OR], mitral stenosis [OR] (moderate [OR] to severe) [OR] or valve repair.
Excess alcohol intake (males : = 28 units/week [OR], females: = 21 units/week. One unit of alcohol = 8 oz beer, 1 oz hard liquor or 4 oz wine).
Intracranial bleed at any point.
History of "Major Bleeding" at any point (defined as overt bleeding  at a critical site including intracranial [OR], intraspinal [OR], intraocular [OR], pericardial [OR], or retroperitoneal [OR]; or bleed [AND] requiring hospitalization).
Foreshortened life-expectancy [OR] or severe comorbidities precluding study follow-up period
Unable to read/understand English
Severe cognitive impairment (defined as score = 5 on the Short Portable Mental Status Questionnaire)
