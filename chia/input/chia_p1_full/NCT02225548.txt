Inclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis of idiopathic Parkinson's disease that is optimally treated (motor fluctuations <20% of subject's awake time). Subjects may be on levodopa therapy but must be stable at the time of entry into the study
Sexually active (i.e. =1 attempt/week) males, 40 - 64 years of age (inclusive) at time of screening
Diagnosis of moderate erectile dysfunction (defined according to the NIH Consensus Development Panel on Impotence) for more than 6 months and demonstrating and incomplete response [AND] to tadalafil alone
Subject demonstrating an IIEF-5 drug-free baseline score that is = 10 but = 16, and an IIEF-5 tadalafil-alone baseline score that is = 18
Subject in a stable heterosexual relationship for at least 6 months. (2)
Subject motivated to seek treatment for erectile dysfunction.
Subject with a total serum testosterone level = 300 ng/dL, with or without supplementation
Hoehn and Yahr Scale score of 1 - 3
Patient able to consent and comply with protocol requirements
Exclusion Criteria:
Subject [NOT] unwilling to cease use of any treatment [AND] for erectile dysfunction during the study, including oral medication [OR], vacuum devices [OR], constrictive devices [OR], injections [OR], urethral suppositories [OR], gels [OR], any over-the-counter [OR] or nonprescription medications, and products purchased via the internet
Subject receiving dopamine agonists [OR], nitrates [OR], alpha-receptor blocking agents [OR], or antihypertensive medication (see other exclusionary medications listed below)
Subject with a history of syncope within the last 6 months prior to screening
Subject with symptomatic postural hypotension (severe dizziness [OR] or fainting
Subject with hypotension [AND] and a resting systolic blood pressure of < 90 mmHG or hypertension with a resting systolic blood pressure [OR] > 170 mmHG or a resting diastolic blood pressure > 110 mmHG
Subject with any underlying cardiovascular condition, including unstable angina pectoris, which [NOT] preclude sexual activity
Subject with a history of myocardial infarction [OR], stroke [OR] or life-threatening arrhythmia within 6 months prior to screening
Subject with uncontrolled atrial fibrillation [OR]/flutter at screening (defined as ventricular response rate = 100 bpm)
Subject with a bleeding disorder
Subject with a history of prostatectomy [AND] because of prostate cancer, including nerve sparing techniques. Subjects with a history of [NOT] surgical procedures for the treatment of benign prostate hypertrophy [AND] are permitted, with the [NOT] exception of cryosurgery [OR], cryotherapy [OR] or cryoablation
Subject with hereditary degenerative retinal disorders such as retinitis pigmentosa
Subject with a history of loss of vision [OR] because of non-arteritic anterior ischemic optic neuropathy (NAION), history of temporary [OR] or permanent loss of vision, including unilateral loss of vision
Subject with a history of congenital QT prolongation
Subject with a penile anatomical abnormality (e.g., penile  fibrosis [OR], fractures [OR], or Peyronie's disease) which, in the investigator's opinion [AND], could significantly impair sexual performance. This will be based on subject's reported medical history (penile exam [NOT] not required)
Subject with primary hypoactive sexual desire.
Subject with a spinal cord injury
Subject with a severe chronic [OR] or acute liver disease, history of moderate (Child-Pugh [AND] B), or severe (Child-Pugh [AND] C) hepatic impairment
Subject with clinically significant chronic hematological disease which could lead to priapism such as sickle cell anemia [OR], multiple myeloma [OR], and leukemia
Subject with active peptic ulceration
Subject with a history of malignancy within the past 5 years ([NOT] other than squamous [OR] or basal cell skin cancer)
Subject with a history of a positive test for [OR] Hepatitis B surface antigen (HbsAg) or Hepatitis C
Subject with a known hypersensitivity [AND] to any component of the investigational medications [OR], monoamine oxidase inhibitors [OR], phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitors [OR] or phenylethylamines
Subjects with a history of drug [OR] or alcohol abuse within the past 6 months
Subjects currently consuming =5 units of alcohol per day
Subject who is illiterate or [NOT] unable to understand the [OR] Informed Consent Form, questionnaires or subject diary
Subject who, in the opinion of the investigator, [AND] will be noncompliant with the visit schedule or study procedures
Subject with any unstable medical [OR], psychiatric [OR], or substance abuse disorder that in the opinion of the investigator [AND] is likely to affect the subject's ability to complete the study [OR] or preclude the subject's participation in the study
Diagnosis of any other neurologic disease
Uncontrolled Diabetes (Hemoglobin A1C > 7.5)
