Inclusion Criteria:
H pylori infection  failed after [AND] at least two eradication therapies
aged 20 years or greater
willingness to receive rescue therapy
Exclusion Criteria:
aged less than 20 years
history of gastric resection surgery
history of allergy [AND] to study drugs
pregnancy [OR] or lactating women
severe underlying illness, such as end stage renal disease [OR], decompensated liver cirrhosis [OR], or non-curative malignancy
