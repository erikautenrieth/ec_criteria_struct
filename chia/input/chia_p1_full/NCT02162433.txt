Inclusion Criteria:
Patients between 3 to 16 years of age undergoing adenotonsillectomy, with or without myringotomy [OR] or myringoplasty
ASA 1 & 2
Exclusion Criteria:
Known allergy [OR] or hypersensitivity [AND] reaction to dexmedetomidine
Organ dysfunction (renal [OR]/hepatic failure [OR] or leukemia)
Cardiac disease (congenital [OR] or acquired)
Airway [OR] or thoracic malformation
Cerebral palsy
Hypotonia
Need for premedication
Current [OR]/recent upper respiratory infection (within four weeks prior to the surgery)
Asthma
Allergy [OR] or intolerance to clonidine
Non-English speaking [OR] parents/patients.
