Inclusion Criteria:
Pediatric patients with deep dental decay in primary molars
Teeth with signs and symptoms of reversible pulpitis
Exclusion Criteria:
Teeth with clinical symptoms of irriversible pulpitis [OR] or pulp necrosis [OR] or acute dental infection
Children with systemic illness [AND] that contraindicated [AND] vital pulp treatment such a sickle cell disease
Teeth that are not restorable
