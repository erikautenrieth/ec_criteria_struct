Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with a histologically [OR] or cytologically proven diagnosis of NSCLC
Unresectable (locally advanced) stage IIIa [OR] or IIIb disease
Initial radiotherapy field of treatment to encompass greater than [OR] or equal to 30% of the esophagus
Life expectancy greater than [OR] or equal to 6 months
Estimated weight loss less than [OR] or equal to 10% in the 3 months before study randomization
Measurable disease
18 years of age or older
Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 - 2
Hemoglobin (hgb) greater than [OR] or equal to 10 g/dL [NOT] without transfusional support [OR] or growth factor use in the 4 weeks before study randomization
Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) greater than [OR] or equal to 1.5 x 10^9/L [NOT] without growth factor use in the 2 weeks before study randomization
Platelet count greater than [OR] or equal to 100 x 10^9/L
Serum bilirubin less than [OR] or equal to 1.5 x institutional upper limit of normal (ULN)
Serum creatinine less than or equal to 2.0 mg/dL [OR] (Note: Patients with a serum creatinine greater than [OR] or equal to 1.4 and less than [OR] or equal to 2.0 mg/dL must demonstrate a 24-hour urinary creatinine clearance greater than [OR] or equal to 50 mL/min)
Females [AND] of childbearing potential: negative serum [OR] or urine pregnancy test
Patient must give written informed consent before participating in any study-specific [OR] procedure [OR], randomization [OR], or receiving investigational product.
Patients with reproductive capability [AND] must agree to practice adequate contraception methods.
Exclusion Criteria:
Metastatic disease (M1)/stage 4 NSCLC
Pleural [OR] or pericardial effusion greater than 100 ml in volume as documented by appropriate imaging (positron emission tomography [PET] [OR], computed tomography [CT] scan [OR] or ultrasound). If an effusion greater than 100 ml is documented by cytology to be free from malignancy and the investigator feels the patient is capable of receiving chemo/radiotherapy for their primary disease/ NSCLC, the investigator should discuss the patient with the study physician at Amgen. Effusions smaller than 100 ml would be acceptable, unless the investigator suspects that the effusion is malignant, in which case the effusions should be evaluated by cytology. Sponsor approval must be obtained before patient is randomized.
Plan to remove the tumor surgically before completing the protocol chemo/radiotherapy course
Shielding of any part of the esophagus during radiotherapy (including posterior spinal cord shielding)
Prior chemotherapy [OR], radiotherapy [OR], or surgery for NSCLC
Prior invasive malignancy during the past 3 years [NOT] other than non-melanomatous skin cancer. Note: Patients with prior [NOT] surgically-cured malignancies [eg, stage I breast cancer or prostate cancer, in-situ carcinoma of the cervix, etc] are not excluded; however, sponsor approval must be obtained before patient is randomized.
Presence or history of dysphagia [OR] or conditions predisposing to dysphagia (eg, uncontrolled gastroesophageal reflux disease [GERD] [OR], dyspepsia, etc)
History of pancreatitis
Four weeks or less since completion of treatment [AND] using an investigational  product [OR]/device  in another clinical study [OR] or presence of any unresolved toxicity [AND] from previous treatment
Previous treatment [AND] on this study or with a fibroblast growth factor
Known to be sero-positive for  human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) [OR], hepatitis C virus (HCV) [OR], or hepatitis B virus (HBV)
Pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding women
Known sensitivity [AND] to E. coli derived products
Compromised ability of the patient to give written informed consent [OR] and/or to comply with study procedures
Refusal to sign an informed consent form  to participate in this study [OR], and sign the hospital information release form, if applicable
Unwilling or unable to complete the patient reported outcome (PRO) questionnaires
Psychological, social, familial, or geographical reasons that would prevent regular follow-up
