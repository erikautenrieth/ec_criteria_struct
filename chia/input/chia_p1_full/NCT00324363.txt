Inclusion Criteria:
Treated with a stable dose of one of the following for at least 3 months prior to screening: * >=1000 mg/day [AND] immediate-release metformin [OR]; or metformin >=1000 mg/day [AND] and sulfonylurea [OR]; or sulfonylurea [AND]/metformin [AND] combination therapy.
HbA1c between 7.1% and 11.0%, inclusive.
Body Mass Index (BMI) >21 kg/m^2 and <35 kg/m^2.
Exclusion Criteria:
Have participated in this study previously [OR], or any other study using exenatide [OR] or GLP-1 analogs.
Have participated in an interventional [OR], medical [OR], surgical [OR], or pharmaceutical study within 30 days of screening.
Have characteristics contraindicating metformin [OR] or sulfonylurea use.
Have been treated with exogenous insulin for more than 1 week within the 3 months prior to screening.
Have used drugs for weight loss within 1 month of screening.
