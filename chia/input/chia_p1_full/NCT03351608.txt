Inclusion Criteria:
Be categorized as American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) Physical Status Class 1 [OR], 2 [OR], or 3.
Have a planned non-emergent surgical procedure [OR] or clinical situation (e.g., intubation) that requires moderate [OR] or deep NMB with either rocuronium [OR] or vecuronium.
Have a planned surgical procedure [OR] or clinical situation that would allow objective neuromuscular monitoring techniques to be applied with access to the arm for neuromuscular transmission monitoring.
Age between 2 to <17 years at Visit 2.
If female, may participate if she is [NOT] not pregnant, [NOT] not breastfeeding, and at least one of the following: 1) [NOT] Not a woman of childbearing potential (WOCBP) [OR]; or 2) A WOCBP who agrees to follow the study contraceptive guidance during the treatment period [OR] and for at least 7 days after the last dose of study treatment.
Exclusion Criteria:
Has any clinically significant condition [OR] or situation (eg, anatomical malformation that complicates intubation) [AND] [NOT] other than the condition being studied that, in the opinion of the investigator [OR] , would interfere with the trial evaluations [OR] or optimal participation in the trial.
Has a neuromuscular disorder that may affect  NMB [OR] and/or trial assessments.
Is dialysis-dependent [OR] or has (or is suspected of having) severe renal insufficiency (defined as estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) <30 ml/min).
Has or is suspected of having a family [OR] or personal history of malignant hyperthermia.
Has or is suspected of having an allergy to study treatments [OR] or its/their excipients [OR], to opioids [OR]/opiates [OR], muscle relaxants [OR] or their excipients [OR], or other medication(s) used during general anesthesia.
Has received or is planned to receive toremifene [OR] and/or fusidic acid via IV administration within 24 hours before [OR] or within 24 hours after administration of study treatment.
Has been previously treated with sugammadex [OR] or has participated in [AND] a sugammadex clinical trial.
Is currently participating in [OR] or has participated in an interventional clinical trial with an investigational compound [OR] or device within 30 days of signing the informed consent/assent for this current trial.
