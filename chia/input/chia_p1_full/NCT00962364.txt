Inclusion Criteria:
acute myocardial infarction or
ischemic cardiomyopathy with or without previous myocardial infarction or
dilated cardiomyopathy  due to valvular heart disease[OR], hypertensive heart disease [OR], history of myocarditis [AND] ([NOT] no active myocardial infection present)
Exclusion Criteria:
none, all patients meeting the inclusion criteria will be eligible.
