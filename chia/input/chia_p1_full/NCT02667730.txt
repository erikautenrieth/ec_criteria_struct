Inclusion Criteria:
Acquired acute ankle injury (injured less than 48 hours ago);
Clinical diagnosis of a Grade I  [OR] or II ankle sprain
Is eligible to receive comprehensive medical care from Garrison Petawawa
Exclusion Criteria:
Diagnosis of ankle fracture [OR] or ligament rupture
Has planned release from the Canadian Armed Forces within one year;
Documented restrictions on military duties
Has known intolerance [OR] or documented adverse reaction to acetaminophen [OR] or naproxen [OR] or celecoxib
Documented history of liver [OR] or kidney problems
pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding
