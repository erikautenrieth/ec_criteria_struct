Inclusion Criteria:
Age 18 years or older
Blunt [OR] or penetrating trauma
Requires VTE [AND] thromboprophylaxis
High-risk for VTE
Exclusion Criteria:
Presence of VTE upon admission
Pregnant or nursing
Inability to give informed consent by patient or healthcare proxy
Contraindication [AND] to enoxaparin
Contraindication [AND] to aspirin
Epidural [OR] or subdural hematoma
Presence [OR], or removal [OR] within the last 12 hours, of an epidural or spinal catheter, or recent (within the last 12 hours) epidural [OR] or spinal anesthesia/procedures
