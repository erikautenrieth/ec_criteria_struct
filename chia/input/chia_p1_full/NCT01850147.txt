Inclusion Criteria:
Histologic [AND] or cytologic  diagnosis [AND] of stage IIIB/IV NSCLC
ECOG PS: 0,1
Unidimensional or bi-dimensional measurable disease
Receive prior treatment including first-line platinum-based chemotherapy [OR], standard second-line chemotherapy [OR] and 1 EGF/EGFR inhibitor
Evidence of disease progression
Life expectancy >12 weeks
Neutrophils > 1.5 109/l, Platelets > 100 109/l, Hemoglobin > 9g/dl, Total bilirubin < 1.5 UNL, AST (SGOT) and ALT (SGPT) < 2.5 UNL, Alkaline phosphatases < 5 UNL, Creatinine < 1 UNL
Exclusion Criteria:
Pre-existing hemoptysis of a severity > grade 3 [AND]  by NCI CTCAE criteria within 4 weeks prior to study entry
Uncontrolled hypertension
CHF [OR], angina [OR] or arrhythmias
LVEF < 1 UNL
Existing a second malignancy within 5 years
Infected with HIV
