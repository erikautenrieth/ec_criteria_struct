Inclusion Criteria:
Above 18 years of age
Symptomatic, permanent AF of at least three months duration
Resting heart rate =80 bpm
Signed informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
Congestive heart failure
Ischemic heart disease
Hypotension (Systolic blood pressure <100 mmHg)
Treatment with class  I [OR] or III antiarrhythmic drugs
Severe hepatic [OR] or renal failure
Pregnancy [OR] or lactation
Hypersensitivity [OR] or contradictions [AND] to study drugs
Atrio-ventricular conduction disturbances
Thyrotoxicosis
Life limiting disease [OR] or substance abuse which may affect participation
