Inclusion Criteria:
Patients after throat surgeries: tonsillectomy, adenotonsillectomy, uvulopalatoplasty, uvulopalatopharyngoplasty
Patients with acute throat diseases: pharyngitis [OR], tonsillitis [OR], pharyngotonsillitis
Exclusion Criteria:
Purulent infection
Refusal to participate
Allergy [AND] to tested material
