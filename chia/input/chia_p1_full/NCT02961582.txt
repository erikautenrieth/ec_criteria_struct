Inclusion Criteria:
An average defecation frequency (DF) [AND]  of <3 per week based on a 3-week defecation diary (patient-reported)
Meet at least one other criterion [AND] of the Rome-IV criteria for idiopathic constipation [AND] based on the 3-week defecation diary (1)
Refractory to conservative treatment
Age: 14-80 years
Straining [AND] during =25% of defecations
Lumpy [OR] or hard stools [AND] in =25% of defecations
Sensation of incomplete evacuation [AND] for =25% of defecations
Sensation of anorectal obstruction [OR]/blockage [AND] for =25% of defecations
Manual manoeuvres [AND] to facilitate =25% of defecations
Exclusion Criteria:
Obstructed outlet syndrome [AND] (objectified by defeacography)
Irritable bowel syndrome (Rome-IV criteria [AND] for irritable bowel syndrome)
Congenital [OR] or organic bowel pathology
Rectal prolapse
Anatomical limitations [NOT] preventing placement of an electrode
Skin [OR] and perineal disease with risk of infection
Previous large bowel [OR]/rectal surgery
Stoma
Coexisting neurological disease
Significant psychological co-morbidity as assessed subjectively by the investigator
Being or attempting to become pregnant during study follow-up
