Inclusion Criteria:
Inclusion Criteria Patients: Fulfilling the diagnostic criteria of schizophrenia [OR] or schizoaffective disorder according to ICD-10 (International Classification of Diseases version 10) [OR] or DSM-IV/V (Diagnostic and Statistical Manual version 4 /5), Age 18-45 years, [NOT] Never treated with antipsychotic compounds or central nervous system (CNS) stimulants, Legally competent
Inclusion criteria controls: Matching patients on age (+/- 2 years), sex and parental socioeconomic status, Age 18-45 years,  [NOT] No psychiatric [OR] or physical disease.
Exclusion Criteria:
Exclusion Criteria patients: Substance abuse [OR] on a daily basis during the last 3 month or patients fulfilling the criteria of ongoing substance abuse  due to ICD-10/DSM-IV/V [OR], Treatment with antidepressant [OR] during the last 30 days, Head injury [OR] with more than 5 minutes of unconsciousness [OR], Patients involuntarily  admitted [OR] or treated, Components of metal [OR] implanted by operation, Pacemaker [OR], Pregnancy [OR], Severe physical illness
Exclusion criteria controls: First degree relatives with psychiatric disease [OR], Substance abuse during the last 3 month [OR]  or positive screening of drugs [OR] in urine-sample, Head injury [OR] with more than 5 minutes of unconsciousness, Components of metal [OR] implanted by operation, Pacemaker [OR], Pregnancy [OR], Severe physical illness
