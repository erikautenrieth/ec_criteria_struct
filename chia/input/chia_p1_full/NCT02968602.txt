Inclusion Criteria:
DSM-IV [OR] or DSM-5 diagnosis of schizophrenia [OR] or schizoaffective disorder
Male [OR] or Female
Age: 18 to 65 years
Caucasian [OR] or Non-Caucasian
Smoke at least 10 cigarettes daily
Urine cotinine level ? 100 ng/ml (NicAlert(r) reading ? 3)
Agrees to wear  a head mounted display (HMD) [AND] for up to 45 minutes
Able to complete the Evaluation to Sign Consent (ESC) with minimum score of 80%
Exclusion Criteria:
History of organic brain disease
DSM-IV diagnosis of Alcohol [OR] or Substance Dependence [AND] within the last six months ([NOT] except nicotine) or DSM-5 diagnosis of [NOT] Substance Use Disorder in the last six months ([NOT] except nicotine)
DSM-IV diagnosis of Alcohol [OR] or Substance Abuse  within the last one month ([NOT] except nicotine) [OR] or DSM-5 diagnosis of Substance Use Disorder [AND] in the last six months ([NOT] except nicotine)
Pregnancy [OR] or lactation
Severe liver dysfunction (LFT 3X upper limit of normal)
Previous known hypersensitivity [AND] to tetracyclines
Current treatment with tetracycline [OR] or derivative
Treatment [AND] with oral contraceptives ([NOT] unless a second form of birth control is used and documented)
Treatment with cholestyramine [OR] or colestipol
Treatment [AND] with Urinary alkalinizers (e.g., sodium lactate [OR], potassium citrate)
Treatment [AND] with warfarin
Treatment with bupropion [OR], varenicline [OR], or nicotine replacement products in the month prior to study inclusion
Less than two months treatment [AND] of adjunctive medications AND less than one month on same dose: beta blockers [OR], antidepressants [OR], mood stabilizers [OR], antianxiety medications.
Medical condition whose pathology or treatment would significantly increase the risk associated with the proposed protocol.
History of head injury [OR], seizures [OR], or stroke
Positive urine toxicology screen for substances of non-therapeutic use prior to craving assessments
