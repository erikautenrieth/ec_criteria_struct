Inclusion Criteria:
1. Age 18 years or older
2. Diagnosis of venous leg ulcer(s), [AND] as clinically determined by the investigator by a positive venous reflux test  (venous refilling <20 seconds)  using Doppler ultrasound [AND] for at least 4 weeks prior to screening day, which have [NOT] not adequately responded to conventional ulcer therapy.
3. Designated venous leg ulcer meets the following criteria at both the screening and baseline visits. If the patient has multiple ulcers, at least one ulcer must meet the following criteria at both the screening and baseline visits:
1. Present for at least 4 weeks
2. CEAP Classification Stage 6
3. Surface ulcer [AND] with an area > 15cm2 post debridement
4. Viable, granulating wound [AND] (investigator discretion)
4. Ulcers that extend through the epidermis  but not through the muscle [OR], tendon, [OR]  or bone (Stage II or III ulcers [AND] as defined by the IAET).
5. Female patients of childbearing potential must have a negative pregnancy test  at screening and must agree to use hormonal contraceptive [OR], intrauterine device [OR], diaphragm with spermicide [OR], condom with spermicide [OR], or abstinence throughout until 2 weeks after the last administration of study drug
6. Signed informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
1. Decrease in size of the designated target ulcer(s) by ≥ 30% during the 7-day screening period
2. Cannot tolerate or comply with compression therapy.
3. An ulcer which shows signs of severe clinical infection, defined as pus oozing from the ulcer site
4. An ulcer positive for β-hemolytic streptococci upon culture
5. The ulcer [AND] has > 50% slough, significant necrotic tissue [OR], bone [OR], tendon [OR], or capsule exposure [OR] or avascular ulcer beds
6. Is highly exuding (i.e. requires daily change of dressing)
7. Ankle brachial pressure index <0.65
8. Patients with active systemic infections
9. Patients with clinically significant  medical conditions [AND] as determined by the investigator including renal [OR], hepatic [OR], hematologic [OR], neurologic [OR] or immune disease. Examples include but are not limited to:
1. Renal insufficiency [AND] as an estimated GFR which is < 30 mL/min/1.7m2
2. Abnormal blood biochemistry defined as 3 times that of the upper limit of the normal range.
3. Hepatic insufficiency defined as total bilirubin > 2 mg/dL [OR] or serum albumin < 25 g/L
4. HbA1c > 9%
5. Hemoglobin < 10 g/dL
6. Hematocrit < 0.30
7. Platelet count < 100,000
10. Presence of an active systemic [OR] or local cancer [OR] or tumor of any kind [AND] ([NOT] with the exception of non-melanoma skin cancer)
11. Patients with severe rheumatoid arthritis  (with more than 20 persistently inflamed joints [OR], or below lower normal limit blood albumin level, or evidence of bone and cartilage damage on x-ray, or inflammation in tissues other than joints) and other collagen vascular diseases.
12. Patients with active connective tissue disease
13. Treatment with systemic corticosteroids (>15 mg/day) [OR] , or current immunosuppressive agents
14. Previous [OR] or current radiation therapy or likelihood to receive this therapy during study participation
15. Pregnant [OR] or nursing patients
16. Known prior inability or unavailability to complete required study visits during study participation
17. Significant peripheral edema as per investigator's discretion
18. A psychiatric condition (e.g., suicidal ideation) [OR] or chronic alcohol [OR] or drug abuse problem, determined from the patient's medical history, which, in the opinion of the investigator, may pose a threat to patient compliance
19. Use of a platelet-derived growth factor within 28 days before screening
20. Use of any investigational  drug [OR] or therapy within 28 days before screening
21. Has any other factor which may, in the opinion of the investigator, compromise participation and/or follow-up in the study
