Inclusion Criteria:
Men [OR] and women older than 18 years, scheduled consecutively to perform a coronary procedure [AND] in the department of hemodynamics [AND] of the National Institute of Cardiology "Ignacio Chavez".
Patients may have any of the following indications [AND] for cardiac catheterization: Thoracic pain under study. Stable chronic coronary disease [OR]. Acute myocardial infarctionwith ST segment elevation [OR] , not perfused ([NOT] without timely reperfusion therapy) with less than 4 weeks of evolution. Acute myocardial infarction  with ST-segment elevation [OR], successful thrombolytic therapy [OR], which will undergo drug-invasive therapy. Acute myocardial infarction [OR] [NOT] without ST segment elevation. Unstable angina [OR]. Any acute coronary syndrome [OR], to intervene non-infarct-related artery. Diseaseof any heart valve. Myocarditis [OR] or pericarditis [OR]. Dilated cardiomyopathy [OR]. Patients in renal [OR] or cardiac transplantation protocol for any etiology. [OR] Congenital heart disease that requires knowing the coronary anatomy prior to surgical correction.
The planned procedure can be any of the following: For diagnostic purposes (coronary angiography only [OR], left catheterization [OR] , left and right catheterization [OR]). For therapeutic purposes: percutaneous coronary intervention [AND] (PCI), with or without stent placement.
A priori access must be right [OR] or left radial artery.
Radial arterial pulse [AND] may be present [OR] or absent by palpation.
Modified Allen [OR] or Barbeau test should be positive (presence of collateral palmar flow).
Exclusion Criteria:
Pregnant.
Not have informed consent for the present clinical trial, or do not fully understand the meaning of informed consent.
With acute myocardial infarction [AND] with ST segment elevation in the first 12 hours from the onset of symptoms.
With any acute coronary syndrome complicated with acute pulmonary edema [OR], cardiogenic shock [OR] and / or malignant ventricular arrhythmias.
In which a cardiac catheterization is planned a priori to be performed via femoral [OR], brachial [OR] or ulnar.
Patients in whom first attempt of arterial puncture is performed by 2nd year interventional cardiology fellow or by physician in charge.
Participating in another clinical trial.
Be allergic [OR] or have contraindications to nitroglycerin [OR] or other nitrates.
Any phosphodiesterase 5 inhibitor (sildenafil [OR], tadalafil [OR], avanafil [OR], vardenafil) has been taken within 72 hours prior to the study.
