Inclusion Criteria:
HBsAg-positive for more than 6 months (HBeAg -positive [OR] or HBeAg-negative).
Age > 20 y/o.
Under lamivudine/adefovir treatment for more than 1 year due to previous lamivudine resistance (LAM-R), current HBV DNA is undetectable (< 20 IU/ml) during enrollment.
Exclusion Criteria:
HCV [OR], HIV [OR], HDV coinfection .
Uncontrolled HCC [OR], malignancy [OR] or decompensated liver cirrhosis (CTP score = 7).
Uremia patients [OR] or Creatinine = 2 mg/dl.
