Inclusion Criteria:
Adults patients aged 18 to 85 years
Diagnosed with Major Depressive Disorder [OR], unipolar [OR] or bipolar depression
Undergoing ECT for treatment of their symptoms
Currently residing [AND] in Manitoba
Exclusion Criteria:
Relative contraindications [AND] to ECT therapy (recent MI [OR] or CVA [OR], increased intracranial pressure [OR], intracranial mass lesion [OR], intracranial aneurysm [OR], epilepsy [OR], known cardiac arrhythmia [OR], pheochromocytoma [OR], pregnancy)
Contraindications [AND] to etomidate (sepsis, primary [OR] or secondary adrenal insufficiency, porphyria)
DSM-V diagnosis of a lifetime history of psychotic spectrum disorder
Drug [OR] or alcohol [OR] dependence [OR], or abuse within the past 3 months, soy-bean oil allergy
