Inclusion Criteria:
all adult patients with a nasal [OR] or facial skin/soft tissue defect [AND] requiring reconstruction limited to or including a full-thickness skin graft
Exclusion Criteria:
current [OR] or recent (within one week of surgery) systemic antibiotic use [OR], intolerance  to both clindamycin and cephalexin [OR], discovery of a persistent cutaneous malignancy at the site of the defect following the reconstructive procedure [AND] and previous reconstruction at the site of the skin/soft-tissue defect.
