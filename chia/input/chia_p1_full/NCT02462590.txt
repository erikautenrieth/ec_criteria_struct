Inclusion Criteria:
Adults = 18 years of age
Admitted to any ICU and receiving invasive mechanical ventilation
Anticipated ventilation of =72 hours at the time of screening, as per the ICU physician.
Exclusion Criteria:
Invasively mechanically ventilated >72 hours at the time of screening;
Patients at potential increased risk of iatrogenic probiotic infection (see Section 2.6 for detailed explanation) [AND]  including specific immunocompromised populations (HIV <200 CD4 cells/µL [OR] , those receiving chronic immunosuppressive medications (e.g., azathioprine [OR], cyclosporine [OR], cyclophosphamide [OR], tacrolimus [OR], methotrexate [OR], mycofenolate [OR], Anti-IL2), previous transplantation (including stem cell) at any time [OR] , malignancy requiring chemotherapy  in the last 3 months [OR], neutropenia [AND] [absolute neutrophil count < 500]). However, patients receiving corticosteroids previously [OR]  or presently or projected to receive corticosteroids are not excluded;
Patients at risk for endovascular infection (previously documented rheumatic heart disease [OR], congenital valve disease [OR], surgically repaired congenital heart disease [OR], unrepaired cyanotic congenital heart disease [OR], any intracardiac repair [AND] with prosthetic material [OR] [mechanical [OR] or bio-prosthetic cardiac valves], previous or current endocarditis, permanent endovascular devices [OR] (e.g., endovascular grafts [OR] [e.g., aortic aneurysm repair [OR], stents involving large arteries such as aorta, femorals and carotids], inferior vena cava filters [OR], dialysis vascular grafts), tunnelled (not short-term) hemodialysis catheters [OR], pacemakers or defibrillators. Patients with temporary central venous catheters [OR], central venous dialysis catheters [OR] or peripherally inserted central catheters (PICCs) [OR] are not excluded and patients with coronary artery stents [OR], coronary artery bypass grafts (CABG) [OR] or neurovascular coils  are not excluded [OR]; patients with [NOT] mitral valve prolapse [OR] or bicuspid aortic valve are not excluded providing they have no other exclusion criteria;
Patients with a primary diagnosis of severe acute pancreatitis [AND], [NOT] without reference to a Ranson score [Ranson 1974]). However, patients with mild [OR] or moderate [NOT] pancreatitis are not excluded;
Patients with percutaneous gastric or jejunal feeding tubes already in situ as per Health Canada guidance;
Strict contraindication  or inability [AND] to receive enteral medications;
Intent to withdraw advanced life support as per the ICU physician;
