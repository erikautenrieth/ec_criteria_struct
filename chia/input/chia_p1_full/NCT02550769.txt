Inclusion Criteria:
Age over 18 years
Patients with rectal cancer stage: cT 1-2-3 [OR], cN 0-1 [OR], cM0.
Tumor equal or below 10 cm from the anal verge, candidates to (ETM) low anterior  resection and anastomosis, with [OR] or without preoperative chemo-radiotherapy.
Adenocarcinoma of low or moderate differentiation
ASA I, II, III.
Exclusion Criteria:
Do not sign informed consent
Pregnant patients
Liver cirrhosis
Undifferentiated adenocarcinoma.
cT4
Metastatic disease (M1)
chronic renal failure on dialysis
ASA IV
BMI <18 and> 35 kg / m2
