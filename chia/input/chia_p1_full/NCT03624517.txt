Inclusion Criteria:
Adult males [OR] and females who are 18 years of age or older.
Evidence [OR] or suspicion of upper gastrointestinal bleed (GIB)
Patient with known [OR] or suspected cirrhosis
Upper GIB secondary [AND] to bleeding esophageal varices [AND] as show by esophageal endoscopy, requiring endoscopic band ligation (EBL) at presentation
Willing and able to provide informed consent for study, or have a Legally authorized representative (LAR) provide consent if the patient is unable to do so
Exclusion Criteria:
Known upper gastrointestinal malignancy
Bleeding [AND] from gastric varices, with or without esophageal varices
Use of  any [NOT] other endoscopic method [AND] to stop GI bleeding beyond endoscopic band ligation
Variceal bleeding in the last 90 days
History of transjugular, intrahepatic, portosystemic shunt (TIPS) [OR] or vascular decompression surgery
Pregnant females
Incarcerated individuals
Myocardial infarct [OR], cerebrovascular accident [OR], sepsis [OR], respiratory failure [OR], or severe intercurrent illness within the previous 6 weeks
Non-cirrhotic portal hypertension [AND] causing esophageal varices
Known [OR] or suspected allergy [AND] to octreotide
