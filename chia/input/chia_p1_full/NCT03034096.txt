Inclusion Criteria:
Lobectomy [OR] or pneumonectomy
Esophagectomy
Radical (total) cystectomy
Pancreatectomy
Partial hepatectomy
Hyperthermic intraperitoneal chemotherapy (HIPEC)
Gastrectomy (subtotal [OR] or total)
Cholecystectomy [OR] or bile duct resection
Exclusion Criteria:
Age less than 18 years
American Society of Anesthesiologist Class 5
Projected life expectancy less than 30 days
Known [OR] or suspected hypersensitivity to either propofol [OR], e.g. egg [OR] or soy allergy [OR], or volatile general anesthetic agents
Known [OR] or suspected history of malignant hyperthermia
