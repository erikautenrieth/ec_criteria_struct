Inclusion Criteria:
Successful cardiac ablation [AND] for AF
Documented [NOT] freedom from AF recurrence [AND] (symptomatic or asymptomatic arrhythmic recurrences lasting longer than 30 seconds) 3 months after successful cardiac ablation (AF recurrence during 3-month blanking period is excluded).
Patient must have been on a commercially approved anticoagulation therapy for at least two (2) months prior to randomization in the OAT Study.
CHADS2 score = 2 [OR] or CHA2DS2-VASc score (=3)
Left ventricular ejection fraction > 25%
LA size < 65
High risk for thromboembolic events (i.e., CHADS2 score = 2 [OR] or CHA2DS2-VASc score = 3) and require OAT before undergoing cardiac ablation
Able and willing to comply with all pre- and follow-up testing and requirements
Signed informed consent form
Age 18 years or older
Exclusion Criteria:
OAT required for reasons [NOT] not related to AF (i.e., prosthetic valve [OR], PV stenosis [OR], previous pulmonary embolism [OR], presence of spontaneous echo contrast [SEC] at standard echo performed at 3-months follow-up).
Any cardiac surgery  within the past 60 days (2 months) [OR] or valvular cardiac surgical procedure at any time (i.e., ventriculotomy [OR], atriotomy [OR], and valve [OR] repair [OR] or replacement and presence of a prosthetic valve)
Previous myocardial infarction [OR] (MI) or a percutaneous coronary intervention PCI within the past 3 months
Awaiting cardiac transplantation [OR] or other cardiac surgery within the next 365 days (12 months)
Documented left atrial thrombus
Significant pulmonary disease, (e.g., restrictive pulmonary disease [OR], constrictive or COPD) or any other disease or malfunction of the lungs or respiratory system that produces chronic symptoms
Significant medical problem that in the opinion of the investigator would preclude enrollment in this study
Women who are pregnant (as evidenced by pregnancy test if pre-menopausal)
Acute illness or active systemic infection [OR] or sepsis
Unstable angina
Contraindication [AND] to anticoagulation (i.e., heparin [OR], warfarin or another commercially available anticoagulation medication)
History of blood clotting [OR] or bleeding abnormalities
Life expectancy less than 360 days (12 months)
Uncontrolled Heart Failure [OR] or NYHA Class III [OR] or IV heart failure
Enrollment in a clinical study evaluating another device or drug, within the past 6 months
Unable or unwilling to comply with protocol requirements
