Inclusion Criteria:
Patients presenting for abdominal myomectomy with documented uterine fibroids [AND] on pelvic imaging (pelvic ultrasound [OR] or MRI) within in last 12 months
Age = 18 years and = 50 years
Pre-operative [AND] hemoglobin >8 g/dl
Willing to have buccal administration of misoprostol [OR] or a placebo at least one hour pre-procedure.
Ability to understand [OR] and the willingness to sign a written informed consent.
Admissible medical/surgical history
Can be previously treated with Depo-Lupron [OR], Depo-Provera [OR], or Oral Contraceptive pills
Intraoperative use of vasopressin and uterine tourniquet is permissible
Can have had prior Cesarean delivery
Exclusion Criteria:
Patients who have had a prior abdominal myomectomy
Post-menopausal women
Patients with known bleeding/clotting disorders
[OR] Patients with a history of gynecologic malignancy
History of [AND] allergic reactions attributed to compounds of similar chemical or biologic composition to misoprostol
Any cases converted to abdominal hysterectomy [OR] or other additional elective surgical procedures performed at time of abdominal myomectomy will be excluded from data analysis
Uncontrolled intercurrent illness including, but not limited to, ongoing [OR] or active infection [OR], symptomatic congestive heart failure [OR], unstable angina pectoris [OR], cardiac arrhythmia [OR], or psychiatric illness [OR]/social situations that would limit compliance with study requirements.
