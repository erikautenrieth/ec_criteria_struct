Inclusion Criteria:
Scheduled for bilateral varus rotational osteotomy (VRO) with or without associated soft tissue and osseous procedures
Exclusion Criteria:
Preoperative use of an anticoagulant (Plavix [OR], warfarin [OR], lovenox, etc.)
History of [AND] hypersensitivity  to EACA
History of thromboembolic event (e.g., PE [OR] or DVT)
History of renal  insufficiency [OR] or failure
Congenital [OR] or acquired coagulopathy [OR] as evidence by INR  >1.4 [OR] or PTT > 1.4 times normal, or Platelets  <150,000/mm3 [AND] on preoperative laboratory testing
Use of hormone replacement therapy [OR] or hormonal contraceptive agents within days prior to surgery
Use of acetylsalicylic acid (ASA) [OR], antiplatelet agents within 7 days prior to surgery
Pregnant
Breastfeeding
[NOT] Not received neuraxial anesthesia
