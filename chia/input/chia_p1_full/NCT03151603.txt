Inclusion Criteria:
Women (18-75 years) [AND] with suspected UTI
at least two symptoms of UTI (dysuria, [OR] urgency of micturition, [OR] frequency, [OR] lower abdominal pain)
Written informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
signs of complicated UTI (e. g. temperature [OR] > 38°C, loin tenderness)
conditions [AND] that may lead to complicated infections (i.e. renal diseases [OR], patients [AND] with urinary catheter)
pregnancy/ breastfeeding
current self-medication with UU preparations e.g. z.B. Cystinol® [OR], Uvalysat® [OR], Arctuvan®
antibiotic use in the last 7 days
previous UTI in the past 2 weeks
history of pyelonephritis
contraindications for trial drugs
serious diseases
inability to understand trial Information
current participation in another clinical trial or participation in another clinical trial within the last 4 weeks
