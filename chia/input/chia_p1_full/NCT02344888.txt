Inclusion Criteria:
Infertile  lean women [AND] with PCOS as defined by the Rotterdam criteria.
CC resistance [AND]  (defined as failure of ovulation after receiving 150 mg/day of CC for 5 consecutive days per cycle, for at least 3 consecutive cycles).
Exclusion Criteria:
Age < 20 or > 35 years.
Body mass index (BMI) < 18.5 kg/m2 or > 25 kg/m2.
Presence of any infertility factor [NOT] other than anovulatory PCOS.
Previous history of ovarian surgery [OR] or surgical removal of one ovary.
Previous exposure to cytotoxic drugs [OR] or pelvic irradiation.
Oral hypoglycemic [OR] or hormonal therapy either currently or in the preceding 3 months.
Metabolic [OR] or hormonal abnormalities
