Inclusion Criteria:
ASA classification II or III females
Age: 18-45 years old
BMI = 50 kg/m2
Singleton pregnancy
Simple prophylactic cervical cerclage
Planning neuraxial anesthesia
Exclusion Criteria:
Abdominal and complex cervical cerclage (e.g. bulging bag)
Contraindication [AND] to neuraxial anesthesia
Known hypersensitivity to chloroprocaine (a.k.a. Ester allergy) [OR] , paraaminobenzoic acid (PABA) [OR] or bupivacaine (a.k.a. Amide allergy)
Pseudocholinesterase deficiency
Concomitant use with ergot-type oxytocic drugs
