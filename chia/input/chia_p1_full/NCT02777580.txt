Inclusion Criteria:
Age equal or greater than 70 years
Onset of symptoms < 3 hours prior to randomisation
= 2 mm ST-elevation across 2 contiguous precordial leads (V1-V6) or leads I and aVL for a minimum combined total of = 4 mm ST-elevation or
= 2 mm ST-elevation in 2 contiguous inferior leads (II, III, aVF) for a minimum combined total of = 4 mm ST-elevation
Informed consent received
Exclusion Criteria:
1. Expected performance of PCI < 60 minutes from diagnosis (qualifying ECG) or inability to arrive at the catheterisation laboratory within 3 hours
Previous CABG
Left bundle branch block [OR] or ventricular pacing
Patients with cardiogenic shock [AND] - Killip Class 4
Patients with a body weight < 55 kg (known or estimated)
Uncontrolled hypertension, defined [AND] as sustained blood pressure = 180/110 mm Hg (systolic BP  = 180 mm Hg [OR] and/or diastolic BP = 110 mm Hg) prior to randomisation
Known prior stroke [OR] or TIA
Recent administration of any i.v. or s.c. anticoagulation within 12 hours, including unfractionated heparin [OR], enoxaparin [OR], and/or bivalirudin [OR] or current use of oral anticoagulation (i.e. warfarin [OR] or a NOACs)
Active bleeding [OR] or known bleeding disorder [OR]/diathesis
Known history of central nervous system damage (i.e. neoplasm [OR], aneurysm [OR], intracranial [OR] or spinal surgery) or recent trauma to the head [OR] or cranium (i.e. < 3 months)
Major surgery [OR], biopsy of a parenchymal organ, [OR]  or significant trauma within the past 2 months (this includes any trauma associated with the current myocardial infarction)
Clinical diagnosis associated with increased risk of bleeding including known active peptic ulceration [OR] and/or neoplasm with increased bleeding risk
Prolonged cardiopulmonary resuscitation (> 2 minutes) within the past 2 weeks
Known acute pericarditis [OR] and/or subacute bacterial endocarditis
Known acute pancreatitis [OR] or known severe hepatic dysfunction, including hepatic failure [OR], cirrhosis [OR], portal hypertension (oesophageal varices) [OR]  and active hepatitis
Dementia
Known severe renal insufficiency
Previous enrolment in this study or treatment with an investigational drug or device under another study protocol in the past 7 days
Known allergic reactions to tenecteplase [OR], clopidogrel [OR], enoxaparin [OR] and aspirin
Inability to follow the protocol and comply with follow-up requirements or any other reason that the investigator feels would place the patient at increased risk if the investigational therapy is initiated.
