Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with a confirmed diagnosis of:
1. Stage 4 colon cancer either s/p metastasectomy [OR] or post-initial chemotherapy [OR] or maintenance "standard of care", either involving 5-fluorouracil/leucovorin (5-FU/LV) [OR] alone or continual bevacizumab alone. Patients in maintenance cohort must have had 2 consecutive CT scans showing stable disease and [NOT] not be experiencing significant prior treatment-related toxicity above Grade 1.
2. Pancreas cancer, either s/p resection and adjuvant chemotherapy [OR] or locally advanced pancreas cancer s/p chemotherapy and radiation. Initial chemotherapy [OR] or radiation therapy may have been stopped between 2 weeks and 2 months prior to study start, and patients must have recovered from prior treatment related toxicity to grade 1 or less.
Prior surgery, including tumor resection [OR] or metastasectomy must have been performed at least 4 weeks prior to study enrollment.
No concomitant anti-cancer treatment is allowed
Age >/= 18 years
Performance status of 0-1
Adequate hepatic, bone marrow, and renal function
Partial thromboplastin time (PTT) must be </= 1.5 x upper normal limit of institution's normal range and INR (International Normalized Ratio) < 1.5.
Life expectancy >/= 4 months for maintenance cohorts [OR] and >/= 6 months for adjuvant cohorts
Women of childbearing potential [AND] must have a negative serum pregnancy test within 14 days prior to initiation of treatment and must [NOT] not be lactating.
Subject is capable of understanding and complying with protocol demands and able to sign and date the informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
Women of child-bearing potential, who are biologically able to conceive, [NOT] not employing two forms of highly effective contraception [OR] or who are pregnant.
Women who are breast-feeding
Fertile males unwilling to use contraception
Patients with brain metastases [OR] or any history of brain metastases
Patients who have undergone major surgery  (e.g., intra [OR]-thoracic, -abdominal, or -pelvic) </= 4 weeks prior to starting study treatment [OR] or who have [NOT] not recovered from such therapy
Patients with a history of pulmonary embolism [OR], or untreated deep vein thrombosis within the past 6 months
Impairment of gastrointestinal (GI) function [OR] or GI disease that may significantly alter the absorption of dovitinib
The subject has had another active malignancy within the past 5 years [NOT] except for cervical cancer in situ [OR], in situ carcinoma of the bladder [OR] or non-melanoma carcinoma of the skin.
Patients who have received the last administration of an anticancer therapy [OR] including chemotherapy [OR], immunotherapy [OR], hormonal therapy [OR] and monoclonal antibodies </= 2 weeks prior to starting the study drug, or who have not recovered from the side effects of such therapy
Cirrhosis [OR], chronic active hepatitis [OR] or chronic persistent hepatitis
Patients who are currently receiving prasugrel
[NOT] No concurrent use of isoniazid [OR], labetolol [OR], trovafloxacin [OR], tolcapone [OR], and felbamate
[NOT] No concurrent use of other investigational drugs [OR] or antineoplastic therapies.
Patients with impaired cardiac function [OR] or clinically significant cardiac diseases.
