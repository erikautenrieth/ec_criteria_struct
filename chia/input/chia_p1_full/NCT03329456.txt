Inclusion Criteria:
. Inclusion criteria are American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) physical status I-III, age between 18 and 70 years and body mass index (BMI) between 20 and 35 kg/m2.
Exclusion Criteria:
Exclusion criteria are pregnancy [OR], patients with contraindications to regional anesthesia [OR], allergy to LAs [OR], patients taking opioids regularly due to chronic pain [OR], use of anticoagulation drugs  [NOT] other than acetylsalicylic acid [OR] or dipyridamole [OR], atrioventricular block [OR], diabetes.
