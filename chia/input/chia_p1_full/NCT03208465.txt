Inclusion Criteria:
Men [OR] or women at least 19 years of age
Type 2 diabetes mellitus
Stable coronary artery disease
Global myocardial perfusion reserve (MPR) index < 2.5
The patient or guardian agrees to the study protocol and the schedule of clinical and dynamic SPECT follow-up, and provides informed, written consent, as approved by the appropriate Institutional Review Board/Ethical Committee of the respective clinical site.
Exclusion Criteria:
Contraindications to empagliflozin [OR], Sitagliptin
DPP4 inhibitors [OR] or Sodium-glucose cotransporter-2(SGLT2) inhibitors within the previous 4 weeks
Insulin [AND] requiring diabetes
Poor glucose control (HbA1C>10 %)
Acute coronary syndrome
Stent placement [AND]  within the previous 6 months
Previous coronary artery bypass graft surgery within the previous 6 months
Planned revascularization within 6 months
Heart failure [AND] requiring loop diuretics
Severe left ventricular hypertrophy [OR] (left ventricular septal wall thickness  > 13mm)
Significant renal disease manifested by creatinine clearance of < 30 ml/min)
Hepatic disease [OR] or biliary tract obstruction [OR], or significant hepatic enzyme elevation  (alanine transaminase [OR] or Aspartate Aminotransferase > 3 times upper limit of normal)
Radiopaque material implanted in the chest wall (metal, silicone, etc.)
Contraindication [AND] to adenosine stress test
Any clinically significant abnormality identified at the screening visit, physical examination, laboratory tests [OR], or electrocardiogram which, in the judgment of the Investigator, would preclude safe completion of the study.
Patient's pregnant [OR] or breast-feeding [OR] or child-bearing potential
Expected life expectancy < 1 year
Unwillingness or inability to comply with the procedures described in this protocol
