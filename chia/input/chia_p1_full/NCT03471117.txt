Inclusion Criteria:
CKD  patients [AND] classified as Stage 3 [OR] and 4 of National Kidney Foundation Classification with estimated glomerular filtration rate (GFR) between 15 and 59 mL/min/1.73 m2 according to the Modification of Diet in Renal Disease (MDRD) formula based on serum creatinine, age, gender, and race.
Men [OR] and women 35 to 70 years of age
Exclusion Criteria:
Allergy [AND] to Glitazones
Myocardial infarction
Heart failure
Angina
History of kidney stones
Liver disease (abnormal liver enzymes)
Anemia (hemoglobin <8 g/dl)
Cancer [AND] with current treatment
Previous organ transplantation
Immunosuppressant therapy
Human immunodeficiency virus infection
Pregnancy [OR] or lactating
Current tobacco use
Dilantin [OR] and oral contraceptive [AND] usage due to potential drug interaction [AND] with glitazones
Self-identified history of hypoglycemia
