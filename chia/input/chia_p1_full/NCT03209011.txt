Inclusion Criteria:
HBsAg [OR] and HBeAg positive  for more than 6 months [OR], HBV DNA detectable [OR] with ALT level abnormal lasted for three months [AND]  and at least time190 IU/L or liver puncture biopsy  demonstrated apparent inflammation , [AND] [NOT] never treated before enrolled.
Exclusion Criteria:
Active consumption of alcohol [OR] and/or drugs
Co-infection with human immunodeficiency virus, [OR] hepatitis C virus, [OR] or hepatitis D virus
History of autoimmune hepatitis
Psychiatric disease
Evidence of neoplastic diseases of the liver
