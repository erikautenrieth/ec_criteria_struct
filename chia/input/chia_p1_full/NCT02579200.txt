Inclusion Criteria:
Previous diagnoses of COPD [OR] and HF under optimized clinical treatment [AND] as judged by the accompanying physician
Reduced left ventricular ejection fraction (<50%)
Non-reversible airway obstruction (post-bronchodilator FEV1/FVC < 0.7 and FEV1 < 80 %)
Respiratory muscle weakness (Pi,max < 70cmH2O)
Persistent dyspnea on daily life (Baseline Dyspnea Index focal score <or= 8).
Exclusion Criteria:
Inability to perform [AND] exercise tests
Diagnosed psychiatric [OR] or cognitive disorders
Progressive neurological or neuromuscular disorders [AND] having a major impact on exercise capacity
