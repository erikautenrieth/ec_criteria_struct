Inclusion Criteria:
• All patients attending for a routine diagnostic endoscopic procedure [AND] at St Mary's Hospital NHS Trust for dyspepsia [OR] and abdominal pain
Exclusion Criteria:
Patients attending for a therapeutic endoscopic procedure e.g. variceal banding [OR], stent insertion [OR], balloon dilatation.
Patients with a known diagnosis e.g. upper gastrointestinal cancer
Patients previously treated with HP eradication therapy
Patients who had taken PPI [OR], H2 receptor antagonists [OR] and antibiotics within 4 weeks
Patients with acute gastrointestinal bleeding
Patients who'd had previous gastric surgery
Patients with chronic liver disease
Patients with abnormal coagulation [OR] or any other contra-indication [AND] to use of standard biopsy in routine diagnostic endoscopic procedures
Patients who are unable or unwilling to give informed consent
Patients under the age of 18 years
