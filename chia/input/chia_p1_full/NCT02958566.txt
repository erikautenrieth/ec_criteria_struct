Inclusion Criteria:
Males [OR] or females above the age of 18
Patients undergoing laparoscopic [OR] or robotic colorectal resections
Exclusion Criteria:
History of constipation
Pre-existing use of narcotics [OR] or opioids
Pre-existing renal [OR] or hepatic failure
Mental illness [OR], mental retardation [OR], or inability to participate in informed consent [AND] due to mental status
Pre-existing dementia
Allergy to any protocol medication
Emergency operation
Subjects who are incarcerated or wards of the state
Minors
Subjects with inflammatory bowel disease [OR], active colitis [OR], or pre-existing intra-abdominal inflammation. [NOT] Diverticulitis [NOT] without active infection [OR]/inflammation will not be excluded.
