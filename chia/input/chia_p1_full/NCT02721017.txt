Inclusion Criteria:
scheduled for Nuss procedure [AND] for pectus excavatum correction
at least 13 years old at the time of the procedure
Exclusion Criteria:
age less than 13 years at time of procedure
use of pain medication prior to procedure
pectus carinatum [OR], Poland's syndrome [OR], or any chest wall anomaly [AND] [NOT] other than pectus excavatum
previous repair of pectus excavatum by any technique
previous thoracic surgery
congenital heart disease
bleeding dyscrasia
major anesthetic risk factors [OR] or history of previous problem with anesthesia
pregnancy
inability to communicate in English
