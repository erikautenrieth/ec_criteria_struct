Inclusion Criteria:
Controlled hypertension: systolic BP < 150 and diastolic BP < 90 mmHg  in persons aged 60 years [OR] or older, systolic BP < 140 and diastolic BP < 90 mmHg [AND] in persons 40 through 59 [AND] years according to the JNC 8th guideline
Evidence of diastolic dysfunction [AND] showing E/E' > 10
The patient agrees to the study protocol and the schedule of clinical and echocardiographic follow-up, and provides informed, written consent, as approved by the appropriate Institutional Review Board/Ethical Committee of the respective clinical site
Exclusion Criteria:
Unwillingness or inability to comply with the procedures described in this protocol
Planned cardiac surgery [OR] or planned major [NOT] non-cardiac surgery within the study period.
Stroke [OR] or coronary revascularization in the past 6 months.
Clinically significant pulmonary disease.
Untreated hyperthyroidism [OR], or hypothyroidism.
A diagnosis of cancer ([NOT] other than superficial squamous [OR] or basal cell skin cancer)  in the past 3 years [OR] or current treatment [AND] for the active cancer.
Female of child-bearing potential who do not use adequate contraception and women who are pregnant or breast-feeding
Any clinically significant abnormality identified at the screening visit, physical examination, laboratory tests, or electrocardiogram which, in the judgment of the Investigator, would preclude safe completion of the study.
LV ejection fraction < 50%.
Significant renal disease [AND] manifested by serum creatinine > 2.5 mg/dL
Hepatic disease [OR] or biliary tract obstruction [OR], or significant hepatic enzyme elevation (ALT [OR] or AST > 3 times upper limit of normal).
History of intolerance to ARB [OR] or amlodipine.
Hypertrophic or restrictive cardiomyopathy.
Moderate [OR] or severe valvular disease.
Constrictive pericarditis
Atrial fibrillation [AND] with a heart rate > 120/min.
Sitting systolic BP < 100 mmHg
