Inclusion Criteria:
Planned non-cardiac surgery at least after 12 months of implantation [AND] of drug eluting stent
Low  [OR] or intermediate risk level surgery
Written informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
Acute coronary syndrome within 1 month
Heart failure [AND] NYHA III to IV
Contraindication [AND] to Aspirin
On anticoagulant therapy
Emergent surgery
Cardiac surgery
High bleeding risk surgeries, e.g., Intra-cranial surgery [OR], Intra-spinal surgery [OR], Retinal surgery
Pregnancy [OR] or breast-feeding
Life expectancy less than 1year
