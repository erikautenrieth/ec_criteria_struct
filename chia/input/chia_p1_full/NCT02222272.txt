Inclusion Criteria:
All adult patients with chronic myeloid leukaemia in any phase (chronic [OR], accelerated [OR] or blastic) who undergo allogeneic stem cell transplantation between 01/01/2010 and 30/09/2013 and have been previously treated with Nilotinib [OR] or Dasatinib, regardless of their response to these drugs.
