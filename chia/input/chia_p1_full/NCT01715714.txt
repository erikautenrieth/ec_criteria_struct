Inclusion Criteria:
Patients on chronic statin  treatment (>30 days) [AND] scheduled for isolated CABG, including on- or off-pump or repeat (redo's) revascularisation procedures
Stable [OR] or unstable angina, including non ST-segment-elevation acute coronary syndrome (NSTE-ACS)
Age = 18 years
Written informed consent
Exclusion Criteria:
Any concomitant cardiovascular procedure to CABG (i.e. valve [OR], aortic [OR] or carotid surgery)
Acute ST-segment-elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI)
NSTE-ACS [AND] with cardiogenic shock  warranting emergent salvage surgery within 12 hrs from hospital admission
History of atrial fibrillation [OR] or muscle disease (myopathy)
Current renal  (creatinine >2x upper limit of normal (ULN) [OR], dialysis [OR], kidney transplant) [OR] or hepatic dysfunction (AST [OR]/ALT>2x ULN [OR], liver transplant [OR] or neoplasm)
Inability of oral drug intake
