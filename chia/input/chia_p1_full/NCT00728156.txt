Inclusion Criteria:
Patients with T2DM and CAS as defined below:
Clinical definitions
T2DM: Diagnosed according to the WHO criteria [53].
CAD:Presence of any one of the following: Angina plus positive exercise tolerance test [OR], enzyme [OR] and/or Q wave positive myocardial infarction [OR], angiographic evidence [OR] ( >50% stenosis of one vessel), percutaneous [OR] or surgical coronary revascularisation.
Aged between 18 and 75
Provided written consent for participation in the trial prior to any study-specific procedures or requirements.
Exclusion Criteria:
Contraindication [AND] to Clopidogrel
Smoking (current smokers and patients who quit smoking less than six months)
Malignancy [AND](diagnosed [OR] or under investigation)
Haematological disorders (Anaemia [OR], malignancy [OR], bleeding disorders)
Women [AND] of child-bearing potential
Use of corticosteroids [OR]/other antithrombotic agents(warfarin)
Chronic liver disease (Cirrhosis [OR], malignancy [OR] and patients with more than twice the upper limit of liver function tests)
Unable to consent.
Use of other investigational study drugs within 1 year prior to study entry
Previous participation in this study
