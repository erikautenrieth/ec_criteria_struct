Inclusion Criteria:
18-65 years old
Male [OR] or female
Diagnosed with GAD [AND] according to DSM-IV
HAMA score=17
Provide with written informed consent
Agree to be washed-out for two weeks if receiving SSRI [OR], SNRI [OR] or NASA.
Exclusion Criteria:
Serious suicidal tendency
The score of the sixth item of HAMA =3
The score of HAMD =21
Pregnant [OR] or lactating women
History of allergic [OR] or hypersensitivity to tandospirone
Serious or unstable cardiac [OR], renal [OR], neurologic [OR], cerebrovascular [OR], metabolic [OR], or pulmonary disease
Secondary anxiety disorders
Drug [OR] or alcohol dependence within 1 year
Patients currently taking benzodiazepine drugs
Drivers [OR] and dangerous machine operators
Participated in other clinical studies in the last 30 days
Patients with clinically significant ECG or laboratory abnormalities
Patients with a history of epilepsy
Patients with abnormal TSH concentration
