Inclusion Criteria:
Type I diabetic patients
Parturients presented for Cesarean section
Exclusion Criteria:
Contraindications [AND] for spinal anesthesia (like bleeding diathesis [OR] or regional infection at site of neuroaxial block)
Known allergy to Granisetron [OR] or local anaesthetic (heavy bupivacaine, Marcaine Spinal 0.5% Heavy, 5mg/ml, AstraZeneca ampule)
Pregnancy induced hypertension
Congenital [OR] or rheumatic heart diseases
Antepartum haemorrhage
Fetal destress [OR] or gestational age < 36 week
