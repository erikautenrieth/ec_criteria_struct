Inclusion Criteria:
Men [OR] and women patients, with age ranging 40-80.
Suspected coronary artery disease who are supposed to undergo invasive coronary angiography with appropriate clinical indications
Patients who are willing to sign the informed consent form
Exclusion Criteria:
Contraindication of CT Known allergy to iodinated contrast media [OR]  or history of contrast-induced nephropathy Decreased renal function: elevated serum creatinine(>1.5mg/dl) Contraindication [AND] to beta-blockers Severe arrhythmia: arterial fibrillation [OR] or uncontrolled tachyarrhythmia [OR], or advanced atrioventricular block (second [OR] or third degree heart block)
Contraindication [AND] of MRI Claustrophobia  Metallic hazards [OR] Pacemaker implant [OR] eGFR<30 ml/min
Unstable [OR] or uncooperative patients
Limited life expectancy due to cancer [OR] or end-stage renal [OR] or liver disease
Evidence of severe symptomatic heart failure (NYHA Class III or IV)
Previous myocardial infarction [OR], coronary artery intervention [OR], coronary artery bypass surgery [OR], or other cardiac surgery
