Inclusion Criteria:
Healthy, women ages 18 to 39yo with BMI <30
Regular menstrual cycles [AND] with duration between 24-35 days
Completion of screening visit where ovulation will be assessed with blood draw for progesterone level (must be 5ng/mL or greater)
Not seeking pregnancy during the study period
Use of a non-hormonal form of contraception, such as: sterilization (tubal ligation [OR], Essure) [OR], copper IUD (intrauterine device) [OR], barrier methods [OR] or abstinence
Must speak English or Spanish
Exclusion Criteria:
Currently pregnant [OR] or breastfeeding
Severe pelvic organ prolapse [OR] or prolapse to any degree that may prevent retention of the vaginal ring after insertion
Use of oral contraceptive pills [OR], patches [OR], implants [OR] or hormonal intrauterine contraception in the month prior to screening
Use of depo medroxyprogesterone within 6 months of screening
Use of medications that interact [AND] with  contraceptive steroid hormones: anti-epileptic medications [OR], rifampin [OR], rifabutin [OR], fosamprenavir, etc
Medical condition [AND] with safety deemed to be category 3 or 4 when using a combined hormonal contraceptive, [AND] as determined by the Center for Disease Control Medical Eligibility Criteria: current [OR] or past history of breast cancer, severe decompensated cirrhosis, history of deep vein thrombosis or pulmonary embolus [OR] , diabetes with nephropathy [OR]/retinopathy [OR]/neuropathy [OR] or other [OR] vascular disease diagnosed more than 20 years ago [OR], current symptomatic gallbladder disease [OR], hypertension [OR], ischemic heart disease [OR], known thrombogenic mutations [OR], hepatocellular adenoma [OR], malignant hepatoma [OR], multiple risk factors for atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease [OR], multiple sclerosis  with prolonged immobility [OR] , history of peripartum cardiomyopathy [OR], cigarette smoking and =35yo [OR], history of complicated solid organ transplant [OR], history of stroke [OR], history of superficial venous thrombosis not associated [AND] with catheter [OR], systemic lupus erythematosus  with positive antiphospholipid antibodies, valvular heart disease complicated by pulmonary hypertension [OR] or atrial fibrillation [OR] or bacterial endocarditis [OR], and acute viral hepatitis
