Inclusion Criteria:
Age = 18 years
American Society of Anesthesiologists Classification I-III
Normal cognitive function in order to sign written, informed consent and to understand trial protocol
Agreement to the trial protocol [OR], including the randomized manner
Exclusion Criteria:
Allergy [AND] to LA
Infection in [OR] or near insertion site of the peripheral nerve catheter
Anatomical abnormalities [AND] preventing successful peripheral catheter [AND] insertion
Habitual use of opioids
Pregnancy [OR] or breastfeeding ([NOT] disproved by a negative pregnancy test before trial inclusion)
