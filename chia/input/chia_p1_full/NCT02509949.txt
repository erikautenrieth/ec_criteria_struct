Inclusion Criteria:
age > 17 and < 60 years;
American Society of Anesthesiology (ASA) I-III;
admitted for living donor renal transplantation.
Exclusion Criteria:
Patients with a history of drug abuse;
preoperative history of schizophrenia, epilepsy, parkinsonism, use of cholinesterase inhibitor, inability to communicate in the preoperative period (coma, [OR] profound dementia, or language barrier).
