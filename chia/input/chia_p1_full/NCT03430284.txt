Inclusion Criteria:
35-75 years old;
diagnosed as type 2 diabetes according to the criteria of the World Health Organization in 1999.
Exclusion Criteria:
type 1 diabetes [OR],specific types of diabetes [OR],gestational diabetes [OR] or pregestational diabetes;
acute cardiovascular or cerebrovascular accidents [OR] within past 3 months;
severe hepatic [OR] or renal dysfunction;
malignant tumor;
allergic [OR] history or contraindication [AND] for any drugs in trials;
taking part in other clinical trials;
obviously poor compliance.
