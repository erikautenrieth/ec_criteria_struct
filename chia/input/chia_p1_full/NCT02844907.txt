Inclusion Criteria:
Body Mass Index (BMI) = 35 kg/m2
HbA1c = 5.7%
Ability to speak [OR] and understand English
Exclusion Criteria:
Rheumatoid arthritis
Diabetes [OR] or immediate family history of diabetes
Coronary artery disease
Congestive heart failure
Pulmonary disorders, including COPD [OR] and asthma
Malabsorptive GI disease, such as celiac disease [OR], or gastric bypass
Significant hepatic disease
Renal insufficiency (eGFR < 60 mL/kg/min)
Anemia (hematocrit < 34%) [AND]  as measured at screening visit
Pregnant females
Consumption of daily medications that alter glucose metabolism of GI function (glucocorticoids [OR], psychotropics [OR], narcotics [OR], metoclopramide)
Consumption  [OR] or injection [AND] of insulin
Apparent sensitivity [AND] to any of the study peptides as determined by the skin test
Diagnosis or h/o PTSD [OR], depression [OR], substance use [OR], mental health problems [OR], sleep disorders [OR], HPA disruption [OR] and/or TBI
