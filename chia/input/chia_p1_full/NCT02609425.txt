Inclusion Criteria:
All patients with esophageal cancer who are deemed candidates [AND] for minimally invasive robot assisted Ivor Lewis esophagogastrectomy.
Patients who provide written informed consent for the study.
Exclusion Criteria:
Any patient with esophageal cancer who is [NOT] not deemed a surgical  candidate [OR] or who is [NOT] not deemed a candidate [AND] for the Ivor Lewis technique of esophagectomy ([AND] with intrathoracic anastomosis).
Any patient less than 18 years of age
