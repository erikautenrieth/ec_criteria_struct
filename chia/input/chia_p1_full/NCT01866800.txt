Inclusion Criteria:
Subject is 65 years old who is able and willing to give an informed consent.
Patients undergoing planned trans-femoral TAVI.
Calculated eGFR below 60ml/min/1.73m2 (MDRD)
Exclusion Criteria:
History of acute coronary syndrome in the past 30 days.
History of congesting heart failure with left ventricular ejection fraction  <30% [OR] or exacerbation in the past 30 days.
Current dialysis treatment.
Known furosemide [AND] hypersensitivity.
Contraindications to placement [AND]  of a Foley catheter in the bladder.
