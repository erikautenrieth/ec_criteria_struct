Patients with a confirmed diagnosis of: 
1. Stage 4 colon cancer either s/p metastasectomy [OR] or post-initial chemotherapy [OR] or maintenance "standard of care", either involving 5-fluorouracil/leucovorin (5-FU/LV) [OR] alone or continual bevacizumab alone. Patients in maintenance cohort must have had 2 consecutive CT scans showing stable disease and [NOT] not be experiencing significant prior treatment-related toxicity above Grade 1. 
2. Pancreas cancer, either s/p resection and adjuvant chemotherapy [OR] or locally advanced pancreas cancer s/p chemotherapy and radiation. Initial chemotherapy [OR] or radiation therapy may have been stopped between 2 weeks and 2 months prior to study start, and patients must have recovered from prior treatment related toxicity to grade 1 or less. 
Prior surgery, including tumor resection [OR] or metastasectomy must have been performed at least 4 weeks prior to study enrollment. 
No concomitant anti-cancer treatment is allowed 
Age >/= 18 years 
Performance status of 0-1 
Adequate hepatic, bone marrow, and renal function 
Partial thromboplastin time (PTT) must be </= 1.5 x upper normal limit of institution's normal range and INR (International Normalized Ratio) < 1.5. 
Life expectancy >/= 4 months for maintenance cohorts [OR] and >/= 6 months for adjuvant cohorts 
Women of childbearing potential [AND] must have a negative serum pregnancy test within 14 days prior to initiation of treatment and must [NOT] not be lactating. 
Subject is capable of understanding and complying with protocol demands and able to sign and date the informed consent
