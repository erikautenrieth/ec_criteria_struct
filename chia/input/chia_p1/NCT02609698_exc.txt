Patients with any contraindications [OR] or hypersensitivity [AND] related to antiplatelet therapy
Patients with Acute Myocardial Infarction (ST elevation myocardial infarction [OR], Non ST elevation myocardial infarction)
Patients who are anticipated to receive treatment [OR] or surgery [AND] that may require desisting the administration of antiplatelet therapy for 2 weeks or longer during the period of the clinical trial
Chronic total occlusion  (CTO) lesions [OR], in-stent restenosis (ISR)
Patients experiencing cardiogenic shock
Women who are breastfeeding, pregnant, or desiring pregnancy
Patients with findings of hemorrhage
Patients with a life expectancy of less than 1 year
Patients who have received a drug-eluting stent (DES) procedure within the past 6 months
Any other patients judged by the investigator to be unsuitable for the trial
