Individuals with a history of malignancy are eligible if they have been disease-free for at least 5 years and are deemed by the investigator to be at low risk for recurrence of that malignancy. Individuals with the following cancer are eligible if diagnosed and adequately treated within the past 5 years: cervical [OR] or breast cancer in situ [OR], and basal cell [OR] or squamous cell carcinoma of the skin
