Diagnosis of primary progressive MS
Inability to complete an MRI (contraindications [AND] for MRI include but are not restricted to weight =140 kg [OR], pacemaker [OR], cochlear implants [OR], presence of foreign substances in the eye [OR], intracranial vascular clips [OR], surgery within 6 weeks of entry into the study [OR], coronary stent implanted [AND] within 8 weeks prior to the time of the intended MRI, etc…)
Gadolinium [AND] intolerance
History of ischemic cerebrovascular disorders (e.g., stroke [OR], transient ischemic attack) [OR] or ischemia of the spinal cord
History or known presence of central nervous system (CNS) [OR] or spinal cord tumor (e.g., meningioma [OR], glioma)
History or known presence of potential metabolic causes [AND] of myelopathy (e.g., untreated vitamin B12 deficiency)
History or known presence of infectious causes [AND] of myelopathy (e.g., syphilis [OR], Lyme disease [OR], human T-lymphotropic virus 1 (HTLV-1) [OR], herpes zoster myelopathy)
History of genetically inherited progressive CNS degenerative disorder (e.g., hereditary paraparesis [OR]; MELAS [mitochondrial myopathy, encephalopathy, lactic acidosis, stroke] syndrome)
Neuromyelitis optica
History or known presence of systemic autoimmune disorders [AND] potentially causing progressive neurologic disease (e.g., lupus [OR], anti-phospholipid antibody syndrome [OR], Sjogren's syndrome [OR], Behçet's disease [OR], sarcoidosis)
History of severe, clinically significant brain [OR] or spinal cord trauma (e.g., cerebral contusion [OR], spinal cord compression)
Vulnerable patients (Patient referred to in Articles L. 1121-5 to L. 1121-8 and L. 1122-1-2 of the French Public Health Code)
