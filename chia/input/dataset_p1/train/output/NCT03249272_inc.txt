Men or women aged 18 years or older
Patients presenting for CMR with the clinical diagnosis of hypertrophic cardiomyopathy [AND] based on left ventricular wall thickness of at least =15 mm in the [NOT] absence of any other cardiac [OR] or systemic cause of hypertrophy
Patients presenting for CMR with the clinical diagnosis of idiopathic dilated cardiomyopathy [AND] based upon left ventricular ejection fraction [OR] =40%, LV end-diastolic diameter=55 mm  [OR] or left ventricular end-systolic diameter =45 mm, and the [NOT] absence of coronary stenoses [AND] on angiography.
