Ovarian [OR], endometrial [OR] or cervical - Gynecologic Oncology Group (GOG) performance score ≤2
If female and of childbearing potential, are willing to use adequate contraception (hormonal [OR], barrier method [OR], abstinence) prior to study entry [AND] and for the duration of study participation.
Normal organ function within 14 days of study entry
Diagnosis of one of the following malignancies:
Metastatic breast cancer (BR)
Metastatic ovarian cancer (OV)
Metastatic endometrial cancer (EM)
Metastatic cervical cancer (CX)
Measurable  metastatic disease (>1cm) [AND] in at least one site other than bone-only
Progression on [OR] or failure to respond [AND] to at least one previous chemotherapy regimen [AND] for metastatic disease
Progression on prior therapy [AND] with a hormonal agent [AND] if estrogen receptor [OR] or progesterone receptor positive [OR], and/or with trastuzumab if HER2-neu positive. If patient has progressed through hormone [OR] or trastuzumab therapy only, [AND] must have received one chemotherapy regimen.
Measurable metastatic disease [AND] as defined by Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST)
Primary tumor must have been diagnosed histologically as either epithelial ovarian cancer [OR], fallopian tube cancer [OR], or primary peritoneal cancer ([NOT] not borderline [OR] or low malignant potential epithelial carcinoma).
Subjects must have failed at least two [AND] previous chemotherapy regimens. Paclitaxel must have been a component of one or both regimens and cisplatin [OR] or carboplatin must have been a component of one or both regimens.
Measurable metastatic disease
Histologically [AND] proven recurrent [OR] or persistent endometrial cancer that is [NOT] not amenable to curative treatment with surgery [OR] and/or radiation therapy [AND] AND has failed 2 previous treatment regimens
Measurable metastatic disease
Histologically [AND] proven recurrent [OR] or persistent squamous cell carcinoma, adenosquamous carcinoma [OR], or adenocarcinoma of the cervix that is [NOT] not amenable to curative treatment [AND] with surgery [OR] and/or radiation therapy [AND] AND has failed 2 previous treatment regimens.
