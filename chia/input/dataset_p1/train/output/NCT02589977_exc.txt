Exclusion Criteria: coronary artery disease [OR], diabetes mellitus [OR], contraindications [OR] to cardiac magnetic resonance imaging (CMR), weight >350 lbs [OR], inability to lie flat for imaging [OR], anemia [OR], contraindications to regadenoson [OR] or aminophylline
HEALTHY: known cardiovascular disease [OR], cardiac risk factors [OR] or use of cardiac medications
