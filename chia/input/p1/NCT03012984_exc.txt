Preoperative history of schizophrenia [OR], epilepsy [OR], parkinsonism [OR] or myasthenia gravis;
Preoperative radio- or chemotherapy [OR];
Inability to communicate in the preoperative period because of coma [OR], profound dementia [OR] or language barrier;
Preoperative obstructive sleep apnea (previously diagnosed as obstructive sleep apnea [OR], or a STOP-Bang score >= 3);
Brain trauma [OR] or neurosurgery;
Preoperative left ventricular ejection fraction [OR] < 30%, sick sinus syndrome [OR], severe sinus bradycardia [OR] (< 50 beats per minute), or second-degree or above atrioventricular block [AND] [NOT] without pacemaker;
Severe hepatic dysfunction (Child-Pugh class C) or severe renal dysfunction (requirement of renal replacement therapy before surgery);
ASA classification >= IV.
