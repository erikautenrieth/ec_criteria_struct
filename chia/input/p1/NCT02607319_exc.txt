Evidence of low ovarian reserve by at least one of the following: AMH [OR] = 1,5 ng/mL and/or basal CD 3 FSH [OR] = 10 mIU/mL and/or basal CD 3 Estradiol [OR] = 60 ng/mL and/or previous egg collection yield = 3 oocytes.
Preexisting medical condition (thyroid disease [OR], diabetes mellitus [OR], hypertension [OR], pulmonary conditions [OR], cardiac condition…).
Severe male factor infertility (Total motile sperm count [OR] < 5 million/ml and/or normal WHO morphology <20%).
Hypersensitivity [AND] to Heparin or its derivatives.
Acquired thrombophilia.
Active hemorrhage [OR] or increased risk of bleeding [AND] due to impairment of homeostasis.
Severe impairment of liver [OR] or pancreatic function.
Severe renal insufficiency (Creatinine Clearance < 30 ml/min).
Injuries [OR] to or operations on the central nervous system [OR], eyes [OR] and ears within the last 2 months.
Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation [AND] (DIC) attributable to heparin-induced thrombocytopenia.
Acute bacterial endocarditis [OR] and endocarditis lenta.
Any organic lesion with high risk of bleeding (e.g.: active peptic ulcer [OR], hemorrhagic stroke [OR], cerebral aneurysm [OR] or cerebral neoplasms).
