The inclusion criteria for the clinical trial on lung function testing are as follows:  
1. Individuals at least 18 years old. 
2. Individuals must be symptomatic with respiratory symptoms. 
3. Individuals must complete the UCAP-Q questionnaire and score a ≥ 6% probability of having either asthma or COPD. 
4. Individuals who have given written informed consent to participate in this trial in accordance with local regulations. 
5. Individual must be able to perform pre and post bronchodilator spirometry to measure lung function. 
 Additional inclusion criteria for the randomized controlled trial (RCT): 
 1. Individuals who have undiagnosed airflow obstruction on spirometry testing (i.e. Asthma or COPD) will be asked to participate in the RCT. 
  The exclusion criteria for the clinical trial on lung function testing are as follows:  
  1. Individuals who report a previous diagnosis of asthma, COPD, history of lung cancer, bronchiectasis, interstitial lung disease, cystic fibrosis or other significant diagnosed lung disease. 2. Individuals currently under the care of a Respirologist. 3. Individuals currently using inhaled corticosteroids or long-acting bronchodilators.
