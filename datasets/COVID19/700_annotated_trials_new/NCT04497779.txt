Inclusion Criteria:

PROSPECTIVE COVID-19 CONVALESCENT PLASMA DONORS: COVID-19 convalescent plasma volunteers must meet all regulatory requirements for conventional plasma and FDA's additional considerations for COVID-19 convalescent plasma
PROSPECTIVE COVID-19 CONVALESCENT PLASMA DONORS: Be willing to complete a questionnaire
PROSPECTIVE COVID-19 CONVALESCENT PLASMA DONORS: Be willing to donate blood samples
PROSPECTIVE COVID-19 CONVALESCENT PLASMA DONORS: Permit medical record review
PROSPECTIVE COVID-19 CONVALESCENT PLASMA DONORS: Have documented informed consent

Note: If volunteers can't provide evidence of COVID-19, but are otherwise eligible, then we will test them for SARS-CoV-2 antibodies to confirm eligibility.
It is possible that other therapeutic protocols using CCP will request assistance from us in donor screening; if this occurs, those protocol eligibility requirements will be required by this City of Hope (COH) study to access donor specimens and patient data.

COVID-19 CONVALESCENT BLOOD DOORS WHO CHOOSE NOT TO DONATE CCP: Evidence of COVID-19 documented by a laboratory test either by: a diagnostic test (e.g., a nasopharyngeal [NP] swab) at the time of illness OR a positive serological test for SARS-CoV-2 antibodies after recovery, if prior diagnostic testing was not performed at the time COVID-19 was suspected
COVID-19 CONVALESCENT BLOOD DOORS WHO CHOOSE NOT TO DONATE CCP: Be willing to complete a questionnaire
COVID-19 CONVALESCENT BLOOD DOORS WHO CHOOSE NOT TO DONATE CCP: Be willing to donate blood samples
