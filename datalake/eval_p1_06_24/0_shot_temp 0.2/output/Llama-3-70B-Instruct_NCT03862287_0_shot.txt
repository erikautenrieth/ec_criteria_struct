<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
  -  ASA I-III male [AND] female patients aged between 18 to 80 years old, [AND] who will receive spinal anesthesia for surgeries lasting less than one hour.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Patients younger than 18 years old.
  -  ASA physical status >3.
  -  Immunosuppression [OR] high risk of infection.
  -  Contraindications to receiving spinal anesthesia (e.g. coagulation impairment).
  -  Patients [AND] who have psychosis.
  -  Patients [AND] with preexisting back pain.
  -  Patients [AND] with cognitive impairment.
  -  Allergies to local anesthetics, [OR] analgesics [OR] any medication used in the study.
  -  Patients [AND] with chronic regular opioid usage.
  -  Presence of preexisting neurological symptoms.
  -  Patients [NOT] able to receive spinal anesthesia.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
