Inclusion Criteria:
  - Participants must be: healthy (determined by self-report) [AND] between the ages of 18-55 years old [AND] current cigarette smokers [AND] willing to provide informed consent [AND] able to attend the lab session [AND] abstain from tobacco/nicotine products as required [AND] agree to use designated products according to the study protocol
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Women if they are breast-feeding [OR] test positive for pregnancy (by urinalysis) at screening
  - Individuals who weigh less than 110 pounds
