Inclusion Criteria:
 - Age >18 years
 - Diagnosis of BCR‐ABL positive CML blast crisis according to the WHO criteria:
      - Blasts 20% or more of peripheral blood white cells or bone marrow cells [OR] or
      - Extramedullary blast proliferation [OR] or
      - Large foci [OR] or clusters of blasts in bone marrow biopsy
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Lack of informed consent
