<EDITED_CRITERIA>
Inclusion Criteria:
Nursing home residents with ≥2 of the following risk factors for atrial fibrillation:
  -  age ≥75 years
  -  female sex
  -  obstructive sleep apnea [OR] peripheral vascular disease
  -  diabetes mellitus [OR] obesity (body mass index [BMI] >30 kg/m2)
  -  hypertension [OR] congestive heart failure

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  [NOT] prior diagnosis of AF
  -  [NOT] continuous rhythm monitoring over the past year
  -  [NOT] pacemaker [OR] implantable cardioverter defibrillator in situ
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
