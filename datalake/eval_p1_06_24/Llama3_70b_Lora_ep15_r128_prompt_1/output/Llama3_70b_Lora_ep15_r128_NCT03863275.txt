Inclusion Criteria:
 - Boys [OR] and girls from 7 to 12 years old [AND] with class II skeletal classification Attending a consultation in the dental clinic of the UNICIEO foundation, to be fitted with myofunctional apparatus type Sn1
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Patients who have had previous orthopedic treatment
 - Patients with signs of condyle injuries
 - Patients of Pierre Robin [OR] or any form of cleft
 - Patients with syndromes that associate craniofacial anomalies [OR] and muscle alterations.
