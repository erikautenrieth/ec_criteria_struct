Inclusion Criteria:
 - a planned operation of coronary artery bypass grafting [AND] with the use of cardiopulmonary bypass
Exclusion Criteria:
 - emergency operations [OR] or re-operations;
 - a known pathology [OR] of the urinary tract [OR] renal failure;
 - chronic use [AND] of the following medications: iron, non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs), immunosuppression, or steroids [AND] in the preoperative period;
 - polycythemia, porphyria or pathological hemoglobin species [NOT] in anamnesis;
 - preoperative signs [OR] of hepatic failure;
 - active autoimmune [OR] or neoplastic diseases, active infection;
 - anticipated significant bleeding [OR] suggesting the use of blood-derived products during the operation [AND] afterward
 - acute myocardial infarction [NOT] after operation
