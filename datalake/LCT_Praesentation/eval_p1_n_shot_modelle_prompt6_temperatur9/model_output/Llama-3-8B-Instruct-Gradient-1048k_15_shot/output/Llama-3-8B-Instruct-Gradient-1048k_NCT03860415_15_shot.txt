Inclusion Criteria:
 - Chronic (more than three months) ESBL colonization [AND] of the same strain as determined by
 - species, phenotype [OR] and antibiogram.
 - Patients must be able to swallow sachets.
 - Must be able to speak [AND] and understand Swedish.
 - Must have a permanent residence in Sweden.
 - Must be able to sign informed consent in Swedish.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [NOT] Immunosuppression (i.e. chemotheraphy, treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors).
 - [NOT] In patient care.
 - immunodeficiency
 - [NOT] psychiatric disorder
 - alcohol [OR] or substance abuse
 - dementia
 - [NOT] Invasive catheters.
