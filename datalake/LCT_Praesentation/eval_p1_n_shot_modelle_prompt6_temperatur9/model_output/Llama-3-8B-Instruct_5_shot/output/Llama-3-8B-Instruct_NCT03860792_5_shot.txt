Inclusion Criteria:
  - Diagnosis of AD by current McKhann et al. criteria [AND] and CDR global score of 0.5 [OR] or 1
  - Agreed cooperation from an appropriate study partner [AND] and speaks English as primary language
  - Age 50 [OR] to 90
  - No medication changes within the past 30 days [NOT]

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Resides in a nursing home [OR] or dementia special care unit [AND] or cannot control diet
  - A potentially confounding serious medical risk [AND] including insulin-requiring diabetes, cancer requiring chemotherapy [OR] or radiation within the past 5 years [AND] or a recent cardiac event (i.e. heart attack, angioplasty, etc.) [NOT]
  - Participating in another clinical trial [OR] or using an investigational drug [OR] or therapy within 30 days of the Screening Visit [AND] or
  - A history of renal stones [NOT]
