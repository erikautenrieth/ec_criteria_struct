Inclusion Criteria:
 - Non-cardiac surgery patients;
 - Age is greater than or equal to 60 years old;
 - Han Nationality, [AND] mother tongue is chinese;
 - The MMSE score: Illiteracy is greater than or equal to 17 points, primary school is greater than or equal to 20 points, higher secondary school is more than 24 points;
 - The people [AND] signed informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - The diagnosis of preoperative delirium;
 - The diagnosis of Unstable hypertension (e.g., pheochromocytoma or aortic dissection);
 - Severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease or congestive heart failure;
 - Severe liver [OR] and kidney dysfunction;
