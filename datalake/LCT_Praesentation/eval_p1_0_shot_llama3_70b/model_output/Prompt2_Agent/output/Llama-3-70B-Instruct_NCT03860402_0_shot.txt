Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Among the pregnant women who were administered cesarean section in this study [AND] who agreed to participate in this study.

Exclusion Criteria:
  -  Fetal distress requiring fast delivery.
  -  Patients [AND] hypersensitivity [OR] allergic response [OR] resistance to the drugs used in this study (fentanyl [OR] ropivacaine [OR] lidocaine).
  -  Patients [NOT] possible for regional anesthesia.
  -  Patients [NOT] refused to participate in this study.
  -  Patients [NOT] able to read [OR] understand the agreement.
  -  Patients whose weight is less than 50kg [OR] exceeds 100kg.
