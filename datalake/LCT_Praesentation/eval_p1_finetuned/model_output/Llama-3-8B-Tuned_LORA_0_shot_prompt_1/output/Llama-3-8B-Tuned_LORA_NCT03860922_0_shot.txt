Inclusion Criteria:
 - patients in different age groups [AND] and both sex referred from orthopedic outpatient clinic with chronic ankle pain [AND] and suspected clinically to have impingement syndrome.
Exclusion Criteria:
 1. Inflammatory arthritis of ankle joint, [OR] infection in ankle joint, [OR] neoplasm around ankle joint.
 2. Patients with contraindications for MRI, e.g. an implanted magnetic device, [OR] pacemakers [OR] or claustrophobia.
