Inclusion Criteria:
 - Patients aged 18-74 [AND] receiving therapy for active ulcerative colitis confirmed by Mayo Score assessment [AND] with evidence of colonic inflammation.
Exclusion Criteria:
 - Evidence of Crohn's disease [OR] indeterminate colitis [OR] presence of bacterial [OR] or parasitic infection.
 - Patients receiving biologic agents [AND] experimental agents are [NOT] excluded.
