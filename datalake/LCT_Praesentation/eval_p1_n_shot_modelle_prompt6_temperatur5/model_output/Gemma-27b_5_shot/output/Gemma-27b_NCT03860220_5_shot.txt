Inclusion Criteria:
 - [AND] 145 mmHg ≤ msSBP ≤ 190 mmHg
 - [AND] Triglycerides < 500 mg/dL
 - [AND] LDL-C ≤ 250 mg/dL
Exclusion Criteria:
 - [AND] sSBP ≥ 20mmHg [AND] sDBP ≥ 10mmHg
 - [AND] symptomatic orthostatic hypotension [AND] secondary/iatrogenic hypertension [AND] dyslipidemia
 - [AND] history of moderate to severe cerebral ischemia, cerebral hemorrhage, transient ischemic attack, myocardial infarction, [OR] unstable angina in the past 6 months; [AND] severe heart failure (New York Heart Association functional class III [AND] IV)
 - [AND] hypersensitivity to telmisartan [OR] rosuvastatin
 - [AND] history of angioedema after treatment with angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors [OR] ARBs; [AND] creatinine phosphokinase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal
 - [AND] estimated glomerular filtration rate ≤30 mL/min; [AND] aspartate aminotransferase [AND] alanine aminotransferase levels ≥3 times the upper limit of normal
 - [AND] potassium levels >5.5 mmol/L; [OR] [NOT] any diseases that could affect the results of the study.
