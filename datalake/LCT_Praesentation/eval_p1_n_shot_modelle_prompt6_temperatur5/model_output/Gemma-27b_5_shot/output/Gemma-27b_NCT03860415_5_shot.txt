Inclusion Criteria:
 -  Chronic (more than three months) ESBL colonization [AND] of the same strain as determined by species, phenotype [AND] and antibiogram.
 -  Patients [AND] must be able to swallow sachets.
 -  [AND] Must be able to speak [AND] understand Swedish.
 -  [AND] Must have a permanent residence in Sweden.
 -  [AND] Must be able to sign informed consent in Swedish.
Exclusion Criteria:
 -  Immunosuppression (i.e. chemotheraphy, treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors).
 -  In patient care.
 -  immunodeficiency
 -  psychiatric disorder
 -  alcohol [OR] or substance abuse
 -  dementia
 -  Invasive catheters.
