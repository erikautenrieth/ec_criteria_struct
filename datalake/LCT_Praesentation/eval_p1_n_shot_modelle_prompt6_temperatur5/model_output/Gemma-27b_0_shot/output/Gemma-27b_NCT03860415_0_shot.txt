Inclusion Criteria:
  - Chronic [AND] more than three months ESBL colonization of the same strain as determined by
  - species, phenotype [OR] antibiogram.
  - Patients must be able to swallow sachets.
  - [AND] must be able to speak [AND] understand Swedish.
  - [AND] must have a permanent residence in Sweden.
  - [AND] must be able to sign informed consent in Swedish.
Exclusion Criteria:
  - Immunosuppression (i.e. chemotheraphy, treatment with TNF-alpha-inhibitors).
  - [OR] In patient care.
  - [OR] immunodeficiency
  - [OR] psychiatric disorder
  - [OR] alcohol [OR] substance abuse
  - [OR] dementia
  - [OR] Invasive catheters.
