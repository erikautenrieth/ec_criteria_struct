  Inclusion Criteria:
  -  Non-cardiac surgery patients [AND] Age is greater than or equal to 60 years old [OR]  Han Nationality, mother tongue is Chinese [OR]  The MMSE score: Illiteracy is greater than or equal to 17 points [OR]  Primary school is greater than or equal to 20 points [OR]  Higher secondary school is more than 24 points [AND]  The people signed informed consent.
Exclusion Criteria:
  -  The diagnosis of preoperative delirium [NOT] 
       -  The diagnosis Unstable hypertension (such as: pheochromocytoma or aortic dissection) [OR]  Severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [OR]  Congestive heart failure [AND]  Severe liver and kidney dysfunction.
