  Inclusion Criteria:
<EDITED_CRITERIA>
 - Patients in different age groups [AND] both sex referred from orthopedic outpatient clinic with chronic ankle pain [AND] suspected clinically to have impingement syndrome.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>

Exclusion Criteria:
<EDITED_CRITERIA>
 - Inflammatory arthritis of ankle joint [OR] infection in ankle joint [OR] neoplasm around ankle joint.
 - Patients with contraindications for MRI [OR] e.g. an implanted magnetic device [OR] pacemakers [OR] claustrophobia.
</EDITED_CRITERIA>
