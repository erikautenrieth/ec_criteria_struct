Inclusion Criteria:
 - 145 mmHg [OR] ≤ msSBP ≤ 190 mmHg
 - Triglycerides [NOT] < 500 mg/dL
 - LDL-C [OR] ≤ 250 mg/dL
Exclusion Criteria:
 - sSBP [NOT] ≥ 20mmHg [AND] and sDBP [NOT] ≥ 10mmHg
 - symptomatic orthostatic hypotension [OR] and secondary/iatrogenic hypertension [OR] and dyslipidemia
 - history of moderate to severe cerebral ischemia, cerebral hemorrhage, transient ischemic attack, myocardial infarction, [OR] or unstable angina in the past 6 months; severe heart failure (New York Heart Association functional class III and IV)
 - hypersensitivity to telmisartan [OR] or rosuvastatin
 - history of angioedema after treatment with angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors [OR] or ARBs; creatinine phosphokinase levels [NOT] ≥3 times the upper limit of normal
 - estimated glomerular filtration rate [NOT] ≤30 mL/min;
 - aspartate aminotransferase and alanine aminotransferase levels [NOT] ≥3 times the upper limit of normal
 - potassium levels [NOT] >5.5 mmol/L; or any diseases that could affect the results of the study.<|im_end|>
